Solved questions from this paper

A few questions with their official answers and worked solutions. All 75 are in the online test.

  1. Question 1 · Mathematics · Relations and Functions
    Let $f : \mathbf{R} \to \mathbf{R}$ be defined as $f(x) = \dfrac{2x^2-3x+2}{3x^2+x+3}$. Then $f$ is :

    1. both one-one and onto
    2. one-one but not onto
    3. onto but not one-one
    4. neither one-one nor onto
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    $3x^2+x+3>0$ for all $x$, so $f$ is continuous on $\mathbf{R}$, and $f(x)\to\dfrac23$ as $x\to\infty$.
    $f(0)=\dfrac23$, $f(1)=\dfrac17$: every value between $\dfrac17$ and $\dfrac23$ is taken once in $(0,1)$ and again in $(1,\infty)$, so $f$ is not one-one.
    Range: $y(3x^2+x+3)=2x^2-3x+2 \Rightarrow (3y-2)x^2+(y+3)x+(3y-2)=0$; real $x$ needs $(y+3)^2\ge4(3y-2)^2$, a bounded interval of $y$.
    So the range is a bounded interval, not $\mathbf{R}$: $f$ is not onto.
    Hence $f$ is neither one-one nor onto.

  2. Question 2 · Mathematics · Complex Numbers
    Consider the quadratic equation $(n^2-2n+2)x^2-3x+(n^2-2n+2)^2=0$, $n \in \mathbf{R}$. Let $\alpha$ be the minimum value of the product of its roots and $\beta$ be the maximum value of the sum of its roots. Then the sum of the first six terms of the G.P., whose first term is $\alpha$ and the common ratio is $\dfrac{\alpha}{\beta}$, is :

    1. $\dfrac{61}{37}$
    2. $\dfrac{121}{81}$
    3. $\dfrac{364}{243}$
    4. $\dfrac{1093}{729}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    Let $m=n^2-2n+2=(n-1)^2+1\ge1$.
    Product of roots $=\dfrac{m^2}{m}=m$, so $\alpha=1$ (at $n=1$).
    Sum of roots $=\dfrac3m$, so $\beta=3$.
    G.P.: first term $1$, ratio $\dfrac13$; $S_6=\dfrac{1-(1/3)^6}{1-1/3}=\dfrac{728}{729}\cdot\dfrac32=\dfrac{364}{243}$.

  3. Question 26 · Physics · Units and Measurements
    The percentage error in the calculated volume of a sphere, if there is 2% error in its diameter measurement, is _________.

    1. $1$
    2. $2$
    3. $6$
    4. $8$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    $V=\dfrac{\pi D^3}{6} \Rightarrow \dfrac{\Delta V}{V}=3\,\dfrac{\Delta D}{D}$.
    Percentage error $=3\times2\%=6\%$.

  4. Question 28 · Physics · System of Particles and Rotational Motion
    A solid sphere $(A)$ of mass $5m$ and a spherical shell $(B)$ of mass $m$, both having same radius, are placed on a rough surface. When a force of same magnitude is applied tangentially at the highest points of $A$ and $B$, they start rolling without slipping with an acceleration of $a_A$ and $a_B$, respectively. The ratio of $a_A$ and $a_B$ is __________.

    1. $5 : 21$
    2. $6 : 10$
    3. $21 : 25$
    4. $1 : 5$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    Let $I=kMR^2$ and friction $f$ act forwards: $F+f=Ma$, $(F-f)R=kMR^2\cdot\dfrac aR$.
    Adding: $2F=Ma(1+k) \Rightarrow a=\dfrac{2F}{M(1+k)}$.
    Solid sphere ($M=5m$, $k=\tfrac25$): $a_A=\dfrac{2F}{7m}$.
    Shell ($M=m$, $k=\tfrac23$): $a_B=\dfrac{6F}{5m}$.
    $\dfrac{a_A}{a_B}=\dfrac{2}{7}\cdot\dfrac56=\dfrac{5}{21}$.

  5. Question 51 · Chemistry · Some Basic Concepts of Chemistry
    Which of the following contain the same number of atoms ?
    (Given : Molar mass in g mol$^{-1}$ of H, He, O and S are 1, 4, 16 and 32 respectively)
    A. 2 g of $\mathrm{O_2}$ gas
    B. 4 g of $\mathrm{SO_2}$ gas
    C. 1400 mL of $\mathrm{O_2}$ at STP
    D. 0.05 L of He at STP
    E. 0.0625 mol of $\mathrm{H_2}$ gas
    Choose the correct answer from the options given below :

    1. A and B only
    2. B and C only
    3. C and D only
    4. A, C and E only
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    A: $\dfrac{2}{32}=\dfrac1{16}$ mol $\mathrm{O_2}$ → $\dfrac18$ mol atoms.
    B: $\dfrac4{64}=\dfrac1{16}$ mol $\mathrm{SO_2}$ → $\dfrac3{16}$ mol atoms.
    C: $\dfrac{1.4}{22.4}=\dfrac1{16}$ mol $\mathrm{O_2}$ → $\dfrac18$ mol atoms.
    D: $\dfrac{0.05}{22.4}\approx0.0022$ mol atoms.
    E: $0.0625=\dfrac1{16}$ mol $\mathrm{H_2}$ → $\dfrac18$ mol atoms.
    A, C and E have the same number of atoms.

  6. Question 52 · Chemistry · Structure of Atom
    The Bohr radius of a hydrogen like species is 70.53 pm. The species and the stationary state (n) are respectively
    (Given : Hydrogen atom Bohr radius is 52.9 pm)

    1. $\mathrm{Li^{2+}}$, 3
    2. $\mathrm{He^{+}}$, 3
    3. $\mathrm{He^{+}}$, 2
    4. $\mathrm{Li^{2+}}$, 2
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    $r_n=52.9\,\dfrac{n^2}{Z}$ pm.
    $\dfrac{n^2}{Z}=\dfrac{70.53}{52.9}=\dfrac43$, so $n=2$, $Z=3$.
    The species is $\mathrm{Li^{2+}}$ in $n=2$.
