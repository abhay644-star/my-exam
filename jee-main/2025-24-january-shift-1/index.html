Solved questions from this paper

A few questions with their official answers and worked solutions. All 75 are in the online test.

  1. Question 1 · Mathematics · Relations and Functions
    Let $f(x)=\dfrac{2^{x+2}+16}{2^{2x+1}+2^{x+4}+32}$. Then the value of $8\left(f\left(\dfrac{1}{15}\right)+f\left(\dfrac{2}{15}\right)+\ldots+f\left(\dfrac{59}{15}\right)\right)$ is equal to

    1. $118$
    2. $108$
    3. $102$
    4. $92$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    Put $t=2^x$: $f(x)=\dfrac{4(t+4)}{2(t+4)^2}=\dfrac{2}{t+4}$.
    For $4-x$, $2^{4-x}=\dfrac{16}{t}$, so $f(4-x)=\dfrac{2t}{16+4t}=\dfrac{t}{2(t+4)}$. Hence $f(x)+f(4-x)=\dfrac12$.
    The arguments $\dfrac{k}{15}$ and $\dfrac{60-k}{15}$ add to 4, giving 29 pairs ($k=1,\ldots,29$) and the middle term $f(2)=\dfrac{2}{8}=\dfrac14$.
    Sum $=\dfrac{29}{2}+\dfrac14=\dfrac{59}{4}$, and $8\times\dfrac{59}{4}=118$.

  2. Question 2 · Mathematics · Complex Numbers
    The product of all the rational roots of the equation $(x^2-9x+11)^2-(x-4)(x-5)=3$, is equal to

    1. $7$
    2. $14$
    3. $21$
    4. $28$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    Let $y=x^2-9x$. Then $(x-4)(x-5)=y+20$ and the equation becomes $(y+11)^2-(y+20)-3=0$, i.e. $y^2+21y+98=0$, so $y=-7$ or $y=-14$.
    $x^2-9x+7=0$ has discriminant $53$, so its roots are irrational.
    $x^2-9x+14=0$ gives $x=2,7$.
    Product of the rational roots $=14$.

  3. Question 26 · Physics · Units and Measurements
    For an experimental expression $y=\dfrac{32.3\times1125}{27.4}$, where all the digits are significant. Then to report the value of $y$ we should write

    1. $y=1326.186$
    2. $y=1326.19$
    3. $y=1326.2$
    4. $y=1330$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    $\dfrac{32.3\times1125}{27.4}=1326.19\ldots$
    In multiplication and division the result keeps as many significant figures as the least precise factor: 32.3 and 27.4 have 3.
    So $y=1330$ (3 significant figures).

  4. Question 27 · Physics · System of Particles and Rotational Motion
    An object of mass 'm' is projected from origin in a vertical xy plane at an angle $45^\circ$ with the x-axis with an initial velocity $v_0$. The magnitude and direction of the angular momentum of the object with respect to origin, when it reaches at the maximum height, will be [g is acceleration due to gravity]

    1. $\dfrac{mv_0^3}{4\sqrt2g}$ along positive z-axis
    2. $\dfrac{mv_0^3}{2\sqrt2g}$ along positive z-axis
    3. $\dfrac{mv_0^3}{4\sqrt2g}$ along negative z-axis
    4. $\dfrac{mv_0^3}{2\sqrt2g}$ along negative z-axis
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    At the top the velocity is $\dfrac{v_0}{\sqrt2}\hat i$ and the height is $H=\dfrac{v_0^2\sin^245^\circ}{2g}=\dfrac{v_0^2}{4g}$.
    $\vec L=m\,\vec r\times\vec v$; only $y=H$ contributes: $\vec L=m\left(H\hat j\right)\times\left(\dfrac{v_0}{\sqrt2}\hat i\right)=-\dfrac{mHv_0}{\sqrt2}\hat k$.
    $|\vec L|=\dfrac{mv_0^3}{4\sqrt2g}$, along the negative z-axis.

  5. Question 51 · Chemistry · Chemical Bonding and Molecular Structure
    Which of the following linear combination of atomic orbitals will lead to formation of molecular orbitals in homonuclear diatomic molecules [internuclear axis in z- direction] ?
    A. $2p_z$ and $2p_x$
    B. $2s$ and $2p_x$
    C. $3d_{xy}$ and $3d_{x^2-y^2}$
    D. $2s$ and $2p_z$
    E. $2p_z$ and $3d_{x^2-y^2}$
    Choose the correct answer from the options given below:

    1. A and B Only
    2. C and D Only
    3. D Only
    4. E Only
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    Atomic orbitals combine only if they have the same symmetry about the internuclear (z) axis, giving non-zero net overlap.
    $2s$ and $2p_z$ are both symmetric about z and overlap along the axis (D).
    $2p_z$/$2p_x$, $2s$/$2p_x$, $3d_{xy}$/$3d_{x^2-y^2}$ and $2p_z$/$3d_{x^2-y^2}$ all have zero net overlap.
    D only.

  6. Question 53 · Chemistry · Thermodynamics
    Let us consider an endothermic reaction which is non-spontaneous at the freezing point of water. However, the reaction is spontaneous at boiling point of water. Choose the correct option.

    1. $\Delta H$ is (-ve) but $\Delta S$ is (+ve)
    2. Both $\Delta H$ and $\Delta S$ are (+ve)
    3. Both $\Delta H$ and $\Delta S$ are (-ve)
    4. $\Delta H$ is (+ve) but $\Delta S$ is (-ve)
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    Endothermic means $\Delta H>0$. $\Delta G=\Delta H-T\Delta S$ is positive at 273 K but negative at 373 K, so $\Delta G$ falls as $T$ rises, which needs $\Delta S>0$.
    Both $\Delta H$ and $\Delta S$ are positive.
