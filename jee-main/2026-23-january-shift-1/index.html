Solved questions from this paper

A few questions with their official answers and worked solutions. All 75 are in the online test.

  1. Question 1 · Mathematics · Relations and Functions
    Let $A=\{-2, -1, 0, 1, 2, 3, 4\}$. Let R be a relation on A defined by $xRy$ if and only if $2x+y\le2$. Let $l$ be the number of elements in R. Let m and n be the minimum number of elements required to be added in R to make it reflexive and symmetric relations respectively. Then $l+m+n$ is equal to :

    1. $35$
    2. $32$
    3. $33$
    4. $34$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    For each $x$, count $y\in A$ with $y\le2-2x$: $x=-2$: 7; $x=-1$: 7; $x=0$: 5; $x=1$: 3; $x=2$: 1; $x=3,4$: 0. So $l=23$.
    $(x,x)\in R$ needs $3x\le2$, i.e. $x\le0$; the pairs $(1,1),(2,2),(3,3),(4,4)$ are missing, so $m=4$.
    Pairs in R whose reverse is not in R: $(-2,3),(-2,4),(-1,2),(-1,3),(-1,4),(0,2)$, so $n=6$.
    $l+m+n=23+4+6=33$.

  2. Question 2 · Mathematics · Complex Numbers
    If $\alpha$ and $\beta$ $(\alpha<\beta)$ are the roots of the equation $\left(-2+\sqrt{3}\right)\left(\left|\sqrt{x}-3\right|\right)+\left(x-6\sqrt{x}\right)+\left(9-2\sqrt{3}\right)=0$, $x\ge0$, then $\sqrt{\dfrac{\beta}{\alpha}}+\sqrt{\alpha\beta}$ is equal to :

    1. $8$
    2. $9$
    3. $10$
    4. $11$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    Put $u=\left|\sqrt{x}-3\right|$. Since $x-6\sqrt{x}+9=u^2$, the equation is $u^2+(\sqrt3-2)u-2\sqrt3=0$, i.e. $(u-2)(u+\sqrt3)=0$.
    So $u=2$: $\sqrt{x}=5$ or $1$, giving $x=25$ or $1$. Thus $\alpha=1$, $\beta=25$.
    $\sqrt{\dfrac{\beta}{\alpha}}+\sqrt{\alpha\beta}=5+5=10$.

  3. Question 26 · Physics · Units and Measurements
    Four persons measure the length of a rod as 20.00 cm, 19.75 cm, 17.01 cm and 18.25 cm. The relative error in the measurement of average length of the rod is :

    1. $0.08$
    2. $0.18$
    3. $0.06$
    4. $0.24$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    Mean $=\dfrac{75.01}{4}=18.75$ cm (18.7525).
    Absolute deviations: 1.25, 1.00, 1.74, 0.50; mean absolute error $\approx1.12$ cm.
    Relative error $=\dfrac{1.12}{18.75}\approx0.06$.

  4. Question 28 · Physics · System of Particles and Rotational Motion
    Two small balls with masses $m$ and $2m$ are attached to both ends of a rigid rod of length $d$ and negligible mass. If angular momentum of this system is $L$ about an axis $(A)$ passing through its centre of mass and perpendicular to the rod then angular velocity of the system about $A$ is :

    1. $\dfrac{2L}{5md^2}$
    2. $\dfrac{2L}{md^2}$
    3. $\dfrac32\dfrac{L}{md^2}$
    4. $\dfrac43\dfrac{L}{md^2}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    The centre of mass is $\dfrac{2d}{3}$ from $m$ and $\dfrac d3$ from $2m$.
    $I=m\left(\dfrac{2d}{3}\right)^2+2m\left(\dfrac d3\right)^2=\dfrac{2md^2}{3}$.
    $\omega=\dfrac LI=\dfrac32\dfrac{L}{md^2}$.

  5. Question 51 · Chemistry · Structure of Atom
    Which of the following statements regarding the energy of the stationary state is true in the following one - electron systems ?

    1. $-1.09\times10^{-18}$ J for second orbit of H atom.
    2. $+2.18\times10^{-18}$ J for second orbit of $\mathrm{He^+}$ ion
    3. $+8.72\times10^{-18}$ J for first orbit of $\mathrm{He^+}$ ion
    4. $-2.18\times10^{-18}$ J for third orbit of $\mathrm{Li^{2+}}$ ion
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    $E_n=-2.18\times10^{-18}\dfrac{Z^2}{n^2}$ J, always negative.
    H, $n=2$: $-5.45\times10^{-19}$ J. $\mathrm{He^+}$, $n=2$: $-2.18\times10^{-18}$ J; $n=1$: $-8.72\times10^{-18}$ J (both options have the wrong sign).
    $\mathrm{Li^{2+}}$, $n=3$: $-2.18\times10^{-18}\times\dfrac99=-2.18\times10^{-18}$ J: true.

  6. Question 53 · Chemistry · Chemical Bonding and Molecular Structure
    Identify the molecule (X) with maximum number of lone pairs of electrons (obtained using Lewis dot structure) among $\mathrm{HNO_3}$, $\mathrm{H_2SO_4}$, $\mathrm{NF_3}$ and $\mathrm{O_3}$. Choose the correct bond angle made by the central atom of the molecule (X).

    1. $116^\circ$
    2. $102^\circ$
    3. $107^\circ$
    4. $120^\circ$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    Lone pairs: $\mathrm{HNO_3}$ 7 (2 + 2 + 3 on the oxygens); $\mathrm{H_2SO_4}$ 8 (2 on each O); $\mathrm{NF_3}$ 10 (3 on each F + 1 on N); $\mathrm{O_3}$ 6.
    X is $\mathrm{NF_3}$, a pyramidal molecule with F-N-F angle about $102^\circ$.
