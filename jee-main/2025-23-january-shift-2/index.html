Solved questions from this paper

A few questions with their official answers and worked solutions. All 75 are in the online test.

  1. Question 1 · Mathematics · Sets
    Let $A=\{(x, y)\in\mathbf{R}\times\mathbf{R}:|x+y|\geq3\}$ and $B=\{(x, y)\in\mathbf{R}\times\mathbf{R}:|x|+|y|\leq3\}$.
    If $C=\{(x, y)\in A\cap B: x=0$ or $y=0\}$, then $\displaystyle\sum_{(x, y)\in C}|x+y|$ is :

    1. $24$
    2. $18$
    3. $15$
    4. $12$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    On $x=0$: $|y|\ge3$ and $|y|\le3$, so $y=\pm3$. On $y=0$: likewise $x=\pm3$.
    $C=\{(0,3),(0,-3),(3,0),(-3,0)\}$, and each point has $|x+y|=3$.
    Sum $=4\times3=12$.

  2. Question 2 · Mathematics · Relations and Functions
    Let $X=\mathbf{R}\times\mathbf{R}$. Define a relation R on X as :
    $(a_1, b_1)\,R\,(a_2, b_2)\Leftrightarrow b_1=b_2$.
    Statement I : R is an equivalence relation.
    Statement II : For some $(a, b)\in X$, the set $S=\{(x, y)\in X:(x, y)\,R\,(a, b)\}$ represents a line parallel to $y=x$.
    In the light of the above statements, choose the correct answer from the options given below :

    1. Both Statement I and Statement II are true
    2. Both Statement I and Statement II are false
    3. Statement I is true but Statement II is false
    4. Statement I is false but Statement II is true
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    "Same second coordinate" is reflexive, symmetric and transitive, so R is an equivalence relation: Statement I is true.
    $S=\{(x,y): y=b\}$ is the horizontal line $y=b$, which is parallel to the $x$-axis, never to $y=x$: Statement II is false.

  3. Question 26 · Physics · Units and Measurements
    Match List - I with List - II.

    List - IList - II
    (A) Permeability of free space(I) $[\mathrm{ML^2T^{-2}}]$
    (B) Magnetic field(II) $[\mathrm{MT^{-2}A^{-1}}]$
    (C) Magnetic moment(III) $[\mathrm{MLT^{-2}A^{-2}}]$
    (D) Torsional constant(IV) $[\mathrm{L^2A}]$
    Choose the correct answer from the options given below :

    1. (A)-(III), (B)-(II), (C)-(IV), (D)-(I)
    2. (A)-(IV), (B)-(III), (C)-(I), (D)-(II)
    3. (A)-(I), (B)-(IV), (C)-(II), (D)-(III)
    4. (A)-(II), (B)-(I), (C)-(III), (D)-(IV)
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    $\mu_0$: from $F=\dfrac{\mu_0I_1I_2l}{2\pi d}$, $[\mu_0]=[\mathrm{MLT^{-2}A^{-2}}]$ (III).
    $B=\dfrac{F}{Il}$: $[\mathrm{MT^{-2}A^{-1}}]$ (II).
    Magnetic moment $=IA$: $[\mathrm{L^2A}]$ (IV).
    Torsional constant $=\dfrac{\text{torque}}{\text{angle}}$: $[\mathrm{ML^2T^{-2}}]$ (I).

  4. Question 28 · Physics · Motion in a Plane
    A ball having kinetic energy KE, is projected at an angle of $60^\circ$ from the horizontal. What will be the kinetic energy of ball at the highest point of its flight ?

    1. $\dfrac{(KE)}{2}$
    2. $\dfrac{(KE)}{8}$
    3. $\dfrac{(KE)}{16}$
    4. $\dfrac{(KE)}{4}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    At the highest point only the horizontal component $u\cos60^\circ=\dfrac u2$ remains.
    KE there $=\dfrac12m\left(\dfrac u2\right)^2=\dfrac{KE}{4}$.

  5. Question 51 · Chemistry · Structure of Atom
    Given below are two statements :
    Statement (I) : For a given shell, the total number of allowed orbitals is given by $n^2$.
    Statement (II) : For any subshell, the spatial orientation of the orbitals is given by $-l$ to $+l$ values including zero.
    In the light of the above statements, choose the correct answer from the options given below :

    1. Both Statement I and Statement II are true
    2. Both Statement I and Statement II are false
    3. Statement I is true but Statement II is false
    4. Statement I is false but Statement II is true
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    Shell $n$ has subshells $l=0$ to $n-1$ with $2l+1$ orbitals each: total $\sum(2l+1)=n^2$. Statement I is true.
    The orientation of the orbitals is given by $m_l=-l,\ldots,0,\ldots,+l$. Statement II is true.

  6. Question 52 · Chemistry · Thermodynamics
    The effect of temperature on spontaneity of reactions are represented as :

    $\Delta H$$\Delta S$TemperatureSpontaneity
    (A)+$-$any TNon spontaneous
    (B)++low Tspontaneous
    (C)$-$$-$low TNon spontaneous
    (D)$-$+any Tspontaneous
    The incorrect combinations are :

    1. (B) and (D) only
    2. (A) and (C) only
    3. (B) and (C) only
    4. (A) and (D) only
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    Use $\Delta G=\Delta H-T\Delta S$.
    (A) $\Delta H>0$, $\Delta S<0$: $\Delta G>0$ always, non-spontaneous: correct.
    (B) $\Delta H>0$, $\Delta S>0$: spontaneous only at high T, so "spontaneous at low T" is incorrect.
    (C) $\Delta H<0$, $\Delta S<0$: spontaneous at low T, so "non-spontaneous at low T" is incorrect.
    (D) $\Delta H<0$, $\Delta S>0$: always spontaneous: correct.
    Incorrect: (B) and (C).
