Solved questions from this paper

A few questions with their official answers and worked solutions. All 75 are in the online test.

  1. Question 1 · Mathematics · Complex Numbers
    Let $\alpha, \beta$ be the roots of the equation $x^2-3x+r=0$, and $\dfrac{\alpha}{2}, 2\beta$ be the roots of the equation $x^2+3x+r=0$. If the roots of the equation $x^2+6x=m$ are $2\alpha+\beta+2r$ and $\alpha-2\beta-\dfrac{r}{2}$, then $m$ is equal to :

    1. $-135$
    2. $-567$
    3. $135$
    4. $567$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    From the first equation: $\alpha+\beta=3$, $\alpha\beta=r$. From the second: $\dfrac{\alpha}{2}+2\beta=-3$ (and the product is again $\alpha\beta=r$).
    $\alpha=3-\beta \Rightarrow \dfrac{3-\beta}{2}+2\beta=-3 \Rightarrow \beta=-3,\ \alpha=6,\ r=-18$.
    Roots of $x^2+6x-m=0$: $2\alpha+\beta+2r=12-3-36=-27$ and $\alpha-2\beta-\dfrac r2=6+6+9=21$ (sum $-6$, as required).
    Product $=-m \Rightarrow -m=(-27)(21)=-567 \Rightarrow m=567$.

  2. Question 3 · Mathematics · Determinants
    If the system of equations

    $\displaystyle x+5y+6z=4,\quad 2x+3y+4z=7,\quad x+6y+az=b$
    has infinitely many solutions, then the point $(a, b)$ lies on the line

    1. $y-x=3$
    2. $x-y=3$
    3. $x+y=11$
    4. $x+y=12$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    For infinitely many solutions the third equation must be a combination $p(\text{I})+q(\text{II})$.
    Coefficients of $x, y$: $p+2q=1$, $5p+3q=6 \Rightarrow q=-\dfrac17,\ p=\dfrac97$.
    $a=6p+4q=\dfrac{54-4}{7}=\dfrac{50}{7}$, $b=4p+7q=\dfrac{36-7}{7}=\dfrac{29}{7}$.
    $a-b=\dfrac{21}{7}=3$, so $(a,b)$ lies on $x-y=3$.

  3. Question 26 · Physics · Units and Measurements
    Dimensions of universal gravitational constant (G) in terms of Planck's constant $(h)$, distance $(L)$, mass $(M)$ and time $(T)$ are __________.

    1. $[hTLM^{-2}]$
    2. $[hT^{-1}LM^{-2}]$
    3. $[hTL^2M^{-2}]$
    4. $[h^{-1}T^{-1}LM^{-2}]$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    $[G]=[M^{-1}L^3T^{-2}]$ and $[h]=[ML^2T^{-1}]$.
    Let $G=h^aL^bM^cT^d$: mass $a+c=-1$, length $2a+b=3$, time $-a+d=-2$.
    With $a=1$: $c=-2$, $b=1$, $d=-1$.
    $[G]=[hT^{-1}LM^{-2}]$.

  4. Question 27 · Physics · Laws of Motion
    A 0.5 kg mass is in contact against the inner wall of a cylindrical drum of radius 4 m rotating about its vertical axis. The minimum rotational speed of the drum to enable the mass to remain stuck to the wall (without falling) is 5 rad/s. The coefficient of friction between the drum's inner wall surface and mass is __________. (Take $g=10$ m/s$^2$)

    1. $0.1$
    2. $0.5$
    3. $0.7$
    4. $0.3$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    The wall supplies the normal force $N=m\omega^2r$; friction must balance the weight: $\mu m\omega^2 r \ge mg$.
    At the minimum speed: $\mu=\dfrac{g}{\omega^2 r}=\dfrac{10}{25\times4}=0.1$.

  5. Question 51 · Chemistry · Some Basic Concepts of Chemistry
    The ratio of mass percentage (w/w) of C : H in a hydrocarbon is 12 : 1. It has two carbon atoms. The weight (in g) of CO$_2$(g) formed when 3.38 g of this hydrocarbon is completely burnt in oxygen is : (Given : Molar mass in g mol$^{-1}$ C : 12, H : 1, O : 16)

    1. $5.68$
    2. $11.44$
    3. $22.74$
    4. $17.05$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    Mole ratio C : H $=\dfrac{12}{12}:\dfrac11=1:1$; with two C atoms the hydrocarbon is C$_2$H$_2$ (26 g/mol).
    Moles $=\dfrac{3.38}{26}=0.13$; each mole gives 2 mol CO$_2$: $0.26$ mol.
    Mass of CO$_2=0.26\times44=11.44$ g.

  6. Question 52 · Chemistry · Equilibrium
    The first and second ionization constants of a weak dibasic acid H$_2$A are $8.1\times10^{-8}$ and $1.0\times10^{-13}$ respectively. 0.1 mol of H$_2$A was dissolved in 1L of 0.1 M HCl solution. The concentration of HA$^-$ in the resultant solution is :

    1. 0.1 M
    2. $9.53\times10^{-6}$ M
    3. $8.1\times10^{-8}$ M
    4. $1.0\times10^{-13}$ M
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    HCl fixes $[\mathrm{H^+}]=0.1$ M and suppresses ionisation, so $[\mathrm{H_2A}]\approx0.1$ M.
    $K_{a1}=\dfrac{[\mathrm{H^+}][\mathrm{HA^-}]}{[\mathrm{H_2A}]} \Rightarrow [\mathrm{HA^-}]=\dfrac{8.1\times10^{-8}\times0.1}{0.1}=8.1\times10^{-8}$ M.
