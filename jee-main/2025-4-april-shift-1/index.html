Solved questions from this paper

A few questions with their official answers and worked solutions. All 75 are in the online test.

  1. Question 1 · Mathematics · Relations and Functions
    Consider the sets $A=\{(x, y)\in\mathbb{R}\times\mathbb{R}: x^2+y^2=25\}$, $B=\{(x, y)\in\mathbb{R}\times\mathbb{R}: x^2+9y^2=144\}$, $C=\left\{(x, y)\in\mathbb{Z}\times\mathbb{Z}: x^2+y^2\le 4\right\}$ and $D=A\cap B$. The total number of one-one functions from the set $D$ to the set $C$ is:

    1. $15120$
    2. $17160$
    3. $18290$
    4. $19320$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    $D$: subtracting the equations gives $8y^2=119$, so $y^2=\dfrac{119}{8}$ and $x^2=\dfrac{81}{8}$: four points (all sign choices), so $n(D)=4$.
    $C$: lattice points with $x^2+y^2\le4$: $x=0$ gives 5 points, $x=\pm1$ give 3 each, $x=\pm2$ give 1 each, so $n(C)=13$.
    One-one functions from a 4-element set to a 13-element set: $13\cdot12\cdot11\cdot10=17160$.

  2. Question 3 · Mathematics · Complex Numbers
    Consider the equation $x^2+4x-n=0$, where $n\in[20, 100]$ is a natural number. Then the number of all distinct values of $n$, for which the given equation has integral roots, is equal to

    1. $5$
    2. $6$
    3. $7$
    4. $8$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    The roots are $-2\pm\sqrt{4+n}$; they are integers exactly when $4+n=k^2$ for an integer $k$.
    $24\le k^2\le104$ gives $k=5, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10$, i.e. $n=21, 32, 45, 60, 77, 96$.
    Number of values $=6$.

  3. Question 26 · Physics · Waves
    In an experiment with a closed organ pipe, it is filled with water by $\left(\dfrac15\right)$th of its volume. The frequency of the fundamental note will change by

    1. $20\%$
    2. $25\%$
    3. $-20\%$
    4. $-25\%$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    For a pipe closed at one end, $f=\dfrac{v}{4L}$, so $f\propto\dfrac1L$.
    Water fills $\dfrac15$ of the pipe, so the air column becomes $\dfrac45L$ and $f'=\dfrac54f$.
    Change $=+25\%$.

  4. Question 27 · Physics · Units and Measurements
    In an electromagnetic system, the quantity representing the ratio of electric flux and magnetic flux has dimension of $M^PL^QT^RA^S$, where value of 'Q' and 'R' are

    1. $(-2, 2)$
    2. $(1, -1)$
    3. $(3, -5)$
    4. $(-2, 1)$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    Electric flux $\Phi_E=EA$ has unit V m; magnetic flux $\Phi_B=BA$ has unit Wb $=$ V s.
    $\dfrac{\Phi_E}{\Phi_B}$ has unit $\dfrac{\text{V m}}{\text{V s}}=$ m s$^{-1}$, i.e. dimensions $[LT^{-1}]$.
    $Q=1$, $R=-1$.

  5. Question 51 · Chemistry · Structure of Atom
    Which one of the following about an electron occupying the 1s orbital in a hydrogen atom is incorrect?
    (Bohr's radius is represented by $a_0$)

    1. The probability density of finding the electron is maximum at the nucleus
    2. The electron can be found at a distance $2a_0$ from the nucleus
    3. The 1s orbital is spherically symmetrical
    4. The total energy of the electron is maximum when it is at a distance $a_0$ from the nucleus
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    For 1s, $\psi^2$ is greatest at the nucleus (1 is correct); the probability is non-zero at any distance, including $2a_0$ (2 is correct); the orbital is spherically symmetrical (3 is correct).
    The total energy of the electron in the 1s state is fixed ($-13.6$ eV); it does not depend on where the electron is found. $a_0$ is only where the radial probability is greatest. So statement (4) is incorrect.

  6. Question 52 · Chemistry · Chemical Bonding and Molecular Structure
    Which of the following molecule(s) show/s paramagnetic behavior?
    A. $\mathrm{O_2}$
    B. $\mathrm{N_2}$
    C. $\mathrm{F_2}$
    D. $\mathrm{S_2}$
    E. $\mathrm{Cl_2}$
    Choose the correct answer from the options given below:

    1. B Only
    2. A & C only
    3. A & D Only
    4. A & E Only
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    In MO theory $\mathrm{O_2}$ has two unpaired electrons in the $\pi^*$ orbitals, so it is paramagnetic; $\mathrm{S_2}$ (vapour) has the same valence configuration and is also paramagnetic.
    $\mathrm{N_2}$, $\mathrm{F_2}$ and $\mathrm{Cl_2}$ have all electrons paired (diamagnetic).
    Answer: A & D.
