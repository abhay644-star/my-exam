Solved questions from this paper

A few questions with their official answers and worked solutions. All 75 are in the online test.

  1. Question 1 · Mathematics · None
    Let $\mathrm{S}=\{x^3+ax^2+bx+c: a, b, c\in\mathbb{N}$ and $a, b, c\le20\}$ be a set of polynomials. Then the number of polynomials in S, which are divisible by $x^2+2$, is

    1. $6$
    2. $10$
    3. $20$
    4. $120$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    $x^3+ax^2+bx+c=(x^2+2)(x+k)=x^3+kx^2+2x+2k$.
    So $b=2$, $a=k$, $c=2a$, with $2a\le20$: $a=1,\ldots,10$, i.e. 10 polynomials.

  2. Question 2 · Mathematics · Relations and Functions
    If $g(x)=3x^2+2x-3$, $f(0)=-3$ and $4g(f(x))=3x^2-32x+72$, then $f(g(2))$ is equal to:

    1. $\dfrac{25}{6}$
    2. $-\dfrac72$
    3. $\dfrac72$
    4. $-\dfrac{25}{6}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    $4(3f^2+2f-3)=3x^2-32x+72$ gives $12f^2+8f=3x^2-32x+84$, so $f$ is linear: $f(x)=px-3$.
    Constant term: $12(9)-24=84$ (checks). $x^2$: $12p^2=3$; $x$: $-72p+8p=-32$, so $p=\tfrac12$.
    $f(x)=\tfrac x2-3$, $g(2)=13$, $f(13)=\tfrac72$.

  3. Question 26 · Physics · Units and Measurements
    When both jaws of vernier callipers touch each other, zero mark of the vernier scale is right to zero mark of main scale, $4^{\text{th}}$ mark on vernier scale coincides with certain mark on the main scale. While measuring the length of a cylinder, observer observes 15 divisions on main scale and $5^{\text{th}}$ division of vernier scale coincides with a main scale division. Measured length of cylinder is _________mm.
    (Least count of Vernier calliper = 0.1 mm)

    1. $15.9$
    2. $15.5$
    3. $15.1$
    4. $15.4$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    Vernier zero to the right of the main-scale zero means a positive zero error: $+4\times0.1=+0.4$ mm.
    Observed reading $=15+5\times0.1=15.5$ mm.
    Corrected length $=15.5-0.4=15.1$ mm.

  4. Question 27 · Physics · Current Electricity
    In the potentiometer, when the cell in the secondary circuit is shunted with $4\ \Omega$ resistance, the balance is obtained at the length 120 cm of wire. Now when the same cell is shunted with $12\ \Omega$ resistance, the balance is shifted to a length of 180 cm. The internal resistance of cell is _______$\Omega$

    1. $3$
    2. $4$
    3. $6$
    4. $12$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    The balance length is proportional to the terminal voltage $\dfrac{ER}{R+r}$.
    $\dfrac{180}{120}=\dfrac{12/(12+r)}{4/(4+r)}=\dfrac{3(4+r)}{12+r}$, so $1.5(12+r)=12+3r$ and $r=4\ \Omega$.

  5. Question 52 · Chemistry · Structure of Atom
    The wave numbers of three spectral lines of H atom are considered. Identify the set of spectral lines belonging to Balmer series.
    (R = Rydberg constant)

    1. $\dfrac{5R}{36},\dfrac{8R}{9},\dfrac{15R}{16}$
    2. $\dfrac{3R}{4},\dfrac{3R}{16},\dfrac{7R}{144}$
    3. $\dfrac{5R}{36},\dfrac{3R}{16},\dfrac{21R}{100}$
    4. $\dfrac{7R}{144},\dfrac{3R}{16},\dfrac{16R}{255}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    Balmer series: $\bar\nu=R\left(\dfrac14-\dfrac1{n^2}\right)$, $n=3,4,5,\ldots$
    $n=3$: $\dfrac{5R}{36}$; $n=4$: $\dfrac{3R}{16}$; $n=5$: $\dfrac{21R}{100}$. This is option (3).

  6. Question 53 · Chemistry · Chemical Bonding and Molecular Structure
    Given below are two statements:
    Statement I: The number of species among $\mathrm{BF_4^-}$, $\mathrm{SiF_4}$, $\mathrm{XeF_4}$ and $\mathrm{SF_4}$, that have unequal E$-$F bond lengths is two. Here, E is the central atom.
    Statement II: Among $\mathrm{O_2^-}$, $\mathrm{O_2^{2-}}$, $\mathrm{F_2}$ and $\mathrm{O_2^+}$, $\mathrm{O_2^-}$ has the highest bond order.
    In the light of the above statements, choose the correct answer from the options given below

    1. Both Statement I and Statement II are true
    2. Both Statement I and Statement II are false
    3. Statement I is true but Statement II is false
    4. Statement I is false but Statement II is true
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    Statement I: $\mathrm{BF_4^-}$ and $\mathrm{SiF_4}$ (tetrahedral) and $\mathrm{XeF_4}$ (square planar) have equal bonds; only $\mathrm{SF_4}$ (see-saw, axial and equatorial bonds) has unequal bonds. One species, not two: false.
    Statement II: bond orders are $\mathrm{O_2^-}$ 1.5, $\mathrm{O_2^{2-}}$ 1, $\mathrm{F_2}$ 1, $\mathrm{O_2^+}$ 2.5. $\mathrm{O_2^+}$ is highest: false.
