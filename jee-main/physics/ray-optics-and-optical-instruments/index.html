  1. JEE Main 2026, 2 April, Shift 1 · Question 40
    For a thin symmetric prism made of glass (refractive index 1.5), the ratio of incident angle and minimum deviation will be ________.

    1. $3:4$
    2. $3:2$
    3. $2:1$
    4. $1:2$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    Thin prism: $\delta_m = (\mu-1)A = 0.5A$.
    At minimum deviation $i = \dfrac{A+\delta_m}{2} = \dfrac{1.5A}{2} = 0.75A$.
    $i:\delta_m = 0.75:0.5 = 3:2$.

  2. JEE Main 2026, 2 April, Shift 1 · Question 41
    Refer the figure given below. $\mu_1$ and $\mu_2$ are refractive indices of air and lens material. The height of image will be ________ cm.

    1. $1$
    2. $0.5$
    3. $1.2$
    4. $0.25$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    Single refracting surface: $\dfrac{\mu_2}{v} - \dfrac{\mu_1}{u} = \dfrac{\mu_2-\mu_1}{R}$ with $u = -40$ cm, $R = -20$ cm (centre C on the object side).
    $\dfrac{1.54}{v} + \dfrac{1}{40} = \dfrac{0.54}{-20} \Rightarrow \dfrac{1.54}{v} = -0.052 \Rightarrow v \approx -29.6$ cm.
    $m = \dfrac{\mu_1 v}{\mu_2 u} = \dfrac{-29.6}{1.54\times(-40)} \approx 0.48$.
    Image height $\approx 0.48\times2 \approx 0.96 \approx 1$ cm.

  3. JEE Main 2026, 2 April, Shift 2 · Question 41
    One side of an equilateral prism is painted by a transparent material of refractive index $n_2$. The refractive index of prism is 1.6. The minimum value of $n_2$ required for total internal reflection from painted face is __________.

    1. $3\sqrt3/1.6$
    2. $\sqrt3$
    3. $3.2/\sqrt3$
    4. $4\sqrt3/5$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    The ray enters the first face normally, so it meets the painted base at an angle of incidence of $60^\circ$ (geometry of the equilateral prism).
    Total internal reflection at the prism-paint boundary needs $1.6\sin60^\circ \ge n_2$.
    Limiting value: $n_2=1.6\times\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}=0.8\sqrt3=\dfrac{4\sqrt3}{5}$.

  4. JEE Main 2026, 2 April, Shift 2 · Question 49
    If sunlight is focused on a paper using convex lens, it starts burning the paper in shortest time when the lens is kept at 30 cm above the paper. If the radius of curvature of the lens is 60 cm then the refractive index of the lens material is $\dfrac{\alpha}{10}$. The value of $\alpha$ is __________.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 20

    The paper burns fastest at the focus: $f=30$ cm.
    For an equiconvex lens with $R=60$ cm: $\dfrac1f=(\mu-1)\dfrac{2}{R} \Rightarrow \dfrac1{30}=\dfrac{\mu-1}{30}$.
    $\mu=2=\dfrac{20}{10}$, so $\alpha=20$.

  5. JEE Main 2026, 4 April, Shift 1 · Question 39
    A rod of length $10$ cm lies along the principle axis of a concave mirror of focal length $10$ cm as shown in figure. The length of the image is ________ cm.

    1. $2.5$
    2. $5$
    3. $7.5$
    4. $7$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    The near end is at $20$ cm $=2f$ (centre of curvature), so its image is also at $20$ cm.
    Far end: $u=-30$ cm, $f=-10$ cm: $\dfrac1v=\dfrac1f-\dfrac1u=-\dfrac1{10}+\dfrac1{30}=-\dfrac1{15}$, so $v=-15$ cm.
    Image length $=20-15=5$ cm.

  6. JEE Main 2026, 4 April, Shift 2 · Question 41
    A convex lens is made from glass material having refractive index of 1.4 with same radius of curvature on both sides. The ratio of its focal length and radius of curvature is ________.

    1. $0.5$
    2. $2.5$
    3. $0.8$
    4. $1.25$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    Lens maker: $\dfrac1f=(1.4-1)\left(\dfrac1R+\dfrac1R\right)=\dfrac{0.8}{R}$.
    $\dfrac fR=\dfrac1{0.8}=1.25$.

  7. JEE Main 2026, 5 April, Shift 1 · Question 40
    A ray of light passing through an equilateral prism is having velocity $2.12 \times 10^8$ m/s in the prism material, then the minimum angle of deviation is ________ degrees.

    1. $45$
    2. $30$
    3. $28$
    4. $58$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    $\mu=\dfrac cv=\dfrac{3\times10^8}{2.12\times10^8}\approx1.414=\sqrt2$.
    $\mu=\dfrac{\sin\frac{A+\delta_m}{2}}{\sin\frac A2}$ with $A=60^\circ$: $\sin\dfrac{60^\circ+\delta_m}{2}=\sqrt2\times\dfrac12=\dfrac1{\sqrt2}$.
    $\dfrac{60^\circ+\delta_m}{2}=45^\circ \Rightarrow \delta_m=30^\circ$.

  8. JEE Main 2026, 5 April, Shift 1 · Question 42
    A compound microscope is designed with two symmetric biconvex lenses. The objective lens is cut vertically, creating two identical plano-convex lenses. One of them is used in place of original objective lens. To retain same magnification keeping the object distance unchanged, the tube length has to be

    1. increased two times
    2. increased $\dfrac{3}{2}$ times
    3. decreased two times
    4. decreased $\dfrac{3}{2}$ times
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    For a symmetric biconvex lens $\dfrac1f=(\mu-1)\dfrac2R$; each plano-convex half has $\dfrac1{f'}=(\mu-1)\dfrac1R$, so $f_o'=2f_o$.
    Magnification of a compound microscope $m\approx\dfrac{L}{f_o}\cdot\dfrac{D}{f_e}$.
    To keep $m$ the same with $f_o$ doubled, the tube length $L$ must be doubled (increased two times).

  9. JEE Main 2026, 5 April, Shift 2 · Question 40
    A thin convex lens and a thin concave lens are kept in contact and are co-axial. Which of the following statements is correct for this combination of two lenses ?

    1. behaves as concave lens if $|f_{\text{convex}}|>|f_{\text{concave}}|$
    2. behaves as concave lens if $|f_{\text{convex}}|<|f_{\text{concave}}|$
    3. behaves as convex lens if $|f_{\text{convex}}|>|f_{\text{concave}}|$
    4. Focal length of the lens system will change if the positions of two lenses are interchanged
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    Power of the combination: $P=\dfrac{1}{|f_{\text{convex}}|}-\dfrac{1}{|f_{\text{concave}}|}$.
    If $|f_{\text{convex}}|>|f_{\text{concave}}|$, then $P<0$: the combination behaves as a concave (diverging) lens.
    For thin lenses in contact, interchanging them does not change $P$.

  10. JEE Main 2026, 5 April, Shift 2 · Question 41
    An object $AB$ is placed 15 cm on the left of a convex lens $P$ of focal length 10 cm. Another convex lens $Q$ is now placed 15 cm right of lens $P$. If the focal length of lens $Q$ is 15 cm, the final image is __________.

    1. virtual, formed at 7.5 cm right of lens $Q$, with a size bigger than that of $AB$
    2. real, formed at 7.5 cm right of lens $Q$, with a size same as that of $AB$
    3. formed at infinity.
    4. real, formed at 7 cm right of lens $Q$, with a size smaller than that of $AB$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    Lens P: $u=-15$, $f=10$: $\dfrac1v=\dfrac1{10}-\dfrac1{15}=\dfrac1{30} \Rightarrow v=30$ cm; $m_1=\dfrac{30}{-15}=-2$.
    This image is 15 cm beyond Q, so it is a virtual object for Q: $u=+15$, $f=15$: $\dfrac1v=\dfrac1{15}+\dfrac1{15} \Rightarrow v=7.5$ cm; $m_2=\dfrac{7.5}{15}=\dfrac12$.
    $m=m_1m_2=-1$: real (inverted) image 7.5 cm right of Q, same size as AB.

  11. JEE Main 2026, 6 April, Shift 1 · Question 41
    A spherical interface lens of radius $R$ separates two media of refractive indices 1 and 1.4 respectively as shown in the figure below. A point source is placed at a distance of $4R$ in front of spherical interface. The magnitude of the magnification of point source image is ________.

    1. $1.66$
    2. $2.33$
    3. $2.66$
    4. $1.33$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    Convex surface facing the source: $u=-4R$, radius $+R$, $n_1=1$, $n_2=1.4$.
    $\dfrac{1.4}{v}-\dfrac{1}{-4R}=\dfrac{0.4}{R}\Rightarrow\dfrac{1.4}{v}=\dfrac{0.15}{R}\Rightarrow v=\dfrac{28R}{3}$.
    $m=\dfrac{n_1v}{n_2u}=\dfrac{28R/3}{1.4\times(-4R)}=-\dfrac53$, so $|m|\approx1.66$.

  12. JEE Main 2026, 6 April, Shift 2 · Question 42
    Angle of minimum deviation is equal to the half of the angle of prism in an equilateral prism. The refractive index of the prism is _________.

    1. $1.5$
    2. $\sqrt3$
    3. $\sqrt2$
    4. $1.65$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    $A=60^\circ$, $\delta_m=30^\circ$.
    $\mu=\dfrac{\sin\frac{A+\delta_m}{2}}{\sin\frac A2}=\dfrac{\sin45^\circ}{\sin30^\circ}=\sqrt2$.

  13. JEE Main 2026, 6 April, Shift 2 · Question 50
    A concave mirror of focal length 10 cm forms an image which is double the size of object when the object is placed at two different positions. The distance between the two positions of the object is __________ cm.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 10

    $m=\dfrac{f}{f-u}$ with $f=-10$ cm.
    Real image, $m=-2$: $\dfrac{-10}{-10-u}=-2 \Rightarrow u=-15$ cm.
    Virtual image, $m=+2$: $\dfrac{-10}{-10-u}=2 \Rightarrow u=-5$ cm.
    Distance between the positions $=15-5=10$ cm.

  14. JEE Main 2026, 8 April, Shift 2 · Question 30
    A thin biconvex lens is prepared from the glass $(\mu=1.5)$ both curved surfaces of which have equal radii of 20 cm each. Left side surface of the lens is silvered from outside to make it reflecting. To have the position of image and object at the same place, the object should be placed, from the lens at a distance of __________ cm.

    1. $10$
    2. $12.5$
    3. $13$
    4. $13.5$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    Lens: $\dfrac1{f_L}=(1.5-1)\left(\dfrac1{20}+\dfrac1{20}\right)=\dfrac1{20}$, so $f_L=20$ cm. Silvered surface: concave mirror with $f_m=\dfrac R2=10$ cm.
    Power of the system: $P=2P_L+P_m=\dfrac2{20}+\dfrac1{10}=\dfrac15$, so it acts as a concave mirror of focal length $F=5$ cm.
    Image coincides with the object when the object is at the centre of curvature: $2F=10$ cm.

  15. JEE Main 2026, 8 April, Shift 2 · Question 42
    Light ray incident along a vector $\vec{AO}$ $\left(\vec{AO}=2\hat{i}-3\hat{j}\right)$ emerges out along vector $\vec{OB}$ $\left(\vec{OB}=C\hat{i}-4\hat{j}\right)$ as shown in the figure below. The value of $C$ is __________.

    1. $1.6$
    2. $0.16$
    3. $11.6$
    4. $16$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    Angles are measured from the normal (the $y$-axis): $\sin\alpha=\dfrac{2}{\sqrt{13}}$, $\sin\beta=\dfrac{C}{\sqrt{C^2+16}}$.
    Snell's law: $1\times\dfrac2{\sqrt{13}}=1.5\,\dfrac{C}{\sqrt{C^2+16}}\Rightarrow\dfrac4{13}=\dfrac{2.25C^2}{C^2+16}$.
    $4C^2+64=29.25C^2\Rightarrow C^2=\dfrac{64}{25.25}\approx2.53\Rightarrow C\approx1.6$.
