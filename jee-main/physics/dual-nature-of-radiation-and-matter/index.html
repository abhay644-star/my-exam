  1. JEE Main 2026, 2 April, Shift 1 · Question 42
    For a certain metal, when monochromatic light of wavelength $\lambda$ is incident, the stopping potential for photoelectrons is $3V_o$. When the same metal is illuminated by light of wavelength $2\lambda$, then the stopping potential becomes $V_o$. The threshold wavelength for photoelectric emission for the given metal is $\alpha\lambda$. The value of $\alpha$ is ______.

    1. $1$
    2. $4$
    3. $2$
    4. $3$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    $\dfrac{hc}{\lambda} - \phi = 3eV_o$ and $\dfrac{hc}{2\lambda} - \phi = eV_o$.
    Subtracting: $\dfrac{hc}{2\lambda} = 2eV_o \Rightarrow eV_o = \dfrac{hc}{4\lambda}$.
    $\phi = \dfrac{hc}{2\lambda} - \dfrac{hc}{4\lambda} = \dfrac{hc}{4\lambda}$, so $\lambda_0 = \dfrac{hc}{\phi} = 4\lambda$ and $\alpha = 4$.

  2. JEE Main 2026, 4 April, Shift 1 · Question 32
    The graph shows variation of stopping potential $V_0$ with the frequency $\nu$ of the incident radiation for three photosensitive metals $X_1$, $X_2$ and $X_3$. Which metal will give out electrons with greater kinetic energy, for the same wavelength of incident radiation?

    1. $X_1$
    2. $X_2$
    3. $X_3$
    4. All the metals will give out photo electrons with same kinetic energies.
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    $eV_0=K_{\max}=h\nu-h\nu_0$: the lines are parallel and the threshold frequency is where each meets the $\nu$-axis.
    $X_1$ has the lowest threshold ($1.0\times10^{14}$ Hz), i.e. the smallest work function.
    For the same incident frequency, $K_{\max}$ is greatest for $X_1$.

  3. JEE Main 2026, 4 April, Shift 2 · Question 44
    The de Broglie wavelength associated with an electron accelerated through a potential difference V is $\lambda_e$ and the de Broglie wavelength associated with a proton accelerated through the same potential difference is $\lambda_p$. If their corresponding masses are $m_e$ and $m_p$, respectively, then the ratio of their de Broglie wavelengths $\left(\dfrac{\lambda_e}{\lambda_p}\right)$ is ________.

    1. $\sqrt{\dfrac{m_p}{m_e}}$
    2. $\sqrt{\dfrac{m_e}{m_p}}$
    3. $\dfrac{m_p}{m_e}$
    4. $\left(\dfrac{m_p}{m_e}\right)^2$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    $\lambda=\dfrac{h}{\sqrt{2mqV}}$ and both carry charge of magnitude $e$.
    $\dfrac{\lambda_e}{\lambda_p}=\sqrt{\dfrac{m_p}{m_e}}$.

  4. JEE Main 2026, 5 April, Shift 1 · Question 35
    An electron of mass $m$ is moving in an electric field $\vec{E} = -2E_0\hat{i}$ ($E_0$ = constant $> 0$), with an initial velocity $\vec{V} = v_0\hat{i}$ ($v_0$ = constant $> 0$). If $\lambda_0 = \dfrac{h}{4mv_0}$, its de Broglie wavelength at time $t$ is _______.
    ($e$ = charge of electron)

    1. $\dfrac{4\lambda_0}{\left[1-\dfrac{E_0e}{2m}\dfrac{t}{v_0}\right]}$
    2. $\dfrac{4\lambda_0}{\left[1+\dfrac{E_0e}{2m}\dfrac{t}{v_0}\right]}$
    3. $\dfrac{4\lambda_0}{\left[1+\dfrac{2E_0e}{m}\dfrac{t}{v_0}\right]}$
    4. $\dfrac{4\lambda_0}{\left[1-\dfrac{2E_0e}{m}\dfrac{t}{v_0}\right]}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    Force on the electron $\vec F=-e\vec E=2eE_0\hat i$, so it speeds up: $v=v_0+\dfrac{2eE_0}{m}t$.
    $\lambda=\dfrac{h}{mv}=\dfrac{h}{mv_0\left(1+\dfrac{2eE_0t}{mv_0}\right)}$.
    With $\dfrac{h}{mv_0}=4\lambda_0$: $\lambda=\dfrac{4\lambda_0}{1+\dfrac{2E_0e}{m}\dfrac{t}{v_0}}$.

  5. JEE Main 2026, 5 April, Shift 1 · Question 41
    Light source having wavelength $331$ nm is used to generate photo-electrons whose stopping potential is $0.2$ V. The work function of the used metal in the experiment is $\alpha \times 10^{-19}$ J. The value of $\alpha$ is _____.
    ($h = 6.62 \times 10^{-34}$ J s, $e = 1.6 \times 10^{-19}$ C and $c = 3 \times 10^8$ m/s)

    1. $3.68$
    2. $4.68$
    3. $5.68$
    4. $2.68$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    Photon energy $E=\dfrac{hc}{\lambda}=\dfrac{6.62\times10^{-34}\times3\times10^8}{331\times10^{-9}}=6.0\times10^{-19}$ J.
    $K_{max}=eV_0=1.6\times10^{-19}\times0.2=0.32\times10^{-19}$ J.
    $\phi=E-K_{max}=5.68\times10^{-19}$ J, so $\alpha=5.68$.

  6. JEE Main 2026, 5 April, Shift 2 · Question 43
    An electron is travelling with a velocity $v$ in free space and when it enters a medium, its velocity is reduced by 20%. The de Broglie wavelength of electron in the medium is $\alpha\lambda_0$, where $\lambda_0$ is its de Broglie wavelength in free space. The value of $\alpha$ is __________.

    1. $1.20$
    2. $1.0$
    3. $1.25$
    4. $0.75$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    $\lambda=\dfrac{h}{mv}\propto\dfrac1v$.
    In the medium $v'=0.8v$, so $\lambda=\dfrac{\lambda_0}{0.8}=1.25\lambda_0$.
    $\alpha=1.25$.

  7. JEE Main 2026, 6 April, Shift 2 · Question 47
    The de Broglie wavelength for an electron accelerated through the potential difference of $V_1$ volt is $\lambda_1$. When the potential difference is changed to $V_2$ volt, the associated de Broglie wavelength is increased by 50%. If $(V_1/V_2)=(9/\alpha)$, then the value of $\alpha$ is _________.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 4

    $\lambda=\dfrac{h}{\sqrt{2meV}}\propto\dfrac1{\sqrt V}$.
    $\lambda_2=1.5\lambda_1 \Rightarrow \dfrac{V_1}{V_2}=(1.5)^2=\dfrac94$.
    $\alpha=4$.

  8. JEE Main 2026, 8 April, Shift 2 · Question 38
    A monochromatic source of light operating at 15 kW emits $2.5\times10^{22}$ photons/s. The region of an electromagnetic spectrum to which the emitted electromagnetic radiation belongs to __________. (Take $h=6.6\times10^{-34}$ J.s and $c=3\times10^8$ m/s).

    1. Microwave
    2. Infrared
    3. Visible
    4. Ultraviolet
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    Energy per photon $E=\dfrac{15\times10^3}{2.5\times10^{22}}=6\times10^{-19}$ J.
    $\lambda=\dfrac{hc}{E}=\dfrac{6.6\times10^{-34}\times3\times10^8}{6\times10^{-19}}=3.3\times10^{-7}$ m $=330$ nm.
    This lies in the ultraviolet region.

  9. JEE Main 2026, 8 April, Shift 2 · Question 43
    $K_1$ and $K_2$ be the maximum kinetic energies of photoelectrons emitted from a surface of a given material for the light of wavelength $\lambda_1$ and $\lambda_2$, respectively. If $\lambda_1=2\lambda_2$ then the work function of material is given by :

    1. $K_2+2K_1$
    2. $2K_2-K_1$
    3. $K_1-2K_2$
    4. $K_2-2K_1$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    $K_1=\dfrac{hc}{\lambda_1}-\phi$ and $K_2=\dfrac{hc}{\lambda_2}-\phi=\dfrac{2hc}{\lambda_1}-\phi$.
    So $K_2=2(K_1+\phi)-\phi=2K_1+\phi$.
    $\phi=K_2-2K_1$.
