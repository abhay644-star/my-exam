  1. JEE Main 2026, 2 April, Shift 1 · Question 49
    A uniform wire of length $l$ of weight w is suspended from the roof with a weight of W at the other end. The stress in the wire at $\dfrac{l}{3}$ distance from the top is $\left(\dfrac{\mathrm{W}}{\mathrm{A}} + \dfrac{2}{\gamma}\dfrac{\mathrm{w}}{\mathrm{A}}\right)$, where, A is the cross sectional area of the wire. The value of $\gamma$ is ________.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 3

    At distance $l/3$ from the top, the wire below has length $\tfrac{2l}{3}$ and weight $\tfrac{2}{3}w$.
    Tension there $= W + \tfrac{2}{3}w$, so stress $= \dfrac{W}{A} + \dfrac{2}{3}\dfrac{w}{A}$.
    Comparing, $\gamma = 3$.

  2. JEE Main 2026, 4 April, Shift 1 · Question 27
    The increase in the pressure required to decrease the volume $(\Delta V)$ of water is $6.3\times10^7\ \mathrm{N/m^2}$. The percentage decrease in the volume is _______.
    (Bulk modulus of water $=2.1\times10^9\ \mathrm{N/m^2}$.)

    1. $2\,\%$
    2. $3\,\%$
    3. $6\,\%$
    4. $4\,\%$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    $B=\dfrac{\Delta p}{\Delta V/V} \Rightarrow \dfrac{\Delta V}{V}=\dfrac{6.3\times10^7}{2.1\times10^9}=0.03$.
    Percentage decrease $=3\%$.

  3. JEE Main 2026, 4 April, Shift 1 · Question 34
    A string $A$ of length $0.314$ m and Young's modulus $2\times10^{10}\ \mathrm{N/m^2}$ is connected to another string $B$ of length and Young's modulus both twice of those of $A$. This series combination of strings is then suspended from a rigid support and its free end is fixed to a load of mass $0.8$ kg. The net change in length of the combination is ________ mm.
    (radius of both the strings is $0.2$ mm and acceleration due to gravity $=10\ \mathrm{m/s^2}$)
    (Mass of both strings is to be neglected as compared to the mass of load)

    1. $3$
    2. $2$
    3. $1.9$
    4. $1$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    Tension $F=0.8\times10=8$ N in both strings; area $A=\pi(0.2\times10^{-3})^2=1.256\times10^{-7}\ \mathrm{m^2}$.
    $\Delta L_A=\dfrac{FL}{AY}=\dfrac{8\times0.314}{1.256\times10^{-7}\times2\times10^{10}}=1\times10^{-3}$ m $=1$ mm.
    For $B$, $L$ and $Y$ are both doubled, so $\Delta L_B=\Delta L_A=1$ mm.
    Net change $=2$ mm.

  4. JEE Main 2026, 4 April, Shift 2 · Question 31
    A metal string $A$ is suspended from a rigid support and its free end is attached to a block of mass $M$. Second block having mass $2M$ is suspended at the bottom of the first block using a string $B$. The area of cross sections of strings $A$ and $B$ are same. The ratio of lengths of strings of $A$ to B is 2 and the ratio of their Young's moduli ($Y_A/Y_B$) is 0.5. The ratio of elongations in $A$ to $B$ is _________.

    1. $1$
    2. $4$
    3. $8$
    4. $6$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    Tensions: $T_A=3Mg$ (supports both blocks), $T_B=2Mg$.
    Elongation $\Delta l=\dfrac{TL}{AY}$, so $\dfrac{\Delta l_A}{\Delta l_B}=\dfrac{T_A}{T_B}\cdot\dfrac{L_A}{L_B}\cdot\dfrac{Y_B}{Y_A}=\dfrac32\times2\times2=6$.

  5. JEE Main 2026, 5 April, Shift 1 · Question 31
    The Young's modulus of steel wire of radius $r$ and length $L$ is $Y$.
    If the radius $r$ and length $L$ of the wire are doubled then the value of $Y$

    1. increases by two times
    2. reduces by half
    3. remains unchanged
    4. becomes one fourth
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    Young's modulus is a property of the material (steel), not of the dimensions of the wire.
    So $Y$ remains unchanged.

  6. JEE Main 2026, 5 April, Shift 1 · Question 43
    Two wires as shown in the figure below, made of steel and have breaking stress of $12 \times 10^8$ N/m$^2$. Area of cross-section of upper wire is $0.008$ cm$^2$ and of lower wire is $0.004$ cm$^2$. The maximum mass that can be added to pan without breaking any wire is ______ kg.
    (take $g = 10$ m/s$^2$)

    1. $56$
    2. $38$
    3. $96$
    4. $5.6$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    Upper wire: $T_{max}=12\times10^8\times0.008\times10^{-4}=960$ N; it carries $(30+10+M)g$, so $M\le56$ kg.
    Lower wire: $T_{max}=12\times10^8\times0.004\times10^{-4}=480$ N; it carries $(10+M)g$, so $M\le38$ kg.
    Both must hold: maximum $M=38$ kg.

  7. JEE Main 2026, 5 April, Shift 1 · Question 46
    A cube has side length $5$ cm and modulus of rigidity $10^5$ N/m$^2$. The displacement produced by a force of $10$ N in the upper face of cube is _____ mm.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 2

    Shear modulus $\eta=\dfrac{F/A}{\Delta x/L} \Rightarrow \Delta x=\dfrac{FL}{\eta A}$.
    $\Delta x=\dfrac{10\times0.05}{10^5\times(0.05)^2}=\dfrac{0.5}{250}=0.002$ m $=2$ mm.

  8. JEE Main 2026, 5 April, Shift 2 · Question 46
    A copper wire of length 3 m is stretched by 3 mm by applying an external force. The volume of the wire is $600\times10^{-6}\ \mathrm{m^3}$. The elastic potential energy stored in the wire in stretched condition would be __________ J.
    (Given Young modulus of copper $=1.1\times10^{11}\ \mathrm{N/m^2}$)

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 33

    Strain $=\dfrac{3\times10^{-3}}{3}=10^{-3}$.
    Energy $=\dfrac12Y(\text{strain})^2\times\text{volume}=\dfrac12\times1.1\times10^{11}\times10^{-6}\times600\times10^{-6}$.
    $=33$ J.

  9. JEE Main 2026, 6 April, Shift 1 · Question 30
    A lift of mass 1600 kg is supported by thick iron wire. If the maximum stress which the wire can withstand is $4\times10^8$ N/m$^2$ and its radius is 4 mm, then maximum acceleration the lift can take is ______ m/s$^2$.
    (take $g=10$ m/s$^2$ and $\pi=3.14$)

    1. $2.56$
    2. $3.89$
    3. $4.32$
    4. $5.16$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    Area $=\pi r^2=3.14\times16\times10^{-6}=5.024\times10^{-5}$ m$^2$.
    Maximum tension $T=4\times10^8\times5.024\times10^{-5}=20096$ N.
    $T-mg=ma\Rightarrow a=\dfrac{20096}{1600}-10=2.56$ m/s$^2$.

  10. JEE Main 2026, 6 April, Shift 1 · Question 34
    The two wires $A$ and $B$ of equal cross-section but of different materials are joined together. The ratio of Young's modulus of wire $A$ and wire $B$ is 20/11. When the joined wire is kept under certain tension the elongations in the wires $A$ and $B$ are equal. If the length of wire $A$ is 2.2 m, then the length of wire $B$ is ________ m.

    1. $1.1$
    2. $2.22$
    3. $1.21$
    4. $4.44$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    Same tension and area: $\Delta L=\dfrac{FL}{AY}$, so equal elongations need $\dfrac{L_A}{Y_A}=\dfrac{L_B}{Y_B}$.
    $L_B=L_A\dfrac{Y_B}{Y_A}=2.2\times\dfrac{11}{20}=1.21$ m.

  11. JEE Main 2026, 6 April, Shift 2 · Question 33
    Figure represents the extension $(\Delta l)$ of a wire of length 1 meter, suspended from the ceiling of the room at one end with a load $W$ connected to the other end. If the cross-sectional area of the wire is $10^{-5}$ m$^2$ then the Young's modulus of the wire is _________ N/m$^2$.

    1. $1.0\times10^{11}$
    2. $2.0\times10^{10}$
    3. $1.0\times10^{10}$
    4. $2.0\times10^{11}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    From the graph, $W=10$ N gives $\Delta l=1\times10^{-4}$ m.
    $Y=\dfrac{W/A}{\Delta l/L}=\dfrac{10/10^{-5}}{10^{-4}/1}=\dfrac{10^6}{10^{-4}}=1.0\times10^{10}$ N/m$^2$.

  12. JEE Main 2026, 23 January, Shift 1 · Question 32
    The strain-stress plot for materials $A$, $B$, $C$ and $D$ is shown in the figure. Which material has the largest Young's modulus ?

    1. $A$
    2. $B$
    3. $C$
    4. $D$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    Here strain is on the vertical axis and stress on the horizontal axis, so the slope is $\dfrac{\text{strain}}{\text{stress}}=\dfrac1Y$.
    The largest Young's modulus has the smallest slope: material $C$.

  13. JEE Main 2026, 28 January, Shift 1 · Question 32
    Two wires $A$ and $B$ made of different materials of lengths 6.0 cm and 5.4 cm, respectively and area of cross sections $3.0\times10^{-5}\ \mathrm{m^2}$ and $4.5\times10^{-5}\ \mathrm{m^2}$, respectively are stretched by the same magnitude under a given load. The ratio of the Young's modulus of $A$ to that of $B$ is $x:3$. The value of $x$ is______.

    1. $1$
    2. $2$
    3. $4$
    4. $5$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    $Y=\dfrac{FL}{A\,\Delta L}$; with the same $F$ and $\Delta L$, $Y\propto\dfrac LA$.
    $\dfrac{Y_A}{Y_B}=\dfrac{6.0/3.0}{5.4/4.5}=\dfrac{2}{1.2}=\dfrac53$, so $x=5$.

  14. JEE Main 2025, 24 January, Shift 2 · Question 48
    The increase in pressure required to decrease the volume of a water sample by 0.2% is $P\times10^5$ Nm$^{-2}$. Bulk modulus of water is $2.15\times10^9$ Nm$^{-2}$. The value of P is __________.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 43

    $\Delta p=B\dfrac{|\Delta V|}{V}=2.15\times10^9\times0.002=4.3\times10^6$ N m$^{-2}=43\times10^5$ N m$^{-2}$.
    $P=43$.

  15. JEE Main 2025, 28 January, Shift 2 · Question 46
    The volume contraction of a solid copper cube of edge length 10 cm, when subjected to a hydraulic pressure of $7\times10^6$ Pa, would be __________ mm$^3$.
    (Given bulk modulus of copper $=1.4\times10^{11}$ N m$^{-2}$)

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 50

    $\Delta V=\dfrac{pV}{K}=\dfrac{7\times10^6\times10^{-3}}{1.4\times10^{11}}=5\times10^{-8}$ m$^3$.
    $1$ m$^3=10^9$ mm$^3$, so $\Delta V=50$ mm$^3$.

  16. JEE Main 2025, 29 January, Shift 1 · Question 31
    The fractional compression $\left(\dfrac{\Delta V}{V}\right)$ of water at the depth of 2.5 km below the sea level is __________ %. Given, the Bulk modulus of water $=2\times10^9$ N m$^{-2}$, density of water $=10^3$ kg m$^{-3}$, acceleration due to gravity $=g=10$ m s$^{-2}$.

    1. $1.0$
    2. $1.25$
    3. $1.5$
    4. $1.75$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    Extra pressure $p=\rho gh=10^3\times10\times2500=2.5\times10^7$ Pa.
    $\dfrac{\Delta V}{V}=\dfrac pB=\dfrac{2.5\times10^7}{2\times10^9}=0.0125=1.25\%$.
