  1. JEE Main 2026, 2 April, Shift 1 · Question 26
    The dimensional formula of $\dfrac{1}{2}\epsilon_o E^2$ ($\epsilon_o$ = permittivity of vacuum and E = electric field) is $\mathrm{M^aL^bT^c}$. The value of $2a-b+c=$ ________.

    1. $0$
    2. $1$
    3. $-1$
    4. $2$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    $\tfrac{1}{2}\epsilon_o E^2$ is energy per unit volume. $[\text{energy density}] = \dfrac{\mathrm{ML^2T^{-2}}}{\mathrm{L^3}} = \mathrm{M^1L^{-1}T^{-2}}$. So $a=1,\ b=-1,\ c=-2$ and $2a-b+c = 2+1-2 = 1$.

  2. JEE Main 2026, 2 April, Shift 1 · Question 27
    The diameter of a wire measured by a screw gauge of least count 0.001 cm is 0.08 cm. The length measured by a scale of least count 0.1 cm is 150 cm. When a weight of 100 N is applied to the wire, the extension in length is 0.5 cm, measured by a micrometer of least count 0.001 cm. The error in the measured Young's modulus is $\alpha\times10^9\ \mathrm{N/m^2}$. The value of $\alpha$ is ________.
    (Ignore the contribution of the load to Young's modulus error calculation)

    1. $1.3$
    2. $1.65$
    3. $0.13$
    4. $0.25$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    $Y = \dfrac{4FL}{\pi d^2\,\Delta L} = \dfrac{4(100)(1.5)}{\pi(8\times10^{-4})^2(5\times10^{-3})} \approx 5.97\times10^{10}\ \mathrm{N/m^2}$.
    $\dfrac{\Delta Y}{Y} = 2\dfrac{\Delta d}{d} + \dfrac{\Delta L}{L} + \dfrac{\Delta(\Delta L)}{\Delta L} = 2\cdot\dfrac{0.001}{0.08} + \dfrac{0.1}{150} + \dfrac{0.001}{0.5} \approx 0.0277$.
    $\Delta Y \approx 5.97\times10^{10}\times0.0277 \approx 1.65\times10^9\ \mathrm{N/m^2}$, so $\alpha = 1.65$.

  3. JEE Main 2026, 2 April, Shift 2 · Question 26
    Dimensions of universal gravitational constant (G) in terms of Planck's constant $(h)$, distance $(L)$, mass $(M)$ and time $(T)$ are __________.

    1. $[hTLM^{-2}]$
    2. $[hT^{-1}LM^{-2}]$
    3. $[hTL^2M^{-2}]$
    4. $[h^{-1}T^{-1}LM^{-2}]$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    $[G]=[M^{-1}L^3T^{-2}]$ and $[h]=[ML^2T^{-1}]$.
    Let $G=h^aL^bM^cT^d$: mass $a+c=-1$, length $2a+b=3$, time $-a+d=-2$.
    With $a=1$: $c=-2$, $b=1$, $d=-1$.
    $[G]=[hT^{-1}LM^{-2}]$.

  4. JEE Main 2026, 2 April, Shift 2 · Question 32
    In a screw gauge the zero of main scale reference line coincides with the fifth division of the circular scale when two studs are in contact. There are 100 divisions in circular scale and pitch of screw gauge is 0.1 mm. When diameter of a sphere is measured, the reading of main scale is 5 mm and $50^{\text{th}}$ division of circular scale coincides with the reference line of main scale. The diameter of sphere is __________ mm.

    1. $5.045$
    2. $5.055$
    3. $5.450$
    4. $5.550$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    Least count $=\dfrac{0.1}{100}=0.001$ mm.
    With the studs in contact the reading is $+5$ divisions: zero error $=+0.005$ mm.
    Observed reading $=5+50\times0.001=5.050$ mm.
    Diameter $=5.050-0.005=5.045$ mm.

  5. JEE Main 2026, 4 April, Shift 1 · Question 26
    In a screw gauge when the circular scale is given five complete rotations it moves linearly by $2.5$ mm. If the circular scale has $100$ divisions, the least count of screw gauge is _________ mm.

    1. $1\times10^{-2}$
    2. $1\times10^{-3}$
    3. $5\times10^{-2}$
    4. $5\times10^{-3}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    Pitch $=\dfrac{2.5}{5}=0.5$ mm.
    Least count $=\dfrac{\text{pitch}}{\text{number of divisions}}=\dfrac{0.5}{100}=5\times10^{-3}$ mm.

  6. JEE Main 2026, 4 April, Shift 2 · Question 26
    Match the LIST-I with LIST-II

    List-IList-II
    A. Planck's constantI. $\mathrm{ML^2T^{-2}}$
    B. Stopping potentialII. $\mathrm{T^{-1}}$
    C. Work functionIII. $\mathrm{ML^2T^{-1}}$
    D. Threshold frequencyIV. $\mathrm{ML^2T^{-3}A^{-1}}$
    Choose the correct answer from the options given below:

    1. A-III, B-IV, C-I, D-II
    2. A-I, B-II, C-III, D-IV
    3. A-IV, B-III, C-I, D-II
    4. A-I, B-IV, C-III, D-II
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    Planck's constant $=\dfrac{E}{\nu}$: $\mathrm{ML^2T^{-1}}$ (III).
    Stopping potential (volt) $=\dfrac{\text{energy}}{\text{charge}}=\dfrac{\mathrm{ML^2T^{-2}}}{\mathrm{AT}}=\mathrm{ML^2T^{-3}A^{-1}}$ (IV).
    Work function is an energy: $\mathrm{ML^2T^{-2}}$ (I). Threshold frequency: $\mathrm{T^{-1}}$ (II).
    A-III, B-IV, C-I, D-II.

  7. JEE Main 2026, 5 April, Shift 1 · Question 26
    In a Vernier calipers, when both jaws touch each other, zero of the Vernier scale is shifted to the right of zero of the main scale and $7^{\text{th}}$ Vernier division coincides with a main scale reading. If the value of $1$ main scale division is $1$ mm and there are $10$ Vernier scale divisions, then the Vernier caliper has

    1. 0.07 cm negative zero error
    2. 0.7 cm negative zero error
    3. 0.07 cm positive zero error
    4. 0.7 cm positive zero error
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    Least count $=\dfrac{1\text{ mm}}{10}=0.1$ mm.
    Vernier zero to the right of main-scale zero means a positive zero error.
    Zero error $=7\times0.1$ mm $=0.7$ mm $=0.07$ cm (positive).

  8. JEE Main 2026, 5 April, Shift 1 · Question 27
    $L$, $C$ and $R$ represents physical quantities inductance, capacitance and resistance respectively. The dimensional formula $\mathrm{M\,L^2\,T^{-4}\,A^{-2}}$ corresponds to _________.

    1. $\dfrac{R}{\sqrt{LC}}$
    2. $\dfrac{R}{LC}$
    3. $\dfrac{C}{\sqrt{LR}}$
    4. $\dfrac{1}{R}\sqrt{\dfrac{L}{C}}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    $[R]=\mathrm{ML^2T^{-3}A^{-2}}$ and $\sqrt{LC}$ is a time, $[\sqrt{LC}]=\mathrm{T}$.
    $\left[\dfrac{R}{\sqrt{LC}}\right]=\mathrm{ML^2T^{-4}A^{-2}}$.
    (Also $\sqrt{L/C}$ has the dimensions of resistance, so option (4) is dimensionless.)

  9. JEE Main 2026, 5 April, Shift 2 · Question 26
    Match List - I with List - II.

    List - IList - II
    A. Meter (L)I. $\sqrt{\dfrac{hc}{G}}$
    B. Second (S)II. $\sqrt{\dfrac{Gh}{c^5}}$
    C. Kilogram (M)III. $\sqrt{\dfrac{K^2L^2c^3}{Gh}}$
    D. Kelvin (K)IV. $\sqrt{\dfrac{Gh}{c^3}}$
    where h (Planck's constant), G (gravitational constant) and c (speed of light in vacuum) as fundamental units.
    Choose the correct answer from the options given below :

    1. A-II, B-IV, C-I, D-III
    2. A-IV, B-II, C-I, D-III
    3. A-IV, B-I, C-II, D-III
    4. A-III, B-I, C-II, D-IV
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    Dimensions: $[h]=ML^2T^{-1}$, $[G]=M^{-1}L^3T^{-2}$, $[c]=LT^{-1}$.
    $\dfrac{Gh}{c^3}$ has dimensions $L^2$, so length $\propto\sqrt{\dfrac{Gh}{c^3}}$ (A-IV).
    Time $=\dfrac{\text{length}}{c}\propto\sqrt{\dfrac{Gh}{c^5}}$ (B-II).
    $\dfrac{hc}{G}$ has dimensions $M^2$, so mass $\propto\sqrt{\dfrac{hc}{G}}$ (C-I).
    The remaining expression III goes with Kelvin (D-III).
    A-IV, B-II, C-I, D-III.

  10. JEE Main 2026, 5 April, Shift 2 · Question 27
    In an experiment to determine the resistance of a given wire using Ohm's law, the voltmeter and ammeter readings are noted as 10 V and 5 A, respectively. The least counts of voltmeter and ammeter are 500 mV and 200 mA, respectively. The estimated error in the resistance measurement is __________ $\Omega$

    1. $0.25$
    2. $2$
    3. $2.5$
    4. $0.18$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    $R=\dfrac{V}{I}=\dfrac{10}{5}=2\ \Omega$.
    $\dfrac{\Delta R}{R}=\dfrac{\Delta V}{V}+\dfrac{\Delta I}{I}=\dfrac{0.5}{10}+\dfrac{0.2}{5}=0.05+0.04=0.09$.
    $\Delta R=0.09\times2=0.18\ \Omega$.

  11. JEE Main 2026, 6 April, Shift 1 · Question 26
    The density $\rho$ of a uniform cylinder is determined by measuring its mass $m$, length $l$ and diameter $d$. The measured values of $m$, $l$ and $d$ are $97.42\pm0.02$ g, $8.35\pm0.05$ mm and $20.20\pm0.02$ mm, respectively. Calculated percentage fractional error in $\rho$ is ________.

    1. $0.63\%$
    2. $0.82\%$
    3. $0.72\%$
    4. $0.25\%$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    $\rho=\dfrac{4m}{\pi d^2l}\Rightarrow\dfrac{\Delta\rho}{\rho}=\dfrac{\Delta m}{m}+\dfrac{\Delta l}{l}+2\dfrac{\Delta d}{d}$.
    $=\dfrac{0.02}{97.42}+\dfrac{0.05}{8.35}+\dfrac{2\times0.02}{20.20}=0.00021+0.00599+0.00198=0.00817$.
    Percentage error $\approx0.82\%$.

  12. JEE Main 2026, 6 April, Shift 1 · Question 27
    The potential energy of a particle changes with distance $x$ from a fixed origin as $V=\dfrac{A\sqrt x}{x+B}$, where $A$ and $B$ are constant with appropriate dimensions. The dimensions of $AB$ are ________.

    1. $[\mathrm{M^1L^{5/2}T^{-2}}]$
    2. $[\mathrm{M^{3/2}L^{5/2}T^{-2}}]$
    3. $[\mathrm{M^1L^2T^{-2}}]$
    4. $[\mathrm{M^1L^{7/2}T^{-2}}]$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    $B$ is added to $x$, so $[B]=[\mathrm{L}]$.
    $[V]=\dfrac{[A]\mathrm{L^{1/2}}}{\mathrm{L}}\Rightarrow[A]=[\mathrm{ML^2T^{-2}}]\mathrm{L^{1/2}}=[\mathrm{ML^{5/2}T^{-2}}]$.
    $[AB]=[\mathrm{M^1L^{7/2}T^{-2}}]$.

  13. JEE Main 2026, 6 April, Shift 2 · Question 26
    The percentage error in the calculated volume of a sphere, if there is 2% error in its diameter measurement, is _________.

    1. $1$
    2. $2$
    3. $6$
    4. $8$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

  14. JEE Main 2026, 6 April, Shift 2 · Question 27
    Match List - I with List - II.

    List-IList-II
    A. Boltzmann constantI. $[\mathrm{M^{-1}L^3T^{-2}}]$
    B. Stefan's constantII. $[\mathrm{ML^2T^{-1}}]$
    C. Planck's constantIII. $[\mathrm{ML^2T^{-2}K^{-1}}]$
    D. Gravitational constantIV. $[\mathrm{ML^0T^{-3}K^{-4}}]$
    Choose the correct answer from the options given below :

    1. A-I, B-II, C-III, D-IV
    2. A-IV, B-III, C-II, D-I
    3. A-III, B-IV, C-II, D-I
    4. A-II, B-I, C-IV, D-III
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

  16. JEE Main 2026, 8 April, Shift 2 · Question 27
    Consider the equation $H=\dfrac{x^p\epsilon^q E^r}{t^s}$
    Where $H=$ magnetic field; $E=$ electric field, $\epsilon=$ permittivity, $x=$ distance, $t=$ time
    The values of $p$, $q$, $r$ and $s$ respectively are :

    1. $1, 1, 1, 1$
    2. $-1, 1, 2, 1$
    3. $1, -1, -2, 1$
    4. $-1, -2, -2, 1$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    $[H]=\text{A L}^{-1}$, $[\epsilon]=\text{M}^{-1}\text{L}^{-3}\text{T}^4\text{A}^2$, $[E]=\text{M L T}^{-3}\text{A}^{-1}$.
    Compare powers: M: $-q+r=0$; L: $p-3q+r=-1$; T: $4q-3r-s=0$; A: $2q-r=1$.
    These give $q=r=1$, $s=1$, $p=1$.
    (Check: $\dfrac{x\,\epsilon E}{t}$ has units $\dfrac{\text{m}\cdot\text{C m}^{-2}}{\text{s}}=\text{A m}^{-1}$, as in $\oint H\,dl=\epsilon\dfrac{d\Phi_E}{dt}$.)
    So $p,q,r,s=1,1,1,1$.

  17. JEE Main 2026, 21 January, Shift 1 · Question 26
    Consider a modified Bernoulli equation.
    $\left(P+\dfrac{A}{Bt^2}\right)+\rho g(h+Bt)+\dfrac{1}{2}\rho V^2=\text{constant}$
    If $t$ has the dimension of time then the dimensions of A and B are _______, _______ respectively.

    1. $[\mathrm{ML^0T^{-1}}]$ and $[\mathrm{M^0LT}]$
    2. $[\mathrm{ML^0T^{-1}}]$ and $[\mathrm{M^0LT^{-1}}]$
    3. $[\mathrm{ML^0T^{-2}}]$ and $[\mathrm{M^0LT^{-1}}]$
    4. $[\mathrm{ML^0T^{-2}}]$ and $[\mathrm{M^0LT^{-2}}]$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    $Bt$ is added to the height $h$, so $[B]=\mathrm{LT^{-1}}$.
    $\dfrac{A}{Bt^2}$ has the dimension of pressure: $[A]=\mathrm{ML^{-1}T^{-2}}\cdot\mathrm{LT^{-1}}\cdot\mathrm{T^2}=\mathrm{ML^0T^{-1}}$.
    So A: $[\mathrm{ML^0T^{-1}}]$, B: $[\mathrm{M^0LT^{-1}}]$.

  18. JEE Main 2026, 21 January, Shift 1 · Question 45
    In an experiment the values of two spring constants were measured as $k_1=(10\pm0.2)$ N/m and $k_2=(20\pm0.3)$ N/m. If these springs are connected in parallel, then the percentage error in equivalent spring constant is :

    1. $2.33\%$

  27. JEE Main 2026, 28 January, Shift 2 · Question 27
    The time period of a simple harmonic oscillator is $T=2\pi\sqrt{\dfrac km}$. Measured value of mass $(m)$ of the object is 10 g with an accuracy of 10 mg and time for 50 oscillations of the spring is found to be 60 s using a watch of 2 s resolution. Percentage error in determination of spring constant $(k)$ is__________%.

    1. $6.76$
    2. $3.35$
    3. $7.60$
    4. $3.43$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    Whichever way the relation is written, $k$ is proportional to $m^{\pm1}T^{\pm2}$, so $\dfrac{\Delta k}{k}=\dfrac{\Delta m}{m}+2\dfrac{\Delta T}{T}$.
    $\dfrac{\Delta m}{m}=\dfrac{10\ \mathrm{mg}}{10\ \mathrm g}=0.1\%$ and $\dfrac{\Delta T}{T}=\dfrac{2}{60}=3.33\%$.
    Total $\approx0.1+6.67\approx6.76\%$.
