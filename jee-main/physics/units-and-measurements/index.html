  1. JEE Main 2026, 2 April, Shift 1 · Question 26
    The dimensional formula of $\dfrac{1}{2}\epsilon_o E^2$ ($\epsilon_o$ = permittivity of vacuum and E = electric field) is $\mathrm{M^aL^bT^c}$. The value of $2a-b+c=$ ________.

    1. $0$
    2. $1$
    3. $-1$
    4. $2$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    $\tfrac{1}{2}\epsilon_o E^2$ is energy per unit volume. $[\text{energy density}] = \dfrac{\mathrm{ML^2T^{-2}}}{\mathrm{L^3}} = \mathrm{M^1L^{-1}T^{-2}}$. So $a=1,\ b=-1,\ c=-2$ and $2a-b+c = 2+1-2 = 1$.

  2. JEE Main 2026, 2 April, Shift 1 · Question 27
    The diameter of a wire measured by a screw gauge of least count 0.001 cm is 0.08 cm. The length measured by a scale of least count 0.1 cm is 150 cm. When a weight of 100 N is applied to the wire, the extension in length is 0.5 cm, measured by a micrometer of least count 0.001 cm. The error in the measured Young's modulus is $\alpha\times10^9\ \mathrm{N/m^2}$. The value of $\alpha$ is ________.
    (Ignore the contribution of the load to Young's modulus error calculation)

    1. $1.3$
    2. $1.65$
    3. $0.13$
    4. $0.25$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    $Y = \dfrac{4FL}{\pi d^2\,\Delta L} = \dfrac{4(100)(1.5)}{\pi(8\times10^{-4})^2(5\times10^{-3})} \approx 5.97\times10^{10}\ \mathrm{N/m^2}$.
    $\dfrac{\Delta Y}{Y} = 2\dfrac{\Delta d}{d} + \dfrac{\Delta L}{L} + \dfrac{\Delta(\Delta L)}{\Delta L} = 2\cdot\dfrac{0.001}{0.08} + \dfrac{0.1}{150} + \dfrac{0.001}{0.5} \approx 0.0277$.
    $\Delta Y \approx 5.97\times10^{10}\times0.0277 \approx 1.65\times10^9\ \mathrm{N/m^2}$, so $\alpha = 1.65$.

  3. JEE Main 2026, 2 April, Shift 2 · Question 26
    Dimensions of universal gravitational constant (G) in terms of Planck's constant $(h)$, distance $(L)$, mass $(M)$ and time $(T)$ are __________.

    1. $[hTLM^{-2}]$
    2. $[hT^{-1}LM^{-2}]$
    3. $[hTL^2M^{-2}]$
    4. $[h^{-1}T^{-1}LM^{-2}]$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    $[G]=[M^{-1}L^3T^{-2}]$ and $[h]=[ML^2T^{-1}]$.
    Let $G=h^aL^bM^cT^d$: mass $a+c=-1$, length $2a+b=3$, time $-a+d=-2$.
    With $a=1$: $c=-2$, $b=1$, $d=-1$.
    $[G]=[hT^{-1}LM^{-2}]$.

    Match List - I with List - II.

    List - IList - II
    A. Meter (L)I. $\sqrt{\dfrac{hc}{G}}$

    C. Kilogram (M)III. $\sqrt{\dfrac{K^2L^2c^3}{Gh}}$
    D. Kelvin (K)IV. $\sqrt{\dfrac{Gh}{c^3}}$

    Choose the correct answer from the options given below :

    In an experiment to determine the resistance of a given wire using Ohm's law, the voltmeter and ammeter readings are noted as 10 V and 5 A, respectively. The least counts of voltmeter and ammeter are 500 mV and 200 mA, respectively. The estimated error in the resistance measurement is __________ $\Omega$

    1. $0.25$
    2. $2$
    3. $2.5$
    4. $0.18$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    $R=\dfrac{V}{I}=\dfrac{10}{5}=2\ \Omega$.
    $\dfrac{\Delta R}{R}=\dfrac{\Delta V}{V}+\dfrac{\Delta I}{I}=\dfrac{0.5}{10}+\dfrac{0.2}{5}=0.05+0.04=0.09$.
    $\Delta R=0.09\times2=0.18\ \Omega$.

    The density $\rho$ of a uniform cylinder is determined by measuring its mass $m$, length $l$ and diameter $d$. The measured values of $m$, $l$ and $d$ are $97.42\pm0.02$ g, $8.35\pm0.05$ mm and $20.20\pm0.02$ mm, respectively. Calculated percentage fractional error in $\rho$ is ________.

    Consider the equation $H=\dfrac{x^p\epsilon^q E^r}{t^s}$
    Where $H=$ magnetic field; $E=$ electric field, $\epsilon=$ permittivity, $x=$ distance, $t=$ time
    The values of $p$, $q$, $r$ and $s$ respectively are :

    1. $1, 1, 1, 1$
    2. $-1, 1, 2, 1$
    3. $1, -1, -2, 1$
    4. $-1, -2, -2, 1$
    Show answer and solution

    Show answer and solution

    In a vernier callipers, $N$ vernier divisions equal $(N-1)$ main scale divisions, so one VSD is smaller than one MSD: Statement I is true.
    The vernier constant is $1\text{ MSD}-1\text{ VSD}=\dfrac{1\text{ MSD}}{N}$, i.e. one MSD divided (not multiplied) by the number of vernier divisions: Statement II is false.

  30. JEE Main 2025, 22 January, Shift 2 · Question 26
    Which one of the following is the correct dimensional formula for the capacitance in F ? M, L, T and C stand for unit of mass, length, time and charge,

    1. $\mathrm{[F]=[CM^{-1}L^{-2}T^2]}$
    2. $\mathrm{[F]=[CM^{-2}L^{-2}T^{-2}]}$
    3. $\mathrm{[F]=[C^2M^{-2}L^2T^2]}$
    4. $\mathrm{[F]=[C^2M^{-1}L^{-2}T^2]}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    Capacitance $=\dfrac{Q}{V}$ and $V=\dfrac{W}{Q}$ has dimensions $\mathrm{ML^2T^{-2}C^{-1}}$.
    $[C]=\dfrac{\mathrm{C}}{\mathrm{ML^2T^{-2}C^{-1}}}=\mathrm{C^2M^{-1}L^{-2}T^2}$.

  31. JEE Main 2025, 22 January, Shift 2 · Question 27
    The maximum percentage error in the measurment of density of a wire is
    [Given, mass of wire $=(0.60\pm0.003)$ g
    radius of wire $=(0.50\pm0.01)$ cm
    length of wire $=(10.00\pm0.05)$ cm]

    1. $8$
    2. $7$
    3. $5$
    4. $4$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    $\rho=\dfrac{m}{\pi r^2l}$, so $\dfrac{\Delta\rho}{\rho}=\dfrac{\Delta m}{m}+2\dfrac{\Delta r}{r}+\dfrac{\Delta l}{l}$.

  32. JEE Main 2025, 23 January, Shift 1 · Question 26
    The position of a particle moving on x-axis is given by $x(t)=A\sin t+B\cos^2t+Ct^2+D$, where $t$ is time. The dimension of $\dfrac{ABC}{D}$ is

    1. L
    2. L$^2$
    3. L$^2$T$^{-2}$
    4. L$^3$T$^{-2}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    Each term has the dimension of length: $[A]=[B]=[D]=$ L and $[C]t^2=$ L, so $[C]=$ LT$^{-2}$.
    $\left[\dfrac{ABC}{D}\right]=\dfrac{\text{L}\cdot\text{L}\cdot\text{LT}^{-2}}{\text{L}}=$ L$^2$T$^{-2}$.

  33. JEE Main 2025, 23 January, Shift 1 · Question 27
    The electric flux is $\phi=\alpha\sigma+\beta\lambda$
    where $\lambda$ and $\sigma$ are linear and surface charge density, respectively. $\left(\dfrac{\alpha}{\beta}\right)$ represents

    1. electric field
    2. charge
    3. area
    4. displacement
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    Both terms have the dimension of flux, so $\alpha\sigma$ and $\beta\lambda$ have the same dimension.
    $\dfrac{\alpha}{\beta}=\dfrac{\lambda}{\sigma}$, with units $\dfrac{\text{C m}^{-1}}{\text{C m}^{-2}}=$ m.
    So $\dfrac{\alpha}{\beta}$ has the dimension of length: displacement.

  35. JEE Main 2025, 23 January, Shift 2 · Question 27
    The energy of a system is given as $E(t)=\alpha^3e^{-\beta t}$, where t is the time and $\beta=0.3$ s$^{-1}$. The errors in the measurement of $\alpha$ and t are 1.2% and 1.6%, respectively. At $t=5$ s, maximum percentage error in the energy is :

    1. 6%
    2. 4%
    3. 11.6%
    4. 8.4%
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    $\ln E=3\ln\alpha-\beta t$, so $\dfrac{\Delta E}{E}=3\dfrac{\Delta\alpha}{\alpha}+\beta t\dfrac{\Delta t}{t}$.
    $=3(1.2\%)+(0.3)(5)(1.6\%)=3.6\%+2.4\%=6\%$.

    For an experimental expression $y=\dfrac{32.3\times1125}{27.4}$, where all the digits are significant. Then to report the value of $y$ we should write

    1. $y=1326.186$
    2. $y=1326.19$
    3. $y=1326.2$
    4. $y=1330$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

  37. JEE Main 2025, 24 January, Shift 1 · Question 46

  38. JEE Main 2025, 24 January, Shift 2 · Question 26
    The energy E and momentum p of a moving body of mass m are related by some equation. Given that c represents the speed of light, identify the correct equation

    1. $E^2=p^2c^2+m^2c^2$
    2. $E^2=pc^2+m^2c^4$
    3. $E^2=p^2c^2+m^2c^4$
    4. $E^2=pc^2+m^2c^2$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    Every term must have the dimensions of $E^2$. Energy has the dimensions of $pc$ (momentum $\times$ speed) and of $mc^2$.
    So the terms must be $p^2c^2$ and $m^2c^4$: $E^2=p^2c^2+m^2c^4$. Each of the other options has a term with different dimensions.

  40. JEE Main 2025, 28 January, Shift 1 · Question 47
    A tiny metallic rectangular sheet has length and breadth of 5 mm and 2.5 mm, respectively. Using a specially designed screw gauge which has pitch of 0.75 mm and 15 divisions in the circular scale, you are asked to find the area of the sheet. In this measurement, the maximum fractional error will be $\dfrac{x}{100}$ where $x$ is ________ .

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 3

    Least count $=\dfrac{0.75}{15}=0.05$ mm.

  41. JEE Main 2025, 28 January, Shift 2 · Question 26
    A bar magnet has total length $2l=20$ units and the field point P is at a distance $d=10$ units from the centre of the magnet. If the relative uncertainty of length measurement is 1%, then uncertainty of the magnetic field at point P is :

    1. 10%
    2. 3%
    3. 5%
    4. 4%
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    P is on the perpendicular bisector of the magnet: $B=\dfrac{\mu_0}{4\pi}\dfrac{m(2l)}{(d^2+l^2)^{3/2}}$.
    Each length ($l$ and $d$) has a relative uncertainty of 1%, so
    $\dfrac{\Delta B}{B}=\dfrac{\Delta l}{l}+\dfrac32\cdot\dfrac{2l^2\frac{\Delta l}{l}+2d^2\frac{\Delta d}{d}}{d^2+l^2}=1\%+3\%=4\%$.

    Note: NTA's final key gives 4% (option ID 6564452384) for all media, and also accepts 3% (option ID 6564452382) for the Hindi and Gujarati papers only, probably because of a translation issue there. The answer shown is the one for the English paper.

    The expression given below shows the variation of velocity $(v)$ with time (t),
    $v=At^2+\dfrac{Bt}{C+t}$. The dimension of ABC is :

    1. $[M^0L^2T^{-2}]$
    2. $[M^0L^1T^{-2}]$
    3. $[M^0L^2T^{-3}]$
    4. $[M^0L^1T^{-3}]$

    where, $a=(60\pm3)$Pa; $b=(20\pm0.1)$m; $c=(40\pm0.2)$Nsm$^{-2}$ and $d=(50\pm0.1)$m, then the percentage error in Q is $\dfrac{x}{1000}$, where $x$ = __________.
