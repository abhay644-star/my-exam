  1. JEE Main 2026, 2 April, Shift 1 · Question 28
    The velocity of a particle is given as $\vec{v} = -x\hat{i} + 2y\hat{j} - z\hat{k}$ m/s. The magnitude of acceleration at point (1, 2, 4) is ________ $\mathrm{m/s^2}$.

    1. $\sqrt{6}$
    2. $9$
    3. $\sqrt{33}$
    4. $0$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    $v_x=-x,\ v_y=2y,\ v_z=-z$. Then $a_x = \dfrac{dv_x}{dt} = -v_x = x$, $a_y = 2v_y = 4y$, $a_z = -v_z = z$.
    At $(1,2,4)$: $\vec{a} = \hat{i} + 8\hat{j} + 4\hat{k}$, so $|\vec{a}| = \sqrt{1+64+16} = 9\ \mathrm{m/s^2}$.

  2. JEE Main 2026, 4 April, Shift 1 · Question 31
    The two projectiles are projected with the same initial velocities at the $15^\circ$ and $30^\circ$ with respect to the horizontal. The ratio of their ranges is $1:x$. The value of $x$ is

    1. $\sqrt2$
    2. $\sqrt3$
    3. $2\sqrt3$
    4. $\dfrac{1}{\sqrt2}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    $R=\dfrac{u^2\sin2\theta}{g}$.
    $\dfrac{R_{15}}{R_{30}}=\dfrac{\sin30^\circ}{\sin60^\circ}=\dfrac{1/2}{\sqrt3/2}=\dfrac{1}{\sqrt3}$, so $x=\sqrt3$.

  3. JEE Main 2026, 4 April, Shift 2 · Question 29
    If $x$ and $y$ coordinates of a projectile as a function of time ($t$) are given as $24t$ and $43.6t-4.9t^2$, respectively, then the angle (in degrees) made by the projectile with horizontal when $t=2$ s is _________.

    1. $60$
    2. $45$
    3. $30$
    4. $75$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    $v_x=24$ m/s, $v_y=43.6-9.8t$.
    At $t=2$ s: $v_y=43.6-19.6=24$ m/s.
    $\tan\theta=\dfrac{v_y}{v_x}=1\Rightarrow\theta=45^\circ$.

  4. JEE Main 2026, 4 April, Shift 2 · Question 47
    A gun mounted on the ground fires bullets in all directions with same speed. The farthest distance the bullets could reach is 6.4 m. The speed of the bullets from the gun is ______m/s.
    (take $g=10$ m/s$^2$)

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 8

    Maximum range (at $45^\circ$): $R_{\max}=\dfrac{u^2}{g}$.
    $u^2=6.4\times10=64\Rightarrow u=8$ m/s.

  5. JEE Main 2026, 8 April, Shift 2 · Question 31
    Two identical bodies, projected with the same speed at two different angles cover the same horizontal range $R$. If the time of flight of these bodies are 5 s and 10 s, respectively, then the value of $R$ is __________ m. (Take $g=10\ \text{m/s}^2$)

    1. $250$
    2. $25$
    3. $500$
    4. $125$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    Equal ranges need complementary angles $\theta$ and $90^\circ-\theta$: $T_1=\dfrac{2u\sin\theta}{g}$, $T_2=\dfrac{2u\cos\theta}{g}$.
    $T_1T_2=\dfrac{2u^2\sin2\theta}{g^2}=\dfrac{2R}{g}$.
    $R=\dfrac{gT_1T_2}{2}=\dfrac{10\times5\times10}{2}=250$ m.
