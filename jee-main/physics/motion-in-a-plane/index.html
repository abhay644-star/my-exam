  1. JEE Main 2026, 2 April, Shift 1 · Question 28
    The velocity of a particle is given as $\vec{v} = -x\hat{i} + 2y\hat{j} - z\hat{k}$ m/s. The magnitude of acceleration at point (1, 2, 4) is ________ $\mathrm{m/s^2}$.

    1. $\sqrt{6}$
    2. $9$
    3. $\sqrt{33}$
    4. $0$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    $v_x=-x,\ v_y=2y,\ v_z=-z$. Then $a_x = \dfrac{dv_x}{dt} = -v_x = x$, $a_y = 2v_y = 4y$, $a_z = -v_z = z$.
    At $(1,2,4)$: $\vec{a} = \hat{i} + 8\hat{j} + 4\hat{k}$, so $|\vec{a}| = \sqrt{1+64+16} = 9\ \mathrm{m/s^2}$.

  2. JEE Main 2026, 4 April, Shift 1 · Question 31
    The two projectiles are projected with the same initial velocities at the $15^\circ$ and $30^\circ$ with respect to the horizontal. The ratio of their ranges is $1:x$. The value of $x$ is

    1. $\sqrt2$
    2. $\sqrt3$
    3. $2\sqrt3$
    4. $\dfrac{1}{\sqrt2}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    $R=\dfrac{u^2\sin2\theta}{g}$.
    $\dfrac{R_{15}}{R_{30}}=\dfrac{\sin30^\circ}{\sin60^\circ}=\dfrac{1/2}{\sqrt3/2}=\dfrac{1}{\sqrt3}$, so $x=\sqrt3$.

  3. JEE Main 2026, 4 April, Shift 2 · Question 29
    If $x$ and $y$ coordinates of a projectile as a function of time ($t$) are given as $24t$ and $43.6t-4.9t^2$, respectively, then the angle (in degrees) made by the projectile with horizontal when $t=2$ s is _________.

    1. $60$
    2. $45$
    3. $30$
    4. $75$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    $v_x=24$ m/s, $v_y=43.6-9.8t$.
    At $t=2$ s: $v_y=43.6-19.6=24$ m/s.
    $\tan\theta=\dfrac{v_y}{v_x}=1\Rightarrow\theta=45^\circ$.

  4. JEE Main 2026, 4 April, Shift 2 · Question 47
    A gun mounted on the ground fires bullets in all directions with same speed. The farthest distance the bullets could reach is 6.4 m. The speed of the bullets from the gun is ______m/s.
    (take $g=10$ m/s$^2$)

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 8

    Maximum range (at $45^\circ$): $R_{\max}=\dfrac{u^2}{g}$.
    $u^2=6.4\times10=64\Rightarrow u=8$ m/s.

  5. JEE Main 2026, 8 April, Shift 2 · Question 31
    Two identical bodies, projected with the same speed at two different angles cover the same horizontal range $R$. If the time of flight of these bodies are 5 s and 10 s, respectively, then the value of $R$ is __________ m. (Take $g=10\ \text{m/s}^2$)

    1. $250$
    2. $25$
    3. $500$
    4. $125$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    Equal ranges need complementary angles $\theta$ and $90^\circ-\theta$: $T_1=\dfrac{2u\sin\theta}{g}$, $T_2=\dfrac{2u\cos\theta}{g}$.
    $T_1T_2=\dfrac{2u^2\sin2\theta}{g^2}=\dfrac{2R}{g}$.
    $R=\dfrac{gT_1T_2}{2}=\dfrac{10\times5\times10}{2}=250$ m.

  6. JEE Main 2026, 21 January, Shift 2 · Question 28
    A river of width 200 m is flowing from west to east with a speed of 18 km/h. A boat, moving with speed of 36 km/h in still water, is made to travel one-round trip (bank to bank of the river). Minimum time taken by the boat for this journey and also the displacement along the river bank are _______ and _______ respectively.

    1. 20 s and 100 m
    2. 40 s and 200 m
    3. 40 s and 0 m
    4. 40 s and 100 m
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    Boat speed 10 m/s, river 5 m/s. Minimum time means heading straight across: $\dfrac{200}{10}=20$ s each way, 40 s in all.
    In each crossing the river carries the boat $5\times20=100$ m downstream (east), in the same direction both ways.
    Total displacement along the bank $=200$ m.

  7. JEE Main 2026, 22 January, Shift 1 · Question 29
    A projectile is thrown upward at an angle $60^\circ$ with the horizontal. The speed of the projectile is 20 m/s when its direction of motion is $45^\circ$ with the horizontal. The initial speed of the projectile is _______ m/s.

    1. $40$
    2. $40\sqrt2$
    3. $20\sqrt2$
    4. $20\sqrt3$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    The horizontal component of velocity does not change: $u\cos60^\circ=20\cos45^\circ$.
    $\dfrac{u}{2}=10\sqrt2\Rightarrow u=20\sqrt2$ m/s.

  8. JEE Main 2026, 23 January, Shift 1 · Question 27
    An object is projected with kinetic energy $K$ from a point $A$ at an angle $60^\circ$ with the horizontal. The ratio of the difference in kinetic energies at points $B$ and $C$ to that at point $A$ (see figure), in the absence of air friction is :

    1. $1:2$
    2. $2:3$
    3. $3:4$
    4. $1:4$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    At the top B only the horizontal velocity $u\cos60^\circ$ remains: $K_B=K\cos^260^\circ=\dfrac K4$.
    C is at the level of A, so $K_C=K$.
    $\dfrac{K_C-K_B}{K_A}=\dfrac{3K/4}{K}=3:4$.

  9. JEE Main 2026, 24 January, Shift 1 · Question 30
    A boy throws a ball into air at $45^\circ$ from the horizontal to land it on a roof of a building of height $H$. If the ball attains maximum height in 2 s and lands on the building in 3 s after launch, then value of $H$ is __________ m.
    ($g=10$ m/s$^2$)

    1. $15$
    2. $25$
    3. $20$
    4. $10$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    Time to the top: $\dfrac{u_y}{g}=2 \Rightarrow u_y=20$ m/s.
    At $t=3$ s: $H=u_yt-\dfrac12gt^2=60-45=15$ m.

  10. JEE Main 2025, 22 January, Shift 1 · Question 47
    A particle is projected at an angle of $30^\circ$ from horizontal at a speed of 60 m/s. The height traversed by the particle in the first second is $h_0$ and height traversed in the last second, before it reaches the maximum height, is $h_1$. The ratio $h_0 : h_1$ is __________.
    [Take, $g=10$ m/s$^2$]

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 5

    Vertical component $u_y=60\sin30^\circ=30$ m/s; time to the top $=3$ s.
    $h_0=30(1)-5(1)^2=25$ m.
    The last second before the top is the reverse of a 1 s fall from rest: $h_1=\dfrac12g(1)^2=5$ m.
    $h_0:h_1=5$.

  11. JEE Main 2025, 22 January, Shift 2 · Question 29
    A ball of mass 100 g is projected with velocity 20 m/s at $60^\circ$ with horizontal. The decrease in kinetic energy of the ball during the motion from point of projection to highest point is

    1. zero
    2. 15 J
    3. 20 J
    4. 5 J
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    At the highest point only the vertical component of velocity is lost.
    Decrease in KE $=\dfrac12m(u\sin60^\circ)^2=\dfrac12\times0.1\times\left(20\times\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}\right)^2=0.05\times300=15$ J.

  12. JEE Main 2025, 23 January, Shift 1 · Question 46
    Two particles are located at equal distance from origin. The position vectors of those are represented by $\vec A=2\hat i+3n\hat j+2\hat k$ and $\vec B=2\hat i-2\hat j+4p\hat k$, respectively. If both the vectors are at right angle to each other, the value of $n^{-1}$ is _____.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 3

    Equal magnitudes: $4+9n^2+4=4+4+16p^2 \Rightarrow 9n^2=16p^2$.
    Perpendicular: $4-6n+8p=0 \Rightarrow 4p=3n-2$.
    So $9n^2=(3n-2)^2 \Rightarrow 12n=4 \Rightarrow n=\dfrac13$.
    $n^{-1}=3$.

  13. JEE Main 2025, 23 January, Shift 2 · Question 28
    A ball having kinetic energy KE, is projected at an angle of $60^\circ$ from the horizontal. What will be the kinetic energy of ball at the highest point of its flight ?

    1. $\dfrac{(KE)}{2}$
    2. $\dfrac{(KE)}{8}$
    3. $\dfrac{(KE)}{16}$
    4. $\dfrac{(KE)}{4}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    At the highest point only the horizontal component $u\cos60^\circ=\dfrac u2$ remains.
    KE there $=\dfrac12m\left(\dfrac u2\right)^2=\dfrac{KE}{4}$.

  14. JEE Main 2025, 24 January, Shift 2 · Question 29
    The position vector of a moving body at any instant of time is given as $\vec r=\left(5t^2\hat i-5t\hat j\right)$m. The magnitude and direction of velocity at $t=2$s is,

    1. $5\sqrt{17}$ m/s, making an angle of $\tan^{-1}4$ with $-$ve Y axis
    2. $5\sqrt{17}$ m/s, making an angle of $\tan^{-1}4$ with $+$ve X axis
    3. $5\sqrt{15}$ m/s, making an angle of $\tan^{-1}4$ with $-$ve Y axis
    4. $5\sqrt{15}$ m/s, making an angle of $\tan^{-1}4$ with $+$ve X axis
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    $\vec v=\dfrac{d\vec r}{dt}=10t\,\hat i-5\hat j$; at $t=2$ s, $\vec v=20\hat i-5\hat j$.
    $|\vec v|=\sqrt{400+25}=5\sqrt{17}$ m/s.
    The angle with the $-$ve Y axis satisfies $\tan\theta=\dfrac{20}{5}=4$ (with the $+$ve X axis it would be $\tan^{-1}\dfrac14$).

  15. JEE Main 2025, 29 January, Shift 1 · Question 28
    Two projectiles are fired with same initial speed from same point on ground at angles of $(45^\circ-\alpha)$ and $(45^\circ+\alpha)$, respectively, with the horizontal direction. The ratio of their maximum heights attained is :

    1. $\dfrac{1-\tan\alpha}{1+\tan\alpha}$
    2. $\dfrac{1+\sin\alpha}{1-\sin\alpha}$
    3. $\dfrac{1-\sin2\alpha}{1+\sin2\alpha}$
    4. $\dfrac{1+\sin2\alpha}{1-\sin2\alpha}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    $H=\dfrac{u^2\sin^2\theta}{2g}$, so the ratio is $\dfrac{\sin^2(45^\circ-\alpha)}{\sin^2(45^\circ+\alpha)}=\left(\dfrac{\cos\alpha-\sin\alpha}{\cos\alpha+\sin\alpha}\right)^2=\dfrac{1-\sin2\alpha}{1+\sin2\alpha}$.

  16. JEE Main 2025, 29 January, Shift 1 · Question 47
    The maximum speed of a boat in still water is 27 km/h. Now this boat is moving downstream in a river flowing at 9 km/h. A man in the boat throws a ball vertically upwards with speed of 10 m/s. Range of the ball as observed by an observer at rest on the river bank, is __________ cm. (Take g = 10 m/s$^2$)

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 2000

    Speed of the boat relative to the bank: $27+9=36$ km/h $=10$ m/s; the ball shares this horizontal velocity.
    Time of flight $=\dfrac{2\times10}{10}=2$ s.
    Range seen from the bank $=10\times2=20$ m $=2000$ cm.
