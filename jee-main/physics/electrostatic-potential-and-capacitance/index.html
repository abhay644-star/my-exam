  1. JEE Main 2026, 2 April, Shift 1 · Question 32
    A parallel plate air capacitor has a capacitance C. When it is half filled as show in figure with a dielectric constant $K = 5$, the percentage increase in the capacitance is ________.

    1. $33.34$
    2. $66.67$
    3. $200$
    4. $400$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    The two layers (each of thickness $d/2$) are in series, with $C = \dfrac{\epsilon_0 A}{d}$.
    Dielectric layer: $C_1 = \dfrac{K\epsilon_0 A}{d/2} = 10C$; air layer: $C_2 = \dfrac{\epsilon_0 A}{d/2} = 2C$.
    $C' = \dfrac{C_1C_2}{C_1+C_2} = \dfrac{20C^2}{12C} = \dfrac{5}{3}C$.
    Increase $= \dfrac{2}{3}\times100\% \approx 66.67\%$.

  2. JEE Main 2026, 2 April, Shift 1 · Question 34
    Two charged conducting spheres $\mathrm{S_1}$ and $\mathrm{S_2}$ of radii 8 cm and 18 cm are connected to each other by a wire. After equilibrium is established, the ratio of electric fields on $\mathrm{S_1}$ and $\mathrm{S_2}$ spheres are $\mathrm{E_{S1}}$ and $\mathrm{E_{S2}}$ respectively. The value of $\dfrac{\mathrm{E_{S1}}}{\mathrm{E_{S2}}}$ is ________.

    1. $\dfrac{3}{2}$
    2. $\dfrac{2}{3}$
    3. $\dfrac{4}{9}$
    4. $\dfrac{9}{4}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    Connected spheres reach the same potential: $\dfrac{kq_1}{r_1} = \dfrac{kq_2}{r_2} = V$.
    Surface field $E = \dfrac{kq}{r^2} = \dfrac{V}{r}$, so $E \propto \dfrac{1}{r}$.
    $\dfrac{E_{S1}}{E_{S2}} = \dfrac{r_2}{r_1} = \dfrac{18}{8} = \dfrac{9}{4}$.

  3. JEE Main 2026, 2 April, Shift 2 · Question 39
    Two metal plates $(A, B)$ are kept horizontally with separation of $\left(\dfrac{12}{\pi}\right)$ cm, with plate A on the top. An atomizer jet sprays oil (density 1.5 g/cm$^3$) droplets of radius 1 mm horizontally. All oil droplets carry a charge 5 nC. The potentials $V_A$ and $V_B$ are required on plates A and B respectively in order to ensure the droplets do not descend. The values of $V_A$ and $V_B$ are __________.
    (Neglect the air resistance to the droplets and take $g=10$ m/s$^2$)

    1. 100 V and 580 V
    2. 580 V and 100 V
    3. 60 V and 400 V
    4. 0 V and $-200$ V
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    Mass of a droplet: $m=1500\times\dfrac43\pi(10^{-3})^3=2\pi\times10^{-6}$ kg.
    Balance: $qE=mg \Rightarrow E=\dfrac{2\pi\times10^{-5}}{5\times10^{-9}}=4\pi\times10^3$ V/m, directed upward (positive charge).
    $V_B-V_A=Ed=4\pi\times10^3\times\dfrac{0.12}{\pi}=480$ V, with the lower plate B at the higher potential.
    Only $V_A=100$ V, $V_B=580$ V fits.

  4. JEE Main 2026, 4 April, Shift 1 · Question 40
    A parallel plate air capacitor is connected to a battery. The plates are pulled apart at uniform speed $v$. If $x$ is the separation between the plates at any instant, then the time rate of change of electrostatic energy of the capacitor is proportional to $x^\alpha$, where $\alpha$ is _______.

    1. $-2$
    2. $1$
    3. $-1$
    4. $2$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    $V$ is constant: $U=\dfrac12CV^2=\dfrac{\epsilon_0AV^2}{2x}$.
    $\dfrac{dU}{dt}=-\dfrac{\epsilon_0AV^2}{2x^2}\dfrac{dx}{dt}=-\dfrac{\epsilon_0AV^2v}{2x^2}\propto x^{-2}$.
    $\alpha=-2$.

  5. JEE Main 2026, 4 April, Shift 2 · Question 37
    From the circuit given below, the capacitance between terminals $A$ and $B$ shown in the circuit is __________ $\mu$F.
    (take $C_1=C_2=C_3=1\ \mu$F and $C_4=2\ \mu$F.)

    1. $2$
    2. $7/2$
    3. $7/3$
    4. $5/2$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    Call $P$ the junction of $C_1$ and $C_2$, and $Q$ the junction of $C_2$ and $C_3$.
    The upper wire joins $A$ to $Q$, so $C_1$ and $C_2$ are both between $A$ and $P$ (in parallel: $2\ \mu$F), and $C_3$ is directly between $A$ and $B$.
    The lower wire puts $C_4$ between $P$ and $B$.
    $A\to P\to B$: $2\ \mu$F in series with $2\ \mu$F $=1\ \mu$F; in parallel with $C_3=1\ \mu$F.
    $C_{AB}=1+1=2\ \mu$F.

  6. JEE Main 2026, 4 April, Shift 2 · Question 38
    Given below are two statements: one is labelled as Assertion A and the other is labelled as Reason R
    Assertion A: In electrostatics, a conductor does not store any net charge inside.
    Reason R: Inside the capacitor (with no dielectric medium), the free charge carriers, if placed between the plates of capacitor, experience force and drift.
    Choose the correct answer from the options given below

    1. Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation of A
    2. Both A and R are true but R is NOT the correct explanation of A
    3. A is true but R is false
    4. A is false but R is true
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    In electrostatic equilibrium the field inside a conductor is zero, so by Gauss's law the net charge inside is zero; any excess charge sits on the surface. A is true.
    Between the plates of a charged capacitor there is a field, so a free charge placed there feels a force $qE$ and drifts. R is true.
    But R (about the gap of a capacitor) does not explain why a conductor holds no charge inside.
    Both A and R are true but R is NOT the correct explanation of A.

  7. JEE Main 2026, 6 April, Shift 1 · Question 49
    A three coulomb charge moves from the point $(0,-2,-5)$ to the point $(5,1,2)$ in an electric field expressed as $\vec E=2x\hat i+3y^2\hat j+4\hat k$ N/C. The work done in moving the charge is ______ J.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 186

    $W=q\displaystyle\int\vec E\cdot d\vec l=3\left[x^2+y^3+4z\right]_{(0,-2,-5)}^{(5,1,2)}$.
    $=3\,[(25-0)+(1+8)+4(2+5)]=3\times62=186$ J.

  8. JEE Main 2026, 6 April, Shift 2 · Question 37
    The electric potential as a function of $x$, $y$ is given by $V=5(x^2-y^2)$ V. The electric field at a point $(2,3)$ m is __________ V/m.

    1. $\left(-20\hat i+30\hat j\right)$
    2. $\left(20\hat i-30\hat j\right)$
    3. $\left(20\hat i+45\hat j\right)$
    4. $\left(-4\hat i+6\hat j\right)$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    $\vec E=-\nabla V=-10x\,\hat i+10y\,\hat j$.
    At $(2,3)$: $\vec E=-20\hat i+30\hat j$ V/m.

  9. JEE Main 2026, 6 April, Shift 2 · Question 40
    A sphere of capacitance 100 pF is charged to a potential of 100 V. Another identical uncharged metal sphere is brought in contact with the charged sphere, then the change in the total energy stored on these spheres, when they touch is $\alpha\times10^{-7}$ J. The value of $\alpha$ is __________. (combined capacitance of spheres is 200 pF)

    1. $5$
    2. $\dfrac52$
    3. $\dfrac72$
    4. $\dfrac92$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    Initial: $U_1=\dfrac12CV^2=\dfrac12(10^{-10})(10^4)=5\times10^{-7}$ J, charge $Q=10^{-8}$ C.
    Final: $U_2=\dfrac{Q^2}{2C'}=\dfrac{10^{-16}}{4\times10^{-10}}=2.5\times10^{-7}$ J.
    Change $=2.5\times10^{-7}$ J, so $\alpha=\dfrac52$.

  10. JEE Main 2026, 8 April, Shift 2 · Question 46
    A parallel plate capacitor is having separation between plates 0.885 mm. It has a capacitance of $1\ \mu\text{F}$ when the space between the plates is filled with an insulating material of resistivity $1\times10^{13}\ \Omega\text{m}$ and resistance $17.7\times10^{14}\ \Omega$. Relative permittivity of the insulating material is $\alpha\times10^7$. The value of $\alpha$ is __________.
    (Take permittivity of free space $=8.85\times10^{-12}$ F/m)

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 2

    $R=\dfrac{\rho d}{A}\Rightarrow A=\dfrac{\rho d}{R}=\dfrac{10^{13}\times0.885\times10^{-3}}{17.7\times10^{14}}=5\times10^{-6}\ \text{m}^2$.
    $C=\dfrac{K\epsilon_0A}{d}\Rightarrow K=\dfrac{Cd}{\epsilon_0A}=\dfrac{10^{-6}\times0.885\times10^{-3}}{8.85\times10^{-12}\times5\times10^{-6}}=2\times10^7$.
    $\alpha=2$.
