  1. JEE Main 2026, 2 April, Shift 1 · Question 32
    A parallel plate air capacitor has a capacitance C. When it is half filled as show in figure with a dielectric constant $K = 5$, the percentage increase in the capacitance is ________.

    1. $33.34$
    2. $66.67$
    3. $200$
    4. $400$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    The two layers (each of thickness $d/2$) are in series, with $C = \dfrac{\epsilon_0 A}{d}$.
    Dielectric layer: $C_1 = \dfrac{K\epsilon_0 A}{d/2} = 10C$; air layer: $C_2 = \dfrac{\epsilon_0 A}{d/2} = 2C$.
    $C' = \dfrac{C_1C_2}{C_1+C_2} = \dfrac{20C^2}{12C} = \dfrac{5}{3}C$.
    Increase $= \dfrac{2}{3}\times100\% \approx 66.67\%$.

  2. JEE Main 2026, 2 April, Shift 1 · Question 34
    Two charged conducting spheres $\mathrm{S_1}$ and $\mathrm{S_2}$ of radii 8 cm and 18 cm are connected to each other by a wire. After equilibrium is established, the ratio of electric fields on $\mathrm{S_1}$ and $\mathrm{S_2}$ spheres are $\mathrm{E_{S1}}$ and $\mathrm{E_{S2}}$ respectively. The value of $\dfrac{\mathrm{E_{S1}}}{\mathrm{E_{S2}}}$ is ________.

    1. $\dfrac{3}{2}$
    2. $\dfrac{2}{3}$
    3. $\dfrac{4}{9}$
    4. $\dfrac{9}{4}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    Connected spheres reach the same potential: $\dfrac{kq_1}{r_1} = \dfrac{kq_2}{r_2} = V$.
    Surface field $E = \dfrac{kq}{r^2} = \dfrac{V}{r}$, so $E \propto \dfrac{1}{r}$.
    $\dfrac{E_{S1}}{E_{S2}} = \dfrac{r_2}{r_1} = \dfrac{18}{8} = \dfrac{9}{4}$.

  3. JEE Main 2026, 2 April, Shift 2 · Question 39
    Two metal plates $(A, B)$ are kept horizontally with separation of $\left(\dfrac{12}{\pi}\right)$ cm, with plate A on the top. An atomizer jet sprays oil (density 1.5 g/cm$^3$) droplets of radius 1 mm horizontally. All oil droplets carry a charge 5 nC. The potentials $V_A$ and $V_B$ are required on plates A and B respectively in order to ensure the droplets do not descend. The values of $V_A$ and $V_B$ are __________.
    (Neglect the air resistance to the droplets and take $g=10$ m/s$^2$)

    1. 100 V and 580 V
    2. 580 V and 100 V
    3. 60 V and 400 V
    4. 0 V and $-200$ V
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    Mass of a droplet: $m=1500\times\dfrac43\pi(10^{-3})^3=2\pi\times10^{-6}$ kg.
    Balance: $qE=mg \Rightarrow E=\dfrac{2\pi\times10^{-5}}{5\times10^{-9}}=4\pi\times10^3$ V/m, directed upward (positive charge).
    $V_B-V_A=Ed=4\pi\times10^3\times\dfrac{0.12}{\pi}=480$ V, with the lower plate B at the higher potential.
    Only $V_A=100$ V, $V_B=580$ V fits.

  4. JEE Main 2026, 4 April, Shift 1 · Question 40
    A parallel plate air capacitor is connected to a battery. The plates are pulled apart at uniform speed $v$. If $x$ is the separation between the plates at any instant, then the time rate of change of electrostatic energy of the capacitor is proportional to $x^\alpha$, where $\alpha$ is _______.

    1. $-2$
    2. $1$
    3. $-1$
    4. $2$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    $V$ is constant: $U=\dfrac12CV^2=\dfrac{\epsilon_0AV^2}{2x}$.
    $\dfrac{dU}{dt}=-\dfrac{\epsilon_0AV^2}{2x^2}\dfrac{dx}{dt}=-\dfrac{\epsilon_0AV^2v}{2x^2}\propto x^{-2}$.
    $\alpha=-2$.

  5. JEE Main 2026, 4 April, Shift 2 · Question 37
    From the circuit given below, the capacitance between terminals $A$ and $B$ shown in the circuit is __________ $\mu$F.
    (take $C_1=C_2=C_3=1\ \mu$F and $C_4=2\ \mu$F.)

    1. $2$
    2. $7/2$
    3. $7/3$
    4. $5/2$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    Call $P$ the junction of $C_1$ and $C_2$, and $Q$ the junction of $C_2$ and $C_3$.
    The upper wire joins $A$ to $Q$, so $C_1$ and $C_2$ are both between $A$ and $P$ (in parallel: $2\ \mu$F), and $C_3$ is directly between $A$ and $B$.
    The lower wire puts $C_4$ between $P$ and $B$.
    $A\to P\to B$: $2\ \mu$F in series with $2\ \mu$F $=1\ \mu$F; in parallel with $C_3=1\ \mu$F.
    $C_{AB}=1+1=2\ \mu$F.

  6. JEE Main 2026, 4 April, Shift 2 · Question 38
    Given below are two statements: one is labelled as Assertion A and the other is labelled as Reason R
    Assertion A: In electrostatics, a conductor does not store any net charge inside.
    Reason R: Inside the capacitor (with no dielectric medium), the free charge carriers, if placed between the plates of capacitor, experience force and drift.
    Choose the correct answer from the options given below

    1. Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation of A
    2. Both A and R are true but R is NOT the correct explanation of A
    3. A is true but R is false
    4. A is false but R is true
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    In electrostatic equilibrium the field inside a conductor is zero, so by Gauss's law the net charge inside is zero; any excess charge sits on the surface. A is true.
    Between the plates of a charged capacitor there is a field, so a free charge placed there feels a force $qE$ and drifts. R is true.
    But R (about the gap of a capacitor) does not explain why a conductor holds no charge inside.
    Both A and R are true but R is NOT the correct explanation of A.

  7. JEE Main 2026, 6 April, Shift 1 · Question 49
    A three coulomb charge moves from the point $(0,-2,-5)$ to the point $(5,1,2)$ in an electric field expressed as $\vec E=2x\hat i+3y^2\hat j+4\hat k$ N/C. The work done in moving the charge is ______ J.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 186

    $W=q\displaystyle\int\vec E\cdot d\vec l=3\left[x^2+y^3+4z\right]_{(0,-2,-5)}^{(5,1,2)}$.
    $=3\,[(25-0)+(1+8)+4(2+5)]=3\times62=186$ J.

  9. JEE Main 2026, 6 April, Shift 2 · Question 40
    A sphere of capacitance 100 pF is charged to a potential of 100 V. Another identical uncharged metal sphere is brought in contact with the charged sphere, then the change in the total energy stored on these spheres, when they touch is $\alpha\times10^{-7}$ J. The value of $\alpha$ is __________. (combined capacitance of spheres is 200 pF)

    1. $5$
    2. $\dfrac52$
    3. $\dfrac72$
    4. $\dfrac92$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    Initial: $U_1=\dfrac12CV^2=\dfrac12(10^{-10})(10^4)=5\times10^{-7}$ J, charge $Q=10^{-8}$ C.
    Final: $U_2=\dfrac{Q^2}{2C'}=\dfrac{10^{-16}}{4\times10^{-10}}=2.5\times10^{-7}$ J.
    Change $=2.5\times10^{-7}$ J, so $\alpha=\dfrac52$.

  10. JEE Main 2026, 8 April, Shift 2 · Question 46
    A parallel plate capacitor is having separation between plates 0.885 mm. It has a capacitance of $1\ \mu\text{F}$ when the space between the plates is filled with an insulating material of resistivity $1\times10^{13}\ \Omega\text{m}$ and resistance $17.7\times10^{14}\ \Omega$. Relative permittivity of the insulating material is $\alpha\times10^7$. The value of $\alpha$ is __________.
    (Take permittivity of free space $=8.85\times10^{-12}$ F/m)

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 2

    $R=\dfrac{\rho d}{A}\Rightarrow A=\dfrac{\rho d}{R}=\dfrac{10^{13}\times0.885\times10^{-3}}{17.7\times10^{14}}=5\times10^{-6}\ \text{m}^2$.
    $C=\dfrac{K\epsilon_0A}{d}\Rightarrow K=\dfrac{Cd}{\epsilon_0A}=\dfrac{10^{-6}\times0.885\times10^{-3}}{8.85\times10^{-12}\times5\times10^{-6}}=2\times10^7$.
    $\alpha=2$.

  11. JEE Main 2026, 21 January, Shift 1 · Question 35
    A parallel plate capacitor has capacitance $C$, when there is vacuum within the parallel plates. A sheet having thickness $\left(\dfrac{1}{3}\right)^{\text{rd}}$ of the separation between the plates and relative permittivity $K$ is introduced between the plates. The new capacitance of the system is :

    1. $\dfrac{3KC}{2K+1}$
    2. $\dfrac{4KC}{3K-1}$
    3. $\dfrac{CK}{2+K}$
    4. $\dfrac{3CK^2}{(2K+1)^2}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    With a slab of thickness $t=d/3$: $C^\prime=\dfrac{\epsilon_0A}{d-t+t/K}=\dfrac{\epsilon_0A}{\dfrac{2d}{3}+\dfrac{d}{3K}}$.
    $=\dfrac{\epsilon_0A}{d}\cdot\dfrac{3K}{2K+1}=\dfrac{3KC}{2K+1}$.

  12. JEE Main 2026, 21 January, Shift 1 · Question 44
    A point charge of $10^{-8}$ C is placed at origin. The work done in moving a point charge 2 $\mu$C from point $A(4, 4, 2)$ m to $B(2, 2, 1)$ m is __________ J. ($\dfrac{1}{4\pi\epsilon_o}=9\times10^9$ in SI units)

    1. $0$
    2. $15\times10^{-6}$
    3. $45\times10^{-6}$
    4. $30\times10^{-6}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    $r_A=\sqrt{16+16+4}=6$ m, $r_B=\sqrt{4+4+1}=3$ m.
    $W=q(V_B-V_A)=2\times10^{-6}\times9\times10^9\times10^{-8}\left(\dfrac13-\dfrac16\right)$.
    $=2\times10^{-6}\times90\times\dfrac16=30\times10^{-6}$ J.

  13. JEE Main 2026, 21 January, Shift 2 · Question 40
    Consider two identical metallic spheres of radius $R$ each having charge $Q$ and mass $m$. Their centers have an initial separation of $4R$. Both the spheres are given an initial speed of $u$ towards each other. The minimum value of $u$, so that they can just touch each other is :
    (Take $k=\dfrac{1}{4\pi\epsilon_0}$ and assume $kQ^2>Gm^2$ where $G$ is the Gravitational constant)

    1. $\sqrt{\dfrac{kQ^2}{4mR}\left(1+\dfrac{Gm^2}{kQ^2}\right)}$
    2. $\sqrt{\dfrac{kQ^2}{4mR}\left(1-\dfrac{Gm^2}{kQ^2}\right)}$
    3. $\sqrt{\dfrac{kQ^2}{2mR}\left(1-\dfrac{Gm^2}{kQ^2}\right)}$
    4. $\sqrt{\dfrac{kQ^2}{2mR}\left(1-\dfrac{Gm^2}{2kQ^2}\right)}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    Net interaction energy $U(d)=\dfrac{kQ^2-Gm^2}{d}$ (repulsive overall).
    From $d=4R$ to touching ($d=2R$): $\Delta U=(kQ^2-Gm^2)\left(\dfrac1{2R}-\dfrac1{4R}\right)=\dfrac{kQ^2-Gm^2}{4R}$.
    Initial KE $2\cdot\dfrac12mu^2=mu^2=\Delta U$, so $u=\sqrt{\dfrac{kQ^2}{4mR}\left(1-\dfrac{Gm^2}{kQ^2}\right)}$.

  14. JEE Main 2026, 22 January, Shift 1 · Question 39
    Electric field in a region is given by $\vec{E}=Ax\hat{i}+By\hat{j}$, where $A=10$ V/m$^2$ and $B=5$ V/m$^2$. If the electric potential at a point $(10, 20)$ is 500 V, then the electric potential at origin is________V.

    1. $0$
    2. $500$
    3. $1000$
    4. $2000$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    $V(10,20)-V(0,0)=-\displaystyle\int\vec{E}\cdot d\vec{r}=-\left(\dfrac{Ax^2}{2}+\dfrac{By^2}{2}\right)=-(500+1000)=-1500$ V.
    $V(0,0)=500+1500=2000$ V.

  15. JEE Main 2026, 22 January, Shift 2 · Question 33
    Three small identical bubbles of water having same charge on each coalesce to form a bigger bubble. Then the ratio of the potentials on one initial bubble and that on the resultant bigger bubble is :

    1. $1:3^{2/3}$
    2. $1:3^{1/3}$
    3. $1:2^{2/3}$
    4. $3^{2/3}:1$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    Treat each as a charged sphere (drop) of radius $r$: $V=\dfrac{kq}{r}$.
    Volume is conserved: $R=3^{1/3}r$; the charge becomes $3q$.
    $V'=\dfrac{k(3q)}{3^{1/3}r}=3^{2/3}V$, so the ratio is $1:3^{2/3}$.

  16. JEE Main 2026, 22 January, Shift 2 · Question 37
    Five positive charges each having charge $q$ are placed at the vertices of a pentagon as shown in the figure. The electric potential $(V)$ and the electric field $\left(\vec{E}\right)$ at the center $O$ of the pentagon due to these five positive charges are :

    1. $V=0$ and $\vec{E}=0$
    2. $V=\dfrac{5q}{4\pi\varepsilon_0r}$ and $\vec{E}=\dfrac{5q}{4\pi\varepsilon_0r^2}\hat{r}$
    3. $V=\dfrac{5q}{4\pi\varepsilon_0r}$ and $\vec{E}=0$
    4. $V=\dfrac{5q}{4\pi\varepsilon_0r}$ and $\vec{E}=\dfrac{5\sqrt3q}{8\pi\varepsilon_0r^2}\hat{r}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    Potential is a scalar, so the five equal contributions add: $V=\dfrac{5q}{4\pi\varepsilon_0r}$.
    The five field vectors have equal magnitude and point along directions $72^\circ$ apart, so they add to zero: $\vec E=0$.

  17. JEE Main 2026, 22 January, Shift 2 · Question 47
    A capacitor $P$ with capacitance $10\times10^{-6}$ F is fully charged with a potential difference of 6.0 V and disconnected from the battery. The charged capacitor $P$ is connected across another capacitor $Q$ with capacitance $20\times10^{-6}$ F. The charge on capacitor $Q$ when equilibrium is established will be $\alpha\times10^{-5}$ C (assume capacitor $Q$ does not have any charge initially), the value of $\alpha$ is __________.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 4

    Initial charge $=10\times10^{-6}\times6=60\ \mu$C, shared at a common potential $V=\dfrac{60\ \mu\text{C}}{30\ \mu\text{F}}=2$ V.
    Charge on Q $=20\ \mu\text{F}\times2\ \text{V}=40\ \mu$C $=4\times10^{-5}$ C, so $\alpha=4$.

  18. JEE Main 2026, 23 January, Shift 1 · Question 50
    The space between the plates of a parallel plate capacitor of capacitance C (without any dielectric) is now filled with three dielectric slabs of dielectric constants $K_1=2$, $K_2=3$ and $K_3=5$ (as shown in figure). If new capacitance is $\dfrac n3C$ then the value of $n$ is __________.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 8

    Upper half: $K_1$ across the full area, thickness $\dfrac d2$: $C_1=2K_1C=4C$.
    Lower half: $K_2$ and $K_3$ side by side, each area $\dfrac A2$, thickness $\dfrac d2$: $C_2=(K_2+K_3)C=8C$.
    In series: $\dfrac{4C\times8C}{12C}=\dfrac83C$, so $n=8$.

  19. JEE Main 2026, 23 January, Shift 2 · Question 35
    A parallel plate capacitor with plate separation 5 mm is charged by a battery. On introducing a mica sheet of 2 mm and maintaining the connections of the plates with the terminals of the battery, it is found that it draws 25% more charge from the battery. The dielectric constant of mica is _________.

    1. $2.5$
    2. $1.0$
    3. $1.5$
    4. $2.0$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    At fixed voltage the charge is proportional to capacitance, so $C'=1.25C$.
    $\dfrac{C'}{C}=\dfrac{5}{3+\frac2K}=1.25 \Rightarrow 3+\dfrac2K=4 \Rightarrow K=2$.

  20. JEE Main 2026, 23 January, Shift 2 · Question 39
    Two charges 7 $\mu$C and $-2\ \mu$C are placed at $(-9, 0, 0)$ cm and $(9, 0, 0)$ cm respectively in an external field $E=\dfrac{A}{r^2}\hat r$, where $A=9\times10^5$ N/C.m$^2$.
    Considering the potential at infinity is 0, the electrostatic energy of the configuration is _______J.

    1. $-90.7$
    2. $1.4$
    3. $49.3$
    4. $24.3$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    The external potential is $V=\dfrac Ar$; both charges are at $r=0.09$ m, where $V=10^7$ V.

  21. JEE Main 2026, 24 January, Shift 1 · Question 38
    There are three co-centric conducting spherical shells $A$, $B$ and $C$ of radii $a$, $b$ and $c$ respectively $(c>b>a)$ and they are charged with charge $q_1$, $q_2$ and $q_3$ respectively. The potentials of the spheres $A$, $B$ and $C$ respectively, are :

    1. $\dfrac{1}{4\pi\epsilon_0}\left(\dfrac{q_1}{a}+\dfrac{q_2}{b}+\dfrac{q_3}{c}\right), \dfrac{1}{4\pi\epsilon_0}\left(\dfrac{q_1+q_2+q_3}{b}\right), \dfrac{1}{4\pi\epsilon_0}\left(\dfrac{q_1+q_2+q_3}{c}\right)$
    2. $\dfrac{1}{4\pi\epsilon_0}\left(\dfrac{q_1}{a}+\dfrac{q_2}{b}+\dfrac{q_3}{c}\right), \dfrac{1}{4\pi\epsilon_0}\left(\dfrac{q_1+q_2}{b}+\dfrac{q_3}{c}\right), \dfrac{1}{4\pi\epsilon_0}\left(\dfrac{q_1+q_2+q_3}{c}\right)$
    3. $\dfrac{1}{4\pi\epsilon_0}\left(\dfrac{q_1+q_2+q_3}{a}\right), \dfrac{1}{4\pi\epsilon_0}\left(\dfrac{q_1+q_2+q_3}{b}\right), \dfrac{1}{4\pi\epsilon_0}\left(\dfrac{q_1+q_2+q_3}{c}\right)$
    4. $\dfrac{1}{4\pi\epsilon_0}\left(\dfrac{q_1+q_2+q_3}{a}\right), \dfrac{1}{4\pi\epsilon_0}\left(\dfrac{q_1+q_2}{b}+\dfrac{q_3}{c}\right), \dfrac{1}{4\pi\epsilon_0}\left(\dfrac{q_1}{a}+\dfrac{q_2}{b}+\dfrac{q_3}{c}\right)$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    A shell of charge $q$ and radius $R$ gives potential $\dfrac{q}{4\pi\epsilon_0R}$ inside and $\dfrac{q}{4\pi\epsilon_0r}$ outside.
    $V_A$: inside all three, $\dfrac{1}{4\pi\epsilon_0}\left(\dfrac{q_1}a+\dfrac{q_2}b+\dfrac{q_3}c\right)$.
    $V_B$: outside A, on B, inside C, $\dfrac{1}{4\pi\epsilon_0}\left(\dfrac{q_1+q_2}b+\dfrac{q_3}c\right)$.
    $V_C$: outside all, $\dfrac{1}{4\pi\epsilon_0}\cdot\dfrac{q_1+q_2+q_3}c$.

  22. JEE Main 2026, 24 January, Shift 2 · Question 37
    Three parallel plate capacitors each with area $A$ and separation $d$ are filled with two dielectric ($k_1$ and $k_2$) in the following fashion. Which of the following is true?
    $(k_1>k_2)$

    1. $C_A>C_C>C_B$
    2. $C_C>C_B>C_A$
    3. $C_B>C_C>C_A$
    4. $C_C>C_A>C_B$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    With $C_0=\dfrac{\epsilon_0A}{d}$:
    (A): $k_1$ layer ($2k_1C_0$) in series with the split layer ($(k_1+k_2)C_0$): $C_A=\dfrac{2k_1(k_1+k_2)}{3k_1+k_2}C_0$.
    (B): the same with $k_1,k_2$ swapped: $C_B=\dfrac{2k_2(k_1+k_2)}{3k_2+k_1}C_0$.
    (C): two columns, each $k_1$ and $k_2$ in series: $C_C=\dfrac{2k_1k_2}{k_1+k_2}C_0$.
    For example $k_1=2$, $k_2=1$: $C_A=1.71C_0$, $C_C=1.33C_0$, $C_B=1.2C_0$, and in general $C_A>C_C>C_B$ for $k_1>k_2$.

    Two point charges of 1 nC and 2 nC are placed at the two corners of equilateral triangle of side 3 cm. The work done in bringing a charge of 3 nC from infinity to the third corner of the triangle is _____ $\mu$J.
    $\dfrac{1}{4\pi\epsilon_0}=9\times10^9\ \mathrm{N.m^2/C^2}$

    1. $3.3$
    2. $5.4$
    3. $27$
    4. $2.7$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    Potential at the third corner: $V=\dfrac{9\times10^9\,(1+2)\times10^{-9}}{0.03}=900$ V.
    $W=qV=3\times10^{-9}\times900=2.7\times10^{-6}$ J $=2.7\ \mu$J.

  24. JEE Main 2026, 28 January, Shift 2 · Question 36
    Identify the correct statements :
    A. Effective capacitance of a series combination of capacitors is always smaller than the smallest capacitance of the capacitor in the combination.
    B. When a dielectric medium is placed between the charged plates of a capacitor, displacement of charges cannot occur due to insulation property of dielectric.
    C. Increasing of area of capacitor plate or decreasing of thickness of dielectric is an alternate method to increase the capacitance.
    D. For a point charge, concentric spherical shells centered at the location of the charge are equipotential surfaces.
    Choose the correct answer from the options given below :

    1. A, B and C Only
    2. B and D Only
    3. C and D Only
    4. A, C and D Only
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    A: $\dfrac1C=\sum\dfrac1{C_i}>\dfrac1{C_{\min}}$, so true.
    B: a dielectric is polarised, i.e. its bound charges are displaced, so false.

  25. JEE Main 2026, 28 January, Shift 2 · Question 39
    Which one of the following is not a measurable quantity ?

    1. Displacement current
    2. Voltage
    3. Voltage difference
    4. Resistance
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    Only differences of potential can be measured: a voltmeter always reads the potential of one point relative to another. The potential (voltage) of a single point depends on the arbitrary choice of zero, so it is not measurable by itself.
    Potential difference, resistance and displacement current ($\varepsilon_0\,d\Phi_E/dt$, inferred from the changing field) are measurable.
