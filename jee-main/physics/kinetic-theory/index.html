  1. JEE Main 2026, 2 April, Shift 2 · Question 34
    A mixture of carbon dioxide and oxygen has volume 8310 cm$^3$, temperature 300 K, pressure 100 kPa and mass 13.2 g. The number of moles of carbon dioxide and oxygen gases in the mixture respectively are __________.
    (Assume both carbon dioxide and oxygen gases behave like ideal gases) [$R=8.31$ J/mol.K]

    1. 0.15 and 0.18
    2. 0.25 and 0.08
    3. 0.21 and 0.12
    4. 0.13 and 0.20
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    Total moles $n=\dfrac{PV}{RT}=\dfrac{10^5\times8.31\times10^{-3}}{8.31\times300}=\dfrac13$.
    Let $x$ mol CO$_2$ (44 g/mol) and $y$ mol O$_2$ (32 g/mol): $x+y=\dfrac13$, $44x+32y=13.2$.
    $12x=13.2-\dfrac{32}{3}=2.53 \Rightarrow x\approx0.21$, $y\approx0.12$.

  2. JEE Main 2026, 4 April, Shift 1 · Question 35
    One gas of $n_1$ mole of molecules at temperature $T_1$, volume $V_1$, and pressure $P_1$, and another gas of $n_2$ mole of molecules at temperature $T_2$, volume $V_2$, and pressure $P_2$, are mixed resulting in pressure $P$ and volume $V$ of the mixture. The temperature of the mixture is _________.

    1. $(T_1+T_2)/2$
    2. $T_1T_2PV/(T_2P_1V_1+T_1P_2V_2)$
    3. $(T_2P_1V_1+T_1P_2V_2)/(T_1T_2PV)$
    4. $|T_1-T_2|/2$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    $n_1=\dfrac{P_1V_1}{RT_1}$, $n_2=\dfrac{P_2V_2}{RT_2}$, and the mixture obeys $PV=(n_1+n_2)RT$.
    $T=\dfrac{PV}{(n_1+n_2)R}=\dfrac{PV}{\dfrac{P_1V_1}{T_1}+\dfrac{P_2V_2}{T_2}}=\dfrac{T_1T_2PV}{T_2P_1V_1+T_1P_2V_2}$.

  3. JEE Main 2026, 4 April, Shift 2 · Question 33
    Given below are two statements: one is labelled as Assertion A and the other is labelled as Reason R
    Assertion A: If the average kinetic energy of $\mathrm{H_2}$ and $\mathrm{O_2}$ molecules, kept in two different sized containers are same, then their temperatures will be same.
    Reason R: The r.m.s. speed of $\mathrm{H_2}$ and $\mathrm{O_2}$ molecules are same at same temperature.
    Choose the correct answer from the options given below

    1. Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation of A
    2. Both A and R are true but R is NOT the correct explanation of A
    3. A is true but R is false
    4. A is false but R is true
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    Average (translational) kinetic energy per molecule $=\dfrac32kT$ depends only on $T$, so equal average KE means equal temperature: A is true.
    $v_{rms}=\sqrt{\dfrac{3RT}{M}}$ depends on molar mass; $\mathrm{H_2}$ ($M=2$) is faster than $\mathrm{O_2}$ ($M=32$) at the same $T$: R is false.
    A is true but R is false.

  4. JEE Main 2026, 6 April, Shift 1 · Question 35
    Two closed vessels of same volume are joined through a narrow tube and both vessels are filled with air of pressure 90 kPa and temperature 400 K. Keeping the temperature of one vessel constant at 400 K the second vessel temperature is raised to 500 K. The final pressure in the vessels is ________ kPa.

    1. $100$
    2. $120$
    3. $90$
    4. $105$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    Total moles are conserved and the final pressure $p$ is common: $\dfrac{2\times90\,V}{R\cdot400}=\dfrac{pV}{R\cdot400}+\dfrac{pV}{R\cdot500}$.
    $0.45=p\left(\dfrac{1}{400}+\dfrac{1}{500}\right)=p\cdot\dfrac{9}{2000}\Rightarrow p=100$ kPa.

  5. JEE Main 2026, 6 April, Shift 2 · Question 35
    If 2 mole of an ideal monoatomic gas at temperature $T$, is mixed with 6 mole of another ideal monoatomic gas at temperature $2T$ then the temperature of mixture is :

    1. $\dfrac52T$
    2. $\dfrac54T$
    3. $\dfrac72T$
    4. $\dfrac74T$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    Internal energy is conserved and both gases have $C_V=\dfrac32R$.
    $2T+6(2T)=8T_{mix} \Rightarrow T_{mix}=\dfrac{14T}{8}=\dfrac74T$.

  6. JEE Main 2026, 21 January, Shift 2 · Question 34
    The r.m.s. speed of oxygen molecules at $47^\circ$C is equal to that of the hydrogen molecules kept at __________ $^\circ$C. (Mass of oxygen molecule/mass of hydrogen molecule $=32/2$)

    1. $-20$
    2. $-253$
    3. $-100$
    4. $-235$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    $v_{rms}=\sqrt{\dfrac{3RT}{M}}$; equal speeds need $\dfrac{T}{M}$ equal.
    $T_{H_2}=320\times\dfrac{2}{32}=20$ K $=-253^\circ$C.

  7. JEE Main 2026, 22 January, Shift 1 · Question 34
    A cylindrical tube $AB$ of length $l$, closed at both ends contains an ideal gas of 1 mol having molecular weight $M$. The tube is rotated in a horizontal plane with constant angular velocity $\omega$ about an axis perpendicular to $AB$ and passing through the edge at end $A$, as shown in the figure. If $P_A$ and $P_B$ are the pressures at $A$ and $B$ respectively, then
    (Consider the temperature is same at all points in the tube)

    1. $P_B=P_A$
    2. $P_B=P_A\exp\left(M\omega^2l^2/2RT\right)$
    3. $P_B=P_A\exp\left(M\omega^2l^2/3RT\right)$
    4. $P_B=P_A\exp\left(M\omega^2l^2/RT\right)$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    For a gas layer at distance $r$ from the axis: $\dfrac{dP}{dr}=\rho\omega^2r$ with $\rho=\dfrac{PM}{RT}$.
    $\dfrac{dP}{P}=\dfrac{M\omega^2}{RT}r\,dr\Rightarrow\ln\dfrac{P_B}{P_A}=\dfrac{M\omega^2l^2}{2RT}$.
    So $P_B=P_A\exp\left(\dfrac{M\omega^2l^2}{2RT}\right)$.

  8. JEE Main 2026, 22 January, Shift 2 · Question 35
    Consider two boxes containing ideal gases $A$ and $B$ such that their temperatures, pressures and number densities are same. The molecular size of $A$ is half of that of $B$ and mass of molecule $A$ is four times that of $B$. If the collision frequency in gas $B$ is $32\times10^{18}$/s then collision frequency in gas $A$ is __________/s.

    1. $2\times10^8$
    2. $4\times10^8$
    3. $8\times10^8$
    4. $32\times10^8$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: Dropped: full marks to everyone

    Collision frequency $f=\sqrt2\pi d^2nv_{\text{avg}}$, with $v_{\text{avg}}\propto\sqrt{\dfrac{T}{m}}$; $n$ and $T$ are the same.
    $\dfrac{f_A}{f_B}=\left(\dfrac{d_A}{d_B}\right)^2\sqrt{\dfrac{m_B}{m_A}}=\dfrac14\cdot\dfrac12=\dfrac18$.
    $f_A=\dfrac{32\times10^{18}}{8}=4\times10^{18}$/s. The options carry $10^8$ instead of $10^{18}$, so none matches exactly.

    Note: Dropped in the official final key: full marks to everyone. The working gives 4 x 10^18 /s, while the options are printed with 10^8.

  9. JEE Main 2026, 22 January, Shift 2 · Question 49
    An insulated cylinder of volume 60 cm$^3$ is filled with a gas at 27 °C and 2 atmospheric pressure. Then the gas is compressed making the final volume as 20 cm$^3$ while allowing the temperature to rise to 77 °C. The final pressure is __________ atmospheric pressure.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 7

    $\dfrac{p_1V_1}{T_1}=\dfrac{p_2V_2}{T_2}\Rightarrow p_2=2\times\dfrac{60}{20}\times\dfrac{350}{300}=7$ atm.

  10. JEE Main 2026, 23 January, Shift 2 · Question 32
    An air bubble of volume 2.9 cm$^3$ rises from the bottom of a swimming pool of 5 m deep. At the bottom of the pool water temperature is 17 $^\circ$C. The volume of the bubble when it reaches the surface, where the water temperature is 27 $^\circ$C, is ____ cm$^3$.
    ($g=10$ m/s$^2$, density of water $=10^3$ kg/m$^3$, and 1 atm pressure is $10^5$ Pa)

    1. $4.2$
    2. $2.0$
    3. $3.0$
    4. $4.5$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    Pressure at the bottom $=10^5+10^3\times10\times5=1.5\times10^5$ Pa; at the surface $10^5$ Pa.
    $\dfrac{P_1V_1}{T_1}=\dfrac{P_2V_2}{T_2}$: $V_2=2.9\times1.5\times\dfrac{300}{290}=4.5$ cm$^3$.

  11. JEE Main 2026, 24 January, Shift 1 · Question 49
    A gas of certain mass filled in a closed cylinder at a pressure of 3.23 kPa has temperature 50 $^\circ$C. The gas is now heated to double its temperature. The modified pressure is __________ Pa.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 3730

    At constant volume $p\propto T$ (in kelvin). The key reads ''double its temperature'' as 50 $^\circ$C to 100 $^\circ$C, i.e. 323 K to 373 K.
    $p=3230\times\dfrac{373}{323}=3730$ Pa.

    Note: The official final key (3730 Pa) doubles the Celsius temperature (50 $^\circ$C to 100 $^\circ$C). Doubling the kelvin temperature (323 K to 646 K) would give 6460 Pa. The key is followed.

  12. JEE Main 2026, 28 January, Shift 2 · Question 34
    The mean free path of a molecule of diameter $5\times10^{-10}$ m at the temperature $41\ ^\circ$C and pressure $1.38\times10^5$ Pa, is given as ________ m. (Given $k_B=1.38\times10^{-23}$ J/K).

    1. $10\sqrt2\times10^{-8}$
    2. $2\sqrt2\times10^{-8}$
    3. $2\sqrt2\times10^{-10}$
    4. $2\times10^{-8}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    $\lambda=\dfrac{k_BT}{\sqrt2\,\pi d^2p}$ with $T=314$ K.
    $\lambda=\dfrac{1.38\times10^{-23}\times314}{\sqrt2\times3.14\times25\times10^{-20}\times1.38\times10^5}=\dfrac{100\times10^{-23}}{\sqrt2\times25\times10^{-15}}=\dfrac{4\times10^{-8}}{\sqrt2}=2\sqrt2\times10^{-8}$ m.

  13. JEE Main 2025, 2 April, Shift 1 · Question 48
    $\gamma_A$ is the specific heat ratio of monoatomic gas A having 3 translational degrees of freedom. $\gamma_B$ is the specific heat ratio of polyatomic gas B having 3 translational, 3 rotational degrees of freedom and 1 vibrational mode. If $\dfrac{\gamma_A}{\gamma_B}=\left(1+\dfrac1n\right)$, then the value of n is __________.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 3

    $\gamma_A=1+\dfrac23=\dfrac53$.
    For B a vibrational mode contributes 2 (kinetic and potential), so $f=3+3+2=8$ and $\gamma_B=1+\dfrac28=\dfrac54$.
    $\dfrac{\gamma_A}{\gamma_B}=\dfrac43=1+\dfrac13$, so $n=3$.

  14. JEE Main 2025, 2 April, Shift 2 · Question 50
    The internal energy of air in $4\ \mathrm{m}\times4\ \mathrm{m}\times3$ m sized room at 1 atmospheric pressure will be __________ $\times10^6$ J.
    (Consider air as diatomic molecule)

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 12

    For a diatomic gas $U=\dfrac52nRT=\dfrac52PV$.
    $U=2.5\times1.013\times10^5\times48\approx1.22\times10^7$ J $\approx12\times10^6$ J.

  15. JEE Main 2025, 3 April, Shift 2 · Question 34
    Pressure of an ideal gas, contained in a closed vessel, is increased by 0.4% when heated by 1$^\circ$C. Its initial temperature must be:

    1. 250$^\circ$C
    2. 2500 K
    3. 25$^\circ$C
    4. 250 K
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    At constant volume $P\propto T$, so $\dfrac{\Delta P}{P}=\dfrac{\Delta T}{T}$.
    $0.004=\dfrac{1}{T}$, so $T=250$ K.

  16. JEE Main 2025, 4 April, Shift 1 · Question 35
    The mean free path and the average speed of oxygen molecules at 300K and 1 atm are $3\times10^{-7}$ m and 600 m/s, respectively. Find the frequency of its collisions.

    1. $2\times10^{10}$ /s
    2. $5\times10^{8}$ /s
    3. $9\times10^{5}$ /s
    4. $2\times10^{9}$ /s
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    Collision frequency $=\dfrac{\text{average speed}}{\text{mean free path}}=\dfrac{600}{3\times10^{-7}}=2\times10^9$ s$^{-1}$.

  17. JEE Main 2025, 4 April, Shift 2 · Question 45
    There are two vessels filled with an ideal gas where volume of one is double the volume of other. The large vessel contains the gas at 8 kPa at 1000 K while the smaller vessel contains the gas at 7 kPa at 500 K. If the vessels are connected to each other by a thin tube allowing the gas to flow and the temperature of both vessels is maintained at 600 K, at steady state the pressure in the vessels will be (in kPa).

    1. 6
    2. 4.4
    3. 18
    4. 24
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    The total number of moles is conserved: $\dfrac{8(2V)}{1000}+\dfrac{7V}{500}=\dfrac{P(3V)}{600}$ (common factor $R$ cancelled).
    $0.016+0.014=\dfrac{P}{200}$, so $P=6$ kPa.

  18. JEE Main 2025, 7 April, Shift 1 · Question 33
    Match the LIST-I with LIST-I

    LIST-ILIST-II
    A. Triatomic rigid gasI. $\dfrac{C_p}{C_v}=\dfrac53$
    B. Diatomic non-rigid gasII. $\dfrac{C_p}{C_v}=\dfrac75$
    C. Monoatomic gasIII. $\dfrac{C_p}{C_v}=\dfrac43$
    D. Diatomic rigid gasIV. $\dfrac{C_p}{C_v}=\dfrac97$
    Choose the correct answer from the options given below:

    1. A-III, B-II, C-IV, D-I
    2. A-III, B-IV, C-I, D-II
    3. A-II, B-IV, C-I, D-III
    4. A-IV, B-II, C-III, D-I
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    $\gamma=1+\dfrac2f$ with $f$ the degrees of freedom.
    Triatomic rigid (non-linear): $f=6$, $\gamma=\frac43$ (III). Diatomic non-rigid (with vibration): $f=7$, $\gamma=\frac97$ (IV). Monoatomic: $f=3$, $\gamma=\frac53$ (I). Diatomic rigid: $f=5$, $\gamma=\frac75$ (II).
    A-III, B-IV, C-I, D-II.

  19. JEE Main 2025, 7 April, Shift 2 · Question 33
    The helium and argon are put in the flask at the same room temperature (300 K). The ratio of average kinetic energies (per molecule) of helium and argon is :
    (Give : Molar mass of helium = 4 g/mol, Molar mass of argon = 40 g/mol)

    1. 1 : 1
    2. 1 : 10
    3. 10 : 1
    4. 1 : $\sqrt{10}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    The average translational kinetic energy per molecule is $\dfrac32kT$, independent of the molar mass. Both are monoatomic at the same temperature, so the ratio is 1 : 1.

  20. JEE Main 2025, 22 January, Shift 2 · Question 34
    For a diatomic gas, if $\gamma_1=\left(\dfrac{C_p}{C_v}\right)$ for rigid molecules and $\gamma_2=\left(\dfrac{C_p}{C_v}\right)$ for another diatomic molecules, but also having vibrational modes. Then, which one of the following options is correct ?
    ($C_p$ and $C_v$ are specific heats of the gas at constant pressure and volume)

    1. $\gamma_2>\gamma_1$
    2. $\gamma_2<\gamma_1$
    3. $\gamma_2=\gamma_1$
    4. $2\gamma_2=\gamma_1$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    Rigid diatomic: $f=5$, $\gamma_1=\dfrac75$. With vibration, $f=7$, $\gamma_2=\dfrac97$.
    More degrees of freedom lower $\gamma=1+\dfrac2f$, so $\gamma_2<\gamma_1$.

  21. JEE Main 2025, 28 January, Shift 1 · Question 31
    For a particular ideal gas which of the following graphs represents the variation of mean squared velocity of the gas molecules with temperature?

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    $\dfrac12m\overline{v^2}=\dfrac32k_BT$, so $\overline{v^2}=\dfrac{3k_BT}{m}\propto T$.
    The correct graph is the straight line through the origin.

  22. JEE Main 2025, 28 January, Shift 2 · Question 34
    The kinetic energy of translation of the molecules in 50 g of $\mathrm{CO_2}$ gas at $17^\circ$C is

    1. 4102.8 J
    2. 3986.3 J
    3. 4205.5 J
    4. 3582.7 J
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    Translational KE $=\dfrac32nRT$ with $n=\dfrac{50}{44}$ mol and $T=290$ K.
    $\dfrac32\times\dfrac{50}{44}\times8.3\times290\approx4102.8$ J (with $R=8.314$ it is about 4110 J; 4102.8 J is the closest option).

  23. JEE Main 2025, 28 January, Shift 2 · Question 35
    The ratio of vapour densities of two gases at the same temperature is $\dfrac{4}{25}$, then the ratio of r.m.s. velocities will be :

    1. $\dfrac{5}{2}$
    2. $\dfrac{2}{5}$
    3. $\dfrac{25}{4}$
    4. $\dfrac{4}{25}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    Vapour density is proportional to molar mass, and $v_{\text{rms}}=\sqrt{\dfrac{3RT}{M}}\propto\dfrac{1}{\sqrt M}$.
    $\dfrac{v_1}{v_2}=\sqrt{\dfrac{25}{4}}=\dfrac52$.

  24. JEE Main 2025, 29 January, Shift 1 · Question 49
    A container of fixed volume contains a gas at 27° C. To double the pressure of the gas, the temperature of gas should be raised to __________ °C.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 327

    At constant volume $p\propto T$ (kelvin). Doubling $p$ needs $T=2\times300=600$ K $=327$ °C.
