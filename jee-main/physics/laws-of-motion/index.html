  1. JEE Main 2026, 2 April, Shift 1 · Question 29
    The position of an object having mass 0.1 kg as a function of time $t$ is given as $\vec{r} = \left(10t^2\hat{i} + 5t^3\hat{j}\right)$ m. At $t = 1$ s, which of the following statements are correct ?
    A. The linear momentum $\vec{p} = \left(2\hat{i} + 1.5\hat{j}\right)$ kg-m/s.
    B. The force acting on the object $\vec{F} = \left(2\hat{i} + 3\hat{j}\right)$N.
    C. The angular momentum of the object about its origin $\vec{L} = 15\ \hat{k}$ J s.
    D. The torque acting on the object about its origin $\vec{\tau} = 20\ \hat{k}$ N m.
    Choose the correct answer from the options given below :

    1. A, B and C only
    2. B, C and D only
    3. A, C and D only
    4. A, B and D only
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    At $t=1$: $\vec{r} = 10\hat{i}+5\hat{j}$, $\vec{v} = 20t\hat{i}+15t^2\hat{j} = 20\hat{i}+15\hat{j}$, $\vec{a} = 20\hat{i}+30t\hat{j} = 20\hat{i}+30\hat{j}$.
    $\vec{p} = m\vec{v} = 2\hat{i}+1.5\hat{j}$ (A true); $\vec{F} = m\vec{a} = 2\hat{i}+3\hat{j}$ (B true).
    $\vec{L} = \vec{r}\times\vec{p} = (10)(1.5)-(5)(2) = 5\hat{k}$ (C false).
    $\vec{\tau} = \vec{r}\times\vec{F} = (10)(3)-(5)(2) = 20\hat{k}$ (D true). Hence A, B and D only.

  2. JEE Main 2026, 2 April, Shift 2 · Question 27
    A 0.5 kg mass is in contact against the inner wall of a cylindrical drum of radius 4 m rotating about its vertical axis. The minimum rotational speed of the drum to enable the mass to remain stuck to the wall (without falling) is 5 rad/s. The coefficient of friction between the drum's inner wall surface and mass is __________. (Take $g=10$ m/s$^2$)

    1. $0.1$
    2. $0.5$
    3. $0.7$
    4. $0.3$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    The wall supplies the normal force $N=m\omega^2r$; friction must balance the weight: $\mu m\omega^2 r \ge mg$.
    At the minimum speed: $\mu=\dfrac{g}{\omega^2 r}=\dfrac{10}{25\times4}=0.1$.

  3. JEE Main 2026, 4 April, Shift 1 · Question 28
    The time taken by a block of mass $m$ to slide down from the highest point to the lowest point on a rough inclined plane is $50\,\%$ more compared to the time taken by the same block on identical inclined smooth plane. Both inclined planes are at $45^\circ$ with the horizontal. The coefficient of kinetic friction between the rough inclined surface and block is _______.

    1. $3/4$
    2. $2/3$
    3. $5/9$
    4. $4/9$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    Same length $L=\dfrac12at^2$, so $a\propto\dfrac1{t^2}$: $\dfrac{a_{\text{smooth}}}{a_{\text{rough}}}=(1.5)^2=\dfrac94$.
    At $45^\circ$: $a_{\text{smooth}}=g\sin45^\circ$, $a_{\text{rough}}=g\sin45^\circ(1-\mu)$.
    $\dfrac{1}{1-\mu}=\dfrac94 \Rightarrow \mu=1-\dfrac49=\dfrac59$.

  4. JEE Main 2026, 4 April, Shift 2 · Question 28
    At $t=0$, a body of mass 100 g starts moving under the influence of a force $(5\hat i+10\hat j)$ N. After 2 s its position is $(2x\hat i+5y\hat j)$ m. The ratio $x:y$ is_______.

    1. $1:2$
    2. $2:5$
    3. $5:2$
    4. $5:4$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    $\vec a=\dfrac{\vec F}{m}=\dfrac{5\hat i+10\hat j}{0.1}=(50\hat i+100\hat j)$ m/s$^2$.
    Starting from rest at the origin: $\vec r=\dfrac12\vec a t^2=(100\hat i+200\hat j)$ m at $t=2$ s.
    $2x=100\Rightarrow x=50$; $5y=200\Rightarrow y=40$.
    $x:y=5:4$.

  5. JEE Main 2026, 5 April, Shift 1 · Question 29
    Three masses $m_1 = 4$ kg, $m_2 = 4$ kg and $m_3 = 6$ kg are suspended from a fixed smooth frictionless pully as shown in the figure below. The value of $T_1/T_2$ is ______
    (take $g = 10$ m/s$^2$)

    1. $5/3$
    2. $2/3$
    3. $3/5$
    4. $2/5$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    Side 1: $4$ kg; side 2: $m_2+m_3=10$ kg. Acceleration $a=\dfrac{(10-4)g}{14}=\dfrac{30}{7}$ m/s$^2$ (side 2 down).
    $T_1=m_1(g+a)=4\left(10+\dfrac{30}{7}\right)=\dfrac{400}{7}$ N.
    $T_2=m_3(g-a)=6\left(10-\dfrac{30}{7}\right)=\dfrac{240}{7}$ N.
    $\dfrac{T_1}{T_2}=\dfrac{400}{240}=\dfrac53$.

  6. JEE Main 2026, 5 April, Shift 1 · Question 30
    A wedge $Y$ with mass of $10$ kg and all frictionless surfaces and the inclined surface making $37^\circ$ with horizontal. A block $X$ with mass $2$ kg is placed at the highest point of the wedge as shown in figure is at rest. At $t = 0$ wedge ($Y$) is pulled toward right with constant force ($f$) of $24$ N. Taking the block $X$ at rest at $t = 0$, the time taken by it to slide down $8.8$ m on the slope, while $Y$ is on the move, is_____ s.
    (take $\tan(37^\circ) = 3/4$ and $g = 10$ m/s$^2$)

    1. $2$
    2. $4$
    3. $\sqrt2$
    4. $2\sqrt2$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    Take the acceleration of the wedge as that of the whole system: $A=\dfrac{f}{M+m}=\dfrac{24}{12}=2$ m/s$^2$ to the right.
    In the wedge frame the block feels a pseudo force $mA$ to the left; the incline faces right, so this force pushes the block into the slope and opposes sliding: along the slope $a_r=g\sin37^\circ-A\cos37^\circ=6-1.6=4.4$ m/s$^2$.
    $s=\dfrac12a_rt^2$: $8.8=\dfrac12(4.4)t^2 \Rightarrow t^2=4$, $t=2$ s.

  7. JEE Main 2026, 6 April, Shift 1 · Question 29
    Two blocks ($P$ and $Q$) with respectively masses 2 kg and 1.5 kg are joined by a massless thread. These blocks are mounted on a frictionless pully which is fixed on the edge of a cube ($S$), as shown in the figure below. Block $P$ is positioned on the top surface which has no friction and block $Q$ is in contact with side-surface, having coefficient friction $\mu$. The cube ($S$) moves towards the right with acceleration of $\dfrac g2$, where $g$ is gravitational acceleration. During this movement the block $P$ and $Q$ remain stationary. The value of $\mu$ is ________.
    (take $g=10$ m/s$^2$)

    1. $0.33$
    2. $0.67$
    3. $1$
    4. $0.5$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    Both blocks move with the cube, acceleration $a=\dfrac g2=5$ m/s$^2$ to the right.
    $P$ (smooth top): only the thread pulls it horizontally, so $T=m_Pa=2\times5=10$ N.
    $Q$: the side face pushes it horizontally, $N=m_Qa=1.5\times5=7.5$ N.
    Vertically for $Q$: $T+f=m_Qg\Rightarrow f=15-10=5$ N.
    No slipping: $f\le\mu N\Rightarrow\mu\ge\dfrac{5}{7.5}=\dfrac23\approx0.67$.

  8. JEE Main 2026, 6 April, Shift 2 · Question 44
    The velocity at which 6 kg mass (shown in figure) strikes the ground when it is released from a height of 6 m above the ground is _________ m/s. Assume pulley is massless and string is light and inextensible. (Take $g=10$ m/s$^2$)

    1. $7.74$
    2. $7.20$
    3. $6.55$
    4. $4.50$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    $a=\dfrac{(6-2)g}{6+2}=5$ m/s$^2$.
    $v=\sqrt{2as}=\sqrt{2\times5\times6}=\sqrt{60}\approx7.74$ m/s.

  9. JEE Main 2026, 6 April, Shift 2 · Question 46
    A block takes $t$ time to slide down a plane inclined at $45^\circ$ to the horizontal. If the surface is made smooth (frictionless), the block takes time $\dfrac t2$ to slide down the plane. The coefficient of friction between the block and the inclined plane is $\left(\dfrac{\alpha}{100}\right)$. The value of $\alpha$ is _________.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 75

    For the same distance from rest, $t\propto\dfrac1{\sqrt a}$, so $a_{rough}=\dfrac{a_{smooth}}{4}$.
    $g(\sin45^\circ-\mu\cos45^\circ)=\dfrac{g\sin45^\circ}{4} \Rightarrow 1-\mu=\dfrac14$.
    $\mu=0.75=\dfrac{75}{100}$, so $\alpha=75$.

  10. JEE Main 2026, 8 April, Shift 2 · Question 28
    A car moving with a speed of 54 km/h takes a turn of radius 20 m. A simple pendulum is suspended from the ceiling of the car. Determine the angle made by the string of the pendulum with the vertical during the turning. (Take $g=10\ \text{m/s}^2$)

    1. $\tan^{-1}(0.5)$
    2. $\tan^{-1}(0.75)$
    3. $\tan^{-1}(1.125)$
    4. $\tan^{-1}(0.25)$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    $v=54$ km/h $=15$ m/s; centripetal acceleration $\dfrac{v^2}{r}=\dfrac{225}{20}=11.25\ \text{m/s}^2$.
    In the car frame the bob is in equilibrium: $\tan\theta=\dfrac{v^2/r}{g}=\dfrac{11.25}{10}=1.125$.
    $\theta=\tan^{-1}(1.125)$.
