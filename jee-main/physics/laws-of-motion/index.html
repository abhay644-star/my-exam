  1. JEE Main 2026, 2 April, Shift 1 · Question 29
    The position of an object having mass 0.1 kg as a function of time $t$ is given as $\vec{r} = \left(10t^2\hat{i} + 5t^3\hat{j}\right)$ m. At $t = 1$ s, which of the following statements are correct ?
    A. The linear momentum $\vec{p} = \left(2\hat{i} + 1.5\hat{j}\right)$ kg-m/s.
    B. The force acting on the object $\vec{F} = \left(2\hat{i} + 3\hat{j}\right)$N.
    C. The angular momentum of the object about its origin $\vec{L} = 15\ \hat{k}$ J s.
    D. The torque acting on the object about its origin $\vec{\tau} = 20\ \hat{k}$ N m.
    Choose the correct answer from the options given below :

    1. A, B and C only
    2. B, C and D only
    3. A, C and D only
    4. A, B and D only
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    At $t=1$: $\vec{r} = 10\hat{i}+5\hat{j}$, $\vec{v} = 20t\hat{i}+15t^2\hat{j} = 20\hat{i}+15\hat{j}$, $\vec{a} = 20\hat{i}+30t\hat{j} = 20\hat{i}+30\hat{j}$.
    $\vec{p} = m\vec{v} = 2\hat{i}+1.5\hat{j}$ (A true); $\vec{F} = m\vec{a} = 2\hat{i}+3\hat{j}$ (B true).
    $\vec{L} = \vec{r}\times\vec{p} = (10)(1.5)-(5)(2) = 5\hat{k}$ (C false).
    $\vec{\tau} = \vec{r}\times\vec{F} = (10)(3)-(5)(2) = 20\hat{k}$ (D true). Hence A, B and D only.

  2. JEE Main 2026, 2 April, Shift 2 · Question 27
    A 0.5 kg mass is in contact against the inner wall of a cylindrical drum of radius 4 m rotating about its vertical axis. The minimum rotational speed of the drum to enable the mass to remain stuck to the wall (without falling) is 5 rad/s. The coefficient of friction between the drum's inner wall surface and mass is __________. (Take $g=10$ m/s$^2$)

    1. $0.1$
    2. $0.5$
    3. $0.7$
    4. $0.3$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    The wall supplies the normal force $N=m\omega^2r$; friction must balance the weight: $\mu m\omega^2 r \ge mg$.
    At the minimum speed: $\mu=\dfrac{g}{\omega^2 r}=\dfrac{10}{25\times4}=0.1$.

  3. JEE Main 2026, 4 April, Shift 1 · Question 28
    The time taken by a block of mass $m$ to slide down from the highest point to the lowest point on a rough inclined plane is $50\,\%$ more compared to the time taken by the same block on identical inclined smooth plane. Both inclined planes are at $45^\circ$ with the horizontal. The coefficient of kinetic friction between the rough inclined surface and block is _______.

    1. $3/4$
    2. $2/3$
    3. $5/9$
    4. $4/9$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    Same length $L=\dfrac12at^2$, so $a\propto\dfrac1{t^2}$: $\dfrac{a_{\text{smooth}}}{a_{\text{rough}}}=(1.5)^2=\dfrac94$.
    At $45^\circ$: $a_{\text{smooth}}=g\sin45^\circ$, $a_{\text{rough}}=g\sin45^\circ(1-\mu)$.
    $\dfrac{1}{1-\mu}=\dfrac94 \Rightarrow \mu=1-\dfrac49=\dfrac59$.

  4. JEE Main 2026, 4 April, Shift 2 · Question 28
    At $t=0$, a body of mass 100 g starts moving under the influence of a force $(5\hat i+10\hat j)$ N. After 2 s its position is $(2x\hat i+5y\hat j)$ m. The ratio $x:y$ is_______.

    1. $1:2$
    2. $2:5$
    3. $5:2$
    4. $5:4$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    $\vec a=\dfrac{\vec F}{m}=\dfrac{5\hat i+10\hat j}{0.1}=(50\hat i+100\hat j)$ m/s$^2$.
    Starting from rest at the origin: $\vec r=\dfrac12\vec a t^2=(100\hat i+200\hat j)$ m at $t=2$ s.
    $2x=100\Rightarrow x=50$; $5y=200\Rightarrow y=40$.
    $x:y=5:4$.

  5. JEE Main 2026, 5 April, Shift 1 · Question 29
    Three masses $m_1 = 4$ kg, $m_2 = 4$ kg and $m_3 = 6$ kg are suspended from a fixed smooth frictionless pully as shown in the figure below. The value of $T_1/T_2$ is ______
    (take $g = 10$ m/s$^2$)

    1. $5/3$
    2. $2/3$
    3. $3/5$
    4. $2/5$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    Side 1: $4$ kg; side 2: $m_2+m_3=10$ kg. Acceleration $a=\dfrac{(10-4)g}{14}=\dfrac{30}{7}$ m/s$^2$ (side 2 down).
    $T_1=m_1(g+a)=4\left(10+\dfrac{30}{7}\right)=\dfrac{400}{7}$ N.
    $T_2=m_3(g-a)=6\left(10-\dfrac{30}{7}\right)=\dfrac{240}{7}$ N.
    $\dfrac{T_1}{T_2}=\dfrac{400}{240}=\dfrac53$.

  6. JEE Main 2026, 5 April, Shift 1 · Question 30
    A wedge $Y$ with mass of $10$ kg and all frictionless surfaces and the inclined surface making $37^\circ$ with horizontal. A block $X$ with mass $2$ kg is placed at the highest point of the wedge as shown in figure is at rest. At $t = 0$ wedge ($Y$) is pulled toward right with constant force ($f$) of $24$ N. Taking the block $X$ at rest at $t = 0$, the time taken by it to slide down $8.8$ m on the slope, while $Y$ is on the move, is_____ s.
    (take $\tan(37^\circ) = 3/4$ and $g = 10$ m/s$^2$)

    1. $2$
    2. $4$
    3. $\sqrt2$
    4. $2\sqrt2$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    Take the acceleration of the wedge as that of the whole system: $A=\dfrac{f}{M+m}=\dfrac{24}{12}=2$ m/s$^2$ to the right.
    In the wedge frame the block feels a pseudo force $mA$ to the left; the incline faces right, so this force pushes the block into the slope and opposes sliding: along the slope $a_r=g\sin37^\circ-A\cos37^\circ=6-1.6=4.4$ m/s$^2$.
    $s=\dfrac12a_rt^2$: $8.8=\dfrac12(4.4)t^2 \Rightarrow t^2=4$, $t=2$ s.

  7. JEE Main 2026, 6 April, Shift 1 · Question 29
    Two blocks ($P$ and $Q$) with respectively masses 2 kg and 1.5 kg are joined by a massless thread. These blocks are mounted on a frictionless pully which is fixed on the edge of a cube ($S$), as shown in the figure below. Block $P$ is positioned on the top surface which has no friction and block $Q$ is in contact with side-surface, having coefficient friction $\mu$. The cube ($S$) moves towards the right with acceleration of $\dfrac g2$, where $g$ is gravitational acceleration. During this movement the block $P$ and $Q$ remain stationary. The value of $\mu$ is ________.
    (take $g=10$ m/s$^2$)

    1. $0.33$
    2. $0.67$
    3. $1$
    4. $0.5$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    Both blocks move with the cube, acceleration $a=\dfrac g2=5$ m/s$^2$ to the right.
    $P$ (smooth top): only the thread pulls it horizontally, so $T=m_Pa=2\times5=10$ N.
    $Q$: the side face pushes it horizontally, $N=m_Qa=1.5\times5=7.5$ N.
    Vertically for $Q$: $T+f=m_Qg\Rightarrow f=15-10=5$ N.
    No slipping: $f\le\mu N\Rightarrow\mu\ge\dfrac{5}{7.5}=\dfrac23\approx0.67$.

  8. JEE Main 2026, 6 April, Shift 2 · Question 44
    The velocity at which 6 kg mass (shown in figure) strikes the ground when it is released from a height of 6 m above the ground is _________ m/s. Assume pulley is massless and string is light and inextensible. (Take $g=10$ m/s$^2$)

    1. $7.74$
    2. $7.20$
    3. $6.55$
    4. $4.50$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    $a=\dfrac{(6-2)g}{6+2}=5$ m/s$^2$.
    $v=\sqrt{2as}=\sqrt{2\times5\times6}=\sqrt{60}\approx7.74$ m/s.

  9. JEE Main 2026, 6 April, Shift 2 · Question 46
    A block takes $t$ time to slide down a plane inclined at $45^\circ$ to the horizontal. If the surface is made smooth (frictionless), the block takes time $\dfrac t2$ to slide down the plane. The coefficient of friction between the block and the inclined plane is $\left(\dfrac{\alpha}{100}\right)$. The value of $\alpha$ is _________.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 75

    For the same distance from rest, $t\propto\dfrac1{\sqrt a}$, so $a_{rough}=\dfrac{a_{smooth}}{4}$.
    $g(\sin45^\circ-\mu\cos45^\circ)=\dfrac{g\sin45^\circ}{4} \Rightarrow 1-\mu=\dfrac14$.
    $\mu=0.75=\dfrac{75}{100}$, so $\alpha=75$.

  10. JEE Main 2026, 8 April, Shift 2 · Question 28
    A car moving with a speed of 54 km/h takes a turn of radius 20 m. A simple pendulum is suspended from the ceiling of the car. Determine the angle made by the string of the pendulum with the vertical during the turning. (Take $g=10\ \text{m/s}^2$)

    1. $\tan^{-1}(0.5)$
    2. $\tan^{-1}(0.75)$
    3. $\tan^{-1}(1.125)$
    4. $\tan^{-1}(0.25)$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    $v=54$ km/h $=15$ m/s; centripetal acceleration $\dfrac{v^2}{r}=\dfrac{225}{20}=11.25\ \text{m/s}^2$.
    In the car frame the bob is in equilibrium: $\tan\theta=\dfrac{v^2/r}{g}=\dfrac{11.25}{10}=1.125$.
    $\theta=\tan^{-1}(1.125)$.

  11. JEE Main 2026, 21 January, Shift 1 · Question 28
    A 4 kg mass moves under the influence of a force $\vec{F}=\left(4t^3\hat{i}-3t\hat{j}\right)$ N where $t$ is the time in second. If mass starts from origin at $t=0$, the velocity and position after $t=2$ s will be :

    1. $\vec{v}=4\hat{i}-\dfrac{3}{2}\hat{j}$, $\vec{r}=\dfrac{6}{5}\hat{i}-\hat{j}$
    2. $\vec{v}=3\hat{i}+\dfrac{3}{2}\hat{j}$, $\vec{r}=\dfrac{6}{5}\hat{i}+\hat{j}$
    3. $\vec{v}=4\hat{i}+\dfrac{5}{2}\hat{j}$, $\vec{r}=\dfrac{8}{5}\hat{i}+2\hat{j}$
    4. $\vec{v}=4\hat{i}-\dfrac{3}{2}\hat{j}$, $\vec{r}=\dfrac{8}{5}\hat{i}-\hat{j}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    $\vec a=\dfrac{\vec F}{4}=t^3\hat i-\dfrac{3t}{4}\hat j$ (starting from rest at the origin).
    $\vec v=\dfrac{t^4}{4}\hat i-\dfrac{3t^2}{8}\hat j$; at $t=2$: $4\hat i-\dfrac32\hat j$.
    $\vec r=\dfrac{t^5}{20}\hat i-\dfrac{t^3}{8}\hat j$; at $t=2$: $\dfrac85\hat i-\hat j$.

  12. JEE Main 2026, 21 January, Shift 2 · Question 32
    A large drum having radius $R$ is spinning around its axis with angular velocity $\omega$, as shown in figure. The minimum value of $\omega$ so that a body of mass $M$ remains stuck to the inner wall of the drum, taking the coefficient of friction between the drum surface and mass $M$ as $\mu$, is :

    1. $\sqrt{\dfrac{g}{2\mu R}}$
    2. $\sqrt{\dfrac{g}{\mu R}}$
    3. $\sqrt{\dfrac{2g}{\mu R}}$
    4. $\sqrt{\dfrac{\mu g}{R}}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    The wall provides the normal reaction $N=M\omega^2R$ (centripetal force).
    Friction must hold the weight: $\mu N\ge Mg \Rightarrow \mu M\omega^2R\ge Mg$.
    $\omega_{min}=\sqrt{\dfrac{g}{\mu R}}$.

  13. JEE Main 2026, 23 January, Shift 2 · Question 29
    A block is sliding down on an inclined plane of slope $\theta$ and at an instant $t=0$ this block is given an upward momentum so that it starts moving up on the inclined surface with velocity $u$. The distance $(S)$ travelled by the block before its velocity become zero, is ________.
    ($g=$ gravitational acceleration)

    1. $\dfrac{u^2}{4g\sin\theta}$
    2. $\dfrac{u^2}{2g\cos\theta}$
    3. $\dfrac{2u^2}{g\cos\theta}$
    4. $\dfrac{u^2}{\sqrt2g\cos\theta}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: Dropped: full marks to everyone

    If the block slides down at constant speed, friction balances the component of gravity: $\mu g\cos\theta=g\sin\theta$. Moving up, the retardation is then $2g\sin\theta$ and $S=\dfrac{u^2}{4g\sin\theta}$.
    On a smooth incline it would be $\dfrac{u^2}{2g\sin\theta}$. The question does not say which, which is why it was dropped.

    Note: Dropped in the official final key: full marks to everyone. The question gives no information about friction, so the distance cannot be fixed from the data given.

  14. JEE Main 2026, 23 January, Shift 2 · Question 30
    A body of mass 14 kg initially at rest explodes and breaks into three fragments of masses in the ratio $2:2:3$. The two pieces of equal masses fly off perpendicular to each other with a speed of 18 m/s each. The velocity of the heavier fragment is ___ m/s.

    1. $12\sqrt2$
    2. $10\sqrt2$
    3. $12$
    4. $24\sqrt2$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    Masses: 4 kg, 4 kg and 6 kg.
    The two 4 kg pieces have perpendicular momenta of $72$ kg m/s each; their resultant is $72\sqrt2$ kg m/s.
    The total momentum stays zero, so $6v=72\sqrt2$ and $v=12\sqrt2$ m/s.

  15. JEE Main 2026, 24 January, Shift 1 · Question 47
    In the given figure the blocks $A$, $B$ and $C$ weigh 4 kg, 6 kg and 8 kg respectively. The co-efficient of sliding friction between any two surfaces is 0.5. The force $\vec{F}$ required to slide the block $C$ with constant speed is __________ N. (Use $g=10$ m/s$^2$)

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 210

    B and C are joined by the cord over the pulley, so when C moves left B moves right. The key's value treats A as held at rest (as in the standard form of this problem, where A is fixed to the wall), so friction acts at every surface.
    A on B: $0.5\times40=20$ N. B on C: $0.5\times(40+60)=50$ N. Ground on C: $0.5\times180=90$ N.
    For B: $T=20+50=70$ N.
    For C: $F=T+50+90=70+50+90=210$ N.

    Note: The official final key gives 210, which assumes block A is held at rest. The figure shows no support for A; if A simply rides on B, the force works out to 190 N. The key is followed.

  16. JEE Main 2026, 24 January, Shift 2 · Question 29
    A flexible chain of mass $m$ hangs between two fixed points at the same level. The inclination of the chain with the horizontal at the two points of support is $30^\circ$. Considering the equilibrium of each half of the chain, the tension of the chain at the lowest point is __________.

    1. $\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}mg$
    2. $\sqrt3mg$
    3. $\dfrac12mg$
    4. $mg$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    For one half (weight $\dfrac{mg}2$): vertical balance $T\sin30^\circ=\dfrac{mg}2 \Rightarrow T=mg$ at the support.
    Horizontal balance: tension at the lowest point $T_0=T\cos30^\circ=\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}mg$.

  17. JEE Main 2026, 28 January, Shift 1 · Question 28
    A particle of mass $m$ falls from rest through a resistive medium having resistive force, $F=-kv$, where $v$ is the velocity of the particle and $k$ is a constant. Which of the following graphs represents velocity $(v)$ versus time $(t)$?

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    $m\dfrac{dv}{dt}=mg-kv$ with $v(0)=0$ gives $v=\dfrac{mg}{k}\left(1-e^{-kt/m}\right)$.
    The velocity starts at zero, rises with decreasing slope and approaches the terminal value $mg/k$: graph (2).

  18. JEE Main 2026, 28 January, Shift 1 · Question 29
    A block of mass 5 kg is moving on an inclined plane which makes an angle of $30^\circ$ with the horizontal. Friction coefficient between the block and inclined plane surface is $\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}$. The force to be applied on the block so that the block will move down without acceleration is _______N.
    $(g=10\ \mathrm{m/s^2})$.

    1. $12.5$
    2. $7.5$
    3. $25$
    4. $15$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    Component of weight down the plane: $mg\sin30^\circ=25$ N.
    Kinetic friction (up the plane while moving down): $\mu mg\cos30^\circ=\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}\cdot50\cdot\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}=37.5$ N.
    For zero acceleration a push of $37.5-25=12.5$ N down the plane is needed.

  19. JEE Main 2026, 28 January, Shift 2 · Question 29
    A small block of mass $m$ slides down from the top of a frictionless inclined surface, while the inclined plane is moving towards left with constant acceleration $a_0$. The angle between the inclined plane and ground is $\theta$ and its base length is $L$. Assuming that initially the small block is at the top of the inclined plane, the time it takes to reach the lowest point of the inclined plane is _________.

    1. $\sqrt{\dfrac{4L}{g\sin2\theta-a_0(1+\cos2\theta)}}$
    2. $\sqrt{\dfrac{2L}{g\sin2\theta-a_0(1+\cos2\theta)}}$
    3. $\sqrt{\dfrac{4L}{g\cos^2\theta-a_0\sin\theta\cos\theta}}$
    4. $\sqrt{\dfrac{2L}{g\sin\theta-a_0\cos\theta}}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    In the frame of the incline a pseudo force $ma_0$ acts to the right, i.e. with a component $a_0\cos\theta$ up the slope (the block slides down towards the left).
    Acceleration along the incline: $g\sin\theta-a_0\cos\theta$; distance $\dfrac{L}{\cos\theta}$.
    $t=\sqrt{\dfrac{2L}{\cos\theta(g\sin\theta-a_0\cos\theta)}}=\sqrt{\dfrac{4L}{g\sin2\theta-a_0(1+\cos2\theta)}}$.

  20. JEE Main 2025, 22 January, Shift 2 · Question 46
    A tube of length 1m is filled completely with an ideal liquid of mass 2M, and closed at both ends. The tube is rotated uniformly in horizontal plane about one of its ends. If the force exerted by the liquid at the other end is F then angular velocity of the tube is $\sqrt{\dfrac{F}{\alpha M}}$ in SI unit. The value of $\alpha$ is __________.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 1

    The closed end supplies the centripetal force for the whole liquid column, whose centre of mass is at $\dfrac L2=0.5$ m.
    $F=(2M)\omega^2(0.5)=M\omega^2$, so $\omega=\sqrt{\dfrac FM}$ and $\alpha=1$.

  21. JEE Main 2025, 24 January, Shift 1 · Question 28
    A car of mass 'm' moves on a banked road having radius 'r' and banking angle $\theta$. To avoid slipping from banked road, the maximum permissible speed of the car is $v_0$. The coefficient of friction $\mu$ between the wheels of the car and the banked road is

    1. $\mu=\dfrac{v_0^2+rg\tan\theta}{rg+v_0^2\tan\theta}$
    2. $\mu=\dfrac{v_0^2+rg\tan\theta}{rg-v_0^2\tan\theta}$
    3. $\mu=\dfrac{v_0^2-rg\tan\theta}{rg+v_0^2\tan\theta}$
    4. $\mu=\dfrac{v_0^2-rg\tan\theta}{rg-v_0^2\tan\theta}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    At the maximum speed, friction acts down the slope: $v_0^2=rg\,\dfrac{\tan\theta+\mu}{1-\mu\tan\theta}$.
    $v_0^2-\mu v_0^2\tan\theta=rg\tan\theta+\mu rg \Rightarrow \mu=\dfrac{v_0^2-rg\tan\theta}{rg+v_0^2\tan\theta}$.

  22. JEE Main 2025, 24 January, Shift 2 · Question 46
    A string of length L is fixed at one end and carries a mass of M at the other end. The mass makes $\left(\dfrac3\pi\right)$ rotations per second about the vertical axis passing through end of the string as shown. The tension in the string is __________ ML.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 36

    For a conical pendulum, the horizontal component of tension provides the centripetal force: $T\sin\theta=M\omega^2(L\sin\theta)$, so $T=M\omega^2L$.
    $\omega=2\pi\times\dfrac3\pi=6$ rad/s, so $T=36ML$.

  23. JEE Main 2025, 28 January, Shift 2 · Question 32
    A balloon and its content having mass M is moving up with an acceleration 'a'. The mass that must be released from the content so that the balloon starts moving up with an acceleration '3a' will be
    (Take 'g' as acceleration due to gravity)

    1. $\dfrac{3Ma}{2a+g}$
    2. $\dfrac{2Ma}{3a+g}$
    3. $\dfrac{3Ma}{2a-g}$
    4. $\dfrac{2Ma}{3a-g}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    Upthrust $U$ stays the same. Initially $U-Mg=Ma$, so $U=M(g+a)$.
    After releasing $m$: $U-(M-m)g=(M-m)3a$, so $M-m=\dfrac{M(g+a)}{g+3a}$.
    $m=M-\dfrac{M(g+a)}{g+3a}=\dfrac{2Ma}{3a+g}$.

  24. JEE Main 2025, 29 January, Shift 2 · Question 27
    A sand dropper drops sand of mass m(t) on a conveyer belt at a rate proportional to the square root of speed $(v)$ of the belt, i.e. $\dfrac{dm}{dt}\propto\sqrt{v}$. If P is the power delivered to run the belt at constant speed then which of the following relationship is true ?

    1. $P\propto v$
    2. $P^2\propto v^3$
    3. $P^2\propto v^5$
    4. $P\propto\sqrt{v}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    Force needed to bring the falling sand up to the belt speed: $F=v\dfrac{dm}{dt}\propto v^{3/2}$.
    $P=Fv\propto v^{5/2}$, so $P^2\propto v^5$.
