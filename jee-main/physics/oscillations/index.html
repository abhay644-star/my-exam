  1. JEE Main 2026, 2 April, Shift 2 · Question 45
    The equation of motion of a particle is given by $x=a\sin\left(50t+\pi/3\right)$ cm. The particle will come to rest at time $t_1$ and it will have zero acceleration at time $t_2$. The $t_1$ and $t_2$ respectively are __________.

    1. $\dfrac{\pi}{300}$ s, $\dfrac{\pi}{75}$ s
    2. $\dfrac{\pi}{75}$ s, $\dfrac{\pi}{300}$ s
    3. $\dfrac{\pi}{300}$ s, $\dfrac{\pi}{25}$ s
    4. $\dfrac{\pi}{50}$ s, $\dfrac{\pi}{100}$ s
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    Velocity $v=50a\cos(50t+\pi/3)=0$ first when $50t+\dfrac\pi3=\dfrac\pi2 \Rightarrow t_1=\dfrac{\pi}{300}$ s.
    Acceleration $\propto\sin(50t+\pi/3)=0$ first (for $t>0$) when $50t+\dfrac\pi3=\pi \Rightarrow t_2=\dfrac{2\pi}{150}=\dfrac{\pi}{75}$ s.

  2. JEE Main 2026, 4 April, Shift 1 · Question 48
    The velocity of a particle executing simple harmonic motion along $x$-axis is described as $v^2=50-x^2$, where $x$ represents displacement. If the time period of motion is $\dfrac{x}{7}$ s, the value of $x$ is ________.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 44

    In SHM $v^2=\omega^2(A^2-x^2)$; comparing, $\omega^2=1$, so $\omega=1$ rad/s.
    $T=\dfrac{2\pi}{\omega}=2\pi=2\times\dfrac{22}{7}=\dfrac{44}{7}$ s.
    So $x=44$.

  3. JEE Main 2026, 4 April, Shift 2 · Question 35
    A uniform disc of radius $R$ and mass $M$ is free to oscillate about the axis $A$ as shown in the figure. For small oscillations the time period is __________.
    ($g$ is acceleration due to gravity)

    1. $2\pi\sqrt{\dfrac{5R}{4g}}$
    2. $2\pi\sqrt{\dfrac{2R}{3g}}$
    3. $2\pi\sqrt{\dfrac{3R}{2g}}$
    4. $2\pi\sqrt{\dfrac{3R}{g}}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    The axis is the tangent at $A$ in the plane of the disc (horizontal), so this is a physical pendulum.
    $I_A=I_{\text{diameter}}+MR^2=\dfrac{MR^2}{4}+MR^2=\dfrac54MR^2$; centre of mass at distance $R$ from the axis.
    $T=2\pi\sqrt{\dfrac{I_A}{MgR}}=2\pi\sqrt{\dfrac{5R}{4g}}$.

  4. JEE Main 2026, 5 April, Shift 2 · Question 35
    Match List - I with List - II.

    List - IList - II
    A. $\sin^2\omega t$I. Periodic with time period $T=\dfrac{\pi}{\omega}$ but not simple harmonic motion (SHM)
    B. $\sin^3(2\omega t)$II. Periodic with time period $T=\dfrac{2\pi}{\omega}$ but Not SHM
    C. $\sin(\omega t)+\cos(\pi\omega t)$III. Periodic with time period $T=\dfrac{\pi}{\omega}$ and SHM
    D. $\cos\omega t+\cos2\omega t$IV. Non-periodic
    Choose the correct answer from the options given below :

    1. A-III, B-I, C-IV, D-II
    2. A-II, B-I, C-III, D-IV
    3. A-III, B-II, C-IV, D-I
    4. A-II, B-I, C-IV, D-III
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    A: $\sin^2\omega t=\dfrac12-\dfrac12\cos2\omega t$, an SHM about $\dfrac12$ with period $\dfrac{\pi}{\omega}$ → III.
    B: $\sin^3(2\omega t)=\dfrac{3\sin2\omega t-\sin6\omega t}{4}$, period $\dfrac{\pi}{\omega}$, not SHM → I.
    C: angular frequencies $\omega$ and $\pi\omega$ have an irrational ratio, so the sum is non-periodic → IV.
    D: periods $\dfrac{2\pi}{\omega}$ and $\dfrac{\pi}{\omega}$, overall period $\dfrac{2\pi}{\omega}$, not SHM → II.
    A-III, B-I, C-IV, D-II.

  5. JEE Main 2026, 6 April, Shift 1 · Question 37
    A particle is executing simple harmonic motion. Its amplitude is $A$ and time period is 5 sec. The time required by it to move from $x=A$ to $x=\dfrac{A}{\sqrt2}$ is ________ sec.

    1. $1/4$
    2. $5/4$
    3. $5/8$
    4. $3/8$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    $x=A\cos\omega t$; $x=\dfrac{A}{\sqrt2}$ when $\omega t=\dfrac\pi4$.
    $t=\dfrac{\pi/4}{2\pi/T}=\dfrac T8=\dfrac58$ s.

  6. JEE Main 2026, 6 April, Shift 2 · Question 36
    A spring stretches by 2 mm when it is loaded with a mass of 200 g. From equilibrium position the mass is further pulled down by 2 mm and released. The frequency associated with the system and maxmimum energy in the spring are __________ Hz and _________ J, respectively. (Take $g=10$ m/s$^2$)

    1. $\dfrac{5\sqrt{50}}{\pi}$ and $8\times10^{-3}$
    2. $\dfrac{5\sqrt{50}}{\pi}$ and $8$
    3. $10\sqrt{50}$ and $2\times10^{-3}$
    4. $\dfrac{5\sqrt{50}}{\pi}$ and $16\times10^{-3}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    $k=\dfrac{mg}{x}=\dfrac{0.2\times10}{0.002}=1000$ N/m.
    $\omega=\sqrt{\dfrac km}=\sqrt{5000}=10\sqrt{50}$ rad/s, so $f=\dfrac{\omega}{2\pi}=\dfrac{5\sqrt{50}}{\pi}$ Hz.
    Maximum extension $=2+2=4$ mm, so maximum spring energy $=\dfrac12(1000)(0.004)^2=8\times10^{-3}$ J.

  7. JEE Main 2026, 8 April, Shift 2 · Question 37
    The frequency of oscillation of a mass $m$ suspended by a spring is $v_1$. If the length of the spring is cut to half, the same mass oscillates with frequency $v_2$. The value of $v_2/v_1$ is __________.

    1. $1$
    2. $2$
    3. $\sqrt{2}$
    4. $\sqrt{3}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    Spring constant $\propto\dfrac1{\text{length}}$, so halving the length doubles $k$.
    $v=\dfrac1{2\pi}\sqrt{\dfrac km}\propto\sqrt k$, hence $\dfrac{v_2}{v_1}=\sqrt2$.
