  1. JEE Main 2026, 2 April, Shift 2 · Question 45
    The equation of motion of a particle is given by $x=a\sin\left(50t+\pi/3\right)$ cm. The particle will come to rest at time $t_1$ and it will have zero acceleration at time $t_2$. The $t_1$ and $t_2$ respectively are __________.

    1. $\dfrac{\pi}{300}$ s, $\dfrac{\pi}{75}$ s
    2. $\dfrac{\pi}{75}$ s, $\dfrac{\pi}{300}$ s
    3. $\dfrac{\pi}{300}$ s, $\dfrac{\pi}{25}$ s
    4. $\dfrac{\pi}{50}$ s, $\dfrac{\pi}{100}$ s
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    Velocity $v=50a\cos(50t+\pi/3)=0$ first when $50t+\dfrac\pi3=\dfrac\pi2 \Rightarrow t_1=\dfrac{\pi}{300}$ s.
    Acceleration $\propto\sin(50t+\pi/3)=0$ first (for $t>0$) when $50t+\dfrac\pi3=\pi \Rightarrow t_2=\dfrac{2\pi}{150}=\dfrac{\pi}{75}$ s.

  2. JEE Main 2026, 4 April, Shift 1 · Question 48
    The velocity of a particle executing simple harmonic motion along $x$-axis is described as $v^2=50-x^2$, where $x$ represents displacement. If the time period of motion is $\dfrac{x}{7}$ s, the value of $x$ is ________.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 44

    In SHM $v^2=\omega^2(A^2-x^2)$; comparing, $\omega^2=1$, so $\omega=1$ rad/s.
    $T=\dfrac{2\pi}{\omega}=2\pi=2\times\dfrac{22}{7}=\dfrac{44}{7}$ s.
    So $x=44$.

  3. JEE Main 2026, 4 April, Shift 2 · Question 35
    A uniform disc of radius $R$ and mass $M$ is free to oscillate about the axis $A$ as shown in the figure. For small oscillations the time period is __________.
    ($g$ is acceleration due to gravity)

    1. $2\pi\sqrt{\dfrac{5R}{4g}}$
    2. $2\pi\sqrt{\dfrac{2R}{3g}}$
    3. $2\pi\sqrt{\dfrac{3R}{2g}}$
    4. $2\pi\sqrt{\dfrac{3R}{g}}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    The axis is the tangent at $A$ in the plane of the disc (horizontal), so this is a physical pendulum.
    $I_A=I_{\text{diameter}}+MR^2=\dfrac{MR^2}{4}+MR^2=\dfrac54MR^2$; centre of mass at distance $R$ from the axis.
    $T=2\pi\sqrt{\dfrac{I_A}{MgR}}=2\pi\sqrt{\dfrac{5R}{4g}}$.

  4. JEE Main 2026, 5 April, Shift 2 · Question 35
    Match List - I with List - II.

    List - IList - II
    A. $\sin^2\omega t$I. Periodic with time period $T=\dfrac{\pi}{\omega}$ but not simple harmonic motion (SHM)
    B. $\sin^3(2\omega t)$II. Periodic with time period $T=\dfrac{2\pi}{\omega}$ but Not SHM
    C. $\sin(\omega t)+\cos(\pi\omega t)$III. Periodic with time period $T=\dfrac{\pi}{\omega}$ and SHM
    D. $\cos\omega t+\cos2\omega t$IV. Non-periodic
    Choose the correct answer from the options given below :

    1. A-III, B-I, C-IV, D-II
    2. A-II, B-I, C-III, D-IV
    3. A-III, B-II, C-IV, D-I
    4. A-II, B-I, C-IV, D-III
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    A: $\sin^2\omega t=\dfrac12-\dfrac12\cos2\omega t$, an SHM about $\dfrac12$ with period $\dfrac{\pi}{\omega}$ → III.
    B: $\sin^3(2\omega t)=\dfrac{3\sin2\omega t-\sin6\omega t}{4}$, period $\dfrac{\pi}{\omega}$, not SHM → I.
    C: angular frequencies $\omega$ and $\pi\omega$ have an irrational ratio, so the sum is non-periodic → IV.
    D: periods $\dfrac{2\pi}{\omega}$ and $\dfrac{\pi}{\omega}$, overall period $\dfrac{2\pi}{\omega}$, not SHM → II.
    A-III, B-I, C-IV, D-II.

  5. JEE Main 2026, 6 April, Shift 1 · Question 37
    A particle is executing simple harmonic motion. Its amplitude is $A$ and time period is 5 sec. The time required by it to move from $x=A$ to $x=\dfrac{A}{\sqrt2}$ is ________ sec.

    1. $1/4$
    2. $5/4$
    3. $5/8$
    4. $3/8$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    $x=A\cos\omega t$; $x=\dfrac{A}{\sqrt2}$ when $\omega t=\dfrac\pi4$.
    $t=\dfrac{\pi/4}{2\pi/T}=\dfrac T8=\dfrac58$ s.

  6. JEE Main 2026, 6 April, Shift 2 · Question 36
    A spring stretches by 2 mm when it is loaded with a mass of 200 g. From equilibrium position the mass is further pulled down by 2 mm and released. The frequency associated with the system and maxmimum energy in the spring are __________ Hz and _________ J, respectively. (Take $g=10$ m/s$^2$)

    1. $\dfrac{5\sqrt{50}}{\pi}$ and $8\times10^{-3}$
    2. $\dfrac{5\sqrt{50}}{\pi}$ and $8$
    3. $10\sqrt{50}$ and $2\times10^{-3}$
    4. $\dfrac{5\sqrt{50}}{\pi}$ and $16\times10^{-3}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    $k=\dfrac{mg}{x}=\dfrac{0.2\times10}{0.002}=1000$ N/m.
    $\omega=\sqrt{\dfrac km}=\sqrt{5000}=10\sqrt{50}$ rad/s, so $f=\dfrac{\omega}{2\pi}=\dfrac{5\sqrt{50}}{\pi}$ Hz.
    Maximum extension $=2+2=4$ mm, so maximum spring energy $=\dfrac12(1000)(0.004)^2=8\times10^{-3}$ J.

  7. JEE Main 2026, 8 April, Shift 2 · Question 37
    The frequency of oscillation of a mass $m$ suspended by a spring is $v_1$. If the length of the spring is cut to half, the same mass oscillates with frequency $v_2$. The value of $v_2/v_1$ is __________.

    1. $1$
    2. $2$
    3. $\sqrt{2}$
    4. $\sqrt{3}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    Spring constant $\propto\dfrac1{\text{length}}$, so halving the length doubles $k$.
    $v=\dfrac1{2\pi}\sqrt{\dfrac km}\propto\sqrt k$, hence $\dfrac{v_2}{v_1}=\sqrt2$.

  8. JEE Main 2026, 21 January, Shift 2 · Question 35
    The kinetic energy of a simple harmonic oscillator is oscillating with angular frequency of 176 rad/s. The frequency of this simple harmonic oscillator is ________ Hz. $\left[\text{take }\pi=\dfrac{22}{7}\right]$

    1. $176$
    2. $28$
    3. $14$
    4. $88$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    $K=\dfrac12m\omega^2A^2\cos^2\omega t=\dfrac14m\omega^2A^2(1+\cos2\omega t)$, so K varies at angular frequency $2\omega$.
    $\omega=88$ rad/s and $f=\dfrac{88}{2\pi}=\dfrac{88\times7}{44}=14$ Hz.

  9. JEE Main 2026, 22 January, Shift 2 · Question 26
    Using a simple pendulum experiment $g$ is determind by measuring its time period $T$. Which of the following plots represent the correct relation between the pendulum length $L$ and time period $T$?

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    $T=2\pi\sqrt{\dfrac{L}{g}}\Rightarrow\dfrac{1}{T^2}=\dfrac{g}{4\pi^2}\cdot\dfrac1L$.
    So $\dfrac1{T^2}$ against $L$ is a rectangular hyperbola, falling steeply and then levelling off: the first graph.

  10. JEE Main 2026, 23 January, Shift 1 · Question 35
    A simple pendulum of string length 30 cm performs 20 oscillations in 10 s. The length of the string required for the pendulum to perform 40 oscillations in the same time duration is __________ cm. [Assume that the mass of the pendulum remains same.]

    1. $0.75$
    2. $7.5$
    3. $15$
    4. $120$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    The period must halve (from 0.5 s to 0.25 s).
    $T\propto\sqrt l$, so the length becomes a quarter: $\dfrac{30}{4}=7.5$ cm.

  11. JEE Main 2026, 24 January, Shift 1 · Question 27
    A spring of force constant 15 N/m is cut into two pieces. If the ratio of their length is 1:3, then the force constant of smaller piece is __________ N/m.

    1. $20$
    2. $45$
    3. $60$
    4. $15$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    For a spring, $kL$ is constant. The smaller piece has length $\dfrac L4$, so $k=4\times15=60$ N/m.

  12. JEE Main 2026, 24 January, Shift 1 · Question 34
    A cylindrical block of mass $M$ and area of cross section $A$ is floating in a liquid of density $\rho$ and with its axis vertical. When depressed a little and released the block starts oscillating. The period of oscillation is __________.

    1. $2\pi\sqrt{\dfrac{M}{\rho Ag}}$
    2. $\pi\sqrt{\dfrac{2M}{\rho Ag}}$
    3. $2\pi\sqrt{\dfrac{\rho A}{Mg}}$
    4. $\pi\sqrt{\dfrac{\rho A}{Mg}}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    Pushing the block down by $x$ adds buoyancy $\rho Agx$ upwards, so the restoring force constant is $k=\rho Ag$.
    $T=2\pi\sqrt{\dfrac{M}{k}}=2\pi\sqrt{\dfrac{M}{\rho Ag}}$.

  13. JEE Main 2026, 28 January, Shift 1 · Question 50
    The displacement of a particle, executing simple harmonic motion with time period $T$, is expressed as $x(t)=A\sin\omega t$, where $A$ is the amplitude. The maximum value of potential energy of this oscillator is found at $t=T/2\beta$. The value of $\beta$ is ______.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 2

    Potential energy is greatest at the extreme position, first reached when $\omega t=\pi/2$, i.e. $t=T/4$.
    $\dfrac{T}{2\beta}=\dfrac T4$ gives $\beta=2$.

  14. JEE Main 2026, 28 January, Shift 2 · Question 28
    As shown in the figure, a spring is kept in a stretched position with some extension by holding the masses 1 kg and 0.2 kg with a separation more than spring natural length and are released. Assuming the horizontal surface to be frictionless, the angular frequency (in SI unit) of the system is :

    1. $5$
    2. $30$
    3. $27$
    4. $20$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    Reduced mass $\mu=\dfrac{1\times0.2}{1.2}=\dfrac16$ kg.
    $\omega=\sqrt{\dfrac k\mu}=\sqrt{150\times6}=30$ rad/s.

  15. JEE Main 2025, 2 April, Shift 1 · Question 33
    A particle is subjected to two simple harmonic motions as :
    $x_1=\sqrt7\sin5t$ cm
    and $x_2=2\sqrt7\sin\left(5t+\dfrac\pi3\right)$ cm
    where $x$ is displacement and $t$ is time in seconds.
    The maximum acceleration of the particle is $x\times10^{-2}$ m s$^{-2}$. The value of $x$ is :

    1. $25\sqrt{7}$
    2. $5\sqrt{7}$
    3. $125$
    4. $175$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    Resultant amplitude: $A^2=7+28+2\cdot\sqrt7\cdot2\sqrt7\cos60^\circ=49$, so $A=7$ cm.
    $a_{\max}=\omega^2A=25\times7=175$ cm s$^{-2}=175\times10^{-2}$ m s$^{-2}$.

  16. JEE Main 2025, 3 April, Shift 1 · Question 36
    Two blocks of masses m and M, $(M>m)$, are placed on a frictionless table as shown in figure. A massless spring with spring constant k is attached with the lower block. If the system is slightly displaced and released, then
    ($\mu$ = coefficient of friction between the two blocks)
    A. The time period of small oscillation of the two blocks is $T=2\pi\sqrt{\dfrac{(m+M)}{k}}$
    B. The acceleration of the blocks is $a=-\dfrac{kx}{M+m}$
    ($x$ = displacement of the blocks from the mean position)
    C. The magnitude of the frictional force on the upper block is $\dfrac{m\mu|x|}{M+m}$
    D. The maximum amplitude of the upper block, if it does not slip, is $\dfrac{\mu(M+m)g}{k}$
    E. Maximum frictional force can be $\mu(M+m)g$.
    Choose the correct answer from the options given below:

    1. A, B, C Only
    2. B, C, D Only
    3. C, D, E Only
    4. A, B, D Only
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    Moving together, the blocks form a mass $M+m$ on the spring: $a=-\dfrac{kx}{M+m}$ and $T=2\pi\sqrt{\dfrac{M+m}{k}}$ (A, B true).
    Friction on the upper block supplies its acceleration: $f=\dfrac{mk|x|}{M+m}$, not $\dfrac{m\mu|x|}{M+m}$ (C false).
    No slipping needs $\dfrac{mkA}{M+m}\le\mu mg$, so $A_{max}=\dfrac{\mu(M+m)g}{k}$ (D true); the largest friction is $\mu mg$ (E false).

  17. JEE Main 2025, 4 April, Shift 1 · Question 34
    Two simple pendulums having lengths $l_1$ and $l_2$ with negligible string mass undergo angular displacements $\theta_1$ and $\theta_2$, from their mean positions, respectively. If the angular accelerations of both pendulums are same, then which expression is correct?

    1. $\theta_1l_1=\theta_2l_2$
    2. $\theta_1l_2=\theta_2l_1$
    3. $\theta_1l_1^2=\theta_2l_2^2$
    4. $\theta_1l_2^2=\theta_2l_1^2$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    For small swings, the angular acceleration of a simple pendulum has magnitude $\alpha=\dfrac{g}{l}\theta$.
    $\dfrac{g\theta_1}{l_1}=\dfrac{g\theta_2}{l_2}$ gives $\theta_1l_2=\theta_2l_1$.

  18. JEE Main 2025, 8 April, Shift 2 · Question 29
    A block of mass 2 kg is attached to one end of a massless spring whose other end is fixed at a wall. The spring-mass system moves on a frictionless horizontal table. The spring's natural length is 2 m and spring constant is 200 N/m. The block is pushed such that the length of the spring becomes 1 m and then released. At distance $x$ m ($x<2$) from the wall, the speed of the block will be

    1. $10\left[1-(2-x)\right]^{\frac32}$ m/s
    2. $10\left[1-(2-x)^2\right]^2$ m/s
    3. $10\left[1-(2-x)^2\right]^{\frac12}$ m/s
    4. $10\left[1-(2-x)^2\right]$ m/s
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    The block performs SHM about $x=2$ m with amplitude 1 m and $\omega=\sqrt{\dfrac{200}{2}}=10$ rad/s.
    At displacement $2-x$ the speed is $\omega\sqrt{A^2-(2-x)^2}=10\left[1-(2-x)^2\right]^{1/2}$ m/s.

  19. JEE Main 2025, 23 January, Shift 1 · Question 34
    A light hollow cube of side length 10 cm and mass 10g, is floating in water. It is pushed down and released to execute simple harmonic oscillations. The time period of oscillations is $y\pi\times10^{-2}$ s, where the value of $y$ is
    (Acceleration due to gravity, $g=10$ m/s$^2$, density of water $=10^3$ kg/m$^3$)

    1. $1$
    2. $4$
    3. $6$
    4. $2$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    Pushing the cube down by $x$ adds buoyant force $\rho Agx$, so the restoring constant is $k=\rho Ag=10^3\times0.01\times10=100$ N/m.
    $T=2\pi\sqrt{\dfrac mk}=2\pi\sqrt{\dfrac{0.01}{100}}=2\pi\times10^{-2}$ s.
    $y=2$.

  20. JEE Main 2025, 24 January, Shift 1 · Question 35
    A particle is executing simple harmonic motion with time period 2 s and amplitude 1 cm. If D and d are the total distance and displacement covered by the particle in 12.5 s, then $\dfrac{D}{d}$ is

    1. $\dfrac{15}{4}$
    2. $25$
    3. $\dfrac{16}{5}$
    4. $10$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    $12.5$ s $=6T+\dfrac{T}{4}$. Six full periods give a distance of $6\times4A=24$ cm and no displacement.
    Starting from the mean position (or from an extreme), the extra quarter period covers $A=1$ cm, with displacement of magnitude 1 cm.
    $\dfrac{D}{d}=\dfrac{25}{1}=25$.

  22. JEE Main 2025, 28 January, Shift 2 · Question 36
    Given below are two statements. One is labelled as <b>Assertion (A)</b> and the other is labelled as <b>Reason (R)</b>.
    <b>Assertion (A) :</b> Knowing initial position $x_0$ and initial momentum $p_0$ is enough to determine the position and momentum at any time t for a simple harmonic motion with a given angular frequency $\omega$.
    <b>Reason (R) :</b> The amplitude and phase can be expressed in terms of $x_0$ and $p_0$.
    In the light of the above statements, choose the <b>correct</b> answer from the options given below :

    1. Both (A) and (R) are true and (R) is the correct explanation of (A)
    2. Both (A) and (R) are true but (R) is NOT the correct explanation of (A)
    3. (A) is true but (R) is false
    4. (A) is false but (R) is true
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    $x=A\sin(\omega t+\phi)$ with $x_0=A\sin\phi$ and $p_0=m\omega A\cos\phi$.
    So $A=\sqrt{x_0^2+\left(\dfrac{p_0}{m\omega}\right)^2}$ and $\tan\phi=\dfrac{m\omega x_0}{p_0}$: amplitude and phase follow from $x_0,p_0$ (R true).
    With $A$, $\phi$ and $\omega$ known, $x(t)$ and $p(t)$ are fixed for all $t$ (A true), and R is the reason.

  23. JEE Main 2025, 29 January, Shift 1 · Question 33
    Given below are two statements : one is labelled as <b>Assertion (A)</b> and the other is labelled as <b>Reason (R)</b>.
    <b>Assertion (A) :</b> Time period of a simple pendulum is longer at the top of a mountain than that at the base of the mountain.
    <b>Reason (R) :</b> Time period of a simple pendulum decreases with increasing value of acceleration due to gravity and vice-versa.
    In the light of the above statements, choose the <b>most appropriate</b> answer from the options given below :

    1. Both <b>(A)</b> and <b>(R)</b> are true and <b>(R)</b> is the correct explanation of <b>(A)</b>
    2. Both <b>(A)</b> and <b>(R)</b> are true but <b>(R)</b> is <b>not</b> the correct explanation of <b>(A)</b>
    3. <b>(A)</b> is true but <b>(R)</b> is false
    4. <b>(A)</b> is false but <b>(R)</b> is true
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    $T=2\pi\sqrt{l/g}$, so $T$ falls as $g$ rises (R is true).
    $g$ is smaller at the top of a mountain, so $T$ is longer there (A is true), and R is exactly the reason.

  24. JEE Main 2025, 29 January, Shift 2 · Question 34
    Two bodies A and B of equal mass are suspended from two massless springs of spring constant $k_1$ and $k_2$, respectively. If the bodies oscillate vertically such that their amplitudes are equal, the ratio of the maximum velocity of A to the maximum velocity of B is

    1. $\sqrt{\dfrac{k_1}{k_2}}$
    2. $\sqrt{\dfrac{k_2}{k_1}}$
    3. $\dfrac{k_1}{k_2}$
    4. $\dfrac{k_2}{k_1}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    $v_{\max}=A\omega=A\sqrt{k/m}$. With equal $A$ and $m$, $\dfrac{v_A}{v_B}=\sqrt{\dfrac{k_1}{k_2}}$.
