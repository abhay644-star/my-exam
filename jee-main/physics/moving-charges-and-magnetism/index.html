  1. JEE Main 2026, 2 April, Shift 1 · Question 39
    Two identical long current carrying wires are bent into the shapes shown in the following figures. If the magnitude of magnetic fields at the centres P and Q of a semicircular arc are $\mathrm{B_1}$ and $\mathrm{B_2}$ respectively, then the ratio $\dfrac{\mathrm{B_1}}{\mathrm{B_2}}$ is ______.

    1. $\dfrac{2+\pi}{1+\pi}$
    2. $\dfrac{1+\pi}{1-\pi}$
    3. $\dfrac{2+\pi}{1-\pi}$
    4. $\dfrac{1+\pi}{2-\pi}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    Semicircular arc at its centre: $\dfrac{\mu_0 I}{4r}$. A semi-infinite wire ending at the foot of the perpendicular from the point: $\dfrac{\mu_0 I}{4\pi r}$. All contributions point the same way.
    (I): two semi-infinite wires + arc: $B_1 = \dfrac{\mu_0 I}{4\pi r}(2+\pi)$.
    (II): the horizontal semi-infinite wire + arc. The vertical wire is taken as lying along a line through Q, so it adds nothing: $B_2 = \dfrac{\mu_0 I}{4\pi r}(1+\pi)$.
    $\dfrac{B_1}{B_2} = \dfrac{2+\pi}{1+\pi}$.

  2. JEE Main 2026, 2 April, Shift 1 · Question 48
    1 $\mu$C charge moving with velocity $\vec{v} = \left(\hat{i} - 2\hat{j} + 3\hat{k}\right)$ m/s in the region of magnetic field $\vec{B} = \left(2\hat{i} + 3\hat{j} - 5\hat{k}\right)$ T. The magnitude of force acting on it is $\sqrt{\alpha}\times10^{-6}$ N. The value of $\alpha$ is ________.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 171

    $\vec{v}\times\vec{B} = \begin{vmatrix} \hat{i} & \hat{j} & \hat{k} \\ 1 & -2 & 3 \\ 2 & 3 & -5 \end{vmatrix} = (10-9)\hat{i} - (-5-6)\hat{j} + (3+4)\hat{k} = \hat{i} + 11\hat{j} + 7\hat{k}$.
    $|\vec{v}\times\vec{B}| = \sqrt{1+121+49} = \sqrt{171}$.
    $F = q|\vec{v}\times\vec{B}| = \sqrt{171}\times10^{-6}$ N, so $\alpha = 171$.

  3. JEE Main 2026, 2 April, Shift 2 · Question 29
    A particle having charge $10^{-9}$ C moving in $x$-$y$ plane in fields of $0.4\,\hat j$ N/C and $4\times10^{-3}\,\hat k$ T experiences a force of $\left(4\hat i+2\hat j\right)\times10^{-10}$ N. The velocity of the particle at that instant is __________ m/s.

    1. $50\hat i+100\hat j$
    2. $100\hat i+50\hat j$
    3. $-50\hat i+100\hat j$
    4. $50\hat i-100\hat j$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    Electric force: $q\vec E=10^{-9}\times0.4\,\hat j=4\times10^{-10}\,\hat j$ N.
    Magnetic force $=\vec F-q\vec E=(4\hat i-2\hat j)\times10^{-10}$ N.
    With $\vec v=v_x\hat i+v_y\hat j$: $q\vec v\times\vec B=qB(v_y\hat i-v_x\hat j)$, $qB=4\times10^{-12}$.
    $4\times10^{-12}v_y=4\times10^{-10} \Rightarrow v_y=100$; $4\times10^{-12}v_x=2\times10^{-10} \Rightarrow v_x=50$.
    $\vec v=50\hat i+100\hat j$ m/s.

  4. JEE Main 2026, 4 April, Shift 1 · Question 37
    A voltmeter with internal resistance of $x\ \Omega$ can be used to measure upto $20$ V. In order to increase its measuring range to $30$ V, the required modification is to____________.

    1. connect resistor of $\dfrac{x}{2}\ \Omega$, in series with voltmeter.
    2. connect resistor of $\dfrac{x}{2}\ \Omega$, in parallel to voltmeter.
    3. connect a resistor of $x\ \Omega$ in series with voltmeter.
    4. connect resistor of $2x\ \Omega$ in parallel to voltmeter.
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    Full-scale current $I=\dfrac{20}{x}$.
    For $30$ V the total resistance must be $\dfrac{30}{I}=1.5x$, so a resistance of $0.5x=\dfrac x2\ \Omega$ is added in series.

  5. JEE Main 2026, 4 April, Shift 1 · Question 41
    An insulated wire is wound so that it forms a flat coil with $N=200$ turns. The radius of the innermost turn is $r_1=3$ cm, and of the outermost turn $r_2=6$ cm. If $20$ mA current flows in it then the magnetic moment will be $\alpha\times10^{-2}\ \mathrm{A\,m^2}$. The value of $\alpha$ is_____.

    1. $4.4$
    2. $2.64$
    3. $3.25$
    4. $1.2$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    Turns per unit radial width: $\dfrac{N}{r_2-r_1}$. A ring of width $dr$ has $\dfrac{N\,dr}{r_2-r_1}$ turns of area $\pi r^2$.
    $m=\int_{r_1}^{r_2}\dfrac{NI\pi r^2}{r_2-r_1}dr=\dfrac{NI\pi(r_2^3-r_1^3)}{3(r_2-r_1)}$.
    $=\dfrac{200\times0.02\times\pi\times(216-27)\times10^{-6}}{3\times0.03}=4\pi\times2.1\times10^{-3}\approx2.64\times10^{-2}\ \mathrm{A\,m^2}$.
    $\alpha=2.64$.

  6. JEE Main 2026, 4 April, Shift 2 · Question 39
    A solenoid has a core made of material with relative permeability 400. The magnetic field produced in the interior of solenoid is 1.0 T. The magnetic intensity in SI units is $\alpha\times10^5$. The value of $\alpha$ is ________.
    (Free space permeability $\mu_0=4\pi\times10^{-7}$ SI units.)

    1. $\dfrac{25}{\pi}$
    2. $\dfrac{1}{16\pi}$
    3. $\dfrac1\pi$
    4. $\dfrac{1}{4\pi}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    $H=\dfrac{B}{\mu_0\mu_r}=\dfrac{1.0}{4\pi\times10^{-7}\times400}=\dfrac{1}{16\pi\times10^{-5}}$ A/m.
    $H=\dfrac{1}{16\pi}\times10^5$, so $\alpha=\dfrac{1}{16\pi}$.

  7. JEE Main 2026, 4 April, Shift 2 · Question 48
    Two identical small bar magnets each of dipole moment $3\sqrt5$ J/T are placed at a center to center separation of 10 cm, with their axes perpendicular to each other as shown in figure. The value of magnetic field at the point P midway between the magnets is $\alpha\times10^{-3}$ T. The value of $\alpha$ is _______.
    ($\mu_0=4\pi\times10^{-7}$ Tm/A)

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 12

    $P$ is $r=5$ cm $=0.05$ m from each magnet; $\dfrac{\mu_0}{4\pi}\dfrac{m}{r^3}=10^{-7}\times\dfrac{3\sqrt5}{1.25\times10^{-4}}=2.4\sqrt5\times10^{-3}$ T.
    $P$ is on the axis of the left magnet: $B_1=2\times2.4\sqrt5\times10^{-3}=4.8\sqrt5\times10^{-3}$ T (along its axis).
    $P$ is on the equatorial line of the right magnet: $B_2=2.4\sqrt5\times10^{-3}$ T (parallel to its axis, perpendicular to $B_1$).
    $B=\sqrt{B_1^2+B_2^2}=2.4\sqrt5\times\sqrt5\times10^{-3}=12\times10^{-3}$ T, so $\alpha=12$.

  8. JEE Main 2026, 4 April, Shift 2 · Question 49
    A circular coil of radius 2 cm and 125 turns carries a current of 1 A. The coil is placed in a uniform magnetic field of magnitude 0.4 T. The axis of the coil makes an angle of $30^\circ$ with the direction of the magnetic field. The torque acting on the coil is $\alpha\times10^{-4}$ N.m. The value of $\alpha$ is_________.
    ($\pi=3.14$)

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 314

    $\tau=NIAB\sin\theta$, with $\theta=30^\circ$ between the axis (magnetic moment) and the field.
    $\tau=125\times1\times3.14\times(0.02)^2\times0.4\times\dfrac12=0.0314$ N m.
    $\tau=314\times10^{-4}$ N m, so $\alpha=314$.

  9. JEE Main 2026, 5 April, Shift 1 · Question 49
    The charged particle moving in a uniform magnetic field of $(3\hat{i} + 2\hat{j})$ T has an acceleration $\left(4\hat{i} - \dfrac{x}{2}\hat{j}\right)$ m/s$^2$. The value of $x$ is

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 12

    The magnetic force $q\vec v\times\vec B$ is perpendicular to $\vec B$, so $\vec a\cdot\vec B=0$.
    $4(3)-\dfrac x2(2)=0 \Rightarrow 12-x=0 \Rightarrow x=12$.

  10. JEE Main 2026, 5 April, Shift 2 · Question 38
    A particle of charge $q$ and mass $m$ is projected from origin with an initial velocity $\vec{v}=\left(\dfrac{v_0}{\sqrt2}\hat{x}+\dfrac{v_0}{\sqrt2}\hat{y}\right)$. There exists a uniform magnetic field $\vec{B}=B_0\hat{z}$ and a space varying electric field $\vec{E}=E_0e^{-\lambda x}\hat{x}$ within the region $0\le x\le L$. After travelling a distance such that $x$-coordinate has changed from $x=0$ to $x=L$, the change in the kinetic energy is __________.

    1. $\dfrac{qE_0}{\lambda}\left[1-e^{-\lambda L}\right]$
    2. $\left(\dfrac{v_0qB_0}{2\lambda}\right)\left[2-e^{-2\lambda L}\right]$
    3. $\dfrac{qE_0}{\lambda}\left[1+e^{-\lambda L}\right]$
    4. $q\left(\dfrac{E_0+v_0B_0}{\lambda}\right)\left[1-e^{-\lambda L/2}\right]$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    The magnetic force is perpendicular to the velocity and does no work.
    Work by the electric field: $W=\displaystyle\int_0^L qE_0e^{-\lambda x}dx=\dfrac{qE_0}{\lambda}\left[1-e^{-\lambda L}\right]$.
    By the work-energy theorem this is the change in kinetic energy.

  11. JEE Main 2026, 6 April, Shift 1 · Question 42
    A small cube of side 1 mm is placed at the centre of a circular loop of radius 10 cm carrying a current of 2 A. The magnetic energy stored inside the cube is $\alpha\times10^{-14}$ J. The value of $\alpha$ is ________.
    ($\mu_0=4\pi\times10^{-7}$ Tm/A, $\pi=3.14$)

    1. $6.28$
    2. $6.28\times10^{-6}$
    3. $628$
    4. $6.28\times10^{-4}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    $B=\dfrac{\mu_0I}{2R}=\dfrac{4\pi\times10^{-7}\times2}{0.2}=4\pi\times10^{-6}$ T.
    Energy density $u=\dfrac{B^2}{2\mu_0}=\dfrac{16\pi^2\times10^{-12}}{8\pi\times10^{-7}}=2\pi\times10^{-5}$ J/m$^3$.
    Energy $=u\times10^{-9}=2\pi\times10^{-14}=6.28\times10^{-14}$ J, so $\alpha=6.28$.

  12. JEE Main 2026, 6 April, Shift 2 · Question 38
    A current of 30 A each flows in opposite directions in two conducting wires, placed parallel to each other at a distance of 8 cm. The magnetic field at the mid point between the two wires is ______ $\mu$T. $\left(\dfrac{\mu_0}{4\pi}=10^{-7}\text{ N/A}^2\right)$

    1. $30$
    2. $300$
    3. $150$
    4. $0.0$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    Each wire at $d=4$ cm: $B=\dfrac{\mu_0I}{2\pi d}=\dfrac{2\times10^{-7}\times30}{0.04}=1.5\times10^{-4}$ T.
    For opposite currents the fields at the mid point are in the same direction and add.
    $B=3\times10^{-4}$ T $=300\ \mu$T.

  13. JEE Main 2026, 6 April, Shift 2 · Question 48
    A moving coil of galvanometer when shunted with 2 $\Omega$ resistance gives a full scale deflection for a current of 500 mA. When a resistance of 470 $\Omega$ is connected in series it gives a full scale deflection for 10 V potential applied on it. The value of resistance of galvanometer coil is _________ $\Omega$.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 50

    Shunt: $I_gG=(0.5-I_g)\times2 \Rightarrow I_g(G+2)=1$.
    Series: $I_g(G+470)=10$.
    Dividing: $\dfrac{G+470}{G+2}=10 \Rightarrow 9G=450 \Rightarrow G=50\ \Omega$.

  14. JEE Main 2026, 8 April, Shift 2 · Question 39
    A current carrying circular loop of radius 2 cm with unit normal $\hat{n}=\dfrac{\hat{k}+\hat{i}}{\sqrt{2}}$ is placed in a magnetic field, $\vec{B}=B_o\left(3\hat{i}+2\hat{k}\right)$. If $B_o=4\times10^{-3}$ T and current $I=100\sqrt{2}$ A, the torque experienced by the loop is __________ Wb.A. $(\pi=3.14)$

    1. $16\times10^{-5}\,\hat{k}$
    2. $5024\times10^{-7}\,\hat{k}$
    3. $5024\times10^{-7}\,\hat{i}$
    4. $5024\times10^{-7}\,\hat{j}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    $\vec m=I\pi r^2\hat n=100\sqrt2\times3.14\times4\times10^{-4}\times\dfrac{\hat i+\hat k}{\sqrt2}=0.1256\,(\hat i+\hat k)$.
    $\vec\tau=\vec m\times\vec B=0.1256\times4\times10^{-3}\,(\hat i+\hat k)\times(3\hat i+2\hat k)$.
    $(\hat i+\hat k)\times(3\hat i+2\hat k)=-2\hat j+3\hat j=\hat j$.
    $\vec\tau=5.024\times10^{-4}\,\hat j=5024\times10^{-7}\,\hat j$.

  15. JEE Main 2026, 8 April, Shift 2 · Question 48
    A 5 mg particle carrying a charge of $5\pi\times10^{-6}$ C is moving with velocity of $\left(3\hat{i}+2\hat{k}\right)\times10^{-2}$ m/s in a region having magnetic field $\vec{B}=0.1\,\hat{k}\ \text{Wb/m}^2$. It moves a distance of $\alpha$ meter along $\hat{k}$ when it completes 5 revolutions. The value of $\alpha$ is __________.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 2

    The path is a helix about $\hat k$. Period $T=\dfrac{2\pi m}{qB}=\dfrac{2\pi\times5\times10^{-6}}{5\pi\times10^{-6}\times0.1}=20$ s.
    Velocity along $\hat k$ is unchanged: $2\times10^{-2}$ m/s.
    5 revolutions take 100 s, so $\alpha=2\times10^{-2}\times100=2$ m.
