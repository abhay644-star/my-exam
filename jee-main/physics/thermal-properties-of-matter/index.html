  1. JEE Main 2026, 4 April, Shift 2 · Question 34
    The temperature of a metal strip having coefficient of linear expansion $\alpha$ is increased from $T_1$ to $T_2$ resulting in increase of its length by $\Delta L_1$. The temperature is further increased from $T_2$ to $T_3$ such that the increase in its length is $\Delta L_2$.
    Given $T_3+T_1=2T_2$ and $T_2-T_1=\Delta T$, the value of $\Delta L_2$ is _______.

    1. $\Delta L_1[1+2\alpha^2(\Delta T)^2]$
    2. $\Delta L_1[1+\alpha^2(\Delta T)^2]$
    3. $\Delta L_1[1+2\alpha\Delta T]$
    4. $\Delta L_1[1+\alpha\Delta T]$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    $T_3-T_2=T_2-T_1=\Delta T$.
    With length $L_1$ at $T_1$: $\Delta L_1=L_1\alpha\Delta T$, and the length at $T_2$ is $L_2=L_1(1+\alpha\Delta T)$.
    $\Delta L_2=L_2\alpha\Delta T=L_1\alpha\Delta T(1+\alpha\Delta T)=\Delta L_1[1+\alpha\Delta T]$.

  2. JEE Main 2026, 5 April, Shift 2 · Question 34
    An ideal gas at pressure $P$ and temperature $T$ is expanding such that $PT^3$ = constant. The coefficient of volume expansion of the gas is __________.

    1. $\dfrac2T$
    2. $\dfrac1T$
    3. $\dfrac4T$
    4. $\dfrac3T$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    $PV=nRT$ and $P\propto T^{-3}$, so $V\propto\dfrac{T}{P}\propto T^4$.
    $\gamma=\dfrac1V\dfrac{dV}{dT}=\dfrac4T$.

  3. JEE Main 2026, 5 April, Shift 2 · Question 47
    The heat extracted out of $x$ gram of water initially at $50\ {}^\circ$C to cool it down to $0\ {}^\circ$C is sufficient to evaporate $(1000-x)$ gram of water also initially at $50\ {}^\circ$C. The value of $x$ (closest integer) is __________.
    (Take latent heat of water 2256 kJ/kg. K, specific heat capacity of water 4200 J/kg. K)

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 922

    Heat released by $x$ g cooling $50\to0\ {}^\circ$C: $x\times4.2\times50=210x$ J.
    Heat to take $(1000-x)$ g from $50$ to $100\ {}^\circ$C and evaporate it: $(1000-x)(4.2\times50+2256)=2466(1000-x)$ J.
    $210x=2466(1000-x) \Rightarrow x=\dfrac{2466000}{2676}\approx921.5$.
    Closest integer: $922$.
