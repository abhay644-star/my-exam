  1. JEE Main 2026, 2 April, Shift 1 · Question 45
    A liquid drop of diameter 2 mm breaks into 512 droplets. The change in surface energy is $\alpha\times10^{-6}$ J. The value of $\alpha$ is ________. (Take surface tension of liquid $= 0.08$ N/m)

    1. $10$
    2. $7$
    3. $8$
    4. $11$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    Volume conserved: $512r^3 = R^3 \Rightarrow r = R/8$, with $R = 1$ mm.
    $\Delta A = 4\pi(512r^2 - R^2) = 4\pi R^2(8-1) = 28\pi\times10^{-6}\ \mathrm{m^2}$.
    $\Delta E = T\Delta A = 0.08\times28\pi\times10^{-6} \approx 7.04\times10^{-6}$ J, so $\alpha \approx 7$.

  2. JEE Main 2026, 2 April, Shift 1 · Question 50
    A tub is filled with water and a wooden cube 10 cm $\times$ 10 cm $\times$ 10 cm is placed in the water. The wooden cube is found to float on the water with a part of it submerged in water. When a metal coin is placed on the wooden cube, the submerged part is increased by 3.87 cm. The mass of the metal coin is ________ gram.
    (Take water density as $1\ \mathrm{g/cm^3}$ and density of wood as $0.4\ \mathrm{g/cm^3}$)

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 387

    Without the coin, 4 cm is submerged ($0.4\times10$). With the coin, 7.87 cm is submerged, which is still less than 10 cm, so the cube still floats.
    Extra buoyancy = weight of coin: $m = \rho_w A\,\Delta h = 1\times100\times3.87 = 387$ g.

  3. JEE Main 2026, 2 April, Shift 2 · Question 33
    The surface tension of a soap bubble is 0.03 N/m. The work done in increasing the diameter of bubble from 2 cm to 6 cm is $\alpha\pi\times10^{-4}$ J. The value of $\alpha$ is __________. (Take $\pi=3.14$)

    1. $0.86$
    2. $0.64$
    3. $1.92$
    4. $7.68$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    A soap bubble has two surfaces: $W=T\times2\times4\pi(r_2^2-r_1^2)$.
    $W=0.03\times8\pi\times(9-1)\times10^{-4}=1.92\pi\times10^{-4}$ J.
    $\alpha=1.92$.

  4. JEE Main 2026, 2 April, Shift 2 · Question 35
    If an air bubble of diameter 2 mm rises steadily through a liquid of density 2000 kg/m$^3$ at a rate of 0.5 cm/s, then the coefficient of viscosity of liquid is __________ Poise. (Take $g=10$ m/s$^2$)

    1. $0.88$
    2. $8.8$
    3. $88.8$
    4. $0.088$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    Terminal velocity (neglecting the density of air): $v=\dfrac{2r^2\rho g}{9\eta}$.
    $\eta=\dfrac{2\times(10^{-3})^2\times2000\times10}{9\times5\times10^{-3}}=\dfrac{0.04}{0.045}=0.89$ Pa s.
    $1$ Pa s $=10$ Poise, so $\eta\approx8.8$ Poise.

  5. JEE Main 2026, 4 April, Shift 1 · Question 47
    The surface tension of a soap solution is $3.5\times10^{-2}$ N/m. The work required to increase the radius of a soap bubble from $1$ cm to $2$ cm is $\alpha\times10^{-6}$ J. The value of $\alpha$ is ________.
    ($\pi=22/7$)

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 264

    A soap bubble has two surfaces: $W=T\times2\times4\pi(r_2^2-r_1^2)$.
    $W=3.5\times10^{-2}\times8\times\dfrac{22}{7}\times(4-1)\times10^{-4}=0.88\times3\times10^{-4}=264\times10^{-6}$ J.
    $\alpha=264$.

  6. JEE Main 2026, 4 April, Shift 2 · Question 32
    A water spray gun is attached to a hose of cross sectional area 30 cm$^2$. The gun comprises of 10 perforations each of cross sectional area of 15 mm$^2$. If the water flows in the hose with the speed of 50 cm/s, calculate the speed at which the water flows out from each perforation. (Neglect any edge effects)

    1. 100 m/s
    2. 10 m/s
    3. 1000 m/s
    4. $15\times10^2$ m/s
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    Continuity: $A_1v_1=A_2v_2$.
    $A_1v_1=30\ \text{cm}^2\times50\ \text{cm/s}=1500\ \text{cm}^3/\text{s}$; total outlet area $=10\times15\ \text{mm}^2=1.5\ \text{cm}^2$.
    $v_2=\dfrac{1500}{1.5}=1000$ cm/s $=10$ m/s.

  7. JEE Main 2026, 5 April, Shift 2 · Question 33
    Eight mercury drops, each of radius $r$, coalesce to form a bigger drop. The surface energy released in this process is __________. (S is the surface tension of mercury).

    1. $8\pi r^2S$
    2. $16\pi r^2S$
    3. $64\pi r^2S$
    4. $4\pi r^2S$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    Volume conserved: $\dfrac43\pi R^3=8\cdot\dfrac43\pi r^3 \Rightarrow R=2r$.
    Decrease in area $=8(4\pi r^2)-4\pi(2r)^2=32\pi r^2-16\pi r^2=16\pi r^2$.
    Energy released $=16\pi r^2S$.

  8. JEE Main 2026, 6 April, Shift 2 · Question 34
    A cylindrical vessel of 40 cm radius is completely filled with water and its capacity is 528 dm$^3$ (dm : decimeter) The vessel is placed on a solid block of exactly same height as vessel. If a small hole is made at 70 cm below the top of water level, then horizontal range of water falling on the ground in the beginning is ________ cm.

    1. $120\sqrt2$
    2. $140\sqrt2$
    3. $140\sqrt3$
    4. $120\sqrt3$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    Height of vessel: $\pi r^2h=528000$ cm$^3$ $\Rightarrow h=\dfrac{528000\times7}{22\times1600}=105$ cm.
    Efflux speed $v=\sqrt{2g(70\text{ cm})}$; the hole is $105-70+105=140$ cm above the ground.
    Range $=v\sqrt{\dfrac{2H}{g}}=2\sqrt{70\times140}=2\times70\sqrt2=140\sqrt2$ cm.

  9. JEE Main 2026, 8 April, Shift 2 · Question 33
    A liquid of density $600\ \text{kg/m}^3$ flowing steadily in a tube of varying cross-section. The cross-section at a point $A$ is $1.0\ \text{cm}^2$ and that at $B$ is $20\ \text{mm}^2$. Both the points $A$ and $B$ are in same horizontal plane, the speed of the liquid at $A$ is 10 cm/s. The difference in pressures at $A$ and $B$ points is __________ Pa.

    1. $18$
    2. $144$
    3. $36$
    4. $72$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    Continuity: $v_B=v_A\dfrac{A_A}{A_B}=0.1\times\dfrac{100}{20}=0.5$ m/s.
    Bernoulli (same level): $P_A-P_B=\dfrac12\rho\left(v_B^2-v_A^2\right)=\dfrac12\times600\times(0.25-0.01)=72$ Pa.

  10. JEE Main 2026, 8 April, Shift 2 · Question 34
    A spherical liquid drop of radius $R$ acquires the terminal velocity $v_1$ when falls through a gas of viscosity $\eta$. Now the drop is broken into 64 identical droplets and each droplet acquires terminal velocity $v_2$ falling through the same gas. The ratio of terminal velocities $v_1/v_2$ is __________.

    1. $4$
    2. $0.25$
    3. $32$
    4. $16$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    Volume is conserved: $64r^3=R^3\Rightarrow r=\dfrac R4$.
    Terminal velocity $v=\dfrac{2r^2(\rho-\sigma)g}{9\eta}\propto r^2$.
    $\dfrac{v_1}{v_2}=\left(\dfrac Rr\right)^2=16$.
