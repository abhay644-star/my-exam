  1. JEE Main 2026, 2 April, Shift 1 · Question 37
    For the given circuit (shown in part (A)) the time dependent input voltage $v_{in}(t)$ and corresponding output $v_o(t)$ are shown in part (B) and part (C), respectively. Identify the components that are used in the circuit between points X and Y.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    In the shunt branch, the ideal diode conducts from X to Y and the Zener ($V_Z = 5$ V) is reverse biased in that direction, so for the positive half cycle $v_o$ is clamped at $+5$ V.
    In the negative half cycle that branch is open, yet the output is clamped at $-5$ V. So between X and Y there must be a path that conducts from Y to X with a 5 V drop.
    That needs an ideal diode pointing towards X (conducting Y $\rightarrow$ X) in series with a Zener that is reverse biased for current Y $\rightarrow$ X, i.e. it breaks down at 5 V.
    Only option (2) has this arrangement: a diode pointing towards X and a Zener whose forward direction is towards Y.

  2. JEE Main 2026, 2 April, Shift 2 · Question 30
    If X and Y are the inputs, the given circuit works as __________.

    1. OR gate
    2. AND gate
    3. NAND gate
    4. NOR gate
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    A NAND gate with both inputs joined acts as a NOT gate, so the first stage gives $\bar X$ and $\bar Y$.
    The middle NAND gives $\overline{\bar X\cdot\bar Y}=X+Y$.
    The last NAND (inputs joined) inverts it: output $=\overline{X+Y}$, a NOR gate.

  3. JEE Main 2026, 4 April, Shift 1 · Question 38
    Two 4 bits binary numbers, $A=1101$ and $B=1010$ are given in the inputs of a logic circuit shown in figure below. The output $(Y)$ will be :

    1. $Y=1101$
    2. $Y=0010$
    3. $Y=0111$
    4. $Y=1000$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    The circuit gives $Y=\overline{\bar A\cdot B}$ (NOT on $A$, then NAND with $B$).
    $\bar A=0010$; $\bar A\cdot B=0010\cdot1010=0010$ (bitwise).
    $Y=\overline{0010}=1101$.

  4. JEE Main 2026, 4 April, Shift 1 · Question 42
    Consider a circuit consisting of a capacitor ($20\ \mu$F), resistor ($100\ \Omega$) and two identical diodes as shown in figure. The resistance of diode under forward biasing condition is $10\ \Omega$. The time constant of the circuit is $\alpha\times10^{-3}$ s. The value of $\alpha$ is __________

    1. $2.2$
    2. $2.0$
    3. $2.1$
    4. $2.4$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    The two diodes are connected in antiparallel, so for either direction of current exactly one is forward biased ($10\ \Omega$) and the other is off.
    Total resistance $=100+10=110\ \Omega$.
    $\tau=RC=110\times20\times10^{-6}=2.2\times10^{-3}$ s, so $\alpha=2.2$.

  5. JEE Main 2026, 4 April, Shift 2 · Question 45
    Given below are two statements: one is labelled as Assertion A and the other is labelled as Reason R
    Assertion A: A diode under reverse-biased condition provides very small current which is nearly independent of voltage until a critical limit at which the current increases drastically.
    Reason R: Below the critical voltage limit, only majority charge carriers flow which increases drastically above critical voltage.
    choose the correct answer from the options given below

    1. Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation of A
    2. Both A and R are true but R is NOT the correct explanation of A
    3. A is true but R is false
    4. A is false but R is true
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    In reverse bias a small, nearly constant (saturation) current flows until breakdown, where it rises sharply: A is true.
    That small reverse current is due to minority carriers, not majority carriers: R is false.
    A is true but R is false.

  6. JEE Main 2026, 4 April, Shift 2 · Question 46
    A diode has Zener voltage of 10 V and maximum power dissipation of 0.5 W, then the minimum resistance to be used in series with this diode for safety when it is connected to a 25 V power supply is _________$\Omega$.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 300

    Maximum Zener current $I=\dfrac{P}{V_Z}=\dfrac{0.5}{10}=0.05$ A.
    Voltage across the series resistor $=25-10=15$ V.
    $R_{\min}=\dfrac{15}{0.05}=300\ \Omega$.

  7. JEE Main 2026, 5 April, Shift 1 · Question 45
    For the given logic circuit, which of the following inputs combination will make both LED$-1$ and LED$-2$ to glow?

    1. A = 0, B = 1, C = 1
    2. A = 1, B = 0, C = 0
    3. A = 1, B = 0, C = 1
    4. A = 1, B = 1, C = 0
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    LED-1 is driven by the OR gate: it glows when $A+B=1$.
    The second gate is AND of $(A+B)$ and $C$; the last gate is AND of $A$ and that output: $Y=A\cdot(A+B)\cdot C=A\cdot C$.
    Both glow when $A=1$, $C=1$ (then $A+B=1$ too): from the options, $A=1, B=0, C=1$.

  8. JEE Main 2026, 5 April, Shift 2 · Question 45
    In a semiconductor p-n diode, the doping concentrations on p-side and n-side are $10^{15}$ atoms/cm$^3$ and $10^{18}$ atoms/cm$^3$, respectively. Which one of the following statements is true ?

    1. Widths of depletion region on either side of the interface are equal
    2. The depletion region width is more on p-side compared to that in n-side
    3. The depletion region width is more on n-side compared to that in p-side
    4. No depletion region forms because of unequal doping concentrations on p and n-sides
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    Charge neutrality of the depletion layer: $N_Ax_p=N_Dx_n$.
    $\dfrac{x_p}{x_n}=\dfrac{N_D}{N_A}=\dfrac{10^{18}}{10^{15}}=1000$.
    So the depletion region extends much more into the lightly doped p-side.

  9. JEE Main 2026, 6 April, Shift 1 · Question 39
    The maximum rated power of the LED is 2 mW and it is used in the circuit with input voltage of 5 V as shown in the figure below. The current through resistance $R_S$ is 0.5 mA.
    The minimum value of the resistance of $R_S$, to ensure that the LED is not damaged is ________ k$\Omega$.

    1. $6$
    2. $2$
    3. $4$
    4. $5$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    The diode in series with $R=1$ k$\Omega$ is reverse biased, so that branch carries no current and the whole $0.5$ mA flows through the LED.
    At its maximum rating, LED voltage $V=\dfrac PI=\dfrac{2\text{ mW}}{0.5\text{ mA}}=4$ V.
    $R_S=\dfrac{5-4}{0.5\times10^{-3}}=2$ k$\Omega$.

  10. JEE Main 2026, 6 April, Shift 2 · Question 43
    Refer to the logic circuit given below. For two inputs $(A=1, B=1)$ and $(A=0, B=1)$, output (Y) will be __________.

    1. 1, 0 respectively
    2. 0, 1 respectively
    3. 0, 0 respectively
    4. 1, 1 respectively
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    The OR gate gets $\bar A$ and $B$; the AND gate gets $\bar A$ and $B$; both feed a NOR gate.
    $Y=\overline{(\bar A+B)+\bar AB}=\overline{\bar A+B}=A\bar B$.
    $A=1,B=1$: $Y=0$. $A=0,B=1$: $Y=0$.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

  12. JEE Main 2026, 21 January, Shift 1 · Question 43
    The given circuit works as :

    1. AND gate
    2. NAND gate
    3. NOR gate
    4. OR gate
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    Each input first passes through a NOT gate, giving $\bar A$ and $\bar B$.

  13. JEE Main 2026, 21 January, Shift 2 · Question 45
    The charge stored by the capacitor C in the given circuit in the steady state is __________ $\mu$C.

    1. $5$
    2. $10$
    3. $7.5$
    4. $12.5$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    In the steady state no current flows in the capacitor branch. The diode with the 3 $\Omega$ resistor is reverse biased; the one with the 4 $\Omega$ resistor conducts.
    Current $=\dfrac{2.5}{1+4}=0.5$ A, so the voltage across the parallel branches (and the capacitor, as its 2 $\Omega$ carries no current) is $0.5\times4=2$ V.
    $Q=CV=5\times2=10\ \mu$C.

  14. JEE Main 2026, 22 January, Shift 1 · Question 45
    Find the correct combination of A, B, C and D inputs which can cause the LED to glow.

    1. 0011
    2. 1101
    3. 0100
    4. 1000
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    Each of A and B feeds both inputs of a NOR gate, giving $\bar{A}$ and $\bar{B}$. The NAND gate then gives $\overline{\bar{A}\,\bar{B}}=A+B$.
    C and D go to a NOR gate: $\overline{C+D}$.
    The LED conducts from the NAND output to the NOR output, so it glows when $A+B=1$ and $\overline{C+D}=0$, i.e. at least one of A, B and at least one of C, D is 1.
    Only 1101 satisfies both.

    The correct truth table for the given input data of the following logic gate is :

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

  18. JEE Main 2026, 24 January, Shift 1 · Question 46
