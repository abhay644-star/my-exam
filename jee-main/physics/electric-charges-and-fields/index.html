  1. JEE Main 2026, 2 April, Shift 1 · Question 36
    Two short electric dipoles $A$ and $B$ having dipole moment $\mathrm{p_1}$ and $\mathrm{p_2}$ respectively are placed with their axis mutually perpendicular as shown in the figure. The resultant electric field at a point $x$ is making an angle of $60^\circ$ with the line joining points O and $x$. The ratio of the dipole moments $\mathrm{p_2/p_1}$ is ________.

    1. $\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$
    2. $2\sqrt{3}$
    3. $\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{3}}$
    4. $\sqrt{3}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    Point $x$ (distance $r$ from O) lies on the axis of dipole A: $E_A = \dfrac{2kp_1}{r^3}$ along the line O$x$.
    It lies on the equatorial line of dipole B: $E_B = \dfrac{kp_2}{r^3}$ perpendicular to O$x$.
    $\tan60^\circ = \dfrac{E_B}{E_A} = \dfrac{p_2}{2p_1}$, so $\dfrac{p_2}{p_1} = 2\sqrt{3}$.

  2. JEE Main 2026, 2 April, Shift 2 · Question 40
    Two point charges $8\ \mu C$ and $-2\ \mu C$ are located at $x=2$ cm and $x=4$ cm, respectively on the $x$-axis. The ratio of electric flux due to these charges through two spheres of radii 3 cm and 5 cm with their centers at the origin is __________.

    1. $4:1$
    2. $3:4$
    3. $4:3$
    4. $4:5$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    Gauss's law: flux $=\dfrac{q_{enc}}{\epsilon_0}$.
    Sphere of radius 3 cm encloses only $8\ \mu C$; sphere of radius 5 cm encloses $8-2=6\ \mu C$.
    Ratio $=8:6=4:3$.

  3. JEE Main 2026, 4 April, Shift 2 · Question 36
    A rigid dipole undergoes a simple harmonic motion about its centre in the presence of an electric field $\vec E_1=E_0\hat x$. If another electric field $\vec E_2=2E_0(\hat y+\hat z)$ is introduced to the system, what will be the percentage change in the frequency of the oscillation (approximate)?

    1. $73\%$
    2. $63\%$
    3. $83\%$
    4. $53\%$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    For small oscillations $\omega=\sqrt{\dfrac{pE}{I}}$, so $f\propto\sqrt E$.
    Net field $=E_0(\hat x+2\hat y+2\hat z)$, of magnitude $3E_0$.
    $\dfrac{f'}{f}=\sqrt3\approx1.73$: the frequency increases by about $73\%$.

  4. JEE Main 2026, 6 April, Shift 1 · Question 38
    A thin half ring of radius 35 cm is uniformly charged with a total charge of $Q$ coulomb. If the magnitude of the electric field at centre of the half ring is 100 V/m, then the value of $Q$ is ________ nC.
    ($\epsilon_0=8.85\times10^{-12}$ C$^2$/Nm$^2$ and $\pi=3.14$)

    1. $2.14$
    2. $2.44$
    3. $3.25$
    4. $0.7$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    For a half ring, $E=\dfrac{2k\lambda}{R}$ with $\lambda=\dfrac{Q}{\pi R}$, so $E=\dfrac{Q}{2\pi^2\epsilon_0R^2}$.
    $Q=2\pi^2\epsilon_0R^2E=2\times9.86\times8.85\times10^{-12}\times0.1225\times100$.
    $Q\approx2.14\times10^{-9}$ C $=2.14$ nC.

  5. JEE Main 2026, 8 April, Shift 2 · Question 41
    Two point charges $q_1=3\ \mu C$ and $q_2=-4\ \mu C$ are placed at points $\left(2\hat{i}+3\hat{j}+3\hat{k}\right)$ and $\left(\hat{i}+\hat{j}+\hat{k}\right)$ respectively. Force on charge $q_2$ is __________ N. $\left(\text{Take } \dfrac{1}{4\pi\epsilon_0}=9\times10^9 \text{ SI Units}\right)$

    1. $\left(12\hat{i}+24\hat{j}+24\hat{k}\right)\times10^{-3}$
    2. $\left(4\hat{i}+8\hat{j}+8\hat{k}\right)\times10^{-3}$
    3. $\left(3\hat{i}+6\hat{j}+6\hat{k}\right)\times10^{-3}$
    4. $\left(-4\hat{i}-8\hat{j}-8\hat{k}\right)\times10^{-3}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    $\vec r=\vec r_2-\vec r_1=-\hat i-2\hat j-2\hat k$, $|\vec r|=3$ m.
    $\vec F_2=\dfrac{kq_1q_2}{r^3}\vec r=\dfrac{9\times10^9\times3\times10^{-6}\times(-4\times10^{-6})}{27}(-\hat i-2\hat j-2\hat k)$.
    $\vec F_2=-0.004\,(-\hat i-2\hat j-2\hat k)=\left(4\hat i+8\hat j+8\hat k\right)\times10^{-3}$ N (attraction, towards $q_1$).

  6. JEE Main 2026, 22 January, Shift 1 · Question 37
    Six point charges are kept $60^\circ$ apart from each other on the circumference of a circle of radius $R$ as shown in figure. The net electric field at the center of the circle is _______.
    ($\epsilon_0$ is permittivity of free space)

    1. $-\dfrac{5Q}{8\pi\epsilon_0R^2}\left(\hat{i}+\sqrt3\hat{j}\right)$
    2. $-\left(\dfrac{5Q}{8\pi\epsilon_0R^2}\right)\left(\hat{i}-3\hat{j}\right)$
    3. $-\dfrac{Q}{4\pi\epsilon_0R^2}\left(\sqrt3\hat{i}-\hat{j}\right)$
    4. $\dfrac{Q}{4\pi\epsilon_0R^2}\left(\sqrt3\hat{i}-\hat{j}\right)$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    Six equal charges $+Q$ would give zero field. The actual set is that, plus a charge $-2Q$ at the position of $-Q$, at $150^\circ$ from the $x$-axis.
    A negative charge pulls the field towards itself: $\vec{E}=\dfrac{2Q}{4\pi\epsilon_0R^2}\left(\cos150^\circ\,\hat{i}+\sin150^\circ\,\hat{j}\right)=\dfrac{Q}{4\pi\epsilon_0R^2}\left(-\sqrt3\hat{i}+\hat{j}\right)$.
    $=-\dfrac{Q}{4\pi\epsilon_0R^2}\left(\sqrt3\hat{i}-\hat{j}\right)$.

  7. JEE Main 2026, 22 January, Shift 1 · Question 38
    A simple pendulum has a bob with mass $m$ and charge $q$. The pendulum string has negligible mass. When a uniform and horizontal electric field $\vec{E}$ is applied, the tension in the string changes. The final tension in the string, when pendulum attains an equilibrium position is __________.
    (g: acceleration due to gravity)

    1. $mg+qE$
    2. $\sqrt{m^2g^2+q^2E^2}$
    3. $mg-qE$
    4. $\sqrt{m^2g^2-q^2E^2}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    At equilibrium the tension balances the resultant of the weight $mg$ (vertical) and the electric force $qE$ (horizontal).
    $T=\sqrt{(mg)^2+(qE)^2}$.

  8. JEE Main 2026, 22 January, Shift 2 · Question 27
    If $\epsilon$, $E$ and $t$ represent the free space permittivity, electric field and time respectively, then the unit of $\dfrac{\epsilon E}{t}$ will be :

    1. A/m$^2$
    2. Am$^2$
    3. Am
    4. A/m
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    $\epsilon E$ has the unit of surface charge density, C/m$^2$ (from Gauss's law, $E=\dfrac{\sigma}{\epsilon}$).
    Dividing by time: C s$^{-1}$ m$^{-2}$ = A/m$^2$ (the displacement current density).

  9. JEE Main 2026, 23 January, Shift 1 · Question 39
    Two point charges $2q$ and $q$ are placed at vertex $A$ and centre of face $CDEF$ of the cube as shown in figure. The electric flux passing through the cube is :

    1. $\dfrac{q}{\epsilon_0}$
    2. $\dfrac{3q}{\epsilon_0}$
    3. $\dfrac{3q}{2\epsilon_0}$
    4. $\dfrac{3q}{4\epsilon_0}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    A charge at a corner is shared by 8 such cubes, so this cube gets $\dfrac{2q}{8}=\dfrac q4$ of enclosed charge.
    A charge at the centre of a face is shared by 2 cubes: $\dfrac q2$.
    Flux $=\dfrac{1}{\epsilon_0}\left(\dfrac q4+\dfrac q2\right)=\dfrac{3q}{4\epsilon_0}$.

  10. JEE Main 2026, 23 January, Shift 2 · Question 38
    Two shorts dipoles $(A, B)$, $A$ having charges $\pm2\ \mu$C and length 1 cm and $B$ having charges $\pm4\ \mu$C and length 1 cm are placed with their centres 80 cm apart as shown in the figure. The electric field at a point P, equi-distant from the centres of both dipoles is _________N/C.

    1. $9\sqrt2\times10^4$
    2. $\dfrac{9}{16}\sqrt2\times10^4$
    3. $\dfrac{9}{16}\sqrt2\times10^5$
    4. $4.5\sqrt2\times10^4$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    P is 40 cm from each centre: on the axis of A and on the equatorial line of B.
    $p_A=2\times10^{-6}\times0.01=2\times10^{-8}$ C m: $E_A=\dfrac{2kp_A}{r^3}=\dfrac{2\times9\times10^9\times2\times10^{-8}}{0.064}=5625$ N/C.
    $p_B=4\times10^{-8}$ C m: $E_B=\dfrac{kp_B}{r^3}=5625$ N/C, at right angles to $E_A$.
    $E=5625\sqrt2=\dfrac9{16}\sqrt2\times10^4$ N/C.

  11. JEE Main 2026, 24 January, Shift 1 · Question 35
    The electrostatic potential in a charged spherical region of radius $r$ varies as $V=ar^3+b$, where $a$ and $b$ are constants. The total charge in the sphere of unit radius is $\alpha\times\pi a\epsilon_0$. The value of $\alpha$ is __________.
    (permittivity of vacuum is $\epsilon_0$)

    1. $-12$
    2. $-6$
    3. $-8$
    4. $-9$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    $E=-\dfrac{dV}{dr}=-3ar^2$ (radial).
    Gauss's law at $r=1$: $q=4\pi\epsilon_0r^2E=4\pi\epsilon_0(-3a)=-12\pi a\epsilon_0$, so $\alpha=-12$.

  12. JEE Main 2026, 24 January, Shift 1 · Question 39
    Three charges $+2q$, $+3q$ and $-4q$ are situated at $(0, -3a)$, $(2a, 0)$ and $(-2a, 0)$ respectively in the $xy$ plane. The resultant dipole moment about origin is __________.

    1. $2qa\left(7\hat{i}-3\hat{j}\right)$
    2. $2qa\left(3\hat{j}-\hat{i}\right)$
    3. $2qa\left(3\hat{i}-7\hat{j}\right)$
    4. $2qa\left(3\hat{j}-7\hat{i}\right)$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    $\vec p=\sum q_i\vec r_i=2q(-3a\hat j)+3q(2a\hat i)-4q(-2a\hat i)=14qa\hat i-6qa\hat j=2qa\left(7\hat i-3\hat j\right)$.

  13. JEE Main 2026, 24 January, Shift 2 · Question 46
    A point charge $q=1\ \mu$C is located at a distance 2 cm from one end of a thin insulating wire of length 10 cm having a charge $Q=24\ \mu$C, distributed uniformly along its length, as shown in figure. Force between $q$ and wire is ______ N.
    (Use : $\dfrac{1}{4\pi\epsilon_0}=9\times10^9$ N.m$^2$/C$^2$)

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 90

    For a uniform rod of charge $Q$, length $L$, with the point charge on its line at distance $a$ from the near end: $F=\dfrac{kqQ}{a(a+L)}$.
    $F=\dfrac{9\times10^9\times10^{-6}\times24\times10^{-6}}{0.02\times0.12}=\dfrac{0.216}{0.0024}=90$ N.

  14. JEE Main 2026, 28 January, Shift 2 · Question 35
    Identify the correct statements :
    A. Electrostatic field lines form closed loops .
    B. The electric field lines point radially outward when charge is greater than zero.
    C. The Gauss - Law is valid only for inverse - square force.
    D. The workdone in moving a charged particle in a static electric field around a closed path is zero.
    E. The motion of a particle under Coulomb's force must take place in a plane.
    Choose the correct answer from the options given below :

    1. A, B, C, D Only
    2. B, C, D, E Only
    3. A, C, E Only
    4. A, B, D, E Only
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    A is false: electrostatic field lines start on positive and end on negative charges and never form closed loops.
    B is true for a positive point charge. C is true: Gauss's law follows from the $1/r^2$ dependence. D is true: the electrostatic field is conservative.
    E is true: the Coulomb force from a fixed charge is central, so angular momentum is conserved and the motion stays in one plane.
