  1. JEE Main 2026, 2 April, Shift 1 · Question 36
    Two short electric dipoles $A$ and $B$ having dipole moment $\mathrm{p_1}$ and $\mathrm{p_2}$ respectively are placed with their axis mutually perpendicular as shown in the figure. The resultant electric field at a point $x$ is making an angle of $60^\circ$ with the line joining points O and $x$. The ratio of the dipole moments $\mathrm{p_2/p_1}$ is ________.

    1. $\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$
    2. $2\sqrt{3}$
    3. $\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{3}}$
    4. $\sqrt{3}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    Point $x$ (distance $r$ from O) lies on the axis of dipole A: $E_A = \dfrac{2kp_1}{r^3}$ along the line O$x$.
    It lies on the equatorial line of dipole B: $E_B = \dfrac{kp_2}{r^3}$ perpendicular to O$x$.
    $\tan60^\circ = \dfrac{E_B}{E_A} = \dfrac{p_2}{2p_1}$, so $\dfrac{p_2}{p_1} = 2\sqrt{3}$.

  2. JEE Main 2026, 2 April, Shift 2 · Question 40
    Two point charges $8\ \mu C$ and $-2\ \mu C$ are located at $x=2$ cm and $x=4$ cm, respectively on the $x$-axis. The ratio of electric flux due to these charges through two spheres of radii 3 cm and 5 cm with their centers at the origin is __________.

    1. $4:1$
    2. $3:4$
    3. $4:3$
    4. $4:5$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    Gauss's law: flux $=\dfrac{q_{enc}}{\epsilon_0}$.
    Sphere of radius 3 cm encloses only $8\ \mu C$; sphere of radius 5 cm encloses $8-2=6\ \mu C$.
    Ratio $=8:6=4:3$.

  3. JEE Main 2026, 4 April, Shift 2 · Question 36
    A rigid dipole undergoes a simple harmonic motion about its centre in the presence of an electric field $\vec E_1=E_0\hat x$. If another electric field $\vec E_2=2E_0(\hat y+\hat z)$ is introduced to the system, what will be the percentage change in the frequency of the oscillation (approximate)?

    1. $73\%$
    2. $63\%$
    3. $83\%$
    4. $53\%$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    For small oscillations $\omega=\sqrt{\dfrac{pE}{I}}$, so $f\propto\sqrt E$.
    Net field $=E_0(\hat x+2\hat y+2\hat z)$, of magnitude $3E_0$.
    $\dfrac{f'}{f}=\sqrt3\approx1.73$: the frequency increases by about $73\%$.

  4. JEE Main 2026, 6 April, Shift 1 · Question 38
    A thin half ring of radius 35 cm is uniformly charged with a total charge of $Q$ coulomb. If the magnitude of the electric field at centre of the half ring is 100 V/m, then the value of $Q$ is ________ nC.
    ($\epsilon_0=8.85\times10^{-12}$ C$^2$/Nm$^2$ and $\pi=3.14$)

    1. $2.14$
    2. $2.44$
    3. $3.25$
    4. $0.7$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    For a half ring, $E=\dfrac{2k\lambda}{R}$ with $\lambda=\dfrac{Q}{\pi R}$, so $E=\dfrac{Q}{2\pi^2\epsilon_0R^2}$.
    $Q=2\pi^2\epsilon_0R^2E=2\times9.86\times8.85\times10^{-12}\times0.1225\times100$.
    $Q\approx2.14\times10^{-9}$ C $=2.14$ nC.

    Two point charges $q_1=3\ \mu C$ and $q_2=-4\ \mu C$ are placed at points $\left(2\hat{i}+3\hat{j}+3\hat{k}\right)$ and $\left(\hat{i}+\hat{j}+\hat{k}\right)$ respectively. Force on charge $q_2$ is __________ N. $\left(\text{Take } \dfrac{1}{4\pi\epsilon_0}=9\times10^9 \text{ SI Units}\right)$

    1. $\left(12\hat{i}+24\hat{j}+24\hat{k}\right)\times10^{-3}$
    2. $\left(4\hat{i}+8\hat{j}+8\hat{k}\right)\times10^{-3}$
    3. $\left(3\hat{i}+6\hat{j}+6\hat{k}\right)\times10^{-3}$
    4. $\left(-4\hat{i}-8\hat{j}-8\hat{k}\right)\times10^{-3}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    $\vec r=\vec r_2-\vec r_1=-\hat i-2\hat j-2\hat k$, $|\vec r|=3$ m.

    $\vec F_2=-0.004\,(-\hat i-2\hat j-2\hat k)=\left(4\hat i+8\hat j+8\hat k\right)\times10^{-3}$ N (attraction, towards $q_1$).

  6. JEE Main 2026, 22 January, Shift 1 · Question 37
    Six point charges are kept $60^\circ$ apart from each other on the circumference of a circle of radius $R$ as shown in figure. The net electric field at the center of the circle is _______.
    ($\epsilon_0$ is permittivity of free space)

    1. $-\dfrac{5Q}{8\pi\epsilon_0R^2}\left(\hat{i}+\sqrt3\hat{j}\right)$
    2. $-\left(\dfrac{5Q}{8\pi\epsilon_0R^2}\right)\left(\hat{i}-3\hat{j}\right)$
    3. $-\dfrac{Q}{4\pi\epsilon_0R^2}\left(\sqrt3\hat{i}-\hat{j}\right)$
    4. $\dfrac{Q}{4\pi\epsilon_0R^2}\left(\sqrt3\hat{i}-\hat{j}\right)$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    Six equal charges $+Q$ would give zero field. The actual set is that, plus a charge $-2Q$ at the position of $-Q$, at $150^\circ$ from the $x$-axis.
    A negative charge pulls the field towards itself: $\vec{E}=\dfrac{2Q}{4\pi\epsilon_0R^2}\left(\cos150^\circ\,\hat{i}+\sin150^\circ\,\hat{j}\right)=\dfrac{Q}{4\pi\epsilon_0R^2}\left(-\sqrt3\hat{i}+\hat{j}\right)$.
    $=-\dfrac{Q}{4\pi\epsilon_0R^2}\left(\sqrt3\hat{i}-\hat{j}\right)$.

    A simple pendulum has a bob with mass $m$ and charge $q$. The pendulum string has negligible mass. When a uniform and horizontal electric field $\vec{E}$ is applied, the tension in the string changes. The final tension in the string, when pendulum attains an equilibrium position is __________.
    (g: acceleration due to gravity)

    1. $mg+qE$
    2. $\sqrt{m^2g^2+q^2E^2}$
    3. $mg-qE$
    4. $\sqrt{m^2g^2-q^2E^2}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    At equilibrium the tension balances the resultant of the weight $mg$ (vertical) and the electric force $qE$ (horizontal).

    2. $\dfrac{3q}{\epsilon_0}$

  14. JEE Main 2026, 28 January, Shift 2 · Question 35
    Identify the correct statements :
    A. Electrostatic field lines form closed loops .
    B. The electric field lines point radially outward when charge is greater than zero.
    C. The Gauss - Law is valid only for inverse - square force.
    D. The workdone in moving a charged particle in a static electric field around a closed path is zero.
    E. The motion of a particle under Coulomb's force must take place in a plane.
    Choose the correct answer from the options given below :

    1. A, B, C, D Only
    2. B, C, D, E Only
    3. A, C, E Only
    4. A, B, D, E Only
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    A is false: electrostatic field lines start on positive and end on negative charges and never form closed loops.
    B is true for a positive point charge. C is true: Gauss's law follows from the $1/r^2$ dependence. D is true: the electrostatic field is conservative.
    E is true: the Coulomb force from a fixed charge is central, so angular momentum is conserved and the motion stays in one plane.

  19. JEE Main 2025, 4 April, Shift 1 · Question 37
    Two infinite identical charged sheets and a charged spherical body of charge density '$\rho$' are arranged as shown in figure. Then the correct relation between the electrical fields at A, B, C and D points is:

    1. $\vec E_A=\vec E_B$; $\vec E_C=\vec E_D$
    2. $\vec E_A>\vec E_B$; $\vec E_C=\vec E_D$
    3. $\vec E_C\ne\vec E_D$; $\vec E_A>\vec E_B$
    4. $\left|\vec E_A\right|=\left|\vec E_B\right|$; $\vec E_C>\vec E_D$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

  27. JEE Main 2025, 23 January, Shift 1 · Question 36
    A point particle of charge Q is located at P along the axis of an electric dipole 1 at a distance r as shown in the figure. The point P is also on the equatorial plane of a second electric dipole 2 at a distance r. The dipoles are made of opposite charge q separated by a distance $2a$. For the charge particle at P not to experience any net force, which of the following correctly describes the situation?

    1. $\dfrac ar\sim0.5$
    2. $\dfrac ar\sim20$
    3. $\dfrac ar\sim3$
    4. $\dfrac ar\sim10$

    $\dfrac ar\sim3$.

    A positive ion A and a negative ion B has charges $6.67\times10^{-19}$ C and $9.6\times10^{-10}$ C, and masses $19.2\times10^{-27}$ kg and $9\times10^{-27}$ kg respectively. At an instant, the ions are separated by a certain distance r. At that instant the ratio of the magnitudes of electrostatic force to gravitational force is $P\times10^{-13}$, where the value of P is ______.
    (Take $\dfrac{1}{4\pi\varepsilon_0}=9\times10^9$ Nm$^2$C$^{-1}$ and universal gravitational constant as $6.67\times10^{-11}$ Nm$^2$ kg$^{-2}$)

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: Dropped: full marks to everyone

    $\dfrac{F_e}{F_g}=\dfrac{kq_1q_2}{Gm_1m_2}$ (the distance cancels).
    $=\dfrac{9\times10^9\times6.67\times10^{-19}\times9.6\times10^{-10}}{6.67\times10^{-11}\times19.2\times10^{-27}\times9\times10^{-27}}=\dfrac{8.64\times10^{-19}}{1.728\times10^{-63}}=5\times10^{44}$.
    This cannot be written as $P\times10^{-13}$ with a reasonable P, so the question has no valid answer as set.

    Note: Dropped in the official final key: full marks to everyone. With the data as printed the ratio is about $5\times10^{44}$, nowhere near the form $P\times10^{-13}$, so no sensible value of P exists. The answer shown (0) is only a placeholder.

  29. JEE Main 2025, 24 January, Shift 1 · Question 50
    A square loop of sides $a=1$ m is held normally in front of a point charge $q=1$C. The flux of the electric field through the shaded region is $\dfrac5p\times\dfrac{1}{\varepsilon_0}\dfrac{\mathrm{Nm^2}}{\mathrm{C}}$, where the value of $p$ is _____.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 48

    With the charge on the axis at distance $\dfrac a2$ from the centre of the loop, the loop is one face of a cube of side $a$ centred on the charge, so the whole loop receives $\dfrac{q}{6\varepsilon_0}$, i.e. $\dfrac{q}{24\varepsilon_0}$ per quarter.
    The shaded region is two full quarters plus half of a third quarter (the triangle cut off by the diagonal), i.e. $2.5$ quarters.
    Flux $=\dfrac{2.5}{24}\cdot\dfrac{1}{\varepsilon_0}=\dfrac{5}{48}\cdot\dfrac1{\varepsilon_0}$, so $p=48$.

    Note: The text does not state the distance of the charge from the loop; NTA's answer (48) corresponds to the charge being at distance $a/2$ from the centre of the loop, so that the loop is one face of a cube centred on the charge.

  30. JEE Main 2025, 24 January, Shift 2 · Question 37
    A small uncharged conducting sphere is placed in contact with an identical sphere but having $4\times10^{-8}$ C charge and then removed to a distance such that the force of repulsion between them is $9\times10^{-3}$ N. The distance between them is (Take $\dfrac{1}{4\pi\epsilon_0}$ as $9\times10^9$ in SI units)

    1. $1$ cm
    2. $2$ cm
    3. $3$ cm
    4. $4$ cm
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    On contact the charge is shared equally: $2\times10^{-8}$ C each.
    $9\times10^{-3}=\dfrac{9\times10^9\times(2\times10^{-8})^2}{r^2}=\dfrac{3.6\times10^{-6}}{r^2} \Rightarrow r^2=4\times10^{-4}$ m$^2$.
    $r=0.02$ m $=2$ cm.

  31. JEE Main 2025, 28 January, Shift 1 · Question 37
    A particle of mass 'm' and charge 'q' is fastened to one end 'A' of a massless string having equilibrium length $l$, whose other end is fixed at point 'O'. The whole system is placed on a frictionless horizontal plane and is initially at rest. If uniform electric field is switched on along the direction as shown in figure, then the speed of the particle when it crosses the x-axis is

    1. $\sqrt{\dfrac{2qEl}{m}}$
    2. $\sqrt{\dfrac{qEl}{m}}$
    3. $\sqrt{\dfrac{qEl}{2m}}$
    4. $\sqrt{\dfrac{qEl}{4m}}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    The force $qE$ along $+x$ swings the particle about O on a circle of radius $l$, from $60^\circ$ above the x-axis down to the x-axis.
    Displacement along the field $=l-l\cos60^\circ=\dfrac{l}{2}$, so the work done is $\dfrac{qEl}{2}$.
    $\dfrac12mv^2=\dfrac{qEl}{2} \Rightarrow v=\sqrt{\dfrac{qEl}{m}}$.

  32. JEE Main 2025, 28 January, Shift 2 · Question 47
    An electric dipole of dipole moment $6\times10^{-6}$ Cm is placed in uniform electric field of magnitude $10^6$ V/m. Initially, the dipole moment is parallel to electric field. The work that needs to be done on the dipole to make its dipole moment opposite to the field, will be __________ J.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 12

    $W=pE(\cos0^\circ-\cos180^\circ)=2pE$.
    $W=2\times6\times10^{-6}\times10^6=12$ J.

    Match <b>List - I</b> with <b>List - II</b>.

    List - IList - II
    (A) Electric field inside (distance r > 0 from center) of a uniformly charged spherical shell with surface charge density $\sigma$, and radius R.(I) $\sigma/\epsilon_0$
    (B) Electric field at distance r>0 from a uniformly charged infinite plane sheet with surface charge density $\sigma$.(II) $\sigma/2\epsilon_0$
    (C) Electric field outside (distance r>0 from center) of a uniformly charged spherical shell with surface charge density $\sigma$, and radius R.(III) 0
    (D) Electric field between 2 oppositely charged infinite plane parallel sheets with uniform surface charge density $\sigma$.(IV) $\dfrac{\sigma}{\epsilon_0r^2}$
    Choose the <b>correct</b> answer from the options given below :

    1. (A)-(IV), (B)-(II), (C)-(III), (D)-(I)
    2. (A)-(III), (B)-(II), (C)-(IV), (D)-(I)
    3. (A)-(II), (B)-(I), (C)-(IV), (D)-(III)
    4. (A)-(IV), (B)-(I), (C)-(III), (D)-(II)
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    (A) Inside a charged shell the field is zero: (III).
    (B) Infinite sheet: $\sigma/2\epsilon_0$: (II).

  34. JEE Main 2025, 29 January, Shift 1 · Question 38
    An electric dipole of mass m, charge q, and length $l$ is placed in a uniform electric field $\vec{E}=E_0\hat{i}$. When the dipole is rotated slightly from its equilibrium position and released, the time period of its oscillations will be :

    1. $\dfrac{1}{2\pi}\sqrt{\dfrac{ml}{2qE_0}}$
    2. $2\pi\sqrt{\dfrac{ml}{2qE_0}}$
    3. $\dfrac{1}{2\pi}\sqrt{\dfrac{2ml}{qE_0}}$
    4. $2\pi\sqrt{\dfrac{ml}{qE_0}}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    Restoring torque for a small angle: $\tau=-pE_0\theta=-qlE_0\theta$.
    Taking a mass m at each end, the moment of inertia about the centre is $I=2m\left(\tfrac l2\right)^2=\tfrac{ml^2}{2}$.
    $T=2\pi\sqrt{\dfrac{I}{qlE_0}}=2\pi\sqrt{\dfrac{ml}{2qE_0}}$.

    Note: The key's answer corresponds to a mass m at each end of the dipole; if m were the total mass, the period would be $2\pi\sqrt{ml/(4qE_0)}$, which is not an option.

  36. JEE Main 2025, 29 January, Shift 2 · Question 37
    An electric dipole is placed at a distance of 2 cm from an infinite plane sheet having positive charge density $\sigma_o$. Choose the correct option from the following.

    1. Torque on dipole is zero and net force acts towards the sheet.
    2. Torque on dipole is zero and net force is directed away from the sheet.
    3. Potential energy of dipole is minimum and torque is zero.
    4. Potential energy and torque both are maximum.
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    The sheet's field is uniform, $\dfrac{\sigma_o}{2\epsilon_0}$, directed away from the sheet.
    The dipole points from $-q$ (near the sheet) to $+q$ (further away), i.e. along the field: $\theta=0$.
    So $\tau=pE\sin\theta=0$ and $U=-pE\cos\theta$ is minimum; in a uniform field the net force is zero.
