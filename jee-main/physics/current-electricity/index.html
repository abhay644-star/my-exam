  1. JEE Main 2026, 2 April, Shift 2 · Question 38
    Two resistors of $200\ \Omega$ and $400\ \Omega$ are connected in series with a battery of 100 V. A bulb rated at 200 V, 100 W is connected across the $400\ \Omega$ resistance. The potential drop across the bulb is __________ V.

    1. $25$
    2. $50$
    3. $66.6$
    4. $100$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    Bulb resistance $=\dfrac{V^2}{P}=\dfrac{200^2}{100}=400\ \Omega$.
    Bulb in parallel with $400\ \Omega$: $200\ \Omega$; in series with $200\ \Omega$, total $400\ \Omega$.
    The 100 V divides equally: potential drop across the bulb $=50$ V.

  3. JEE Main 2026, 5 April, Shift 1 · Question 34
    Refer to the figure given below. The values of $I_1$, $I_2$ and $I_3$ are _______.

    1. $I_1 = 2.5$ A, $I_2 = 1.875$ A, $I_3 = 1.875$ A
    2. $I_1 = 1.875$ A, $I_2 = 2.5$ A, $I_3 = 1.875$ A
    3. $I_1 = 1.875$ A, $I_2 = 1.875$ A, $I_3 = 2.5$ A
    4. $I_1 = 2.5$ A, $I_2 = 2.5$ A, $I_3 = 1.875$ A
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    Take the bottom node as $0$ V; the 5 V cell (via the right vertex) holds the top node at $5$ V. Let the left node be $V_L$ and the middle node $V_M$.
    Middle node: $\dfrac{V_M-5}{2}+\dfrac{V_M}{2}=I_1$ (current $I_1$ leaves M through the $1\,\Omega$ branch towards L), so $V_M=2.5-I_1$.
    Left node: $I_1=\dfrac{V_L-5}{4}+\dfrac{V_L}{4}$; the 10 V cell gives $V_L=V_M+10-I_1(1)=12.5-2I_1$.
    So $I_1=\dfrac{2(12.5-2I_1)-5}{4}=5-I_1 \Rightarrow I_1=2.5$ A, $V_L=7.5$ V, $V_M=0$.
    $I_3=\dfrac{7.5}{4}=1.875$ A; top node: in from L $\dfrac{2.5}{4}=0.625$ A, out to M $\dfrac52=2.5$ A, so $I_2=2.5-0.625=1.875$ A.

    $V_A=V_B$ means the bridge is balanced: $\dfrac{10}{20}=\dfrac{R}{40}$, so $R=20\ \Omega$ and no current flows in the 30 $\Omega$ resistor.
    Upper branch $10+20=30\ \Omega$, lower branch $20+40=60\ \Omega$; in parallel, $20\ \Omega$.
    $I=\dfrac{40}{20}=2$ A.
