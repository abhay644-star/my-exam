  1. JEE Main 2026, 2 April, Shift 2 · Question 38
    Two resistors of $200\ \Omega$ and $400\ \Omega$ are connected in series with a battery of 100 V. A bulb rated at 200 V, 100 W is connected across the $400\ \Omega$ resistance. The potential drop across the bulb is __________ V.

    1. $25$
    2. $50$
    3. $66.6$
    4. $100$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    Bulb resistance $=\dfrac{V^2}{P}=\dfrac{200^2}{100}=400\ \Omega$.
    Bulb in parallel with $400\ \Omega$: $200\ \Omega$; in series with $200\ \Omega$, total $400\ \Omega$.
    The 100 V divides equally: potential drop across the bulb $=50$ V.

  2. JEE Main 2026, 4 April, Shift 1 · Question 43
    The voltage and the current between $A$ and $B$ points shown in the circuit are____________.

    1. $24$ V, $12$ A
    2. $24$ V, $4$ A
    3. $18$ V, $12$ A
    4. $27$ V, $4$ A
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    Between $A$ and $B$ there are four identical source branches ($27$ V with $3\ \Omega$; the third has $14+13=27$ V) and one load branch of $3+3=6\ \Omega$.
    The four sources in parallel are equivalent to $27$ V with internal resistance $\dfrac34\ \Omega$.
    $I=\dfrac{27}{6+0.75}=4$ A through the $6\ \Omega$ branch, and $V_{AB}=4\times6=24$ V.

  3. JEE Main 2026, 5 April, Shift 1 · Question 34
    Refer to the figure given below. The values of $I_1$, $I_2$ and $I_3$ are _______.

    1. $I_1 = 2.5$ A, $I_2 = 1.875$ A, $I_3 = 1.875$ A
    2. $I_1 = 1.875$ A, $I_2 = 2.5$ A, $I_3 = 1.875$ A
    3. $I_1 = 1.875$ A, $I_2 = 1.875$ A, $I_3 = 2.5$ A
    4. $I_1 = 2.5$ A, $I_2 = 2.5$ A, $I_3 = 1.875$ A
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    Take the bottom node as $0$ V; the 5 V cell (via the right vertex) holds the top node at $5$ V. Let the left node be $V_L$ and the middle node $V_M$.
    Middle node: $\dfrac{V_M-5}{2}+\dfrac{V_M}{2}=I_1$ (current $I_1$ leaves M through the $1\,\Omega$ branch towards L), so $V_M=2.5-I_1$.
    Left node: $I_1=\dfrac{V_L-5}{4}+\dfrac{V_L}{4}$; the 10 V cell gives $V_L=V_M+10-I_1(1)=12.5-2I_1$.
    So $I_1=\dfrac{2(12.5-2I_1)-5}{4}=5-I_1 \Rightarrow I_1=2.5$ A, $V_L=7.5$ V, $V_M=0$.
    $I_3=\dfrac{7.5}{4}=1.875$ A; top node: in from L $\dfrac{2.5}{4}=0.625$ A, out to M $\dfrac52=2.5$ A, so $I_2=2.5-0.625=1.875$ A.

  4. JEE Main 2026, 5 April, Shift 1 · Question 38
    Refer to the figure given below, current between terminals $A$ and $B$ is __________A.

    1. $12.5$
    2. $1.25$
    3. $7.5$
    4. $5$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    Each of the top three branches has emf $3\times5=15$ V and resistance $3\times3=9\,\Omega$; the three in parallel act as one source of $15$ V with internal resistance $3\,\Omega$.
    The bottom branch between A and B has $9\,\Omega$ and no cell.
    Current through it $=\dfrac{15}{3+9}=1.25$ A.

  5. JEE Main 2026, 5 April, Shift 2 · Question 37
    Under steady state condition the potential difference across the capacitor in the circuit is __________ V.

    1. $0.5$
    2. $1.5$
    3. $0$
    4. $2$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    In steady state no current flows in the capacitor branch (capacitor + $4\ \Omega$).
    Current in the loop of the 2 V cell, $6\ \Omega$ and $2\ \Omega$: $I=\dfrac{2}{6+2}=0.25$ A.
    P.d. across the $2\ \Omega$ resistor (= p.d. across the capacitor branch) $=0.25\times2=0.5$ V; no drop across the $4\ \Omega$, so the capacitor has $0.5$ V.

  6. JEE Main 2026, 5 April, Shift 2 · Question 49
    When an external resistance of $5\ \Omega$ is connected across terminals of a cell, a current of 0.25 A flows through it. When the $5\ \Omega$ resistor is replaced by a $2\ \Omega$ resistor, a current of 0.5 A flows through it. The internal resistance of the cell is __________ $\Omega$.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 1

    $E=0.25(5+r)=0.5(2+r)$.
    $1.25+0.25r=1+0.5r \Rightarrow r=1\ \Omega$.

  7. JEE Main 2026, 6 April, Shift 1 · Question 46
    Refer to the circuit diagram given below. The heat generated across the 6 $\Omega$ resistance in 100 second is $\dfrac{\alpha}{100}$ J. The value of $\alpha$ is ________. (Nearest integer)

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 3477

    Take the right junction as $0$ V and let the left junction of the parallel part (after the $3\ \Omega$) be at $x$. With the polarities shown, the left end of the $3\ \Omega$ is at $+2$ V, and the $6\ \Omega$ branch current from left to right is $\dfrac{x-3}{6}$.
    KCL at $x$: $\dfrac{2-x}{3}=\dfrac x4+\dfrac{x-3}{6}\Rightarrow8-4x=5x-6\Rightarrow x=\dfrac{14}{9}$ V.
    Current in $6\ \Omega$: $\left|\dfrac{x-3}{6}\right|=\dfrac{13}{54}$ A.
    Heat in 100 s $=\left(\dfrac{13}{54}\right)^2\times6\times100=\dfrac{101400}{2916}\approx34.77$ J $=\dfrac{3477}{100}$ J.
    $\alpha\approx3477$.

  8. JEE Main 2026, 6 April, Shift 2 · Question 49
    Two cells of emfs 1 V and 2 V and internal resistance 2 $\Omega$ and 1 $\Omega$, respectively connected in parallel, gave a current of 1 A through an external resistance. If the polarity of one cell is reversed, then value of current through the external resistance will be $\dfrac{\alpha}{5}$ A. The value of $\alpha$ is _________.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 3

    Parallel combination: $r_{eq}=\dfrac{2\times1}{3}=\dfrac23\ \Omega$, $E_{eq}=\dfrac{\frac12+\frac21}{\frac12+1}=\dfrac53$ V.
    $1=\dfrac{5/3}{R+2/3} \Rightarrow R=1\ \Omega$.
    Reversing the 1 V cell: $E_{eq}=\dfrac{-\frac12+2}{\frac32}=1$ V.
    $I=\dfrac{1}{1+\frac23}=\dfrac35$ A, so $\alpha=3$.

  9. JEE Main 2026, 8 April, Shift 2 · Question 49
    The stored charge in the capacitor in steady state of the following circuit is __________ $\mu$C.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 200

    In steady state no current flows through the capacitor branch (or its $10\ \Omega$), so the capacitor voltage equals the voltage across the right-hand $4\ \Omega$.
    Right section: $4+4+2=10\ \Omega$, in parallel with the $10\ \Omega$: $5\ \Omega$. Adding $5+2$: $12\ \Omega$ in total, connected directly across the 12 V cell (in parallel with the $12\ \Omega$).
    Current into this ladder $=\dfrac{12}{12}=1$ A; voltage across the $10\ \Omega$ $=1\times5=5$ V.
    Current in the right section $=\dfrac5{10}=0.5$ A, so the voltage across the $4\ \Omega$ $=2$ V.
    $Q=CV=100\ \mu\text{F}\times2\ \text{V}=200\ \mu$C.
