  1. JEE Main 2026, 2 April, Shift 1 · Question 43
    An electromagnetic wave travelling in $x$-direction is described by field equation $E_y = 300\sin\omega\left(t - \dfrac{x}{c}\right)$. If the electron is restricted to move in $y$-direction only with speed of $1.5\times10^6$ m/s then ratio of maximum electric and magnetic forces acting on the electron is ________.

    1. $200$
    2. $150$
    3. $400$
    4. $300$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    $B_0 = \dfrac{E_0}{c}$ (along $z$), perpendicular to the electron's velocity along $y$.
    $\dfrac{F_E}{F_B} = \dfrac{eE_0}{evB_0} = \dfrac{c}{v} = \dfrac{3\times10^8}{1.5\times10^6} = 200$.

  2. JEE Main 2026, 2 April, Shift 2 · Question 37
    An electromagnetic wave travels in free space along the $x$-direction. At a particular point in space and time, $\vec B=2\times10^{-7}\,\hat j$ T is associated with this wave. The value of corresponding electric field $\vec E$ at this point is __________ V/m.

    1. $60\,\hat k$
    2. $-60\,\hat k$
    3. $30\,\hat k$
    4. $-600\,\hat k$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    $|\vec E|=c|\vec B|=3\times10^8\times2\times10^{-7}=60$ V/m.
    $\vec E\times\vec B$ must point along $+\hat i$: $(-\hat k)\times\hat j=\hat i$, so $\vec E$ is along $-\hat k$.
    $\vec E=-60\,\hat k$ V/m.

  3. JEE Main 2026, 4 April, Shift 2 · Question 40
    A magnetic field vector in an electromagnetic wave is represented by $\vec B=B_0\sin\left(2\pi\nu t-\dfrac{2\pi x}{\lambda}\right)\hat j$. Its associated electric field vector is _________.

    1. $\vec E=-\nu\lambda B_0\sin\left(2\pi\nu t-\dfrac{2\pi x}{\lambda}\right)\hat k$
    2. $\vec E=-\nu\lambda B_0\sin\left(2\pi\nu t-\dfrac{2\pi x}{\lambda}\right)\hat i$
    3. $\vec E=\nu\lambda B_0\sin\left(2\pi\nu t-\dfrac{2\pi x}{\lambda}\right)\hat k$
    4. $\vec E=\nu\lambda B_0\sin\left(2\pi\nu t-\dfrac{2\pi x}{\lambda}\right)\hat i$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    The wave travels along $+x$; $\vec E$ is perpendicular to $\hat i$ and $\hat j$, so along $\pm\hat k$, with $E_0=cB_0=\nu\lambda B_0$.
    $\vec E\times\vec B$ must point along $+\hat i$: $(-\hat k)\times\hat j=\hat i$, so $\vec E$ is along $-\hat k$ when $\vec B$ is along $+\hat j$.
    $\vec E=-\nu\lambda B_0\sin\left(2\pi\nu t-\dfrac{2\pi x}{\lambda}\right)\hat k$.

  4. JEE Main 2026, 5 April, Shift 1 · Question 37
    A displacement current of $4.0$ A can be set up in the space between two parallel plates of $6\ \mu$F capacitor. The rate of change of potential difference across the plates of the capacitor is nearly $\alpha \times 10^6$ V/s. The value of $\alpha$ is _______.

    1. $0.58$
    2. $0.67$
    3. $0.82$
    4. $0.75$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    Displacement current $I_d=\epsilon_0\dfrac{d\Phi_E}{dt}=C\dfrac{dV}{dt}$.
    $\dfrac{dV}{dt}=\dfrac{4.0}{6\times10^{-6}}\approx0.67\times10^6$ V/s, so $\alpha=0.67$.

  5. JEE Main 2026, 5 April, Shift 2 · Question 39
    Given below are two statements : one is labelled as Assertion (A) and the other is labelled as Reason (R).
    Assertion (A) : The electromagnetic wave exerts pressure on the surface on which they are allowed to fall.
    Reason (R) : There is no mass associated with the electromagnetic waves.
    In the light of the above statements, choose the correct answer from the options given below :

    1. Both (A) and (R) are true and (R) is the correct explanation of (A)
    2. Both (A) and (R) are true but (R) is not the correct explanation of (A)
    3. (A) is true but (R) is false
    4. (A) is false but (R) is true
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    EM waves carry momentum $p=\dfrac{U}{c}$; when they fall on a surface, momentum is transferred, giving radiation pressure. (A) is true.
    EM waves (photons) have no rest mass, so (R) is true.
    But the pressure arises from the momentum carried by the wave, not from the absence of mass, so (R) does not explain (A).

  6. JEE Main 2026, 6 April, Shift 1 · Question 40
    A point light source emits E.M. waves in free space. A detector, placed at a distance of $L$ m, measures the intensity as $I_0$. The detector is now shifted to another location on the same spherical surface ensuring the angle between original location and new location as $45^\circ$. The measured intensity at new location will be ________.

    1. $\dfrac{I_0}{4}$
    2. $I_0$
    3. $\dfrac{I_0}{\sqrt2}$
    4. $\dfrac{I_0}{2}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    A point source radiates uniformly in all directions: $I=\dfrac{P}{4\pi L^2}$ depends only on the distance.
    The new position is on the same sphere (same $L$), so the intensity is still $I_0$.

  7. JEE Main 2026, 6 April, Shift 2 · Question 31
    For an electromagnetic wave propagating through vacuum, $\vec k$, $\vec E$ and $\omega$ represent propagation vector, electric field and angular frequency, respectively. The magnetic field associated with this wave is represented by :

    1. $\dfrac{\vec E\times\vec k}{\omega}$
    2. $\dfrac{\vec k\times\vec E}{\omega}$
    3. $\omega\left(\vec E\times\vec k\right)$
    4. $\omega\left(\vec k\times\vec E\right)$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    $\vec E$, $\vec B$, $\vec k$ form a right-handed set with $\vec E\times\vec B$ along $\vec k$, so $\vec B$ is along $\vec k\times\vec E$.
    Magnitude: $B=\dfrac Ec=\dfrac{kE}{\omega}$ since $c=\dfrac\omega k$.
    Hence $\vec B=\dfrac{\vec k\times\vec E}{\omega}$.

  8. JEE Main 2026, 21 January, Shift 1 · Question 31
    The electric field in a plane electromagnetic wave is given by :
    $E_y=69\sin[0.6\times10^3x-1.8\times10^{11}t]$ V/m.
    The expression for magnetic field associated with this electromagnetic wave is __________ T.

    1. $B_y=69\sin[0.6\times10^3x+1.8\times10^{11}t]$
    2. $B_y=2.3\times10^{-7}\sin[0.6\times10^3x-1.8\times10^{11}t]$
    3. $B_z=2.3\times10^{-7}\sin[0.6\times10^3x-1.8\times10^{11}t]$
    4. $B_z=2.3\times10^{-7}\sin[0.6\times10^3x+1.8\times10^{11}t]$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    The phase $kx-\omega t$ shows travel along $+x$ (and $\omega/k=3\times10^8$ m/s).
    $\vec E$ is along $y$, so $\vec B$ must be along $z$ (so that $\vec E\times\vec B$ points along $+x$), in phase with $\vec E$.
    $B_0=\dfrac{E_0}{c}=\dfrac{69}{3\times10^8}=2.3\times10^{-7}$ T, giving $B_z=2.3\times10^{-7}\sin[0.6\times10^3x-1.8\times10^{11}t]$.

  9. JEE Main 2026, 21 January, Shift 2 · Question 48
    An electromagnetic wave of frequency 100 MHz propagates through a medium of conductivity, $\sigma=10$ mho/m. The ratio of maximum conduction current density to maximum displacement current density is __________.
    $\left[\text{Take }\dfrac{1}{4\pi\epsilon_o}=9\times10^9\ \mathrm{Nm^2/C^2}\right]$

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 1800

    Conduction current density $\sigma E_0$; displacement current density $\epsilon_0\omega E_0$ (taking the permittivity as $\epsilon_0$).
    Ratio $=\dfrac{\sigma}{\omega\epsilon_0}=\dfrac{10\times4\pi\times9\times10^9}{2\pi\times10^8}=1800$.

  10. JEE Main 2026, 22 January, Shift 1 · Question 49
    The electric field of a plane electromagnetic wave, travelling in an unknown non-magnetic medium is given by,
    $E_y=20\sin(3\times10^6x-4.5\times10^{14}t)$ V/m
    (where $x$, t and other values have S.I. units). The dielectric constant of the medium is __________
    (speed of light in free space is $3\times10^8$ m/s)

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 4

    Speed in the medium $v=\dfrac{\omega}{k}=\dfrac{4.5\times10^{14}}{3\times10^6}=1.5\times10^8$ m/s.
    Refractive index $n=\dfrac{c}{v}=2$; for a non-magnetic medium the dielectric constant is $n^2=4$.

  11. JEE Main 2026, 22 January, Shift 2 · Question 39
    A laser beam has intensity of $4.0\times10^{14}$ W/m$^2$. The amplitude of magnetic field associated with beam is ___________T. (Take $\epsilon_0=8.85\times10^{-12}$ C$^2$/Nm$^2$ and $c=3\times10^8$ m/s)

    1. $5.5$
    2. $18.3$
    3. $1.83$
    4. $2.0$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    $I=\dfrac{cB_0^2}{2\mu_0}$ with $\mu_0=\dfrac{1}{\epsilon_0c^2}$, so $B_0^2=\dfrac{2I}{\epsilon_0c^3}$.
    $B_0^2=\dfrac{8.0\times10^{14}}{8.85\times10^{-12}\times2.7\times10^{25}}\approx3.35\Rightarrow B_0\approx1.83$ T.

  12. JEE Main 2026, 23 January, Shift 1 · Question 38
    Match List - I with List - II.

    List - I
    Relation    		List - II
    Law
    A. $\displaystyle\oint\vec E\cdot\vec{dl}=-\frac{d}{dt}\oint\vec B\cdot\vec{da}$I. Ampere's circuital law
    B. $\displaystyle\oint\vec B\cdot\vec{dl}=\mu_0\left(I+\epsilon_0\frac{d\phi_E}{dt}\right)$II. Faraday's laws of electromagnetic induction
    C. $\displaystyle\oint\vec E\cdot\vec{da}=\frac1{\epsilon_0}\int_v\rho\,dv$III. Ampere - Maxwell law
    D. $\displaystyle\oint\vec B\cdot\vec{dl}=\mu_0I$IV. Gauss's law of electrostatics
    Choose the correct answer from the options given below :

    1. A-II, B-III, C-I, D-IV
    2. A-II, B-III, C-IV, D-I
    3. A-I, B-IV, C-III, D-II
    4. A-IV, B-I, C-II, D-III
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    A: induced EMF equals minus the rate of change of magnetic flux: Faraday (II).
    B: conduction plus displacement current: Ampere-Maxwell (III).
    C: electric flux through a closed surface equals enclosed charge over $\epsilon_0$: Gauss (IV).
    D: Ampere's circuital law (I).

  13. JEE Main 2026, 23 January, Shift 1 · Question 48
    The equation of the electric field of an electromagnetic wave propagating through free space is given by : $E=\sqrt{377}\sin\left(6.27\times10^3t-2.09\times10^{-5}x\right)$ N/C
    The average power of the electromagnetic wave is $\left(\dfrac1\alpha\right)$ W/m$^2$. The value of $\alpha$ is __________
    $\left(\text{Take }\sqrt{\dfrac{\mu_0}{\varepsilon_0}}=377\text{ in SI units}\right)$

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 2

    Average intensity $I=\dfrac12c\varepsilon_0E_0^2=\dfrac{E_0^2}{2\sqrt{\mu_0/\varepsilon_0}}$.
    $I=\dfrac{377}{2\times377}=\dfrac12$ W/m$^2$, so $\alpha=2$.

  14. JEE Main 2026, 23 January, Shift 2 · Question 41
    The ratio of speeds of electromagnetic waves in vacuum and a medium, having dielectric constant $k=3$ and permeability of $\mu=2\mu_0$, is
    ($\mu_0=$ permeability of vacuum)

    1. $3:2$
    2. $6:1$
    3. $36:1$
    4. $\sqrt6:1$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    $v=\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{\mu\varepsilon}}=\dfrac{c}{\sqrt{\mu_r\varepsilon_r}}$.
    $\dfrac cv=\sqrt{2\times3}=\sqrt6$, so the ratio is $\sqrt6:1$.

  16. JEE Main 2026, 28 January, Shift 1 · Question 41
    The electric field of an electromagnetic wave travelling through a medium is given by $\vec E(x,t)=25\sin\left(2.0\times10^{15}t-10^7x\right)\hat n$ then the refractive index of the medium is _________.
    (All given measurement are in SI units)

    1. $1.5$
    2. $1.2$
    3. $2$
    4. $1.7$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    Speed in the medium $v=\dfrac{\omega}{k}=\dfrac{2.0\times10^{15}}{10^7}=2\times10^8$ m/s.
    $n=\dfrac cv=\dfrac{3\times10^8}{2\times10^8}=1.5$.

  17. JEE Main 2026, 28 January, Shift 2 · Question 40
    A plane electromagnetic wave is moving in free space with velocity $c=3\times10^8$ m/s and its electric field is given as $\vec E=54\sin(kz-\omega t)\,\hat j$ V/m, where $\hat j$ is the unit vector along $y$-axis. The magnetic field vector $\vec B$ of the wave is :

    1. $-1.8\times10^{-7}\sin(kz-\omega t)\,\hat i$ T
    2. $1.4\times10^{-7}\sin(kz-\omega t)\,\hat k$ T
    3. $+1.8\times10^{-7}\sin(kz-\omega t)\,\hat i$ T
    4. $1.4\times10^{-7}\sin(kz-\omega t)\,\hat i$ T
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    $B_0=\dfrac{E_0}{c}=\dfrac{54}{3\times10^8}=1.8\times10^{-7}$ T.
    The disturbance travels along $+z$ and $\vec E\times\vec B$ must point along $+z$: $\hat j\times(-\hat i)=\hat k$, so $\vec B$ is along $-\hat i$.
    $\vec B=-1.8\times10^{-7}\sin(kz-\omega t)\,\hat i$ T.
