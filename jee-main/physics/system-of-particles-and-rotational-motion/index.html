  1. JEE Main 2026, 2 April, Shift 1 · Question 31
    A particle is rotating in a circular path and at any instant its motion can be described as $\theta = \dfrac{5t^4}{40} - \dfrac{t^3}{3}$. The angular acceleration of the particle after 10 seconds is ________ $\mathrm{rad/s^2}$.

    1. $150$
    2. $120$
    3. $130$
    4. $170$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    $\omega = \dfrac{d\theta}{dt} = \dfrac{t^3}{2} - t^2$.
    $\alpha = \dfrac{d\omega}{dt} = \dfrac{3t^2}{2} - 2t$.
    At $t=10$ s: $\alpha = 150 - 20 = 130\ \mathrm{rad/s^2}$.

  2. JEE Main 2026, 2 April, Shift 2 · Question 28
    Two blocks of masses 2 kg and 1 kg respectively, are tied to the ends of a string which passes over a light frictionless pulley as shown in the figure below. The masses are held at rest at the same horizontal level and then released. The distance traversed by the centre of mass in 2 s is __________ m. (Take $g=10$ m/s$^2$)

    1. $3.33$
    2. $3.12$
    3. $2.22$
    4. $1.42$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    Acceleration of each block: $a=\dfrac{(2-1)g}{2+1}=\dfrac{10}{3}$ m/s$^2$ (2 kg down, 1 kg up).
    $a_{cm}=\dfrac{2a-1\cdot a}{3}=\dfrac a3=\dfrac{10}{9}$ m/s$^2$ (downward), starting from rest.
    Distance $=\dfrac12\cdot\dfrac{10}{9}\cdot2^2=\dfrac{20}{9}\approx2.22$ m.

  3. JEE Main 2026, 2 April, Shift 2 · Question 50
    Moment of inertia about an axis $AB$ for a rod of mass 40 kg and length 3 m is same as that of a solid sphere of mass of 10 kg and radius $R$ about an axis parallel to $AB$ axis with separation of 3 m as shown in figure below. The value of $R$ is given as $\sqrt{\dfrac{\alpha}{2}}$. The value of $\alpha$ is __________.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 60

    The rod rotates about AB through its end: $I_{rod}=\dfrac{ML^2}{3}=\dfrac{40\times9}{3}=120$ kg m$^2$.
    As drawn, the sphere's axis is its own diameter (the dotted line through its centre, parallel to AB and 3 m from it): $I_{sphere}=\dfrac25\times10\times R^2=4R^2$.
    $4R^2=120 \Rightarrow R^2=30=\dfrac{60}{2}$.
    $\alpha=60$.

  4. JEE Main 2026, 4 April, Shift 1 · Question 30
    A solid sphere of mass $M$ and radius $R$ is divided into two unequal parts. The smaller part having mass $M/8$ is converted into a sphere of radius $r$ and the larger part is converted into a circular disc of thickness $t$ and radius $2R$. If $I_1$ is moment of inertia of a sphere having radius $r$ about an axis through its centre and $I_2$ is the moment of inertia of a disc about its diameter, the ratio of their moment of inertia $I_2/I_1=$ _____.

    1. $35$
    2. $70$
    3. $140$
    4. $210$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    Same density: mass $\dfrac M8$ means volume $\dfrac18$, so $r=\dfrac R2$.
    $I_1=\dfrac25\cdot\dfrac M8\cdot\dfrac{R^2}{4}=\dfrac{MR^2}{80}$.
    Disc of mass $\dfrac{7M}{8}$, radius $2R$ (thin), about a diameter: $I_2=\dfrac14\cdot\dfrac{7M}{8}(2R)^2=\dfrac{7MR^2}{8}$.
    $\dfrac{I_2}{I_1}=\dfrac78\times80=70$.

  5. JEE Main 2026, 5 April, Shift 2 · Question 30
    A wheel initially at rest is subjected to a uniform angular acceleration about its axis. In the first 2 s it rotates through an angle $\theta_1$ and in the next 2 s it rotates through an angle $\theta_2$. The ratio $\dfrac{\theta_2}{\theta_1}$ is __________.

    1. $6$
    2. $3$
    3. $4$
    4. $\dfrac13$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    From rest, $\theta=\dfrac12\alpha t^2$.
    $\theta_1=\dfrac12\alpha(2)^2=2\alpha$; $\theta_1+\theta_2=\dfrac12\alpha(4)^2=8\alpha \Rightarrow \theta_2=6\alpha$.
    $\dfrac{\theta_2}{\theta_1}=3$.

  6. JEE Main 2026, 5 April, Shift 2 · Question 31
    An object of uniform density rolls up the curved path with the initial velocity $v_o$ as shown in the figure. If the maximum height attained by an object is $\dfrac{7v_o^2}{10\,g}$ ($g$ = acceleration due to gravity), the object is a __________.

    1. solid cylinder
    2. ring
    3. disc
    4. solid sphere
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    Rolling without slipping: total KE $=\dfrac12mv_o^2\left(1+\dfrac{k^2}{R^2}\right)$, all converted to $mgh$.
    $h=\dfrac{v_o^2}{2g}\left(1+\dfrac{k^2}{R^2}\right)=\dfrac{7v_o^2}{10g} \Rightarrow 1+\dfrac{k^2}{R^2}=\dfrac75 \Rightarrow \dfrac{k^2}{R^2}=\dfrac25$.
    $k^2=\dfrac25R^2$ is a solid sphere.

  7. JEE Main 2026, 6 April, Shift 1 · Question 31
    A solid sphere of radius 4 cm and mass 5 kg is rotating (rotation axis is passing through the centre of the sphere) with an angular velocity of 1200 rpm. It is brought to rest in 10 s by applying a constant torque. The torque applied and the number of rotations it made before it comes to rest are _____ and ________ respectively.

    1. $0.128\,\pi$ Nm, $100$
    2. $0.0128\,\pi$ Nm, $50$
    3. $0.128\,\pi$ Nm, $50$
    4. $0.0128\,\pi$ Nm, $100$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    $I=\dfrac25MR^2=\dfrac25\times5\times(0.04)^2=0.0032$ kg m$^2$.
    $\omega_0=1200$ rpm $=40\pi$ rad/s, so $\alpha=\dfrac{40\pi}{10}=4\pi$ rad/s$^2$.
    $\tau=I\alpha=0.0128\,\pi$ Nm.
    $\theta=\dfrac{\omega_0t}{2}=200\pi$ rad $=100$ rotations.

  8. JEE Main 2026, 6 April, Shift 1 · Question 33
    The position of center of mass of three masses 2 kg, 3 kg and 15 kg placed with respect to mid point ($p$) of normal bisector, as shown in the figure is ________.

    1. $\left(\dfrac{\sqrt3}{4},1.25\right)$
    2. $\left(\dfrac{\sqrt3}{4},1.0\right)$
    3. $(0,0)$
    4. $(1.25,0)$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    Half base $=10\sin60^\circ=5\sqrt3$ m, height $=10\cos60^\circ=5$ m, so $p$ is $2.5$ m above the base.
    With $p$ as origin: 2 kg at $(-5\sqrt3,-2.5)$, 3 kg at $(5\sqrt3,-2.5)$, 15 kg at $(0,2.5)$.
    $x_{cm}=\dfrac{-10\sqrt3+15\sqrt3}{20}=\dfrac{\sqrt3}{4}$.
    $y_{cm}=\dfrac{-5-7.5+37.5}{20}=1.25$.

  9. JEE Main 2026, 6 April, Shift 2 · Question 28
    A solid sphere $(A)$ of mass $5m$ and a spherical shell $(B)$ of mass $m$, both having same radius, are placed on a rough surface. When a force of same magnitude is applied tangentially at the highest points of $A$ and $B$, they start rolling without slipping with an acceleration of $a_A$ and $a_B$, respectively. The ratio of $a_A$ and $a_B$ is __________.

    1. $5 : 21$
    2. $6 : 10$
    3. $21 : 25$
    4. $1 : 5$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    Let $I=kMR^2$ and friction $f$ act forwards: $F+f=Ma$, $(F-f)R=kMR^2\cdot\dfrac aR$.
    Adding: $2F=Ma(1+k) \Rightarrow a=\dfrac{2F}{M(1+k)}$.
    Solid sphere ($M=5m$, $k=\tfrac25$): $a_A=\dfrac{2F}{7m}$.
    Shell ($M=m$, $k=\tfrac23$): $a_B=\dfrac{6F}{5m}$.
    $\dfrac{a_A}{a_B}=\dfrac{2}{7}\cdot\dfrac56=\dfrac{5}{21}$.

  10. JEE Main 2026, 6 April, Shift 2 · Question 32
    Two identical bodies A and B of equal masses have initial velocities $\vec v_1=4\hat i$ m/s and $\vec v_2=4\hat j$ m/s respectively. The body A has acceleration $\vec a_1=6\hat i+6\hat j$ m/s$^2$ while the acceleration of the other body B is zero. The centre of mass of the two bodies moves in __________ path.

    1. circular
    2. parabolic
    3. straight line
    4. elliptical
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    $\vec v_{cm}=\dfrac{\vec v_1+\vec v_2}{2}=2\hat i+2\hat j$, $\vec a_{cm}=\dfrac{\vec a_1+\vec 0}{2}=3\hat i+3\hat j$.
    The acceleration is parallel to the initial velocity, so the centre of mass moves in a straight line.

  11. JEE Main 2026, 8 April, Shift 2 · Question 32
    A solid cylinder having radius $R$ and length $L$ is slipping on a rough horizontal plane. At time $t=0$ the cylinder has a translational velocity $v_o=49$ m/s, perpendicular to its axis and a rotational velocity $v_o/4R$ about the centre. The time taken by the cylinder to start rolling is __________ seconds. (coefficient of kinetic friction $\mu_K=0.25$ and $g=9.8\ \text{m/s}^2$)

    1. $15$
    2. $5$
    3. $10$
    4. $7.5$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    Since $v_o>\omega_oR$, kinetic friction $\mu_Kmg$ acts backwards: $v=v_o-\mu_Kgt$.
    Its torque speeds up the rotation: $\alpha=\dfrac{\mu_KmgR}{\frac12mR^2}=\dfrac{2\mu_Kg}{R}$, so $\omega R=\dfrac{v_o}{4}+2\mu_Kgt$.
    Rolling starts when $v=\omega R$: $v_o-\dfrac{v_o}4=3\mu_Kgt\Rightarrow t=\dfrac{v_o}{4\mu_Kg}=\dfrac{49}{4\times0.25\times9.8}=5$ s.
