  1. JEE Main 2026, 2 April, Shift 2 · Question 42
    The figure given below shows an $LCR$ series circuit with two switches S$_1$ and S$_2$. When switch S$_1$ is closed keeping S$_2$ open, the phase difference $(\phi)$ between the current and source voltage is $30^\circ$ and phase difference is $60^\circ$ when S$_2$ is closed keeping S$_1$ open. The value of $(3L_1-L_2)$ is __________ H.

    1. $\dfrac92$
    2. $\dfrac29$
    3. $\dfrac13$
    4. $3$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    Closing S$_1$ shorts $L_2$: series $C, R, L_1$. Closing S$_2$ shorts $L_1$: series $C, R, L_2$.
    $X_C=\dfrac{1}{\omega C}=\dfrac{1}{300\times10^{-4}}=\dfrac{100}{3}\ \Omega$.
    $\tan30^\circ=\dfrac{\omega L_1-X_C}{R}$, $\tan60^\circ=\dfrac{\omega L_2-X_C}{R}$, so $\omega L_2-X_C=3(\omega L_1-X_C)$.
    $\omega(3L_1-L_2)=2X_C \Rightarrow 3L_1-L_2=\dfrac{2\times100/3}{300}=\dfrac29$ H.

  2. JEE Main 2026, 4 April, Shift 1 · Question 50
    An inductor of $10$ mH, capacitor of $0.1\ \mu$F and a resistor of $100\ \Omega$ are connected in series across an a.c power supply $220$ V, $70$ Hz. The power factor of the given circuit is $0.5$. The difference in the inductive reactance and capacitance reactance is $\sqrt3\,\alpha\ \Omega$. The value of $\alpha$ is ________.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 100

    $\cos\phi=0.5 \Rightarrow \phi=60^\circ$, and $\tan\phi=\dfrac{|X_L-X_C|}{R}=\sqrt3$.
    $|X_L-X_C|=\sqrt3\times100\ \Omega$, so $\alpha=100$.

  3. JEE Main 2026, 5 April, Shift 1 · Question 44
    An a.c. source of angular frequency $\omega$ is connected across a resistor $R$ and a capacitor $C$ in series. The current is observed as $I$. Now the frequency of the source is changed to $\omega/4$, (keeping the voltage unchanged) the current is found to be $I/3$. The ratio of resistance to reactance at frequency $\omega$ is

    1. $\sqrt{\dfrac{6}{7}}$
    2. $\sqrt{\dfrac{3}{5}}$
    3. $\sqrt{\dfrac{7}{8}}$
    4. $\sqrt{\dfrac{3}{4}}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    At $\omega$: $I=\dfrac{V}{\sqrt{R^2+X^2}}$. At $\omega/4$ the capacitive reactance becomes $4X$: $\dfrac I3=\dfrac{V}{\sqrt{R^2+16X^2}}$.
    So $R^2+16X^2=9(R^2+X^2) \Rightarrow 7X^2=8R^2$.
    $\dfrac RX=\sqrt{\dfrac78}$.

  4. JEE Main 2026, 5 April, Shift 2 · Question 48
    A series LCR circuit with $R=20\ \Omega$, $L=1.6$ H and $C=40\ \mu$F is connected to a variable frequency a.c. source. The inductive reactance at resonant frequency is __________ $\Omega$.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 200

    $\omega_0=\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{LC}}=\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{1.6\times40\times10^{-6}}}=\dfrac{1}{8\times10^{-3}}=125$ rad/s.
    $X_L=\omega_0L=125\times1.6=200\ \Omega$.

  5. JEE Main 2026, 6 April, Shift 1 · Question 45
    A LCR series circuit driven with $E_{rms}=90$ V at frequency $f_d=30$ Hz has resistance $R=80\ \Omega$, an inductance with inductive reactance $X_L=20.0\ \Omega$ and capacitance with capacitive reactance $X_C=80.0\ \Omega$. The power factor of the circuit is ______.

    1. $0.8$
    2. $0.64$
    3. $0.9$
    4. $0.5$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    $Z=\sqrt{R^2+(X_C-X_L)^2}=\sqrt{80^2+60^2}=100\ \Omega$.
    Power factor $=\dfrac RZ=0.8$.

  6. JEE Main 2026, 21 January, Shift 2 · Question 36
    A capacitor $C$ is first charged fully with potential difference of $V_0$ and disconnected from the battery. The charged capacitor is connected across an inductor having inductance $L$. In $t$ s 25% of the initial energy in the capacitor is transferred to the inductor. The value of $t$ is _________s.

    1. $\pi\sqrt{\dfrac{LC}{2}}$
    2. $\dfrac{\pi\sqrt{LC}}{2}$
    3. $\dfrac{\pi\sqrt{LC}}{3}$
    4. $\dfrac{\pi\sqrt{LC}}{6}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    $q=Q\cos\omega t$ with $\omega=\dfrac1{\sqrt{LC}}$, so the capacitor energy is $U_0\cos^2\omega t$.
    25% transferred means $\cos^2\omega t=\dfrac34$, i.e. $\omega t=\dfrac\pi6$ (first time).
    $t=\dfrac{\pi\sqrt{LC}}{6}$.

  7. JEE Main 2026, 23 January, Shift 1 · Question 46
    Using a variable frequency a.c. voltage source the maximum current measured in the given $LCR$ circuit is 50 mA for $V=5\sin(100t)$ The values of $L$ and $R$ are shown in the figure. The capacitance of the capacitor (C) used is __________ $\mu$F.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 50

    The current is largest at resonance, where $I_0=\dfrac{V_0}{R}=\dfrac{5}{100}=50$ mA, as measured.
    So $\omega=100$ rad/s is the resonant frequency: $\omega^2=\dfrac1{LC}$.
    $C=\dfrac{1}{(100)^2\times2}=5\times10^{-5}$ F $=50\ \mu$F.

  8. JEE Main 2026, 24 January, Shift 1 · Question 37
    For the series $LCR$ circuit connected with 220 V, 50 Hz a.c source as shown in the figure, the power factor is $\dfrac{\alpha}{10}$. The value of $\alpha$ is __________.

    1. $4$
    2. $6$
    3. $8$
    4. $10$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    $Z=\sqrt{R^2+(X_C-X_L)^2}=\sqrt{60^2+80^2}=100\ \Omega$.
    Power factor $=\dfrac RZ=0.6=\dfrac{6}{10}$, so $\alpha=6$.

  9. JEE Main 2026, 28 January, Shift 1 · Question 38
    The electric current in the circuit is given as $i=i_0(t/T)$. The r.m.s current for the period $t=0$ to $t=T$ is_______.

    1. $i_0$
    2. $\dfrac{i_0}{\sqrt2}$
    3. $\dfrac{i_0}{\sqrt3}$
    4. $\dfrac{i_0}{\sqrt6}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    $i_{\text{rms}}^2=\dfrac1T\displaystyle\int_0^T i_0^2\dfrac{t^2}{T^2}\,dt=\dfrac{i_0^2}{3}$.
    $i_{\text{rms}}=\dfrac{i_0}{\sqrt3}$.

  10. JEE Main 2026, 28 January, Shift 2 · Question 48
    An inductor stores 16 J of magnetic field energy and dissipates 32 W of thermal energy due to its resistance when an a.c. current of 2 A (rms) and frequency 50 Hz flows through it. The ratio of inductive reactance to its resistance is __________. $(\pi=3.14)$

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 314

    Resistance: $I_{\text{rms}}^2R=32$ gives $R=8\ \Omega$.
    Energy: $\tfrac12LI^2=16$ with $I=2$ A gives $L=8$ H.
    $X_L=2\pi fL=2(3.14)(50)(8)=2512\ \Omega$, so $\dfrac{X_L}{R}=314$.
