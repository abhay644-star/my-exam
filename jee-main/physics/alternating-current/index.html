  1. JEE Main 2026, 2 April, Shift 2 · Question 42
    The figure given below shows an $LCR$ series circuit with two switches S$_1$ and S$_2$. When switch S$_1$ is closed keeping S$_2$ open, the phase difference $(\phi)$ between the current and source voltage is $30^\circ$ and phase difference is $60^\circ$ when S$_2$ is closed keeping S$_1$ open. The value of $(3L_1-L_2)$ is __________ H.

    1. $\dfrac92$
    2. $\dfrac29$
    3. $\dfrac13$
    4. $3$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    Closing S$_1$ shorts $L_2$: series $C, R, L_1$. Closing S$_2$ shorts $L_1$: series $C, R, L_2$.
    $X_C=\dfrac{1}{\omega C}=\dfrac{1}{300\times10^{-4}}=\dfrac{100}{3}\ \Omega$.
    $\tan30^\circ=\dfrac{\omega L_1-X_C}{R}$, $\tan60^\circ=\dfrac{\omega L_2-X_C}{R}$, so $\omega L_2-X_C=3(\omega L_1-X_C)$.
    $\omega(3L_1-L_2)=2X_C \Rightarrow 3L_1-L_2=\dfrac{2\times100/3}{300}=\dfrac29$ H.

  2. JEE Main 2026, 4 April, Shift 1 · Question 50
    An inductor of $10$ mH, capacitor of $0.1\ \mu$F and a resistor of $100\ \Omega$ are connected in series across an a.c power supply $220$ V, $70$ Hz. The power factor of the given circuit is $0.5$. The difference in the inductive reactance and capacitance reactance is $\sqrt3\,\alpha\ \Omega$. The value of $\alpha$ is ________.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 100

    $\cos\phi=0.5 \Rightarrow \phi=60^\circ$, and $\tan\phi=\dfrac{|X_L-X_C|}{R}=\sqrt3$.
    $|X_L-X_C|=\sqrt3\times100\ \Omega$, so $\alpha=100$.

  3. JEE Main 2026, 5 April, Shift 1 · Question 44
    An a.c. source of angular frequency $\omega$ is connected across a resistor $R$ and a capacitor $C$ in series. The current is observed as $I$. Now the frequency of the source is changed to $\omega/4$, (keeping the voltage unchanged) the current is found to be $I/3$. The ratio of resistance to reactance at frequency $\omega$ is

    1. $\sqrt{\dfrac{6}{7}}$
    2. $\sqrt{\dfrac{3}{5}}$
    3. $\sqrt{\dfrac{7}{8}}$
    4. $\sqrt{\dfrac{3}{4}}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    At $\omega$: $I=\dfrac{V}{\sqrt{R^2+X^2}}$. At $\omega/4$ the capacitive reactance becomes $4X$: $\dfrac I3=\dfrac{V}{\sqrt{R^2+16X^2}}$.
    So $R^2+16X^2=9(R^2+X^2) \Rightarrow 7X^2=8R^2$.
    $\dfrac RX=\sqrt{\dfrac78}$.

  4. JEE Main 2026, 5 April, Shift 2 · Question 48
    A series LCR circuit with $R=20\ \Omega$, $L=1.6$ H and $C=40\ \mu$F is connected to a variable frequency a.c. source. The inductive reactance at resonant frequency is __________ $\Omega$.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 200

    $\omega_0=\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{LC}}=\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{1.6\times40\times10^{-6}}}=\dfrac{1}{8\times10^{-3}}=125$ rad/s.
    $X_L=\omega_0L=125\times1.6=200\ \Omega$.

  5. JEE Main 2026, 6 April, Shift 1 · Question 45
    A LCR series circuit driven with $E_{rms}=90$ V at frequency $f_d=30$ Hz has resistance $R=80\ \Omega$, an inductance with inductive reactance $X_L=20.0\ \Omega$ and capacitance with capacitive reactance $X_C=80.0\ \Omega$. The power factor of the circuit is ______.

    1. $0.8$
    2. $0.64$
    3. $0.9$
    4. $0.5$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    $Z=\sqrt{R^2+(X_C-X_L)^2}=\sqrt{80^2+60^2}=100\ \Omega$.
    Power factor $=\dfrac RZ=0.8$.

  6. JEE Main 2026, 21 January, Shift 2 · Question 36
    A capacitor $C$ is first charged fully with potential difference of $V_0$ and disconnected from the battery. The charged capacitor is connected across an inductor having inductance $L$. In $t$ s 25% of the initial energy in the capacitor is transferred to the inductor. The value of $t$ is _________s.

    1. $\pi\sqrt{\dfrac{LC}{2}}$
    2. $\dfrac{\pi\sqrt{LC}}{2}$
    3. $\dfrac{\pi\sqrt{LC}}{3}$
    4. $\dfrac{\pi\sqrt{LC}}{6}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    $q=Q\cos\omega t$ with $\omega=\dfrac1{\sqrt{LC}}$, so the capacitor energy is $U_0\cos^2\omega t$.
    25% transferred means $\cos^2\omega t=\dfrac34$, i.e. $\omega t=\dfrac\pi6$ (first time).
    $t=\dfrac{\pi\sqrt{LC}}{6}$.

  7. JEE Main 2026, 23 January, Shift 1 · Question 46
    Using a variable frequency a.c. voltage source the maximum current measured in the given $LCR$ circuit is 50 mA for $V=5\sin(100t)$ The values of $L$ and $R$ are shown in the figure. The capacitance of the capacitor (C) used is __________ $\mu$F.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 50

    The current is largest at resonance, where $I_0=\dfrac{V_0}{R}=\dfrac{5}{100}=50$ mA, as measured.
    So $\omega=100$ rad/s is the resonant frequency: $\omega^2=\dfrac1{LC}$.
    $C=\dfrac{1}{(100)^2\times2}=5\times10^{-5}$ F $=50\ \mu$F.

  8. JEE Main 2026, 24 January, Shift 1 · Question 37
    For the series $LCR$ circuit connected with 220 V, 50 Hz a.c source as shown in the figure, the power factor is $\dfrac{\alpha}{10}$. The value of $\alpha$ is __________.

    1. $4$
    2. $6$
    3. $8$
    4. $10$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    $Z=\sqrt{R^2+(X_C-X_L)^2}=\sqrt{60^2+80^2}=100\ \Omega$.
    Power factor $=\dfrac RZ=0.6=\dfrac{6}{10}$, so $\alpha=6$.

  9. JEE Main 2026, 28 January, Shift 1 · Question 38
    The electric current in the circuit is given as $i=i_0(t/T)$. The r.m.s current for the period $t=0$ to $t=T$ is_______.

    1. $i_0$
    2. $\dfrac{i_0}{\sqrt2}$
    3. $\dfrac{i_0}{\sqrt3}$
    4. $\dfrac{i_0}{\sqrt6}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    $i_{\text{rms}}^2=\dfrac1T\displaystyle\int_0^T i_0^2\dfrac{t^2}{T^2}\,dt=\dfrac{i_0^2}{3}$.
    $i_{\text{rms}}=\dfrac{i_0}{\sqrt3}$.

  10. JEE Main 2026, 28 January, Shift 2 · Question 48
    An inductor stores 16 J of magnetic field energy and dissipates 32 W of thermal energy due to its resistance when an a.c. current of 2 A (rms) and frequency 50 Hz flows through it. The ratio of inductive reactance to its resistance is __________. $(\pi=3.14)$

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 314

    Resistance: $I_{\text{rms}}^2R=32$ gives $R=8\ \Omega$.
    Energy: $\tfrac12LI^2=16$ with $I=2$ A gives $L=8$ H.
    $X_L=2\pi fL=2(3.14)(50)(8)=2512\ \Omega$, so $\dfrac{X_L}{R}=314$.

  11. JEE Main 2025, 3 April, Shift 2 · Question 37
    An electric bulb rated as 100W-220V is connected to an ac source of rms voltage 220V. The peak value of current through the bulb is :

    1. 0.45 A
    2. 0.32 A
    3. 0.64 A
    4. 2.2 A
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    $I_{rms}=\dfrac{P}{V_{rms}}=\dfrac{100}{220}=0.455$ A.
    $I_0=\sqrt2I_{rms}\approx0.64$ A.

  12. JEE Main 2025, 4 April, Shift 1 · Question 36
    An alternating current is represented by the equation, $i=100\sqrt2\sin(100\pi t)$ ampere. The RMS value of current and the frequency of the given alternating current are

    1. $50\sqrt2$ A, 50 Hz
    2. $100\sqrt2$ A, 100 Hz
    3. $\dfrac{100}{\sqrt2}$ A, 100 Hz
    4. 100 A, 50 Hz
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    $I_{rms}=\dfrac{I_0}{\sqrt2}=\dfrac{100\sqrt2}{\sqrt2}=100$ A.
    $\omega=100\pi=2\pi f$ gives $f=50$ Hz.

  13. JEE Main 2025, 4 April, Shift 2 · Question 48
    An inductor of self inductance 1 H is connected in series with a resistor of $100\pi$ ohm and an ac supply of $100\pi$ volt, 50 Hz. Maximum current flowing in the circuit is __________ A.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 1

    $X_L=2\pi fL=100\pi\ \Omega$, so $Z=\sqrt{(100\pi)^2+(100\pi)^2}=100\sqrt2\,\pi\ \Omega$.
    $I_{rms}=\dfrac{100\pi}{100\sqrt2\,\pi}=\dfrac1{\sqrt2}$ A (taking $100\pi$ V as the rms value).
    $I_{max}=\sqrt2\,I_{rms}=1$ A.

  14. JEE Main 2025, 7 April, Shift 1 · Question 35
    An ac current is represented as
    $i=5\sqrt2+10\cos\left(650\pi t+\dfrac{\pi}{6}\right)$ Amp
    The r.m.s value of the current is

    1. $5\sqrt2$ Amp
    2. $10$ Amp
    3. $50$ Amp
    4. $100$ Amp
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    The mean of $i^2$ over a period is $(5\sqrt2)^2+\dfrac{10^2}{2}=50+50=100$ (the cross term averages to zero).
    $i_{rms}=\sqrt{100}=10$ A.

  15. JEE Main 2025, 7 April, Shift 1 · Question 49
    For ac circuit shown in figure, R = 100 k$\Omega$ and C = 100 pF and the phase difference between $V_{in}$ and $(V_B-V_A)$ is $90^\circ$. The input signal frequency is $10^x$ rad/sec, where '$x$' is ______.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 5

    Each branch divides $V_{in}$: $V_A$ is the drop across C in the R-C branch and $V_B$ the drop across R in the C-R branch. With $X=\dfrac1{\omega C}$,
    $V_B-V_A=V_{in}\dfrac{R+jX}{R-jX}$, which has the same magnitude as $V_{in}$ and leads it by $2\tan^{-1}\dfrac XR$.
    A $90^\circ$ difference needs $X=R$: $\omega=\dfrac1{RC}=\dfrac1{10^5\times10^{-10}}=10^5$ rad/s, so $x=5$.

  16. JEE Main 2025, 7 April, Shift 2 · Question 48
    An inductor of reactance 100 $\Omega$, a capacitor of reactance 50 $\Omega$, and a resistor of resistance 50 $\Omega$ are connected in series with an AC source of 10 V, 50 Hz. Average power dissipated by the circuit is ______ W.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 1

    $Z=\sqrt{50^2+(100-50)^2}=50\sqrt2\ \Omega$, $I_{rms}=\dfrac{10}{50\sqrt2}$ A.
    $P=I_{rms}^2R=\dfrac{100}{5000}\times50=1$ W.

  17. JEE Main 2025, 22 January, Shift 2 · Question 39
    A series LCR circuit is connected to an alternating source of emf E. The current amplitude at resonant frequency is $I_0$. If the value of resistance R becomes twice of its initial value then amplitude of current at resonance will be

    1. $I_0$
    2. $\dfrac{I_0}{2}$
    3. $2I_0$
    4. $\dfrac{I_0}{\sqrt{2}}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    At resonance the impedance equals R, so $I_0=\dfrac{E_0}{R}$.
    Doubling R halves the amplitude: $\dfrac{I_0}{2}$.

  18. JEE Main 2025, 23 January, Shift 2 · Question 49
    In a series LCR circuit, a resistor of 300 $\Omega$, a capacitor of 25 nF and an inductor of 100 mH are used. For maximum current in the circuit, the angular frequency of the ac source is __________ $\times10^4$ radians s$^{-1}$.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 2

    Maximum current at resonance: $\omega=\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{LC}}$.
    $LC=0.1\times25\times10^{-9}=2.5\times10^{-9}$ s$^2$, $\sqrt{LC}=5\times10^{-5}$ s.
    $\omega=2\times10^4$ rad s$^{-1}$.

  19. JEE Main 2025, 24 January, Shift 1 · Question 36
    An alternating current is given by $I=I_A\sin\omega t+I_B\cos\omega t$. The r.m.s current will be

    1. $\dfrac{\sqrt{I_A^2+I_B^2}}{2}$
    2. $\sqrt{\dfrac{I_A^2+I_B^2}{2}}$
    3. $\dfrac{|I_A+I_B|}{\sqrt2}$
    4. $\sqrt{I_A^2+I_B^2}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    $I=I_0\sin(\omega t+\phi)$ with $I_0=\sqrt{I_A^2+I_B^2}$.
    $I_{rms}=\dfrac{I_0}{\sqrt2}=\sqrt{\dfrac{I_A^2+I_B^2}{2}}$.

  20. JEE Main 2025, 29 January, Shift 1 · Question 37
    Given below are two statements : one is labelled as <b>Assertion (A)</b> and the other is labelled as <b>Reason (R)</b>.
    <b>Assertion (A) :</b> Choke coil is simply a coil having a large inductance but a small resistance. Choke coils are used with fluorescent mercury-tube fittings. If household electric power is directly connected to a mercury tube, the tube will be damaged.
    <b>Reason (R) :</b> By using the choke coil, the voltage across the tube is reduced by a factor $\left(R/\sqrt{R^2+\omega^2L^2}\right)$, where $\omega$ is frequency of the supply across resistor R and inductor L. If the choke coil were not used, the voltage across the resistor would be the same as the applied voltage.
    In the light of the above statements, choose the <b>most appropriate answer</b> from the options given below :

    1. Both <b>(A)</b> and <b>(R)</b> are true and <b>(R)</b> is the correct explanation of <b>(A)</b>
    2. Both <b>(A)</b> and <b>(R)</b> are true but <b>(R)</b> is <b>not</b> the correct explanation of <b>(A)</b>
    3. <b>(A)</b> is true but <b>(R)</b> is false
    4. <b>(A)</b> is false but <b>(R)</b> is true
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    A: a choke (large L, small R) limits the current through the tube; connected straight to the mains, the tube would draw too much current and be damaged. A is true.
    R: for a series R-L circuit the voltage across R is $V\dfrac{R}{\sqrt{R^2+\omega^2L^2}}$, and without L it would be the full $V$. R is true.
    R explains A: the choke takes up most of the supply voltage, so the tube does not get the full mains voltage that would damage it.
