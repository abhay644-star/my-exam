  1. JEE Main 2026, 2 April, Shift 1 · Question 38
    When a coil is placed in a time dependent magnetic field the power dissipated in it is $P$. The number of turns, area of the coil and radius of the coil wire are $N$, $A$ and $r$ respectively. For a second coils number of turns, area of the coil and radius of the coil wire are $2N$, $2A$ and $3r$ respectively. When the first coil is replaced with second coil the power dissipated in it is $\sqrt{2}\,\alpha P$. The value of $\alpha$ is ______.

    1. $36$
    2. $128\sqrt{2}$
    3. $16$
    4. $64$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    Induced emf $\varepsilon = NA\dfrac{dB}{dt} \propto NA$.
    Wire length $\ell \propto N\sqrt{A}$ (circumference $\propto\sqrt{A}$), so $R = \dfrac{\rho\ell}{\pi r^2} \propto \dfrac{N\sqrt{A}}{r^2}$.
    $P = \dfrac{\varepsilon^2}{R} \propto N A^{3/2} r^2$.
    $\dfrac{P'}{P} = 2\times(2)^{3/2}\times9 = 36\sqrt{2}$, so $\alpha = 36$.

  2. JEE Main 2026, 2 April, Shift 2 · Question 43
    A circular current loop of radius $R$ is placed inside square loop of side length $L$ $(L \gg R)$ such that they are co-planar and their centers coincide. The permeability of free space is $\mu_0$. The mutual inductance between circular loop and square loop is __________.

    1. $2\sqrt2\dfrac{\mu_0L^2}{R}$
    2. $\sqrt2\dfrac{\mu_0L^2}{R}$
    3. $\sqrt2\dfrac{\mu_0R^2}{L}$
    4. $2\sqrt2\dfrac{\mu_0R^2}{L}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    Field at the centre of the square carrying current $I$: $B=4\times\dfrac{\mu_0I}{4\pi(L/2)}\times2\sin45^\circ=\dfrac{2\sqrt2\mu_0I}{\pi L}$.
    Since $L \gg R$, this field is uniform over the small circle: $\Phi=B\pi R^2=\dfrac{2\sqrt2\mu_0IR^2}{L}$.
    $M=\dfrac{\Phi}{I}=2\sqrt2\dfrac{\mu_0R^2}{L}$.

  3. JEE Main 2026, 5 April, Shift 1 · Question 50
    In the given circuit below inductance values of $L_1$, $L_2$ and $L_3$ are same. The magnetic energy stored in the entire circuit is $(U_t)$ and that stored in the $L_2$ inductor is $(U_l)$. $U_t / U_l$ is _______.
    (Ignore the mutual inductance if any)

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 6

    Current $I$ through $L_1$ splits equally, $\dfrac I2$ through each of $L_2$ and $L_3$ (equal inductances).
    $U_t=\dfrac12LI^2+2\cdot\dfrac12L\left(\dfrac I2\right)^2=\dfrac34LI^2$; $U_l=\dfrac12L\left(\dfrac I2\right)^2=\dfrac18LI^2$.
    $\dfrac{U_t}{U_l}=\dfrac{3/4}{1/8}=6$.

  4. JEE Main 2026, 5 April, Shift 2 · Question 36
    A metal rod of length $L$ rotates about one end at origin with a uniform angular velocity $\omega$. The magnetic field radially falls off as $B(r)=B_0e^{-\lambda r}$; $\lambda$ being a positive constant. The emf induced (neglecting the centripetal force on electrons in the rod) is :

    1. $B_0\omega\left[\dfrac{1}{\lambda^2}-e^{-\lambda L}\left(\dfrac{1}{\lambda^2}+\dfrac{L}{\lambda}\right)\right]$
    2. $B_0\omega\left[\dfrac{1}{\lambda^2}+e^{-\lambda L}\left(\dfrac{1}{\lambda^2}+\dfrac{L}{\lambda}\right)\right]$
    3. $B_0\omega\left[\dfrac{4}{\lambda^2}-e^{-2\lambda L}\left(\dfrac{1}{\lambda^2}+\dfrac{2L}{\lambda}\right)\right]$
    4. $B_0\omega\left[\dfrac{3}{\lambda^2}-e^{-3\lambda L}\left(\dfrac{3}{\lambda^2}+\dfrac{L}{\lambda}\right)\right]$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    An element $dr$ at distance $r$ moves with speed $\omega r$: $d\varepsilon=B(r)\,\omega r\,dr$.
    $\varepsilon=B_0\omega\displaystyle\int_0^L re^{-\lambda r}dr=B_0\omega\left[-\dfrac{re^{-\lambda r}}{\lambda}-\dfrac{e^{-\lambda r}}{\lambda^2}\right]_0^L$.
    $=B_0\omega\left[\dfrac{1}{\lambda^2}-e^{-\lambda L}\left(\dfrac{1}{\lambda^2}+\dfrac{L}{\lambda}\right)\right]$.

  5. JEE Main 2026, 5 April, Shift 2 · Question 50
    A circular loop of radius 20 cm and resistance $2\ \Omega$ is placed in a time varying magnetic field $\vec{B}=(2t^2+2t+3)$ T. At $t=0$, for the plane of the loop being perpendicular to the magnetic field and, the induced current in the loop at $t=3$ s is $\dfrac{\alpha}{50}$ A. The value of $\alpha$ is __________.
    (Take $\pi=22/7$)

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 44

    Flux $\phi=BA$ with $A=\pi(0.2)^2=0.04\pi\ \mathrm{m^2}$.
    $\varepsilon=A\dfrac{dB}{dt}=0.04\pi(4t+2)$; at $t=3$ s: $\varepsilon=0.04\times\dfrac{22}{7}\times14=1.76$ V.
    $I=\dfrac{1.76}{2}=0.88$ A $=\dfrac{44}{50}$ A.
    $\alpha=44$.

  6. JEE Main 2026, 6 April, Shift 1 · Question 43
    An inductor of inductance 10 mH having resistance of 100 $\Omega$ is connected to battery of E.M.F. 1.0 V through a switch as shown in the figure below. After switch is closed, the ratio of instantaneous voltages across the inductor when the current passing through it is 2 mA and 4 mA is ________.

    1. $4/3$
    2. $3/4$
    3. $5/3$
    4. $3/5$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    Voltage across the inductive part: $V_L=E-iR$.
    $i=2$ mA: $V_L=1-0.2=0.8$ V; $i=4$ mA: $V_L=1-0.4=0.6$ V.
    Ratio $=\dfrac{0.8}{0.6}=\dfrac43$.

  7. JEE Main 2026, 6 April, Shift 2 · Question 39
    A square loop of side 2 cm is placed in a time varying magnetic field with magnitude as $B=0.4\sin(300t)$ Tesla. The normal to the plane of loop makes an angle of $60^\circ$ with the field. The maximum induced emf produced in the loop is ___________ mV.

    1. $12$
    2. $18$
    3. $21$
    4. $24$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    $\phi=BA\cos60^\circ=0.4\sin(300t)\times(4\times10^{-4})\times\dfrac12$.
    $\varepsilon_{max}=0.4\times300\times4\times10^{-4}\times\dfrac12=0.024$ V $=24$ mV.

  8. JEE Main 2026, 8 April, Shift 2 · Question 40
    A 30 cm long solenoid has 10 turns per cm and area of $5\ \text{cm}^2$. The current through the solenoid coil varies from 2 A to 4 A in 3.14 s. The e.m.f. induced in the coil is $\alpha\times10^{-5}$ V. The value $\alpha$ is __________.

    1. $60$
    2. $12$
    3. $120$
    4. $34$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    $n=1000$ turns/m, $l=0.3$ m, $A=5\times10^{-4}\ \text{m}^2$.
    $L=\mu_0n^2Al=4\pi\times10^{-7}\times10^6\times5\times10^{-4}\times0.3=6\pi\times10^{-5}$ H.
    $\varepsilon=L\dfrac{\Delta I}{\Delta t}=6\pi\times10^{-5}\times\dfrac{2}{3.14}=12\times10^{-5}$ V, so $\alpha=12$.
