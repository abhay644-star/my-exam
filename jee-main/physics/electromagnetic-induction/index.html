  1. JEE Main 2026, 2 April, Shift 1 · Question 38
    When a coil is placed in a time dependent magnetic field the power dissipated in it is $P$. The number of turns, area of the coil and radius of the coil wire are $N$, $A$ and $r$ respectively. For a second coils number of turns, area of the coil and radius of the coil wire are $2N$, $2A$ and $3r$ respectively. When the first coil is replaced with second coil the power dissipated in it is $\sqrt{2}\,\alpha P$. The value of $\alpha$ is ______.

    1. $36$
    2. $128\sqrt{2}$
    3. $16$
    4. $64$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    Induced emf $\varepsilon = NA\dfrac{dB}{dt} \propto NA$.
    Wire length $\ell \propto N\sqrt{A}$ (circumference $\propto\sqrt{A}$), so $R = \dfrac{\rho\ell}{\pi r^2} \propto \dfrac{N\sqrt{A}}{r^2}$.
    $P = \dfrac{\varepsilon^2}{R} \propto N A^{3/2} r^2$.
    $\dfrac{P'}{P} = 2\times(2)^{3/2}\times9 = 36\sqrt{2}$, so $\alpha = 36$.

  2. JEE Main 2026, 2 April, Shift 2 · Question 43
    A circular current loop of radius $R$ is placed inside square loop of side length $L$ $(L \gg R)$ such that they are co-planar and their centers coincide. The permeability of free space is $\mu_0$. The mutual inductance between circular loop and square loop is __________.

    1. $2\sqrt2\dfrac{\mu_0L^2}{R}$
    2. $\sqrt2\dfrac{\mu_0L^2}{R}$
    3. $\sqrt2\dfrac{\mu_0R^2}{L}$
    4. $2\sqrt2\dfrac{\mu_0R^2}{L}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    Field at the centre of the square carrying current $I$: $B=4\times\dfrac{\mu_0I}{4\pi(L/2)}\times2\sin45^\circ=\dfrac{2\sqrt2\mu_0I}{\pi L}$.
    Since $L \gg R$, this field is uniform over the small circle: $\Phi=B\pi R^2=\dfrac{2\sqrt2\mu_0IR^2}{L}$.
    $M=\dfrac{\Phi}{I}=2\sqrt2\dfrac{\mu_0R^2}{L}$.

  3. JEE Main 2026, 5 April, Shift 1 · Question 50
    In the given circuit below inductance values of $L_1$, $L_2$ and $L_3$ are same. The magnetic energy stored in the entire circuit is $(U_t)$ and that stored in the $L_2$ inductor is $(U_l)$. $U_t / U_l$ is _______.
    (Ignore the mutual inductance if any)

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 6

    Current $I$ through $L_1$ splits equally, $\dfrac I2$ through each of $L_2$ and $L_3$ (equal inductances).
    $U_t=\dfrac12LI^2+2\cdot\dfrac12L\left(\dfrac I2\right)^2=\dfrac34LI^2$; $U_l=\dfrac12L\left(\dfrac I2\right)^2=\dfrac18LI^2$.
    $\dfrac{U_t}{U_l}=\dfrac{3/4}{1/8}=6$.

  4. JEE Main 2026, 5 April, Shift 2 · Question 36
    A metal rod of length $L$ rotates about one end at origin with a uniform angular velocity $\omega$. The magnetic field radially falls off as $B(r)=B_0e^{-\lambda r}$; $\lambda$ being a positive constant. The emf induced (neglecting the centripetal force on electrons in the rod) is :

    1. $B_0\omega\left[\dfrac{1}{\lambda^2}-e^{-\lambda L}\left(\dfrac{1}{\lambda^2}+\dfrac{L}{\lambda}\right)\right]$
    2. $B_0\omega\left[\dfrac{1}{\lambda^2}+e^{-\lambda L}\left(\dfrac{1}{\lambda^2}+\dfrac{L}{\lambda}\right)\right]$
    3. $B_0\omega\left[\dfrac{4}{\lambda^2}-e^{-2\lambda L}\left(\dfrac{1}{\lambda^2}+\dfrac{2L}{\lambda}\right)\right]$
    4. $B_0\omega\left[\dfrac{3}{\lambda^2}-e^{-3\lambda L}\left(\dfrac{3}{\lambda^2}+\dfrac{L}{\lambda}\right)\right]$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    An element $dr$ at distance $r$ moves with speed $\omega r$: $d\varepsilon=B(r)\,\omega r\,dr$.
    $\varepsilon=B_0\omega\displaystyle\int_0^L re^{-\lambda r}dr=B_0\omega\left[-\dfrac{re^{-\lambda r}}{\lambda}-\dfrac{e^{-\lambda r}}{\lambda^2}\right]_0^L$.
    $=B_0\omega\left[\dfrac{1}{\lambda^2}-e^{-\lambda L}\left(\dfrac{1}{\lambda^2}+\dfrac{L}{\lambda}\right)\right]$.

  5. JEE Main 2026, 5 April, Shift 2 · Question 50
    A circular loop of radius 20 cm and resistance $2\ \Omega$ is placed in a time varying magnetic field $\vec{B}=(2t^2+2t+3)$ T. At $t=0$, for the plane of the loop being perpendicular to the magnetic field and, the induced current in the loop at $t=3$ s is $\dfrac{\alpha}{50}$ A. The value of $\alpha$ is __________.
    (Take $\pi=22/7$)

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 44

    Flux $\phi=BA$ with $A=\pi(0.2)^2=0.04\pi\ \mathrm{m^2}$.
    $\varepsilon=A\dfrac{dB}{dt}=0.04\pi(4t+2)$; at $t=3$ s: $\varepsilon=0.04\times\dfrac{22}{7}\times14=1.76$ V.
    $I=\dfrac{1.76}{2}=0.88$ A $=\dfrac{44}{50}$ A.
    $\alpha=44$.

  6. JEE Main 2026, 6 April, Shift 1 · Question 43
    An inductor of inductance 10 mH having resistance of 100 $\Omega$ is connected to battery of E.M.F. 1.0 V through a switch as shown in the figure below. After switch is closed, the ratio of instantaneous voltages across the inductor when the current passing through it is 2 mA and 4 mA is ________.

    1. $4/3$
    2. $3/4$
    3. $5/3$
    4. $3/5$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    Voltage across the inductive part: $V_L=E-iR$.
    $i=2$ mA: $V_L=1-0.2=0.8$ V; $i=4$ mA: $V_L=1-0.4=0.6$ V.
    Ratio $=\dfrac{0.8}{0.6}=\dfrac43$.

  7. JEE Main 2026, 6 April, Shift 2 · Question 39
    A square loop of side 2 cm is placed in a time varying magnetic field with magnitude as $B=0.4\sin(300t)$ Tesla. The normal to the plane of loop makes an angle of $60^\circ$ with the field. The maximum induced emf produced in the loop is ___________ mV.

    1. $12$
    2. $18$
    3. $21$
    4. $24$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    $\phi=BA\cos60^\circ=0.4\sin(300t)\times(4\times10^{-4})\times\dfrac12$.
    $\varepsilon_{max}=0.4\times300\times4\times10^{-4}\times\dfrac12=0.024$ V $=24$ mV.

  8. JEE Main 2026, 8 April, Shift 2 · Question 40
    A 30 cm long solenoid has 10 turns per cm and area of $5\ \text{cm}^2$. The current through the solenoid coil varies from 2 A to 4 A in 3.14 s. The e.m.f. induced in the coil is $\alpha\times10^{-5}$ V. The value $\alpha$ is __________.

    1. $60$
    2. $12$
    3. $120$
    4. $34$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    $n=1000$ turns/m, $l=0.3$ m, $A=5\times10^{-4}\ \text{m}^2$.
    $L=\mu_0n^2Al=4\pi\times10^{-7}\times10^6\times5\times10^{-4}\times0.3=6\pi\times10^{-5}$ H.
    $\varepsilon=L\dfrac{\Delta I}{\Delta t}=6\pi\times10^{-5}\times\dfrac{2}{3.14}=12\times10^{-5}$ V, so $\alpha=12$.

  9. JEE Main 2026, 21 January, Shift 1 · Question 37
    A conducting circular loop of area 1.0 m$^2$ is placed perpendicular to a magnetic field which varies as $B=\sin(100\,t)$ Tesla. If the resistance of the loop is 100 $\Omega$, then the average thermal energy dissipated in the loop in one period is __________ J.

    1. $\pi$
    2. $2\pi$
    3. $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$
    4. $\pi^2$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    emf $\varepsilon=A\dfrac{dB}{dt}=100\cos(100t)$ V.
    Mean power $=\dfrac{\varepsilon_0^2}{2R}=\dfrac{100^2}{200}=50$ W.
    One period $T=\dfrac{2\pi}{100}$ s, so energy $=50\times\dfrac{2\pi}{100}=\pi$ J.

  10. JEE Main 2026, 21 January, Shift 1 · Question 38
    A 1 m long metal rod AB completes the circuit as shown in figure. The area of circuit is perpendicular to the magnetic field of 0.10 T. If the resistance of the total circuit is 2 $\Omega$ then the force needed to move the rod towards right with constant speed $(v)$ of 1.5 m/s is _________N.

    1. $7.5\times10^{-3}$
    2. $7.5\times10^{-2}$
    3. $5.7\times10^{-3}$
    4. $5.7\times10^{-2}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    Motional emf $\varepsilon=Blv=0.1\times1\times1.5=0.15$ V; current $I=\dfrac{0.15}{2}=0.075$ A.
    At constant speed the applied force balances the magnetic force: $F=BIl=0.1\times0.075\times1=7.5\times10^{-3}$ N.

  11. JEE Main 2026, 22 January, Shift 1 · Question 36
    $XPQY$ is a vertical smooth long loop having a total resistance $R$ where $PX$ is parallel to $QY$ and separation between them is $l$. A constant magnetic field $B$ perpendicular to the plane of the loop exists in the entire space. A rod $CD$ of length $L\ (L>l)$ and mass $m$ is made to slide down from rest under the gravity as shown in figure. The terminal speed acquired by the rod is __________ m/s. ($g$ = acceleration due to gravity)

    1. $\dfrac{mgR}{B^2l^2}$
    2. $\dfrac{2mgR}{B^2l^2}$
    3. $\dfrac{2mgR}{B^2L^2}$
    4. $\dfrac{8mgR}{B^2l^2}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    Only the length $l$ between the rails is in the circuit. EMF $=Blv$, current $I=\dfrac{Blv}{R}$, magnetic force $=BIl=\dfrac{B^2l^2v}{R}$.
    At terminal speed this balances the weight: $\dfrac{B^2l^2v}{R}=mg\Rightarrow v=\dfrac{mgR}{B^2l^2}$.

  12. JEE Main 2026, 22 January, Shift 1 · Question 40
    Three identical coils $C_1$, $C_2$ and $C_3$ are closely placed such that they share a common axis. $C_2$ is exactly midway. $C_1$ carries current $I$ in anti-clockwise direction while $C_3$ carries current $I$ in clockwise direction. An induced current flows through $C_2$ will be in clockwise direction when

    1. $C_1$ and $C_3$ move with equal speeds towards $C_2$
    2. $C_1$ and $C_3$ move with equal speeds away from $C_2$
    3. $C_1$ moves towards $C_2$ and $C_3$ moves away from $C_2$
    4. $C_1$ moves away from $C_2$ and $C_3$ moves towards $C_2$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    The opposite currents in $C_1$ and $C_3$ give opposite fields at $C_2$. Seen from the same side, $C_1$'s anticlockwise current makes a field pointing towards the viewer through $C_2$.
    If both coils move symmetrically, the two fields at $C_2$ still cancel, so no current is induced.
    If $C_1$ approaches and $C_3$ recedes, the flux towards the viewer grows. By Lenz's law, $C_2$ opposes this with a field away from the viewer, i.e. a clockwise current.

  13. JEE Main 2026, 22 January, Shift 1 · Question 48
    Inductance of a coil with $10^4$ turns is 10 mH and it is connected to a dc source of 10 V with internal resistance of 10 $\Omega$. The energy density in the inductor when the current reaches $\left(\dfrac1e\right)$ of its maximum value is $\alpha\pi\times\dfrac{1}{e^2}$ J/m$^3$. The value of $\alpha$ is ________.
    ($\mu_0=4\pi\times10^{-7}$ Tm/A).

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 20

    Maximum current $=\dfrac{10}{10}=1$ A, so $I=\dfrac1e$ A.
    The length of the coil is not given; the intended working takes the $10^4$ turns as $n=10^4$ turns per metre, so $B=\mu_0nI$.
    $u=\dfrac{B^2}{2\mu_0}=\dfrac{\mu_0n^2I^2}{2}=\dfrac{4\pi\times10^{-7}\times10^8}{2e^2}=\dfrac{20\pi}{e^2}$ J/m$^3$, so $\alpha=20$.

  14. JEE Main 2026, 22 January, Shift 2 · Question 34
    Figure shows the circuit that contains three resistances (9 $\Omega$ each) and two inductors (4 mH each). The reading of ammeter at the moment switch $K$ is turned ON, is __________A.

    1. $1$
    2. $2$
    3. $3$
    4. zero
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    At the instant the switch is closed, an inductor carries no current (it opposes any sudden change), so both branches containing an inductor act as open circuits.
    Only the middle $9\ \Omega$ branch conducts: $I=\dfrac{9}{9}=1$ A, which is the ammeter reading.

  15. JEE Main 2026, 22 January, Shift 2 · Question 50
    A conducting circular loop is rotated about its diameter at a constant angular speed of 100 rad/s in a magnetic field of 0.5 T perpendicular to the axis of rotation. When the loop is rotated by $30^\circ$ from the horizontal position, the induced EMF is 15.4 mV. The radius of the loop is ________ mm.
    $\left(\text{Take }\pi=\dfrac{22}{7}\right)$

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 14

    Taking the flux as greatest in the horizontal position, $\Phi=BA\cos\omega t$ and $\varepsilon=BA\omega\sin\omega t$.
    At $30^\circ$: $15.4\times10^{-3}=0.5\times\pi r^2\times100\times\dfrac12=25\pi r^2$.
    $r^2=\dfrac{15.4\times10^{-3}\times7}{25\times22}=1.96\times10^{-4}$ m$^2$, so $r=0.014$ m $=14$ mm.

  16. JEE Main 2026, 23 January, Shift 1 · Question 36
    A 20 m long uniform copper wire held horizontally is allowed to fall under the gravity $(g=10\text{ m/s}^2)$ through a uniform horizontal magnetic field of 0.5 Gauss perpendicular to the length of the wire. The induced EMF across the wire when it travells a vertical distance of 200 m is __________ mV.

    1. $200\sqrt{10}$
    2. $20\sqrt{10}$
    3. $2\sqrt{10}$
    4. $0.2\sqrt{10}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    Speed after falling 200 m: $v=\sqrt{2\times10\times200}=20\sqrt{10}$ m/s.
    $\varepsilon=Blv=0.5\times10^{-4}\times20\times20\sqrt{10}=0.02\sqrt{10}$ V $=20\sqrt{10}$ mV.

  17. JEE Main 2026, 23 January, Shift 1 · Question 49
    A simple pendulum made of mass 10 g and a metallic wire of length 10 cm is suspended vertically in a uniform magnetic field of 2 T. The magnetic field direction is perpendicular to the plane of oscillations of the pendulum. If the pendulum is released from an angle of $60^\circ$ with vertical, then maximum induced EMF between the point of suspension and point of oscillation is __________ mV. (Take $g=10$ m/s$^2$)

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 100

    Greatest speed at the lowest point: $v=\sqrt{2gl(1-\cos60^\circ)}=\sqrt{2\times10\times0.1\times0.5}=1$ m/s, so $\omega=\dfrac vl=10$ rad/s.
    EMF across a rod rotating about one end: $\varepsilon=\dfrac12B\omega l^2=\dfrac12\times2\times10\times(0.1)^2=0.1$ V $=100$ mV.

  18. JEE Main 2026, 23 January, Shift 2 · Question 36
    Suppose a long solenoid of 100 cm length, radius 2 cm having 500 turns per unit length, carries a current $I=10\sin(\omega t)$ A, where $\omega=1000$ rad./s. A circular conducting loop $(B)$ of radius 1 cm coaxially slided through the solenoid at a speed $v=1$ cm/s. The r.m.s. current through the loop when the coil $B$ is inserted 10 cm inside the solenoid is $\alpha/\sqrt2$ $\mu$A. The value of $\alpha$ is ______.
    [Resistance of the loop $=10\ \Omega$]

    1. $100$
    2. $280$
    3. $197$
    4. $80$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    Inside the solenoid the field is uniform, so only the changing current induces an EMF: $\Phi=\mu_0nI\pi r^2$.
    Peak EMF $=\mu_0n\pi r^2I_0\omega=4\pi\times10^{-7}\times500\times\pi\times10^{-4}\times10\times1000\approx1.97\times10^{-3}$ V (taking $n=500$ per metre).
    Peak current $=\dfrac{1.97\times10^{-3}}{10}\approx197\ \mu$A, so the r.m.s. current is $\dfrac{197}{\sqrt2}\ \mu$A and $\alpha=197$.

  19. JEE Main 2026, 23 January, Shift 2 · Question 40
    A circular loop of radius 7 cm is placed in uniform magnetic field of 0.2 T directed perpendicular to plane of loop. The loop is converted into a square loop in 0.5 s. The EMF induced in the loop is________ mV.

    1. $1.32$
    2. $13.2$
    3. $6.6$
    4. $8.25$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    Wire length $=2\pi\times7=44$ cm, so the square has side 11 cm.
    Change in area $=154-121=33$ cm$^2=33\times10^{-4}$ m$^2$.
    $\varepsilon=\dfrac{B\Delta A}{\Delta t}=\dfrac{0.2\times33\times10^{-4}}{0.5}=1.32\times10^{-3}$ V $=1.32$ mV.
