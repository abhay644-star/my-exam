  1. JEE Main 2026, 4 April, Shift 2 · Question 27
    Two cars $A$ and $B$ are moving in the same direction along a straight line with speeds 100 km/h and 80 km/h, respectively such that car $A$ is moving ahead of car $B$. A person in car $B$ throws a stone with a speed $v$ so that it hits the car $A$ with a speed of 5 m/s. The value of $v$ is _______ km/h.

    1. $18$
    2. $28$
    3. $38$
    4. $48$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    Relative to car B, car A moves ahead at $100-80=20$ km/h.
    The stone's speed relative to A on impact is $5$ m/s $=18$ km/h.
    So $v-20=18\Rightarrow v=38$ km/h (speed relative to car B).

  2. JEE Main 2026, 5 April, Shift 1 · Question 47
    From $18$ m height above the ground a ball is dropped from rest . The height above the ground at which the magnitude of velocity equal to the magnitude of acceleration (in the same set of units) due to gravity is ____ m.
    (Take $g = 10$ m/s$^2$ and neglect the air resistance)

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 13

    Need $v=10$ m/s. Distance fallen $=\dfrac{v^2}{2g}=\dfrac{100}{20}=5$ m.
    Height above the ground $=18-5=13$ m.

  3. JEE Main 2026, 5 April, Shift 2 · Question 29
    The velocity $(v)$ versus time $(t)$ plot of a particle is shown in the figure, for a time interval of 40 s. The total distance travelled by the particle and the average velocity during this period are, respectively __________.

    1. 25 m and zero
    2. 50 m and zero
    3. 100 m and zero
    4. 100 m and 2.5 m/s
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    Distance = total area between the graph and the time axis.
    0 to 20 s: triangle of base 20 s and height 5 m/s, area $=50$ m (positive).
    20 to 40 s: triangle of base 20 s and height 5 m/s below the axis, area $=50$ m (negative velocity).
    Distance $=50+50=100$ m; displacement $=50-50=0$, so average velocity is zero.

  4. JEE Main 2026, 8 April, Shift 2 · Question 29
    A gas balloon is going up with a constant velocity of 10 m/s. When this balloon reached a height of 75 m, a stone is dropped from it and balloon keeps moving up with the same velocity. The height of the balloon when the stone hits the ground is __________ m. (Take $g=10\ \text{m/s}^2$)

    1. $85$
    2. $150$
    3. $129$
    4. $125$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    The stone starts with $10$ m/s upward from 75 m: $-75=10t-5t^2\Rightarrow t^2-2t-15=0\Rightarrow t=5$ s.
    In 5 s the balloon rises $10\times5=50$ m more.
    Height of the balloon $=75+50=125$ m.

  5. JEE Main 2026, 8 April, Shift 2 · Question 50
    Two masses of 3.4 kg and 2.5 kg are accelerated from an initial speed of 5 m/s and 12 m/s, respectively. The distances traversed by the masses in the $5^{\text{th}}$ second are 104 m and 129 m, respectively. The ratio of their momenta after 10 s is $\dfrac{x}{8}$. The value of $x$ is __________.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 9

    Distance in the $n^{\text{th}}$ second: $s_n=u+\dfrac a2(2n-1)$.
    $104=5+4.5a_1\Rightarrow a_1=22\ \text{m/s}^2$; $129=12+4.5a_2\Rightarrow a_2=26\ \text{m/s}^2$.
    After 10 s: $v_1=5+220=225$ m/s, $v_2=12+260=272$ m/s.
    $\dfrac{p_1}{p_2}=\dfrac{3.4\times225}{2.5\times272}=\dfrac{765}{680}=\dfrac98$, so $x=9$.

  6. JEE Main 2026, 23 January, Shift 2 · Question 27
    A paratrooper jumps from an aeroplane and opens a parachute after 2 s of free fall and starts deaccelerating with 3 m/s$^2$. At 10 m height from ground, while descending with the help of parachute, the speed of paratrooper is 5 m/s. The initial height of the airplane is _______m.
    $(g=10\text{ m/s}^2)$

    1. $82.5$
    2. $62.5$
    3. $92.5$
    4. $20$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    Free fall for 2 s: falls $\dfrac12\times10\times2^2=20$ m and reaches 20 m/s.
    Slowing at 3 m/s$^2$ from 20 m/s to 5 m/s: distance $=\dfrac{20^2-5^2}{2\times3}=62.5$ m.
    Initial height $=20+62.5+10=92.5$ m.

  7. JEE Main 2026, 24 January, Shift 2 · Question 28
    The velocity $(v)$ $-$ Distance $(x)$ graph is shown in figure. Which graph represents acceleration$(a)$ versus distance $(x)$ variation of this system?

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    The graph is a straight line $v=v_0-kx$ ($v_0,k>0$).
    $a=v\dfrac{dv}{dx}=(v_0-kx)(-k)=-kv_0+k^2x$: a straight line with positive slope, negative at $x=0$ and crossing zero where $v=0$.
    That is graph (3).

  8. JEE Main 2026, 28 January, Shift 1 · Question 30
    Water drops fall from a tap on the floor, 5 m below, at regular intervals of time, the first drop strikes the floor when the sixth drop begins to fall. The height at which the fourth drop will be from ground, at the instant when the first drop strikes the ground is _________m.
    $(g=10\ \mathrm{m/s^2})$

    1. $4.0$
    2. $2.5$
    3. $4.2$
    4. $3.8$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    Time to fall 5 m: $5=\tfrac12(10)t^2$, $t=1$ s. Five intervals fit in this time, so each is 0.2 s.
    The fourth drop has been falling for $0.4$ s: distance $=\tfrac12(10)(0.4)^2=0.8$ m.
    Height above the ground $=5-0.8=4.2$ m.

  9. JEE Main 2026, 28 January, Shift 2 · Question 30
    A particle starts moving from time $t=0$ and its coordinate is given as $x(t)=4t^3-3t$
    A. The particle returns to its original position (origin) 0.866 units later
    B. The particle is 1 unit away from origin at its turning point
    C. Acceleration of the particle is non-negative
    D. The particle is 0.5 units away from origin at its turning point
    E. Particle never turns back as acceleration is non-negative
    Choose the correct answer from the options given below :

    1. A, C Only
    2. A, C, D Only
    3. A, B, C Only
    4. C, E Only
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    $x=0$ again at $4t^2=3$, $t=\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}\approx0.866$: A true.
    $v=12t^2-3=0$ at $t=0.5$, where $x=0.5-1.5=-1$: 1 unit from the origin, so B true and D false; the particle does turn back, so E false.
    $a=24t\ge0$ for $t\ge0$: C true. Answer: A, B, C.

  10. JEE Main 2025, 2 April, Shift 1 · Question 49
    A person travelling on a straight line moves with a uniform velocity $v_1$ for a distance $x$ and with a uniform velocity $v_2$ for the next $\dfrac32x$ distance. The average velocity in this motion is $\dfrac{50}{7}$ m/s. If $v_1$ is 5 m/s then $v_2=$ __________ m/s.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 10

    Total time $=\dfrac x5+\dfrac{1.5x}{v_2}$ and total distance $=2.5x$.
    $\dfrac{2.5x}{50/7}=0.35x$, so $\dfrac{1.5}{v_2}=0.35-0.2=0.15$ and $v_2=10$ m/s.

  11. JEE Main 2025, 3 April, Shift 1 · Question 30
    Which of the following curves possibly represent one-dimensional motion of a particle?
    Choose the correct answer from the options given below:

    1. A, B and C only
    2. A, C and D only
    3. A and B only
    4. A, B and D only
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    A: phase increasing linearly with time describes simple harmonic motion: possible.
    B: a closed velocity-displacement curve (an ellipse, here drawn as a circle with suitable scales) is also simple harmonic motion: possible.
    C: a circle on a velocity-time graph would give two velocities at the same instant: impossible.
    D: total distance never decreases with time: possible. Hence A, B and D.

  12. JEE Main 2025, 3 April, Shift 2 · Question 28
    A particle moves along the $x$-axis and has its displacement $x$ varying with time t according to the equation:
    $x=c_0(t^2-2)+c(t-2)^2$
    where $c_0$ and c are constants of appropriate dimensions.
    Then, which of the following statements is correct?

    1. the initial velocity of the particle is 4c
    2. the acceleration of the particle is $2c_0$
    3. the acceleration of the particle is 2c
    4. the acceleration of the particle is $2(c+c_0)$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    $v=\dfrac{dx}{dt}=2c_0t+2c(t-2)$, so the initial velocity is $-4c$ (option 1 is wrong).
    $a=\dfrac{dv}{dt}=2c_0+2c=2(c+c_0)$.

  13. JEE Main 2025, 4 April, Shift 2 · Question 30
    The displacement $x$ versus time graph is shown below.
    (A) The average velocity during 0 to 3 s is 10 m/s
    (B) The average velocity during 3 to 5 s is 0 m/s
    (C) The instantaneous velocity at t = 2 s is 5 m/s
    (D) The average velocity during 5 to 7 s and instantaneous velocity at t = 6.5 s are equal
    (E) The average velocity from t = 0 to t = 9 s is zero
    Choose the correct answer from the options given below :

    1. (A), (D), (E) only
    2. (B), (C), (D) only
    3. (B), (D), (E) only
    4. (B), (C), (E) only
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    From the graph: $x(0)=0$, $x(1)=-5$, $x(2)=0$, $x(3)=5$, $x(5)=5$, $x(6)=10$, $x(7)=0$, $x(8)=-5$, $x(9)=0$ m.
    (A) $\dfrac{5-0}{3}=\dfrac53$ m/s: false. (B) $\dfrac{5-5}{2}=0$: true. (C) slope of AC $=\dfrac{5-(-5)}{3-1}=5$ m/s: true.
    (D) average from 5 to 7 s $=\dfrac{0-5}{2}=-2.5$ m/s, but the slope of EF at 6.5 s is $-10$ m/s: false. (E) $x(9)=x(0)$: true.
    Answer: (B), (C), (E).

  14. JEE Main 2025, 23 January, Shift 1 · Question 29
    The motion of an airplane is represented by velocity-time graph as shown below. The distance covered by airplane in the first 30.5 second is _____ km.

    1. $3$
    2. $6$
    3. $9$
    4. $12$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    Distance is the area under the v-t graph.
    From 0 to 2 s (200 to 400 m/s): $\dfrac{200+400}{2}\times2=600$ m.
    From 2 s to 30.5 s at 400 m/s: $400\times28.5=11400$ m.
    Total $=12000$ m $=12$ km.

  15. JEE Main 2025, 28 January, Shift 2 · Question 28
    The velocity-time graph of an object moving along a straight line is shown in figure. What is the distance covered by the object between $t=0$ to $t=4$ s ?

    1. 10 m
    2. 11 m
    3. 13 m
    4. 30 m
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    Distance = area under the $v$-$t$ graph from 0 to 4 s.
    0 to 2 s: triangle, $\dfrac12\times2\times10=10$ m. 2 to 4 s: rectangle, $2\times10=20$ m.
    Total $=30$ m.

  16. JEE Main 2025, 29 January, Shift 2 · Question 47
    Two cars P and Q are moving on a road in the same direction. Accleration of car P increases linearly with time whereas car Q moves with a constant accleration. Both cars cross each other at time t = 0, for the first time. The maximum possible number of crossing(s) (including the crossing at t = 0) is __________.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 3

    With $a_P=\alpha+\beta t$ and $a_Q$ constant, the relative position $x_P-x_Q$ is a cubic in $t$ that vanishes at $t=0$.
    A cubic has at most three real roots, so there can be at most 3 crossings (including $t=0$).
