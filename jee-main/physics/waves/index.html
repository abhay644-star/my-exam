  1. JEE Main 2026, 2 April, Shift 1 · Question 35
    The equation of a plane progressive wave is given by $y = 5\cos\pi\left(200\,t - \dfrac{x}{150}\right)$ where $x$ and $y$ are in cm and $t$ is in second. The velocity of the wave is ________ m/s.

    1. $120$
    2. $150$
    3. $200$
    4. $300$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    $\omega = 200\pi\ \mathrm{rad/s}$, $k = \dfrac{\pi}{150}\ \mathrm{cm^{-1}}$.
    $v = \dfrac{\omega}{k} = 200\times150 = 30000\ \mathrm{cm/s} = 300\ \mathrm{m/s}$.

  2. JEE Main 2026, 5 April, Shift 1 · Question 48
    A transverse wave on a string is described by $y = 3\sin(36t + 0.018x + \pi/4)$. where $x, y$ are in cm and $t$ in seconds. The least distance between the two successive crests in the wave is ____ cm. (Nearest integer)
    ($\pi = 3.14$)

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 349

    Wave number $k=0.018$ rad/cm.
    Distance between successive crests $=\lambda=\dfrac{2\pi}{k}=\dfrac{6.28}{0.018}\approx348.9$ cm.
    Nearest integer: $349$.

  3. JEE Main 2026, 21 January, Shift 1 · Question 32
    Two strings $(A, B)$ having linear densities $\mu_A=2\times10^{-4}$ kg/m and, $\mu_B=4\times10^{-4}$ kg/m and lengths $L_A=2.5$ m and $L_B=1.5$ m respectively are joined. Free ends of $A$ and $B$ are tied to two rigid supports $C$ and $D$, respectively creating a tension of 500 N in the wire. Two identical pulses, sent from $C$ and $D$ ends, take time $t_1$ and $t_2$, respectively, to reach the joint. The ratio $t_1/t_2$ is :

    1. $1.08$
    2. $1.18$
    3. $1.67$
    4. $1.90$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    Pulse speed $v=\sqrt{T/\mu}$, so the travel time is $t=L\sqrt{\mu/T}$.
    $\dfrac{t_1}{t_2}=\dfrac{L_A}{L_B}\sqrt{\dfrac{\mu_A}{\mu_B}}=\dfrac{2.5}{1.5}\times\dfrac{1}{\sqrt2}\approx1.18$.

  4. JEE Main 2026, 22 January, Shift 1 · Question 47
    Two loudspeakers ($L_1$ and $L_2$) are placed with a separation of 10 m, as shown in figure. Both speakers are fed with an audio input signal of same frequency with constant volume. A voice recorder, initially at point $A$, at equidistance to both loud speakers, is moved by 25 m along the line $AB$ while monitoring the audio signal. The measured signal was found to undergo 10 cycles of minima and maxima during the movement. The frequency of the input signal is ______Hz
    (Speed of sound in air is 324 m/s and $\sqrt5=2.23$)

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 600

    Take the speakers at $(0,\pm5)$ m and AB along $x=40$ m, from $y=0$ to $y=25$ m.
    At A the path difference is $0$. At B: $\sqrt{40^2+30^2}-\sqrt{40^2+20^2}=50-20\sqrt5=50-44.6=5.4$ m.
    10 cycles of maxima and minima means the path difference changed by $10\lambda$: $\lambda=0.54$ m.
    $f=\dfrac{324}{0.54}=600$ Hz.

  5. JEE Main 2026, 22 January, Shift 2 · Question 36
    In an open organ pipe $\nu_3$ and $\nu_6$ are 3$^{\text{rd}}$ and 6$^{\text{th}}$ harmonic frequencies, respectively. If $\nu_6-\nu_3=2200$ Hz then length of the pipe is __________ mm.
    (Take velocity of sound in air is 330 m/s.)

    1. $200$
    2. $225$
    3. $250$
    4. $275$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    For an open pipe $\nu_n=\dfrac{nv}{2L}$, so $\nu_6-\nu_3=\dfrac{3v}{2L}=2200$.
    $L=\dfrac{3\times330}{2\times2200}=0.225$ m $=225$ mm.

  6. JEE Main 2026, 23 January, Shift 2 · Question 48
    The velocity of sound in air is doubled when the temperature is raised from 0 $^\circ$C to $\alpha$ $^\circ$C. The value of $\alpha$ is ________.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 819

    $v\propto\sqrt T$, so doubling $v$ needs $4T$: $4\times273=1092$ K.
    $\alpha=1092-273=819\ ^\circ$C.

  7. JEE Main 2026, 24 January, Shift 2 · Question 33
    The fifth harmonic of a closed organ pipe is found to be in unison with the first harmonic of an open pipe. The ratio of lengths of closed pipe to that of the open pipe is $5/x$. The value of $x$ is______.

    1. $2$
    2. $3$
    3. $4$
    4. $1$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    Closed pipe, fifth harmonic: $\dfrac{5v}{4L_c}$. Open pipe, first harmonic: $\dfrac{v}{2L_o}$.
    Equal frequencies: $\dfrac{L_c}{L_o}=\dfrac52$, so $x=2$.

  8. JEE Main 2026, 28 January, Shift 2 · Question 33
    The speed of a longitudinal wave in a metallic bar is 400 m/s. If the density and Young's modulus of the bar material are increased by 0.5% and 1%, respectively then the speed of the wave is changed approximately to __________ m/s.

    1. $398$
    2. $401$
    3. $402$
    4. $399$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    $v=\sqrt{Y/\rho}$, so $\dfrac{\Delta v}{v}=\dfrac12\left(\dfrac{\Delta Y}{Y}-\dfrac{\Delta\rho}{\rho}\right)=\dfrac12(1-0.5)\%=0.25\%$.
    New speed $\approx400\times1.0025=401$ m/s.

  9. JEE Main 2026, 28 January, Shift 2 · Question 47
    Two tuning forks $A$ and $B$ are sounded together giving rise to 8 beats in 2 s. When fork $A$ is loaded with wax, the beat frequency is reduced to 4 beats in 2 s. If the original frequency of tuning fork $B$ is 380 Hz then original frequency of tuning fork $A$ is _________ Hz.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 384

    Beat frequency 4 Hz, so $f_A=384$ or $376$ Hz. Loading A with wax lowers $f_A$.
    The beat frequency falls to 2 Hz, which happens only if $f_A$ was above 380 Hz: $f_A=384$ Hz.

  10. JEE Main 2025, 22 January, Shift 1 · Question 33
    A closed organ and an open organ tube are filled by two different gases having same bulk modulus but different densities $\rho_1$ and $\rho_2$, respectively. The frequency of 9$^{\text{th}}$ harmonic of closed tube is identical with 4$^{\text{th}}$ harmonic of open tube. If the length of the closed tube is 10 cm and the density ratio of the gases is $\rho_1 : \rho_2=1 : 16$, then the length of the open tube is :

    1. $\dfrac{15}{7}$ cm
    2. $\dfrac{15}{9}$ cm
    3. $\dfrac{20}{7}$ cm
    4. $\dfrac{20}{9}$ cm
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    Speed of sound $v=\sqrt{B/\rho}$, so $\dfrac{v_1}{v_2}=\sqrt{\dfrac{\rho_2}{\rho_1}}=4$.
    $\dfrac{9v_1}{4L_1}=\dfrac{4v_2}{2L_2} \Rightarrow L_2=\dfrac{8L_1v_2}{9v_1}=\dfrac{8\times10}{9\times4}=\dfrac{20}{9}$ cm.

  11. JEE Main 2025, 23 January, Shift 2 · Question 35
    The equation of a transverse wave travelling along a string is
    $y(x, t)=4.0\sin[20\times10^{-3}x+600t]$mm, where $x$ is in mm and $t$ is in second. The velocity of the wave is :

    1. $+30$ m/s
    2. $-30$ m/s
    3. $+60$ m/s
    4. $-60$ m/s
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    For $\sin(kx+\omega t)$ the disturbance moves in the $-x$ direction with speed $\dfrac\omega k$.
    $\dfrac{\omega}{k}=\dfrac{600}{20\times10^{-3}}=3\times10^4$ mm/s $=30$ m/s.
    Velocity $=-30$ m/s.

  12. JEE Main 2025, 28 January, Shift 1 · Question 33
    Given below are two statements: one is labelled as <b>Assertion A</b> and the other is labelled as <b>Reason R</b>
    <b>Assertion A:</b> A sound wave has higher speed in solids than gases.
    <b>Reason R:</b> Gases have higher value of Bulk modulus than solids.
    In the light of the above statements, choose the <b><i>correct</i></b> answer from the options given below

    1. Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation of A
    2. Both A and R are true but R is NOT the correct explanation of A
    3. A is true but R is false
    4. A is false but R is true
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    Sound travels much faster in solids than in gases: A is true.
    Solids have far larger elastic moduli than gases (which are easily compressed), so R is false.
    Answer: A is true but R is false.
