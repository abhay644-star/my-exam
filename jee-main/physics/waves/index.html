  1. JEE Main 2026, 2 April, Shift 1 · Question 35
    The equation of a plane progressive wave is given by $y = 5\cos\pi\left(200\,t - \dfrac{x}{150}\right)$ where $x$ and $y$ are in cm and $t$ is in second. The velocity of the wave is ________ m/s.

    1. $120$
    2. $150$
    3. $200$
    4. $300$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    $\omega = 200\pi\ \mathrm{rad/s}$, $k = \dfrac{\pi}{150}\ \mathrm{cm^{-1}}$.
    $v = \dfrac{\omega}{k} = 200\times150 = 30000\ \mathrm{cm/s} = 300\ \mathrm{m/s}$.

  2. JEE Main 2026, 5 April, Shift 1 · Question 48
    A transverse wave on a string is described by $y = 3\sin(36t + 0.018x + \pi/4)$. where $x, y$ are in cm and $t$ in seconds. The least distance between the two successive crests in the wave is ____ cm. (Nearest integer)
    ($\pi = 3.14$)

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 349

    Wave number $k=0.018$ rad/cm.
    Distance between successive crests $=\lambda=\dfrac{2\pi}{k}=\dfrac{6.28}{0.018}\approx348.9$ cm.
    Nearest integer: $349$.
