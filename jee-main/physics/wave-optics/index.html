  1. JEE Main 2026, 2 April, Shift 1 · Question 46
    In single slit diffraction pattern, the wavelength of light used is 628 nm and slit width is 0.2 mm, the angular width of central maximum is $\alpha\times10^{-2}$ degrees. The value of $\alpha$ is ________.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 36

    Angular width of central maximum $= \dfrac{2\lambda}{a} = \dfrac{2\times628\times10^{-9}}{0.2\times10^{-3}} = 6.28\times10^{-3}$ rad.
    In degrees: $6.28\times10^{-3}\times\dfrac{180}{\pi} \approx 0.36^\circ = 36\times10^{-2}$ degrees, so $\alpha = 36$.

  2. JEE Main 2026, 2 April, Shift 2 · Question 46
    In a Young's double slit experiment, the intensity at some point on the screen is found to be $\dfrac34$ times of the maximum of the interference pattern. The path difference between the interfering waves at this point is $\dfrac{\lambda}{x}$ where $\lambda$ is wavelength of the incident light. The value of $x$ is __________.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 6

    $I=I_{max}\cos^2\dfrac{\phi}{2}=\dfrac34I_{max} \Rightarrow \cos\dfrac\phi2=\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2} \Rightarrow \phi=\dfrac\pi3$.
    Path difference $=\dfrac{\lambda}{2\pi}\phi=\dfrac{\lambda}{6}$.
    $x=6$.

  5. JEE Main 2026, 4 April, Shift 1 · Question 45
    An unpolarized light is incident on the plane interface of air-dielectric medium shown in figure. If the incident angle is equal to Brewster angle, identify the expression representing reflected wave.

    1. $(E_x\hat{i}+E_y\hat{j})\sin(kx-kz-\omega t)$
    2. $(E_x\hat{i}+E_z\hat{k})\sin(kx+ky-\omega t)$
    3. $(E_x\hat{j}+E_y\hat{k})\sin(ky+kz-\omega t)$
    4. $(E_x\hat{i}+E_y\hat{j}+E_z\hat{k})\sin(kx+ky-kz-\omega t)$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    The interface is the $xy$-plane and the medium is on the $+z$ side; the plane of incidence is the $xz$-plane.
    The incident wave travels along $+x$ and $+z$; the reflected wave keeps its $x$-component and reverses its $z$-component, so its phase is $(kx-kz-\omega t)$, with no $y$-dependence.
    Only option (A) has this phase. At the Brewster angle the reflected light is plane polarised perpendicular to the plane of incidence (along $\hat j$).
    Official answer: (A).

  6. JEE Main 2026, 4 April, Shift 2 · Question 42
    An unpolarized light of certain intensity passes through a combination of two polarizers whose transmission axes are at $30^\circ$ and $90^\circ$, respectively, with respect to the horizontal axis. A third polarizer with its transmission axis at $60^\circ$ with the horizontal axis is placed between the two existing polarizers. The ratio of the output intensities with and without the third polarizer is ________.

    1. $3/4$
    2. $4/3$
    3. $9/4$
    4. $4/9$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    Without the third polarizer: $I=\dfrac{I_0}{2}\cos^260^\circ=\dfrac{I_0}{8}$.
    With it: $I'=\dfrac{I_0}{2}\cos^230^\circ\cos^230^\circ=\dfrac{I_0}{2}\cdot\dfrac{9}{16}=\dfrac{9I_0}{32}$.
    $\dfrac{I'}{I}=\dfrac{9/32}{1/8}=\dfrac94$.

  9. JEE Main 2026, 5 April, Shift 2 · Question 42
    The maximum intensity in a Young's double slit experiment is $I_o$. Distance between the slits $(d)$ is $5\lambda$, where $\lambda$ is the wavelength of light used. The intensity of the fringe, exactly opposite to one of the slits on the screen, placed at $D=10d$ is __________.

    1. $\dfrac{I_o}{4}$
    2. $\dfrac{I_o}{2}$
    3. $I_o$
    4. $\dfrac{3I_o}{4}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    Opposite a slit, $y=\dfrac d2$: path difference $=\dfrac{yd}{D}=\dfrac{d^2}{2D}=\dfrac{d}{20}=\dfrac{5\lambda}{20}=\dfrac{\lambda}{4}$.
    Phase difference $\phi=\dfrac{2\pi}{\lambda}\cdot\dfrac{\lambda}{4}=\dfrac{\pi}{2}$.
    $I=I_o\cos^2\dfrac{\phi}{2}=I_o\cos^2\dfrac{\pi}{4}=\dfrac{I_o}{2}$.

  10. JEE Main 2026, 6 April, Shift 1 · Question 36
    In interference experiment the path difference between two interfering waves at a point $A$ on the screen is $\lambda/3$, where $\lambda$ is the wavelength of these waves, and at another point $B$ the path difference is $\lambda/6$. The ratio of intensities at points $A$ and $B$ is ________.

    1. $3$
    2. $4$
    3. $1/3$
    4. $1/4$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    $I=4I_0\cos^2\dfrac\phi2$ with $\phi=\dfrac{2\pi}{\lambda}\Delta x$.

    $I_A:I_B=1/3$.

  18. JEE Main 2026, 22 January, Shift 2 · Question 42
    Which of the following are true for a single slit diffraction ?
    A. Width of central maxima increases with increase in wavelength keeping slit width constant.
    B. Width of central maxima increases with decrease in wavelength keeping slit width constant.
    C. Width of central maxima increases with decrease in slit width at constant wavelength.
    D. Width of central maxima increases with increase in slit width at constant wavelength.
    E. Brightness of central maxima increases for decrease in wavelength at constant slit width.

    1. A, D, E only
    2. A, D only
    3. B, D only
    4. B, C only
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: Dropped: full marks to everyone

    Angular width of the central maximum $=\dfrac{2\lambda}{a}$.

    Given below are two statements : one is labelled as Assertion (A) and the other is labelled as Reason (R).
    Assertion-(A) : If Young's double slit experiment is performed in an optically denser medium than air, then the consecutive fringes come closer.
    Reason-(R) : The speed of light reduces in an optically denser medium than air while its frequency does not change.
    In the light of the above statements, choose the most appropriate answer from the options given below :

    1. Both (A) and (R) are true and (R) is the correct explanation of (A)
    2. Both (A) and (R) are true but (R) is not the correct explanation of (A)
    3. (A) is true but (R) is false
    4. (A) is false but (R) is true
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    In a denser medium the speed falls to $c/n$ while the frequency stays the same, so the wavelength becomes $\lambda/n$.
    Fringe width $\beta=\dfrac{\lambda D}{d}$ therefore decreases by the factor $n$: fringes come closer.
    Both are true and (R) explains (A).

    Given below are two statements. One is labelled as Assertion (A) and the other is labelled as Reason (R).
    Assertion (A) : In Young's double slit experiment, the fringes produced by red light are closer as compared to those produced by blue light.
    Reason (R) : The fringe width is directly proportional to the wavelength of light.
    In the light of the above statements, choose the correct answer from the options given below :

    1. Both (A) and (R) are true and (R) is the correct explanation of (A)
    2. Both (A) and (R) are true but (R) is NOT the correct explanation of (A)
    3. (A) is true but (R) is false
    4. (A) is false but (R) is true
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    $\beta=\dfrac{\lambda D}{d}$, so fringe width is proportional to wavelength: (R) is true.
    Red light has the longer wavelength, so its fringes are wider apart, not closer: (A) is false.

  37. JEE Main 2025, 23 January, Shift 2 · Question 41
    The width of one of the two slits in Young's double slit experiment is d while that of the other slit is $x$d. If the ratio of the maximum to the minimum intensity in the interference pattern on the screen is $9:4$ then what is the value of $x$ ?
    (Assume that the field strength varies according to the slit width.)

    1. $4$
    2. $5$
    3. $3$
    4. $2$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    The field amplitudes are in the ratio $1:x$.
    $\dfrac{I_{\max}}{I_{\min}}=\left(\dfrac{x+1}{x-1}\right)^2=\dfrac94 \Rightarrow \dfrac{x+1}{x-1}=\dfrac32 \Rightarrow x=5$.

  38. JEE Main 2025, 24 January, Shift 1 · Question 39
    The Young's double slit interference experiment is performed using light consisting of 480 nm and 600 nm wavelengths to form interference patterns. The least number of the bright fringes of 480 nm light that are required for the first coincidence with the bright fringes formed by 600 nm light is

    1. $4$
    2. $5$
    3. $6$
    4. $8$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    Bright fringes coincide when $n_1(480)=n_2(600)$, i.e. $4n_1=5n_2$.
    The smallest solution is $n_1=5$, $n_2=4$: the 5th bright fringe of 480 nm light falls on the 4th of 600 nm light.
    So 5 bright fringes of 480 nm light are needed.

  39. JEE Main 2025, 24 January, Shift 2 · Question 42
    Young's double slit inteference apparatus is immersed in a liquid of refractive index 1.44. It has slit separation of 1.5 mm. The slits are illuminated by a parallel beam of light whose wavelength in air is 690 nm. The fringe-width on a screen placed behind the plane of slits at a distance of 0.72 m, will be :

    1. $0.63$ mm
    2. $0.33$ mm
    3. $0.46$ mm
    4. $0.23$ mm
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    In the liquid the wavelength is $\dfrac{\lambda}{\mu}$, so $\beta=\dfrac{\lambda D}{\mu d}=\dfrac{690\times10^{-9}\times0.72}{1.44\times1.5\times10^{-3}}=2.3\times10^{-4}$ m.
    $\beta=0.23$ mm.

  40. JEE Main 2025, 24 January, Shift 2 · Question 43
    In a Young's double slit experiment, three polarizers are kept as shown in the figure. The transmission axes of $P_1$ and $P_2$ are orthogonal to each other. The polarizer $P_3$ covers both the slits with its transmission axis at $45^\circ$ to those of $P_1$ and $P_2$. An unpolarized light of wavelength $\lambda$ and intensity $I_0$ is incident on $P_1$ and $P_2$. The intensity at a point after $P_3$ where the path difference between the light waves from $s_1$ and $s_2$ is $\dfrac{\lambda}{3}$, is

    1. $I_0$
    2. $\dfrac{I_0}{2}$
    3. $\dfrac{I_0}{3}$
    4. $\dfrac{I_0}{4}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    Each polariser passes half of the unpolarised light: $\dfrac{I_0}{2}$ through each slit, polarised at right angles to each other.
    $P_3$ at $45^\circ$ passes $\dfrac{I_0}{2}\cos^245^\circ=\dfrac{I_0}{4}$ from each, now with the same polarisation, so they interfere.
    Path difference $\dfrac\lambda3$ gives phase difference $\dfrac{2\pi}{3}$: $I=\dfrac{I_0}{4}+\dfrac{I_0}{4}+2\cdot\dfrac{I_0}{4}\cos\dfrac{2\pi}{3}=\dfrac{I_0}{4}$.

  41. JEE Main 2025, 28 January, Shift 1 · Question 50
    A double slit interference experiment performed with a light of wavelength 600 nm forms an interference fringe pattern on a screen with 10th bright fringe having its centre at a distance of 10 mm from the central maximum. Distance of the centre of the same 10th bright fringe from the central maximum when the source of light is replaced by another source of wavelength 660 nm would be______mm.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 11

    $y=10\times\dfrac{660}{600}=11$ mm.
