  1. JEE Main 2026, 2 April, Shift 1 · Question 33
    Heat is supplied to a diatomic gas at constant pressure. Then the ratio of $\Delta Q : \Delta U : \Delta W$ is ______.

    1. $2:3:5$
    2. $5:3:2$
    3. $2:5:7$
    4. $7:5:2$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    For a diatomic gas $C_V = \tfrac{5}{2}R$, $C_P = \tfrac{7}{2}R$.
    At constant pressure: $\Delta Q = nC_P\Delta T$, $\Delta U = nC_V\Delta T$, $\Delta W = nR\Delta T$.
    $\Delta Q:\Delta U:\Delta W = \tfrac{7}{2}:\tfrac{5}{2}:1 = 7:5:2$.

  2. JEE Main 2026, 2 April, Shift 1 · Question 47
    A vessel contains $0.15\ \mathrm{m^3}$ of a gas at pressure 8 bar and temperature 140 °C with $c_p = 3R$ and $c_v = 2R$. It is expanded adiabatically till pressure falls to 1 bar. The work done during this process is ________ k J. (R is gas constant)

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 120

    $\gamma = \dfrac{c_p}{c_v} = 1.5$.
    $V_2 = V_1\left(\dfrac{P_1}{P_2}\right)^{1/\gamma} = 0.15\times8^{2/3} = 0.6\ \mathrm{m^3}$.
    $W = \dfrac{P_1V_1 - P_2V_2}{\gamma-1} = \dfrac{8\times10^5\times0.15 - 10^5\times0.6}{0.5} = \dfrac{0.6\times10^5}{0.5} = 1.2\times10^5$ J $= 120$ kJ.

  3. JEE Main 2026, 2 April, Shift 2 · Question 48
    5 moles of unknown gas is heated at constant volume from 10 $^\circ$C to 20 $^\circ$C. The molar specific heat of this gas at constant pressure $c_p=8$ cal/mol.$^\circ$C and $R=8.36$ J/mol.$^\circ$C. The change in the internal energy of the gas is __________ calorie.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 300

    $R=8.36$ J/mol $^\circ$C $=\dfrac{8.36}{4.18}=2$ cal/mol $^\circ$C.
    $c_v=c_p-R=8-2=6$ cal/mol $^\circ$C.
    $\Delta U=nc_v\Delta T=5\times6\times10=300$ cal.

  4. JEE Main 2026, 4 April, Shift 1 · Question 36
    An ideal gas undergoes a process maintaining relation between pressure $(P)$ and volume $(V)$ as $P=P_0\left(1+\left(\dfrac{V_0}{V}\right)^2\right)^{-1}$, where $P_0$ and $V_0$ are constants. If two samples $A$ and $B$ (two moles each) with initial volumes $V_0$ and $3V_0$ respectively undergo above mentioned process and attain same pressure, then the difference at the temperatures of these samples, $T_B-T_A$ is __________.
    ($R=$ gas constant)

    1. $\dfrac{9P_0V_0}{8R}$
    2. $\dfrac{11P_0V_0}{10R}$
    3. $\dfrac{7P_0V_0}{6R}$
    4. $\dfrac{13P_0V_0}{11R}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: Dropped: full marks to everyone

    Both samples follow the same $P$–$V$ relation, so at the same pressure they would have the same volume and the same temperature; the question as worded is ambiguous.
    The intended reading compares the two initial states, with $T=\dfrac{PV}{nR}$, $n=2$:
    $A$: $V=V_0$, $P=\dfrac{P_0}{2}$, $T_A=\dfrac{P_0V_0}{4R}$.
    $B$: $V=3V_0$, $P=\dfrac{P_0}{1+\frac19}=\dfrac{9P_0}{10}$, $T_B=\dfrac{27P_0V_0}{20R}$.
    $T_B-T_A=\dfrac{27-5}{20}\cdot\dfrac{P_0V_0}{R}=\dfrac{11P_0V_0}{10R}$.

    Note: Dropped in the official final key: full marks to everyone.

  5. JEE Main 2026, 5 April, Shift 1 · Question 32
    Given below are two statements: one is labelled as Assertion A and the other is labelled as Reason R
    Statement I: Change in internal energy of a system containing $n$ mole of ideal gas can be written as $\Delta U = nC_v(T_f - T_i) = \dfrac{nR}{\gamma-1}(T_f - T_i)$, where $\gamma = \dfrac{C_p}{C_v}$, $T_i$ = initial temperature, $T_f$ = final temperature.
    Statement II: Relation between degree of freedom $f$ and $\gamma\ (= C_p/C_v)$ is $\left(\gamma = 1 + \dfrac{2}{f}\right)$
    Choose the correct answer from the options given below

    1. Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation of A
    2. Both A and R are true but R is NOT the correct explanation of A
    3. A is true but R is false
    4. A is false but R is true
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    A: for an ideal gas $\Delta U=nC_v\Delta T$ and $C_p-C_v=R \Rightarrow C_v=\dfrac{R}{\gamma-1}$. True.
    R: $C_v=\dfrac f2R$, $C_p=\left(\dfrac f2+1\right)R \Rightarrow \gamma=1+\dfrac2f$. True.
    A follows from Mayer's relation $C_p-C_v=R$, not from the degrees-of-freedom relation, so R is not the correct explanation of A.

  6. JEE Main 2026, 5 April, Shift 1 · Question 33
    Consider the following statements:
    A. Zeroth law of thermodynamics gives concept of temperature
    B. First law of thermodynamics gives concept of internal energy
    C. In isothermal expansion of ideal gas, $\Delta Q \neq \Delta W$
    D. Product of intensive and extensive variables is extensive
    E. The ratio of any extensive variable to mass will be an extensive variable
    Choose the correct combination of statements from the options given below:

    1. C, D and E Only
    2. A, B and C Only
    3. A, B and D Only
    4. B, C and D Only
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    A: true (thermal equilibrium defines temperature). B: true (first law introduces internal energy as a state function).
    C: false — for an ideal gas at constant $T$, $\Delta U=0$, so $\Delta Q=\Delta W$.
    D: true — e.g. $P\times V$ scales with the size of the system.
    E: false — extensive/mass (e.g. specific volume) is intensive.
    Correct: A, B and D.

  7. JEE Main 2026, 6 April, Shift 1 · Question 50
    A certain gas is isothermally compressed to $\left(\dfrac13\right)^{\text{rd}}$ of its initial volume ($V_0=3$ litre) by applying required pressure. If the bulk modulus of the gas is $3\times10^5$ N/m$^2$, the magnitude of work done on the gas is _____ J.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 600

    For an isothermal process the bulk modulus equals the pressure, $B=p$, so the applied pressure is taken as $p=3\times10^5$ N/m$^2$.
    Change in volume $\Delta V=3-1=2$ litre $=2\times10^{-3}$ m$^3$.
    $W=p\,\Delta V=3\times10^5\times2\times10^{-3}=600$ J (the official answer).
    (Note: this treats the pressure as constant; the exact isothermal work $p_0V_0\ln3$ would be about $989$ J.)

    Note: The official final key gives 600, which treats the pressure as constant (W = pΔV with p = B). The exact isothermal work is p₀V₀ ln 3 ≈ 989 J; the key is followed for marking.

  8. JEE Main 2026, 6 April, Shift 2 · Question 30
    A cylinder with adiabatic walls is closed at both ends and is divided into two compartments by a frictionless adiabatic piston. Ideal gas is filled in both (left and right) the compartments at same $P$, $V$, $T$. Heating is started from left side until pressure changes to $\dfrac{27P}{8}$. If initial volume of each compartment was 9 litres then the final volume in right-hand side compartment is ________ litres. (for this ideal gas $C_P/C_V=1.5$)

    1. $3$
    2. $4$
    3. $14$
    4. $9$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    The gas on the right is compressed slowly and adiabatically, and its final pressure equals the left side's, $\dfrac{27P}{8}$.
    $PV^{\gamma}=$ constant: $V_2=9\left(\dfrac{8}{27}\right)^{1/1.5}=9\left(\dfrac{8}{27}\right)^{2/3}=9\cdot\dfrac49=4$ litres.

  9. JEE Main 2026, 8 April, Shift 2 · Question 35
    One mole of diatomic gas having rotational modes only is kept in a cylinder with a piston system. The cross-section area of the cylinder is $4\ \text{cm}^2$. The gas is heated slowly to raise the temperature by $1.2\ {}^\circ\text{C}$ during which the piston moves by 25 mm. The amount of heat supplied to the gas is __________ J. (Atmospheric pressure $=100$ kPa, $R=8.3$ J/mol.K) (Neglect mass of the piston)

    1. $24.8$
    2. $25$
    3. $15.04$
    4. $29.98$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a) or (b) (either is accepted)

    Translational and rotational modes only: $C_v=\dfrac52R$, so $\Delta U=nC_v\Delta T=1\times\dfrac52\times8.3\times1.2=24.9$ J.
    Work against the atmosphere: $W=P\,A\,x=10^5\times4\times10^{-4}\times0.025=1$ J.
    The data are not consistent with each other (for 1 mol at constant pressure $P\Delta V$ should equal $nR\Delta T\approx10$ J), so $Q=\Delta U+W$ does not give a listed value exactly. The intended answer is the heat $\approx nC_v\Delta T\approx25$ J.
    The official final key accepts both $24.8$ and $25$.

    Note: Official final key: (1) or (2) (either option is awarded marks).

  10. JEE Main 2026, 8 April, Shift 2 · Question 36
    Initial pressure and volume of a monoatomic ideal gas are $P$ and $V$. The change in internal energy of this gas in adiabatic expansion to volume $V_{final}=27\,V$ is __________ J.

    1. $-2PV\left(3\sqrt{3}-1\right)$
    2. $\dfrac{4}{3}PV$
    3. $-\dfrac{4}{3}PV$
    4. $\dfrac{3}{4}PV$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    Monoatomic: $\gamma=\dfrac53$, $TV^{2/3}=$ constant, so $T_f=T\left(\dfrac1{27}\right)^{2/3}=\dfrac T9$.
    $\Delta U=\dfrac32nR(T_f-T)=\dfrac32PV\left(\dfrac19-1\right)=-\dfrac43PV$.
