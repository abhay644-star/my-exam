    Given below are two statements :
    Statement (I) : 1,2,3-Trihydroxypropane can be separated from water by simple distillation.
    Statement (II) : An azeotropic mixture cannot be separated by fractional distillation.
    In the light of the above statements, choose the correct answer from the options given below :

    1. Both Statement I and Statement II are true
    2. Both Statement I and Statement II are false
    3. Statement I is true but Statement II is false
    4. Statement I is false but Statement II is true
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    1,2,3-Trihydroxypropane (glycerol) decomposes at/below its normal boiling point (~563 K), so it is separated/purified by distillation under reduced pressure, not by simple distillation: Statement I is false.
    An azeotrope boils at constant temperature with vapour of the same composition as the liquid, so fractional distillation cannot separate it: Statement II is true.

    1. x > y > z

    4. x > z > y
    Show answer and solution
