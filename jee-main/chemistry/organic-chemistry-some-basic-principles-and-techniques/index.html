  1. JEE Main 2026, 2 April, Shift 1 · Question 63
    Given below are two statements :
    Statement (I) : 1,2,3-Trihydroxypropane can be separated from water by simple distillation.
    Statement (II) : An azeotropic mixture cannot be separated by fractional distillation.
    In the light of the above statements, choose the correct answer from the options given below :

    1. Both Statement I and Statement II are true
    2. Both Statement I and Statement II are false
    3. Statement I is true but Statement II is false
    4. Statement I is false but Statement II is true
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    1,2,3-Trihydroxypropane (glycerol) decomposes at/below its normal boiling point (~563 K), so it is separated/purified by distillation under reduced pressure, not by simple distillation: Statement I is false.
    An azeotrope boils at constant temperature with vapour of the same composition as the liquid, so fractional distillation cannot separate it: Statement II is true.

    In IUPAC nomenclature, the correct order of decreasing priority of functional group is :

    1. $\mathrm{-CONH_2}$, $\mathrm{>C=O}$, $\mathrm{-CHO}$, $\mathrm{-NH_2}$, $\mathrm{-C\equiv C-}$
    2. $\mathrm{-CONH_2}$, $\mathrm{-COOCH_3}$, $\mathrm{-CHO}$, $\mathrm{-NH_2}$, $\mathrm{-OH}$
    3. $\mathrm{-CONH_2}$, $\mathrm{-CHO}$, $\mathrm{>C=O}$, $\mathrm{-NH_2}$, $\mathrm{-C\equiv C-}$
    4. $\mathrm{-CONH_2}$, $\mathrm{-CHO}$, $\mathrm{-CN}$, $\mathrm{-NH_2}$, $\mathrm{-C\equiv C-}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    IUPAC seniority: acid > ester > acid halide > amide > nitrile > aldehyde > ketone > alcohol > amine > alkene/alkyne.
    So $\mathrm{-CONH_2 > -CHO > \ >C=O > -NH_2 > -C\equiv C-}$.
    (Ester ranks above amide and $\mathrm{-OH}$ above $\mathrm{-NH_2}$; nitrile ranks above aldehyde — so the other options are wrong.)

  4. JEE Main 2026, 2 April, Shift 2 · Question 63
    Given below are two statements :
    Statement I : In $\mathrm{O_2N{-}C_6H_4{-}\overset{\oplus}{C}H{-}C_6H_4{-}OCH_3}$ (both benzene rings para-substituted), the carbocation is stabilised by $+R$ effect of $-\mathrm{OCH_3}$ group.
    Statement II : In $\mathrm{O_2N{-}C_6H_4{-}\overset{\ominus}{C}H{-}C_6H_4{-}OCH_3}$ (both benzene rings para-substituted), the carbanion is stabilised by $-R$ effect of $-\mathrm{NO_2}$ group.
    In the light of the above statements, choose the correct answer from the options given below :

    1. Both Statement I and Statement II are true
    2. Both Statement I and Statement II are false
    3. Statement I is true but Statement II is false
    4. Statement I is false but Statement II is true
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    The para $-\mathrm{OCH_3}$ group donates its lone pair through the ring ($+R$) into the empty p orbital of the carbocation carbon, stabilising it. Statement I is true.

    Both statements are true.

  9. JEE Main 2026, 4 April, Shift 2 · Question 61
    Consider the following molecules/species:
    The correct order of carbon – oxygen double bond length is :

    1. x > y > z
    2. y > z > x
    3. z > x > y
    4. x > z > y
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    The more single-bond character the C=O bond has, the longer it is.
    (y) Acetone: an ordinary C=O with little delocalisation, so it is the shortest.
    (z) Acetate: the negative charge is shared equally by the two oxygens, C–O bond order 1.5.
    (x) Tropone: the dipolar form $\mathrm{C^+{-}O^-}$ gives an aromatic tropylium ring ($6\pi$ electrons), so this form contributes very strongly and the C=O bond has the most single-bond character.
    Order of C=O bond length: x > z > y.

    Statement II :
    The boiling point of a liquid increases as the external pressure is reduced.
    In the light of the above statements, choose the correct answer from the options given below :

    1. Both Statement I and Statement II are true
    2. Both Statement I and Statement II are false
    3. Statement I is true but Statement II is false
    4. Statement I is false but Statement II is true
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    Sublimation purifies compounds that pass directly from solid to vapour (sublimable compounds), not compounds simply because they have a low melting point: Statement I is false.
    A liquid boils when its vapour pressure equals the external pressure, so lowering the pressure lowers the boiling point (the basis of distillation under reduced pressure): Statement II is false.

    Given below are two statements:
    Statement I: $\mathrm{(CH_3)_3\overset{\oplus}{C}}$ is more stable than $\mathrm{\overset{\oplus}{C}H_3}$ as nine hyperconjugation interactions are possible in $\mathrm{(CH_3)_3\overset{\oplus}{C}}$.
    Statement II: $\mathrm{\overset{\oplus}{C}H_3}$ is less stable than $\mathrm{(CH_3)_3\overset{\oplus}{C}}$ as only three hyperconjugation interactions are possible in $\mathrm{\overset{\oplus}{C}H_3}$.
    In the light of the above statements, choose the correct answer from the options given below

    1. Both Statement I and Statement II are true
    2. Both Statement I and Statement II are false
    3. Statement I is true but Statement II is false
    4. Statement I is false but Statement II is true
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    The tert-butyl cation has 9 C-H bonds next to the positive carbon, giving nine hyperconjugation structures, so it is much more stable: Statement I is true.

  33. JEE Main 2026, 24 January, Shift 1 · Question 63
    Arrange the following carbanions in the decreasing order of stability.
    I. $p-\mathrm{Br-C_6H_4-\overset{\ominus}{\ddot{C}}H_2}$
    II. $\mathrm{C_6H_5-\overset{\ominus}{\ddot{C}}H_2}$
    III. $p-\mathrm{CH_3O-C_6H_4-\overset{\ominus}{\ddot{C}}H_2}$
    IV. $p-\mathrm{CHO-C_6H_4-\overset{\ominus}{\ddot{C}}H_2}$
    V. $p-\mathrm{CH_3-C_6H_4-\overset{\ominus}{\ddot{C}}H_2}$
    Choose the correct answer from the options given below:

    1. IV > I > II > V > III
    2. IV > II > I > III > V
    3. I > IV > II > V > III
    4. I > II > IV > V > III
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    Electron-withdrawing groups stabilise a benzylic carbanion; electron-donating groups destabilise it.
    p-CHO ($-M$) stabilises most; p-Br ($-I$) stabilises a little; H is the reference; p-$\mathrm{CH_3}$ (+I, hyperconjugation) destabilises; p-$\mathrm{OCH_3}$ (+M) destabilises most.
    IV > I > II > V > III.

    Find out the statements which are not true.

    Choose the correct answer from the options given below:

    3. B, D & E only
    4. A, D & E only
    Show answer and solution

    0.25 g of an organic compound "A" containing carbon, hydrogen and oxygen was analysed using the combustion method. There was an increase in mass of $\mathrm{CaCl_2}$ tube and potash tube at the end of the experiment. The amount was found to be 0.15 g and 0.1837 g, respectively. The percentage of oxygen in compound A is ______ %. (Nearest integer)
    (Given: molar mass in g mol$^{-1}$ H : 1, C : 12, O : 16)

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 73

    $\mathrm{CaCl_2}$ absorbs water: H $=0.15\times\dfrac2{18}=0.0167$ g. Potash absorbs $\mathrm{CO_2}$: C $=0.1837\times\dfrac{12}{44}=0.0501$ g.

  37. JEE Main 2026, 28 January, Shift 1 · Question 62
    Method used for separation of mixture of products (B and C) obtained in the following reaction is

    1. sublimation
    2. steam distillation
    3. fractional distillation
    4. simple distillation
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    Benzene with $\mathrm{Br_2/FeBr_3}$ gives bromobenzene (A); nitration then gives 1-bromo-2-nitrobenzene and 1-bromo-4-nitrobenzene (B and C).
    The two isomers have fairly close boiling points (roughly $261\ ^\circ$C and $256\ ^\circ$C), so among the methods listed the final key takes fractional distillation, the method used when boiling points are close.
    Neither isomer sublimes, and unlike o-nitrophenol neither is separated by steam distillation (no intramolecular hydrogen bonding).

    Note: The official final key gives fractional distillation. In practice the two isomers are also commonly separated by fractional crystallisation (their melting points differ widely), which is not among the options.
