  1. JEE Main 2026, 2 April, Shift 1 · Question 63
    Given below are two statements :
    Statement (I) : 1,2,3-Trihydroxypropane can be separated from water by simple distillation.
    Statement (II) : An azeotropic mixture cannot be separated by fractional distillation.
    In the light of the above statements, choose the correct answer from the options given below :

    1. Both Statement I and Statement II are true
    2. Both Statement I and Statement II are false
    3. Statement I is true but Statement II is false
    4. Statement I is false but Statement II is true
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    1,2,3-Trihydroxypropane (glycerol) decomposes at/below its normal boiling point (~563 K), so it is separated/purified by distillation under reduced pressure, not by simple distillation: Statement I is false.
    An azeotrope boils at constant temperature with vapour of the same composition as the liquid, so fractional distillation cannot separate it: Statement II is true.

  2. JEE Main 2026, 2 April, Shift 1 · Question 65
    In IUPAC nomenclature, the correct order of decreasing priority of functional group is :

    1. $\mathrm{-CONH_2}$, $\mathrm{>C=O}$, $\mathrm{-CHO}$, $\mathrm{-NH_2}$, $\mathrm{-C\equiv C-}$
    2. $\mathrm{-CONH_2}$, $\mathrm{-COOCH_3}$, $\mathrm{-CHO}$, $\mathrm{-NH_2}$, $\mathrm{-OH}$
    3. $\mathrm{-CONH_2}$, $\mathrm{-CHO}$, $\mathrm{>C=O}$, $\mathrm{-NH_2}$, $\mathrm{-C\equiv C-}$
    4. $\mathrm{-CONH_2}$, $\mathrm{-CHO}$, $\mathrm{-CN}$, $\mathrm{-NH_2}$, $\mathrm{-C\equiv C-}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    IUPAC seniority: acid > ester > acid halide > amide > nitrile > aldehyde > ketone > alcohol > amine > alkene/alkyne.
    So $\mathrm{-CONH_2 > -CHO > \ >C=O > -NH_2 > -C\equiv C-}$.
    (Ester ranks above amide and $\mathrm{-OH}$ above $\mathrm{-NH_2}$; nitrile ranks above aldehyde — so the other options are wrong.)

  3. JEE Main 2026, 2 April, Shift 2 · Question 62
    Complete combustion of X g of an organic compound gave 0.25 g of CO$_2$ and 0.12 g of H$_2$O. If the % of carbon is 25% and of hydrogen is 4.89%, then X = _____ $\times10^{-3}$ g. (Nearest integer)
    (Molar mass of C, H and O are 12, 1 and 16 g mol$^{-1}$ respectively.)

    1. $273$
    2. $27$
    3. $2730$
    4. $227$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    Mass of C $=0.25\times\dfrac{12}{44}=0.0682$ g; this is 25% of X.
    $X=\dfrac{0.0682}{0.25}=0.273$ g $=273\times10^{-3}$ g.
    Check: H $=0.12\times\dfrac{2}{18}=0.01333$ g, and $\dfrac{0.01333}{0.273}\times100=4.89\%$.

  4. JEE Main 2026, 2 April, Shift 2 · Question 63
    Given below are two statements :
    Statement I : In $\mathrm{O_2N{-}C_6H_4{-}\overset{\oplus}{C}H{-}C_6H_4{-}OCH_3}$ (both benzene rings para-substituted), the carbocation is stabilised by $+R$ effect of $-\mathrm{OCH_3}$ group.
    Statement II : In $\mathrm{O_2N{-}C_6H_4{-}\overset{\ominus}{C}H{-}C_6H_4{-}OCH_3}$ (both benzene rings para-substituted), the carbanion is stabilised by $-R$ effect of $-\mathrm{NO_2}$ group.
    In the light of the above statements, choose the correct answer from the options given below :

    1. Both Statement I and Statement II are true
    2. Both Statement I and Statement II are false
    3. Statement I is true but Statement II is false
    4. Statement I is false but Statement II is true
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    The para $-\mathrm{OCH_3}$ group donates its lone pair through the ring ($+R$) into the empty p orbital of the carbocation carbon, stabilising it. Statement I is true.
    The para $-\mathrm{NO_2}$ group withdraws the negative charge of the carbanion through the ring ($-R$), delocalising it onto oxygen. Statement II is true.
    Both statements are true.

  5. JEE Main 2026, 2 April, Shift 2 · Question 75
    Consider the following reactions sequence (starting from 4-nitrotoluene, shown in the figure; reagents (i) Sn/HCl; OH$^-$ (ii) (CH$_3$CO)$_2$O (iii) Br$_2$/AlBr$_3$ (iv) H$_3$O$^+$, giving Major Product (P)).
    When the product (P) is subjected to Carius analysis using AgNO$_3$, 1.0 g of the product (P) will produce __________ g of the precipitate of AgBr. (Nearest Integer)
    (Given : molar mass in g mol$^{-1}$ C : 12, H : 1, O : 16, N : 14, Br : 80, Ag : 108)

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 1

    (i) 4-nitrotoluene $\to$ 4-methylaniline; (ii) acetylation $\to$ 4-methylacetanilide.
    (iii) Bromination ortho to the more activating $-\mathrm{NHCOCH_3}$ group; (iv) hydrolysis $\to$ P = 2-bromo-4-methylaniline, C$_7$H$_8$BrN, $M=186$ g/mol.
    1 mol P gives 1 mol AgBr (188 g): mass from 1.0 g $=\dfrac{188}{186}\approx1.01$ g.
    Nearest integer: 1.

  6. JEE Main 2026, 4 April, Shift 1 · Question 63
    Increasing order of electron withdrawing power of following functional groups is:
    a. $-\mathrm{CN}$
    b. $-\mathrm{COOH}$
    c. $-\mathrm{NO_2}$
    d. $-\mathrm{I}$

    1. c < b < d < a
    2. c < a < b < d
    3. d < b < a < c
    4. a < b < c < d
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    Electron-withdrawing (−I) strength: $-\mathrm{NO_2}>-\mathrm{CN}>-\mathrm{COOH}>-\mathrm{I}$.
    Increasing order: d < b < a < c.

  7. JEE Main 2026, 4 April, Shift 1 · Question 73
    $2.0$ g of a bromo hydrocarbon (X) was subjected to Carius analysis, gave $3.36$ g of AgBr. The percentage of carbon in the compound (X) is $26.7\%$. Total number of carbon atoms in the empirical formula for compound (X) is _______.
    (Given molar mass in $\mathrm{g\,mol^{-1}}$ H:1, C:12, Br:80, Ag:108)

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 5

    %Br $=\dfrac{80}{188}\times\dfrac{3.36}{2.0}\times100=71.5\%$; %H $=100-71.5-26.7=1.8\%$.
    Mole ratio C : H : Br $=\dfrac{26.7}{12}:\dfrac{1.8}{1}:\dfrac{71.5}{80}=2.225:1.8:0.894\approx2.5:2:1$.
    Empirical formula $\mathrm{C_5H_4Br_2}$: $5$ carbon atoms.

  8. JEE Main 2026, 4 April, Shift 2 · Question 56
    Match the LIST-I with LIST-II

    List-I
    Reaction    		List-II
    Mechanism
    A. Williamson SynthesisI. Electrophilic addition
    B. Friedel Craft ReactionII. Free radical substitution
    C. Bromination of vinyl benzeneIII. Nucleophilic substitution
    D. Chlorination of toluene in lightIV. Electrophilic substitution
    Choose the correct answer from the options given below:

    1. A-III, B-I, C-II, D-IV
    2. A-III, B-IV, C-II, D-I
    3. A-III, B-IV, C-I, D-II
    4. A-I, B-III, C-IV, D-II
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    D: Side-chain chlorination of toluene in light is free radical substitution (II).
    A-III, B-IV, C-I, D-II.

  9. JEE Main 2026, 4 April, Shift 2 · Question 61
    Consider the following molecules/species:
    The correct order of carbon – oxygen double bond length is :

    1. x > y > z
    2. y > z > x
    3. z > x > y
    4. x > z > y
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    The more single-bond character the C=O bond has, the longer it is.
    (y) Acetone: an ordinary C=O with little delocalisation, so it is the shortest.
    (z) Acetate: the negative charge is shared equally by the two oxygens, C–O bond order 1.5.
    (x) Tropone: the dipolar form $\mathrm{C^+{-}O^-}$ gives an aromatic tropylium ring ($6\pi$ electrons), so this form contributes very strongly and the C=O bond has the most single-bond character.
    Order of C=O bond length: x > z > y.

    In sulphur estimation, $2.0\times10^{-3}$ mol of an organic compound (X) (molar mass 76 g mol$^{-1}$) gave 0.4813 g of barium sulphate (molar mass 233 g mol$^{-1}$). The percentage of sulphur in the compound (X) is ___________ $\times10^{-1}$ % (Nearest integer)

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 435

    Mass of X $=2.0\times10^{-3}\times76=0.152$ g.
    Mass of S $=\dfrac{32}{233}\times0.4813=0.06610$ g.
    % S $=\dfrac{0.06610}{0.152}\times100=43.49\%=434.9\times10^{-1}\%$.
    Nearest integer: $435$.

  11. JEE Main 2026, 5 April, Shift 1 · Question 63
    Given below are two statements:
    Statement I: On the basis of inductive effect, the order of stability of alkyl carbanions is $\mathrm{CH_3^-} > \mathrm{CH_3{-}CH_2^-} > \mathrm{(CH_3)_2CH^-} > \mathrm{(CH_3)_3C^-}$.
    Statement II: Allyl and benzyl carbanions are more stabilised by inductive effect and not by resonance effect.
    In the light of the above statements, choose the correct answer from the options given below

    1. Both Statement I and Statement II are correct
    2. Both Statement I and Statement II are incorrect
    3. Statement I is correct but Statement II is incorrect
    4. Statement I is incorrect but Statement II is correct
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    I: alkyl groups are electron-releasing (+I) and destabilise a carbanion, so stability falls from methyl to tertiary. Correct.
    II: allyl and benzyl carbanions are stabilised by resonance (delocalisation of the negative charge), not by the inductive effect. Incorrect.

  12. JEE Main 2026, 5 April, Shift 1 · Question 72
    In an estimation of sulphur by Carius method $0.2$ g of the substance gave $0.6$ g of $\mathrm{BaSO_4}$. The percentage of sulphur in the substance is _______%.
    (Given molar mass in g mol$^{-1}$ S : $32$, $\mathrm{BaSO_4}$ : $231$)

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 41 or 42

    % S $=\dfrac{32}{M(\mathrm{BaSO_4})}\times\dfrac{m(\mathrm{BaSO_4})}{m(\text{substance})}\times100$.
    $=\dfrac{32}{231}\times\dfrac{0.6}{0.2}\times100=\dfrac{9600}{231}\approx41.56$.
    The official final key accepts $41$ or $42$.

    Note: Official final key: 41 or 42 (either answer is awarded marks).

  13. JEE Main 2026, 5 April, Shift 2 · Question 62
    Match List - I with List - II.

    List - I
    Purification technique    		List - II
    Used to separate
    A. Simple distillationI. Steam volatile compound
    B. Fractional distillationII. Two liquids with large difference in boiling points
    C. Steam distillationIII. Liquid decomposing at its boiling point
    D. Distillation under reduced pressureIV. Two liquids with close boiling points
    Choose the correct answer from the options given below :

    1. A-II, B-III, C-I, D-IV
    2. A-II, B-IV, C-I, D-III

    1. 1, 1 - Dimethylcyclopropane
    2. 2, 2 - Dimethylpropane
    3. 2 - Methylbutane
    4. n-pentane
    Show answer and solution

  15. JEE Main 2026, 6 April, Shift 1 · Question 63
    $R_f$ value for 2-methylpropene in a solvent system (Ethyl acetate + ether) is 0.42. 2-methylpropene is treated with dilute $\mathrm{H_2SO_4}$ to give major organic product (X). $R_f$ value for (X) in the same solvent system under identical condition will be:

    1. $0.42$
    2. $0.82$
    3. $0.62$
    4. $0.12$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    Acid-catalysed hydration (Markovnikov) gives X $=$ tert-butyl alcohol, $\mathrm{(CH_3)_3COH}$.
    The alcohol is far more polar than the alkene and is held more strongly by the stationary phase, so it moves less.
    Its $R_f$ must be lower than 0.42: 0.12.

  16. JEE Main 2026, 6 April, Shift 1 · Question 64
    Given below are two statements:
    Statement I: 2,6-diethylcyclohexanone and 6-methyl-2-n-propylcyclohexanone are metamers.
    Statement II: 2,2,6,6 - tetramethylcyclohexanone exhibits keto-enol tautomerism.
    In the light of the above statements, choose the correct answer from the options given below

    1. Both Statement I and Statement II are true
    2. Both Statement I and Statement II are false
    3. Statement I is true but Statement II is false
    4. Statement I is false but Statement II is true
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    Statement I: both are $\mathrm{C_{10}H_{18}O}$ ketones that differ only in the alkyl groups on the two sides of the C=O group (ethyl/ethyl versus methyl/n-propyl): metamers, true.

    Statement II : Rose essence from rose petals is seperated by steam distillation due to its high volatility and insolubility in $\mathrm{H_2O}$.

  18. JEE Main 2026, 6 April, Shift 2 · Question 63
    Shown below is the structure of methyl acetate with three different $\alpha$, $\beta$ and $\gamma$ carbon - oxygen bonds. The correct order of bond lengths of these bonds is :

    1. $\alpha>\beta>\gamma$
    2. $\alpha<\beta<\gamma$
    3. $\alpha=\beta=\gamma$
    4. $\alpha<\beta=\gamma$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    $\alpha$ is the C=O double bond: shortest.
    $\beta$ (acyl C–O) gains partial double bond character by resonance of the O lone pair with C=O: $\mathrm{CH_3C(=O)-OCH_3 \leftrightarrow CH_3C(-O^-)=O^+CH_3}$.
    $\gamma$ ($\mathrm{O-CH_3}$) is a pure single bond: longest.
    Order: $\alpha<\beta<\gamma$.

  19. JEE Main 2026, 8 April, Shift 2 · Question 62
    Given below are two statements :
    Statement I : Vapours of the liquid with higher boiling point condense before vapours of the liquid with lower boiling points in fractional distillation.
    Statement II : The vapours rising up in the fractionating column become richer in high boiling component of the mixture.
    In the light of the above statements, choose the correct answer from the options given below :

    1. Both Statement I and Statement II are true
    2. Both Statement I and Statement II are false
    3. Statement I is true but Statement II is false
    4. Statement I is false but Statement II is true
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    In the fractionating column the higher boiling component condenses first and runs back, so Statement I is true.
    Hence the vapours rising up become richer in the lower boiling component, so Statement II is false.
