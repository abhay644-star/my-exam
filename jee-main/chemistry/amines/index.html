  1. JEE Main 2026, 2 April, Shift 1 · Question 66
    For the given molecule, “$x$”, the preferred site for the attack of the electrophile is :

    1. Predominantly at “r”
    2. “r” and “u”
    3. “p” and “s”
    4. Predominantly at “u”
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    $x$ is benzanilide, $\mathrm{C_6H_5CO{-}NH{-}C_6H_5}$.
    The ring on N carries $\mathrm{-NHCOR}$: the N lone pair activates it and directs ortho/para.
    The ring on C=O carries $\mathrm{-CONHR}$, which is deactivating (meta-directing), so it is not attacked.
    On the activated ring the bulky amide group hinders the ortho position (s), so attack is predominantly para, at “u”.

  2. JEE Main 2026, 2 April, Shift 1 · Question 68
    Consider the three aromatic molecules (P, Q and R) whose structures have been given below :
    The correct order regarding the reactivity of these compounds with $\mathrm{Ph-\underset{(+)}{N}\equiv N\,Cl^{(-)}}$ under optimum but slightly acidic medium is :

    1. P > Q > R
    2. R > P > Q
    3. R > Q > P
    4. P > R > Q
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    Diazo coupling is electrophilic substitution at the para position, which needs the $\mathrm{NMe_2}$ lone pair in resonance with the ring.
    Ortho-methyl groups force the $\mathrm{NMe_2}$ group out of the ring plane (steric inhibition of resonance), reducing activation.
    P (no ortho Me) is most activated, Q (one ortho Me) less, R (two ortho Me) least.
    Order: P > Q > R.

  3. JEE Main 2026, 2 April, Shift 2 · Question 68
    Identify compounds A and E in the following reaction sequence.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    In 4-nitroethylbenzene the ethyl group directs ortho/para and the NO$_2$ group directs meta; both favour the position ortho to C$_2$H$_5$. A = 2-bromo-1-ethyl-4-nitrobenzene.
    Sn/HCl reduces NO$_2$ to NH$_2$ (B); NaNO$_2$/HCl at 273-278 K gives the diazonium salt (C).

  4. JEE Main 2026, 4 April, Shift 1 · Question 67
    Product C of the following reaction sequence will be

    1. 1-Bromo-4-nitrobenzene
    2. 1, 3, 5-Tribromo-2-nitrobenzene
    3. 4-Bromo-1-nitrobenzene
    4. 1, 3, 5-Tribromobenzene
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    Aniline with bromine water gives 2,4,6-tribromoaniline (A).
    Diazotisation at 273–278 K gives 2,4,6-tribromobenzenediazonium chloride (B).
    $\mathrm{HBF_4}$ gives the diazonium fluoroborate, which with $\mathrm{NaNO_2}$/Cu on heating replaces $\mathrm{-N_2^+}$ by $\mathrm{-NO_2}$.
    C is 2,4,6-tribromonitrobenzene = 1,3,5-tribromo-2-nitrobenzene.

  5. JEE Main 2026, 4 April, Shift 2 · Question 67
    The strongest conjugate acid will result from:

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    The strongest conjugate acid comes from the weakest base.
    $-\mathrm{NO_2}$ at the para position withdraws electron density from $-\mathrm{NH_2}$ (by $-R$ and $-I$), making p-nitroaniline the weakest base.
    $-\mathrm{OCH_3}$ and $-\mathrm{CH_3}$ donate electrons and make the amine more basic than aniline.
    So p-nitroanilinium ion is the strongest conjugate acid: option (3).

  6. JEE Main 2026, 5 April, Shift 1 · Question 66
    Benzyl isocyanide can be obtained from
    Choose the correct answer from the options given below:

    1. A and B Only
    2. A and C Only
    3. B and D Only
    4. D and E Only
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    A: benzyl bromide + AgCN — the covalent Ag–C bond leaves N to attack, giving benzyl isocyanide $\mathrm{C_6H_5CH_2NC}$. Correct.
    B: benzylamine + $\mathrm{CHCl_3}$/aq NaOH (carbylamine reaction) gives $\mathrm{C_6H_5CH_2NC}$. Correct.
    C: bromobenzene does not undergo this substitution (and would not give a benzyl compound).
    D: aniline gives phenyl isocyanide, not benzyl isocyanide.
    E: 2-phenylethyl bromide + KCN gives a nitrile ($\mathrm{C_6H_5CH_2CH_2CN}$).
    Correct: A and B.

  7. JEE Main 2026, 5 April, Shift 1 · Question 68
    Identify the incorrect statements.
    Choose the correct answer from the options given below:

    1. A and D Only
    2. A and C Only
    3. B and C Only
    4. A and B Only
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    A: benzylamine ($\mathrm{p}K_b\approx4.7$) is a stronger base than aniline ($\mathrm{p}K_b\approx9.4$), whose lone pair is delocalised into the ring. Correct statement.
    B: Gabriel synthesis cannot make aryl amines such as 4-methoxyaniline (aryl halides do not undergo $S_N2$ with phthalimide anion). Incorrect.
    C: Hofmann bromamide reaction of phenylacetamide gives benzylamine $\mathrm{C_6H_5CH_2NH_2}$, not a primary aromatic amine (NH$_2$ is not on the ring). Incorrect.
    D: 4-nitroaniline $\to$ diazonium salt $\to$ (warming) 4-nitrophenol, which is acidic and dissolves in NaOH. Correct statement.
    Incorrect statements: B and C.

  8. JEE Main 2026, 5 April, Shift 2 · Question 67
    Given below are two statements :
    Statement I : Heating benzamide with bromine in an ethanolic solution of sodium hydroxide will give benzylamine.
    Statement II : Nitration of aniline with $\mathrm{HNO_3/H_2SO_4}$ at 288 K produces m-nitroaniline in higher amount than o-nitroaniline (pH adjusted).
    In the light of the above statements, choose the correct answer from the options given below :

    1. Both Statement I and Statement II are true
    2. Both Statement I and Statement II are false
    3. Statement I is true but Statement II is false
    4. Statement I is false but Statement II is true
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    Hofmann bromamide degradation removes one carbon: $\mathrm{C_6H_5CONH_2 \rightarrow C_6H_5NH_2}$ (aniline), not benzylamine. Statement I is false.
    In strongly acidic medium aniline is protonated to anilinium ion (meta-directing), so nitration gives p-nitroaniline 51%, m-nitroaniline 47%, o-nitroaniline 2%. Statement II is true.

  9. JEE Main 2026, 6 April, Shift 1 · Question 68
    Arrange the following compounds according to increasing order of boiling points.
    n - $\mathrm{C_4H_9OH}$ (A), n - $\mathrm{C_4H_9NH_2}$ (B), n - $\mathrm{C_4H_{10}}$ (C) and $\mathrm{C_2H_5NHC_2H_5}$ (D).

    1. C < B < A < D
    2. D < C < B < A
    3. C < D < B < A
    4. D < B < A < C
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    n-Butane has no hydrogen bonding (lowest). Diethylamine (2$^\circ$, one N-H) bonds less than n-butylamine (1$^\circ$, two N-H). O-H hydrogen bonds are the strongest, so n-butanol is highest.
    Approximate b.p.: C (273 K) < D (329 K) < B (351 K) < A (391 K).
    C < D < B < A.

  10. JEE Main 2026, 6 April, Shift 1 · Question 72
    Consider the following sequence of reactions.
    The percentage of nitrogen in the yellow product (X) formed is ________%. (Nearest Integer)
    (Given Molar mass in g mol$^{-1}$ H:1, C:12, N:14)

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 21

    Diazoaminobenzene $\mathrm{C_6H_5N{=}N{-}NHC_6H_5}$ rearranges in acid with excess aniline (warm) to p-aminoazobenzene, $\mathrm{C_6H_5N{=}NC_6H_4NH_2}$, a yellow dye = X.
    X is $\mathrm{C_{12}H_{11}N_3}$: $M=144+11+42=197$ g mol$^{-1}$.
    %N $=\dfrac{42}{197}\times100\approx21.3\%$, i.e. $21$.

  11. JEE Main 2026, 6 April, Shift 2 · Question 67
    Consider the following organic reaction sequence. Choose the final product (X) from the following (consider the major product in all intermediate reactions)
    Propanoic acid: (i) $\mathrm{NH_3/\Delta}$ (ii) $\mathrm{NaOH/Br_2}$ (iii) $\mathrm{HNO_2/H_2O}$, 0°C (iv) $\mathrm{C_6H_5N_2Cl}$, 0°C → (X) Major Product

    1. Benzene
    2. Phenol
    3. Propanol
    4. Chlorobenzene
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    (i) $\mathrm{CH_3CH_2COOH+NH_3}$, heat → $\mathrm{CH_3CH_2CONH_2}$.
    (ii) Hofmann bromamide degradation → $\mathrm{CH_3CH_2NH_2}$.
    (iii) $\mathrm{HNO_2}$: the aliphatic diazonium salt decomposes to ethanol, $\mathrm{CH_3CH_2OH}$.
    (iv) Ethanol reduces benzenediazonium chloride: $\mathrm{C_6H_5N_2Cl+CH_3CH_2OH\to C_6H_6+N_2+CH_3CHO+HCl}$.
    X is benzene.

  12. JEE Main 2026, 6 April, Shift 2 · Question 68
    The number of compounds from the following which can undergo reaction with $\mathrm{Br_2}$/KOH (alcoholic) to give respective products and these respective products can also be obtained separately by Gabriel phthalimide reaction is :

    1. $5$
    2. $4$
    3. $3$
    4. $6$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    Hofmann bromamide degradation needs a primary amide ($\mathrm{-CONH_2}$); the N-substituted amides $\mathrm{C_6H_{11}CONHCH_2CH_3}$ and $\mathrm{(CH_3)_3CCONHCH_3}$ do not react.
    $\mathrm{C_6H_5CONH_2}\to\mathrm{C_6H_5NH_2}$: aniline cannot be made by the Gabriel synthesis (aryl halides do not react).
    $\mathrm{C_6H_5CH_2CONH_2}\to\mathrm{C_6H_5CH_2NH_2}$, $\mathrm{CH_3CONH_2}\to\mathrm{CH_3NH_2}$, $\mathrm{C_6H_{11}CONH_2}\to\mathrm{C_6H_{11}NH_2}$: primary alkyl amines, also obtainable by Gabriel synthesis.
    Number $=3$.

  13. JEE Main 2026, 8 April, Shift 2 · Question 67
    Consider the following reaction.
    The major product (P) formed is :

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    (i) $\mathrm{NaBH_4}$/MeOH reduces only the aldehyde to $\mathrm{CH_2OH}$; the lactam (amide) carbonyl is not reduced.

  14. JEE Main 2026, 8 April, Shift 2 · Question 68
    Which statements are True ?
    A. In Hoffmann bromamide degradation, 4 moles of NaOH and 2 moles of $\mathrm{Br_2}$ are consumed per mole of an amide
    B. Hoffmann bromamide reaction is not given by alkyl amides.
    C. Primary amines can be synthesized by Hoffmann bromamide degradation.
    D. Secondary amide on reaction with $\mathrm{Br_2}$ and NaOH will give secondary amine.
    E. The by-products of Hoffmann degradation are $\mathrm{Na_2CO_3}$, NaBr and $\mathrm{H_2O}$.
    Choose the correct answer from the options given below :

    1. A, C and E only
    2. B, C and D only
    3. C and E only
    4. C, D and E only
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    $\mathrm{RCONH_2+Br_2+4NaOH\to RNH_2+Na_2CO_3+2NaBr+2H_2O}$.
    A: only 1 mol $\mathrm{Br_2}$ is used: false. B: alkyl amides do react: false.
    C: the reaction gives primary amines: true. D: only primary amides react (an N–H pair is needed): false.
    E: by-products $\mathrm{Na_2CO_3}$, NaBr and $\mathrm{H_2O}$: true.
    C and E only.
