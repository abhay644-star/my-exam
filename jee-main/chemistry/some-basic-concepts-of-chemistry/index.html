    The mass of iron converted into $\mathrm{Fe_3O_4}$ by the action of 18 g of steam is :
    (Given : Molar mass of H, O and Fe are 1, 16 and 56 g mol$^{-1}$ respectively)
    Assume iron is present in excess :

    1. 2.1 g
    2. 4.2 g
    3. 21 g
    4. 42 g
    Show answer and solution

  10. JEE Main 2026, 21 January, Shift 1 · Question 51
    80 mL of a hydrocarbon on mixing with 264 mL of oxygen in a closed U-tube undergoes complete combustion. The residual gases after cooling to 273 K occupy 224 mL. When the system is treated with KOH solution, the volume decreases to 64 mL. The formula of the hydrocarbon is :

    1. $\mathrm{C_2H_4}$
    2. $\mathrm{C_2H_2}$
    3. $\mathrm{C_4H_{10}}$
    4. $\mathrm{C_2H_6}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    KOH absorbs $\mathrm{CO_2}$: $224-64=160$ mL, and the remaining 64 mL is unused $\mathrm{O_2}$ (water has condensed).
    $\mathrm{C_xH_y+\left(x+\tfrac y4\right)O_2\rightarrow xCO_2+\tfrac y2H_2O}$: $80x=160 \Rightarrow x=2$.
    $\mathrm{O_2}$ used $=264-64=200=80\left(2+\dfrac y4\right) \Rightarrow y=2$.
    The hydrocarbon is $\mathrm{C_2H_2}$.

This entire amount of $\mathrm{CO_2}$ gas was later passed through an aqueous solution of $\mathrm{Ca(OH)_2}$. The excess unreacted $\mathrm{Ca(OH)_2}$ was later neutralized with 0.1 M of 40 mL HCl. If the volume of the sealed container of $\mathrm{CO_2}$ was x, then x is __________ cm$^3$ (nearest integer).
    [Given : The entire amount of $\mathrm{CO_2(g)}$ reacted with exactly half the initial amount of $\mathrm{Ca(OH)_2}$ present in the aqueous solution.]

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 45

    HCl used $=0.1\times0.040=4\times10^{-3}$ mol, which neutralises $2\times10^{-3}$ mol of $\mathrm{Ca(OH)_2}$.
    This excess is half the original amount, so the other half, $2\times10^{-3}$ mol, reacted with $\mathrm{CO_2}$ ($\mathrm{CO_2+Ca(OH)_2\rightarrow CaCO_3+H_2O}$): $n(\mathrm{CO_2})=2\times10^{-3}$ mol.
    At 1 atm and 273 K: $V=2\times10^{-3}\times22400=44.8\ \mathrm{cm^3}\approx45$.

    20 mL of 2 M NaOH solution is added to 400 mL of 0.5 M NaOH solution. The final concentration of the solution is __________ $\times10^{-2}$ M. (Nearest integer)

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 57

    Moles of NaOH $=0.020\times2+0.400\times0.5=0.04+0.20=0.24$.

  33. JEE Main 2025, 23 January, Shift 2 · Question 71
    When 81.0 g of aluminium is allowed to react with 128.0 g of oxygen gas, the mass of aluminium oxide produced in grams is __________. (Nearest integer)
    Given :
    Molar mass of Al is 27.0 g mol$^{-1}$
    Molar mass of O is 16.0 g mol$^{-1}$

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 153

    $\mathrm{4Al+3O_2\rightarrow2Al_2O_3}$.
    Al: $\dfrac{81}{27}=3$ mol; $\mathrm{O_2}$: $\dfrac{128}{32}=4$ mol. 3 mol Al needs only 2.25 mol $\mathrm{O_2}$, so Al is limiting.
    $\mathrm{Al_2O_3}$ formed $=1.5$ mol $\times102$ g mol$^{-1}=153$ g.

    Consider the following reaction occurring in the blast furnace:
    $\mathrm{Fe_3O_4(s)+4CO(g)\rightarrow3Fe(l)+4CO_2(g)}$
    '$x$' kg of iron is produced when $2.32\times10^3$ kg $\mathrm{Fe_3O_4}$ and $2.8\times10^2$ kg CO are brought together in the furnace. The value of '$x$' is _____. (nearest integer)
    {Given:
    molar mass of $\mathrm{Fe_3O_4}=232$ g mol$^{-1}$
    molar mass of CO $=28$ g mol$^{-1}$
    molar mass of Fe $=56$ g mol$^{-1}$}

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 420

    $\mathrm{Fe_3O_4}$: $\dfrac{2.32\times10^6}{232}=10^4$ mol. CO: $\dfrac{2.8\times10^5}{28}=10^4$ mol.

  40. JEE Main 2025, 29 January, Shift 1 · Question 51
    Choose the <b>correct</b> statements.
    (A) Weight of a substance is the amount of matter present in it.
    (B) Mass is the force exerted by gravity on an object.
    (C) Volume is the amount of space occupied by a substance.
    (D) Temperatures below 0°C are possible in Celsius scale, but in Kelvin scale negative temperature is not possible.
    (E) Precision refers to the closeness of various measurements for the same quantity.
    Choose the <b>correct</b> answer from the options given below :

    1. (A), (B) and (C) Only
    2. (B), (C) and (D) Only

    4. (A), (D) and (E) Only
    Show answer and solution

  41. JEE Main 2025, 29 January, Shift 2 · Question 51
    0.1 M solution of KI reacts with excess of $\mathrm{H_2SO_4}$ and $\mathrm{KIO_3}$ solutions. According to equation
    $\mathrm{5I^-+IO_3^-+6H^+\rightarrow3I_2+3H_2O}$
    Identify the <b>correct</b> statements :
    (A) 200 mL of KI solution reacts with 0.004 mol of $\mathrm{KIO_3}$
    (B) 200 mL of KI solution reacts with 0.006 mol of $\mathrm{H_2SO_4}$
    (C) 0.5 L of KI solution produced 0.005 mol of $\mathrm{I_2}$
    (D) Equivalent weight of $\mathrm{KIO_3}$ is equal to $\left(\dfrac{\text{Molecular weight}}{5}\right)$
    Choose the <b>correct</b> answer from the options given below :

    1. (A) and (B) only
    2. (B) and (C) only
    3. (A) and (D) only
    4. (C) and (D) only
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    200 mL of 0.1 M KI is 0.02 mol $\mathrm{I^-}$: it needs 0.004 mol $\mathrm{IO_3^-}$ (A true) and 0.024 mol $\mathrm{H^+}$, i.e. 0.012 mol $\mathrm{H_2SO_4}$ (B false).
    0.5 L is 0.05 mol $\mathrm{I^-}$, giving 0.03 mol $\mathrm{I_2}$ (C false).
    In $\mathrm{IO_3^-\rightarrow I_2}$ iodine goes from +5 to 0, so the equivalent weight is $M/5$ (D true).
