  1. JEE Main 2026, 2 April, Shift 1 · Question 51
    The mass of iron converted into $\mathrm{Fe_3O_4}$ by the action of 18 g of steam is :
    (Given : Molar mass of H, O and Fe are 1, 16 and 56 g mol$^{-1}$ respectively)
    Assume iron is present in excess :

    1. 2.1 g
    2. 4.2 g
    3. 21 g
    4. 42 g
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    $\mathrm{3Fe + 4H_2O \rightarrow Fe_3O_4 + 4H_2}$.
    18 g steam = 1 mol $\mathrm{H_2O}$.
    Fe reacted $= \tfrac{3}{4}$ mol $= 0.75 \times 56 = 42$ g.

  2. JEE Main 2026, 2 April, Shift 2 · Question 51
    The ratio of mass percentage (w/w) of C : H in a hydrocarbon is 12 : 1. It has two carbon atoms. The weight (in g) of CO$_2$(g) formed when 3.38 g of this hydrocarbon is completely burnt in oxygen is : (Given : Molar mass in g mol$^{-1}$ C : 12, H : 1, O : 16)

    1. $5.68$
    2. $11.44$
    3. $22.74$
    4. $17.05$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    Mole ratio C : H $=\dfrac{12}{12}:\dfrac11=1:1$; with two C atoms the hydrocarbon is C$_2$H$_2$ (26 g/mol).
    Moles $=\dfrac{3.38}{26}=0.13$; each mole gives 2 mol CO$_2$: $0.26$ mol.
    Mass of CO$_2=0.26\times44=11.44$ g.

  3. JEE Main 2026, 4 April, Shift 1 · Question 51
    Number of moles and number of molecules in $1.4187$ L of $\mathrm{SO_2}$ at STP respectively are

    1. $0.1266;\ 3.812\times10^{22}$
    2. $0.0633;\ 3.812\times10^{22}$
    3. $0.1266;\ 7.6238\times10^{22}$
    4. $0.0633;\ 7.6238\times10^{22}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    At STP one mole of gas occupies $22.414$ L: $n=\dfrac{1.4187}{22.414}=0.0633$ mol.
    Molecules $=0.0633\times6.022\times10^{23}=3.812\times10^{22}$.

  4. JEE Main 2026, 4 April, Shift 2 · Question 51
    The correct order of total number of atoms present in
    (A) 2 moles of cyclohexane
    (B) 684 g of sucrose
    (C) 90.8 L of dihydrogen at STP
    is:

    1. C > A > B
    2. C > B > A
    3. B > C > A
    4. B > A > C
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    (A) $\mathrm{C_6H_{12}}$ has 18 atoms: $2\times18=36$ mol of atoms.
    (B) Sucrose $\mathrm{C_{12}H_{22}O_{11}}$, $M=342$: $684$ g $=2$ mol; 45 atoms each: $90$ mol of atoms.
    (C) At STP (22.7 L mol$^{-1}$): $\dfrac{90.8}{22.7}=4$ mol $\mathrm{H_2}$ = $8$ mol of atoms.
    Order: B > A > C.

  5. JEE Main 2026, 5 April, Shift 1 · Question 51
    How many grams of residue is obtained by heating $2.76$ g of silver carbonate?
    (Given : Molar mass of C, O and Ag are $12$, $16$ and $108$ g mol$^{-1}$ respectively)

    1. 1.08 g
    2. 2.16 g
    3. 3.24 g
    4. 4.32 g
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    On strong heating: $\mathrm{Ag_2CO_3\rightarrow 2Ag+CO_2+\tfrac12O_2}$ (the $\mathrm{Ag_2O}$ formed first decomposes to silver).
    $M(\mathrm{Ag_2CO_3})=216+12+48=276$ g mol$^{-1}$, so $2.76$ g $=0.01$ mol.
    Residue: $0.02$ mol Ag $=0.02\times108=2.16$ g.

  6. JEE Main 2026, 5 April, Shift 2 · Question 51
    What volume of hydrogen gas at STP would be liberated by action of 50 mL of $\mathrm{H_2SO_4}$ of 50% purity (density $=1.3\ \mathrm{g\ mL^{-1}}$) on 20 g of zinc ?
    Given : Molar mass of H, O, S, Zn are 1, 16, 32, 65 $\mathrm{g\ mol^{-1}}$ respectively.

    1. 5.824 L
    2. 7.428 L
    3. 6.892 L
    4. 8.375 L
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    $\mathrm{Zn + H_2SO_4 \rightarrow ZnSO_4 + H_2}$.
    Mass of $\mathrm{H_2SO_4}=50\times1.3\times0.5=32.5$ g $=\dfrac{32.5}{98}=0.332$ mol.
    Zn $=\dfrac{20}{65}=0.308$ mol, so Zn is limiting.
    $V(\mathrm{H_2})=\dfrac{20}{65}\times22.4=6.892$ L.

  7. JEE Main 2026, 6 April, Shift 1 · Question 51
    An oxide of iron contains 69.9% iron, its empirical formula, is:
    (Given : Molar mass of Fe and O are 56 and 16 g mol$^{-1}$ respectively.)

    1. $\mathrm{FeO}$
    2. $\mathrm{Fe_2O_3}$
    3. $\mathrm{Fe_3O_4}$
    4. $\mathrm{FeO_3}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    Moles in 100 g: Fe $=\dfrac{69.9}{56}=1.248$, O $=\dfrac{30.1}{16}=1.881$.
    Ratio Fe : O $=1:1.507\approx2:3$.
    Empirical formula $\mathrm{Fe_2O_3}$.

  8. JEE Main 2026, 6 April, Shift 2 · Question 51
    Which of the following contain the same number of atoms ?
    (Given : Molar mass in g mol$^{-1}$ of H, He, O and S are 1, 4, 16 and 32 respectively)
    A. 2 g of $\mathrm{O_2}$ gas
    B. 4 g of $\mathrm{SO_2}$ gas
    C. 1400 mL of $\mathrm{O_2}$ at STP
    D. 0.05 L of He at STP
    E. 0.0625 mol of $\mathrm{H_2}$ gas
    Choose the correct answer from the options given below :

    1. A and B only
    2. B and C only
    3. C and D only
    4. A, C and E only
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    A: $\dfrac{2}{32}=\dfrac1{16}$ mol $\mathrm{O_2}$ → $\dfrac18$ mol atoms.
    B: $\dfrac4{64}=\dfrac1{16}$ mol $\mathrm{SO_2}$ → $\dfrac3{16}$ mol atoms.
    C: $\dfrac{1.4}{22.4}=\dfrac1{16}$ mol $\mathrm{O_2}$ → $\dfrac18$ mol atoms.
    D: $\dfrac{0.05}{22.4}\approx0.0022$ mol atoms.
    E: $0.0625=\dfrac1{16}$ mol $\mathrm{H_2}$ → $\dfrac18$ mol atoms.
    A, C and E have the same number of atoms.

  9. JEE Main 2026, 8 April, Shift 2 · Question 51
    Match List - I with List - II.

    List - I
    Mass of substance    		List - II
    Number of atoms
    A. 1.8 mg waterI. $2\times10^{-4}\times N_A$
    B. 9.8 mg sulphuric acidII. $1.5\times10^{-4}\times N_A$
    C. 1.8 mg carbonIII. $3\times10^{-4}\times N_A$
    D. 5.85 mg salt (NaCl)IV. $7\times10^{-4}\times N_A$
    Choose the correct answer from the options given below :

    1. A-IV, B-III, C-I, D-II
    2. A-III, B-II, C-IV, D-I
    3. A-III, B-IV, C-II, D-I
    4. A-III, B-IV, C-I, D-II
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    A: $\dfrac{1.8\times10^{-3}}{18}=10^{-4}$ mol $\mathrm{H_2O}$, 3 atoms each: $3\times10^{-4}N_A$ (III).
    B: $\dfrac{9.8\times10^{-3}}{98}=10^{-4}$ mol $\mathrm{H_2SO_4}$, 7 atoms each: $7\times10^{-4}N_A$ (IV).
    C: $\dfrac{1.8\times10^{-3}}{12}=1.5\times10^{-4}$ mol C: $1.5\times10^{-4}N_A$ (II).
    D: $\dfrac{5.85\times10^{-3}}{58.5}=10^{-4}$ mol NaCl, 2 atoms (ions) each: $2\times10^{-4}N_A$ (I).
    A-III, B-IV, C-II, D-I.

  10. JEE Main 2026, 21 January, Shift 1 · Question 51
    80 mL of a hydrocarbon on mixing with 264 mL of oxygen in a closed U-tube undergoes complete combustion. The residual gases after cooling to 273 K occupy 224 mL. When the system is treated with KOH solution, the volume decreases to 64 mL. The formula of the hydrocarbon is :

    1. $\mathrm{C_2H_4}$
    2. $\mathrm{C_2H_2}$
    3. $\mathrm{C_4H_{10}}$
    4. $\mathrm{C_2H_6}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    KOH absorbs $\mathrm{CO_2}$: $224-64=160$ mL, and the remaining 64 mL is unused $\mathrm{O_2}$ (water has condensed).
    $\mathrm{C_xH_y+\left(x+\tfrac y4\right)O_2\rightarrow xCO_2+\tfrac y2H_2O}$: $80x=160 \Rightarrow x=2$.
    $\mathrm{O_2}$ used $=264-64=200=80\left(2+\dfrac y4\right) \Rightarrow y=2$.
    The hydrocarbon is $\mathrm{C_2H_2}$.

  11. JEE Main 2026, 21 January, Shift 1 · Question 52
    14.0 g of calcium metal is allowed to react with excess HCl at 1.0 atm pressure and 273 K. Which of the following statements is incorrect ?
    [Given : Molar mass in g mol$^{-1}$ of Ca-40, Cl-35.5, H-1]

    1. 0.35 mol of $\mathrm{H_2}$ gas is evolved.
    2. 7.84 L of $\mathrm{H_2}$ gas is evolved.
    3. 33.3 g of $\mathrm{CaCl_2}$ is produced.
    4. The limiting reagent is calcium metal.
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    $\mathrm{Ca+2HCl\rightarrow CaCl_2+H_2}$; moles of Ca $=\dfrac{14}{40}=0.35$, and Ca is limiting since HCl is in excess.
    $\mathrm{H_2}$: 0.35 mol $=0.35\times22.4=7.84$ L at STP.
    $\mathrm{CaCl_2}$: $0.35\times111=38.85$ g, not 33.3 g, so that statement is incorrect.

  12. JEE Main 2026, 21 January, Shift 2 · Question 51
    Aqueous HCl reacts with $\mathrm{MnO_2(s)}$ to form $\mathrm{MnCl_2(aq)}$, $\mathrm{Cl_2(g)}$ and $\mathrm{H_2O}(l)$. What is the weight (in g) of $\mathrm{Cl_2}$ liberated when 8.7 g of $\mathrm{MnO_2(s)}$ is reacted with excess aqueous HCl solution ? (Given Molar mass in g mol$^{-1}$ Mn $=55$, Cl $=35.5$, O $=16$, H $=1$)

    1. $14.2$
    2. $21.3$
    3. $7.1$
    4. $71$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    $\mathrm{MnO_2+4HCl\rightarrow MnCl_2+Cl_2+2H_2O}$.
    $\mathrm{MnO_2}$: $\dfrac{8.7}{87}=0.1$ mol, giving 0.1 mol $\mathrm{Cl_2}=7.1$ g.

  13. JEE Main 2026, 22 January, Shift 1 · Question 51
    In the reaction,
    $\mathrm{2Al(s)+6HCl(aq)\longrightarrow2Al^{3+}(aq)+6Cl^-(aq)+3H_2(g)}$

    1. 12 L HCl(aq) is consumed for every 6L $\mathrm{H_2(g)}$ produced.
    2. 33.6 L $\mathrm{H_2(g)}$ is produced regardless of temperature and pressure for every mole of Al that reacts.
    3. 67.2 L $\mathrm{H_2(g)}$ at STP is produced for every mole of Al that reacts.
    4. 11.2 L $\mathrm{H_2(g)}$ at STP is produced for every mole of HCl consumed.
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    6 mol HCl give 3 mol $\mathrm{H_2}$, so 1 mol HCl gives 0.5 mol $\mathrm{H_2}=11.2$ L at STP. True.
    1 mol Al gives 1.5 mol $\mathrm{H_2}=33.6$ L only at STP (not regardless of conditions), not 67.2 L.
    HCl is in solution, so a gas-volume ratio does not apply to it.

  14. JEE Main 2026, 22 January, Shift 2 · Question 51
    $\mathrm{A+2B\longrightarrow AB_2}$
    36.0 g of 'A' (Molar mass : 60 g mol$^{-1}$) and 56.0 g of 'B' (Molar mass : 80 g mol$^{-1}$) are allowed to react. Which of the following statements are correct ?
    A. 'A' is the limiting reagent.
    B. 77.0 g of $\mathrm{AB_2}$ is formed.
    C. Molar mass of $\mathrm{AB_2}$ is 140 g mol$^{-1}$.
    D. 15.0 g of A is left unreacted after the completion of reaction.
    Choose the correct answer from the options given below :

    1. A and B Only
    2. B and D Only
    3. C and D Only
    4. A and C Only
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    A: $\dfrac{36}{60}=0.6$ mol; B: $\dfrac{56}{80}=0.7$ mol. 0.7 mol B needs only 0.35 mol A, so B is limiting (A false).
    $\mathrm{AB_2}$: 0.35 mol, molar mass $60+2\times80=220$ g mol$^{-1}$ (C false), mass $0.35\times220=77.0$ g (B true).
    A left: $0.25$ mol $=15.0$ g (D true). Answer: B and D only.

  15. JEE Main 2026, 23 January, Shift 1 · Question 71
    $x$ mg of pure HCl was used to make an aqueous solution. 25.0 mL of 0.1 M $\mathrm{Ba(OH)_2}$ solution is used when the HCl solution was titrated against it. The numerical value of $x$ is __________ $\times10^{-1}$. (Nearest integer)
    Given : Molar mass of HCl and $\mathrm{Ba(OH)_2}$ are 36.5 and 171.0 g mol$^{-1}$ respectively.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 1825

    $\mathrm{Ba(OH)_2}$: $25.0\times0.1=2.5$ mmol, which neutralises $2\times2.5=5$ mmol HCl.
    Mass of HCl $=5\times36.5=182.5$ mg $=1825\times10^{-1}$ mg.

  16. JEE Main 2026, 28 January, Shift 2 · Question 51
    For the given reaction;
    $\mathrm{CaCO_3+2HCl\longrightarrow CaCl_2+H_2O+CO_2}$
    If 90 g $\mathrm{CaCO_3}$ is added to 300 mL of HCl which contains 38.55% HCl by mass and has density 1.13 g mL$^{-1}$, then which of the following option is correct ?
    Given molar mass of H, Cl, Ca and O are 1, 35.5, 40 and 16 g mol$^{-1}$ respectively.

    1. 60.32 g of HCl remains unreacted
    2. 64.97 g of HCl remains unreacted
    3. 32.85 g of $\mathrm{CaCO_3}$ remains unreacted
    4. 97.30 g of HCl reacted
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    Mass of HCl $=300\times1.13\times0.3855=130.68$ g. $\mathrm{CaCO_3}=0.9$ mol needs $1.8$ mol $=65.7$ g HCl, so $\mathrm{CaCO_3}$ is the limiting reagent.
    HCl left $=130.68-65.7\approx64.97$ g.
