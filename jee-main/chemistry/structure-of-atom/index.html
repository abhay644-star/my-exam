  1. JEE Main 2026, 2 April, Shift 1 · Question 52
    What is the energy (in J atom$^{-1}$) required for the following process ?
    $\mathrm{Li^{2+}(g) \rightarrow Li^{3+}(g) + e^-}$
    (Take the ionization energy for the H atom in the ground state as $2.18 \times 10^{-18}$ J atom$^{-1}$)

    1. $8.72 \times 10^{-18}$
    2. $1.962 \times 10^{-18}$
    3. $1.962 \times 10^{-17}$
    4. $6.54 \times 10^{-17}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    $\mathrm{Li^{2+}}$ is hydrogen-like with $Z = 3$, electron in $n = 1$.
    $IE = Z^2 \times IE_\mathrm{H} = 9 \times 2.18 \times 10^{-18}$
    $= 1.962 \times 10^{-17}$ J atom$^{-1}$.

  2. JEE Main 2026, 2 April, Shift 2 · Question 71
    The surface of sodium metal is irradiated with radiation of wavelength $x$ nm. The kinetic energy of ejected electrons is $2.8\times10^{-20}$ J. The work function of sodium is 2.3 eV. The value of $x$ is __________ $\times10^2$ nm. (Nearest integer)
    (Given : $h=6.6\times10^{-34}$ J s ; 1 eV $=1.6\times10^{-19}$ J; $c=3.0\times10^8$ m s$^{-1}$)

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 5

    Photon energy $=\phi+K=2.3\times1.6\times10^{-19}+0.28\times10^{-19}=3.96\times10^{-19}$ J.
    $\lambda=\dfrac{hc}{E}=\dfrac{6.6\times10^{-34}\times3\times10^8}{3.96\times10^{-19}}=5\times10^{-7}$ m $=500$ nm.
    $500=5\times10^2$ nm, so the answer is $5$.

  3. JEE Main 2026, 4 April, Shift 1 · Question 52
    What is the ratio of wave number of first line (lowest energy line) of Balmer series of H atomic spectrum to first line of its Brackett series?

    1. $5:1$
    2. $5:0.81$
    3. $5:1.75$
    4. $5:27$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    Balmer, first line ($3\to2$): $\bar\nu=R\left(\dfrac14-\dfrac19\right)=\dfrac{5R}{36}$.
    Brackett, first line ($5\to4$): $\bar\nu=R\left(\dfrac1{16}-\dfrac1{25}\right)=\dfrac{9R}{400}$.
    Ratio $=\dfrac{5}{36}\cdot\dfrac{400}{9}=\dfrac{2000}{324}\approx6.17=5:0.81$.

  4. JEE Main 2026, 4 April, Shift 1 · Question 53
    Which of the following is correct set of $4$ quantum numbers of $19^{\text{th}}$ electron in Chromium (Atomic number $=24$) in accordance with Aufbau principle?

    1. $n=3,\ l=2,\ m=+2,\ s=+\dfrac12$
    2. $n=3,\ l=2,\ m=-2,\ s=+\dfrac12$
    3. $n=4,\ l=1,\ m=0,\ s=+\dfrac12$
    4. $n=4,\ l=0,\ m=0,\ s=+\dfrac12$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    Aufbau order: $1s^2\,2s^2\,2p^6\,3s^2\,3p^6$ accounts for $18$ electrons; the $19^{\text{th}}$ enters $4s$ (lower $n+l$ than $3d$).
    For $4s$: $n=4$, $l=0$, $m=0$, $s=+\dfrac12$.

  5. JEE Main 2026, 4 April, Shift 2 · Question 52
    The species having identical radii according to the Bohr's theory are:
    A. H (first orbit)
    B. $\mathrm{He^+}$ (first orbit)
    C. $\mathrm{He^+}$ (Second orbit)
    D. $\mathrm{Li^{2+}}$ (first orbit)
    E. $\mathrm{Be^{3+}}$ (Second orbit)
    Choose the correct answer from the options given below:

    1. A and C Only
    2. A and E Only
    3. B and E Only
    4. C and D Only
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    Bohr radius $r_n=0.529\,\dfrac{n^2}{Z}$ Å, so $r\propto\dfrac{n^2}{Z}$.
    A: $\dfrac11=1$; B: $\dfrac12$; C: $\dfrac42=2$; D: $\dfrac13$; E: $\dfrac44=1$.
    A and E have identical radii.

  6. JEE Main 2026, 5 April, Shift 1 · Question 52
    Arrange the following atomic orbitals of multi electron atoms in order of increasing energy.
    A. $n = 3, l = 2, m = +1$
    B. $n = 4, l = 0, m = 0$
    C. $n = 6, l = 1, m = 0$
    D. $n = 5, l = 1, m = +1$
    E. $n = 2, l = 1, m = +1$
    Choose the correct answer from the options given below:

    1. C < D < B < A < E
    2. B < A < E < C < D
    3. E < C < D < B < A
    4. E < B < A < D < C
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    Orbitals: A $=3d$, B $=4s$, C $=6p$, D $=5p$, E $=2p$.
    $(n+l)$ rule: $2p\,(3)$, $4s\,(4)$, $3d\,(5)$, $5p\,(6)$, $6p\,(7)$.
    Increasing energy: E < B < A < D < C.

  7. JEE Main 2026, 5 April, Shift 1 · Question 53
    Identify the correct statements from the following :
    A. Heisenberg uncertainty principle is applicable to electrons.
    B. The size of $2p_x$ orbital is less than the size of $3p_x$ orbital.
    C. The energy of $2s$ orbital of H atom is equal to the energy of $2s$ orbital of Li.
    D. The electronic configuration of Cr is $[\mathrm{Ar}]\,3d^5\,4s^1$
    Choose the correct answer from the options given below:

    1. A, B and C Only
    2. A, B and D Only
    3. B, C and D Only
    4. A, C and D Only
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    A: true — the uncertainty principle matters for microscopic particles such as electrons.
    B: true — orbital size increases with $n$.
    C: false — orbital energy depends on nuclear charge (and shielding), so the $2s$ energies of H and Li differ.
    D: true — half-filled $3d^5$ stability gives $[\mathrm{Ar}]3d^54s^1$.
    Correct: A, B and D.

  8. JEE Main 2026, 5 April, Shift 2 · Question 52
    Which of the following statement(s) is/are true ?
    A. If two orbitals have the same value of $(n+l)$, the orbital with lower value of n will have lower energy.
    B. Energies of the orbitals in the same subshell increase with increase in atomic number.
    C. The size of $2p_x$ orbital is less than the size of $3p_x$ orbital.
    D. Among 5f, 6s, 4d, 5p and 5d orbitals, none of the orbitals have 2 radial nodes.
    Choose the correct answer from the options given below :

    1. A, B and C only
    2. A and C only
    3. C and D only
    4. A only
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    A: true (the $(n+l)$ rule).
    B: false; with increasing atomic number the effective nuclear charge increases and orbital energies decrease.
    C: true; size increases with $n$.
    D: radial nodes $=n-l-1$: 5f 1, 6s 5, 4d 1, 5p 3, 5d 2. The 5d orbital has 2 radial nodes, so D is false.
    A and C only.

  9. JEE Main 2026, 6 April, Shift 1 · Question 52
    If shortest wavelength of hydrogen atom in Lyman series is $x$, then longest wavelength in Balmer series of He$^+$ is:

    1. $\dfrac{9x}{5}$
    2. $\dfrac{36x}{5}$
    3. $\dfrac{x}{4}$
    4. $\dfrac{5x}{9}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    H, Lyman shortest ($\infty\to1$): $\dfrac1x=R_H$.
    He$^+$ ($Z=2$), Balmer longest ($3\to2$): $\dfrac1\lambda=4R_H\left(\dfrac14-\dfrac19\right)=\dfrac{5R_H}{9}$.
    $\lambda=\dfrac{9}{5R_H}=\dfrac{9x}{5}$.

  10. JEE Main 2026, 6 April, Shift 1 · Question 53
    Match the LIST-I with LIST-II

    List-I
    Orbital    		List-II
    Radial nodes and nodal plane
    A. 2sI. 1 Radial node + two nodal planes
    B. 3sII. 1 Radial node + one nodal plane
    C. 3pIII. 2 Radial nodes + No nodal plane
    D. 4dIV. 1 Radial node + No nodal plane
    Choose the correct answer from the options given below:

    1. A-IV, B-I, C-III, D-II
    2. A-IV, B-II, C-III, D-I
    3. A-III, B-I, C-IV, D-II
    4. A-IV, B-III, C-II, D-I
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    Radial nodes $=n-l-1$; nodal planes $=l$.
    2s: 1 radial, 0 planes (IV). 3s: 2 radial, 0 planes (III).
    3p: 1 radial, 1 plane (II). 4d: 1 radial, 2 planes (I).
    A-IV, B-III, C-II, D-I.

  11. JEE Main 2026, 6 April, Shift 2 · Question 52
    The Bohr radius of a hydrogen like species is 70.53 pm. The species and the stationary state (n) are respectively
    (Given : Hydrogen atom Bohr radius is 52.9 pm)

    1. $\mathrm{Li^{2+}}$, 3
    2. $\mathrm{He^{+}}$, 3
    3. $\mathrm{He^{+}}$, 2
    4. $\mathrm{Li^{2+}}$, 2
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    $r_n=52.9\,\dfrac{n^2}{Z}$ pm.
    $\dfrac{n^2}{Z}=\dfrac{70.53}{52.9}=\dfrac43$, so $n=2$, $Z=3$.
    The species is $\mathrm{Li^{2+}}$ in $n=2$.

  12. JEE Main 2026, 8 April, Shift 2 · Question 73
    Consider two radiations of wavelengths
    1. $\lambda_1=2000$ Å
    2. $\lambda_2=6000$ Å
    The ratio of the energies of these two radiations $\left(\dfrac{E_1}{E_2}\right)$ is __________ (Nearest integer).

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 3

    $E=\dfrac{hc}{\lambda}$, so $\dfrac{E_1}{E_2}=\dfrac{\lambda_2}{\lambda_1}=\dfrac{6000}{2000}=3$.
