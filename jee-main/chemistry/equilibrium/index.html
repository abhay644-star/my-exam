  1. JEE Main 2026, 2 April, Shift 1 · Question 56
    The solubility product constants of $\mathrm{Ag_2CrO_4}$ and AgBr are $32x$ and $4y$ respectively at 298 K.
    The value of $\left(\dfrac{\text{molarity of } \mathrm{Ag_2CrO_4}}{\text{molarity of AgBr}}\right)$ can be expressed as :

    1. $\dfrac{2\sqrt[3]{x}}{y}$
    2. $2\sqrt{\dfrac{x}{y}}$
    3. $\sqrt{\dfrac{x}{y}}$
    4. $\dfrac{\sqrt[3]{x}}{\sqrt{y}}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    $\mathrm{Ag_2CrO_4}$: $K_{sp} = 4s_1^3 = 32x \Rightarrow s_1 = 2\sqrt[3]{x}$.
    AgBr: $K_{sp} = s_2^2 = 4y \Rightarrow s_2 = 2\sqrt{y}$.
    $\dfrac{s_1}{s_2} = \dfrac{\sqrt[3]{x}}{\sqrt{y}}$.

  2. JEE Main 2026, 2 April, Shift 1 · Question 70
    A salt with few drops of conc. HCl gives apple green colour in flame test. The group precipitate of the salt is dissolved in acetic acid and treated with $\mathrm{K_2CrO_4}$ to give yellow precipitate. When the sodium carbonate extract of the salt solution is heated with conc. $\mathrm{HNO_3}$ and ammonium molybdate, it resulted a canary yellow precipitate. The cation and anion present in the salt are respectively,

    1. $\mathrm{Ca^{2+}}$ and $\mathrm{SO_4^{2-}}$
    2. $\mathrm{Ba^{2+}}$ and $\mathrm{PO_4^{3-}}$
    3. $\mathrm{Mn^{2+}}$ and $\mathrm{PO_4^{3-}}$
    4. $\mathrm{Ba^{2+}}$ and $\mathrm{SO_4^{2-}}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    Apple green flame and yellow $\mathrm{BaCrO_4}$ with $\mathrm{K_2CrO_4}$ in acetic acid → $\mathrm{Ba^{2+}}$.
    Canary yellow precipitate with ammonium molybdate/conc. $\mathrm{HNO_3}$ (ammonium phosphomolybdate) → $\mathrm{PO_4^{3-}}$.

  3. JEE Main 2026, 2 April, Shift 2 · Question 52
    The first and second ionization constants of a weak dibasic acid H$_2$A are $8.1\times10^{-8}$ and $1.0\times10^{-13}$ respectively. 0.1 mol of H$_2$A was dissolved in 1L of 0.1 M HCl solution. The concentration of HA$^-$ in the resultant solution is :

    1. 0.1 M
    2. $9.53\times10^{-6}$ M
    3. $8.1\times10^{-8}$ M
    4. $1.0\times10^{-13}$ M
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    HCl fixes $[\mathrm{H^+}]=0.1$ M and suppresses ionisation, so $[\mathrm{H_2A}]\approx0.1$ M.
    $K_{a1}=\dfrac{[\mathrm{H^+}][\mathrm{HA^-}]}{[\mathrm{H_2A}]} \Rightarrow [\mathrm{HA^-}]=\dfrac{8.1\times10^{-8}\times0.1}{0.1}=8.1\times10^{-8}$ M.

  4. JEE Main 2026, 2 April, Shift 2 · Question 56
    At 25$^\circ$C, 20.0 mL of 0.2 M weak monoprotic acid HX is titrated against 0.2 M NaOH. The pH of the solution (a) at the start of the titration (when NaOH has not been added) and (b) when 10 mL of NaOH is added respectively, are :
    Given : $K_a=5\times10^{-4}$, $\mathrm{p}K_a=3.3$, $\alpha \ll 1$

    1. (a) 0.7, (b) 2.0
    2. (a) 2.0, (b) 3.3
    3. (a) 1.1, (b) 2.2
    4. (a) 3.0, (b) 2.2
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    (a) Weak acid alone: $\mathrm{pH}=\dfrac12(\mathrm pK_a-\log C)=\dfrac12(3.3+0.7)=2.0$.
    (b) 10 mL NaOH neutralises half of the acid (2 mmol of 4 mmol): $[\mathrm{X^-}]=[\mathrm{HX}]$.
    Buffer at half-neutralisation: $\mathrm{pH}=\mathrm pK_a=3.3$.

  5. JEE Main 2026, 4 April, Shift 1 · Question 55
    At $T$(K), the equilibrium constant of $\mathrm{A_2(g)+B_2(g)\rightleftharpoons C(g)}$ is $2.7\times10^{-5}$. What is the equilibrium constant for $\dfrac13\mathrm{A_2(g)}+\dfrac13\mathrm{B_2(g)}\rightleftharpoons\dfrac13\mathrm{C(g)}$ at the same temperature?

    1. $(2.7\times10^{-5})^3$
    2. $6\times10^{-2}$
    3. $\sqrt{2.7\times10^{-5}}$
    4. $3\times10^{-2}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    Multiplying an equation by $\dfrac13$ raises $K$ to the power $\dfrac13$.
    $K'=(2.7\times10^{-5})^{1/3}=(27\times10^{-6})^{1/3}=3\times10^{-2}$.

  6. JEE Main 2026, 4 April, Shift 1 · Question 74
    The pH of a solution obtained by mixing $5$ mL of $0.1$ M $\mathrm{NH_4OH}$ solution with $250$ mL of $0.1$ M $\mathrm{NH_4Cl}$ solution is _______ $\times10^{-2}$. (Nearest integer)
    Given: $\mathrm{p}K_b(\mathrm{NH_4OH})=4.74$
    $\log2=0.30$
    $\log3=0.48$
    $\log5=0.70$

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 756

    Basic buffer: base $=0.5$ mmol, salt $=25$ mmol.
    $\mathrm{pOH}=\mathrm pK_b+\log\dfrac{[\text{salt}]}{[\text{base}]}=4.74+\log50=4.74+1.70=6.44$.
    $\mathrm{pH}=14-6.44=7.56=756\times10^{-2}$.

  7. JEE Main 2026, 4 April, Shift 2 · Question 55
    20 mL of a solution of acetic acid required 28.4 mL of 0.1 M NaOH for its neutralization. A solution (X) was prepared by mixing 20 mL of the above acetic acid and 14.2 mL of 0.1 M NaOH solution. What is the pH of the solution (X)? ($\mathrm{pK_a}$ value of acetic acid is 4.75).

    1. $7.0$
    2. $4.75$
    3. $3.5$
    4. $4.82$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    Acetic acid in 20 mL $=28.4\times0.1=2.84$ mmol.
    NaOH added $=14.2\times0.1=1.42$ mmol, exactly half: $1.42$ mmol $\mathrm{CH_3COOH}$ and $1.42$ mmol $\mathrm{CH_3COO^-}$ remain.
    Henderson equation: $\mathrm{pH}=\mathrm{pK_a}+\log\dfrac{[\mathrm{salt}]}{[\mathrm{acid}]}=4.75+\log1=4.75$.

  8. JEE Main 2026, 4 April, Shift 2 · Question 72
    For the following reaction at 50 °C and at 2 atm pressure,
    $\mathrm{2N_2O_5(g)\rightleftharpoons2N_2O_4(g)+O_2(g)}$
    $\mathrm{N_2O_5}$ is 50% dissociated.
    The magnitude of standard free energy change at this temperature is $x$.
    $x=$ ________ J mol$^{-1}$ [Nearest integer].
    Given : $R=8.314$ J mol$^{-1}$ K$^{-1}$, $\log2=0.30$, $\log3=0.48$, $\ln10=2.303$, °C + 273 = K

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 2474

    Start with 1 mol $\mathrm{N_2O_5}$: at equilibrium $\mathrm{N_2O_5}$ 0.5, $\mathrm{N_2O_4}$ 0.5, $\mathrm{O_2}$ 0.25; total 1.25 mol.
    At 2 atm: $p_{\mathrm{N_2O_5}}=p_{\mathrm{N_2O_4}}=0.8$ atm, $p_{\mathrm{O_2}}=0.4$ atm.
    $K_p=\dfrac{(0.8)^2(0.4)}{(0.8)^2}=0.4$; $\log0.4=2\log2-1=-0.40$.
    $\Delta G^\circ=-2.303RT\log K_p=2.303\times8.314\times323\times0.40=2473.8$ J mol$^{-1}$.
    $x\approx2474$.

  9. JEE Main 2026, 5 April, Shift 1 · Question 55
    $\mathrm{M_3A_2}$ is a sparingly soluble salt of molar mass $y$ g mol$^{-1}$ and solubility $x$ g L$^{-1}$. The ratio of the molar concentration of the anion ($\mathrm{A^{3-}}$) to the solubility product of the salt is

    1. $\dfrac{1}{54}\cdot\dfrac{y^4}{x^4}$
    2. $\dfrac{y^5}{108x^4}$
    3. $108\cdot\dfrac{x^5}{y^5}$
    4. $\dfrac{1}{108}\dfrac{y^4}{x^4}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    Molar solubility $s=\dfrac xy$ mol L$^{-1}$. $\mathrm{M_3A_2\rightleftharpoons 3M^{2+}+2A^{3-}}$.
    $[\mathrm{A^{3-}}]=2s$, $K_{sp}=(3s)^3(2s)^2=108s^5$.
    Ratio $=\dfrac{2s}{108s^5}=\dfrac{1}{54s^4}=\dfrac1{54}\cdot\dfrac{y^4}{x^4}$.

  10. JEE Main 2026, 5 April, Shift 1 · Question 56
    Arrange the following resultant mixtures in increasing order of their pH values
    A. $10$ mL $0.2$ M $\mathrm{Ca(OH)_2}$ + $25$ mL $0.1$ M HCl
    B. $10$ mL $0.01$ M $\mathrm{H_2SO_4}$ + $10$ mL $0.01$ M $\mathrm{Ca(OH)_2}$
    C. $10$ mL $0.1$ M $\mathrm{H_2SO_4}$ + $10$ mL $0.1$ M KOH
    Choose the correct answer from the options given below:

    1. B < C < A
    2. C < A < B
    3. C < B < A
    4. A < C < B
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    A: $\mathrm{OH^-}=10\times0.4=4$ mmol, $\mathrm{H^+}=2.5$ mmol, excess $1.5$ mmol $\mathrm{OH^-}$ — basic.
    B: $\mathrm{H^+}=0.2$ mmol, $\mathrm{OH^-}=0.2$ mmol — neutral (pH $7$).
    C: $\mathrm{H^+}=2$ mmol, $\mathrm{OH^-}=1$ mmol, excess $1$ mmol $\mathrm{H^+}$ — acidic.
    Increasing pH: C < B < A.

  11. JEE Main 2026, 5 April, Shift 1 · Question 70
    Given below are two statements:
    Statement I: Sodium dichromate and potassium dichromate are classified as primary standards in titrimetric analysis.
    Statement II: Phenolphthalein is a weak base, therefore it dissociates in acidic medium.
    In the light of the above statements, choose the correct answer from the options given below

    1. Both Statement I and Statement II are ture
    2. Both Statement I and Statement II are false
    3. Statement I is true but Statement II is false
    4. Statement I is false but Statement II is true
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    I: potassium dichromate is a primary standard, but sodium dichromate is hygroscopic and is not. False.
    II: phenolphthalein is a weak acid; it ionises (pink form) in basic medium, not in acidic medium. False.

  12. JEE Main 2026, 5 April, Shift 1 · Question 74
    The values of pressure equilibrium constant recorded at different temperatures for the following equilibrium reaction have been given below
    $\mathrm{A(g) \rightleftharpoons B(g) + C(g)}$ $$\begin{array}{|c|c|}\hline \dfrac{1}{T}\ (\mathrm{K^{-1}}) & \log_{10}K_p \\ \hline 0.05 & 3.5 \\ \hline 0.06 & 2.5 \\ \hline 0.07 & 1.5 \\ \hline\end{array}$$ The magnitude of $\dfrac{\Delta H^\circ}{R}$ calculated from the above data is _______. (Nearest integer)

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 230

    van 't Hoff: $\log_{10}K_p=-\dfrac{\Delta H^\circ}{2.303R}\cdot\dfrac1T+\text{constant}$.
    Slope $=\dfrac{2.5-3.5}{0.06-0.05}=-100=-\dfrac{\Delta H^\circ}{2.303R}$.
    $\left|\dfrac{\Delta H^\circ}{R}\right|=230.3\approx230$.

  13. JEE Main 2026, 5 April, Shift 2 · Question 56
    The reaction $\mathrm{A(g)\rightleftharpoons B(g)+C(g)}$ was initiated with the amount 'a' of A(g). At equilibrium it is found that the amount of A(g) remaining is $(a-x)$ at a total pressure of p.
    The equilibrium constant Kp of the reaction can be calculated from the expression :

    1. $\dfrac{x^2}{a^2+x^2}\times p$
    2. $\dfrac{x^2}{a^2-x^2}\times p$
    3. $\dfrac{a+x^2}{x^2}\times p$
    4. $\dfrac{a^2-x^2}{x^2}\times p$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    At equilibrium: A $=a-x$, B $=x$, C $=x$; total $=a+x$.
    $K_p=\dfrac{\left(\dfrac{x}{a+x}p\right)^2}{\dfrac{a-x}{a+x}p}=\dfrac{x^2p}{(a+x)(a-x)}=\dfrac{x^2}{a^2-x^2}\times p$.

  14. JEE Main 2026, 6 April, Shift 1 · Question 57
    One mole each of He and A(g) are taken in a 10 L closed flask and heated to 400 K to establish the following equilibrium.
    $\mathrm{A(g)}\rightleftharpoons\mathrm{B(g)}$
    $K_c$ for this reaction at 400 K is 4.0. The partial pressures (in atm) of He and B(g) are respectively (at equilibrium)
    (Assume He, A(g) and B(g) behave as ideal gases)
    (Given : R = 0.082 L atm K$^{-1}$ mol$^{-1}$)

    1. $3.28,\ 2.624$
    2. $2.624,\ 3.28$
    3. $3.28,\ 0.656$
    4. $0.656,\ 6.56$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    $K_c=\dfrac{[B]}{[A]}=\dfrac{x}{1-x}=4\Rightarrow x=0.8$ mol B.
    $\dfrac{RT}{V}=\dfrac{0.082\times400}{10}=3.28$ atm per mol.
    $p_{He}=1\times3.28=3.28$ atm; $p_B=0.8\times3.28=2.624$ atm.

  15. JEE Main 2026, 6 April, Shift 2 · Question 56
    Given is a concentrated solution of a weak electrolyte $\mathrm{A_xB_y}$ of concentration '$c$' and dissociation constant '$K$'. The degree of dissociation is given by :

    1. $\left[K\times c^{x+y-1}x^xy^y\right]^{x+y}$
    2. $\left(\dfrac{K}{c^{x+y-1}\,x^xy^y}\right)^{\frac{1}{x+y}}$
    3. $\left(\dfrac{c^{x+y-1}\,x^xy^y}{K}\right)^{x+y}$
    4. $\left(\dfrac{c^{x+y-1}\,x^xy^y}{K}\right)^{\frac{1}{x+y}}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    $\mathrm{A_xB_y}\rightleftharpoons x\mathrm A^{y+}+y\mathrm B^{x-}$: at equilibrium $c(1-\alpha)$, $xc\alpha$, $yc\alpha$.
    $K=\dfrac{(xc\alpha)^x(yc\alpha)^y}{c(1-\alpha)}\approx x^xy^y\,c^{x+y-1}\alpha^{x+y}$ for a weak electrolyte ($1-\alpha\approx1$).
    $\alpha=\left(\dfrac{K}{c^{x+y-1}x^xy^y}\right)^{\frac1{x+y}}$.

  16. JEE Main 2026, 6 April, Shift 2 · Question 74
    In a closed flask at 600 K, one mole of $\mathrm{X_2Y_4(g)}$ attains equilibrium as given below :

    $\displaystyle \mathrm{X_2Y_4(g)\rightleftharpoons2XY_2(g)}$
    At equilibrium, 75% $\mathrm{X_2Y_4(g)}$ was dissociated and the total pressure is 1 atm. The magnitude of $\Delta_rG^\ominus$ (in kJ mol$^{-1}$) at this temperature is _________. (Nearest Integer)
    (Given : $R=8.3$ J mol$^{-1}$ K$^{-1}$; $\ln10=2.3$, $\log2=0.3$, $\log3=0.48$, $\log5=0.69$, $\log7=0.84$)

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 8

    At equilibrium: $\mathrm{X_2Y_4}=0.25$ mol, $\mathrm{XY_2}=1.5$ mol, total $1.75$ mol, $P=1$ atm.
    $K_p=\dfrac{(1.5/1.75)^2}{0.25/1.75}=\dfrac{2.25}{0.4375}=\dfrac{36}{7}$.
    $\log K_p=2(0.3+0.48)-0.84=0.72$.
    $\Delta_rG^\ominus=-2.3RT\log K_p=-2.3\times8.3\times600\times0.72\approx-8247$ J mol$^{-1}$.
    Magnitude $\approx8$ kJ mol$^{-1}$.

  17. JEE Main 2026, 8 April, Shift 2 · Question 55
    Consider the following reactions in which all the reactants and products are present in gaseous state
    $2\,xy\rightleftharpoons x_2+y_2\quad K_1=2.5\times10^5$
    $xy+\dfrac{1}{2}z_2\rightleftharpoons xyz\quad K_2=5\times10^{-3}$
    The value of $K_3$ for the equilibrium $\dfrac{1}{2}x_2+\dfrac{1}{2}y_2+\dfrac{1}{2}z_2\rightleftharpoons xyz$ is :

    1. $2.5\times10^{-3}$
    2. $2.5\times10^{3}$
    3. $1.0\times10^{-5}$
    4. $5\times10^{-3}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    Reverse and halve the first reaction: $\dfrac12x_2+\dfrac12y_2\rightleftharpoons xy$, $K=\dfrac1{\sqrt{K_1}}=\dfrac1{500}=2\times10^{-3}$.
    Add the second reaction: $K_3=2\times10^{-3}\times5\times10^{-3}=1.0\times10^{-5}$.

  18. JEE Main 2026, 8 April, Shift 2 · Question 75
    Solid carbon, CaO and $\mathrm{CaCO_3}$ are mixed and allowed to attain equilibrium at T K.
    $\mathrm{CaCO_3(s)\rightleftharpoons CaO(s)+CO_2(g)}\quad K_{p_1}=0.08$ atm
    $\mathrm{C(s)+CO_2(g)\rightleftharpoons2CO(g)}\quad K_{p_2}=2$ atm
    The partial pressure of CO is __________ $\times10^{-1}$ atm

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 4

    From the first equilibrium, $p_{\mathrm{CO_2}}=K_{p_1}=0.08$ atm.
    Second: $\dfrac{p_{\mathrm{CO}}^2}{p_{\mathrm{CO_2}}}=2\Rightarrow p_{\mathrm{CO}}^2=0.16\Rightarrow p_{\mathrm{CO}}=0.4$ atm $=4\times10^{-1}$ atm.
