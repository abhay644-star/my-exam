  1. JEE Main 2026, 2 April, Shift 1 · Question 56
    The solubility product constants of $\mathrm{Ag_2CrO_4}$ and AgBr are $32x$ and $4y$ respectively at 298 K.
    The value of $\left(\dfrac{\text{molarity of } \mathrm{Ag_2CrO_4}}{\text{molarity of AgBr}}\right)$ can be expressed as :

    1. $\dfrac{2\sqrt[3]{x}}{y}$
    2. $2\sqrt{\dfrac{x}{y}}$
    3. $\sqrt{\dfrac{x}{y}}$
    4. $\dfrac{\sqrt[3]{x}}{\sqrt{y}}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    $\mathrm{Ag_2CrO_4}$: $K_{sp} = 4s_1^3 = 32x \Rightarrow s_1 = 2\sqrt[3]{x}$.
    AgBr: $K_{sp} = s_2^2 = 4y \Rightarrow s_2 = 2\sqrt{y}$.
    $\dfrac{s_1}{s_2} = \dfrac{\sqrt[3]{x}}{\sqrt{y}}$.

    The first and second ionization constants of a weak dibasic acid H$_2$A are $8.1\times10^{-8}$ and $1.0\times10^{-13}$ respectively. 0.1 mol of H$_2$A was dissolved in 1L of 0.1 M HCl solution. The concentration of HA$^-$ in the resultant solution is :

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    For the following reaction at 50 °C and at 2 atm pressure,
    $\mathrm{2N_2O_5(g)\rightleftharpoons2N_2O_4(g)+O_2(g)}$

    The reaction $\mathrm{A(g)\rightleftharpoons B(g)+C(g)}$ was initiated with the amount 'a' of A(g). At equilibrium it is found that the amount of A(g) remaining is $(a-x)$ at a total pressure of p.
    The equilibrium constant Kp of the reaction can be calculated from the expression :

    1. $\dfrac{x^2}{a^2+x^2}\times p$
    2. $\dfrac{x^2}{a^2-x^2}\times p$
    3. $\dfrac{a+x^2}{x^2}\times p$
    4. $\dfrac{a^2-x^2}{x^2}\times p$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    At equilibrium: A $=a-x$, B $=x$, C $=x$; total $=a+x$.
    $K_p=\dfrac{\left(\dfrac{x}{a+x}p\right)^2}{\dfrac{a-x}{a+x}p}=\dfrac{x^2p}{(a+x)(a-x)}=\dfrac{x^2}{a^2-x^2}\times p$.

    $K_p=\dfrac{(xp)\left(\tfrac x2p\right)^{1/2}}{(1-x)p}\approx\dfrac{x^{3/2}p^{1/2}}{\sqrt2}$.
    $x^3=\dfrac{2K_p^2}{p}$, so $x=\sqrt[3]{\dfrac{2K_p^2}{p}}$.

  54. JEE Main 2025, 29 January, Shift 2 · Question 56
    Consider the equilibrium
    $\mathrm{CO(g)+3H_2(g)\rightleftharpoons CH_4(g)+H_2O(g)}$
    If the pressure applied over the system increases by two fold at constant temperature then
    (A) Concentration of reactants and products increases.
    (B) Equilibrium will shift in forward direction.
    (C) Equilibrium constant increases since concentration of products increases.
    (D) Equilibrium constant remains unchanged as concentration of reactants and products remain same.
    Choose the <b>correct</b> answer from the options given below :

    1. (A), (B) and (C) only
    2. (B) and (C) only
    3. (A) and (B) only
    4. (A), (B) and (D) only
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    Compressing the gas raises every concentration (A true). The forward reaction reduces the number of gas moles (4 to 2), so the equilibrium shifts forward (B true).
    K depends only on temperature, so it does not change; but the concentrations do change, so the reason in D is wrong, and C is wrong too.
