  1. JEE Main 2026, 2 April, Shift 1 · Question 59
    Given below are two statements :
    Statement (I) : The first ionisation enthalpy of the elements Na, Mg, Cl and Ar follows the order Na > Mg > Cl > Ar.
    Statement (II) : Among Ca, Al, Fe and B, the third ionisation enthalpy is very high for Ca.
    In the light of the above statements, choose the correct answer from the options given below :

    1. Both Statement I and Statement II are true
    2. Both Statement I and Statement II are false
    3. Statement I is true but Statement II is false
    4. Statement I is false but Statement II is true
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    Across period 3 the first ionisation enthalpy increases: Ar > Cl > Mg > Na, so Statement I is false.
    $\mathrm{Ca^{2+}}$ has a noble-gas (Ar) configuration, so removing a third electron needs very high energy; Al, B and Fe lose a third electron relatively easily. Statement II is true.

  2. JEE Main 2026, 2 April, Shift 2 · Question 58
    The correct set that contains all kinds (basic, acidic, amphoteric and neutral) of oxides is :

    1. Na$_2$O, K$_2$O, Al$_2$O$_3$ and As$_2$O$_3$
    2. Al$_2$O$_3$, As$_2$O$_3$, CO and NO
    3. K$_2$O, Cl$_2$O$_7$, As$_2$O$_3$ and NO
    4. Na$_2$O, N$_2$O, Al$_2$O$_3$ and CO
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    K$_2$O is basic, Cl$_2$O$_7$ is acidic, As$_2$O$_3$ is amphoteric and NO is neutral.
    The other sets each miss at least one kind (e.g. no acidic oxide in the first, second and fourth sets).
    Answer: K$_2$O, Cl$_2$O$_7$, As$_2$O$_3$ and NO.

  3. JEE Main 2026, 2 April, Shift 2 · Question 59
    Given below are two statements :
    Statement I : The second ionization enthalpy of B, Al and Ga is in the order of B > Al > Ga.
    Statement II : The correct order in terms of first ionization enthalpy is Si < Ge < Pb < Sn.
    In the light of the above statements, choose the correct answer from the options given below :

    1. Both Statement I and Statement II are true
    2. Both Statement I and Statement II are false
    3. Statement I is true but Statement II is false
    4. Statement I is false but Statement II is true
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    Second ionization enthalpies (kJ/mol): B 2427, Al 1817, Ga 1979, so the order is B > Ga > Al (poor d-shielding in Ga). Statement I is false.
    First ionization enthalpies (kJ/mol): Si 786, Ge 762, Sn 709, Pb 716, so the order is Sn < Pb < Ge < Si. Statement II is false.
    Both statements are false.

  4. JEE Main 2026, 4 April, Shift 1 · Question 58
    A monoatomic anion $(\mathrm{A^-})$ has $45$ neutrons and $36$ electrons. Atomic mass, group in the periodic table and physical state at room temperature of the element (A) respectively are

    1. $80$, $17$, liquid
    2. $81$, $16$, solid
    3. $80$, $16$, gas
    4. $81$, $15$, gas
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    $\mathrm{A^-}$ has $36$ electrons, so $Z=35$: bromine.
    Mass number $=35+45=80$; bromine is in group $17$ and is a liquid at room temperature.

  5. JEE Main 2026, 4 April, Shift 2 · Question 57
    The $1^{\text{st}}$ ionization enthalpy for Mg is +737 kJ/mol. The most probable estimated value of the $2^{\text{nd}}$ ionization enthalpy of Mg is _________.

    1. $-906$ kJ/mol
    2. $-856$ kJ/mol
    3. $+1450$ kJ/mol
    4. $+590$ kJ/mol
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    Ionization enthalpies are always positive, and removing an electron from the positive ion $\mathrm{Mg^+}$ needs more energy than the first ionization.
    So $\Delta_iH_2>737$ kJ/mol; the only such value is $+1450$ kJ/mol.

  6. JEE Main 2026, 4 April, Shift 2 · Question 58
    The electronegativity of a group 13 element 'E' is same as that of Ge (on Pauling scale and upto one decimal point). The CORRECT statements about $\mathrm{E^{3+}}$ are
    A. It can act as a reducing agent.
    B. It can act as an oxidizing agent.
    C. $\mathrm{E^{3+}}$ is more stable than $\mathrm{E^+}$.
    D. The standard electrode potential value for $\mathrm{E^{3+}/E}$ is positive.
    Choose the correct answer from the options given below:

    1. A and C Only
    2. B and C Only
    3. B and D Only
    4. A and D Only
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    Pauling electronegativity of Ge is 1.8; in group 13 (B 2.0, Al 1.5, Ga 1.6, In 1.7, Tl 1.8) this matches Tl, so E = Tl.
    Due to the inert pair effect $\mathrm{Tl^+}$ is more stable than $\mathrm{Tl^{3+}}$ (C false), so $\mathrm{Tl^{3+}}$ readily gains electrons: it is an oxidizing agent (B true), not a reducing agent (A false).
    $E^\circ(\mathrm{Tl^{3+}/Tl})\approx+1.26$ V is positive (D true).
    B and D only.

  7. JEE Main 2026, 5 April, Shift 1 · Question 58
    Given below are two statements:
    Statement I: The correct order of electronegativity of fluorine, oxygen and nitrogen is F > O > N.
    Statement II: The oxidation state of oxygen in $\mathrm{OF_2}$ is $+2$ and in $\mathrm{Na_2O}$ is $-2$.
    In the light of the above statements, choose the correct answer from the options given below

    1. Both Statement I and Statement II are true
    2. Both Statement I and Statement II are false
    3. Statement I is true but Statement II is false
    4. Statement I is false but Statement II is true
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    I: electronegativity F ($4.0$) > O ($3.5$) > N ($3.0$). True.
    II: in $\mathrm{OF_2}$, F is $-1$, so O is $+2$; in $\mathrm{Na_2O}$, O is $-2$. True.

  8. JEE Main 2026, 5 April, Shift 1 · Question 59
    Correct statements from the following are:
    A. Nitrogen in oxidation states from $+1$ to $+4$ disproportionates in acid medium.
    B. Nitrogen has the ability to form $d\pi$ - $p\pi$ multiple bonds with itself and other elements with small size and high electronegativity.
    C. N-N single bond is stronger than P-P single bond.
    D. Nitrogen has highest density in its group due to small size.
    E. The maximum covalency of nitrogen is four since it has only four valence orbitals for bonding.
    Choose the correct answer from the options given below:

    1. B, C and D Only
    2. C, D and E Only
    3. A, C and E Only
    4. A and E Only
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    A: true — oxidation states $+1$ to $+4$ of nitrogen tend to disproportionate in acid solution.
    B: false — nitrogen forms $p\pi$–$p\pi$ multiple bonds; it has no $d$ orbitals for $d\pi$–$p\pi$ bonding.
    C: false — the N–N single bond is weaker than P–P (lone-pair repulsion at the short bond length).
    D: false — density increases down the group; nitrogen has the lowest.
    E: true — only $2s$ and $2p$ orbitals are available, so maximum covalency is $4$.
    Correct: A and E.

  9. JEE Main 2026, 5 April, Shift 2 · Question 58
    Given below are two statements :
    Statement I : The number of pairs among $[\mathrm{Al_2O_3, Cr_2O_3}]$, $[\mathrm{Cl_2O_7, Mn_2O_7}]$, $[\mathrm{Na_2O, V_2O_3}]$ and $[\mathrm{CO, N_2O}]$ that contain oxides of same nature (acidic, basic, neutral or amphoteric) is 4.
    Statement II : Among $\mathrm{Na_2O}$, $\mathrm{Al_2O_3}$, CO and $\mathrm{Cl_2O_7}$, the most basic and acidic oxides are $\mathrm{Na_2O}$ and $\mathrm{Cl_2O_7}$, respectively.
    In the light of the above statements, choose the correct answer from the options given below :

    1. Both Statement I and Statement II are true
    2. Both Statement I and Statement II are false
    3. Statement I is true but Statement II is false
    4. Statement I is false but Statement II is true
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    $\mathrm{Al_2O_3}$, $\mathrm{Cr_2O_3}$: both amphoteric. $\mathrm{Cl_2O_7}$, $\mathrm{Mn_2O_7}$: both acidic. $\mathrm{Na_2O}$, $\mathrm{V_2O_3}$: both basic. CO, $\mathrm{N_2O}$: both neutral.
    All 4 pairs match, so Statement I is true.
    $\mathrm{Na_2O}$ is strongly basic and $\mathrm{Cl_2O_7}$ (anhydride of $\mathrm{HClO_4}$) is strongly acidic; $\mathrm{Al_2O_3}$ is amphoteric and CO neutral. Statement II is true.

  10. JEE Main 2026, 6 April, Shift 1 · Question 59
    Given below are two statements:
    Statement I: Aluminium is more electropositive than thallium as the standard electrode potential value of $E^\circ_{\mathrm{Al^{3+}/Al}}$ is negative and $E^\circ_{\mathrm{Tl^{3+}/Tl}}$ is positive.
    Statement II: The sum of first three ionization enthalpies of boron is very high when compared to that of aluminium. Due to this reason boron forms covalent compounds only and aluminium forms $\mathrm{Al^{3+}}$ ion.
    In the light of the above statements, choose the correct answer from the options given below

    1. Both Statement I and Statement II are true
    2. Both Statement I and Statement II are false
    3. Statement I is true but Statement II is false
    4. Statement I is false but Statement II is true
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    Statement I: $E^\circ_{\mathrm{Al^{3+}/Al}}=-1.66$ V and $E^\circ_{\mathrm{Tl^{3+}/Tl}}=+1.26$ V, so Al readily forms $\mathrm{Al^{3+}}$ and is more electropositive: true.
    Statement II: boron's very high $\Delta_iH_1+\Delta_iH_2+\Delta_iH_3$ prevents $\mathrm{B^{3+}}$, so boron forms only covalent compounds; Al, with lower values, forms $\mathrm{Al^{3+}}$: true.
    Both statements are true.

  11. JEE Main 2026, 6 April, Shift 1 · Question 71
    First and second ionization enthalpies of lithium are 520 kJ mol$^{-1}$ and 7297 kJ mol$^{-1}$ respectively. Energy required to convert 3.5 mg lithium (g) into $\mathrm{Li^{2+}(g)}$ $[\mathrm{Li(g)}\to\mathrm{Li^{2+}(g)}]$ is ________ kJ mol$^{-1}$. (nearest integer)
    [Molar mass of Li $=7$ g mol$^{-1}$]

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 4

    Moles of Li $=\dfrac{3.5\times10^{-3}}{7}=5\times10^{-4}$ mol.
    Energy per mole for $\mathrm{Li}\to\mathrm{Li^{2+}}$ $=520+7297=7817$ kJ.
    Energy $=5\times10^{-4}\times7817\approx3.91$ kJ, i.e. $4$ to the nearest integer.

  12. JEE Main 2026, 6 April, Shift 2 · Question 58
    In a period, the first ionisation enthalpy of the element at extreme left and the negative electron gain enthalpy of the extreme right element, except noble gases, are respectively.

    1. lowest and lowest
    2. highest and lowest
    3. lowest and highest
    4. highest and highest
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    Across a period, ionisation enthalpy increases, so the alkali metal at the extreme left has the lowest first ionisation enthalpy.
    The halogen at the extreme right (noble gases excluded) has the most negative electron gain enthalpy, i.e. the highest negative electron gain enthalpy.
    So: lowest and highest.

  13. JEE Main 2026, 8 April, Shift 2 · Question 58
    Match List - I with List - II.

    List - I
    Electronic configuration of neutral atom (where n = 2)    		List - II
    1$^{\text{st}}$ Ionization Energy (kJ mol$^{-1}$)
    A. $ns^2$I. 2080
    B. $ns^2np^1$II. 899
    C. $ns^2np^3$III. 800
    D. $ns^2np^6$IV. 1402
    Choose the correct answer from the options given below :

    1. A-II, B-III, C-IV, D-I
    2. A-IV, B-III, C-II, D-I
    3. A-III, B-II, C-IV, D-I
    4. A-III, B-II, C-I, D-IV
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    With $n=2$ the atoms are Be ($2s^2$), B ($2s^22p^1$), N ($2s^22p^3$) and Ne ($2s^22p^6$).
    First ionization energies: Be 899, B 800 (lower than Be: $2p$ electron is easier to remove), N 1402 (half-filled $2p$), Ne 2080 kJ mol$^{-1}$.
    A-II, B-III, C-IV, D-I.

  14. JEE Main 2026, 8 April, Shift 2 · Question 59
    Find the correct statements related to group 15 hydrides.
    A. Reducing nature increases from $\mathrm{NH_3}$ to $\mathrm{BiH_3}$
    B. Tendency to donate lone pair of electrons decreases from $\mathrm{NH_3}$ to $\mathrm{BiH_3}$
    C. The stability of hydrides decreases from $\mathrm{NH_3}$ to $\mathrm{BiH_3}$
    D. HEH bond angle decreases from $\mathrm{NH_3}$ to $\mathrm{SbH_3}$ (E = Elements of group 15)
    Choose the correct answer from the options given below :

    1. A and B only
    2. B and C only
    3. A, B, C and D
    4. A, C and D Only
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    Down the group the E–H bond becomes longer and weaker, so thermal stability decreases (C) and reducing character increases (A).
    The lone pair is in a larger, more diffuse orbital, so basicity (lone pair donation) decreases from $\mathrm{NH_3}$ to $\mathrm{BiH_3}$ (B).
    Bond angles: $\mathrm{NH_3}$ 107.8°, $\mathrm{PH_3}$ 93.6°, $\mathrm{AsH_3}$ 91.8°, $\mathrm{SbH_3}$ 91.3°: decreasing (D).
    All of A, B, C and D are correct.
