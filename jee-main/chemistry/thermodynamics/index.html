  1. JEE Main 2026, 2 April, Shift 1 · Question 54
    Consider the following data.
    (i) $\mathrm{2Al(s) + 6HCl(aq) \rightarrow Al_2Cl_6(aq) + 3H_2(g) + 1200\ kJ/mol}$
    (ii) $\mathrm{H_2(g) + Cl_2(g) \rightarrow 2HCl(g) + 164\ kJ/mol.}$
    (iii) $\mathrm{HCl(g) + aq \rightarrow HCl(aq) + 83\ kJ/mol.}$
    (iv) $\mathrm{Al_2Cl_6(s) + aq \rightarrow Al_2Cl_6(aq) + 663\ kJ/mol}$
    The enthalpy of formation of anhydrous solid $\mathrm{Al_2Cl_6}$ is :

    1. $-648\ \mathrm{kJ\ mol^{-1}}$
    2. $-1350\ \mathrm{kJ\ mol^{-1}}$
    3. $-2002\ \mathrm{kJ\ mol^{-1}}$
    4. $-1527\ \mathrm{kJ\ mol^{-1}}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    Target: $\mathrm{2Al(s) + 3Cl_2(g) \rightarrow Al_2Cl_6(s)}$.
    Heat released means $\Delta H$ negative: (i) $-1200$, (ii) $-164$, (iii) $-83$, (iv) $-663$ kJ.
    Target = (i) + 3(ii) + 6(iii) $-$ (iv).
    $\Delta_f H = -1200 - 492 - 498 + 663 = -1527$ kJ mol$^{-1}$.

  2. JEE Main 2026, 2 April, Shift 1 · Question 75
    At the transition temperature T, $\mathrm{A \rightleftharpoons B}$ and $\Delta G^0 = 105 - 35\log T$ where A and B are two states of substance X. The transition temperature in °C when pressure is 1 atm is ________. (Nearest integer)

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 727

    At the transition temperature (1 atm) the two states are in equilibrium under standard conditions, so $\Delta G^0 = 0$.
    $105 = 35\log T \Rightarrow \log T = 3 \Rightarrow T = 1000$ K.
    $t = 1000 - 273 = 727$°C.

  3. JEE Main 2026, 2 April, Shift 2 · Question 54
    Gas 'A' undergoes change from state 'X' to state 'Y'. In this process, the heat absorbed and work done by the gas is 10 J and 18 J respectively. Now gas is brought back to state 'X' by another process during which 6 J of heat is evolved. In the reverse process of 'Y' to 'X',

    1. 18 J of the work is done by the gas 'A'.
    2. 2 J of the work is done by the gas 'A'.
    3. 12 J of the work is done on the gas 'A' by the surrounding.
    4. 14 J of the work is done on the gas 'A' by the surrounding.
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    X $\to$ Y: $\Delta U=q-w_{by}=10-18=-8$ J.
    Y $\to$ X: $\Delta U=+8$ J with $q=-6$ J.
    $\Delta U=q+w_{on} \Rightarrow w_{on}=8+6=14$ J: 14 J of work is done on the gas.

  4. JEE Main 2026, 4 April, Shift 1 · Question 54
    Given below are two statements:
    Statement I: For an ideal gas, heat capacity at constant volume is always greater than the heat capacity at constant pressure.
    Statement II: In a constant volume process, no work is produced and all the heat withdrawn goes into the chaotic motion and is reflected by a temperature increase of the ideal gas.
    In the light of the above statements, choose the correct answer from the options given below

    1. Both Statement I and Statement II are true
    2. Both Statement I and Statement II are false
    3. Statement I is true but Statement II is false
    4. Statement I is false but Statement II is true
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    For an ideal gas $C_p-C_v=R>0$, so $C_v$ is smaller: Statement I is false.
    At constant volume $w=0$, so $q_V=\Delta U$: all the heat goes into the random (chaotic) motion of the molecules, seen as a rise in temperature. Statement II is true.

  5. JEE Main 2026, 4 April, Shift 2 · Question 71
    If 3.365g of ethanol ($l$) is burnt completely in a bomb calorimeter at 298.15 K, the heat produced is 99.472 kJ. The $|\Delta H_f^\circ|$ of ethanol at 298.15 K is ____________ $\times10^2$ kJ mol$^{-1}$. (Nearest integer)
    Given: Standard enthalpy for combustion of graphite $=-393.5$ kJ mol$^{-1}$
    Standard enthalpy of formation of water (l) $=-285.8$ kJ mol$^{-1}$
    Molar mass in g mol$^{-1}$ of C, H, O are 12 , 1 and 16 respectively

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 3

    Moles of ethanol $=\dfrac{3.365}{46}=0.07315$; $\Delta_cU=-\dfrac{99.472}{0.07315}=-1359.8$ kJ mol$^{-1}$ (bomb calorimeter, constant volume).
    $\mathrm{C_2H_5OH(l)}+3\mathrm{O_2}\to2\mathrm{CO_2}+3\mathrm{H_2O(l)}$: $\Delta n_g=-1$, so $\Delta_cH=\Delta_cU+\Delta n_gRT=-1359.8-2.48=-1362.3$ kJ mol$^{-1}$.
    $\Delta_fH(\text{ethanol})=2(-393.5)+3(-285.8)-(-1362.3)=-282.1$ kJ mol$^{-1}$.
    $|\Delta H_f^\circ|=2.82\times10^2\approx3\times10^2$ kJ mol$^{-1}$, so the answer is $3$.

  6. JEE Main 2026, 5 April, Shift 2 · Question 54
    Consider the following data for the reaction $\mathrm{X_2(g)+Y_2(g)\rightleftharpoons 2XY(g)}$ at 600 K. The $\Delta_rG^\ominus$ (in $\mathrm{kJ\ mol^{-1}}$) for the reaction is :$$\begin{array}{|c|c|c|}\hline \text{Compound} & \Delta_fH^\ominus_{600\,K}\ (\mathrm{kJ\ mol^{-1}}) & S^\ominus_{600\,K}\ (\mathrm{J\ mol^{-1}\ K^{-1}}) \\ \hline \mathrm{XY(g)} & 42 & 200 \\ \hline \mathrm{X_2(g)} & 8 & 140 \\ \hline \mathrm{Y_2(g)} & 80 & 250 \\ \hline \end{array}$$

    1. $-21000$
    2. $-10$
    3. $-1000$
    4. $-9.012$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    $\Delta_rH^\ominus=2(42)-(8+80)=-4\ \mathrm{kJ\ mol^{-1}}$.

  7. JEE Main 2026, 5 April, Shift 2 · Question 55
    The correct order of molar heat capacities measured at 298 K and 1 bar is :

    1. Copper(s) > Bromine(l) > Helium(g)
    2. Bromine(l) > Copper(s) > Helium(g)
    3. Helium(g) > Bromine(l) > Copper(s)
    4. Helium(g) > Bromine(l) = Copper(s)
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    Molar heat capacities ($C_p$, J $\mathrm{K^{-1}\ mol^{-1}}$) at 298 K: $\mathrm{Br_2(l)}\approx75.7$, Cu(s) $\approx24.4$, He(g) $=\dfrac52R\approx20.8$.
    A polyatomic liquid has many more modes to store energy than a metal (Dulong-Petit $\approx3R$) or a monatomic gas.
    Order: Bromine(l) > Copper(s) > Helium(g).

  8. JEE Main 2026, 6 April, Shift 1 · Question 55
    Arrange the following isothermal processes in order of the magnitude of the work $(p-V)$ involved between states 1 and 2.
    A. Expansion in single stage $w_A$
    B. Expansion in multi stages $w_B$
    C. Compression in single stage $w_C$
    D. Compression in multi stages $w_D$
    Choose the correct option.

    1. $|w_B|>|w_A|>|w_C|>|w_D|$
    2. $|w_C|>|w_D|>|w_A|>|w_B|$
    3. $|w_C|>|w_D|>|w_B|>|w_A|$
    4. $|w_B|>|w_A|>|w_D|>|w_C|$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    Expansion work is largest when reversible, so $|w_B|>|w_A|$; compression work is smallest when reversible, so $|w_C|>|w_D|$.
    Compression work is always at least the reversible work, and expansion work at most the reversible work, between the same states.
    So $|w_C|>|w_D|>|w_B|>|w_A|$.

  9. JEE Main 2026, 6 April, Shift 1 · Question 75
    Consider the reaction $\mathrm{X}\rightleftharpoons\mathrm{Y}$ at 300 K. If $\Delta H^\theta$ and K are 28.40 kJ mol$^{-1}$ and $1.8\times10^{-7}$ at the same temperature, then the magnitude of $\Delta S^\theta$ for the reaction in J K$^{-1}$ mol$^{-1}$ is________. (Nearest integer)
    (Given : R = 8.3 J K$^{-1}$ mol$^{-1}$, ln 10 = 2.3, log 3 = 0.48, log 2 = 0.30)

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 34

    $\log(1.8\times10^{-7})=\log2+2\log3-7-1=0.30+0.96-8=-6.74$, so $\ln K=2.3\times(-6.74)=-15.502$.
    $\Delta G^\theta=-RT\ln K=8.3\times300\times15.502\approx38600$ J mol$^{-1}$.
    $\Delta S^\theta=\dfrac{\Delta H^\theta-\Delta G^\theta}{T}=\dfrac{28400-38600}{300}=-34$ J K$^{-1}$ mol$^{-1}$.
    Magnitude $=34$.

  10. JEE Main 2026, 6 April, Shift 2 · Question 54
    Match List - I with List - II.
    Given $\mathrm{V_1}$ and $\mathrm{V_2}$ are initial and final volumes respectively.

    List-I (Isothermal process)List-II (Expression)
    A. Reversible expansionI. $q=0$
    B. Free expansionII. $q=nRT\ln\dfrac{V_2}{V_1}$
    C. Irreversible CompressionIII. $w=-p_{ext}(V_1-V_2)$
    D. Cyclic reversibleIV. $\dfrac{q_{rev}}{T}=0$
    Choose the correct answer from the options given below :

    1. A-II, B-III, C-I, D-IV
    2. A-II, B-I, C-IV, D-III
    3. A-II, B-I, C-III, D-IV
    4. A-I, B-II, C-III, D-IV
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    A. Isothermal reversible expansion: $\Delta U=0$, $q=-w=nRT\ln\dfrac{V_2}{V_1}$ → II.
    B. Free expansion of an ideal gas: $w=0$ and $\Delta U=0$, so $q=0$ → I.
    C. Irreversible compression against a constant external pressure: work given by the $p_{ext}$ expression → III.
    D. Cyclic reversible process: entropy is a state function, so $\oint\dfrac{q_{rev}}{T}=0$ → IV.
    So A-II, B-I, C-III, D-IV.

  12. JEE Main 2026, 21 January, Shift 1 · Question 55
    For the reaction, $\mathrm{N_2O_4\rightleftharpoons2NO_2}$, graph is plotted as shown below. Identify correct statements.
    A. Standard free energy change for the reaction is $-5.40$ kJ mol$^{-1}$.
    B. As $\Delta G^\ominus$ in graph is positive, $\mathrm{N_2O_4}$ will not dissociate into $\mathrm{NO_2}$ at all.
    C. Reverse reaction will go to completion.
    D. When 1 mole of $\mathrm{N_2O_4}$ changes into equilibrium mixture, value of $\Delta G^\ominus=-0.84$ kJ mol$^{-1}$
    E. When 2 mole of $\mathrm{NO_2}$ changes into equilibrium mixture, $\Delta G^\ominus$ for equilibrium mixture is $-6.24$ kJ mol$^{-1}$.
    Choose the correct answer from the options given below :

    1. A and D only
    2. C and E only
    3. D and E only
    4. B and C only
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    From the graph, pure $\mathrm{N_2O_4}$ (A) to pure $\mathrm{NO_2}$ (B) is $+5.40$ kJ mol$^{-1}$, so A is false.
    A positive $\Delta G^\ominus$ only means $K<1$; some dissociation still happens and the minimum E is the equilibrium mixture, so B and C are false.
    A to E: $G$ falls by 0.84, so D is true. B to E: $G$ falls by $5.40+0.84=6.24$, so E is true.
    Answer: D and E only.

  13. JEE Main 2026, 21 January, Shift 1 · Question 56
    Which of the following graphs between pressure 'p' versus volume 'V' represents the maximum work done ?

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    Work is the area under the p-V path (net area for a cycle).

    Use the following data :

    Substance$\Delta_fH^\ominus$ (500K) / kJ mol$^{-1}$$S^\ominus$ (500K) / J K$^{-1}$ mol$^{-1}$

  15. JEE Main 2026, 21 January, Shift 2 · Question 54
    Consider the following data :
    $\Delta_fH^\ominus$ (methane, g) $=-X$ kJ mol$^{-1}$
    Enthalpy of sublimation of graphite $=Y$ kJ mol$^{-1}$
    Dissociation enthalpy of $\mathrm{H_2}=Z$ kJ mol$^{-1}$
    The bond enthalpy of C-H bond is given by :

    1. $X+Y+Z$
    2. $\dfrac{X+Y+2Z}{4}$
    3. $\dfrac{X+Y+4Z}{2}$
    4. $\dfrac{-X+Y+Z}{4}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    Atomisation of methane: $\mathrm{CH_4(g)\rightarrow C(g)+4H(g)}$, $\Delta H=4\varepsilon_{\mathrm{C-H}}$.
    By Hess's law: $\Delta H=-\Delta_fH^\ominus(\mathrm{CH_4})+\Delta_{sub}H(\mathrm{C})+2\Delta_{diss}H(\mathrm{H_2})=X+Y+2Z$.
    $\varepsilon_{\mathrm{C-H}}=\dfrac{X+Y+2Z}{4}$.

    Match the LIST-I with LIST-II

    List-I
    Thermodynamic Process    		List-II
    Magnitude in kJ
    A. Work done in reversible, isothermal expansion of 2 mol of ideal gas from 2 dm$^3$ to 20 dm$^3$ at 300 K.I. 4
    B. Work done in irreversible isothermal expansion of 1 mol ideal gas from 1 m$^3$ to 3 m$^3$ at 300 K against a constant pressure of 3kPa.II. 11.5

    If the enthalpy of sublimation of Li is 155 kJ mol$^{-1}$, enthalpy of dissociation of $\mathrm{F_2}$ is 150 kJ mol$^{-1}$, ionization enthalpy of Li is 520 kJ mol$^{-1}$, electron gain enthalpy of F is $-313$ kJ mol$^{-1}$, standard enthalpy of formation of LiF is $-594$ kJ mol$^{-1}$. The magnitude of lattice enthalpy of LiF is __________ kJ mol$^{-1}$. (Nearest Integer)

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 1031

  18. JEE Main 2026, 23 January, Shift 1 · Question 54
    A cup of water at 5$^\circ$C (system) is placed in a microwave oven and the oven is turned on for one minute during which the water begins to boil. Which of the following option is true ?

    1. q $=$ +ve, w $=$ $-$ve, $\Delta$U $=$ +ve
    2. q $=$ +ve, w $=$ 0, $\Delta$U $=$ $-$ve
    3. q $=$ +ve, w $=$ $-$ve, $\Delta$U $=$ $-$ve
    4. q $=$ $-$ve, w $=$ $-$ve, $\Delta$U $=$ $-$ve
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    The water absorbs energy and its temperature rises: q is positive.
    As it heats and starts to boil it expands against the atmosphere, so work is done by the system: w is negative (small).
    The internal energy increases: $\Delta U$ is positive.

  20. JEE Main 2026, 24 January, Shift 2 · Question 54
    The heat of atomisation of methane and ethane are 'x' kJ mol$^{-1}$ and 'y' kJ mol$^{-1}$ respectively. The longest wavelength $(\lambda)$ of light capable of breaking the C$-$C bond can be expressed in SI unit as:

    1. $\dfrac{N_Ahc}{250(4y-6x)}$
    2. $\dfrac{hc}{1000}\left(\dfrac{y-6x}{4}\right)^{-1}$
    3. $\dfrac{N_Ahc}{250(y-6x)}$
    4. $N_Ahc\left(y-\dfrac{6x}{4}\right)^{-1}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    C$-$H bond enthalpy $=\dfrac x4$; ethane: $\mathrm{C-C}+6\times\dfrac x4=y$, so C$-$C $=y-\dfrac{3x}{2}$ kJ mol$^{-1}$.
    Energy per bond $=\dfrac{1000\left(y-\frac{3x}2\right)}{N_A}$ J $=\dfrac{250(4y-6x)}{N_A}$ J.
    $\lambda=\dfrac{hc}{E}=\dfrac{N_Ahc}{250(4y-6x)}$.

  21. JEE Main 2026, 28 January, Shift 1 · Question 54
    $20.0\ \mathrm{dm^3}$ of an ideal gas 'X' at 600 K and 0.5 MPa undergoes isothermal reversible expansion until pressure of the gas is 0.2 MPa. Which of the following option is correct?
    (Given: log 2 = 0.3010 and log 5 = 0.6989)

    1. w = $-$3.9 kJ, $\Delta$U = 0, $\Delta$H = 0; q = 3.9 kJ
    2. w = 9.1 J, $\Delta$U = 9.1 J, $\Delta$H = 0; q = 0
    3. w = $-$9.1 kJ, $\Delta$U = 0, $\Delta$H = 0, q = 9.1 kJ
    4. w = + 4.1 kJ, $\Delta$U = 0 , $\Delta$H = 0; q = $-$4.1 kJ
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    Isothermal ideal gas: $\Delta U=\Delta H=0$ and $q=-w$.
    $w=-p_1V_1\ln\dfrac{p_1}{p_2}=-(0.5\times10^6)(20\times10^{-3})(2.303)\log2.5$.
    $\log2.5=0.6989-0.3010=0.3979$, so $w=-10^4\times0.916\approx-9.1$ kJ and $q=+9.1$ kJ.
