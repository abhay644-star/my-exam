  1. JEE Main 2026, 4 April, Shift 1 · Question 66
    Amongst the following, the total number of compounds soluble in aqueous NaOH at room temperature is:

    1. $5$
    2. $4$
    3. $6$
    4. $3$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    Phenols and carboxylic acids dissolve in aqueous NaOH (forming salts).
    Soluble: (II) 1-naphthol, (III) 4-(dimethylamino)phenol, (IV) p-cresol, (V) benzoic acid, (VII) 1-naphthoic acid.
    Insoluble: (I) benzaldehyde, (VI) a tertiary amine, (VIII) a hydrocarbon, (IX) 1-naphthylmethanol (an alcohol, not acidic enough).
    Total $=5$.

  2. JEE Main 2026, 4 April, Shift 1 · Question 70
    Identify the colour of compound 'X' in the sequence of the reaction.

    1. Violet
    2. Green
    3. Red
    4. Colourless
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    Phenol and phthalic anhydride in conc. $\mathrm{H_2SO_4}$ give phenolphthalein (colourless lactone form).
    In NaOH it opens to the quinonoid dianion, which is pink (extended conjugation).
    In excess NaOH, $\mathrm{OH^-}$ adds to the central carbon, giving the carbinol trianion X; the central carbon becomes $sp^3$ and the conjugation is broken, so X is colourless.

  3. JEE Main 2026, 4 April, Shift 2 · Question 66
    The descending order of acidity among the following compounds is:
    Choose the correct answer from the options given below:

    1. B > D > E > A > C
    2. D > B > E > A > C
    3. C > A > B > D > E
    4. D > E > B > A > C
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    Carboxylic acids are much stronger than phenols: D (p-nitrobenzoic acid, $\mathrm{pK_a}\approx3.4$) > E (benzoic acid, $\approx4.2$).
    Among phenols, $-\mathrm{NO_2}$ (electron-withdrawing) increases acidity and $-\mathrm{OCH_3}$ (electron-donating, +R) decreases it: B (p-nitrophenol, $\approx7.1$) > A (phenol, $\approx10$) > C (p-methoxyphenol, $\approx10.2$).
    Order: D > E > B > A > C.

  4. JEE Main 2026, 4 April, Shift 2 · Question 70
    Match the LIST-I with LIST-II
    Choose the correct answer from the options given below:

    1. A-III, B-I, C-IV, D-II
    2. A-III, B-IV, C-I, D-II
    3. A-I, B-III, C-II, D-IV
    4. A-I, B-II, C-III, D-IV
    Show answer and solution

  5. JEE Main 2026, 5 April, Shift 1 · Question 67
    Consider compounds A, B and C with following structural formulae
    $\mathrm{A = CH_3-CH_2-CH_2-CH_2-CH_2-OH}$
    $\mathrm{B = CH_2=CH-CH_2-CH_2-CH_3}$
    $\mathrm{C = HO-CH_2-CH_2-CH(OH)-CH_3}$
    For the conversion of B from A, reagent (D) required is _________ and structural formula of product (E) obtained when C undergoes same reaction using excess reagent (D) is ________.

    1. D: Conc. $\mathrm{H_2SO_4}$; E: $\mathrm{CH_2=CH-CH(OH)CH_3}$
    2. D: PCC; E: $\mathrm{HO-CH_2-CH_2-CH=CH_2}$
    3. D: PCC; E: $\mathrm{CH_2=CH-CH=CH_2}$
    4. D: Conc. $\mathrm{H_2SO_4}$ or $\mathrm{H_3PO_4}$; E: $\mathrm{CH_2=CH-CH=CH_2}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    A $\to$ B is dehydration of pentan-1-ol to pent-1-ene: reagent D is a dehydrating acid, conc. $\mathrm{H_2SO_4}$ or $\mathrm{H_3PO_4}$ (PCC would oxidise the alcohol instead).
    C is butane-1,3-diol; with excess acid both OH groups are eliminated.
    E $=\mathrm{CH_2=CH-CH=CH_2}$ (buta-1,3-diene, conjugated).

  6. JEE Main 2026, 5 April, Shift 1 · Question 73
    One mole of phenol is treated with dilute $\mathrm{HNO_3}$ at $298$ K to give a mixture of products. The mixture is separated by steam distillation. The steam volatile compound (X) is separated. The increase in percentage of oxygen in (X) with respect to phenol is _______$\times10^{-1}$ %
    (Given molar mass in g mol$^{-1}$ H:1, C:12, N:14, O:16)

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 175

    Dilute $\mathrm{HNO_3}$ gives o- and p-nitrophenol; o-nitrophenol (intramolecular H-bonding) is steam volatile, so X $=\mathrm{C_6H_5NO_3}$, $M=139$.
    % O in X $=\dfrac{48}{139}\times100=34.53\%$; % O in phenol ($M=94$) $=\dfrac{16}{94}\times100=17.02\%$.
    Increase $=17.51\%\approx175\times10^{-1}\%$.

  7. JEE Main 2026, 6 April, Shift 1 · Question 67
    Consider the following sequence of reactions

    $\displaystyle \mathrm{(CH_3)_3C{-}OH}\ \xrightarrow[\text{(ii) H}^+\text{, PhCOOH}]{\text{(i) Cu / 573K}}\ \mathrm{P}$
    The major product P is:

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    Tertiary alcohols are dehydrated by Cu at 573 K: $\mathrm{(CH_3)_3COH}\to\mathrm{(CH_3)_2C{=}CH_2}$ (2-methylpropene).
    With $\mathrm{H^+}$ the alkene gives the tert-butyl carbocation, which alkylates the ring of benzoic acid.
    COOH is a meta-directing group, so P is 3-tert-butylbenzoic acid, option (2).

  8. JEE Main 2026, 6 April, Shift 1 · Question 73
    4.7 g of phenol is heated with Zn to give product X. If this reaction goes to 60% completion then the number of moles of compound X formed will be ____ $\times10^{-2}$. (Nearest Integer)
    (Given molar mass in g mol$^{-1}$ : H:1, C:12, O:16)

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 3

    $\mathrm{C_6H_5OH}+\mathrm{Zn}\to\mathrm{C_6H_6}+\mathrm{ZnO}$, so X is benzene (1 : 1).
    Moles of phenol $=\dfrac{4.7}{94}=0.05$.
    At 60% completion: $0.6\times0.05=0.03=3\times10^{-2}$ mol.

  9. JEE Main 2026, 6 April, Shift 2 · Question 66
    Consider the following reaction (benzyl phenyl ether with HI).
    Statement I : In the above reaction, product formed will be a mixture of benzyl alcohol and iodobenzene.
    Statement II : In the above reaction, the $-\mathrm{O-CH_2}-$ bond is cleaved to give the product.
    In the light of the above statements, choose the correct answer from the options given below :

    1. Both Statement I and Statement II are true
    2. Both Statement I and Statement II are false
    3. Statement I is true but Statement II is false
    4. Statement I is false but Statement II is true
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    The O–aryl bond has partial double bond character and the phenyl carbon does not undergo $S_N$ attack.
    After protonation, $\mathrm{I^-}$ attacks the benzylic $\mathrm{CH_2}$, so the $\mathrm{O-CH_2}$ bond is cleaved: Statement II is true.
    Products: phenol and benzyl iodide, not benzyl alcohol and iodobenzene: Statement I is false.

  10. JEE Main 2026, 22 January, Shift 1 · Question 63
    Given below are two statements:
    Statement I: Phenol on treatment with $\mathrm{CHCl_3}$/aq. KOH under refluxing condition, followed by acidification produces $p$-hydroxy benzaldehyde as the major product and $o$-hydroxy benzaldehyde as the minor product.
    Statement II: The mixture of $p$-hydroxybenzaldehyde and $o$-hydroxybenzaldehyde can be easily separated through steam distillation.
    In the light of the above statements, choose the correct answer from the options given below

    1. Both Statement I and Statement II are true
    2. Both Statement I and Statement II are false
    3. Statement I is true but Statement II is false
    4. Statement I is false but Statement II is true
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    Statement I: in the Reimer-Tiemann reaction the major product is $o$-hydroxybenzaldehyde (salicylaldehyde), not the para isomer. False.
    Statement II: the ortho isomer has intramolecular hydrogen bonding and is steam volatile; the para isomer is held by intermolecular hydrogen bonds and is not. So steam distillation separates them. True.

  11. JEE Main 2026, 22 January, Shift 2 · Question 68
    3, 3-Dimethyl-2-butanol cannot be prepared by :
    Choose the correct answer from the options given below :

    1. B and C Only
    2. B Only
    3. B, C and E Only
    4. B and E Only
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    Target: $\mathrm{(CH_3)_3C{-}CH(OH){-}CH_3}$.
    A: $\mathrm{(CH_3)_3CCHO}$ with $\mathrm{CH_3MgBr}$ gives it directly.
    B: the alkene is 3,3-dimethylpent-1-ene, $\mathrm{CH_2{=}CH{-}C(CH_3)_2{-}CH_2CH_3}$, a $\mathrm{C_7}$ compound; its acid-catalysed hydration (with carbocation rearrangement) gives $\mathrm{C_7}$ alcohols, not the $\mathrm{C_6}$ target. Cannot.
    C: ozonolysis of $\mathrm{(CH_3)_3C(CH_3)C{=}C(CH_3)C(CH_3)_3}$ gives two molecules of $\mathrm{(CH_3)_3CCOCH_3}$, which $\mathrm{NaBH_4}$ reduces to the target.
    D: $\mathrm{LiAlH_4}$ reduces $\mathrm{(CH_3)_3CCOCH_3}$ to the target.
    E: hydration of $\mathrm{(CH_3)_3CC{\equiv}CH}$ gives the ketone $\mathrm{(CH_3)_3CCOCH_3}$, not the alcohol. Cannot.
    Answer: B and E only.

  12. JEE Main 2026, 23 January, Shift 2 · Question 66
    A mixed ether (P), when heated with excess of hot concentrated hydrogen iodide produces two different alkyl iodides which when treated with aq. NaOH give compounds (Q) and (R). Both (Q) and (R) give yellow precipitate with NaOI. Identify the mixed ether (P):

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    Excess HI breaks both C-O bonds, giving two alkyl iodides, and aq. NaOH turns them into the two alcohols. Both must give iodoform, so each must be ethanol or have a $\mathrm{CH_3CH(OH)-}$ group.
    (3) is the ether of ethanol and butan-2-ol (ethyl sec-butyl ether), and both alcohols give iodoform.
    (1) gives a tertiary alcohol, (2) gives benzyl alcohol and (4) gives 1-phenylpropan-1-ol, none of which gives iodoform.

  13. JEE Main 2026, 24 January, Shift 1 · Question 67
    Consider the following two reactions A and B.
    Numerical value of [molar mass of x + molar mass of y] is ____.

    1. $46$
    2. $160$
    3. $88$
    4. $4$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    (A) Phenol + Na gives sodium phenoxide and hydrogen: x = $\mathrm{H_2}$ (2 g mol$^{-1}$).
    (B) Benzoic acid + $\mathrm{NaHCO_3}$ gives sodium benzoate, water and carbon dioxide: y = $\mathrm{CO_2}$ (44 g mol$^{-1}$).
    Sum $=46$.

  14. JEE Main 2026, 24 January, Shift 1 · Question 69
    A hydroxy compound (X) with molar mass 122 g mol$^{-1}$ is acetylated with acetic anhydride, using a large excess of the reagent ensuring complete acetylation of all hydroxyl groups. The product obtained has a molar mass of 290 g mol$^{-1}$. The number of hydroxyl groups present in compound (X) is:

    1. $3$
    2. $5$
    3. $2$
    4. $4$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    Each acetylation replaces $-$OH by $-\mathrm{OCOCH_3}$, adding $42$ g mol$^{-1}$.
    Number of OH groups $=\dfrac{290-122}{42}=4$.

  15. JEE Main 2026, 24 January, Shift 2 · Question 67
    From the following, how many compounds contain at least one secondary alcohol?
    Choose the correct answer from the options given below:

    1. Three
    2. Five
    3. Two
    4. Four
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    A secondary alcohol has OH on a carbon bonded to two other carbons.
    (I): only $\mathrm{CH_2OH}$ groups (primary). (II): butane-1,2-diol, the CH(OH) is secondary. (III): both OH groups are on tertiary carbons.
    (IV): the side-chain CH(OH) (and the ring CH(OH)) are secondary. (V): the CH(OH)$\mathrm{CH_3}$ group is secondary. (VI): two tertiary OH and one primary.
    So II, IV and V: three compounds.

  16. JEE Main 2026, 28 January, Shift 1 · Question 66
    Consider the following reaction sequence
    Compound (y) develops characteristic colour with neutral $\mathrm{FeCl_3}$ solution.
    Identify the INCORRECT statement from the following for the above sequence.

    1. Both compounds x and y will dissolve in NaOH.
    2. Compound y will dissolve in $\mathrm{NaHCO_3}$ and evolve a gas.
    3. Both compounds x and y will burn with sooty flame.
    4. Compound x is more acidic than compound y.
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    Molar mass $=2\times47=94$; C: $0.766\times94=72$ (6 C), H: $0.0638\times94=6$ (6 H), remainder 16 (1 O). So x is phenol, $\mathrm{C_6H_5OH}$.

  18. JEE Main 2025, 4 April, Shift 1 · Question 68
    An organic compound (X) with molecular formula $\mathrm{C_3H_6O}$ is not readily oxidised. On reduction it gives $\mathrm{C_3H_8O}$(Y) which reacts with HBr to give a bromide (Z) which is converted to Grignard reagent. This Grignard reagent on reaction with (X) followed by hydrolysis give 2, 3-dimethylbutan-2-ol. Compounds (X), (Y) and (Z) respectively are:

    1. $\mathrm{CH_3COCH_3}$, $\mathrm{CH_3CH_2CH_2OH}$, $\mathrm{CH_3CH(Br)CH_3}$
    2. $\mathrm{CH_3CH_2CHO}$, $\mathrm{CH_3CH=CH_2}$, $\mathrm{CH_3CH(Br)CH_3}$
    3. $\mathrm{CH_3COCH_3}$, $\mathrm{CH_3CH(OH)CH_3}$, $\mathrm{CH_3CH(Br)CH_3}$
    4. $\mathrm{CH_3CH_2CHO}$, $\mathrm{CH_3CH_2CH_2OH}$, $\mathrm{CH_3CH_2CH_2Br}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    Not readily oxidised, $\mathrm{C_3H_6O}$: a ketone, acetone (X).

    Hydrolysis gives $\mathrm{(CH_3)_2C(OH)CH(CH_3)_2}$, 2,3-dimethylbutan-2-ol, as required. Option (3).

  20. JEE Main 2025, 7 April, Shift 1 · Question 64
    Given below are two statements:
    Statement I: Dimethyl ether is completely soluble in water. However, diethyl ether is soluble in water to a very small extent.
    Statement II: Sodium metal can be used to dry diethyl ether and not ethyl alcohol.
    In the light of given statements. choose the correct answer from the options given below

    1. Both Statement I and Statement II are true
    2. Both Statement I and Statement II are False
    3. Statement I is true but Statement II is false
    4. Statement I is false but Statement II are true
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    Statement I: dimethyl ether is miscible with water (hydrogen bonding with a small alkyl part), while diethyl ether dissolves only slightly. True.

  21. JEE Main 2025, 7 April, Shift 1 · Question 68
    Which of the following compounds is least likely to give effervescence of $\mathrm{CO_2}$ in presence of aq. $\mathrm{NaHCO_3}$ ?

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    Only acids stronger than carbonic acid ($\mathrm{p}K_a\approx6.4$) release $\mathrm{CO_2}$ from $\mathrm{NaHCO_3}$.
    4-Nitrobenzoic acid ($\mathrm{p}K_a\approx3.4$), anilinium chloride ($\mathrm{p}K_a\approx4.6$) and picric acid ($\mathrm{p}K_a\approx0.4$) do; 3-nitrophenol ($\mathrm{p}K_a\approx8.4$) does not, because a meta nitro group cannot stabilise the phenoxide by resonance.
    Answer: 3-nitrophenol, option (3).

    (iso-Bu $\Rightarrow$ isobutyl, sec-Bu $\Rightarrow$ sec-butyl, nPr $\Rightarrow$ n-propyl, $^t$Bu $\Rightarrow$ tert-butyl, Et $\Rightarrow$ ethyl)

    Answer: (b)

    (Given molar mass in g mol$^{-1}$ of C, H, O, Br are 12, 1, 16, 80 respectively )

    1. 4.0 g
    2. 6.0 g
    3. 10.22 g
    4. 20.44 g
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    In the light of the above statements, choose the correct answer from the options given below :
