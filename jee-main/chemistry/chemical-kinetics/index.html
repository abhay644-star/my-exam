  1. JEE Main 2026, 2 April, Shift 1 · Question 58
    $\mathrm{t_{100\%}}$ is the time required for the 100% completion of the reaction while $\mathrm{t_{1/2}}$ is the time required for 50% of the reaction to be completed. Which of the following option correctly represents the relation between $\mathrm{t_{100\%}}$ and $\mathrm{t_{1/2}}$ for zero and first order reactions respectively ?

    1. $\mathrm{t_{100\%}} = (\mathrm{t_{1/2}})^2$ and $\mathrm{t_{100\%}} = (\mathrm{t_{1/2}})^{-\infty}$
    2. $\mathrm{t_{100\%}} = 2\mathrm{t_{1/2}}$ and $\mathrm{t_{100\%}} = (\mathrm{t_{1/2}})^{\infty}$
    3. $\mathrm{t_{100\%}} = 2\mathrm{t_{1/2}}$ and $\mathrm{t_{100\%}} = (2\mathrm{t_{1/2}})^2$
    4. $\mathrm{t_{100\%}} = (\mathrm{t_{1/2}})^{\infty}$ and $\mathrm{t_{100\%}} = 2\mathrm{t_{1/2}}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    Zero order: $[A] = [A]_0 - kt$, so $t_{100\%} = [A]_0/k$ and $t_{1/2} = [A]_0/2k$, giving $t_{100\%} = 2t_{1/2}$.
    First order: $t = \dfrac{1}{k}\ln\dfrac{[A]_0}{[A]}$ diverges as $[A] \to 0$, so 100% completion takes infinite time, written $(t_{1/2})^{\infty}$.

  2. JEE Main 2026, 2 April, Shift 1 · Question 74
    For reaction $\mathrm{A \rightarrow P}$, rate constant $\mathrm{k = 1.5 \times 10^3\ s^{-1}}$ at 27°C
    If activation energy for the above reaction is 60 kJ mol$^{-1}$, then the temperature (in °C) at which rate constant, $\mathrm{k = 4.5 \times 10^3\ s^{-1}}$ is ________. (Nearest integer)
    Given : log 2 $=$ 0.30, log 3 $=$ 0.48, R $=$ 8.3 J K$^{-1}$ mol$^{-1}$, ln 10 $=$ 2.3

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 41

    $\ln\dfrac{k_2}{k_1} = \dfrac{E_a}{R}\left(\dfrac{1}{T_1} - \dfrac{1}{T_2}\right)$, with $k_2/k_1 = 3$, $\ln 3 = 2.3 \times 0.48 = 1.104$.
    $\dfrac{E_a}{R} = \dfrac{60000}{8.3} = 7229$ K.
    $\dfrac{1}{T_2} = \dfrac{1}{300} - \dfrac{1.104}{7229} = 0.0031806 \Rightarrow T_2 \approx 314.4$ K.
    $t \approx 41$°C.

  3. JEE Main 2026, 2 April, Shift 2 · Question 57
    Consider the reaction aX $\to$ bY, for which the rate constant at 30$^\circ$C is $1\times10^{-3}$ mol$^{-1}$ L s$^{-1}$. Which of the following statements are true ?
    A. When concentration of 'X' is increased to four times, the rate of reaction becomes 16 times.
    B. The reaction is a second order reaction.
    C. The half-life period is independent of the concentration of X.
    D. Decomposition of N$_2$O$_5$ is an example of the above reaction.
    E. The plot of $\ln\dfrac{[R_0]}{[R]}$ against time shown in the figure is valid for the above reaction.
    Choose the correct answer from the options given below :

    1. A and B Only
    2. A, B and C Only
    3. A, B, D and E Only
    4. C and D Only
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    Units mol$^{-1}$ L s$^{-1}$ mean a second order reaction: B is true.
    Rate $\propto[\mathrm X]^2$: four times the concentration gives 16 times the rate, so A is true.
    For second order $t_{1/2}=\dfrac{1}{k[\mathrm X]_0}$ depends on concentration: C false.
    N$_2$O$_5$ decomposition is first order, and a straight line of $\ln\dfrac{[R_0]}{[R]}$ vs $t$ is the first-order plot: D and E false.
    Answer: A and B only.

  4. JEE Main 2026, 2 April, Shift 2 · Question 72
    Consider the following gas phase reaction being carried out in a closed vessel at 25$^\circ$C.

    $\displaystyle 2\mathrm{A(g)}\longrightarrow4\mathrm{B(g)}+\mathrm{C(g)}$
    $$\begin{array}{c|c}\text{time (min)} & \text{total pressure of the system (mm Hg)}\\\hline 30 & 300\\\hline \infty & 600\end{array}$$The pressure of C(g) at 30 minutes time interval would be __________ mm Hg. (nearest integer)

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 20

    Let the initial pressure of A be $p_0$. At completion 2 A give 5 molecules: $\dfrac52p_0=600 \Rightarrow p_0=240$ mm Hg.
    If $p_C=x$ at 30 min: $p_A=240-2x$, $p_B=4x$, total $=240+3x=300 \Rightarrow x=20$.
    Pressure of C $=20$ mm Hg.

  5. JEE Main 2026, 4 April, Shift 1 · Question 57
    Consider the first order reaction $\mathrm{R\to P}$.
    The fraction of molecules decomposed in the given first order reaction can be expressed as

    1. $1-e^{k_1t}$
    2. $1+e^{k_1t}$
    3. $1+e^{-k_1t}$
    4. $1-e^{-k_1t}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    First order: $[\mathrm R]=[\mathrm R]_0e^{-k_1t}$.
    Fraction decomposed $=\dfrac{[\mathrm R]_0-[\mathrm R]}{[\mathrm R]_0}=1-e^{-k_1t}$.

  6. JEE Main 2026, 4 April, Shift 2 · Question 63
    Consider the given graph showing variation of reactant concentration with time.
    Three different reactions were started with identical initial concentration of reactants. Which of the following statement is correct?

    1. The order of all the three reactions is same.
    2. The rate constant of reaction 3 is larger than the rate constant of reaction 2 if the order of reaction is same for both.
    3. The SI unit of rate constant of reaction 1 is $\mathrm{s^{-1}}$.
    4. Thermal decomposition of HI on gold surface is an example of reaction 2.
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    Reaction 1 is a straight line ($[R]$ falls linearly): zero order. Reactions 2 and 3 are curved (e.g. first order), so the orders are not all the same.
    Zero-order rate constant has unit $\mathrm{mol\,L^{-1}s^{-1}}$, not $\mathrm{s^{-1}}$; decomposition of HI on gold is zero order, i.e. like reaction 1, not 2.
    For the same order and same $[R]_0$, curve 3 falls faster than curve 2, so $k_3>k_2$.
    Correct statement: option (2).

  7. JEE Main 2026, 4 April, Shift 2 · Question 74
    For a first order reaction $\mathrm{A\to B}$
    $t$/min: $0$, $x$, $20$
    $[\mathrm{A}]$/M: $0.6500$, $0.0650$, $0.00065$
    $x=$ ____________ min. (Nearest integer)

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 7

    First order: $t=\dfrac{2.303}{k}\log\dfrac{[\mathrm{A}]_0}{[\mathrm{A}]}$.
    At $t=x$: $\log\dfrac{0.65}{0.065}=1$; at $t=20$ min: $\log\dfrac{0.65}{0.00065}=3$.
    So $\dfrac{x}{20}=\dfrac13\Rightarrow x=6.67\approx7$ min.

  8. JEE Main 2026, 5 April, Shift 1 · Question 57
    First order gas phase reaction
    $\mathrm{A \rightarrow B + C}$
    $p_i$ = initial pressure of gas A, $p_t$ = total pressure of the reaction mixture at time $t$
    Expression of rate constant ($k$) is

    1. $\dfrac{1}{t}\ln\dfrac{p_i}{2p_i-p_t}$
    2. $\dfrac{1}{t}\ln\dfrac{2p_i}{p_i-p_t}$
    3. $\dfrac{1}{t}\ln\dfrac{p_i}{3p_i-2p_t}$
    4. $\dfrac{1}{t}\ln\dfrac{3p_i}{4p_i-p_t}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    If A falls by $x$: $p_A=p_i-x$, and $p_t=(p_i-x)+x+x=p_i+x$.
    So $x=p_t-p_i$ and $p_A=2p_i-p_t$.
    First order: $k=\dfrac1t\ln\dfrac{p_i}{p_A}=\dfrac1t\ln\dfrac{p_i}{2p_i-p_t}$.

  9. JEE Main 2026, 5 April, Shift 1 · Question 75
    If the half life of a first order reaction is $6.93$ minutes then the time required for completion of $99\%$ of the reaction will be ________ minutes .
    (Given : $\log 2 = 0.3010$)

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 46

    $k=\dfrac{0.693}{6.93}=0.1$ min$^{-1}$.
    $t_{99\%}=\dfrac{2.303}{k}\log\dfrac{100}{1}=\dfrac{2.303\times2}{0.1}=46.06$ min $\approx46$ min.

  10. JEE Main 2026, 5 April, Shift 2 · Question 75
    For a reaction $\mathrm{A\rightarrow P}$ at T K, the half life $(t_{1/2})$ is plotted as a function of initial concentration $[\mathrm{A}]_o$ of A as given below.
    The value of $x$ in the given figure is __________ s (Nearest integer)

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 90

    The graph is a straight line through the origin: $t_{1/2}\propto[\mathrm{A}]_o$, a zero-order reaction ($t_{1/2}=\dfrac{[\mathrm{A}]_o}{2k}$).
    $\dfrac{x}{240}=\dfrac{1.5\times10^{-3}}{4\times10^{-3}} \Rightarrow x=90$ s.

  11. JEE Main 2026, 6 April, Shift 1 · Question 74
    Sucrose hydrolyses in acidic medium into glucose and fructose by first order rate law with $t_{1/2}=3$ hour. The percentage of sucrose remaining after 6 hours is _____. (Nearest integer)
    (Given : log 2 = 0.3010 and log 3 = 0.4771)

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 25

    6 hours $=2$ half-lives.
    Fraction remaining $=\left(\dfrac12\right)^2=\dfrac14$, i.e. $25\%$.

  12. JEE Main 2026, 6 April, Shift 2 · Question 75
    Decomposition of a hydrocarbon follows the equation $k=\left(5.5\times10^{11}\,\mathrm{s^{-1}}\right)e^{-28000\,\mathrm K/T}$. The activation energy of reaction is __________ kJ mol$^{-1}$. (Nearest Integer)
    Given : $R=8.3$ J K$^{-1}$ mol$^{-1}$

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 232

    Comparing with $k=Ae^{-E_a/RT}$: $\dfrac{E_a}{R}=28000$ K.
    $E_a=28000\times8.3=232400$ J mol$^{-1}\approx232$ kJ mol$^{-1}$.

  13. JEE Main 2026, 8 April, Shift 2 · Question 57
    Given below are two statements :
    $R=8.314\ \mathrm{J\,K^{-1}\,mol^{-1}}$ and 1 cal $=4.2$ J
    Statement I : When $E_a=12.6$ kcal/mol, the room temperature rate constant is doubled by a $10\ {}^\circ$C increase in temperature (298 K to 308 K)
    Statement II : For a first order reactions $\mathrm{A\to B}$, the graph of $t_{1/2}$ (s) against $[\mathrm{A}]_0$ (mol L$^{-1}$) is as shown below.
    Here $[\mathrm{A}]_0$ is the initial concentration of A and $t_{1/2}$ is half life of reaction.
    In the light of the above statements, choose the correct answer from the options given below :

    1. Both Statement I and Statement II are true
    2. Both Statement I and Statement II are false
    3. Statement I is true but Statement II is false
    4. Statement I is false but Statement II is true
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    $E_a=12.6\times4.2=52.92$ kJ/mol. $\ln\dfrac{k_2}{k_1}=\dfrac{E_a}{R}\left(\dfrac1{298}-\dfrac1{308}\right)=\dfrac{52920}{8.314}\times\dfrac{10}{298\times308}\approx0.693$.
    So $\dfrac{k_2}{k_1}\approx2$: Statement I is true.
    For a first order reaction $t_{1/2}=\dfrac{0.693}{k}$ is independent of $[\mathrm A]_0$, so the graph should be a horizontal line, not a straight line through the origin: Statement II is false.
