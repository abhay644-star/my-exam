  1. JEE Main 2026, 2 April, Shift 1 · Question 58
    $\mathrm{t_{100\%}}$ is the time required for the 100% completion of the reaction while $\mathrm{t_{1/2}}$ is the time required for 50% of the reaction to be completed. Which of the following option correctly represents the relation between $\mathrm{t_{100\%}}$ and $\mathrm{t_{1/2}}$ for zero and first order reactions respectively ?

    1. $\mathrm{t_{100\%}} = (\mathrm{t_{1/2}})^2$ and $\mathrm{t_{100\%}} = (\mathrm{t_{1/2}})^{-\infty}$
    2. $\mathrm{t_{100\%}} = 2\mathrm{t_{1/2}}$ and $\mathrm{t_{100\%}} = (\mathrm{t_{1/2}})^{\infty}$
    3. $\mathrm{t_{100\%}} = 2\mathrm{t_{1/2}}$ and $\mathrm{t_{100\%}} = (2\mathrm{t_{1/2}})^2$
    4. $\mathrm{t_{100\%}} = (\mathrm{t_{1/2}})^{\infty}$ and $\mathrm{t_{100\%}} = 2\mathrm{t_{1/2}}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    Zero order: $[A] = [A]_0 - kt$, so $t_{100\%} = [A]_0/k$ and $t_{1/2} = [A]_0/2k$, giving $t_{100\%} = 2t_{1/2}$.
    First order: $t = \dfrac{1}{k}\ln\dfrac{[A]_0}{[A]}$ diverges as $[A] \to 0$, so 100% completion takes infinite time, written $(t_{1/2})^{\infty}$.

  2. JEE Main 2026, 2 April, Shift 1 · Question 74
    For reaction $\mathrm{A \rightarrow P}$, rate constant $\mathrm{k = 1.5 \times 10^3\ s^{-1}}$ at 27°C
    If activation energy for the above reaction is 60 kJ mol$^{-1}$, then the temperature (in °C) at which rate constant, $\mathrm{k = 4.5 \times 10^3\ s^{-1}}$ is ________. (Nearest integer)
    Given : log 2 $=$ 0.30, log 3 $=$ 0.48, R $=$ 8.3 J K$^{-1}$ mol$^{-1}$, ln 10 $=$ 2.3

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 41

    $\ln\dfrac{k_2}{k_1} = \dfrac{E_a}{R}\left(\dfrac{1}{T_1} - \dfrac{1}{T_2}\right)$, with $k_2/k_1 = 3$, $\ln 3 = 2.3 \times 0.48 = 1.104$.
    $\dfrac{E_a}{R} = \dfrac{60000}{8.3} = 7229$ K.
    $\dfrac{1}{T_2} = \dfrac{1}{300} - \dfrac{1.104}{7229} = 0.0031806 \Rightarrow T_2 \approx 314.4$ K.
    $t \approx 41$°C.

  3. JEE Main 2026, 2 April, Shift 2 · Question 57
    Consider the reaction aX $\to$ bY, for which the rate constant at 30$^\circ$C is $1\times10^{-3}$ mol$^{-1}$ L s$^{-1}$. Which of the following statements are true ?
    A. When concentration of 'X' is increased to four times, the rate of reaction becomes 16 times.
    B. The reaction is a second order reaction.
    C. The half-life period is independent of the concentration of X.
    D. Decomposition of N$_2$O$_5$ is an example of the above reaction.
    E. The plot of $\ln\dfrac{[R_0]}{[R]}$ against time shown in the figure is valid for the above reaction.
    Choose the correct answer from the options given below :

    1. A and B Only
    2. A, B and C Only
    3. A, B, D and E Only
    4. C and D Only
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    Units mol$^{-1}$ L s$^{-1}$ mean a second order reaction: B is true.
    Rate $\propto[\mathrm X]^2$: four times the concentration gives 16 times the rate, so A is true.
    For second order $t_{1/2}=\dfrac{1}{k[\mathrm X]_0}$ depends on concentration: C false.
    N$_2$O$_5$ decomposition is first order, and a straight line of $\ln\dfrac{[R_0]}{[R]}$ vs $t$ is the first-order plot: D and E false.

    First order gas phase reaction
    $\mathrm{A \rightarrow B + C}$
    $p_i$ = initial pressure of gas A, $p_t$ = total pressure of the reaction mixture at time $t$
    Expression of rate constant ($k$) is

    1. $\dfrac{1}{t}\ln\dfrac{p_i}{2p_i-p_t}$
    2. $\dfrac{1}{t}\ln\dfrac{2p_i}{p_i-p_t}$
    3. $\dfrac{1}{t}\ln\dfrac{p_i}{3p_i-2p_t}$
    4. $\dfrac{1}{t}\ln\dfrac{3p_i}{4p_i-p_t}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    If A falls by $x$: $p_A=p_i-x$, and $p_t=(p_i-x)+x+x=p_i+x$.
