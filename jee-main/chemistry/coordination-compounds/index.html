    Match List - I with List - II.

    List - I
    Chromium (III) Complexes

    5.33 g of $\mathrm{CrCl_3\cdot 6H_2O}$, which is a 1 : 3 electrolyte, is dissolved in water and is passed through a cation exchanger. The chloride ions in the eluted solution, on treatment with $\mathrm{AgNO_3}$ results in 8.61 g of AgCl. The ratio of moles of complex reacted and moles of AgCl formed is ________ $\times 10^{-2}$. (Nearest integer)
    [Molar mass in g mol$^{-1}$ Cr : 52, Ag : 108, Cl : 35.5, H : 1, O : 16]

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 33

    Molar mass of $\mathrm{CrCl_3\cdot 6H_2O} = 52 + 106.5 + 108 = 266.5$; moles $= 5.33/266.5 = 0.02$.
    Moles AgCl $= 8.61/143.5 = 0.06$ (all three Cl ionisable: $\mathrm{[Cr(H_2O)_6]Cl_3}$, 1 : 3 electrolyte).
    Ratio $= 0.02/0.06 = 0.333 = 33 \times 10^{-2}$.

  3. JEE Main 2026, 2 April, Shift 2 · Question 61
    Which of the following complexes will show coordination isomerism ?
    A. $[\mathrm{Ag(NH_3)_2}][\mathrm{Ag(CN)_2}]$
    B. $[\mathrm{Co(NH_3)_6}][\mathrm{Cr(CN)_6}]$
    C. $[\mathrm{Co(NH_3)_6}][\mathrm{Co(CN)_6}]$
    D. $[\mathrm{Fe(NH_3)_6}][\mathrm{Co(CN)_6}]$
    E. $[\mathrm{Co(NH_3)_6}][\mathrm{Fe(CN)_6}]$
    Choose the correct answer from the options given below :

    1. B, C and D Only
    2. B, D and E Only
    3. A, C and D Only
    4. C, D and E Only
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    Coordination isomerism arises from interchange of ligands between the cationic and anionic complex entities of different metal ions, e.g. $[\mathrm{Co(NH_3)_6}][\mathrm{Cr(CN)_6}]$ and $[\mathrm{Cr(NH_3)_6}][\mathrm{Co(CN)_6}]$.
    B, D and E have different metals in the cation and the anion, so the interchange gives a different compound.
    In A and C both centres are the same metal, so this interchange gives back the same compound.
    Answer: B, D and E only.

  5. JEE Main 2026, 4 April, Shift 2 · Question 60
    Consider the metal complexes $\mathrm{[Ni(en)_3]^{2+}}$ (A), $\mathrm{[NiCl_4]^{2-}}$ (B) and $\mathrm{[Ni(NH_3)_6]^{2+}}$ (C). Choose the CORRECT option by considering the number of unpaired electrons present in (A), (B) and (C) respectively and the order of frequency of absorption.

    1. 2, 2, 2 and (A) > (C) > (B)
    2. 0, 2, 0 and (A) > (C) > (B)
    3. 2, 2, 0 and (B) > (C) > (A)
    4. 2, 2, 2 and (C) > (A) > (B)
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    $\mathrm{Ni^{2+}}$ is $d^8$. Octahedral $d^8$ ($t_{2g}^6e_g^2$) always has 2 unpaired electrons, so (A) and (C) have 2; tetrahedral $\mathrm{[NiCl_4]^{2-}}$ also has 2.
    Crystal field splitting: en > $\mathrm{NH_3}$ (spectrochemical series), and tetrahedral $\mathrm{Cl^-}$ complex has the smallest splitting ($\Delta_t\approx\tfrac49\Delta_o$, weak ligand).
    Frequency of absorption $\propto\Delta$: (A) > (C) > (B).
    2, 2, 2 and (A) > (C) > (B).

    The correct statements about metal carbonyls are
    A. The metal-carbon bonds in metal carbonyls possess both $\sigma$ and $\pi$-character.

    Answer: 2

    3. Statement I is true but Statement II is false
    4. Statement I is false but Statement II is true
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a) or (d) (either is accepted)

    The wavelength of light absorbed for the following complexes are in the order

    2. III < I < II < IV < V
    3. III < I < IV < II < V
    4. III < I < IV < V < II
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

  30. JEE Main 2026, 24 January, Shift 2 · Question 71
    A chromium complex with a formula $\mathrm{CrCl_3.6H_2O}$ has a spin only magnetic moment value of 3.87 BM and its solution conductivity corresponds to 1 : 2 electrolyte. 2.75 g of the complex solution was initially passed through a cation exchanger. The solution obtained after the process was reacted with excess of $\mathrm{AgNO_3}$. The amount of AgCl formed in the above process is ____ g. (Nearest integer)
    [Given: Molar mass in g mol$^{-1}$ Cr : 52; Cl: 35.5, Ag:108, O:16, H:1]

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 3

    A 1 : 2 electrolyte is $\mathrm{[Cr(H_2O)_5Cl]Cl_2\cdot H_2O}$ (3.87 BM: three unpaired electrons, Cr(III)).
    Molar mass $=52+106.5+108=266.5$ g mol$^{-1}$, so $\dfrac{2.75}{266.5}=1.03\times10^{-2}$ mol.
    The cation exchanger keeps the complex cation (with its coordinated Cl) and releases $\mathrm{H^+}$; the two ionisable $\mathrm{Cl^-}$ per formula stay in solution.
    AgCl $=2\times1.03\times10^{-2}\times143.5\approx2.96\approx3$ g.
