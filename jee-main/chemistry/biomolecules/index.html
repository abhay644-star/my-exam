  3. JEE Main 2026, 4 April, Shift 1 · Question 68
    Given below are two statements:
    Statement I: The structure of Maltose is given below (structure (I) in the figure). Maltose is a non-reducing sugar.
    Statement II: The structure of Lactose is given below (structure (II) in the figure). Lactose is a reducing sugar.
    In the light of the above statements, choose the correct answer from the options given below

    1. Both Statement I and Statement II are true
    2. Both Statement I and Statement II are false
    3. Statement I is true but Statement II is false
    4. Statement I is false but Statement II is true
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    Maltose has a free hemiacetal (anomeric $-\mathrm{OH}$) on its second glucose unit, which can open to an aldehyde: maltose is a reducing sugar, so Statement I is false.
    Lactose ($\beta$-D-galactose C-1 linked to C-4 of D-glucose) keeps a free anomeric $-\mathrm{OH}$ on the glucose unit: it is a reducing sugar. Statement II is true.

    A D-aldotetrose on oxidation with concentrated $\mathrm{HNO_3}$ resulted in optically inactive dicarboxylic acid. The structure of the D-aldotetrose is:

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    $\mathrm{HNO_3}$ oxidises both CHO and $\mathrm{CH_2OH}$ to COOH, giving tartaric acid.

  6. JEE Main 2026, 5 April, Shift 1 · Question 69
    Identify the correct statements.
    A. Glucose exists in two anomeric forms.
    B. Anomers of glucose differ in configuration at C$-1$ in cyclic hemiacetal structure.
    C. Melting point of $\alpha$ - anomer of glucose is greater than $\beta$ - anomer.
    D. Specific rotation of $\alpha$ - anomer is $+19^\circ$ while for $\beta$ - anomer is $+112^\circ$
    E. $\alpha$ and $\beta$ - anomers of glucose are prepared by crystallization of saturated glucose solution at $303$ K and $371$ K respectively.

    1. A and B Only
    2. B and C Only
    3. A, B and D Only
    4. A, B and E Only
    Show answer and solution

    A and B: true — $\alpha$- and $\beta$-D-glucopyranose differ only at C-1 (the anomeric carbon).

  7. JEE Main 2026, 5 April, Shift 2 · Question 68
    Identify the incorrect statement about tertiary structure of proteins.

    1. They can be fibrous or globular in structure.
    2. The main forces that stabilize the structure are hydrogen bonding, disulphide links, van der Waals and electrostatic forces of attraction.
    3. The structure remains intact when exposed to pH changes.
    4. A linear polypeptide chain will convert to a secondary structure and then further folding of the secondary structure will convert to tertiary structure.
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    The tertiary structure (fibrous or globular) arises from further folding of the secondary structure and is held by H-bonds, disulphide links, van der Waals and electrostatic forces.
    A change in pH disrupts these interactions and denatures the protein, so the structure does not remain intact.
    The incorrect statement is the third one.

    1. A and B
    2. A and D
    3. B and D

  28. JEE Main 2025, 4 April, Shift 1 · Question 74
    The total number of hydrogen bonds of a DNA-double Helix strand whose one strand has the following sequence of bases is __________
    5'- G - G-C-A-A-A-T-C-G-G-C-T-A-3'

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 33

    Identify the number of structure/s from the following which can be correlated to D-glyceraldehyde.

    1. one
    2. two
    3. three
    4. four
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    A sugar belongs to the D-series when, with CHO at the top of the Fischer projection, the OH on the chiral carbon farthest from CHO points to the right.
    (A) and (B): that OH is on the right, so D. (C): it is on the left, so L.
    (D) is drawn with CHO at the bottom; turning it through $180^\circ$ puts CHO at the top and the lowest chiral OH on the right, so D.
    Three structures: (A), (B) and (D).

    The $\alpha$ - Helix and $\beta$ - Pleated sheet structures of protein are associated with its :

    1. primary structure
    2. secondary structure
    3. tertiary structure
    4. quaternary structure
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)
