  1. JEE Main 2026, 2 April, Shift 1 · Question 69
    Match List - I with List - II.

    List - I
    Vitamin    		List - II
    Name
    A. Vitamin $\mathrm{B_1}$I. Pyridoxine
    B. Vitamin $\mathrm{B_2}$II. Ascorbic acid
    C. Vitamin $\mathrm{B_6}$III. Thiamine
    D. Vitamin CIV. Riboflavin
    Choose the correct answer from the options given below :

    1. A-II, B-I, C-III, D-IV
    2. A-IV, B-III, C-II, D-I
    3. A-III, B-IV, C-I, D-II

  2. JEE Main 2026, 2 April, Shift 2 · Question 69
    Identify the correct pair having amino acid (A) and the hormone (B) that is iodinated derivative of the amino acid (A).
    (T and Y represent one letter code for amino acids)

    1. Amino acid (A): T; Hormone (B): Insulin
    2. Amino acid (A): T; Hormone (B): Thyroxine
    3. Amino acid (A): Y; Hormone (B): Thyroxine
    4. Amino acid (A): Y; Hormone (B): Insulin
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    Thyroxine (a thyroid hormone) is an iodinated derivative of the amino acid tyrosine.
    The one-letter code of tyrosine is Y (T is threonine); insulin is a peptide hormone, not an iodinated amino acid.
    Answer: Y and thyroxine.

  3. JEE Main 2026, 4 April, Shift 1 · Question 68
    Given below are two statements:
    Statement I: The structure of Maltose is given below (structure (I) in the figure). Maltose is a non-reducing sugar.
    Statement II: The structure of Lactose is given below (structure (II) in the figure). Lactose is a reducing sugar.
    In the light of the above statements, choose the correct answer from the options given below

    1. Both Statement I and Statement II are true
    2. Both Statement I and Statement II are false
    3. Statement I is true but Statement II is false
    4. Statement I is false but Statement II is true
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    Maltose has a free hemiacetal (anomeric $-\mathrm{OH}$) on its second glucose unit, which can open to an aldehyde: maltose is a reducing sugar, so Statement I is false.
    Lactose ($\beta$-D-galactose C-1 linked to C-4 of D-glucose) keeps a free anomeric $-\mathrm{OH}$ on the glucose unit: it is a reducing sugar. Statement II is true.

  5. JEE Main 2026, 4 April, Shift 2 · Question 68
    A D-aldotetrose on oxidation with concentrated $\mathrm{HNO_3}$ resulted in optically inactive dicarboxylic acid. The structure of the D-aldotetrose is:

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    $\mathrm{HNO_3}$ oxidises both CHO and $\mathrm{CH_2OH}$ to COOH, giving tartaric acid.
    D-sugar: the OH on the lowest chiral carbon (C-3) is on the right; this rules out options (1) and (2).
    For the tartaric acid to be optically inactive (meso), both OH groups must be on the same side: D-erythrose, with both OH on the right.
    Option (3).

  6. JEE Main 2026, 5 April, Shift 1 · Question 69
    Identify the correct statements.
    A. Glucose exists in two anomeric forms.
    B. Anomers of glucose differ in configuration at C$-1$ in cyclic hemiacetal structure.
    C. Melting point of $\alpha$ - anomer of glucose is greater than $\beta$ - anomer.
    D. Specific rotation of $\alpha$ - anomer is $+19^\circ$ while for $\beta$ - anomer is $+112^\circ$
    E. $\alpha$ and $\beta$ - anomers of glucose are prepared by crystallization of saturated glucose solution at $303$ K and $371$ K respectively.
    Choose the correct answer from the options given below:

    1. A and B Only
    2. B and C Only
    3. A, B and D Only
    4. A, B and E Only
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    A and B: true — $\alpha$- and $\beta$-D-glucopyranose differ only at C-1 (the anomeric carbon).
    C: false — $\alpha$-glucose melts at $419$ K (146 °C), $\beta$-glucose at $423$ K (150 °C).
    D: false — the values are reversed: $\alpha$ is $+112^\circ$, $\beta$ is $+19^\circ$.
    E: true — $\alpha$ crystallises from a concentrated solution at $303$ K, $\beta$ from a hot saturated solution at $371$ K.
    Correct: A, B and E.

  7. JEE Main 2026, 5 April, Shift 2 · Question 68
    Identify the incorrect statement about tertiary structure of proteins.

    1. They can be fibrous or globular in structure.
    2. The main forces that stabilize the structure are hydrogen bonding, disulphide links, van der Waals and electrostatic forces of attraction.
    3. The structure remains intact when exposed to pH changes.
    4. A linear polypeptide chain will convert to a secondary structure and then further folding of the secondary structure will convert to tertiary structure.
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    The tertiary structure (fibrous or globular) arises from further folding of the secondary structure and is held by H-bonds, disulphide links, van der Waals and electrostatic forces.
    A change in pH disrupts these interactions and denatures the protein, so the structure does not remain intact.
    The incorrect statement is the third one.

  8. JEE Main 2026, 5 April, Shift 2 · Question 69
    Given below are two statements :
    Statement I : The two structures shown in the figure are two anomers of D-(+)-glucose.
    Statement II : The open chain forms of D-glucose and D- fructose contain three similar chiral carbons at $C_3$, $C_4$ and $C_5$.
    In the light of the above statements, choose the correct answer from the options given below :

    1. Both Statement I and Statement II are true
    2. Both Statement I and Statement II are false
    3. Statement I is true but Statement II is false
    4. Statement I is false but Statement II is true
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    The two Haworth structures are identical except at C-1 (OH down in one, up in the other): they are α- and β-D-glucopyranose, the two anomers of D-(+)-glucose. Statement I is true.
    Open-chain D-glucose has chiral C-2 to C-5; D-fructose (C-2 is the C=O) has chiral C-3, C-4, C-5 with the same configurations as in glucose. Statement II is true.

  10. JEE Main 2026, 6 April, Shift 1 · Question 70
    Match the LIST-I with LIST-II

    List-I
    Amino acid    		List-II
    Positive reaction/Test for functional group present in side chain of amino acid
    A. GlutamineI. Hinsberg's test
    B. LysineII. Neutral $\mathrm{FeCl_3}$ test
    C. TyrosineIII. Ceric ammonium nitrate test
    D. SerineIV. Hoffman bromamide degradation
    Choose the correct answer from the options given below:

    1. A-IV, B-II, C-I, D-III
    2. A-IV, B-I, C-II, D-III
    3. A-III, B-II, C-I, D-IV
    4. A-IV, B-I, C-III, D-II
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    A-IV, B-I, C-II, D-III.

  11. JEE Main 2026, 8 April, Shift 2 · Question 69
    The incorrect statement from the following with respect to carbohydrates is :

    1. All monosaccharides are reducing sugars.
    2. The monosaccharide units obtained from hydrolysis of oligosaccharides are always the same.
    3. Starch and cellulose are typical examples of polysaccharides, which are very high molecular weight compounds of more than ten monosaccharide units.
    4. Open chain and cyclic structures co-exist at equilibrium that are responsible for certain properties as in the case of D$-(+)-$glucose.
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    Oligosaccharides may give the same or different monosaccharides on hydrolysis: maltose gives two glucose units but sucrose gives glucose and fructose.
    So statement (2) is incorrect; the others are correct.

  12. JEE Main 2026, 8 April, Shift 2 · Question 70
    Which of the following amino acid will give violet coloured complex with neutral ferric chloride solution ?

    1. Threonine
    2. Serine
    3. Tyrosine
    4. Cysteine
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    Neutral $\mathrm{FeCl_3}$ gives a violet colour with phenols.
    Tyrosine has a phenolic –OH (p-hydroxyphenyl side chain); threonine and serine have alcoholic –OH and cysteine has –SH.
    Answer: tyrosine.
