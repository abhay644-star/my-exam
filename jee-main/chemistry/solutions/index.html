  1. JEE Main 2026, 2 April, Shift 1 · Question 55
    19.5 g of fluoro acetic acid (molar mass $=$ 78 g mol$^{-1}$) is dissolved in 500 g of water at 298 K. The depression in the freezing point of water was 1°C. What is $\mathrm{K_a}$ of fluoro acetic acid ? (For water, $\mathrm{K_f = 1.86\ K\ kg\ mol^{-1}}$). Assume molarity and molality to have same values.

    1. $10^{-6}$
    2. $4 \times 10^{-4}$
    3. $3 \times 10^{-5}$
    4. $3 \times 10^{-3}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    $m = \dfrac{19.5/78}{0.5} = 0.5$ mol kg$^{-1}$.
    $\Delta T_f = i K_f m \Rightarrow i = \dfrac{1}{1.86 \times 0.5} = 1.075$, so $\alpha = i - 1 = 0.075$.
    $K_a = \dfrac{C\alpha^2}{1-\alpha} = \dfrac{0.5 \times (0.075)^2}{0.925} \approx 3 \times 10^{-3}$.

  2. JEE Main 2026, 2 April, Shift 2 · Question 55
    Solution A is prepared by dissolving 1 g of a protein (molar mass $=50000$ g mol$^{-1}$) in 0.5 L of water at 300 K. Its osmotic pressure is $x$ bar. Solution B is made by dissolving 2 g of same protein in 1 L of water at 300 K. Osmotic pressure of solution B is $y$ bar. Entire solution of A is mixed with entire solution of B at same temperature. The osmotic pressure of resultant solution is $z$ bar. $x$, $y$ and $z$ respectively are :
    ($R=0.083$ L bar mol$^{-1}$ K$^{-1}$)

    1. $9.96\times10^{-4}$; $9.96\times10^{-4}$; $9.96\times10^{-4}$
    2. $9.96\times10^{-4}$; $9.96\times10^{-4}$; $19.92\times10^{-4}$
    3. $4.98\times10^{-4}$; $4.98\times10^{-4}$; $9.96\times10^{-4}$
    4. $4.98\times10^{-4}$; $4.98\times10^{-4}$; $4.98\times10^{-4}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    A: $C=\dfrac{1/50000}{0.5}=4\times10^{-5}$ M; B: $C=\dfrac{2/50000}{1}=4\times10^{-5}$ M; mixture: $\dfrac{3/50000}{1.5}=4\times10^{-5}$ M.
    All three have the same concentration: $\pi=CRT=4\times10^{-5}\times0.083\times300=9.96\times10^{-4}$ bar.
    $x=y=z=9.96\times10^{-4}$.

  3. JEE Main 2026, 4 April, Shift 1 · Question 75
    A non-volatile, non-electrolyte solid solute when dissolved in $40$ g of a solvent, the vapour pressure of the solvent decreased from $760$ mm Hg to $750$ mm Hg. If the same solution boils at $320$ K, then the number of moles of the solvent present in the solution is _______. (Nearest integer)
    [Given: boiling point of the pure solvent $=319.5$ K, $K_b$ of the solvent $=0.3\ \mathrm{K\,kg\,mol^{-1}}$]

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 5

    $\Delta T_b=0.5=K_bm \Rightarrow m=\dfrac53\ \mathrm{mol\,kg^{-1}}$, so moles of solute $n_2=\dfrac53\times0.040=\dfrac1{15}$.
    Relative lowering: $\dfrac{760-750}{760}=\dfrac{1}{76}=\dfrac{n_2}{n_1+n_2}$, so $n_1+n_2=76n_2$, i.e. $n_1=75n_2$.
    $n_1=\dfrac{75}{15}=5$.

  4. JEE Main 2026, 4 April, Shift 2 · Question 54
    At 27 °C, 0.1 M, 1 L $\mathrm{K_4[Fe(CN)_6]}$ aqueous solution and 0.1 M, 1 L $\mathrm{FeCl_3}$ aqueous solution are placed in a container separated by a semi permeable membrane AB. Assume complete dissociation of both the solutes. Which of the following statement is correct?

    1. Blue color is formed on both sides.
    2. Ionic solutes in aqueous solution can pass through semi-permeable membrane.
    3. Solution on side 'y' is hypotonic.
    4. To cause the reverse flow of solvent during osmosis, external pressure (any value) should be applied to side 'x'.
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    $\mathrm{K_4[Fe(CN)_6]}\to4\mathrm{K^+}+\mathrm{[Fe(CN)_6]^{4-}}$: $i=5$, particle concentration $0.5$ M (side x).
    $\mathrm{FeCl_3}\to\mathrm{Fe^{3+}}+3\mathrm{Cl^-}$: $i=4$, $0.4$ M (side y).
    Solutes cannot cross the semipermeable membrane, so no Prussian blue forms and (1), (2) are wrong.
    Side y has the lower osmotic pressure: it is hypotonic. Reverse osmosis needs a pressure on x greater than the osmotic pressure difference, not any value.
    Correct statement: side 'y' is hypotonic.

  5. JEE Main 2026, 5 April, Shift 1 · Question 54
    What is the mole fraction of water in $10\%$ by weight (w/w) of aqueous urea solution?
    [Given: Molar mass of H, O, C and N are $1$, $16$, $12$ and $14$ g mol$^{-1}$ respectively.]

    1. $0.825$
    2. $0.032$
    3. $0.867$
    4. $0.967$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    In $100$ g of solution: $10$ g urea ($M=60$) $=\dfrac16$ mol, $90$ g water $=5$ mol.
    $x_{\mathrm{H_2O}}=\dfrac{5}{5+0.1667}\approx0.967$.

  6. JEE Main 2026, 5 April, Shift 2 · Question 73
    20 g hemoglobin in a 1 L aqueous solution (A) at 300 K is separated from pure water by semi permeable membrane. At equilibrium the height of solution in a tube dipped in a solution (A) is found to be 80.0 mm higher than the tube dipped in water.
    The molar mass of hemoglobin is __________ $\mathrm{kg\ mol^{-1}}$. (Nearest integer)
    (Given : $g=10\ \mathrm{m\ s^{-2}}$, $R=8.3\ \mathrm{kPa\ dm^3\ K^{-1}mol^{-1}}$, density of solution $=1000\ \mathrm{kg\ m^{-3}}$)

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 62

    Osmotic pressure $\pi=h\rho g=0.080\times1000\times10=800$ Pa $=0.8$ kPa.
    $\pi=\dfrac{w}{MV}RT \Rightarrow M=\dfrac{wRT}{\pi V}=\dfrac{20\times8.3\times300}{0.8\times1}=62250\ \mathrm{g\ mol^{-1}}$.
    $M\approx62\ \mathrm{kg\ mol^{-1}}$.

  7. JEE Main 2026, 6 April, Shift 1 · Question 56
    When 0.25 moles of a non-volatile, non-ionizable solute was dissolved in 1 mole of a solvent the vapor pressure of solution was $x$ % of vapor pressure of pure solvent. What is $x$ %?

    1. $50\%$
    2. $60\%$
    3. $70\%$
    4. $80\%$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    Raoult's law: $\dfrac{p}{p^0}=x_{\text{solvent}}=\dfrac{1}{1+0.25}=0.8$.
    $x=80\%$.

  8. JEE Main 2026, 6 April, Shift 2 · Question 55
    Given below are two statements :
    (Chamber 1: 18 g glucose in 100 mL aqueous solution; Chamber 2: 30 g glucose in 250 mL aqueous solution; the chambers are separated by a semi-permeable membrane.)
    Statement I : $\mathrm{H_2O}$ molecules move from the chamber 1 to chamber 2.
    Statement II : The osmotic pressure of a solution prepared by dissolving 50 mg of potassium sulphate (molar mass $=174$ g/mol) in 2 L of water (at 27 °C) is 0.0107 bar. (Given: $R=0.083$ dm$^3$ bar K$^{-1}$ mol$^{-1}$ and assume complete dissociation of electrolyte)
    In the light of the above statements, choose the correct answer from the options given below :

    1. Both Statement I and Statement II are true
    2. Both Statement I and Statement II are false
    3. Statement I is true but Statement II is false
    4. Statement I is false but Statement II is true
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    Chamber 1: $\dfrac{18}{180}=0.1$ mol in 0.1 L → 1 M. Chamber 2: $\dfrac{30}{180}=\dfrac16$ mol in 0.25 L → 0.667 M.
    Water moves by osmosis from the dilute side (chamber 2) to the concentrated side (chamber 1), so Statement I is false.
    $\mathrm{K_2SO_4}$: $i=3$, $C=\dfrac{0.05/174}{2}=1.437\times10^{-4}$ M.
    $\pi=iCRT=3\times1.437\times10^{-4}\times0.083\times300\approx0.0107$ bar, so Statement II is true.

  9. JEE Main 2026, 8 April, Shift 2 · Question 52
    Given below are two statements :
    Given : Molar mass of C, H, O, Cl are 12, 1, 16 and 35.5 g mol$^{-1}$, respectively
    Statement I : In 30% (w/w) solution of methanol in $\mathrm{CCl_4}$ (at T K), the mole fraction of $\mathrm{CCl_4}$ is equal to 0.33.
    Statement II : Mixture of methanol and $\mathrm{CCl_4}$ shows positive deviation from Raoult's law.
    In the light of the above statements, choose the correct answer from the options given below :

    1. Both Statement I and Statement II are true
    2. Both Statement I and Statement II are false
    3. Statement I is true but Statement II is false
    4. Statement I is false but Statement II is true
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    In 100 g: 30 g $\mathrm{CH_3OH}$ $=\dfrac{30}{32}=0.9375$ mol; 70 g $\mathrm{CCl_4}$ $=\dfrac{70}{154}=0.4545$ mol.
    $x_{\mathrm{CCl_4}}=\dfrac{0.4545}{1.392}\approx0.33$: Statement I is true.
    $\mathrm{CCl_4}$ breaks the hydrogen bonding of methanol, so the mixture shows positive deviation: Statement II is true.

  10. JEE Main 2026, 8 April, Shift 2 · Question 54
    Which of the following statements are not correct ?
    A. For water, magnitude of $K_b$ is more than the magnitude of $K_f$.
    B. The elevation in boiling point of water when a non-volatile solute is added to it is larger in magnitude than its depression in freezing point.
    C. Osmotic pressure measurement is preferred over any other colligative property to determine molar mass of proteins and polymers.
    D. The dimerised form of benzoic acid in benzene is $\mathrm{C_6H_5{-}\overset{\displaystyle O}{\overset{\|}{C}}{-}OH\ \cdots\ O{=}\overset{\displaystyle OH}{\overset{|}{C}}{-}C_6H_5}$
    Choose the correct answer from the options given below :

    1. A and B only
    2. A and D only
    3. A, B and D only
    4. A, C and D only
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    A: for water $K_b=0.52$ and $K_f=1.86$ K kg mol$^{-1}$, so A is not correct.
    B: since $K_b<K_f$, $\Delta T_b<\Delta T_f$ for the same molality, so B is not correct.
    C is correct (osmotic pressure is large and measured at room temperature).
    D: the benzoic acid dimer is cyclic, held by two O–H···O hydrogen bonds, not the single hydrogen bond shown, so D is not correct.
    Not correct: A, B and D.
