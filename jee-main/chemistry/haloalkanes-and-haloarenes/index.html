  1. JEE Main 2026, 2 April, Shift 1 · Question 64
    Given below are two statements :
    Statement (I) : Benzyl chloride reacts faster in $\mathrm{S_N1}$ mechanism than ethyl chloride.
    Statement (II) : Ethyl carbocation intermediate is less stabilized by hyperconjugation than benzyl carbocation by resonance.
    In the light of the above statements, choose the correct answer from the options given below :

    1. Both Statement I and Statement II are true
    2. Both Statement I and Statement II are false
    3. Statement I is true but Statement II is false
    4. Statement I is false but Statement II is true
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    $\mathrm{S_N1}$ rate depends on carbocation stability.
    The benzyl cation is delocalised over the ring by resonance, far more stable than the ethyl cation (only hyperconjugation).
    So benzyl chloride reacts faster; both statements are true.

  2. JEE Main 2026, 2 April, Shift 2 · Question 65
    Correct statements regarding alkyl halides (R$-$X) among the following are :
    A. Alcohol being less polar solvent as compared to water, alcoholic KOH favours elimination reaction with R$-$X.
    B. Order of reactivity towards S$_\mathrm{N}$1 mechanism is C$_6$H$_5-$CH$_2-$Cl > C$_6$H$_5-$CHCl$-$C$_6$H$_5$.
    C. Non substituted aryl halides exhibit properties similar to alkyl halides.
    D. Vinyl chloride is an example of haloalkene and allyl chloride is an example of haloalkyne.
    E. R$-$Cl can be prepared by reacting R$-$OH with SOCl$_2$ but Ar$-$Cl cannot be prepared by reacting Ar$-$OH with SOCl$_2$.
    Choose the correct answer from the options given below :

    1. A, B and C Only
    2. B and D Only
    3. A and E Only
    4. D and E Only
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    A: true; the less polar alcoholic medium (with the stronger base RO$^-$) favours elimination.
    B: false; the diphenylmethyl cation is more stabilised than the benzyl cation, so C$_6$H$_5$CHClC$_6$H$_5$ reacts faster by S$_\mathrm{N}$1.
    C: false; aryl halides are much less reactive (partial double bond character of C$-$X).
    D: false; allyl chloride (CH$_2$=CHCH$_2$Cl) is also a haloalkene.
    E: true; the C$-$O bond of phenol has partial double bond character and is not replaced by SOCl$_2$.
    Answer: A and E only.

  3. JEE Main 2026, 4 April, Shift 1 · Question 65
    Match the LIST-I with LIST-II

    List-I
    Name of reaction    		List-II
    Reagent or catalyst used
    A. Finkelstein reactionI. $\mathrm{SbF_3}$
    B. Swarts reactionII. Na, dry ether

    Choose the correct answer from the options given below:

    3. A-IV, B-II, C-I, D-III

  4. JEE Main 2026, 4 April, Shift 2 · Question 65
    The following structures are

    1. enantiomers.
    2. identical molecules.
    3. diastereomers.
    4. meso compounds.
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    In both structures the central carbon carries Br, Cl and two methyl groups ($\mathrm{CH_3}$ and Me are the same group).
    A carbon with two identical groups is not a stereocentre, so the molecule (2-bromo-2-chloropropane) is achiral.
    The two drawings are the same molecule: identical molecules.

  5. JEE Main 2026, 5 April, Shift 2 · Question 72
    RMgI when treated with ice cold water liberated a gas which occupied $1.4\ \mathrm{dm^3/g}$ at STP. The gas produced is further reacted with iodine in presence of $\mathrm{HIO_3}$ to give compound (X). Compound (X) in presence of Na and dry ether produced compound (Y). Molar mass of compound (Y) is __________ $\mathrm{g\ mol^{-1}}$. (Nearest integer)

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 30

    Molar mass of the gas $=\dfrac{22.4}{1.4}=16\ \mathrm{g\ mol^{-1}}$, so the gas is $\mathrm{CH_4}$ (R = $\mathrm{CH_3}$).
    $\mathrm{CH_4 + I_2 \xrightarrow{HIO_3} CH_3I}$ (X).
    Wurtz reaction: $\mathrm{2CH_3I + 2Na \xrightarrow{dry\ ether} C_2H_6}$ (Y).
    Molar mass of Y $=30\ \mathrm{g\ mol^{-1}}$.

  6. JEE Main 2026, 6 April, Shift 1 · Question 66
    Given below are two statements:
    Statement I: 3-phenylpropene reacts with HBr and gives secondary alkyl bromide having a chiral carbon atom as the major product.
    Statement II: Aryl chlorides and aryl cyanides can be prepared by Sandmeyer reaction as well as Gattermann reaction.
    In the light of the above statements, choose the correct answer from the options given below

    1. Both Statement I and Statement II are true
    2. Both Statement I and Statement II are false
    3. Statement I is true but Statement II is false
    4. Statement I is false but Statement II is true
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    Statement I: $\mathrm{C_6H_5CH_2CH{=}CH_2}+\mathrm{HBr}\to\mathrm{C_6H_5CH_2CHBrCH_3}$ (Markovnikov), a secondary bromide whose C-2 carries H, Br, $\mathrm{CH_3}$ and $\mathrm{CH_2C_6H_5}$: chiral, true.
    Statement II: aryl chlorides can be made by both reactions, but the Gattermann reaction (Cu/HX) is used for aryl chlorides and bromides, not aryl cyanides (CuCN, Sandmeyer): false.
    Statement I is true but Statement II is false.

  7. JEE Main 2026, 8 April, Shift 2 · Question 65
    n-Butane on monochlorination under photochemical condition gives an optically active compound “P”. “P” on further chlorination gives dichloro compounds.
    The number of dichloro compounds obtained (ignore stereoisomers) is :

    1. $3$
    2. $4$
    3. $5$
    4. $6$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    P is 2-chlorobutane, $\mathrm{CH_3CHClCH_2CH_3}$ (chiral).
    A second Cl can go on C-1, C-2, C-3 or C-4: 1,2-dichloro-, 2,2-dichloro-, 2,3-dichloro- and 1,3-dichlorobutane (C-4 substitution gives 2,4- = 1,3-dichlorobutane).
    Number of dichloro compounds $=4$.

  8. JEE Main 2026, 8 April, Shift 2 · Question 66
    Given below are two statements :
    Statement I : Due to increase in van der Waals forces, the order of boiling points is $\mathrm{CH_3CH_2CH_2I>CH_3CH_2I>CH_3I}$.
    Statement II : As p-dichlorobenzene is more symmetric, its melting point is higher than o-dichlorobenzene, however its boiling point is lower than o-dichlorobenzene (structures as shown below).
    In the light of the above statements, choose the correct answer from the options given below :

    1. Both Statement I and Statement II are true
    2. Both Statement I and Statement II are false
    3. Statement I is true but Statement II is false
    4. Statement I is false but Statement II is true
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    Statement I: boiling points rise with molecular size because van der Waals forces increase: $\mathrm{CH_3CH_2CH_2I}$ (375 K) $>$ $\mathrm{CH_3CH_2I}$ (345 K) $>$ $\mathrm{CH_3I}$ (316 K). True.
    Statement II: p-dichlorobenzene (m.p. 326 K) packs better in the crystal than o-dichlorobenzene (m.p. 256 K), but its boiling point (447 K) is lower than that of the polar o-isomer (453 K). True.
    Both statements are true.

  9. JEE Main 2026, 21 January, Shift 2 · Question 62
    The correct order of the rate of the reaction for the following reaction with respect to nucleophiles is :
    $\mathrm{CH_3Br+Nu^\ominus\longrightarrow CH_3Nu+Br^\ominus}$

    1. $\mathrm{^-OH>PhO^->CH_3COO^->ClO_4^-}$
    2. $\mathrm{CH_3COO^->PhO^->{}^-OH>ClO_4^-}$
    3. $\mathrm{PhO^->{}^-OH>CH_3COO^->ClO_4^-}$
    4. $\mathrm{ClO_4^->CH_3COO^->{}^-OH>PhO^-}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    This is an $S_N2$ reaction at a methyl carbon, so the rate follows nucleophilicity, which here parallels basicity (same attacking atom, O).
    The more the negative charge is delocalised, the weaker the nucleophile: $\mathrm{OH^-}$ (localised) > $\mathrm{PhO^-}$ (ring delocalisation) > $\mathrm{CH_3COO^-}$ (over two O) > $\mathrm{ClO_4^-}$ (over four O).

  10. JEE Main 2026, 21 January, Shift 2 · Question 65
    Given below are four compounds :
    (a) n-propyl choride (b) iso-propyl chloride
    (c) sec-butyl chloride (d) neo-pentyl chloride
    Percentage of carbon in the one which exhibits optical isomerism is :

    1. $52$
    2. $56$
    3. $46$
    4. $40$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    Only sec-butyl chloride, $\mathrm{CH_3CH_2CH(Cl)CH_3}$, has a chiral carbon.
    $\mathrm{C_4H_9Cl}$: $M=48+9+35.5=92.5$.
    %C $=\dfrac{48}{92.5}\times100\approx52$.

  11. JEE Main 2026, 21 January, Shift 2 · Question 66
    The correct order of reactivity of the following benzyl halides towards reaction with KCN is :

    1. b > a > d > c
    2. a > b > d > c
    3. b > a > c > d
    4. a > b > c > d
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    The final key ranks them by how well the ring substituent stabilises a positive charge on the benzylic carbon.
    $\mathrm{-NH_2}$ (b) is the strongest electron donor, then $\mathrm{-OH}$ (a). $\mathrm{-NO_2}$ withdraws electrons; at the meta position (d) it acts only inductively, while at the para position (c) it also withdraws by resonance, so c is the least reactive.
    Order: b > a > d > c.

  12. JEE Main 2026, 22 January, Shift 1 · Question 64
    The correct order of reactivity of $\mathrm{CH_3Br}$ in methanol with the following nucleophiles is
    $\mathrm{F^-, I^-, C_2H_5O^-}$ and $\mathrm{C_6H_5O^-}$

    1. $\mathrm{I^->F^->C_6H_5O^->C_2H_5O^-}$
    2. $\mathrm{I^->C_6H_5O^->F^->C_2H_5O^-}$
    3. $\mathrm{I^->C_2H_5O^->C_6H_5O^->F^-}$
    4. $\mathrm{I^->C_2H_5O^->F^->C_6H_5O^-}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    In a protic solvent such as methanol, small anions are strongly solvated, so $\mathrm{F^-}$ is the weakest nucleophile and the large, polarisable $\mathrm{I^-}$ the strongest.
    Ethoxide is a stronger nucleophile than phenoxide, whose charge is delocalised into the ring.
    Order: $\mathrm{I^->C_2H_5O^->C_6H_5O^->F^-}$.

  13. JEE Main 2026, 22 January, Shift 1 · Question 66
    The correct order of the rate of reaction of the following reactants with nucleophile by $S_N1$ mechanism is :
    (Given : Structures I and II are rigid)

    1. III < I < II < IV
    2. I < II < III < IV
    3. IV < III < II < I
    4. II < I < III < IV
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    $S_N1$ rate follows carbocation stability.
    I and II are rigid bridgehead bromides: the bridgehead cation cannot become planar, so both react very slowly. The more strained bicyclo[2.2.1] cation (II) is even less stable than the bicyclo[2.2.2] cation (I).
    III gives the tertiary butyl cation, and IV the triphenylmethyl cation, which is strongly resonance-stabilised.
    Order: II < I < III < IV.

  14. JEE Main 2026, 22 January, Shift 1 · Question 67
    As compared with chlorocyclohexane, which of the following statements correctly apply to chlorobenzene?
    A. The magnitude of negative charge is more on chlorine atom.
    B. The C $-$ Cl bond has partial double bond character.
    C. C $-$ Cl bond is less polar.
    D. C $-$ Cl bond is longer due to repulsion between delocalised electrons of the aromatic ring and lone pairs of electrons of chlorine.
    E. The C $-$ Cl bond is formed using $sp^2$ hybridised orbital of carbon.
    Choose the correct answer from the options given below:

    1. B, C and E Only
    2. A, D and E Only
    3. B, C and D Only
    4. A, C and E Only
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    In chlorobenzene a lone pair of Cl is delocalised into the ring, giving the C-Cl bond partial double-bond character (B true). The bond is therefore shorter, not longer (D false).
    The carbon is $sp^2$ (E true); being more electronegative than $sp^3$ carbon, it makes the bond less polar (C true) and leaves less negative charge on Cl (A false).
    Correct: B, C and E only.

  15. JEE Main 2026, 23 January, Shift 1 · Question 66
    The correct sequence of reagents for the above conversion of X to Y is :

    1. (i) $\mathrm{B_2H_6/H_2O_2}$ (ii) NaOEt (iii) Jones reagent
    2. (i) Jones reagent (ii) NaOEt (iii) Hot $\mathrm{KMnO_4/KOH}$
    3. (i) NaOEt (ii) $\mathrm{B_2H_6/H_2O_2}$ (iii) Jones reagent
    4. (i) NaOH (aq) (ii) Jones reagent (iii) $\mathrm{H_3O^+}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    X is 1-bromo-1-phenylethane and Y is phenylethanoic acid, with the COOH on the end carbon.
    NaOEt eliminates HBr to give styrene, $\mathrm{C_6H_5CH=CH_2}$.
    Hydroboration-oxidation adds water anti-Markovnikov: $\mathrm{C_6H_5CH_2CH_2OH}$.
    Jones reagent oxidises the primary alcohol to $\mathrm{C_6H_5CH_2COOH}$.

  16. JEE Main 2026, 23 January, Shift 1 · Question 75
    Consider all the structural isomers with molecular formula $\mathrm{C_5H_{11}Br}$ are separately treated with KOH(aq) to give respective substitution products, without any rearrangement. The number of products which can exhibit optical isomerism from these is __________.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 3 or 6

    The 8 structural isomers of $\mathrm{C_5H_{11}Br}$ give the 8 alcohols $\mathrm{C_5H_{11}OH}$ with the same skeletons.
    Chiral ones: pentan-2-ol, 2-methylbutan-1-ol and 3-methylbutan-2-ol. (Pentan-1-ol, pentan-3-ol, 3-methylbutan-1-ol, 2-methylbutan-2-ol and 2,2-dimethylpropan-1-ol are not chiral.)
    Answer: 3.

    Note: Official final key: 3 or 6 (either answer is awarded marks). 3 counts the chiral alcohols by structure; 6 counts each of their two enantiomers.

  17. JEE Main 2026, 24 January, Shift 1 · Question 65
    Given below are two statements:
    Statement I: 'C $-$ Cl' bond is stronger in $\mathrm{CH_2=CH-Cl}$ than $\mathrm{CH_3-CH_2-Cl}$
    Statement II: The given optically active molecule, shown below, on hydrolysis gives a solution that can rotate the plane polarized light.
    In the light of the above statements, choose the correct answer from the options given below

    1. Both Statement I and Statement II are true
    2. Both Statement I and Statement II are false
    3. Statement I is true but Statement II is false
    4. Statement I is false but Statement II is true
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    Statement I is true: in vinyl chloride the C-Cl bond has partial double-bond character (resonance) and the carbon is $sp^2$, so the bond is shorter and stronger.
    Statement II is false: the tertiary benzylic chloride hydrolyses by $S_N1$ through a planar carbocation, giving a racemic alcohol that does not rotate plane-polarised light.

  18. JEE Main 2026, 24 January, Shift 1 · Question 66
    Match the LIST-I with LIST-II

    List-I
    Chloro derivative    		List-II
    Example

    B. Benzyl ChlorideII. $\mathrm{CH_3-CH(Cl)CH_3}$
    C. Alkyl ChlorideIII. $\mathrm{CH_2=CHCl}$
    D. Allyl ChlorideIV. $\mathrm{C_6H_5-CH(Cl)-CH_3}$
    Choose the correct answer from the options given below:

    2. A-III, B-IV, C-II, D-I

  19. JEE Main 2026, 28 January, Shift 2 · Question 63
    Identify the correct statements :
    The presence of $-\mathrm{NO_2}$ group in benzene ring
    A. activates the ring towards electrophilic substitutions.
    B. deactivates the ring towards electrophilic substitutions.
    C. activates the ring towards nucleophilic substitutions.
    D. deactivates the ring towards nucleophilic substitutions.
    Choose the correct answer from the options given below :

    1. A and D Only
    2. C and A Only
    3. B and C Only
    4. B and D Only
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    The nitro group withdraws electron density from the ring ($-R$ and $-I$). This makes attack by electrophiles harder (B) and stabilises the negatively charged intermediate formed in nucleophilic substitution, e.g. of o- and p-nitrochlorobenzene (C).
