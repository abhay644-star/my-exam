  1. JEE Main 2026, 2 April, Shift 1 · Question 57
    An electrochemical cell is constructed using half cells in the direction of spontaneous change
    $\mathrm{Fe(OH)_2(s) + 2e^- \rightarrow Fe(s) + 2OH^-(aq)} \qquad \mathrm{E^{\theta} = -0.88\ V}$
    and $\mathrm{AgBr(s) + e^- \rightarrow Ag(s) + Br^-(aq)} \qquad \mathrm{E^{\theta} = +0.07\ V}$
    Which of the following option is correct ?

    1. Overall reaction $\mathrm{Fe(s) + 2OH^-(aq) + 2AgBr(s) \rightleftharpoons Fe(OH)_2(s) + 2Ag(s) + 2Br^-(aq)}$
    2. $\mathrm{E^{\theta}_{cell} = -0.95\ V}$
    3. Fe is reduced in the electrochemical cell
    4. $\mathrm{E^{\theta}_{cell}}$ is an extensive property
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    Higher reduction potential (AgBr/Ag, $+0.07$ V) is the cathode; Fe/Fe(OH)$_2$ is the anode (Fe is oxidised).
    Overall: $\mathrm{Fe + 2OH^- + 2AgBr \rightleftharpoons Fe(OH)_2 + 2Ag + 2Br^-}$.
    $E^\theta_{cell} = 0.07 - (-0.88) = +0.95$ V, and $E^\theta_{cell}$ is intensive.
    Only the first option is correct.

  2. JEE Main 2026, 2 April, Shift 2 · Question 73
    Consider the following two half-cell reactions along with the standard reduction potential given :
    $\mathrm{CO_2+6H^++6e^-\longrightarrow CH_3OH+H_2O}$ $\quad E^\circ_{red}=0.02$ V
    $\mathrm{\dfrac12O_2+2H^++2e^-\longrightarrow H_2O}$ $\quad E^\circ_{red}=1.23$ V
    A fuel cell was set up using the above two reactions such that the cell operates under the standard condition of 1 bar pressure and 298 K temperature. The fuel cell works with 80% efficiency. If the work derived from the cell using 1 mol of CH$_3$OH is used to compress an ideal gas isothermally against a constant pressure of 1 kPa, then the change in the volume of the gas, $\Delta V=$ __________ m$^3$. (nearest integer)
    Given : $F=96500$ C mol$^{-1}$

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 560

    Cell reaction: $\mathrm{CH_3OH+\tfrac32O_2\to CO_2+2H_2O}$, $n=6$, $E^\circ_{cell}=1.23-0.02=1.21$ V.
    $-\Delta G^\circ=6\times96500\times1.21=700590$ J per mol CH$_3$OH.
    Useful work at 80%: $0.8\times700590=560472$ J.
    $W=P\Delta V \Rightarrow \Delta V=\dfrac{560472}{1000}\approx560$ m$^3$ (magnitude).

  3. JEE Main 2026, 4 April, Shift 2 · Question 73
    An electrochemical cell, consist of the following two redox couples, $\mathrm{M^{x+}(aq)/M(s)}$ $[E^\ominus_{red}=+0.15\ \mathrm{V}]$ and $\mathrm{Fe^{3+}(aq)/Fe(s)}$ $[E^\ominus_{red}=-0.036\ \mathrm{V}]$. The cell EMF ($E_{cell}$) is recorded to be 0.2057 V. If the reaction quotient of the electrochemical reaction is found to be $10^{-2}$, then the value of $x$ is ____________.(Nearest integer)
    [Given : M is a p-block metal and $\dfrac{2.303RT}{F}=0.059$ V]

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 2

    Fe is the anode and M the cathode: $E^\circ_{cell}=0.15-(-0.036)=0.186$ V.
    Cell reaction $3\mathrm{M^{x+}}+x\,\mathrm{Fe}\to3\mathrm{M}+x\,\mathrm{Fe^{3+}}$ transfers $n=3x$ electrons.
    Nernst: $0.2057=0.186-\dfrac{0.059}{n}\log10^{-2}=0.186+\dfrac{0.118}{n}\Rightarrow n=\dfrac{0.118}{0.0197}\approx6$.
    $3x=6\Rightarrow x=2$.

  4. JEE Main 2026, 5 April, Shift 2 · Question 57
    One half cell in a voltaic cell is constructed by dipping silver rod in $\mathrm{AgNO_3}$ solution of unknown concentration, other half cell is Zn rod dipped in 1 molar solution of $\mathrm{ZnSO_4}$.
    A voltage of 1.60 V is measured at 298 K for this cell. What is the concentration of $\mathrm{Ag^+}$ ions used in terms of $\log x$ $(x=[\mathrm{Ag^+}])$ ?
    $E^\ominus_{\mathrm{Zn^{2+}/Zn}}=-0.76$ V, $E^\ominus_{\mathrm{Ag^+/Ag}}=+0.80$ V, $\dfrac{2.303RT}{F}=0.059$ V

    1. $\dfrac{2}{3.9}$
    2. $\dfrac{4}{5.9}$
    3. $\dfrac{2.9}{2}$
    4. $\dfrac{5.9}{4}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    Cell: $\mathrm{Zn + 2Ag^+ \rightarrow Zn^{2+} + 2Ag}$, $E^\ominus_{cell}=0.80-(-0.76)=1.56$ V, $n=2$.
    $E=1.56-\dfrac{0.059}{2}\log\dfrac{[\mathrm{Zn^{2+}}]}{[\mathrm{Ag^+}]^2}=1.56+0.059\log x$.
    $1.60=1.56+0.059\log x \Rightarrow \log x=\dfrac{0.04}{0.059}=\dfrac{4}{5.9}$.

  5. JEE Main 2026, 5 April, Shift 2 · Question 74
    At 298 K, the molar conductivity of $x$% (w/w) MX solution (aqueous) is $123.5\ \mathrm{S\ cm^2\ mol^{-1}}$. The conductance of same solution is $1.9\times10^{-3}$ S. The value of $x$ is __________ $\times10^{-2}$.
    (Given : cell constant $=1.3\ \mathrm{cm^{-1}}$; molar mass of MX is $75\ \mathrm{g\ mol^{-1}}$, density of aqueous solution of MX at 298 K is $1.0\ \mathrm{g\ mL^{-1}}$)

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 15

    $\kappa=G\times\text{cell constant}=1.9\times10^{-3}\times1.3=2.47\times10^{-3}\ \mathrm{S\ cm^{-1}}$.
    $c=\dfrac{1000\kappa}{\Lambda_m}=\dfrac{2.47}{123.5}=0.02\ \mathrm{mol\ L^{-1}}$.
    1 L of solution (1000 g) contains $0.02\times75=1.5$ g MX, so $x=\dfrac{1.5}{1000}\times100=0.15=15\times10^{-2}$.

  6. JEE Main 2026, 6 April, Shift 1 · Question 58
    Consider the following data.

    Electrolyte$\Lambda^\circ_m$ (S cm$^2$ mol$^{-1}$)
    $\mathrm{BaCl_2}$$x_1$
    $\mathrm{H_2SO_4}$$x_2$
    HCl$x_3$
    $\mathrm{BaSO_4}$ is sparingly soluble in water. If the conductivity of the saturated $\mathrm{BaSO_4}$ solution is $x$ S cm$^{-1}$ then the solubility product of $\mathrm{BaSO_4}$ can be given as
    (Here $\Lambda_m=\Lambda^\circ_m$)

    1. $\dfrac{10^6x^2}{\alpha^2\left(x_1+x_2-2x_3\right)^2}$
    2. $\dfrac{x^2}{\left(x_1+x_2-2x_3\right)^2}$
    3. $\dfrac{\alpha^2\left(x_1+x_2-2x_3\right)^2}{10^6x^2}$
    4. $\dfrac{x^2}{\left(x_1+x_2+2x_3\right)^2}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    Kohlrausch: $\Lambda^\circ_m(\mathrm{BaSO_4})=\Lambda^\circ_m(\mathrm{BaCl_2})+\Lambda^\circ_m(\mathrm{H_2SO_4})-2\Lambda^\circ_m(\mathrm{HCl})=x_1+x_2-2x_3$.
    Solubility $s=\dfrac{1000\,\kappa}{\Lambda_m}=\dfrac{10^3x}{x_1+x_2-2x_3}$ mol L$^{-1}$.
    $K_{sp}=s^2=\dfrac{10^6x^2}{(x_1+x_2-2x_3)^2}$, which is option (1) with the degree of dissociation $\alpha=1$ (since $\Lambda_m=\Lambda^\circ_m$).

  7. JEE Main 2026, 6 April, Shift 2 · Question 57
    For a general redox reaction
    Anode: $\mathrm{Red_1\to Ox_1^{n_1+}+n_1e^-}$
    Cathode: $\mathrm{Ox_2+n_2e^-\to Red_2^{n_2-}}$
    Which of the following statement is incorrect ?

    1. The overall reaction can be written as $\mathrm{n_2Red_1+n_1Ox_2\rightleftharpoons n_2Ox_1^{n_1+}+n_1Red_2^{n_2-}}$
    2. The electrons do not appear in the overall reaction because electrons produced at the anode are consumed at the cathode.
    4. If the reaction is carried out reversibly, the electrical work done is equal to the ratio of charge and potential difference through which charge is moved.
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    (1) Multiplying the anode reaction by $n_2$ and the cathode reaction by $n_1$ balances the electrons: correct.
    (2) Electrons released at the anode are consumed at the cathode: correct.
    (3) Nernst: $\dfrac{E-E^\circ}{RT/F}=-\dfrac{2.303}{n}\log_{10}Q$, a straight line through the origin with slope proportional to $\dfrac1n$: correct.
    (4) Reversible electrical work $=$ charge $\times$ potential difference ($w=-nFE$), not their ratio: incorrect.

  8. JEE Main 2026, 8 April, Shift 2 · Question 56
    Given at 298 K : $E^{\ominus}_{\mathrm{Fe^{2+}/Fe}}=X$ Volt, $E^{\ominus}_{\mathrm{Fe^{3+}/Fe}}=Y$ Volt
    The $E^{\ominus}_{\mathrm{Fe^{3+}/Fe^{2+}}}$ in Volt at 298 K is given by :

    1. $2X-3Y$
    2. $3Y-2X$
    3. $3Y+2X$
    4. $Y+X$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    $\mathrm{Fe^{3+}+3e^-\to Fe}$: $\Delta G_1^\circ=-3FY$. $\mathrm{Fe^{2+}+2e^-\to Fe}$: $\Delta G_2^\circ=-2FX$.
    $\mathrm{Fe^{3+}+e^-\to Fe^{2+}}$: $\Delta G^\circ=\Delta G_1^\circ-\Delta G_2^\circ=-3FY+2FX=-FE^\circ$.
    $E^\circ=3Y-2X$.

  9. JEE Main 2026, 21 January, Shift 1 · Question 74
    The pH and conductance of a weak acid (HX) was found to be 5 and $4\times10^{-5}$ S, respectively. The conductance was measured under standard condition using a cell where the electrode plates having a surface area of 1 cm$^2$ were at a distance of 15 cm apart. The value of the limiting molar conductivity is __________ S m$^2$ mol$^{-1}$. (nearest integer)
    (Given : degree of dissociation of the weak acid $(\alpha)$ << 1)

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 6

    Conductivity $\kappa=G\dfrac{l}{A}=4\times10^{-5}\times\dfrac{15}{1}=6\times10^{-4}$ S cm$^{-1}=6\times10^{-2}$ S m$^{-1}$.
    $\Lambda_m=\dfrac{\kappa}{c}$ and $\alpha=\dfrac{\Lambda_m}{\Lambda_m^\circ}$, so $\Lambda_m^\circ=\dfrac{\kappa}{c\alpha}=\dfrac{\kappa}{[\mathrm{H^+}]}$.
    $[\mathrm{H^+}]=10^{-5}$ mol L$^{-1}=10^{-2}$ mol m$^{-3}$, so $\Lambda_m^\circ=\dfrac{6\times10^{-2}}{10^{-2}}=6$ S m$^2$ mol$^{-1}$.

  11. JEE Main 2026, 21 January, Shift 2 · Question 74
    MX is a sparingly soluble salt that follows the given solubility equilibrium at 298 K.
    $\mathrm{MX(s)\rightleftharpoons M^+(aq)+X^-(aq)}$; $K_{sp}=10^{-10}$
    If the standard reduction potential for $\mathrm{M^+(aq)\xrightarrow{+e^-}M(s)}$ is $\left(E^\ominus_{\mathrm{M^+/M}}\right)=0.79$ V, then the value of the standard reduction potential for the metal/metal insoluble salt electrode $E^\ominus_{\mathrm{X^-/MX(s)/M}}$ is _________ mV. (nearest integer)
    $\left[\text{Given : }\dfrac{2.303RT}{F}=0.059\text{ V}\right]$

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 200

    $\mathrm{MX(s)+e^-\rightarrow M(s)+X^-}$ is $\mathrm{M^++e^-\rightarrow M}$ combined with $\mathrm{MX\rightarrow M^++X^-}$.
    $E^\ominus_{\mathrm{X^-/MX/M}}=E^\ominus_{\mathrm{M^+/M}}+0.059\log K_{sp}=0.79+0.059(-10)=0.20$ V.
    $=200$ mV.

  12. JEE Main 2026, 22 January, Shift 1 · Question 72
    Consider the following electrochemical cell at 298K
    $\mathrm{Pt\,|\,HSnO_2^-(aq)\,|\,Sn(OH)_6^{2-}(aq)\,|\,OH^-(aq)\,|\,Bi_2O_3(s)\,|\,Bi(s)}$.
    If the reaction quotient at a given time is $10^6$, then the cell EMF ($E_{\text{cell}}$) is __________ $\times10^{-1}$ V (Nearest integer).
    Given the standard half-cell reduction potential as
    $E^\circ_{\mathrm{Bi_2O_3/Bi,OH^-}}=-0.44$ V and $E^\circ_{\mathrm{Sn(OH)_6^{2-}/HSnO_2^-,OH^-}}=-0.90$ V

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 4

    Anode: $\mathrm{HSnO_2^-}\to\mathrm{Sn(OH)_6^{2-}}$ (Sn(II) to Sn(IV), 2 e$^-$). Cathode: $\mathrm{Bi_2O_3}\to2\mathrm{Bi}$ (6 e$^-$). Balanced overall: $n=6$.
    $E^\circ_{\text{cell}}=-0.44-(-0.90)=0.46$ V.
    $E=0.46-\dfrac{0.0591}{6}\log10^6=0.46-0.059\approx0.40$ V $=4\times10^{-1}$ V.

  13. JEE Main 2026, 22 January, Shift 2 · Question 55
    Consider the following reduction processes :
    $\mathrm{Al^{3+}+3e^-\longrightarrow Al(s)}$, $E^\circ=-1.66$ V
    $\mathrm{Fe^{3+}+e^-\longrightarrow Fe^{2+}}$, $E^\circ=+0.77$ V
    $\mathrm{Co^{3+}+e^-\longrightarrow Co^{2+}}$, $E^\circ=+1.81$ V
    $\mathrm{Cr^{3+}+3e^-\longrightarrow Cr(s)}$, $E^\circ=-0.74$ V
    The tendency to act as reducing agent decreases in the order :

    1. $\mathrm{Al>Cr>Co^{2+}>Fe^{2+}}$
    2. $\mathrm{Al>Cr>Fe^{2+}>Co^{2+}}$
    3. $\mathrm{Al>Fe^{2+}>Cr>Co^{2+}}$
    4. $\mathrm{Cr>Fe^{2+}>Al>Co^{2+}}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    The lower the reduction potential, the stronger the reduced form is as a reducing agent.
    $E^\circ$: Al $-1.66$ < Cr $-0.74$ < $\mathrm{Fe^{2+}}$ $+0.77$ < $\mathrm{Co^{2+}}$ $+1.81$.
    Order: $\mathrm{Al>Cr>Fe^{2+}>Co^{2+}}$.

  14. JEE Main 2026, 22 January, Shift 2 · Question 72
    Consider the following electrochemical cell :
    $\mathrm{Pt\,|\,O_2(g)(1bar)\,|\,HCl(aq)\,||\,M^{2+}(aq, 1.0M)\,|\,M(s)}$
    The pH above which, oxygen gas would start to evolve at anode is __________ (nearest integer).
    [Given : $E^\circ_{\mathrm{M^{2+}/M}}=0.994$ V, $E^\circ_{\mathrm{O_2/H_2O}}=1.23$ V (standard reduction potential) and $\dfrac{RT}{F}(2.303)=0.059$ V at the given condition]

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 4

    Anode: $\mathrm{2H_2O\to O_2+4H^++4e^-}$, with $E_{\mathrm{O_2/H_2O}}=1.23-0.059\,\text{pH}$ (for 1 bar $\mathrm{O_2}$).
    The cell works spontaneously (oxygen evolves) when $E_{\text{cell}}=0.994-(1.23-0.059\,\text{pH})>0$.
    $0.059\,\text{pH}>0.236\Rightarrow\text{pH}>4$.

  15. JEE Main 2026, 23 January, Shift 1 · Question 57
    In the given electrochemical cell, $\mathrm{Ag(s)\,|\,AgCl(s)\,|\,FeCl_2(aq), FeCl_3(aq)\,|\,Pt(s)}$ at 298 K, the cell potential $\left(E_{\text{cell}}\right)$ will increase when :
    A. Concentration of $\mathrm{Fe^{2+}}$ is increased.
    B. Concentration of $\mathrm{Fe^{3+}}$ is decreased.
    C. Concentration of $\mathrm{Fe^{2+}}$ is decreased.
    D. Concentration of $\mathrm{Fe^{3+}}$ is increased.
    E. Concentration of $\mathrm{Cl^-}$ is increased.
    Choose the correct answer from the options given below :

    1. A and B Only
    2. A and E Only
    3. B Only
    4. C, D and E Only
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    Cell reaction: $\mathrm{Ag+Cl^-+Fe^{3+}\rightarrow AgCl+Fe^{2+}}$.
    $E_{\text{cell}}=E^\circ_{\text{cell}}-0.059\log\dfrac{[\mathrm{Fe^{2+}}]}{[\mathrm{Fe^{3+}}][\mathrm{Cl^-}]}$.
    $E_{\text{cell}}$ rises when $[\mathrm{Fe^{2+}}]$ falls or $[\mathrm{Fe^{3+}}]$ or $[\mathrm{Cl^-}]$ rises: C, D and E.

  16. JEE Main 2026, 23 January, Shift 2 · Question 55
    Consider the above electrochemical cell where a metal electrode (M) is undergoing redox reaction by forming $\mathrm{M^+}$ $\left(\mathrm{M\rightarrow M^++e^-}\right)$. The cation $\mathrm{M^+}$ is present in two different concentrations $c_1$ and $c_2$ as shown above. Which of the following statement is correct for generating a positive cell potential?

    1. If $c_1$ is present at cathode, then $c_1<c_2$.
    2. If $c_1$ is present at anode, then $c_1>c_2$.
    3. If $c_1$ is present at cathode, then $c_1>c_2$.
    4. If $c_1$ is present at anode, then $c_1=c_2$.
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    This is a concentration cell: $E_{\text{cell}}=\dfrac{0.059}{1}\log\dfrac{[\mathrm{M^+}]_{\text{cathode}}}{[\mathrm{M^+}]_{\text{anode}}}$.
    $E_{\text{cell}}>0$ needs the larger concentration at the cathode.
    So if $c_1$ is at the cathode, $c_1>c_2$.

    Electricity is passed through an acidic solution of $\mathrm{Cu^{2+}}$ till all the $\mathrm{Cu^{2+}}$ was exhausted, leading to the deposition of 300 mg of Cu metal. However, a current of 600 mA was continued to pass through the same solution for another 28 minutes by keeping the total volume of the solution fixed at 200 mL. The total volume of oxygen evolved at STP during the entire process is ____ mL. (Nearest integer)
    [Given:
    $\mathrm{Cu^{2+}(aq)+2e^-\rightarrow Cu(s)}$ $E^\circ_{red}=+0.34$ V
    $\mathrm{O_2(g)+4H^++4e^-\rightarrow2H_2O}$ $E^\circ_{red}=+1.23$ V
    Molar mass of Cu = 63.54 g mol$^{-1}$
    Molar mass of $\mathrm{O_2}$ = 32 g mol$^{-1}$
    Faraday Constant = 96500 C mol$^{-1}$
    Molar volume at STP = 22.4 L]

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 111

    Copper stage: $\dfrac{0.300}{63.54}=4.72\times10^{-3}$ mol Cu needs $9.44\times10^{-3}$ mol $e^-$, which releases $2.36\times10^{-3}$ mol $\mathrm{O_2}$ at the anode.

  18. JEE Main 2026, 24 January, Shift 2 · Question 74
    Molar conductivity of a weak acid HQ of concentration 0.18 M was found to be 1/30 of the molar conductivity of another weak acid HZ with concentration of 0.02 M. If $\lambda^\circ_{\mathrm{Q^-}}$ happened to be equal with $\lambda^\circ_{\mathrm{Z^-}}$, then the difference of the $\mathrm{p}K_a$ values of the two weak acids $(\mathrm{p}K_a(\mathrm{HQ})-\mathrm{p}K_a(\mathrm{HZ}))$ is ______ (Nearest integer).
    [Given: degree of dissociation $(\alpha)$ << 1 for both weak acids, $\lambda^\circ$ : limiting molar conductivity of ions]

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 2

    Equal $\Lambda^\circ$ for both acids, so $\alpha=\dfrac{\Lambda_m}{\Lambda^\circ}$ gives $\alpha_{\mathrm{HQ}}=\dfrac{\alpha_{\mathrm{HZ}}}{30}$.
    $K_a=c\alpha^2$: $\dfrac{K_a(\mathrm{HQ})}{K_a(\mathrm{HZ})}=\dfrac{0.18}{0.02}\times\dfrac1{900}=\dfrac1{100}$.
    $\mathrm{p}K_a(\mathrm{HQ})-\mathrm{p}K_a(\mathrm{HZ})=\log100=2$.

  19. JEE Main 2026, 28 January, Shift 1 · Question 74
    Consider the following redox reaction taking place in acidic medium
    $\mathrm{BH_4^-(aq)+ClO_3^-(aq)\longrightarrow H_2BO_3^-(aq)+Cl^-(aq)}$
    If the Nernst equation for the above balanced reaction is
    $\mathrm{E_{cell}=E^\circ_{cell}-\dfrac{RT}{nF}\ln Q}$,
    then the value of n is _______ .(Nearest integer)

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 24

    In $\mathrm{BH_4^-}$ hydrogen is $-1$; in $\mathrm{H_2BO_3^-}$ it is $+1$ (B stays $+3$). Each $\mathrm{BH_4^-}$ loses $4\times2=8$ electrons.
    Chlorine goes from $+5$ to $-1$: each $\mathrm{ClO_3^-}$ gains 6 electrons.
    Balanced: $3\,\mathrm{BH_4^-}+4\,\mathrm{ClO_3^-}\to3\,\mathrm{H_2BO_3^-}+4\,\mathrm{Cl^-}+3\,\mathrm{H_2O}$, with $n=24$ electrons transferred.

  20. JEE Main 2026, 28 January, Shift 2 · Question 72
    For strong electrolyte $\Lambda_m$ increases slowly with dilution and can be represented by the equation
    $\Lambda_m=\Lambda^\circ_m-Ac^{1/2}$
    Molar conductivity values of the solutions of strong electrolyte AB at 18$^\circ$C are given below :

    c [mol L$^{-1}$]0.040.090.160.25
    $\Lambda_m$ [S cm$^2$ mol$^{-1}$]96.195.795.394.9
    The value of constant A based on the above data [in S cm$^2$ mol$^{-1}$/(mol/L)$^{1/2}$] unit is __________.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 4

    $\sqrt c=0.2,\ 0.3,\ 0.4,\ 0.5$ and $\Lambda_m$ falls by 0.4 for each step of 0.1 in $\sqrt c$.

  21. JEE Main 2026, 28 January, Shift 2 · Question 73
    A volume of $x$ mL of 5 M $\mathrm{NaHCO_3}$ solution was mixed with 10 mL of 2 M $\mathrm{H_2CO_3}$ solution to make an electrolytic buffer. If the same buffer was used in the following electrochemical cell to record a cell potential of 235.3 mV, then the value of $x$ = __________ mL (nearest integer).
    $\mathrm{Sn(s)\,|\,Sn(OH)_6^{2-}\,(0.5\ M)\,|\,HSnO_2^-\,(0.05\ M)\,|\,OH^-\,|\,Bi_2O_3(s)\,|\,Bi(s)}$
    Consider upto one place of decimal for intermediate calculations
    Given : $\mathrm{E^\circ_{HSnO_2^-|Sn(OH)_6^{2-}}=-0.9\ V}$
    $\mathrm{E^\circ_{Bi_2O_3|Bi}=-0.44\ V}$
    $\mathrm{pKa_{(H_2CO_3)}}=6.11$
    $\dfrac{2.303\,RT}{F}=0.059$ V
    Antilog (1.29) = 19.5

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 78

    Anode: $\mathrm{HSnO_2^-+3OH^-+H_2O\to Sn(OH)_6^{2-}+2e^-}$; cathode: $\mathrm{Bi_2O_3+3H_2O+6e^-\to2Bi+6OH^-}$. $E^\circ_{\text{cell}}=-0.44-(-0.9)=0.46$ V.
    $E=0.46-\dfrac{0.059}{2}\log\dfrac{[\mathrm{Sn(OH)_6^{2-}}]}{[\mathrm{HSnO_2^-}][\mathrm{OH^-}]}=0.46-0.0295\log\dfrac{10}{[\mathrm{OH^-}]}=0.2353$.
    $\log\dfrac{10}{[\mathrm{OH^-}]}=7.6$, so pOH $=6.6$ and pH $=7.4$.
    Buffer: $7.4=6.11+\log\dfrac{5x}{20}$, so $\dfrac{5x}{20}=$ antilog $1.29=19.5$ and $x=78$ mL.
