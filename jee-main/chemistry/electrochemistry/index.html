  1. JEE Main 2026, 2 April, Shift 1 · Question 57
    An electrochemical cell is constructed using half cells in the direction of spontaneous change
    $\mathrm{Fe(OH)_2(s) + 2e^- \rightarrow Fe(s) + 2OH^-(aq)} \qquad \mathrm{E^{\theta} = -0.88\ V}$
    and $\mathrm{AgBr(s) + e^- \rightarrow Ag(s) + Br^-(aq)} \qquad \mathrm{E^{\theta} = +0.07\ V}$
    Which of the following option is correct ?

    1. Overall reaction $\mathrm{Fe(s) + 2OH^-(aq) + 2AgBr(s) \rightleftharpoons Fe(OH)_2(s) + 2Ag(s) + 2Br^-(aq)}$
    2. $\mathrm{E^{\theta}_{cell} = -0.95\ V}$
    3. Fe is reduced in the electrochemical cell
    4. $\mathrm{E^{\theta}_{cell}}$ is an extensive property
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    Higher reduction potential (AgBr/Ag, $+0.07$ V) is the cathode; Fe/Fe(OH)$_2$ is the anode (Fe is oxidised).
    Overall: $\mathrm{Fe + 2OH^- + 2AgBr \rightleftharpoons Fe(OH)_2 + 2Ag + 2Br^-}$.
    $E^\theta_{cell} = 0.07 - (-0.88) = +0.95$ V, and $E^\theta_{cell}$ is intensive.
    Only the first option is correct.

  2. JEE Main 2026, 2 April, Shift 2 · Question 73
    Consider the following two half-cell reactions along with the standard reduction potential given :
    $\mathrm{CO_2+6H^++6e^-\longrightarrow CH_3OH+H_2O}$ $\quad E^\circ_{red}=0.02$ V
    $\mathrm{\dfrac12O_2+2H^++2e^-\longrightarrow H_2O}$ $\quad E^\circ_{red}=1.23$ V
    A fuel cell was set up using the above two reactions such that the cell operates under the standard condition of 1 bar pressure and 298 K temperature. The fuel cell works with 80% efficiency. If the work derived from the cell using 1 mol of CH$_3$OH is used to compress an ideal gas isothermally against a constant pressure of 1 kPa, then the change in the volume of the gas, $\Delta V=$ __________ m$^3$. (nearest integer)
    Given : $F=96500$ C mol$^{-1}$

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 560

    Cell reaction: $\mathrm{CH_3OH+\tfrac32O_2\to CO_2+2H_2O}$, $n=6$, $E^\circ_{cell}=1.23-0.02=1.21$ V.
    $-\Delta G^\circ=6\times96500\times1.21=700590$ J per mol CH$_3$OH.
    Useful work at 80%: $0.8\times700590=560472$ J.
    $W=P\Delta V \Rightarrow \Delta V=\dfrac{560472}{1000}\approx560$ m$^3$ (magnitude).

  3. JEE Main 2026, 4 April, Shift 2 · Question 73
    An electrochemical cell, consist of the following two redox couples, $\mathrm{M^{x+}(aq)/M(s)}$ $[E^\ominus_{red}=+0.15\ \mathrm{V}]$ and $\mathrm{Fe^{3+}(aq)/Fe(s)}$ $[E^\ominus_{red}=-0.036\ \mathrm{V}]$. The cell EMF ($E_{cell}$) is recorded to be 0.2057 V. If the reaction quotient of the electrochemical reaction is found to be $10^{-2}$, then the value of $x$ is ____________.(Nearest integer)
    [Given : M is a p-block metal and $\dfrac{2.303RT}{F}=0.059$ V]

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 2

    Fe is the anode and M the cathode: $E^\circ_{cell}=0.15-(-0.036)=0.186$ V.
    Cell reaction $3\mathrm{M^{x+}}+x\,\mathrm{Fe}\to3\mathrm{M}+x\,\mathrm{Fe^{3+}}$ transfers $n=3x$ electrons.
    Nernst: $0.2057=0.186-\dfrac{0.059}{n}\log10^{-2}=0.186+\dfrac{0.118}{n}\Rightarrow n=\dfrac{0.118}{0.0197}\approx6$.
    $3x=6\Rightarrow x=2$.

  4. JEE Main 2026, 5 April, Shift 2 · Question 57
    One half cell in a voltaic cell is constructed by dipping silver rod in $\mathrm{AgNO_3}$ solution of unknown concentration, other half cell is Zn rod dipped in 1 molar solution of $\mathrm{ZnSO_4}$.
    A voltage of 1.60 V is measured at 298 K for this cell. What is the concentration of $\mathrm{Ag^+}$ ions used in terms of $\log x$ $(x=[\mathrm{Ag^+}])$ ?
    $E^\ominus_{\mathrm{Zn^{2+}/Zn}}=-0.76$ V, $E^\ominus_{\mathrm{Ag^+/Ag}}=+0.80$ V, $\dfrac{2.303RT}{F}=0.059$ V

    1. $\dfrac{2}{3.9}$
    2. $\dfrac{4}{5.9}$
    3. $\dfrac{2.9}{2}$
    4. $\dfrac{5.9}{4}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    Cell: $\mathrm{Zn + 2Ag^+ \rightarrow Zn^{2+} + 2Ag}$, $E^\ominus_{cell}=0.80-(-0.76)=1.56$ V, $n=2$.

    At 298 K, the molar conductivity of $x$% (w/w) MX solution (aqueous) is $123.5\ \mathrm{S\ cm^2\ mol^{-1}}$. The conductance of same solution is $1.9\times10^{-3}$ S. The value of $x$ is __________ $\times10^{-2}$.
    (Given : cell constant $=1.3\ \mathrm{cm^{-1}}$; molar mass of MX is $75\ \mathrm{g\ mol^{-1}}$, density of aqueous solution of MX at 298 K is $1.0\ \mathrm{g\ mL^{-1}}$)

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 15

    $\kappa=G\times\text{cell constant}=1.9\times10^{-3}\times1.3=2.47\times10^{-3}\ \mathrm{S\ cm^{-1}}$.

    1 L of solution (1000 g) contains $0.02\times75=1.5$ g MX, so $x=\dfrac{1.5}{1000}\times100=0.15=15\times10^{-2}$.

  7. JEE Main 2026, 6 April, Shift 2 · Question 57
    For a general redox reaction
    Anode: $\mathrm{Red_1\to Ox_1^{n_1+}+n_1e^-}$
    Cathode: $\mathrm{Ox_2+n_2e^-\to Red_2^{n_2-}}$
    Which of the following statement is incorrect ?

    1. The overall reaction can be written as $\mathrm{n_2Red_1+n_1Ox_2\rightleftharpoons n_2Ox_1^{n_1+}+n_1Red_2^{n_2-}}$
    2. The electrons do not appear in the overall reaction because electrons produced at the anode are consumed at the cathode.
    4. If the reaction is carried out reversibly, the electrical work done is equal to the ratio of charge and potential difference through which charge is moved.
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    (1) Multiplying the anode reaction by $n_2$ and the cathode reaction by $n_1$ balances the electrons: correct.
    (2) Electrons released at the anode are consumed at the cathode: correct.
    (3) Nernst: $\dfrac{E-E^\circ}{RT/F}=-\dfrac{2.303}{n}\log_{10}Q$, a straight line through the origin with slope proportional to $\dfrac1n$: correct.
    (4) Reversible electrical work $=$ charge $\times$ potential difference ($w=-nFE$), not their ratio: incorrect.

    Given at 298 K : $E^{\ominus}_{\mathrm{Fe^{2+}/Fe}}=X$ Volt, $E^{\ominus}_{\mathrm{Fe^{3+}/Fe}}=Y$ Volt
    The $E^{\ominus}_{\mathrm{Fe^{3+}/Fe^{2+}}}$ in Volt at 298 K is given by :

    1. $2X-3Y$
    2. $3Y-2X$
    3. $3Y+2X$
    4. $Y+X$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    $\mathrm{Fe^{3+}+3e^-\to Fe}$: $\Delta G_1^\circ=-3FY$. $\mathrm{Fe^{2+}+2e^-\to Fe}$: $\Delta G_2^\circ=-2FX$.

    The pH and conductance of a weak acid (HX) was found to be 5 and $4\times10^{-5}$ S, respectively. The value of the limiting molar conductivity is __________ S m$^2$ mol$^{-1}$. (nearest integer)
    (Given : degree of dissociation of the weak acid $(\alpha)$ << 1)

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 6

    $[\mathrm{H^+}]=10^{-5}$ mol L$^{-1}=10^{-2}$ mol m$^{-3}$, so $\Lambda_m^\circ=\dfrac{6\times10^{-2}}{10^{-2}}=6$ S m$^2$ mol$^{-1}$.

    $\left[\text{Given : } E^\circ_{\mathrm{Ag^+/Ag}}=+0.8\ \text{V and } \dfrac{2.303RT}{F}=0.06\ \text{V}\right]$
    The $\mathrm{p}K_b$ value of the ammonium halide salt ($\mathrm{NH_4X}$) used here is __________. (nearest integer)

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 6

    For $\mathrm{Q+2H^++2e^-\rightarrow QH_2}$: $E=0.7-\dfrac{0.06}{2}\log\dfrac{1}{[\mathrm{H^+}]^2}=0.7-0.06\,\mathrm{pH}$.
    $E_{\text{cell}}=E_{\text{cathode}}-E_{\text{anode}}=0.8-(0.7-0.06\,\mathrm{pH})=0.4$, so $\mathrm{pH}=5$.
