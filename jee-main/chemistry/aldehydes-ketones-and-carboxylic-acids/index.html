  1. JEE Main 2026, 2 April, Shift 1 · Question 67
    Match List - I with List - II.

    List - I
    Mixture of Compounds    		List - II
    Reagent used to distinguish
    A. Diethyl amine + Ethyl amineI. Bromine water
    B. Acetaldehyde + AcetoneII. $\mathrm{CHCl_3 + KOH, \Delta}$
    C. Ethanol + PhenolIII. Neutral $\mathrm{FeCl_3}$
    D. Benzoic acid + Cinnamic acidIV. Ammonical silver nitrate
    Choose the correct answer from the options given below :

    1. A-IV, B-II, C-I, D-III
    2. A-IV, B-II, C-III, D-I
    3. A-II, B-IV, C-I, D-III

  2. JEE Main 2026, 2 April, Shift 2 · Question 66
    An organic compound \"$x$\" where molar ratio of C, O and H are equal, on treatment with 50% KOH under reflux followed by acidification produced \"$y$\". The most likely structure of \"$y$\" is :
    [Molar mass of '$x$' is 58 g mol$^{-1}$]

    1. $\mathrm{CH_2{=}CH{-}C({=}O){-}OH}$
    2. $\mathrm{CH_3-CH=CH-CH=O}$
    3. $\mathrm{O{=}C(OH){-}CH_2{-}OH}$
    4. $\mathrm{CH_3{-}C({=}O){-}OH}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    Empirical formula CHO (29); molar mass 58 gives C$_2$H$_2$O$_2$: glyoxal, OHC$-$CHO.
    With concentrated KOH glyoxal undergoes an intramolecular Cannizzaro reaction: one CHO is oxidised, the other reduced, giving HOCH$_2$COO$^-$K$^+$.
    Acidification gives glycolic acid, HOCH$_2$COOH.

  3. JEE Main 2026, 2 April, Shift 2 · Question 67
    A molecule (X) with following structure under mild acidic condition is hydrolysed to produce (Y) and (Z). Identify the correct statements about (Y) and (Z).
    A. Both (Y) and (Z) have same molar mass.
    B. (Y) and (Z) can be distinguished from each other by NaHCO$_3$.
    C. (Y) and (Z) react with HCN with same rates.
    D. (Y) and (Z) undergo addition reaction with 2,4-DNP.
    Choose the correct answer from the options given below :

    1. A, B and C Only
    2. B and C Only
    3. C and D Only
    4. A and D Only
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    X is CH$_3$CH=CH$-$O$-$C(CH$_3$)=CH$_2$, a divinyl-type ether. Acid hydrolysis gives two enols that tautomerise: propanal CH$_3$CH$_2$CHO and acetone CH$_3$COCH$_3$.
    A: both C$_3$H$_6$O, 58 g/mol: true.
    B: neither is acidic, so NaHCO$_3$ cannot distinguish them: false.
    C: the aldehyde adds HCN faster than the ketone: false.
    D: both carbonyl compounds react with 2,4-DNP: true.
    Answer: A and D only.

  4. JEE Main 2026, 4 April, Shift 1 · Question 72
    Consider the following sequence of reactions to give the major product (X)
    P g of the major product (X) formed is reacted with $\mathrm{NaHCO_3}$ solution to liberate a gas which occupied $11.2\ \mathrm{dm^3}$ at STP.
    $\mathrm{P}=$ ________ g.
    (Given molar mass in $\mathrm{g\,mol^{-1}}$ H:1, C:12, O:16, Cl:35.5)

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 78

    (i) Friedel–Crafts methylation gives toluene. (ii) $\mathrm{Cl_2/FeCl_3}$ chlorinates the ring; the major product is 4-chlorotoluene. (iii) $\mathrm{K_2Cr_2O_7/H_2SO_4}$ oxidises $\mathrm{-CH_3}$ to $\mathrm{-COOH}$: X = 4-chlorobenzoic acid, $\mathrm{C_7H_5ClO_2}$, $M=156.5\ \mathrm{g\,mol^{-1}}$.
    $\mathrm{ArCOOH+NaHCO_3\to ArCOONa+CO_2+H_2O}$; $11.2\ \mathrm{dm^3}$ $\mathrm{CO_2}$ at STP $=0.5$ mol, so $0.5$ mol acid.
    $\mathrm{P}=0.5\times156.5=78.25\approx78$ g.

  5. JEE Main 2026, 5 April, Shift 1 · Question 64
    "P" is a hydrocarbon of molecular formula:- $\mathrm{C_8H_{14}}$. On ozonolysis, "P" forms "Q". "Q" on treatment with alkali under reflux condition produces "R", which on treatment with $\mathrm{I_2}$/ NaOH gives a yellow precipitate. Acidification of the solution gives "S". The structure of "S" is given below:-
    The correct structure of "P" is

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    S is 2-methylcyclopent-1-ene-1-carboxylic acid, so R (which gives iodoform) is 1-acetyl-2-methylcyclopentene.
    R comes from intramolecular aldol condensation of Q $=\mathrm{CH_3CO(CH_2)_4COCH_3}$ (octane-2,7-dione): the enolate at C-3 attacks the C-7 carbonyl, closing a five-membered ring, and dehydration gives the conjugated enone.
    A diketone with both C=O in one chain comes from ozonolysis of a cyclic alkene: 1,2-dimethylcyclohexene ($\mathrm{C_8H_{14}}$) gives octane-2,7-dione.
    R $+\ \mathrm{I_2/NaOH}\to$ $\mathrm{CHI_3}$ (yellow) and the carboxylate; acidification gives S. So P is option (4).

  7. JEE Main 2026, 5 April, Shift 2 · Question 66
    Given below are two statements :
    Statement I : The condensation reaction between $\mathrm{CH_3-CH{=}O}$ and $\mathrm{H_2N-NH-C(=O)-NH_2}$ under optimum pH will produce $\mathrm{CH_3-CH{=}N-C(=O)-NH-NH_2}$
    Statement II : The molecule, $\mathrm{Ph-CH(OH)(O-CH_3)}$ will generate $\mathrm{Ph-CH{=}O}$ in the presence of dilute acid.
    In the light of the above statements, choose the correct answer from the options given below :

    1. Both Statement I and Statement II are true
    2. Both Statement I and Statement II are false
    3. Statement I is true but Statement II is false
    4. Statement I is false but Statement II is true
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    In semicarbazide only the terminal $\mathrm{NH_2}$ of the hydrazine part is nucleophilic (the amide $\mathrm{NH_2}$ is delocalised into C=O), so the product is the semicarbazone $\mathrm{CH_3-CH{=}N-NH-C(=O)-NH_2}$, not the structure given. Statement I is false.

    ‘$x$’ is the product which is obtained from benzene by reacting it with carbon monoxide and hydrogen chloride in the presence of cuprous chloride. ‘$y$’ is the major product obtained from the benzene by reacting it with ethanoyl chloride in the presence of anhydrous $\mathrm{AlCl_3}$.     Answer: (c)

    (4) already has a free CHO; (1) and (2) are ethers that give no aldehyde on hydrolysis.

    A. Methanol and Ethanol

    Given below are two statements:
    Statement I: Cross aldol condensation between two different aldehydes will always produce four different products.
    Statement II: When semicarbazide reacts with a mixture of benzaldehyde and acetophenone under optimum pH, it forms a condensation product with acetophenone only.
    In the light of the above statements, choose the correct answer from the options given below

    1. Both Statement I and Statement II are true
    2. Both Statement I and Statement II are false
    3. Statement I is true but Statement II is false
    4. Statement I is false but Statement II is true
    Show answer and solution
