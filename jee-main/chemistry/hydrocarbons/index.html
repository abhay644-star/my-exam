  1. JEE Main 2026, 2 April, Shift 1 · Question 72
    Consider the isomers of hydrocarbon with molecular formula $\mathrm{C_5H_{10}}$. These isomers do not decolourise $\mathrm{KMnO_4}$ solution. These isomers are subjected to chlorination with chlorine in presence of light to give monochloro compounds. The total number of monochloro compounds (structural isomers only) formed is ________.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 14

    Not decolourising $\mathrm{KMnO_4}$ → cycloalkanes $\mathrm{C_5H_{10}}$.
    Cyclopentane: 1; methylcyclobutane: 4 ($\mathrm{CH_2Cl}$, C1, C2, C3); ethylcyclopropane: 4 ($\mathrm{CH_3}$, $\mathrm{CH_2}$, C1, ring $\mathrm{CH_2}$); 1,1-dimethylcyclopropane: 2 ($\mathrm{CH_3}$, ring $\mathrm{CH_2}$); 1,2-dimethylcyclopropane: 3 ($\mathrm{CH_3}$, C1, C3).
    Total $= 1 + 4 + 4 + 2 + 3 = 14$.

  2. JEE Main 2026, 2 April, Shift 1 · Question 73
    One mole of an alkane (x) requires 8 mole oxygen for complete combustion. Sum of number of carbon and hydrogen atoms in the alkane (x) is ________.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 17

    $\mathrm{C_nH_{2n+2} + \dfrac{3n+1}{2}O_2 \rightarrow nCO_2 + (n+1)H_2O}$.
    $\dfrac{3n+1}{2} = 8 \Rightarrow n = 5$, alkane $\mathrm{C_5H_{12}}$.
    $5 + 12 = 17$.

  3. JEE Main 2026, 2 April, Shift 2 · Question 64
    The compound (X) on
    (i) on heating in the presence of anhydrous AlCl$_3$ and HCl gas gives 2,4-dimethyl pentane
    (ii) aromatization gives toulene and
    (iii) cyclisation gives methyl cyclohexane
    The correct name of compound (X) is :

    1. Hept-2-ene
    2. Hept-1,3,5-triene
    3. Heptane
    4. Hept-2,4,6-triene
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    Heating with anhydrous AlCl$_3$/HCl isomerises an n-alkane to a branched isomer: n-heptane gives 2,4-dimethylpentane (C$_7$H$_{16}$).
    Aromatisation (Cr$_2$O$_3$/V$_2$O$_5$/Mo$_2$O$_3$, heat) of n-heptane gives toluene; cyclisation gives methylcyclohexane.
    X is heptane.

  4. JEE Main 2026, 4 April, Shift 1 · Question 64
    An alkene (X) on ozonolysis followed by reduction gives following products.
    $\mathrm{H{-}CO{-}H}$ (2 moles), $\mathrm{H{-}CO{-}CO{-}CO{-}H}$, $\mathrm{CH_3{-}CO{-}CO{-}CO{-}CH_3}$
    The alkene (X) is:

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    Products contain $2+3+5=10$ C. Two HCHO come from two exocyclic $=\mathrm{CH_2}$ groups; the other two products come from a six-membered ring cut at two ring C=C bonds.
    $\mathrm{OHC{-}CO{-}CHO}$: a three-carbon ring segment $\mathrm{CH{=}}$, $\mathrm{C(=CH_2)}$, $\mathrm{{=}CH}$.
    $\mathrm{CH_3CO{-}CO{-}COCH_3}$: a three-carbon segment $\mathrm{C(CH_3){=}}$, $\mathrm{C(=CH_2)}$, $\mathrm{{=}C(CH_3)}$.
    So X is a cyclohexadiene with two ring C=C bonds, two exocyclic $=\mathrm{CH_2}$ groups and two methyl groups, one on each side of the same exocyclic carbon; this is the structure in option (D).

  5. JEE Main 2026, 4 April, Shift 2 · Question 64
    Compound (X) is subjected to the sequence of reactions as shown above. Molar mass of the major product (Y) formed is _______________ $\mathrm{g\,mol^{-1}}$.
    (Given molar mass in $\mathrm{g\,mol^{-1}}$ C:12, H: 1, O: 16)

    1. $90$
    2. $118$
    3. $160$
    4. $125$
    Show answer and solution

    (i) $\mathrm{Br_2/CHCl_3}$ adds to styrene: $\mathrm{C_6H_5CHBrCH_2Br}$.

  6. JEE Main 2026, 5 April, Shift 1 · Question 65
    For the following Friedel Craft's alkylation reaction, which of the statements are correct?
    A. Major product is n-propyl benzene.
    B. iso-propyl carbocation intermediate is also generated.
    C. Multiple substitution is inevitable.
    D. Introducing electron-donating substituent on benzene will not produce any alkyl benzene.
    Choose the correct answer from the options given below:

    1. A and D only
    2. B and C only
    3. A and C only
    4. B and D only
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    With $\mathrm{AlCl_3}$, 1-chloropropane gives a primary carbocation (complex) that rearranges by hydride shift to the more stable isopropyl carbocation, so B is true and the major product is isopropylbenzene (cumene), so A is false.
    The alkyl group activates the ring, so further alkylation (polysubstitution) occurs: C is true.
    Electron-donating groups activate the ring and still give alkylbenzenes: D is false.
    Correct: B and C.

  7. JEE Main 2026, 5 April, Shift 2 · Question 63
    IUPAC name of the some alkenes are given below.
    Find out the correct stability order.
    A. 2-Methylbut-2-ene
    B. cis-But-2-ene
    C. 2, 3-Dimethylbut-2-ene
    D. Prop-1-ene
    Choose the correct answer from the options given below :

    1. C > A > B > D
    2. C > A > D > B
    3. B > D > A > C
    4. A > B > C > D
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    Alkene stability increases with the number of alkyl groups on the C=C (hyperconjugation).
    C tetrasubstituted, A trisubstituted, B disubstituted, D monosubstituted.
    Order: C > A > B > D.

    Given below are two statements:
    Statement I: Methane can be prepared by decarboxylation of sodium ethanoate, Kolbe's electrolysis of sodium acetate and reaction of $\mathrm{CH_3MgBr}$ with water.
    Statement II: Methane cannot be prepared from unsaturated hydrocarbons and by Wurtz reaction.
    In the light of the above statements, choose the correct answer from the options given below

    1. Both Statement I and Statement II are true
    2. Both Statement I and Statement II are false
    3. Statement I is true but Statement II is false
    4. Statement I is false but Statement II is true
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    Statement I: decarboxylation of $\mathrm{CH_3COONa}$ (soda lime) and $\mathrm{CH_3MgBr}+\mathrm{H_2O}$ give methane, but Kolbe's electrolysis of sodium acetate gives ethane: false.
    Statement II: hydrogenation of alkenes/alkynes gives at least ethane, and Wurtz coupling gives at least ethane: true.
    Statement I is false but Statement II is true.

  9. JEE Main 2026, 6 April, Shift 2 · Question 65
    An optically active alkyl bromide $\mathrm{C_4H_9Br}$, reacts with ethanolic KOH to form major compound [A] which reacts with bromine to give compound [B]. Compound [B] reacts with ethanolic KOH and sodamide to give compound [C]. One molecule of water adds to compound [C] on warming with mercuric sulphate and dilute sulphuric acid at 333 K to form compound [D]. The functional group in compound D will be confirmed by :

    1. Haloform test
    2. Lucas test
    3. Silver mirror test
    4. Benedict test
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    The optically active $\mathrm{C_4H_9Br}$ is 2-bromobutane.
    Ethanolic KOH → but-2-ene [A] (Saytzeff); $\mathrm{Br_2}$ → 2,3-dibromobutane [B].
    Ethanolic KOH then $\mathrm{NaNH_2}$ → but-2-yne [C].
    $\mathrm{Hg^{2+}/H_2SO_4}$ hydration → butan-2-one [D], $\mathrm{CH_3COCH_2CH_3}$.
    A methyl ketone is confirmed by the haloform test.

  10. JEE Main 2026, 6 April, Shift 2 · Question 72
    An alkane (Y) requires 8 moles of oxygen for complete combustion and on chlorination with $\mathrm{Cl_2}/h\nu$, (Y) gives only one monochlorinated product (Z). The total number of primary carbon atoms in (Y) is __________.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 4

    $\mathrm{C_nH_{2n+2}+\dfrac{3n+1}{2}O_2}$: $\dfrac{3n+1}{2}=8 \Rightarrow n=5$.
    The $\mathrm{C_5H_{12}}$ isomer giving only one monochloro product is neopentane, $\mathrm{C(CH_3)_4}$.
    It has 4 primary carbon atoms.

  11. JEE Main 2026, 8 April, Shift 2 · Question 63
    The major product of which of the following reaction is not obtained by rearrangement reaction ?

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    (1) The 1° propyl cation from 1-chloropropane/$\mathrm{AlCl_3}$ rearranges (hydride shift) to the 2° isopropyl cation, giving cumene: rearrangement.
    (2) tert-Butyl alcohol gives the 3° tert-butyl cation directly, which loses $\mathrm{H^+}$ to give 2-methylpropene: no rearrangement.

  12. JEE Main 2026, 8 April, Shift 2 · Question 64
    The total number of aromatic compounds/species from the following is

    1. $6$
    2. $4$
    3. $3$
    4. $5$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    p-Benzoquinone: the ring has no cyclic $(4n+2)\pi$ system (two C=O groups take the conjugation out of the ring): non-aromatic.
    Tropylium cation: planar, $6\pi$ electrons: aromatic.
    Phenanthrene: $14\pi$ electrons in a fully conjugated planar system: aromatic.
    Cyclobutadienyl dication: $2\pi$ electrons ($n=0$): aromatic.
    1,2-Dihydronaphthalene: the whole ring system is not continuously conjugated (two $\mathrm{sp^3}$ $\mathrm{CH_2}$ groups), and the cyclohexadienyl anion also has an $\mathrm{sp^3}$ $\mathrm{CH_2}$ in the ring: neither is counted as aromatic as a species.
    Total $=3$ (as per the official key).

  15. JEE Main 2026, 22 January, Shift 1 · Question 74
    The cycloalkene (X) on bromination consumes one mole of bromine per mole of (X) and gives the product (Y) in which C:Br ratio is 3:1. The percentage of bromine in the product (Y) is ___________%. (Nearest integer)
    (Given : molar mass in g mol$^{-1}$ H : 1, C : 12, O : 16, Br : 80)

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 66

    A cycloalkene with one double bond is $\mathrm{C_nH_{2n-2}}$; adding one $\mathrm{Br_2}$ gives $\mathrm{C_nH_{2n-2}Br_2}$.
    C:Br $=3:1$ gives $n=6$: Y is $\mathrm{C_6H_{10}Br_2}$, $M=72+10+160=242$.
    %Br $=\dfrac{160}{242}\times100\approx66.1$, i.e. $66$.

    2. 2-methylhex-3-yne
    3. 2-methylhex-2-yne

  17. JEE Main 2026, 22 January, Shift 2 · Question 66
    The dibromo compound [P] (molecular formula : $\mathrm{C_9H_{10}Br_2}$) when heated with excess sodamide followed by treatment with dilute HCl gives [Q]. On warming [Q] with mercuric sulphate and dilute sulphuric acid yield [R] which gives positive Iodoform test but negative Tollen's test. The compound [P] is :

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    Excess sodamide removes two HBr from a gem- or vic-dibromide (with H on the right carbons) to give an alkyne (as its sodium salt for a terminal alkyne; dilute HCl frees it).
    For the second structure, $p\text{-}\mathrm{CH_3C_6H_4CBr_2CH_3}$ (formula $\mathrm{C_9H_{10}Br_2}$), Q is $p\text{-}\mathrm{CH_3C_6H_4C\equiv CH}$.
    $\mathrm{HgSO_4}$/dilute $\mathrm{H_2SO_4}$ hydrates it (Markovnikov) to $p\text{-}\mathrm{CH_3C_6H_4COCH_3}$, a methyl ketone: iodoform positive, Tollens' negative.
    In the first structure the carbon carrying Br and $\mathrm{CH_3}$ has no H, so an alkyne cannot form; the third (two separate $\mathrm{CH_2Br}$ groups) cannot give an alkyne either. Only the second structure fits all the observations.

  18. JEE Main 2026, 23 January, Shift 1 · Question 64
    Consider the following compounds
    Arrange these compounds in the increasing order of reactivity with nitrating mixture.

    1. c $<$ b $<$ a
    2. b $<$ c $<$ a
    3. c $<$ a $<$ b
    4. b $<$ a $<$ c
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

  21. JEE Main 2026, 24 January, Shift 1 · Question 64
    Arrange the following alkenes in decreasing order of stability.
    Choose the correct answer from the options given below:

    1. III > II > I > IV
    2. III > I > II > IV
    3. I > III > II > IV
    4. I > III > IV > II
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

  22. JEE Main 2026, 24 January, Shift 2 · Question 63
    Given below are two statements:
    Statement I: There are several conformers for n$-$butane. Out of those conformers, (X), shown below, is the least stable and most stable conformer is (Y), shown below.
    Statement II: As the dihedral angle increases, torsional strain decreases from (X) to (Y).
    In the light of the above statements, choose the correct answer from the options given below

    1. Both Statement I and Statement II are true
    2. Both Statement I and Statement II are false
    3. Statement I is true but Statement II is false
    4. Statement I is false but Statement II is true
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    (X) is the fully eclipsed form (the two $\mathrm{CH_3}$ groups eclipsed, dihedral angle $0^\circ$), the least stable; (Y) is the anti form ($180^\circ$), the most stable: Statement I is true.
    Going from the eclipsed $0^\circ$ form to the $180^\circ$ anti form, torsional strain falls: Statement II is true.

  23. JEE Main 2026, 28 January, Shift 1 · Question 64
    $\mathrm{Ph-CH=CH_2\xrightarrow[\text{HBr}]{(\text{PhCOO})_2}}$ Product
    Consider the above reaction
    A. The reaction proceeds through a more stable radical intermediate.
    B. The role of peroxide is to generate $\mathrm{H^\bullet}$ (Hydrogen radical).
    C. During this reaction, benzene is formed as a byproduct.
    D. 1$-$Bromo$-$2$-$phenylethane is formed as the minor product.
    E. The same reaction in absence of peroxide proceeds via carbocation intermediate.
    Identify the correct statements. Choose the correct answer from the options given below:

    1. A & E Only
    2. A, B & D Only
    3. A, C & E Only
    4. C, D & E Only
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    Peroxide effect: $(\mathrm{PhCOO})_2\to2\,\mathrm{PhCOO^\bullet}\to2\,\mathrm{Ph^\bullet}+2\,\mathrm{CO_2}$; $\mathrm{Ph^\bullet}+\mathrm{HBr}\to\mathrm{C_6H_6}+\mathrm{Br^\bullet}$. So benzene is a by-product (C true) and the peroxide generates $\mathrm{Br^\bullet}$, not $\mathrm{H^\bullet}$ (B false).
    $\mathrm{Br^\bullet}$ adds to give the more stable benzylic radical (A true), so 1-bromo-2-phenylethane is the major product (D false).
    Without peroxide, electrophilic addition goes through a carbocation (E true). Answer: A, C and E.
