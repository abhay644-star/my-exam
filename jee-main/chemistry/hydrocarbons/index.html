  1. JEE Main 2026, 2 April, Shift 1 · Question 72
    Consider the isomers of hydrocarbon with molecular formula $\mathrm{C_5H_{10}}$. These isomers do not decolourise $\mathrm{KMnO_4}$ solution. These isomers are subjected to chlorination with chlorine in presence of light to give monochloro compounds. The total number of monochloro compounds (structural isomers only) formed is ________.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 14

    Not decolourising $\mathrm{KMnO_4}$ → cycloalkanes $\mathrm{C_5H_{10}}$.
    Cyclopentane: 1; methylcyclobutane: 4 ($\mathrm{CH_2Cl}$, C1, C2, C3); ethylcyclopropane: 4 ($\mathrm{CH_3}$, $\mathrm{CH_2}$, C1, ring $\mathrm{CH_2}$); 1,1-dimethylcyclopropane: 2 ($\mathrm{CH_3}$, ring $\mathrm{CH_2}$); 1,2-dimethylcyclopropane: 3 ($\mathrm{CH_3}$, C1, C3).
    Total $= 1 + 4 + 4 + 2 + 3 = 14$.

    One mole of an alkane (x) requires 8 mole oxygen for complete combustion. Sum of number of carbon and hydrogen atoms in the alkane (x) is ________.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 17

    $\mathrm{C_nH_{2n+2} + \dfrac{3n+1}{2}O_2 \rightarrow nCO_2 + (n+1)H_2O}$.

  4. JEE Main 2026, 4 April, Shift 1 · Question 64
    An alkene (X) on ozonolysis followed by reduction gives following products.
    $\mathrm{H{-}CO{-}H}$ (2 moles), $\mathrm{H{-}CO{-}CO{-}CO{-}H}$, $\mathrm{CH_3{-}CO{-}CO{-}CO{-}CH_3}$
    The alkene (X) is:

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    Products contain $2+3+5=10$ C. Two HCHO come from two exocyclic $=\mathrm{CH_2}$ groups; the other two products come from a six-membered ring cut at two ring C=C bonds.

    So X is a cyclohexadiene with two ring C=C bonds, two exocyclic $=\mathrm{CH_2}$ groups and two methyl groups, one on each side of the same exocyclic carbon; this is the structure in option (D).

    For the following Friedel Craft's alkylation reaction, which of the statements are correct?
    A. Major product is n-propyl benzene.
    B. iso-propyl carbocation intermediate is also generated.
    C. Multiple substitution is inevitable.
    D. Introducing electron-donating substituent on benzene will not produce any alkyl benzene.

    2. B and C only
    3. A and C only
    4. B and D only

    2. Q
