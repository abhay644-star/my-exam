  1. JEE Main 2026, 2 April, Shift 1 · Question 60
    Given below are two statements :
    Statement (I) : Oxidising power of halogens decreases in the order $\mathrm{F_2 > Cl_2 > Br_2 > I_2}$, which is the basis of “Layer test”.
    Statement (II) : “Layer test” to identify $\mathrm{Br_2}$ and $\mathrm{I_2}$ in aqueous solution involves the oxidation of bromide or iodide into $\mathrm{Br_2}$ or $\mathrm{I_2}$ respectively with $\mathrm{Cl_2}$, which is a type of displacement redox reaction.
    In the light of the above statements, choose the correct answer from the options given below :

    1. Both Statement I and Statement II are true
    2. Both Statement I and Statement II are false
    3. Statement I is true but Statement II is false
    4. Statement I is false but Statement II is true
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    Oxidising power: $\mathrm{F_2 > Cl_2 > Br_2 > I_2}$, so a halogen displaces the halide ions below it.
    In the layer test $\mathrm{Cl_2}$ water oxidises $\mathrm{Br^-}$/$\mathrm{I^-}$ to $\mathrm{Br_2}$/$\mathrm{I_2}$, which colour the organic layer (orange/violet) — a displacement redox reaction.
    Both statements are true.

  2. JEE Main 2026, 4 April, Shift 1 · Question 56
    In order to oxidise a mixture of $1$ mole each of $\mathrm{FeC_2O_4}$, $\mathrm{Fe_2(C_2O_4)_3}$, $\mathrm{FeSO_4}$ and $\mathrm{Fe_2(SO_4)_3}$ in acidic medium, the number of moles of $\mathrm{KMnO_4}$ required is

    1. $3$
    2. $2$
    3. $5$
    4. $7$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    Electrons released: $\mathrm{Fe^{2+}\to Fe^{3+}}$ gives $1$; each $\mathrm{C_2O_4^{2-}\to 2CO_2}$ gives $2$.
    $\mathrm{FeC_2O_4}$: $1+2=3$; $\mathrm{Fe_2(C_2O_4)_3}$: $3\times2=6$; $\mathrm{FeSO_4}$: $1$; $\mathrm{Fe_2(SO_4)_3}$: $0$. Total $10$ mol electrons.
    $\mathrm{MnO_4^-\to Mn^{2+}}$ takes $5$ electrons, so $\dfrac{10}{5}=2$ mol $\mathrm{KMnO_4}$.

  3. JEE Main 2026, 6 April, Shift 2 · Question 73
    500 mL of 0.2 M $\mathrm{MnO_4^-}$ solution in basic medium when mixed with 500 mL of 1.5 M KI solution, oxidises iodide ions to liberate molecular iodine. This liberated iodine is then titrated with a standard $x$ M thiosulphate solution in presence of starch till the end point. If 300 mL of thiosulphate was consumed, then the value of $x$ is __________.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 1

    In basic medium $\mathrm{MnO_4^-\to MnO_2}$ (3 electrons): $\mathrm{2MnO_4^-+6I^-+4H_2O\to2MnO_2+3I_2+8OH^-}$.
    $\mathrm{MnO_4^-}=0.1$ mol (iodide, 0.75 mol, is in excess), so $\mathrm{I_2}=0.15$ mol.
    $\mathrm{I_2+2S_2O_3^{2-}\to2I^-+S_4O_6^{2-}}$: thiosulphate $=0.3$ mol.
    $x=\dfrac{0.3}{0.3}=1$.

  4. JEE Main 2026, 23 January, Shift 2 · Question 73
    200 cc of $x\times10^{-3}$ M potassium dichromate is required to oxidise 750 cc of 0.6 M Mohr's salt solution in acidic medium.
    Here $x=$ ______.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 375

    $\mathrm{Cr_2O_7^{2-}}$ takes 6 electrons and each $\mathrm{Fe^{2+}}$ gives 1, so mol $\mathrm{Fe^{2+}}=6\times$ mol dichromate.
    $750\times0.6=6\times200\times x\times10^{-3}$ (in mmol): $450=1.2x$, so $x=375$.

  5. JEE Main 2026, 24 January, Shift 1 · Question 71
    X and Y are the number of electrons involved, respectively during the oxidation of $\mathrm{I^-}$ to $\mathrm{I_2}$ and $\mathrm{S^{2-}}$ to S by acidified $\mathrm{K_2Cr_2O_7}$. The value of X + Y is ____.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 12

    Each $\mathrm{Cr_2O_7^{2-}}$ takes 6 electrons ($\mathrm{2Cr^{6+}\to2Cr^{3+}}$), so the balanced reactions transfer 6 electrons each:
    $\mathrm{Cr_2O_7^{2-}+6I^-+14H^+\rightarrow2Cr^{3+}+3I_2+7H_2O}$ (X = 6)
    $\mathrm{Cr_2O_7^{2-}+3S^{2-}+14H^+\rightarrow2Cr^{3+}+3S+7H_2O}$ (Y = 6)
    X + Y = 12.

    Note: The official final key gives 12, counting the electrons transferred in each balanced reaction with one dichromate ion (6 + 6). Counting per $\mathrm{I_2}$ and per S atom formed would give 2 + 2 = 4. The key is followed.

  6. JEE Main 2026, 24 January, Shift 2 · Question 51
    One mole of $\mathrm{Cl_2(g)}$ was passed into 2 L of cold 2M KOH solution. After the reaction, the concentrations of $\mathrm{Cl^-}$, $\mathrm{ClO^-}$ and $\mathrm{OH^-}$ are respectively (assume volume remains constant)

    1. 1M, 1M, 1M
    2. 0.5M, 0.5M, 1M
    3. 0.5M, 0.5M, 0.5M
    4. 0.75M, 0.75M, 1M
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    Cold dilute alkali: $\mathrm{Cl_2+2OH^-\rightarrow Cl^-+ClO^-+H_2O}$ (disproportionation).
    KOH present: 4 mol; 1 mol $\mathrm{Cl_2}$ uses 2 mol, leaving 2 mol $\mathrm{OH^-}$ and forming 1 mol each of $\mathrm{Cl^-}$ and $\mathrm{ClO^-}$.
    In 2 L: 0.5 M, 0.5 M, 1 M.

  7. JEE Main 2026, 28 January, Shift 1 · Question 73
    500 mL of 1.2 M KI solution is mixed with 500 mL of 0.2 M $\mathrm{KMnO_4}$ solution in basic medium. The liberated iodine was titrated with standard 0.1 M $\mathrm{Na_2S_2O_3}$ solution in the presence of starch indicator till the blue color disappeared. The volume (in L) of $\mathrm{Na_2S_2O_3}$ consumed is _____. (Nearest integer)

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 3

    In basic medium $\mathrm{MnO_4^-}$ is reduced to $\mathrm{MnO_2}$ (3 electrons each). $\mathrm{KMnO_4}=0.1$ mol accepts 0.3 mol of electrons; $\mathrm{KI}=0.6$ mol is in excess.
    Iodine liberated $=0.15$ mol $\mathrm{I_2}$, which needs $2\times0.15=0.3$ mol $\mathrm{Na_2S_2O_3}$ ($\mathrm{I_2+2S_2O_3^{2-}\to2I^-+S_4O_6^{2-}}$).
    Volume $=\dfrac{0.3}{0.1}=3$ L.
