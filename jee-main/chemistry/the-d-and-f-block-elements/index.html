  1. JEE Main 2026, 2 April, Shift 1 · Question 61
    Which of the following sets includes all the species that will change the orange colour of $\mathrm{K_2Cr_2O_7}$ in acidic medium ?

    1. $\mathrm{Fe^{2+}, Sn^{2+}, I^-, S^{2-}}$
    2. $\mathrm{S^{2-}, Fe^{3+}, I^-, C_2O_4{}^{2-}}$
    3. $\mathrm{Fe^{2+}, NO_2^-, SO_2, Sn^{4+}}$
    4. $\mathrm{Fe^{3+}, SO_4^{2-}, S^{2-}, Sn^{4+}}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    Acidified dichromate (orange) turns green ($\mathrm{Cr^{3+}}$) only with reducing agents.
    $\mathrm{Fe^{2+}, Sn^{2+}, I^-, S^{2-}}$ are all reducing.
    $\mathrm{Fe^{3+}}$, $\mathrm{Sn^{4+}}$ and $\mathrm{SO_4^{2-}}$ are in their higher oxidation states and cannot reduce it, so every other set contains a non-reacting species.

  2. JEE Main 2026, 2 April, Shift 2 · Question 60
    Given below are two statements :
    Statement I : Among Zn, Mn, Sc and Cu, the energy required to remove the third valence electron is highest for Zn and lowest for Sc.
    Statement II : The correct order of the following complexes in terms of CFSE is $[\mathrm{Co(H_2O)_6}]^{2+}$ < $[\mathrm{Co(H_2O)_6}]^{3+}$ < $[\mathrm{Co(en)_3}]^{3+}$.
    In the light of the above statements, choose the correct answer from the options given below :

    1. Both Statement I and Statement II are true
    2. Both Statement I and Statement II are false
    3. Statement I is true but Statement II is false
    4. Statement I is false but Statement II is true
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    Third ionization enthalpies: Zn (from stable 3d$^{10}$) is highest, about 3833 kJ/mol; Sc (giving the noble-gas core Sc$^{3+}$) is lowest, about 2389 kJ/mol. Statement I is true.
    CFSE rises with the oxidation state of the metal (Co$^{3+}$ > Co$^{2+}$) and with ligand strength (en > H$_2$O), so the given order is correct. Statement II is true.
    Both statements are true.

  3. JEE Main 2026, 2 April, Shift 2 · Question 70
    Among Fe$^{2+}$, Fe$^{3+}$, Cr$^{2+}$ and Zn$^{2+}$, the ion that shows positive borax bead test and with highest ionisation enthalpy is :

    1. Fe$^{2+}$
    2. Zn$^{2+}$
    3. Cr$^{2+}$
    4. Fe$^{3+}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    Borax bead test needs coloured metaborates: Fe$^{2+}$, Fe$^{3+}$ and Cr$^{2+}$ respond; Zn$^{2+}$ ($d^{10}$, colourless) does not.
    Removing an electron from Fe$^{3+}$ is the fourth ionisation enthalpy of Fe (about 5290 kJ/mol), far above the third ionisation enthalpies of Fe (about 2957) and Cr (about 2987) needed for Fe$^{2+}$ and Cr$^{2+}$.
    Answer: Fe$^{3+}$.

  4. JEE Main 2026, 2 April, Shift 2 · Question 74
    Number of paramagnetic ions among the following d- and f-block metal ions is __________.
    Mn$^{2+}$, Cu$^{2+}$, Zn$^{2+}$, Yb$^{2+}$, Sc$^{3+}$, La$^{3+}$, Gd$^{3+}$, Lu$^{3+}$, Ti$^{4+}$, Ce$^{4+}$
    (Atomic number of Mn $=25$, Cu $=29$, Zn $=30$, Yb $=70$, Sc $=21$, La $=57$, Gd $=64$, Lu $=71$, Ti $=22$, Ce $=58$)

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 3

    Mn$^{2+}$ $3d^5$ and Cu$^{2+}$ $3d^9$: unpaired electrons. Gd$^{3+}$ $4f^7$: unpaired electrons.
    Zn$^{2+}$ $3d^{10}$, Yb$^{2+}$ $4f^{14}$, Lu$^{3+}$ $4f^{14}$, Sc$^{3+}$, La$^{3+}$, Ti$^{4+}$, Ce$^{4+}$ (noble-gas cores): diamagnetic.
    Paramagnetic ions: 3.

  5. JEE Main 2026, 4 April, Shift 1 · Question 60
    The correct statements among the following are,
    A. Mo(VI) and W(VI) are less stable than Cr(VI).
    B. $\mathrm{Ce^{4+}}$ and $\mathrm{Tb^{4+}}$ are oxidant while $\mathrm{Eu^{2+}}$ and $\mathrm{Yb^{2+}}$ are reductant.
    C. Cm and Am have seven unpaired electrons.
    D. Actinoid contraction is greater from element to element than lanthanoid contraction.
    Choose the correct answer from the options given below:

    1. A and B Only
    2. C and D Only
    3. B and D Only
    4. A and C Only
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    A: false. Higher oxidation states become more stable down a group, so Mo(VI) and W(VI) are more stable than Cr(VI).
    B: true. $\mathrm{Ce^{4+}}$, $\mathrm{Tb^{4+}}$ tend to return to $+3$ (oxidants); $\mathrm{Eu^{2+}}$, $\mathrm{Yb^{2+}}$ tend to go to $+3$ (reductants).
    C: false. Am ($5f^7 7s^2$) has $7$ unpaired electrons but Cm ($5f^7 6d^1 7s^2$) has $8$.
    D: true. Poor shielding by $5f$ electrons makes the actinoid contraction greater from element to element.
    B and D only.

  6. JEE Main 2026, 4 April, Shift 1 · Question 61
    Correct statements from the following are
    A. Potassium dichromate is an oxidising agent and it oxidises $\mathrm{FeSO_4}$ to $\mathrm{Fe_2(SO_4)_3}$ in acidic medium.
    B. Sodium dichromate can be used as primary standard in volumetric estimation.
    C. $\mathrm{CrO_4^{2-}}$ and $\mathrm{Cr_2O_7^{2-}}$ are interconvertible in aqueous solution by varying the pH of the solution.
    D. Cr-O-Cr bond angle in $\mathrm{Cr_2O_7^{2-}}$ is $126^\circ$.
    Choose the correct answer from the options given below:

    1. A, B and C Only
    2. A, C and D Only
    3. A and C Only
    4. B and D Only
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    A: true. $\mathrm{Cr_2O_7^{2-}+14H^++6Fe^{2+}\to2Cr^{3+}+6Fe^{3+}+7H_2O}$.
    B: false. Sodium dichromate is hygroscopic; potassium dichromate is the primary standard.
    C: true. $\mathrm{2CrO_4^{2-}+2H^+\rightleftharpoons Cr_2O_7^{2-}+H_2O}$ shifts with pH.
    D: true. The Cr–O–Cr angle in dichromate is $126^\circ$.
    A, C and D only.

  7. JEE Main 2026, 4 April, Shift 2 · Question 59
    Pairs of elements with the same number of electrons in their respective 4f orbital are
    [Atomic number. Eu-63, Gd-64, Dy-66, Ho-67, Tm-69, Yb-70, Lu-71, Hf-72]
    A. (Eu and Gd)
    B. (Dy and Ho)
    C. (Yb and Hf)
    D. (Lu and Tm)
    Choose the correct answer from the options given below:

    1. B and C Only
    2. A and B Only
    3. A and D Only
    4. A and C Only
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    Eu $[\mathrm{Xe}]4f^76s^2$ and Gd $[\mathrm{Xe}]4f^75d^16s^2$: both $4f^7$ (A).
    Dy $4f^{10}$, Ho $4f^{11}$: different (B).
    Yb $4f^{14}6s^2$ and Hf $4f^{14}5d^26s^2$: both $4f^{14}$ (C).
    Lu $4f^{14}5d^1$, Tm $4f^{13}$: different (D).
    A and C only.

  8. JEE Main 2026, 4 April, Shift 2 · Question 62
    Consider $|x|$ is the difference in oxidation states of Mn in highest manganese fluoride and highest manganese oxide. The ions with $|x|$ number of unpaired electrons from the following are:
    A. $\mathrm{Sc^{3+}}$
    B. $\mathrm{Zn^{2+}}$
    C. $\mathrm{V^{2+}}$
    D. $\mathrm{Fe^{2+}}$
    E. $\mathrm{Co^{2+}}$
    Choose the correct answer from the options given below:

    1. A and B Only
    2. C, D and E Only
    3. C and E Only
    4. B and E Only
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    Highest fluoride $\mathrm{MnF_4}$ (+4), highest oxide $\mathrm{Mn_2O_7}$ (+7): $|x|=3$.
    Unpaired electrons: $\mathrm{Sc^{3+}}$ $d^0$: 0; $\mathrm{Zn^{2+}}$ $d^{10}$: 0; $\mathrm{V^{2+}}$ $d^3$: 3; $\mathrm{Fe^{2+}}$ $d^6$: 4; $\mathrm{Co^{2+}}$ $d^7$: 3.
    C and E only.

  9. JEE Main 2026, 4 April, Shift 2 · Question 69
    Among $\mathrm{Fe^{3+}}$, $\mathrm{Pb^{2+}}$, $\mathrm{Cu^{2+}}$ and $\mathrm{Mn^{2+}}$, identify the one that gets precipitated out while passing $\mathrm{H_2S}$ in presence of $\mathrm{NH_4OH}$ as group reagent. The highest possible oxidation state of the corresponding metal is

    1. $+3$
    2. $+4$
    3. $+2$
    4. $+7$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    $\mathrm{H_2S}$ in the presence of $\mathrm{NH_4OH}$ is the group IV reagent: it precipitates $\mathrm{Mn^{2+}}$ as MnS ($\mathrm{Pb^{2+}}$, $\mathrm{Cu^{2+}}$ are removed earlier with $\mathrm{H_2S}$ in acid; $\mathrm{Fe^{3+}}$ is group III).
    Highest oxidation state of Mn is $+7$ (as in $\mathrm{KMnO_4}$, $\mathrm{Mn_2O_7}$).

  10. JEE Main 2026, 5 April, Shift 1 · Question 60
    Which of the following is NOT a physical or chemical characteristics of interstitial compounds?

    1. They have high melting points, higher than those of pure metals.
    2. They are very soft and ionic in nature.
    3. They retain metallic conductivity.
    4. They are chemically inert and usually non-stoichiometric.
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    Interstitial compounds (small atoms such as H, C, N trapped in metal lattices) have high melting points, are very hard, retain metallic conductivity and are chemically inert and non-stoichiometric.
    They are not soft and not ionic, so option (2) is NOT a characteristic.

  11. JEE Main 2026, 5 April, Shift 2 · Question 60
    Given below are two statements :
    Statement I : Presence of large number of unpaired electrons in transition metal atoms results in higher enthalpies of their atomisation.
    Statement II : $d_{xy}=d_{xz}=d_{yz}<d_{x^2-y^2}=d_{z^2}$ and $d_{x^2-y^2}=d_{z^2}<d_{xy}=d_{xz}=d_{yz}$ are the d-orbital splittings in $[\mathrm{Fe(H_2O)_6}]^{3+}$ and $[\mathrm{Ni(Cl)_4}]^{2-}$ complex ions respectively.
    In the light of the above statements, choose the correct answer from the options given below :

    1. Both Statement I and Statement II are correct
    2. Both Statement I and Statement II are incorrect
    3. Statement I is correct but Statement II is incorrect
    4. Statement I is incorrect but Statement II is correct
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    More unpaired d electrons give stronger metallic bonding, so higher enthalpy of atomisation (the maxima near the middle of each series). Statement I is correct.
    $[\mathrm{Fe(H_2O)_6}]^{3+}$ is octahedral: $t_{2g}$ ($d_{xy}, d_{xz}, d_{yz}$) lower than $e_g$ ($d_{x^2-y^2}, d_{z^2}$).
    $[\mathrm{NiCl_4}]^{2-}$ is tetrahedral: $e$ ($d_{x^2-y^2}, d_{z^2}$) lower than $t_2$. Statement II is correct.

  12. JEE Main 2026, 5 April, Shift 2 · Question 70
    A paper dipped in a dil. $\mathrm{H_2SO_4}$ solution of 'X' upon treatment with $\mathrm{SO_2}$ gas turns into green. The compound 'X' is :

    1. KI-starch
    2. $\mathrm{KMnO_4}$
    3. $\mathrm{Pb(CH_3COO)_2}$
    4. $\mathrm{K_2Cr_2O_7}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    $\mathrm{SO_2}$ reduces acidified dichromate (orange) to $\mathrm{Cr^{3+}}$ (green):
    $\mathrm{Cr_2O_7^{2-} + 3SO_2 + 2H^+ \rightarrow 2Cr^{3+} + 3SO_4^{2-} + H_2O}$.
    X is $\mathrm{K_2Cr_2O_7}$.

    A. Basic vanadium oxide is used in the manufacture of $\mathrm{H_2SO_4}$.

  14. JEE Main 2026, 6 April, Shift 2 · Question 60
    The correct order of first $(\Delta_iH_1)$ and second $(\Delta_iH_2)$ ionisation enthalpy values of Cr and Mn are :
    A. $\Delta_iH_1$ : Cr $>$ Mn
    B. $\Delta_iH_2$ : Cr $>$ Mn
    C. $\Delta_iH_1$ : Mn $>$ Cr
    D. $\Delta_iH_2$ : Mn $>$ Cr
    Choose the correct answer from the options given below :

    1. A and B only
    2. B and C only
    3. A and D only
    4. C and D only
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    First ionisation: Mn ($3d^54s^2$, 717 kJ mol$^{-1}$) $>$ Cr ($3d^54s^1$, 653 kJ mol$^{-1}$), so C is correct.
    Second ionisation: $\mathrm{Cr^+}$ is $3d^5$ (stable half-filled), so Cr (1592) $>$ Mn (1509), so B is correct.
    Answer: B and C only.

  15. JEE Main 2026, 8 April, Shift 2 · Question 60
    Given below are two statements :
    Statement I : The number of pairs among $[\mathrm{Ti^{4+},V^{2+}}]$, $[\mathrm{V^{2+},Mn^{2+}}]$, $[\mathrm{Mn^{2+},Fe^{3+}}]$ and $[\mathrm{V^{2+},Cr^{2+}}]$ in which both ions are coloured is 3.
    Statement II : The number of pairs among $[\mathrm{La^{3+},Yb^{2+}}]$, $[\mathrm{Lu^{3+},Ce^{4+}}]$ and $[\mathrm{Ac^{3+},Lr^{3+}}]$ ions in which both are diamagnetic is 3.
    In the light of the above statements, choose the correct from the options given below :

    1. Both Statement I and Statement II are correct
    2. Both Statement I and Statement II are incorrect
    3. Statement I is correct but Statement II is incorrect
    4. Statement I is incorrect but Statement II is correct
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    Statement I: $\mathrm{Ti^{4+}}$ ($3d^0$) is colourless; $\mathrm{V^{2+}}$ ($3d^3$), $\mathrm{Mn^{2+}}$ ($3d^5$), $\mathrm{Fe^{3+}}$ ($3d^5$), $\mathrm{Cr^{2+}}$ ($3d^4$) are coloured. Pairs with both coloured: 3. Correct.
    Statement II: $\mathrm{La^{3+}}$ ($4f^0$), $\mathrm{Yb^{2+}}$ ($4f^{14}$), $\mathrm{Lu^{3+}}$ ($4f^{14}$), $\mathrm{Ce^{4+}}$ ($4f^0$), $\mathrm{Ac^{3+}}$ ($5f^0$), $\mathrm{Lr^{3+}}$ ($5f^{14}$) are all diamagnetic. Pairs: 3. Correct.
    Both statements are correct.

  16. JEE Main 2026, 8 April, Shift 2 · Question 61
    Given below are two statements for catalytic properties of transition metals.
    Statement I : First row transition metals which act as catalyst utilise their 3d electrons only for formation of bonds between reactant molecules and atoms on the surface of catalyst.
    Statement II : There is increase in the concentration of reactants on the surface of catalyst which strengthens the bonds in reacting molecules.
    In the light of the above statements, choose the correct answer from the options given below :

    1. Both Statement I and Statement II are correct
    2. Both Statement I and Statement II are incorrect
    3. Statement I is correct but Statement II is incorrect
    4. Statement I is incorrect but Statement II is correct
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    First row transition metals use both their 3d and 4s electrons to form bonds with the reactant molecules at the surface, so Statement I (3d only) is incorrect.
    This bonding increases the concentration of reactants at the surface and weakens (not strengthens) the bonds in the reacting molecules, so Statement II is incorrect.
    Both statements are incorrect.
