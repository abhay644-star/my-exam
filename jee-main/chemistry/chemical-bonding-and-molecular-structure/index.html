  1. JEE Main 2026, 2 April, Shift 1 · Question 53
    Given below are two statements :
    Statement (I) : The correct sequence of bond lengths in the following species is :
    $\mathrm{O_2^+ < O_2 < O_2^- < O_2^{2-}}$
    Statement (II) : The correct sequence of number of unpaired electrons in the following species is :
    $\mathrm{O_2 > O_2^+ > O_2^- > O_2^{2-}}$
    In the light of the above statements, choose the correct answer from the options given below :

    1. Both Statement I and Statement II are true
    2. Both Statement I and Statement II are false
    3. Statement I is true but Statement II is false
    4. Statement I is false but Statement II is true
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    Bond orders (MOT): $\mathrm{O_2^+}$ 2.5, $\mathrm{O_2}$ 2, $\mathrm{O_2^-}$ 1.5, $\mathrm{O_2^{2-}}$ 1.
    Bond length increases as bond order decreases, so Statement I is true.
    Unpaired electrons: $\mathrm{O_2}$ 2, $\mathrm{O_2^+}$ 1, $\mathrm{O_2^-}$ 1, $\mathrm{O_2^{2-}}$ 0.
    Since $\mathrm{O_2^+}$ and $\mathrm{O_2^-}$ both have 1, the order $\mathrm{O_2^+ > O_2^-}$ is wrong, so Statement II is false.

  2. JEE Main 2026, 2 April, Shift 2 · Question 53
    SF$_4$ is isostructural with :
    A. BrF$_4^{\ominus}$
    B. CH$_4$
    C. IF$_4^{\oplus}$
    D. XeF$_4$
    E. XeO$_2$F$_2$
    Choose the correct answer from the options given below :

    1. C Only
    2. C and E Only
    3. A and D Only
    4. B and E Only
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    SF$_4$: 4 bond pairs + 1 lone pair, see-saw.
    BrF$_4^-$ and XeF$_4$: 4 bp + 2 lp, square planar. CH$_4$: tetrahedral.
    IF$_4^+$: I has $7-1=6$ valence electrons, 4 bp + 1 lp, see-saw. XeO$_2$F$_2$: 4 $\sigma$ bonds + 1 lp, see-saw.
    Isostructural: C and E only.

  3. JEE Main 2026, 4 April, Shift 1 · Question 59
    Given below are two statements:
    Statement I: The covalency of oxygen is generally two but it can exceed upto four. The oxidation state of oxygen in $\mathrm{SO_2}$ is $-2$ and in $\mathrm{OF_2}$ it is $+2$.
    Statement II: The anomalous behaviour of oxygen when compared to the other elements of group $16$ is due to its small size and high electronegativity.
    In the light of the above statements, choose the correct answer from the options given below

    1. Both Statement I and Statement II are true
    2. Both Statement I and Statement II are false
    3. Statement I is true but Statement II is false
    4. Statement I is false but Statement II is true
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    Oxygen has no $d$ orbitals, so its covalency is usually two and at most four. In $\mathrm{SO_2}$ it is $-2$; in $\mathrm{OF_2}$, F is more electronegative, so O is $+2$. Statement I is true.
    Oxygen's anomalous behaviour arises from its small size, high electronegativity (and absence of $d$ orbitals). Statement II is true.

  4. JEE Main 2026, 4 April, Shift 1 · Question 71
    According to Lewis theory, the total number of $\sigma$ bond-pairs and lone pair of electrons around the central atom of $\mathrm{XeO_6^{4-}}$ ion is ________.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 6

    In $\mathrm{XeO_6^{4-}}$ (perxenate), Xe is bonded to six O atoms and uses all eight valence electrons in bonding (oxidation state $+8$).
    So there are $6$ $\sigma$ bond pairs and $0$ lone pairs on Xe (octahedral).
    Total $=6$.

  5. JEE Main 2026, 4 April, Shift 2 · Question 53
    Which of the following pictorial diagram most correctly represents the $\pi^*$ ($\pi$-antibonding) molecular orbital between two atoms if the internuclear axis is taken to be in the z-direction ($\xrightarrow{\text{z-axis}}$)?

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    A $\pi$ MO is formed by sideways overlap of p orbitals perpendicular to the internuclear ($z$) axis ($p_x$ or $p_y$), so the lobes lie above and below the axis; options (1) and (4) are not of this type.
    In the bonding $\pi$ MO, lobes of the same phase are on the same side (option (2): both upper lobes shaded).
    In $\pi^*$ the two p orbitals combine out of phase: the upper lobe of one atom and the lower lobe of the other have the same phase, giving an extra nodal plane between the nuclei.
    This is option (3).

  6. JEE Main 2026, 5 April, Shift 1 · Question 71
    Consider the following species:
    $\mathrm{BrF_5, XeF_5^-, BF_4^-, ICl_4^-, XeF_4, SF_4, NH_4^+, ClF_3, XeF_2, ICl_2^-}$
    Number of species having $sp^3d$ hybridized central atom is ________.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 4

    Steric number (bond pairs + lone pairs) of the central atom:
    $\mathrm{BrF_5}$ 6 ($sp^3d^2$), $\mathrm{XeF_5^-}$ 7 ($sp^3d^3$), $\mathrm{BF_4^-}$ 4 ($sp^3$), $\mathrm{ICl_4^-}$ 6 ($sp^3d^2$), $\mathrm{XeF_4}$ 6 ($sp^3d^2$).
    $\mathrm{SF_4}$ 5, $\mathrm{ClF_3}$ 5, $\mathrm{XeF_2}$ 5, $\mathrm{ICl_2^-}$ 5 — all $sp^3d$; $\mathrm{NH_4^+}$ 4 ($sp^3$).
    Number with $sp^3d$: $4$.

  7. JEE Main 2026, 5 April, Shift 2 · Question 53
    The covalent radii of atoms A and B are $r_A$ and $r_B$, respectively. The covalent bond length and total length of AB molecule are respectively

    1. $(r_A+r_B)$, $2(r_A+r_B)$
    2. $\dfrac12(r_A+r_B)$, $(r_A+r_B)$
    3. $(r_A+r_B)$, $(r_A+r_B)$
    4. $2(r_A+r_B)$, $\dfrac12(r_A+r_B)$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    Bond length = distance between the two nuclei $=r_A+r_B$.
    The total length of the molecule runs from the outer edge of A to the outer edge of B: $r_A+(r_A+r_B)+r_B=2(r_A+r_B)$.

  8. JEE Main 2026, 5 April, Shift 2 · Question 59
    Given below are two statements :
    Statement I : Aluminium upon reaction with NaOH forms $[\mathrm{Al(OH)_6}]^{3-}$ ion.
    Statement II : The geometry of $\mathrm{ICl_4^-}$, $\mathrm{ClO_3^-}$ and $\mathrm{IBr_2^-}$ is square planar, pyramidal and linear respectively.
    In the light of the above statements, choose the correct answer from the options given below :

    1. Both Statement I and Statement II are true
    2. Both Statement I and Statement II are false
    3. Statement I is true but Statement II is false
    4. Statement I is false but Statement II is true
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    $\mathrm{2Al + 2NaOH + 6H_2O \rightarrow 2Na^+[Al(OH)_4]^- + 3H_2}$: the product is tetrahydroxidoaluminate, $[\mathrm{Al(OH)_4}]^-$. Statement I is false.
    $\mathrm{ICl_4^-}$: 4 bp + 2 lp, square planar. $\mathrm{ClO_3^-}$: 3 bp + 1 lp, pyramidal. $\mathrm{IBr_2^-}$: 2 bp + 3 lp, linear. Statement II is true.

  9. JEE Main 2026, 6 April, Shift 1 · Question 54
    The pairs among $A=\left[\mathrm{SO_3^{2-},CO_3^{2-}}\right]$, $B=\left[\mathrm{O_2^{2-},F_2}\right]$, $C=\left[\mathrm{CN^-,CO}\right]$, $D=\left[\mathrm{NH_3,H_3O^+}\right]$ and $E=\left[\mathrm{MnO_4^{2-},CrO_4^{2-}}\right]$ that do not have similar Lewis dot structure are

    1. A, B and E
    2. A and E
    3. B, C and D
    4. C and D
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    A: $\mathrm{SO_3^{2-}}$ has a lone pair on S (pyramidal), $\mathrm{CO_3^{2-}}$ has none (planar): not similar.
    B: $\mathrm{O_2^{2-}}$ and $\mathrm{F_2}$ are isoelectronic, single bond with three lone pairs on each atom: similar.
    C: $\mathrm{CN^-}$ and CO both have a triple bond and one lone pair on each atom: similar. D: $\mathrm{NH_3}$ and $\mathrm{H_3O^+}$: three bonds and one lone pair: similar.
    E: $\mathrm{MnO_4^{2-}}$ (Mn(VI), $d^1$, one unpaired electron) differs from $\mathrm{CrO_4^{2-}}$ (Cr(VI), $d^0$): not similar.
    Answer: A and E.

  10. JEE Main 2026, 6 April, Shift 2 · Question 53
    Given below are two statements :
    Statement I : The number of compounds among $\mathrm{SO_2}$, $\mathrm{SO_3}$, $\mathrm{SF_4}$, $\mathrm{SF_6}$ and $\mathrm{H_2S}$ in which sulphur does not obey the Octet rule is 3.
    Statement II : Among $[\mathrm{H_2O, ClF_3, SF_4}]$, $[\mathrm{NH_3, BrF_5, SF_4}]$, $[\mathrm{BrF_5, ClF_3, XeF_4}]$ and $[\mathrm{XeF_4, ClF_3, H_2O}]$, the number of sets in which all the molecules have one lone pair of electrons on the central atom is 1.
    In the light of the above statements, choose the correct answer from the options given below :

    1. Both Statement I and Statement II are true
    2. Both Statement I and Statement II are false
    3. Statement I is true but Statement II is false
    4. Statement I is false but Statement II is true
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    Statement I: S has an expanded octet in $\mathrm{SO_2}$ (10 e$^-$), $\mathrm{SO_3}$ (12), $\mathrm{SF_4}$ (10) and $\mathrm{SF_6}$ (12); only $\mathrm{H_2S}$ obeys the octet. That is 4 compounds, so Statement I is false.
    Lone pairs on the central atom: $\mathrm{H_2O}$ 2, $\mathrm{ClF_3}$ 2, $\mathrm{SF_4}$ 1, $\mathrm{NH_3}$ 1, $\mathrm{BrF_5}$ 1, $\mathrm{XeF_4}$ 2.
    Only the set $[\mathrm{NH_3, BrF_5, SF_4}]$ has one lone pair on every central atom, so Statement II is true.

  11. JEE Main 2026, 6 April, Shift 2 · Question 59
    Given below are two statements :
    Statement I : $\mathrm{F_2O < H_2O < Cl_2O}$ is the correct trend in terms of bond angle.
    Statement II : $\mathrm{SiF_4}$, $\mathrm{SnF_4}$ and $\mathrm{PbF_4}$ are ionic in nature.
    In the light of the above statements, choose the correct answer from the options given below :

    1. Both Statement I and Statement II are true
    2. Both Statement I and Statement II are false
    3. Statement I is true but Statement II is false
    4. Statement I is false but Statement II is true
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    Bond angles: $\mathrm{F_2O}\approx103^\circ$ (bond pairs drawn towards F), $\mathrm{H_2O}\approx104.5^\circ$, $\mathrm{Cl_2O}\approx111^\circ$ (bulky Cl atoms). Statement I is true.
    $\mathrm{SnF_4}$ and $\mathrm{PbF_4}$ are ionic, but $\mathrm{SiF_4}$ is a covalent molecule (a gas). Statement II is false.

  12. JEE Main 2026, 8 April, Shift 2 · Question 53
    Bromine trifluoride autoionizes to form $\mathrm{BrF_2^{\oplus}}$ and $\mathrm{BrF_4^{\ominus}}$. The shapes of the cation and anion are respectively __________, and __________.

    1. bent, square planar
    2. linear, square planar
    3. bent, see-saw
    4. linear, tetrahedral
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    $\mathrm{BrF_2^+}$: Br has 7 − 1 = 6 valence electrons; 2 bond pairs + 2 lone pairs ($sp^3$) give a bent shape.
    $\mathrm{BrF_4^-}$: 8 valence electrons; 4 bond pairs + 2 lone pairs ($sp^3d^2$) give a square planar shape.
    Bent, square planar.
