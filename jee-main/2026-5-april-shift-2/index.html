Solved questions from this paper

A few questions with their official answers and worked solutions. All 75 are in the online test.

  1. Question 1 · Mathematics · Sequences and Series
    Let $\alpha, \beta$ be the roots of the equation $x^2-x+p=0$ and $\gamma, \delta$ be the roots the equation $x^2-4x+q=0$; $p, q \in \mathbb{Z}$. If $\alpha, \beta, \gamma, \delta$ are in G.P., then $|p+q|$ equals :

    1. $16$
    2. $32$
    3. $34$
    4. $38$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    Let the G.P. be $\alpha, \alpha r, \alpha r^2, \alpha r^3$.
    $\alpha+\beta=\alpha(1+r)=1$ and $\gamma+\delta=\alpha r^2(1+r)=4$, so $r^2=4$, $r=\pm2$.
    $r=2$: $\alpha=\dfrac13$, $p=\alpha\beta=\dfrac29\notin\mathbb{Z}$, rejected.
    $r=-2$: $\alpha=-1$, terms $-1, 2, -4, 8$; $p=-2$, $q=(-4)(8)=-32$.
    $|p+q|=34$.

  2. Question 2 · Mathematics · Complex Numbers
    Let $z_1, z_2 \in \mathbb{C}$ be the distinct solutions of the equation $z^2+4z-(1+12i)=0$.
    Then $|z_1|^2+|z_2|^2$ is equal to :

    1. $18$
    2. $22$
    3. $29$
    4. $34$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    $z=-2\pm\sqrt{4+1+12i}=-2\pm\sqrt{5+12i}$.
    $(3+2i)^2=5+12i$, so $z=-2\pm(3+2i)$: $z_1=1+2i$, $z_2=-5-2i$.
    $|z_1|^2+|z_2|^2=5+29=34$.

  3. Question 26 · Physics · Units and Measurements
    Match List - I with List - II.

    List - IList - II
    A. Meter (L)I. $\sqrt{\dfrac{hc}{G}}$
    B. Second (S)II. $\sqrt{\dfrac{Gh}{c^5}}$
    C. Kilogram (M)III. $\sqrt{\dfrac{K^2L^2c^3}{Gh}}$
    D. Kelvin (K)IV. $\sqrt{\dfrac{Gh}{c^3}}$
    where h (Planck's constant), G (gravitational constant) and c (speed of light in vacuum) as fundamental units.
    Choose the correct answer from the options given below :

    1. A-II, B-IV, C-I, D-III
    2. A-IV, B-II, C-I, D-III
    3. A-IV, B-I, C-II, D-III
    4. A-III, B-I, C-II, D-IV
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    Dimensions: $[h]=ML^2T^{-1}$, $[G]=M^{-1}L^3T^{-2}$, $[c]=LT^{-1}$.
    $\dfrac{Gh}{c^3}$ has dimensions $L^2$, so length $\propto\sqrt{\dfrac{Gh}{c^3}}$ (A-IV).
    Time $=\dfrac{\text{length}}{c}\propto\sqrt{\dfrac{Gh}{c^5}}$ (B-II).
    $\dfrac{hc}{G}$ has dimensions $M^2$, so mass $\propto\sqrt{\dfrac{hc}{G}}$ (C-I).
    The remaining expression III goes with Kelvin (D-III).
    A-IV, B-II, C-I, D-III.

  4. Question 28 · Physics · Work, Energy and Power
    A mass of 1 kg is kept on a inclined plane with $30^\circ$ inclination with respect to horizontal plane and it is at rest initially. Then the whole assembly is moved up with constant velocity of 4 m/s. The work done by the frictional force in time 2 s is __________ J. (Take $g=10\ \mathrm{m/s^2}$)

    1. $20$
    2. $25$
    3. $30$
    4. $10$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    The block stays at rest relative to the incline, and the assembly moves with constant velocity, so static friction balances the component of gravity along the incline: $f=mg\sin30^\circ=5$ N, directed up the incline.
    In 2 s the block moves $4\times2=8$ m vertically upward.
    Angle between the friction (along the incline, up) and the vertical displacement is $60^\circ$.
    $W_f=5\times8\times\cos60^\circ=20$ J.

  5. Question 51 · Chemistry · Some Basic Concepts of Chemistry
    What volume of hydrogen gas at STP would be liberated by action of 50 mL of $\mathrm{H_2SO_4}$ of 50% purity (density $=1.3\ \mathrm{g\ mL^{-1}}$) on 20 g of zinc ?
    Given : Molar mass of H, O, S, Zn are 1, 16, 32, 65 $\mathrm{g\ mol^{-1}}$ respectively.

    1. 5.824 L
    2. 7.428 L
    3. 6.892 L
    4. 8.375 L
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    $\mathrm{Zn + H_2SO_4 \rightarrow ZnSO_4 + H_2}$.
    Mass of $\mathrm{H_2SO_4}=50\times1.3\times0.5=32.5$ g $=\dfrac{32.5}{98}=0.332$ mol.
    Zn $=\dfrac{20}{65}=0.308$ mol, so Zn is limiting.
    $V(\mathrm{H_2})=\dfrac{20}{65}\times22.4=6.892$ L.

  6. Question 52 · Chemistry · Structure of Atom
    Which of the following statement(s) is/are true ?
    A. If two orbitals have the same value of $(n+l)$, the orbital with lower value of n will have lower energy.
    B. Energies of the orbitals in the same subshell increase with increase in atomic number.
    C. The size of $2p_x$ orbital is less than the size of $3p_x$ orbital.
    D. Among 5f, 6s, 4d, 5p and 5d orbitals, none of the orbitals have 2 radial nodes.
    Choose the correct answer from the options given below :

    1. A, B and C only
    2. A and C only
    3. C and D only
    4. A only
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    A: true (the $(n+l)$ rule).
    B: false; with increasing atomic number the effective nuclear charge increases and orbital energies decrease.
    C: true; size increases with $n$.
    D: radial nodes $=n-l-1$: 5f 1, 6s 5, 4d 1, 5p 3, 5d 2. The 5d orbital has 2 radial nodes, so D is false.
    A and C only.
