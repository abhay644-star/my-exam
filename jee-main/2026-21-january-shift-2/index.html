Solved questions from this paper

A few questions with their official answers and worked solutions. All 75 are in the online test.

  1. Question 1 · Mathematics · Relations and Functions
    Let $A=\{2, 3, 5, 7, 9\}$. Let R be the relation on A defined by $xRy$ if and only if $2x\le3y$. Let $l$ be the number of elements in R, and m be the minimum number of elements required to be added in R to make it a symmetric relation. Then $l+m$ is equal to :

    1. $21$
    2. $23$
    3. $25$
    4. $27$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    Count pairs with $y\ge\dfrac{2x}{3}$: $x=2$: 5; $x=3$: 5; $x=5$: $y\in\{5,7,9\}$, 3; $x=7$: $y\in\{5,7,9\}$, 3; $x=9$: $y\in\{7,9\}$, 2. So $l=18$.
    A pair $(x,y)\in R$ needs $(y,x)$ added when $2y>3x$: $(2,5),(2,7),(2,9),(3,5),(3,7),(3,9),(5,9)$, so $m=7$.
    $l+m=25$.

  2. Question 2 · Mathematics · Complex Numbers
    Let $z$ be the complex number satisfying $|z-5|\le3$ and having maximum positive principal argument. Then $34\left|\dfrac{5z-12}{5iz+16}\right|^2$ is equal to :

    1. $12$
    2. $16$
    3. $20$
    4. $26$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    The region is the disc with centre 5 and radius 3. The argument is largest at the point where the tangent from O touches the circle: $|z|=\sqrt{25-9}=4$ and $\sin\theta=\dfrac35$.
    So $z=4\left(\dfrac45+\dfrac35i\right)=\dfrac{16+12i}{5}$.
    $5z-12=4+12i$ and $5iz+16=(-12+16i)+16=4+16i$.
    $34\cdot\dfrac{16+144}{16+256}=34\cdot\dfrac{160}{272}=20$.

  3. Question 26 · Physics · Units and Measurements
    Keeping the significant figures in view, the sum of the physical quantities 52.01 m, 153.2 m and 0.123 m is :

    1. 205 m
    2. 205.3 m
    3. 205.33 m
    4. 205.333 m
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    Exact sum $=205.333$ m. In addition the result keeps the fewest decimal places among the terms (one, from 153.2 m).
    So the sum is 205.3 m.

  4. Question 27 · Physics · Mechanical Properties of Fluids
    A spherical body of radius $r$ and density $\sigma$ falls freely through a viscous liquid having density $\rho$ and viscosity $\eta$ and attains a terminal velocity $v_0$. Estimated maximum error in the quantity $\eta$ is : (Ignore errors associated with $\sigma$, $\rho$ and $g$, gravitational acceleration)

    1. $2\left[\dfrac{\Delta r}{r}+\dfrac{\Delta v_o}{v_o}\right]$
    2. $2\left[\dfrac{\Delta r}{r}-\dfrac{\Delta v_o}{v_o}\right]$
    3. $2\dfrac{\Delta r}{r}-\dfrac{\Delta v_o}{v_o}$
    4. $\dfrac{2\Delta r}{r}+\dfrac{\Delta v_o}{v_o}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    Terminal velocity: $v_0=\dfrac{2r^2(\sigma-\rho)g}{9\eta}$, so $\eta=\dfrac{2r^2(\sigma-\rho)g}{9v_0}\propto\dfrac{r^2}{v_0}$.
    Maximum relative error: $\dfrac{\Delta\eta}{\eta}=\dfrac{2\Delta r}{r}+\dfrac{\Delta v_0}{v_0}$.

  5. Question 51 · Chemistry · Some Basic Concepts of Chemistry
    Aqueous HCl reacts with $\mathrm{MnO_2(s)}$ to form $\mathrm{MnCl_2(aq)}$, $\mathrm{Cl_2(g)}$ and $\mathrm{H_2O}(l)$. What is the weight (in g) of $\mathrm{Cl_2}$ liberated when 8.7 g of $\mathrm{MnO_2(s)}$ is reacted with excess aqueous HCl solution ? (Given Molar mass in g mol$^{-1}$ Mn $=55$, Cl $=35.5$, O $=16$, H $=1$)

    1. $14.2$
    2. $21.3$
    3. $7.1$
    4. $71$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    $\mathrm{MnO_2+4HCl\rightarrow MnCl_2+Cl_2+2H_2O}$.
    $\mathrm{MnO_2}$: $\dfrac{8.7}{87}=0.1$ mol, giving 0.1 mol $\mathrm{Cl_2}=7.1$ g.

  6. Question 52 · Chemistry · Structure of Atom
    Consider the following spectral lines for atomic hydrogen :
    A. First line of Paschen series
    B. Second line of Balmer series
    C. Third line of Paschen series
    D. Fourth line of Bracket series
    The correct arrangement of the above lines in ascending order of energy is :

    1. A < B < C < D
    2. D < A < C < B
    3. D < C < A < B
    4. C < D < B < A
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    Energy $\propto\dfrac1{n_1^2}-\dfrac1{n_2^2}$:
    A ($4\to3$): $\dfrac19-\dfrac1{16}=0.0486$; B ($4\to2$): $\dfrac14-\dfrac1{16}=0.1875$; C ($6\to3$): $\dfrac19-\dfrac1{36}=0.0833$; D ($8\to4$): $\dfrac1{16}-\dfrac1{64}=0.0469$.
    Ascending: D < A < C < B.
