Solved questions from this paper

A few questions with their official answers and worked solutions. All 75 are in the online test.

  1. Question 1 · Mathematics · Relations and Functions
    Let the domains of the functions $f(x)=\log_4\log_3\log_7\left(8-\log_2(x^2+4x+5)\right)$ and $g(x)=\sin^{-1}\left(\dfrac{7x+10}{x-2}\right)$ be $(\alpha, \beta)$ and $[\gamma, \delta]$, respectively. Then $\alpha^2+\beta^2+\gamma^2+\delta^2$ is equal to :

    1. $13$
    2. $14$
    3. $15$
    4. $16$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    $f$: we need $\log_3\log_7(\cdots)>0$, i.e. $\log_7\left(8-\log_2(x^2+4x+5)\right)>1$, i.e. $\log_2(x^2+4x+5)<1$, i.e. $x^2+4x+3<0$: $x\in(-3, -1)$. So $\alpha=-3$, $\beta=-1$.
    $g$: $\dfrac{7x+10}{x-2}\le1\iff\dfrac{6x+12}{x-2}\le0\iff-2\le x<2$; $\dfrac{7x+10}{x-2}\ge-1\iff\dfrac{8x+8}{x-2}\ge0\iff x\le-1$ or $x>2$. So the domain is $[-2, -1]$: $\gamma=-2$, $\delta=-1$.
    $9+1+4+1=15$.

  2. Question 3 · Mathematics · Complex Numbers
    Let the product of $\omega_1=(8+i)\sin\theta+(7+4i)\cos\theta$ and $\omega_2=(1+8i)\sin\theta+(4+7i)\cos\theta$ be $\alpha+i\beta$, $i=\sqrt{-1}$. Let p and q be the maximum and the minimum values of $\alpha+\beta$ respectively. Then $p+q$ is equal to :

    1. $130$
    2. $140$
    3. $150$
    4. $160$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    $1+8i=i(8-i)$ and $4+7i=i(7-4i)$, so $\omega_2=i\,\overline{\omega_1}$ and $\omega_1\omega_2=i|\omega_1|^2$: $\alpha=0$, $\beta=|\omega_1|^2$.
    $|\omega_1|^2=(8\sin\theta+7\cos\theta)^2+(\sin\theta+4\cos\theta)^2=65+120\sin\theta\cos\theta=65+60\sin2\theta$.
    $p=125$, $q=5$, $p+q=130$.

  3. Question 26 · Physics · Units and Measurements
    For the determination of refractive index of glass slab, a travelling microscope is used whose main scale contains 300 equal divisions equals to 15 cm. The vernier scale attached to the microscope has 25 divisions equals to 24 divisions of main scale. The least count (LC) of the travelling microscope is (in cm) :

    1. 0.001
    2. 0.002
    3. 0.0025
    4. 0.0005
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    1 MSD $=\dfrac{15}{300}=0.05$ cm; 25 VSD $=24$ MSD, so 1 VSD $=\dfrac{24}{25}$ MSD.
    LC $=1$ MSD $-$ 1 VSD $=\dfrac{1}{25}\times0.05=0.002$ cm.

  4. Question 28 · Physics · System of Particles and Rotational Motion
    A wheel is rolling on a plane surface. The speed of a particle on the highest point of the rim is 8 m/s. The speed of the particle on the rim of the wheel at the same level as the centre of wheel, will be :

    1. 4 m/s
    2. 8 m/s
    3. $4\sqrt2$ m/s
    4. $8\sqrt2$ m/s
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    In rolling without slipping the top point moves at $2v$, so $v=4$ m/s for the centre.
    A rim point level with the centre has velocity $v$ (forward) plus $v$ (vertical, from rotation), at right angles.
    Speed $=\sqrt2\,v=4\sqrt2$ m/s.

  5. Question 51 · Chemistry · Structure of Atom
    Consider the ground state of chromium atom $(Z=24)$. How many electrons are with Azimuthal quantum number $l=1$ and $l=2$ respectively ?

    1. 12 and 4
    2. 12 and 5
    3. 16 and 4
    4. 16 and 5
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    Cr: $1s^2\,2s^2\,2p^6\,3s^2\,3p^6\,3d^5\,4s^1$.
    $l=1$ (p): $6+6=12$ electrons; $l=2$ (d): 5 electrons.

  6. Question 52 · Chemistry · Thermodynamics
    Consider the given data :
    (a) $\mathrm{HCl(g)+10\,H_2O(l)\rightarrow HCl.10\,H_2O}$ $\Delta H=-69.01$ kJ mol$^{-1}$
    (b) $\mathrm{HCl(g)+40\,H_2O(l)\rightarrow HCl.40\,H_2O}$ $\Delta H=-72.79$ kJ mol$^{-1}$
    Choose the correct statement :

    1. The heat of solution depends on the amount of solvent.
    2. The heat of dilution for the HCl ($\mathrm{HCl.10\,H_2O}$ to $\mathrm{HCl.40\,H_2O}$) is 3.78 kJ mol$^{-1}$.
    3. Dissolution of gas in water is an endothermic process.
    4. The heat of formation of HCl solution is represented by both (a) and (b).
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    The two enthalpies differ only in the amount of water, so the heat of solution depends on the amount of solvent: (1) is correct.
    Heat of dilution $=-72.79-(-69.01)=-3.78$ kJ mol$^{-1}$ (heat is released), not $+3.78$: (2) is wrong. Both values are negative, so dissolution is exothermic: (3) is wrong. (a) and (b) are enthalpies of solution, not of formation: (4) is wrong.
