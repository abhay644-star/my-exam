Solved questions from this paper

A few questions with their official answers and worked solutions. All 75 are in the online test.

  1. Question 1 · Mathematics · Conic Sections
    Let the focal chord PQ of the parabola $y^2=4x$ make an angle of $60^\circ$ with the positive $x$-axis, where P lies in the first quadrant. If the circle, whose one diameter is PS, S being the focus of the parabola, touches the $y$-axis at the point $(0, \alpha)$, then $5\alpha^2$ is equal to :

    1. $15$
    2. $20$
    3. $25$
    4. $30$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    S is $(1,0)$. Take $P=(t^2, 2t)$; the slope of SP is $\dfrac{2t}{t^2-1}=\sqrt3$, so $\sqrt3t^2-2t-\sqrt3=0$ and, with $t>0$, $t=\sqrt3$. Thus $P=(3, 2\sqrt3)$.
    The circle on PS as diameter has centre $(2, \sqrt3)$ and radius $\dfrac{PS}{2}=\dfrac{t^2+1}{2}=2$, which equals the distance of the centre from the $y$-axis, so it touches the $y$-axis at $(0, \sqrt3)$.
    $\alpha^2=3$, so $5\alpha^2=15$.

  2. Question 2 · Mathematics · Complex Numbers
    Let $z$ be a complex number such that $|z|=1$. If $\dfrac{2+k^2z}{k+\bar{z}}=kz$, $k\in\mathbf{R}$, then the maximum distance of $k+ik^2$ from the circle $|z-(1+2i)|=1$ is :

    1. $\sqrt{3}+1$
    2. $2$
    3. $\sqrt{5}+1$
    4. $3$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    Cross-multiplying: $2+k^2z=k^2z+kz\bar z=k^2z+k$ (since $z\bar z=1$), so $k=2$.
    The point is $k+ik^2=2+4i$. Its distance from the centre $1+2i$ is $\sqrt{1^2+2^2}=\sqrt5$.
    Maximum distance from the circle $=\sqrt5+1$.

  3. Question 26 · Physics · Units and Measurements
    The equation for real gas is given by $\left(P+\dfrac{a}{V^2}\right)(V-b)=RT$, where P, V, T and R are the pressure, volume, temperature and gas constant, respectively. The dimension of $ab^{-2}$ is equivalent to that of :

    1. Compressibility
    2. Energy density
    3. Planck's constant
    4. Strain
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    $\dfrac{a}{V^2}$ has the dimensions of pressure, so $[a]=[PV^2]$; $b$ has the dimensions of volume.
    $[ab^{-2}]=[P]=[\mathrm{ML^{-1}T^{-2}}]$, the same as energy per unit volume, i.e. energy density.

  4. Question 29 · Physics · Motion in a Plane
    A river is flowing from west to east direction with speed of 9 km h$^{-1}$. If a boat capable of moving at a maximum speed of 27 km h$^{-1}$ in still water, crosses the river in half a minute, while moving with maximum speed at an angle of $150^\circ$ to direction of river flow, then the width of the river is :

    1. 112.5 m
    2. $112.5\times\sqrt{3}$ m
    3. 75 m
    4. 300 m
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    Only the component of the boat's velocity (relative to water) across the river carries it across: $27\sin150^\circ=13.5$ km h$^{-1}=3.75$ m s$^{-1}$.
    Width $=3.75\times30=112.5$ m.

  5. Question 51 · Chemistry · Some Basic Concepts of Chemistry
    $\mathrm{CaCO_3(s)+2HCl(aq)\rightarrow CaCl_2(aq)+CO_2(g)+H_2O(l)}$
    Consider the above reaction, what mass of $\mathrm{CaCl_2}$ will be formed if 250 mL of 0.76 M HCl reacts with 1000 g of $\mathrm{CaCO_3}$ ?
    (Given : Molar mass of Ca, C, O, H and Cl are 40, 12, 16, 1 and 35.5 g mol$^{-1}$, respectively)

    1. 5.272 g
    2. 2.636 g
    3. 3.908 g
    4. 10.545 g
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    HCl $=0.250\times0.76=0.19$ mol; 1000 g of $\mathrm{CaCO_3}$ is 10 mol, a large excess, so HCl is limiting.
    $\mathrm{CaCl_2}$ formed $=\dfrac{0.19}{2}=0.095$ mol $\times111$ g mol$^{-1}=10.545$ g.

  6. Question 52 · Chemistry · Structure of Atom
    According to Bohr's model of hydrogen atom, which of the following statement is incorrect ?

    1. Radius of 4$^{\text{th}}$ orbit is four times larger than that of 2$^{\text{nd}}$ orbit
    2. Radius of 6$^{\text{th}}$ orbit is three times larger than that of 4$^{\text{th}}$ orbit
    3. Radius of 8$^{\text{th}}$ orbit is four times larger than that of 4$^{\text{th}}$ orbit
    4. Radius of 3$^{\text{rd}}$ orbit is nine times larger than that of 1$^{\text{st}}$ orbit
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    $r_n\propto n^2$.
    $\dfrac{r_4}{r_2}=4$, $\dfrac{r_8}{r_4}=4$, $\dfrac{r_3}{r_1}=9$, but $\dfrac{r_6}{r_4}=\dfrac{36}{16}=2.25$, not 3.
    The incorrect statement is the one about the 6th and 4th orbits.
