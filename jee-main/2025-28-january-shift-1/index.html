Solved questions from this paper

A few questions with their official answers and worked solutions. All 75 are in the online test.

  1. Question 1 · Mathematics · Relations and Functions
    The relation $R=\{(x, y): x, y\in\mathbf{Z} \text{ and } x+y \text{ is even}\}$ is:

    1. reflexive and transitive but not symmetric
    2. reflexive and symmetric but not transitive
    3. symmetric and transitive but not reflexive
    4. an equivalence relation
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    Reflexive: $x+x=2x$ is even.
    Symmetric: $x+y=y+x$.
    Transitive: if $x+y$ and $y+z$ are even, then $x+z=(x+y)+(y+z)-2y$ is even.
    So $R$ is an equivalence relation.

  2. Question 3 · Mathematics · Complex Numbers
    Let O be the origin, the point A be $z_1=\sqrt{3}+2\sqrt{2}i$, the point B $(z_2)$ be such that $\sqrt{3}|z_2|=|z_1|$ and $\arg(z_2)=\arg(z_1)+\dfrac{\pi}{6}$. Then

    1. ABO is an obtuse angled isosceles triangle
    2. ABO is a scalene triangle
    3. area of triangle ABO is $\dfrac{11}{\sqrt{3}}$
    4. area of triangle ABO is $\dfrac{11}{4}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    $OA=|z_1|=\sqrt{3+8}=\sqrt{11}$, $OB=\dfrac{\sqrt{11}}{\sqrt3}$ and $\angle AOB=\dfrac{\pi}{6}$.
    $AB^2=11+\dfrac{11}{3}-2\cdot\sqrt{11}\cdot\dfrac{\sqrt{11}}{\sqrt3}\cdot\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}=\dfrac{11}{3}$, so $AB=OB$: the triangle is isosceles.
    $OA^2=11>OB^2+AB^2=\dfrac{22}{3}$, so the angle at B is obtuse.
    (Area $=\dfrac12\cdot\sqrt{11}\cdot\dfrac{\sqrt{11}}{\sqrt3}\cdot\dfrac12=\dfrac{11}{4\sqrt3}$, which matches neither area option.)

  3. Question 28 · Physics · Work, Energy and Power
    Given below are two statements: one is labelled as <b>Assertion A</b> and the other is labelled as <b>Reason R</b>
    <b>Assertion A:</b> In a central force field, the work done is independent of the path chosen.
    <b>Reason R:</b> Every force encountered in mechanics does not have an associated potential energy.
    In the light of the above statements, choose the <b><i>most appropriate</i></b> answer from the options given below

    1. Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation of A
    2. Both A and R are true but R is NOT the correct explanation of A
    3. A is true but R is false
    4. A is false but R is true
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    A central force $F(r)\hat{r}$ is conservative, so the work done between two points does not depend on the path: A is true.
    Not every force has a potential energy (friction, for example, is non-conservative): R is true.
    R is a general remark and does not explain why a central force is conservative, so it is not the correct explanation of A.

  4. Question 29 · Physics · Mechanical Properties of Fluids
    Consider following statements:
    A. Surface tension arises due to extra energy of the molecules at the interior as compared to the molecules at the surface, of a liquid.
    B. As the temperature of liquid rises, the coefficient of viscosity increases.
    C. As the temperature of gas increases, the coefficient of viscosity increases
    D. The onset of turbulence is determined by Reynold's number.
    E. In a steady flow two stream lines never intersect.
    Choose the correct answer from the options given below:

    1. A, D, E Only
    2. C, D, E Only
    3. A, B, C Only
    4. B, C, D Only
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    A is false: the molecules at the surface (not the interior) have extra energy.
    B is false: the viscosity of a liquid falls as the temperature rises.
    C is true for gases. D is true: Reynolds number decides the onset of turbulence. E is true: two streamlines cannot cross, or the velocity at the crossing point would not be unique.
    Answer: C, D, E only.

  5. Question 51 · Chemistry · Structure of Atom
    In a multielectron atom, which of the following orbitals described by three quantum numbers will have same energy in absence of electric and magnetic fields?
    A. $n=1, l=0, m_l=0$
    B. $n=2, l=0, m_l=0$
    C. $n=2, l=1, m_l=1$
    D. $n=3, l=2, m_l=1$
    E. $n=3, l=2, m_l=0$
    Choose the <b><i>correct</i></b> answer from the options given below:

    1. D and E Only
    2. C and D Only
    3. B and C Only
    4. A and B Only
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    In a multielectron atom the orbital energy depends on both $n$ and $l$ (not on $m_l$ without external fields).
    Only D and E share $n=3$, $l=2$ (both 3d orbitals), so they are degenerate. B (2s) and C (2p) differ in $l$.
    Answer: D and E only.

  6. Question 52 · Chemistry · Chemical Bonding and Molecular Structure
    The molecules having square pyramidal geometry are

    1. $\mathrm{BrF_5}$ & $\mathrm{PCl_5}$
    2. $\mathrm{SbF_5}$ & $\mathrm{XeOF_4}$
    3. $\mathrm{BrF_5}$ & $\mathrm{XeOF_4}$
    4. $\mathrm{SbF_5}$ & $\mathrm{PCl_5}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    $\mathrm{BrF_5}$: 5 bond pairs + 1 lone pair (sp$^3$d$^2$), square pyramidal.
    $\mathrm{XeOF_4}$: 5 bonded atoms + 1 lone pair on Xe, square pyramidal.
    $\mathrm{PCl_5}$ and $\mathrm{SbF_5}$ have 5 bond pairs and no lone pair: trigonal bipyramidal.
