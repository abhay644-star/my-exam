Solved questions from this paper

A few questions with their official answers and worked solutions. All 75 are in the online test.

  1. Question 1 · Mathematics · Relations and Functions
    Define a relation R on the interval $\left[0, \dfrac{\pi}{2}\right)$ by $x$ R $y$ if and only if $\sec^2x-\tan^2y=1$. Then R is :

    1. reflexive but neither symmetric not transitive
    2. both reflexive and symmetric but not transitive
    3. both reflexive and transitive but not symmetric
    4. an equivalence relation
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    $\sec^2x-\tan^2y=1\iff\tan^2x=\tan^2y$. On $\left[0,\tfrac\pi2\right)$, $\tan$ is non-negative and increasing, so this means $x=y$.
    Equality is reflexive, symmetric and transitive: R is an equivalence relation.

  2. Question 2 · Mathematics · Complex Numbers
    Let $|z_1-8-2i|\le1$ and $|z_2-2+6i|\le2$, $z_1, z_2\in\mathbf{C}$. Then the minimum value of $|z_1-z_2|$ is :

    1. $10$
    2. $3$
    3. $7$
    4. $13$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    $z_1$ lies in a disc of centre $8+2i$, radius 1; $z_2$ in a disc of centre $2-6i$, radius 2.
    Distance between centres $=\sqrt{6^2+8^2}=10$.
    Minimum $|z_1-z_2|=10-1-2=7$.

  3. Question 26 · Physics · Units and Measurements
    The pair of physical quantities not having same dimensions is :

    1. Pressure and Young's modulus
    2. Surface tension and impulse
    3. Torque and energy
    4. Angular momentum and Planck's constant
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    Surface tension: $[MT^{-2}]$; impulse: $[MLT^{-1}]$, which differ.
    Torque and energy are both $[ML^2T^{-2}]$; pressure and Young's modulus are both $[ML^{-1}T^{-2}]$; angular momentum and $h$ are both $[ML^2T^{-1}]$.

  4. Question 28 · Physics · Motion in a Plane
    Two projectiles are fired with same initial speed from same point on ground at angles of $(45^\circ-\alpha)$ and $(45^\circ+\alpha)$, respectively, with the horizontal direction. The ratio of their maximum heights attained is :

    1. $\dfrac{1-\tan\alpha}{1+\tan\alpha}$
    2. $\dfrac{1+\sin\alpha}{1-\sin\alpha}$
    3. $\dfrac{1-\sin2\alpha}{1+\sin2\alpha}$
    4. $\dfrac{1+\sin2\alpha}{1-\sin2\alpha}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    $H=\dfrac{u^2\sin^2\theta}{2g}$, so the ratio is $\dfrac{\sin^2(45^\circ-\alpha)}{\sin^2(45^\circ+\alpha)}=\left(\dfrac{\cos\alpha-\sin\alpha}{\cos\alpha+\sin\alpha}\right)^2=\dfrac{1-\sin2\alpha}{1+\sin2\alpha}$.

  5. Question 51 · Chemistry · Some Basic Concepts of Chemistry
    Choose the <b>correct</b> statements.
    (A) Weight of a substance is the amount of matter present in it.
    (B) Mass is the force exerted by gravity on an object.
    (C) Volume is the amount of space occupied by a substance.
    (D) Temperatures below 0°C are possible in Celsius scale, but in Kelvin scale negative temperature is not possible.
    (E) Precision refers to the closeness of various measurements for the same quantity.
    Choose the <b>correct</b> answer from the options given below :

    1. (A), (B) and (C) Only
    2. (B), (C) and (D) Only
    3. (C), (D) and (E) Only
    4. (A), (D) and (E) Only
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    (A) and (B) have the definitions swapped: mass is the amount of matter, weight is the gravitational force. Both are false.
    (C), (D) and (E) are correct statements.

  6. Question 52 · Chemistry · Structure of Atom
    If $a_0$ is denoted as the Bohr radius of hydrogen atom, then what is the de-Broglie wavelength $(\lambda)$ of the electron present in the second orbit of hydrogen atom ? [n : any integer]

    1. $\dfrac{8\pi a_0}{n}$
    2. $\dfrac{4\pi a_0}{n}$
    3. $\dfrac{4n}{\pi a_0}$
    4. $\dfrac{2a_0}{n\pi}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    Radius of the second orbit: $r_2=2^2a_0=4a_0$.
    Bohr's quantum condition in de Broglie form is $2\pi r=n\lambda$, so $\lambda=\dfrac{2\pi(4a_0)}{n}=\dfrac{8\pi a_0}{n}$ (with $n=2$ this is $4\pi a_0$).
