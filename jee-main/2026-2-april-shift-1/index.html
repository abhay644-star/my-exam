Solved questions from this paper

A few questions with their official answers and worked solutions. All 75 are in the online test.

  1. Question 1 · Mathematics · Complex Numbers
    Let $\alpha, \alpha+2, \alpha \in \mathbb{Z}$, be the roots of the quadratic equation

    $\displaystyle x(x+2)+(x+1)(x+3)+(x+2)(x+4)+\ldots+(x+n-1)(x+n+1)=4n \quad \text{for some } n \in \mathbb{N}.$
    Then $n+\alpha$ is equal to :

    1. $0$
    2. $1$
    3. $2$
    4. $3$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    The LHS is $\sum_{k=0}^{n-1}(x+k)(x+k+2) = nx^2 + n(n+1)x + \dfrac{(n-1)n(2n-1)}{6} + n(n-1)$.
    Dividing by $n$: $x^2+(n+1)x+\dfrac{(n-1)(2n-1)}{6}+n-5=0$.
    Roots differ by $2$, so discriminant $=4$: $(n+1)^2-\dfrac{2(2n^2-3n+1)}{3}-4n+20=4$.
    This simplifies to $n^2=49$, so $n=7$.
    Sum of roots $2\alpha+2=-(n+1)=-8 \Rightarrow \alpha=-5$.
    Hence $n+\alpha=2$.

  2. Question 3 · Mathematics · Determinants
    Let $\alpha, \beta \in \mathbb{R}$ be such that the system of linear equations $$\begin{aligned} x+2y+z&=5\\ 2x+y+\alpha z&=5\\ 8x+4y+\beta z&=18 \end{aligned}$$ has no solution. Then $\dfrac{\beta}{\alpha}$ is equal to :

    1. $-4$
    2. $4$
    3. $8$
    4. $-8$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    For no solution, $\Delta=\begin{vmatrix}1&2&1\\2&1&\alpha\\8&4&\beta\end{vmatrix}=12\alpha-3\beta=0$, so $\beta=4\alpha$.
    Then the third equation's LHS is $4\times$ the second's: $8x+4y+4\alpha z$, but $4\times5=20\neq18$, so the system is inconsistent.
    Hence $\dfrac{\beta}{\alpha}=4$.

  3. Question 26 · Physics · Units and Measurements
    The dimensional formula of $\dfrac{1}{2}\epsilon_o E^2$ ($\epsilon_o$ = permittivity of vacuum and E = electric field) is $\mathrm{M^aL^bT^c}$. The value of $2a-b+c=$ ________.

    1. $0$
    2. $1$
    3. $-1$
    4. $2$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    $\tfrac{1}{2}\epsilon_o E^2$ is energy per unit volume. $[\text{energy density}] = \dfrac{\mathrm{ML^2T^{-2}}}{\mathrm{L^3}} = \mathrm{M^1L^{-1}T^{-2}}$. So $a=1,\ b=-1,\ c=-2$ and $2a-b+c = 2+1-2 = 1$.

  4. Question 28 · Physics · Motion in a Plane
    The velocity of a particle is given as $\vec{v} = -x\hat{i} + 2y\hat{j} - z\hat{k}$ m/s. The magnitude of acceleration at point (1, 2, 4) is ________ $\mathrm{m/s^2}$.

    1. $\sqrt{6}$
    2. $9$
    3. $\sqrt{33}$
    4. $0$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    $v_x=-x,\ v_y=2y,\ v_z=-z$. Then $a_x = \dfrac{dv_x}{dt} = -v_x = x$, $a_y = 2v_y = 4y$, $a_z = -v_z = z$.
    At $(1,2,4)$: $\vec{a} = \hat{i} + 8\hat{j} + 4\hat{k}$, so $|\vec{a}| = \sqrt{1+64+16} = 9\ \mathrm{m/s^2}$.

  5. Question 51 · Chemistry · Some Basic Concepts of Chemistry
    The mass of iron converted into $\mathrm{Fe_3O_4}$ by the action of 18 g of steam is :
    (Given : Molar mass of H, O and Fe are 1, 16 and 56 g mol$^{-1}$ respectively)
    Assume iron is present in excess :

    1. 2.1 g
    2. 4.2 g
    3. 21 g
    4. 42 g
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    $\mathrm{3Fe + 4H_2O \rightarrow Fe_3O_4 + 4H_2}$.
    18 g steam = 1 mol $\mathrm{H_2O}$.
    Fe reacted $= \tfrac{3}{4}$ mol $= 0.75 \times 56 = 42$ g.

  6. Question 52 · Chemistry · Structure of Atom
    What is the energy (in J atom$^{-1}$) required for the following process ?
    $\mathrm{Li^{2+}(g) \rightarrow Li^{3+}(g) + e^-}$
    (Take the ionization energy for the H atom in the ground state as $2.18 \times 10^{-18}$ J atom$^{-1}$)

    1. $8.72 \times 10^{-18}$
    2. $1.962 \times 10^{-18}$
    3. $1.962 \times 10^{-17}$
    4. $6.54 \times 10^{-17}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    $\mathrm{Li^{2+}}$ is hydrogen-like with $Z = 3$, electron in $n = 1$.
    $IE = Z^2 \times IE_\mathrm{H} = 9 \times 2.18 \times 10^{-18}$
    $= 1.962 \times 10^{-17}$ J atom$^{-1}$.
