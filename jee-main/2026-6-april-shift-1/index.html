Solved questions from this paper

A few questions with their official answers and worked solutions. All 75 are in the online test.

  1. Question 1 · Mathematics · Inverse Trigonometric Functions
    Let $[\cdot]$ denote the greatest integer function. If the domain of the function $f(x)=\sin^{-1}\left(\dfrac{x+[x]}{3}\right)$ is $[\alpha,\beta)$, then $\alpha^2+\beta^2$ is equal to:

    1. $2$
    2. $5$
    3. $10$
    4. $13$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    Need $-3\le x+[x]\le3$. Write $x=n+f$, $n=[x]$, $0\le f<1$: $-3\le 2n+f\le3$.
    $n=1$: $2+f\le3$ always holds, so $[1,2)$ works; $n=2$ gives $4+f>3$ (fails).
    $n=-1$: $-2+f\ge-3$ holds, so $[-1,0)$ works; $n=-2$ needs $f\ge1$ (fails). $n=0$ works.
    Domain $=[-1,2)$, so $\alpha=-1$, $\beta=2$ and $\alpha^2+\beta^2=5$.

  2. Question 2 · Mathematics · Complex Numbers
    Let one root of the quadratic equation in $x$:
    $(k^2-15k+27)x^2+9(k-1)x+18=0$
    be twice the other. Then the length of the latus rectum of the parabola $y^2=6kx$ is equal to:

    1. $4$
    2. $6$
    3. $8$
    4. $12$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    Let $A=k^2-15k+27$ and the roots be $r,2r$.
    Sum: $3r=-\dfrac{9(k-1)}{A}$; product: $2r^2=\dfrac{18}{A}\Rightarrow r^2=\dfrac{9}{A}$.
    From the sum, $9r^2=\dfrac{81(k-1)^2}{A^2}$, so $\dfrac{81}{A}=\dfrac{81(k-1)^2}{A^2}\Rightarrow A=(k-1)^2$.
    $k^2-15k+27=k^2-2k+1\Rightarrow 13k=26\Rightarrow k=2$.
    Parabola $y^2=12x$: latus rectum $=12$.

  3. Question 26 · Physics · Units and Measurements
    The density $\rho$ of a uniform cylinder is determined by measuring its mass $m$, length $l$ and diameter $d$. The measured values of $m$, $l$ and $d$ are $97.42\pm0.02$ g, $8.35\pm0.05$ mm and $20.20\pm0.02$ mm, respectively. Calculated percentage fractional error in $\rho$ is ________.

    1. $0.63\%$
    2. $0.82\%$
    3. $0.72\%$
    4. $0.25\%$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    $\rho=\dfrac{4m}{\pi d^2l}\Rightarrow\dfrac{\Delta\rho}{\rho}=\dfrac{\Delta m}{m}+\dfrac{\Delta l}{l}+2\dfrac{\Delta d}{d}$.
    $=\dfrac{0.02}{97.42}+\dfrac{0.05}{8.35}+\dfrac{2\times0.02}{20.20}=0.00021+0.00599+0.00198=0.00817$.
    Percentage error $\approx0.82\%$.

  4. Question 28 · Physics · Work, Energy and Power
    The rain drop of mass 1 g, starts with zero velocity from a height of 1 km. It hits the ground with a speed of 5 m/s. The work done by the unknown resistive force is ________ J.
    (take $g=10$ m/s$^2$)

    1. $-8.75$
    2. $-8.35$
    3. $-9.55$
    4. $-9.98$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    Work-energy theorem: $W_g+W_r=\Delta K$.
    $W_g=mgh=10^{-3}\times10\times1000=10$ J; $\Delta K=\dfrac12\times10^{-3}\times25=0.0125$ J.
    $W_r=0.0125-10=-9.9875\approx-9.98$ J.

  5. Question 51 · Chemistry · Some Basic Concepts of Chemistry
    An oxide of iron contains 69.9% iron, its empirical formula, is:
    (Given : Molar mass of Fe and O are 56 and 16 g mol$^{-1}$ respectively.)

    1. $\mathrm{FeO}$
    2. $\mathrm{Fe_2O_3}$
    3. $\mathrm{Fe_3O_4}$
    4. $\mathrm{FeO_3}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    Moles in 100 g: Fe $=\dfrac{69.9}{56}=1.248$, O $=\dfrac{30.1}{16}=1.881$.
    Ratio Fe : O $=1:1.507\approx2:3$.
    Empirical formula $\mathrm{Fe_2O_3}$.

  6. Question 52 · Chemistry · Structure of Atom
    If shortest wavelength of hydrogen atom in Lyman series is $x$, then longest wavelength in Balmer series of He$^+$ is:

    1. $\dfrac{9x}{5}$
    2. $\dfrac{36x}{5}$
    3. $\dfrac{x}{4}$
    4. $\dfrac{5x}{9}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    H, Lyman shortest ($\infty\to1$): $\dfrac1x=R_H$.
    He$^+$ ($Z=2$), Balmer longest ($3\to2$): $\dfrac1\lambda=4R_H\left(\dfrac14-\dfrac19\right)=\dfrac{5R_H}{9}$.
    $\lambda=\dfrac{9}{5R_H}=\dfrac{9x}{5}$.
