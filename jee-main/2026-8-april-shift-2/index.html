Solved questions from this paper

A few questions with their official answers and worked solutions. All 75 are in the online test.

  1. Question 1 · Mathematics · Relations and Functions
    Consider the relation R on the set $\{-2,-1,0,1,2\}$ defined by $(a,b)\in R$ if and only if $1+ab>0$. Then, among the statements :
    I. The number of elements in R is 17
    II. R is an equivalence relation

    1. Only I is true
    2. Only II is true
    3. Both I and II are true
    4. Neither I nor II is true
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    $1+ab>0 \iff ab\ge 0$ (as $ab$ is an integer).
    Pairs with $ab<0$: $a,b$ non-zero with opposite signs: $2\cdot2\cdot2=8$ pairs. So $|R|=25-8=17$: I is true.
    $R$ is reflexive ($1+a^2>0$) and symmetric, but $(1,0),(0,-1)\in R$ while $(1,-1)\notin R$ ($1-1=0$). Not transitive, so II is false.
    Only I is true.

  2. Question 2 · Mathematics · Conic Sections
    The number of values of $z\in\mathbb{C}$, satisfying the equations

    $\displaystyle |z-(4+8i)|=\sqrt{10} \text{ and } |z-(3+5i)|+|z-(5+11i)|=4\sqrt{5},$
    is :

    1. $0$
    2. $2$
    3. $1$
    4. $4$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    The first is a circle with centre $(4,8)$ and radius $\sqrt{10}$.
    The second is an ellipse with foci $(3,5)$, $(5,11)$: centre $(4,8)$, $2a=4\sqrt5$, $2c=\sqrt{4+36}=2\sqrt{10}$.
    So $a^2=20$, $c^2=10$, $b^2=a^2-c^2=10$, i.e. $b=\sqrt{10}$.
    The circle has the same centre and radius equal to the semi-minor axis, so it touches the ellipse only at the two ends of the minor axis.
    Number of values $=2$.

  3. Question 26 · Physics · Units and Measurements
    A new unit $(\alpha)$ of length is chosen such that it is equal to the speed of light in vacuum. What is the distance between Venus and Earth in terms of $\alpha$ units if light takes 6 min. 40 s to cover this distance ?

    1. $200\,\alpha$
    2. $400\,\alpha$
    3. $300\,\alpha$
    4. $500\,\alpha$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    One unit $\alpha$ is the distance light travels in 1 s.
    Time taken $=6\times60+40=400$ s, so the distance $=400\,\alpha$.

  4. Question 28 · Physics · Laws of Motion
    A car moving with a speed of 54 km/h takes a turn of radius 20 m. A simple pendulum is suspended from the ceiling of the car. Determine the angle made by the string of the pendulum with the vertical during the turning. (Take $g=10\ \text{m/s}^2$)

    1. $\tan^{-1}(0.5)$
    2. $\tan^{-1}(0.75)$
    3. $\tan^{-1}(1.125)$
    4. $\tan^{-1}(0.25)$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    $v=54$ km/h $=15$ m/s; centripetal acceleration $\dfrac{v^2}{r}=\dfrac{225}{20}=11.25\ \text{m/s}^2$.
    In the car frame the bob is in equilibrium: $\tan\theta=\dfrac{v^2/r}{g}=\dfrac{11.25}{10}=1.125$.
    $\theta=\tan^{-1}(1.125)$.

  5. Question 51 · Chemistry · Some Basic Concepts of Chemistry
    Match List - I with List - II.

    List - I
    Mass of substance    		List - II
    Number of atoms
    A. 1.8 mg waterI. $2\times10^{-4}\times N_A$
    B. 9.8 mg sulphuric acidII. $1.5\times10^{-4}\times N_A$
    C. 1.8 mg carbonIII. $3\times10^{-4}\times N_A$
    D. 5.85 mg salt (NaCl)IV. $7\times10^{-4}\times N_A$
    Choose the correct answer from the options given below :

    1. A-IV, B-III, C-I, D-II
    2. A-III, B-II, C-IV, D-I
    3. A-III, B-IV, C-II, D-I
    4. A-III, B-IV, C-I, D-II
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    A: $\dfrac{1.8\times10^{-3}}{18}=10^{-4}$ mol $\mathrm{H_2O}$, 3 atoms each: $3\times10^{-4}N_A$ (III).
    B: $\dfrac{9.8\times10^{-3}}{98}=10^{-4}$ mol $\mathrm{H_2SO_4}$, 7 atoms each: $7\times10^{-4}N_A$ (IV).
    C: $\dfrac{1.8\times10^{-3}}{12}=1.5\times10^{-4}$ mol C: $1.5\times10^{-4}N_A$ (II).
    D: $\dfrac{5.85\times10^{-3}}{58.5}=10^{-4}$ mol NaCl, 2 atoms (ions) each: $2\times10^{-4}N_A$ (I).
    A-III, B-IV, C-II, D-I.

  6. Question 52 · Chemistry · Solutions
    Given below are two statements :
    Given : Molar mass of C, H, O, Cl are 12, 1, 16 and 35.5 g mol$^{-1}$, respectively
    Statement I : In 30% (w/w) solution of methanol in $\mathrm{CCl_4}$ (at T K), the mole fraction of $\mathrm{CCl_4}$ is equal to 0.33.
    Statement II : Mixture of methanol and $\mathrm{CCl_4}$ shows positive deviation from Raoult's law.
    In the light of the above statements, choose the correct answer from the options given below :

    1. Both Statement I and Statement II are true
    2. Both Statement I and Statement II are false
    3. Statement I is true but Statement II is false
    4. Statement I is false but Statement II is true
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    In 100 g: 30 g $\mathrm{CH_3OH}$ $=\dfrac{30}{32}=0.9375$ mol; 70 g $\mathrm{CCl_4}$ $=\dfrac{70}{154}=0.4545$ mol.
    $x_{\mathrm{CCl_4}}=\dfrac{0.4545}{1.392}\approx0.33$: Statement I is true.
    $\mathrm{CCl_4}$ breaks the hydrogen bonding of methanol, so the mixture shows positive deviation: Statement II is true.
