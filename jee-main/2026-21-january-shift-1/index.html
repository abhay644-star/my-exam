Solved questions from this paper

A few questions with their official answers and worked solutions. All 75 are in the online test.

  1. Question 1 · Mathematics · Relations and Functions
    The number of relations, defined on the set $\{a, b, c, d\}$, which are both reflexive and symmetric, is equal to :

    1. $16$
    2. $64$
    3. $256$
    4. $1024$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    A reflexive relation must contain all 4 pairs $(x,x)$.
    Symmetry means the off-diagonal pairs come in pairs $\{(x,y),(y,x)\}$; there are $\binom{4}{2}=6$ such pairs, each either in or out.
    Number of relations $=2^6=64$.

  2. Question 2 · Mathematics · Inverse Trigonometric Functions
    If the domain of the function $f(x)=\cos^{-1}\left(\dfrac{2x-5}{11-3x}\right)+\sin^{-1}\left(2x^2-3x+1\right)$ is the interval $[\alpha, \beta]$, then $\alpha+2\beta$ is equal to :

    1. $1$
    2. $2$
    3. $3$
    4. $5$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    $\sin^{-1}$ term: $-1\le 2x^2-3x+1\le 1$. The left inequality always holds; the right gives $x(2x-3)\le0$, i.e. $0\le x\le\dfrac32$.
    On $\left[0,\dfrac32\right]$, $11-3x>0$, so $-1\le\dfrac{2x-5}{11-3x}\le1 \iff 3x-11\le 2x-5\le 11-3x \iff x\le6$ and $x\le\dfrac{16}{5}$, both true.
    Domain $=\left[0,\dfrac32\right]$, so $\alpha+2\beta=0+3=3$.

  3. Question 26 · Physics · Units and Measurements
    Consider a modified Bernoulli equation.
    $\left(P+\dfrac{A}{Bt^2}\right)+\rho g(h+Bt)+\dfrac{1}{2}\rho V^2=\text{constant}$
    If $t$ has the dimension of time then the dimensions of A and B are _______, _______ respectively.

    1. $[\mathrm{ML^0T^{-1}}]$ and $[\mathrm{M^0LT}]$
    2. $[\mathrm{ML^0T^{-1}}]$ and $[\mathrm{M^0LT^{-1}}]$
    3. $[\mathrm{ML^0T^{-2}}]$ and $[\mathrm{M^0LT^{-1}}]$
    4. $[\mathrm{ML^0T^{-2}}]$ and $[\mathrm{M^0LT^{-2}}]$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    $Bt$ is added to the height $h$, so $[B]=\mathrm{LT^{-1}}$.
    $\dfrac{A}{Bt^2}$ has the dimension of pressure: $[A]=\mathrm{ML^{-1}T^{-2}}\cdot\mathrm{LT^{-1}}\cdot\mathrm{T^2}=\mathrm{ML^0T^{-1}}$.
    So A: $[\mathrm{ML^0T^{-1}}]$, B: $[\mathrm{M^0LT^{-1}}]$.

  4. Question 28 · Physics · Laws of Motion
    A 4 kg mass moves under the influence of a force $\vec{F}=\left(4t^3\hat{i}-3t\hat{j}\right)$ N where $t$ is the time in second. If mass starts from origin at $t=0$, the velocity and position after $t=2$ s will be :

    1. $\vec{v}=4\hat{i}-\dfrac{3}{2}\hat{j}$, $\vec{r}=\dfrac{6}{5}\hat{i}-\hat{j}$
    2. $\vec{v}=3\hat{i}+\dfrac{3}{2}\hat{j}$, $\vec{r}=\dfrac{6}{5}\hat{i}+\hat{j}$
    3. $\vec{v}=4\hat{i}+\dfrac{5}{2}\hat{j}$, $\vec{r}=\dfrac{8}{5}\hat{i}+2\hat{j}$
    4. $\vec{v}=4\hat{i}-\dfrac{3}{2}\hat{j}$, $\vec{r}=\dfrac{8}{5}\hat{i}-\hat{j}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    $\vec a=\dfrac{\vec F}{4}=t^3\hat i-\dfrac{3t}{4}\hat j$ (starting from rest at the origin).
    $\vec v=\dfrac{t^4}{4}\hat i-\dfrac{3t^2}{8}\hat j$; at $t=2$: $4\hat i-\dfrac32\hat j$.
    $\vec r=\dfrac{t^5}{20}\hat i-\dfrac{t^3}{8}\hat j$; at $t=2$: $\dfrac85\hat i-\hat j$.

  5. Question 51 · Chemistry · Some Basic Concepts of Chemistry
    80 mL of a hydrocarbon on mixing with 264 mL of oxygen in a closed U-tube undergoes complete combustion. The residual gases after cooling to 273 K occupy 224 mL. When the system is treated with KOH solution, the volume decreases to 64 mL. The formula of the hydrocarbon is :

    1. $\mathrm{C_2H_4}$
    2. $\mathrm{C_2H_2}$
    3. $\mathrm{C_4H_{10}}$
    4. $\mathrm{C_2H_6}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    KOH absorbs $\mathrm{CO_2}$: $224-64=160$ mL, and the remaining 64 mL is unused $\mathrm{O_2}$ (water has condensed).
    $\mathrm{C_xH_y+\left(x+\tfrac y4\right)O_2\rightarrow xCO_2+\tfrac y2H_2O}$: $80x=160 \Rightarrow x=2$.
    $\mathrm{O_2}$ used $=264-64=200=80\left(2+\dfrac y4\right) \Rightarrow y=2$.
    The hydrocarbon is $\mathrm{C_2H_2}$.

  6. Question 53 · Chemistry · Chemical Bonding and Molecular Structure
    Given below are two statements :
    Statement I :
    The number of species among $\mathrm{SF_4}$, $\mathrm{NH_4^+}$, $\mathrm{[NiCl_4]^{2-}}$, $\mathrm{XeF_4}$, $\mathrm{[PtCl_4]^{2-}}$, $\mathrm{SeF_4}$ and $\mathrm{[Ni(CN)_4]^{2-}}$, that have tetrahedral geometry is 3.
    Statement II :
    In the set $\mathrm{[NO_2, BeH_2, BF_3, AlCl_3]}$, all the molecules have incomplete octet around central atom.
    In the light of the above statements, choose the correct answer from the options given below :

    1. Both Statement I and Statement II are true
    2. Both Statement I and Statement II are false
    3. Statement I is true but Statement II is false
    4. Statement I is false but Statement II is true
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    Tetrahedral: $\mathrm{NH_4^+}$ and $\mathrm{[NiCl_4]^{2-}}$ only. $\mathrm{SF_4}$, $\mathrm{SeF_4}$ are see-saw; $\mathrm{XeF_4}$, $\mathrm{[PtCl_4]^{2-}}$, $\mathrm{[Ni(CN)_4]^{2-}}$ are square planar. The count is 2, so Statement I is false.
    $\mathrm{NO_2}$ (odd electron, 7 around N), $\mathrm{BeH_2}$ (4), $\mathrm{BF_3}$ and $\mathrm{AlCl_3}$ (6) all have fewer than 8 electrons around the central atom, so Statement II is true.
