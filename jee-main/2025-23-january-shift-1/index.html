Solved questions from this paper

A few questions with their official answers and worked solutions. All 75 are in the online test.

  1. Question 1 · Mathematics · Relations and Functions
    Let $f(x)=\log_e x$ and $g(x)=\dfrac{x^4-2x^3+3x^2-2x+2}{2x^2-2x+1}$. Then the domain of $fog$ is

    1. $\mathbb{R}$
    2. $[0,\infty)$
    3. $(0,\infty)$
    4. $[1,\infty)$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    $f\circ g$ needs $g(x)>0$.
    Denominator: $2x^2-2x+1=x^2+(x-1)^2>0$ for every $x$.
    Numerator: $x^4-2x^3+3x^2-2x+2=(x^2-x)^2+2(x^2-x+1)>0$, since $x^2-x+1>0$ always.
    So $g(x)>0$ for all real $x$ and the domain is $\mathbb{R}$.

  2. Question 3 · Mathematics · Complex Numbers
    Let $\left|\dfrac{\bar z-i}{2\bar z+i}\right|=\dfrac13$, $z\in\mathbb{C}$, be the equation of a circle with center at C. If the area of the triangle, whose vertices are at the points $(0,0)$, C and $(\alpha,0)$ is 11 square units, then $\alpha^2$ equals:

    1. $50$
    2. $100$
    3. $\dfrac{81}{25}$
    4. $\dfrac{121}{25}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    Let $\bar z=x+iy$. Then $9|\bar z-i|^2=|2\bar z+i|^2$:
    $9\left(x^2+(y-1)^2\right)=4x^2+(2y+1)^2 \Rightarrow 5x^2+5y^2-22y+8=0$.
    So $\bar z$ lies on a circle with centre $\left(0,\dfrac{11}{5}\right)$; $z$ is its mirror image in the real axis, so C $=\left(0,-\dfrac{11}{5}\right)$.
    Area $=\dfrac12\cdot|\alpha|\cdot\dfrac{11}{5}=11 \Rightarrow |\alpha|=10$, so $\alpha^2=100$.

  3. Question 26 · Physics · Units and Measurements
    The position of a particle moving on x-axis is given by $x(t)=A\sin t+B\cos^2t+Ct^2+D$, where $t$ is time. The dimension of $\dfrac{ABC}{D}$ is

    1. L
    2. L$^2$
    3. L$^2$T$^{-2}$
    4. L$^3$T$^{-2}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    Each term has the dimension of length: $[A]=[B]=[D]=$ L and $[C]t^2=$ L, so $[C]=$ LT$^{-2}$.
    $\left[\dfrac{ABC}{D}\right]=\dfrac{\text{L}\cdot\text{L}\cdot\text{LT}^{-2}}{\text{L}}=$ L$^2$T$^{-2}$.

  4. Question 28 · Physics · System of Particles and Rotational Motion
    A solid sphere of mass 'm' and radius 'r' is allowed to roll without slipping from the highest point of an inclined plane of length 'L' and makes an angle $30^\circ$ with the horizontal. The speed of the particle at the bottom of the plane is $v_1$. If the angle of inclination is increased to $45^\circ$ while keeping L constant. Then the new speed of the sphere at the bottom of the plane is $v_2$. The ratio $v_1^2:v_2^2$ is

    1. $1:\sqrt2$
    2. $1:\sqrt3$
    3. $1:2$
    4. $1:3$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    For rolling without slipping, $mgh=\dfrac12mv^2\left(1+\dfrac{k^2}{r^2}\right)$, so $v^2\propto h=L\sin\theta$.
    $v_1^2:v_2^2=\sin30^\circ:\sin45^\circ=\dfrac12:\dfrac{1}{\sqrt2}=1:\sqrt2$.

  5. Question 51 · Chemistry · Some Basic Concepts of Chemistry
    $2.8\times10^{-3}$ mol of $\mathrm{CO_2}$ is left after removing $10^{21}$ molecules from its '$x$' mg sample. The mass of $\mathrm{CO_2}$ taken initially is
    Given: $N_A=6.02\times10^{23}$ mol$^{-1}$

    1. 48.2 mg
    2. 98.3 mg
    3. 150.4 mg
    4. 196.2 mg
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    Moles removed $=\dfrac{10^{21}}{6.02\times10^{23}}=1.66\times10^{-3}$ mol.
    Initial moles $=2.8\times10^{-3}+1.66\times10^{-3}=4.46\times10^{-3}$ mol.
    Mass $=4.46\times10^{-3}\times44$ g $\approx0.196$ g $=196.2$ mg.

  6. Question 52 · Chemistry · Structure of Atom
    Heat treatment of muscular pain involves radiation of wavelength of about 900 nm. Which spectral line of H atom is suitable for this?
    Given : Rydberg constant $R_H=10^5$ cm$^{-1}$, $h=6.6\times10^{-34}$ J s, $c=3\times10^8$ m/s)

    1. Paschen series, $\infty\to3$
    2. Paschen series, $5\to3$
    3. Balmer series, $\infty\to2$
    4. Lyman series, $\infty\to1$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    $\dfrac1\lambda=\dfrac{1}{900\times10^{-7}\text{ cm}}=1.11\times10^4$ cm$^{-1}$.
    $R_H\left(\dfrac{1}{n_1^2}-\dfrac{1}{n_2^2}\right)=1.11\times10^4 \Rightarrow \dfrac{1}{n_1^2}-\dfrac{1}{n_2^2}=\dfrac19$.
    This is $n_1=3$, $n_2=\infty$: the Paschen series limit, $\infty\to3$.
