Solved questions from this paper

A few questions with their official answers and worked solutions. All 75 are in the online test.

  1. Question 1 · Mathematics · Relations and Functions
    Let the relation R on the set $M=\{1, 2, 3, \ldots, 16\}$ be given by
    $R=\{(x, y): 4y=5x-3,\ x, y \in M\}$.
    Then the minimum number of elements required to be added in R, in order to make the relation symmetric, is equal to

    1. $2$
    2. $3$
    3. $4$
    4. $1$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    $4y=5x-3$ needs $5x\equiv3 \pmod 4$, i.e. $x\equiv3 \pmod 4$.
    $x=3\Rightarrow y=3$; $x=7\Rightarrow y=8$; $x=11\Rightarrow y=13$; $x=15\Rightarrow y=18\notin M$.
    $R=\{(3,3),(7,8),(11,13)\}$. For symmetry add $(8,7)$ and $(13,11)$: $2$ elements.

  2. Question 2 · Mathematics · Inverse Trigonometric Functions
    If the domain of the function $f(x)=\sin^{-1}\left(\dfrac{5-x}{3+2x}\right)+\dfrac{1}{\log_e(10-x)}$ is $(-\infty, \alpha] \cup [\beta, \gamma)-\{\delta\}$, then $6(\alpha+\beta+\gamma+\delta)$ is equal to

    1. $66$
    2. $67$
    3. $68$
    4. $70$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    $\dfrac{5-x}{3+2x}\le1 \iff \dfrac{3x-2}{2x+3}\ge0 \iff x<-\dfrac32$ or $x\ge\dfrac23$.
    $\dfrac{5-x}{3+2x}\ge-1 \iff \dfrac{x+8}{2x+3}\ge0 \iff x\le-8$ or $x>-\dfrac32$.
    Together: $x\le-8$ or $x\ge\dfrac23$. The log term needs $x<10$ and $10-x\ne1$, i.e. $x\ne9$.
    Domain $(-\infty,-8]\cup\left[\dfrac23,10\right)-\{9\}$, so $6\left(-8+\dfrac23+10+9\right)=6\cdot\dfrac{35}{3}=70$.

  3. Question 26 · Physics · Units and Measurements
    Match the LIST-I with LIST-II

    List-IList-II
    A. Spring constantI. $ML^2T^{-2}K^{-1}$
    B. Thermal conductivityII. $ML^0T^{-2}$
    C. Boltzmann constantIII. $ML^2T^{-3}A^{-2}$
    D. Inductive reactanceIV. $MLT^{-3}K^{-1}$
    Choose the correct answer from the options given below:

    1. A-I, B-IV, C-II, D-III
    2. A-III, B-II, C-IV, D-I
    3. A-II, B-IV, C-I, D-III
    4. A-II, B-I, C-IV, D-III
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    Spring constant: N/m $=MT^{-2}=ML^0T^{-2}$ (II).
    Thermal conductivity: W m$^{-1}$K$^{-1}$ $=MLT^{-3}K^{-1}$ (IV).
    Boltzmann constant: J/K $=ML^2T^{-2}K^{-1}$ (I).
    Inductive reactance: ohm $=ML^2T^{-3}A^{-2}$ (III).
    A-II, B-IV, C-I, D-III.

  4. Question 28 · Physics · Gravitation
    The escape velocity from a spherical planet $A$ is 10 km/s. The escape velocity from another planet $B$ whose density and radius are 10% of those of planet $A$, is ______ m/s.

    1. $1000$
    2. $1000\sqrt2$
    3. $100\sqrt{10}$
    4. $200\sqrt5$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    $v_e=\sqrt{\dfrac{2GM}{R}}=R\sqrt{\dfrac{8\pi G\rho}{3}}$, so $v_e\propto R\sqrt{\rho}$.
    $v_B=10\ \text{km/s}\times0.1\times\sqrt{0.1}=\dfrac{10^4}{10\sqrt{10}}\ \text{m/s}=100\sqrt{10}$ m/s.

  5. Question 51 · Chemistry · Some Basic Concepts of Chemistry
    In the reaction,
    $\mathrm{2Al(s)+6HCl(aq)\longrightarrow2Al^{3+}(aq)+6Cl^-(aq)+3H_2(g)}$

    1. 12 L HCl(aq) is consumed for every 6L $\mathrm{H_2(g)}$ produced.
    2. 33.6 L $\mathrm{H_2(g)}$ is produced regardless of temperature and pressure for every mole of Al that reacts.
    3. 67.2 L $\mathrm{H_2(g)}$ at STP is produced for every mole of Al that reacts.
    4. 11.2 L $\mathrm{H_2(g)}$ at STP is produced for every mole of HCl consumed.
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    6 mol HCl give 3 mol $\mathrm{H_2}$, so 1 mol HCl gives 0.5 mol $\mathrm{H_2}=11.2$ L at STP. True.
    1 mol Al gives 1.5 mol $\mathrm{H_2}=33.6$ L only at STP (not regardless of conditions), not 67.2 L.
    HCl is in solution, so a gas-volume ratio does not apply to it.

  6. Question 52 · Chemistry · Structure of Atom
    The energy required by electrons, present in the first Bohr orbit of hydrogen atom to be excited to second Bohr orbit is ____________J mol$^{-1}$.
    Given: $R_H=2.18\times10^{-11}$ ergs.

    1. $1.635\times10^{-11}$
    2. $9.835\times10^{5}$
    3. $9.835\times10^{12}$
    4. $1.635\times10^{-18}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    $R_H=2.18\times10^{-11}$ erg $=2.18\times10^{-18}$ J.
    Per atom: $\Delta E=R_H\left(1-\dfrac14\right)=1.635\times10^{-18}$ J.
    Per mole: $1.635\times10^{-18}\times6.02\times10^{23}\approx9.8\times10^5$ J mol$^{-1}$, the option $9.835\times10^5$.
