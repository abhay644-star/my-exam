Solved questions from this paper

A few questions with their official answers and worked solutions. All 75 are in the online test.

  1. Question 1 · Mathematics · Relations and Functions
    For the function $f:[1,\infty)\to[1,\infty)$ defined by $f(x)=(x-1)^4+1$, among the two statements:
    (I) The set $S=\{x\in[1,\infty): f(x)=f^{-1}(x)\}$ contains exactly two elements, and
    (II) The set $S=\{x\in[1,\infty): f(x)=f^{-1}(x+1)\}$ is an empty set,

    1. only (I) is TRUE
    2. only (II) is TRUE
    3. both (I) and (II) are TRUE
    4. neither (I) nor (II) is TRUE
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    $f$ is strictly increasing on $[1,\infty)$, so $f(x)=f^{-1}(x)\iff f(x)=x$.
    $(x-1)^4+1=x\iff (x-1)^4=x-1\iff x=1$ or $x=2$. So (I) is true.
    For (II): $f(x)=f^{-1}(x+1)\iff f(f(x))=x+1$. Let $h(x)=f(f(x))-x-1$.
    $h(2)=f(2)-3=2-3=-1<0$, while $h(x)\to\infty$ as $x\to\infty$, and $h$ is continuous, so $h$ has a root in $(2,\infty)$.
    So the set in (II) is not empty: (II) is false. Only (I) is TRUE.

  2. Question 2 · Mathematics · Complex Numbers
    Let $S=\{z\in\mathbb{C}: z^2+4z+16=0\}$. Then $\displaystyle\sum_{z\in S}\left|z+\sqrt3\,i\right|^2$ is equal to:

    1. $42$
    2. $23$
    3. $27$
    4. $38$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    $z=\dfrac{-4\pm\sqrt{16-64}}{2}=-2\pm2\sqrt3\,i$.
    $z_1+\sqrt3 i=-2+3\sqrt3 i$: $|\,\cdot\,|^2=4+27=31$.
    $z_2+\sqrt3 i=-2-\sqrt3 i$: $|\,\cdot\,|^2=4+3=7$.
    Sum $=31+7=38$.

  3. Question 26 · Physics · Units and Measurements
    Match the LIST-I with LIST-II

    List-IList-II
    A. Planck's constantI. $\mathrm{ML^2T^{-2}}$
    B. Stopping potentialII. $\mathrm{T^{-1}}$
    C. Work functionIII. $\mathrm{ML^2T^{-1}}$
    D. Threshold frequencyIV. $\mathrm{ML^2T^{-3}A^{-1}}$
    Choose the correct answer from the options given below:

    1. A-III, B-IV, C-I, D-II
    2. A-I, B-II, C-III, D-IV
    3. A-IV, B-III, C-I, D-II
    4. A-I, B-IV, C-III, D-II
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    Planck's constant $=\dfrac{E}{\nu}$: $\mathrm{ML^2T^{-1}}$ (III).
    Stopping potential (volt) $=\dfrac{\text{energy}}{\text{charge}}=\dfrac{\mathrm{ML^2T^{-2}}}{\mathrm{AT}}=\mathrm{ML^2T^{-3}A^{-1}}$ (IV).
    Work function is an energy: $\mathrm{ML^2T^{-2}}$ (I). Threshold frequency: $\mathrm{T^{-1}}$ (II).
    A-III, B-IV, C-I, D-II.

  4. Question 27 · Physics · Motion in a Straight Line
    Two cars $A$ and $B$ are moving in the same direction along a straight line with speeds 100 km/h and 80 km/h, respectively such that car $A$ is moving ahead of car $B$. A person in car $B$ throws a stone with a speed $v$ so that it hits the car $A$ with a speed of 5 m/s. The value of $v$ is _______ km/h.

    1. $18$
    2. $28$
    3. $38$
    4. $48$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    Relative to car B, car A moves ahead at $100-80=20$ km/h.
    The stone's speed relative to A on impact is $5$ m/s $=18$ km/h.
    So $v-20=18\Rightarrow v=38$ km/h (speed relative to car B).

  5. Question 51 · Chemistry · Some Basic Concepts of Chemistry
    The correct order of total number of atoms present in
    (A) 2 moles of cyclohexane
    (B) 684 g of sucrose
    (C) 90.8 L of dihydrogen at STP
    is:

    1. C > A > B
    2. C > B > A
    3. B > C > A
    4. B > A > C
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    (A) $\mathrm{C_6H_{12}}$ has 18 atoms: $2\times18=36$ mol of atoms.
    (B) Sucrose $\mathrm{C_{12}H_{22}O_{11}}$, $M=342$: $684$ g $=2$ mol; 45 atoms each: $90$ mol of atoms.
    (C) At STP (22.7 L mol$^{-1}$): $\dfrac{90.8}{22.7}=4$ mol $\mathrm{H_2}$ = $8$ mol of atoms.
    Order: B > A > C.

  6. Question 52 · Chemistry · Structure of Atom
    The species having identical radii according to the Bohr's theory are:
    A. H (first orbit)
    B. $\mathrm{He^+}$ (first orbit)
    C. $\mathrm{He^+}$ (Second orbit)
    D. $\mathrm{Li^{2+}}$ (first orbit)
    E. $\mathrm{Be^{3+}}$ (Second orbit)
    Choose the correct answer from the options given below:

    1. A and C Only
    2. A and E Only
    3. B and E Only
    4. C and D Only
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    Bohr radius $r_n=0.529\,\dfrac{n^2}{Z}$ Å, so $r\propto\dfrac{n^2}{Z}$.
    A: $\dfrac11=1$; B: $\dfrac12$; C: $\dfrac42=2$; D: $\dfrac13$; E: $\dfrac44=1$.
    A and E have identical radii.
