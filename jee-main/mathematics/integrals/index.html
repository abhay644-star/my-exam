  1. JEE Main 2026, 2 April, Shift 1 · Question 19
    Let $[\cdot]$ denote the greatest integer function. Then the value of $\displaystyle\int_0^3\left(\frac{e^x+e^{-x}}{[x]!}\right)dx$ is :

    1. $e^2+e^3-\dfrac{1}{e^2}-\dfrac{1}{e^3}$
    2. $\dfrac{1}{2}\left(e^2+e^3-\dfrac{1}{e^2}-\dfrac{1}{e^3}\right)$
    3. $e^2+e^3-\dfrac{1}{2e^2}-\dfrac{1}{2e^3}$
    4. $\dfrac{1}{2}\left(e^2+e^3\right)-\dfrac{1}{e^2}-\dfrac{1}{e^3}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    Let $F(x)=e^x-e^{-x}$, an antiderivative of $e^x+e^{-x}$; $F(0)=0$.
    Integral $=\dfrac{F(1)-F(0)}{0!}+\dfrac{F(2)-F(1)}{1!}+\dfrac{F(3)-F(2)}{2!}$.
    $=F(2)+\dfrac{F(3)-F(2)}{2}=\dfrac{F(2)+F(3)}{2}$.
    $=\dfrac12\left(e^2+e^3-\dfrac1{e^2}-\dfrac1{e^3}\right)$.

  2. JEE Main 2026, 2 April, Shift 1 · Question 25
    If $\alpha=\displaystyle\int_0^{2\sqrt{3}}\log_2\left(x^2+4\right)dx+\int_2^4\sqrt{2^x-4}\,dx$, then $\alpha^2$ is equal to __________.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 192

    Let $g(x)=\log_2(x^2+4)$ on $[0,2\sqrt3]$; it increases from $g(0)=2$ to $g(2\sqrt3)=4$.
    Its inverse is $g^{-1}(y)=\sqrt{2^y-4}$ on $[2,4]$.
    $\displaystyle\int_0^{2\sqrt3}g+\int_2^4g^{-1}=2\sqrt3\cdot4-0\cdot2=8\sqrt3$.
    $\alpha^2=192$.

  3. JEE Main 2026, 2 April, Shift 2 · Question 17
    The value of $\displaystyle\int_0^{20\pi}(\sin^4x+\cos^4x)\,dx$ is equal to :

    1. $\dfrac{15\pi}{2}$
    2. $25\pi$
    3. $15\pi$
    4. $\dfrac{25\pi}{2}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    $\sin^4x+\cos^4x=1-\dfrac12\sin^22x=\dfrac34+\dfrac14\cos4x$.
    Over $[0, 20\pi]$ the cosine term integrates to $0$.
    Integral $=\dfrac34\cdot20\pi=15\pi$.

  4. JEE Main 2026, 2 April, Shift 2 · Question 19
    Let $f(x)=\displaystyle\int\left(\dfrac{16x+24}{x^2+2x-15}\right)dx$. If $f(4)=14\log_e(3)$ and $f(7)=\log_e(2^\alpha\cdot3^\beta)$, $\alpha, \beta \in \mathbf{N}$, then $\alpha+\beta$ is equal to :

    1. $31$
    2. $37$
    3. $39$
    4. $41$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    $\dfrac{16x+24}{(x-3)(x+5)}=\dfrac{9}{x-3}+\dfrac{7}{x+5}$.
    $f(x)=9\ln|x-3|+7\ln|x+5|+C$.
    $f(4)=7\ln9+C=14\ln3 \Rightarrow C=0$.
    $f(7)=9\ln4+7\ln12=32\ln2+7\ln3=\ln(2^{32}\cdot3^7)$.
    $\alpha+\beta=39$.

  5. JEE Main 2026, 4 April, Shift 1 · Question 17
    Let $f$ be a real polynomial of degree $n$ such that $f(x)=f'(x)f''(x)$, for all $x\in\mathbb{R}$. If $f(0)=0$, then $36\left(f'(2)+f''(2)+\int_0^2 f(x)\,dx\right)$ is equal to:

    1. $42$
    2. $46$
    3. $56$
    4. $66$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    Degrees: $n=(n-1)+(n-2) \Rightarrow n=3$. Let $f=ax^3+bx^2+cx$ (as $f(0)=0$).
    $f'f''=(3ax^2+2bx+c)(6ax+2b)=18a^2x^3+18abx^2+(4b^2+6ac)x+2bc$.
    Comparing: $a=18a^2 \Rightarrow a=\dfrac1{18}$; $c=4b^2+\dfrac c3 \Rightarrow c=6b^2$; $2bc=0 \Rightarrow b=c=0$.
    $f(x)=\dfrac{x^3}{18}$: $f'(2)=\dfrac23$, $f''(2)=\dfrac23$, $\int_0^2 f=\dfrac{16}{72}=\dfrac29$.
    $36\left(\dfrac43+\dfrac29\right)=36\cdot\dfrac{14}{9}=56$.

    Let $\displaystyle\int_{-2}^{2}\left(|\sin x|+[x\sin x]\right)dx=2(3-\cos2)+\beta$, where $[\cdot]$ is the greatest integer function. Then $\beta\sin\left(\dfrac{\beta}{2}\right)$ equals:

    1. $1$
    2. $2$
    3. $4$
    4. $8$
    Show answer and solution

    Total $=2(3-\cos2)-2x_0$, so $\beta=-2x_0$.

    The integral $\displaystyle\int_0^1\cot^{-1}\left(1+x+x^2\right)dx$ is equal to:

    1. $2\tan^{-1}2+\dfrac12\log_e\left(\dfrac54\right)+\dfrac\pi2$
    2. $2\tan^{-1}2+\dfrac12\log_e\left(\dfrac54\right)-\dfrac\pi2$

    Show answer and solution

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 17
