  1. JEE Main 2026, 2 April, Shift 1 · Question 19
    Let $[\cdot]$ denote the greatest integer function. Then the value of $\displaystyle\int_0^3\left(\frac{e^x+e^{-x}}{[x]!}\right)dx$ is :

    1. $e^2+e^3-\dfrac{1}{e^2}-\dfrac{1}{e^3}$
    2. $\dfrac{1}{2}\left(e^2+e^3-\dfrac{1}{e^2}-\dfrac{1}{e^3}\right)$
    3. $e^2+e^3-\dfrac{1}{2e^2}-\dfrac{1}{2e^3}$
    4. $\dfrac{1}{2}\left(e^2+e^3\right)-\dfrac{1}{e^2}-\dfrac{1}{e^3}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    Let $F(x)=e^x-e^{-x}$, an antiderivative of $e^x+e^{-x}$; $F(0)=0$.
    Integral $=\dfrac{F(1)-F(0)}{0!}+\dfrac{F(2)-F(1)}{1!}+\dfrac{F(3)-F(2)}{2!}$.
    $=F(2)+\dfrac{F(3)-F(2)}{2}=\dfrac{F(2)+F(3)}{2}$.
    $=\dfrac12\left(e^2+e^3-\dfrac1{e^2}-\dfrac1{e^3}\right)$.

  2. JEE Main 2026, 2 April, Shift 1 · Question 25
    If $\alpha=\displaystyle\int_0^{2\sqrt{3}}\log_2\left(x^2+4\right)dx+\int_2^4\sqrt{2^x-4}\,dx$, then $\alpha^2$ is equal to __________.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 192

    Let $g(x)=\log_2(x^2+4)$ on $[0,2\sqrt3]$; it increases from $g(0)=2$ to $g(2\sqrt3)=4$.
    Its inverse is $g^{-1}(y)=\sqrt{2^y-4}$ on $[2,4]$.
    $\displaystyle\int_0^{2\sqrt3}g+\int_2^4g^{-1}=2\sqrt3\cdot4-0\cdot2=8\sqrt3$.
    $\alpha^2=192$.

  3. JEE Main 2026, 2 April, Shift 2 · Question 17
    The value of $\displaystyle\int_0^{20\pi}(\sin^4x+\cos^4x)\,dx$ is equal to :

    1. $\dfrac{15\pi}{2}$
    2. $25\pi$
    3. $15\pi$
    4. $\dfrac{25\pi}{2}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    $\sin^4x+\cos^4x=1-\dfrac12\sin^22x=\dfrac34+\dfrac14\cos4x$.
    Over $[0, 20\pi]$ the cosine term integrates to $0$.
    Integral $=\dfrac34\cdot20\pi=15\pi$.

  4. JEE Main 2026, 2 April, Shift 2 · Question 19
    Let $f(x)=\displaystyle\int\left(\dfrac{16x+24}{x^2+2x-15}\right)dx$. If $f(4)=14\log_e(3)$ and $f(7)=\log_e(2^\alpha\cdot3^\beta)$, $\alpha, \beta \in \mathbf{N}$, then $\alpha+\beta$ is equal to :

    1. $31$
    2. $37$
    3. $39$
    4. $41$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    $\dfrac{16x+24}{(x-3)(x+5)}=\dfrac{9}{x-3}+\dfrac{7}{x+5}$.
    $f(x)=9\ln|x-3|+7\ln|x+5|+C$.
    $f(4)=7\ln9+C=14\ln3 \Rightarrow C=0$.
    $f(7)=9\ln4+7\ln12=32\ln2+7\ln3=\ln(2^{32}\cdot3^7)$.
    $\alpha+\beta=39$.

  5. JEE Main 2026, 4 April, Shift 1 · Question 17
    Let $f$ be a real polynomial of degree $n$ such that $f(x)=f'(x)f''(x)$, for all $x\in\mathbb{R}$. If $f(0)=0$, then $36\left(f'(2)+f''(2)+\int_0^2 f(x)\,dx\right)$ is equal to:

    1. $42$
    2. $46$
    3. $56$
    4. $66$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    Degrees: $n=(n-1)+(n-2) \Rightarrow n=3$. Let $f=ax^3+bx^2+cx$ (as $f(0)=0$).
    $f'f''=(3ax^2+2bx+c)(6ax+2b)=18a^2x^3+18abx^2+(4b^2+6ac)x+2bc$.
    Comparing: $a=18a^2 \Rightarrow a=\dfrac1{18}$; $c=4b^2+\dfrac c3 \Rightarrow c=6b^2$; $2bc=0 \Rightarrow b=c=0$.
    $f(x)=\dfrac{x^3}{18}$: $f'(2)=\dfrac23$, $f''(2)=\dfrac23$, $\int_0^2 f=\dfrac{16}{72}=\dfrac29$.
    $36\left(\dfrac43+\dfrac29\right)=36\cdot\dfrac{14}{9}=56$.

  6. JEE Main 2026, 4 April, Shift 1 · Question 19
    Let $\displaystyle\int_{-2}^{2}\left(|\sin x|+[x\sin x]\right)dx=2(3-\cos2)+\beta$, where $[\cdot]$ is the greatest integer function. Then $\beta\sin\left(\dfrac{\beta}{2}\right)$ equals:

    1. $1$
    2. $2$
    3. $4$
    4. $8$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    $\int_{-2}^{2}|\sin x|\,dx=2\int_0^2\sin x\,dx=2(1-\cos2)$.
    $x\sin x$ is even, increasing on $[0,2]$ from $0$ to $2\sin2\approx1.82$. Let $x_0\in(0,2)$ with $x_0\sin x_0=1$.
    So $[x\sin x]=1$ for $x_0\le|x|\le2$ and $0$ otherwise: $\int_{-2}^{2}[x\sin x]\,dx=2(2-x_0)$.
    Total $=2(3-\cos2)-2x_0$, so $\beta=-2x_0$.
    $\beta\sin\dfrac\beta2=(-2x_0)\sin(-x_0)=2x_0\sin x_0=2$.

  7. JEE Main 2026, 4 April, Shift 2 · Question 4
    If $\alpha=1$ and $\beta=1+i\sqrt2$, where $i=\sqrt{-1}$ are two roots of the equation $x^3+ax^2+bx+c=0$, $a,b,c\in\mathbb{R}$, then $\displaystyle\int_{-1}^{1}\left(x^3+ax^2+bx+c\right)dx$ is equal to:

    1. $-2$
    2. $-4$
    3. $-8$
    4. $-10$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    Real coefficients, so $1-i\sqrt2$ is also a root.
    $x^3+ax^2+bx+c=(x-1)\left[(x-1)^2+2\right]=(x-1)^3+2(x-1)$.
    Put $t=x-1$: $\displaystyle\int_{-2}^{0}(t^3+2t)\,dt=\left[\dfrac{t^4}{4}+t^2\right]_{-2}^{0}=0-(4+4)=-8$.

  8. JEE Main 2026, 4 April, Shift 2 · Question 20
    The integral $\displaystyle\int_0^1\cot^{-1}\left(1+x+x^2\right)dx$ is equal to:

    1. $2\tan^{-1}2+\dfrac12\log_e\left(\dfrac54\right)+\dfrac\pi2$
    2. $2\tan^{-1}2+\dfrac12\log_e\left(\dfrac54\right)-\dfrac\pi2$
    3. $2\tan^{-1}2-\dfrac12\log_e\left(\dfrac54\right)+\dfrac\pi2$
    4. $2\tan^{-1}2-\dfrac12\log_e\left(\dfrac54\right)-\dfrac\pi2$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    $\cot^{-1}(1+x+x^2)=\tan^{-1}\dfrac{(x+1)-x}{1+x(x+1)}=\tan^{-1}(x+1)-\tan^{-1}x$.
    Integral $=\displaystyle\int_1^2\tan^{-1}t\,dt-\int_0^1\tan^{-1}t\,dt$, with $\int\tan^{-1}t\,dt=t\tan^{-1}t-\dfrac12\ln(1+t^2)$.
    $\int_1^2=2\tan^{-1}2-\dfrac12\ln5-\dfrac\pi4+\dfrac12\ln2$; $\int_0^1=\dfrac\pi4-\dfrac12\ln2$.
    Difference $=2\tan^{-1}2-\dfrac12\ln5+\ln2-\dfrac\pi2=2\tan^{-1}2-\dfrac12\log_e\left(\dfrac54\right)-\dfrac\pi2$.

  9. JEE Main 2026, 5 April, Shift 1 · Question 18
    The value of the integral $\displaystyle\int_0^{\infty}\frac{\log_e(x)}{x^2+4}\,dx$ is:

    1. $\dfrac{\pi\log_e(2)}{2}$
    2. $\dfrac{\pi\log_e(2)}{4}$
    3. $1+\pi\log_e(2)$
    4. $2+\pi\log_e(2)$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    Put $x=2t$: $I=\displaystyle\int_0^\infty\frac{\log_e2+\log_et}{4(t^2+1)}\,2\,dt=\frac12\int_0^\infty\frac{\log_e2}{1+t^2}dt+\frac12\int_0^\infty\frac{\log_et}{1+t^2}dt$.
    The second integral is $0$ (substituting $t\to\dfrac1t$ changes its sign).
    $I=\dfrac12\cdot\log_e2\cdot\dfrac\pi2=\dfrac{\pi\log_e2}{4}$.

  10. JEE Main 2026, 5 April, Shift 1 · Question 20
    The value of the integral $\displaystyle\int_{\pi/6}^{\pi/3}\left(\frac{4-\operatorname{cosec}^2x}{\cos^4x}\right)dx$ is:

    1. $\dfrac{11}{\sqrt3}$
    2. $\dfrac{16}{\sqrt3}$
    3. $\dfrac{32}{3\sqrt3}$
    4. $\dfrac{64}{3\sqrt3}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    Put $t=\tan x$, $dx=\dfrac{dt}{1+t^2}$; $\sec^2x=1+t^2$ and $\operatorname{cosec}^2x=\dfrac{1+t^2}{t^2}$.
    Integrand: $(1+t^2)^2\left(4-\dfrac{1+t^2}{t^2}\right)\dfrac{1}{1+t^2}=4(1+t^2)-\dfrac{(1+t^2)^2}{t^2}=2+3t^2-\dfrac{1}{t^2}$.
    $\displaystyle\int_{1/\sqrt3}^{\sqrt3}\left(2+3t^2-\frac1{t^2}\right)dt=\left[2t+t^3+\frac1t\right]_{1/\sqrt3}^{\sqrt3}$.
    $=\left(2\sqrt3+3\sqrt3+\dfrac1{\sqrt3}\right)-\left(\dfrac2{\sqrt3}+\dfrac1{3\sqrt3}+\sqrt3\right)=\dfrac{16}{\sqrt3}-\dfrac{16}{3\sqrt3}=\dfrac{32}{3\sqrt3}$.

  11. JEE Main 2026, 5 April, Shift 2 · Question 18
    Let $(2^{1-a}+2^{1+a})$, $f(a)$, $(3^a+3^{-a})$ be in A.P. and $\alpha$ be the minimum value of $f(a)$. Then the value of the integral $\displaystyle\int_{\log_e(\alpha-1)}^{\log_e(\alpha)}\dfrac{dx}{(e^{2x}-e^{-2x})}$ is :

    1. $\dfrac12\log_e\left(\dfrac43\right)$
    2. $\dfrac14\log_e\left(\dfrac43\right)$
    3. $\dfrac12\log_e\left(\dfrac85\right)$
    4. $\dfrac14\log_e\left(\dfrac85\right)$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    $f(a)=\dfrac{2(2^a+2^{-a})+(3^a+3^{-a})}{2}\ge\dfrac{4+2}{2}=3$ (equality at $a=0$), so $\alpha=3$.
    $I=\displaystyle\int_{\ln2}^{\ln3}\dfrac{e^{2x}\,dx}{e^{4x}-1}$. Put $u=e^{2x}$, $du=2u\,dx$: $I=\dfrac12\displaystyle\int_4^9\dfrac{du}{u^2-1}=\dfrac14\left[\ln\dfrac{u-1}{u+1}\right]_4^9$.
    $=\dfrac14\left(\ln\dfrac45-\ln\dfrac35\right)=\dfrac14\log_e\left(\dfrac43\right)$.

  12. JEE Main 2026, 6 April, Shift 1 · Question 18
    The value of the integral $\displaystyle\int_{-\pi/4}^{\pi/4}\left(\dfrac{32\cos^4x}{1+e^{\sin x}}\right)dx$ is:

    1. $4\pi+2$
    2. $3\pi+8$
    3. $3\pi+4$
    4. $4\pi+3$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    Replacing $x$ by $-x$ and adding: $2I=\displaystyle\int_{-\pi/4}^{\pi/4}32\cos^4x\,dx$, so $I=32\displaystyle\int_0^{\pi/4}\cos^4x\,dx$.
    $\cos^4x=\dfrac{3+4\cos2x+\cos4x}{8}$, and $\displaystyle\int_0^{\pi/4}\cos^4x\,dx=\dfrac18\left(\dfrac{3\pi}4+2+0\right)$.
    $I=4\left(\dfrac{3\pi}{4}+2\right)=3\pi+8$.

  13. JEE Main 2026, 6 April, Shift 2 · Question 20
    The value of the integral $\displaystyle\int_{-1}^{1}\left(\dfrac{x^3+|x|+1}{x^2+2|x|+1}\right)dx$ is equal to :

    1. $3\log_e2$
    2. $2\log_e2$
    3. $5\log_e3$
    4. $3\log_e3$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    The part $\dfrac{x^3}{x^2+2|x|+1}$ is odd, so its integral is $0$.
    The rest is even: $2\displaystyle\int_0^1\dfrac{x+1}{(x+1)^2}\,dx=2\int_0^1\dfrac{dx}{x+1}=2\log_e2$.

  14. JEE Main 2026, 8 April, Shift 2 · Question 16
    The value of the integral $\displaystyle\int_0^2\dfrac{\sqrt{x\left(x^2+x+1\right)}}{\left(\sqrt{x+1}\right)\left(\sqrt{x^4+x^2+1}\right)}\,dx$ is equal to :

    1. $\dfrac{1}{3}\log_e\left(3-2\sqrt{2}\right)$
    2. $\dfrac{2}{3}\log_e\left(4+\sqrt{2}\right)$
    3. $\dfrac{2}{3}\log_e\left(3+2\sqrt{2}\right)$
    4. $\dfrac{1}{3}\log_e\left(1+6\sqrt{2}\right)$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    $x^4+x^2+1=(x^2+x+1)(x^2-x+1)$, so the integrand is $\dfrac{\sqrt x}{\sqrt{(x+1)(x^2-x+1)}}=\dfrac{\sqrt x}{\sqrt{1+x^3}}$.
    Put $t=x^{3/2}$, $dt=\dfrac32\sqrt x\,dx$: $I=\dfrac23\displaystyle\int_0^{2\sqrt2}\dfrac{dt}{\sqrt{1+t^2}}=\dfrac23\left[\ln\left(t+\sqrt{1+t^2}\right)\right]_0^{2\sqrt2}$.
    $I=\dfrac23\log_e\left(2\sqrt2+3\right)$.

  15. JEE Main 2026, 8 April, Shift 2 · Question 22
    If $\displaystyle\int_{\pi/6}^{\pi/4}\left(\cot\left(x-\dfrac{\pi}{3}\right)\cot\left(x+\dfrac{\pi}{3}\right)+1\right)dx=\alpha\log_e\left(\sqrt{3}-1\right)$, then $9\alpha^2$ is equal to __________.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 12

    Let $A=x-\dfrac\pi3$, $B=x+\dfrac\pi3$, so $B-A=\dfrac{2\pi}3$ and $\cot(B-A)=\dfrac{\cot A\cot B+1}{\cot A-\cot B}$.
    Hence $\cot A\cot B+1=-\dfrac1{\sqrt3}(\cot A-\cot B)$, and the integral is $-\dfrac1{\sqrt3}\left[\ln\left|\dfrac{\sin(x-\pi/3)}{\sin(x+\pi/3)}\right|\right]_{\pi/6}^{\pi/4}$.
    At $\dfrac\pi4$: $\dfrac{\sin15^\circ}{\sin105^\circ}=\dfrac{\sqrt3-1}{\sqrt3+1}=2-\sqrt3$. At $\dfrac\pi6$: $\dfrac{1/2}{1}=\dfrac12$.
    Integral $=-\dfrac1{\sqrt3}\ln\left(2(2-\sqrt3)\right)=-\dfrac1{\sqrt3}\ln(\sqrt3-1)^2=-\dfrac2{\sqrt3}\ln(\sqrt3-1)$.
    $\alpha=-\dfrac2{\sqrt3}$, so $9\alpha^2=12$.
