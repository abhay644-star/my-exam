  1. JEE Main 2026, 2 April, Shift 1 · Question 23
    Let $a, b, c \in \{1,2,3,4\}$. If the probability, that $ax^2+2\sqrt{2}\,bx+c>0$ for all $x \in \mathbb{R}$, is $\dfrac{m}{n}$, $\gcd(m,n)=1$, then $m+n$ is equal to __________.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 81

    Since $a>0$, we need discriminant $8b^2-4ac<0$, i.e. $ac>2b^2$. Total choices $4^3=64$.
    $b=1$: $ac>2$; of the 16 pairs only $(1,1),(1,2),(2,1)$ fail, so $13$.
    $b=2$: $ac>8$: $(3,3),(3,4),(4,3),(4,4)$, so $4$.
    $b=3,4$: $ac>18$ impossible.
    Probability $=\dfrac{17}{64}$, so $m+n=81$.

  2. JEE Main 2026, 4 April, Shift 2 · Question 21
    From a month of 31 days, 3 different dates are selected at random. If the probability that these dates are in an increasing A.P. is equal to $\dfrac ab$, where $a,b\in\mathbb{N}$ and $\gcd(a,b)=1$, then $a+b$ is equal to __________

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 944

    Total selections: ${}^{31}C_3=4495$.
    A.P.s $a,a+d,a+2d$ with $a+2d\le31$: for common difference $d$ there are $31-2d$ of them, $d=1,\ldots,15$.
    Favourable $=\sum_{d=1}^{15}(31-2d)=465-240=225$.
    Probability $=\dfrac{225}{4495}=\dfrac{45}{899}$, so $a+b=944$.

  3. JEE Main 2026, 23 January, Shift 1 · Question 23
    From the first 100 natural numbers, two numbers first a and then b are selected randomly without replacement. If the probability that $a-b\ge10$ is $\dfrac mn$, $\gcd(m, n)=1$, then $m+n$ is equal to __________.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 311

    Ordered pairs $(a,b)$, $a\ne b$: $100\times99=9900$.
    For $a-b=d$ ($10\le d\le99$) there are $100-d$ pairs, so the favourable count is $1+2+\ldots+90=4095$.
    $\dfrac{4095}{9900}=\dfrac{91}{220}$, so $m+n=311$.

  4. JEE Main 2026, 24 January, Shift 2 · Question 22
    Let S be a set of 5 elements and P(S) denote the power set of S. Let E be an event of choosing an ordered pair (A, B) from the set $P(S)\times P(S)$ such that $A\cap B=\emptyset$. If the probability of the event E is $\dfrac{3^p}{2^q}$, where $p, q\in\mathbb{N}$, then $p+q$ is equal to __________

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 15

    Each element of S can be in A only, in B only, or in neither: $3^5$ favourable pairs out of $(2^5)^2=2^{10}$.
    Probability $=\dfrac{3^5}{2^{10}}$, so $p+q=5+10=15$.

  5. JEE Main 2025, 23 January, Shift 1 · Question 8
    One die has two faces marked 1, two faces marked 2, one face marked 3 and one face marked 4. Another die has one face marked 1, two faces marked 2, two faces marked 3 and one face marked 4. The probability of getting the sum of numbers to be 4 or 5, when both the dice are thrown together, is

    1. $\dfrac23$
    2. $\dfrac12$
    3. $\dfrac49$
    4. $\dfrac35$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    Count the 36 equally likely face pairs.
    Sum 4: (1,3) $2\times2=4$, (2,2) $2\times2=4$, (3,1) $1\times1=1$: 9 ways.
    Sum 5: (1,4) $2\times1=2$, (2,3) $2\times2=4$, (3,2) $1\times2=2$, (4,1) $1\times1=1$: 9 ways.
    Probability $=\dfrac{18}{36}=\dfrac12$.

  6. JEE Main 2025, 23 January, Shift 2 · Question 7
    A board has 16 squares as shown in the figure :
    Out of these 16 squares, two squares are chosen at random. The probability that they have no side in common is :

    1. $\dfrac{23}{30}$
    2. $\dfrac{3}{5}$
    3. $\dfrac{7}{10}$
    4. $\dfrac{4}{5}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    Number of ways to choose 2 squares $=\dbinom{16}{2}=120$.
    Pairs sharing a side: each of the 4 rows has 3 adjacent pairs and so does each of the 4 columns, giving $24$.
    Probability $=\dfrac{120-24}{120}=\dfrac{96}{120}=\dfrac45$.

  7. JEE Main 2025, 28 January, Shift 1 · Question 9
    Two number $k_1$ and $k_2$ are randomly chosen from the set of natural numbers. Then, the probability that the value of $i^{k_1}+i^{k_2}$, $(i=\sqrt{-1})$ is non-zero, equals

    1. $\dfrac{1}{4}$
    2. $\dfrac{1}{2}$
    3. $\dfrac{3}{4}$
    4. $\dfrac{2}{3}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    Only the remainders of $k_1, k_2$ on division by 4 matter, and each of the 16 pairs of remainders is equally likely.
    $i^{k_1}+i^{k_2}=0$ exactly when $i^{k_2}=-i^{k_1}$, i.e. $k_2\equiv k_1+2 \pmod 4$: 4 of the 16 pairs.
    Probability of a non-zero value $=1-\dfrac{4}{16}=\dfrac34$.

  8. JEE Main 2025, 28 January, Shift 2 · Question 8
    Let S be the set of all the words that can be formed by arranging all the letters of the word GARDEN. From the set S, one word is selected at random. The probability that the selected word will <b>NOT</b> have vowels in alphabetical order is :

    1. $\dfrac{1}{3}$
    2. $\dfrac{1}{2}$
    3. $\dfrac{2}{3}$
    4. $\dfrac{1}{4}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    The vowels are A and E. In any arrangement A comes before E or after E, and by symmetry each happens in exactly half of the $6!$ words.
    So the probability that they are not in alphabetical order is $\dfrac12$.
