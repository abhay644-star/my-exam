  1. JEE Main 2026, 2 April, Shift 1 · Question 9
    Let the mid points of the sides of a triangle $ABC$ be $\left(\dfrac{5}{2},7\right), \left(\dfrac{5}{2},3\right)$ and $(4,5)$. If its incentre is $(h,k)$, then $3h+k$ is equal to :

    1. $11$
    2. $12$
    3. $13$
    4. $14$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    With midpoints $D\left(\tfrac52,7\right), E\left(\tfrac52,3\right), F(4,5)$, the vertices are $E+F-D=(4,1)$, $D+F-E=(4,9)$, $D+E-F=(1,5)$.
    Take $A(4,1), B(4,9), C(1,5)$: $a=BC=5$, $b=CA=5$, $c=AB=8$.
    $h=\dfrac{5\cdot4+5\cdot4+8\cdot1}{18}=\dfrac{8}{3}$, $k=\dfrac{5\cdot1+5\cdot9+8\cdot5}{18}=5$.
    $3h+k=8+5=13$.

  2. JEE Main 2026, 4 April, Shift 1 · Question 11
    Let the line $L_1: x+3=0$ intersect the lines $L_2: x-y=0$ and $L_3: 3x+y=0$ at the points $A$ and $B$, respectively. Let the bisector of the obtuse angle between the lines $L_2$ and $L_3$ intersect the line $L_1$ at the point $C$. Then $BC^2:AC^2$ is equal to:

    1. $5:1$
    2. $1:5$
    3. $2:3$
    4. $3:2$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    $L_2$ and $L_3$ meet at the origin $O$. $A=(-3,-3)$, $B=(-3,9)$.
    $\overrightarrow{OA}\cdot\overrightarrow{OB}=9-27<0$, so $\angle AOB$ is the obtuse angle between $L_2$ and $L_3$, and its bisector meets $AB$ (on $L_1$) at $C$.
    By the angle bisector theorem, $\dfrac{BC}{AC}=\dfrac{OB}{OA}=\dfrac{\sqrt{90}}{\sqrt{18}}=\sqrt5$.
    So $BC^2:AC^2=5:1$.

  3. JEE Main 2026, 4 April, Shift 1 · Question 12
    Let the vertex $A$ of a triangle $ABC$ be $(1,2)$, and the mid-point of the side $AB$ be $(5,-1)$. If the centroid of this triangle is $(3,4)$ and its circumcenter is $(\alpha,\beta)$, then $21(\alpha+\beta)$ is equal to:

    1. $309$
    2. $403$
    3. $497$
    4. $524$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    $B=(2\cdot5-1,\ 2(-1)-2)=(9,-4)$; centroid $(3,4)$ gives $C=(9-1-9,\ 12-2+4)=(-1,14)$.
    Circumcentre $(x,y)$ equidistant from $A$ and $B$: $4x-3y=23$.
    Equidistant from $A$ and $C$: $-x+6y=48$.
    Solving: $y=\dfrac{215}{21}$, $x=\dfrac{282}{21}$.
    $21(\alpha+\beta)=282+215=497$.

  4. JEE Main 2026, 4 April, Shift 2 · Question 23
    Let A, B be points on the two half-lines $x-\sqrt3|y|=\alpha$, $\alpha>0$ at a distance of $\alpha$ from their point of intersection P. The line segment AB meets the angle bisector of the given half-lines at the point Q. If $PQ=\dfrac92$ and R is the radius of the circumcircle of $\triangle PAB$, then $\dfrac{\alpha^2}{R}$ is equal to __________

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 9

    The half-lines $x=\alpha+\sqrt3|y|$ start at $P(\alpha,0)$ and make angles $\pm30^\circ$ with the $x$-axis, so $\angle APB=60^\circ$.
    With $PA=PB=\alpha$, triangle $PAB$ is equilateral of side $\alpha$; the bisector (the $x$-axis) meets $AB$ at its midpoint $Q$.
    $PQ=\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}\alpha=\dfrac92\Rightarrow\alpha=3\sqrt3$.
    $R=\dfrac{\alpha}{\sqrt3}=3$, so $\dfrac{\alpha^2}{R}=\dfrac{27}{3}=9$.

  5. JEE Main 2026, 5 April, Shift 1 · Question 11
    In an equilateral triangle PQR, let the vertex P be at $(3, 5)$ and the side QR be along the line $x + y = 4$. If the orthocentre of the triangle PQR is $(\alpha, \beta)$, then $9(\alpha + \beta)$ is equal to:

    1. $16$
    2. $27$
    3. $36$
    4. $48$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    In an equilateral triangle the orthocentre is the centroid, which lies on the median from P at $\dfrac23$ of the way to the midpoint M of QR.
    M is the foot of the perpendicular from P to $x+y=4$: $M=(3,5)-\dfrac{3+5-4}{2}(1,1)=(1,3)$.
    Centroid $=\dfrac{P+2M}{3}=\left(\dfrac53,\dfrac{11}{3}\right)$.
    $9(\alpha+\beta)=9\cdot\dfrac{16}{3}=48$.

  6. JEE Main 2026, 5 April, Shift 2 · Question 22
    From the point $(-1,-1)$, two rays are sent making angles of $45^\circ$ with the line $x+y=0$. These rays get reflected from the mirror $x+2y=1$. If the equations of the reflected rays are $ax+by=9$ and $cx+dy=7$, $a,b,c,d\in\mathbb{Z}$, then the value of $ad+bc$ is __________.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 7

    Lines through $(-1,-1)$ at $45^\circ$ to $x+y=0$ are $y=-1$ and $x=-1$. They meet the mirror at $(3,-1)$ and $(-1,1)$.
    Each reflected ray passes through the image of $(-1,-1)$ in $x+2y-1=0$: $(-1,-1)-\dfrac{2(-4)}{5}(1,2)=\left(\dfrac35,\dfrac{11}{5}\right)$.
    Through $(3,-1)$: slope $-\dfrac43$, line $4x+3y=9$, so $a=4$, $b=3$.
    Through $(-1,1)$: slope $\dfrac34$, line $-3x+4y=7$, so $c=-3$, $d=4$.
    $ad+bc=16-9=7$.

    2. $x+y-14xy=0$

  8. JEE Main 2026, 21 January, Shift 1 · Question 10
    Let a point A lie between the parallel lines $L_1$ and $L_2$ such that its distances from $L_1$ and $L_2$ are 6 and 3 units, respectively. Then the area (in sq. units) of the equilateral triangle ABC, where the points B and C lie on the lines $L_1$ and $L_2$, respectively, is :

    1. $12\sqrt{2}$
    2. $15\sqrt{6}$
    3. $21\sqrt{3}$
    4. $27$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    Take A at the origin, $L_1: y=6$, $L_2: y=-3$. Let AB make angle $\theta$ with the $x$-axis and AC angle $\theta-60^\circ$, side $s$.
    $s\sin\theta=6$ and $s\sin(\theta-60^\circ)=-3$, so $\sin\theta=-2\sin(\theta-60^\circ)=-\sin\theta+\sqrt3\cos\theta \Rightarrow \tan\theta=\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}$.
    Then $\sin\theta=\sqrt{\dfrac37}$ and $s^2=\dfrac{36}{3/7}=84$.
    Area $=\dfrac{\sqrt3}{4}\cdot84=21\sqrt3$.

    Let $A(1, 2)$ and $C(-3, -6)$ be two diagonally opposite vertices of a rhombus, whose sides AD and BC are parallel to the line $7x-y=14$. If $B(\alpha, \beta)$ and $D(\gamma, \delta)$ are the other two vertices, then $|\alpha+\beta+\gamma+\delta|$ is equal to

    1. $1$
    2. $3$
    3. $6$
    4. $9$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    Diagonals bisect each other at right angles at $M(-1,-2)$. AC has slope 2, so BD has slope $-\dfrac12$: $x+2y+5=0$.
    AD has slope 7 through A: $y-2=7(x-1)$. Meeting BD: $D=\left(\dfrac13,-\dfrac83\right)$, and $B=2M-D=\left(-\dfrac73,-\dfrac43\right)$.
    $\alpha+\beta+\gamma+\delta=-\dfrac73-\dfrac43+\dfrac13-\dfrac83=-6$, so the answer is 6.

    Let ABC be an equilateral triangle with orthocenter at the origin and the side BC on the line $x+2\sqrt2\,y=4$. If the co-ordinates of the vertex A are $(\alpha, \beta)$, then the greatest integer less than or equal to $\left|\alpha+\sqrt2\beta\right|$ is

    Answer: 28

    Take the lines $y=0$ and $y=5$, and $P(0, 1)$. Let side $s$ and $\vec{PQ}=s(\cos\theta, \sin\theta)$ with $s\sin\theta=-1$; $R$ is obtained by turning $\vec{PQ}$ through $60^\circ$, and must have $y$-increase 4.
    $s\sin(\theta+60^\circ)=\dfrac{-1}{2}+\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}s\cos\theta=4$, so $s\cos\theta=3\sqrt3$.
    $s^2=1+27=28$.

  28. JEE Main 2025, 23 January, Shift 1 · Question 9
    Let the area of a $\Delta$PQR with vertices P(5, 4), Q($-2$, 4) and R(a, b) be 35 square units. If its orthocenter and centroid are $\mathrm{O}\left(2,\dfrac{14}{5}\right)$ and C(c, d) respectively, then $c+2d$ is equal to

    1. $2$
    2. $\dfrac73$
    3. $\dfrac83$
    4. $3$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    PQ lies on $y=4$, so the altitude from R is vertical and passes through O: $a=2$.
    The altitude from P is perpendicular to QR $=(4,\,b-4)$ and passes through O: $(2-5)\cdot4+\left(\dfrac{14}{5}-4\right)(b-4)=0 \Rightarrow b-4=-10$, so $b=-6$ (the area is then $\dfrac12\cdot7\cdot10=35$, as required).
    Centroid: $c=\dfrac{5-2+2}{3}=\dfrac53$, $d=\dfrac{4+4-6}{3}=\dfrac23$.
    $c+2d=\dfrac53+\dfrac43=3$.

  29. JEE Main 2025, 23 January, Shift 2 · Question 8
    A rod of length eight units moves such that its ends A and B always lie on the lines $x-y+2=0$ and $y+2=0$, respectively. If the locus of the point P, that divides the rod AB internally in the ratio $2:1$ is $9(x^2+\alpha y^2+\beta xy+\gamma x+28y)-76=0$, then $\alpha-\beta-\gamma$ is equal to :

    1. $21$
    2. $22$
    3. $23$
    4. $24$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    Let $A=(a, a+2)$ and $B=(b,-2)$. Then $P=\dfrac{A+2B}{3}=\left(\dfrac{a+2b}{3},\dfrac{a-2}{3}\right)=(h,k)$, so $a=3k+2$ and $b=\dfrac{3h-3k-2}{2}$.
    $AB=8$: $(a-b)^2+(a+4)^2=64$, with $a-b=\dfrac{9k-3h+6}{2}$ and $a+4=3k+6$.
    $\Rightarrow 9(3k-h+2)^2+36(k+2)^2=256 \Rightarrow 9h^2+117k^2-54hk-36h+252k-76=0$.
    Locus: $9(x^2+13y^2-6xy-4x+28y)-76=0$, so $\alpha=13$, $\beta=-6$, $\gamma=-4$.
    $\alpha-\beta-\gamma=13+6+4=23$.

  30. JEE Main 2025, 24 January, Shift 1 · Question 9
    Let the lines $3x-4y-\alpha=0$, $8x-11y-33=0$, and $2x-3y+\lambda=0$ be concurrent. If the image of the point $(1, 2)$ in the line $2x-3y+\lambda=0$ is $\left(\dfrac{57}{13}, \dfrac{-40}{13}\right)$, then $|\alpha\lambda|$ is equal to

    1. $101$
    2. $84$
    3. $91$
    4. $113$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    The midpoint of $(1,2)$ and its image, $\left(\dfrac{35}{13},-\dfrac{7}{13}\right)$, lies on the line: $\dfrac{70}{13}+\dfrac{21}{13}+\lambda=0 \Rightarrow \lambda=-7$.
    $2x-3y=7$ and $8x-11y=33$ meet at $(11,5)$.
    Concurrency: $33-20-\alpha=0 \Rightarrow \alpha=13$.
    $|\alpha\lambda|=91$.

  31. JEE Main 2025, 24 January, Shift 2 · Question 11
    Let the points $\left(\dfrac{11}{2}, \alpha\right)$ lie on or inside the triangle with sides $x+y=11$, $x+2y=16$ and $2x+3y=29$. Then the product of the smallest and the largest values of $\alpha$ is equal to :

    1. $22$
    2. $33$
    3. $44$
    4. $55$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    The vertices are $(6,5)$, $(4,7)$ and $(10,3)$.
    The line $x=\dfrac{11}{2}$ lies between $x=4$ and $x=6$, so it cuts the sides $x+y=11$ (at $y=\dfrac{11}{2}$) and $2x+3y=29$ (at $y=6$).
    So $\dfrac{11}{2}\le\alpha\le6$ and the product is $33$.

  32. JEE Main 2025, 28 January, Shift 2 · Question 10
    Two equal sides of an isosceles triangle are along $-x+2y=4$ and $x+y=4$. If m is the slope of its third side, then the sum, of all possible distinct values of m, is :

    1. $12$
    2. $6$
    3. $-6$
    4. $-2\sqrt{10}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    The equal sides have slopes $\dfrac12$ and $-1$; the third side makes equal angles with both.
    $\left|\dfrac{m-\frac12}{1+\frac m2}\right|=\left|\dfrac{m+1}{1-m}\right|$, i.e. $\dfrac{2m-1}{2+m}=\pm\dfrac{m+1}{1-m}$.
    With $+$: $3m^2+3=0$, no real root. With $-$: $m^2-6m-1=0$, two real roots with sum $6$.

  33. JEE Main 2025, 29 January, Shift 1 · Question 10
    Let ABC be a triangle formed by the lines $7x-6y+3=0$, $x+2y-31=0$ and $9x-2y-19=0$. Let the point (h, k) be the image of the centroid of $\Delta$ABC in the line $3x+6y-53=0$. Then $h^2+k^2+hk$ is equal to :

    1. $36$
    2. $40$
    3. $47$
    4. $37$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    Vertices: $(9,11)$, $(5,13)$, $(3,4)$; centroid $G=\left(\tfrac{17}{3},\tfrac{28}{3}\right)$.
    $3x+6y-53$ at $G$ equals $17+56-53=20$, so the image is $G-\dfrac{2\cdot20}{45}(3,6)=(3,4)$.

  34. JEE Main 2025, 29 January, Shift 2 · Question 10
    Let the line $x+y=1$ meet the axes of $x$ and $y$ at A and B, respectively. A right angled triangle AMN is inscribed in the triangle OAB, where O is the origin and the points M and N lie on the lines OB and AB, respectively. If the area of the triangle AMN is $\dfrac49$ of the area of the triangle OAB and AN : NB $=\lambda:1$, then the sum of all possible value(s) of is $\lambda$ :

    1. $\dfrac12$
    2. $2$
    3. $\dfrac52$
    4. $\dfrac{13}{6}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    $A=(1,0)$, $B=(0,1)$, and $N=\left(\dfrac{1}{1+\lambda},\dfrac{\lambda}{1+\lambda}\right)$. With the right angle at N, MN is perpendicular to AB, so M is where the line through N with slope 1 meets the $y$-axis: $M=\left(0,\dfrac{\lambda-1}{\lambda+1}\right)$, which lies on OB only if $\lambda\ge1$.
    $AN=\dfrac{\sqrt2\lambda}{1+\lambda}$, $MN=\dfrac{\sqrt2}{1+\lambda}$, so area $=\dfrac{\lambda}{(1+\lambda)^2}=\dfrac49\cdot\dfrac12$.
    $2\lambda^2-5\lambda+2=0\Rightarrow\lambda=2$ or $\tfrac12$; only $\lambda=2$ keeps M on OB. Sum $=2$.
