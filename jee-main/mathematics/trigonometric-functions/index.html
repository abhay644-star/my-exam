  1. JEE Main 2026, 2 April, Shift 1 · Question 11
    If $\sin\left(\dfrac{\pi}{18}\right)\sin\left(\dfrac{5\pi}{18}\right)\sin\left(\dfrac{7\pi}{18}\right)=K$, then the value of $\sin\left(\dfrac{10K\pi}{3}\right)$ is :

    1. $\dfrac{\sqrt{3}+1}{2\sqrt{2}}$
    2. $\dfrac{\sqrt{3}-1}{\sqrt{2}}$
    3. $\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$
    4. $\dfrac{1}{2}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    $K=\sin10^\circ\sin50^\circ\sin70^\circ=\sin10^\circ\sin(60^\circ-10^\circ)\sin(60^\circ+10^\circ)=\dfrac{\sin30^\circ}{4}=\dfrac18$.
    $\dfrac{10K\pi}{3}=\dfrac{10\pi}{24}=\dfrac{5\pi}{12}$.
    $\sin75^\circ=\dfrac{\sqrt3+1}{2\sqrt2}$.

  2. JEE Main 2026, 2 April, Shift 1 · Question 12
    Let $S=\{x \in [-\pi,\pi] : \sin x\,(\sin x+\cos x)=a,\ a \in \mathbb{Z}\}$. Then $n(S)$ is equal to :

    1. $3$
    2. $6$
    3. $7$
    4. $9$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    $\sin x(\sin x+\cos x)=\dfrac12+\dfrac{1}{\sqrt2}\sin\left(2x-\dfrac\pi4\right)\in\left[\dfrac{1-\sqrt2}{2},\dfrac{1+\sqrt2}{2}\right]$, so $a\in\{0,1\}$.
    $a=0$: $\sin x=0 \Rightarrow x=-\pi,0,\pi$; or $\tan x=-1 \Rightarrow x=-\dfrac\pi4,\dfrac{3\pi}4$: $5$ solutions.
    $a=1$: $\sin x\cos x=\cos^2x \Rightarrow \cos x=0$ ($x=\pm\dfrac\pi2$) or $\tan x=1$ ($x=\dfrac\pi4,-\dfrac{3\pi}4$): $4$ solutions.
    $n(S)=9$.

  3. JEE Main 2026, 2 April, Shift 2 · Question 13
    Let $P=\{\theta \in [0, 4\pi] : \tan^2\theta \neq 1\}$ and $S=\{a \in \mathbf{Z} : 2(\cos^8\theta-\sin^8\theta)\sec 2\theta=a^2,\ \theta \in P\}$. Then $n(S)$ is :

    1. $0$
    2. $1$
    3. $2$
    4. $3$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    $\cos^8\theta-\sin^8\theta=(\cos^4\theta-\sin^4\theta)(\cos^4\theta+\sin^4\theta)=\cos2\theta\left(1-\dfrac12\sin^22\theta\right)$.
    $\tan^2\theta\neq1 \Rightarrow \cos2\theta\neq0$, so the expression is $2-\sin^22\theta$ with $\sin^22\theta\neq1$.
    Hence $a^2 \in (1, 2]$, which contains no perfect square.
    $n(S)=0$.

  4. JEE Main 2026, 4 April, Shift 1 · Question 23
    If $A=\dfrac{\sin3^\circ}{\cos9^\circ}+\dfrac{\sin9^\circ}{\cos27^\circ}+\dfrac{\sin27^\circ}{\cos81^\circ}$ and $B=\tan81^\circ-\tan3^\circ$, then $\dfrac{B}{A}$ is equal to ______.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 2

    $\tan3\theta-\tan\theta=\dfrac{\sin2\theta}{\cos3\theta\cos\theta}=\dfrac{2\sin\theta}{\cos3\theta}$, so $\dfrac{\sin\theta}{\cos3\theta}=\dfrac12(\tan3\theta-\tan\theta)$.
    $A=\dfrac12\left[(\tan9^\circ-\tan3^\circ)+(\tan27^\circ-\tan9^\circ)+(\tan81^\circ-\tan27^\circ)\right]=\dfrac12(\tan81^\circ-\tan3^\circ)=\dfrac B2$.
    $\dfrac BA=2$.

  5. JEE Main 2026, 5 April, Shift 1 · Question 6
    Let $\tan A, \tan B$, where $A, B \in \left(-\dfrac{\pi}{2}, \dfrac{\pi}{2}\right)$, be the roots of the quadratic equation $x^2 - 2x - 5 = 0$. Then $20\sin^2\left(\dfrac{A+B}{2}\right)$ is equal to:

    1. $10+\sqrt{10}$
    2. $10-2\sqrt{10}$
    3. $10-3\sqrt{10}$
    4. $10-\sqrt{10}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    $\tan A+\tan B=2$, $\tan A\tan B=-5$, so $\tan(A+B)=\dfrac{2}{1+5}=\dfrac13$.
    The roots have opposite signs, so $A+B\in\left(-\dfrac\pi2,\dfrac\pi2\right)$; with $\tan(A+B)>0$, $A+B$ is acute and $\cos(A+B)=\dfrac{3}{\sqrt{10}}$.
    $20\sin^2\dfrac{A+B}{2}=10\left(1-\cos(A+B)\right)=10-\dfrac{30}{\sqrt{10}}=10-3\sqrt{10}$.

  6. JEE Main 2026, 5 April, Shift 1 · Question 12
    The sum of all the integral values of $p$ such that the equation $3\sin^2 x + 12\cos x - 3 = p$, $x \in \mathbb{R}$, has at least one solution, is:

    1. $-54$
    2. $-60$
    3. $-75$
    4. $-84$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    $3\sin^2x+12\cos x-3=3-3\cos^2x+12\cos x-3=-3c^2+12c$, with $c=\cos x\in[-1,1]$.
    $g(c)=-3c^2+12c$ has its vertex at $c=2$, so it is increasing on $[-1,1]$: range $[g(-1),g(1)]=[-15,9]$.
    Integers $p=-15,-14,\ldots,9$: $25$ values with sum $\dfrac{25(-15+9)}{2}=-75$.

  7. JEE Main 2026, 5 April, Shift 2 · Question 23
    If $S=\left\{\theta\in[-\pi,\pi] : \cos\theta\cos\dfrac{5\theta}{2}=\cos7\theta\cos\dfrac{7\theta}{2}\right\}$, then $n(S)$ is equal to __________.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 19

    Multiply by 2: $\cos\dfrac{7\theta}{2}+\cos\dfrac{3\theta}{2}=\cos\dfrac{21\theta}{2}+\cos\dfrac{7\theta}{2}$, so $\cos\dfrac{21\theta}{2}=\cos\dfrac{3\theta}{2}$.
    $\dfrac{21\theta}{2}=2n\pi\pm\dfrac{3\theta}{2} \Rightarrow \theta=\dfrac{2n\pi}{9}$ or $\theta=\dfrac{n\pi}{6}$.
    In $[-\pi,\pi]$: $\dfrac{2n\pi}{9}$, $n=-4,\ldots,4$: 9 values; $\dfrac{n\pi}{6}$, $n=-6,\ldots,6$: 13 values.
    Common: $0,\pm\dfrac{2\pi}{3}$ (3 values).
    $n(S)=9+13-3=19$.

  8. JEE Main 2026, 6 April, Shift 1 · Question 14
    Let $S=\left\{\theta\in(-2\pi,2\pi):\cos\theta+1=\sqrt3\sin\theta\right\}$. Then $\sum_{\theta\in S}\theta$ is equal to:

    1. $-\dfrac{2\pi}{3}$
    2. $-\dfrac{4\pi}{3}$
    3. $\dfrac{2\pi}{3}$
    4. $\dfrac{4\pi}{3}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    $\sqrt3\sin\theta-\cos\theta=1\Rightarrow\sin\left(\theta-\dfrac\pi6\right)=\dfrac12$.
    $\theta-\dfrac\pi6=\dfrac\pi6+2n\pi$ or $\dfrac{5\pi}6+2n\pi$, i.e. $\theta=\dfrac\pi3+2n\pi$ or $\theta=\pi+2n\pi$.
    In $(-2\pi,2\pi)$: $\dfrac\pi3,-\dfrac{5\pi}3,\pi,-\pi$.
    Sum $=\dfrac\pi3-\dfrac{5\pi}3=-\dfrac{4\pi}3$.

  9. JEE Main 2026, 21 January, Shift 1 · Question 13
    The value of $\operatorname{cosec}10^\circ-\sqrt{3}\sec10^\circ$ is equal to :

    1. $2$
    2. $4$
    3. $6$

  10. JEE Main 2026, 22 January, Shift 1 · Question 24
    If $\dfrac{\cos^2 48^\circ-\sin^2 12^\circ}{\sin^2 24^\circ-\sin^2 6^\circ}=\dfrac{\alpha+\beta\sqrt5}{2}$, where $\alpha, \beta\in\mathbb{N}$, then $\alpha+\beta$ is equal to ________

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 4

    $\cos^2A-\sin^2B=\cos(A+B)\cos(A-B)$ and $\sin^2A-\sin^2B=\sin(A+B)\sin(A-B)$.
    Numerator $=\cos60^\circ\cos36^\circ$, denominator $=\sin30^\circ\sin18^\circ$.
    Ratio $=\dfrac{\cos36^\circ}{\sin18^\circ}=\dfrac{\sqrt5+1}{\sqrt5-1}=\dfrac{(\sqrt5+1)^2}{4}=\dfrac{3+\sqrt5}{2}$.
    So $\alpha=3$, $\beta=1$, and $\alpha+\beta=4$.

  12. JEE Main 2026, 23 January, Shift 1 · Question 11
    Number of solutions of $\sqrt3\cos2\theta+8\cos\theta+3\sqrt3=0$, $\theta\in[-3\pi, 2\pi]$ is :

    1. $5$
    2. $4$
    3. $3$
    4. $0$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

  13. JEE Main 2026, 23 January, Shift 1 · Question 13
    Let $\alpha$ and $\beta$ respectively be the maximum and the minimum values of the function $f(\theta)=4\left(\sin^4\left(\dfrac{7\pi}{2}-\theta\right)+\sin^4(11\pi+\theta)\right)-2\left(\sin^6\left(\dfrac{3\pi}{2}-\theta\right)+\sin^6(9\pi-\theta)\right)$, $\theta\in\mathbf{R}$.

    1. $3$
    2. $6$
    3. $4$
    4. $5$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    $\sin\left(\dfrac{7\pi}{2}-\theta\right)=-\cos\theta$, $\sin(11\pi+\theta)=-\sin\theta$, $\sin\left(\dfrac{3\pi}{2}-\theta\right)=-\cos\theta$, $\sin(9\pi-\theta)=\sin\theta$.
    With $p=\sin^2\theta\cos^2\theta$: $\sin^4+\cos^4=1-2p$ and $\sin^6+\cos^6=1-3p$.
    $f=4(1-2p)-2(1-3p)=2-2p$, and $0\le p\le\dfrac14$. So $\alpha=2$, $\beta=\dfrac32$, and $\alpha+2\beta=5$.

    Answer: (a)

    Put $\phi=\theta+\dfrac{\pi}{6}$ and $t=\tan\phi=\dfrac{y}{2}$. Then $\theta+\dfrac{\pi}{3}=\phi+\dfrac{\pi}{6}$, so $\dfrac{x}{3}=\dfrac{t+\frac{1}{\sqrt3}}{1-\frac{t}{\sqrt3}}=\dfrac{\sqrt3\,t+1}{\sqrt3-t}$.
    Hence $x(\sqrt3-t)=3(\sqrt3\,t+1)$; with $t=\dfrac y2$ and multiplying by 2: $xy-2\sqrt3\,x+3\sqrt3\,y+6=0$.
    So $\alpha=-2\sqrt3$, $\beta=3\sqrt3$, $\gamma=6$ and $\alpha^2+\beta^2+\gamma^2=12+27+36=75$.

  27. JEE Main 2025, 7 April, Shift 2 · Question 14
    The number of solutions of the equation $\cos2\theta\cos\dfrac\theta2+\cos\dfrac{5\theta}{2}=2\cos^3\dfrac{5\theta}{2}$ in $\left[-\dfrac\pi2, \dfrac\pi2\right]$ is :

    1. $5$
    2. $6$
    3. $7$
    4. $9$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    $\cos2\theta\cos\dfrac\theta2=\dfrac12\left(\cos\dfrac{5\theta}{2}+\cos\dfrac{3\theta}{2}\right)$ and $2\cos^3A=\dfrac12(\cos3A+3\cos A)$ with $A=\dfrac{5\theta}{2}$.
    The equation reduces to $\cos\dfrac{3\theta}{2}=\cos\dfrac{15\theta}{2}$, so $6\theta=2k\pi$ or $9\theta=2k\pi$.
    In $\left[-\frac\pi2, \frac\pi2\right]$: $\theta=0, \pm\dfrac\pi3$ and $\theta=\pm\dfrac{2\pi}9, \pm\dfrac{4\pi}9$: 7 solutions.

  28. JEE Main 2025, 22 January, Shift 2 · Question 12
    The sum of all values of $\theta\in[0, 2\pi]$ satisfying $2\sin^2\theta=\cos2\theta$ and $2\cos^2\theta=3\sin\theta$ is

    1. $\dfrac{5\pi}{6}$
    2. $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$
    3. $\pi$
    4. $4\pi$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    $2\sin^2\theta=1-2\sin^2\theta$ gives $\sin^2\theta=\dfrac14$.
    $2-2\sin^2\theta=3\sin\theta$ gives $(2\sin\theta-1)(\sin\theta+2)=0$, so $\sin\theta=\dfrac12$.
    Both hold for $\theta=\dfrac\pi6, \dfrac{5\pi}{6}$; sum $=\pi$.

  29. JEE Main 2025, 23 January, Shift 1 · Question 11
    The value of $(\sin70^\circ)(\cot10^\circ\cot70^\circ-1)$ is

    1. $0$
    2. $1$
    3. $3/2$
    4. $2/3$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    $\cot10^\circ\cot70^\circ-1=\dfrac{\cos10^\circ\cos70^\circ-\sin10^\circ\sin70^\circ}{\sin10^\circ\sin70^\circ}=\dfrac{\cos80^\circ}{\sin10^\circ\sin70^\circ}$.
    Multiplying by $\sin70^\circ$ gives $\dfrac{\cos80^\circ}{\sin10^\circ}=1$.

  30. JEE Main 2025, 23 January, Shift 2 · Question 11
    Let the range of the function $f(x)=6+16\cos x\cdot\cos\left(\dfrac{\pi}{3}-x\right)\cdot\cos\left(\dfrac{\pi}{3}+x\right)\cdot\sin3x\cdot\cos6x$, $x\in\mathbf{R}$ be $[\alpha, \beta]$. Then the distance of the point $(\alpha, \beta)$ from the line $3x+4y+12=0$ is :

    1. $8$
    2. $9$
    3. $10$
    4. $11$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    $\cos x\cos\left(\dfrac\pi3-x\right)\cos\left(\dfrac\pi3+x\right)=\dfrac{\cos3x}{4}$.
    So $f(x)=6+4\sin3x\cos3x\cos6x=6+2\sin6x\cos6x=6+\sin12x$, with range $[5,7]$.
    Distance of $(5,7)$ from $3x+4y+12=0$: $\dfrac{|15+28+12|}{5}=11$.

  31. JEE Main 2025, 24 January, Shift 2 · Question 2
    Let $A=\left\{x\in(0, \pi)-\left\{\dfrac{\pi}{2}\right\}: \log_{(2/\pi)}|\sin x|+\log_{(2/\pi)}|\cos x|=2\right\}$ and
    $B=\left\{x\ge0: \sqrt x\left(\sqrt x-4\right)-3\left|\sqrt x-2\right|+6=0\right\}$. Then $n(A\cup B)$ is equal to :

    1. $2$
    2. $4$
    3. $6$
    4. $8$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    A: $|\sin x\cos x|=\dfrac{4}{\pi^2}$, i.e. $|\sin2x|=\dfrac{8}{\pi^2}\approx0.81$. With $2x\in(0,2\pi)$ this has 4 solutions.
    B: put $t=\sqrt x\ge0$. For $t\ge2$: $t^2-7t+12=0$, $t=3,4$. For $t<2$: $t^2-t=0$, $t=0,1$. So $B=\{0,1,9,16\}$.
    No element of B satisfies the condition for A (for example $|\sin2|\approx0.91$), so $n(A\cup B)=4+4=8$.

  32. JEE Main 2025, 28 January, Shift 2 · Question 13
    If $\displaystyle\sum_{r=1}^{13}\left\{\frac{1}{\sin\left(\frac{\pi}{4}+(r-1)\frac{\pi}{6}\right)\sin\left(\frac{\pi}{4}+\frac{r\pi}{6}\right)}\right\}=a\sqrt{3}+b$, $a, b\in\mathbf{Z}$, then $a^2+b^2$ is equal to :

    1. $2$
    2. $4$
    3. $10$
    4. $8$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    With $\theta_r=\dfrac\pi4+(r-1)\dfrac\pi6$, the angles differ by $\dfrac\pi6$, and $\dfrac{1}{\sin\theta_r\sin\theta_{r+1}}=\dfrac{\cot\theta_r-\cot\theta_{r+1}}{\sin\frac\pi6}=2(\cot\theta_r-\cot\theta_{r+1})$.
    Sum $=2\left(\cot\dfrac\pi4-\cot\left(\dfrac\pi4+\dfrac{13\pi}{6}\right)\right)=2\left(1-\cot\dfrac{5\pi}{12}\right)=2(1-(2-\sqrt3))=2\sqrt3-2$.
    $a=2$, $b=-2$, $a^2+b^2=8$.

  33. JEE Main 2025, 29 January, Shift 2 · Question 13
    If $\sin x+\sin^2x=1$, $x\in\left(0, \dfrac{\pi}{2}\right)$, then
    $(\cos^{12}x+\tan^{12}x)+3(\cos^{10}x+\tan^{10}x+\cos^8x+\tan^8x)+(\cos^6x+\tan^6x)$
    is equal to :

    1. $1$
    2. $2$
    3. $3$
    4. $4$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    $\sin x=1-\sin^2x=\cos^2x$, and $\tan^2x=\dfrac{\sin^2x}{\cos^2x}=\sin x$. So $\cos^2x=\tan^2x=s$ with $s+s^2=1$.
    The expression is $2(s^6+3s^5+3s^4+s^3)=2s^3(1+s)^3=2(s+s^2)^3=2$.
