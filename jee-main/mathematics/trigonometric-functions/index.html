  1. JEE Main 2026, 2 April, Shift 1 · Question 11
    If $\sin\left(\dfrac{\pi}{18}\right)\sin\left(\dfrac{5\pi}{18}\right)\sin\left(\dfrac{7\pi}{18}\right)=K$, then the value of $\sin\left(\dfrac{10K\pi}{3}\right)$ is :

    1. $\dfrac{\sqrt{3}+1}{2\sqrt{2}}$
    2. $\dfrac{\sqrt{3}-1}{\sqrt{2}}$
    3. $\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$
    4. $\dfrac{1}{2}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    $K=\sin10^\circ\sin50^\circ\sin70^\circ=\sin10^\circ\sin(60^\circ-10^\circ)\sin(60^\circ+10^\circ)=\dfrac{\sin30^\circ}{4}=\dfrac18$.
    $\dfrac{10K\pi}{3}=\dfrac{10\pi}{24}=\dfrac{5\pi}{12}$.
    $\sin75^\circ=\dfrac{\sqrt3+1}{2\sqrt2}$.

  2. JEE Main 2026, 2 April, Shift 1 · Question 12
    Let $S=\{x \in [-\pi,\pi] : \sin x\,(\sin x+\cos x)=a,\ a \in \mathbb{Z}\}$. Then $n(S)$ is equal to :

    1. $3$
    2. $6$
    3. $7$
    4. $9$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    $\sin x(\sin x+\cos x)=\dfrac12+\dfrac{1}{\sqrt2}\sin\left(2x-\dfrac\pi4\right)\in\left[\dfrac{1-\sqrt2}{2},\dfrac{1+\sqrt2}{2}\right]$, so $a\in\{0,1\}$.
    $a=0$: $\sin x=0 \Rightarrow x=-\pi,0,\pi$; or $\tan x=-1 \Rightarrow x=-\dfrac\pi4,\dfrac{3\pi}4$: $5$ solutions.
    $a=1$: $\sin x\cos x=\cos^2x \Rightarrow \cos x=0$ ($x=\pm\dfrac\pi2$) or $\tan x=1$ ($x=\dfrac\pi4,-\dfrac{3\pi}4$): $4$ solutions.
    $n(S)=9$.

  3. JEE Main 2026, 2 April, Shift 2 · Question 13
    Let $P=\{\theta \in [0, 4\pi] : \tan^2\theta \neq 1\}$ and $S=\{a \in \mathbf{Z} : 2(\cos^8\theta-\sin^8\theta)\sec 2\theta=a^2,\ \theta \in P\}$. Then $n(S)$ is :

    1. $0$
    2. $1$
    3. $2$
    4. $3$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    $\cos^8\theta-\sin^8\theta=(\cos^4\theta-\sin^4\theta)(\cos^4\theta+\sin^4\theta)=\cos2\theta\left(1-\dfrac12\sin^22\theta\right)$.
    $\tan^2\theta\neq1 \Rightarrow \cos2\theta\neq0$, so the expression is $2-\sin^22\theta$ with $\sin^22\theta\neq1$.
    Hence $a^2 \in (1, 2]$, which contains no perfect square.
    $n(S)=0$.

  4. JEE Main 2026, 4 April, Shift 1 · Question 23
    If $A=\dfrac{\sin3^\circ}{\cos9^\circ}+\dfrac{\sin9^\circ}{\cos27^\circ}+\dfrac{\sin27^\circ}{\cos81^\circ}$ and $B=\tan81^\circ-\tan3^\circ$, then $\dfrac{B}{A}$ is equal to ______.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 2

    $\tan3\theta-\tan\theta=\dfrac{\sin2\theta}{\cos3\theta\cos\theta}=\dfrac{2\sin\theta}{\cos3\theta}$, so $\dfrac{\sin\theta}{\cos3\theta}=\dfrac12(\tan3\theta-\tan\theta)$.
    $A=\dfrac12\left[(\tan9^\circ-\tan3^\circ)+(\tan27^\circ-\tan9^\circ)+(\tan81^\circ-\tan27^\circ)\right]=\dfrac12(\tan81^\circ-\tan3^\circ)=\dfrac B2$.
    $\dfrac BA=2$.

  5. JEE Main 2026, 5 April, Shift 1 · Question 6
    Let $\tan A, \tan B$, where $A, B \in \left(-\dfrac{\pi}{2}, \dfrac{\pi}{2}\right)$, be the roots of the quadratic equation $x^2 - 2x - 5 = 0$. Then $20\sin^2\left(\dfrac{A+B}{2}\right)$ is equal to:

    1. $10+\sqrt{10}$
    2. $10-2\sqrt{10}$
    3. $10-3\sqrt{10}$
    4. $10-\sqrt{10}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    $\tan A+\tan B=2$, $\tan A\tan B=-5$, so $\tan(A+B)=\dfrac{2}{1+5}=\dfrac13$.
    The roots have opposite signs, so $A+B\in\left(-\dfrac\pi2,\dfrac\pi2\right)$; with $\tan(A+B)>0$, $A+B$ is acute and $\cos(A+B)=\dfrac{3}{\sqrt{10}}$.
    $20\sin^2\dfrac{A+B}{2}=10\left(1-\cos(A+B)\right)=10-\dfrac{30}{\sqrt{10}}=10-3\sqrt{10}$.

  6. JEE Main 2026, 5 April, Shift 1 · Question 12
    The sum of all the integral values of $p$ such that the equation $3\sin^2 x + 12\cos x - 3 = p$, $x \in \mathbb{R}$, has at least one solution, is:

    1. $-54$
    2. $-60$
    3. $-75$
    4. $-84$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    $3\sin^2x+12\cos x-3=3-3\cos^2x+12\cos x-3=-3c^2+12c$, with $c=\cos x\in[-1,1]$.
    $g(c)=-3c^2+12c$ has its vertex at $c=2$, so it is increasing on $[-1,1]$: range $[g(-1),g(1)]=[-15,9]$.
    Integers $p=-15,-14,\ldots,9$: $25$ values with sum $\dfrac{25(-15+9)}{2}=-75$.

  7. JEE Main 2026, 5 April, Shift 2 · Question 23
    If $S=\left\{\theta\in[-\pi,\pi] : \cos\theta\cos\dfrac{5\theta}{2}=\cos7\theta\cos\dfrac{7\theta}{2}\right\}$, then $n(S)$ is equal to __________.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 19

    Multiply by 2: $\cos\dfrac{7\theta}{2}+\cos\dfrac{3\theta}{2}=\cos\dfrac{21\theta}{2}+\cos\dfrac{7\theta}{2}$, so $\cos\dfrac{21\theta}{2}=\cos\dfrac{3\theta}{2}$.
    $\dfrac{21\theta}{2}=2n\pi\pm\dfrac{3\theta}{2} \Rightarrow \theta=\dfrac{2n\pi}{9}$ or $\theta=\dfrac{n\pi}{6}$.
    In $[-\pi,\pi]$: $\dfrac{2n\pi}{9}$, $n=-4,\ldots,4$: 9 values; $\dfrac{n\pi}{6}$, $n=-6,\ldots,6$: 13 values.
    Common: $0,\pm\dfrac{2\pi}{3}$ (3 values).
    $n(S)=9+13-3=19$.

  8. JEE Main 2026, 6 April, Shift 1 · Question 14
    Let $S=\left\{\theta\in(-2\pi,2\pi):\cos\theta+1=\sqrt3\sin\theta\right\}$. Then $\sum_{\theta\in S}\theta$ is equal to:

    1. $-\dfrac{2\pi}{3}$
    2. $-\dfrac{4\pi}{3}$
    3. $\dfrac{2\pi}{3}$
    4. $\dfrac{4\pi}{3}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    $\sqrt3\sin\theta-\cos\theta=1\Rightarrow\sin\left(\theta-\dfrac\pi6\right)=\dfrac12$.
    $\theta-\dfrac\pi6=\dfrac\pi6+2n\pi$ or $\dfrac{5\pi}6+2n\pi$, i.e. $\theta=\dfrac\pi3+2n\pi$ or $\theta=\pi+2n\pi$.
    In $(-2\pi,2\pi)$: $\dfrac\pi3,-\dfrac{5\pi}3,\pi,-\pi$.
    Sum $=\dfrac\pi3-\dfrac{5\pi}3=-\dfrac{4\pi}3$.
