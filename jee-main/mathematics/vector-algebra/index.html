  1. JEE Main 2026, 2 April, Shift 1 · Question 14
    If $\vec{a}$ and $\vec{b}$ are two vectors such that $|\vec{a}|=2$ and $|\vec{b}|=3$, then the maximum value of $3\left|\left(3\vec{a}+2\vec{b}\right)\right|+4\left|\left(3\vec{a}-2\vec{b}\right)\right|$ is :

    1. $30$
    2. $36$
    3. $60$
    4. $72$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    Let $u=|3\vec a+2\vec b|$, $v=|3\vec a-2\vec b|$. Then $u^2+v^2=2(9|\vec a|^2+4|\vec b|^2)=144$.
    By Cauchy–Schwarz, $3u+4v\le\sqrt{3^2+4^2}\sqrt{u^2+v^2}=5\cdot12=60$.
    Equality at $u=7.2,\ v=9.6$, i.e. $u^2-v^2=24\,\vec a\cdot\vec b=-40.32$, $\vec a\cdot\vec b=-1.68\in[-6,6]$, so attainable.
    Maximum $=60$.

  2. JEE Main 2026, 2 April, Shift 2 · Question 14
    Let the vectors $\vec a=-\hat i+\hat j+3\hat k$ and $\vec b=\hat i+3\hat j+\hat k$. For some $\lambda, \mu \in \mathbf{R}$, let $\vec c=\lambda\vec a+\mu\vec b$. If $\vec c\cdot\left(3\hat i-6\hat j+2\hat k\right)=10$ and $\vec c\cdot\left(\hat i+\hat j+\hat k\right)=-2$, then $|\vec c|^2$ is equal to :

    1. $8$
    2. $12$
    3. $14$
    4. $15$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    $\vec c=(-\lambda+\mu,\ \lambda+3\mu,\ 3\lambda+\mu)$.
    $\vec c\cdot(3,-6,2)=-3\lambda-13\mu=10$ and $\vec c\cdot(1,1,1)=3\lambda+5\mu=-2$.
    Adding: $-8\mu=8 \Rightarrow \mu=-1$, $\lambda=1$.
    $\vec c=(-2,-2,2)$, $|\vec c|^2=12$.

  3. JEE Main 2026, 2 April, Shift 2 · Question 16
    Two adjacent sides of a parallelogram PQRS are given by $\overrightarrow{PQ}=\hat j+\hat k$ and $\overrightarrow{PS}=\hat i-\hat j$. If the side PS is rotated about the point P by an acute angle $\alpha$ in the plane of the parallelogram so that it becomes perpendicular to the side PQ, then $\sin^2\left(\dfrac{5\alpha}{2}\right)-\sin^2\left(\dfrac{\alpha}{2}\right)$ is equal to :

    1. $\dfrac12$
    2. $\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}$
    3. $\dfrac{\sqrt3}{4}$
    4. $\dfrac{2\sqrt3}{5}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    $\cos\angle QPS=\dfrac{(0,1,1)\cdot(1,-1,0)}{\sqrt2\cdot\sqrt2}=-\dfrac12 \Rightarrow \angle QPS=120^\circ$.
    To become perpendicular to PQ, PS turns through the acute angle $\alpha=30^\circ$.
    $\sin^2 75^\circ-\sin^2 15^\circ=\sin(75^\circ+15^\circ)\sin(75^\circ-15^\circ)=\sin90^\circ\sin60^\circ=\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}$.

  4. JEE Main 2026, 4 April, Shift 1 · Question 24
    Let $\vec{a}_k=(\tan\theta_k)\hat{i}+\hat{j}$ and $\vec{b}_k=\hat{i}-(\cot\theta_k)\hat{j}$, where $\theta_k=\dfrac{2^{k-1}\pi}{2^n+1}$, for some $n\in\mathbb{N}$, $n>5$. Then the value of $\dfrac{\sum_{k=1}^{n}|\vec{a}_k|^2}{\sum_{k=1}^{n}|\vec{b}_k|^2}$ is ______.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 3

    $|\vec a_k|^2=1+\tan^2\theta_k=\sec^2\theta_k$ and $|\vec b_k|^2=1+\cot^2\theta_k=\csc^2\theta_k$. Let $S=\sum_{k=1}^n\csc^2\theta_k$.
    $\sec^2\theta+\csc^2\theta=\dfrac{1}{\sin^2\theta\cos^2\theta}=4\csc^2 2\theta$, so $\sec^2\theta_k=4\csc^2\theta_{k+1}-\csc^2\theta_k$.
    $\theta_{n+1}=\dfrac{2^n\pi}{2^n+1}=\pi-\theta_1$, so $\csc^2\theta_{n+1}=\csc^2\theta_1$ and $\sum_{k=1}^n\csc^2\theta_{k+1}=S$.
    $\sum\sec^2\theta_k=4S-S=3S$, so the ratio is $3$.

  5. JEE Main 2026, 4 April, Shift 2 · Question 15
    Let $\hat u$ and $\hat v$ be unit vectors inclined at an acute angle such that $|\hat u\times\hat v|=\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}$. If $\vec A=\lambda\hat u+\hat v+(\hat u\times\hat v)$, then $\lambda$ is equal to:

    1. $\dfrac43(\vec A\cdot\hat u)-\dfrac23(\vec A\cdot\hat v)$
    2. $\dfrac23(\vec A\cdot\hat u)-\dfrac13(\vec A\cdot\hat v)$
    3. $\dfrac43(\vec A\cdot\hat u)+\dfrac23(\vec A\cdot\hat v)$
    4. $(\vec A\cdot\hat u)-\dfrac12(\vec A\cdot\hat v)$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    The angle is $60^\circ$, so $\hat u\cdot\hat v=\dfrac12$.
    $\vec A\cdot\hat u=\lambda+\dfrac12$, $\vec A\cdot\hat v=\dfrac\lambda2+1$.
    $\vec A\cdot\hat u-\dfrac12\vec A\cdot\hat v=\dfrac{3\lambda}{4}$.
    $\lambda=\dfrac43(\vec A\cdot\hat u)-\dfrac23(\vec A\cdot\hat v)$.

  6. JEE Main 2026, 5 April, Shift 1 · Question 14
    Let $\vec{a} = \sqrt{7}\hat{i} + \hat{j} - \hat{k}$ and $\vec{b} = \hat{j} + 2\hat{k}$. If $\vec{r}$ is a vector such that $\vec{r} \times \vec{a} + \vec{a} \times \vec{b} = \vec{0}$ and $\vec{r} \cdot \vec{a} = 0$, then $|3\vec{r}|^2$ is equal to:

    1. $44$
    2. $54$
    3. $86$
    4. $132$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    $\vec r\times\vec a=\vec b\times\vec a \Rightarrow (\vec r-\vec b)\times\vec a=\vec 0 \Rightarrow \vec r=\vec b+\lambda\vec a$.
    $\vec r\cdot\vec a=0$: $\vec a\cdot\vec b+\lambda|\vec a|^2=0$, with $\vec a\cdot\vec b=1-2=-1$ and $|\vec a|^2=9$, so $\lambda=\dfrac19$.
    $3\vec r=3\vec b+\dfrac13\vec a=\left(\dfrac{\sqrt7}{3},\,\dfrac{10}{3},\,\dfrac{17}{3}\right)$.
    $|3\vec r|^2=\dfrac{7+100+289}{9}=\dfrac{396}{9}=44$.

  7. JEE Main 2026, 5 April, Shift 2 · Question 16
    Let O be the origin, $\vec{OP}=\vec{a}$ and $\vec{OQ}=\vec{b}$. If R is the point on $\vec{OP}$ such that $\vec{OP}=5\vec{OR}$, and M is the point such that $\vec{OQ}=5\vec{RM}$, then $\vec{PM}$ is equal to :

    1. $\dfrac15\left(\vec{a}-4\vec{b}\right)$
    2. $\dfrac15\left(\vec{b}-4\vec{a}\right)$
    3. $\dfrac15\left(-\vec{a}+4\vec{b}\right)$
    4. $\dfrac15\left(-\vec{b}+4\vec{a}\right)$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    $\vec{OR}=\dfrac{\vec a}{5}$ and $\vec{RM}=\dfrac{\vec b}{5}$, so $\vec{OM}=\dfrac{\vec a+\vec b}{5}$.
    $\vec{PM}=\vec{OM}-\vec{OP}=\dfrac{\vec a+\vec b}{5}-\vec a=\dfrac15\left(\vec b-4\vec a\right)$.

  8. JEE Main 2026, 6 April, Shift 1 · Question 23
    If $\vec a=\hat i+\hat j+\hat k$, $\vec b=\hat j-\hat k$ and $\vec c$ be three vectors such that $\vec a\times\vec c=\vec b$ and $\vec a\cdot\vec c=3$, then $\vec c\cdot(\vec a-2\vec b)$ is equal to ________.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 3

    $\vec b=\vec a\times\vec c$ is perpendicular to $\vec c$, so $\vec c\cdot\vec b=0$.
    $\vec c\cdot(\vec a-2\vec b)=3-0=3$.
    (Indeed $\vec c=\dfrac{3\vec a-\vec a\times\vec b}{|\vec a|^2}=\dfrac{5\hat i+2\hat j+2\hat k}{3}$.)

  9. JEE Main 2026, 6 April, Shift 2 · Question 15
    Let $\vec a=2\hat i+3\hat j+3\hat k$ and $\vec b=6\hat i+3\hat j+3\hat k$. Then the square of the area of the triangle with adjacent sides determined by the vectors $\left(2\vec a+3\vec b\right)$ and $\left(\vec a-\vec b\right)$ is :

    1. $450$
    2. $900$
    3. $1800$
    4. $2400$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    $(2\vec a+3\vec b)\times(\vec a-\vec b)=-2\,\vec a\times\vec b+3\,\vec b\times\vec a=-5\,\vec a\times\vec b$.
    $\vec a\times\vec b=(9-9)\hat i-(6-18)\hat j+(6-18)\hat k=12\hat j-12\hat k$, $|\vec a\times\vec b|=12\sqrt2$.
    Area $=\dfrac12\cdot5\cdot12\sqrt2=30\sqrt2$; square $=1800$.

  10. JEE Main 2026, 8 April, Shift 2 · Question 14
    Let $\vec{a}=4\hat{i}-\hat{j}+3\hat{k}$, $\vec{b}=10\hat{i}+2\hat{j}-\hat{k}$ and a vector $\vec{c}$ be such that $2\left(\vec{a}\times\vec{b}\right)+3\left(\vec{b}\times\vec{c}\right)=\vec{0}$. If $\vec{a}\cdot\vec{c}=15$, then $\vec{c}\cdot\left(\hat{i}+\hat{j}-3\hat{k}\right)$ is equal to :

    1. $-6$
    2. $-5$
    3. $-4$
    4. $-3$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    $2(\vec a\times\vec b)+3(\vec b\times\vec c)=\vec b\times(3\vec c-2\vec a)=\vec 0$, so $3\vec c=2\vec a+\lambda\vec b$.
    $\vec a\cdot\vec a=26$, $\vec a\cdot\vec b=40-2-3=35$. Then $3\vec a\cdot\vec c=52+35\lambda=45\Rightarrow\lambda=-\dfrac15$.
    With $\vec d=\hat i+\hat j-3\hat k$: $\vec a\cdot\vec d=4-1-9=-6$, $\vec b\cdot\vec d=10+2+3=15$.
    $\vec c\cdot\vec d=\dfrac{2(-6)-\frac15(15)}{3}=\dfrac{-15}{3}=-5$.
