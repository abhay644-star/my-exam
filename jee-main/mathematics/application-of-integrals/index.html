  1. JEE Main 2026, 2 April, Shift 2 · Question 24
    If the area of the region bounded by $16x^2-9y^2=144$ and $8x-3y=24$ is A, then $3(A+6\log_e(3))$ is equal to __________.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 24

    Hyperbola $\dfrac{x^2}{9}-\dfrac{y^2}{16}=1$; the line $y=\dfrac{8x-24}{3}$ meets it where $x^2-8x+15=0$: $x=3$ (point $(3,0)$) and $x=5$ (point $(5,\tfrac{16}{3})$).
    Between them the hyperbola $y=\dfrac43\sqrt{x^2-9}$ lies above the line.
    $\displaystyle\int_3^5\sqrt{x^2-9}\,dx=\left[\dfrac x2\sqrt{x^2-9}-\dfrac92\ln\left(x+\sqrt{x^2-9}\right)\right]_3^5=10-\dfrac92\ln3$.
    $A=\dfrac43\left(10-\dfrac92\ln3\right)-\dfrac13\left[4x^2-24x\right]_3^5=\dfrac{40}{3}-6\ln3-\dfrac{16}{3}=8-6\ln3$.
    $3(A+6\ln3)=24$.

  2. JEE Main 2026, 4 April, Shift 1 · Question 18
    The area of the region $\{(x,y): y\le\pi-|x|,\ y\le|x\sin x|,\ y\ge0\}$ is:

    1. $1+\dfrac{\pi^2}{8}$
    2. $2+\dfrac{\pi^2}{4}$
    3. $\dfrac{\pi^2}{8}-1$
    4. $4+\dfrac{\pi^2}{2}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    The region is symmetric about the $y$-axis and needs $|x|\le\pi$. For $0\le x\le\pi$, $x\sin x\ge0$.
    $x\sin x=\pi-x$ at $x=\dfrac\pi2$; $x\sin x$ is the lower curve on $\left[0,\dfrac\pi2\right]$ and $\pi-x$ on $\left[\dfrac\pi2,\pi\right]$.
    Right half: $\int_0^{\pi/2}x\sin x\,dx+\int_{\pi/2}^{\pi}(\pi-x)\,dx=\left[\sin x-x\cos x\right]_0^{\pi/2}+\dfrac{\pi^2}{8}=1+\dfrac{\pi^2}{8}$.
    Total area $=2\left(1+\dfrac{\pi^2}{8}\right)=2+\dfrac{\pi^2}{4}$.

  3. JEE Main 2026, 4 April, Shift 2 · Question 18
    The area of the region bounded by the curves $x+3y^2=0$ and $x+4y^2=1$ is equal to:

    1. $\dfrac13$
    2. $\dfrac23$
    3. $\dfrac43$
    4. $\dfrac53$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    Intersections: $-3y^2=1-4y^2\Rightarrow y=\pm1$.
    For $|y|\le1$, $x$ runs from $-3y^2$ to $1-4y^2$.
    Area $=\displaystyle\int_{-1}^{1}(1-y^2)\,dy=2-\dfrac23=\dfrac43$.

  4. JEE Main 2026, 5 April, Shift 1 · Question 16
    The area of the region $R = \{(x, y) : xy \le 27,\ 1 \le y \le x^2\}$ is equal to:

    1. $78\log_e 3-\dfrac{52}{3}$
    2. $54\log_e 3-\dfrac{52}{3}$
    3. $54\log_e 3-\dfrac{26}{3}$
    4. $54\log_e 3+\dfrac{26}{3}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    Take the region with $x>0$ (the bounded part intended). $y=x^2$ meets $xy=27$ at $x=3$, and $xy=27$ meets $y=1$ at $x=27$.
    For $1\le x\le3$: $y$ from $1$ to $x^2$, area $\int_1^3(x^2-1)\,dx=\left[\dfrac{x^3}{3}-x\right]_1^3=6+\dfrac23=\dfrac{20}{3}$.
    For $3\le x\le27$: $y$ from $1$ to $\dfrac{27}{x}$, area $\int_3^{27}\left(\dfrac{27}{x}-1\right)dx=27\log_e9-24=54\log_e3-24$.
    Total $=54\log_e3-24+\dfrac{20}{3}=54\log_e3-\dfrac{52}{3}$.

  5. JEE Main 2026, 5 April, Shift 2 · Question 24
    Let $f:\mathbb{R}\to\mathbb{R}$ be a function such that $f(x)+3f\left(\dfrac\pi2-x\right)=\sin x$, $x\in\mathbb{R}$. Let the maximum value of $f$ on $\mathbb{R}$ be $\alpha$. If the area of the region bounded by the curves $g(x)=x^2$ and $h(x)=\beta x^3$, $\beta>0$, is $\alpha^2$, then $30\beta^3$ is equal to __________.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 16

    Replacing $x$ by $\dfrac\pi2-x$: $f\left(\dfrac\pi2-x\right)+3f(x)=\cos x$.
    Solving the two equations: $f(x)=\dfrac{3\cos x-\sin x}{8}$, so $\alpha=\dfrac{\sqrt{10}}{8}$ and $\alpha^2=\dfrac{5}{32}$.
    Curves meet at $x=0$ and $x=\dfrac1\beta$: area $=\displaystyle\int_0^{1/\beta}(x^2-\beta x^3)\,dx=\dfrac{1}{3\beta^3}-\dfrac{1}{4\beta^3}=\dfrac{1}{12\beta^3}$.
    $\dfrac{1}{12\beta^3}=\dfrac{5}{32} \Rightarrow \beta^3=\dfrac{8}{15}$.
    $30\beta^3=16$.

  6. JEE Main 2026, 6 April, Shift 1 · Question 19
    The area of the region $\{(x,y):0\le y\le6-x,\ y^2\ge4x-3,\ x\ge0\}$ is:

    1. $8$
    2. $9$
    3. $12$
    4. $15$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    For $y\ge0$ the region is $0\le x\le\min\left(\dfrac{y^2+3}{4},\,6-y\right)$.
    The curves meet where $y^2+3=24-4y\Rightarrow y=3$; below $y=3$ the parabola is the smaller bound.
    Area $=\displaystyle\int_0^3\dfrac{y^2+3}{4}dy+\int_3^6(6-y)\,dy=\dfrac{9+9}{4}+\dfrac92=9$.

  7. JEE Main 2026, 6 April, Shift 1 · Question 20
    Let $e$ be the base of natural logarithm and let $f:\{1,2,3,4\}\to\{1,e,e^2,e^3\}$ and $g:\{1,e,e^2,e^3\}\to\left\{1,\dfrac12,\dfrac13,\dfrac14\right\}$ be two bijective functions such that $f$ is strictly decreasing and $g$ is strictly increasing. If $\phi(x)=\left[f^{-1}\left\{g^{-1}\left(\dfrac12\right)\right\}\right]^x$, then the area of the region $R=\{(x,y):x^2\le y\le\phi(x),\ 0\le x\le1\}$ is:

    1. $\dfrac{3-\log_e(2)}{3\log_e(2)}$
    2. $\dfrac{1}{3\log_e(2)}$
    3. $3+\log_e(2)$
    4. $\dfrac{3+\log_e(2)}{2+\log_e(3)}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    $g$ increasing: $g(1)=\dfrac14$, $g(e)=\dfrac13$, $g(e^2)=\dfrac12$, $g(e^3)=1$, so $g^{-1}\left(\dfrac12\right)=e^2$.
    $f$ decreasing: $f(1)=e^3$, $f(2)=e^2$, so $f^{-1}(e^2)=2$ and $\phi(x)=2^x$.
    $2^x\ge x^2$ on $[0,1]$: area $=\displaystyle\int_0^1(2^x-x^2)\,dx=\dfrac{1}{\ln2}-\dfrac13=\dfrac{3-\log_e2}{3\log_e2}$.

  8. JEE Main 2026, 6 April, Shift 2 · Question 19
    The area of the region $\{(x,y) : x^2-8x\le y\le-x\}$ is :

    1. $\dfrac{343}{6}$
    2. $\dfrac{637}{6}$
    3. $\dfrac{437}{6}$
    4. $\dfrac{523}{6}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    Need $x^2-8x\le-x \Rightarrow x^2-7x\le0 \Rightarrow 0\le x\le7$.
    Area $=\displaystyle\int_0^7\left(-x-x^2+8x\right)dx=\int_0^7(7x-x^2)\,dx=\dfrac{343}{2}-\dfrac{343}{3}=\dfrac{343}{6}$.

  9. JEE Main 2026, 8 April, Shift 2 · Question 18
    Let $f:(1,\infty)\to\mathbf{R}$ be a function defined as $f(x)=\dfrac{x-1}{x+1}$. Let $f^{i+1}(x)=f\left(f^{i}(x)\right)$, $i=1,2,\ldots,25$, where $f^1(x)=f(x)$. If $g(x)+f^{26}(x)=0$, $x\in(1,\infty)$, then the area of the region bounded by the curves $y=g(x)$, $2y=2x-3$, $y=0$ and $x=4$ is :

    1. $\dfrac{1}{8}+\log_e2$
    2. $\dfrac{1}{4}+\log_e2$
    3. $\dfrac{5}{6}+3\log_e2$
    4. $\dfrac{5}{6}+\log_e2$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    $f^2(x)=\dfrac{\frac{x-1}{x+1}-1}{\frac{x-1}{x+1}+1}=-\dfrac1x$, so $f^4(x)=x$ and $f^{26}=f^2$. Hence $g(x)=\dfrac1x$.
    $y=\dfrac1x$ meets $y=x-\dfrac32$ at $x=2$; the line meets $y=0$ at $x=\dfrac32$.
    The region lies under the line for $\dfrac32\le x\le2$ and under $y=\dfrac1x$ for $2\le x\le4$.
    Area $=\displaystyle\int_{3/2}^{2}\left(x-\dfrac32\right)dx+\int_2^4\dfrac{dx}{x}=\dfrac18+\log_e2$.
