  1. JEE Main 2026, 2 April, Shift 1 · Question 8
    If the mean of the data $$\begin{array}{|c|c|c|c|c|c|c|}\hline \text{Class} & 5\text{-}10 & 10\text{-}15 & 15\text{-}20 & 20\text{-}25 & 25\text{-}30 & 30\text{-}35 \\ \hline \text{Frequency} & 2 & k & 28 & 54 & k+1 & 5 \\ \hline \end{array}$$ is $21$, then $k$ is one of the roots of the equation :

    1. $2x^2-23x-10=0$
    2. $4x^2-35x+24=0$
    3. $2x^2-19x-10=0$
    4. $2x^2-35x+98=0$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    Class marks: $7.5, 12.5, 17.5, 22.5, 27.5, 32.5$.
    $\sum f=90+2k$, $\sum fx=15+12.5k+490+1215+27.5(k+1)+162.5=1910+40k$.
    Mean $21$: $1910+40k=21(90+2k)=1890+42k \Rightarrow k=10$.
    Check: $2(100)-19(10)-10=0$, so $k$ is a root of $2x^2-19x-10=0$.

  2. JEE Main 2026, 2 April, Shift 2 · Question 9
    The mean and variance of $n$ observations are 8 and 16, respectively. If the sum of the first $(n-1)$ observations is 48 and the sum of squares of the first $(n-1)$ observations is 496, then the value of $n$ is :

    1. $21$
    2. $16$
    3. $13$
    4. $7$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    $\sum x_i=8n$ and $\sum x_i^2=n(16+64)=80n$.
    Last observation $x_n=8n-48$ and $x_n^2=80n-496$.
    $(8n-48)^2=80n-496 \Rightarrow 64n^2-848n+2800=0 \Rightarrow 4n^2-53n+175=0$.
    $n=\dfrac{53\pm3}{8}=7$ or $\dfrac{25}{4}$; $n$ is an integer, so $n=7$.

  3. JEE Main 2026, 4 April, Shift 1 · Question 10
    Suppose that the mean and median of the non-negative numbers $21, 8, 17, a, 51, 103, b, 13, 67$, $(a>b)$, are $40$ and $21$, respectively. If the mean deviation about the median is $26$, then $2a$ is equal to:

    1. $109$
    2. $117$
    3. $161$
    4. $131$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    Mean $40$ with $9$ numbers: sum $=360$, so $a+b=360-280=80$.
    Mean deviation about the median $21$ is $26$: $\sum|x_i-21|=234$.
    Known numbers give $0+13+4+30+82+8+46=183$, so $|a-21|+|b-21|=51$.
    Median is $21$ with $a>b$, so take $b\le21\le a$: $a-b=51$.
    With $a+b=80$: $a=65.5$, $b=14.5$ (sorted list $8,13,14.5,17,21,51,65.5,67,103$ has median $21$).
    $2a=131$.

  4. JEE Main 2026, 4 April, Shift 2 · Question 8
    For 10 observations $x_1,x_2,\ldots,x_{10}$, if $\displaystyle\sum_{i=1}^{10}(x_i+2)^2=180$ and $\displaystyle\sum_{i=1}^{10}(x_i-1)^2=90$, then their standard deviation is:

    1. $2$
    2. $\sqrt3$
    3. $2\sqrt2$
    4. $3$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    $\sum x_i^2+4\sum x_i+40=180$ and $\sum x_i^2-2\sum x_i+10=90$.
    Subtracting: $6\sum x_i+30=90\Rightarrow\sum x_i=10$, so $\sum x_i^2=100$.
    Mean $=1$, variance $=\dfrac{100}{10}-1^2=9$.
    Standard deviation $=3$.

  5. JEE Main 2026, 5 April, Shift 1 · Question 8
    The mean deviation about the mean for the data $$\begin{array}{|c|c|c|c|c|c|c|}\hline x_i & 5 & 7 & 9 & 10 & 12 & 15 \\ \hline f_i & 8 & 6 & 2 & 2 & 2 & 6 \\ \hline\end{array}$$ is equal to:

    1. $\dfrac{40}{13}$
    2. $\dfrac{42}{13}$
    3. $\dfrac{44}{13}$
    4. $\dfrac{46}{13}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    $N=\sum f_i=26$, $\sum f_ix_i=40+42+18+20+24+90=234$, so mean $\bar x=9$.
    $\sum f_i|x_i-9|=8(4)+6(2)+2(0)+2(1)+2(3)+6(6)=32+12+0+2+6+36=88$.
    Mean deviation $=\dfrac{88}{26}=\dfrac{44}{13}$.

  6. JEE Main 2026, 5 April, Shift 2 · Question 10
    A variable X takes values $0, 0, 2, 6, 12, 20, \ldots, n(n-1)$ with frequencies ${}^nC_0, {}^nC_1, {}^nC_2, {}^nC_3, {}^nC_4, {}^nC_5, \ldots, {}^nC_n$, respectively. If the mean of this data is 60, then its median is :

    1. $56$
    2. $42$
    3. $72$
    4. $90$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    The value $r(r-1)$ has frequency ${}^nC_r$. $\sum r(r-1){}^nC_r=n(n-1)2^{n-2}$ and $\sum{}^nC_r=2^n$.
    Mean $=\dfrac{n(n-1)}{4}=60 \Rightarrow n(n-1)=240 \Rightarrow n=16$.
    Values increase with $r$ and the frequencies ${}^{16}C_r$ are symmetric about $r=8$, so the middle observations (the $2^{15}$-th and next) both fall at $r=8$.
    Median $=8\times7=56$.

  7. JEE Main 2026, 6 April, Shift 1 · Question 9
    A data consists of 20 observations $x_1,x_2,\ldots,x_{20}$. If $\sum_{i=1}^{20}(x_i+5)^2=2500$ and $\sum_{i=1}^{20}(x_i-5)^2=100$, then the ratio of mean to standard deviation of this data is:

    1. $2:1$
    2. $3:1$
    3. $3:2$
    4. $4:1$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    Subtracting: $20\sum x_i=2400\Rightarrow\sum x_i=120$, mean $=6$.
    Adding: $2\sum x_i^2+2\cdot20\cdot25=2600\Rightarrow\sum x_i^2=800$.
    Variance $=\dfrac{800}{20}-36=4$, so $\sigma=2$.
    Mean : SD $=6:2=3:1$.

  8. JEE Main 2026, 6 April, Shift 2 · Question 8
    Let the mean and the variance of seven observations $2, 4, \alpha, 8, \beta, 12, 14$, $\alpha < \beta$, be 8 and 16 respectively. Then the quadratic equation whose roots are $3\alpha+2$ and $2\beta+1$ is :

    1. $x^2-35x+306=0$
    2. $x^2-41x+420=0$
    3. $x^2-45x+506=0$
    4. $x^2-37x+342=0$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    Mean: $40+\alpha+\beta=56 \Rightarrow \alpha+\beta=16$.
    Variance: $\dfrac{\sum x^2}{7}-64=16 \Rightarrow \sum x^2=560$; $4+16+64+144+196=424$, so $\alpha^2+\beta^2=136$.
    $\alpha\beta=\dfrac{256-136}{2}=60$, so $\alpha=6$, $\beta=10$.
    Roots $3\alpha+2=20$ and $2\beta+1=21$: sum $41$, product $420$.
    Equation: $x^2-41x+420=0$.

  9. JEE Main 2026, 8 April, Shift 2 · Question 7
    A set of four observations has mean 1 and variance 13. Another set of six observations has mean 2 and variance 1. Then, the variance of all these 10 observations is equal to :

    1. $5.96$
    2. $6.14$
    3. $6.04$
    4. $6.24$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    First set: $\sum x=4$, $\sum x^2=4(13+1^2)=56$.
    Second set: $\sum x=12$, $\sum x^2=6(1+2^2)=30$.
    Combined: mean $=\dfrac{16}{10}=1.6$, $\dfrac{\sum x^2}{10}=8.6$.
    Variance $=8.6-1.6^2=8.6-2.56=6.04$.

  10. JEE Main 2026, 21 January, Shift 1 · Question 8
    Let the mean and variance of 7 observations $2, 4, 10, x, 12, 14, y$, $x>y$, be 8 and 16 respectively. Two numbers are chosen from $\{1, 2, 3, x-4, y, 5\}$ one after another without replacement, then the probability, that the smaller number among the two chosen numbers is less than 4, is :

    1. $\dfrac{4}{5}$
    2. $\dfrac{3}{5}$
    3. $\dfrac{2}{5}$
    4. $\dfrac{1}{3}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    Sum $=56 \Rightarrow x+y=14$. $\sum x_i^2=7(16+64)=560 \Rightarrow x^2+y^2=560-460=100$.
    So $xy=48$ and, with $x>y$, $x=8,\ y=6$.
    The set is $\{1,2,3,4,6,5\}=\{1,\ldots,6\}$.
    The smaller number is at least 4 only if both are from $\{4,5,6\}$: probability $\dfrac36\cdot\dfrac25=\dfrac15$.
    Required probability $=1-\dfrac15=\dfrac45$.

  11. JEE Main 2026, 22 January, Shift 2 · Question 8
    If the mean deviation about the median of the numbers $k, 2k, 3k, \ldots, 1000k$ is 500, then $k^2$ is equal to :

    1. $9$
    2. $16$
    3. $4$
    4. $1$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    Median $=\dfrac{500k+501k}{2}=500.5k$ (taking $k>0$).
    $\sum|ik-500.5k|=2k\sum_{j=1}^{500}(j-0.5)=2k\left(\dfrac{500\cdot501}{2}-250\right)=250000k$.
    Mean deviation $=\dfrac{250000k}{1000}=250k=500\Rightarrow k=2$, so $k^2=4$.

  12. JEE Main 2026, 23 January, Shift 1 · Question 8
    Let the mean and variance of 8 numbers $-10, -7, -1, x, y, 9, 2, 16$ be $\dfrac72$ and $\dfrac{293}{4}$, respectively. Then the mean of 4 numbers $x, y, x+y+1, |x-y|$ is :

    1. $9$
    2. $10$
    3. $11$
    4. $12$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    Sum $=28$, and the known numbers add to $9$, so $x+y=19$.
    $\sum x_i^2=8\left(\dfrac{293}{4}+\dfrac{49}{4}\right)=684$, and the known squares add to $491$, so $x^2+y^2=193$.
    Then $xy=84$, so $\{x,y\}=\{12,7\}$ and $|x-y|=5$.
    Mean $=\dfrac{19+20+5}{4}=11$.

  13. JEE Main 2026, 23 January, Shift 2 · Question 9
    If the mean and the variance of the data

    Class4$-$88$-$1212$-$1616$-$20
    Frequency3$\lambda$47
    are $\mu$ and 19 respectively, then the value of $\lambda+\mu$ is

    1. $18$
    2. $19$
    3. $20$
    4. $21$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    Class marks 6, 10, 14, 18. $N=14+\lambda$, $\sum fx=200+10\lambda$, $\sum fx^2=3160+100\lambda$.
    Try $\lambda=6$: $N=20$, mean $=13$, variance $=\dfrac{3760}{20}-169=19$. This fits (and the variance condition gives a quadratic in $\lambda$ whose other root is not a whole number).
    So $\lambda=6$, $\mu=13$, $\lambda+\mu=19$.

  14. JEE Main 2026, 24 January, Shift 1 · Question 8
    The mean and variance of a data of 10 observations are 10 and 2, respectively. If an observations $\alpha$ in this data is replaced by $\beta$, then the mean and variance become 10.1 and 1.99, respectively. Then $\alpha+\beta$ equals

    1. $5$
    2. $10$
    3. $15$
    4. $20$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    $\sum x$ changes from 100 to 101, so $\beta-\alpha=1$.
    $\sum x^2=10(2+10^2)=1020$ becomes $10(1.99+10.1^2)=10(1.99+102.01)=1040$, so $\beta^2-\alpha^2=20$.
    $\beta+\alpha=\dfrac{20}{1}=20$.

  15. JEE Main 2026, 24 January, Shift 2 · Question 9
    Let $X=\{x\in\mathbb{N} : 1\le x\le19\}$ and for some $a, b\in\mathbb{R}$, $Y=\{ax+b : x\in X\}$.
    If the mean and variance of the elements of Y are 30 and 750, respectively, then the sum of all possible values of $b$ is

    1. $20$
    2. $60$
    3. $80$
    4. $100$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    For $X$: mean $10$, variance $\dfrac{19^2-1}{12}=30$.
    For $Y$: $10a+b=30$ and $30a^2=750 \Rightarrow a=\pm5$.
    $a=5$ gives $b=-20$; $a=-5$ gives $b=80$. Sum $=60$.

  16. JEE Main 2026, 28 January, Shift 1 · Question 8
    The mean and variance of 10 observations are 9 and 34.2, respectively. If 8 of these observations are 2, 3, 5, 10, 11, 13, 15, 21, then the mean deviation about the median of all the 10 observations is

    1. $7$
    2. $6$
    3. $5$
    4. $4$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    Sum $=90$, and the eight given values add to 80, so the other two add to 10.
    Sum of squares $=10(34.2+81)=1152$; the eight give 1094, so the other two give 58. Hence they are 3 and 7.
    Ordered data: 2, 3, 3, 5, 7, 10, 11, 13, 15, 21; median $=8.5$.
    Sum of $|x_i-8.5|=50$, so the mean deviation is $5$.

  17. JEE Main 2025, 2 April, Shift 2 · Question 8
    If the mean and the variance of 6, 4, a, 8, b, 12, 10, 13 are 9 and 9.25 respectively, then $a+b+ab$ is equal to :

    1. $100$
    2. $103$
    3. $105$
    4. $106$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    Mean: $53+a+b=72$, so $a+b=19$.
    Variance: $\dfrac{\sum x^2}{8}-81=9.25$, so $\sum x^2=722$ and $a^2+b^2=722-529=193$.
    $2ab=361-193=168$, $ab=84$; $a+b+ab=103$.

  18. JEE Main 2025, 3 April, Shift 2 · Question 9
    Let the Mean and Variance of five observations $x_1=1$, $x_2=3$, $x_3=a$, $x_4=7$ and $x_5=b$, $a>b$, be 5 and 10 respectively. Then the Variance of the observations $n+x_n$, $n=1, 2, \ldots, 5$ is

    1. $16$
    2. $16.4$
    3. $17$
    4. $17.4$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    Mean: $11+a+b=25$, so $a+b=14$. Variance: $\dfrac{59+a^2+b^2}{5}-25=10$, so $a^2+b^2=116$; hence $a=10$, $b=4$.
    New observations $n+x_n$: 2, 5, 13, 11, 9, with mean 8.
    Variance $=\dfrac{4+25+169+121+81}{5}-64=80-64=16$.

  19. JEE Main 2025, 4 April, Shift 2 · Question 8
    Let the mean and the standard deviation of the observation 2, 3, 3, 4, 5, 7, a, b be 4 and $\sqrt2$ respectively. Then the mean deviation about the mode of these observations is :

    1. $2$
    2. $\dfrac12$
    3. $\dfrac34$
    4. $1$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    Mean 4: $24+a+b=32$, so $a+b=8$. Variance 2: $\dfrac{112+a^2+b^2}{8}-16=2$, so $a^2+b^2=32$; hence $a=b=4$.
    Data: 2, 3, 3, 4, 4, 4, 5, 7; mode $=4$.
    Mean deviation about 4 $=\dfrac{2+1+1+0+0+0+1+3}{8}=1$.

  20. JEE Main 2025, 7 April, Shift 1 · Question 9
    The mean and standard deviation of 100 observations are 40 and 5.1, respectively. By mistake one observation is taken as 50 instead of 40. If the correct mean and the correct standard deviation are $\mu$ and $\sigma$ respectively, then $10(\mu+\sigma)$ is equal to

    1. $451$
    2. $449$
    3. $447$
    4. $445$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    Incorrect $\sum x=4000$, $\sum x^2=100(5.1^2+40^2)=162601$.
    Correct $\sum x=3990$, so $\mu=39.9$; correct $\sum x^2=162601-2500+1600=161701$.
    $\sigma^2=1617.01-39.9^2=1617.01-1592.01=25$, $\sigma=5$.
    $10(\mu+\sigma)=10\times44.9=449$.

  21. JEE Main 2025, 23 January, Shift 1 · Question 17
    Marks obtains by all the students of class 12 are presented in a freqency distribution with classes of equal width. Let the median of this grouped data be 14 with median class interval 12-18 and median class frequency 12. If the number of students whose marks are less than 12 is 18, then the total number of students is

    1. $40$
    2. $44$
    3. $48$
    4. $52$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    Median $=l+\dfrac{\frac N2-cf}{f}\times h$ with $l=12$, $h=6$, $f=12$, $cf=18$.
    $14=12+\dfrac{\frac N2-18}{12}\times6 \Rightarrow \dfrac N2-18=4 \Rightarrow N=44$.

  22. JEE Main 2025, 23 January, Shift 2 · Question 24
    The variance of the numbers 8, 21, 34, 47, ..., 320 is __________.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 8788

    This is an A.P. with $d=13$: $8+13(n-1)=320 \Rightarrow n=25$.
    For $n$ terms of an A.P., variance $=\dfrac{d^2(n^2-1)}{12}=\dfrac{169\times624}{12}=169\times52=8788$.

  23. JEE Main 2025, 24 January, Shift 1 · Question 7
    For a statistical data $x_1, x_2, \ldots, x_{10}$ of 10 values, a student obtained the mean as 5.5 and $\displaystyle\sum_{i=1}^{10}x_i^2=371$. He later found that he had noted two values in the data incorrectly as 4 and 5, instead of the correct values 6 and 8, respectively. The variance of the corrected data is

    1. $4$
    2. $5$
    3. $7$
    4. $9$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    Original sum $=55$. Corrected sum $=55-4-5+6+8=60$, so the mean is $6$.
    Corrected $\sum x_i^2=371-16-25+36+64=430$.
    Variance $=\dfrac{430}{10}-6^2=43-36=7$.

  24. JEE Main 2025, 29 January, Shift 1 · Question 9
    Let $x_1, x_2, \ldots, x_{10}$ be ten observations such that $\displaystyle\sum_{i=1}^{10}(x_i-2)=30$, $\displaystyle\sum_{i=1}^{10}(x_i-\beta)^2=98$, $\beta>2$, and their variance is $\dfrac{4}{5}$. If $\mu$ and $\sigma^2$ are respectively the mean and the variance of $2(x_1-1)+4\beta$, $2(x_2-1)+4\beta$, $\ldots$, $2(x_{10}-1)+4\beta$, then $\dfrac{\beta\mu}{\sigma^2}$ is equal to :

    1. $100$
    2. $90$
    3. $110$
    4. $120$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    $\sum x_i=50$, so the mean is 5, and $\sum(x_i-5)^2=10\cdot\tfrac45=8$.
    $\sum(x_i-\beta)^2=8+10(5-\beta)^2=98\Rightarrow(5-\beta)^2=9\Rightarrow\beta=8$ (as $\beta>2$).
    New data $2x_i+30$: $\mu=2(5)+30=40$, $\sigma^2=4\cdot\tfrac45=\tfrac{16}{5}$.
    $\dfrac{\beta\mu}{\sigma^2}=\dfrac{8\cdot40\cdot5}{16}=100$.
