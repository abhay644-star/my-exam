  1. JEE Main 2026, 2 April, Shift 2 · Question 25
    The number of points in the interval $[2, 4]$, at which the function $f(x)=\left[x^2-x-\dfrac12\right]$, where $[\cdot]$ denotes the greatest integer function, is discontinuous, is __________.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 10

    $g(x)=x^2-x-\dfrac12$ is increasing on $[2,4]$, from $g(2)=\dfrac32$ to $g(4)=\dfrac{23}{2}$.
    $f$ is discontinuous where $g(x)$ crosses an integer: $g(x)=2,3,\ldots,11$.
    Number of points $=10$.

  2. JEE Main 2026, 4 April, Shift 1 · Question 16
    If $y=\tan^{-1}\left(\dfrac{3\cos x-4\sin x}{4\cos x+3\sin x}\right)+2\tan^{-1}\left(\dfrac{x}{1+\sqrt{1-x^2}}\right)$, then $\dfrac{dy}{dx}$ at $x=\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}$ is equal to:

    1. $3$
    2. $-1$
    3. $1$
    4. $2$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    Divide by $4\cos x$: $\dfrac{\frac34-\tan x}{1+\frac34\tan x}=\tan(\theta-x)$ with $\tan\theta=\dfrac34$; near $x=\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}$, $\theta-x\in\left(-\dfrac\pi2,\dfrac\pi2\right)$, so the first term is $\theta-x$, derivative $-1$.
    Put $x=\sin\phi$: $\dfrac{\sin\phi}{1+\cos\phi}=\tan\dfrac\phi2$, so the second term is $\phi=\sin^{-1}x$, derivative $\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{1-x^2}}$.
    At $x=\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}$: $\dfrac{dy}{dx}=-1+\dfrac{1}{1/2}=1$.

  3. JEE Main 2026, 4 April, Shift 1 · Question 25
    The number of points, at which the function $f(x)=\max\{6x,\ 2+3x^2\}+|x-1|\cos\left|x^2-\dfrac14\right|$, $x\in(-\pi,\pi)$, is not differentiable, is ______.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 3

    $\max\{6x,2+3x^2\}$: $3x^2-6x+2=0$ at $x=1\pm\dfrac{1}{\sqrt3}$ (both in $(-\pi,\pi)$). The slopes there are $6$ and $6x\neq6$, so there are $2$ corners.
    $\cos\left|x^2-\dfrac14\right|=\cos\left(x^2-\dfrac14\right)$ is smooth, and $|x-1|\cos\left(x^2-\dfrac14\right)$ has a corner at $x=1$ since $\cos\dfrac34\neq0$.
    At $x=1$ the max part ($=6x$) is smooth. Total: $3$ points.

  4. JEE Main 2026, 4 April, Shift 2 · Question 22
    Let $f(x)=\begin{cases}e^{x-1}, & x<0\\ x^2-5x+6, & x\ge0\end{cases}$ and $g(x)=f(|x|)+|f(x)|$. If the number of points where $g$ is not continuous and is not differentiable are $\alpha$ and $\beta$ respectively, then $\alpha+\beta$ is equal to ________

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 4

    $f(|x|)=x^2-5|x|+6$ for all $x$.
    For $x<0$: $g(x)=x^2+5x+6+e^{x-1}$. For $x\ge0$: $g(x)=(x-2)(x-3)+|(x-2)(x-3)|$, which is $2(x-2)(x-3)$ outside $(2,3)$ and $0$ on $[2,3]$.
    At $x=0$: $g(0^-)=6+e^{-1}$, $g(0)=12$, so $g$ is discontinuous only at $0$: $\alpha=1$.
    At $x=2$: $g'(2^-)=2(2\cdot2-5)=-2$, $g'(2^+)=0$; at $x=3$: $g'(3^-)=0$, $g'(3^+)=2$.
    Not differentiable at $0,2,3$: $\beta=3$. Hence $\alpha+\beta=4$.

  5. JEE Main 2026, 6 April, Shift 2 · Question 25
    Let $f(x)=\begin{cases}x^3+8\,; & x<0,\\ x^2-4\,; & x\ge0,\end{cases}$ and $g(x)=\begin{cases}(x-8)^{1/3}\,; & x<0,\\ (x+4)^{1/2}\,; & x\ge0.\end{cases}$
    Then the number of points, where the function $gof$ is discontinuous, is __________.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 3

    $g$ is continuous except at $0$: $g(0^-)=(-8)^{1/3}=-2$, $g(0)=2$. $f$ is continuous except at $0$: $f(0^-)=8$, $f(0)=-4$.
    So $g\circ f$ can fail only where $f$ crosses $0$ or at $x=0$.
    $x=-2$: $f$ changes sign, $g\circ f$ jumps between $-2$ and $2$ → discontinuous.
    $x=2$: $f=x^2-4$ changes sign → discontinuous.
    $x=0$: $g(f(0^-))=g(8)=\sqrt{12}$, $g(f(0))=g(-4)=(-12)^{1/3}$ → discontinuous.
    Number of points $=3$.

  6. JEE Main 2026, 8 April, Shift 2 · Question 13
    For the function $f(x)=e^{\sin|x|}-|x|$, $x\in\mathbf{R}$, consider the following statements :
    Statement I : $f$ is differentiable for all $x\in\mathbf{R}$.
    Statement II : $f$ is increasing in $\left(-\pi,-\dfrac{\pi}{2}\right)$.
    In the light of the above statements, choose the correct answer from the options given below :

    1. Both Statement I and Statement II are true
    2. Both Statement I and Statement II are false
    3. Statement I is true but Statement II is false
    4. Statement I is false but Statement II is true
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    For $x>0$: $f'(x)=e^{\sin x}\cos x-1\to0$ as $x\to0^+$. For $x<0$: $f(x)=e^{-\sin x}+x$, $f'(x)=1-e^{-\sin x}\cos x\to0$ as $x\to0^-$.
    Both one-sided derivatives at $0$ are $0$, and $f$ is differentiable elsewhere: Statement I is true.
    On $\left(-\pi,-\dfrac\pi2\right)$, $\cos x<0$, so $f'(x)=1-e^{-\sin x}\cos x>0$: $f$ is increasing, Statement II is true.
    Both statements are true.

  7. JEE Main 2026, 8 April, Shift 2 · Question 19
    Let $f(x)=\begin{cases}\dfrac{1}{3}, & x\le\dfrac{\pi}{2}\\[2mm] \dfrac{b(1-\sin x)}{(\pi-2x)^2}, & x>\dfrac{\pi}{2}\end{cases}$. If $f$ is continuous at $x=\pi/2$, then the value of $\displaystyle\int_0^{3b-6}\left|x^2+2x-3\right|dx$ is :

    1. $5$
    2. $2$
    3. $3$
    4. $4$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    Put $x=\dfrac\pi2+h$: $\dfrac{b(1-\cos h)}{4h^2}\to\dfrac b8$. Continuity: $\dfrac b8=\dfrac13\Rightarrow b=\dfrac83$, so $3b-6=2$.
    $x^2+2x-3=(x+3)(x-1)$ is negative on $(0,1)$ and positive on $(1,2)$.
    $\displaystyle\int_0^1(3-2x-x^2)dx=\dfrac53$, $\displaystyle\int_1^2(x^2+2x-3)dx=\dfrac73$.
    Value $=\dfrac{12}{3}=4$.

  8. JEE Main 2026, 22 January, Shift 2 · Question 16
    Let $[\cdot]$ denote the greatest integer function, and let $f(x)=\min\{\sqrt2x, x^2\}$. Let $S=\{x\in(-2, 2):$ the function $g(x)=|x|[x^2]$ is discontinuous at $x\}$. Then $\displaystyle\sum_{x\in S}f(x)$ equals

    1. $2-\sqrt2$
    2. $1-\sqrt2$
    3. $\sqrt6-2\sqrt2$
    4. $2\sqrt6-3\sqrt2$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    $[x^2]$ jumps where $x^2=1,2,3$, i.e. $x=\pm1,\pm\sqrt2,\pm\sqrt3$; there $|x|\ne0$, so $g$ is discontinuous. (At $x=0$, $g$ is continuous.)
    For $x>0$: $f(1)=1$, $f(\sqrt2)=2$, $f(\sqrt3)=\min(\sqrt6,3)=\sqrt6$.
    For $x<0$, $\sqrt2x<0<x^2$: $f=-\sqrt2,-2,-\sqrt6$.
    Sum $=1+2+\sqrt6-\sqrt2-2-\sqrt6=1-\sqrt2$.

  9. JEE Main 2026, 23 January, Shift 1 · Question 17
    Let $f(x)=\begin{cases}\dfrac{ax^2+2ax+3}{4x^2+4x-3}, & x\neq-\dfrac32, \dfrac12\\ b, & x=-\dfrac32, \dfrac12\end{cases}$
    be continuous at $x=-\dfrac32$. If $fof(x)=\dfrac75$, then $x$ is equal to :

    1. $0$
    2. $1$
    3. $1.4$
    4. $2$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    $4x^2+4x-3=(2x+3)(2x-1)$. For a finite limit at $x=-\dfrac32$ the numerator must vanish there: $\dfrac{9a}{4}-3a+3=0 \Rightarrow a=4$.
    Then $f(x)=\dfrac{(2x+1)(2x+3)}{(2x+3)(2x-1)}=\dfrac{2x+1}{2x-1}$ (and $b=\dfrac12$).
    $f(f(x))=\dfrac{6x+1}{2x+3}=\dfrac75 \Rightarrow 30x+5=14x+21 \Rightarrow x=1$. Check: $f(1)=3$, $f(3)=\dfrac75$.

  10. JEE Main 2026, 23 January, Shift 2 · Question 17
    If $f(x)=\begin{cases}\dfrac{a|x|+x^2-2\left(\sin|x|\right)\left(\cos|x|\right)}{x}, & x\neq0\\ b, & x=0\end{cases}$
    is continuous at $x=0$, then $a+b$ is equal to

    1. $0$
    2. $1$
    3. $2$
    4. $4$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    The numerator is $a|x|+x^2-\sin2|x|$.
    As $x\to0^+$: $\dfrac{ax+x^2-\sin2x}{x}\to a-2$. As $x\to0^-$ ($|x|=-x$): $\dfrac{-ax+x^2+\sin2x}{x}\to-a+2$.
    Continuity needs $a-2=2-a=b$, so $a=2$, $b=0$ and $a+b=2$.

  11. JEE Main 2026, 24 January, Shift 1 · Question 17
    If the function $f(x)=\dfrac{e^x\left(e^{\tan x-x}-1\right)+\log_e(\sec x+\tan x)-x}{\tan x-x}$ is continuous at $x=0$, then the value of $f(0)$ is equal to

    1. $\dfrac{1}{2}$
    2. $\dfrac{2}{3}$
    3. $\dfrac{3}{2}$
    4. $2$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    $e^x\left(e^{\tan x-x}-1\right)=e^{\tan x}-e^x$, and $\dfrac{e^{\tan x}-e^x}{\tan x-x}=e^x\cdot\dfrac{e^{\tan x-x}-1}{\tan x-x}\to1$.
    Near 0: $\log_e(\sec x+\tan x)=x+\dfrac{x^3}6+\ldots$ and $\tan x-x=\dfrac{x^3}3+\ldots$, so the second part $\to\dfrac{1/6}{1/3}=\dfrac12$.
    $f(0)=1+\dfrac12=\dfrac32$.

  12. JEE Main 2026, 24 January, Shift 1 · Question 18
    Let $\alpha, \beta\in\mathbb{R}$ be such that the function $f(x)=\begin{cases}2\alpha\left(x^2-2\right)+2\beta x, & x<1\\(\alpha+3)x+(\alpha-\beta), & x\ge1\end{cases}$
    be differentiable at all $x\in\mathbb{R}$. Then $34(\alpha+\beta)$ is equal to

    1. $24$
    2. $36$
    3. $48$
    4. $84$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    Continuity at 1: $-2\alpha+2\beta=2\alpha+3-\beta \Rightarrow 3\beta-4\alpha=3$.
    Equal derivatives at 1: $4\alpha+2\beta=\alpha+3 \Rightarrow 3\alpha+2\beta=3$.
    Solving: $\alpha=\dfrac3{17}$, $\beta=\dfrac{21}{17}$, so $34(\alpha+\beta)=34\times\dfrac{24}{17}=48$.

  13. JEE Main 2026, 24 January, Shift 2 · Question 16
    Let $[t]$ denote the greatest integer less than or equal to $t$. If the function
    $f(x)=\begin{cases}b^2\sin\left(\dfrac{\pi}{2}\left[\dfrac{\pi}{2}(\cos x+\sin x)\cos x\right]\right), & x<0\\ \dfrac{\sin x-\frac12\sin2x}{x^3}, & x>0\\ a, & x=0\end{cases}$
    is continuous at $x=0$, then $a^2+b^2$ is equal to

    1. $\dfrac{3}{4}$
    2. $\dfrac{9}{16}$
    3. $\dfrac{1}{2}$
    4. $\dfrac{5}{8}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    Right limit: $\dfrac{\sin x(1-\cos x)}{x^3}\to1\times\dfrac12=\dfrac12$, so $a=\dfrac12$.
    Left limit: for small $x<0$, $(\cos x+\sin x)\cos x=\cos^2x+\sin x\cos x$ is just below 1, so $\dfrac\pi2(\ldots)$ is just below $1.57$ and its greatest integer is 1.
    Left limit $=b^2\sin\dfrac\pi2=b^2=\dfrac12$.
    $a^2+b^2=\dfrac14+\dfrac12=\dfrac34$.

  14. JEE Main 2026, 28 January, Shift 2 · Question 16
    Let $f(x)=\displaystyle\lim_{\theta\to0}\left(\dfrac{\cos\pi x-x^{\left(\frac2\theta\right)}\sin(x-1)}{1+x^{\left(\frac2\theta\right)}(x-1)}\right)$, $x\in\mathbf{R}$. Consider the following two statements :
    (I) $f(x)$ is discontinous at $x=1$.
    (II) $f(x)$ is continous at $x=-1$.
    Then,

    1. Only (I) is True
    2. Only (II) is True
    3. Both (I) and (II) are True
    4. Neither (I) nor (II) is True
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    Write $n=2/\theta\to\infty$ (an even power in the intended reading). For $|x|<1$, $x^n\to0$ and $f(x)=\cos\pi x$. For $|x|>1$, $f(x)=-\dfrac{\sin(x-1)}{x-1}$.
    At $x=1$: $f(1)=-1$, left limit $\cos\pi=-1$, right limit $-1$. Continuous, so (I) is false.
    At $x=-1$: right limit $\cos(-\pi)=-1$, left limit $-\dfrac{\sin(-2)}{-2}=-\dfrac{\sin2}{2}\ne-1$. Discontinuous, so (II) is false.
