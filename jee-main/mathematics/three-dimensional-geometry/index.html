  1. JEE Main 2026, 2 April, Shift 1 · Question 13
    If the point of intersection of the lines $\dfrac{x+1}{3}=\dfrac{y+a}{5}=\dfrac{z+b+1}{7}$ and $\dfrac{x-2}{1}=\dfrac{y-b}{4}=\dfrac{z-2a}{7}$ lies on $xy$-plane, then the value of $a+b$ is :

    1. $2$
    2. $5$
    3. $7$
    4. $9$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    Points: $(3t-1,\,5t-a,\,7t-b-1)$ and $(s+2,\,4s+b,\,7s+2a)$.
    $z=0$: $t=\dfrac{b+1}{7}$, $s=-\dfrac{2a}{7}$.
    Equal $x$: $3(b+1)-7=-2a+14 \Rightarrow 2a+3b=18$.
    Equal $y$: $5(b+1)-7a=-8a+7b \Rightarrow a=2b-5$.
    So $b=4,\ a=3$ and $a+b=7$.

  2. JEE Main 2026, 2 April, Shift 1 · Question 15
    Let a line $L$ passing through the point $(1,1,1)$ be perpendicular to both the vectors $2\hat{i}+2\hat{j}+\hat{k}$ and $\hat{i}+2\hat{j}+2\hat{k}$. If $P(a,b,c)$ is the foot of perpendicular from the origin on the line $L$, then the value of $34(a+b+c)$ is :

    1. $50$
    2. $80$
    3. $100$
    4. $120$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    Direction of $L$: $(2,2,1)\times(1,2,2)=(2,-3,2)$.
    General point $P=(1+2t,\,1-3t,\,1+2t)$.
    $\overrightarrow{OP}\perp L$: $2(1+2t)-3(1-3t)+2(1+2t)=1+17t=0 \Rightarrow t=-\dfrac1{17}$.
    $P=\left(\dfrac{15}{17},\dfrac{20}{17},\dfrac{15}{17}\right)$, so $34(a+b+c)=34\cdot\dfrac{50}{17}=100$.

  3. JEE Main 2026, 2 April, Shift 2 · Question 15
    Let the point A be the foot of perpendicular drawn from the point $P(a, b, 0)$ on the line $\dfrac{x-1}{2}=\dfrac{y-2}{1}=\dfrac{z-\alpha}{3}$. If the midpoint of the line segment PA is $\left(0, \dfrac34, \dfrac{-1}{4}\right)$, then the value of $a^2+b^2+\alpha^2$ is equal to :

    1. $1$
    2. $2$
    3. $6$
    4. $9$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    Let $A=(1+2t,\ 2+t,\ \alpha+3t)$.
    Midpoint: $a=-1-2t$, $b=-\dfrac12-t$, $\alpha=-\dfrac12-3t$.
    $\overrightarrow{PA}=\left(2+4t,\ \dfrac52+2t,\ -\dfrac12\right)$ is perpendicular to $(2,1,3)$: $4+8t+\dfrac52+2t-\dfrac32=0 \Rightarrow t=-\dfrac12$.
    $a=0$, $b=0$, $\alpha=1$, so $a^2+b^2+\alpha^2=1$.

  4. JEE Main 2026, 4 April, Shift 1 · Question 14
    A line with direction ratios $1,-1,2$ intersects the lines $\dfrac{x}{2}=\dfrac{y}{3}=\dfrac{z+1}{3}$ and $\dfrac{x+1}{-1}=\dfrac{y-2}{1}=\dfrac{z}{4}$ at the points $P$ and $Q$, respectively. If the length of the line segment $PQ$ is $\alpha$, then $225\alpha^2$ is equal to:

    1. $1024$
    2. $1014$
    3. $1104$
    4. $1204$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    $P=(2s,3s,3s-1)$, $Q=(-1-t,\ 2+t,\ 4t)$, and $\overrightarrow{PQ}=\lambda(1,-1,2)$.
    $-1-t-2s=\lambda$ and $2+t-3s=-\lambda$; adding gives $1-5s=0$, so $s=\dfrac15$.
    Then $\lambda=-t-\dfrac75$, and $4t-3s+1=2\lambda$ gives $4t+\dfrac25=-2t-\dfrac{14}{5} \Rightarrow t=-\dfrac{8}{15}$, $\lambda=-\dfrac{13}{15}$.
    $\alpha=|\lambda|\sqrt6$, so $\alpha^2=\dfrac{169\times6}{225}$ and $225\alpha^2=1014$.

  5. JEE Main 2026, 4 April, Shift 1 · Question 15
    The square of the distance of the point $(-2,-8,6)$ from the line $\dfrac{x-1}{1}=\dfrac{y-1}{2}=\dfrac{z}{-1}$ along the line $\dfrac{x+5}{1}=\dfrac{y+5}{-1}=\dfrac{z}{2}$ is equal to:

    1. $3$
    2. $6$
    3. $8$
    4. $12$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    Move from $(-2,-8,6)$ along direction $(1,-1,2)$: point $(-2+t,\ -8-t,\ 6+2t)$.
    It lies on the first line when $t-3=\dfrac{-9-t}{2}=-6-2t$, giving $t=-1$ (point $(-3,-7,4)$).
    Distance$^2=t^2(1+1+4)=6$.

  6. JEE Main 2026, 4 April, Shift 2 · Question 13
    The shortest distance between the lines $\vec r=\left(\dfrac13\hat i+2\hat j+\dfrac83\hat k\right)+\lambda\left(2\hat i-5\hat j+6\hat k\right)$ and $\vec r=\left(-\dfrac23\hat i-\dfrac13\hat k\right)+\mu\left(\hat j-\hat k\right)$, $\lambda,\mu\in\mathbb{R}$, is:

    1. $\sqrt5$
    2. $3$
    3. $2\sqrt3$
    4. $\sqrt{15}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    $\vec b_1\times\vec b_2=(2,-5,6)\times(0,1,-1)=(-1,2,2)$, of magnitude $3$.
    $\vec a_1-\vec a_2=(1,2,3)$.
    Shortest distance $=\dfrac{|(1,2,3)\cdot(-1,2,2)|}{3}=\dfrac{9}{3}=3$.

  7. JEE Main 2026, 4 April, Shift 2 · Question 14
    If $\left(2\alpha+1,\ \alpha^2-3\alpha,\ \dfrac{\alpha-1}{2}\right)$ is the image of $(\alpha,2\alpha,1)$ in the line $\dfrac{x-2}{3}=\dfrac{y-1}{2}=\dfrac z1$, then the possible value(s) of $\alpha$ is (are)

    1. Only $3$
    2. Only $3$ and $-1$
    3. Only $3$, $\dfrac14$ and $-1$
    4. Only $3$ and $\dfrac14$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    $PP'=\left(\alpha+1,\ \alpha^2-5\alpha,\ \dfrac{\alpha-3}{2}\right)$ must be perpendicular to $(3,2,1)$:
    $3(\alpha+1)+2(\alpha^2-5\alpha)+\dfrac{\alpha-3}{2}=0\Rightarrow4\alpha^2-13\alpha+3=0\Rightarrow\alpha=3$ or $\dfrac14$.
    Midpoint $\left(\dfrac{3\alpha+1}{2},\dfrac{\alpha^2-\alpha}{2},\dfrac{\alpha+1}{4}\right)$ must lie on the line: $\dfrac{x-2}{3}=\dfrac{\alpha-1}{2}$ and $z=\dfrac{\alpha+1}{4}$.
    $\dfrac{\alpha-1}{2}=\dfrac{\alpha+1}{4}\Rightarrow\alpha=3$ (then $\dfrac{y-1}{2}=\dfrac{3-1}{2}=1$ also matches); $\alpha=\dfrac14$ fails.
    So only $\alpha=3$.

  8. JEE Main 2026, 5 April, Shift 1 · Question 13
    The square of the distance of the point $P(5, 6, 7)$ from the line $\dfrac{x-2}{2} = \dfrac{y-5}{3} = \dfrac{z-2}{4}$ is equal to:

    1. $3$
    2. $5$
    3. $6$
    4. $8$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    A point on the line: $Q=(2+2t,\,5+3t,\,2+4t)$, so $\vec{PQ}=(2t-3,\,3t-1,\,4t-5)$.
    $\vec{PQ}\perp(2,3,4)$: $2(2t-3)+3(3t-1)+4(4t-5)=29t-29=0 \Rightarrow t=1$.
    $\vec{PQ}=(-1,2,-1)$, so the squared distance is $1+4+1=6$.

  9. JEE Main 2026, 5 April, Shift 1 · Question 15
    The square of the distance of the point of intersection of the lines $\vec{r} = (\hat{i} + \hat{j} - \hat{k}) + \lambda(a\hat{i} - \hat{j})$, $a \neq 0$ and $\vec{r} = (4\hat{i} - \hat{k}) + \mu(2\hat{i} + a\hat{k})$ from the origin is:

    1. $5$
    2. $10$
    3. $17$
    4. $26$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    Points: $(1+a\lambda,\,1-\lambda,\,-1)$ and $(4+2\mu,\,0,\,-1+a\mu)$.
    $z$: $-1=-1+a\mu \Rightarrow \mu=0$ (as $a\neq0$); $y$: $1-\lambda=0 \Rightarrow \lambda=1$; $x$: $1+a=4 \Rightarrow a=3$.
    Intersection point $(4,0,-1)$; squared distance from O $=16+0+1=17$.

  10. JEE Main 2026, 5 April, Shift 2 · Question 14
    Let a triangle PQR be such that P and Q lie on the line $\dfrac{x+3}{8}=\dfrac{y-4}{2}=\dfrac{z+1}{2}$ and are at a distance of 6 units from $R(1,2,3)$. If $(\alpha,\beta,\gamma)$ is the centroid of $\Delta PQR$, then $\alpha+\beta+\gamma$ is equal to :

    1. $4$
    2. $5$
    3. $6$
    4. $8$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    Since $RP=RQ$, the midpoint of PQ is the foot M of the perpendicular from R to the line.
    Point on line: $(-3+8t, 4+2t, -1+2t)$; $(4-8t, -2-2t, 4-2t)\cdot(8,2,2)=36-72t=0 \Rightarrow t=\dfrac12$, $M=(1,5,0)$ (and $RM=\sqrt{18}<6$).
    Centroid $=\dfrac{P+Q+R}{3}=\dfrac{2M+R}{3}=\dfrac{(3,12,3)}{3}=(1,4,1)$.
    $\alpha+\beta+\gamma=6$.

  11. JEE Main 2026, 5 April, Shift 2 · Question 15
    If the distance of the point $(a,2,5)$ from the image of the point $(1,2,7)$ in the line $\dfrac{x}{1}=\dfrac{y-1}{1}=\dfrac{z-2}{2}$ is 4, then the sum of all possible values of $a$ is equal to :

    1. $11$
    2. $9$
    3. $6$
    4. $4$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    Point on line: $(t, 1+t, 2+2t)$. Foot: $(t-1, t-1, 2t-5)\cdot(1,1,2)=6t-12=0 \Rightarrow t=2$, foot $(2,3,6)$.
    Image of $(1,2,7)$: $2(2,3,6)-(1,2,7)=(3,4,5)$.
    $(a-3)^2+(2-4)^2+0=16 \Rightarrow (a-3)^2=12 \Rightarrow a=3\pm2\sqrt3$.
    Sum of values $=6$.

  12. JEE Main 2026, 6 April, Shift 1 · Question 15
    Let the image of the point $P(1,6,a)$ in the line $L:\dfrac{x}{1}=\dfrac{y-1}{2}=\dfrac{z-a+1}{b}$, $b>0$, be $\left(\dfrac a3,0,a+c\right)$. If $S(\alpha,\beta,\gamma)$, $\alpha>0$, is the point on $L$ such that the distance of $S$ from the foot of perpendicular from the point $P$ on $L$ is $2\sqrt{14}$, then $\alpha+\beta+\gamma$ is equal to:

    1. $19$
    2. $20$
    3. $21$
    4. $22$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    A point of $L$ is $(t,1+2t,a-1+bt)$. The midpoint of $P$ and its image is $\left(\dfrac{1+a/3}{2},3,\dfrac{2a+c}{2}\right)$ and lies on $L$.
    $y$: $1+2t=3\Rightarrow t=1$; $x$: $\dfrac{1+a/3}{2}=1\Rightarrow a=3$; $z$: $2+b=\dfrac{6+c}{2}\Rightarrow c=2b-2$.
    $P'-P=(0,-6,c)$ is perpendicular to $(1,2,b)$: $-12+bc=0\Rightarrow b(2b-2)=12\Rightarrow b=3$, $c=4$.
    Foot $M=(1,3,5)$, direction $(1,2,3)$ with length $\sqrt{14}$, so $S=M\pm2(1,2,3)$.
    $\alpha>0$: $S=(3,7,11)$, and $\alpha+\beta+\gamma=21$.

  13. JEE Main 2026, 6 April, Shift 1 · Question 16
    Let a line L be perpendicular to both the lines $L_1:\dfrac{x+1}{3}=\dfrac{y+3}{5}=\dfrac{z+5}{7}$ and $L_2:\dfrac{x-2}{1}=\dfrac{y-4}{4}=\dfrac{z-6}{7}$.
    If $\theta$ is the acute angle between the lines L and $L_3:\dfrac{x-\frac87}{2}=\dfrac{y-\frac47}{1}=\dfrac{z}{2}$, then $\tan\theta$ is equal to:

    1. $\dfrac32\sqrt2$
    2. $\dfrac52\sqrt2$
    3. $\dfrac53\sqrt2$
    4. $\dfrac43\sqrt2$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    Direction of L: $(3,5,7)\times(1,4,7)=(7,-14,7)\parallel(1,-2,1)$.
    Direction of $L_3$: $(2,1,2)$.
    $\cos\theta=\dfrac{|2-2+2|}{\sqrt6\cdot3}=\dfrac{2}{3\sqrt6}$, so $\sin\theta=\sqrt{1-\dfrac{4}{54}}=\dfrac{\sqrt{50}}{3\sqrt6}$.
    $\tan\theta=\dfrac{\sqrt{50}}{2}=\dfrac52\sqrt2$.

  14. JEE Main 2026, 6 April, Shift 2 · Question 14
    The shortest distance between the lines $\dfrac{x-4}{1}=\dfrac{y-3}{2}=\dfrac{z-2}{-3}$ and $\dfrac{x+2}{2}=\dfrac{y-6}{4}=\dfrac{z-5}{-5}$ is :

    1. $\dfrac{5\sqrt6}{6}$
    2. $2\sqrt5$
    3. $3\sqrt5$
    4. $4\sqrt5$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    Points $A(4,3,2)$, $B(-2,6,5)$; directions $(1,2,-3)$, $(2,4,-5)$.
    $\vec n=(1,2,-3)\times(2,4,-5)=(2,-1,0)$, $|\vec n|=\sqrt5$.
    $\vec{AB}=(-6,3,3)$, $\vec{AB}\cdot\vec n=-12-3=-15$.
    Shortest distance $=\dfrac{15}{\sqrt5}=3\sqrt5$.

  15. JEE Main 2026, 6 April, Shift 2 · Question 24
    Let the image of the point P$(0,-5,0)$ in the line $\dfrac{x-1}{2}=\dfrac y1=\dfrac{z+1}{-2}$ be the point R and the image of the point Q$\left(0,\dfrac{-1}{2},0\right)$ in the line $\dfrac{x-1}{-1}=\dfrac{y+9}{4}=\dfrac{z+1}{1}$ be the point S. Then the square of the area of the parallelogram PQRS is __________.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 162

    Foot of P on the first line: $(1+2\lambda,\lambda,-1-2\lambda)$ with $(1+2\lambda,\lambda+5,-1-2\lambda)\cdot(2,1,-2)=0 \Rightarrow 9\lambda+9=0$, $\lambda=-1$: foot F $=(-1,-1,1)$, so R $=(-2,3,2)$.
    Foot of Q on the second line: $(1-\mu,-9+4\mu,-1+\mu)$ with $\left(1-\mu,-\tfrac{17}{2}+4\mu,-1+\mu\right)\cdot(-1,4,1)=0 \Rightarrow 18\mu-36=0$, $\mu=2$: foot $(-1,-1,1)=$ F, so S $=\left(-2,-\tfrac32,2\right)$.
    F is the mid point of both PR and QS, so PQRS is a parallelogram with diagonals $\vec{PR}=(-2,8,2)$, $\vec{QS}=(-2,-1,2)$.
    $\vec{PR}\times\vec{QS}=(18,0,18)$, magnitude $18\sqrt2$; area $=\dfrac12\cdot18\sqrt2=9\sqrt2$.
    Square of area $=162$.

  16. JEE Main 2026, 8 April, Shift 2 · Question 15
    Let the foot of perpendicular from the point $(\lambda,2,3)$ on the line $\dfrac{x-4}{1}=\dfrac{y-9}{2}=\dfrac{z-5}{1}$ be the point $(1,\mu,2)$. Then the distance between the lines $\dfrac{x-1}{2}=\dfrac{y-2}{3}=\dfrac{z+4}{6}$ and $\dfrac{x-\lambda}{2}=\dfrac{y-\mu}{3}=\dfrac{z+5}{6}$ is equal to :

    1. $\dfrac{12}{7}$
    2. $\dfrac{\sqrt{145}}{7}$
    3. $\dfrac{\sqrt{146}}{7}$
    4. $\dfrac{\sqrt{143}}{7}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    A point of the line is $(4+t,9+2t,5+t)$; $x=1$ gives $t=-3$, so the foot is $(1,3,2)$ and $\mu=3$.
    Perpendicularity: $(\lambda-1,\,2-3,\,3-2)\cdot(1,2,1)=\lambda-1-2+1=0\Rightarrow\lambda=2$.
    The lines are parallel with direction $(2,3,6)$, $|\vec d|=7$, through $(1,2,-4)$ and $(2,3,-5)$.
    Joining vector $(1,1,-1)$; $(1,1,-1)\times(2,3,6)=(9,-8,1)$, of length $\sqrt{146}$.
    Distance $=\dfrac{\sqrt{146}}{7}$.

  17. JEE Main 2026, 8 April, Shift 2 · Question 23
    Let a line $L_1$ pass through the origin and be perpendicular to the lines
    $L_2:\ \vec{r}=(3+t)\hat{i}+(2t-1)\hat{j}+(2t+4)\hat{k}$ and
    $L_3:\ \vec{r}=(3+2s)\hat{i}+(3+2s)\hat{j}+(2+s)\hat{k}$, $t,s\in\mathbf{R}$.
    If $(a,b,c)$, $a\in\mathbf{Z}$, is the point on $L_3$ at a distance of $\sqrt{17}$ from the point of intersection of $L_1$ and $L_2$, then $(a+b+c)^2$ is equal to __________.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 4

    Directions: $L_2$: $(1,2,2)$, $L_3$: $(2,2,1)$; their cross product is $(-2,3,-2)$, the direction of $L_1$.
    $L_1\cap L_2$: $(3+t,2t-1,2t+4)=\lambda(-2,3,-2)$. The $x$ and $z$ equations give $3+t=2t+4\Rightarrow t=-1$, point $(2,-3,2)$ ($\lambda=-1$, consistent).
    Point on $L_3$: $(3+2s,3+2s,2+s)$; distance$^2$: $(1+2s)^2+(6+2s)^2+s^2=17\Rightarrow9s^2+28s+20=0$.
    $s=-2$ or $s=-\dfrac{10}9$; $a\in\mathbf Z$ needs $s=-2$: point $(-1,-1,0)$.
    $(a+b+c)^2=4$.
