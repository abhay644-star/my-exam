  1. JEE Main 2026, 2 April, Shift 1 · Question 5
    Let $A$ be the set of first $101$ terms of an A.P., whose first term is $1$ and the common difference is $5$ and let $B$ be the set of first $71$ terms of an A.P., whose first term is $9$ and the common difference is $7$. Then the number of elements in $A \cap B$, which are divisible by $3$, is :

    1. $4$
    2. $5$
    3. $6$
    4. $7$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    $A=\{1,6,\ldots,501\}$ ($x\equiv1 \pmod 5$), $B=\{9,16,\ldots,499\}$ ($x\equiv2 \pmod 7$).
    Common terms: $x\equiv16 \pmod{35}$, i.e. $x=16+35t \le 499 \Rightarrow t=0,1,\ldots,13$.
    $16+35t\equiv 1+2t \equiv 0 \pmod 3 \Rightarrow t\equiv1 \pmod 3$: $t=1,4,7,10,13$.
    So $5$ elements.

  2. JEE Main 2026, 2 April, Shift 1 · Question 22
    If $\displaystyle\sum_{k=1}^{n}a_k=6n^3$, then $\displaystyle\sum_{k=1}^{6}\left(\frac{a_{k+1}-a_k}{36}\right)^2$ is equal to __________.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 91

    $a_k=6k^3-6(k-1)^3=6(3k^2-3k+1)$.
    $a_{k+1}-a_k=6\left[3(2k+1)-3\right]=36k$.
    So the sum is $\sum_{k=1}^{6}k^2=91$.

  3. JEE Main 2026, 2 April, Shift 2 · Question 4
    Let $a_1, a_2, a_3, \ldots$ be an A.P. and $g_1=a_1, g_2, g_3, \ldots$ be an increasing G.P. If $a_1=a_2+g_2=1$ and $a_3+g_3=4$, then $a_{10}+g_5$ is equal to :

    1. $81$
    2. $76$
    3. $62$
    4. $55$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    $a_1=g_1=1$; let the A.P. have common difference $d$ and the G.P. ratio $q$.
    $a_2+g_2=1+d+q=1 \Rightarrow d=-q$.
    $a_3+g_3=1+2d+q^2=4 \Rightarrow q^2-2q-3=0 \Rightarrow q=3$ (increasing G.P.), $d=-3$.
    $a_{10}=1+9(-3)=-26$, $g_5=3^4=81$.
    $a_{10}+g_5=55$.

  4. JEE Main 2026, 2 April, Shift 2 · Question 5
    The sum $\dfrac{1^3}{1}+\dfrac{1^3+2^3}{1+3}+\dfrac{1^3+2^3+3^3}{1+3+5}+\ldots$ up to 8 terms, is :

    1. $70$
    2. $71$
    3. $72$
    4. $73$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    $n$th term $=\dfrac{\left(\frac{n(n+1)}{2}\right)^2}{n^2}=\dfrac{(n+1)^2}{4}$.
    Sum $=\dfrac14\left(2^2+3^2+\ldots+9^2\right)=\dfrac14(285-1)=71$.

  5. JEE Main 2026, 4 April, Shift 1 · Question 7
    The first term of an A.P. of $30$ non-negative terms is $\dfrac{10}{3}$. If the sum of this A.P. is the cube of its last term, then its common difference is:

    1. $\dfrac{5}{87}$
    2. $\dfrac{25}{83}$
    3. $\dfrac{15}{29}$
    4. $\dfrac{5}{29}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    Let $l=a+29d$ be the last term. $S_{30}=15(a+l)=l^3$.
    With $a=\dfrac{10}{3}$: $15\left(\dfrac{10}{3}+l\right)=l^3 \Rightarrow l^3-15l-50=0 \Rightarrow (l-5)(l^2+5l+10)=0$.
    So $l=5$ (the quadratic has no real root).
    $29d=5-\dfrac{10}{3}=\dfrac53 \Rightarrow d=\dfrac{5}{87}$.

  6. JEE Main 2026, 4 April, Shift 2 · Question 7
    Let $\alpha=\dfrac14+\dfrac18+\dfrac1{16}+\ldots\infty$ and $\beta=\dfrac13+\dfrac19+\dfrac1{27}+\ldots\infty$. Then the value of $(0.2)^{\log_{\sqrt5}(\alpha)}+(0.04)^{\log_5(\beta)}$ is equal to:

    1. $4$
    2. $5$
    3. $8$
    4. $25$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    $\alpha=\dfrac{1/4}{1-1/2}=\dfrac12$, $\beta=\dfrac{1/3}{1-1/3}=\dfrac12$.
    $(0.2)^{\log_{\sqrt5}(1/2)}=5^{-2\log_5(1/2)}=\left(\dfrac12\right)^{-2}=4$.
    $(0.04)^{\log_5(1/2)}=5^{-2\log_5(1/2)}=4$.
    Sum $=8$.

  7. JEE Main 2026, 5 April, Shift 1 · Question 2
    Let the sum of the first $n$ terms of an A.P. be $3n^2 + 5n$. Then the sum of squares of the first $10$ terms of the A.P. is:

    1. $10220$
    2. $12860$
    3. $15220$
    4. $19780$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    $a_n=S_n-S_{n-1}=(3n^2+5n)-(3(n-1)^2+5(n-1))=6n+2$.
    $\sum_{n=1}^{10}(6n+2)^2=36\sum n^2+24\sum n+4\cdot10$.
    $=36(385)+24(55)+40=13860+1320+40=15220$.

  8. JEE Main 2026, 5 April, Shift 1 · Question 5

    $\displaystyle \sum_{n=1}^{10}\left(\frac{528}{n(n+1)(n+2)}\right)$
    is equal to:

    1. $65$
    2. $130$
    3. $220$
    4. $440$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    $\dfrac{1}{n(n+1)(n+2)}=\dfrac12\left[\dfrac{1}{n(n+1)}-\dfrac{1}{(n+1)(n+2)}\right]$.
    Telescoping: $\sum_{n=1}^{10}=\dfrac12\left[\dfrac12-\dfrac{1}{132}\right]=\dfrac12\cdot\dfrac{65}{132}=\dfrac{65}{264}$.
    $528\times\dfrac{65}{264}=130$.

  9. JEE Main 2026, 5 April, Shift 2 · Question 1
    Let $\alpha, \beta$ be the roots of the equation $x^2-x+p=0$ and $\gamma, \delta$ be the roots the equation $x^2-4x+q=0$; $p, q \in \mathbb{Z}$. If $\alpha, \beta, \gamma, \delta$ are in G.P., then $|p+q|$ equals :

    1. $16$
    2. $32$
    3. $34$
    4. $38$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    Let the G.P. be $\alpha, \alpha r, \alpha r^2, \alpha r^3$.
    $\alpha+\beta=\alpha(1+r)=1$ and $\gamma+\delta=\alpha r^2(1+r)=4$, so $r^2=4$, $r=\pm2$.
    $r=2$: $\alpha=\dfrac13$, $p=\alpha\beta=\dfrac29\notin\mathbb{Z}$, rejected.
    $r=-2$: $\alpha=-1$, terms $-1, 2, -4, 8$; $p=-2$, $q=(-4)(8)=-32$.
    $|p+q|=34$.

  10. JEE Main 2026, 5 April, Shift 2 · Question 5
    If the sum of the first 10 terms of the series

    $\displaystyle \dfrac{1}{1+1^4\times4}+\dfrac{2}{1+2^4\times4}+\dfrac{3}{1+3^4\times4}+\dfrac{4}{1+4^4\times4}+\ldots$
    is $\dfrac{m}{n}$, $\gcd(m,n)=1$, then $m+n$ is equal to :

    1. $256$
    2. $264$
    3. $276$
    4. $284$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    $1+4n^4=(2n^2+2n+1)(2n^2-2n+1)$, and the difference of these factors is $4n$.
    So $T_n=\dfrac{n}{1+4n^4}=\dfrac14\left[\dfrac{1}{2n^2-2n+1}-\dfrac{1}{2n^2+2n+1}\right]$.
    Since $2(n+1)^2-2(n+1)+1=2n^2+2n+1$, the sum telescopes: $S_{10}=\dfrac14\left[1-\dfrac{1}{221}\right]=\dfrac{55}{221}$.
    $\gcd(55,221)=1$, so $m+n=276$.

  11. JEE Main 2026, 5 April, Shift 2 · Question 6
    Let $A_1, A_2, A_3, \ldots, A_{39}$ be 39 arithmetic means between the numbers 59 and 159. Then the mean of $A_{25}, A_{28}, A_{31}$ and $A_{36}$ is equal to :

    1. $129$
    2. $136$
    3. $131.50$
    4. $134$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    There are 41 terms in all, so $d=\dfrac{159-59}{40}=2.5$ and $A_k=59+2.5k$.
    Mean of the four $=59+2.5\times\dfrac{25+28+31+36}{4}=59+2.5\times30=134$.

  12. JEE Main 2026, 6 April, Shift 1 · Question 5
    The value of $1^3-2^3+3^3-\ldots+15^3$ is:

    1. $1706$
    2. $1856$
    3. $1982$
    4. $2403$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    Sum $=\sum_{k=1}^{15}k^3-2\sum_{k=1}^{7}(2k)^3=\left(\dfrac{15\cdot16}{2}\right)^2-16\left(\dfrac{7\cdot8}{2}\right)^2$.
    $=14400-16\times784=14400-12544=1856$.

  13. JEE Main 2026, 6 April, Shift 1 · Question 6
    The sum of the first ten terms of an A.P. is 160 and the sum of the first two terms of a G.P. is 8. If the first term of the A.P. is equal to the common ratio of the G.P. and the first term of the G.P. is equal to common difference of the A.P., then the sum of all possible values of the first term of the G.P. is:

    1. $\dfrac{34}{9}$
    2. $\dfrac{34}{13}$
    3. $\dfrac{32}{9}$
    4. $\dfrac{32}{13}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    A.P.: first term $a$, difference $d$: $5(2a+9d)=160\Rightarrow a=\dfrac{32-9d}{2}$.
    G.P.: first term $d$, ratio $a$: $d(1+a)=8\Rightarrow d\cdot\dfrac{34-9d}{2}=8$.
    $9d^2-34d+16=0$ (real roots, discriminant $1156-576>0$).
    Sum of the possible first terms $d$ of the G.P. $=\dfrac{34}{9}$.

  14. JEE Main 2026, 6 April, Shift 1 · Question 24
    For the functions $f(\theta)=\alpha\tan^2\theta+\beta\cot^2\theta$, and $g(\theta)=\alpha\sin^2\theta+\beta\cos^2\theta$, $\alpha>\beta>0$, let $\min\limits_{0<\theta<\frac\pi2}f(\theta)=\max\limits_{0<\theta<\pi}g(\theta)$. If the first term of a G.P. is $\left(\dfrac{\alpha}{2\beta}\right)$, its common ratio is $\left(\dfrac{2\beta}{\alpha}\right)$ and the sum of its first 10 terms is $\dfrac mn$, $\gcd(m,n)=1$, then $m+n$ is equal to ________.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 1279

    By AM-GM, $\min f=2\sqrt{\alpha\beta}$. $g=\beta+(\alpha-\beta)\sin^2\theta$ has maximum $\alpha$ (at $\theta=\dfrac\pi2$).
    $2\sqrt{\alpha\beta}=\alpha\Rightarrow\alpha=4\beta$.
    G.P.: first term $2$, ratio $\dfrac12$: $S_{10}=\dfrac{2(1-2^{-10})}{1/2}=\dfrac{1023}{256}$.
    $m+n=1023+256=1279$.

  15. JEE Main 2026, 6 April, Shift 2 · Question 6
    The sum $1+\dfrac12\left(1^2+2^2\right)+\dfrac13\left(1^2+2^2+3^2\right)+\ldots$ upto 10 terms is equal to :

    1. $130$
    2. $155$
    3. $\dfrac{315}{2}$
    4. $\dfrac{325}{2}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    $k$-th term $=\dfrac1k\cdot\dfrac{k(k+1)(2k+1)}{6}=\dfrac{2k^2+3k+1}{6}$.
    Sum for $k=1$ to $10$: $\dfrac{2(385)+3(55)+10}{6}=\dfrac{945}{6}=\dfrac{315}{2}$.

  16. JEE Main 2026, 8 April, Shift 2 · Question 5
    Let $\alpha=3+4+8+9+13+14+\ldots$ upto 40 terms. If $(\tan\beta)^{\frac{\alpha}{1020}}$ is a root of the equation $x^2+x-2=0$, $\beta\in\left(0,\dfrac{\pi}{2}\right)$, then $\sin^2\beta+3\cos^2\beta$ is equal to :

    1. $2$
    2. $\dfrac{7}{4}$
    3. $\dfrac{5}{2}$
    4. $\dfrac{3}{2}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    The terms pair up as $(5k+3)+(5k+4)=10k+7$, $k=0,1,\ldots,19$.
    $\alpha=10\cdot190+7\cdot20=2040$, so $\dfrac{\alpha}{1020}=2$.
    Roots of $x^2+x-2=0$ are $1,-2$; $\tan^2\beta\ge0$ so $\tan^2\beta=1$, $\beta=\dfrac{\pi}{4}$.
    $\sin^2\beta+3\cos^2\beta=\dfrac12+\dfrac32=2$.

  17. JEE Main 2026, 8 April, Shift 2 · Question 25
    Let $f$ be a polynomial function such that

    $\displaystyle \log_2(f(x))=\left(\log_2\left(2+\dfrac{2}{3}+\dfrac{2}{9}+\ldots\infty\right)\right)\cdot\log_3\left(1+\dfrac{f(x)}{f\left(\frac{1}{x}\right)}\right),\ x>0$
    and $f(6)=37$. Then $\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{10}f(n)$ is equal to __________.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 395

    $2+\dfrac23+\dfrac29+\ldots=\dfrac{2}{1-\frac13}=3$, so the right side is $\log_2 3\cdot\log_3\left(1+\dfrac{f(x)}{f(1/x)}\right)=\log_2\left(1+\dfrac{f(x)}{f(1/x)}\right)$.
    Hence $f(x)=1+\dfrac{f(x)}{f(1/x)}$, i.e. $f(x)f(1/x)=f(x)+f(1/x)$, so $f(x)=1\pm x^n$.
    $f(6)=37=1+6^2$ gives $f(x)=x^2+1$.
    $\sum_{n=1}^{10}(n^2+1)=385+10=395$.
