Solved questions from this paper

A few questions with their official answers and worked solutions. All 75 are in the online test.

  1. Question 1 · Mathematics · Inverse Trigonometric Functions
    If the domain of the function $f(x)=\sin^{-1}\left(\dfrac{1}{x^2-2x-2}\right)$, is
    $(-\infty, \alpha]\cup[\beta, \gamma]\cup[\delta, \infty)$, then $\alpha+\beta+\gamma+\delta$ is equal to

    1. $2$
    2. $3$
    3. $4$
    4. $5$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    Need $\left|\dfrac{1}{x^2-2x-2}\right|\le1$, i.e. $|x^2-2x-2|\ge1$.
    $x^2-2x-2\ge1 \iff (x-3)(x+1)\ge0 \iff x\le-1$ or $x\ge3$.
    $x^2-2x-2\le-1 \iff x^2-2x-1\le0 \iff 1-\sqrt2\le x\le1+\sqrt2$.
    Domain $=(-\infty,-1]\cup\left[1-\sqrt2,1+\sqrt2\right]\cup[3,\infty)$, so the sum is $-1+2+3=4$.

  2. Question 2 · Mathematics · Relations and Functions
    Let $f$ be a function such that $3f(x)+2f\left(\dfrac{m}{19x}\right)=5x,\ x\neq0$, where
    $m=\displaystyle\sum_{i=1}^{9}(i)^2$. Then $f(5)-f(2)$ is equal to

    1. $-9$
    2. $9$
    3. $18$
    4. $36$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    $m=\dfrac{9\cdot10\cdot19}{6}=285$, so $\dfrac{m}{19x}=\dfrac{15}{x}$.
    $3f(x)+2f\left(\dfrac{15}x\right)=5x$ and, replacing $x$ by $\dfrac{15}x$, $3f\left(\dfrac{15}x\right)+2f(x)=\dfrac{75}x$.
    Eliminating: $5f(x)=15x-\dfrac{150}{x}$, so $f(x)=3x-\dfrac{30}{x}$.
    $f(5)-f(2)=(15-6)-(6-15)=18$.

  3. Question 26 · Physics · Units and Measurements
    In a vernier callipers, 50 vernier scale divisions are equal to 48 main scale divisions. If one main scale division = 0.05 mm, then the least count of the vernier callipers is ______ mm.

    1. $0.02$
    2. $0.002$
    3. $0.05$
    4. $0.005$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    1 VSD $=\dfrac{48}{50}$ MSD $=0.96\times0.05=0.048$ mm.
    Least count $=1$ MSD $-$ 1 VSD $=0.05-0.048=0.002$ mm.

  4. Question 29 · Physics · Laws of Motion
    A flexible chain of mass $m$ hangs between two fixed points at the same level. The inclination of the chain with the horizontal at the two points of support is $30^\circ$. Considering the equilibrium of each half of the chain, the tension of the chain at the lowest point is __________.

    1. $\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}mg$
    2. $\sqrt3mg$
    3. $\dfrac12mg$
    4. $mg$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    For one half (weight $\dfrac{mg}2$): vertical balance $T\sin30^\circ=\dfrac{mg}2 \Rightarrow T=mg$ at the support.
    Horizontal balance: tension at the lowest point $T_0=T\cos30^\circ=\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}mg$.

  5. Question 51 · Chemistry · Redox Reactions
    One mole of $\mathrm{Cl_2(g)}$ was passed into 2 L of cold 2M KOH solution. After the reaction, the concentrations of $\mathrm{Cl^-}$, $\mathrm{ClO^-}$ and $\mathrm{OH^-}$ are respectively (assume volume remains constant)

    1. 1M, 1M, 1M
    2. 0.5M, 0.5M, 1M
    3. 0.5M, 0.5M, 0.5M
    4. 0.75M, 0.75M, 1M
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    Cold dilute alkali: $\mathrm{Cl_2+2OH^-\rightarrow Cl^-+ClO^-+H_2O}$ (disproportionation).
    KOH present: 4 mol; 1 mol $\mathrm{Cl_2}$ uses 2 mol, leaving 2 mol $\mathrm{OH^-}$ and forming 1 mol each of $\mathrm{Cl^-}$ and $\mathrm{ClO^-}$.
    In 2 L: 0.5 M, 0.5 M, 1 M.

  6. Question 52 · Chemistry · Structure of Atom
    The wavelength of spectral line obtained in the spectrum of $\mathrm{Li^{2+}}$ ion, when the transition takes place between two levels whose sum is 4 and difference is 2, is

    1. $2.28\times10^{-6}$ cm
    2. $1.14\times10^{-6}$ cm
    3. $1.14\times10^{-7}$ cm
    4. $2.28\times10^{-7}$ cm
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    $n_1+n_2=4$, $n_2-n_1=2$, so the levels are 1 and 3.
    $\dfrac1\lambda=R_HZ^2\left(1-\dfrac19\right)=109677\times9\times\dfrac89=877416$ cm$^{-1}$, so $\lambda\approx1.14\times10^{-6}$ cm.
