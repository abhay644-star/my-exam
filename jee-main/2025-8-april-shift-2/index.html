Solved questions from this paper

A few questions with their official answers and worked solutions. All 75 are in the online test.

  1. Question 1 · Mathematics · Relations and Functions
    Let $A=\{0, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5\}$. Let $R$ be a relation on $A$ defined by $(x, y)\in R$ if and only if $\max\{x, y\}\in\{3, 4\}$. Then among the statements
    $(S_1)$ : The number of elements in $R$ is 18, and
    $(S_2)$ : The relation $R$ is symmetric but neither reflexive nor transitive

    1. both are true
    2. both are false
    3. only $(S_1)$ is true
    4. only $(S_2)$ is true
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    Pairs with maximum 3: $4^2-3^2=7$; pairs with maximum 4: $5^2-4^2=9$. So $n(R)=16$ and $(S_1)$ is false.
    The condition is symmetric in $x, y$, so $R$ is symmetric. $(0, 0)\notin R$, so it is not reflexive. $(1, 3), (3, 1)\in R$ but $(1, 1)\notin R$, so it is not transitive. $(S_2)$ is true.

  2. Question 2 · Mathematics · Complex Numbers
    The sum of the squares of the roots of $|x-2|^2+|x-2|-2=0$ and the squares of the roots of $x^2-2|x-3|-5=0$, is

    1. $24$
    2. $26$
    3. $30$
    4. $36$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    First equation: with $t=|x-2|\ge0$, $t^2+t-2=0$ gives $t=1$, so $x=1, 3$; squares add to $10$.
    Second equation: for $x\ge3$, $x^2-2x+1=0$ gives $x=1$, rejected. For $x<3$, $x^2+2x-11=0$ gives $x=-1\pm2\sqrt3$, both less than 3; squares add to $(-2)^2-2(-11)=26$.
    Total $=10+26=36$.

  3. Question 26 · Physics · Units and Measurements
    A quantity Q is formulated as $X^{-2}Y^{+\frac32}Z^{-\frac25}$. X, Y and Z are independent parameters which have fractional errors of 0.1, 0.2 and 0.5, respectively in measurement. The maximum fractional error of Q is

    1. $0.8$
    2. $0.1$
    3. $0.7$
    4. $0.6$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    Maximum fractional error $=2(0.1)+\dfrac32(0.2)+\dfrac25(0.5)=0.2+0.3+0.2=0.7$.

  4. Question 27 · Physics · Laws of Motion
    A body of mass 2 kg moving with velocity of $\vec{v}_{in}=3\hat{i}+4\hat{j}$ ms$^{-1}$ enters into a constant force field of 6N directed along positive z-axis. If the body remains in the field for a period of $\dfrac53$ seconds, then velocity of the body when it emerges from force field is.

    1. $4\hat{i}+3\hat{j}+5\hat{k}$
    2. $3\hat{i}+4\hat{j}-5\hat{k}$
    3. $3\hat{i}+4\hat{j}+5\hat{k}$
    4. $3\hat{i}+4\hat{j}+\sqrt5\hat{k}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    Acceleration $=\dfrac62=3$ m s$^{-2}$ along $+z$; in $\dfrac53$ s the body gains $v_z=5$ m/s.
    The $x$ and $y$ components are unchanged: $\vec v=3\hat i+4\hat j+5\hat k$.

  5. Question 51 · Chemistry · Structure of Atom
    Correct statements for an element with atomic number 9 are
    A. There can be 5 electrons for which $m_s=+\dfrac12$ and 4 electrons for which $m_s=-\dfrac12$
    B. There is only one electron in $p_z$ orbital.
    C. The last electron goes to orbital with $n=2$ and $l=1$.
    D. The sum of angular nodes of all the atomic orbitals is 1.
    Choose the correct answer from the options given below:

    1. A and B Only
    2. C and D Only
    3. A and C Only
    4. A, C and D Only
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    Fluorine: $1s^22s^22p^5$, with one unpaired electron.
    A: 4 pairs plus one unpaired electron, so 5 and 4 is possible. Correct.
    B: the unpaired electron need not be in $p_z$; $p_z$ may hold two. Not correct.
    C: the last electron enters a $2p$ orbital ($n=2$, $l=1$). Correct.
    D: each of the three occupied $2p$ orbitals has one angular node and $s$ orbitals have none, so the sum is 3. Not correct.

  6. Question 52 · Chemistry · Solutions
    Which of the following binary mixture does not show the behaviour of minimum boiling azeotropes?

    1. $\mathrm{H_2O+CH_3COC_2H_5}$
    2. $\mathrm{CS_2+CH_3COCH_3}$
    3. $\mathrm{CH_3OH+CHCl_3}$
    4. $\mathrm{C_6H_5OH+C_6H_5NH_2}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    Minimum boiling azeotropes come from positive deviation from Raoult's law (weaker attractions in the mixture).
    Phenol and aniline form strong O-H$\cdots$N hydrogen bonds with each other: negative deviation, giving a maximum boiling azeotrope instead.
