Solved questions from this paper

A few questions with their official answers and worked solutions. All 75 are in the online test.

  1. Question 1 · Mathematics · Relations and Functions
    Let R be a relation defined on the set $\{1, 2, 3, 4\}\times\{1, 2, 3, 4\}$ by
    $R=\{((a, b), (c, d)) : 2a+3b=3c+4d\}$.
    Then the number of elements in R is

    1. $6$
    2. $12$
    3. $15$
    4. $18$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    $2a+3b=3c+4d \iff 2(a-2d)=3(c-b)$, so $a-2d$ is a multiple of 3 and $c-b=\dfrac{2(a-2d)}{3}$.
    With $a,d\in\{1,\ldots,4\}$, $a-2d\in\{-7,\ldots,2\}$, so $a-2d\in\{-6,-3,0\}$, giving $c-b\in\{-4,-2,0\}$; $-4$ is impossible.
    $a-2d=0$: $(a,d)=(2,1),(4,2)$ with $c=b$ (4 ways): 8 elements.
    $a-2d=-3$: $(a,d)=(1,2),(3,3)$ with $c=b-2$, $b\in\{3,4\}$: 4 elements.
    Total $=12$.

  2. Question 3 · Mathematics · Complex Numbers
    Let $S=\left\{z\in\mathbb{C}:\left|\dfrac{z-6i}{z-2i}\right|=1 \text{ and } \left|\dfrac{z-8+2i}{z+2i}\right|=\dfrac{3}{5}\right\}$.
    Then $\displaystyle\sum_{z\in S}|z|^2$ is equal to

    1. $385$
    2. $398$
    3. $413$
    4. $423$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    $|z-6i|=|z-2i|$: $z$ lies on the perpendicular bisector of $6i$ and $2i$, so $\operatorname{Im}z=4$. Let $z=x+4i$.
    $25\left[(x-8)^2+36\right]=9\left(x^2+36\right) \Rightarrow 16x^2-400x+2176=0 \Rightarrow x^2-25x+136=0 \Rightarrow x=8$ or $17$.
    $\sum|z|^2=(64+16)+(289+16)=385$.

  3. Question 26 · Physics · Moving Charges and Magnetism
    Match the LIST-I with LIST-II

    List-IList-II
    A. Magnetic inductionI. $\mathrm{MLT^{-2}A^{-2}}$
    B. Magnetic fluxII. $\mathrm{ML^2T^{-2}A^{-2}}$
    C. Magnetic permeabilityIII. $\mathrm{ML^0T^{-2}A^{-1}}$
    D. Self inductanceIV. $\mathrm{ML^2T^{-2}A^{-1}}$
    Choose the correct answer from the options given below:

    1. A-III, B-IV, C-II, D-I
    2. A-IV, B-III, C-I, D-II
    3. A-I, B-III, C-IV, D-II
    4. A-III, B-IV, C-I, D-II
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    $B=\dfrac{F}{IL}$: $\mathrm{MT^{-2}A^{-1}}$ (III). $\Phi=BA$: $\mathrm{ML^2T^{-2}A^{-1}}$ (IV).
    $\mu_0$ from $F/L=\dfrac{\mu_0I^2}{2\pi d}$: $\mathrm{MLT^{-2}A^{-2}}$ (I). $L=\dfrac{\Phi}{I}$: $\mathrm{ML^2T^{-2}A^{-2}}$ (II).
    A-III, B-IV, C-I, D-II.

  4. Question 27 · Physics · Oscillations
    A spring of force constant 15 N/m is cut into two pieces. If the ratio of their length is 1:3, then the force constant of smaller piece is __________ N/m.

    1. $20$
    2. $45$
    3. $60$
    4. $15$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    For a spring, $kL$ is constant. The smaller piece has length $\dfrac L4$, so $k=4\times15=60$ N/m.

  5. Question 51 · Chemistry · Coordination Compounds
    Consider a mixture 'X' which is made by dissolving 0.4 mol of $\mathrm{[Co(NH_3)_5SO_4]Br}$ and 0.4 mol of $\mathrm{[Co(NH_3)_5Br]SO_4}$ in water to make 4 L of solution. When 2 L of mixture 'X' is allowed to react with excess of $\mathrm{AgNO_3}$, it forms precipitate 'Y'. The rest 2 L of mixture 'X' reacts with excess $\mathrm{BaCl_2}$ to form precipitate 'Z'. Which of the following statements is CORRECT?

    1. 0.4 mol of 'Z' is formed.
    2. 0.1 mol of 'Y' is formed.
    3. 0.2 mol of 'Z' is formed.
    4. 'Y' is $\mathrm{BaSO_4}$ and 'Z' is AgBr.
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    Each 2 L portion holds 0.2 mol of each complex.
    Only the ionisable (outer-sphere) ions precipitate: $\mathrm{Br^-}$ of $\mathrm{[Co(NH_3)_5SO_4]Br}$ gives 0.2 mol AgBr (Y), and $\mathrm{SO_4^{2-}}$ of $\mathrm{[Co(NH_3)_5Br]SO_4}$ gives 0.2 mol $\mathrm{BaSO_4}$ (Z).
    So 0.2 mol of Z is formed.

  6. Question 52 · Chemistry · Thermodynamics
    Match the LIST-I with LIST-II

    List-I
    Isothermal process for ideal gas system    		List-II
    Work done $(V_f>V_i)$
    A. Reversible expansionI. $w=0$
    B. Free expansionII. $w=-nRT\ln\dfrac{V_f}{V_i}$
    C. Irreversible expansionIII. $w=-p_{ex}(V_f-V_i)$
    D. Irreversible compressionIV. $w=-p_{ex}(V_i-V_f)$
    Choose the correct answer from the options given below:

    1. A-IV, B-II, C-III, D-I
    2. A-II, B-I, C-III, D-IV
    3. A-IV, B-I, C-III, D-II
    4. A-I, B-III, C-II, D-IV
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    Reversible isothermal expansion: $w=-nRT\ln\dfrac{V_f}{V_i}$ (II). Free expansion against zero pressure: $w=0$ (I).
    Irreversible expansion against $p_{ex}$: $w=-p_{ex}(V_f-V_i)$ (III). Irreversible compression: the remaining form (IV).
    A-II, B-I, C-III, D-IV.
