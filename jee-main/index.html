Questions students ask

Are these the official JEE Main papers?

Yes. The questions and the final answer keys are the official ones published by the National Testing Agency (NTA). The worked solutions are written by All in All Exam Practice.

How is the paper marked?

+4 for a correct answer, −1 for a wrong answer and 0 for a question not answered, in both Section A and Section B. Dropped questions give full marks to everyone, as in the official result.

Do I need to sign up or pay?

No. It is free, with no sign-up. Your answers and scores stay on your own device.
