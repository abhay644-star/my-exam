Solved questions from this paper

A few questions with their official answers and worked solutions. All 75 are in the online test.

  1. Question 1 · Mathematics · Inverse Trigonometric Functions
    Let $[x]$ denote the greatest integer less than or equal to $x$. Then the domain of $f(x)=\sec^{-1}(2[x]+1)$ is :

    1. $(-\infty, \infty)$
    2. $(-\infty, \infty)-\{0\}$
    3. $(-\infty, -1]\cup[0, \infty)$
    4. $(-\infty, -1]\cup[1, \infty)$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    $\sec^{-1}t$ needs $|t|\ge1$. With $t=2[x]+1$ (an odd integer), $|2[x]+1|\ge1$ always holds, since an odd integer is never 0.
    Check: $[x]\ge0$ gives $t\ge1$; $[x]\le-1$ gives $t\le-1$.
    So the domain is $(-\infty,\infty)$.

  2. Question 2 · Mathematics · Relations and Functions
    Let $f:[0, 3]\to A$ be defined by $f(x)=2x^3-15x^2+36x+7$ and $g:[0, \infty)\to B$ be defined by $g(x)=\dfrac{x^{2025}}{x^{2025}+1}$. If both the functions are onto and $S=\{x\in\mathbf{Z}: x\in A \text{ or } x\in B\}$, then $n(S)$ is equal to :

    1. $36$
    2. $31$
    3. $30$
    4. $29$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    $f'(x)=6x^2-30x+36=6(x-2)(x-3)$, so $f$ rises on $[0,2]$ and falls on $[2,3]$.
    $f(0)=7$, $f(2)=35$, $f(3)=34$, so $A=[7,35]$.
    $g(x)=1-\dfrac{1}{x^{2025}+1}$ takes every value in $[0,1)$, so $B=[0,1)$.
    Integers in $A\cup B$: $0$ and $7,8,\ldots,35$, i.e. $1+29=30$.

  3. Question 27 · Physics · Units and Measurements
    Match <b>List - I</b> with <b>List - II</b>.

    List - IList - II
    (A)Angular Impulse(I)$[\mathrm{M^0L^2T^{-2}}]$
    (B)Latent Heat(II)$[\mathrm{ML^2T^{-3}A^{-1}}]$
    (C)Electrical resistivity(III)$[\mathrm{ML^2T^{-1}}]$
    (D)Electromotive force(IV)$[\mathrm{ML^3T^{-3}A^{-2}}]$
    Choose the <b>correct</b> answer from the options given below :

    1. (A)-(III), (B)-(I), (C)-(II), (D)-(IV)
    2. (A)-(II), (B)-(I), (C)-(IV), (D)-(III)
    3. (A)-(III), (B)-(I), (C)-(IV), (D)-(II)
    4. (A)-(I), (B)-(III), (C)-(IV), (D)-(II)
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    Angular impulse = change in angular momentum: $\mathrm{ML^2T^{-1}}$ (III).
    Latent heat = energy per unit mass: $\mathrm{L^2T^{-2}}$ (I).
    Resistivity $\rho=RA/l$: $\mathrm{ML^2T^{-3}A^{-2}}\times\mathrm{L}=\mathrm{ML^3T^{-3}A^{-2}}$ (IV).
    EMF = work per unit charge: $\mathrm{ML^2T^{-3}A^{-1}}$ (II).

  4. Question 29 · Physics · Gravitation
    Earth has mass 8 times and radius 2 times that of a planet. If the escape velocity from the earth is 11.2 km/s, the escape velocity in km/s from the planet will be :

    1. 2.8
    2. 5.6
    3. 11.2
    4. 8.4
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    $v_e=\sqrt{\dfrac{2GM}{R}}\propto\sqrt{\dfrac MR}$.
    $\dfrac{v_{\text{earth}}}{v_{\text{planet}}}=\sqrt{\dfrac{8}{2}}=2$, so $v_{\text{planet}}=5.6$ km/s.

  5. Question 51 · Chemistry · Structure of Atom
    Which of the following is/are not correct with respect to energy of atomic orbitals of hydrogen atom ?
    (A) 1s < 2p < 3d < 4s
    (B) 1s < 2s = 2p < 3s = 3p
    (C) 1s < 2s < 2p < 3s < 3p
    (D) 1s < 2s < 4s < 3d
    Choose the <b>correct</b> answer from the options given below :

    1. (A) and (B) only
    2. (C) and (D) only
    3. (A) and (C) only
    4. (B) and (D) only
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    In the hydrogen atom the orbital energy depends only on $n$.
    (A) $n$ increases along the list: correct. (B) equal $n$ shown as equal: correct.
    (C) puts $2s<2p$ and $3s<3p$: not correct. (D) puts $4s<3d$: not correct.
    So (C) and (D) are not correct.

  6. Question 54 · Chemistry · Some Basic Concepts of Chemistry
    Concentrated nitric acid is labelled as 75% by mass. The volume in mL of the solution which contains 30 g of nitric acid is __________.
    Given : Density of nitric acid solution is 1.25 g/mL.

    1. 40
    2. 45
    3. 32
    4. 55
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    Mass of solution containing 30 g acid $=\dfrac{30}{0.75}=40$ g.
    Volume $=\dfrac{40}{1.25}=32$ mL.
