About All in All Exam Practice

All in All Exam Practice (my-exam.in) is a free website for practising school and entrance exams. Every test is marked as soon as you finish, with the correct answers and an explanation or worked solution for each question.

What you can practise

  • CBSE Classes 1 to 12: every main subject, chapter by chapter, following the NCERT textbooks. Take a quick quiz, a chapter test or a full mock paper, or set your own number of questions, time and difficulty. Choose your class and subject.
  • Entrance exams: official previous year papers of JEE Main, JEE Advanced and NEET (UG), as timed tests with the real marking scheme.
  • Revision: questions you get wrong are saved on your device in My mistakes, so you can practise them again later.

Who runs the site

All in All Exam Practice is an independent project. It is free to use, has no adverts and needs no sign-up, and your answers and scores stay on your own device (see Privacy). It is not connected with CBSE, NCERT, NTA or the IITs.

How the questions are made and checked

  • School questions follow the NCERT textbooks and the current, rationalised CBSE syllabus. Each question has its answer and a short explanation, and is set at one of four levels, from Easy to Challenging.
  • Mathematics questions are generated fresh for every test, each with a worked solution, so you can practise a chapter again and again.
  • Official papers use the question papers and final answer keys published by the National Testing Agency (NTA) and the organising IIT. Questions dropped by them give full marks to everyone, and where the final key accepts more than one answer, each is marked correct. The worked solutions are our own.
  • Checks before every update: automated tests confirm that every official paper adds up to its real maximum marks, that every answer key points to a real option, that every school answer is accepted when typed, and that the marking rules (such as +4 / −1 and JEE Advanced partial marks) give the right scores.

Found a mistake?

We correct mistakes as soon as we hear about them. After you finish a test, use the Report a problem with this question button under the question in your marked paper, or the "Send us a message" link at the bottom of the home page. You can also email aa.exampractice@gmail.com. Please mention the class, subject and question.
