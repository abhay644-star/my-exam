Free online class tests for CBSE teachers

Set the same auto-marked test for your whole class with one link. Students open it on any phone or computer, take the test and see their marks and worked answers straight away. No sign-up for you or your students.

Set a class test now

How to set a class test

  1. Open School and choose the class, subject and chapters.
  2. Choose Exam for a timed test, or Practice for homework where students check each answer as they go.
  3. Open Challenge your class or friends, pick the length (Quick quiz, Chapter test, Full mock paper or your own settings) and, if you like, give it a name such as "Class 10B weekly test".
  4. Tap Create challenge link and send it to your class group on WhatsApp, or copy it into any chat or email.

What your students see

  • A banner with the test's name and its four-character code, and a Start the challenge button.
  • Exactly the same questions as everyone else, with a timer in Exam mode.
  • Their marks, the correct answers and a worked solution for every question as soon as they finish, and the chapter to practise next if one needs work.

Collecting scores

The site does not collect students' answers or scores. At the end of the test, each student taps Share my score and sends you a message like "Challenge DHTS (Class 10B weekly test): I scored 16/20 (80%) in 12 min 4 s". Ask them to send it to you or to the class group.

Ideas for using it

  • A weekly chapter test on the chapter you finished in class.
  • Homework in Practice mode, so students learn from each explanation as they go.
  • Revision before exams with a full mock paper across several chapters.
  • Mathematics practice: Mathematics questions are generated with new numbers, so after the class test students can practise the same chapter again with fresh questions.

What is covered

Every main subject for CBSE Classes 1 to 12, chapter by chapter, following the NCERT textbooks (the CBSE syllabus for Computer Science in Classes 9 to 12), at four levels from Easy to Challenging. For Classes 11 and 12 there are also official JEE Main, JEE Advanced and NEET (UG) papers. Popular chapter pages: Class 10 Mathematics, Class 10 Science, Class 9 Mathematics, Class 12 Physics.

Questions

Do my students need to sign up?
No. Students open the link and start. Neither you nor your students need an account, and nothing is installed.

How do I see my students' scores?
Each student sends you their score with the Share my score button at the end of the test. The message names the challenge code, the score and the time taken. The site does not collect scores, so there is no list of results on the site.

Will every student get the same questions?
Yes. Everyone who opens the link gets exactly the same questions in the same order. Use the link within a few weeks: when questions are added to a chapter, an older link may pick a slightly different set.

Is it free?
Yes. All in All Exam Practice is free, has no adverts and does not collect students' answers or scores (see Privacy).

Found a mistake in a question?
Use Report a problem with this question under the question in the marked paper, or email contact@my-exam.in. We correct mistakes as soon as we hear about them.
