Terms of use

All in All Exam Practice (my-exam.in) is a free practice site for school and competitive exams. By using the site, you agree to these terms. Last updated: 13 November 2026.

In short

  • The site is free, needs no sign-up and has no adverts.
  • Use it for your own study, or to set practice tests for your class.
  • We work hard to keep questions and answers correct, but we cannot promise there are no mistakes.
  • Your scores are for practice only and do not predict your result in a real exam.

Using the site

Students, parents and teachers may use the site for personal study and for teaching. Teachers may share challenge links and print papers for their own classes. Please do not copy the questions, solutions or pages in bulk, sell them, or publish them on another site without asking us first.

Official papers

Previous year papers and answer keys are published by the National Testing Agency (NTA) and the organising IIT. We use the final official answer keys for marking. The worked solutions are our own. The site is not connected with CBSE, NCERT, NTA or the IITs, and always check the exam's official website for dates, patterns and rules.

Accuracy

Questions are checked before they are published, and we correct mistakes as soon as we hear about them (see About). The site is provided as it is, and we are not responsible for any loss that comes from relying on a question, answer or score.

Challenge links and messages

When you create a challenge link or send us a message, please do not include anything offensive or anyone's personal details. How we handle the messages you send is set out in our Privacy page.

Changes

We may change or remove features, and update these terms. The date at the top of this page shows when they last changed.
