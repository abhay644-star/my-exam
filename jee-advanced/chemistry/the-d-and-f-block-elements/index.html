  1. JEE Advanced 2025, Paper 1 · Question 35
    One of the products formed from the reaction of permanganate ion with iodide ion in neutral aqueous medium is

    1. $\mathrm{I_2}$
    2. $\mathrm{IO_3^-}$
    3. $\mathrm{IO_4^-}$
    4. $\mathrm{IO_2^-}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    In neutral (or faintly alkaline) solution permanganate is reduced to $\mathrm{MnO_2}$ and iodide is oxidised to iodate:
    $2\mathrm{MnO_4^-} + \mathrm{I^-} + \mathrm{H_2O} \rightarrow 2\mathrm{MnO_2} + \mathrm{IO_3^-} + 2\mathrm{OH^-}$.
    Answer (B).

  2. JEE Advanced 2025, Paper 1 · Question 38
    The pair(s) of diamagnetic ions is(are)

    1. $\mathrm{La^{3+}}$, $\mathrm{Ce^{4+}}$
    2. $\mathrm{Yb^{2+}}$, $\mathrm{Lu^{3+}}$
    3. $\mathrm{La^{2+}}$, $\mathrm{Ce^{3+}}$
    4. $\mathrm{Yb^{3+}}$, $\mathrm{Lu^{2+}}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a), (b)

    (A) $\mathrm{La^{3+}}$ [Xe] and $\mathrm{Ce^{4+}}$ [Xe] ($4f^0$): both diamagnetic.
    (B) $\mathrm{Yb^{2+}}$ [Xe]$4f^{14}$ and $\mathrm{Lu^{3+}}$ [Xe]$4f^{14}$: both diamagnetic.
    (C) $\mathrm{La^{2+}}$ ($5d^1$) and $\mathrm{Ce^{3+}}$ ($4f^1$): paramagnetic.
    (D) $\mathrm{Yb^{3+}}$ ($4f^{13}$) and $\mathrm{Lu^{2+}}$ ($4f^{14}5d^1$): paramagnetic.
    Answer: (A), (B).

  3. JEE Advanced 2023, Paper 1 · Question 38
    In the scheme given below, $\mathbf{X}$ and $\mathbf{Y}$, respectively, are

    $\displaystyle \text{Metal halide} \xrightarrow{\text{aq. NaOH}} \text{White precipitate }(\mathbf{P}) + \text{Filtrate }(\mathbf{Q})$
    $\displaystyle \mathbf{P} \xrightarrow[\text{heat}]{\text{aq. }\mathrm{H_2SO_4},\ \mathrm{PbO_2}\text{ (excess)}} \mathbf{X}\ \text{(a coloured species in solution)}$
    $\displaystyle \mathbf{Q} \xrightarrow[\text{warm}]{\mathrm{MnO(OH)_2},\ \text{Conc. }\mathrm{H_2SO_4}} \mathbf{Y}\ \text{(gives blue-coloration with KI-starch paper)}$

    1. $\mathrm{CrO_4^{2-}}$ and $\mathrm{Br_2}$
    2. $\mathrm{MnO_4^{2-}}$ and $\mathrm{Cl_2}$
    3. $\mathrm{MnO_4^{-}}$ and $\mathrm{Cl_2}$
    4. $\mathrm{MnSO_4}$ and $\mathrm{HOCl}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    The white precipitate with NaOH is $\mathrm{Mn(OH)_2}$, so the salt is $\mathrm{MnCl_2}$ and the filtrate contains $\mathrm{Cl^-}$.
    $\mathrm{PbO_2}$ in acid oxidises $\mathrm{Mn^{2+}}$ to purple $\mathrm{MnO_4^-}$: X $= \mathrm{MnO_4^-}$.
    $\mathrm{MnO(OH)_2}$ with conc. $\mathrm{H_2SO_4}$ oxidises $\mathrm{Cl^-}$ to $\mathrm{Cl_2}$, which liberates $\mathrm{I_2}$ from KI (blue with starch): Y $= \mathrm{Cl_2}$.
    Answer (C).
