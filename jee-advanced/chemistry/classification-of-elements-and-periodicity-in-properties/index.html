  1. JEE Advanced 2026, Paper 2 · Question 43
    The correct statement(s) regarding the periodic properties of elements is(are)

    1. Second ionization enthalpy of carbon atom is less than that of boron atom.
    2. Increasing order of ionic radii: $\mathrm{Al^{3+}} < \mathrm{Mg^{2+}} < \mathrm{Na^+}$
    3. Under identical conditions, in solid state, the density of potassium metal is more than density of sodium metal.
    4. The H–H bond is weaker than F–F bond.
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a), (b)

    (A) $\mathrm{B^+}$ is $1s^22s^2$: its 2nd IE removes an electron from a filled 2s subshell (≈2427 kJ mol$^{-1}$). $\mathrm{C^+}$ is $2s^22p^1$: the 2p electron is removed more easily (≈2353 kJ mol$^{-1}$) → true.
    (B) Isoelectronic ions: higher nuclear charge gives a smaller radius, so $\mathrm{Al^{3+}} < \mathrm{Mg^{2+}} < \mathrm{Na^+}$ → true.
    (C) Density of K (0.86 g cm$^{-3}$) is less than Na (0.97 g cm$^{-3}$) → false.
    (D) H–H (436 kJ mol$^{-1}$) is much stronger than F–F (≈158 kJ mol$^{-1}$) → false.
