  1. JEE Advanced 2026, Paper 1 · Question 40
    In the following reaction sequence, Q, R, S and T are the major products.
    The correct statement(s) about Q, R, S and T is(are)

    1. S on warming with ammoniacal $\mathrm{AgNO_3}$ results in the formation of silver mirror.
    2. Q on treatment with $\mathrm{Cl_2}$(excess)/UV gives gammaxane.
    3. T is a heterocyclic compound.
    4. R on acid catalyzed intramolecular cyclization followed by treatment with Zn-Hg/HCl gives 9,10-dihydroxyanthracene.
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a), (b), (c)

    Kolbe electrolysis of sodium butanoate gives n-hexane; $\mathrm{V_2O_5}$ at 500 °C aromatises it, so Q = benzene. Benzene + excess $\mathrm{Cl_2}$/UV gives $\mathrm{C_6H_6Cl_6}$ (gammaxane): (B) is true.
    Friedel–Crafts acylation with phthalic anhydride gives R = 2-benzoylbenzoic acid.
    $\mathrm{PCl_5}$ forms the acid chloride and Rosenmund reduction gives S = 2-benzoylbenzaldehyde. As an aldehyde, S gives a silver mirror with Tollens' reagent: (A) is true.
    $\mathrm{NH_2NH_2}$ condenses with both carbonyls of this 1,4-dicarbonyl (1,2-disubstituted benzene) to form T = 1-phenylphthalazine, a N-heterocycle: (C) is true.
    Cyclisation of R gives anthraquinone; Clemmensen reduction removes the C=O oxygens, giving anthracene/9,10-dihydroanthracene, not a dihydroxy compound: (D) is false.

  2. JEE Advanced 2026, Paper 1 · Question 44
    Treatment of buta-1,3-diyne with $\mathrm{NaNH_2}$ (2 equivalents), followed by reaction with excess of $\mathit{trans}$-$\mathrm{CH_3}$-CH=CH-$\mathrm{CH_2}$-Br gives X as the major product. The maximum number of carbon atoms that are collinear (in a straight line) in X is ____.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 6

    Two equivalents of $\mathrm{NaNH_2}$ remove both terminal H atoms: $\mathrm{Na^+\,{}^-C{\equiv}C{-}C{\equiv}C^-\,Na^+}$.
    Double $S_N2$ alkylation with crotyl bromide gives X = $\mathrm{CH_3CH{=}CHCH_2{-}C{\equiv}C{-}C{\equiv}C{-}CH_2CH{=}CHCH_3}$ (both alkenes trans).
    The four sp carbons and the two $\mathrm{CH_2}$ carbons bonded to them all lie on one straight line.
    The next carbons are sp$^2$ CH groups attached to sp$^3$ $\mathrm{CH_2}$, so they lie off that line.
    Maximum number of collinear carbons = 6.

  3. JEE Advanced 2024, Paper 2 · Question 37
    In the following reaction sequence, the major product $\mathbf{Q}$ is

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    HI/$\Delta$ fully reduces glucose (L or D) to n-hexane.

  4. JEE Advanced 2022, Paper 2 · Question 42
    The number of isomeric tetraenes (NOT containing $sp$-hybridized carbon atoms) that can be formed from the following reaction sequence is ________.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 2

    Na/liquid $\mathrm{NH_3}$ reduces the alkyne to the trans alkene: ring–$\mathrm{CH_2CH{=}CHCH_3}$.
    Excess $\mathrm{Br_2}$ adds to both C=C, giving two vicinal dibromides (one in the ring, one in the chain).
    Alc. KOH removes 2 HBr from each. Avoiding allenes and alkynes, the ring gives only the conjugated cyclohexa-1,3-diene, and the chain gives only $\mathrm{{-}CH{=}CH{-}CH{=}CH_2}$.
    Product: 5-(buta-1,3-dienyl)cyclohexa-1,3-diene, whose ring–$\mathrm{CH{=}CH}$ double bond can be E or Z.
    Number of isomeric tetraenes $= 2$.

  5. JEE Advanced 2022, Paper 2 · Question 43
    The number of $-\mathrm{CH_2}-$ (methylene) groups in the product formed from the following reaction sequence is ________.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 0

    The alkene is oct-4-ene. Ozonolysis gives 2 butanal; $\mathrm{KMnO_4}$ oxidises them to butanoic acid.
    Kolbe electrolysis of sodium butanoate couples two propyl radicals: n-hexane.
    $\mathrm{Cr_2O_3}$, 770 K, 20 atm aromatises n-hexane to benzene.
    Benzene has no $\mathrm{CH_2}$ groups: answer $0$.

  6. JEE Advanced 2022, Paper 2 · Question 44
    The total number of chiral molecules formed from one molecule of $\mathbf{P}$ on complete ozonolysis ($\mathrm{O_3}$, $\mathrm{Zn/H_2O}$) is ________.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 2

    $\mathbf{P}$ has six C=C bonds, so ozonolysis gives seven fragments.
    The two ends give $\mathrm{CH_3CHO}$ (achiral).
    The three fragments whose carbinol carbon lies between two trisubstituted-alkene carbons give $\mathrm{CH_3CO{-}C(OH)(CH_3){-}COCH_3}$: two identical acetyl groups, achiral.
    The two fragments whose carbinol carbon lies next to a $\mathrm{{=}CH{-}}$ carbon give $\mathrm{OHC{-}C(OH)(CH_3){-}COCH_3}$: four different groups, chiral.
    Chiral molecules formed $= 2$.

  7. JEE Advanced 2021, Paper 1 · Question 39
    The major product formed in the following reaction is

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    The substrate is deca-1,7-diyne: one terminal and one internal $\mathrm{C{\equiv}C}$.
    $\mathrm{NaNH_2}$ first converts the terminal alkyne into its sodium acetylide, which is not reduced by Na/liq. $\mathrm{NH_3}$.
    The internal alkyne is reduced by Na/liq. $\mathrm{NH_3}$ (dissolving-metal reduction) to the trans (E) alkene.
    On work-up the terminal alkyne is regenerated: (E)-dec-7-en-1-yne → (B).

  8. JEE Advanced 2021, Paper 1 · Question 43
    For the following reaction scheme, percentage yields are given along the arrow:
    $\mathbf{x}$ g and $\mathbf{y}$ g are mass of $\mathbf{R}$ and $\mathbf{U}$, respectively.
    (Use: Molar mass (in g mol$^{-1}$) of H, C and O as 1, 12 and 16, respectively)

    The value of $\mathbf{x}$ is ___.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 1.62

    $\mathrm{Mg_2C_3 + 4H_2O \rightarrow 2Mg(OH)_2 + CH_3C{\equiv}CH}$: P is propyne ($M = 40$); $4.0$ g $= 0.1$ mol.
    $\mathrm{NaNH_2}$ then MeI: Q is but-2-yne, $0.1 \times 0.75 = 0.075$ mol.
    Red hot iron tube (873 K) trimerises the alkyne: R is hexamethylbenzene, $\mathrm{C_{12}H_{18}}$ ($M = 162$); moles $= \dfrac{0.075}{3} \times 0.40 = 0.01$.
    $\mathbf{x} = 0.01 \times 162 = 1.62$.

  9. JEE Advanced 2021, Paper 1 · Question 55
    The maximum number of possible isomers (including stereoisomers) which may be formed on mono-bromination of 1-methylcyclohex-1-ene using $\mathrm{Br_2}$ and UV light is ___.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 9 or 13 or 14

    $\mathrm{Br_2}$/UV brominates at the allylic positions through resonance-stabilised allylic radicals: C-3, C-6 and the $\mathrm{CH_3}$ group.
    C-3 radical: 3-bromo-1-methylcyclohexene (chiral, 2) and 3-bromo-3-methylcyclohexene (chiral, 2).
    C-6 radical: both ends give 6-bromo-1-methylcyclohexene (chiral, 2).
    $\mathrm{CH_2}$ radical: 1-(bromomethyl)cyclohexene (achiral, 1) and 2-bromo-1-methylidenecyclohexane (chiral, 2).
    Total $= 2 + 2 + 2 + 1 + 2 = 9$.
    (Also counting substitution at C-4 and C-5 (2 + 2) gives 13, and adding the vinylic product gives 14; the official key accepts 9, 13 or 14.)

    Note: Official final key: 9 or 13 or 14 (any of these values is awarded marks).

  10. JEE Advanced 2021, Paper 2 · Question 52
    The amount of energy required to break a bond is same as the amount of energy released when the same bond is formed. In gaseous state, the energy required for homolytic cleavage of a bond is called Bond Dissociation Energy (BDE) or Bond Strength. BDE is affected by s-character of the bond and the stability of the radicals formed. Shorter bonds are typically stronger bonds. BDEs for some bonds are given below:

    $\displaystyle \mathrm{H_3C{-}H_{(g)} \longrightarrow H_3C^{\bullet}_{(g)} + H^{\bullet}_{(g)}} \qquad \Delta H^\circ = 105\ \text{kcal mol}^{-1}$
    $\displaystyle \mathrm{Cl{-}Cl_{(g)} \longrightarrow Cl^{\bullet}_{(g)} + Cl^{\bullet}_{(g)}} \qquad \Delta H^\circ = 58\ \text{kcal mol}^{-1}$
    $\displaystyle \mathrm{H_3C{-}Cl_{(g)} \longrightarrow H_3C^{\bullet}_{(g)} + Cl^{\bullet}_{(g)}} \qquad \Delta H^\circ = 85\ \text{kcal mol}^{-1}$
    $\displaystyle \mathrm{H{-}Cl_{(g)} \longrightarrow H^{\bullet}_{(g)} + Cl^{\bullet}_{(g)}} \qquad \Delta H^\circ = 103\ \text{kcal mol}^{-1}$

    For the following reaction
    $\displaystyle \mathrm{CH_{4\,(g)} + Cl_{2\,(g)} \xrightarrow{\text{light}} CH_3Cl_{(g)} + HCl_{(g)}}$
    the correct statement is

    1. Initiation step is exothermic with $\Delta H^\circ = -58$ kcal mol$^{-1}$.
    2. Propagation step involving ${}^\bullet\mathrm{CH_3}$ formation is exothermic with $\Delta H^\circ = -2$ kcal mol$^{-1}$.
    3. Propagation step involving $\mathrm{CH_3Cl}$ formation is endothermic with $\Delta H^\circ = +27$ kcal mol$^{-1}$.
    4. The reaction is exothermic with $\Delta H^\circ = -25$ kcal mol$^{-1}$.
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    Initiation: $\mathrm{Cl_2 \to 2Cl^\bullet}$, $\Delta H^\circ = +58$ kcal mol$^{-1}$ (endothermic) → (A) false.
    Propagation 1: $\mathrm{CH_4 + Cl^\bullet \to {}^\bullet CH_3 + HCl}$, $\Delta H^\circ = 105 - 103 = +2$ (endothermic) → (B) false.
    Propagation 2: $\mathrm{{}^\bullet CH_3 + Cl_2 \to CH_3Cl + Cl^\bullet}$, $\Delta H^\circ = 58 - 85 = -27$ (exothermic) → (C) false.
    Overall: $\Delta H^\circ = (105 + 58) - (85 + 103) = -25$ kcal mol$^{-1}$ → (D).
