  1. JEE Advanced 2026, Paper 1 · Question 48
    Match the major products obtained in the reactions given in List-I with the corresponding structures in List-II and choose the correct option.

    1. P $\rightarrow$ 2; Q $\longrightarrow$ 1; R $\rightarrow$ 5; S $\longrightarrow$ 4
    2. P $\rightarrow$ 1; Q $\longrightarrow$ 2; R $\rightarrow$ 4; S $\longrightarrow$ 5
    3. P $\rightarrow$ 1; Q $\longrightarrow$ 2; R $\rightarrow$ 3; S $\longrightarrow$ 4
    4. P $\rightarrow$ 2; Q $\longrightarrow$ 1; R $\rightarrow$ 3; S $\longrightarrow$ 5
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    P: In the aldoxime the OH is anti to the aldehydic H, so aqueous NaOH causes anti-elimination of water to give the nitrile. The Br is para to $\mathrm{NO_2}$ and ortho to CN, so it is strongly activated, and OH$^-$ replaces it by $S_NAr$. Product (1).
    Q: $\mathrm{Ac_2O}$ forms the O-acetyl oxime, and mild $\mathrm{Na_2CO_3}$ eliminates AcOH to give the nitrile; Br is retained. Product (2).
    R: In the ketoxime the oxime O faces the C–Br. NaOH forms the oximate, which displaces Br intramolecularly ($S_NAr$) to give 3-methyl-5-nitro-1,2-benzisoxazole. Product (4).
    S: The O-acetyl group points away from the ring (E-geometry), so cyclisation is impossible. Aqueous $\mathrm{Na_2CO_3}$ only hydrolyses the ester back to the ketoxime. Product (5).
    Hence P→1, Q→2, R→4, S→5.

  2. JEE Advanced 2025, Paper 1 · Question 39
    For the reaction sequence given below, the correct statement(s) is(are)
    (In the options, X is any atom other than carbon and hydrogen, and it is different in $\mathbf{P}$, $\mathbf{Q}$ and $\mathbf{R}$)

    1. C$-$X bond length in $\mathbf{P}$, $\mathbf{Q}$ and $\mathbf{R}$ follows the order $\mathbf{Q}$ $\gt$ $\mathbf{R}$ $\gt$ $\mathbf{P}$.
    2. C$-$X bond enthalpy in $\mathbf{P}$, $\mathbf{Q}$ and $\mathbf{R}$ follows the order $\mathbf{R}$ $\gt$ $\mathbf{P}$ $\gt$ $\mathbf{Q}$.
    3. Relative reactivity toward $\mathrm{S_N2}$ reaction in $\mathbf{P}$, $\mathbf{Q}$ and $\mathbf{R}$ follows the order $\mathbf{P}$ $\gt$ $\mathbf{R}$ $\gt$ $\mathbf{Q}$.
    4. p$K_\mathrm{a}$ value of the conjugate acids of the leaving groups in $\mathbf{P}$, $\mathbf{Q}$ and $\mathbf{R}$ follows the order $\mathbf{R}$ $\gt$ $\mathbf{Q}$ $\gt$ $\mathbf{P}$.
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    Cyclohexene + HBr → bromocyclohexane ($\mathbf{P}$, X = Br).
    Finkelstein (NaI/acetone) → iodocyclohexane ($\mathbf{Q}$, X = I); Swarts (e.g. AgF) → fluorocyclohexane ($\mathbf{R}$, X = F).
    (A) Bond length C–I $\gt$ C–Br $\gt$ C–F: Q $\gt$ P $\gt$ R, not Q $\gt$ R $\gt$ P: false.
    (B) Bond enthalpy C–F $\gt$ C–Br $\gt$ C–I: R $\gt$ P $\gt$ Q: true.
    (C) $\mathrm{S_N2}$ reactivity follows leaving-group ability $\mathrm{I^-} \gt \mathrm{Br^-} \gt \mathrm{F^-}$: Q $\gt$ P $\gt$ R: false.
    (D) p$K_\mathrm{a}$: HF ($\approx 3$) $\gt$ HBr ($\approx -9$) $\gt$ HI ($\approx -10$): R $\gt$ P $\gt$ Q, not R $\gt$ Q $\gt$ P: false.
    Answer: (B).

  3. JEE Advanced 2023, Paper 1 · Question 50
    Match the reactions in List-I with the features of their products in List-II and choose the correct option.

    List-IList-II
    (P) (-)-1-Bromo-2-ethylpentane (single enantiomer) $\xrightarrow[\mathrm{S_N2}\text{ reaction}]{\text{aq. NaOH}}$(1) Inversion of configuration
    (Q) (-)-2-Bromopentane (single enantiomer) $\xrightarrow[\mathrm{S_N2}\text{ reaction}]{\text{aq. NaOH}}$(2) Retention of configuration
    (R) (-)-3-Bromo-3-methylhexane (single enantiomer) $\xrightarrow[\mathrm{S_N1}\text{ reaction}]{\text{aq. NaOH}}$(3) Mixture of enantiomers
    (S) The compound shown in the figure (single enantiomer) $\xrightarrow[\mathrm{S_N1}\text{ reaction}]{\text{aq. NaOH}}$(4) Mixture of structural isomers
    (5) Mixture of diastereomers

    1. P → 1; Q → 2; R → 5; S → 3
    2. P → 2; Q → 1; R → 3; S → 5
    3. P → 1; Q → 2; R → 5; S → 4
    4. P → 2; Q → 4; R → 3; S → 5
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    (P) $\mathrm{S_N2}$ occurs at the $\mathrm{CH_2Br}$ carbon, not at the stereocentre (C-2), so its configuration is retained → (2).
    (Q) $\mathrm{S_N2}$ at the stereocentre (C-2): inversion of configuration → (1).
    (R) $\mathrm{S_N1}$ at the only stereocentre: the planar carbocation is attacked from both faces, giving a racemic mixture of enantiomers → (3).
    (S) $\mathrm{S_N1}$ at the C-Br stereocentre while the other stereocentre (C-Me, H) is unchanged: the two products are diastereomers → (5).
    Answer (B).

  4. JEE Advanced 2023, Paper 2 · Question 37
    In the following reactions, P, Q, R, and S are the major products.
    The correct statement about P, Q, R, and S is

    1. P is a primary alcohol with four carbons.
    2. Q undergoes Kolbe’s electrolysis to give an eight-carbon product.
    3. R has six carbons and it undergoes Cannizzaro reaction.
    4. S is a primary amine with six carbons.
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    Starting material: 1-chloro-2-methylpropane, $\mathrm{(CH_3)_2CHCH_2Cl}$.
    P: Grignard then water gives the alkane $\mathrm{(CH_3)_2CHCH_3}$, not an alcohol → (A) false.
    Q: Grignard + $\mathrm{CO_2}$, then $\mathrm{H_3O^+}$ gives $\mathrm{(CH_3)_2CHCH_2COOH}$; NaOH gives its sodium salt. Kolbe electrolysis couples two isobutyl radicals: $\mathrm{(CH_3)_2CHCH_2CH_2CH(CH_3)_2}$, an eight-carbon product → (B) true.
    R: with $\mathrm{CH_3CHO}$: 4-methylpentan-2-ol; $\mathrm{CrO_3}$ gives 4-methylpentan-2-one, a ketone with α-H, no Cannizzaro → (C) false.
    S: $\mathrm{RCH_2CN} \to \mathrm{RCH_2CH_2NH_2}$ (carbylamine) $\to$ isocyanide $\to$ $\mathrm{LiAlH_4}$ gives the secondary amine $\mathrm{(CH_3)_2CHCH_2CH_2NHCH_3}$ → (D) false.
    Answer (B).

  5. JEE Advanced 2021, Paper 1 · Question 50
    The reaction of $\mathbf{Q}$ with PhSNa yields an organic compound (major product) that gives positive Carius test on treatment with $\mathrm{Na_2O_2}$ followed by addition of $\mathrm{BaCl_2}$. The correct option(s) for $\mathbf{Q}$ is(are)

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a), (d)

    A positive test (white $\mathrm{BaSO_4}$) means the product contains sulfur, so $\mathrm{PhS^-}$ must actually substitute into Q.
    (A) 1-fluoro-2,4-dinitrobenzene: F is ortho and para to $\mathrm{NO_2}$ groups, so $\mathrm{S_NAr}$ gives the aryl phenyl sulfide: yes.
    (B) and (C) The halogen is only meta to the $\mathrm{NO_2}$ group(s), so the ring is not activated and no substitution takes place: no product is formed.
    (D) A benzylic chloride: $\mathrm{S_N2}$ with $\mathrm{PhS^-}$ gives $\mathrm{ArCH_2SPh}$: yes.
    Answer (A), (D).
