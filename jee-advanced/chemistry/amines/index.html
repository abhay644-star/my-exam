  1. JEE Advanced 2026, Paper 2 · Question 44
    In the following reaction sequence, P, Q, S and T are the major products.
    The correct statement(s) about P, Q, S and T is(are)

    1. Q on treatment with ethanol generates an aromatic aldehyde.
    2. S gives positive phthalein dye test.
    3. P is a dinitro compound.
    4. T is a coloured compound.
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b), (d)

    Aniline → acetanilide (protects $\mathrm{NH_2}$) → mononitration at para: P = 4-nitroacetanilide.
    Hydrolysis gives 4-nitroaniline; diazotisation at 273–278 K gives Q = 4-nitrobenzenediazonium chloride.
    Cumene → cumene hydroperoxide → phenol (+ acetone); Kolbe reaction then acidification gives S = salicylic acid.
    Q couples with S in aqueous NaOH to give an azo dye: T is coloured → (D) true.
    S has a free phenolic OH, so it gives the phthalein dye test → (B) true.
    (A) Ethanol reduces $\mathrm{ArN_2^+}$ to ArH (nitrobenzene), not an aldehyde → false. (C) P is mononitro → false.

  2. JEE Advanced 2025, Paper 1 · Question 47
    The major products obtained from the reactions in List-II are the reactants for the named reactions mentioned in List-I. Match each entry in List-I with the appropriate entry in List-II and choose the correct option.

    List-IList-II
    (P) Stephen reaction(1) Toluene $\xrightarrow{\text{(i) } \mathrm{CrO_2Cl_2/CS_2};\ \text{(ii) } \mathrm{H_3O^+}}$
    (Q) Sandmeyer reaction(2) Benzoic acid $\xrightarrow{\text{(i) } \mathrm{PCl_5};\ \text{(ii) } \mathrm{NH_3};\ \text{(iii) } \mathrm{P_4O_{10}},\ \Delta}$
    (R) Hoffmann bromamide degradation reaction(3) Nitrobenzene $\xrightarrow{\text{(i) Fe, HCl};\ \text{(ii) HCl, } \mathrm{NaNO_2}\text{ (273-278 K), } \mathrm{H_2O}}$
    (S) Cannizzaro reaction(4) Toluene $\xrightarrow{\text{(i) } \mathrm{Cl_2}/h\nu,\ \mathrm{H_2O};\ \text{(ii) Tollen's reagent};\ \text{(iii) } \mathrm{SO_2Cl_2};\ \text{(iv) } \mathrm{NH_3}}$
    (5) Aniline $\xrightarrow{\text{(i) } \mathrm{(CH_3CO)_2O}\text{, Pyridine};\ \text{(ii) } \mathrm{HNO_3, H_2SO_4}\text{, 288 K};\ \text{(iii) aq. NaOH}}$

    1. P $\rightarrow$ 2; Q $\rightarrow$ 4; R $\rightarrow$ 1; S $\rightarrow$ 3
    2. P $\rightarrow$ 2; Q $\rightarrow$ 3; R $\rightarrow$ 4; S $\rightarrow$ 1
    3. P $\rightarrow$ 5; Q $\rightarrow$ 3; R $\rightarrow$ 4; S $\rightarrow$ 2
    4. P $\rightarrow$ 5; Q $\rightarrow$ 4; R $\rightarrow$ 2; S $\rightarrow$ 1
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    (1) Étard reaction: toluene → benzaldehyde, which has no α-H: reactant for the Cannizzaro reaction → S.
    (2) Benzoic acid → benzoyl chloride → benzamide → (dehydration) benzonitrile: reactant for the Stephen reaction → P.
    (3) Nitrobenzene → aniline → benzenediazonium chloride: reactant for the Sandmeyer reaction → Q.
    (4) Toluene → benzal chloride → (hydrolysis) benzaldehyde → benzoic acid → benzoyl chloride → benzamide: reactant for the Hoffmann bromamide degradation → R.
    (5) gives p-nitroaniline, which fits none of these.
    Match: P→2, Q→3, R→4, S→1 → (B).

  3. JEE Advanced 2025, Paper 1 · Question 48
    Match the compounds in List-I with the appropriate observations in List-II and choose the correct option.

    List-IList-II
    (P) Compound (P) in the figure(1) Reaction with phenyl diazonium salt gives yellow dye.
    (Q) Compound (Q) in the figure(2) Reaction with ninhydrin gives purple color and it also reacts with $\mathrm{FeCl_3}$ to give violet color.
    (R) Compound (R) in the figure(3) Reaction with glucose will give corresponding hydrazone.
    (S) Compound (S) in the figure(4) Lassiagne extract of the compound treated with dilute HCl followed by addition of aqueous $\mathrm{FeCl_3}$ gives blood red color.
    (5) After complete hydrolysis, it will give ninhydrin test and it DOES NOT give positive phthalein dye test.

    1. P $\rightarrow$ 1; Q $\rightarrow$ 5; R $\rightarrow$ 4; S $\rightarrow$ 2
    2. P $\rightarrow$ 2; Q $\rightarrow$ 5; R $\rightarrow$ 1; S $\rightarrow$ 3
    3. P $\rightarrow$ 5; Q $\rightarrow$ 2; R $\rightarrow$ 1; S $\rightarrow$ 4
    4. P $\rightarrow$ 2; Q $\rightarrow$ 1; R $\rightarrow$ 5; S $\rightarrow$ 3
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    (P) has a free α-$\mathrm{NH_2}$ (ninhydrin: purple) and a phenolic OH ($\mathrm{FeCl_3}$: violet) → (2).
    (Q) has no free $\mathrm{NH_2}$ (N-acetyl, peptide); complete hydrolysis frees amino acids (ninhydrin positive), and it has no phenol, so no phthalein dye test → (5).
    (R) anilinium chloride gives aniline, which couples with benzenediazonium salt to give a yellow dye (diazoamino/aminoazobenzene) → (1).
    (S) an arylhydrazine: condenses with the aldehyde group of glucose to give the hydrazone → (3).
    (None contains both N and S, so (4) fits none.)
    Match: P→2, Q→5, R→1, S→3 → (B).

  4. JEE Advanced 2025, Paper 2 · Question 39
    For the reaction sequence given below, the correct statement(s) is(are)

    1. Both $\mathbf{X}$ and $\mathbf{Y}$ are oxygen containing compounds.
    2. $\mathbf{Y}$ on heating with $\mathrm{CHCl_3/KOH}$ forms isocyanide.
    3. $\mathbf{Z}$ reacts with Hinsberg's reagent.
    4. $\mathbf{Z}$ is an aromatic primary amine.
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a), (c)

    Hot acidic $\mathrm{KMnO_4}$ oxidises tetralin's saturated ring to phthalic acid; $\mathrm{NH_3}$ with heat ($-2\mathrm{H_2O}$) gives phthalimide = $\mathbf{X}$.
    Gabriel synthesis: ethanolic KOH gives potassium phthalimide, and R–Br gives N-alkylphthalimide = $\mathbf{Y}$.
    NaOH hydrolysis gives sodium phthalate (aromatic) + $\mathrm{R{-}NH_2}$ = $\mathbf{Z}$, an aliphatic primary amine.
    (A) X and Y both contain C=O: true. (B) Y has no free $\mathrm{NH_2}$, so no carbylamine reaction: false. (C) A primary amine reacts with Hinsberg's reagent: true. (D) Z is aliphatic: false.
    Answer (A), (C).

  5. JEE Advanced 2023, Paper 1 · Question 47
    The total number of $sp^2$ hybridised carbon atoms in the major product $\mathbf{P}$ (a non-heterocyclic compound) of the following reaction is ______.
    [Reagents: (i) $\mathrm{LiAlH_4}$ (excess), then $\mathrm{H_2O}$; (ii) Acetophenone (excess)]

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 28

    The reactant is 1,1,2,2-tetracyanoethane, $\mathrm{(NC)_2CH{-}CH(CN)_2}$.
    Excess $\mathrm{LiAlH_4}$ reduces all four $\mathrm{C{\equiv}N}$ to $\mathrm{CH_2NH_2}$: $\mathrm{(H_2NCH_2)_2CH{-}CH(CH_2NH_2)_2}$.
    Each $\mathrm{NH_2}$ condenses with acetophenone to an imine $\mathrm{{-}CH_2N{=}C(CH_3)Ph}$ (four imines; the product is acyclic, not heterocyclic).
    $sp^2$ carbons per imine unit: $1$ (C=N) $+ 6$ (phenyl) $= 7$; total $4\times7 = 28$.

  6. JEE Advanced 2023, Paper 2 · Question 47
    The reaction of 4-methyloct-1-ene (P, 2.52 g) with HBr in the presence of $\mathrm{(C_6H_5CO)_2O_2}$ gives two isomeric bromides in a 9 : 1 ratio, with a combined yield of 50%. Of these, the entire amount of the primary alkyl bromide was reacted with an appropriate amount of diethylamine followed by treatment with aq. $\mathrm{K_2CO_3}$ to give a non-ionic product S in 100% yield.
    The mass (in mg) of S obtained is ________.
    [Use molar mass (in g mol$^{-1}$): H = 1, C = 12, N = 14, Br = 80]

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 1791

    4-Methyloct-1-ene, $\mathrm{C_9H_{18}}$, $M = 126$: $2.52/126 = 0.02$ mol.
    Peroxide (anti-Markovnikov) addition: the primary bromide is the major (9 parts) product; 50% combined yield = 0.01 mol, so primary bromide $= 0.009$ mol.
    With $\mathrm{Et_2NH}$ then base: the free tertiary amine S = $\mathrm{CH_3(CH_2)_3CH(CH_3)CH_2CH_2CH_2NEt_2}$, $\mathrm{C_{13}H_{29}N}$, $M = 156 + 29 + 14 = 199$.
    Mass $= 0.009\times199 = 1.791$ g $= 1791$ mg.

  7. JEE Advanced 2023, Paper 2 · Question 50
    A trinitro compound, 1,3,5-tris-(4-nitrophenyl)benzene, on complete reaction with an excess of Sn/HCl gives a major product, which on treatment with an excess of $\mathrm{NaNO_2}$/HCl at 0 °C provides P as the product. P, upon treatment with excess of $\mathrm{H_2O}$ at room temperature, gives the product Q. Bromination of Q in aqueous medium furnishes the product R. The compound P upon treatment with an excess of phenol under basic conditions gives the product S.
    The molar mass difference between compounds Q and R is 474 g mol$^{-1}$ and between compounds P and S is 172.5 g mol$^{-1}$.

    The number of heteroatoms present in one molecule of R is ________.
    [Use: Molar mass (in g mol$^{-1}$): H = 1, C = 12, N = 14, O = 16, Br = 80, Cl = 35.5. Atoms other than C and H are considered as heteroatoms]

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 9

    Sn/HCl: 1,3,5-tris(4-aminophenyl)benzene; $\mathrm{NaNO_2}$/HCl: P = the tris-diazonium chloride.
    Warm water: Q = 1,3,5-tris(4-hydroxyphenyl)benzene.
    Bromine water brominates each phenol ring at its two free ortho positions (para is occupied): 6 Br; check $6\times(80 - 1) = 474$ ✓.
    R contains 3 O + 6 Br = 9 heteroatoms.

  8. JEE Advanced 2023, Paper 2 · Question 51
    A trinitro compound, 1,3,5-tris-(4-nitrophenyl)benzene, on complete reaction with an excess of Sn/HCl gives a major product, which on treatment with an excess of $\mathrm{NaNO_2}$/HCl at 0 °C provides P as the product. P, upon treatment with excess of $\mathrm{H_2O}$ at room temperature, gives the product Q. Bromination of Q in aqueous medium furnishes the product R. The compound P upon treatment with an excess of phenol under basic conditions gives the product S.
    The molar mass difference between compounds Q and R is 474 g mol$^{-1}$ and between compounds P and S is 172.5 g mol$^{-1}$.

    The total number of carbon atoms and heteroatoms present in one molecule of S is ________.
    [Use: Molar mass (in g mol$^{-1}$): H = 1, C = 12, N = 14, O = 16, Br = 80, Cl = 35.5. Atoms other than C and H are considered as heteroatoms]

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 51

    Coupling of each $\mathrm{-N_2^+Cl^-}$ with phenol gives $\mathrm{-N{=}N{-}C_6H_4{-}OH}$; mass change per group $= 93 - 35.5 = 57.5$, $\times3 = 172.5$ ✓.
    S = 1,3,5-tris[4-(4-hydroxyphenylazo)phenyl]benzene.
    Carbon: $6 + 3\times6 + 3\times6 = 42$; heteroatoms: 6 N + 3 O = 9.
    Total $= 42 + 9 = 51$.

  9. JEE Advanced 2022, Paper 1 · Question 49
    Considering the reaction sequence given below, the correct statement(s) is(are)

    1. $\mathbf{P}$ can be reduced to a primary alcohol using $\mathrm{NaBH_4}$.
    2. Treating $\mathbf{P}$ with conc. $\mathrm{NH_4OH}$ solution followed by acidification gives $\mathbf{Q}$.
    3. Treating $\mathbf{Q}$ with a solution of $\mathrm{NaNO_2}$ in aq. HCl liberates $\mathrm{N_2}$.
    4. $\mathbf{P}$ is more acidic than $\mathrm{CH_3CH_2COOH}$.
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b), (c), (d)

    HVZ reaction: P $=\mathrm{CH_3CHBrCOOH}$ (2-bromopropanoic acid).
    Gabriel synthesis with potassium phthalimide, then hydrolysis, gives Q $=\mathrm{CH_3CH(NH_2)COOH}$ (alanine) and phthalic acid.
    (A) $\mathrm{NaBH_4}$ does not reduce carboxylic acids — wrong.
    (B) Excess ammonia substitutes the $\alpha$-Br; acidification gives alanine — correct.
    (C) A primary aliphatic amine with $\mathrm{HNO_2}$ gives $\mathrm{N_2}$ — correct.
    (D) The $-I$ effect of the $\alpha$-Br makes P more acidic than propanoic acid — correct.

  11. JEE Advanced 2021, Paper 2 · Question 39
    The reaction sequence(s) that would lead to o-xylene as the major product is(are)

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a), (b)

    (A) o-Toluidine → diazonium salt → (CuCN) o-tolunitrile → (DIBAL-H, then $\mathrm{H_3O^+}$) o-tolualdehyde → (Wolff–Kishner) $\mathrm{CHO \to CH_3}$: o-xylene ✓.
    (B) o-Bromotoluene → Grignard, $\mathrm{CO_2}$ → o-toluic acid → ($\mathrm{SOCl_2}$) acid chloride → (Rosenmund) o-tolualdehyde → (Clemmensen) o-xylene ✓.

    (D) Ozonolysis of indene gives $\mathrm{C_6H_4(CHO)(CH_2CHO)}$; Wolff–Kishner gives $\mathrm{C_6H_4(CH_3)(CH_2CH_3)}$: 2-ethyltoluene ✗.

  12. JEE Advanced 2021, Paper 2 · Question 40
    Correct option(s) for the following sequence of reactions is(are)

    1. $\mathbf{Q} = \mathrm{KNO_2}$, $\mathbf{W} = \mathrm{LiAlH_4}$
    2. $\mathbf{R}$ = benzenamine, $\mathbf{V} = \mathrm{KCN}$
    3. $\mathbf{Q} = \mathrm{AgNO_2}$, $\mathbf{R}$ = phenylmethanamine
    4. $\mathbf{W} = \mathrm{LiAlH_4}$, $\mathbf{V} = \mathrm{AgCN}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c), (d)

    $\mathbf{P}$ = $\mathrm{PhCH_2Br}$ (benzylic bromination).
    $\mathbf{S}$ is a foul-smelling isocyanide (carbylamine test), so $\mathbf{R}$ is a primary amine.
    $\mathbf{P} \to \mathbf{R}$: $\mathrm{AgNO_2}$ gives the nitro compound $\mathrm{PhCH_2NO_2}$ (KNO$_2$ gives mainly the nitrite ester); $\mathrm{H_2}$, Pd/C reduces it to $\mathrm{PhCH_2NH_2}$, phenylmethanamine. So $\mathbf{Q}$ = $\mathrm{AgNO_2}$ and $\mathbf{R}$ is not benzenamine.
    $\mathbf{T}$ = $\mathrm{PhCOOH}$, $\mathbf{U}$ = $\mathrm{PhCONH_2}$; $\mathrm{LiAlH_4}$ reduces the amide to $\mathrm{PhCH_2NH_2}$, so $\mathbf{W}$ = $\mathrm{LiAlH_4}$.
    $\mathbf{P} \to \mathbf{S}$ directly: $\mathrm{AgCN}$ gives $\mathrm{PhCH_2NC}$ (KCN would give the nitrile), so $\mathbf{V}$ = AgCN.
    Answer (C), (D).
