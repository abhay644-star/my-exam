  1. JEE Advanced 2026, Paper 1 · Question 41
    Two cylinders, both fitted with frictionless pistons, are filled with mixtures of He and Ar gases. In the first cylinder, the masses of He and Ar are $m_1$ and $m_2$, respectively. In the second cylinder, the masses of He and Ar are $m_2$ and $m_1$, respectively. The molar mass of Ar is 10 times the molar mass of He. The external pressure applied by the piston on the first cylinder needs to be 5 times that on the second cylinder so that the volume of the gas mixtures in both the cylinders are equal at the same temperature. Assuming He and Ar behave like ideal gases, the value of $(m_1/m_2)$ is ____.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 9.8

    Let the molar mass of He be $M$, so Ar has $10M$.
    Moles: $n_1 = \dfrac{m_1}{M} + \dfrac{m_2}{10M}$, $n_2 = \dfrac{m_2}{M} + \dfrac{m_1}{10M}$.
    At equal $V$ and $T$, $P \propto n$, so $n_1 = 5n_2$: $m_1 + \dfrac{m_2}{10} = 5m_2 + \dfrac{m_1}{2}$.
    $\dfrac{m_1}{2} = 4.9\,m_2 \Rightarrow m_1/m_2 = 9.80$.

  2. JEE Advanced 2025, Paper 2 · Question 41
    The density (in g cm$^{-3}$) of the metal which forms a cubic close packed (ccp) lattice with an axial distance (edge length) equal to 400 pm is ________.
    Use: Atomic mass of metal $=105.6$ amu and Avogadro's constant $=6\times10^{23}$ mol$^{-1}$

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 10.85 to 11.1

    ccp = fcc: $Z=4$ atoms per unit cell; $a=400$ pm $=4\times10^{-8}$ cm, $a^3=6.4\times10^{-23}$ cm$^3$.
    $\rho=\dfrac{ZM}{N_Aa^3}=\dfrac{4\times105.6}{6\times10^{23}\times6.4\times10^{-23}}=\dfrac{422.4}{38.4}$.
    $\rho=11.0$ g cm$^{-3}$.

  3. JEE Advanced 2024, Paper 1 · Question 35
    A closed vessel contains 10 g of an ideal gas X at 300 K, which exerts 2 atm pressure. At the same temperature, 80 g of another ideal gas Y is added to it and the pressure becomes 6 atm. The ratio of root mean square velocities of X and Y at 300 K is

    1. $2\sqrt{2} : \sqrt{3}$
    2. $2\sqrt{2} : 1$
    3. $1 : 2$
    4. $2 : 1$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    Same $V$ and $T$: partial pressures are proportional to moles. $p_X = 2$ atm, $p_Y = 6 - 2 = 4$ atm, so $n_Y = 2n_X$.
    $\dfrac{80}{M_Y} = 2\cdot\dfrac{10}{M_X} \Rightarrow M_Y = 4M_X$.
    $\dfrac{u_X}{u_Y} = \sqrt{\dfrac{M_Y}{M_X}} = 2$, i.e. $2 : 1$ → (D).

  4. JEE Advanced 2024, Paper 2 · Question 42
    To form a complete monolayer of acetic acid on 1g of charcoal, 100 mL of 0.5 M acetic acid was used. Some of the acetic acid remained unadsorbed. To neutralize the unadsorbed acetic acid, 40 mL of 1 M NaOH solution was required. If each molecule of acetic acid occupies $\mathbf{P}$ $\times10^{-23}\ \mathrm{m^2}$ surface area on charcoal, the value of $\mathbf{P}$ is _____.
    [Use given data: Surface area of charcoal $=1.5\times10^2\ \mathrm{m^2g^{-1}}$; Avogadro’s number $(N_A)=6.0\times10^{23}\ \mathrm{mol^{-1}}$]

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 2500

    Acetic acid taken $=0.1\times0.5=0.05$ mol; unadsorbed $=0.04\times1=0.04$ mol; adsorbed $=0.01$ mol.
    Molecules adsorbed $=0.01\times6.0\times10^{23}=6.0\times10^{21}$.
    Area per molecule $=\dfrac{150}{6.0\times10^{21}}=2.5\times10^{-20}=2500\times10^{-23}\ \mathrm{m^2}$.
    $\mathbf{P}$ $=2500$.

  5. JEE Advanced 2023, Paper 1 · Question 42
    The stoichiometric reaction of $516$ g of dimethyldichlorosilane with water results in a tetrameric cyclic product $\mathbf{X}$ in $75\%$ yield. The weight (in g) of $\mathbf{X}$ obtained is ______.
    [Use, molar mass (g $\text{mol}^{-1}$): H = 1, C = 12, O = 16, Si = 28, Cl = 35.5]

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 222

    $\mathrm{(CH_3)_2SiCl_2}$: $M = 30 + 28 + 71 = 129$, so $516$ g $= 4$ mol.
    Hydrolysis and condensation: $\mathrm{4(CH_3)_2SiCl_2 + 4H_2O \to [(CH_3)_2SiO]_4 + 8HCl}$, giving $1$ mol of the cyclic tetramer.
    $M(\mathbf X) = 4\times74 = 296$; at $75\%$ yield: $0.75\times296 = 222$ g.

  6. JEE Advanced 2023, Paper 1 · Question 43
    A gas has a compressibility factor of $0.5$ and a molar volume of $0.4\ \text{dm}^3\,\text{mol}^{-1}$ at a temperature of $800$ K and pressure $\mathbf{x}$ atm. If it shows ideal gas behaviour at the same temperature and pressure, the molar volume will be $\mathbf{y}\ \text{dm}^3\,\text{mol}^{-1}$. The value of $\mathbf{x/y}$ is ______.
    [Use: Gas constant, $R = 8\times10^{-2}\ \text{L atm K}^{-1}\text{mol}^{-1}$]

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 100

    $Z = \dfrac{PV_m}{RT}$: $0.5 = \dfrac{x\times0.4}{0.08\times800} \Rightarrow x = 80$ atm.
    Ideal: $y = \dfrac{RT}{P} = \dfrac{64}{80} = 0.8\ \text{dm}^3\,\text{mol}^{-1}$.
    $\dfrac xy = \dfrac{80}{0.8} = 100$.

  7. JEE Advanced 2023, Paper 2 · Question 40
    Atoms of metals x, y, and z form face-centred cubic (fcc) unit cell of edge length $\mathrm{L_x}$, body-centred cubic (bcc) unit cell of edge length $\mathrm{L_y}$, and simple cubic unit cell of edge length $\mathrm{L_z}$, respectively.
    If $\mathrm{r_z} = \dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}\mathrm{r_y}$; $\mathrm{r_y} = \dfrac{8}{\sqrt3}\mathrm{r_x}$; $\mathrm{M_z} = \dfrac32\mathrm{M_y}$ and $\mathrm{M_z} = 3\mathrm{M_x}$, then the correct statement(s) is(are)
    [Given: $\mathrm{M_x}$, $\mathrm{M_y}$, and $\mathrm{M_z}$ are molar masses of metals x, y, and z, respectively. $\mathrm{r_x}$, $\mathrm{r_y}$, and $\mathrm{r_z}$ are atomic radii of metals x, y, and z, respectively.]

    1. Packing efficiency of unit cell of x > Packing efficiency of unit cell of y > Packing efficiency of unit cell of z
    2. $\mathrm{L_y} > \mathrm{L_z}$
    3. $\mathrm{L_x} > \mathrm{L_y}$
    4. Density of x > Density of y
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a), (b), (d)

    $\mathrm{r_y} = \dfrac{8}{\sqrt3}\mathrm{r_x}$, $\mathrm{r_z} = 4\mathrm{r_x}$; $\mathrm{M_y} = 2\mathrm{M_x}$, $\mathrm{M_z} = 3\mathrm{M_x}$.
    $\mathrm{L_x} = 2\sqrt2\,\mathrm{r_x} \approx 2.83\mathrm{r_x}$; $\mathrm{L_y} = \dfrac{4\mathrm{r_y}}{\sqrt3} = \dfrac{32}{3}\mathrm{r_x} \approx 10.67\mathrm{r_x}$; $\mathrm{L_z} = 2\mathrm{r_z} = 8\mathrm{r_x}$.
    (A) fcc 74% > bcc 68% > simple cubic 52% ✓. (B) $\mathrm{L_y} > \mathrm{L_z}$ ✓. (C) $\mathrm{L_x} > \mathrm{L_y}$ ✗.
    (D) $\rho \propto \dfrac{ZM}{L^3}$: x: $\dfrac{4\mathrm{M_x}}{22.6\,\mathrm{r_x^3}} \approx 0.177$; y: $\dfrac{2(2\mathrm{M_x})}{1214\,\mathrm{r_x^3}} \approx 0.0033$ (units of $\mathrm{M_x/(N_A r_x^3)}$) → density of x > density of y ✓.
    Answer (A), (B), (D).

  8. JEE Advanced 2022, Paper 1 · Question 41
    Dissolving 1.24 g of white phosphorous in boiling NaOH solution in an inert atmosphere gives a gas Q. The amount of $\mathrm{CuSO_4}$ (in g) required to completely consume the gas Q is ______.
    [Given: Atomic mass of H = 1, O = 16, Na = 23, P = 31, S = 32, Cu = 63]

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 2.37 to 2.41

    $\mathrm{P_4}$: $\dfrac{1.24}{124}=0.01$ mol. $\mathrm{P_4+3NaOH+3H_2O\to PH_3+3NaH_2PO_2}$, so Q $=\mathrm{PH_3}$, 0.01 mol.
    $\mathrm{3CuSO_4+2PH_3\to Cu_3P_2+3H_2SO_4}$: $\mathrm{CuSO_4}$ needed $=0.015$ mol.
    $M(\mathrm{CuSO_4})=63+32+64=159$; mass $=0.015\times159=2.385\approx2.39$ g.

  9. JEE Advanced 2022, Paper 2 · Question 45
    To check the principle of multiple proportions, a series of pure binary compounds ($\mathrm{P_mQ_n}$) were analyzed and their composition is tabulated below. The correct option(s) is(are)
    Compound 1: weight % of P $= 50$, weight % of Q $= 50$.
    Compound 2: weight % of P $= 44.4$, weight % of Q $= 55.6$.
    Compound 3: weight % of P $= 40$, weight % of Q $= 60$.

    1. If empirical formula of compound 3 is $\mathrm{P_3Q_4}$, then the empirical formula of compound 2 is $\mathrm{P_3Q_5}$.
    2. If empirical formula of compound 3 is $\mathrm{P_3Q_2}$ and atomic weight of element P is 20, then the atomic weight of Q is 45.
    3. If empirical formula of compound 2 is PQ, then the empirical formula of the compound 1 is $\mathrm{P_5Q_4}$.
    4. If atomic weight of P and Q are 70 and 35, respectively, then the empirical formula of compound 1 is $\mathrm{P_2Q}$.
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b), (c)

    Mass of Q per unit mass of P: $1$, $1.25$, $1.5$ (ratio $4:5:6$).
    (A) Compound 3 = $\mathrm{P_3Q_4}$: $\dfrac{4Q}{3P} = 1.5 \Rightarrow \dfrac QP = 1.125$. Compound 2: $\dfrac nm = \dfrac{1.25}{1.125} = \dfrac{10}9$, i.e. $\mathrm{P_9Q_{10}}$, not $\mathrm{P_3Q_5}$: false.
    (B) Compound 3 = $\mathrm{P_3Q_2}$, P = 20: $\dfrac{2Q}{60} = 1.5 \Rightarrow Q = 45$: true.
    (C) Compound 2 = PQ: $\dfrac QP = 1.25$; compound 1: $\dfrac nm = \dfrac1{1.25} = \dfrac45$, i.e. $\mathrm{P_5Q_4}$: true.
    (D) P = 70, Q = 35: compound 1 has $\dfrac{50}{70} : \dfrac{50}{35} = 1 : 2$, i.e. $\mathrm{PQ_2}$, not $\mathrm{P_2Q}$: false.
    Answer (B), (C).

  10. JEE Advanced 2022, Paper 2 · Question 51
    Atom X occupies the fcc lattice sites as well as alternate tetrahedral voids of the same lattice. The packing efficiency (in %) of the resultant solid is closest to

    1. 25
    2. 35
    3. 55
    4. 75
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    Per unit cell: 4 atoms at fcc sites + 4 in alternate tetrahedral voids = 8 atoms (diamond-like).
    Atoms in the voids touch the fcc atoms along the body diagonal: $2r = \dfrac{\sqrt3a}{4}$, so $r = \dfrac{\sqrt3a}{8}$.
    Packing fraction $= \dfrac{8\cdot\frac43\pi r^3}{a^3} = \dfrac{32\pi}{3}\cdot\dfrac{3\sqrt3}{512} = \dfrac{\sqrt3\pi}{16} \approx 0.34$.
    About 34%, closest to 35 → (B).

  11. JEE Advanced 2021, Paper 1 · Question 41
    For the given close packed structure of a salt made of cation $\mathbf{X}$ and anion $\mathbf{Y}$ shown below (ions of only one face are shown for clarity), the packing fraction is approximately (packing fraction $= \dfrac{\text{packing efficiency}}{100}$)

    1. 0.74
    2. 0.63
    3. 0.52
    4. 0.48
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    Anions Y sit at the cube corners and touch along the edge: $a = 2r_Y$. Cation X sits at each face centre, touching the corner anions along the face diagonal: $\sqrt2\,a = 2r_Y + 2r_X$, so $r_X = (\sqrt2 - 1)r_Y \approx 0.414\,r_Y$.
    Per unit cell: Y $= 8 \times \frac18 = 1$; X $= 6 \times \frac12 = 3$.
    Packing fraction $= \dfrac{\frac43\pi\left(r_Y^3 + 3r_X^3\right)}{(2r_Y)^3} = \dfrac{\pi}{6}\left(1 + 3 \times 0.0711\right) \approx 0.5236 \times 1.213 \approx 0.63$ → (B).

  12. JEE Advanced 2021, Paper 2 · Question 47
    Reaction of $\mathbf{x}$ g of Sn with HCl quantitatively produced a salt. Entire amount of the salt reacted with $\mathbf{y}$ g of nitrobenzene in the presence of required amount of HCl to produce 1.29 g of an organic salt (quantitatively).
    (Use Molar masses (in g mol$^{-1}$) of H, C, N, O, Cl and Sn as 1, 12, 14, 16, 35 and 119, respectively).

    The value of $\mathbf{x}$ is ___.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 3.57

    $\mathrm{Sn + 2HCl \to SnCl_2 + H_2}$; $\mathrm{C_6H_5NO_2 + 3SnCl_2 + 7HCl \to C_6H_5NH_3^+Cl^- + 3SnCl_4 + 2H_2O}$.
    Organic salt anilinium chloride, $M = 72 + 8 + 14 + 35 = 129$: $\dfrac{1.29}{129} = 0.01$ mol.
    $\mathrm{SnCl_2}$ needed $= 3 \times 0.01 = 0.03$ mol, so Sn $= 0.03 \times 119 = 3.57$ g.

  13. JEE Advanced 2021, Paper 2 · Question 48
    Reaction of $\mathbf{x}$ g of Sn with HCl quantitatively produced a salt. Entire amount of the salt reacted with $\mathbf{y}$ g of nitrobenzene in the presence of required amount of HCl to produce 1.29 g of an organic salt (quantitatively).
    (Use Molar masses (in g mol$^{-1}$) of H, C, N, O, Cl and Sn as 1, 12, 14, 16, 35 and 119, respectively).

    The value of $\mathbf{y}$ is ___.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 1.23

    $\mathrm{Sn + 2HCl \to SnCl_2 + H_2}$; $\mathrm{C_6H_5NO_2 + 3SnCl_2 + 7HCl \to C_6H_5NH_3^+Cl^- + 3SnCl_4 + 2H_2O}$.
    Organic salt anilinium chloride, $M = 72 + 8 + 14 + 35 = 129$: $\dfrac{1.29}{129} = 0.01$ mol.
    Nitrobenzene $= 0.01$ mol $\times 123$ g mol$^{-1}$ $= 1.23$ g.
