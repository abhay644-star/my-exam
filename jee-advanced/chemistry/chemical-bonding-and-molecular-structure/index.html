  1. JEE Advanced 2026, Paper 1 · Question 35
    The correct order of dipole moments for the given species is

    1. $\mathrm{BF_3} = \mathrm{NH_4^+} \lt \mathrm{NF_3} \lt \mathrm{NH_3}$
    2. $\mathrm{BF_3} \lt \mathrm{NH_4^+} \lt \mathrm{NF_3} \lt \mathrm{NH_3}$
    3. $\mathrm{NH_4^+} \lt \mathrm{BF_3} \lt \mathrm{NH_3} \lt \mathrm{NF_3}$
    4. $\mathrm{BF_3} \lt \mathrm{NH_4^+} \lt \mathrm{NH_3} \lt \mathrm{NF_3}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    $\mathrm{BF_3}$ (trigonal planar) and $\mathrm{NH_4^+}$ (regular tetrahedral) are symmetric, so their dipole moments are both zero.
    In $\mathrm{NH_3}$ the N–H bond moments point towards N and add to the lone-pair moment: about 1.47 D.
    In $\mathrm{NF_3}$ the N–F bond moments point away from N and partly cancel the lone-pair moment: about 0.23 D.
    Order: $\mathrm{BF_3} = \mathrm{NH_4^+} \lt \mathrm{NF_3} \lt \mathrm{NH_3}$.

  2. JEE Advanced 2026, Paper 1 · Question 38
    Correct statement(s) about the compounds X, Y and Z is(are)

    1. X is used for sterilizing drinking water.
    2. Y has a planar structure.
    3. Z is used in the enrichment of $^{235}$U.
    4. Y is a stronger Lewis base than ammonia.
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a), (c)

    $\mathrm{MnO_2 + 4HCl \rightarrow MnCl_2 + Cl_2 + 2H_2O}$: X = $\mathrm{Cl_2}$ (greenish yellow), which is used to sterilise drinking water, so (A) is true.
    $\mathrm{NH_3 + 3Cl_2(excess) \rightarrow NCl_3 + 3HCl}$: Y = $\mathrm{NCl_3}$. It is pyramidal (sp$^3$ N with one lone pair), not planar, so (B) is false.
    The Cl atoms withdraw electron density from N, so $\mathrm{NCl_3}$ is a weaker Lewis base than $\mathrm{NH_3}$: (D) is false.
    $\mathrm{Cl_2 + 3F_2(excess) \xrightarrow{573\,K} 2ClF_3}$: Z = $\mathrm{ClF_3}$, used to make $\mathrm{UF_6}$ for $^{235}$U enrichment, so (C) is true.

  3. JEE Advanced 2026, Paper 1 · Question 39
    Reaction of $\mathrm{PtF_6}$ with oxygen ($\mathrm{O_2}$) gas results in the formation of an ionic compound, $\mathbf{X}^+\mathbf{Y}^-$.
    Correct statement(s) is(are)

    1. The bond order of $\mathbf{X}^+$ is 1.5.
    2. Valence $d$-orbitals of the metal ion in $\mathbf{X}^+\mathbf{Y}^-$ has 5 electrons.
    3. $\mathrm{PtF_6}$ acts as an oxidant in this reaction.
    4. $\mathrm{PtF_6}$ acts as a fluorinating agent in this reaction.
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b), (c)

    $\mathrm{O_2 + PtF_6 \rightarrow O_2^+[PtF_6]^-}$, so X$^+$ = $\mathrm{O_2^+}$ and Y$^-$ = $\mathrm{[PtF_6]^-}$.
    $\mathrm{O_2^+}$ has 15 electrons and bond order $(10-5)/2 = 2.5$, so (A) is false.
    In $\mathrm{[PtF_6]^-}$, Pt is +5: Pt ($5d^9 6s^1$) loses 5 electrons to give $5d^5$, so (B) is true.
    $\mathrm{PtF_6}$ takes an electron from $\mathrm{O_2}$, so it is the oxidant: (C) is true.
    No F is transferred to oxygen (no O–F bond forms), so it is not acting as a fluorinating agent: (D) is false.

  4. JEE Advanced 2026, Paper 1 · Question 46
    Consider the following species:

    $\mathrm{SOCl_2}$, $\mathrm{XeOF_4}$, $\mathrm{ClF_3}$, $\mathrm{ClF_5}$, $\mathrm{XeF_5^+}$, $\mathrm{SO_3^{2-}}$, $\mathrm{XeF_3^+}$, $\mathrm{SF_4}$

    List-I contains different molecular shapes and List-II contains total number of species with the same molecular shapes from the given species. Match each entry in List-I with the appropriate entry in List-II and choose the correct option.

    List-IList-II
    (P) See-saw(1) one
    (Q) T-Shaped(2) two
    (R) Trigonal Planar(3) three
    (S) Square Pyramidal(4) four
    (5) zero

    1. P $\rightarrow$ 1; Q $\rightarrow$ 2; R $\rightarrow$ 5; S $\rightarrow$ 3
    2. P $\rightarrow$ 5; Q $\rightarrow$ 4; R $\rightarrow$ 2; S $\rightarrow$ 3
    3. P $\rightarrow$ 3; Q $\rightarrow$ 2; R $\rightarrow$ 1; S $\rightarrow$ 4
    4. P $\rightarrow$ 1; Q $\rightarrow$ 3; R $\rightarrow$ 5; S $\rightarrow$ 4
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    $\mathrm{SOCl_2}$: 3 bond pairs + 1 lone pair, so trigonal pyramidal. $\mathrm{SO_3^{2-}}$: 3 bond pairs + 1 lone pair, so trigonal pyramidal.
    $\mathrm{SF_4}$: 4 bond pairs + 1 lone pair, so see-saw. That is the only one: P→1.
    $\mathrm{ClF_3}$ and $\mathrm{XeF_3^+}$: 3 bond pairs + 2 lone pairs, so T-shaped. That makes two: Q→2.
    $\mathrm{XeOF_4}$, $\mathrm{ClF_5}$ and $\mathrm{XeF_5^+}$: 5 bond pairs + 1 lone pair, so square pyramidal. That makes three: S→3.
    None is trigonal planar ($\mathrm{SO_3^{2-}}$ is pyramidal): R→5.

  5. JEE Advanced 2026, Paper 2 · Question 38
    The correct order of ONO bond angle in the given species is

    1. $\mathrm{NO_2^+} < \mathrm{NO_2} < \mathrm{NO_3^-} < \mathrm{NO_2^-}$
    2. $\mathrm{NO_2^-} < \mathrm{NO_3^-} < \mathrm{NO_2} < \mathrm{NO_2^+}$
    3. $\mathrm{NO_3^-} < \mathrm{NO_2^-} < \mathrm{NO_2} < \mathrm{NO_2^+}$
    4. $\mathrm{NO_2^-} < \mathrm{NO_3^-} < \mathrm{NO_2^+} < \mathrm{NO_2}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    $\mathrm{NO_2^+}$: N has no lone electron, sp, linear → $180^\circ$.
    $\mathrm{NO_2}$: one unpaired electron on N repels weakly → about $134^\circ$.
    $\mathrm{NO_3^-}$: sp$^2$, trigonal planar → $120^\circ$.
    $\mathrm{NO_2^-}$: a full lone pair on N compresses the angle → about $115^\circ$.
    Order: $\mathrm{NO_2^-} < \mathrm{NO_3^-} < \mathrm{NO_2} < \mathrm{NO_2^+}$ → (B).

  6. JEE Advanced 2026, Paper 2 · Question 42
    Correct statement(s) about the compounds P, Q and R is(are)

    $\displaystyle \underset{(1\,:\,5\ \text{ratio})}{\mathrm{Xe(g)} + \mathrm{F_2(g)}} \xrightarrow{873\ \text{K},\ 7\ \text{bar}} \mathbf{P}$
    $\displaystyle \mathbf{P} + \mathrm{O_2F_2} \xrightarrow{143\ \text{K}} \mathbf{Q} + \mathrm{O_2}$
    $\displaystyle \mathbf{Q} + \mathrm{H_2O} \xrightarrow{\text{complete hydrolysis}} \mathbf{R} + \mathrm{HF}$

    1. P has two lone pairs of electrons on the central atom.
    2. Q has a perfect octahedral geometry.
    3. Q can act as a fluorinating agent.
    4. The molecular structure of R is trigonal pyramidal.
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a), (c), (d)

    Xe : F$_2$ = 1 : 5 at 873 K, 7 bar gives P = $\mathrm{XeF_4}$.
    $\mathrm{XeF_4 + O_2F_2 \to XeF_6 + O_2}$, so Q = $\mathrm{XeF_6}$.
    $\mathrm{XeF_6 + 3H_2O \to XeO_3 + 6HF}$, so R = $\mathrm{XeO_3}$.
    (A) $\mathrm{XeF_4}$: 4 bond pairs + 2 lone pairs, square planar → true.
    (B) $\mathrm{XeF_6}$ has 6 bond pairs + 1 lone pair → distorted octahedron → false.
    (C) $\mathrm{XeF_6}$ is a strong fluorinating agent → true.
    (D) $\mathrm{XeO_3}$: 3 bonds + 1 lone pair → trigonal pyramidal → true.

  7. JEE Advanced 2025, Paper 1 · Question 37
    Regarding the molecular orbital (MO) energy levels for homonuclear diatomic molecules, the INCORRECT statement(s) is(are)

    1. Bond order of $\mathrm{Ne_2}$ is zero.
    2. The highest occupied molecular orbital (HOMO) of $\mathrm{F_2}$ is $\sigma$-type.
    3. Bond energy of $\mathrm{O_2^+}$ is smaller than the bond energy of $\mathrm{O_2}$.
    4. Bond length of $\mathrm{Li_2}$ is larger than the bond length of $\mathrm{B_2}$.
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b), (c)

    (A) $\mathrm{Ne_2}$: $10$ bonding and $10$ antibonding electrons, bond order $0$: correct statement.
    (B) $\mathrm{F_2}$: the HOMO is $\pi^*2p$ (the $\pi^*$ pair is filled last), not $\sigma$-type: incorrect.
    (C) Bond order of $\mathrm{O_2^+}$ is $2.5$, of $\mathrm{O_2}$ is $2$, so $\mathrm{O_2^+}$ has the larger bond energy: incorrect.
    (D) $\mathrm{Li_2}$ (bond order 1, large 2s orbitals, about 267 pm) is longer than $\mathrm{B_2}$ (bond order 1, about 159 pm): correct statement.
    Incorrect statements: (B), (C).

  8. JEE Advanced 2025, Paper 2 · Question 37
    The correct statement(s) about intermolecular forces is(are)

    1. The potential energy between two point charges approaches zero more rapidly than the potential energy between a point dipole and a point charge as the distance between them approaches infinity.
    2. The average potential energy of two rotating polar molecules that are separated by a distance $r$ has $1/r^3$ dependence.
    3. The dipole-induced dipole average interaction energy is independent of temperature.
    4. Nonpolar molecules attract one another even though neither has a permanent dipole moment.
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c), (d)

    (A) Charge–charge energy $\propto\dfrac1r$, charge–dipole $\propto\dfrac1{r^2}$: the charge–charge energy falls off more slowly. False.
    (B) For freely rotating polar molecules the Boltzmann-averaged (Keesom) energy $\propto\dfrac{1}{r^6}$, not $\dfrac1{r^3}$. False.
    (C) The induced dipole always lines up with the permanent dipole, so the dipole–induced dipole (Debye) energy does not depend on temperature. True.
    (D) Nonpolar molecules attract through London dispersion forces (instantaneous dipoles). True.
    Answer (C), (D).

  9. JEE Advanced 2025, Paper 2 · Question 38
    The compound(s) with P$-$H bond(s) is(are)

    1. $\mathrm{H_3PO_4}$
    2. $\mathrm{H_3PO_3}$
    3. $\mathrm{H_4P_2O_7}$
    4. $\mathrm{H_3PO_2}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b), (d)

    $\mathrm{H_3PO_4}$: $\mathrm{O{=}P(OH)_3}$, no P–H.
    $\mathrm{H_3PO_3}$ (phosphorous acid): $\mathrm{O{=}PH(OH)_2}$, one P–H (dibasic).
    $\mathrm{H_4P_2O_7}$: $\mathrm{(HO)_2P(O)-O-P(O)(OH)_2}$, no P–H.
    $\mathrm{H_3PO_2}$ (hypophosphorous acid): $\mathrm{O{=}PH_2(OH)}$, two P–H (monobasic).
    Answer (B), (D).

  10. JEE Advanced 2024, Paper 1 · Question 41
    The option(s) in which at least three molecules follow Octet Rule is(are)

    1. $\mathrm{CO_2}$, $\mathrm{C_2H_4}$, $\mathrm{NO}$ and $\mathrm{HCl}$
    2. $\mathrm{NO_2}$, $\mathrm{O_3}$, $\mathrm{HCl}$ and $\mathrm{H_2SO_4}$
    3. $\mathrm{BCl_3}$, $\mathrm{NO}$, $\mathrm{NO_2}$ and $\mathrm{H_2SO_4}$
    4. $\mathrm{CO_2}$, $\mathrm{BCl_3}$, $\mathrm{O_3}$ and $\mathrm{C_2H_4}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a), (d)

    Follow the octet rule: $\mathrm{CO_2}$, $\mathrm{C_2H_4}$, HCl, $\mathrm{O_3}$.
    Do not: NO and $\mathrm{NO_2}$ (odd-electron molecules), $\mathrm{BCl_3}$ (B has only 6 electrons), $\mathrm{H_2SO_4}$ (S has an expanded octet).
    (A) $\mathrm{CO_2}$, $\mathrm{C_2H_4}$, HCl: three. ✓
    (B) $\mathrm{O_3}$, HCl: two. ✗ (C) none. ✗
    (D) $\mathrm{CO_2}$, $\mathrm{O_3}$, $\mathrm{C_2H_4}$: three. ✓
    Answer: (A), (D).

  11. JEE Advanced 2024, Paper 1 · Question 49
    Based on VSEPR model, match the xenon compounds given in List-I with the corresponding geometries and the number of lone pairs on xenon given in List-II and choose the correct option.

    List-IList-II
    (P) $\mathrm{XeF_2}$(1) Trigonal bipyramidal and two lone pair of electrons
    (Q) $\mathrm{XeF_4}$(2) Tetrahedral and one lone pair of electrons
    (R) $\mathrm{XeO_3}$(3) Octahedral and two lone pair of electrons
    (S) $\mathrm{XeO_3F_2}$(4) Trigonal bipyramidal and no lone pair of electrons
    (5) Trigonal bipyramidal and three lone pair of electrons

    1. P-5, Q-2, R-3, S-1
    2. P-5, Q-3, R-2, S-4
    3. P-4, Q-3, R-2, S-1
    4. P-4, Q-2, R-5, S-3
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    Electron-pair geometry (VSEPR) and lone pairs on Xe:
    $\mathrm{XeF_2}$: 2 bond pairs + 3 lone pairs = 5 pairs, trigonal bipyramidal, three lone pairs → (5).
    $\mathrm{XeF_4}$: 4 + 2 = 6 pairs, octahedral, two lone pairs → (3).
    $\mathrm{XeO_3}$: 3 σ bond pairs + 1 lone pair = 4 pairs, tetrahedral, one lone pair → (2).
    $\mathrm{XeO_3F_2}$: 5 σ bond pairs, no lone pair, trigonal bipyramidal → (4).
    P-5, Q-3, R-2, S-4 → (B).

  12. JEE Advanced 2024, Paper 2 · Question 38
    The species formed on fluorination of phosphorus pentachloride in a polar organic solvent are

    1. $\mathrm{[PF_4]^+[PF_6]^-}$ and $\mathrm{[PCl_4]^+[PF_6]^-}$
    2. $\mathrm{[PCl_4]^+[PCl_4F_2]^-}$ and $\mathrm{[PCl_4]^+[PF_6]^-}$
    3. $\mathrm{PF_3}$ and $\mathrm{PCl_3}$
    4. $\mathrm{PF_5}$ and $\mathrm{PCl_3}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a) or (b) (either is accepted)

    In a polar solvent $\mathrm{PCl_5}$ ionises as $\mathrm{[PCl_4]^+[PCl_6]^-}$, so its fluorination gives ionic chloro/fluoro-phosphonium salts, not neutral molecules.
    Fluoride replaces chloride on phosphorus to give hexafluorophosphate salts such as $\mathrm{[PCl_4]^+[PF_6]^-}$ (in both A and B), together with $\mathrm{[PF_4]^+[PF_6]^-}$ or the partly fluorinated $\mathrm{[PCl_4]^+[PCl_4F_2]^-}$.
    (C) and (D) involve reduction to P(III) or neutral $\mathrm{PF_5}$, which a simple halide exchange does not give.
    The official final key accepts (A) or (B).

    Note: Official final key: A or B (either option is awarded marks).

  13. JEE Advanced 2024, Paper 2 · Question 41
    The compound(s) having peroxide linkage is(are)

    1. $\mathrm{H_2S_2O_7}$
    2. $\mathrm{H_2S_2O_8}$
    3. $\mathrm{H_2S_2O_5}$
    4. $\mathrm{H_2SO_5}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b), (d)

    $\mathrm{H_2S_2O_7}$ (pyrosulphuric acid): S–O–S bridge, no peroxide.
    $\mathrm{H_2S_2O_8}$ (peroxodisulphuric acid): $\mathrm{HO_3S{-}O{-}O{-}SO_3H}$, has O–O.
    $\mathrm{H_2S_2O_5}$ (disulphurous acid): S–S bond, no peroxide.
    $\mathrm{H_2SO_5}$ (Caro’s acid, peroxomonosulphuric acid): $\mathrm{HO{-}O{-}SO_2{-}OH}$, has O–O.
    Answer: (B), (D).

  14. JEE Advanced 2023, Paper 2 · Question 35
    The correct molecular orbital diagram for $\mathrm{F_2}$ molecule in the ground state is

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    For $\mathrm{O_2}$ and $\mathrm{F_2}$ there is little s–p mixing, so the $\sigma_{2p}$ orbital (labelled $2\sigma$) lies below the $\pi_{2p}$ pair ($1\pi$); this rules out (A) and (B).

  15. JEE Advanced 2023, Paper 2 · Question 43
    Among $\mathrm{[I_3]^+}$, $\mathrm{[SiO_4]^{4-}}$, $\mathrm{SO_2Cl_2}$, $\mathrm{XeF_2}$, $\mathrm{SF_4}$, $\mathrm{ClF_3}$, $\mathrm{Ni(CO)_4}$, $\mathrm{XeO_2F_2}$, $\mathrm{[PtCl_4]^{2-}}$, $\mathrm{XeF_4}$, and $\mathrm{SOCl_2}$, the total number of species having $sp^3$ hybridised central atom is ________.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 5

    $sp^3$: $\mathrm{[I_3]^+}$ (2 bond pairs + 2 lone pairs), $\mathrm{[SiO_4]^{4-}}$, $\mathrm{SO_2Cl_2}$, $\mathrm{Ni(CO)_4}$ (tetrahedral Ni(0)), $\mathrm{SOCl_2}$ (3 bonds + 1 lone pair).
    Not $sp^3$: $\mathrm{XeF_2}$, $\mathrm{SF_4}$, $\mathrm{ClF_3}$, $\mathrm{XeO_2F_2}$ ($sp^3d$); $\mathrm{XeF_4}$ ($sp^3d^2$); $\mathrm{[PtCl_4]^{2-}}$ ($dsp^2$).
    Total $= 5$.

  16. JEE Advanced 2022, Paper 1 · Question 45
    For diatomic molecules, the correct statement(s) about the molecular orbitals formed by the overlap of two $2p_z$ orbitals is(are)

    1. $\sigma$ orbital has a total of two nodal planes.
    2. $\sigma^*$ orbital has one node in the $xz$-plane containing the molecular axis.
    3. $\pi$ orbital has one node in the plane which is perpendicular to the molecular axis and goes through the center of the molecule.
    4. $\pi^*$ orbital has one node in the $xy$-plane containing the molecular axis.
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a), (d); the final key also accepts (d)

    (B) $\sigma^*(2p_z)$ (end-on overlap along $z$) has its nodal plane perpendicular to the axis, through the centre — not a plane containing the axis: wrong.
    (C) A $\pi$ bonding orbital has no node perpendicular to the axis; its nodal plane contains the axis: wrong.
    (D) When two $2p_z$ orbitals overlap sideways (molecular axis along $x$ or $y$), the $\pi^*$ orbital has the $xy$-plane, which contains the molecular axis, as a nodal plane: correct.
    (A) The $\sigma(2p_z)$ orbital keeps a nodal surface behind each nucleus (from the nodes of the $p$ orbitals), two in all; the final key accepts (A) together with (D) as well as (D) alone.

    Note: Official final key: AD or D (either set gets full marks).

  17. JEE Advanced 2022, Paper 1 · Question 48
    The treatment of galena with $\mathrm{HNO_3}$ produces a gas that is

    1. paramagnetic
    2. bent in geometry
    3. an acidic oxide
    4. colorless
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a), (d)

    $\mathrm{3PbS+8HNO_3\to3Pb(NO_3)_2+3S+2NO+4H_2O}$: the gas is NO.
    NO has 11 valence electrons (one unpaired): paramagnetic — (A).
    It is diatomic, hence linear, not bent — (B) wrong; it is a neutral oxide — (C) wrong; it is colourless — (D).

  18. JEE Advanced 2022, Paper 2 · Question 41
    Thermal decomposition of $\mathrm{AgNO_3}$ produces two paramagnetic gases. The total number of electrons present in the antibonding molecular orbitals of the gas that has the higher number of unpaired electrons is ________.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 6

    $\mathrm{2AgNO_3 \rightarrow 2Ag + 2NO_2 + O_2}$: $\mathrm{NO_2}$ has 1 unpaired electron, $\mathrm{O_2}$ has 2.
    $\mathrm{O_2}$: $\sigma1s^2\,\sigma^*1s^2\,\sigma2s^2\,\sigma^*2s^2\,\sigma2p_z^2\,\pi2p^4\,\pi^*2p^2$.
    Antibonding electrons: $\sigma^*1s^2 + \sigma^*2s^2 + \pi^*2p^2 = 6$.

  19. JEE Advanced 2022, Paper 2 · Question 47
    The compound(s) which react(s) with $\mathrm{NH_3}$ to give boron nitride (BN) is(are)

    1. B
    2. $\mathrm{B_2H_6}$
    3. $\mathrm{B_2O_3}$
    4. $\mathrm{HBF_4}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b), (c); the final key also accepts (a), (b), (c)

    $\mathrm{B_2H_6}$ with $\mathrm{NH_3}$ gives $\mathrm{[BH_2(NH_3)_2]^+[BH_4]^-}$, which on strong heating gives borazine and finally BN.
    $\mathrm{B_2O_3 + 2NH_3 \rightarrow 2BN + 3H_2O}$ (on heating).
    Elemental boron also forms BN with $\mathrm{NH_3}$ at high temperature ($\mathrm{2B + 2NH_3 \rightarrow 2BN + 3H_2}$); $\mathrm{HBF_4}$ does not.
    Answer (B), (C); the official key also accepts (A), (B), (C).

    Note: Official final key: B, C or A, B, C
