  1. JEE Advanced 2025, Paper 1 · Question 33
    The heating of $\mathrm{NH_4NO_2}$ at $60-70\ {}^\circ$C and $\mathrm{NH_4NO_3}$ at $200-250\ {}^\circ$C is associated with the formation of nitrogen containing compounds X and Y, respectively. X and Y, respectively, are

    1. $\mathrm{N_2}$ and $\mathrm{N_2O}$
    2. $\mathrm{NH_3}$ and $\mathrm{NO_2}$
    3. $\mathrm{NO}$ and $\mathrm{N_2O}$
    4. $\mathrm{N_2}$ and $\mathrm{NH_3}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    $\mathrm{NH_4NO_2} \xrightarrow{\Delta} \mathrm{N_2} + 2\mathrm{H_2O}$, so X $= \mathrm{N_2}$.
    $\mathrm{NH_4NO_3} \xrightarrow{200-250\,{}^\circ\mathrm{C}} \mathrm{N_2O} + 2\mathrm{H_2O}$, so Y $= \mathrm{N_2O}$.
    Answer (A).

  2. JEE Advanced 2025, Paper 2 · Question 34
    The complete hydrolysis of ICl, $\mathrm{ClF_3}$ and $\mathrm{BrF_5}$, respectively, gives

    1. $\mathrm{IO^-}$, $\mathrm{ClO_2^-}$ and $\mathrm{BrO_3^-}$
    2. $\mathrm{IO_3^-}$, $\mathrm{ClO_2^-}$ and $\mathrm{BrO_3^-}$
    3. $\mathrm{IO^-}$, $\mathrm{ClO^-}$ and $\mathrm{BrO_2^-}$
    4. $\mathrm{IO_3^-}$, $\mathrm{ClO_4^-}$ and $\mathrm{BrO_2^-}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    On hydrolysis the central (less electronegative) halogen keeps its oxidation state and forms an oxoacid; the more electronegative halogen gives $\mathrm{HX}$.
    $\mathrm{ICl}$: I is $+1$ → $\mathrm{HOI}$, i.e. $\mathrm{IO^-}$.
    $\mathrm{ClF_3}$: Cl is $+3$ → $\mathrm{HClO_2}$, i.e. $\mathrm{ClO_2^-}$.
    $\mathrm{BrF_5}$: Br is $+5$ → $\mathrm{HBrO_3}$, i.e. $\mathrm{BrO_3^-}$.
    Answer (A).

  3. JEE Advanced 2024, Paper 1 · Question 36
    At room temperature, disproportionation of an aqueous solution of in situ generated nitrous acid ($\mathrm{HNO_2}$) gives the species

    1. $\mathrm{H_3O^+}$, $\mathrm{NO_3^-}$ and $\mathrm{NO}$
    2. $\mathrm{H_3O^+}$, $\mathrm{NO_3^-}$ and $\mathrm{NO_2}$
    3. $\mathrm{H_3O^+}$, $\mathrm{NO^-}$ and $\mathrm{NO_2}$
    4. $\mathrm{H_3O^+}$, $\mathrm{NO_3^-}$ and $\mathrm{N_2O}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    Nitrous acid (N in +3) disproportionates to N(+5) and N(+2): $3\mathrm{HNO_2} \to \mathrm{HNO_3} + 2\mathrm{NO} + \mathrm{H_2O}$.
    In water $\mathrm{HNO_3}$ is ionised: $\mathrm{H_3O^+} + \mathrm{NO_3^-}$.
    Species: $\mathrm{H_3O^+}$, $\mathrm{NO_3^-}$ and NO → (A).

  4. JEE Advanced 2024, Paper 2 · Question 36
    In a metal deficient oxide sample, $\mathbf{M_XY_2O_4}$ ($\mathbf{M}$ and $\mathbf{Y}$ are metals), $\mathbf{M}$ is present in both +2 and +3 oxidation states and $\mathbf{Y}$ is in +3 oxidation state. If the fraction of $\mathbf{M^{2+}}$ ions present in $\mathbf{M}$ is $\dfrac13$, the value of $\mathbf{X}$ is _____.

    1. $0.25$
    2. $0.33$
    3. $0.67$
    4. $0.75$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    Charge balance: $2(+3)+X\cdot(\text{average charge of M})=8$, so $X\times(\text{average charge})=2$.
    Average charge of M $=\dfrac13(2)+\dfrac23(3)=\dfrac83$.
    $X=\dfrac{2}{8/3}=0.75$ → (D).

  5. JEE Advanced 2023, Paper 1 · Question 35
    The correct statement(s) related to processes involved in the extraction of metals is(are)

    1. Roasting of Malachite produces Cuprite.
    2. Calcination of Calamine produces Zincite.
    3. Copper pyrites is heated with silica in a reverberatory furnace to remove iron.
    4. Impure silver is treated with aqueous KCN in the presence of oxygen followed by reduction with zinc metal.
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b), (c), (d)

    (A) Malachite $\mathrm{CuCO_3\cdot Cu(OH)_2}$ on heating gives $\mathrm{CuO}$ (tenorite), not cuprite $\mathrm{Cu_2O}$. False.
    (B) Calamine $\mathrm{ZnCO_3} \xrightarrow{\Delta} \mathrm{ZnO}$ (zincite) $+ \mathrm{CO_2}$. True.
    (C) In the reverberatory furnace iron is removed as slag: $\mathrm{FeO + SiO_2 \to FeSiO_3}$. True.
    (D) Cyanide process: $\mathrm{4Ag + 8CN^- + O_2 + 2H_2O \to 4[Ag(CN)_2]^- + 4OH^-}$, then $\mathrm{2[Ag(CN)_2]^- + Zn \to [Zn(CN)_4]^{2-} + 2Ag}$. True.
    Answer (B), (C), (D).

  6. JEE Advanced 2023, Paper 1 · Question 48
    Match the reactions (in the given stoichiometry of the reactants) in List-I with one of their products given in List-II and choose the correct option.

    List-IList-II
    (P) $\mathrm{P_2O_3 + 3H_2O \to}$(1) $\mathrm{P(O)(OCH_3)Cl_2}$
    (Q) $\mathrm{P_4 + 3NaOH + 3H_2O \to}$(2) $\mathrm{H_3PO_3}$
    (R) $\mathrm{PCl_5 + CH_3COOH \to}$(3) $\mathrm{PH_3}$
    (S) $\mathrm{H_3PO_2 + 2H_2O + 4AgNO_3 \to}$(4) $\mathrm{POCl_3}$
    (5) $\mathrm{H_3PO_4}$

    1. P → 2; Q → 3; R → 1; S → 5
    2. P → 3; Q → 5; R → 4; S → 2
    3. P → 5; Q → 2; R → 1; S → 3
    4. P → 2; Q → 3; R → 4; S → 5
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    (P) $\mathrm{P_2O_3 + 3H_2O \to 2H_3PO_3}$ → (2).
    (Q) $\mathrm{P_4 + 3NaOH + 3H_2O \to PH_3 + 3NaH_2PO_2}$ → (3).
    (R) $\mathrm{PCl_5 + CH_3COOH \to CH_3COCl + POCl_3 + HCl}$ → (4).
    (S) $\mathrm{H_3PO_2 + 2H_2O + 4AgNO_3 \to H_3PO_4 + 4Ag + 4HNO_3}$ → (5).
    Answer (D).

  7. JEE Advanced 2023, Paper 2 · Question 42
    $\mathrm{H_2S}$ (5 moles) reacts completely with acidified aqueous potassium permanganate solution. In this reaction, the number of moles of water produced is x, and the number of moles of electrons involved is y. The value of (x + y) is ________.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 18

    $5\mathrm{H_2S} + 2\mathrm{MnO_4^-} + 6\mathrm{H^+} \to 5\mathrm{S} + 2\mathrm{Mn^{2+}} + 8\mathrm{H_2O}$.
    Water: $x = 8$ mol.
    Electrons: each S goes from $-2$ to $0$, $5\times2 = 10$ (equivalently $2\times5$ for Mn), so $y = 10$.
    $x + y = 18$.

  8. JEE Advanced 2023, Paper 2 · Question 44
    Consider the following molecules: $\mathrm{Br_3O_8}$, $\mathrm{F_2O}$, $\mathrm{H_2S_4O_6}$, $\mathrm{H_2S_5O_6}$, and $\mathrm{C_3O_2}$.
    Count the number of atoms existing in their zero oxidation state in each molecule.
    Their sum is ________.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 6

    $\mathrm{Br_3O_8}$ ($\mathrm{O_3Br{-}BrO_2{-}BrO_3}$): all Br positive (+6, +4, +6): 0.
    $\mathrm{F_2O}$: F $= -1$, O $= +2$: 0.
    $\mathrm{H_2S_4O_6}$ ($\mathrm{HO_3S{-}S{-}S{-}SO_3H}$): the two bridging S are 0: 2.
    $\mathrm{H_2S_5O_6}$: the three bridging S are 0: 3.
    $\mathrm{C_3O_2}$ ($\mathrm{O{=}C{=}C{=}C{=}O}$): the central C is 0: 1.
    Sum $= 0 + 0 + 2 + 3 + 1 = 6$.

  9. JEE Advanced 2022, Paper 1 · Question 40
    The treatment of an aqueous solution of 3.74 g of $\mathrm{Cu(NO_3)_2}$ with excess KI results in a brown solution along with the formation of a precipitate. Passing $\mathrm{H_2S}$ through this brown solution gives another precipitate X. The amount of X (in g) is ______.
    [Given: Atomic mass of H = 1, N = 14, O = 16, S = 32, K = 39, Cu = 63, I = 127]

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 0.31 to 0.33

    $M(\mathrm{Cu(NO_3)_2})=63+2(62)=187$; $\dfrac{3.74}{187}=0.02$ mol.
    $\mathrm{2Cu^{2+}+4I^-\to2CuI\downarrow+I_2}$; the $\mathrm{I_2}$ (0.01 mol) stays in solution as brown $\mathrm{I_3^-}$.
    $\mathrm{H_2S+I_2\to2HI+S\downarrow}$: X is sulphur, 0.01 mol $=0.32$ g.

  10. JEE Advanced 2022, Paper 1 · Question 47
    The electrochemical extraction of aluminum from bauxite ore involves

    1. the reaction of $\mathrm{Al_2O_3}$ with coke (C) at a temperature > 2500 °C.
    2. the neutralization of aluminate solution by passing $\mathrm{CO_2}$ gas to precipitate hydrated alumina ($\mathrm{Al_2O_3\cdot3H_2O}$).
    3. the dissolution of $\mathrm{Al_2O_3}$ in hot aqueous NaOH.
    4. the electrolysis of $\mathrm{Al_2O_3}$ mixed with $\mathrm{Na_3AlF_6}$ to give Al and $\mathrm{CO_2}$.
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b), (c), (d)

    Bayer process: bauxite is leached with hot aqueous NaOH, $\mathrm{Al_2O_3+2NaOH+3H_2O\to2Na[Al(OH)_4]}$ — (C).
    The aluminate solution is neutralised with $\mathrm{CO_2}$ to precipitate hydrated alumina, which is ignited to $\mathrm{Al_2O_3}$ — (B).
    Hall–Héroult: $\mathrm{Al_2O_3}$ dissolved in molten cryolite is electrolysed; Al forms at the cathode and the carbon anodes burn to $\mathrm{CO/CO_2}$ — (D).
    Carbon reduction of $\mathrm{Al_2O_3}$ is not used — (A) wrong.

  11. JEE Advanced 2022, Paper 1 · Question 52
    LIST-I contains compounds and LIST-II contains reactions

    LIST-ILIST-II
    (I) $\mathrm{H_2O_2}$(P) $\mathrm{Mg(HCO_3)_2+Ca(OH)_2\to}$
    (II) $\mathrm{Mg(OH)_2}$(Q) $\mathrm{BaO_2+H_2SO_4\to}$
    (III) $\mathrm{BaCl_2}$(R) $\mathrm{Ca(OH)_2+MgCl_2\to}$
    (IV) $\mathrm{CaCO_3}$(S) $\mathrm{BaO_2+HCl\to}$
    (T) $\mathrm{Ca(HCO_3)_2+Ca(OH)_2\to}$
    Match each compound in LIST-I with its formation reaction(s) in LIST-II, and choose the correct option

    1. I → Q; II → P; III → S; IV → R
    2. I → T; II → P; III → Q; IV → R
    3. I → T; II → R; III → Q; IV → P
    4. I → Q; II → R; III → S; IV → P
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    (Q) $\mathrm{BaO_2+H_2SO_4\to BaSO_4\downarrow+H_2O_2}$ — forms $\mathrm{H_2O_2}$ (I).
    (R) $\mathrm{Ca(OH)_2+MgCl_2\to Mg(OH)_2\downarrow+CaCl_2}$ — forms $\mathrm{Mg(OH)_2}$ (II).
    (S) $\mathrm{BaO_2+2HCl\to BaCl_2+H_2O_2}$ — forms $\mathrm{BaCl_2}$ (III).
    (P) $\mathrm{Mg(HCO_3)_2+2Ca(OH)_2\to2CaCO_3\downarrow+Mg(OH)_2\downarrow+2H_2O}$ — forms $\mathrm{CaCO_3}$ (IV). (T) also gives $\mathrm{CaCO_3}$, but no option pairs I with T correctly, since T gives no $\mathrm{H_2O_2}$.
    Only option (D) is consistent: I → Q, II → R, III → S, IV → P.

  12. JEE Advanced 2022, Paper 2 · Question 40
    The reaction of Xe and $\mathrm{O_2F_2}$ gives a Xe compound $\mathbf{P}$. The number of moles of HF produced by the complete hydrolysis of $1$ mol of $\mathbf{P}$ is ________.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 2 or 4 or 6

    $\mathrm{Xe + O_2F_2 \rightarrow XeF_2 + O_2}$, so $\mathbf{P} = \mathrm{XeF_2}$.
    Hydrolysis: $\mathrm{2XeF_2 + 2H_2O \rightarrow 2Xe + 4HF + O_2}$, i.e. $2$ mol HF per mol of $\mathbf{P}$.
    With excess $\mathrm{O_2F_2}$, $\mathrm{XeF_4}$ ($\mathrm{6XeF_4 + 12H_2O \rightarrow 4Xe + 2XeO_3 + 24HF + 3O_2}$, 4 HF) or $\mathrm{XeF_6}$ ($\mathrm{XeF_6 + 3H_2O \rightarrow XeO_3 + 6HF}$, 6 HF) can form.
    Answer: $2$ (the official key also accepts $4$ and $6$).

    Note: Official final key: 2 or 4 or 6 (any of these values is awarded marks).

  13. JEE Advanced 2022, Paper 2 · Question 48
    The correct option(s) related to the extraction of iron from its ore in the blast furnace operating in the temperature range $900 - 1500$ K is(are)

    1. Limestone is used to remove silicate impurity.
    2. Pig iron obtained from blast furnace contains about 4% carbon.
    3. Coke (C) converts $\mathrm{CO_2}$ to CO.
    4. Exhaust gases consist of $\mathrm{NO_2}$ and CO.
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a), (b), (c)

    (A) $\mathrm{CaCO_3 \rightarrow CaO + CO_2}$; $\mathrm{CaO + SiO_2 \rightarrow CaSiO_3}$ (slag): true.
    (B) Pig iron contains about 4% carbon: true.
    (C) $\mathrm{C + CO_2 \rightarrow 2CO}$ in the furnace: true.
    (D) The exhaust gases are mainly $\mathrm{CO}$, $\mathrm{CO_2}$ and $\mathrm{N_2}$, not $\mathrm{NO_2}$: false.
    Answer (A), (B), (C).

  14. JEE Advanced 2022, Paper 2 · Question 52
    The reaction of $\mathrm{HClO_3}$ with HCl gives a paramagnetic gas, which upon reaction with $\mathrm{O_3}$ produces

    1. $\mathrm{Cl_2O}$
    2. $\mathrm{ClO_2}$
    3. $\mathrm{Cl_2O_6}$
    4. $\mathrm{Cl_2O_7}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    $\mathrm{2HClO_3 + 2HCl \rightarrow 2ClO_2 + Cl_2 + 2H_2O}$; $\mathrm{ClO_2}$ (odd electron) is the paramagnetic gas.
    $\mathrm{2ClO_2 + 2O_3 \rightarrow Cl_2O_6 + 2O_2}$ → (C).

  15. JEE Advanced 2021, Paper 1 · Question 53
    The correct statement(s) related to the metal extraction processes is(are)

    1. A mixture of PbS and PbO undergoes self-reduction to produce Pb and $\mathrm{SO_2}$.
    2. In the extraction process of copper from copper pyrites, silica is added to produce copper silicate.
    3. Partial oxidation of sulphide ore of copper by roasting, followed by self-reduction produces blister copper.
    4. In cyanide process, zinc powder is utilized to precipitate gold from $\mathrm{Na[Au(CN)_2]}$.
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a), (c), (d)

    (A) $\mathrm{PbS + 2PbO \rightarrow 3Pb + SO_2}$: true.
    (B) Silica removes iron as slag, $\mathrm{FeO + SiO_2 \rightarrow FeSiO_3}$, not copper silicate: false.
    (C) $\mathrm{2Cu_2S + 3O_2 \rightarrow 2Cu_2O + 2SO_2}$, then $\mathrm{2Cu_2O + Cu_2S \rightarrow 6Cu + SO_2}$ gives blister copper: true.
    (D) $\mathrm{2Na[Au(CN)_2] + Zn \rightarrow Na_2[Zn(CN)_4] + 2Au}$: true.
    Answer (A), (C), (D).

  16. JEE Advanced 2021, Paper 2 · Question 44
    The correct statement(s) related to oxoacids of phosphorous is(are)

    1. Upon heating, $\mathrm{H_3PO_3}$ undergoes disproportionation reaction to produce $\mathrm{H_3PO_4}$ and $\mathrm{PH_3}$.
    2. While $\mathrm{H_3PO_3}$ can act as reducing agent, $\mathrm{H_3PO_4}$ cannot.
    3. $\mathrm{H_3PO_3}$ is a monobasic acid.
    4. The H atom of P–H bond in $\mathrm{H_3PO_3}$ is not ionizable in water.
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a), (b), (d)

    (A) $\mathrm{4H_3PO_3 \xrightarrow{\Delta} 3H_3PO_4 + PH_3}$ (P goes from +3 to +5 and $-3$): disproportionation ✓.
    (B) $\mathrm{H_3PO_3}$ has a P–H bond and P(+3), so it reduces; $\mathrm{H_3PO_4}$ (P(+5), no P–H) cannot ✓.
    (C) $\mathrm{H_3PO_3}$, $\mathrm{HPO(OH)_2}$, has two ionisable O–H groups: dibasic, not monobasic ✗.
    (D) The H on P is not ionisable ✓.
    Answer (A), (B), (D).

  17. JEE Advanced 2021, Paper 2 · Question 49
    A sample (5.6 g) containing iron is completely dissolved in cold dilute HCl to prepare a 250 mL of solution. Titration of 25.0 mL of this solution requires 12.5 mL of 0.03 M $\mathrm{KMnO_4}$ solution to reach the end point. Number of moles of $\mathrm{Fe^{2+}}$ present in 250 mL solution is $\mathbf{x} \times 10^{-2}$ (consider complete dissolution of $\mathrm{FeCl_2}$). The amount of iron present in the sample is $\mathbf{y}$% by weight.
    (Assume: $\mathrm{KMnO_4}$ reacts only with $\mathrm{Fe^{2+}}$ in the solution
    Use: Molar mass of iron as 56 g mol$^{-1}$)

    The value of $\mathbf{x}$ is ___.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 1.87 to 1.88

    $\mathrm{MnO_4^- + 5Fe^{2+} + 8H^+ \to Mn^{2+} + 5Fe^{3+} + 4H_2O}$.
    $\mathrm{KMnO_4}$ used $= 12.5 \times 0.03 = 0.375$ mmol, so $\mathrm{Fe^{2+}}$ in 25 mL $= 1.875$ mmol; in 250 mL $= 18.75$ mmol $= 1.875 \times 10^{-2}$ mol.
    $x = 1.875 \approx 1.87$ (or 1.88).

  18. JEE Advanced 2021, Paper 2 · Question 50
    A sample (5.6 g) containing iron is completely dissolved in cold dilute HCl to prepare a 250 mL of solution. Titration of 25.0 mL of this solution requires 12.5 mL of 0.03 M $\mathrm{KMnO_4}$ solution to reach the end point. Number of moles of $\mathrm{Fe^{2+}}$ present in 250 mL solution is $\mathbf{x} \times 10^{-2}$ (consider complete dissolution of $\mathrm{FeCl_2}$). The amount of iron present in the sample is $\mathbf{y}$% by weight.
    (Assume: $\mathrm{KMnO_4}$ reacts only with $\mathrm{Fe^{2+}}$ in the solution
    Use: Molar mass of iron as 56 g mol$^{-1}$)

    The value of $\mathbf{y}$ is ___.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 18.7 to 18.8

    $\mathrm{MnO_4^- + 5Fe^{2+} + 8H^+ \to Mn^{2+} + 5Fe^{3+} + 4H_2O}$.
    $\mathrm{KMnO_4}$ used $= 12.5 \times 0.03 = 0.375$ mmol, so $\mathrm{Fe^{2+}}$ in 25 mL $= 1.875$ mmol; in 250 mL $= 18.75$ mmol $= 1.875 \times 10^{-2}$ mol.
    Mass of iron $= 0.01875 \times 56 = 1.05$ g; $y = \dfrac{1.05}{5.6} \times 100 = 18.75$.

  19. JEE Advanced 2021, Paper 2 · Question 57
    Ozonolysis of $\mathrm{ClO_2}$ produces an oxide of chlorine. The average oxidation state of chlorine in this oxide is ___.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 6

    $\mathrm{2ClO_2 + 2O_3 \to Cl_2O_6 + 2O_2}$.
    In $\mathrm{Cl_2O_6}$: $2x = 12$, so the average oxidation state of Cl is $+6$.
