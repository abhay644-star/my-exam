  1. JEE Advanced 2025, Paper 2 · Question 40
    For the reaction sequence given below, the correct statement(s) is(are)

    1. $\mathbf{P}$ is optically active.
    2. $\mathbf{S}$ gives Bayer's test.
    3. $\mathbf{Q}$ gives effervescence with aq. $\mathrm{NaHCO_3}$.
    4. $\mathbf{R}$ is an alkyne.
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b), (c)

    The lactone is 4-benzyl-tetrahydropyran-2-one. $\mathrm{LiAlH_4}$ reduces it to the diol $\mathrm{HOCH_2CH_2CH(CH_2Ph)CH_2CH_2OH}$ = $\mathbf{P}$; both arms are identical, so P is achiral (A false).
    $\mathrm{CrO_3{-}H_2SO_4}$ oxidises both $\mathrm{CH_2OH}$ groups → $\mathrm{HOOCCH_2CH(CH_2Ph)CH_2COOH}$ = $\mathbf{Q}$, a dicarboxylic acid: effervescence with $\mathrm{NaHCO_3}$ (C true).
    Soda-lime decarboxylation removes both COOH → $\mathrm{(CH_3)_2CHCH_2Ph}$ = $\mathbf{R}$, an alkane side chain, not an alkyne (D false).
    Conc. $\mathrm{H_2SO_4}$ at 443 K dehydrates the diol to the diene $\mathbf{S}$, which decolourises Baeyer's reagent (B true).
    Answer (B), (C).

  2. JEE Advanced 2024, Paper 1 · Question 51
    List-I contains various reaction sequences and List-II contains different phenolic compounds. Match each entry in List-I with the appropriate entry in List-II and choose the correct option.

    1. P-2, Q-3, R-4, S-5
    2. P-2, Q-3, R-5, S-1
    3. P-3, Q-5, R-4, S-1
    4. P-3, Q-2, R-5, S-4
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    (P) Benzenesulfonic acid + molten NaOH, then $\mathrm{H_3O^+}$ → phenol; conc. $\mathrm{HNO_3}$ → 2,4,6-trinitrophenol (picric acid) → (3).
    (Q) Nitrobenzene → 1,3-dinitrobenzene → (Sn/HCl) benzene-1,3-diamine → bis-diazonium salt → ($\mathrm{H_2O}$) resorcinol → (nitration) 2,4,6-trinitroresorcinol → (5).
    (R) Resorcinol + conc. $\mathrm{H_2SO_4}$ sulfonates positions 4 and 6 (blocking groups); $\mathrm{HNO_3}$ then nitrates C-2; $\mathrm{H_3O^+}$, Δ removes the $\mathrm{SO_3H}$ groups: 2-nitroresorcinol → (4).
    (S) Toluene → benzoic acid → (nitration with heat) 3,5-dinitrobenzoic acid → ($\mathrm{SOCl_2}$, $\mathrm{NH_3}$) amide → (Hofmann, $\mathrm{Br_2}$/NaOH) 3,5-dinitroaniline → (diazotisation, $\mathrm{H_2O}$) 3,5-dinitrophenol → (1).
    P-3, Q-5, R-4, S-1 → (C).

  3. JEE Advanced 2023, Paper 1 · Question 41
    In the given reaction scheme, $\mathbf{P}$ is a phenyl alkyl ether, $\mathbf{Q}$ is an aromatic compound; $\mathbf{R}$ and $\mathbf{S}$ are the major products.

    $\displaystyle \mathbf{P} \xrightarrow{\text{HI}} \mathbf{Q} \xrightarrow[\text{(iii) }\mathrm{H_3O^+}]{\text{(i) NaOH; (ii) }\mathrm{CO_2}} \mathbf{R} \xrightarrow[\text{(ii) }\mathrm{H_3O^+}]{\text{(i) }\mathrm{(CH_3CO)_2O}} \mathbf{S}$
    The correct statement about $\mathbf{S}$ is

    1. It primarily inhibits noradrenaline degrading enzymes.
    2. It inhibits the synthesis of prostaglandin.
    3. It is a narcotic drug.
    4. It is ortho-acetylbenzoic acid.
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    HI cleaves the phenyl alkyl ether to phenol (Q).
    Kolbe reaction (NaOH, $\mathrm{CO_2}$, then acid) gives salicylic acid (R).
    Acetylation of the OH gives acetylsalicylic acid, aspirin (S): a non-narcotic analgesic that inhibits prostaglandin synthesis. It is 2-acetoxybenzoic acid, not ortho-acetylbenzoic acid.
    Answer (B).

  4. JEE Advanced 2022, Paper 1 · Question 43
    The weight percentage of hydrogen in $\mathbf{Q}$, formed in the following reaction sequence, is ______.
    [Given: Atomic mass of H = 1, C = 12, N = 14, O = 16, S = 32, Cl = 35]

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 1.3 to 1.32

    NaOH at 623 K, 300 atm (Dow process) converts chlorobenzene to phenol (via sodium phenoxide).
    Phenol with conc. $\mathrm{H_2SO_4}$ (sulphonation) and then conc. $\mathrm{HNO_3}$ gives picric acid, 2,4,6-trinitrophenol, $\mathrm{C_6H_3N_3O_7}$.
    $M=72+3+42+112=229$; %H $=\dfrac{3}{229}\times100\approx1.31$.

  5. JEE Advanced 2022, Paper 1 · Question 44
    If the reaction sequence given below is carried out with 15 moles of acetylene, the amount of the product $\mathbf{D}$ formed (in g) is ______.
    The yields of $\mathbf{A}$, $\mathbf{B}$, $\mathbf{C}$ and $\mathbf{D}$ are given in parentheses.
    [Given: Atomic mass of H = 1, C = 12, O = 16, Cl = 35]

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 135.8 to 136.2

    A: benzene by cyclic trimerisation; 15 mol acetylene → 5 mol × 80% = 4 mol.
    B: Friedel–Crafts with 1-chloropropane gives cumene (the carbocation rearranges to isopropyl); 50% → 2 mol.
    C: cumene process ($\mathrm{O_2}$, then $\mathrm{H_3O^+}$, acetone removed) gives phenol; 50% → 1 mol.
    D: acetylation with $\mathrm{CH_3COCl}$/pyridine gives phenyl acetate, $\mathrm{C_8H_8O_2}$, $M=136$; 100% → 136 g.
