  1. JEE Advanced 2026, Paper 1 · Question 33
    An ideal gas (0.5 mol), initially at 2 bar pressure, is compressed at a constant temperature of 600 K in two steps: first, against a constant external pressure of $P$ bar ($2 \lt P \lt 8$), and then against constant external pressure of 8 bar. At each step, the compression is stopped only when the pressure of the gas becomes equal to the external pressure. The total work done on the gas in these steps is $W$. Considering all possible values of $P$ ($2 \lt P \lt 8$) and taking the gas constant as $R$ (in J K$^{-1}$ mol$^{-1}$), the minimum value of $|W|$ (in J) is

    1. $207R$
    2. $600R$
    3. $630R$
    4. $900R$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    $nRT = 0.5 \times R \times 600 = 300R$.
    Step 1 (against $P$): $W_1 = P\left(\dfrac{nRT}{2} - \dfrac{nRT}{P}\right) = nRT\left(\dfrac{P}{2} - 1\right)$.
    Step 2 (against 8 bar): $W_2 = 8\left(\dfrac{nRT}{P} - \dfrac{nRT}{8}\right) = nRT\left(\dfrac{8}{P} - 1\right)$.
    $|W| = nRT\left(\dfrac{P}{2} + \dfrac{8}{P} - 2\right)$, minimised when $\dfrac{1}{2} = \dfrac{8}{P^2}$, i.e. $P = 4$ bar.
    $|W|_{\min} = 300R\,(2 + 2 - 2) = 600R$.

  2. JEE Advanced 2026, Paper 1 · Question 45
    List-I contains various physical/chemical processes, and List-II contains combinations of changes in enthalpy ($\Delta H$) and entropy ($\Delta S$). Match each entry in List-I to the appropriate entry in List-II, and choose the correct option.

    List-IList-II
    (P) Physisorption(1) $\Delta H \gt 0$ and $\Delta S \gt 0$
    (Q) Diamond $\longrightarrow$ Graphite(2) $\Delta H \lt 0$ and $\Delta S \lt 0$
    (R) Denaturation of protein(3) $\Delta H \lt 0$ and $\Delta S = 0$
    (S) Propene $\longrightarrow$ Cyclopropane(4) $\Delta H \gt 0$ and $\Delta S \lt 0$
    (5) $\Delta H \lt 0$ and $\Delta S \gt 0$

    1. P $\rightarrow$ 2; Q $\rightarrow$ 3; R $\rightarrow$ 5; S $\rightarrow$ 4
    2. P $\rightarrow$ 4; Q $\rightarrow$ 3; R $\rightarrow$ 5; S $\rightarrow$ 1
    3. P $\rightarrow$ 2; Q $\rightarrow$ 5; R $\rightarrow$ 1; S $\rightarrow$ 4
    4. P $\rightarrow$ 2; Q $\rightarrow$ 5; R $\rightarrow$ 1; S $\rightarrow$ 3
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    P: In physisorption, gas molecules become fixed on a surface. It is exothermic and lowers entropy: (2).
    Q: Diamond → graphite is slightly exothermic ($\Delta H \approx -1.9$ kJ mol$^{-1}$). Graphite has the higher molar entropy, so $\Delta S \gt 0$: (5).
    R: Protein denaturation breaks many H-bonds ($\Delta H \gt 0$) and unfolds an ordered chain ($\Delta S \gt 0$): (1).
    S: Propene → cyclopropane goes to a strained ring ($\Delta H \gt 0$) and loses rotational freedom on ring closure ($\Delta S \lt 0$): (4).
    So P→2, Q→5, R→1, S→4.

  3. JEE Advanced 2025, Paper 1 · Question 42
    Molar volume ($V_\mathrm{m}$) of a van der Waals gas can be calculated by expressing the van der Waals equation as a cubic equation with $V_\mathrm{m}$ as the variable. The ratio (in mol dm$^{-3}$) of the coefficient of $V_\mathrm{m}^2$ to the coefficient of $V_\mathrm{m}$ for a gas having van der Waals constants $a = 6.0$ dm$^6$ atm mol$^{-2}$ and $b = 0.060$ dm$^3$ mol$^{-1}$ at $300$ K and $300$ atm is ______.
    Use: Universal gas constant ($R$) $= 0.082$ dm$^3$ atm mol$^{-1}$ K$^{-1}$

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: -7.2 to -7

    $\left(P + \dfrac{a}{V_\mathrm{m}^2}\right)(V_\mathrm{m} - b) = RT$; multiplying by $V_\mathrm{m}^2$: $PV_\mathrm{m}^3 - (Pb + RT)V_\mathrm{m}^2 + aV_\mathrm{m} - ab = 0$.
    Ratio $= \dfrac{-(Pb + RT)}{a}$.
    $Pb = 300 \times 0.060 = 18$; $RT = 0.082 \times 300 = 24.6$.
    Ratio $= -\dfrac{42.6}{6.0} = -7.1$ mol dm$^{-3}$.

  4. JEE Advanced 2025, Paper 1 · Question 43
    Considering ideal gas behavior, the expansion work done (in kJ) when $144$ g of water is electrolyzed completely under constant pressure at $300$ K is ______.
    Use: Universal gas constant ($R$) $= 8.3$ J K$^{-1}$ mol$^{-1}$; Atomic mass (in amu): H $= 1$, O $= 16$

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: -29.95 to -29.8 or 29.8 to 29.95

    $144$ g water $= 8$ mol. $2\mathrm{H_2O(l)} \rightarrow 2\mathrm{H_2(g)} + \mathrm{O_2(g)}$ gives $8$ mol $\mathrm{H_2}$ + $4$ mol $\mathrm{O_2}$ = $12$ mol gas (liquid volume negligible).
    $w = -P\Delta V = -\Delta n_gRT = -12 \times 8.3 \times 300 = -29880$ J.
    $w = -29.88$ kJ (work done on the system by the IUPAC sign convention; the magnitude of work done by the gas is $29.88$ kJ).
    The official final key accepts $-29.88$ or $+29.88$ (within the given ranges).

    Note: Official final key: [-29.95 to -29.8] or [29.8 to 29.95] (either range is awarded marks).

  5. JEE Advanced 2024, Paper 1 · Question 42
    Consider the following volume$-$temperature (V$-$T) diagram for the expansion of 5 moles of an ideal monoatomic gas.
    Considering only P-V work is involved, the total change in enthalpy (in Joule) for the transformation of state in the sequence X$\to$Y$\to$Z is ______.
    [Use the given data: Molar heat capacity of the gas for the given temperature range, $C_{V,m} = 12$ J K$^{-1}$ mol$^{-1}$ and gas constant, $R = 8.3$ J K$^{-1}$ mol$^{-1}$]

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 8120 or 8000 or 8300

    For an ideal gas $H$ depends only on $T$.
    X → Y is at constant $T$ (335 K): $\Delta H = 0$.
    Y → Z: $T$ rises from 335 K to 415 K, $\Delta T = 80$ K. $C_{p,m} = C_{V,m} + R = 12 + 8.3 = 20.3$ J K$^{-1}$ mol$^{-1}$.
    $\Delta H = nC_{p,m}\Delta T = 5 \times 20.3 \times 80 = 8120$ J.
    The official key also accepts 8000 and 8300 (for example $C_{p,m} = \dfrac52R = 20.75$ gives 8300).

    Note: Official final key: 8000 or 8120 or 8300 (any of these values is awarded marks).

  6. JEE Advanced 2023, Paper 1 · Question 45
    One mole of an ideal monoatomic gas undergoes two reversible processes ($\mathrm{A \to B}$ and $\mathrm{B \to C}$) as shown in the given figure:
    $\mathrm{A \to B}$ is an adiabatic process. If the total heat absorbed in the entire process ($\mathrm{A \to B}$ and $\mathrm{B \to C}$) is $RT_2\ln10$, the value of $2\log V_3$ is ______.
    [Use, molar heat capacity of the gas at constant pressure, $C_{\mathrm{p,m}} = \dfrac52R$]

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 7

    $\mathrm{A \to B}$ is adiabatic ($q = 0$), so all the heat is absorbed in the isothermal $\mathrm{B \to C}$: $RT_2\ln\dfrac{V_3}{V_2} = RT_2\ln10 \Rightarrow V_3 = 10V_2$.
    Adiabatic, $\gamma = \dfrac53$: $T_1V_1^{2/3} = T_2V_2^{2/3} \Rightarrow 600\times10^{2/3} = 60\,V_2^{2/3} \Rightarrow V_2^{2/3} = 10^{5/3} \Rightarrow V_2 = 10^{5/2}$.
    $V_3 = 10^{7/2}$, so $2\log V_3 = 7$.

  7. JEE Advanced 2023, Paper 2 · Question 48
    The entropy versus temperature plot for phases α and β at 1 bar pressure is given. $S_\mathrm{T}$ and $S_0$ are entropies of the phases at temperatures T and 0 K, respectively.
    The transition temperature for α to β phase change is 600 K and $C_{\mathrm{p},\beta} - C_{\mathrm{p},\alpha} = 1$ J mol$^{-1}$ K$^{-1}$. Assume $(C_{\mathrm{p},\beta} - C_{\mathrm{p},\alpha})$ is independent of temperature in the range of 200 to 700 K. $C_{\mathrm{p},\alpha}$ and $C_{\mathrm{p},\beta}$ are heat capacities of α and β phases, respectively.

    The value of entropy change, $\mathrm{S_\beta - S_\alpha}$ (in J mol$^{-1}$ K$^{-1}$), at 300 K is ________.
    [Use: ln 2 = 0.69. Given: $\mathrm{S_\beta - S_\alpha} = 0$ at 0 K]

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 0.3 to 0.32

    From the graph at 600 K: $S_\mathrm{T} - S_0 = 6$ (β) and 5 (α); with $\mathrm{S_\beta - S_\alpha} = 0$ at 0 K, $\Delta S(600) = 1$ J mol$^{-1}$ K$^{-1}$.
    $\dfrac{d(\Delta S)}{dT} = \dfrac{\Delta C_p}{T}$, so $\Delta S(300) = \Delta S(600) + \Delta C_p\ln\dfrac{300}{600} = 1 - 0.69 = 0.31$ J mol$^{-1}$ K$^{-1}$.

  8. JEE Advanced 2023, Paper 2 · Question 49
    The entropy versus temperature plot for phases α and β at 1 bar pressure is given. $S_\mathrm{T}$ and $S_0$ are entropies of the phases at temperatures T and 0 K, respectively.
    The transition temperature for α to β phase change is 600 K and $C_{\mathrm{p},\beta} - C_{\mathrm{p},\alpha} = 1$ J mol$^{-1}$ K$^{-1}$. Assume $(C_{\mathrm{p},\beta} - C_{\mathrm{p},\alpha})$ is independent of temperature in the range of 200 to 700 K. $C_{\mathrm{p},\alpha}$ and $C_{\mathrm{p},\beta}$ are heat capacities of α and β phases, respectively.

    The value of enthalpy change, $\mathrm{H_\beta - H_\alpha}$ (in J mol$^{-1}$), at 300 K is ________.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 300

    At the transition temperature the phases are in equilibrium: $\Delta H(600) = T\Delta S = 600\times1 = 600$ J mol$^{-1}$.
    Kirchhoff: $\Delta H(300) = \Delta H(600) + \Delta C_p(300 - 600) = 600 - 300 = 300$ J mol$^{-1}$.

  9. JEE Advanced 2022, Paper 1 · Question 37
    2 mol of $\mathrm{Hg(g)}$ is combusted in a fixed volume bomb calorimeter with excess of $\mathrm{O_2}$ at 298 K and 1 atm into $\mathrm{HgO(s)}$. During the reaction, temperature increases from 298.0 K to 312.8 K. If heat capacity of the bomb calorimeter and enthalpy of formation of $\mathrm{Hg(g)}$ are $20.00\ \mathrm{kJ\,K^{-1}}$ and $61.32\ \mathrm{kJ\,mol^{-1}}$ at 298 K, respectively, the calculated standard molar enthalpy of formation of $\mathrm{HgO(s)}$ at 298 K is $X\ \mathrm{kJ\,mol^{-1}}$. The value of $|X|$ is ______.
    [Given: Gas constant $R=8.3\ \mathrm{J\,K^{-1}\,mol^{-1}}$]

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 89 to 91

    Heat released at constant volume: $20.00\times(312.8-298.0)=296$ kJ, so for $\mathrm{2Hg(g)+O_2(g)\to2HgO(s)}$, $\Delta U=-296$ kJ.
    $\Delta n_g=0-3=-3$: $\Delta H=\Delta U+\Delta n_gRT=-296-3\times8.3\times10^{-3}\times298=-303.42$ kJ.
    $\Delta H=2\Delta_fH(\mathrm{HgO})-2\Delta_fH(\mathrm{Hg,g})\Rightarrow2\Delta_fH(\mathrm{HgO})=-303.42+2(61.32)=-180.78$ kJ.
    $\Delta_fH(\mathrm{HgO})=-90.39\ \mathrm{kJ\,mol^{-1}}$, so $|X|\approx90.39$.

  10. JEE Advanced 2022, Paper 2 · Question 46
    The correct option(s) about entropy (S) is(are)
    [R = gas constant, F = Faraday constant, T = Temperature]

    1. For the reaction, $\mathrm{M}(s) + 2\mathrm{H^+}(aq) \rightarrow \mathrm{H_2}(g) + \mathrm{M^{2+}}(aq)$, if $\dfrac{dE_{cell}}{dT} = \dfrac{R}{F}$, then the entropy change of the reaction is R (assume that entropy and internal energy changes are temperature independent).
    2. The cell reaction, $\mathrm{Pt}(s)\,|\,\mathrm{H_2}(g, 1\text{ bar})\,|\,\mathrm{H^+}(aq, 0.01\text{ M})\,\|\,\mathrm{H^+}(aq, 0.1\text{ M})\,|\,\mathrm{H_2}(g, 1\text{ bar})\,|\,\mathrm{Pt}(s)$, is an entropy driven process.
    3. For racemization of an optically active compound, $\Delta S > 0$.
    4. $\Delta S > 0$, for $\mathrm{[Ni(H_2O)_6]^{2+} + 3\,en \rightarrow [Ni(en)_3]^{2+} + 6H_2O}$ (where en = ethylenediamine).
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b), (c), (d)

    (A) $\Delta S = nF\dfrac{dE_\text{cell}}{dT} = 2F\cdot\dfrac RF = 2R$, not $R$: false.
    (B) A hydrogen concentration cell has $\Delta H = 0$, so $\Delta G = -T\Delta S < 0$ is driven entirely by entropy: true.
    (C) Racemisation converts one enantiomer into a 50:50 mixture; the entropy of mixing makes $\Delta S > 0$: true.
    (D) Chelation: 4 particles on the left give 7 on the right, so $\Delta S > 0$: true.
    Answer (B), (C), (D).

  11. JEE Advanced 2021, Paper 1 · Question 45
    For the reaction, $\mathbf{X}(s) \rightleftharpoons \mathbf{Y}(s) + \mathbf{Z}(g)$, the plot of $\ln\dfrac{p_\mathbf{Z}}{p^\ominus}$ versus $\dfrac{10^4}{T}$ is given below (in solid line), where $p_\mathbf{Z}$ is the pressure (in bar) of the gas $\mathbf{Z}$ at temperature $T$ and $p^\ominus = 1$ bar.
    (Given, $\dfrac{d(\ln K)}{d\left(\frac1T\right)} = -\dfrac{\Delta H^\ominus}{R}$, where the equilibrium constant, $K = \dfrac{p_\mathbf{Z}}{p^\ominus}$ and the gas constant, $R = 8.314$ J K$^{-1}$ mol$^{-1}$)

    The value of standard enthalpy, $\Delta H^\ominus$ (in kJ mol$^{-1}$) for the given reaction is ___.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 166.2 to 166.3

    From the graph, $\ln K = -3$ at $\dfrac{10^4}{T} = 10$ and $\ln K = -7$ at $\dfrac{10^4}{T} = 12$.
    Slope $\dfrac{d\ln K}{d(1/T)} = \dfrac{-7 - (-3)}{(12 - 10)\times10^{-4}} = -2 \times 10^4$ K.
    $\Delta H^\ominus = 2 \times 10^4 \times 8.314 = 166280$ J mol$^{-1} \approx 166.28$ kJ mol$^{-1}$.

  12. JEE Advanced 2021, Paper 1 · Question 46
    For the reaction, $\mathbf{X}(s) \rightleftharpoons \mathbf{Y}(s) + \mathbf{Z}(g)$, the plot of $\ln\dfrac{p_\mathbf{Z}}{p^\ominus}$ versus $\dfrac{10^4}{T}$ is given below (in solid line), where $p_\mathbf{Z}$ is the pressure (in bar) of the gas $\mathbf{Z}$ at temperature $T$ and $p^\ominus = 1$ bar.
    (Given, $\dfrac{d(\ln K)}{d\left(\frac1T\right)} = -\dfrac{\Delta H^\ominus}{R}$, where the equilibrium constant, $K = \dfrac{p_\mathbf{Z}}{p^\ominus}$ and the gas constant, $R = 8.314$ J K$^{-1}$ mol$^{-1}$)

    The value of $\Delta S^\ominus$ (in J K$^{-1}$ mol$^{-1}$) for the given reaction, at 1000 K is ___.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 141 to 142

    At 1000 K, $\dfrac{10^4}{T} = 10$ and $\ln K = -3$.
    $\Delta G^\ominus = -RT\ln K = 8.314 \times 1000 \times 3 = 24942$ J mol$^{-1}$.
    From the slope, $\Delta H^\ominus = 2\times10^4 \times 8.314 = 166280$ J mol$^{-1}$.
    $\Delta S^\ominus = \dfrac{\Delta H^\ominus - \Delta G^\ominus}{T} = \dfrac{166280 - 24942}{1000} \approx 141.34$ J K$^{-1}$ mol$^{-1}$.

  13. JEE Advanced 2021, Paper 1 · Question 52
    An ideal gas undergoes a reversible isothermal expansion from state I to state II followed by a reversible adiabatic expansion from state II to state III. The correct plot(s) representing the changes from state I to state III is(are)
    ($p$: pressure, $V$: volume, $T$: temperature, $H$: enthalpy, $S$: entropy)

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a), (b), (d)

    I → II (isothermal expansion): $T$ and $H$ constant, $p$ falls, $V$ and $S$ increase.
    II → III (reversible adiabatic expansion): $S$ constant, $T$ and $H$ fall, $p$ falls more steeply than on the isotherm.
    (A) $p$–$V$: hyperbola-like fall, then a steeper fall: correct.
    (B) $p$–$T$: vertical drop in $p$ at constant $T$, then both $p$ and $T$ fall: correct.
    (C) $H$–$S$: II → III should be a vertical fall in $H$, not a rise: wrong.
    (D) $T$–$S$: horizontal (constant $T$, $S$ up), then vertical drop in $T$: correct.
    Answer (A), (B), (D).

  14. JEE Advanced 2021, Paper 2 · Question 55
    One mole of an ideal gas at 900 K, undergoes two reversible processes, $\mathbf{I}$ followed by $\mathbf{II}$, as shown below. If the work done by the gas in the two processes are same, the value of $\ln\dfrac{V_3}{V_2}$ is ___.
    ($U$: internal energy, $S$: entropy, $p$: pressure, $V$: volume, $R$: gas constant)
    (Given: molar heat capacity at constant volume, $C_{V,m}$ of the gas is $\dfrac52R$)

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 10

    $U = \dfrac52RT$, so $\dfrac{U}{R} = 2.5T$: $T_1 = 900$ K (consistent) and $T_2 = \dfrac{450}{2.5} = 180$ K.
    Process I is at constant $S$ (reversible adiabatic): $w_\text{I} = -\Delta U = R(2250 - 450) = 1800R$.
    Process II is at constant $U$, i.e. isothermal at 180 K: $w_\text{II} = RT_2\ln\dfrac{V_3}{V_2} = 180R\ln\dfrac{V_3}{V_2}$.
    Equal work: $\ln\dfrac{V_3}{V_2} = \dfrac{1800}{180} = 10$.
