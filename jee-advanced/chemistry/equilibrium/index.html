  1. JEE Advanced 2026, Paper 2 · Question 47
    At a given temperature, 0.45 g of acetic acid in 50 mL of water is shaken with 1.0 g of charcoal and the pH of the resulting solution is 3.0. Assume, the adsorption of acetic acid from the aqueous solution by charcoal follows Freundlich isotherm,

    $\displaystyle \frac{x}{m} = kC^{1/n}$
    If the plot of $\log_{10}(x/m)$ against $\log_{10}C$ gives a straight line with slope 1, the value of $k$ in L mol$^{-1}$ is ____.

    Given: The molar mass of acetic acid is 60 g mol$^{-1}$.
    The acid dissociation constant of acetic acid is $1.0\times10^{-5}$ at the given temperature.
    $x$ is the mass (in grams) of acetic acid adsorbed.
    $m$ is the mass (in grams) of charcoal.
    $C$ is the equilibrium concentration of acetic acid in the solution after the adsorption is complete.
    $k$ and $n$ are constants for acetic acid–charcoal system at the given temperature.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 1.45 to 1.52

    pH 3: $[\mathrm{H^+}] = 10^{-3} = \sqrt{K_aC}$ → $C = \dfrac{10^{-6}}{10^{-5}} = 0.10$ mol L$^{-1}$.
    Initial: $\dfrac{0.45}{60} = 7.5\times10^{-3}$ mol in 0.050 L = 0.15 mol L$^{-1}$.
    Adsorbed: $(0.15-0.10)\times0.050 = 2.5\times10^{-3}$ mol = 0.15 g, so $x/m = 0.15/1.0 = 0.15$.
    Slope $1/n = 1$ → $x/m = kC$ → $k = \dfrac{0.15}{0.10} = 1.5$ L mol$^{-1}$.

  2. JEE Advanced 2025, Paper 1 · Question 41
    At $25\ {}^\circ$C, the concentration of $\mathrm{H^+}$ ions in $1.00\times10^{-3}$ M aqueous solution of a weak monobasic acid having acid dissociation constant ($K_a$) of $4.00\times10^{-11}$ is $\boldsymbol{X}\times10^{-7}$ M. The value of $\boldsymbol{X}$ is ______.
    Use: Ionic product of water ($K_w$) $= 1.00\times10^{-14}$ at $25\ {}^\circ$C

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 2.2 to 2.3

    The acid is so weak that water's own ionisation matters. With $[\mathrm{HA}] \approx C$: $[\mathrm{H^+}] = [\mathrm{A^-}] + [\mathrm{OH^-}] = \dfrac{K_aC}{[\mathrm{H^+}]} + \dfrac{K_w}{[\mathrm{H^+}]}$.
    $[\mathrm{H^+}]^2 = K_aC + K_w = 4.00\times10^{-14} + 1.00\times10^{-14} = 5.00\times10^{-14}$.
    $[\mathrm{H^+}] = 2.24\times10^{-7}$ M, so $X = 2.24$.

  3. JEE Advanced 2025, Paper 1 · Question 46
    The correct match of the group reagents in List-I for precipitating the metal ion given in List-II from solutions, is

    List-IList-II
    (P) Passing $\mathrm{H_2S}$ in the presence of $\mathrm{NH_4OH}$(1) $\mathrm{Cu^{2+}}$
    (Q) $\mathrm{(NH_4)_2CO_3}$ in the presence of $\mathrm{NH_4OH}$(2) $\mathrm{Al^{3+}}$
    (R) $\mathrm{NH_4OH}$ in the presence of $\mathrm{NH_4Cl}$(3) $\mathrm{Mn^{2+}}$
    (S) Passing $\mathrm{H_2S}$ in the presence of dilute HCl(4) $\mathrm{Ba^{2+}}$
    (5) $\mathrm{Mg^{2+}}$

    1. P $\rightarrow$ 3; Q $\rightarrow$ 4; R $\rightarrow$ 2; S $\rightarrow$ 1
    2. P $\rightarrow$ 4; Q $\rightarrow$ 2; R $\rightarrow$ 3; S $\rightarrow$ 1
    3. P $\rightarrow$ 3; Q $\rightarrow$ 4; R $\rightarrow$ 1; S $\rightarrow$ 5
    4. P $\rightarrow$ 5; Q $\rightarrow$ 3; R $\rightarrow$ 2; S $\rightarrow$ 4
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    (P) $\mathrm{H_2S}$ in ammoniacal medium is the group IV reagent: MnS precipitates → (3).
    (Q) $\mathrm{(NH_4)_2CO_3}$ with $\mathrm{NH_4OH}$ is the group V reagent: $\mathrm{BaCO_3}$ → (4).
    (R) $\mathrm{NH_4OH}$/$\mathrm{NH_4Cl}$ is the group III reagent: $\mathrm{Al(OH)_3}$ → (2).
    (S) $\mathrm{H_2S}$ in dilute HCl is the group II reagent: CuS → (1).
    ($\mathrm{Mg^{2+}}$ is group VI and is not precipitated by any of these.)
    Match: P→3, Q→4, R→2, S→1 → (A).

  4. JEE Advanced 2025, Paper 2 · Question 42
    The solubility of barium iodate in an aqueous solution prepared by mixing 200 mL of 0.010 M barium nitrate with 100 mL of 0.10 M sodium iodate is $\boldsymbol{X}\times10^{-6}$ mol dm$^{-3}$. The value of $\boldsymbol{X}$ is ________.
    Use: Solubility product constant ($K_{sp}$) of barium iodate $=1.58\times10^{-9}$

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 3.85 to 4.15

    $\mathrm{Ba^{2+}}$: $200\times0.010=2$ mmol; $\mathrm{IO_3^-}$: $100\times0.10=10$ mmol; total volume 300 mL.
    $\mathrm{Ba(IO_3)_2}$ precipitates: 2 mmol $\mathrm{Ba^{2+}}$ uses 4 mmol $\mathrm{IO_3^-}$, leaving 6 mmol in 300 mL, so $[\mathrm{IO_3^-}]=0.020$ M.
    Solubility $s=\dfrac{K_{sp}}{[\mathrm{IO_3^-}]^2}=\dfrac{1.58\times10^{-9}}{4\times10^{-4}}=3.95\times10^{-6}$ mol dm$^{-3}$.
    $X=3.95$.

  5. JEE Advanced 2023, Paper 1 · Question 40
    On decreasing the $p$H from 7 to 2, the solubility of a sparingly soluble salt (MX) of a weak acid (HX) increased from $10^{-4}\ \text{mol L}^{-1}$ to $10^{-3}\ \text{mol L}^{-1}$. The $p\mathrm{K_a}$ of HX is

    1. 3
    2. 4
    3. 5
    4. 2
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    With hydrolysis of $\mathrm{X^-}$ by acid: $s^2 = K_{sp}\left(1 + \dfrac{[\mathrm{H^+}]}{K_a}\right)$.
    At pH 7 the bracket is $\approx 1$, so $K_{sp} = 10^{-8}$.
    At pH 2: $10^{-6} = 10^{-8}\left(1 + \dfrac{10^{-2}}{K_a}\right) \Rightarrow \dfrac{10^{-2}}{K_a} = 99 \Rightarrow K_a \approx 10^{-4}$.
    $p\mathrm{K_a} = 4$. Answer (B).

  6. JEE Advanced 2023, Paper 1 · Question 46
    In a one-litre flask, 6 moles of A undergoes the reaction $\mathrm{A(g) \rightleftharpoons P(g)}$. The progress of product formation at two temperatures (in Kelvin), $T_1$ and $T_2$, is shown in the figure:
    If $T_1 = 2T_2$ and $\left(\Delta G_2^{\ominus} - \Delta G_1^{\ominus}\right) = RT_2\ln x$, then the value of x is ______.
    [$\Delta G_1^{\ominus}$ and $\Delta G_2^{\ominus}$ are standard Gibb’s free energy change for the reaction at temperatures $T_1$ and $T_2$, respectively.]

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 8

    At $T_1$: $[\mathrm P] = 4$, $[\mathrm A] = 2$, $K_1 = 2$. At $T_2$: $[\mathrm P] = 2$, $[\mathrm A] = 4$, $K_2 = \dfrac12$.
    $\Delta G_1^{\ominus} = -RT_1\ln2 = -2RT_2\ln2$; $\Delta G_2^{\ominus} = -RT_2\ln\dfrac12 = RT_2\ln2$.
    $\Delta G_2^{\ominus} - \Delta G_1^{\ominus} = 3RT_2\ln2 = RT_2\ln8$, so $x = 8$.

  7. JEE Advanced 2022, Paper 1 · Question 39
    A solution is prepared by mixing 0.01 mol each of $\mathrm{H_2CO_3}$, $\mathrm{NaHCO_3}$, $\mathrm{Na_2CO_3}$, and $\mathrm{NaOH}$ in 100 mL of water. pH of the resulting solution is ______.
    [Given: $pK_{a1}$ and $pK_{a2}$ of $\mathrm{H_2CO_3}$ are 6.37 and 10.32, respectively; $\log2=0.30$]

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 10 to 10.04

    $\mathrm{NaOH}$ neutralises $\mathrm{H_2CO_3}$: $\mathrm{H_2CO_3+OH^-\to HCO_3^-+H_2O}$ (0.01 mol each).
    Left: $\mathrm{HCO_3^-}$ 0.02 mol and $\mathrm{CO_3^{2-}}$ 0.01 mol — a buffer of the second dissociation.
    $\mathrm{pH}=pK_{a2}+\log\dfrac{[\mathrm{CO_3^{2-}}]}{[\mathrm{HCO_3^-}]}=10.32+\log\dfrac{0.01}{0.02}=10.32-0.30=10.02$.

  8. JEE Advanced 2022, Paper 2 · Question 37
    Concentration of $\mathrm{H_2SO_4}$ and $\mathrm{Na_2SO_4}$ in a solution is $1$ M and $1.8\times10^{-2}$ M, respectively. Molar solubility of $\mathrm{PbSO_4}$ in the same solution is $\mathrm{X}\times10^{-\mathrm{Y}}$ M (expressed in scientific notation). The value of Y is ________.
    [Given: Solubility product of $\mathrm{PbSO_4}$ $(K_{sp}) = 1.6\times10^{-8}$. For $\mathrm{H_2SO_4}$, $K_{a1}$ is very large and $K_{a2} = 1.2\times10^{-2}$]

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 6

    First ionisation is complete: $[\mathrm{H^+}] \approx 1$ M, $[\mathrm{HSO_4^-}] \approx 1$ M.
    The strong acid suppresses $\mathrm{SO_4^{2-}}$: $\mathrm{SO_4^{2-}}$ from $\mathrm{Na_2SO_4}$ largely takes up $\mathrm{H^+}$, and $[\mathrm{SO_4^{2-}}] = K_{a2}\dfrac{[\mathrm{HSO_4^-}]}{[\mathrm{H^+}]} \approx 1.2\times10^{-2}\times\dfrac{1.018}{0.982} \approx 1.2\times10^{-2}$ M.
    $s = \dfrac{K_{sp}}{[\mathrm{SO_4^{2-}}]} \approx \dfrac{1.6\times10^{-8}}{1.2\times10^{-2}} \approx 1.3\times10^{-6}$ M.
    Y $= 6$.

  9. JEE Advanced 2021, Paper 1 · Question 54
    A mixture of two salts is used to prepare a solution $\mathbf{S}$, which gives the following results:
    The correct option(s) for the salt mixture is(are)

    1. $\mathrm{Pb(NO_3)_2}$ and $\mathrm{Zn(NO_3)_2}$
    2. $\mathrm{Pb(NO_3)_2}$ and $\mathrm{Bi(NO_3)_3}$
    3. $\mathrm{AgNO_3}$ and $\mathrm{Bi(NO_3)_3}$
    4. $\mathrm{Pb(NO_3)_2}$ and $\mathrm{Hg(NO_3)_2}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a), (b)

    Both reagents must give only white precipitates.
    (A) Dilute NaOH: $\mathrm{Pb(OH)_2}$ and $\mathrm{Zn(OH)_2}$, both white; dilute HCl: $\mathrm{PbCl_2}$, white: fits.
    (B) Dilute NaOH: $\mathrm{Pb(OH)_2}$ and $\mathrm{Bi(OH)_3}$, both white; dilute HCl: $\mathrm{PbCl_2}$ and BiOCl (from hydrolysis of $\mathrm{BiCl_3}$), both white: fits.
    (C) NaOH gives brown $\mathrm{Ag_2O}$: no.
    (D) NaOH gives yellow HgO: no.
    Answer (A), (B).
