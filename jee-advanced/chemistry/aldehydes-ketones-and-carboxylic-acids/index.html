  1. JEE Advanced 2026, Paper 1 · Question 43
    The sum of total number of carbonyl groups ( >C=O ) present in the major products X and Y in the following reactions is ____.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 4

    The first compound has both $\mathrm{CO_2H}$ groups β to the ketone, so heating gives double decarboxylation.
    X = $\mathrm{CH_3CH_2COCH_2CH_3}$ (pentan-3-one): 1 C=O.
    In the second compound the $\mathrm{CO_2H}$ groups are γ to the ketone, so no decarboxylation occurs. They are cis-1,2 (succinic-acid type), so heating removes water and forms the cyclic anhydride.
    Y = the fused bicyclic keto-anhydride: 1 ketone C=O + 2 anhydride C=O = 3.
    Total = 1 + 3 = 4.

  2. JEE Advanced 2026, Paper 1 · Question 47
    The List-II contains products obtained from the reaction of compounds in List-I with $\mathrm{O_3/Zn\text{-}H_2O}$ followed by cyclization (via more stable enolate) in the presence of aqueous NaOH. Match each entry in List-I with appropriate entry in List-II and choose the correct option.

    1. P $\longrightarrow$ 2; Q $\longrightarrow$ 4; R $\longrightarrow$ 1; S $\longrightarrow$ 3
    2. P $\longrightarrow$ 3; Q $\longrightarrow$ 4; R $\longrightarrow$ 5; S $\longrightarrow$ 2
    3. P $\longrightarrow$ 2; Q $\longrightarrow$ 1; R $\longrightarrow$ 5; S $\longrightarrow$ 3
    4. P $\longrightarrow$ 3; Q $\longrightarrow$ 5; R $\longrightarrow$ 4; S $\longrightarrow$ 2
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    Ozonolysis cleaves the ring-fusion C=C, giving a 10-membered cyclic 1,6-dione.
    Transannular aldol: the more substituted enolate forms (at the α-CH bearing $\mathrm{CH_3}$) and attacks the other C=O, forming a new bicyclic β-hydroxy ketone. The enolate carbon becomes a quaternary C with $\mathrm{CH_3}$, next to the remaining C=O.
    P (6-6; the two $\mathrm{CH_3}$ groups sit next to different fusion carbons, one in each ring): the CH($\mathrm{CH_3}$) enolate attacks the C=O four carbons away, closing a 5-membered ring and leaving a 7-membered ring. Product: a 5-7 system with the ring CH($\mathrm{CH_3}$) next to C–OH in the 7-ring (2).
    Q (7-5): the same type of closure gives two 6-membered rings. Product: the decalone with ring CH($\mathrm{CH_3}$) next to C–OH in the ketone ring (1).
    R (7-5; both $\mathrm{CH_3}$ groups on the 7-ring): gives a 6-6 system in which the ring with CH($\mathrm{CH_3}$) next to C–OH has no C=O (5).
    S (6-6; both $\mathrm{CH_3}$ groups on one ring): gives a 5-7 system with CH($\mathrm{CH_3}$) next to C–OH in the 5-ring and C=O in the 7-ring (3).
    Hence P→2, Q→1, R→5, S→3.

  3. JEE Advanced 2026, Paper 2 · Question 39
    Natural rubber on complete ozonolysis ($\mathrm{O_3}$/Zn-$\mathrm{H_2O}$) gives compound X as the major product. X gives positive iodoform and Tollen’s tests. X on heating with aqueous NaOH gives Y as the major product. Y is

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    Natural rubber is cis-1,4-polyisoprene, $[-\mathrm{CH_2C(CH_3){=}CHCH_2}-]_n$.
    Cleaving every C=C gives $\mathrm{CH_3COCH_2CH_2CHO}$ (4-oxopentanal) = X: methyl ketone (iodoform +) and aldehyde (Tollens +).
    In aqueous NaOH the enolate of the $\mathrm{CH_3CO}$ group attacks the CHO carbon intramolecularly, closing a five-membered ring (3-hydroxycyclopentanone).
    On heating this dehydrates to the conjugated enone, cyclopent-2-en-1-one = Y → (A).

  4. JEE Advanced 2025, Paper 1 · Question 45
    The reaction sequence given below is carried out with $16$ moles of $\mathbf{X}$. The yield of the major product in each step is given below the product in parentheses. The amount (in grams) of $\mathbf{S}$ produced is ______.
    Use: Atomic mass (in amu): H $= 1$, C $= 12$, O $= 16$, Br $= 80$

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 175

    X is the ethylene acetal of 2-bromobenzaldehyde. Na, dry ether (Wurtz–Fittig coupling) joins two aryl groups; $\mathrm{H_3O^+}$ frees the aldehydes: $\mathbf{P}$ = biphenyl-2,2′-dicarbaldehyde, $\mathrm{C_{14}H_{10}O_2}$ = $210$ g mol$^{-1}$ (matches). $16$ mol X → $8$ mol P.
    NaOH, $\Delta$: intramolecular Cannizzaro → after acidification $\mathbf{Q}$ = 2′-(hydroxymethyl)biphenyl-2-carboxylic acid: $4$ mol (50%).
    NaOH/CaO, $\Delta$: decarboxylation → $\mathbf{R}$ = (biphenyl-2-yl)methanol, $\mathrm{C_{13}H_{12}O}$: $2$ mol (50%).
    $\mathrm{PBr_3}$ → $\mathbf{T}$ = 2-(bromomethyl)biphenyl: $1$ mol (50%).
    $\mathbf{T}$ + $\mathbf{R}$/NaH (Williamson) → $\mathbf{S}$ = bis(biphenyl-2-ylmethyl) ether, $\mathrm{C_{26}H_{22}O}$ = $312 + 22 + 16 = 350$ g mol$^{-1}$: $0.5$ mol (50%).
    Mass of S $= 0.5 \times 350 = 175$ g.

  5. JEE Advanced 2025, Paper 2 · Question 35
    Monocyclic compounds $\mathbf{P}$, $\mathbf{Q}$, $\mathbf{R}$ and $\mathbf{S}$ are the major products formed in the reaction sequences given below.
    The product having the highest number of unsaturated carbon atom(s) is

    1. $\mathbf{P}$
    2. $\mathbf{Q}$
    3. $\mathbf{R}$
    4. $\mathbf{S}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    $\mathbf{P}$: Hell–Volhard–Zelinsky α-bromination gives $\mathrm{PhCH_2CHBrCOOH}$: 6 ring + 1 COOH = 7 unsaturated C.

    $\mathbf{S}$ has the most. Answer (D).

  7. JEE Advanced 2024, Paper 1 · Question 40
    Reaction of iso-propylbenzene with $\mathrm{O_2}$ followed by the treatment with $\mathrm{H_3O^+}$ forms phenol and a by-product P. Reaction of P with 3 equivalents of $\mathrm{Cl_2}$ gives compound Q. Treatment of Q with $\mathrm{Ca(OH)_2}$ produces compound R and calcium salt S.
    The correct statement(s) regarding P, Q, R and S is(are)

    1. Reaction of P with R in the presence of KOH followed by acidification gives $\mathrm{(H_3C)_2C(OH)CCl_3}$
    2. Reaction of R with $\mathrm{O_2}$ in the presence of light gives phosgene gas
    3. Q reacts with aqueous NaOH to produce $\mathrm{Cl_3CCH_2OH}$ and $\mathrm{Cl_3CCOONa}$
    4. S on heating gives P
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a), (b), (d)

    Cumene process: P = acetone, $\mathrm{CH_3COCH_3}$.
    Q = $\mathrm{CH_3COCCl_3}$ (α-chlorination of the methyl group).
    $\mathrm{Ca(OH)_2}$ cleaves Q (haloform): R = $\mathrm{CHCl_3}$, S = $\mathrm{(CH_3COO)_2Ca}$.
    (A) $\mathrm{CCl_3^-}$ (from $\mathrm{CHCl_3}$ + KOH) adds to acetone; acidification gives chloretone, $\mathrm{(CH_3)_2C(OH)CCl_3}$. Correct.
    (B) $2\mathrm{CHCl_3} + \mathrm{O_2} \xrightarrow{h\nu} 2\mathrm{COCl_2} + 2\mathrm{HCl}$. Correct.
    (C) Q with NaOH gives $\mathrm{CHCl_3}$ and $\mathrm{CH_3COONa}$, not those products. Wrong.
    (D) Dry distillation of calcium acetate gives acetone. Correct.
    Answer: (A), (B), (D).

  8. JEE Advanced 2024, Paper 1 · Question 44
    Complete reaction of acetaldehyde with excess formaldehyde, upon heating with conc. NaOH solution, gives P and Q. Compound P does not give Tollens' test, whereas Q on acidification gives positive Tollens' test. Treatment of P with excess cyclohexanone in the presence of catalytic amount of p-toluenesulfonic acid (PTSA) gives product R.
    Sum of the number of methylene groups (-CH$_2$-) and oxygen atoms in R is ______.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 18

    Three crossed aldols: $\mathrm{CH_3CHO} + 3\mathrm{HCHO} \to \mathrm{(HOCH_2)_3C{-}CHO}$; then crossed Cannizzaro with HCHO gives P = pentaerythritol $\mathrm{C(CH_2OH)_4}$ and Q = $\mathrm{HCOONa}$ (formic acid on acidification, Tollens positive).
    P + excess cyclohexanone (PTSA): each pair of $\mathrm{CH_2OH}$ groups forms a cyclic ketal, giving a dispiro bis-ketal R: $\mathrm{C(CH_2O)_4}$ bridged by two cyclohexylidene units.
    $\mathrm{CH_2}$ groups: 4 (from pentaerythritol) + $2 \times 5$ (cyclohexane rings) = 14. Oxygen atoms: 4.
    Sum $= 18$.

  9. JEE Advanced 2024, Paper 1 · Question 46
    In the following reaction sequence, the major product P is formed.
    Glycerol reacts completely with excess P in the presence of an acid catalyst to form Q. Reaction of Q with excess NaOH followed by the treatment with $\mathrm{CaCl_2}$ yields Ca-soap R, quantitatively. Starting with one mole of Q, the amount of R produced in gram is ______.
    [Given, atomic weight: H = 1, C = 12, N = 14, O = 16, Na = 23, Cl = 35, Ca = 40]

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 909

    Start: $\mathrm{HC{\equiv}C(CH_2)_{15}CO_2Et}$.
    (i) $\mathrm{Hg^{2+}/H_3O^+}$ hydrates the terminal alkyne to the methyl ketone $\mathrm{CH_3CO(CH_2)_{15}CO_2Et}$.
    (ii) Clemmensen reduction: C=O → $\mathrm{CH_2}$, giving $\mathrm{CH_3(CH_2)_{16}CO_2Et}$. (iii) Ester hydrolysis: P = stearic acid $\mathrm{C_{17}H_{35}COOH}$.
    Q = glyceryl tristearate. Saponification gives 3 mol $\mathrm{C_{17}H_{35}COO^-}$, which form 1.5 mol R = $\mathrm{(C_{17}H_{35}COO)_2Ca}$.
    $M(\mathrm{R}) = 2 \times 283 + 40 = 606$; mass $= 1.5 \times 606 = 909$ g.

  10. JEE Advanced 2024, Paper 1 · Question 50
    List-I contains various reaction sequences and List-II contains the possible products. Match each entry in List-I with the appropriate entry in List-II and choose the correct option.

    1. P-3, Q-5, R-4, S-1
    2. P-3, Q-2, R-4, S-1
    3. P-3, Q-5, R-1, S-4
    4. P-5, Q-2, R-4, S-1
    Show answer and solution

  11. JEE Advanced 2024, Paper 2 · Question 40
    The option(s) with correct sequence of reagents for the conversion of $\mathbf{P}$ to $\mathbf{Q}$ is(are)

    1. i) Lindlar’s catalyst, $\mathrm{H_2}$; ii) $\mathrm{SnCl_2/HCl}$; iii) $\mathrm{NaBH_4}$; iv) $\mathrm{H_3O^+}$
    2. i) Lindlar’s catalyst, $\mathrm{H_2}$; ii) $\mathrm{H_3O^+}$; iii) $\mathrm{SnCl_2/HCl}$; iv) $\mathrm{NaBH_4}$
    3. i) $\mathrm{NaBH_4}$; ii) $\mathrm{SnCl_2/HCl}$; iii) $\mathrm{H_3O^+}$; iv) Lindlar’s catalyst, $\mathrm{H_2}$

  12. JEE Advanced 2024, Paper 2 · Question 47
    An organic compound $\mathbf{P}$ having molecular formula $\mathrm{C_6H_6O_3}$ gives ferric chloride test and does not have intramolecular hydrogen bond. The compound $\mathbf{P}$ reacts with 3 equivalents of $\mathrm{NH_2OH}$ to produce oxime $\mathbf{Q}$. Treatment of $\mathbf{P}$ with excess methyl iodide in the presence of KOH produces compound $\mathbf{R}$ as the major product. Reaction of $\mathbf{R}$ with excess iso-butylmagnesium bromide followed by treatment with $\mathrm{H_3O^+}$ gives compound $\mathbf{S}$ as the major product.
    The total number of methyl $(-\mathrm{CH_3})$ group(s) in compound $\mathbf{S}$ is _____.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 6 or 12

    $\mathbf{P}$ is phloroglucinol (1,3,5-trihydroxybenzene): phenolic ($\mathrm{FeCl_3}$ test), OH groups meta so no intramolecular H-bond, and its keto form (cyclohexane-1,3,5-trione) gives a trioxime $\mathbf{Q}$.

    If instead the Grignard adds, each iso-butyl brings 2 more $\mathrm{CH_3}$, giving $6+6=12$.
    Answer: $6$ (the official key also accepts $12$).

    Note: Official final key: 6 or 12 (either value is awarded marks).

    2. P, Q, and R are dicarboxylic acids.
    3. Compounds Q and R are the same.
    4. R does not undergo aldol condensation and S does not undergo Cannizzaro reaction.
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c), (d)

    P: hot alkaline $\mathrm{KMnO_4}$ oxidises the ethyl group to COOH; the tert-butyl group has no benzylic H and survives → 4-tert-butylbenzoic acid (a monocarboxylic acid).
    Q: NaOH hydrolyses both the ester and the acid chloride; acidification gives benzene-1,3-dicarboxylic acid (isophthalic acid).
    R: $\mathrm{H_3O^+}$/heat hydrolyses the ester and the nitrile and decarboxylates the β-keto acid → 3-acetylbenzoic acid; $\mathrm{H_2CrO_4}$ oxidises the acetyl group → isophthalic acid. So Q and R are the same → (C) true.
    S: Grignard + $\mathrm{CO_2}$ and acid (which also removes the acetal) gives 4-formylbenzoic acid; Tollens' reagent oxidises CHO → terephthalic acid.
    (A) false: P is not terephthalic acid, and glyptal uses phthalic (1,2-) acid, not Q. (B) false: P is monocarboxylic.
    (D) true: R (isophthalic acid) has no carbonyl α-H for aldol, and S has no CHO for Cannizzaro.
    Answer (C), (D).

  19. JEE Advanced 2022, Paper 2 · Question 49
    Considering the following reaction sequence, the correct statement(s) is(are)

    1. Compounds $\mathbf{P}$ and $\mathbf{Q}$ are carboxylic acids.
    2. Compound $\mathbf{S}$ decolorizes bromine water.
    3. Compounds $\mathbf{P}$ and $\mathbf{S}$ react with hydroxylamine to give the corresponding oximes.
    4. Compound $\mathbf{R}$ reacts with dialkylcadmium to give the corresponding tertiary alcohol.
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a), (b), (c); the final key also accepts (a), (c)

    $\mathbf{P}$: Friedel–Crafts acylation with succinic anhydride gives $\mathrm{PhCO(CH_2)_2COOH}$ (a keto acid).
    $\mathbf{Q}$: Clemmensen reduction gives $\mathrm{Ph(CH_2)_3COOH}$; $\mathbf{R}$: the acid chloride $\mathrm{Ph(CH_2)_3COCl}$.
    $\mathbf{S}$: intramolecular Friedel–Crafts acylation gives α-tetralone; Clemmensen then gives tetralin (the hydrocarbon).
    (A) $\mathbf{P}$ and $\mathbf{Q}$ are carboxylic acids: true. (C) $\mathbf{P}$ and $\mathbf{S}$ are ketones and form oximes: true.
    (B) α-Tetralone is an enolisable ketone that can be α-brominated by bromine water, so it is taken as true.
    (D) Acid chlorides react with dialkylcadmium to give ketones, not tertiary alcohols: false.
    Answer (A), (B), (C); the official key also accepts (A), (C).

    Note: Official final key: A, B, C or A, C

  20. JEE Advanced 2021, Paper 1 · Question 44
    For the following reaction scheme, percentage yields are given along the arrow:
    $\mathbf{x}$ g and $\mathbf{y}$ g are mass of $\mathbf{R}$ and $\mathbf{U}$, respectively.
    (Use: Molar mass (in g mol$^{-1}$) of H, C and O as 1, 12 and 16, respectively)

    The value of $\mathbf{y}$ is ___.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 3.2 or 3.9 to 3.91

    P (propyne, $0.1$ mol) with $\mathrm{Hg^{2+}/H^+}$ gives S = acetone, $0.1$ mol (100%).
    $\mathrm{Ba(OH)_2}$, heat: aldol condensation of two acetone molecules to T = mesityl oxide, $\mathrm{(CH_3)_2C{=}CHCOCH_3}$; $\dfrac{0.1}{2} \times 0.80 = 0.04$ mol.
    NaOCl (haloform reaction): U = 3-methylbut-2-enoic acid, $\mathrm{(CH_3)_2C{=}CHCOOH}$ ($M = 100$), which still has the C=C and decolourises Baeyer's reagent; $0.04 \times 0.80 = 0.032$ mol.
    $\mathbf{y} = 0.032 \times 100 = 3.20$.
    (Taking U as the sodium salt formed in the NaOCl medium, $M = 122$, gives $3.90$; the official key accepts both.)

    Note: Official final key: [3.20 to 3.20] or [3.90 to 3.91] (either range is awarded marks).

  21. JEE Advanced 2021, Paper 1 · Question 56
    In the reaction given below, the total number of atoms having $sp^2$ hybridization in the major product $\mathbf{P}$ is ___.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 8 or 12

    The substrate has two tetrasubstituted C=C bonds, each shared by the eight-membered ring and a five-membered ring.
    Ozonolysis (excess $\mathrm{O_3}$, then Zn/$\mathrm{H_2O}$) cleaves both, opening all three rings into one 14-membered ring with four C=O groups: cyclotetradecane-1,4,8,11-tetraone.
