  1. JEE Advanced 2026, Paper 2 · Question 48
    In a solvent S, a compound B is partially dissociated into C and D as given below:

    $\displaystyle \mathbf{B} \rightleftharpoons 2\mathbf{C} + 2\mathbf{D}$
    B, C and D are non-volatile in nature. The molar mass of B is 10 times the molar mass of S. The standard boiling point and the standard enthalpy of vaporization of S are 400 K and $10R$ J mol$^{-1}$, respectively ($R$ is the gas constant in J K$^{-1}$ mol$^{-1}$). A solution of B in S with an initial concentration of B as 0.25% (mass/mass) has a boiling point of 408 K at 1 bar pressure. In this solution, the mole percent of B that has been dissociated is ____.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 31.6 to 33.33

    Let the molar mass of S be $M$ (kg mol$^{-1}$); B has $10M$.
    $K_b = \dfrac{RT_b^2M}{\Delta_{vap}H} = \dfrac{R(400)^2M}{10R} = 16000\,M$ K kg mol$^{-1}$.
    0.25 g B in 99.75 g S: $m = \dfrac{0.25\times10^{-3}/(10M)}{0.09975} = \dfrac{2.506\times10^{-4}}{M}$ mol kg$^{-1}$, so $K_bm = 4.01$ K.
    $\Delta T_b = 8 = i\times4.01$ → $i \approx 1.995$.
    $\mathrm{B \rightleftharpoons 2C + 2D}$: particles $= 1-\alpha+4\alpha = 1+3\alpha$ → $\alpha = \dfrac{0.995}{3} \approx 0.332$.
    Mole percent dissociated ≈ 33.2%.

  2. JEE Advanced 2026, Paper 2 · Question 51
    Two volatile liquids A and B form an ideal solution. Consider a 5 molal solution of B in A inside a closed container having a total vapour pressure of 100 mm Hg at 300 K. The vapour pressure of pure A at 300K is 105 mm Hg. Assume that A and B behave as ideal gases in the vapour phase.

    Given:
    The gas constant $R$ = 0.08 L atm K$^{-1}$ mol$^{-1}$
    Molar mass of A is 50 g mol$^{-1}$
    Molar mass of B is 57 g mol$^{-1}$
    Density of liquid B at 300 K is 0.5 g/mL
    1 atm = 760 mm Hg

    At 300 K, the ratio of the molar volume of pure B in vapour phase to its molar volume in liquid phase is ____.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 1991 to 2053

    5 molal: 5 mol B per 1000 g A = 20 mol A → $x_B = 5/25 = 0.2$, $x_A = 0.8$.
    $100 = 0.8(105) + 0.2\,p_B^\circ$ → $p_B^\circ = 80$ mm Hg.
    Vapour of pure B at its vapour pressure: $V_m = \dfrac{RT}{p} = \dfrac{0.08\times300}{80/760} = 228$ L mol$^{-1}$.
    Liquid: $V_m = \dfrac{57}{0.5} = 114$ mL = 0.114 L mol$^{-1}$.
    Ratio $= 228/0.114 = 2000$.

  3. JEE Advanced 2026, Paper 2 · Question 52
    Two volatile liquids A and B form an ideal solution. Consider a 5 molal solution of B in A inside a closed container having a total vapour pressure of 100 mm Hg at 300 K. The vapour pressure of pure A at 300K is 105 mm Hg. Assume that A and B behave as ideal gases in the vapour phase.

    Given:
    The gas constant $R$ = 0.08 L atm K$^{-1}$ mol$^{-1}$
    Molar mass of A is 50 g mol$^{-1}$
    Molar mass of B is 57 g mol$^{-1}$
    Density of liquid B at 300 K is 0.5 g/mL
    1 atm = 760 mm Hg

    The mole fraction of B in vapour phase which is in equilibrium with this solution is ____.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 0.16

    5 mol B in 1000 g (20 mol) A → $x_B = 0.2$.
    From $100 = 0.8(105) + 0.2\,p_B^\circ$: $p_B^\circ = 80$ mm Hg.
    $p_B = 0.2\times80 = 16$ mm Hg.
    $y_B = p_B/P = 16/100 = 0.16$.

  4. JEE Advanced 2025, Paper 2 · Question 43
    Adsorption of phenol from its aqueous solution on to fly ash obeys Freundlich isotherm. At a given temperature, from 10 mg g$^{-1}$ and 16 mg g$^{-1}$ aqueous phenol solutions, the concentrations of adsorbed phenol are measured to be 4 mg g$^{-1}$ and 10 mg g$^{-1}$, respectively. At this temperature, the concentration (in mg g$^{-1}$) of adsorbed phenol from 20 mg g$^{-1}$ aqueous solution of phenol will be ________.
    Use: $\log_{10}2=0.3$

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 15.5 to 16.5

    Freundlich: $\dfrac xm=kC^{1/n}$.
    $\dfrac{10}{4}=\left(\dfrac{16}{10}\right)^{1/n}\Rightarrow\log2.5=\dfrac1n\log1.6$.
    $\log2.5=1-2(0.3)=0.4$, $\log1.6=4(0.3)-1=0.2$, so $\dfrac1n=2$.
    At $C=20$: $\dfrac xm=4\times\left(\dfrac{20}{10}\right)^2=16$ mg g$^{-1}$.

  5. JEE Advanced 2025, Paper 2 · Question 45
    At 300 K, an ideal dilute solution of a macromolecule exerts osmotic pressure that is expressed in terms of the height ($h$) of the solution (density $=1.00$ g cm$^{-3}$) where $h$ is equal to 2.00 cm. If the concentration of the dilute solution of the macromolecule is 2.00 g dm$^{-3}$, the molar mass of the macromolecule is calculated to be $\boldsymbol{X}\times10^4$ g mol$^{-1}$. The value of $\boldsymbol{X}$ is ________.
    Use: Universal gas constant ($R$) $=8.3$ J K$^{-1}$ mol$^{-1}$ and acceleration due to gravity ($g$) $=10$ m s$^{-2}$

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 2.4 to 2.55

    $\pi=h\rho g=0.02\times1000\times10=200$ Pa.
    $\pi=\dfrac{c}{M}RT$ with $c=2.00$ g dm$^{-3}=2000$ g m$^{-3}$.
    $M=\dfrac{cRT}{\pi}=\dfrac{2000\times8.3\times300}{200}=24900$ g mol$^{-1}=2.49\times10^4$ g mol$^{-1}$.
    $X=2.49$.

  6. JEE Advanced 2024, Paper 2 · Question 43
    Vessel-1 contains $w_2$ g of a non-volatile solute $\mathbf{X}$ dissolved in $w_1$ g of water. Vessel-2 contains $w_2$ g of another non-volatile solute $\mathbf{Y}$ dissolved in $w_1$ g of water. Both the vessels are at the same temperature and pressure. The molar mass of $\mathbf{X}$ is 80% of that of $\mathbf{Y}$. The van’t Hoff factor for $\mathbf{X}$ is 1.2 times of that of $\mathbf{Y}$ for their respective concentrations.
    The elevation of boiling point for solution in Vessel-1 is _____ % of the solution in Vessel-2.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 150

    $\Delta T_b=iK_b\dfrac{w_2\times1000}{M\,w_1}$, so with equal $w_1$, $w_2$: $\dfrac{\Delta T_{b,1}}{\Delta T_{b,2}}=\dfrac{i_X}{i_Y}\cdot\dfrac{M_Y}{M_X}$.
    $=1.2\times\dfrac{1}{0.8}=1.5$.
    So it is $150\%$.

  7. JEE Advanced 2023, Paper 2 · Question 36
    Consider the following statements related to colloids.
    (I) Lyophobic colloids are not formed by simple mixing of dispersed phase and dispersion medium.
    (II) For emulsions, both the dispersed phase and the dispersion medium are liquid.
    (III) Micelles are produced by dissolving a surfactant in any solvent at any temperature.
    (IV) Tyndall effect can be observed from a colloidal solution with dispersed phase having the same refractive index as that of the dispersion medium.

    The option with the correct set of statements is

    1. (I) and (II)
    2. (II) and (III)
    3. (III) and (IV)
    4. (II) and (IV)
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    (I) True: lyophobic sols need special methods (e.g. Bredig's arc, peptisation), not simple mixing.
    (II) True: an emulsion is a liquid dispersed in a liquid.
    (III) False: micelles form only above the critical micelle concentration and above the Kraft temperature.
    (IV) False: scattering (Tyndall effect) needs a difference in refractive index between the two phases.
    Answer (A).

  8. JEE Advanced 2023, Paper 2 · Question 46
    50 mL of 0.2 molal urea solution (density = 1.012 g mL$^{-1}$ at 300 K) is mixed with 250 mL of a solution containing 0.06 g of urea. Both the solutions were prepared in the same solvent. The osmotic pressure (in Torr) of the resulting solution at 300 K is ________.
    [Use: Molar mass of urea = 60 g mol$^{-1}$; gas constant, R = 62 L Torr K$^{-1}$ mol$^{-1}$; Assume, $\Delta_\mathrm{mix}H = 0$, $\Delta_\mathrm{mix}V = 0$]

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 682

    First solution: mass $= 50\times1.012 = 50.6$ g. With $w$ g solvent, urea $= 0.2w/1000$ mol and mass $= w(1 + 0.012) = 50.6 \Rightarrow w = 50$ g, urea $= 0.010$ mol.
    Second solution: $0.06/60 = 0.001$ mol.
    Total $0.011$ mol in $300$ mL ($\Delta_\mathrm{mix}V = 0$).
    $\pi = \dfrac{n}{V}RT = \dfrac{0.011}{0.3}\times62\times300 = 682$ Torr.

  9. JEE Advanced 2022, Paper 2 · Question 38
    An aqueous solution is prepared by dissolving $0.1$ mol of an ionic salt in $1.8$ kg of water at $35\ ^\circ$C. The salt remains $90\%$ dissociated in the solution. The vapour pressure of the solution is $59.724$ mm of Hg. Vapor pressure of water at $35\ ^\circ$C is $60.000$ mm of Hg. The number of ions present per formula unit of the ionic salt is ________.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 5

    Relative lowering $= \dfrac{60.000 - 59.724}{60.000} = 0.0046$ = mole fraction of solute particles.
    Water: $\dfrac{1800}{18} = 100$ mol. $\dfrac{0.1i}{100 + 0.1i} = 0.0046 \Rightarrow i \approx 4.62$.
    $i = 1 + \alpha(n - 1) = 1 + 0.9(n-1) = 4.62 \Rightarrow n - 1 \approx 4$.
    $n = 5$ ions per formula unit.

  10. JEE Advanced 2021, Paper 1 · Question 47
    The boiling point of water in a 0.1 molal silver nitrate solution (solution $\mathbf{A}$) is $\mathbf{x}$ °C. To this solution $\mathbf{A}$, an equal volume of 0.1 molal aqueous barium chloride solution is added to make a new solution $\mathbf{B}$. The difference in the boiling points of water in the two solutions $\mathbf{A}$ and $\mathbf{B}$ is $\mathbf{y} \times 10^{-2}$ °C.
    (Assume: Densities of the solutions $\mathbf{A}$ and $\mathbf{B}$ are the same as that of water and the soluble salts dissociate completely.
    Use: Molal elevation constant (Ebullioscopic Constant), $K_b = 0.5$ K kg mol$^{-1}$; Boiling point of pure water as 100 °C.)

    The value of $\mathbf{x}$ is ___.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 100.1

    $\mathrm{AgNO_3}$ gives two ions: $i = 2$.
    $\Delta T_b = iK_bm = 2 \times 0.5 \times 0.1 = 0.1$ K.
    $\mathbf{x} = 100 + 0.1 = 100.1$.

  11. JEE Advanced 2021, Paper 1 · Question 48
    The boiling point of water in a 0.1 molal silver nitrate solution (solution $\mathbf{A}$) is $\mathbf{x}$ °C. To this solution $\mathbf{A}$, an equal volume of 0.1 molal aqueous barium chloride solution is added to make a new solution $\mathbf{B}$. The difference in the boiling points of water in the two solutions $\mathbf{A}$ and $\mathbf{B}$ is $\mathbf{y} \times 10^{-2}$ °C.
    (Assume: Densities of the solutions $\mathbf{A}$ and $\mathbf{B}$ are the same as that of water and the soluble salts dissociate completely.
    Use: Molal elevation constant (Ebullioscopic Constant), $K_b = 0.5$ K kg mol$^{-1}$; Boiling point of pure water as 100 °C.)

    The value of $|\mathbf{y}|$ is ___.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 2.5

    Take 1 kg water in each solution. A: $0.1$ mol $\mathrm{Ag^+}$ and $0.1$ mol $\mathrm{NO_3^-}$; added: $0.1$ mol $\mathrm{Ba^{2+}}$ and $0.2$ mol $\mathrm{Cl^-}$.
    $0.1$ mol AgCl precipitates. Ions left: $0.1\ \mathrm{NO_3^-} + 0.1\ \mathrm{Ba^{2+}} + 0.1\ \mathrm{Cl^-} = 0.3$ mol in 2 kg water, i.e. $0.15$ molal of ions.
    $\Delta T_b(\mathbf{B}) = 0.5 \times 0.15 = 0.075$ K, while $\Delta T_b(\mathbf{A}) = 0.1$ K.
    Difference $= 0.025$ °C $= 2.5 \times 10^{-2}$ °C, so $|\mathbf{y}| = 2.5$.

  12. JEE Advanced 2021, Paper 1 · Question 51
    The correct statement(s) related to colloids is(are)

    1. The process of precipitating colloidal sol by an electrolyte is called peptization.
    2. Colloidal solution freezes at higher temperature than the true solution at the same concentration.
    3. Surfactants form micelle above critical micelle concentration (CMC). CMC depends on temperature.
    4. Micelles are macromolecular colloids.
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b), (c)

    (A) Precipitating a sol with an electrolyte is coagulation; peptization is the reverse (turning a precipitate into a sol): false.
    (B) At the same mass concentration a colloid has far fewer particles, so its freezing point depression is smaller and it freezes at a higher temperature: true.
    (C) Micelles form only above the CMC, and the CMC varies with temperature (micelles form above the Kraft temperature): true.
    (D) Micelles are associated colloids, not macromolecular colloids: false.
    Answer (B), (C).
