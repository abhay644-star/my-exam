  1. JEE Advanced 2026, Paper 2 · Question 37
    At 300 K, the molar conductivities of the aqueous solutions of three salts at two different concentrations are given below:

    SaltConcentration (M)Molar conductivity (S cm$^2$ mol$^{-1}$)
    $\mathrm{NaNO_3}$0.01111
    0.04101
    NaCl0.01117
    0.04107
    $\mathrm{AgNO_3}$0.01125
    0.04116

    The conductivity of a saturated aqueous solution of AgCl is $1.40\times10^{-6}$ S cm$^{-1}$ at 300 K. If the solubility of AgCl in water at 300 K is $\boldsymbol{X}$ mol L$^{-1}$, then $\log_{10}(\boldsymbol{X}^{-1})$ is

    (Assume that AgCl dissolved in water ionizes completely and that the molar conductivity of saturated AgCl solution is equal to its limiting molar conductivity.)

    1. 3
    2. 4
    3. 5
    4. 6
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    For strong electrolytes $\Lambda_m = \Lambda_m^\circ - A\sqrt{c}$; $\sqrt{0.01}=0.1$, $\sqrt{0.04}=0.2$.
    Each salt drops by $\Delta\Lambda$ over $\Delta\sqrt c = 0.1$, so extrapolating to $\sqrt c = 0$: $\Lambda^\circ(\mathrm{NaNO_3}) = 121$, $\Lambda^\circ(\mathrm{NaCl}) = 127$, $\Lambda^\circ(\mathrm{AgNO_3}) = 134$ S cm$^2$ mol$^{-1}$.
    Kohlrausch: $\Lambda^\circ(\mathrm{AgCl}) = 134 + 127 - 121 = 140$ S cm$^2$ mol$^{-1}$.
    $X = \dfrac{1000\,\kappa}{\Lambda^\circ} = \dfrac{1000\times1.40\times10^{-6}}{140} = 1.0\times10^{-5}$ mol L$^{-1}$.
    $\log_{10}(X^{-1}) = 5$ → (C).

  2. JEE Advanced 2025, Paper 1 · Question 40
    In an electrochemical cell, dichromate ions in aqueous acidic medium are reduced to $\mathrm{Cr^{3+}}$. The current (in amperes) that flows through the cell for $48.25$ minutes to produce $1$ mole of $\mathrm{Cr^{3+}}$ is ______.
    Use: $1$ Faraday $= 96500$ C mol$^{-1}$

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 100

    $\mathrm{Cr_2O_7^{2-}} + 14\mathrm{H^+} + 6e^- \rightarrow 2\mathrm{Cr^{3+}} + 7\mathrm{H_2O}$: $3$ mol electrons per mol $\mathrm{Cr^{3+}}$.
    Charge $= 3 \times 96500 = 289500$ C; time $= 48.25 \times 60 = 2895$ s.
    $I = \dfrac{289500}{2895} = 100$ A.

  3. JEE Advanced 2025, Paper 2 · Question 46
    An electrochemical cell is fueled by the combustion of butane at 1 bar and 298 K. Its cell potential is $\dfrac{\boldsymbol{X}}{F}\times10^3$ volts, where $F$ is the Faraday constant. The value of $\boldsymbol{X}$ is ________.
    Use: Standard Gibbs energies of formation at 298 K are: $\Delta_fG^\circ_{\mathrm{CO_2}}=-394$ kJ mol$^{-1}$; $\Delta_fG^\circ_{\text{water}}=-237$ kJ mol$^{-1}$; $\Delta_fG^\circ_{\text{butane}}=-18$ kJ mol$^{-1}$

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 105.4 to 105.6

    $\mathrm{C_4H_{10}+\tfrac{13}{2}O_2\rightarrow4CO_2+5H_2O}$.
    $\Delta G^\circ=4(-394)+5(-237)-(-18)=-2743$ kJ mol$^{-1}$.
    Electrons transferred: carbon goes from $-\tfrac52$ to $+4$, so $n=4\times6.5=26$.
    $E=-\dfrac{\Delta G^\circ}{nF}=\dfrac{2743\times10^3}{26F}=\dfrac{105.5}{F}\times10^3$ V.
    $X=105.5$.

  4. JEE Advanced 2024, Paper 1 · Question 48
    In a conductometric titration, small volume of titrant of higher concentration is added stepwise to a larger volume of titrate of much lower concentration, and the conductance is measured after each addition.
    The limiting ionic conductivity ($\Lambda_0$) values (in mS m$^2$ mol$^{-1}$) for different ions in aqueous solutions are given below:
    $\mathrm{Ag^+}$: 6.2, $\mathrm{K^+}$: 7.4, $\mathrm{Na^+}$: 5.0, $\mathrm{H^+}$: 35.0, $\mathrm{NO_3^-}$: 7.2, $\mathrm{Cl^-}$: 7.6, $\mathrm{SO_4^{2-}}$: 16.0, $\mathrm{OH^-}$: 19.9, $\mathrm{CH_3COO^-}$: 4.1
    For different combinations of titrates and titrants given in List-I, the graphs of 'conductance' versus 'volume of titrant' are given in List-II (see the figure).
    Match each entry in List-I with the appropriate entry in List-II and choose the correct option.

    List-IList-II
    (P) Titrate: KCl; Titrant: $\mathrm{AgNO_3}$(1) Graph (1) in the figure
    (Q) Titrate: $\mathrm{AgNO_3}$; Titrant: KCl(2) Graph (2) in the figure
    (R) Titrate: NaOH; Titrant: HCl(3) Graph (3) in the figure
    (S) Titrate: NaOH; Titrant: $\mathrm{CH_3COOH}$(4) Graph (4) in the figure
    (5) Graph (5) in the figure

    1. P-4, Q-3, R-2, S-5
    2. P-2, Q-4, R-3, S-1
    3. P-3, Q-4, R-2, S-5
    4. P-4, Q-3, R-2, S-1
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    (P) $\mathrm{Cl^-}$ (7.6) is replaced by $\mathrm{NO_3^-}$ (7.2) as AgCl precipitates: slight fall; after the end point $\mathrm{Ag^+}$ and $\mathrm{NO_3^-}$ add: rise → graph (3).
    (Q) $\mathrm{Ag^+}$ (6.2) is replaced by $\mathrm{K^+}$ (7.4): slight rise; after the end point $\mathrm{K^+}$ and $\mathrm{Cl^-}$ add: steeper rise → graph (4).
    (R) $\mathrm{OH^-}$ (19.9) is replaced by $\mathrm{Cl^-}$ (7.6): steep fall; after the end point $\mathrm{H^+}$ (35.0) and $\mathrm{Cl^-}$ add: steep rise → graph (2).
    (S) $\mathrm{OH^-}$ is replaced by $\mathrm{CH_3COO^-}$ (4.1): fall; after the end point the weak acid hardly ionises: nearly flat → graph (5).
    P-3, Q-4, R-2, S-5 → (C).

  5. JEE Advanced 2024, Paper 2 · Question 39
    An aqueous solution of hydrazine ($\mathrm{N_2H_4}$) is electrochemically oxidized by $\mathrm{O_2}$, thereby releasing chemical energy in the form of electrical energy. One of the products generated from the electrochemical reaction is $\mathrm{N_2(g)}$.
    Choose the correct statement(s) about the above process

    1. $\mathrm{OH^-}$ ions react with $\mathrm{N_2H_4}$ at the anode to form $\mathrm{N_2(g)}$ and water, releasing 4 electrons to the anode.
    2. At the cathode, $\mathrm{N_2H_4}$ breaks to $\mathrm{N_2(g)}$ and nascent hydrogen released at the electrode reacts with oxygen to form water.
    3. At the cathode, molecular oxygen gets converted to $\mathrm{OH^-}$.
    4. Oxides of nitrogen are major by-products of the electrochemical process.
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a), (c)

    Hydrazine fuel cell (alkaline):
    Anode: $\mathrm{N_2H_4+4OH^-\to N_2+4H_2O+4e^-}$ → (A) true.
    Cathode: $\mathrm{O_2+2H_2O+4e^-\to4OH^-}$ → (C) true; hydrazine is not oxidised at the cathode → (B) false.
    Overall: $\mathrm{N_2H_4+O_2\to N_2+2H_2O}$; no oxides of nitrogen → (D) false.
    Answer: (A), (C).

  6. JEE Advanced 2023, Paper 1 · Question 39
    Plotting $1/\Lambda_{\mathrm m}$ against $c\Lambda_{\mathrm m}$ for aqueous solutions of a monobasic weak acid (HX) resulted in a straight line with y-axis intercept of P and slope of S. The ratio P/S is
    [$\Lambda_{\mathrm m}$ = molar conductivity, $\Lambda_{\mathrm m}^{\mathrm o}$ = limiting molar conductivity, c = molar concentration, $\mathrm{K_a}$ = dissociation constant of HX]

    1. $\mathrm{K_a}\Lambda_{\mathrm m}^{\mathrm o}$
    2. $\mathrm{K_a}\Lambda_{\mathrm m}^{\mathrm o}/2$
    3. $2\,\mathrm{K_a}\Lambda_{\mathrm m}^{\mathrm o}$
    4. $1/(\mathrm{K_a}\Lambda_{\mathrm m}^{\mathrm o})$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    With $\alpha = \dfrac{\Lambda_{\mathrm m}}{\Lambda_{\mathrm m}^{\mathrm o}}$ and $K_a = \dfrac{c\alpha^2}{1-\alpha}$: $K_a(1-\alpha) = c\alpha^2$.
    $K_a\left(1 - \dfrac{\Lambda_{\mathrm m}}{\Lambda_{\mathrm m}^{\mathrm o}}\right) = \dfrac{c\Lambda_{\mathrm m}^2}{(\Lambda_{\mathrm m}^{\mathrm o})^2}$; divide by $K_a\Lambda_{\mathrm m}$: $\dfrac{1}{\Lambda_{\mathrm m}} = \dfrac{1}{\Lambda_{\mathrm m}^{\mathrm o}} + \dfrac{c\Lambda_{\mathrm m}}{K_a(\Lambda_{\mathrm m}^{\mathrm o})^2}$.
    $P = \dfrac{1}{\Lambda_{\mathrm m}^{\mathrm o}}$, $S = \dfrac{1}{K_a(\Lambda_{\mathrm m}^{\mathrm o})^2}$, so $\dfrac PS = K_a\Lambda_{\mathrm m}^{\mathrm o}$. Answer (A).

  7. JEE Advanced 2022, Paper 1 · Question 38
    The reduction potential ($E^0$, in V) of $\mathrm{MnO_4^-(aq)/Mn(s)}$ is ______.
    [Given: $E^0_{\left(\mathrm{MnO_4^-(aq)/MnO_2(s)}\right)}=1.68$ V; $E^0_{\left(\mathrm{MnO_2(s)/Mn^{2+}(aq)}\right)}=1.21$ V; $E^0_{\left(\mathrm{Mn^{2+}(aq)/Mn(s)}\right)}=-1.03$ V]

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 0.74 to 0.8

    Add the free energies ($\Delta G^0=-nFE^0$) of the three steps: $\mathrm{MnO_4^-\to MnO_2}$ ($n=3$), $\mathrm{MnO_2\to Mn^{2+}}$ ($n=2$), $\mathrm{Mn^{2+}\to Mn}$ ($n=2$); overall $n=7$.
    $E^0=\dfrac{3(1.68)+2(1.21)+2(-1.03)}{7}=\dfrac{5.04+2.42-2.06}{7}=\dfrac{5.40}{7}\approx0.77$ V.

  8. JEE Advanced 2022, Paper 2 · Question 39
    Consider the strong electrolytes $\mathrm{Z_mX_n}$, $\mathrm{U_mY_p}$ and $\mathrm{V_mX_n}$. Limiting molar conductivity ($\Lambda^0$) of $\mathrm{U_mY_p}$ and $\mathrm{V_mX_n}$ are $250$ and $440$ S cm$^2$ mol$^{-1}$, respectively. The value of $(m + n + p)$ is ________.
    Given: limiting molar conductivity of ions, $\lambda^0$ (S cm$^2$ mol$^{-1}$): $\mathrm{Z^{n+}}$ $50.0$, $\mathrm{U^{p+}}$ $25.0$, $\mathrm{V^{n+}}$ $100.0$, $\mathrm{X^{m-}}$ $80.0$, $\mathrm{Y^{m-}}$ $100.0$.
    The plot of molar conductivity ($\Lambda$) of $\mathrm{Z_mX_n}$ vs $c^{1/2}$ is given below.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 7

    From the plot, $\Lambda$ falls by $1$ for every $0.01$ in $c^{1/2}$ ($339$ at $0.01$, $336$ at $0.04$), so $\Lambda^0(\mathrm{Z_mX_n}) = 339 + 1 = 340$.
    $\mathrm{Z_mX_n}$: $50m + 80n = 340$; $\mathrm{V_mX_n}$: $100m + 80n = 440$.
    Subtracting: $50m = 100$, $m = 2$; then $80n = 240$, $n = 3$.
    $\mathrm{U_mY_p}$: $25m + 100p = 250 \Rightarrow 100p = 200$, $p = 2$.
    $m + n + p = 7$.

  9. JEE Advanced 2021, Paper 2 · Question 42
    Some standard electrode potentials at 298 K are given below:
    $\mathrm{Pb^{2+}/Pb}$ $\quad$ $-0.13$ V
    $\mathrm{Ni^{2+}/Ni}$ $\quad$ $-0.24$ V
    $\mathrm{Cd^{2+}/Cd}$ $\quad$ $-0.40$ V
    $\mathrm{Fe^{2+}/Fe}$ $\quad$ $-0.44$ V
    To a solution containing 0.001 M of $\mathbf{X}^{2+}$ and 0.1 M of $\mathbf{Y}^{2+}$, the metal rods $\mathbf{X}$ and $\mathbf{Y}$ are inserted (at 298 K) and connected by a conducting wire. This resulted in dissolution of $\mathbf{X}$. The correct combination(s) of $\mathbf{X}$ and $\mathbf{Y}$, respectively, is(are)
    (Given: Gas constant, R = 8.314 J K$^{-1}$ mol$^{-1}$, Faraday constant, F = 96500 C mol$^{-1}$)

    1. Cd and Ni
    2. Cd and Fe
    3. Ni and Pb
    4. Ni and Fe
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a), (b), (c)

    X dissolves, so X is the anode: $\mathrm{X + Y^{2+} \to X^{2+} + Y}$ must have $E > 0$.
    $E = E^\circ_{\mathrm Y} - E^\circ_{\mathrm X} - \dfrac{0.0591}{2}\log\dfrac{0.001}{0.1} = \left(E^\circ_{\mathrm Y} - E^\circ_{\mathrm X}\right) + 0.059$ V.
    (A) Cd, Ni: $0.16 + 0.059 > 0$ ✓.
    (B) Cd, Fe: $-0.04 + 0.059 = +0.019 > 0$ ✓.
    (C) Ni, Pb: $0.11 + 0.059 > 0$ ✓.
    (D) Ni, Fe: $-0.20 + 0.059 < 0$ ✗.
    Answer (A), (B), (C).

  10. JEE Advanced 2021, Paper 2 · Question 45
    At 298 K, the limiting molar conductivity of a weak monobasic acid is $4 \times 10^2$ S cm$^2$ mol$^{-1}$. At 298 K, for an aqueous solution of the acid the degree of dissociation is $\boldsymbol{\alpha}$ and the molar conductivity is $\mathbf{y} \times 10^2$ S cm$^2$ mol$^{-1}$. At 298 K, upon 20 times dilution with water, the molar conductivity of the solution becomes $3\mathbf{y} \times 10^2$ S cm$^2$ mol$^{-1}$.

    The value of $\boldsymbol{\alpha}$ is ___.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 0.2 to 0.22

    $\alpha_1 = \dfrac{y}{4}$ and, after dilution, $\alpha_2 = \dfrac{3y}{4}$ (with $\Lambda = \alpha\Lambda^\circ$).
    $K_a$ is unchanged: $\dfrac{c\,\alpha_1^2}{1 - \alpha_1} = \dfrac{(c/20)\,\alpha_2^2}{1 - \alpha_2}$
    $\Rightarrow \dfrac{1/16}{1 - y/4} = \dfrac{9/320}{1 - 3y/4} \Rightarrow 320 - 240y = 144 - 36y \Rightarrow y = \dfrac{176}{204} = 0.863$.
    $\alpha = \dfrac{y}{4} = 0.216 \approx 0.22$.

  11. JEE Advanced 2021, Paper 2 · Question 46

At 298 K, upon 20 times dilution with water, the molar conductivity of the solution becomes $3\mathbf{y} \times 10^2$ S cm$^2$ mol$^{-1}$.

    The value of $\mathbf{y}$ is ___.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 0.8 to 0.9
