  1. JEE Advanced 2026, Paper 2 · Question 5
    Let $\mathbb{R}$ denote the set of all real numbers. Consider the polynomial function $f : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ defined by

    $\displaystyle f(x) = \frac{d^{10}}{dx^{10}}\left((x^2-1)^{10}\right), \qquad \text{for all } x \in \mathbb{R}.$
    Here $\frac{d^{10}}{dx^{10}}((x^2-1)^{10})$ is the $10^{\text{th}}$ order derivative of the function $(x^2-1)^{10}$ .

    Then which of the following statements is (are) TRUE ?

    1. The coefficient of $x^8$ in the polynomial $f(x)$ is $(-10)\left(\frac{18!}{8!}\right)$
    2. The value of $f(1) + f(-1)$ is equal to $10!\,2^{11}$
    3. The degree of the polynomial $f(x)$ is $10$
    4. The constant term of the polynomial $f(x)$ is $-\left(\frac{10!}{5!}\right)$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a), (b), (c)

    $(x^2-1)^{10}=\sum_k\binom{10}{k}(-1)^{k}x^{2k}$ (since $(-1)^{10-k}=(-1)^k$), so $f(x)=\sum_{k=5}^{10}\binom{10}{k}(-1)^k\dfrac{(2k)!}{(2k-10)!}x^{2k-10}$.
    (A) $x^8$: $k=9$, coefficient $-10\cdot\frac{18!}{8!}$ — true.
    (C) top term $k=10$ gives $x^{10}$ — degree 10, true.
    (D) constant: $k=5$, $-\binom{10}{5}10!\ne-\frac{10!}{5!}$ — false.
    (B) By Rodrigues' formula $f=2^{10}10!\,P_{10}(x)$, $P_{10}(\pm1)=1$, so $f(1)+f(-1)=2^{11}\,10!$ — true.

  2. JEE Advanced 2025, Paper 2 · Question 10
    Let $a_0,a_1,\ldots,a_{23}$ be real numbers such that

    $\displaystyle \left(1+\dfrac{2}{5}x\right)^{23}=\sum_{i=0}^{23}a_ix^i$
    for every real number $x$. Let $a_r$ be the largest among the numbers $a_j$ for $0\le j\le23$.
    Then the value of $r$ is ________.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 6

    $a_j=\binom{23}{j}\left(\dfrac25\right)^j$, all positive.
    $\dfrac{a_{j+1}}{a_j}=\dfrac{23-j}{j+1}\cdot\dfrac25\ge1\iff46-2j\ge5j+5\iff j\le\dfrac{41}{7}\approx5.86$.
    So $a_j$ increases up to $j=6$ and decreases after: $a_6$ is the largest.
    $r=6$.

  3. JEE Advanced 2023, Paper 1 · Question 13
    Let $a$ and $b$ be two nonzero real numbers. If the coefficient of $x^5$ in the expansion of $\left(ax^2 + \dfrac{70}{27bx}\right)^4$ is equal to the coefficient of $x^{-5}$ in the expansion of $\left(ax - \dfrac{1}{bx^2}\right)^7$, then the value of $2b$ is ______.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 3

    First expansion: $T_{r+1} = \binom4r (ax^2)^{4-r}\left(\dfrac{70}{27bx}\right)^r$ has power $8 - 3r = 5 \Rightarrow r = 1$: coefficient $4a^3\cdot\dfrac{70}{27b} = \dfrac{280a^3}{27b}$.
    Second: $T_{r+1} = \binom7r (ax)^{7-r}\left(-\dfrac{1}{bx^2}\right)^r$ has power $7 - 3r = -5 \Rightarrow r = 4$: coefficient $\dfrac{35a^3}{b^4}$.
    $\dfrac{280}{27b} = \dfrac{35}{b^4} \Rightarrow b^3 = \dfrac{27}{8} \Rightarrow b = \dfrac32$.
    $2b = 3$.
