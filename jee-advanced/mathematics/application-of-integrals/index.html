  1. JEE Advanced 2026, Paper 2 · Question 16
    Consider the curve $C_1$ given by

    $\displaystyle y = e^{-x} \qquad \text{for } x \in [0, 10\pi],$
    and the curve $C_2$ given by
    $\displaystyle y = e^{-x}(\sin x + \cos x) \qquad \text{for } x \in [0, 10\pi].$
    Let $n$ be the total number of points of intersection of the curves $C_1$ and $C_2$ .

    Suppose that $\alpha_1 , \alpha_2 , \ldots, \alpha_n \in [0, 10\pi]$ are the $x$ - coordinates of the points of intersection of the curves $C_1$ and $C_2$ such that
    $\displaystyle \alpha_1 < \alpha_2 < \cdots < \alpha_n .$


    Let $\beta$ be the area of the region enclosed between the curves $C_1$ , $C_2$ , and the lines $x = \alpha_1$ and $x = \alpha_4$ . Then the value of
    $\displaystyle -\frac{1}{\pi}\log_e\left(\beta - 2\,e^{-\frac{\pi}{2}}\right)$
    is ________.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 2.4 to 2.6

    $\alpha_1=0,\ \alpha_2=\frac\pi2,\ \alpha_3=2\pi,\ \alpha_4=\frac{5\pi}2$.
    $F(x)=e^{-x}(1-\cos x)$ satisfies $F'(x)=e^{-x}(\sin x+\cos x-1)$.
    $F(0)=0,\ F(\frac\pi2)=e^{-\pi/2},\ F(2\pi)=0,\ F(\frac{5\pi}2)=e^{-5\pi/2}$; the integrand changes sign at each $\alpha_i$.
    $\beta=e^{-\pi/2}+e^{-\pi/2}+e^{-5\pi/2}$, so $\beta-2e^{-\pi/2}=e^{-5\pi/2}$.
    Value $=-\frac1\pi\cdot\left(-\frac{5\pi}{2}\right)=2.5$.

  2. JEE Advanced 2026, Paper 2 · Question 18
    Consider the ellipses given by

    $\displaystyle x^2 + 4y^2 = 1 \quad \text{and} \quad 4x^2 + y^2 = 1 .$


    If $\alpha$ is the area of the common region that lies inside both the given ellipses, then the value of $\cot\alpha$ is ________.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 0.74 to 0.76

    By symmetry, $\alpha=8\times$ the area of the part with $0\le y\le x$; there $4x^2+y^2=1$ is the binding curve (since $4x^2+y^2-(x^2+4y^2)=3(x^2-y^2)\ge0$).
    Map $4x^2+y^2=1$ to the unit circle by $u=2x$: the line $y=x$ becomes $y=u/2$, i.e. angle $\arctan\frac12$ from the $u$-axis.
    Sector of the unit circle from angle $0$ to $\arctan\frac12$ has area $\frac12\arctan\frac12$; scaling back halves it: $\frac14\arctan\frac12$.
    $\alpha=8\cdot\frac14\arctan\frac12=2\arctan\frac12$.
    $\tan\alpha=\dfrac{2\cdot\frac12}{1-\frac14}=\dfrac43$, so $\cot\alpha=0.75$.

  3. JEE Advanced 2025, Paper 2 · Question 2
    Let $\mathbb{R}$ denote the set of all real numbers. Then the area of the region

    $\displaystyle \left\{(x,y)\in\mathbb{R}\times\mathbb{R} : x>0,\ y>\dfrac{1}{x},\ 5x-4y-1>0,\ 4x+4y-17<0\right\}$
    is

    1. $\dfrac{17}{16}-\log_e 4$
    2. $\dfrac{33}{8}-\log_e 4$
    3. $\dfrac{57}{8}-\log_e 4$
    4. $\dfrac{17}{2}-\log_e 4$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    The region lies above $y=\dfrac1x$ and below both lines $y=\dfrac{5x-1}{4}$ and $y=\dfrac{17-4x}{4}$.
    The lines meet at $(2,\tfrac94)$; $y=\dfrac1x$ meets $5x-4y=1$ at $x=1$ ($5x^2-x-4=0$) and $4x+4y=17$ at $x=4$ ($4x^2-17x+4=0$).
    Area $=\displaystyle\int_1^2\dfrac{5x-1}{4}dx+\int_2^4\dfrac{17-4x}{4}dx-\int_1^4\dfrac{dx}{x}$.
    $=\dfrac14\left[\dfrac{5x^2}{2}-x\right]_1^2+\dfrac14\left[17x-2x^2\right]_2^4-\ln4=\dfrac{13}{8}+\dfrac{5}{2}-\ln4$.
    $=\dfrac{33}{8}-\log_e4$. Answer (B).

  4. JEE Advanced 2025, Paper 2 · Question 6
    Let $S$ denote the locus of the mid-points of those chords of the parabola $y^2=x$, such that the area of the region enclosed between the parabola and the chord is $\dfrac{4}{3}$. Let $\mathcal{R}$ denote the region lying in the first quadrant, enclosed by the parabola $y^2=x$, the curve $S$, and the lines $x=1$ and $x=4$.

    Then which of the following statements is (are) TRUE?

    1. $(4,\sqrt{3})\in S$
    2. $(5,\sqrt{2})\in S$
    3. Area of $\mathcal{R}$ is $\dfrac{14}{3}-2\sqrt{3}$
    4. Area of $\mathcal{R}$ is $\dfrac{14}{3}-\sqrt{3}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a), (c)

    Take the chord through $(y_1^2,y_1)$ and $(y_2^2,y_2)$: $x=(y_1+y_2)y-y_1y_2$.
    Area $=\displaystyle\int_{y_1}^{y_2}\left[(y_1+y_2)y-y_1y_2-y^2\right]dy=\dfrac{|y_2-y_1|^3}{6}=\dfrac43\Rightarrow|y_2-y_1|=2$.
    Mid-point: $k=\dfrac{y_1+y_2}{2}$, $h=\dfrac{y_1^2+y_2^2}{2}=\dfrac{(y_1+y_2)^2+(y_1-y_2)^2}{4}=k^2+1$. So $S: x=y^2+1$.
    $(4,\sqrt3)$: $3+1=4$ (A true); $(5,\sqrt2)$: $2+1\ne5$ (B false).
    Area of $\mathcal R=\displaystyle\int_1^4\left(\sqrt x-\sqrt{x-1}\right)dx=\dfrac23(8-1)-\dfrac23\cdot3\sqrt3=\dfrac{14}{3}-2\sqrt3$ (C true, D false).
    Answer (A), (C).

  5. JEE Advanced 2024, Paper 2 · Question 2
    Let $S=\left\{(x,y)\in\mathbb{R}\times\mathbb{R} : x\ge 0,\ y\ge 0,\ y^2\le 4x,\ y^2\le 12-2x \text{ and } 3y+\sqrt8\,x\le 5\sqrt8\right\}$. If the area of the region $S$ is $\alpha\sqrt2$, then $\alpha$ is equal to

    1. $\dfrac{17}{2}$
    2. $\dfrac{17}{3}$
    3. $\dfrac{17}{4}$
    4. $\dfrac{17}{5}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    $y^2=4x$ and $y^2=12-2x$ meet at $(2,2\sqrt2)$; the line $3y+2\sqrt2\,x=10\sqrt2$ also passes through $(2,2\sqrt2)$ and meets $y=0$ at $x=5$ (while $y^2=12-2x$ meets it at $x=6$).
    So for $0\le y\le 2\sqrt2$ the region runs from the parabola $x=\dfrac{y^2}{4}$ to the line $x=\dfrac{10\sqrt2-3y}{2\sqrt2}=5-\dfrac{3y}{2\sqrt2}$.
    Area $=\displaystyle\int_0^{2\sqrt2}\left(5-\dfrac{3y}{2\sqrt2}-\dfrac{y^2}{4}\right)dy=10\sqrt2-3\sqrt2-\dfrac{4\sqrt2}{3}=\dfrac{17}{3}\sqrt2$.
    $\alpha=\dfrac{17}{3}$ → (B).

  6. JEE Advanced 2023, Paper 1 · Question 3
    Let $f : [0,1] \to [0,1]$ be the function defined by $f(x) = \dfrac{x^3}{3} - x^2 + \dfrac{5}{9}x + \dfrac{17}{36}$. Consider the square region $S = [0,1]\times[0,1]$. Let $G = \{(x,y) \in S : y > f(x)\}$ be called the green region and $R = \{(x,y) \in S : y < f(x)\}$ be called the red region. Let $L_h = \{(x,h) \in S : x \in [0,1]\}$ be the horizontal line drawn at a height $h \in [0,1]$. Then which of the following statements is(are) true?

    1. There exists an $h \in \left[\dfrac14, \dfrac23\right]$ such that the area of the green region above the line $L_h$ equals the area of the green region below the line $L_h$
    2. There exists an $h \in \left[\dfrac14, \dfrac23\right]$ such that the area of the red region above the line $L_h$ equals the area of the red region below the line $L_h$
    3. There exists an $h \in \left[\dfrac14, \dfrac23\right]$ such that the area of the green region above the line $L_h$ equals the area of the red region below the line $L_h$
    4. There exists an $h \in \left[\dfrac14, \dfrac23\right]$ such that the area of the red region above the line $L_h$ equals the area of the green region below the line $L_h$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b), (c), (d)

    $f^{\prime}(x) = x^2 - 2x + \dfrac59 = \left(x-\dfrac13\right)\left(x-\dfrac53\right)$, so on $[0,1]$ the maximum is $f\left(\dfrac13\right) \approx 0.559$; also $f(0) = \dfrac{17}{36}$, $f(1) = \dfrac{13}{36}$. Hence $0.36 \le f \le 0.56$.
    $\int_0^1 f\,dx = \dfrac{1}{12} - \dfrac13 + \dfrac{5}{18} + \dfrac{17}{36} = \dfrac12$, so red area $=$ green area $= \dfrac12$.
    Write $G_a, G_b, R_a, R_b$ for the green/red areas above/below $L_h$. Then $R_a - G_b = \int_0^1 (f-h)\,dx = \dfrac12 - h$ and $G_a + R_a = 1-h$, $G_b + R_b = h$.
    (A) Needs $G_b = \dfrac14$. But $G_b(h)$ increases with $h$ and $G_b\left(\dfrac23\right) = \dfrac23 - \dfrac12 = \dfrac16 < \dfrac14$. False.
    (B) Needs $R_b = \dfrac14$. At $h = \dfrac14$ the whole strip below $L_h$ is red ($f > \dfrac14$), so $R_b = \dfrac14$. True.
    (C) $G_a - R_b = (1 - h - R_a) - (h - G_b) = 1 - 2h - (R_a - G_b) = \dfrac12 - h$, zero at $h = \dfrac12$. True.
    (D) $R_a - G_b = \dfrac12 - h$, zero at $h = \dfrac12$. True.
    Answer (B), (C), (D).

  7. JEE Advanced 2023, Paper 1 · Question 9
    Let $n \ge 2$ be a natural number and $f : [0,1] \to \mathbb{R}$ be the function defined by $$f(x) = \begin{cases} n(1-2nx) & \text{if } 0 \le x \le \dfrac{1}{2n} \\ 2n(2nx-1) & \text{if } \dfrac{1}{2n} \le x \le \dfrac{3}{4n} \\ 4n(1-nx) & \text{if } \dfrac{3}{4n} \le x \le \dfrac{1}{n} \\ \dfrac{n}{n-1}(nx-1) & \text{if } \dfrac{1}{n} \le x \le 1 \end{cases}$$ If $n$ is such that the area of the region bounded by the curves $x = 0$, $x = 1$, $y = 0$ and $y = f(x)$ is $4$, then the maximum value of the function $f$ is ______.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 8

    Each piece is linear and the graph is four triangles:
    $\left[0, \dfrac1{2n}\right]$: from $n$ to $0$, area $\dfrac12\cdot\dfrac1{2n}\cdot n = \dfrac14$.
    $\left[\dfrac1{2n}, \dfrac3{4n}\right]$: from $0$ to $n$, area $\dfrac18$; $\left[\dfrac3{4n}, \dfrac1n\right]$: from $n$ to $0$, area $\dfrac18$.
    $\left[\dfrac1n, 1\right]$: from $0$ to $n$, area $\dfrac12\left(1 - \dfrac1n\right)n = \dfrac{n-1}{2}$.
    Total $= \dfrac12 + \dfrac{n-1}2 = \dfrac n2 = 4 \Rightarrow n = 8$.
    The maximum value of $f$ is $n = 8$.

  8. JEE Advanced 2022, Paper 2 · Question 8
    Consider the functions $f, g : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ defined by $$f(x) = x^2 + \frac{5}{12} \qquad \text{and} \qquad g(x) = \begin{cases} 2\left(1 - \dfrac{4|x|}{3}\right), & |x| \le \dfrac34, \\ 0, & |x| > \dfrac34. \end{cases}$$ If $\alpha$ is the area of the region

    $\displaystyle \left\{(x, y) \in \mathbb{R}\times\mathbb{R} : |x| \le \frac34,\ 0 \le y \le \min\{f(x), g(x)\}\right\},$
    then the value of $9\alpha$ is ________.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 6

    For $x \ge 0$: $x^2 + \dfrac5{12} = 2 - \dfrac{8x}{3} \Rightarrow 12x^2 + 32x - 19 = 0 \Rightarrow x = \dfrac12$.
    On $\left[0, \dfrac12\right]$ the minimum is $f$; on $\left[\dfrac12, \dfrac34\right]$ it is $g$ (both even functions).
    $\displaystyle\int_0^{1/2}\left(x^2 + \frac5{12}\right)dx = \frac1{24} + \frac5{24} = \frac14$.
    On $\left[\dfrac12, \dfrac34\right]$, $g$ falls linearly from $\dfrac23$ to $0$: area $= \dfrac12\cdot\dfrac14\cdot\dfrac23 = \dfrac1{12}$.
    $\alpha = 2\left(\dfrac14 + \dfrac1{12}\right) = \dfrac23$, so $9\alpha = 6$.

  9. JEE Advanced 2021, Paper 1 · Question 2
    The area of the region

    $\displaystyle \left\{(x, y) : 0 \le x \le \dfrac{9}{4},\quad 0 \le y \le 1,\quad x \ge 3y,\quad x + y \ge 2\right\}$
    is

    1. $\dfrac{11}{32}$
    2. $\dfrac{35}{96}$
    3. $\dfrac{37}{96}$
    4. $\dfrac{13}{32}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    The lines $x = 3y$ and $x + y = 2$ meet at $\left(\dfrac32, \dfrac12\right)$; the line $x = 3y$ meets $x = \dfrac94$ at $y = \dfrac34$.
    For $0 \le y \le \dfrac12$: $x$ runs from $2 - y$ to $\dfrac94$. For $\dfrac12 \le y \le \dfrac34$: $x$ runs from $3y$ to $\dfrac94$.
    Area $= \displaystyle\int_0^{1/2}\left(y + \dfrac14\right)dy + \int_{1/2}^{3/4}\left(\dfrac94 - 3y\right)dy = \dfrac14 + \dfrac{3}{32} = \dfrac{11}{32}$ → (A).
