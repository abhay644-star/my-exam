  1. JEE Advanced 2026, Paper 1 · Question 15
    For real numbers $\alpha,\ \beta,\ \gamma,\ \delta$ and $\mu$ , consider the matrix
    $$M = \begin{bmatrix} \alpha & \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} & -\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} \\ \frac{1}{\sqrt{3}} & \beta & \frac{1}{\sqrt{3}} \\ \gamma & \delta & \mu \end{bmatrix}.$$Suppose that $MM^T = I$ , where $M^T$ is the transpose of the matrix $M$, and $I$ is the $3 \times 3$ identity matrix. Let

    $\displaystyle \vec{u} = \alpha\,\hat{\imath} + \frac{1}{\sqrt{3}}\hat{\jmath} + \gamma\,\hat{k}\,, \qquad \vec{v} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\hat{\imath} + \beta\hat{\jmath} + \delta\hat{k} \quad\text{and}\quad \vec{w} = -\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\hat{\imath} + \frac{1}{\sqrt{3}}\hat{\jmath} + \mu\hat{k}\,.$
    Match each entry in List-I to the correct entry in List-II and choose the correct option.
    List-IList-II
    (P) The value of $\gamma^2 + \delta^2$ is(1) $0$
    (Q) If $x\vec{u} + y\vec{v} + z\vec{w} = \hat{\jmath}$ for some real numbers $x,\ y$ and $z$, then the value of $x$ is(2) $1$
    (R) The value of $|\vec{u} \cdot (\vec{v} \times \vec{w})|$ is(3) $\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}$
    (S) The value of $|\vec{u} \times (\vec{v} \times \vec{w})|$ is(4) $\frac{1}{\sqrt{3}}$
    (5) $\frac{5}{6}$

    1. (P) → (5), (Q) → (4), (R) → (2), (S) → (1)
    2. (P) → (4), (Q) → (5), (R) → (1), (S) → (2)
    3. (P) → (5), (Q) → (3), (R) → (2), (S) → (1)
    4. (P) → (5), (Q) → (4), (R) → (1), (S) → (2)
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    $M$ is orthogonal, so its rows and its columns are orthonormal; $\vec u, \vec v, \vec w$ are exactly the columns of $M$.
    (P) Column 3: $\frac12 + \frac13 + \mu^2 = 1 \Rightarrow \mu^2 = \frac16$; row 3: $\gamma^2 + \delta^2 = 1 - \mu^2 = \frac56$ → (5).
    (Q) Dot with $\vec u$: $x = \vec u \cdot \hat\jmath = \frac{1}{\sqrt3}$ → (4).
    (R) $|\vec u \cdot (\vec v \times \vec w)| = |\det M| = 1$ → (2).
    (S) $\vec u \times (\vec v \times \vec w) = (\vec u\cdot\vec w)\vec v - (\vec u \cdot \vec v)\vec w = \vec 0$ → (1).
    Answer (A).

  2. JEE Advanced 2026, Paper 2 · Question 1
    Let $\vec{a}, \vec{b}$ be two vectors, and let $P, Q$ and $R$ be the points with position vectors $\vec{a}$ , $\vec{b}$ and $\vec{a}+\vec{b}$, respectively, with respect to the origin $O$ . If $|\vec{a}+\vec{b}| = \sqrt{21}$ , $|\vec{a}-\vec{b}| = 3$ , and $\vec{a}$ and $(\vec{a}-\vec{b})$ are perpendicular to each other, then the area of the triangle $OPR$ is

    1. $\sqrt{3}$
    2. $\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$
    3. $\dfrac{3\sqrt{3}}{2}$
    4. $\dfrac{3}{2}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    Adding and subtracting $|\vec a+\vec b|^2=21$ and $|\vec a-\vec b|^2=9$: $|\vec a|^2+|\vec b|^2=15$, $\vec a\cdot\vec b=3$.
    $\vec a\perp(\vec a-\vec b)\Rightarrow |\vec a|^2=\vec a\cdot\vec b=3$, so $|\vec b|^2=12$.
    Area $OPR=\tfrac12|\vec a\times(\vec a+\vec b)|=\tfrac12|\vec a\times\vec b|$.
    $|\vec a\times\vec b|^2=3\cdot12-3^2=27$, so area $=\dfrac{3\sqrt3}{2}$.

  3. JEE Advanced 2025, Paper 1 · Question 9
    For any two points $M$ and $N$ in the $XY$-plane, let $\overrightarrow{MN}$ denote the vector from $M$ to $N$, and $\vec{0}$ denote the zero vector. Let $P, Q$ and $R$ be three distinct points in the $XY$-plane. Let $S$ be a point inside the triangle $\Delta PQR$ such that

    $\displaystyle \overrightarrow{SP} + 5\,\overrightarrow{SQ} + 6\,\overrightarrow{SR} = \vec{0}.$
    Let $E$ and $F$ be the mid-points of the sides $PR$ and $QR$, respectively. Then the value of
    $\displaystyle \dfrac{\text{length of the line segment } EF}{\text{length of the line segment } ES}$
    is ______.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 1.15 to 1.25

    With position vectors: $(\vec p - \vec s) + 5(\vec q - \vec s) + 6(\vec r - \vec s) = \vec0 \Rightarrow \vec s = \dfrac{\vec p + 5\vec q + 6\vec r}{12}$.
    $\vec e = \dfrac{\vec p + \vec r}{2}$, $\vec f = \dfrac{\vec q + \vec r}{2}$.
    $\overrightarrow{EF} = \dfrac{\vec q - \vec p}{2}$, so $EF = \dfrac12 PQ$.
    $\overrightarrow{ES} = \dfrac{\vec p + 5\vec q + 6\vec r - 6\vec p - 6\vec r}{12} = \dfrac{5(\vec q - \vec p)}{12}$, so $ES = \dfrac{5}{12}PQ$.
    Ratio $= \dfrac{1/2}{5/12} = \dfrac65 = 1.2$.

  4. JEE Advanced 2025, Paper 1 · Question 16
    Let $\vec{w} = \hat{i} + \hat{j} - 2\hat{k}$, and $\vec{u}$ and $\vec{v}$ be two vectors, such that $\vec{u}\times\vec{v} = \vec{w}$ and $\vec{v}\times\vec{w} = \vec{u}$. Let $\alpha, \beta, \gamma$, and $t$ be real numbers such that

    $\displaystyle \vec{u} = \alpha\hat{i} + \beta\hat{j} + \gamma\hat{k}, \quad -t\alpha + \beta + \gamma = 0, \quad \alpha - t\beta + \gamma = 0, \quad \text{and} \quad \alpha + \beta - t\gamma = 0.$
    Match each entry in List-I to the correct entry in List-II and choose the correct option.
    List-IList-II
    (P) $|\vec{v}|^2$ is equal to(1) $0$
    (Q) If $\alpha = \sqrt{3}$, then $\gamma^2$ is equal to(2) $1$
    (R) If $\alpha = \sqrt{3}$, then $(\beta + \gamma)^2$ is equal to(3) $2$
    (S) If $\alpha = \sqrt{2}$, then $t + 3$ is equal to(4) $3$
    (5) $5$

    1. (P) $\rightarrow$ (2) (Q) $\rightarrow$ (1) (R) $\rightarrow$ (4) (S) $\rightarrow$ (5)
    2. (P) $\rightarrow$ (2) (Q) $\rightarrow$ (4) (R) $\rightarrow$ (3) (S) $\rightarrow$ (5)
    3. (P) $\rightarrow$ (2) (Q) $\rightarrow$ (1) (R) $\rightarrow$ (4) (S) $\rightarrow$ (3)
    4. (P) $\rightarrow$ (5) (Q) $\rightarrow$ (4) (R) $\rightarrow$ (1) (S) $\rightarrow$ (3)
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    $\vec w = \vec u\times\vec v$ is perpendicular to $\vec u, \vec v$, and $\vec u = \vec v\times\vec w$ is perpendicular to $\vec v$; so $\vec u, \vec v, \vec w$ are mutually perpendicular.
    Then $|\vec w| = |\vec u||\vec v|$ and $|\vec u| = |\vec v||\vec w|$, giving $|\vec v|^2 = 1$ → (P)→(2), and $|\vec u| = |\vec w| = \sqrt6$.
    Also $\vec u\cdot\vec w = 0$: $\alpha + \beta - 2\gamma = 0$.
    The homogeneous system has a non-zero solution, so its determinant $(2 - t)(t + 1)^2 = 0$: $t = 2$ or $t = -1$.
    $t = 2$: $\alpha = \beta = \gamma$, and $3\alpha^2 = 6 \Rightarrow \alpha = \pm\sqrt2$.
    $t = -1$: $\alpha + \beta + \gamma = 0$ with $\alpha + \beta = 2\gamma$ gives $\gamma = 0$, $\beta = -\alpha$, and $2\alpha^2 = 6 \Rightarrow \alpha = \pm\sqrt3$.
    (Q) $\alpha = \sqrt3$: $\gamma^2 = 0$ → (1). (R) $(\beta + \gamma)^2 = 3$ → (4). (S) $\alpha = \sqrt2$: $t = 2$, $t + 3 = 5$ → (5).
    Match: P→2, Q→1, R→4, S→5 → (A).

  5. JEE Advanced 2025, Paper 2 · Question 12
    Consider the vectors

    $\displaystyle \vec{x}=\hat{i}+2\hat{j}+3\hat{k},\quad \vec{y}=2\hat{i}+3\hat{j}+\hat{k},\quad\text{and}\quad \vec{z}=3\hat{i}+\hat{j}+2\hat{k}.$
    For two distinct positive real numbers $\alpha$ and $\beta$, define
    $\displaystyle \vec{X}=\alpha\vec{x}+\beta\vec{y}-\vec{z},\quad \vec{Y}=\alpha\vec{y}+\beta\vec{z}-\vec{x},\quad\text{and}\quad \vec{Z}=\alpha\vec{z}+\beta\vec{x}-\vec{y}.$
    If the vectors $\vec{X}$, $\vec{Y}$, and $\vec{Z}$ lie in a plane, then the value of $\alpha+\beta-3$ is ________.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: -2

    $[\vec x\ \vec y\ \vec z]=\begin{vmatrix}1&2&3\\2&3&1\\3&1&2\end{vmatrix}=-18\ne0$, so $\vec x,\vec y,\vec z$ are independent.
    Hence $[\vec X\ \vec Y\ \vec Z]=0\iff\begin{vmatrix}\alpha&\beta&-1\\-1&\alpha&\beta\\\beta&-1&\alpha\end{vmatrix}=0$.
    This circulant determinant with $a=\alpha$, $b=\beta$, $c=-1$ equals $a^3+b^3+c^3-3abc=(a+b+c)\cdot\tfrac12\left[(a-b)^2+(b-c)^2+(c-a)^2\right]$.
    The second factor is non-zero since $\alpha\ne\beta$, so $\alpha+\beta-1=0$.
    $\alpha+\beta-3=-2$.

  6. JEE Advanced 2024, Paper 1 · Question 12
    Let $\overrightarrow{OP} = \dfrac{\alpha - 1}{\alpha}\hat{i} + \hat{j} + \hat{k}$, $\overrightarrow{OQ} = \hat{i} + \dfrac{\beta - 1}{\beta}\hat{j} + \hat{k}$ and $\overrightarrow{OR} = \hat{i} + \hat{j} + \dfrac{1}{2}\hat{k}$ be three vectors, where $\alpha, \beta \in \mathbb{R} - \{0\}$ and $O$ denotes the origin. If $\left(\overrightarrow{OP} \times \overrightarrow{OQ}\right) \cdot \overrightarrow{OR} = 0$ and the point $(\alpha, \beta, 2)$ lies on the plane $3x + 3y - z + l = 0$, then the value of $l$ is ________.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 5

    Let $p = 1 - \dfrac1\alpha$, $q = 1 - \dfrac1\beta$. Then $\begin{vmatrix} p & 1 & 1 \\ 1 & q & 1 \\ 1 & 1 & \frac12 \end{vmatrix} = p\left(\dfrac q2 - 1\right) + \dfrac12 + (1 - q) = 0$, i.e. $pq - 2p - 2q + 3 = 0$.
    With $u = \dfrac1\alpha$, $v = \dfrac1\beta$: $(1-u)(1-v) - 2(1-u) - 2(1-v) + 3 = uv + u + v = 0$.
    Multiplying by $\alpha\beta$: $1 + \beta + \alpha = 0$, so $\alpha + \beta = -1$.
    On the plane: $3(\alpha + \beta) - 2 + l = 0 \Rightarrow -3 - 2 + l = 0 \Rightarrow l = 5$.

  7. JEE Advanced 2024, Paper 2 · Question 11
    Let $\vec p=2\hat i+\hat j+3\hat k$ and $\vec q=\hat i-\hat j+\hat k$. If for some real numbers $\alpha,\beta$, and $\gamma$, we have

    $\displaystyle 15\hat i+10\hat j+6\hat k=\alpha\left(2\vec p+\vec q\right)+\beta\left(\vec p-2\vec q\right)+\gamma\left(\vec p\times\vec q\right),$
    then the value of $\gamma$ is _______.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 2

    $\vec p\times\vec q=\begin{vmatrix}\hat i&\hat j&\hat k\\2&1&3\\1&-1&1\end{vmatrix}=4\hat i+\hat j-3\hat k$, and $|\vec p\times\vec q|^2=26$.
    $\vec p\times\vec q$ is perpendicular to $2\vec p+\vec q$ and $\vec p-2\vec q$, so dotting both sides with it:
    $15(4)+10(1)+6(-3)=26\gamma\Rightarrow 52=26\gamma\Rightarrow\gamma=2$.

  8. JEE Advanced 2023, Paper 1 · Question 12
    Let $P$ be the plane $\sqrt3x + 2y + 3z = 16$ and let

    $\displaystyle S = \left\{\alpha\hat{i} + \beta\hat{j} + \gamma\hat{k} : \alpha^2 + \beta^2 + \gamma^2 = 1 \text{ and the distance of } (\alpha,\beta,\gamma) \text{ from the plane } P \text{ is } \dfrac72\right\}.$
    Let $\vec{u}, \vec{v}$ and $\vec{w}$ be three distinct vectors in $S$ such that $|\vec{u}-\vec{v}| = |\vec{v}-\vec{w}| = |\vec{w}-\vec{u}|$. Let $V$ be the volume of the parallelepiped determined by vectors $\vec{u}, \vec{v}$ and $\vec{w}$. Then the value of $\dfrac{80}{\sqrt3}V$ is ______.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 45

    The normal $(\sqrt3, 2, 3)$ has length $4$. Distance $\dfrac{|\sqrt3\alpha + 2\beta + 3\gamma - 16|}{4} = \dfrac72 \Rightarrow \sqrt3\alpha + 2\beta + 3\gamma = 2$ (the value $30$ is impossible, as the left side is at most $4$).
    So the tips of the vectors in $S$ lie on a circle: the plane is at distance $\dfrac24 = \dfrac12$ from $O$, radius $\sqrt{1 - \frac14} = \dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}$.
    $\vec u, \vec v, \vec w$ form an equilateral triangle inscribed in it: side $= \sqrt3 \cdot \dfrac{\sqrt3}{2} = \dfrac32$, area $= \dfrac{\sqrt3}{4}\cdot\dfrac94 = \dfrac{9\sqrt3}{16}$.
    $V = 6\times$ volume of tetrahedron $O\,UVW = 6\cdot\dfrac13\cdot\dfrac{9\sqrt3}{16}\cdot\dfrac12 = \dfrac{9\sqrt3}{16}$.
    $\dfrac{80}{\sqrt3}V = \dfrac{80\times9}{16} = 45$.

  9. JEE Advanced 2023, Paper 2 · Question 4
    Let the position vectors of the points $P, Q, R$ and $S$ be $\vec a = \hat\imath + 2\hat\jmath - 5\hat k$, $\vec b = 3\hat\imath + 6\hat\jmath + 3\hat k$, $\vec c = \dfrac{17}{5}\hat\imath + \dfrac{16}{5}\hat\jmath + 7\hat k$ and $\vec d = 2\hat\imath + \hat\jmath + \hat k$, respectively. Then which of the following statements is true?

    1. The points $P, Q, R$ and $S$ are NOT coplanar
    2. $\dfrac{\vec b + 2\vec d}{3}$ is the position vector of a point which divides $PR$ internally in the ratio $5:4$
    3. $\dfrac{\vec b + 2\vec d}{3}$ is the position vector of a point which divides $PR$ externally in the ratio $5:4$
    4. The square of the magnitude of the vector $\vec b \times \vec d$ is $95$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    $\dfrac{\vec b + 2\vec d}{3} = \left(\dfrac73, \dfrac83, \dfrac53\right)$.
    Point dividing $PR$ internally in $5:4$: $\dfrac{4\vec a + 5\vec c}{9} = \dfrac{(4+17,\ 8+16,\ -20+35)}{9} = \left(\dfrac73, \dfrac83, \dfrac53\right)$ → (B) is true, so (C) is false.
    (A): $\vec{PQ} = (2,4,8)$, $\vec{PR} = \left(\dfrac{12}{5}, \dfrac65, 12\right)$, $\vec{PS} = (1,-1,6)$; their scalar triple product is $\dfrac{192 - 48 - 144}{5} = 0$, so the points are coplanar: (A) false.
    (D): $\vec b \times \vec d = (3, 3, -9)$, $|\vec b\times\vec d|^2 = 99 \ne 95$: false.
    Answer (B).

  10. JEE Advanced 2022, Paper 2 · Question 13
    Let $\hat\imath$, $\hat\jmath$ and $\hat k$ be the unit vectors along the three positive coordinate axes. Let

    $\displaystyle \vec a = 3\hat\imath + \hat\jmath - \hat k,$
    $\displaystyle \vec b = \hat\imath + b_2\hat\jmath + b_3\hat k, \quad b_2, b_3 \in \mathbb{R},$
    $\displaystyle \vec c = c_1\hat\imath + c_2\hat\jmath + c_3\hat k, \quad c_1, c_2, c_3 \in \mathbb{R}$
    be three vectors such that $b_2b_3 > 0$, $\vec a\cdot\vec b = 0$ and $$\begin{pmatrix} 0 & -c_3 & c_2 \\ c_3 & 0 & -c_1 \\ -c_2 & c_1 & 0 \end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix} 1 \\ b_2 \\ b_3 \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} 3 - c_1 \\ 1 - c_2 \\ -1 - c_3 \end{pmatrix}.$$ Then, which of the following is/are TRUE ?

    1. $\vec a\cdot\vec c = 0$
    2. $\vec b\cdot\vec c = 0$
    3. $|\vec b| > \sqrt{10}$
    4. $|\vec c| \le \sqrt{11}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b), (c), (d)

    The matrix product is $\vec c\times\vec b$, so $\vec c\times\vec b = \vec a - \vec c$.
    Dot with $\vec c$: $0 = \vec a\cdot\vec c - |\vec c|^2$, so $\vec a\cdot\vec c = |\vec c|^2$. If $\vec a\cdot\vec c = 0$ then $\vec c = \vec 0$ and $\vec a = \vec 0$, impossible: (A) false.
    Dot with $\vec b$: $0 = \vec a\cdot\vec b - \vec b\cdot\vec c = -\vec b\cdot\vec c$: (B) true.
    $\vec a\cdot\vec b = 0 \Rightarrow b_3 = b_2 + 3$; $b_2b_3 > 0 \Rightarrow b_2 > 0$ or $b_2 < -3$, so $b_2^2 + (b_2+3)^2 > 9$ and $|\vec b|^2 > 10$: (C) true.
    $|\vec c\times\vec b|^2 = |\vec c|^2|\vec b|^2$ (as $\vec b\perp\vec c$) and $|\vec a - \vec c|^2 = 11 - 2|\vec c|^2 + |\vec c|^2 = 11 - |\vec c|^2$.
    So $|\vec c|^2(|\vec b|^2 + 1) = 11$, $|\vec c|^2 < 11$: (D) true.
    Answer (B), (C), (D).

  11. JEE Advanced 2021, Paper 1 · Question 19
    Let $\vec u$, $\vec v$ and $\vec w$ be vectors in three-dimensional space, where $\vec u$ and $\vec v$ are unit vectors which are not perpendicular to each other and

    $\displaystyle \vec u \cdot \vec w = 1,\quad \vec v \cdot \vec w = 1,\quad \vec w \cdot \vec w = 4$
    If the volume of the parallelopiped, whose adjacent sides are represented by the vectors $\vec u$, $\vec v$ and $\vec w$, is $\sqrt2$, then the value of $|3\vec u + 5\vec v|$ is ___ .

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 7

    Let $\vec u \cdot \vec v = c \ne 0$. Then $[\vec u\ \vec v\ \vec w]^2 = \begin{vmatrix} 1 & c & 1 \\ c & 1 & 1 \\ 1 & 1 & 4 \end{vmatrix} = 3 - c(4c - 1) + (c - 1) = 2 + 2c - 4c^2$.
    Volume$^2 = 2$: $2c - 4c^2 = 0$, so $c = \dfrac12$ (as $c \ne 0$).
    $|3\vec u + 5\vec v|^2 = 9 + 25 + 30c = 49$, so $|3\vec u + 5\vec v| = 7$.

  12. JEE Advanced 2021, Paper 2 · Question 5
    Let $O$ be the origin and $\overrightarrow{OA} = 2\hat{i} + 2\hat{j} + \hat{k}$, $\overrightarrow{OB} = \hat{i} - 2\hat{j} + 2\hat{k}$ and $\overrightarrow{OC} = \dfrac12\left(\overrightarrow{OB} - \lambda\overrightarrow{OA}\right)$ for some $\lambda > 0$. If $\left|\overrightarrow{OB} \times \overrightarrow{OC}\right| = \dfrac92$, then which of the following statements is (are) TRUE ?

    1. Projection of $\overrightarrow{OC}$ on $\overrightarrow{OA}$ is $-\dfrac32$
    2. Area of the triangle $OAB$ is $\dfrac92$
    3. Area of the triangle $ABC$ is $\dfrac92$
    4. The acute angle between the diagonals of the parallelogram with adjacent sides $\overrightarrow{OA}$ and $\overrightarrow{OC}$ is $\dfrac{\pi}{3}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a), (b), (c)

    $\overrightarrow{OA} \times \overrightarrow{OB} = (6, -3, -6)$, of magnitude $9$.
    $\overrightarrow{OB} \times \overrightarrow{OC} = -\dfrac{\lambda}{2}\,\overrightarrow{OB} \times \overrightarrow{OA}$, so $\dfrac{9\lambda}{2} = \dfrac92 \Rightarrow \lambda = 1$ and $\overrightarrow{OC} = \left(-\dfrac12, -2, \dfrac12\right)$.
    (A) $\dfrac{\overrightarrow{OC}\cdot\overrightarrow{OA}}{|\overrightarrow{OA}|} = \dfrac{-1 - 4 + \frac12}{3} = -\dfrac32$ → true.
    (B) Area $OAB = \dfrac12 \cdot 9 = \dfrac92$ → true.
    (C) $\overrightarrow{AB} = (-1, -4, 1)$, $\overrightarrow{AC} = \left(-\dfrac52, -4, -\dfrac12\right)$, $\overrightarrow{AB} \times \overrightarrow{AC} = (6, -3, -6)$; area $= \dfrac92$ → true.
    (D) Diagonals $\vec d_1 = \overrightarrow{OA} + \overrightarrow{OC} = \left(\dfrac32, 0, \dfrac32\right)$, $\vec d_2 = \overrightarrow{OA} - \overrightarrow{OC} = \left(\dfrac52, 4, \dfrac12\right)$.
    $\cos\theta = \dfrac{4.5}{\frac{3}{\sqrt2}\sqrt{22.5}} = \dfrac{1}{\sqrt5} \ne \dfrac12$ → false.
    Answer (A), (B), (C).
