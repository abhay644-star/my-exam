  1. JEE Advanced 2023, Paper 1 · Question 6
    Let $X = \left\{(x,y) \in \mathbb{Z}\times\mathbb{Z} : \dfrac{x^2}{8} + \dfrac{y^2}{20} < 1 \text{ and } y^2 < 5x\right\}$. Three distinct points $P$, $Q$ and $R$ are randomly chosen from $X$. Then the probability that $P$, $Q$ and $R$ form a triangle whose area is a positive integer, is

    1. $\dfrac{71}{220}$
    2. $\dfrac{73}{220}$
    3. $\dfrac{79}{220}$
    4. $\dfrac{83}{220}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    $y^2 < 5x$ needs $x \ge 1$, and $x^2 < 8$ gives $x \in \{1,2\}$.
    $x=1$: $y^2 < 5$ (and $< 17.5$): $y \in \{-2,\ldots,2\}$, 5 points. $x=2$: $y^2 < 10$: $y \in \{-3,\ldots,3\}$, 7 points. Total 12, so $\binom{12}{3} = 220$ choices.
    A triangle uses two points on one vertical line (gap $d$) and one on the other; the lines are 1 apart, so area $= \dfrac d2$, an integer iff $d$ is even.
    On $x=1$: pairs with even gap $= 3 + 1 = 4$, times 7 points on $x=2$: $28$.
    On $x=2$: pairs with even gap $= 5 + 3 + 1 = 9$, times 5 points on $x=1$: $45$.
    Probability $= \dfrac{73}{220}$. Answer (B).

  2. JEE Advanced 2021, Paper 2 · Question 17
    A number is chosen at random from the set $\{1, 2, 3, \ldots, 2000\}$. Let $p$ be the probability that the chosen number is a multiple of 3 or a multiple of 7. Then the value of $500p$ is ___ .

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 214

    Multiples of 3: $\left[\dfrac{2000}{3}\right] = 666$; of 7: $285$; of 21: $95$.
    Favourable $= 666 + 285 - 95 = 856$, $p = \dfrac{856}{2000}$.
    $500p = \dfrac{856}{4} = 214$.
