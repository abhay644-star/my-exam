  1. JEE Advanced 2026, Paper 1 · Question 6
    Let $P$ be the plane such that it contains the straight line $\frac{x-1}{2} = \frac{y-3}{3} = \frac{z+2}{1}$ and is perpendicular to the plane $x + 2y + 3z = 4$ . Let $P_1$ be the plane which passes through the point $(4, 2, 2)$ and is parallel to $P$ .

    Then which of the following statements is (are) TRUE ?

    1. The equation of the plane $P$ is $7x - 5y + z = -10$
    2. The distance between the planes $P$ and $P_1$ is $30$
    3. The distance of the plane $P$ from the origin is $2\sqrt{3}$
    4. The acute angle between the plane $P$ and the plane $2x + 2y + z = 3$ is $\cos^{-1}\left(\frac{1}{3\sqrt{3}}\right)$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a), (d)

    Normal of $P$ is perpendicular to $(2,3,1)$ and $(1,2,3)$: $(2,3,1)\times(1,2,3) = (7,-5,1)$.
    Through $(1,3,-2)$: $7(x-1) - 5(y-3) + (z+2) = 0 \Rightarrow 7x - 5y + z = -10$. (A) true.
    $P_1$: $7x - 5y + z = 28 - 10 + 2 = 20$. Distance $= \frac{30}{\sqrt{75}} = 2\sqrt3 \ne 30$. (B) false.
    Distance of $P$ from origin $= \frac{10}{5\sqrt3} = \frac{2}{\sqrt3}$. (C) false.
    $\cos\theta = \frac{|14 - 10 + 1|}{5\sqrt3 \cdot 3} = \frac{1}{3\sqrt3}$. (D) true. Answer (A), (D).

  2. JEE Advanced 2026, Paper 2 · Question 7
    Let $L$ be the straight line joining the points $P(1, 2, -1)$ and $Q(2, 3, 1)$. Let $S$ be the foot of the perpendicular drawn from the point $R(4, -1, 5)$ to the line $L$ . Another line passing through $R$ intersects $L$ at a point $T$ such that the point $S$ divides the line segment $PT$ internally in the ratio $|PS| : |ST| = 1 : 2$ , where $|PS|$ and $|ST|$ are the lengths of the line segments $PS$ and $ST$, respectively.

    Then which of the following statements is (are) TRUE ?

    1. The orthocentre of the triangle $PRT$ is $\left(\frac{23}{5}, -4, \frac{31}{5}\right)$
    2. The orthocentre of the triangle $PRT$ is $(4, 3, 5)$
    3. The area of the triangle $PRT$ is $6\sqrt{5}$
    4. The area of the triangle $PRT$ is $18\sqrt{5}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a), (d)

    $L$: $P+t(1,1,2)$. $(P+t\,\vec d-R)\cdot\vec d=6t-12=0\Rightarrow t=2$, $S=(3,4,3)$.
    $PS:ST=1:2\Rightarrow T=P+3(S-P)=(7,8,11)$.
    $RS\perp PT$: area $=\tfrac12|PT||RS|=\tfrac12\cdot6\sqrt6\cdot\sqrt{30}=18\sqrt5$.
    Orthocentre lies on altitude $RS$: $H=R+\lambda(S-R)=(4-\lambda,-1+5\lambda,5-2\lambda)$.
    $PH\perp RT$, $RT\parallel(1,3,2)$: $(3-\lambda)+3(5\lambda-3)+2(6-2\lambda)=6+10\lambda=0\Rightarrow\lambda=-\tfrac35$.
    $H=\left(\tfrac{23}{5},-4,\tfrac{31}{5}\right)$. So (A), (D).

  3. JEE Advanced 2025, Paper 1 · Question 5
    Let $L_1$ be the line of intersection of the planes given by the equations

    $\displaystyle 2x + 3y + z = 4 \quad \text{and} \quad x + 2y + z = 5.$
    Let $L_2$ be the line passing through the point $P(2, -1, 3)$ and parallel to $L_1$. Let $M$ denote the plane given by the equation
    $\displaystyle 2x + y - 2z = 6.$
    Suppose that the line $L_2$ meets the plane $M$ at the point $Q$. Let $R$ be the foot of the perpendicular drawn from $P$ to the plane $M$.

    Then which of the following statements is (are) TRUE?

    1. The length of the line segment $PQ$ is $9\sqrt{3}$
    2. The length of the line segment $QR$ is $15$
    3. The area of $\Delta PQR$ is $\dfrac{3}{2}\sqrt{234}$
    4. The acute angle between the line segments $PQ$ and $PR$ is $\cos^{-1}\left(\dfrac{1}{2\sqrt{3}}\right)$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a), (c)

    Direction of $L_1$: $(2,3,1)\times(1,2,1) = (1,-1,1)$.
    $L_2$: $(2+t,\,-1-t,\,3+t)$. In $M$: $2(2+t) + (-1-t) - 2(3+t) = 6 \Rightarrow -3 - t = 6 \Rightarrow t = -9$, so $Q = (-7, 8, -6)$.
    $PQ = 9\sqrt3$: (A) true.
    For $P$: $2(2) - 1 - 6 - 6 = -9$, so $R = P + \dfrac{9}{9}(2,1,-2) = (4,0,1)$ and $PR = 3$.
    $\angle PRQ = 90^\circ$, so $QR = \sqrt{243 - 9} = \sqrt{234} \neq 15$: (B) false.
    Area $= \dfrac12 \cdot PR \cdot QR = \dfrac32\sqrt{234}$: (C) true.
    $\cos\angle QPR = \dfrac{PR}{PQ} = \dfrac{3}{9\sqrt3} = \dfrac{1}{3\sqrt3} \neq \dfrac{1}{2\sqrt3}$: (D) false.
    Answer: (A), (C).

  4. JEE Advanced 2024, Paper 1 · Question 7
    Let $\mathbb{R}^3$ denote the three-dimensional space. Take two points $P = (1, 2, 3)$ and $Q = (4, 2, 7)$. Let $dist(X, Y)$ denote the distance between two points $X$ and $Y$ in $\mathbb{R}^3$. Let

    $\displaystyle S = \left\{X \in \mathbb{R}^3 : \left(dist(X, P)\right)^2 - \left(dist(X, Q)\right)^2 = 50\right\}$
    and
    $\displaystyle T = \left\{Y \in \mathbb{R}^3 : \left(dist(Y, Q)\right)^2 - \left(dist(Y, P)\right)^2 = 50\right\}.$
    Then which of the following statements is (are) TRUE?

    1. There is a triangle whose area is 1 and all of whose vertices are from $S$.
    2. There are two distinct points $L$ and $M$ in $T$ such that each point on the line segment $LM$ is also in $T$.
    3. There are infinitely many rectangles of perimeter 48, two of whose vertices are from $S$ and the other two vertices are from $T$.
    4. There is a square of perimeter 48, two of whose vertices are from $S$ and the other two vertices are from $T$.
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a), (b), (c), (d)

    For $X = (x, y, z)$: $|X-P|^2 - |X-Q|^2 = 2X\cdot(Q - P) + |P|^2 - |Q|^2$, with $Q - P = (3, 0, 4)$, $|P|^2 = 14$, $|Q|^2 = 69$.
    $S$: $6x + 8z - 55 = 50$, i.e. the plane $6x + 8z = 105$. $T$: $-(6x + 8z) + 55 = 50$, i.e. the plane $6x + 8z = 5$.
    (A), (B): $S$ and $T$ are planes, so they contain triangles of any area and whole line segments. TRUE.
    The planes are parallel, at distance $\dfrac{105 - 5}{\sqrt{36 + 64}} = 10$.
    (C) Take a rectangle with two sides joining the planes (each of length $\ell \ge 10$, at a suitable slant) and the other two sides of length $24 - \ell$ parallel to the planes. Any $\ell \in [10, 24)$ (and any position) works: infinitely many. TRUE.
    (D) A square of perimeter 48 has side $12 \ge 10$, so the same construction with $\ell = 12$ works. TRUE.
    Answer: (A), (B), (C), (D).

  5. JEE Advanced 2024, Paper 1 · Question 16
    Let $\gamma \in \mathbb{R}$ be such that the lines $L_1 : \dfrac{x+11}{1} = \dfrac{y+21}{2} = \dfrac{z+29}{3}$ and $L_2 : \dfrac{x+16}{3} = \dfrac{y+11}{2} = \dfrac{z+4}{\gamma}$ intersect. Let $R_1$ be the point of intersection of $L_1$ and $L_2$. Let $O = (0, 0, 0)$, and $\hat{n}$ denote a unit normal vector to the plane containing both the lines $L_1$ and $L_2$.
    Match each entry in List-I to the correct entry in List-II.

    List-IList-II
    (P) $\gamma$ equals(1) $-\hat{i} - \hat{j} + \hat{k}$
    (Q) A possible choice for $\hat{n}$ is(2) $\sqrt{\dfrac{3}{2}}$
    (R) $\overrightarrow{OR_1}$ equals(3) $1$
    (S) A possible value of $\overrightarrow{OR_1} \cdot \hat{n}$ is(4) $\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{6}}\hat{i} - \dfrac{2}{\sqrt{6}}\hat{j} + \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{6}}\hat{k}$
    (5) $\sqrt{\dfrac{2}{3}}$

    1. P→3, Q→4, R→1, S→2
    2. P→5, Q→4, R→1, S→2
    3. P→3, Q→4, R→1, S→5
    4. P→3, Q→1, R→4, S→5
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    Points of $L_1$: $(-11 + t, -21 + 2t, -29 + 3t)$; of $L_2$: $(-16 + 3s, -11 + 2s, -4 + \gamma s)$.
    $x$: $t = 3s - 5$; $y$: $2t = 10 + 2s \Rightarrow t = s + 5$. So $s = 5$, $t = 10$, and $R_1 = (-1, -1, 1)$.
    $z$: $-29 + 30 = 1 = -4 + 5\gamma \Rightarrow \gamma = 1$.
    Normal $\propto (1, 2, 3) \times (3, 2, 1) = (-4, 8, -4) \propto (1, -2, 1)$, so $\hat n = \dfrac{1}{\sqrt6}(\hat i - 2\hat j + \hat k)$.
    $\overrightarrow{OR_1}\cdot\hat n = \dfrac{-1 + 2 + 1}{\sqrt6} = \dfrac{2}{\sqrt6} = \sqrt{\dfrac23}$.
    P→3, Q→4, R→1, S→5 → (C).

  6. JEE Advanced 2024, Paper 2 · Question 6
    A straight line drawn from the point $P(1,3,2)$, parallel to the line $\dfrac{x-2}{1}=\dfrac{y-4}{2}=\dfrac{z-6}{1}$, intersects the plane $L_1 : x-y+3z=6$ at the point $Q$. Another straight line which passes through $Q$ and is perpendicular to the plane $L_1$ intersects the plane $L_2 : 2x-y+z=-4$ at the point $R$. Then which of the following statements is (are) TRUE?

    1. The length of the line segment $PQ$ is $\sqrt6$
    2. The coordinates of $R$ are $(1,6,3)$
    3. The centroid of the triangle $PQR$ is $\left(\dfrac43,\dfrac{14}{3},\dfrac53\right)$
    4. The perimeter of the triangle $PQR$ is $\sqrt2+\sqrt6+\sqrt{11}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a), (c)

    Line through $P$: $(1+t,\,3+2t,\,2+t)$. In $L_1$: $(1+t)-(3+2t)+3(2+t)=6\Rightarrow 2t=2$, $t=1$, so $Q=(2,5,3)$ and $PQ=\sqrt{1+4+1}=\sqrt6$ → (A) true.
    Line through $Q$ along $(1,-1,3)$: $(2+s,\,5-s,\,3+3s)$. In $L_2$: $2(2+s)-(5-s)+(3+3s)=-4\Rightarrow 6s=-6$, $s=-1$, so $R=(1,6,0)$ → (B) false.
    Centroid $=\left(\dfrac{1+2+1}{3},\dfrac{3+5+6}{3},\dfrac{2+3+0}{3}\right)=\left(\dfrac43,\dfrac{14}3,\dfrac53\right)$ → (C) true.
    $QR=\sqrt{1+1+9}=\sqrt{11}$, $PR=\sqrt{0+9+4}=\sqrt{13}$, so perimeter $=\sqrt6+\sqrt{11}+\sqrt{13}$ → (D) false.
    Answer: (A), (C).

  7. JEE Advanced 2023, Paper 1 · Question 5
    Let $Q$ be the cube with the set of vertices $\left\{(x_1,x_2,x_3) \in \mathbb{R}^3 : x_1, x_2, x_3 \in \{0,1\}\right\}$. Let $F$ be the set of all twelve lines containing the diagonals of the six faces of the cube $Q$. Let $S$ be the set of all four lines containing the main diagonals of the cube $Q$; for instance, the line passing through the vertices $(0,0,0)$ and $(1,1,1)$ is in $S$. For lines $\ell_1$ and $\ell_2$, let $d(\ell_1,\ell_2)$ denote the shortest distance between them. Then the maximum value of $d(\ell_1,\ell_2)$, as $\ell_1$ varies over $F$ and $\ell_2$ varies over $S$, is

    1. $\dfrac{1}{\sqrt6}$
    2. $\dfrac{1}{\sqrt8}$
    3. $\dfrac{1}{\sqrt3}$
    4. $\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{12}}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    By symmetry take $\ell_2$ through $(0,0,0)$ with direction $(1,1,1)$. A face diagonal that shares a vertex with it meets it (distance $0$).
    The others are skew, e.g. the diagonal from $(1,0,0)$ to $(0,1,0)$, direction $(-1,1,0)$.
    $(1,1,1)\times(-1,1,0) = (-1,-1,2)$, of length $\sqrt6$; joining vector $(1,0,0)$ gives $|(1,0,0)\cdot(-1,-1,2)| = 1$.
    So $d = \dfrac{1}{\sqrt6}$; every skew pair is equivalent to this one by symmetry. Answer (A).

  8. JEE Advanced 2023, Paper 1 · Question 16
    Let $\ell_1$ and $\ell_2$ be the lines $\vec{r}_1 = \lambda(\hat{i} + \hat{j} + \hat{k})$ and $\vec{r}_2 = (\hat{j} - \hat{k}) + \mu(\hat{i} + \hat{k})$, respectively. Let $X$ be the set of all the planes $H$ that contain the line $\ell_1$. For a plane $H$, let $d(H)$ denote the smallest possible distance between the points of $\ell_2$ and $H$. Let $H_0$ be a plane in $X$ for which $d(H_0)$ is the maximum value of $d(H)$ as $H$ varies over all planes in $X$.
    Match each entry in List-I to the correct entries in List-II.

    List-IList-II
    (P) The value of $d(H_0)$ is(1) $\sqrt3$
    (Q) The distance of the point $(0,1,2)$ from $H_0$ is(2) $\dfrac{1}{\sqrt3}$
    (R) The distance of origin from $H_0$ is(3) $0$
    (S) The distance of origin from the point of intersection of planes $y = z$, $x = 1$ and $H_0$ is(4) $\sqrt2$
    (5) $\dfrac{1}{\sqrt2}$

    1. (P) → (2), (Q) → (4), (R) → (5), (S) → (1)
    2. (P) → (5), (Q) → (4), (R) → (3), (S) → (1)
    3. (P) → (2), (Q) → (1), (R) → (3), (S) → (2)
    4. (P) → (5), (Q) → (1), (R) → (4), (S) → (2)
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    If $\ell_2$ is not parallel to $H$ it meets $H$ and $d(H) = 0$. So $H_0$ contains $\ell_1$ and is parallel to $\ell_2$.
    Normal: $(1,1,1)\times(1,0,1) = (1, 0, -1)$; $H_0$ passes through $O$: $x - z = 0$.
    (P) $d(H_0)$ = distance of $(0,1,-1)$: $\dfrac{|0 + 1|}{\sqrt2} = \dfrac{1}{\sqrt2}$ → (5).
    (Q) $(0,1,2)$: $\dfrac{|0 - 2|}{\sqrt2} = \sqrt2$ → (4). (R) $O$ lies on $H_0$: $0$ → (3).
    (S) $y = z$, $x = 1$, $x = z$ meet at $(1,1,1)$, at distance $\sqrt3$ from $O$ → (1).
    Answer (B).

  9. JEE Advanced 2022, Paper 1 · Question 11
    Let $P_1$ and $P_2$ be two planes given by

    $\displaystyle P_1:\ 10x+15y+12z-60=0,\qquad P_2:\ -2x+5y+4z-20=0.$
    Which of the following straight lines can be an edge of some tetrahedron whose two faces lie on $P_1$ and $P_2$ ?

    1. $\dfrac{x-1}{0}=\dfrac{y-1}{0}=\dfrac{z-1}{5}$
    2. $\dfrac{x-6}{-5}=\dfrac{y}{2}=\dfrac{z}{3}$
    3. $\dfrac{x}{-2}=\dfrac{y-4}{5}=\dfrac{z}{4}$
    4. $\dfrac{x}{1}=\dfrac{y-4}{-2}=\dfrac{z}{3}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a), (b), (d)

    The two faces share an edge on the line $L=P_1\cap P_2$. The other vertices are a point $U\in P_1$ and a point $V\in P_2$, off $L$. So an edge is $L$ itself, a line in $P_1$ or $P_2$ meeting $L$, or a line $UV$ meeting $P_1$ and $P_2$ at two different points.
    (A) Through $(1,1,1)$, direction $(0,0,1)$: meets $P_1$ at $\left(1,1,\tfrac{35}{12}\right)$ and $P_2$ at $\left(1,1,\tfrac{17}{4}\right)$, neither on $L$ — possible.
    (B) $(6,0,0)$ lies on $P_1$ (not on $P_2$); the line meets $P_2$ at $(1,2,3)$, which is not on $P_1$ — possible.
    (C) $(0,4,0)$ lies on both planes (on $L$), and the direction $(-2,5,4)$ is parallel to neither plane, so the line meets each plane only at that one point: it cannot hold two vertices — not possible.
    (D) Direction $(1,-2,3)$ satisfies $-2-10+12=0$, so it is parallel to $P_2$ and passes through $(0,4,0)\in L$: it lies in $P_2$ and meets $L$ — possible.
    Answer: (A), (B), (D).

  10. JEE Advanced 2022, Paper 1 · Question 12
    Let $S$ be the reflection of a point $Q$ with respect to the plane given by

    $\displaystyle \vec{r}=-(t+p)\hat{i}+t\hat{j}+(1+p)\hat{k}$
    where $t,p$ are real parameters and $\hat{i},\hat{j},\hat{k}$ are the unit vectors along the three positive coordinate axes. If the position vectors of $Q$ and $S$ are $10\hat{i}+15\hat{j}+20\hat{k}$ and $\alpha\hat{i}+\beta\hat{j}+\gamma\hat{k}$ respectively, then which of the following is/are TRUE ?

    1. $3(\alpha+\beta)=-101$
    2. $3(\beta+\gamma)=-71$
    3. $3(\gamma+\alpha)=-86$
    4. $3(\alpha+\beta+\gamma)=-121$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a), (b), (c)

    $x=-(t+p)$, $y=t$, $z=1+p$ give $x+y+z=1$: the plane is $x+y+z=1$.
    Reflection of $(10,15,20)$: $\lambda=-\dfrac{2(10+15+20-1)}{3}=-\dfrac{88}{3}$, so $S=\left(-\dfrac{58}{3},-\dfrac{43}{3},-\dfrac{28}{3}\right)$.
    $3(\alpha+\beta)=-101$, $3(\beta+\gamma)=-71$, $3(\gamma+\alpha)=-86$, $3(\alpha+\beta+\gamma)=-129$.
    So (A), (B), (C) are true and (D) is false.

  11. JEE Advanced 2021, Paper 1 · Question 8
    Let $\alpha, \beta$ and $\gamma$ be real numbers such that the system of linear equations $$\begin{aligned} x + 2y + 3z &= \alpha \\ 4x + 5y + 6z &= \beta \\ 7x + 8y + 9z &= \gamma - 1 \end{aligned}$$ is consistent. Let $|M|$ represent the determinant of the matrix $$M = \begin{bmatrix} \alpha & 2 & \gamma \\ \beta & 1 & 0 \\ -1 & 0 & 1 \end{bmatrix}$$ Let $P$ be the plane containing all those $(\alpha, \beta, \gamma)$ for which the above system of linear equations is consistent, and $D$ be the square of the distance of the point $(0, 1, 0)$ from the plane $P$.

    The value of $D$ is ___ .

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 1.45 to 1.55

    From the consistency condition, $P$ is the plane $x - 2y + z = 1$.
    Distance of $(0, 1, 0)$: $\dfrac{|0 - 2 + 0 - 1|}{\sqrt{1 + 4 + 1}} = \dfrac{3}{\sqrt6}$.
    $D = \dfrac{9}{6} = 1.5$.
