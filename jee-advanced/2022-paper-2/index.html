Solved questions from this paper

A few questions with their official answers and worked solutions. All 54 are in the online test.

  1. Question 15 · Mathematics · Permutations and Combinations
    Consider 4 boxes, where each box contains 3 red balls and 2 blue balls. Assume that all 20 balls are distinct. In how many different ways can 10 balls be chosen from these 4 boxes so that from each box at least one red ball and one blue ball are chosen ?

    1. $21816$
    2. $85536$
    3. $12096$
    4. $156816$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    From one box, with at least one red and one blue: 2 balls in $3\cdot2 = 6$ ways, 3 balls in $\binom32\cdot2 + 3\cdot1 = 9$ ways, 4 balls in $2 + 3 = 5$ ways, 5 balls in $1$ way.
    Each box gives at least 2 balls (8 in all), so 2 extra balls are spread over the boxes.
    One box takes 4 balls: $4\cdot5\cdot6^3 = 4320$.
    Two boxes take 3 balls each: $\binom42\cdot9^2\cdot6^2 = 17496$.
    Total $= 21816$ → (A).

  2. Question 16 · Mathematics · Matrices
    If $M = \begin{pmatrix} \frac52 & \frac32 \\ -\frac32 & -\frac12 \end{pmatrix}$, then which of the following matrices is equal to $M^{2022}$ ?

    1. $\begin{pmatrix} 3034 & 3033 \\ -3033 & -3032 \end{pmatrix}$
    2. $\begin{pmatrix} 3034 & -3033 \\ 3033 & -3032 \end{pmatrix}$
    3. $\begin{pmatrix} 3033 & 3032 \\ -3032 & -3031 \end{pmatrix}$
    4. $\begin{pmatrix} 3032 & 3031 \\ -3031 & -3030 \end{pmatrix}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    $M = I + N$ with $N = \begin{pmatrix} \frac32 & \frac32 \\ -\frac32 & -\frac32 \end{pmatrix}$, and $N^2 = O$.
    So $M^n = I + nN$; for $n = 2022$, $nN$ has entries $\pm2022\cdot\dfrac32 = \pm3033$.
    $M^{2022} = \begin{pmatrix} 3034 & 3033 \\ -3033 & -3032 \end{pmatrix}$ → (A).

  3. Question 19 · Physics · System of Particles and Rotational Motion
    A particle of mass $1$ kg is subjected to a force which depends on the position as $\vec F = -k(x\hat\imath + y\hat\jmath)$ kg m s$^{-2}$ with $k = 1$ kg s$^{-2}$. At time $t = 0$, the particle’s position $\vec r = \left(\dfrac{1}{\sqrt2}\hat\imath + \sqrt2\hat\jmath\right)$ m and its velocity $\vec v = \left(-\sqrt2\hat\imath + \sqrt2\hat\jmath + \dfrac{2}{\pi}\hat k\right)$ m s$^{-1}$. Let $v_x$ and $v_y$ denote the $x$ and the $y$ components of the particle’s velocity, respectively. Ignore gravity. When $z = 0.5$ m, the value of $(x v_y - y v_x)$ is ________ m$^2$s$^{-1}$.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 3

    The force has no $z$ component and in the $xy$ plane it points towards the $z$-axis, so its torque about the $z$-axis is zero.
    Hence $L_z = m(xv_y - yv_x)$ is conserved, whatever the value of $z$.
    At $t = 0$: $xv_y - yv_x = \dfrac1{\sqrt2}\cdot\sqrt2 - \sqrt2\cdot(-\sqrt2) = 1 + 2 = 3$ m$^2$s$^{-1}$.

  4. Question 34 · Physics · Dual Nature of Radiation and Matter
    When light of a given wavelength is incident on a metallic surface, the minimum potential needed to stop the emitted photoelectrons is $6.0$ V. This potential drops to $0.6$ V if another source with wavelength four times that of the first one and intensity half of the first one is used. What are the wavelength of the first source and the work function of the metal, respectively? [Take $\dfrac{hc}{e} = 1.24\times10^{-6}$ J m C$^{-1}$.]

    1. $1.72\times10^{-7}$ m, $1.20$ eV
    2. $1.72\times10^{-7}$ m, $5.60$ eV
    3. $3.78\times10^{-7}$ m, $5.60$ eV
    4. $3.78\times10^{-7}$ m, $1.20$ eV
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    In eV: $6.0 = \dfrac{1.24\times10^{-6}}{\lambda} - \phi$ and $0.6 = \dfrac{1.24\times10^{-6}}{4\lambda} - \phi$ (intensity does not affect stopping potential).
    Subtracting: $5.4 = \dfrac34\cdot\dfrac{1.24\times10^{-6}}{\lambda}$, so $\dfrac{1.24\times10^{-6}}{\lambda} = 7.2$ eV and $\lambda = 1.72\times10^{-7}$ m.
    $\phi = 7.2 - 6.0 = 1.20$ eV → (A).

  5. Question 51 · Chemistry · Some Basic Concepts of Chemistry
    Atom X occupies the fcc lattice sites as well as alternate tetrahedral voids of the same lattice. The packing efficiency (in %) of the resultant solid is closest to

    1. 25
    2. 35
    3. 55
    4. 75
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    Per unit cell: 4 atoms at fcc sites + 4 in alternate tetrahedral voids = 8 atoms (diamond-like).
    Atoms in the voids touch the fcc atoms along the body diagonal: $2r = \dfrac{\sqrt3a}{4}$, so $r = \dfrac{\sqrt3a}{8}$.
    Packing fraction $= \dfrac{8\cdot\frac43\pi r^3}{a^3} = \dfrac{32\pi}{3}\cdot\dfrac{3\sqrt3}{512} = \dfrac{\sqrt3\pi}{16} \approx 0.34$.
    About 34%, closest to 35 → (B).

  6. Question 52 · Chemistry · Redox Reactions
    The reaction of $\mathrm{HClO_3}$ with HCl gives a paramagnetic gas, which upon reaction with $\mathrm{O_3}$ produces

    1. $\mathrm{Cl_2O}$
    2. $\mathrm{ClO_2}$
    3. $\mathrm{Cl_2O_6}$
    4. $\mathrm{Cl_2O_7}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    $\mathrm{2HClO_3 + 2HCl \rightarrow 2ClO_2 + Cl_2 + 2H_2O}$; $\mathrm{ClO_2}$ (odd electron) is the paramagnetic gas.
    $\mathrm{2ClO_2 + 2O_3 \rightarrow Cl_2O_6 + 2O_2}$ → (C).
