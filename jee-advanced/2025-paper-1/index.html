A few questions with their official answers and worked solutions. All 48 are in the online test.

  1. Question 1 · Mathematics · Relations and Functions
    Let $\mathbb{R}$ denote the set of all real numbers. Let $a_i, b_i \in \mathbb{R}$ for $i \in \{1, 2, 3\}$.
    Define the functions $f : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$, $g : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$, and $h : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ by

    $\displaystyle f(x) = a_1 + 10x + a_2x^2 + a_3x^3 + x^4,$
    $\displaystyle g(x) = b_1 + 3x + b_2x^2 + b_3x^3 + x^4,$
    $\displaystyle h(x) = f(x+1) - g(x+2).$
    If $f(x) \neq g(x)$ for every $x \in \mathbb{R}$, then the coefficient of $x^3$ in $h(x)$ is

    1. $8$
    2. $2$
    3. $-4$
    4. $-6$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    $f(x) - g(x) = (a_3-b_3)x^3 + (a_2-b_2)x^2 + 7x + (a_1-b_1)$.
    A real cubic always has a real root, so $f - g$ can never be zero only if it is not a cubic: $a_3 = b_3$.
    Coefficient of $x^3$ in $f(x+1)$: from $(x+1)^4$ it is $4$, from $a_3(x+1)^3$ it is $a_3$.
    Coefficient of $x^3$ in $g(x+2)$: from $(x+2)^4$ it is $4\cdot 2 = 8$, from $b_3(x+2)^3$ it is $b_3$.
    Coefficient in $h$: $4 + a_3 - 8 - b_3 = -4$ → (C).

  2. Question 2 · Mathematics · Probability
    Three students $S_1, S_2$, and $S_3$ are given a problem to solve. Consider the following events:
    $U$: At least one of $S_1, S_2$, and $S_3$ can solve the problem,
    $V$: $S_1$ can solve the problem, given that neither $S_2$ nor $S_3$ can solve the problem,
    $W$: $S_2$ can solve the problem and $S_3$ cannot solve the problem,
    $T$: $S_3$ can solve the problem.

    For any event $E$, let $P(E)$ denote the probability of $E$. If

    $\displaystyle P(U) = \dfrac{1}{2}, \quad P(V) = \dfrac{1}{10}, \quad \text{and} \quad P(W) = \dfrac{1}{12},$
    then $P(T)$ is equal to

    1. $\dfrac{13}{36}$
    2. $\dfrac{1}{3}$
    3. $\dfrac{19}{60}$
    4. $\dfrac{1}{4}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    Let $p_1, p_2, p_3$ be the (independent) probabilities that $S_1, S_2, S_3$ solve the problem.
    $P(V) = p_1 = \dfrac{1}{10}$ (by independence).
    $P(U) = 1 - (1-p_1)(1-p_2)(1-p_3) = \dfrac12 \Rightarrow (1-p_2)(1-p_3) = \dfrac{1/2}{9/10} = \dfrac59$.
    $P(W) = p_2(1-p_3) = \dfrac{1}{12}$.
    Adding: $(1-p_3) = \dfrac59 + \dfrac1{12} = \dfrac{23}{36}$, so $P(T) = p_3 = \dfrac{13}{36}$ → (A).

  3. Question 22 · Physics · Units and Measurements
    Length, breadth and thickness of a strip having a uniform cross section are measured to be $10.5$ cm, $0.05$ mm, and $6.0\ \mu$m, respectively. Which of the following option(s) give(s) the volume of the strip in cm$^3$ with correct significant figures:

    1. $3.2\times10^{-5}$
    2. $32.0\times10^{-6}$
    3. $3.0\times10^{-5}$
    4. $3\times10^{-5}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    $V = 10.5 \times 0.005 \times 6.0\times10^{-4}$ cm$^3 = 3.15\times10^{-5}$ cm$^3$.
    The breadth $0.05$ mm has only one significant figure, so the product is quoted to one significant figure.
    $V = 3\times10^{-5}$ cm$^3$ → only (D).

  4. Question 24 · Physics · Laws of Motion
    A person sitting inside an elevator performs a weighing experiment with an object of mass $50$ kg. Suppose that the variation of the height $y$ (in m) of the elevator, from the ground, with time $t$ (in s) is given by $y = 8\left[1 + \sin\left(\dfrac{2\pi t}{T}\right)\right]$, where $T = 40\pi$ s. Taking acceleration due to gravity, $g = 10$ m/s$^2$, the maximum variation of the object’s weight (in N) as observed in the experiment is ______

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 2

    $\dfrac{2\pi}{T} = \dfrac{2\pi}{40\pi} = \dfrac{1}{20}$ s$^{-1}$, so $a = \ddot y = -8\left(\dfrac{1}{20}\right)^2\sin\dfrac{2\pi t}{T}$, with $|a|_{\max} = \dfrac{8}{400} = 0.02$ m/s$^2$.
    Observed weight $= m(g + a)$ ranges from $m(g - 0.02)$ to $m(g + 0.02)$.
    Maximum variation $= 50 \times 2 \times 0.02 = 2$ N.

  5. Question 33 · Chemistry · Redox Reactions
    The heating of $\mathrm{NH_4NO_2}$ at $60-70\ {}^\circ$C and $\mathrm{NH_4NO_3}$ at $200-250\ {}^\circ$C is associated with the formation of nitrogen containing compounds X and Y, respectively. X and Y, respectively, are

    1. $\mathrm{N_2}$ and $\mathrm{N_2O}$
    2. $\mathrm{NH_3}$ and $\mathrm{NO_2}$
    3. $\mathrm{NO}$ and $\mathrm{N_2O}$
    4. $\mathrm{N_2}$ and $\mathrm{NH_3}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    $\mathrm{NH_4NO_2} \xrightarrow{\Delta} \mathrm{N_2} + 2\mathrm{H_2O}$, so X $= \mathrm{N_2}$.
    $\mathrm{NH_4NO_3} \xrightarrow{200-250\,{}^\circ\mathrm{C}} \mathrm{N_2O} + 2\mathrm{H_2O}$, so Y $= \mathrm{N_2O}$.
    Answer (A).

  6. Question 34 · Chemistry · Coordination Compounds
    The correct order of the wavelength maxima of the absorption band in the ultraviolet-visible region for the given complexes is

    1. $[\mathrm{Co(CN)_6}]^{3-} \lt [\mathrm{Co(NH_3)_6}]^{3+} \lt [\mathrm{Co(NH_3)_5(H_2O)}]^{3+} \lt [\mathrm{Co(NH_3)_5(Cl)}]^{2+}$
    2. $[\mathrm{Co(NH_3)_5(Cl)}]^{2+} \lt [\mathrm{Co(NH_3)_5(H_2O)}]^{3+} \lt [\mathrm{Co(NH_3)_6}]^{3+} \lt [\mathrm{Co(CN)_6}]^{3-}$
    3. $[\mathrm{Co(CN)_6}]^{3-} \lt [\mathrm{Co(NH_3)_5(Cl)}]^{2+} \lt [\mathrm{Co(NH_3)_5(H_2O)}]^{3+} \lt [\mathrm{Co(NH_3)_6}]^{3+}$
    4. $[\mathrm{Co(NH_3)_6}]^{3+} \lt [\mathrm{Co(CN)_6}]^{3-} \lt [\mathrm{Co(NH_3)_5(Cl)}]^{2+} \lt [\mathrm{Co(NH_3)_5(H_2O)}]^{3+}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    All are Co(III) complexes; $\lambda_{\max} \propto \dfrac{1}{\Delta_o}$.
    Spectrochemical series: $\mathrm{CN^-} \gt \mathrm{NH_3} \gt \mathrm{H_2O} \gt \mathrm{Cl^-}$, so $\Delta_o$: $[\mathrm{Co(CN)_6}]^{3-} \gt [\mathrm{Co(NH_3)_6}]^{3+} \gt [\mathrm{Co(NH_3)_5(H_2O)}]^{3+} \gt [\mathrm{Co(NH_3)_5Cl}]^{2+}$.
    $\lambda_{\max}$ follows the reverse order → (A).
