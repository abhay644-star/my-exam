Questions students ask

Are these the official JEE Advanced papers?

Yes. The questions and the final answer keys are the official ones published by the organising IIT. The worked solutions are written by All in All Exam Practice.

Does the site use the real partial marking?

Yes. One-or-more-correct questions give +4 when all correct options are chosen and +3, +2 or +1 when only some correct options and no wrong option are chosen; any wrong option gives −1. Numerical questions have no negative marks.

Do I need to sign up or pay?

No. It is free, with no sign-up. Your answers and scores stay on your own device.
