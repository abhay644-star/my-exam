Solved questions from this paper

A few questions with their official answers and worked solutions. All 57 are in the online test.

  1. Question 1 · Mathematics · Conic Sections
    Consider a triangle $\Delta$ whose two sides lie on the $x$-axis and the line $x + y + 1 = 0$. If the orthocenter of $\Delta$ is $(1, 1)$, then the equation of the circle passing through the vertices of the triangle $\Delta$ is

    1. $x^2 + y^2 - 3x + y = 0$
    2. $x^2 + y^2 + x + 3y = 0$
    3. $x^2 + y^2 + 2y - 1 = 0$
    4. $x^2 + y^2 + x + y = 0$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    The two given sides meet at $A = (-1, 0)$. Let $B = (b, 0)$ lie on the $x$-axis and $C$ on $x + y + 1 = 0$.
    The altitude from $C$ is perpendicular to the $x$-axis and passes through $H(1, 1)$, so $C$ has $x = 1$: $C = (1, -2)$.
    The altitude from $B$ is perpendicular to $x + y + 1 = 0$ (slope $1$) and passes through $H$: it is $y = x$, which meets the $x$-axis at $B = (0, 0)$.
    Circle $x^2 + y^2 + Dx + Ey + F = 0$ through $(0,0)$: $F = 0$; through $(-1, 0)$: $1 - D = 0$, $D = 1$; through $(1, -2)$: $5 + 1 - 2E = 0$, $E = 3$.
    So the circle is $x^2 + y^2 + x + 3y = 0$ → (B).

  2. Question 2 · Mathematics · Application of Integrals
    The area of the region

    $\displaystyle \left\{(x, y) : 0 \le x \le \dfrac{9}{4},\quad 0 \le y \le 1,\quad x \ge 3y,\quad x + y \ge 2\right\}$
    is

    1. $\dfrac{11}{32}$
    2. $\dfrac{35}{96}$
    3. $\dfrac{37}{96}$
    4. $\dfrac{13}{32}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    The lines $x = 3y$ and $x + y = 2$ meet at $\left(\dfrac32, \dfrac12\right)$; the line $x = 3y$ meets $x = \dfrac94$ at $y = \dfrac34$.
    For $0 \le y \le \dfrac12$: $x$ runs from $2 - y$ to $\dfrac94$. For $\dfrac12 \le y \le \dfrac34$: $x$ runs from $3y$ to $\dfrac94$.
    Area $= \displaystyle\int_0^{1/2}\left(y + \dfrac14\right)dy + \int_{1/2}^{3/4}\left(\dfrac94 - 3y\right)dy = \dfrac14 + \dfrac{3}{32} = \dfrac{11}{32}$ → (A).

  3. Question 23 · Physics · Nuclei
    A heavy nucleus $Q$ of half-life 20 minutes undergoes alpha-decay with probability of 60% and beta-decay with probability of 40%. Initially, the number of $Q$ nuclei is 1000. The number of alpha-decays of $Q$ in the first one hour is

    1. 50
    2. 75
    3. 350
    4. 525
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    One hour is three half-lives, so the nuclei left are $1000 \times \left(\dfrac12\right)^3 = 125$.
    Total decays $= 1000 - 125 = 875$.
    Alpha-decays $= 0.6 \times 875 = 525$ → (D).

  4. Question 24 · Physics · Laws of Motion
    A projectile is thrown from a point O on the ground at an angle $45^\circ$ from the vertical and with a speed $5\sqrt2$ m/s. The projectile at the highest point of its trajectory splits into two equal parts. One part falls vertically down to the ground, 0.5 s after the splitting. The other part, $t$ seconds after the splitting, falls to the ground at a distance $x$ meters from the point O. The acceleration due to gravity $g = 10$ m/s$^2$.

    The value of $t$ is ___ .

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 0.45 to 0.55

    Velocity components: $u_x = u_y = 5$ m/s. Highest point after $0.5$ s, at height $H = \dfrac{5^2}{2 \times 10} = 1.25$ m and $2.5$ m from O.
    A part falling from rest through $1.25$ m takes $\sqrt{2H/g} = 0.5$ s, as stated, so that part has zero velocity just after splitting.
    Momentum conservation: $m(5) = \dfrac m2(0) + \dfrac m2 v \Rightarrow v = 10$ m/s, horizontal.
    The other part also starts with zero vertical velocity, so it too lands after $t = 0.5$ s.

  5. Question 42 · Chemistry · Coordination Compounds
    The calculated spin only magnetic moments of $\mathrm{[Cr(NH_3)_6]^{3+}}$ and $\mathrm{[CuF_6]^{3-}}$ in BM, respectively, are
    (Atomic numbers of Cr and Cu are 24 and 29, respectively)

    1. 3.87 and 2.84
    2. 4.90 and 1.73
    3. 3.87 and 1.73
    4. 4.90 and 2.84
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    $\mathrm{Cr^{3+}}$ is $d^3$: three unpaired electrons whatever the ligand; $\mu = \sqrt{3 \times 5} = 3.87$ BM.
    $\mathrm{Cu^{3+}}$ is $d^8$; in the octahedral complex with weak-field $\mathrm{F^-}$ the configuration is $t_{2g}^6e_g^2$, two unpaired electrons; $\mu = \sqrt{2 \times 4} = 2.84$ BM.
    Answer (A).

  6. Question 51 · Chemistry · Solutions
    The correct statement(s) related to colloids is(are)

    1. The process of precipitating colloidal sol by an electrolyte is called peptization.
    2. Colloidal solution freezes at higher temperature than the true solution at the same concentration.
    3. Surfactants form micelle above critical micelle concentration (CMC). CMC depends on temperature.
    4. Micelles are macromolecular colloids.
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b), (c)

    (A) Precipitating a sol with an electrolyte is coagulation; peptization is the reverse (turning a precipitate into a sol): false.
    (B) At the same mass concentration a colloid has far fewer particles, so its freezing point depression is smaller and it freezes at a higher temperature: true.
    (C) Micelles form only above the CMC, and the CMC varies with temperature (micelles form above the Kraft temperature): true.
    (D) Micelles are associated colloids, not macromolecular colloids: false.
    Answer (B), (C).
