  1. JEE Advanced 2026, Paper 1 · Question 24
    The electric field associated with an electromagnetic wave travelling in vacuum is given by $E_0\sin(3y + 4z + \omega t)\hat{\imath}$, where $\omega$ is the angular frequency. All quantities are in SI units. The correct statement(s) about this wave is/are:
    [Given: speed of light in vacuum $c = 3 \times 10^8$ ms$^{-1}$.]

    1. The wave is travelling in $-\frac{1}{5}(3\hat{\jmath} + 4\hat{k})$ direction.
    2. The magnitude of the wave vector is $0.5$ m$^{-1}$.
    3. The value of $\omega$ is $1.5 \times 10^9$ rad s$^{-1}$.
    4. The magnetic field associated with this wave is given by $\frac{E_0}{c}\sin(3y + 4z + \omega t)(4\hat{\jmath} - 3\hat{k})$.
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a), (c)

    Phase $\vec k\cdot\vec r + \omega t$ with $\vec k = 3\hat\jmath + 4\hat k$ means propagation along $-\vec k$, unit vector $-\tfrac15(3\hat\jmath + 4\hat k)$: (A) true.
    $|\vec k| = 5$ m$^{-1}$: (B) false.
    $\omega = c|\vec k| = 1.5\times10^9$ rad s$^{-1}$: (C) true.
    $\vec B = \tfrac1c\,\hat n \times \vec E$ with $\hat n = -\tfrac15(3\hat\jmath + 4\hat k)$: $\hat n \times \hat\imath = \tfrac15(3\hat k - 4\hat\jmath)$, so $\vec B = \tfrac{E_0}{5c}\sin(\dots)(3\hat k - 4\hat\jmath)$ — (D) has the wrong sign and magnitude: false.

  2. JEE Advanced 2025, Paper 1 · Question 25
    A cube of unit volume contains $35\times10^7$ photons of frequency $10^{15}$ Hz. If the energy of all the photons is viewed as the average energy being contained in the electromagnetic waves within the same volume, then the amplitude of the magnetic field is $\alpha\times10^{-9}$ T. Taking permeability of free space $\mu_0 = 4\pi\times10^{-7}$ Tm/A, Planck’s constant $h = 6\times10^{-34}$ Js and $\pi = \dfrac{22}{7}$, the value of $\alpha$ is ______

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 21 to 25

    Energy density $u = Nh\nu = 35\times10^7 \times 6\times10^{-34} \times 10^{15} = 2.1\times10^{-10}$ J m$^{-3}$.
    Average energy density of an EM wave: $u = \dfrac{B_0^2}{2\mu_0}$.
    $B_0^2 = 2\mu_0u = 2 \times 4 \times \dfrac{22}{7} \times 10^{-7} \times 2.1\times10^{-10} = 5.28\times10^{-16}$ T$^2$.
    $B_0 = 2.30\times10^{-8}$ T $= 23\times10^{-9}$ T, so $\alpha \approx 23$.

  3. JEE Advanced 2023, Paper 2 · Question 24
    The electric field associated with an electromagnetic wave propagating in a dielectric medium is given by $\vec E = 30(2\hat x + \hat y)\sin\left[2\pi\left(5\times10^{14}\,t - \dfrac{10^7}{3}z\right)\right]$ V m$^{-1}$. Which of the following option(s) is(are) correct?
    [Given: The speed of light in vacuum, $c = 3\times10^8$ m s$^{-1}$]

    1. $B_x = -2\times10^{-7}\sin\left[2\pi\left(5\times10^{14}\,t - \dfrac{10^7}{3}z\right)\right]$ Wb m$^{-2}$.
    2. $B_y = 2\times10^{-7}\sin\left[2\pi\left(5\times10^{14}\,t - \dfrac{10^7}{3}z\right)\right]$ Wb m$^{-2}$.
    3. The wave is polarized in the $xy$-plane with polarization angle $30^\circ$ with respect to the $x$-axis.
    4. The refractive index of the medium is $2$.
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a), (d)

    $\omega = 2\pi\times5\times10^{14}$, $k = 2\pi\times\dfrac{10^7}{3}$, so $v = \dfrac{\omega}{k} = 1.5\times10^8$ m/s and $n = \dfrac cv = 2$ → (D).
    The wave travels along $+z$: $\vec B = \dfrac1v\,\hat z\times\vec E = \dfrac{30}{1.5\times10^8}(2\hat y - \hat x)\sin[\ldots] = (-2\hat x + 4\hat y)\times10^{-7}\sin[\ldots]$.
    So $B_x = -2\times10^{-7}\sin[\ldots]$ → (A); $B_y = 4\times10^{-7}\sin[\ldots]$, so (B) is false.
    Polarisation angle with the $x$-axis: $\tan^{-1}\dfrac12 \approx 26.6^\circ \ne 30^\circ$: (C) false.
    Answer (A), (D).
