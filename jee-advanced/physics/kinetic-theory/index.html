  1. JEE Advanced 2023, Paper 2 · Question 21
    An ideal gas is in thermodynamic equilibrium. The number of degrees of freedom of a molecule of the gas is $n$. The internal energy of one mole of the gas is $U_n$ and the speed of sound in the gas is $\mathrm{v}_n$. At a fixed temperature and pressure, which of the following is the correct option?

    1. $\mathrm{v}_3 < \mathrm{v}_6$ and $U_3 > U_6$
    2. $\mathrm{v}_5 > \mathrm{v}_3$ and $U_3 > U_5$
    3. $\mathrm{v}_5 > \mathrm{v}_7$ and $U_5 < U_7$
    4. $\mathrm{v}_6 < \mathrm{v}_7$ and $U_6 < U_7$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    $U_n = \dfrac n2 RT$ increases with $n$.
    $\mathrm{v}_n = \sqrt{\dfrac{\gamma RT}{M}}$ with $\gamma = 1 + \dfrac2n$, which decreases as $n$ increases.
    So $\mathrm{v}_5 > \mathrm{v}_7$ and $U_5 < U_7$ → (C). (A), (B), (D) each contradict one of these trends.
