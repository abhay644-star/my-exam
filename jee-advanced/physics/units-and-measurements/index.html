  1. JEE Advanced 2026, Paper 2 · Question 28
    Two thin wires, Wire-1 of diameter $0.650$ mm and Wire-2 of unknown diameter $d$ are given. To obtain the value of $d$, the diameters of the two wires are measured with a screw gauge. The screw gauge has a pitch of $0.5$ mm and there are $100$ divisions on the circular scale (CS). The smallest division on the linear scale (LS) is $0.5$ mm. The table shows the readings of LS and CS for the measurements. The value of $d$ (in μm) is:

    Readings
    LS (mm)CS
    Wire-10.542
    Wire-21.595

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 1915

    Least count $= 0.5/100 = 0.005$ mm.
    Wire-1 reading $= 0.5 + 42(0.005) = 0.710$ mm; true value $0.650$ mm, so zero error $= +0.060$ mm.
    Wire-2 reading $= 1.5 + 95(0.005) = 1.975$ mm.
    $d = 1.975 - 0.060 = 1.915$ mm $= 1915\ \mu$m.

  2. JEE Advanced 2026, Paper 2 · Question 29
    In a single slit diffraction experiment, a slit of width $(0.016 \pm 0.002)$ mm is used to measure the wavelength of a monochromatic light source. In the diffraction pattern, the angular distance between the central maximum and first minimum is measured to be $(2^\circ \pm 40')$. The value of the fractional error in the measurement of wavelength is:
    [Given: $\sin(2^\circ) = 0.035$]

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 0.43 to 0.5

    First minimum: $a\sin\theta = \lambda$.
    $\dfrac{\Delta\lambda}{\lambda} = \dfrac{\Delta a}{a} + \cot\theta\,\Delta\theta$.
    $\dfrac{\Delta a}{a} = \dfrac{0.002}{0.016} = 0.125$.
    $\Delta\theta = 40' = \tfrac{2}{3}^\circ = 0.01164$ rad, $\cot 2^\circ \approx \dfrac{1}{0.035} \approx 28.6$, giving $\approx 0.33$.
    Fractional error $\approx 0.125 + 0.33 \approx 0.46$.

  3. JEE Advanced 2026, Paper 2 · Question 32
    In a new system of units, the units of mass, length, time and current are $5$ kg, $5$ m, $5$ s and $5$ A, respectively. If $\mu_0$ and $\epsilon_0$ are the permeability and permittivity of free space, respectively, then in this new system of units, the magnitude of one SI unit of $\sqrt{\mu_0/\epsilon_0}$, is:

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 25

    $\sqrt{\mu_0/\epsilon_0}$ has the dimensions of resistance: $[M L^2 T^{-3} A^{-2}]$.
    New unit $= 5\cdot5^2\cdot5^{-3}\cdot5^{-2} = 5^{-2}$ SI units $= \tfrac{1}{25}$ SI unit.
    So one SI unit $= 25$ new units.

  4. JEE Advanced 2025, Paper 1 · Question 20
    Figure 1 shows the configuration of main scale and Vernier scale before measurement. Fig. 2 shows the configuration corresponding to the measurement of diameter $D$ of a tube. The measured value of $D$ is:

    1. 0.12 cm
    2. 0.11 cm
    3. 0.13 cm
    4. 0.14 cm
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    Main scale: $1$ cm has $10$ divisions, so $1$ MSD $= 1$ mm.
    Fig. 1: the zeros coincide (no zero error) and $10$ Vernier divisions span $7$ MSD, so $1$ VSD $= 0.7$ mm.
    Fig. 2: the Vernier zero lies just past the $1$ mm mark and the $1$st Vernier mark coincides with the $2$ mm main-scale mark.
    Position of the Vernier zero $= 2 - 1\times0.7 = 1.3$ mm.
    $D = 0.13$ cm → (C).

  5. JEE Advanced 2025, Paper 1 · Question 22
    Length, breadth and thickness of a strip having a uniform cross section are measured to be $10.5$ cm, $0.05$ mm, and $6.0\ \mu$m, respectively. Which of the following option(s) give(s) the volume of the strip in cm$^3$ with correct significant figures:

    1. $3.2\times10^{-5}$
    2. $32.0\times10^{-6}$
    3. $3.0\times10^{-5}$
    4. $3\times10^{-5}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    $V = 10.5 \times 0.005 \times 6.0\times10^{-4}$ cm$^3 = 3.15\times10^{-5}$ cm$^3$.
    The breadth $0.05$ mm has only one significant figure, so the product is quoted to one significant figure.
    $V = 3\times10^{-5}$ cm$^3$ → only (D).

  6. JEE Advanced 2025, Paper 1 · Question 28
    A single slit diffraction experiment is performed to determine the slit width using the equation, $\dfrac{bd}{D} = m\lambda$, where $b$ is the slit width, $D$ the shortest distance between the slit and the screen, $d$ the distance between the $m^{\text{th}}$ diffraction maximum and the central maximum, and $\lambda$ is the wavelength. $D$ and $d$ are measured with scales of least count of $1$ cm and $1$ mm, respectively. The values of $\lambda$ and $m$ are known precisely to be $600$ nm and $3$, respectively. The absolute error (in $\mu$m) in the value of $b$ estimated using the diffraction maximum that occurs for $m = 3$ with $d = 5$ mm and $D = 1$ m is ______

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 75 to 79 or 94 to 95

    $b = \dfrac{m\lambda D}{d} = \dfrac{3 \times 600\times10^{-9} \times 1}{5\times10^{-3}} = 3.6\times10^{-4}$ m $= 360\ \mu$m.
    $\dfrac{\Delta b}{b} = \dfrac{\Delta D}{D} + \dfrac{\Delta d}{d} = \dfrac{0.01}{1} + \dfrac{1}{5} = 0.21$.
    $\Delta b = 0.21 \times 360 = 75.6\ \mu$m.
    (Taking instead the extreme value $b_{\max} = \dfrac{3 \times 600\times10^{-9} \times 1.01}{4\times10^{-3}} = 454.5\ \mu$m gives $\Delta b \approx 94.5\ \mu$m; the official final key accepts both ranges.)

    Note: Official final key: [75 to 79] or [94 to 95] (either range is awarded marks).

  7. JEE Advanced 2025, Paper 2 · Question 17
    A temperature difference can generate e.m.f. in some materials. Let $S$ be the e.m.f. produced per unit temperature difference between the ends of a wire, $\sigma$ the electrical conductivity and $\kappa$ the thermal conductivity of the material of the wire. Taking $M, L, T, I$ and $K$ as dimensions of mass, length, time, current and temperature, respectively, the dimensional formula of the quantity $Z=\dfrac{S^2\sigma}{\kappa}$ is:

    1. $[M^0L^0T^0I^0K^0]$
    2. $[M^0L^0T^0I^0K^{-1}]$
    3. $[M^1L^2T^{-2}I^{-1}K^{-1}]$
    4. $[M^1L^2T^{-4}I^{-1}K^{-1}]$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    Voltage: $[ML^2T^{-3}I^{-1}]$, so $[S]=[ML^2T^{-3}I^{-1}K^{-1}]$.
    Resistivity $\rho=\dfrac{RA}{l}$: $[ML^3T^{-3}I^{-2}]$, so $[\sigma]=[M^{-1}L^{-3}T^3I^2]$.
    Thermal conductivity (W m$^{-1}$ K$^{-1}$): $[\kappa]=[MLT^{-3}K^{-1}]$.
    $[Z]=\dfrac{[M^2L^4T^{-6}I^{-2}K^{-2}][M^{-1}L^{-3}T^3I^2]}{[MLT^{-3}K^{-1}]}=[M^0L^0T^0I^0K^{-1}]$. Answer (B).

  8. JEE Advanced 2024, Paper 1 · Question 18
    A dimensionless quantity is constructed in terms of electronic charge $e$, permittivity of free space $\varepsilon_0$, Planck's constant $h$, and speed of light $c$. If the dimensionless quantity is written as $e^{\alpha}\varepsilon_0^{\beta}h^{\gamma}c^{\delta}$ and $n$ is a non-zero integer, then $(\alpha, \beta, \gamma, \delta)$ is given by

    1. $(2n, -n, -n, -n)$
    2. $(n, -n, -2n, -n)$
    3. $(n, -n, -n, -2n)$
    4. $(2n, -n, -2n, -2n)$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    $\dfrac{e^2}{4\pi\varepsilon_0 r}$ is an energy and $\dfrac{hc}{r}$ is an energy, so $\dfrac{e^2}{\varepsilon_0 hc}$ is dimensionless (it equals twice the fine-structure constant $\dfrac{e^2}{4\pi\varepsilon_0\hbar c}$).
    Any power of it is dimensionless: $\left(\dfrac{e^2}{\varepsilon_0 hc}\right)^n = e^{2n}\varepsilon_0^{-n}h^{-n}c^{-n}$.
    $(\alpha, \beta, \gamma, \delta) = (2n, -n, -n, -n)$ → (A).

  9. JEE Advanced 2024, Paper 2 · Question 25
    The dimensions of a cone are measured using a scale with a least count of $2$ mm. The diameter of the base and the height are both measured to be $20.0$ cm. The maximum percentage error in the determination of the volume is ______.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 3

    $V=\dfrac{\pi D^2h}{12}$, so $\dfrac{\Delta V}{V}=2\dfrac{\Delta D}{D}+\dfrac{\Delta h}{h}$.
    With $\Delta D=\Delta h=0.2$ cm: $\dfrac{\Delta V}{V}=2\left(\dfrac{0.2}{20}\right)+\dfrac{0.2}{20}=0.03$.
    Maximum percentage error $=3$.

  10. JEE Advanced 2023, Paper 2 · Question 19
    Young’s modulus of elasticity $Y$ is expressed in terms of three derived quantities, namely, the gravitational constant $G$, Planck’s constant $h$ and the speed of light $c$, as $Y = c^{\alpha} h^{\beta} G^{\gamma}$. Which of the following is the correct option?

    1. $\alpha = 7,\ \beta = -1,\ \gamma = -2$
    2. $\alpha = -7,\ \beta = -1,\ \gamma = -2$
    3. $\alpha = 7,\ \beta = -1,\ \gamma = 2$
    4. $\alpha = -7,\ \beta = 1,\ \gamma = -2$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    $[Y] = \mathrm{ML^{-1}T^{-2}}$, $[c] = \mathrm{LT^{-1}}$, $[h] = \mathrm{ML^2T^{-1}}$, $[G] = \mathrm{M^{-1}L^3T^{-2}}$.
    M: $\beta - \gamma = 1$; L: $\alpha + 2\beta + 3\gamma = -1$; T: $-\alpha - \beta - 2\gamma = -2$.
    Adding L and T: $\beta + \gamma = -3$, with $\beta - \gamma = 1$: $\beta = -1$, $\gamma = -2$.
    Then $\alpha = 2 - \beta - 2\gamma = 2 + 1 + 4 = 7$ → (A).

  11. JEE Advanced 2022, Paper 2 · Question 22
    In a particular system of units, a physical quantity can be expressed in terms of the electric charge $e$, electron mass $m_e$, Planck’s constant $h$, and Coulomb’s constant $k = \dfrac{1}{4\pi\epsilon_0}$, where $\epsilon_0$ is the permittivity of vacuum. In terms of these physical constants, the dimension of the magnetic field is $[B] = [e]^\alpha[m_e]^\beta[h]^\gamma[k]^\delta$. The value of $\alpha + \beta + \gamma + \delta$ is ________.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 4

    $[B] = \mathrm{M\,T^{-1}Q^{-1}}$, $[e] = \mathrm Q$, $[m_e] = \mathrm M$, $[h] = \mathrm{ML^2T^{-1}}$, $[k] = \mathrm{ML^3T^{-2}Q^{-2}}$.
    M: $\beta + \gamma + \delta = 1$; L: $2\gamma + 3\delta = 0$; T: $-\gamma - 2\delta = -1$; Q: $\alpha - 2\delta = -1$.
    From T and L: $\gamma = 1 - 2\delta$, $2 - \delta = 0$, so $\delta = 2$, $\gamma = -3$; then $\beta = 2$, $\alpha = 3$.
    $\alpha + \beta + \gamma + \delta = 3 + 2 - 3 + 2 = 4$.

  12. JEE Advanced 2022, Paper 2 · Question 35
    Area of the cross-section of a wire is measured using a screw gauge. The pitch of the main scale is $0.5$ mm. The circular scale has $100$ divisions and for one full rotation of the circular scale, the main scale shifts by two divisions. The measured readings are listed below.
    Two arms of gauge touching each other without wire: main scale reading 0 division, circular scale reading 4 divisions.
    Attempt-1, with wire: main scale reading 4 divisions, circular scale reading 20 divisions.
    Attempt-2, with wire: main scale reading 4 divisions, circular scale reading 16 divisions.
    What are the diameter and cross-sectional area of the wire measured using the screw gauge?

    1. $2.22 \pm 0.02$ mm, $\pi(1.23 \pm 0.02)$ mm$^2$
    2. $2.22 \pm 0.01$ mm, $\pi(1.23 \pm 0.01)$ mm$^2$
    3. $2.14 \pm 0.02$ mm, $\pi(1.14 \pm 0.02)$ mm$^2$
    4. $2.14 \pm 0.01$ mm, $\pi(1.14 \pm 0.01)$ mm$^2$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: Dropped: full marks to everyone

    One rotation moves $2\times0.5 = 1.0$ mm, so the least count is $\dfrac{1.0}{100} = 0.01$ mm. Zero error $= +4\times0.01 = +0.04$ mm.
    Attempt-1: $4\times0.5 + 20\times0.01 - 0.04 = 2.16$ mm; Attempt-2: $2.0 + 0.16 - 0.04 = 2.12$ mm.
    Mean $d = 2.14$ mm, mean absolute deviation $0.02$ mm: $d = 2.14 \pm 0.02$ mm.
    $A = \pi\left(\dfrac d2\right)^2 = \pi(1.07)^2 \approx \pi(1.14)$ mm$^2$, with $\dfrac{\Delta A}{A} = \dfrac{2\Delta d}{d}$, giving $\Delta \approx 0.02$: (C).
    Taking the least count $0.01$ mm as the error instead gives (D), so the question is ambiguous; the official final key dropped it (marks to all).

    Note: Dropped in the official final key: full marks to everyone.

  13. JEE Advanced 2021, Paper 1 · Question 20
    The smallest division on the main scale of a Vernier calipers is 0.1 cm. Ten divisions of the Vernier scale correspond to nine divisions of the main scale. The figure below on the left shows the reading of this calipers with no gap between its two jaws. The figure on the right shows the reading with a solid sphere held between the jaws. The correct diameter of the sphere is

    1. 3.07 cm
    2. 3.11 cm
    3. 3.15 cm
    4. 3.17 cm
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    Least count $= 1\text{ MSD} - 1\text{ VSD} = 0.1 - 0.09 = 0.01$ cm.
    Zero reading (left): the Vernier zero lies to the left of the main-scale zero and the 6th Vernier division coincides with a main-scale mark, so the zero error is $-(10 - 6) \times 0.01 = -0.04$ cm.
    With the sphere (right): main-scale reading $3.1$ cm, and the 1st Vernier division coincides: reading $= 3.1 + 1 \times 0.01 = 3.11$ cm.
    Correct diameter $= 3.11 - (-0.04) = 3.15$ cm → (C).

  14. JEE Advanced 2021, Paper 2 · Question 23
    A physical quantity $\vec{S}$ is defined as $\vec{S} = (\vec{E} \times \vec{B})/\mu_0$, where $\vec{E}$ is electric field, $\vec{B}$ is magnetic field and $\mu_0$ is the permeability of free space. The dimensions of $\vec{S}$ are the same as the dimensions of which of the following quantity(ies) ?

    1. $\dfrac{\text{Energy}}{\text{Charge} \times \text{Current}}$
    2. $\dfrac{\text{Force}}{\text{Length} \times \text{Time}}$
    3. $\dfrac{\text{Energy}}{\text{Volume}}$
    4. $\dfrac{\text{Power}}{\text{Area}}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b), (d)

    $\vec S$ is the Poynting vector: energy flow per unit area per unit time, $[S] = \dfrac{\text{W}}{\text{m}^2} = [\mathrm{MT^{-3}}]$.
    (A) $\dfrac{\mathrm{ML^2T^{-2}}}{\mathrm{AT}\cdot\mathrm{A}} = [\mathrm{ML^2T^{-3}A^{-2}}]$ → no.
    (B) $\dfrac{\mathrm{MLT^{-2}}}{\mathrm{L}\cdot\mathrm{T}} = [\mathrm{MT^{-3}}]$ → yes.
    (C) $\dfrac{\mathrm{ML^2T^{-2}}}{\mathrm{L^3}} = [\mathrm{ML^{-1}T^{-2}}]$ → no.
    (D) $\dfrac{\mathrm{ML^2T^{-3}}}{\mathrm{L^2}} = [\mathrm{MT^{-3}}]$ → yes.
    Answer (B), (D).
