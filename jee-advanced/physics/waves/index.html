  1. JEE Advanced 2026, Paper 1 · Question 29
    List-I shows four configurations made of straight and semi-circular narrow tubes containing air. A sound wave of wavelength $\lambda = 0.29$ m enters these structures at the point $S$ and a sound detector is placed at $D$. Between the points $S$ and $D$, the sound travels only through the tubes. List-II contains the possible smallest values of $l$ (refer to the figures) for which the detector $D$ records maximum amplitude. Ignore effects of sharp corners. [Given $\cos(15^{\circ}) = 0.97$]
    Choose the option that best describes the match between the entries in List-I to those in List-II.

    List-IList-II
    (P) Configuration (P) in the figure(1) 1.32 m
    (Q) Configuration (Q) in the figure(2) 1.19 m
    (R) Configuration (R) in the figure(3) 0.51 m
    (S) Configuration (S) in the figure(4) 0.29 m
    (5) 0.13 m

    1. P→4, Q→3, R→5, S→1
    2. P→4, Q→3, R→1, S→5
    3. P→3, Q→4, R→1, S→2
    4. P→3, Q→4, R→5, S→2
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (d)

    Maximum at $D$ when the path difference between the two branches is $\lambda$ (smallest $l$).
    (P) semicircle on diameter $l$: $\Delta = l(\pi/2 - 1) = 0.571l = 0.29 \Rightarrow l = 0.51$ m → (3).
    (Q) rectangle of height $0.5l$: $\Delta = 2l - l = l = 0.29$ m → (4).
    (R) side $l$ plus semicircle on the diagonal $l\sqrt2$: $\Delta = l + \pi l/\sqrt2 - l = 2.22l = 0.29 \Rightarrow l = 0.13$ m → (5).
    (S) triangle with angles $45^\circ, 105^\circ, 30^\circ$: other sides $= l\dfrac{\sin30^\circ + \sin45^\circ}{\sin105^\circ} = 1.244l$; $\Delta = 0.244l = 0.29 \Rightarrow l = 1.19$ m → (2).
    Match: P→3, Q→4, R→5, S→2 → (D).

  2. JEE Advanced 2025, Paper 1 · Question 23
    Consider a system of three connected strings, $S_1$, $S_2$ and $S_3$ with uniform linear mass densities $\mu$ kg/m, $4\mu$ kg/m and $16\mu$ kg/m, respectively, as shown in the figure. $S_1$ and $S_2$ are connected at the point $P$, whereas $S_2$ and $S_3$ are connected at the point $Q$, and the other end of $S_3$ is connected to a wall. A wave generator O is connected to the free end of $S_1$. The wave from the generator is represented by $y = y_0\cos(\omega t - kx)$ cm, where $y_0$, $\omega$ and $k$ are constants of appropriate dimensions. Which of the following statements is/are correct:

    1. When the wave reflects from $P$ for the first time, the reflected wave is represented by $y = \alpha_1y_0\cos(\omega t + kx + \pi)$ cm, where $\alpha_1$ is a positive constant.
    2. When the wave transmits through $P$ for the first time, the transmitted wave is represented by $y = \alpha_2y_0\cos(\omega t - kx)$ cm, where $\alpha_2$ is a positive constant.
    3. When the wave reflects from $Q$ for the first time, the reflected wave is represented by $y = \alpha_3y_0\cos(\omega t - kx + \pi)$ cm, where $\alpha_3$ is a positive constant.
    4. When the wave transmits through $Q$ for the first time, the transmitted wave is represented by $y = \alpha_4y_0\cos(\omega t - 4kx)$ cm, where $\alpha_4$ is a positive constant.
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a), (d)

    The tension $T$ is the same throughout; $v = \sqrt{T/\mu}$ and $k = \omega/v \propto \sqrt\mu$. So $k_1 = k$, $k_2 = 2k$, $k_3 = 4k$.
    (A) At $P$ the wave goes into a denser string, so the reflected wave (in $S_1$, moving along $-x$) is inverted: $\alpha_1y_0\cos(\omega t + kx + \pi)$: true.
    (B) The transmitted wave in $S_2$ has wave number $2k$, not $k$: false.
    (C) The wave reflected at $Q$ travels along $-x$ in $S_2$ (wave number $2k$), so it cannot be $\cos(\omega t - kx + \pi)$: false.
    (D) The transmitted wave in $S_3$ travels along $+x$ with wave number $4k$ and is not inverted: $\alpha_4y_0\cos(\omega t - 4kx)$: true.
    Answer: (A), (D).

  3. JEE Advanced 2025, Paper 2 · Question 32
    An audio transmitter (T) and a receiver (R) are hung vertically from two identical massless strings of length 8 m with their pivots well separated along the $X$ axis. They are pulled from the equilibrium position in opposite directions along the $X$ axis by a small angular amplitude $\theta_0=\cos^{-1}(0.9)$ and released simultaneously. If the natural frequency of the transmitter is 660 Hz and the speed of sound in air is 330 m/s, the maximum variation in the frequency (in Hz) as measured by the receiver (Take the acceleration due to gravity $g=10$ m/s$^2$) is ________.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 26 to 33

    Maximum speed of each bob (at the lowest point, moving along $X$): $v=\sqrt{2gL(1-\cos\theta_0)}=\sqrt{2\times10\times8\times0.1}=4$ m/s.
    The two pendulums move as mirror images, so at the lowest point they approach each other, or recede, each at 4 m/s.
    Approaching: $f_{\max}=660\times\dfrac{330+4}{330-4}=660\times\dfrac{334}{326}\approx676.2$ Hz.
    Receding: $f_{\min}=660\times\dfrac{326}{334}\approx644.2$ Hz.
    Maximum variation $=f_{\max}-f_{\min}\approx32$ Hz.

  4. JEE Advanced 2024, Paper 1 · Question 23
    Two uniform strings of mass per unit length $\mu$ and $4\mu$, and length $L$ and $2L$, respectively, are joined at point O, and tied at two fixed ends P and Q, as shown in the figure. The strings are under a uniform tension $T$. If we define the frequency $\nu_0 = \dfrac{1}{2L}\sqrt{\dfrac{T}{\mu}}$, which of the following statement(s) is(are) correct?

    1. With a node at O, the minimum frequency of vibration of the composite string is $\nu_0$.
    2. With an antinode at O, the minimum frequency of vibration of the composite string is $2\nu_0$.
    3. When the composite string vibrates at the minimum frequency with a node at O, it has 6 nodes, including the end nodes.
    4. No vibrational mode with an antinode at O is possible for the composite string.
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a), (c), (d)

    Wave speeds: $v_1 = \sqrt{T/\mu}$, $v_2 = \sqrt{T/4\mu} = v_1/2$.
    Node at O: each part is fixed at both ends. Part 1: $f = p\dfrac{v_1}{2L} = p\nu_0$. Part 2: $f = q\dfrac{v_2}{4L} = \dfrac{q}{4}\nu_0$.
    Equal frequencies need $q = 4p$; minimum $p = 1$, $q = 4$, $f = \nu_0$: (A) correct.
    Nodes: P and O on part 1; part 2 has 4 loops, so 3 interior nodes plus Q. Total $2 + 3 + 1 = 6$: (C) correct.
    Antinode at O: each part is fixed–free. Part 1: $f = \dfrac{(2p-1)v_1}{4L} = \dfrac{2p-1}{2}\nu_0$; part 2: $f = \dfrac{(2q-1)v_2}{8L} = \dfrac{2q-1}{8}\nu_0$.
    Equality needs $4(2p - 1) = 2q - 1$: even = odd, impossible. So no such mode: (D) correct, (B) wrong.
    Answer: (A), (C), (D).

  5. JEE Advanced 2024, Paper 1 · Question 28
    A source (S) of sound has frequency 240 Hz. When the observer (O) and the source move towards each other at a speed $v$ with respect to the ground (as shown in Case 1 in the figure), the observer measures the frequency of the sound to be 288 Hz. However, when the observer and the source move away from each other at the same speed $v$ with respect to the ground (as shown in Case 2 in the figure), the observer measures the frequency of sound to be $n$ Hz. The value of $n$ is _____.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 200

    Case 1 (approaching): $288 = 240\,\dfrac{c + v}{c - v} \Rightarrow \dfrac{c + v}{c - v} = 1.2$ (with $c$ the speed of sound).
    Case 2 (receding): $n = 240\,\dfrac{c - v}{c + v} = \dfrac{240}{1.2} = 200$.

  6. JEE Advanced 2023, Paper 2 · Question 27
    A string of length 1 m and mass $2\times10^{-5}$ kg is under tension $T$. When the string vibrates, two successive harmonics are found to occur at frequencies 750 Hz and 1000 Hz. The value of tension $T$ is ________ Newton.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 5

    Successive harmonics differ by the fundamental: $f_1 = 1000 - 750 = 250$ Hz.
    $\mu = 2\times10^{-5}$ kg/m; $f_1 = \dfrac{1}{2L}\sqrt{\dfrac T\mu} \Rightarrow \sqrt{\dfrac{T}{2\times10^{-5}}} = 500$.
    $T = 2.5\times10^5\times2\times10^{-5} = 5$ N.

  7. JEE Advanced 2023, Paper 2 · Question 31
    $S_1$ and $S_2$ are two identical sound sources of frequency 656 Hz. The source $S_1$ is located at $O$ and $S_2$ moves anti-clockwise with a uniform speed $4\sqrt2$ m s$^{-1}$ on a circular path around $O$, as shown in the figure. There are three points $P$, $Q$ and $R$ on this path such that $P$ and $R$ are diametrically opposite while $Q$ is equidistant from them. A sound detector is placed at point $P$. The source $S_1$ can move along direction $OP$.
    [Given: The speed of sound in air is 324 m s$^{-1}$]

    When only $S_2$ is emitting sound and it is at $Q$, the frequency of sound measured by the detector in Hz is ________.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 648

    Take $O$ as origin, $P = (0, -r)$, $Q = (r, 0)$. At $Q$ the anticlockwise velocity of $S_2$ is $4\sqrt2\,\hat y$.
    Unit vector from $Q$ to $P$: $\dfrac{(-1, -1)}{\sqrt2}$; component of velocity towards $P$ $= -4$ m/s, i.e. receding at 4 m/s.
    $f = 656\times\dfrac{324}{324 + 4} = 656\times\dfrac{324}{328} = 648$ Hz.

  8. JEE Advanced 2023, Paper 2 · Question 32
    $S_1$ and $S_2$ are two identical sound sources of frequency 656 Hz. The source $S_1$ is located at $O$ and $S_2$ moves anti-clockwise with a uniform speed $4\sqrt2$ m s$^{-1}$ on a circular path around $O$, as shown in the figure. There are three points $P$, $Q$ and $R$ on this path such that $P$ and $R$ are diametrically opposite while $Q$ is equidistant from them. A sound detector is placed at point $P$. The source $S_1$ can move along direction $OP$.
    [Given: The speed of sound in air is 324 m s$^{-1}$]

    Consider both sources emitting sound. When $S_2$ is at $R$ and $S_1$ approaches the detector with a speed 4 m s$^{-1}$, the beat frequency measured by the detector is ________ Hz.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 8 to 8.4

    At $R$ the velocity of $S_2$ is horizontal, perpendicular to $RP$, so the detector hears $656$ Hz from $S_2$.
    $S_1$ approaches at 4 m/s: $f_1 = 656\times\dfrac{324}{324 - 4} = 656\times\dfrac{324}{320} = 664.2$ Hz.
    Beat frequency $= 664.2 - 656 = 8.2$ Hz.

  9. JEE Advanced 2021, Paper 2 · Question 21
    A source, approaching with speed $u$ towards the open end of a stationary pipe of length $L$, is emitting a sound of frequency $f_s$. The farther end of the pipe is closed. The speed of sound in air is $v$ and $f_0$ is the fundamental frequency of the pipe. For which of the following combination(s) of $u$ and $f_s$, will the sound reaching the pipe lead to a resonance?

    1. $u = 0.8v$ and $f_s = f_0$
    2. $u = 0.8v$ and $f_s = 2f_0$
    3. $u = 0.8v$ and $f_s = 0.5f_0$
    4. $u = 0.5v$ and $f_s = 1.5f_0$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a), (d)

    A closed pipe resonates at odd harmonics: $f_0, 3f_0, 5f_0, \ldots$
    Frequency reaching the pipe: $f = f_s\dfrac{v}{v - u}$.
    $u = 0.8v$: $f = 5f_s$. (A) $5f_0$ → resonance; (B) $10f_0$ → no; (C) $2.5f_0$ → no.
    $u = 0.5v$: $f = 2f_s$. (D) $3f_0$ → resonance.
    Answer (A), (D).
