  1. JEE Advanced 2026, Paper 1 · Question 22
    A particle is thrown with a speed $v$ from a point $O$ at an angle $\theta$ with the horizontal plane such that it passes through the point $P$ at a height of 1 m and horizontal distance of 5 m from $O$, as shown in the figure. If acceleration due to gravity is $g$ ms$^{-2}$, then the correct statement(s) is/are:

    1. If $\theta = 45^{\circ}$, then $v = \dfrac{5\sqrt{g}}{2}$ ms$^{-1}$.
    2. If $\theta = 45^{\circ}$, the particle reaches its maximum height before it reaches $P$.
    3. If $\theta = 30^{\circ}$, the particle reaches its maximum height after reaching $P$.
    4. If $\theta = \tan^{-1}\left(\dfrac{1}{5}\right)$, then $v = 125\sqrt{g}$ ms$^{-1}$.
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a), (b)

    Trajectory: $1 = 5\tan\theta - \dfrac{25g}{2v^2\cos^2\theta}$.
    $\theta = 45^\circ$: $1 = 5 - 25g/v^2 \Rightarrow v = \tfrac{5}{2}\sqrt g$: (A) true. Apex at $x = v^2/(2g) = 25/8 = 3.125$ m $< 5$ m: (B) true.
    $\theta = 30^\circ$: $\dfrac{50g}{3v^2} = \dfrac{5}{\sqrt3} - 1 \Rightarrow v^2 \approx 8.83g$; apex at $x = v^2\sin60^\circ/(2g) \approx 3.8$ m $< 5$ m, i.e. before $P$: (C) false.
    $\tan\theta = 1/5$: $5\tan\theta = 1$ leaves no room for the gravity term (it would need $v \to \infty$): (D) false.

  2. JEE Advanced 2024, Paper 2 · Question 26
    A ball is thrown from the location $(x_0,y_0)=(0,0)$ of a horizontal playground with an initial speed $v_0$ at an angle $\theta_0$ from the $+x$-direction. The ball is to be hit by a stone, which is thrown at the same time from the location $(x_1,y_1)=(L,0)$. The stone is thrown at an angle $(180-\theta_1)$ from the $+x$-direction with a suitable initial speed. For a fixed $v_0$, when $(\theta_0,\theta_1)=(45^\circ,45^\circ)$, the stone hits the ball after time $T_1$, and when $(\theta_0,\theta_1)=(60^\circ,30^\circ)$, it hits the ball after time $T_2$. In such a case, $(T_1/T_2)^2$ is ______.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 2

    Both have the same acceleration $g$, so their relative velocity is constant. To meet, the vertical components must be equal: $u\sin\theta_1=v_0\sin\theta_0$.
    Closing speed along $x$: $v_0\cos\theta_0+u\cos\theta_1$, so $T=\dfrac{L}{v_0(\cos\theta_0+\sin\theta_0\cot\theta_1)}=\dfrac{L\sin\theta_1}{v_0\sin(\theta_0+\theta_1)}$.
    $T_1=\dfrac{L\sin45^\circ}{v_0}$, $T_2=\dfrac{L\sin30^\circ}{v_0}$ (both with $\sin90^\circ=1$).
    $\left(\dfrac{T_1}{T_2}\right)^2=\left(\dfrac{1/\sqrt2}{1/2}\right)^2=2$.

  3. JEE Advanced 2022, Paper 1 · Question 26
    A projectile is fired from horizontal ground with speed $v$ and projection angle $\theta$. When the acceleration due to gravity is $g$, the range of the projectile is $d$. If at the highest point in its trajectory, the projectile enters a different region where the effective acceleration due to gravity is $g'=\dfrac{g}{0.81}$, then the new range is $d'=nd$. The value of $n$ is ______.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 0.93 to 0.97

    Up to the highest point the motion is unchanged: horizontal distance $\dfrac d2$, height $H$.
    The fall from height $H$ takes $\sqrt{\dfrac{2H}{g'}}=\sqrt{0.81}\sqrt{\dfrac{2H}{g}}=0.9\times$ the original fall time, at the same horizontal speed.
    So the second half covers $0.9\times\dfrac d2=0.45d$.
    $d'=0.5d+0.45d=0.95d\Rightarrow n=0.95$.

  4. JEE Advanced 2022, Paper 1 · Question 34
    List I describes four systems, each with two particles $A$ and $B$ in relative motion as shown in figures. List II gives possible magnitudes of their relative velocities (in $\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$) at time $t=\dfrac\pi3$ s.

    List-IList-II
    (I) $A$ and $B$ are moving on a horizontal circle of radius $1$ m with uniform angular speed $\omega=1\ \mathrm{rad\,s^{-1}}$. The initial angular positions of $A$ and $B$ at time $t=0$ are $\theta=0$ and $\theta=\frac\pi2$, respectively.(P) $\frac{\sqrt3+1}{2}$
    (II) Projectiles $A$ and $B$ are fired (in the same vertical plane) at $t=0$ and $t=0.1$ s respectively, with the same speed $v=\frac{5\pi}{\sqrt2}\ \mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$ and at $45^\circ$ from the horizontal plane. The initial separation between $A$ and $B$ is large enough so that they do not collide. ($g=10\ \mathrm{m\,s^{-2}}$).(Q) $\frac{\sqrt3-1}{\sqrt2}$
    (III) Two harmonic oscillators $A$ and $B$ moving in the $x$ direction according to $x_A=x_0\sin\frac{t}{t_0}$ and $x_B=x_0\sin\left(\frac{t}{t_0}+\frac\pi2\right)$ respectively, starting from $t=0$. Take $x_0=1$ m, $t_0=1$ s.(R) $\sqrt{10}$
    (IV) Particle $A$ is rotating in a horizontal circular path of radius $1$ m on the $xy$ plane, with constant angular speed $\omega=1\ \mathrm{rad\,s^{-1}}$. Particle $B$ is moving up at a constant speed $3\ \mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$ in the vertical direction as shown in the figure. (Ignore gravity.)(S) $\sqrt2$
    (T) $\sqrt{25\pi^2+1}$
    Which one of the following options is correct?

    1. I → R, II → T, III → P, IV → S
    2. I → S, II → P, III → Q, IV → R
    3. I → S, II → T, III → P, IV → R
    4. I → T, II → P, III → R, IV → S
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    (I) Both have speed 1 m/s and their velocities stay $90^\circ$ apart: $|v_{rel}|=\sqrt2$ (S).
    (II) The $45^\circ$ angles are marked on the facing sides, so A and B are fired towards each other. Horizontal: $v\cos45^\circ-(-v\cos45^\circ)=2\cdot\frac{5\pi}{2}=5\pi$; vertical: $(v\sin45^\circ-gt)-(v\sin45^\circ-g(t-0.1))=-1$. Both are still in flight at $t=\frac\pi3$ (flight time $\frac\pi2$ s). $|v_{rel}|=\sqrt{25\pi^2+1}$ (T).
    (III) $v_A=\cos t$, $v_B=-\sin t$; $|v_A-v_B|=\cos\frac\pi3+\sin\frac\pi3=\frac{\sqrt3+1}{2}$ (P).
    (IV) $A$ moves horizontally at 1 m/s, $B$ vertically at 3 m/s: $\sqrt{10}$ (R).
    Answer (C).
