  1. JEE Advanced 2025, Paper 1 · Question 31
    A circuit with an electrical load having impedance $Z$ is connected with an AC source as shown in the diagram. The source voltage varies in time as $V(t) = 300\sin(400t)$ V, where $t$ is time in s. List-I shows various options for the load. The possible currents $i(t)$ in the circuit as a function of time are given in List-II.
    Choose the option that describes the correct match between the entries in List-I to those in List-II.

    List-IList-II
    (P) Load (P) in the figure: $30\ \Omega$(1) Graph (1) in the figure
    (Q) Load (Q) in the figure: $30\ \Omega$, $100$ mH(2) Graph (2) in the figure
    (R) Load (R) in the figure: $50\ \mu$F, $30\ \Omega$, $25$ mH(3) Graph (3) in the figure
    (S) Load (S) in the figure: $50\ \mu$F, $60\ \Omega$, $125$ mH(4) Graph (4) in the figure
    (5) Graph (5) in the figure

    1. P→3, Q→5, R→2, S→1
    2. P→1, Q→5, R→2, S→3
    3. P→3, Q→4, R→2, S→1
    4. P→1, Q→4, R→2, S→5
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    $\omega = 400$ rad/s. $X_L = 400L$, $X_C = \dfrac{1}{400C}$; for $50\ \mu$F, $X_C = 50\ \Omega$.
    (P) $R = 30\ \Omega$: $i = 10\sin400t$ (in phase, amplitude 10) → (3).
    (Q) $X_L = 40\ \Omega$, $Z = 50\ \Omega$: $i_0 = 6$ A, lagging by $\tan^{-1}\dfrac43 = 53^\circ$: $i = 6\sin(400t - 53^\circ)$, negative at $t = 0$ → (5).
    (R) $X_L = 10$, $X_C = 50$: $X = -40\ \Omega$, $Z = 50\ \Omega$: $i_0 = 6$ A, leading by $53^\circ$: $i = 6\sin(400t + 53^\circ)$, positive at $t = 0$ with the peak before $400t = \pi/2$ → (2).
    (S) $X_L = 50 = X_C$: resonance, $Z = 60\ \Omega$, $i = 5\sin400t$ → (1).
    Match: P→3, Q→5, R→2, S→1 → (A).

  2. JEE Advanced 2024, Paper 1 · Question 34
    The circuit shown in the figure contains an inductor $L$, a capacitor $C_0$, a resistor $R_0$ and an ideal battery. The circuit also contains two keys $K_1$ and $K_2$. Initially, both the keys are open and there is no charge on the capacitor. At an instant, key $K_1$ is closed and immediately after this the current in $R_0$ is found to be $I_1$. After a long time, the current attains a steady state value $I_2$. Thereafter, $K_2$ is closed and simultaneously $K_1$ is opened and the voltage across $C_0$ oscillates with amplitude $V_0$ and angular frequency $\omega_0$.
    Match the quantities mentioned in List-I with their values in List-II and choose the correct option.

    List-IList-II
    (P) The value of $I_1$ in Ampere is(1) $0$
    (Q) The value of $I_2$ in Ampere is(2) $2$
    (R) The value of $\omega_0$ in kilo-radians/s is(3) $4$
    (S) The value of $V_0$ in Volt is(4) $20$
    (5) $200$

    1. P→1, Q→3, R→2, S→5
    2. P→1, Q→2, R→3, S→5
    3. P→1, Q→3, R→2, S→4
    4. P→2, Q→5, R→3, S→4
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    With $K_1$ closed ($K_2$ open) the battery, $R_0$ and $L$ form a series circuit.
    Just after closing, the inductor blocks any sudden current: $I_1 = 0$ → (1).
    Steady state: $I_2 = \dfrac{20}{5} = 4$ A → (3).
    Then $L$ and $C_0$ form an isolated LC loop: $\omega_0 = \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{LC_0}} = \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{25\times10^{-3}\times10\times10^{-6}}} = 2000$ rad/s $= 2$ krad/s → (2).
    Energy: $\dfrac12LI_2^2 = \dfrac12C_0V_0^2 \Rightarrow V_0 = I_2\sqrt{\dfrac{L}{C_0}} = 4\sqrt{2500} = 200$ V → (5).
    P→1, Q→3, R→2, S→5 → (A).

  3. JEE Advanced 2023, Paper 1 · Question 33
    A series LCR circuit is connected to a $45\sin(\omega t)$ Volt source. The resonant angular frequency of the circuit is $10^5\ \text{rad s}^{-1}$ and current amplitude at resonance is $I_0$. When the angular frequency of the source is $\omega = 8\times10^4\ \text{rad s}^{-1}$, the current amplitude in the circuit is $0.05\,I_0$. If $L = 50$ mH, match each entry in List-I with an appropriate value from List-II and choose the correct option.

    List-IList-II
    (P) $I_0$ in mA(1) $44.4$
    (Q) The quality factor of the circuit(2) $18$
    (R) The bandwidth of the circuit in $\text{rad s}^{-1}$(3) $400$
    (S) The peak power dissipated at resonance in Watt(4) $2250$
    (5) $500$

    1. P → 2, Q → 3, R → 5, S → 1
    2. P → 3, Q → 1, R → 4, S → 2
    3. P → 4, Q → 5, R → 3, S → 1
    4. P → 4, Q → 2, R → 1, S → 5
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    $C = \dfrac{1}{\omega_0^2L} = \dfrac{1}{10^{10}\times0.05} = 2\times10^{-9}$ F.
    At $\omega = 8\times10^4$: $\omega L = 4000\ \Omega$, $\dfrac{1}{\omega C} = 6250\ \Omega$, $X = 2250\ \Omega$.
    Current $0.05I_0$ means $Z = 20R$: $R^2 + 2250^2 = 400R^2 \Rightarrow R = \dfrac{2250}{\sqrt{399}} \approx 112.6\ \Omega$.
    (P) $I_0 = \dfrac{45}{112.6} \approx 0.4$ A $= 400$ mA → (3). (Q) $Q = \dfrac{\omega_0L}{R} = \dfrac{5000}{112.6} \approx 44.4$ → (1).
    (R) Bandwidth $= \dfrac RL \approx 2250\ \text{rad s}^{-1}$ → (4). (S) $P = I_0^2R \approx 0.16\times112.6 \approx 18$ W → (2).
    Answer (B).

  4. JEE Advanced 2021, Paper 2 · Question 30
    In a circuit, a metal filament lamp is connected in series with a capacitor of capacitance C $\mu$F across a 200 V, 50 Hz supply. The power consumed by the lamp is 500 W while the voltage drop across it is 100 V. Assume that there is no inductive load in the circuit. Take rms values of the voltages. The magnitude of the phase-angle (in degrees) between the current and the supply voltage is $\varphi$. Assume, $\pi\sqrt3 \approx 5$.

    The value of C is ___.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 90 to 105

    Lamp: $I = \dfrac{500}{100} = 5$ A, $R = \dfrac{100}{5} = 20\ \Omega$.
    $V_C = \sqrt{200^2 - 100^2} = 100\sqrt3$ V, so $X_C = \dfrac{100\sqrt3}{5} = 20\sqrt3\ \Omega$.
    $C = \dfrac{1}{2\pi f X_C} = \dfrac{1}{100\pi \times 20\sqrt3} = \dfrac{1}{2000\pi\sqrt3} \approx \dfrac{1}{10^4}$ F $= 100\ \mu$F.

  5. JEE Advanced 2021, Paper 2 · Question 31
    In a circuit, a metal filament lamp is connected in series with a capacitor of capacitance C $\mu$F across a 200 V, 50 Hz supply. The power consumed by the lamp is 500 W while the voltage drop across it is 100 V. Assume that there is no inductive load in the circuit. Take rms values of the voltages. The magnitude of the phase-angle (in degrees) between the current and the supply voltage is $\varphi$. Assume, $\pi\sqrt3 \approx 5$.

    The value of $\varphi$ is ___.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 55 to 62

    Lamp: $I = \dfrac{500}{100} = 5$ A, $R = \dfrac{100}{5} = 20\ \Omega$.
    $V_C = \sqrt{200^2 - 100^2} = 100\sqrt3$ V, so $X_C = \dfrac{100\sqrt3}{5} = 20\sqrt3\ \Omega$.
    $\tan\varphi = \dfrac{X_C}{R} = \sqrt3 \Rightarrow \varphi = 60^\circ$.
