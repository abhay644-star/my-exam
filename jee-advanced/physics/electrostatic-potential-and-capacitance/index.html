  1. JEE Advanced 2026, Paper 2 · Question 25
    Two charges $Q_1 = q$ and $Q_2 = mq$ are placed at the points $P_1(a, b)$ and $P_2\ (ma, mb)$, respectively, in the $XY$ plane, where $a, b \neq 0$ and $m \neq 0, 1$. If $V_1$ is the potential at a point in the $XY$ plane due to charge $Q_1$ and $V_2$ is the potential at that point due to charge $Q_2$. Correct statement(s) for the points at which $|V_1| = |V_2|$ is/are:

    1. For $m = -1$, locus of these points is $ax + by = 0$.
    2. For $m = 2$, the locus of these points is a circle of radius $\frac{2}{3}\sqrt{a^2+b^2}$ centered at $\left(\frac{2}{3}a, \frac{2}{3}b\right)$
    3. For $m = -2$, the locus of these points is a circle of radius $2\sqrt{a^2+b^2}$ centered at $(2a, 2b)$
    4. For $m = -3$, locus of these points is $3bx + 3ay = 0$.
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a), (b), (c)

    $|V_1| = |V_2| \Rightarrow r_2 = |m|\,r_1$. Write $\vec P = (a,b)$, $\vec X = (x,y)$.
    $m=-1$: equidistant from $\vec P$ and $-\vec P$: perpendicular bisector through origin, $ax+by=0$ — (A) true.
    $m=2$: $|\vec X-2\vec P|^2 = 4|\vec X-\vec P|^2 \Rightarrow 3X^2 - 4\vec X\cdot\vec P = 0$: centre $\tfrac23\vec P$, radius $\tfrac23|\vec P|$ — (B) true.
    $m=-2$: $|\vec X+2\vec P|^2 = 4|\vec X-\vec P|^2 \Rightarrow X^2 - 4\vec X\cdot\vec P = 0$: centre $2\vec P$, radius $2|\vec P|$ — (C) true.
    $m=-3$: $|m|\ne1$ gives a circle, not a line — (D) false.

  2. JEE Advanced 2026, Paper 2 · Question 34
    A container of height $2$ m, length $2$ m and breadth $1$ m is made of insulating vertical walls and two large area horizontal metal plates ($\mathrm{M_1}$ and $\mathrm{M_2}$) which extend far beyond the vertical walls in all directions. The container is partitioned into two equal chambers with a thin insulating vertical wall. The partition wall contains a small hole of cross-sectional area $\sqrt{10}\ \mathrm{cm^2}$ near its bottom edge. Initially the hole is closed and the left chamber of the container is completely filled with a liquid of dielectric constant $\epsilon_r = 15$ and the right chamber is empty ($\epsilon_r = 1$). At time $t = 0$, the hole is opened and the liquid flows from the left chamber to the right chamber. In both the chambers, the space above the liquid has $\epsilon_r = 1$ and is maintained at atmospheric pressure. The schematic of the container at a time $t > 0$ is shown in the figure.
    [Given: acceleration due to gravity is $10\ \mathrm{ms^{-2}}$.]

    The difference in the capacitance (in F) between the metal plates at $t = 0$ and that at $t = 500$ s is $(8 - n)\epsilon_0$, where $\epsilon_0$ is the permittivity of free space. The value of $n$ is:

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 1.9 to 2.1

    The region outside the container is unchanged, so only the two 1 m² chambers matter; each is a pair of series layers between plates 2 m apart.
    $t=0$: left $\dfrac{15\epsilon_0}{2} = 7.5\epsilon_0$, right $\dfrac{\epsilon_0}{2} = 0.5\epsilon_0$; total $8\epsilon_0$.
    $t=500$ s ($h_1 = 1.25$, $h_2 = 0.75$): left $C = \dfrac{\epsilon_0}{1.25/15 + 0.75} = 1.2\epsilon_0$; right $C = \dfrac{\epsilon_0}{0.75/15 + 1.25} = 0.769\epsilon_0$.
    Total $\approx 1.97\epsilon_0$, difference $= (8 - 1.97)\epsilon_0 \Rightarrow n \approx 1.97$.

  3. JEE Advanced 2025, Paper 2 · Question 21
    A positive point charge of $10^{-8}$ C is kept at a distance of 20 cm from the center of a neutral conducting sphere of radius 10 cm. The sphere is then grounded and the charge on the sphere is measured. The grounding is then removed and subsequently the point charge is moved by a distance of 10 cm further away from the center of the sphere along the radial direction. Taking $\dfrac{1}{4\pi\epsilon_0}=9\times10^9$ Nm$^2$/C$^2$ (where $\epsilon_0$ is the permittivity of free space), which of the following statements is/are correct:

    1. Before the grounding, the electrostatic potential of the sphere is 450 V.
    2. Charge flowing from the sphere to the ground because of grounding is $5\times10^{-9}$ C.
    3. After the grounding is removed, the charge on the sphere is $-5\times10^{-9}$ C.
    4. The final electrostatic potential of the sphere is 300 V.
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a), (b), (c)

    The sphere is equipotential, so its potential equals that at its centre, where the induced charges (all at distance $0.1$ m) contribute $\dfrac{kq_{\text{sphere}}}{0.1}$.
    Neutral: $V=\dfrac{kq}{0.2}=\dfrac{9\times10^9\times10^{-8}}{0.2}=450$ V (A true).
    Grounded: $\dfrac{kq}{0.2}+\dfrac{kq'}{0.1}=0\Rightarrow q'=-\dfrac q2=-5\times10^{-9}$ C. So $+5\times10^{-9}$ C flows from the sphere to the ground (B true) and the sphere keeps $-5\times10^{-9}$ C after the ground is removed (C true).
    Charge moved to $0.3$ m: $V=\dfrac{kq}{0.3}+\dfrac{kq'}{0.1}=300-450=-150$ V (D false).
    Answer (A), (B), (C).

  4. JEE Advanced 2024, Paper 1 · Question 32
    Four identical thin, square metal sheets, $S_1$, $S_2$, $S_3$ and $S_4$, each of side $a$ are kept parallel to each other with equal distance $d\ (\ll a)$ between them, as shown in the figure. Let $C_0 = \varepsilon_0 a^2/d$, where $\varepsilon_0$ is the permittivity of free space.
    Match the quantities mentioned in List-I with their values in List-II and choose the correct option.

    List-IList-II
    (P) The capacitance between $S_1$ and $S_4$, with $S_2$ and $S_3$ not connected, is(1) $3C_0$
    (Q) The capacitance between $S_1$ and $S_4$, with $S_2$ shorted to $S_3$, is(2) $C_0/2$
    (R) The capacitance between $S_1$ and $S_3$, with $S_2$ shorted to $S_4$, is(3) $C_0/3$
    (S) The capacitance between $S_1$ and $S_2$, with $S_3$ shorted to $S_1$, and $S_2$ shorted to $S_4$, is(4) $2C_0/3$
    (5) $2C_0$

    1. P→3, Q→2, R→4, S→5
    2. P→2, Q→3, R→2, S→1
    3. P→3, Q→2, R→4, S→1
    4. P→3, Q→2, R→2, S→5
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    Each gap between neighbouring sheets is a capacitor $C_0$.
    (P) Three gaps in series: $C_0/3$ → (3).
    (Q) The $S_2$–$S_3$ gap is shorted (no field); $S_1S_2$ and $S_3S_4$ in series: $C_0/2$ → (2).
    (R) Node $A = S_1$, node $B = \{S_2, S_4\}$, node $S_3$. $S_1S_2$: $C_0$ between $A$ and $B$. $S_2S_3$ and $S_3S_4$: both between $B$ and $S_3$, in parallel ($2C_0$). Series: $\dfrac{C_0\cdot2C_0}{3C_0} = \dfrac{2C_0}{3}$ → (4).
    (S) Nodes $\{S_1, S_3\}$ and $\{S_2, S_4\}$: all three gaps lie between these two nodes, in parallel: $3C_0$ → (1).
    P→3, Q→2, R→4, S→1 → (C).

  5. JEE Advanced 2024, Paper 2 · Question 29
    An infinitely long thin wire, having a uniform charge density per unit length of $5$ nC/m, is passing through a spherical shell of radius $1$ m, as shown in the figure. A $10$ nC charge is distributed uniformly over the spherical shell. If the configuration of the charges remains static, the magnitude of the potential difference between points P and R, in Volt, is ______.
    [Given: In SI units $\dfrac{1}{4\pi\epsilon_0}=9\times10^9$, $\ln2=0.7$. Ignore the area pierced by the wire.]

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 171

    P is $0.5$ m and R is $2$ m from the wire (and from the centre).
    Shell: $V_P=\dfrac{kQ}{1}=90$ V (inside), $V_R=\dfrac{kQ}{2}=45$ V; difference $45$ V.
    Wire: $V_P-V_R=2k\lambda\ln\dfrac{2}{0.5}=2(9\times10^9)(5\times10^{-9})(2\ln2)=90\times1.4=126$ V.
    Both contributions make P higher: $|V_P-V_R|=126+45=171$ V.

  6. JEE Advanced 2023, Paper 1 · Question 22
    A container has a base of $50\ \text{cm}\times5\ \text{cm}$ and height $50$ cm, as shown in the figure. It has two parallel electrically conducting walls each of area $50\ \text{cm}\times50\ \text{cm}$. The remaining walls of the container are thin and non-conducting. The container is being filled with a liquid of dielectric constant $3$ at a uniform rate of $250\ \text{cm}^3\,\text{s}^{-1}$. What is the value of the capacitance of the container after $10$ seconds?
    [Given: Permittivity of free space $\epsilon_0 = 9\times10^{-12}\ \text{C}^2\text{N}^{-1}\text{m}^{-2}$, the effects of the non-conducting walls on the capacitance are negligible]

    1. $27$ pF
    2. $63$ pF
    3. $81$ pF
    4. $135$ pF
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    After $10$ s the volume is $2500\ \text{cm}^3$; base area $250\ \text{cm}^2$, so the liquid height is $10$ cm. Plate separation $d = 5$ cm $= 0.05$ m.
    Filled part (area $50\times10\ \text{cm}^2 = 0.05\ \text{m}^2$): $C_1 = \dfrac{3\times9\times10^{-12}\times0.05}{0.05} = 27$ pF.
    Empty part (area $0.20\ \text{m}^2$): $C_2 = \dfrac{9\times10^{-12}\times0.20}{0.05} = 36$ pF.
    The parts are in parallel: $C = 63$ pF. Answer (B).

  7. JEE Advanced 2023, Paper 2 · Question 18
    An electric dipole is formed by two charges $+q$ and $-q$ located in $xy$-plane at $(0, 2)$ mm and $(0, -2)$ mm, respectively, as shown in the figure. The electric potential at point P $(100, 100)$ mm due to the dipole is $V_0$. The charges $+q$ and $-q$ are then moved to the points $(-1, 2)$ mm and $(1, -2)$ mm, respectively. What is the value of electric potential at P due to the new dipole?

    1. $V_0/4$
    2. $V_0/2$
    3. $V_0/\sqrt{2}$
    4. $3V_0/4$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    P is far away, so $V = \dfrac{k\,\vec p\cdot\hat r}{r^2}$ with $\hat r = \dfrac{(1,1)}{\sqrt2}$ and the same $r$ in both cases.
    Original: $\vec p = q(0, 4)$ mm, $\vec p\cdot\hat r = \dfrac{4q}{\sqrt2}$.
    New: $\vec p = q[(-1,2) - (1,-2)] = q(-2, 4)$ mm, $\vec p\cdot\hat r = \dfrac{2q}{\sqrt2}$.
    $V = \dfrac{V_0}{2}$ → (B).

  8. JEE Advanced 2022, Paper 1 · Question 21
    In the following circuit $C_1=12\ \mu F$, $C_2=C_3=4\ \mu F$ and $C_4=C_5=2\ \mu F$. The charge stored in $C_3$ is ______ $\mu C$.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 7.9 to 8.1

    The whole bottom wire is one node, and the apex of the triangle is joined by a plain wire along the top to the upper terminal of the 2 V battery. ($C_1$ lies between the 6 V battery's upper terminal and the apex.)
    So $C_3$ (like $C_2$, $C_4$, $C_5$) is connected directly across the 2 V battery.
    $Q_3=C_3V=4\ \mu F\times2\ V=8\ \mu C$.

  9. JEE Advanced 2022, Paper 1 · Question 27
    A medium having dielectric constant $K>1$ fills the space between the plates of a parallel plate capacitor. The plates have large area, and the distance between them is $d$. The capacitor is connected to a battery of voltage $V$, as shown in Figure (a). Now, both the plates are moved by a distance of $\dfrac d2$ from their original positions, as shown in Figure (b).
    In the process of going from the configuration depicted in Figure (a) to that in Figure (b), which of the following statement(s) is(are) correct?

    1. The electric field inside the dielectric material is reduced by a factor of $2K$.
    2. The capacitance is decreased by a factor of $\dfrac{1}{K+1}$.
    3. The voltage between the capacitor plates is increased by a factor of $(K+1)$.
    4. The work done in the process <b>DOES NOT</b> depend on the presence of the dielectric material.
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    Initially $C=\dfrac{K\varepsilon_0A}{d}$. Finally the gap is $2d$: dielectric of thickness $d$ in series with air of total thickness $d$: $C'=\dfrac{\varepsilon_0A}{d+d/K}=\dfrac{K\varepsilon_0A}{(K+1)d}=\dfrac{C}{K+1}$ — (B) correct.
    The battery keeps $V$ fixed — (C) wrong.
    Field in the dielectric: initially $\dfrac Vd$; finally $E_{air}=KE_{d}$ and $V=E_{air}d+E_dd=(K+1)E_dd$, so $E_d=\dfrac{V}{(K+1)d}$: reduced by $(K+1)$, not $2K$ — (A) wrong.
    The work done depends on the change in $C$, which depends on $K$ — (D) wrong.

  10. JEE Advanced 2022, Paper 2 · Question 30
    A disk of radius $R$ with uniform positive charge density $\sigma$ is placed on the $xy$ plane with its center at the origin. The Coulomb potential along the $z$-axis is

    $\displaystyle V(z) = \frac{\sigma}{2\epsilon_0}\left(\sqrt{R^2 + z^2} - z\right).$
    A particle of positive charge $q$ is placed initially at rest at a point on the $z$ axis with $z = z_0$ and $z_0 > 0$. In addition to the Coulomb force, the particle experiences a vertical force $\vec F = -c\,\hat k$ with $c > 0$. Let $\beta = \dfrac{2c\epsilon_0}{q\sigma}$. Which of the following statement(s) is(are) correct?

    1. For $\beta = \dfrac14$ and $z_0 = \dfrac{25}{7}R$, the particle reaches the origin.
    2. For $\beta = \dfrac14$ and $z_0 = \dfrac37R$, the particle reaches the origin.
    3. For $\beta = \dfrac14$ and $z_0 = \dfrac{R}{\sqrt3}$, the particle returns back to $z = z_0$.
    4. For $\beta > 1$ and $z_0 > 0$, the particle always reaches the origin.
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a), (c), (d)

    With $K = \dfrac{q\sigma}{2\epsilon_0}$, $c = \beta K$ and the total potential energy is $U(z) = K\left[\sqrt{R^2+z^2} - (1-\beta)z\right]$.
    $\beta = \dfrac14$: $U'(z) = K\left[\dfrac{z}{\sqrt{R^2+z^2}} - \dfrac34\right]$, zero at $z = \dfrac{3R}{\sqrt7}$ (a minimum of $U$).
    Starting beyond this point the particle moves down; it reaches the origin if $U(z_0) \ge U(0) = KR$: $\sqrt{R^2+z_0^2} \ge R + \dfrac{3z_0}{4} \iff z_0 \ge \dfrac{24R}{7}$.
    (A) $z_0 = \dfrac{25R}{7} > \dfrac{24R}{7}$: reaches the origin, true.
    (B) $z_0 = \dfrac{3R}{7}$ and (C) $z_0 = \dfrac{R}{\sqrt3}$ are below $\dfrac{3R}{\sqrt7}$: the net force is upward, the particle oscillates about the minimum and returns to $z_0$. (B) false, (C) true.
    (D) $\beta > 1$: $U'(z) > 0$ for all $z > 0$, so the force is always downward and the particle reaches the origin: true.
    Answer (A), (C), (D).

  11. JEE Advanced 2021, Paper 1 · Question 28
    Two point charges $-Q$ and $+Q/\sqrt3$ are placed in the $xy$-plane at the origin $(0, 0)$ and a point $(2, 0)$, respectively, as shown in the figure. This results in an equipotential circle of radius $R$ and potential $V = 0$ in the $xy$-plane with its center at $(b, 0)$. All lengths are measured in meters.

    The value of $R$ is ___ meter.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 1.7 to 1.8

    $V = 0$: $\dfrac{Q}{\sqrt3\,r_2} = \dfrac{Q}{r_1}$, so $r_1^2 = 3r_2^2$ with $r_1, r_2$ the distances from $(0,0)$ and $(2,0)$.
    $x^2 + y^2 = 3\left[(x - 2)^2 + y^2\right] \Rightarrow x^2 + y^2 - 6x + 6 = 0$.
    Centre $(3, 0)$, $R^2 = 9 - 6 = 3$, so $R = \sqrt3 \approx 1.73$ m.

  12. JEE Advanced 2021, Paper 1 · Question 29
    Two point charges $-Q$ and $+Q/\sqrt3$ are placed in the $xy$-plane at the origin $(0, 0)$ and a point $(2, 0)$, respectively, as shown in the figure. This results in an equipotential circle of radius $R$ and potential $V = 0$ in the $xy$-plane with its center at $(b, 0)$. All lengths are measured in meters.

    The value of $b$ is ___ meter.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 2.9 to 3.1

    $V = 0$ requires $r_1 = \sqrt3\,r_2$: $x^2 + y^2 = 3\left[(x - 2)^2 + y^2\right]$.
    This simplifies to $(x - 3)^2 + y^2 = 3$.
    The centre is at $(3, 0)$, so $b = 3$ m.
