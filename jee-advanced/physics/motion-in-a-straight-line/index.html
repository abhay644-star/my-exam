  1. JEE Advanced 2023, Paper 1 · Question 29
    A person of height $1.6$ m is walking away from a lamp post of height $4$ m along a straight path on the flat ground. The lamp post and the person are always perpendicular to the ground. If the speed of the person is $60\ \text{cm s}^{-1}$, the speed of the tip of the person’s shadow on the ground with respect to the person is ______ $\text{cm s}^{-1}$.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 40

    Let the person be at $x$ and the shadow tip at $s$ from the post. Similar triangles: $\dfrac{4}{s} = \dfrac{1.6}{s - x} \Rightarrow s = \dfrac53x$.
    Tip speed $= \dfrac53\times60 = 100\ \text{cm s}^{-1}$; relative to the person: $100 - 60 = 40\ \text{cm s}^{-1}$.
