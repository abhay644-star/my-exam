  1. JEE Advanced 2026, Paper 1 · Question 28
    A hollow, right circular cone of base radius $R$ and height $h$, with its tip at the origin is rotating about the $Z$-axis with an angular velocity $\omega$, as shown in the figure. The cone carries a total charge $Q$ uniformly distributed on its curved surface. The magnitude of magnetic field at a point $(0, 0, z)$, where $z \gg R$ and $z \gg h$, is $\dfrac{n\mu_0}{4\pi}\dfrac{QR^2\omega}{z^3}$. The value of $n$ is:

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 0.5

    Far away the cone acts as a magnetic dipole $\mu = \tfrac{\omega}{2}\int \rho^2\,dq$.
    With slant length $\ell$, a ring at slant distance $s$ has $\rho = sR/\ell$ and $dq = \dfrac{2Qs\,ds}{\ell^2}$.
    $\int\rho^2dq = \dfrac{2QR^2}{\ell^4}\int_0^\ell s^3ds = \dfrac{QR^2}{2}$, so $\mu = \dfrac{QR^2\omega}{4}$.
    On the axis: $B = \dfrac{\mu_0}{4\pi}\dfrac{2\mu}{z^3} = \dfrac{\mu_0}{4\pi}\dfrac{QR^2\omega}{2z^3}$.
    $n = 0.5$.

  2. JEE Advanced 2026, Paper 2 · Question 24
    In a vacuum chamber, a particle of charge $1\ \mu\mathrm{C}$ and mass $1$ mg is projected with a velocity $(\hat{\imath} + 2\hat{\jmath})\ \mathrm{ms^{-1}}$ from the $XZ$ plane at time $t = 0$ in an electric field of $1\hat{\imath}\ \mathrm{Vm^{-1}}$. At $t = 0.2$ s, the electric field is switched off and a magnetic field of $6\hat{\jmath}$ T is switched on. The acceleration due to gravity is $-10\hat{\jmath}\ \mathrm{ms^{-2}}$. Correct option(s) is/are:

    1. The vertical distance of the particle from the $XZ$ plane at $t = 0.3$ s is $15$ cm.
    2. The vertical distance of the particle from the $XZ$ plane at $t = 0.4$ s is $10$ cm.
    3. The radius of the trajectory of the particle for $t > 0.2$ s is $20$ cm.
    4. The particle will be in the $XZ$ plane at $t = 0.35$ s.
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a), (c)

    $q/m = 10^{-6}/10^{-6} = 1$ C/kg, so $a_x = 1\ \mathrm{m/s^2}$ while $E$ is on.
    At $t=0.2$ s: $v_x = 1.2$ m/s, $v_y = 2 - 10(0.2) = 0$, $y = 2(0.2) - 5(0.2)^2 = 0.2$ m.
    $\vec B$ is along $\hat\jmath$, so the magnetic force acts only in the $XZ$ plane: circle of radius $r = \dfrac{mv_x}{qB} = \dfrac{1.2}{6} = 0.2$ m $=20$ cm — (C) true.
    Vertical motion is free fall from rest at $y=0.2$ m: $y = 0.2 - 5(t-0.2)^2$.
    $t=0.3$ s: $y = 0.15$ m $=15$ cm — (A) true; $t=0.4$ s: $y=0$ — (B) false; the particle reaches the $XZ$ plane at $t=0.4$ s, not $0.35$ s — (D) false.

  3. JEE Advanced 2025, Paper 2 · Question 25
    A conducting solid sphere of radius $R$ and mass $M$ carries a charge $Q$. The sphere is rotating about an axis passing through its center with a uniform angular speed $\omega$. The ratio of the magnitudes of the magnetic dipole moment to the angular momentum about the same axis is given as $\alpha\dfrac{Q}{2M}$. The value of $\alpha$ is ________.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 1.65 to 1.67

    On a conductor the charge sits on the surface: a rotating charged shell has $\mu=\dfrac{QR^2\omega}{3}$ (sum of rings $dq\cdot\dfrac{\omega}{2\pi}\cdot\pi R^2\sin^2\theta$, with $\langle\sin^2\theta\rangle=\tfrac23$).
    The mass is spread through the solid sphere: $L=\dfrac25MR^2\omega$.
    $\dfrac{\mu}{L}=\dfrac{Q/3}{2M/5}=\dfrac{5Q}{6M}=\dfrac53\cdot\dfrac{Q}{2M}$.
    $\alpha=\dfrac53\approx1.67$.

  4. JEE Advanced 2024, Paper 1 · Question 19
    An infinitely long wire, located on the $z$-axis, carries a current $I$ along the $+z$-direction and produces the magnetic field $\vec{B}$. The magnitude of the line integral $\int \vec{B} \cdot \vec{dl}$ along a straight line from the point $(-\sqrt{3}a, a, 0)$ to $(a, a, 0)$ is given by
    [$\mu_0$ is the magnetic permeability of free space.]

    1. $7\mu_0 I/24$
    2. $7\mu_0 I/12$
    3. $\mu_0 I/8$
    4. $\mu_0 I/6$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    $\vec B = \dfrac{\mu_0 I}{2\pi r}\hat\phi$, so $\vec B\cdot\vec{dl} = \dfrac{\mu_0 I}{2\pi r}\, r\,d\phi = \dfrac{\mu_0 I}{2\pi}d\phi$: the integral depends only on the angle swept.
    Start $(-\sqrt3a, a)$: $\phi = 150^\circ$. End $(a, a)$: $\phi = 45^\circ$. The line ($y = a$) does not cross the wire, so $|\Delta\phi| = 105^\circ = \dfrac{7\pi}{12}$.
    $\left|\int\vec B\cdot\vec{dl}\right| = \dfrac{\mu_0 I}{2\pi}\cdot\dfrac{7\pi}{12} = \dfrac{7\mu_0 I}{24}$ → (A).

  5. JEE Advanced 2024, Paper 2 · Question 21
    A thin stiff insulated metal wire is bent into a circular loop with its two ends extending tangentially from the same point of the loop. The wire loop has mass $m$ and radius $r$ and it is in a uniform vertical magnetic field $B_0$, as shown in the figure. Initially, it hangs vertically downwards, because of acceleration due to gravity $g$, on two conducting supports at P and Q. When a current $I$ is passed through the loop, the loop turns about the line PQ by an angle $\theta$ given by

    1. $\tan\theta=\pi rIB_0/(mg)$
    2. $\tan\theta=2\pi rIB_0/(mg)$
    3. $\tan\theta=\pi rIB_0/(2mg)$
    4. $\tan\theta=mg/(\pi rIB_0)$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    Magnetic moment $\mu=I\pi r^2$, along the normal to the loop. When the loop has turned by $\theta$ about PQ, its normal makes angle $90^\circ-\theta$ with the vertical field.
    Magnetic torque about PQ: $I\pi r^2B_0\sin(90^\circ-\theta)=I\pi r^2B_0\cos\theta$.
    Gravity acts at the centre, a distance $r$ from PQ: torque $mgr\sin\theta$.
    Balance: $I\pi r^2B_0\cos\theta=mgr\sin\theta\Rightarrow\tan\theta=\dfrac{\pi rIB_0}{mg}$ → (A).

  6. JEE Advanced 2024, Paper 2 · Question 24
    A positive, singly ionized atom of mass number $A_{\text{M}}$ is accelerated from rest by the voltage $192$ V. Thereafter, it enters a rectangular region of width $w$ with magnetic field $\vec B_0=0.1\hat k$ Tesla, as shown in the figure. The ion finally hits a detector at the distance $x$ below its starting trajectory.
    [Given: Mass of neutron/proton $=(5/3)\times10^{-27}$ kg, charge of the electron $=1.6\times10^{-19}$ C.]
    Which of the following option(s) is(are) correct?

    1. The value of $x$ for $H^+$ ion is $4$ cm.
    2. The value of $x$ for an ion with $A_{\text{M}}=144$ is $48$ cm.
    3. For detecting ions with $1\le A_{\text{M}}\le196$, the minimum height $(x_1-x_0)$ of the detector is $55$ cm.
    4. The minimum width $w$ of the region of the magnetic field for detecting ions with $A_{\text{M}}=196$ is $56$ cm.
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a), (b)

    Radius $R=\dfrac{\sqrt{2mqV}}{qB}$. For $H^+$: $\sqrt{2\cdot\frac53\times10^{-27}\cdot1.6\times10^{-19}\cdot192}=3.2\times10^{-22}$, so $R=\dfrac{3.2\times10^{-22}}{1.6\times10^{-20}}=0.02$ m.
    The ion turns through a semicircle and comes back out, so $x=2R=4$ cm → (A) true.
    $R\propto\sqrt{A_{\text{M}}}$: for $A_{\text{M}}=144$, $x=12\times4=48$ cm → (B) true.
    $x_0=4$ cm ($A_{\text{M}}=1$), $x_1=14\times4=56$ cm ($A_{\text{M}}=196$): height $52$ cm → (C) false.
    Minimum width for $A_{\text{M}}=196$ is $R=28$ cm → (D) false.
    Answer: (A), (B).

  7. JEE Advanced 2022, Paper 2 · Question 36
    Which one of the following options represents the magnetic field $\vec B$ at O due to the current flowing in the given wire segments lying on the $xy$ plane?

    1. $\vec B = \dfrac{-\mu_0 I}{L}\left(\dfrac32 + \dfrac{1}{4\sqrt2\pi}\right)\hat k$
    2. $\vec B = -\dfrac{\mu_0 I}{L}\left(\dfrac32 + \dfrac{1}{2\sqrt2\pi}\right)\hat k$
    3. $\vec B = \dfrac{-\mu_0 I}{L}\left(1 + \dfrac{1}{4\sqrt2\pi}\right)\hat k$
    4. $\vec B = \dfrac{-\mu_0 I}{L}\left(1 + \dfrac{1}{4\pi}\right)\hat k$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    Straight segments whose lines pass through O (the horizontal $L/2$ wire and segment on the dashed line, and the vertical $3L/4$ wire below O) give no field at O.
    Semicircle of radius $\dfrac L2$ centred at O, traversed clockwise: $\dfrac{\mu_0 I}{4(L/2)} = \dfrac{\mu_0 I}{2L}$, into the page ($-\hat k$).
    Quarter circle of radius $\dfrac L4$, clockwise: $\dfrac{\mu_0 I}{8(L/4)} = \dfrac{\mu_0 I}{2L}$, into the page.
    Left vertical wire of length $L$, at distance $L$ from O, with O level with its top end: $\dfrac{\mu_0 I}{4\pi L}\sin45^\circ = \dfrac{\mu_0 I}{4\sqrt2\pi L}$; current upward, O to its right, so into the page.
    $\vec B = -\dfrac{\mu_0 I}{L}\left(1 + \dfrac1{4\sqrt2\pi}\right)\hat k$ → (C).

  8. JEE Advanced 2021, Paper 1 · Question 36
    An $\alpha$-particle (mass 4 amu) and a singly charged sulfur ion (mass 32 amu) are initially at rest. They are accelerated through a potential $V$ and then allowed to pass into a region of uniform magnetic field which is normal to the velocities of the particles. Within this region, the $\alpha$-particle and the sulfur ion move in circular orbits of radii $r_\alpha$ and $r_S$, respectively. The ratio $(r_S/r_\alpha)$ is ___.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 4

    After acceleration $\dfrac12mv^2 = qV$, so $mv = \sqrt{2mqV}$.
    $r = \dfrac{mv}{qB} = \dfrac1B\sqrt{\dfrac{2mV}{q}} \propto \sqrt{\dfrac mq}$.
    $\dfrac{r_S}{r_\alpha} = \sqrt{\dfrac{32/1}{4/2}} = \sqrt{16} = 4$.

  9. JEE Advanced 2021, Paper 2 · Question 25
    Two concentric circular loops, one of radius $R$ and the other of radius $2R$, lie in the $xy$-plane with the origin as their common center, as shown in the figure. The smaller loop carries current $I_1$ in the anti-clockwise direction and the larger loop carries current $I_2$ in the clockwise direction, with $I_2 > 2I_1$. $\vec{B}(x, y)$ denotes the magnetic field at a point $(x, y)$ in the $xy$-plane. Which of the following statement(s) is(are) correct?

    1. $\vec{B}(x, y)$ is perpendicular to the $xy$-plane at any point in the plane
    2. $|\vec{B}(x, y)|$ depends on $x$ and $y$ only through the radial distance $r = \sqrt{x^2 + y^2}$
    3. $|\vec{B}(x, y)|$ is non-zero at all points for $r < R$
    4. $\vec{B}(x, y)$ points normally outward from the $xy$-plane for all the points between the two loops
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a), (b)

    (A) Every current element lies in the $xy$-plane, so $d\vec l \times \vec r$ for a field point in that plane is along $\pm\hat k$ → true.
    (B) The arrangement is symmetric under rotation about the $z$-axis → true.
    (C) At the centre: $B = \dfrac{\mu_0I_1}{2R} - \dfrac{\mu_0I_2}{4R} = \dfrac{\mu_0}{4R}(2I_1 - I_2) < 0$ (into the plane). Near the inner wire the field of $I_1$ grows without limit (out of the plane). So $B$ changes sign, and is zero somewhere in $r < R$ → false.
    (D) Between the loops, the anticlockwise inner loop gives a field into the plane (outside it), and the clockwise outer loop also gives a field into the plane → false.
    Answer (A), (B).
