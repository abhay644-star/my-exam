  1. JEE Advanced 2026, Paper 1 · Question 27
    As shown in the figure, an insulated container is fitted with a thermally conducting but immovable partition ($P_1$) and a freely movable but thermally insulated piston ($P_2$). The partition $P_1$ with thermal conductivity $K$, cross sectional area $A$ and width $x$ divides the container into two sections, $S_1$ and $S_2$, each containing one mole of a monoatomic gas. The piston $P_2$ moves freely such that the gas in $S_2$ is always at the atmospheric pressure. Initially, the difference between the temperatures of $S_1$ and $S_2$ is $\Delta T_0$. The time it takes for the temperature difference to become $\dfrac{\Delta T_0}{2}$ is $nxR/KA$, where $R$ is the universal gas constant. The value of $n$ is:
    [ Given: $\ln 2 \approx 0.7$ ]

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 0.63 to 0.7

    Heat current $H = KA\,\Delta T/x$ from the hotter to the colder section.
    $S_1$ (fixed volume): $C_V = \tfrac32R$; $S_2$ (constant pressure): $C_P = \tfrac52R$.
    $\dfrac{d(\Delta T)}{dt} = -H\left(\dfrac{2}{3R} + \dfrac{2}{5R}\right) = -\dfrac{16}{15}\dfrac{KA}{xR}\Delta T$.
    $t_{1/2} = \dfrac{15}{16}\ln2\,\dfrac{xR}{KA} = 0.656\,\dfrac{xR}{KA}$.
    $n \approx 0.66$.

  2. JEE Advanced 2025, Paper 1 · Question 26
    Two identical plates P and Q, radiating as perfect black bodies, are kept in vacuum at constant absolute temperatures $T_P$ and $T_Q$, respectively, with $T_Q \lt T_P$, as shown in Fig. 1. The radiated power transferred per unit area from P to Q is $W_0$. Subsequently, two more plates, identical to P and Q, are introduced between P and Q, as shown in Fig. 2. Assume that heat transfer takes place only between adjacent plates. If the power transferred per unit area in the direction from P to Q (Fig. 2) in the steady state is $W_S$, then the ratio $\dfrac{W_0}{W_S}$ is ______

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 3

    Fig. 1: $W_0 = \sigma(T_P^4 - T_Q^4)$.
    Fig. 2: with plates at $T_1, T_2$ in between, in the steady state the same flux crosses each of the three gaps: $W_S = \sigma(T_P^4 - T_1^4) = \sigma(T_1^4 - T_2^4) = \sigma(T_2^4 - T_Q^4)$.
    Adding: $3W_S = \sigma(T_P^4 - T_Q^4) = W_0$.
    $\dfrac{W_0}{W_S} = 3$.

  3. JEE Advanced 2024, Paper 1 · Question 25
    The specific heat capacity of a substance is temperature dependent and is given by the formula $C = kT$, where $k$ is a constant of suitable dimensions in SI units, and $T$ is the absolute temperature. If the heat required to raise the temperature of 1 kg of the substance from $-73\ ^\circ$C to $27\ ^\circ$C is $nk$, the value of $n$ is _____.
    [Given: $0$ K $= -273\ ^\circ$C.]

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 25000

    $T_1 = 200$ K, $T_2 = 300$ K, $m = 1$ kg.
    $Q = \int_{200}^{300} kT\,dT = \dfrac k2(300^2 - 200^2) = \dfrac k2(50000) = 25000k$.
    $n = 25000$.

  4. JEE Advanced 2021, Paper 1 · Question 38
    A small object is placed at the center of a large evacuated hollow spherical container. Assume that the container is maintained at 0 K. At time $t = 0$, the temperature of the object is 200 K. The temperature of the object becomes 100 K at $t = t_1$ and 50 K at $t = t_2$. Assume the object and the container to be ideal black bodies. The heat capacity of the object does not depend on temperature.
    The ratio $(t_2/t_1)$ is ___.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 9

    With the surroundings at 0 K: $C\dfrac{dT}{dt} = -\sigma A T^4$.
    Integrating: $t = \dfrac{C}{3\sigma A}\left(\dfrac{1}{T^3} - \dfrac{1}{T_0^3}\right)$ with $T_0 = 200$ K.
    $t_1 \propto \dfrac{1}{100^3} - \dfrac{1}{200^3} = \dfrac{8 - 1}{200^3}$; $t_2 \propto \dfrac{1}{50^3} - \dfrac{1}{200^3} = \dfrac{64 - 1}{200^3}$.
    $\dfrac{t_2}{t_1} = \dfrac{63}{7} = 9$.
