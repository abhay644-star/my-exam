  1. JEE Advanced 2026, Paper 1 · Question 25
    A tank contains two immiscible liquids of densities $6\rho$ and $2\rho$. The higher density liquid is filled up to a height $L/2$ from the bottom. A thin rod of density $\rho$ and length $L$ is fully immersed and hinged at the bottom so that it can oscillate freely, as shown in the figure. If the rod is slightly disturbed from its equilibrium, the time period of small oscillations is $\dfrac{2\pi}{n}\sqrt{\dfrac{L}{g}}$, where $g$ is the acceleration due to gravity. The value of $n$ is:

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 1.7 to 1.75

    Let the rod have cross-section $A$; tilt it by a small angle $\theta$ about the hinge.
    Upward buoyancy: $6\rho A\tfrac L2 g$ acting at $L/4$ and $2\rho A\tfrac L2 g$ at $3L/4$; weight $\rho ALg$ at $L/2$.
    Restoring torque $= \theta gA\rho L^2\left(\tfrac34 + \tfrac34 - \tfrac12\right) = \rho AgL^2\theta$.
    $I = \tfrac13\rho AL\cdot L^2$, so $\omega^2 = \dfrac{\rho AgL^2}{\rho AL^3/3} = \dfrac{3g}{L}$.
    $T = \dfrac{2\pi}{\sqrt3}\sqrt{\dfrac Lg} \Rightarrow n = \sqrt3 \approx 1.73$.

  2. JEE Advanced 2025, Paper 1 · Question 17
    The center of a disk of radius $r$ and mass $m$ is attached to a spring of spring constant $k$, inside a ring of radius $R \gt r$ as shown in the figure. The other end of the spring is attached on the periphery of the ring. Both the ring and the disk are in the same vertical plane. The disk can only roll along the inside periphery of the ring, without slipping. The spring can only be stretched or compressed along the periphery of the ring, following the Hooke’s law. In equilibrium, the disk is at the bottom of the ring. Assuming small displacement of the disc, the time period of oscillation of center of mass of the disk is written as $T = \dfrac{2\pi}{\omega}$. The correct expression for $\omega$ is ($g$ is the acceleration due to gravity):

    1. $\sqrt{\dfrac{2}{3}\left(\dfrac{g}{R-r} + \dfrac{k}{m}\right)}$
    2. $\sqrt{\dfrac{2g}{3(R-r)} + \dfrac{k}{m}}$
    3. $\sqrt{\dfrac{1}{6}\left(\dfrac{g}{R-r} + \dfrac{k}{m}\right)}$
    4. $\sqrt{\dfrac{1}{4}\left(\dfrac{g}{R-r} + \dfrac{k}{m}\right)}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    Let the centre of the disk move a small arc length $s = (R-r)\phi$ from the bottom; the spring changes length by $s$.
    Rolling without slipping: KE $= \dfrac12mv^2 + \dfrac12\cdot\dfrac12mr^2\cdot\dfrac{v^2}{r^2} = \dfrac34mv^2$, with $v = \dot s$.
    PE $= mg(R-r)(1 - \cos\phi) + \dfrac12ks^2 \approx \dfrac12\left(\dfrac{mg}{R-r} + k\right)s^2$.
    $\omega^2 = \dfrac{\frac{mg}{R-r} + k}{\frac32m} = \dfrac23\left(\dfrac{g}{R-r} + \dfrac km\right)$ → (A).

  3. JEE Advanced 2025, Paper 2 · Question 19
    As shown in the figures, a uniform rod $OO'$ of length $l$ is hinged at the point $O$ and held in place vertically between two walls using two massless springs of same spring constant. The springs are connected at the midpoint and at the top-end ($O'$) of the rod, as shown in Fig. 1 and the rod is made to oscillate by a small angular displacement. The frequency of oscillation of the rod is $f_1$. On the other hand, if both the springs are connected at the midpoint of the rod, as shown in Fig. 2 and the rod is made to oscillate by a small angular displacement, then the frequency of oscillation is $f_2$. Ignoring gravity and assuming motion only in the plane of the diagram, the value of $\dfrac{f_1}{f_2}$ is:

    1. $2$
    2. $\sqrt{2}$
    3. $\sqrt{\dfrac{5}{2}}$
    4. $\sqrt{\dfrac{2}{5}}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    For a small angle $\theta$, a spring attached at distance $d$ from $O$ stretches by $d\theta$ and gives restoring torque $kd^2\theta$. The moment of inertia $I$ is the same in both cases, and $f\propto\sqrt{\tau/\theta}$.
    Fig. 1: $\tau=k\left(\dfrac l2\right)^2\theta+kl^2\theta=\dfrac54kl^2\theta$.
    Fig. 2: $\tau=2k\left(\dfrac l2\right)^2\theta=\dfrac12kl^2\theta$.
    $\dfrac{f_1}{f_2}=\sqrt{\dfrac{5/4}{1/2}}=\sqrt{\dfrac52}$. Answer (C).

  4. JEE Advanced 2024, Paper 1 · Question 21
    A block of mass 5 kg moves along the $x$-direction subject to the force $F = (-20x + 10)$ N, with the value of $x$ in metre. At time $t = 0$ s, it is at rest at position $x = 1$ m. The position and momentum of the block at $t = (\pi/4)$ s are

    1. $-0.5$ m, $5$ kg m/s
    2. $0.5$ m, $0$ kg m/s
    3. $0.5$ m, $-5$ kg m/s
    4. $-1$ m, $5$ kg m/s
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    $F = -20(x - 0.5)$: SHM about $x = 0.5$ m with $\omega = \sqrt{20/5} = 2$ rad/s.
    Released from rest at $x = 1$ m, so amplitude $0.5$ m: $x = 0.5 + 0.5\cos 2t$, $v = -\sin 2t$ m/s.
    At $t = \pi/4$: $x = 0.5$ m, $v = -1$ m/s, $p = 5 \times (-1) = -5$ kg m/s → (C).

  5. JEE Advanced 2024, Paper 2 · Question 34
    Two particles, 1 and 2, each of mass $m$, are connected by a massless spring, and are on a horizontal frictionless plane, as shown in the figure. Initially, the two particles, with their center of mass at $x_0$, are oscillating with amplitude $a$ and angular frequency $\omega$. Thus, their positions at time $t$ are given by $x_1(t)=(x_0+d)+a\sin\omega t$ and $x_2(t)=(x_0-d)-a\sin\omega t$, respectively, where $d>2a$. Particle 3 of mass $m$ moves towards this system with speed $u_0=a\omega/2$, and undergoes instantaneous elastic collision with particle 2, at time $t_0$. Finally, particles 1 and 2 acquire a center of mass speed $v_{\text{cm}}$ and oscillate with amplitude $b$ and the same angular frequency $\omega$.

    If the collision occurs at time $t_0=\pi/(2\omega)$, then the value of $4b^2/a^2$ will be ________.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 4.2 to 4.3

    At $t_0=\dfrac{\pi}{2\omega}$ both particles are momentarily at rest, each displaced by $a$ from its equilibrium relative to the CM (spring at maximum stretch).
    After the collision particle 2 has $\dfrac{a\omega}{2}$ and particle 1 has $0$, so $v_{\text{cm}}=\dfrac{a\omega}{4}$; relative to the CM each particle moves at $\dfrac{a\omega}{4}$ while displaced by $a$.
    $b^2=a^2+\left(\dfrac{a\omega/4}{\omega}\right)^2=\dfrac{17}{16}a^2$.
    $\dfrac{4b^2}{a^2}=\dfrac{17}{4}=4.25$.

  6. JEE Advanced 2023, Paper 1 · Question 30
    Two point-like objects of masses $20$ gm and $30$ gm are fixed at the two ends of a rigid massless rod of length $10$ cm. This system is suspended vertically from a rigid ceiling using a thin wire attached to its center of mass, as shown in the figure. The resulting torsional pendulum undergoes small oscillations. The torsional constant of the wire is $1.2\times10^{-8}\ \text{N m rad}^{-1}$. The angular frequency of the oscillations in $n\times10^{-3}\ \text{rad s}^{-1}$. The value of $n$ is ______.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 10

    Centre of mass: $4$ cm from the $30$ gm mass and $6$ cm from the $20$ gm mass.
    $I = 0.03(0.04)^2 + 0.02(0.06)^2 = 4.8\times10^{-5} + 7.2\times10^{-5} = 1.2\times10^{-4}\ \text{kg m}^2$.
    $\omega = \sqrt{\dfrac{k}{I}} = \sqrt{\dfrac{1.2\times10^{-8}}{1.2\times10^{-4}}} = 10^{-2} = 10\times10^{-3}\ \text{rad s}^{-1}$. So $n = 10$.

  7. JEE Advanced 2022, Paper 2 · Question 25
    On a frictionless horizontal plane, a bob of mass $m = 0.1$ kg is attached to a spring with natural length $l_0 = 0.1$ m. The spring constant is $k_1 = 0.009$ N m$^{-1}$ when the length of the spring $l > l_0$ and is $k_2 = 0.016$ N m$^{-1}$ when $l < l_0$. Initially the bob is released from $l = 0.15$ m. Assume that Hooke’s law remains valid throughout the motion. If the time period of the full oscillation is $T = (n\pi)$ s, then the integer closest to $n$ is ________.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 6

    The bob spends half a period of each SHM on each side of $l_0$.
    $T = \pi\sqrt{\dfrac{m}{k_1}} + \pi\sqrt{\dfrac{m}{k_2}} = \pi\left(\sqrt{\dfrac{0.1}{0.009}} + \sqrt{\dfrac{0.1}{0.016}}\right) = \pi\left(\dfrac{10}3 + \dfrac52\right) = \dfrac{35}{6}\pi$ s.
    $n = 5.83$, closest integer $6$.
