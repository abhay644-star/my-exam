  1. JEE Advanced 2026, Paper 1 · Question 23
    A quasi-static cycle of a monoatomic ideal gas contains an isothermal process ($\boldsymbol{ab}$), followed by an isochoric process ($\boldsymbol{bc}$) and an adiabatic process ($\boldsymbol{ca}$) as shown in the figure. The volumes of the gas are $V_1$ and $V_2$ at $\boldsymbol{a}$ and $\boldsymbol{b}$, respectively. If the cycle has heat input $Q_{\text{in}}$ and output $Q_{\text{out}}$, then the efficiency of the cycle is defined as $\eta = \dfrac{Q_{\text{in}} - Q_{\text{out}}}{Q_{\text{in}}}$. The correct statement(s) is/are:
    [Given: $\ln 2 \approx 0.7$]

    1. If $V_2/V_1 = 8$, the heat released in the process $\boldsymbol{bc}$ is smaller than the heat absorbed in the process $\boldsymbol{ab}$.
    2. For a given value of $V_2/V_1$, $\eta$ does not depend on the temperature of the isothermal process.
    3. If $V_2/V_1 = 8$, then the temperature of the gas at $\boldsymbol{a}$ is 4 times the temperature of the gas at $\boldsymbol{c}$.
    4. If $V_2/V_1 = 8$, then the pressure of the gas at $\boldsymbol{a}$ is 4 times the pressure of the gas at $\boldsymbol{b}$.
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a), (b), (c)

    Adiabatic $ca$: $T_aV_1^{2/3} = T_cV_2^{2/3} \Rightarrow T_a/T_c = 8^{2/3} = 4$: (C) true.
    Per mole: $Q_{ab} = RT_a\ln 8 = 2.1RT_a$ absorbed; $Q_{bc} = \tfrac32R(T_a - T_a/4) = 1.125RT_a$ released, smaller: (A) true.
    $\eta = 1 - \dfrac{\tfrac32(1 - r^{-2/3})}{\ln r}$ with $r = V_2/V_1$, independent of $T_a$: (B) true.
    Isothermal $ab$: $P_a/P_b = V_2/V_1 = 8$, not 4: (D) false.

  2. JEE Advanced 2026, Paper 1 · Question 26
    As shown in the figure, five Carnot engines, each with efficiency $\eta$ and same number of cycles per unit time, are operating between six heat reservoirs. The amount of heat released per cycle by one engine is completely absorbed by the next engine. Consider $Q_0$ to be the amount of heat absorbed per cycle by the first engine and $W$ as the amount of total work done by all the engines per cycle, then the net efficiency of the system is found to be $\eta_{\text{net}} = \dfrac{W}{Q_0} = \dfrac{211}{243}$. The value of $\eta$ is:

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 0.32 to 0.34

    Each engine rejects a fraction $(1-\eta)$ of what it absorbs: $Q_k = (1-\eta)^kQ_0$.
    $W = Q_0 - Q_5 = Q_0[1 - (1-\eta)^5]$.
    $1 - (1-\eta)^5 = \tfrac{211}{243} \Rightarrow (1-\eta)^5 = \tfrac{32}{243} = \left(\tfrac23\right)^5$.
    $\eta = 1/3 \approx 0.33$.

  3. JEE Advanced 2026, Paper 2 · Question 27
    Ten moles of an ideal monoatomic gas, initially in state $\boldsymbol{a}$ at atmospheric pressure and temperature $T_a = 27^\circ\mathrm{C}$, is enclosed in a metal cylinder of volume $V_0$ fitted with a frictionless piston. The gas is suddenly compressed to state $\boldsymbol{b}$ with volume $V_0/3$. Now, keeping the piston stationary, the cylinder is submerged in a water bath of temperature $11^\circ\mathrm{C}$ until the gas reaches the temperature of the water bath, which is denoted as state $\boldsymbol{c}$. Finally, while still in the water bath, the piston is brought slowly to its initial position, which is denoted as state $\boldsymbol{f}$. If $R$ is universal gas constant, then the correct option(s) is/are:
    [Given: $9^{1/3} = 2.08$]

    2. The change in internal energy in going from state $\boldsymbol{a}$ to $\boldsymbol{b}$ is $4860$R.
    3. The net change in the internal energy in the whole process is $-240$R.
    4. The pressure and temperature of the state $\boldsymbol{b}$ are $2.08$ times the atmospheric pressure and $624$ K, respectively.
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a), (b), (c)

    $a\to b$ adiabatic, $\gamma = 5/3$: $T_b = 300\cdot 3^{2/3} = 300\cdot 9^{1/3} = 624$ K; $P_b = P_0\,3^{5/3} = 3(2.08)P_0 = 6.24P_0$ — (D) false.
    $\Delta U_{ab} = nC_v\Delta T = 10\cdot\tfrac32R\cdot 324 = 4860R$ — (B) true.
    $b\to c$ isochoric cooling to 284 K, $c\to f$ isothermal expansion back to $V_0$ at 284 K, so $f$ lies below $a$ on the same vertical line, matching the sketch — (A) true.
    Net $\Delta U = 10\cdot\tfrac32R(284-300) = -240R$ — (C) true.

  4. JEE Advanced 2025, Paper 2 · Question 24
    The efficiency of a Carnot engine operating with a hot reservoir kept at a temperature of 1000 K is 0.4. It extracts 150 J of heat per cycle from the hot reservoir. The work extracted from this engine is being fully used to run a heat pump which has a coefficient of performance 10. The hot reservoir of the heat pump is at a temperature of 300 K. Which of the following statements is/are correct:

    1. Work extracted from the Carnot engine in one cycle is 60 J.
    2. Temperature of the cold reservoir of the Carnot engine is 600 K.
    3. Temperature of the cold reservoir of the heat pump is 270 K.
    4. Heat supplied to the hot reservoir of the heat pump in one cycle is 540 J.
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a), (b), (c)

    $W=\eta Q_H=0.4\times150=60$ J (A true).
    $\eta=1-\dfrac{T_C}{1000}=0.4\Rightarrow T_C=600$ K (B true).
    Heat pump: $\text{COP}=\dfrac{T_H}{T_H-T_C}=\dfrac{300}{300-T_C}=10\Rightarrow T_C=270$ K (C true).
    Heat delivered to its hot reservoir $=\text{COP}\times W=10\times60=600$ J, not 540 J (D false).
    Answer (A), (B), (C).

  5. JEE Advanced 2025, Paper 2 · Question 27
    An ideal monatomic gas of $n$ moles is taken through a cycle $WXYZW$ consisting of consecutive adiabatic and isobaric quasi-static processes, as shown in the schematic $V$-$T$ diagram. The volume of the gas at $W$, $X$ and $Y$ points are, 64 cm$^3$, 125 cm$^3$ and 250 cm$^3$, respectively. If the absolute temperature of the gas $T_W$ at the point $W$ is such that $nRT_W=1$ J ($R$ is the universal gas constant), then the amount of heat absorbed (in J) by the gas along the path $XY$ is ________.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 1.6

    On the $V$-$T$ diagram, $XY$ and $ZW$ lie on straight lines through the origin (isobaric); $WX$ and $YZ$ are adiabatic.
    $W\to X$ adiabatic: $TV^{\gamma-1}=$ const with $\gamma-1=\dfrac23$: $T_X=T_W\left(\dfrac{64}{125}\right)^{2/3}=\dfrac{16}{25}T_W$.
    $X\to Y$ isobaric: $T_Y=T_X\dfrac{V_Y}{V_X}=2T_X=\dfrac{32}{25}T_W$.
    $Q_{XY}=nC_p(T_Y-T_X)=\dfrac52\cdot\dfrac{16}{25}nRT_W=1.6$ J.

  6. JEE Advanced 2025, Paper 2 · Question 29
    The left and right compartments of a thermally isolated container of length $L$ are separated by a thermally conducting, movable piston of area $A$. The left and right compartments are filled with $\dfrac{3}{2}$ and 1 moles of an ideal gas, respectively. In the left compartment the piston is attached by a spring with spring constant $k$ and natural length $\dfrac{2L}{5}$. In thermodynamic equilibrium, the piston is at a distance $\dfrac{L}{2}$ from the left and right edges of the container as shown in the figure. Under the above conditions, if the pressure in the right compartment is $P=\dfrac{kL}{A}\alpha$, then the value of $\alpha$ is ________.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 0.2

    The piston conducts heat, so both gases have the same temperature; the volumes are equal ($AL/2$ each). So $P_{\text{left}}=\dfrac32P$.
    The spring is stretched by $\dfrac L2-\dfrac{2L}{5}=\dfrac{L}{10}$ and pulls the piston to the left with force $\dfrac{kL}{10}$.
    Piston equilibrium: $P_{\text{left}}A=PA+\dfrac{kL}{10}\Rightarrow\dfrac12PA=\dfrac{kL}{10}\Rightarrow P=\dfrac{kL}{5A}$.
    $\alpha=0.2$.

  7. JEE Advanced 2024, Paper 1 · Question 31
    One mole of a monatomic ideal gas undergoes the cyclic process J $\to$ K $\to$ L $\to$ M $\to$ J, as shown in the P-T diagram.
    Match the quantities mentioned in List-I with their values in List-II and choose the correct option.
    [$\mathcal{R}$ is the gas constant.]

    List-IList-II
    (P) Work done in the complete cyclic process(1) $\mathcal{R}T_0 - 4\mathcal{R}T_0\ln 2$
    (Q) Change in the internal energy of the gas in the process JK(2) $0$
    (R) Heat given to the gas in the process KL(3) $3\mathcal{R}T_0$
    (S) Change in the internal energy of the gas in the process MJ(4) $-2\mathcal{R}T_0\ln 2$
    (5) $-3\mathcal{R}T_0\ln 2$

    1. P→1, Q→3, R→5, S→4
    2. P→4, Q→3, R→5, S→2
    3. P→4, Q→1, R→2, S→2
    4. P→2, Q→5, R→3, S→4
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    JK: isobaric at $P_0$, $T_0 \to 3T_0$: $W = \mathcal R(2T_0)$, $\Delta U = \dfrac32\mathcal R(2T_0) = 3\mathcal RT_0$.
    KL: isothermal at $3T_0$, $P_0 \to 2P_0$: $W = 3\mathcal RT_0\ln\dfrac{P_0}{2P_0} = -3\mathcal RT_0\ln2 = Q$.
    LM: isobaric at $2P_0$, $3T_0 \to T_0$: $W = -2\mathcal RT_0$.
    MJ: isothermal at $T_0$, $2P_0 \to P_0$: $W = \mathcal RT_0\ln2$, $\Delta U = 0$.
    Net work $= 2\mathcal RT_0 - 3\mathcal RT_0\ln2 - 2\mathcal RT_0 + \mathcal RT_0\ln2 = -2\mathcal RT_0\ln2$.
    P→4, Q→3, R→5, S→2 → (B).

  8. JEE Advanced 2023, Paper 1 · Question 23
    One mole of an ideal gas expands adiabatically from an initial state $(T_{\mathrm A}, V_0)$ to final state $(T_{\mathrm f}, 5V_0)$. Another mole of the same gas expands isothermally from a different initial state $(T_{\mathrm B}, V_0)$ to the same final state $(T_{\mathrm f}, 5V_0)$. The ratio of the specific heats at constant pressure and constant volume of this ideal gas is $\gamma$. What is the ratio $T_{\mathrm A}/T_{\mathrm B}$?

    1. $5^{\gamma-1}$
    2. $5^{1-\gamma}$
    3. $5^{\gamma}$
    4. $5^{1+\gamma}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    Adiabatic: $T_{\mathrm A}V_0^{\gamma-1} = T_{\mathrm f}(5V_0)^{\gamma-1} \Rightarrow T_{\mathrm A} = 5^{\gamma-1}T_{\mathrm f}$.
    Isothermal: $T_{\mathrm B} = T_{\mathrm f}$.
    $T_{\mathrm A}/T_{\mathrm B} = 5^{\gamma-1}$. Answer (A).

  9. JEE Advanced 2023, Paper 1 · Question 28
    A closed container contains a homogeneous mixture of two moles of an ideal monatomic gas $(\gamma = 5/3)$ and one mole of an ideal diatomic gas $(\gamma = 7/5)$. Here, $\gamma$ is the ratio of the specific heats at constant pressure and constant volume of an ideal gas. The gas mixture does a work of $66$ Joule when heated at constant pressure. The change in its internal energy is ______ Joule.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 121

    At constant pressure $W = nR\Delta T = 3R\Delta T = 66 \Rightarrow R\Delta T = 22$ J.
    $\Delta U = \left(2\cdot\dfrac32 + 1\cdot\dfrac52\right)R\Delta T = \dfrac{11}{2}\times22 = 121$ J.

  10. JEE Advanced 2023, Paper 2 · Question 30
    One mole of an ideal gas undergoes two different cyclic processes I and II, as shown in the $P$-$V$ diagrams below. In cycle I, processes $a, b, c$ and $d$ are isobaric, isothermal, isobaric and isochoric, respectively. In cycle II, processes $a', b', c'$ and $d'$ are isothermal, isochoric, isobaric and isochoric, respectively. The total work done during cycle I is $W_I$ and that during cycle II is $W_{II}$. The ratio $W_I/W_{II}$ is ________.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 2

    Cycle I: $a$: $4P_0(2V_0 - V_0) = 4P_0V_0$; $b$ (isothermal, $PV = 8P_0V_0$, $2V_0 \to 4V_0$): $8P_0V_0\ln2$; $c$: $2P_0(V_0 - 4V_0) = -6P_0V_0$; $d$: 0.
    $W_I = P_0V_0(8\ln2 - 2)$.
    Cycle II: $a'$ (isothermal, $PV = 4P_0V_0$, $V_0 \to 2V_0$): $4P_0V_0\ln2$; $c'$: $P_0(V_0 - 2V_0) = -P_0V_0$; $b'$, $d'$: 0.
    $W_{II} = P_0V_0(4\ln2 - 1)$.
    $\dfrac{W_I}{W_{II}} = 2$.

  11. JEE Advanced 2022, Paper 1 · Question 35
    List I describes thermodynamic processes in four different systems. List II gives the magnitudes (either exactly or as a close approximation) of possible changes in the internal energy of the system due to the process.

    List-IList-II
    (I) $10^{-3}$ kg of water at $100^\circ$C is converted to steam at the same temperature, at a pressure of $10^5$ Pa. The volume of the system changes from $10^{-6}\ \mathrm{m^3}$ to $10^{-3}\ \mathrm{m^3}$ in the process. Latent heat of water $=2250$ kJ/kg.(P) 2 kJ
    (II) 0.2 moles of a rigid diatomic ideal gas with volume $V$ at temperature $500$ K undergoes an isobaric expansion to volume $3V$. Assume $R=8.0\ \mathrm{J\,mol^{-1}K^{-1}}$.(Q) 7 kJ
    (III) One mole of a monatomic ideal gas is compressed adiabatically from volume $V=\frac13\ \mathrm{m^3}$ and pressure 2 kPa to volume $\frac V8$.(R) 4 kJ
    (IV) Three moles of a diatomic ideal gas whose molecules can vibrate, is given 9 kJ of heat and undergoes isobaric expansion.(S) 5 kJ
    (T) 3 kJ
    Which one of the following options is correct?

    1. I → T, II → R, III → S, IV → Q
    2. I → S, II → P, III → T, IV → P
    3. I → P, II → R, III → T, IV → Q
    4. I → Q, II → R, III → S, IV → T
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    (I) $Q=mL=2.25$ kJ, $W=P\Delta V=10^5(10^{-3}-10^{-6})\approx0.1$ kJ, $\Delta U\approx2.15$ kJ $\approx2$ kJ (P).
    (II) $T$: 500 K → 1500 K; $\Delta U=nC_v\Delta T=0.2\times\frac52\times8\times1000=4$ kJ (R).
    (III) $TV^{2/3}$ constant: $V\to V/8$ multiplies $T$ (and $PV$) by 4. $\Delta U=\frac32(P_2V_2-P_1V_1)=\frac32\times\frac{2000}{3}\times3=3$ kJ (T).
    (IV) With vibration $C_v=\frac72R$, $C_p=\frac92R$; isobaric: $\Delta U=Q\dfrac{C_v}{C_p}=9\times\frac79=7$ kJ (Q).
    Answer (C).

  12. JEE Advanced 2022, Paper 2 · Question 32
    In the given $P$-$V$ diagram, a monoatomic gas $\left(\gamma = \dfrac53\right)$ is first compressed adiabatically from state $A$ to state $B$. Then it expands isothermally from state $B$ to state $C$. [Given: $\left(\dfrac13\right)^{0.6} \simeq 0.5$, $\ln 2 \simeq 0.7$].
    Which of the following statement(s) is(are) correct?

    1. The magnitude of the total work done in the process $A \to B \to C$ is $144$ kJ.
    2. The magnitude of the work done in the process $B \to C$ is $84$ kJ.
    3. The magnitude of the work done in the process $A \to B$ is $60$ kJ.
    4. The magnitude of the work done in the process $C \to A$ is zero.
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: Dropped: full marks to everyone

    $A$: $100$ kPa, $0.8$ m$^3$. $B$: $300$ kPa, $V_B = 0.8\left(\dfrac13\right)^{3/5} = 0.8\times0.5 = 0.4$ m$^3$.
    $|W_{AB}| = \dfrac{P_BV_B - P_AV_A}{\gamma - 1} = \dfrac{120 - 80}{2/3} = 60$ kJ: (C).
    $|W_{BC}| = P_BV_B\ln\dfrac{V_C}{V_B} = 120\ln2 = 84$ kJ: (B).
    $C \to A$ is at constant volume, so its work is zero: (D).
    Net work $A\to B\to C$ is $-60 + 84 = 24$ kJ (the sum of magnitudes is $144$ kJ), so (A) depends on how “total work” is read.
    The official final key dropped this question (marks to all).

  13. JEE Advanced 2021, Paper 1 · Question 21
    An ideal gas undergoes a four step cycle as shown in the $P - V$ diagram below. During this cycle, heat is absorbed by the gas in

    1. steps 1 and 2
    2. steps 1 and 3
    3. steps 1 and 4
    4. steps 2 and 4
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    Step 1: isobaric expansion, so $T$ rises and $Q = nC_p\Delta T > 0$ (absorbed).
    Step 2: isochoric fall in pressure, $T$ falls: heat released.
    Step 3: isobaric compression, $T$ falls: heat released.
    Step 4: isochoric rise in pressure, $T$ rises: heat absorbed.
    Heat is absorbed in steps 1 and 4 → (C).

  14. JEE Advanced 2021, Paper 2 · Question 34
    A thermally insulating cylinder has a thermally insulating and frictionless movable partition in the middle, as shown in the figure below. On each side of the partition, there is one mole of an ideal gas, with specific heat at constant volume, $C_V = 2R$. Here, $R$ is the gas constant. Initially, each side has a volume $V_0$ and temperature $T_0$. The left side has an electric heater, which is turned on at very low power to transfer heat $Q$ to the gas on the left side. As a result the partition moves slowly towards the right reducing the right side volume to $V_0/2$. Consequently, the gas temperatures on the left and the right sides become $T_L$ and $T_R$, respectively. Ignore the changes in the temperatures of the cylinder, heater and the partition.

    The value of $\dfrac{T_R}{T_0}$ is

    1. $\sqrt2$
    2. $\sqrt3$
    3. $2$
    4. $3$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    The right gas is compressed slowly and adiabatically; $\gamma = \dfrac{C_V + R}{C_V} = \dfrac32$.
    $TV^{\gamma - 1} = $ const: $T_R = T_0\left(\dfrac{V_0}{V_0/2}\right)^{1/2} = \sqrt2\,T_0$ → (A).

  15. JEE Advanced 2021, Paper 2 · Question 35
    A thermally insulating cylinder has a thermally insulating and frictionless movable partition in the middle, as shown in the figure below. On each side of the partition, there is one mole of an ideal gas, with specific heat at constant volume, $C_V = 2R$. Here, $R$ is the gas constant. Initially, each side has a volume $V_0$ and temperature $T_0$. The left side has an electric heater, which is turned on at very low power to transfer heat $Q$ to the gas on the left side. As a result the partition moves slowly towards the right reducing the right side volume to $V_0/2$. Consequently, the gas temperatures on the left and the right sides become $T_L$ and $T_R$, respectively. Ignore the changes in the temperatures of the cylinder, heater and the partition.

    The value of $\dfrac{Q}{RT_0}$ is

    1. $4(2\sqrt2 + 1)$
    2. $4(2\sqrt2 - 1)$
    3. $(5\sqrt2 + 1)$
    4. $(5\sqrt2 - 1)$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    Final pressure (equal on both sides): $p = \dfrac{RT_R}{V_0/2} = 2\sqrt2\,p_0$, where $p_0 = \dfrac{RT_0}{V_0}$.
    Left side: $V = \dfrac32V_0$, so $T_L = \dfrac{pV}{R} = 2\sqrt2 \cdot \dfrac32\,T_0 = 3\sqrt2\,T_0$.
    No work leaves the insulated cylinder, so $Q = \Delta U_L + \Delta U_R = 2R(3\sqrt2 - 1)T_0 + 2R(\sqrt2 - 1)T_0$.
    $\dfrac{Q}{RT_0} = 8\sqrt2 - 4 = 4(2\sqrt2 - 1)$ → (B).
