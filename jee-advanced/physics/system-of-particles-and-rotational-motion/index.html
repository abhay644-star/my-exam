  1. JEE Advanced 2026, Paper 1 · Question 17
    Consider a large disk of radius $R$ and two smaller disks, each of radius $r = R/50$, lying on its circumference, as shown in the figure. The smaller disks are initially in contact with each other, with an angular separation $\Delta\theta$ between their centers. They are made to roll without slipping in opposite directions, with constant angular velocities $\omega$ and $2\omega$ while the large disk is held stationary. The time $\tau$ at which the smaller disks are again in contact is:
    [Use $\sin(\Delta\theta) = \Delta\theta$ and ignore gravity.]

    1. $\tau = 51 \times \left(2\pi - \dfrac{4}{51}\right)/\omega$
    2. $\tau = 51 \times \left(2\pi - \dfrac{2}{51}\right)/3\omega$
    3. $\tau = 51 \times \left(2\pi - \dfrac{4}{51}\right)/3\omega$
    4. $\tau = 51 \times \left(2\pi - \dfrac{2}{51}\right)/\omega$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c)

    Centres of the small disks move on a circle of radius $R + r = 51r$.
    Rolling without slipping on the fixed disk: centre speed $= \omega_{\text{spin}} r$, so the centres revolve about $O$ at $\omega/51$ and $2\omega/51$, in opposite senses; relative rate $3\omega/51$.
    Initial separation: $2r = 51r\,\Delta\theta \Rightarrow \Delta\theta = 2/51$.
    They touch again when they meet from the other side, i.e. after a relative rotation of $2\pi - 2\Delta\theta = 2\pi - 4/51$.
    $\tau = \dfrac{51}{3\omega}\left(2\pi - \dfrac{4}{51}\right)$ → (C).

  2. JEE Advanced 2026, Paper 1 · Question 19
    A solid cylinder of radius $R$ rolls without slipping with a center of mass speed $v_0 = \sqrt{\dfrac{gR}{3}}$ on a horizontal surface with a vertical edge, as shown in the figure. Here, $g$ is the acceleration due to the gravity. At the moment when the cylinder loses contact with the surface due to rotation around the corner, the speed of its center of mass is:

    1. $0$
    2. $\sqrt{\dfrac{5gR}{7}}$
    3. $\sqrt{\dfrac{gR}{15}}$
    4. $\sqrt{\dfrac{3gR}{7}}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b)

    At the edge the cylinder simply continues rotating about the corner, $I_{\text{corner}} = \tfrac{3}{2}mR^2$.
    Energy after turning by $\theta$: $\tfrac{3}{4}m(v^2 - v_0^2) = mgR(1-\cos\theta) \Rightarrow v^2 = v_0^2 + \tfrac{4}{3}gR(1-\cos\theta)$.
    Loses contact when normal force is zero: $mg\cos\theta = mv^2/R$.
    $gR\cos\theta = \tfrac{gR}{3} + \tfrac{4}{3}gR - \tfrac{4}{3}gR\cos\theta \Rightarrow \cos\theta = 5/7$.
    $v^2 = gR\cos\theta = \tfrac{5gR}{7}$ → (B).

  3. JEE Advanced 2026, Paper 1 · Question 32
    List-I shows four planar structures made of uniform solid rods each of mass $m$ and length $l$. In the List-II the possible moment of inertia of these structures about an axis $OCO'$, which lies in the plane of the structures, are given.
    Choose the option that describes the correct match between the entries in List-I to those in List-II.

    List-IList-II
    (P) Structure (P) in the figure(1) $\frac{5}{4}ml^2$
    (Q) Structure (Q) in the figure(2) $\frac{1}{6}ml^2$
    (R) Structure (R) in the figure(3) $\frac{1}{12}ml^2$
    (S) Structure (S) in the figure(4) $\frac{2}{3}ml^2$
    (5) $\frac{1}{3}\,ml^2$

    1. P→ 5, Q→ 1, R→ 4, S→ 2
    2. P→ 1, Q→ 3, R→ 4, S→ 2
    3. P→ 5, Q→ 3, R→ 2, S→ 1
    4. P→ 5, Q→ 4, R→ 2, S→ 1
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    A rod with one end on the axis at angle $\alpha$ to it: $I = \tfrac13ml^2\sin^2\alpha$.
    (P) two rods at $45^\circ$: $2\cdot\tfrac16ml^2 = \tfrac13ml^2$ → (5).
    (Q) $CA$, $CB$ at $60^\circ$: $2\cdot\tfrac14ml^2$; $AB$ parallel to the axis at distance $\tfrac{\sqrt3}{2}l$: $\tfrac34ml^2$; total $\tfrac54ml^2$ → (1).
    (R) square about a diagonal: four rods at $45^\circ$, each with one end on the axis: $4\cdot\tfrac16ml^2 = \tfrac23ml^2$ → (4).
    (S) two rods at $30^\circ$: $2\cdot\tfrac{1}{12}ml^2 = \tfrac16ml^2$ → (2).
    Match → (A).

  4. JEE Advanced 2026, Paper 2 · Question 35
    A uniform circular disk of radius $0.2$ m and mass $1$ kg is pivoted at its top point $C$ such that it can rotate freely around $C$ in the $XY$ plane, as shown in the figure. Initially, when the disk is at rest, a particle of mass $20$ g, travelling along negative $x$ direction in the $XY$ plane with speed $100\ \mathrm{ms^{-1}}$, hits the circumference of the disk at a point $P$. After collision the particle moves along negative $y$ direction at a speed of $90\ \mathrm{ms^{-1}}$.
    [Given: the acceleration due to gravity (g) $= -\,10\,\hat{\jmath}\ \mathrm{ms^{-2}}$]

    After the collision the disk starts to rotate around point $C$ in the $XY$ plane. The maximum change in the height (in m) of its center $O$ is:

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 0.14 to 0.16

    Take $O$ as origin: $C = (0, 0.2)$, $P = (0.2\sin45^\circ, -0.2\cos45^\circ) = (0.1414, -0.1414)$; $\vec r_{CP} = (0.1414, -0.3414)$.
    Angular momentum about $C$ is conserved (pivot impulse passes through $C$).
    Particle before: $L_z = 0.02[-(-0.3414)(-100)] = -0.683$; after: $L_z = 0.02[0.1414(-90)] = -0.255$ (SI units).
    Disk: $I_C\omega = 0.428$, $I_C = \tfrac32MR^2 = 0.06 \Rightarrow \omega = 7.14$ rad/s, $K = \tfrac12(0.06)(7.14)^2 = 1.53$ J.
    $Mg\,\Delta h = K \Rightarrow \Delta h = 1.53/10 \approx 0.15$ m.

  5. JEE Advanced 2026, Paper 2 · Question 36
    A uniform circular disk of radius $0.2$ m and mass $1$ kg is pivoted at its top point $C$ such that it can rotate freely around $C$ in the $XY$ plane, as shown in the figure. Initially, when the disk is at rest, a particle of mass $20$ g, travelling along negative $x$ direction in the $XY$ plane with speed $100\ \mathrm{ms^{-1}}$, hits the circumference of the disk at a point $P$. After collision the particle moves along negative $y$ direction at a speed of $90\ \mathrm{ms^{-1}}$.
    [Given: the acceleration due to gravity (g) $= -\,10\,\hat{\jmath}\ \mathrm{ms^{-2}}$]

    Amount of energy loss (in J) in the collision is:

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 17 to 18

    Particle KE before: $\tfrac12(0.02)(100)^2 = 100$ J; after: $\tfrac12(0.02)(90)^2 = 81$ J.
    Disk KE just after collision (from Q.17): $\approx 1.53$ J.

  6. JEE Advanced 2024, Paper 1 · Question 26
    A disc of mass $M$ and radius $R$ is free to rotate about its vertical axis as shown in the figure. A battery operated motor of negligible mass is fixed to this disc at a point on its circumference. Another disc of the same mass $M$ and radius $R/2$ is fixed to the motor's thin shaft. Initially, both the discs are at rest. The motor is switched on so that the smaller disc rotates at a uniform angular speed $\omega$. If the angular speed at which the large disc rotates is $\omega/n$, then the value of $n$ is _____.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 12

    No external torque about the large disc's axis, so the total angular momentum about it stays zero.
    Small disc spin: $\dfrac12 M\left(\dfrac R2\right)^2\omega = \dfrac{MR^2\omega}{8}$.
    Large disc rotating at $\Omega$ (opposite sense) carries the small disc's centre round a circle of radius $R$: $\left(\dfrac12MR^2 + MR^2\right)\Omega = \dfrac32MR^2\Omega$.
    $\dfrac32MR^2\Omega = \dfrac{MR^2\omega}{8} \Rightarrow \Omega = \dfrac{\omega}{12}$, so $n = 12$.

  7. JEE Advanced 2024, Paper 1 · Question 30
    A thin uniform rod of length $L$ and certain mass is kept on a frictionless horizontal table with a massless string of length $L$ fixed to one end (top view is shown in the figure). The other end of the string is pivoted to a point O. If a horizontal impulse $P$ is imparted to the rod at a distance $x = L/n$ from the mid-point of the rod (see figure), then the rod and string revolve together around the point O, with the rod remaining aligned with the string. In such a case, the value of $n$ is _____.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 18

    The rod's centre is at $\dfrac{3L}{2}$ from O. To move with the string, the rod must rotate about O with some $\omega$: $v_{cm} = \dfrac{3L}{2}\omega$.
    The string's impulse acts along the rod through the centre, so it has no effect on rotation about the centre and none perpendicular to the rod.
    Linear: $P = m\cdot\dfrac{3L}{2}\omega$. Angular about the centre: $Px = \dfrac{mL^2}{12}\omega$.
    Dividing: $x = \dfrac{L^2/12}{3L/2} = \dfrac{L}{18}$, so $n = 18$.

  8. JEE Advanced 2024, Paper 2 · Question 33
    Two particles, 1 and 2, each of mass $m$, are connected by a massless spring, and are on a horizontal frictionless plane, as shown in the figure. Initially, the two particles, with their center of mass at $x_0$, are oscillating with amplitude $a$ and angular frequency $\omega$. Thus, their positions at time $t$ are given by $x_1(t)=(x_0+d)+a\sin\omega t$ and $x_2(t)=(x_0-d)-a\sin\omega t$, respectively, where $d>2a$. Particle 3 of mass $m$ moves towards this system with speed $u_0=a\omega/2$, and undergoes instantaneous elastic collision with particle 2, at time $t_0$. Finally, particles 1 and 2 acquire a center of mass speed $v_{\text{cm}}$ and oscillate with amplitude $b$ and the same angular frequency $\omega$.

    If the collision occurs at time $t_0=0$, the value of $v_{\text{cm}}/(a\omega)$ will be __________.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 0.74 to 0.76

    At $t=0$: $\dot x_1=a\omega$, $\dot x_2=-a\omega$ (particle 2 moving towards particle 3).
    Elastic collision of equal masses exchanges velocities: particle 2 gets $+\dfrac{a\omega}{2}$.
    $v_{\text{cm}}=\dfrac{a\omega+a\omega/2}{2}=\dfrac34a\omega$.
    $v_{\text{cm}}/(a\omega)=0.75$.

  9. JEE Advanced 2023, Paper 1 · Question 21
    A bar of mass $M = 1.00$ kg and length $L = 0.20$ m is lying on a horizontal frictionless surface. One end of the bar is pivoted at a point about which it is free to rotate. A small mass $m = 0.10$ kg is moving on the same horizontal surface with $5.00\ \text{m s}^{-1}$ speed on a path perpendicular to the bar. It hits the bar at a distance $L/2$ from the pivoted end and returns back on the same path with speed $v$. After this elastic collision, the bar rotates with an angular velocity $\omega$. Which of the following statement is correct?

    1. $\omega = 6.98\ \text{rad s}^{-1}$ and $v = 4.30\ \text{m s}^{-1}$
    2. $\omega = 3.75\ \text{rad s}^{-1}$ and $v = 4.30\ \text{m s}^{-1}$
    3. $\omega = 3.75\ \text{rad s}^{-1}$ and $v = 10.0\ \text{m s}^{-1}$
    4. $\omega = 6.80\ \text{rad s}^{-1}$ and $v = 4.10\ \text{m s}^{-1}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    $I = \dfrac{ML^2}{3} = \dfrac{0.04}{3}\ \text{kg m}^2$; the impact point is at $\dfrac L2 = 0.1$ m.
    Angular momentum about the pivot: $0.1\times5\times0.1 = I\omega - 0.1\,v\times0.1 \Rightarrow 0.05 = \dfrac{0.04}{3}\omega - 0.01v$.
    Elastic (relative speed at the contact point reversed): $\omega\dfrac L2 + v = 5 \Rightarrow 0.1\omega + v = 5$.
    Solving: $\omega = \dfrac{10}{1.4333} = 6.98\ \text{rad s}^{-1}$, $v = 4.30\ \text{m s}^{-1}$. Answer (A).

  10. JEE Advanced 2023, Paper 2 · Question 23
    An annular disk of mass $M$, inner radius $a$ and outer radius $b$ is placed on a horizontal surface with coefficient of friction $\mu$, as shown in the figure. At some time, an impulse $\mathcal{J}_0\hat x$ is applied at a height $h$ above the center of the disk. If $h = h_m$ then the disk rolls without slipping along the $x$-axis. Which of the following statement(s) is(are) correct?

    1. For $\mu \ne 0$ and $a \to 0$, $h_m = b/2$.
    2. For $\mu \ne 0$ and $a \to b$, $h_m = b$.
    3. For $h = h_m$, the initial angular velocity does not depend on the inner radius $a$.
    4. For $\mu = 0$ and $h = 0$, the wheel always slides without rolling.
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a), (b), (c), (d)

    With no friction impulse needed for pure rolling: $\mathcal J_0 = Mv$ and $\mathcal J_0 h = I\omega$ with $I = \dfrac{M(a^2 + b^2)}{2}$.
    Rolling: $v = \omega b \Rightarrow h_m = \dfrac{I}{Mb} = \dfrac{a^2 + b^2}{2b}$.
    (A) $a \to 0$: $h_m = \dfrac b2$ ✓. (B) $a \to b$: $h_m = b$ ✓.
    (C) $\omega = \dfrac vb = \dfrac{\mathcal J_0}{Mb}$, independent of $a$ ✓.
    (D) $\mu = 0$, $h = 0$: no torque about the centre at any time, so $\omega$ stays 0 and the disk only slides ✓.
    Answer (A), (B), (C), (D).

  11. JEE Advanced 2023, Paper 2 · Question 25
    A thin circular coin of mass 5 gm and radius $4/3$ cm is initially in a horizontal $xy$-plane. The coin is tossed vertically up ($+z$ direction) by applying an impulse of $\sqrt{\dfrac{\pi}{2}}\times10^{-2}$ N-s at a distance $2/3$ cm from its center. The coin spins about its diameter and moves along the $+z$ direction. By the time the coin reaches back to its initial position, it completes $n$ rotations. The value of $n$ is ________.
    [Given: The acceleration due to gravity $g = 10$ m s$^{-2}$]

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 30

    $m = 5\times10^{-3}$ kg, $R = \dfrac43\times10^{-2}$ m, $J = \sqrt{\dfrac\pi2}\times10^{-2}$ N s, lever arm $\dfrac23\times10^{-2}$ m.
    $v = \dfrac Jm = 2\sqrt{\dfrac\pi2}$ m/s; time of flight $T = \dfrac{2v}{g} = 0.4\sqrt{\dfrac\pi2}$ s.
    $I = \dfrac{mR^2}{4} = \dfrac{20}{9}\times10^{-7}$ kg m$^2$; $J\cdot\dfrac23\times10^{-2} = I\omega \Rightarrow \omega = 300\sqrt{\dfrac\pi2}$ rad/s.
    Angle turned $= \omega T = 300\times0.4\times\dfrac\pi2 = 60\pi$.
    $n = \dfrac{60\pi}{2\pi} = 30$.

  12. JEE Advanced 2022, Paper 1 · Question 23
    At time $t=0$, a disk of radius $1$ m starts to roll without slipping on a horizontal plane with an angular acceleration of $\alpha=\dfrac23\ \mathrm{rad\,s^{-2}}$. A small stone is stuck to the disk. At $t=0$, it is at the contact point of the disk and the plane. Later, at time $t=\sqrt\pi$ s, the stone detaches itself and flies off tangentially from the disk. The maximum height (in m) reached by the stone measured from the plane is $\dfrac12+\dfrac{x}{10}$. The value of $x$ is ______. [Take $g=10\ \mathrm{m\,s^{-2}}$.]

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 0.48 to 0.56

    At $t=\sqrt\pi$: $\omega=\alpha t=\dfrac{2\sqrt\pi}{3}$ rad/s and the disk has turned $\theta=\dfrac12\alpha t^2=\dfrac\pi3$.
    The stone is then at angle $\dfrac\pi3$ from the lowest point: height $R-R\cos\dfrac\pi3=\dfrac12$ m above the plane.
    Its velocity is $\omega\times$(distance from contact point), perpendicular to the line joining it to the contact point; this gives components $\omega R(1-\cos\theta)$ horizontally and $\omega R\sin\theta$ vertically.
    $v_y=\dfrac{2\sqrt\pi}{3}\cdot\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}=\sqrt{\dfrac\pi3}$, so the extra rise is $\dfrac{v_y^2}{2g}=\dfrac{\pi/3}{20}=\dfrac{\pi}{60}$.
    $H=\dfrac12+\dfrac{\pi}{60}=\dfrac12+\dfrac{x}{10}\Rightarrow x=\dfrac\pi6\approx0.52$.

  13. JEE Advanced 2022, Paper 1 · Question 24
    A solid sphere of mass $1$ kg and radius $1$ m rolls without slipping on a fixed inclined plane with an angle of inclination $\theta=30^\circ$ from the horizontal. Two forces of magnitude $1$ N each, parallel to the incline, act on the sphere, both at distance $r=0.5$ m from the center of the sphere, as shown in the figure. The acceleration of the sphere down the plane is ______ $\mathrm{m\,s^{-2}}$. (Take $g=10\ \mathrm{m\,s^{-2}}$.)

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 2.8 to 2.92

    The two forces are equal and opposite, so they add no net force; they form a couple of moment $F\times(2r)=1\times1=1$ N m.
    From the figure, the down-slope force acts below the centre and the up-slope force above it, so the couple turns the sphere opposite to the rolling-down sense.
    Let $f$ = friction up the slope, $I=\dfrac25mR^2=0.4$ kg m$^2$, $a=\alpha R$.
    Translation: $ma=mg\sin\theta-f=5-f$. Rotation (rolling-down sense): $I\alpha=fR-1\Rightarrow0.4a=f-1$.
    Adding: $1.4a=4\Rightarrow a=\dfrac{20}{7}\approx2.86\ \mathrm{m\,s^{-2}}$.

  14. JEE Advanced 2022, Paper 2 · Question 19
    A particle of mass $1$ kg is subjected to a force which depends on the position as $\vec F = -k(x\hat\imath + y\hat\jmath)$ kg m s$^{-2}$ with $k = 1$ kg s$^{-2}$. At time $t = 0$, the particle’s position $\vec r = \left(\dfrac{1}{\sqrt2}\hat\imath + \sqrt2\hat\jmath\right)$ m and its velocity $\vec v = \left(-\sqrt2\hat\imath + \sqrt2\hat\jmath + \dfrac{2}{\pi}\hat k\right)$ m s$^{-1}$. Let $v_x$ and $v_y$ denote the $x$ and the $y$ components of the particle’s velocity, respectively. Ignore gravity. When $z = 0.5$ m, the value of $(x v_y - y v_x)$ is ________ m$^2$s$^{-1}$.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 3

    The force has no $z$ component and in the $xy$ plane it points towards the $z$-axis, so its torque about the $z$-axis is zero.

  15. JEE Advanced 2022, Paper 2 · Question 33
    A flat surface of a thin uniform disk $A$ of radius $R$ is glued to a horizontal table. Another thin uniform disk $B$ of mass $M$ and with the same radius $R$ rolls without slipping on the circumference of $A$, as shown in the figure. A flat surface of $B$ also lies on the plane of the table. The center of mass of $B$ has fixed angular speed $\omega$ about the vertical axis passing through the center of $A$. The angular momentum of $B$ is $nM\omega R^2$ with respect to the center of $A$. Which of the following is the value of $n$?

    2. $5$
    3. $\dfrac72$
    4. $\dfrac92$

  16. JEE Advanced 2021, Paper 1 · Question 30
    A horizontal force $F$ is applied at the center of mass of a cylindrical object of mass $m$ and radius $R$, perpendicular to its axis as shown in the figure. The coefficient of friction between the object and the ground is $\mu$. The center of mass of the object has an acceleration $a$. The acceleration due to gravity is $g$. Given that the object rolls without slipping, which of the following statement(s) is(are) correct?

    1. For the same $F$, the value of $a$ does not depend on whether the cylinder is solid or hollow
    2. For a solid cylinder, the maximum possible value of $a$ is $2\mu g$
    3. The magnitude of the frictional force on the object due to the ground is always $\mu mg$
    4. For a thin-walled hollow cylinder, $a = \dfrac{F}{2m}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (b), (d)

    Translation: $F - f = ma$; rotation: $fR = I\alpha = I\dfrac{a}{R}$, so $f = \dfrac{Ia}{R^2}$ and $a = \dfrac{F}{m + I/R^2}$.
    (A) $a$ depends on $I$, so it differs for solid and hollow cylinders: false.
    (B) Solid: $f = \dfrac{ma}{2} \le \mu mg$, so $a_{\max} = 2\mu g$: true.
    (C) Friction is static and only as large as needed ($f = Ia/R^2$): false.
    (D) Thin-walled: $I = mR^2$, $a = \dfrac{F}{2m}$: true.
    Answer (B), (D).

The particle is accelerated at time $t = 0$ with a constant acceleration $a = 10$ m/s$^2$ along the positive $x$-direction. Its angular momentum and torque with respect to the origin, in SI units, are represented by $\vec L$ and $\vec\tau$, respectively. $\hat\imath$, $\hat\jmath$ and $\hat k$ are unit vectors along the positive $x$, $y$ and $z$-directions, respectively. If $\hat k = \hat\imath \times \hat\jmath$ then which of the following statement(s) is(are) correct?

    Show answer and solution

  18. JEE Advanced 2021, Paper 1 · Question 37
    A thin rod of mass $M$ and length $a$ is free to rotate in horizontal plane about a fixed vertical axis passing through point O. An outside stationary observer finds the rod rotating with an angular velocity $\Omega$ and the disc rotating about its vertical axis with angular velocity $4\Omega$.   19. JEE Advanced 2021, Paper 2 · Question 20
    One end of a horizontal uniform beam of weight $W$ and length $L$ is hinged on a vertical wall at point $O$ and its other end is supported by a light inextensible rope. The other end of the rope is fixed at point $Q$, at a height $L$ above the hinge at point $O$. A block of weight $\alpha W$ is attached at the point $P$ of the beam, as shown in the figure (not to scale). The rope can sustain a maximum tension of $(2\sqrt2)W$. Which of the following statement(s) is(are) correct?

    1. The vertical component of reaction force at $O$ does not depend on $\alpha$
    2. The horizontal component of reaction force at $O$ is equal to $W$ for $\alpha = 0.5$
    3. The tension in the rope is $2W$ for $\alpha = 0.5$
    4. The rope breaks if $\alpha > 1.5$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a), (b), (d)

    $OQ = OP = L$, so the rope makes $45^\circ$ with the beam.
    Torque about $O$: $T\sin45^\circ \cdot L = W\dfrac{L}{2} + \alpha WL \Rightarrow T = \sqrt2\,W\left(\dfrac12 + \alpha\right)$.
    Horizontal reaction $H = T\cos45^\circ = W\left(\dfrac12 + \alpha\right)$; vertical reaction $V = W + \alpha W - T\sin45^\circ = \dfrac{W}{2}$.
    (A) $V = W/2$ is independent of $\alpha$ → true.
    (B) $\alpha = 0.5$: $H = W$ → true.
    (C) $\alpha = 0.5$: $T = \sqrt2\,W \ne 2W$ → false.
    (D) $T > 2\sqrt2\,W \iff \dfrac12 + \alpha > 2 \iff \alpha > 1.5$ → true.
    Answer (A), (B), (D).

  20. JEE Advanced 2021, Paper 2 · Question 28
    A pendulum consists of a bob of mass $m = 0.1$ kg and a massless inextensible string of length $L = 1.0$ m. It is suspended from a fixed point at height $H = 0.9$ m above a frictionless horizontal floor. Initially, the bob of the pendulum is lying on the floor at rest vertically below the point of suspension. A horizontal impulse $P = 0.2$ kg-m/s is imparted to the bob at some instant. After the bob slides for some distance, the string becomes taut and the bob lifts off the floor. The magnitude of the angular momentum of the pendulum about the point of suspension just before the bob lifts off is $J$ kg-m$^2$/s. The kinetic energy of the pendulum just after the lift-off is $K$ Joules.

    The value of $J$ is ___ .

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 0.16 to 0.2

    The bob slides on the frictionless floor with $v = \dfrac{P}{m} = 2$ m/s.
    Its line of motion is at perpendicular distance $H = 0.9$ m from the point of suspension.
    $J = mvH = 0.1 \times 2 \times 0.9 = 0.18$ kg m$^2$/s.

  21. JEE Advanced 2021, Paper 2 · Question 29
    A pendulum consists of a bob of mass $m = 0.1$ kg and a massless inextensible string of length $L = 1.0$ m. It is suspended from a fixed point at height $H = 0.9$ m above a frictionless horizontal floor. Initially, the bob of the pendulum is lying on the floor at rest vertically below the point of suspension. A horizontal impulse $P = 0.2$ kg-m/s is imparted to the bob at some instant. After the bob slides for some distance, the string becomes taut and the bob lifts off the floor. The magnitude of the angular momentum of the pendulum about the point of suspension just before the bob lifts off is $J$ kg-m$^2$/s. The kinetic energy of the pendulum just after the lift-off is $K$ Joules.

    The value of $K$ is ___.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 0.14 to 0.18

    When the string becomes taut, the impulsive tension (and floor reaction) destroy the velocity component along the string; angular momentum about the suspension point is conserved.
    The string then makes angle $\phi$ with the vertical, $\cos\phi = \dfrac{H}{L} = 0.9$; the surviving perpendicular component is $v\cos\phi = 2 \times 0.9 = 1.8$ m/s (equivalently $J/(mL) = 0.18/0.1$).
    $K = \dfrac12 \times 0.1 \times 1.8^2 = 0.162$ J $\approx 0.16$.
