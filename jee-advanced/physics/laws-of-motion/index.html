  1. JEE Advanced 2025, Paper 1 · Question 24
    A person sitting inside an elevator performs a weighing experiment with an object of mass $50$ kg. Suppose that the variation of the height $y$ (in m) of the elevator, from the ground, with time $t$ (in s) is given by $y = 8\left[1 + \sin\left(\dfrac{2\pi t}{T}\right)\right]$, where $T = 40\pi$ s. Taking acceleration due to gravity, $g = 10$ m/s$^2$, the maximum variation of the object’s weight (in N) as observed in the experiment is ______

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 2

    $\dfrac{2\pi}{T} = \dfrac{2\pi}{40\pi} = \dfrac{1}{20}$ s$^{-1}$, so $a = \ddot y = -8\left(\dfrac{1}{20}\right)^2\sin\dfrac{2\pi t}{T}$, with $|a|_{\max} = \dfrac{8}{400} = 0.02$ m/s$^2$.
    Observed weight $= m(g + a)$ ranges from $m(g - 0.02)$ to $m(g + 0.02)$.
    Maximum variation $= 50 \times 2 \times 0.02 = 2$ N.

  2. JEE Advanced 2025, Paper 2 · Question 31
    A projectile of mass 200 g is launched in a viscous medium at an angle $60^\circ$ with the horizontal, with an initial velocity of 270 m/s. It experiences a viscous drag force $\vec{F}=-c\vec{v}$ where the drag coefficient $c=0.1$ kg/s and $\vec{v}$ is the instantaneous velocity of the projectile. The projectile hits a vertical wall after 2 s. Taking $e=2.7$, the horizontal distance of the wall from the point of projection (in m) is ________.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 167 to 171

    Horizontal motion: $m\dfrac{dv_x}{dt}=-cv_x\Rightarrow v_x=u_xe^{-ct/m}$, with $\dfrac cm=\dfrac{0.1}{0.2}=0.5$ s$^{-1}$ and $u_x=270\cos60^\circ=135$ m/s.
    $x=\dfrac{u_xm}{c}\left(1-e^{-ct/m}\right)=135\times2\times\left(1-e^{-1}\right)$ at $t=2$ s.
    $x=270\left(1-\dfrac{1}{2.7}\right)=270\times\dfrac{1.7}{2.7}=170$ m.

  3. JEE Advanced 2023, Paper 2 · Question 20
    A particle of mass $m$ is moving in the $xy$-plane such that its velocity at a point $(x, y)$ is given as $\vec{\mathrm v} = \alpha(y\hat x + 2x\hat y)$, where $\alpha$ is a non-zero constant. What is the force $\vec F$ acting on the particle?

    1. $\vec F = 2m\alpha^2(x\hat x + y\hat y)$
    2. $\vec F = m\alpha^2(y\hat x + 2x\hat y)$
    3. $\vec F = 2m\alpha^2(y\hat x + x\hat y)$
    4. $\vec F = m\alpha^2(x\hat x + 2y\hat y)$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a)

    $v_x = \alpha y$, $v_y = 2\alpha x$.
    $a_x = \dfrac{dv_x}{dt} = \alpha\dfrac{dy}{dt} = \alpha(2\alpha x) = 2\alpha^2 x$.
    $a_y = \dfrac{dv_y}{dt} = 2\alpha\dfrac{dx}{dt} = 2\alpha(\alpha y) = 2\alpha^2 y$.
    $\vec F = m\vec a = 2m\alpha^2(x\hat x + y\hat y)$ → (A).

  4. JEE Advanced 2021, Paper 1 · Question 24
    A projectile is thrown from a point O on the ground at an angle $45^\circ$ from the vertical and with a speed $5\sqrt2$ m/s. The projectile at the highest point of its trajectory splits into two equal parts. One part falls vertically down to the ground, 0.5 s after the splitting. The other part, $t$ seconds after the splitting, falls to the ground at a distance $x$ meters from the point O. The acceleration due to gravity $g = 10$ m/s$^2$.

    The value of $t$ is ___ .

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 0.45 to 0.55

    Velocity components: $u_x = u_y = 5$ m/s. Highest point after $0.5$ s, at height $H = \dfrac{5^2}{2 \times 10} = 1.25$ m and $2.5$ m from O.
    A part falling from rest through $1.25$ m takes $\sqrt{2H/g} = 0.5$ s, as stated, so that part has zero velocity just after splitting.
    Momentum conservation: $m(5) = \dfrac m2(0) + \dfrac m2 v \Rightarrow v = 10$ m/s, horizontal.
    The other part also starts with zero vertical velocity, so it too lands after $t = 0.5$ s.

  5. JEE Advanced 2021, Paper 1 · Question 25
    A projectile is thrown from a point O on the ground at an angle $45^\circ$ from the vertical and with a speed $5\sqrt2$ m/s. The projectile at the highest point of its trajectory splits into two equal parts. One part falls vertically down to the ground, 0.5 s after the splitting. The other part, $t$ seconds after the splitting, falls to the ground at a distance $x$ meters from the point O. The acceleration due to gravity $g = 10$ m/s$^2$.

    The value of $x$ is ___ .

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 7.3 to 7.7

    At the top: $u_x = 5$ m/s, horizontal distance from O $= 5 \times 0.5 = 2.5$ m, height $1.25$ m.
    The part that falls vertically has zero velocity after splitting, so the other part moves off horizontally at $v = 2 \times 5 = 10$ m/s (momentum conservation).
    It takes $0.5$ s to fall, covering $10 \times 0.5 = 5$ m more.
    $x = 2.5 + 5 = 7.5$ m.
