Sample questions

A few questions from the Class 1 English bank. Every test picks a fresh mix.

  1. Poem: Two Little Hands
    Two little hands go clap, clap, ______.

    1. snap
    2. tap
    3. flap
    4. clap
    Show answer

    Answer: clap

    In the poem the hands go clap, clap, clap.

  2. Greetings
    In the morning we say, 'Good ______!'

    1. night
    2. morning
    3. afternoon
    4. evening
    Show answer

    Answer: morning

    We greet people with 'Good morning' in the morning.

  3. Alphabet
    Which letter comes after A?

    1. C
    2. D
    3. Z
    4. B
    Show answer

    Answer: B

    The alphabet begins A, B, C.

  4. Opposites
    The opposite of big is ______.

    1. long
    2. fat
    3. tall
    4. small
    Show answer

    Answer: small

    Big and small are opposites.

  5. Rhyming Words
    Which word rhymes with cat: cow, bat, cup?

    1. bat
    2. cup
    3. dog
    4. cow
    Show answer

    Answer: bat

    Cat and bat end with the same sound -at.
