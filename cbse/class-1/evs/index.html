Sample questions

A few questions from the Class 1 EVS bank. Every test picks a fresh mix.

  1. My Body and Sense Organs
    We see with our ______.

    1. eyes
    2. tongue
    3. nose
    4. ears
    Show answer

    Answer: eyes

    Eyes are the sense organs that help us see.

  2. My Family
    My father's mother is my ______.

    1. aunt
    2. cousin
    3. grandmother
    4. sister
    Show answer

    Answer: grandmother

    Father's mother is our grandmother. We call her Dadi.

  3. Food We Eat
    We get milk from a ______.

    1. dog
    2. hen
    3. cow
    4. cat
    Show answer

    Answer: cow

    Cows, buffaloes and goats give us milk.

  4. Plants Around Us
    The part of a plant under the soil is the ______.

    1. leaf
    2. root
    3. fruit
    4. flower
    Show answer

    Answer: root

    Roots grow under the soil. They hold the plant.

  5. Animals Around Us
    A dog lives in a ______.

    1. kennel
    2. hive
    3. nest
    4. stable
    Show answer

    Answer: kennel

    A kennel is a home for a dog.
