Sample questions

A few questions from the Class 4 EVS bank. Every test picks a fresh mix.

  1. Going to School
    In Kerala, some children cross the backwaters to school in a small boat called a ______.

    1. shikara
    2. vallam
    3. raft
    4. jahaz
    Show answer

    Answer: vallam

    A vallam is a small wooden boat used in the backwaters of Kerala.

  2. Ear to Ear
    Animals whose ears can be seen from outside have ______ or fur on their bodies.

    1. hair
    2. scales
    3. shells
    4. feathers
    Show answer

    Answer: hair

    Animals with outer ears, like cows and dogs, have hair or fur on their bodies.

  3. A Day with Nandu
    An elephant herd is led by the oldest ______ elephant.

    1. female
    2. male
    3. young
    4. baby
    Show answer

    Answer: female

    The oldest female elephant leads the herd.

  4. The Story of Amrita
    Amrita Devi belonged to the ______ community.

    1. Gond
    2. Bhil
    3. Santhal
    4. Bishnoi
    Show answer

    Answer: Bishnoi

    The Bishnoi community is known for protecting trees and animals.

  5. Anita and the Honeybees
    Keeping bees to get honey is called ______.

    1. fishing
    2. beekeeping
    3. sericulture
    4. poultry
    Show answer

    Answer: beekeeping

    Anita learnt beekeeping and sold honey to earn money.
