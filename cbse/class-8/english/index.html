Sample questions

A few questions from the Class 8 English bank. Every test picks a fresh mix.

  1. Verbs and Tenses
    Write the past participle of 'write'.

    1. writen
    2. writed
    3. wrote
    4. written
    Show answer

    Answer: written

    The three forms are write, wrote, written.

  2. Subject–Verb Agreement
    One of my friends ______ from Kerala.

    1. were
    2. is
    3. are
    4. come
    Show answer

    Answer: is

    The subject is 'one', which is singular.

  3. Modals
    We ______ respect our elders. (moral duty)

    1. should
    2. may
    3. might
    4. can
    Show answer

    Answer: should

    'Should' or 'ought to' expresses moral duty.

  4. Active and Passive Voice
    In 'The match was won by India', who performed the action?

    1. match
    2. India
    3. was
    4. won
    Show answer

    Answer: India

    In the passive voice, the doer appears after 'by'.

  5. Reported Speech
    He said, "I am tired."
    He said that he ______ tired.

    1. is
    2. was
    3. am
    4. were
    Show answer

    Answer: was

    When the reporting verb is in the past, present tense changes to past.
