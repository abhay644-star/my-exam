Sample questions

A few questions from the Class 8 Science bank. Every test picks a fresh mix.

  1. The Invisible Living World
    Microorganisms that cause diseases are called ______.

    1. vaccines
    2. pathogens
    3. antibiotics
    4. antibodies
    Show answer

    Answer: pathogens

    Disease-causing microbes such as some bacteria and viruses are called pathogens.

  2. Health and Disease
    Malaria is caused by a microorganism called ______.

    1. Lactobacillus
    2. Plasmodium
    3. Rhizobium
    4. Amoeba
    Show answer

    Answer: Plasmodium

    Malaria is caused by the protozoan Plasmodium.

  3. Magnetic and Heating Effects
    A coil of insulated wire wound on an iron core that acts as a magnet only while current flows is called an ______.

    1. electromagnet
    2. compass
    3. fuse
    4. permanent magnet
    Show answer

    Answer: electromagnet

    An electromagnet is a temporary magnet produced by electric current.

  4. Exploring Forces
    A push or a pull on an object is called a ______.

    1. energy
    2. pressure
    3. work
    4. force
    Show answer

    Answer: force

    Any push or pull acting on an object is a force.

  5. Pressure, Winds and Cyclones
    The SI unit of pressure is the ______.

    1. watt
    2. newton
    3. joule
    4. pascal
    Show answer

    Answer: pascal

    Pressure is force per unit area, measured in pascals (1 Pa = 1 N/m²).
