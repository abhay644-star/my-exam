Sample questions

A few questions from the Class 8 Social Science bank. Every test picks a fresh mix.

  1. Geography: Natural Resources and Their Use
    Resources that are replenished naturally, such as sunlight and wind, are called ______ resources.

    1. Fossil
    2. Mineral
    3. Renewable
    4. Non-renewable
    Show answer

    Answer: Renewable

    Renewable resources are renewed by natural processes.

  2. Geography: Agriculture and Industries
    Crops sown with the onset of the monsoon and harvested in autumn, such as rice and cotton, are called ______ crops.

    1. Rabi
    2. Zaid
    3. Plantation
    4. Kharif
    Show answer

    Answer: Kharif

    Kharif crops grow during the rainy season.

  3. History: Reshaping India's Political Map
    Who founded the Mughal Empire in India after winning the First Battle of Panipat?

    1. Sher Shah Suri
    2. Humayun
    3. Babur
    4. Akbar
    Show answer

    Answer: Babur

    Babur defeated Ibrahim Lodi at Panipat in 1526 and founded Mughal rule.

  4. History: The Rise of the Marathas
    Shivaji was crowned Chhatrapati in 1674 at which fort?

    1. Shivneri
    2. Raigad
    3. Sinhagad
    4. Pratapgad
    Show answer

    Answer: Raigad

    Shivaji's coronation took place at Raigad fort in 1674.

  5. History: The Colonial Era in India
    In which year was the Battle of Plassey fought?

    1. 1707
    2. 1757
    3. 1857
    4. 1764
    Show answer

    Answer: 1757

    In 1757 Robert Clive's forces defeated Siraj-ud-daulah at Plassey.
