Sample questions

A few questions from the Class 2 English bank. Every test picks a fresh mix.

  1. Poem: Little Drops of Water
    Little drops of ______, little grains of sand.

    1. rain
    2. milk
    3. oil
    4. water
    Show answer

    Answer: water

    The poem begins: Little drops of water, little grains of sand.

  2. Story: The Monkey and the Crocodile
    In 'The Smart Monkey', the monkey's friend is a ______.

    1. lion
    2. crocodile
    3. rabbit
    4. fox
    Show answer

    Answer: crocodile

    The monkey makes friends with a crocodile who lives in the river.

  3. Opposites
    The opposite of fast is ______.

    1. fat
    2. late
    3. quick
    4. slow
    Show answer

    Answer: slow

    Fast and slow are opposites.

  4. Rhyming Words
    Which word rhymes with star: car, sun, sky?

    1. moon
    2. sun
    3. sky
    4. car
    Show answer

    Answer: car

    Star and car end with the same sound -ar.

  5. One and Many
    One book, two ______.

    1. bookies
    2. book
    3. bookes
    4. books
    Show answer

    Answer: books

    We add s to book to make books.
