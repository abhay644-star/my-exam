Sample questions

A few questions from the Class 2 EVS bank. Every test picks a fresh mix.

  1. Water
    Rain falls from the ______.

    1. mountains
    2. sun
    3. clouds
    4. trees
    Show answer

    Answer: clouds

    Rain comes from the clouds in the sky.

  2. Air
    Moving air is called ______.

    1. water
    2. smoke
    3. wind
    4. cloud
    Show answer

    Answer: wind

    When air moves, we call it wind.

  3. Clothes We Wear
    We wear ______ clothes in winter.

    1. cotton
    2. silk
    3. woollen
    4. thin
    Show answer

    Answer: woollen

    Woollen clothes keep us warm in winter.

  4. Our Houses
    A small house made of mud and straw is a ______.

    1. tent
    2. hut
    3. flat
    4. palace
    Show answer

    Answer: hut

    A hut is made of mud, straw and bamboo.

  5. Transport
    An ______ flies in the sky.

    1. ship
    2. bus
    3. aeroplane
    4. train
    Show answer

    Answer: aeroplane

    Aeroplanes and helicopters fly in the sky.
