Sample questions

A few questions from the Class 5 EVS bank. Every test picks a fresh mix.

  1. Super Senses
    An ant finds its way by following the ______ left by other ants.

    1. light
    2. colour
    3. smell
    4. sound
    Show answer

    Answer: smell

    Ants leave a smell on the path; other ants follow it.

  2. A Snake Charmer's Story
    Snake charmers play a wind instrument called the ______.

    1. been
    2. tabla
    3. sitar
    4. dhol
    Show answer

    Answer: been

    The been (pungi) is made from a dried gourd.

  3. From Tasting to Digesting
    We taste sweet, sour, salty and bitter with our ______.

    1. nose
    2. lips
    3. tongue
    4. teeth
    Show answer

    Answer: tongue

    Different parts of the tongue help us sense different tastes.

  4. Seeds and Seeds
    Coconut seeds are spread by ______.

    1. ants
    2. water
    3. wind
    4. birds
    Show answer

    Answer: water

    Coconuts can float and are carried by water to new places.

  5. Every Drop Counts
    The Ghadsisar lake is in ______, Rajasthan.

    1. Jaipur
    2. Udaipur
    3. Jodhpur
    4. Jaisalmer
    Show answer

    Answer: Jaisalmer

    Ghadsisar lake in Jaisalmer was built to collect rainwater.
