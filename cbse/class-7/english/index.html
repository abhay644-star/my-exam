Sample questions

A few questions from the Class 7 English bank. Every test picks a fresh mix.

  1. Subject–Verb Agreement
    My brother ______ cricket every evening. (play)

    1. play
    2. played
    3. plays
    4. playing
    Show answer

    Answer: plays

    A singular third-person subject takes a verb ending in '-s' in the simple present.

  2. Verbs and Tenses
    Write the past tense of 'catch'.

    1. catched
    2. catches
    3. caught
    4. cought
    Show answer

    Answer: caught

    'Catch' is an irregular verb; its past form is 'caught'.

  3. Articles and Determiners
    There isn't ______ milk left in the jug.

    1. any
    2. many
    3. a
    4. few
    Show answer

    Answer: any

    'Any' or 'much' is used with uncountable nouns in negative sentences.

  4. Prepositions
    The book is ______ the shelf.

    1. at
    2. since
    3. into
    4. on
    Show answer

    Answer: on

    'On' shows something resting on a surface.

  5. Conjunctions
    He was tired, ______ he went to bed early.

    1. although
    2. or
    3. but
    4. so
    Show answer

    Answer: so

    'So' introduces a result.
