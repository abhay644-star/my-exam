Sample questions

A few questions from the Class 7 Social Science bank. Every test picks a fresh mix.

  1. Geography: Geographical Diversity of India
    The word 'Himalaya' means 'abode of ______'.

    1. Gods
    2. Clouds
    3. Rivers
    4. Snow
    Show answer

    Answer: Snow

    In Sanskrit, hima means snow and alaya means abode.

  2. Geography: Understanding the Weather
    Which instrument measures air temperature?

    1. Barometer
    2. Rain gauge
    3. Anemometer
    4. Thermometer
    Show answer

    Answer: Thermometer

    A thermometer measures how hot or cold the air is.

  3. Geography: Climates of India
    The seasonal winds that bring most of India's rainfall are called the ______.

    1. Western disturbances
    2. Monsoon
    3. Loo
    4. Sea breeze
    Show answer

    Answer: Monsoon

    The south-west monsoon winds bring most of India's annual rain from June to September.

  4. History: New Beginnings: Cities and States
    The sixteen large kingdoms of ancient India around the 6th century BCE, the 'great janapadas', are called ______.

    1. Janapadas
    2. Gana-sanghas
    3. Mahajanapadas
    4. Samantas
    Show answer

    Answer: Mahajanapadas

    Mahajanapada means 'great janapada'; texts list sixteen of them.

  5. History: The Rise of Empires
    Who founded the Maurya Empire?

    1. Bindusara
    2. Ashoka
    3. Chandragupta Maurya
    4. Samudragupta
    Show answer

    Answer: Chandragupta Maurya

    Chandragupta Maurya, guided by Chanakya, founded the Maurya Empire around 321 BCE.
