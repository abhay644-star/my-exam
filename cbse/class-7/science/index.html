Sample questions

A few questions from the Class 7 Science bank. Every test picks a fresh mix.

  1. Acidic, Basic and Neutral Substances
    The natural indicator litmus is obtained from ______.

    1. mosses
    2. fungi
    3. lichens
    4. algae
    Show answer

    Answer: lichens

    Litmus is a dye extracted from lichens.

  2. Electric Circuits
    A device used to open or close an electric circuit is called a ______.

    1. bulb
    2. cell
    3. wire
    4. switch
    Show answer

    Answer: switch

    A switch completes or breaks the circuit to turn a device on or off.

  3. Metals and Non-metals
    The property of metals by which they can be beaten into thin sheets is called ______.

    1. brittleness
    2. ductility
    3. sonority
    4. malleability
    Show answer

    Answer: malleability

    Malleability lets metals like aluminium be beaten into foils.

  4. Physical and Chemical Changes
    Rusting of iron is a ______ change.

    1. reversible
    2. periodic
    3. physical
    4. chemical
    Show answer

    Answer: chemical

    Rust is a new substance (iron oxide) formed, so rusting is a chemical change.

  5. Adolescence
    The period of life between childhood and adulthood is called ______.

    1. old age
    2. menopause
    3. adolescence
    4. infancy
    Show answer

    Answer: adolescence

    Adolescence is the stage of growth and change between childhood and adulthood.
