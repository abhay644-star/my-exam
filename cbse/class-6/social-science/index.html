Sample questions

A few questions from the Class 6 Social Science bank. Every test picks a fresh mix.

  1. Geography: Locating Places on Earth
    The imaginary line at 0° latitude that divides the Earth into the Northern and Southern Hemispheres is called the ______.

    1. Arctic Circle
    2. Prime Meridian
    3. Tropic of Cancer
    4. Equator
    Show answer

    Answer: Equator

    The Equator is the 0° line of latitude, midway between the two poles.

  2. Geography: Oceans and Continents
    Which is the largest ocean on Earth?

    1. Indian Ocean
    2. Atlantic Ocean
    3. Pacific Ocean
    4. Arctic Ocean
    Show answer

    Answer: Pacific Ocean

    The Pacific Ocean is the largest and deepest ocean, covering about one-third of the Earth's surface.

  3. Geography: Landforms and Life
    What is the highest mountain peak in the world?

    1. Mount Everest
    2. Kangchenjunga
    3. K2
    4. Nanda Devi
    Show answer

    Answer: Mount Everest

    Mount Everest (Sagarmatha in Nepal, Chomolungma in Tibet) in the Himalayas is the world's highest peak.

  4. History: Timeline and Sources of History
    Scientists who study the past by digging up and examining material remains such as tools, pottery and buildings are called ______.

    1. Botanists
    2. Astronomers
    3. Geologists
    4. Archaeologists
    Show answer

    Answer: Archaeologists

    Archaeologists excavate and study material remains to reconstruct the past.

  5. History: India, That Is Bharat
    Article 1 of the Constitution says: 'India, that is ______, shall be a Union of States.'

    1. Bharat
    2. Hindustan
    3. Jambudvipa
    4. Aryavarta
    Show answer

    Answer: Bharat

    Article 1 of the Constitution names the country as 'India, that is Bharat'.
