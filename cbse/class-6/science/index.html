Sample questions

A few questions from the Class 6 Science bank. Every test picks a fresh mix.

  1. Diversity in the Living World
    Plants with weak stems that take the support of other objects to grow upwards are called ______.

    1. herbs
    2. climbers
    3. creepers
    4. shrubs
    Show answer

    Answer: climbers

    Climbers like the money plant and pea use tendrils or twining stems to climb on supports.

  2. Mindful Eating
    Rice and wheat are rich sources of which nutrient?

    1. vitamins
    2. carbohydrates
    3. fats
    4. proteins
    Show answer

    Answer: carbohydrates

    Cereals like rice and wheat mainly provide carbohydrates (starch) that give us energy.

  3. Exploring Magnets
    The end of a freely suspended magnet that points towards the geographic north is called its ______ pole.

    1. north
    2. south
    3. neutral
    4. east
    Show answer

    Answer: north

    The north-seeking end of a freely suspended magnet is called its north pole.

  4. Measurement and Motion
    The SI unit of length is the ______.

    1. metre
    2. kilometre
    3. centimetre
    4. foot
    Show answer

    Answer: metre

    The metre (m) is the SI unit of length.

  5. Materials Around Us
    A material through which we can see objects clearly is called ______.

    1. translucent
    2. lustrous
    3. opaque
    4. transparent
    Show answer

    Answer: transparent

    Transparent materials like clear glass allow light to pass through completely.
