Sample questions

A few questions from the Class 6 English bank. Every test picks a fresh mix.

  1. Nouns
    Fill in the collective noun: A ______ of cows was grazing in the field.

    1. herd
    2. swarm
    3. pack
    4. flock
    Show answer

    Answer: herd

    A group of cattle such as cows is called a herd.

  2. Pronouns
    Riya lost her pen, so Aman lent ______ his.

    1. herself
    2. she
    3. her
    4. hers
    Show answer

    Answer: her

    The object form of 'she' is 'her'.

  3. Adjectives
    Write the comparative form of 'tall'.

    1. most tall
    2. taller
    3. tallest
    4. more tall
    Show answer

    Answer: taller

    Short adjectives form the comparative by adding '-er'.

  4. Adverbs
    The tortoise walked ______. (slow)

    1. slow
    2. slower
    3. slowest
    4. slowly
    Show answer

    Answer: slowly

    An adverb of manner is usually formed by adding '-ly' to the adjective.

  5. Verbs and Tenses
    The sun ______ in the east. (rise)

    1. rises
    2. is rising
    3. rose
    4. rising
    Show answer

    Answer: rises

    Universal truths are expressed in the simple present tense.
