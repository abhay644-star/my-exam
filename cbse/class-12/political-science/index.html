Sample questions

A few questions from the Class 12 Political Science bank. Every test picks a fresh mix.

  1. The End of Bipolarity
    The Bolshevik (socialist) revolution in Russia, which led to the formation of the Soviet Union, took place in ______.

    1. 1914
    2. 1917
    3. 1922
    4. 1945
    Show answer

    Answer: 1917

    The Bolshevik revolution of 1917 was inspired by socialist ideals and led to the formation of the Soviet system.

  2. New Centres of Power
    ASEAN was established in ______ by five countries signing the Bangkok Declaration.

    1. 1967
    2. 1985
    3. 1957
    4. 1977
    Show answer

    Answer: 1967

    Indonesia, Malaysia, the Philippines, Singapore and Thailand set up ASEAN in 1967 to speed up economic growth and social progress.

  3. Contemporary South Asia
    SAARC (South Asian Association for Regional Cooperation) was set up in ______.

    1. 1991
    2. 2004
    3. 1975
    4. 1985
    Show answer

    Answer: 1985

    SAARC was set up in 1985 by the South Asian countries to promote regional cooperation.

  4. International Organisations
    The United Nations was founded in ______ after the Second World War.

    1. 1919
    2. 1948
    3. 1950
    4. 1945
    Show answer

    Answer: 1945

    The UN was founded on 24 October 1945 as a successor to the League of Nations.

  5. Security in the Contemporary World
    The 1968 treaty that allowed only five countries to keep nuclear weapons and aimed to stop their spread is the ______.

    1. BWC
    2. NPT
    3. CWC
    4. CTBT
    Show answer

    Answer: NPT

    The NPT (1968) recognised only five nuclear weapon states; India refused to sign it, calling it discriminatory.
