A few questions from this chapter. Open each one to see the answer.

  1. The interaction in which one species benefits and the other is neither harmed nor benefited is called ______.

    1. parasitism
    2. mutualism
    3. commensalism
    4. amensalism
    Show answer

    Answer: commensalism

    An orchid growing on a mango branch is an example of commensalism.

  2. The logistic (Verhulst-Pearl) growth curve of a population is ______-shaped.

    1. linear
    2. sigmoid
    3. bell
    4. J
    Show answer

    Answer: sigmoid

    With limited resources, growth slows as the population nears carrying capacity, giving a sigmoid curve.

  3. A pond had 20 lotus plants last year; through reproduction 8 new plants were added this year. Calculate the birth rate (per lotus per year).

    1. 0.2
    2. 0.28
    3. 0.4
    4. 2.5
    Show answer

    Answer: 0.4

    Birth rate = 8 ÷ 20 = 0.4 offspring per lotus per year.

  4. The interaction in which both interacting species benefit is called ______.

    1. competition
    2. commensalism
    3. amensalism
    4. mutualism
    Show answer

    Answer: mutualism

    Lichens (fungus and alga) and mycorrhizae (fungus and plant roots) are examples of mutualism.

  5. The interaction in which one species is harmed and the other is unaffected is called ______.

    1. amensalism
    2. predation
    3. commensalism
    4. mutualism
    Show answer

    Answer: amensalism

    Amensalism is a (-, 0) interaction.

  6. On the rocky sea coasts of Scotland, the larger barnacle Balanus dominates the intertidal area and excludes the smaller barnacle ______.

    1. Pisaster
    2. Chthamalus
    3. Ophrys
    4. Cuscuta
    Show answer

    Answer: Chthamalus

    Connell's field experiments showed this competitive exclusion of Chthamalus by Balanus.

  7. The population interaction in which both interacting species are harmed is called ______.

    1. mutualism
    2. predation
    3. parasitism
    4. competition
    Show answer

    Answer: competition

    In competition the fitness of one species is lowered in the presence of another that shares limited resources.

  8. An age pyramid with a broad base (triangular shape) indicates a(n) ______ population.

    1. stable
    2. declining
    3. ageing
    4. expanding
    Show answer

    Answer: expanding

    A broad base shows many pre-reproductive individuals; a bell shape shows a stable population and an urn shape a declining one.
