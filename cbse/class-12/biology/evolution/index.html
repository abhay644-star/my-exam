A few questions from this chapter. Open each one to see the answer.

  1. The theory of evolution by natural selection was proposed by Charles ______.

    1. Mendel
    2. Darwin
    3. de Vries
    4. Lamarck
    Show answer

    Answer: Darwin

    Charles Darwin, based on his voyage on HMS Beagle, explained evolution through natural selection.

  2. Wings of a butterfly and of a bird perform the same function but are anatomically different. They are ______ organs.

    1. vestigial
    2. rudimentary
    3. analogous
    4. homologous
    Show answer

    Answer: analogous

    Analogous organs result from convergent evolution: similar function, different structure and origin.

  3. In England after industrialisation, dark-winged moths outnumbered white-winged moths on soot-covered tree trunks. This phenomenon is called industrial ______.

    1. pollution
    2. melanism
    3. revolution
    4. mutation
    Show answer

    Answer: melanism

    Dark moths were camouflaged on dark trunks and escaped predators, an example of natural selection.

  4. In a population in Hardy-Weinberg equilibrium, the frequency of recessive allele a (q) is 0.3. What is the frequency of heterozygotes (2pq)?

    1. 0.21
    2. 0.49
    3. 0.42
    4. 0.09
    Show answer

    Answer: 0.42

    p = 1 - 0.3 = 0.7, so 2pq = 2 × 0.7 × 0.3 = 0.42.

  5. The Earth was formed about how many billion years ago?

    1. 3 billion years
    2. 20 billion years
    3. 4.5 billion years
    4. 1.5 billion years
    Show answer

    Answer: 4.5 billion years

    The universe is about 20 billion years old; the Earth formed about 4.5 billion years ago.

  6. The evolution of different varieties of finches from a seed-eating ancestor on the Galapagos Islands is an example of ______ radiation.

    1. random
    2. adaptive
    3. parallel
    4. convergent
    Show answer

    Answer: adaptive

    Darwin's finches show adaptive radiation: evolution of different forms starting from one point and radiating to other habitats.

  7. In a population in Hardy-Weinberg equilibrium, 16% of individuals show the recessive phenotype (aa). What is the frequency of the dominant allele (p)?

    1. 0.84
    2. 0.16
    3. 0.6
    4. 0.4
    Show answer

    Answer: 0.6

    q² = 0.16, so q = 0.4 and p = 1 - 0.4 = 0.6.

  8. Hugo de Vries, working on the evening primrose, proposed that evolution is caused by ______, not by minor variations.

    1. mutation
    2. natural selection
    3. gene flow
    4. use and disuse
    Show answer

    Answer: mutation

    de Vries believed large mutations cause speciation in a single step, which he called saltation.
