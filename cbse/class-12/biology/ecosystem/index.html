A few questions from this chapter. Open each one to see the answer.

  1. The vertical distribution of different species occupying different levels in an ecosystem (e.g. trees at the top, shrubs below, herbs and grasses at the bottom) is called ______.

    1. succession
    2. stratification
    3. eutrophication
    4. zonation
    Show answer

    Answer: stratification

    NCERT describes this vertical layering of species in an ecosystem, such as a forest, as stratification.

  2. During decomposition, water-soluble inorganic nutrients go down into the soil horizon and get precipitated as unavailable salts. This process is called ______.

    1. humification
    2. leaching
    3. fragmentation
    4. catabolism
    Show answer

    Answer: leaching

    Leaching is the downward movement of water-soluble nutrients into the soil, where they are precipitated as unavailable salts.

  3. The producers of a food chain fix 20,000 J of energy. Using the 10 per cent law, how much energy is available to the secondary consumers?

    1. 20 J
    2. 2 J
    3. 200 J
    4. 2000 J
    Show answer

    Answer: 200 J

    Only about 10% passes to each next trophic level: 20,000 J → 2,000 J (primary consumers) → 200 J (secondary consumers).

  4. The pyramid of biomass in the sea is generally inverted because the biomass of fishes far exceeds that of ______.

    1. phytoplankton
    2. decomposers
    3. zooplankton
    4. seaweeds
    Show answer

    Answer: phytoplankton

    In the sea the producers (phytoplankton) have a small standing biomass, which is far exceeded by the biomass of fishes, giving an inverted pyramid of biomass.

  5. The ecological pyramid of ______ is always upright and can never be inverted.

    1. standing crop
    2. numbers
    3. energy
    4. biomass
    Show answer

    Answer: energy

    Energy is lost as heat at every trophic level, so each higher level always has less energy; the pyramid of energy is therefore always upright.

  6. Plants capture only 2–10 per cent of the ______ (the part of sunlight usable in photosynthesis, less than 50% of incident solar radiation).

    1. infrared radiation
    2. cosmic radiation
    3. PAR
    4. ultraviolet radiation
    Show answer

    Answer: PAR

    Less than 50% of incident solar radiation is photosynthetically active radiation (PAR), and plants capture only 2–10% of PAR.

  7. According to NCERT, the annual net primary productivity of the whole biosphere is approximately how many billion tons (dry weight) of organic matter?

    1. 55 billion tons
    2. 115 billion tons
    3. 250 billion tons
    4. 170 billion tons
    Show answer

    Answer: 170 billion tons

    Annual NPP of the biosphere is about 170 billion tons (dry weight); of this the oceans contribute only about 55 billion tons.

  8. In a terrestrial ecosystem, a much larger fraction of energy flows through the ______ food chain than through the grazing food chain.

    1. detritus
    2. predator
    3. parasitic
    4. grazing
    Show answer

    Answer: detritus

    In aquatic ecosystems the grazing food chain is the major conduit of energy flow, but in terrestrial ecosystems a much larger fraction flows through the detritus food chain.
