Sample questions

A few questions from the Class 12 Biology bank. Every test picks a fresh mix.

  1. Sexual Reproduction in Flowering Plants
    The hard outer layer of the pollen grain wall, made of sporopollenin, is called the ______.

    1. endothecium
    2. tapetum
    3. intine
    4. exine
    Show answer

    Answer: exine

    Exine is made of sporopollenin, one of the most resistant organic materials known; the inner wall is the intine.

  2. Human Reproduction
    In humans, fertilisation normally takes place in the ______ region of the fallopian tube.

    1. ampullary
    2. infundibulum
    3. fimbriae
    4. isthmus
    Show answer

    Answer: ampullary

    Fertilisation can occur only if the ovum and sperms reach the ampullary region of the fallopian tube at the same time.

  3. Reproductive Health
    The foetal sex determination test based on analysing the chromosomal pattern in the amniotic fluid is called ______.

    1. amniocentesis
    2. angiography
    3. ultrasound
    4. laparoscopy
    Show answer

    Answer: amniocentesis

    Amniocentesis is meant to detect genetic disorders; its misuse for sex determination is banned in India.

  4. Principles of Inheritance and Variation
    Mendel conducted his hybridisation experiments on the garden ______.

    1. bean
    2. maize
    3. snapdragon
    4. pea
    Show answer

    Answer: pea

    Mendel worked on garden pea (Pisum sativum) for seven years (1856-1863).

  5. Molecular Basis of Inheritance
    The discontinuously synthesised fragments (Okazaki fragments) are joined by the enzyme DNA ______.

    1. polymerase
    2. gyrase
    3. helicase
    4. ligase
    Show answer

    Answer: ligase

    DNA ligase joins Okazaki fragments on the lagging strand during replication.
