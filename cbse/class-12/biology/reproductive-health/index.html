A few questions from this chapter. Open each one to see the answer.

  1. The foetal sex determination test based on analysing the chromosomal pattern in the amniotic fluid is called ______.

    1. amniocentesis
    2. angiography
    3. ultrasound
    4. laparoscopy
    Show answer

    Answer: amniocentesis

    Amniocentesis is meant to detect genetic disorders; its misuse for sex determination is banned in India.

  2. CuT, Cu7 and Multiload 375 are examples of ______-releasing IUDs.

    1. iron
    2. hormone
    3. zinc
    4. copper
    Show answer

    Answer: copper

    Cu ions released by these IUDs suppress sperm motility and the fertilising capacity of sperms.

  3. The Medical Termination of Pregnancy (Amendment) Act was enacted by the Government of India in which year?

    1. 1951
    2. 1971
    3. 2017
    4. 2010
    Show answer

    Answer: 2017

    The MTP (Amendment) Act, 2017 was enacted to reduce maternal deaths from unsafe abortions; it lays down when and by whom a pregnancy may be terminated.

  4. The surgical method of contraception in males, in which a small part of the vas deferens is removed or tied up, is called ______.

    1. vasectomy
    2. amniocentesis
    3. circumcision
    4. tubectomy
    Show answer

    Answer: vasectomy

    Vasectomy blocks the transport of sperms; it is a terminal (permanent) method.

  5. In assisted reproductive technology, transfer of the zygote or early embryo (up to 8 blastomeres) into the fallopian tube is called ______.

    1. ICSI
    2. GIFT
    3. IUT
    4. ZIFT
    Show answer

    Answer: ZIFT

    In ZIFT the embryo is placed in the fallopian tube; embryos with more than 8 blastomeres are transferred into the uterus (IUT).

  6. In GIFT, what is transferred into the fallopian tube of a female who cannot produce one but can provide suitable conditions for fertilisation?

    1. zygote
    2. embryo
    3. sperm
    4. ovum
    Show answer

    Answer: ovum

    GIFT is gamete intra fallopian transfer: an ovum collected from a donor is transferred into the fallopian tube.

  7. The surgical method of contraception in females, in which a small part of the fallopian tube is removed or tied up, is called ______.

    1. amniocentesis
    2. laparoscopy
    3. tubectomy
    4. vasectomy
    Show answer

    Answer: tubectomy

    Tubectomy blocks the transport of the ovum; reversibility is very poor.

  8. The once-a-week oral contraceptive pill 'Saheli' was developed by scientists at which institute in Lucknow?

    1. CDRI
    2. NBRI
    3. AIIMS
    4. ICMR
    Show answer

    Answer: CDRI

    Saheli, a non-steroidal pill with very few side effects and high contraceptive value, was developed at CDRI, Lucknow.
