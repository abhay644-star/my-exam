A few questions from this chapter. Open each one to see the answer.

  1. Typhoid fever is caused by the bacterium Salmonella ______.

    1. coli
    2. vivax
    3. aureus
    4. typhi
    Show answer

    Answer: typhi

    Salmonella typhi enters the small intestine through contaminated food and water; Widal test confirms typhoid.

  2. Plasmodium enters the human body through the bite of an infected female Anopheles mosquito as ______.

    1. gametocytes
    2. merozoites
    3. sporozoites
    4. trophozoites
    Show answer

    Answer: sporozoites

    Sporozoites are the infectious form; they first multiply in liver cells and then attack RBCs.

  3. After entering the body, HIV replicates in macrophages and then attacks which type of lymphocytes, decreasing their number?

    1. helper T cells
    2. cytotoxic T cells
    3. B cells
    4. plasma cells
    Show answer

    Answer: helper T cells

    HIV destroys helper T-lymphocytes, which weakens the immune system and leads to AIDS.

  4. Malaria is caused by the protozoan ______.

    1. Salmonella
    2. Plasmodium
    3. Entamoeba
    4. Ascaris
    Show answer

    Answer: Plasmodium

    Different species of Plasmodium (vivax, malariae, falciparum) cause malaria; P. falciparum causes the most serious form.

  5. The yellowish colostrum secreted by the mother in the initial days of lactation contains abundant antibodies of type ______.

    1. IgG
    2. IgM
    3. IgE
    4. IgA
    Show answer

    Answer: IgA

    IgA in colostrum protects the infant; this is an example of passive immunity.

  6. After infecting a cell, HIV makes a DNA copy of its RNA genome using the enzyme ______.

    1. reverse transcriptase
    2. DNA ligase
    3. RNA polymerase
    4. restriction endonuclease
    Show answer

    Answer: reverse transcriptase

    HIV is a retrovirus; the viral DNA gets incorporated into the host cell DNA and directs the formation of new virus particles.

  7. Pneumonia in humans is caused by bacteria such as Streptococcus pneumoniae and ______ influenzae.

    1. Haemophilus
    2. Salmonella
    3. Plasmodium
    4. Entamoeba
    Show answer

    Answer: Haemophilus

    These bacteria infect the alveoli of the lungs, which fill with fluid, causing problems in breathing.

  8. Elephantiasis (filariasis) is caused by filarial worms of the genus ______.

    1. Wuchereria
    2. Ascaris
    3. Trichophyton
    4. Microsporum
    Show answer

    Answer: Wuchereria

    Wuchereria bancrofti and W. malayi are transmitted by female mosquito vectors and cause chronic inflammation of the lymphatic vessels.
