A few questions from this chapter. Open each one to see the answer.

  1. Mendel conducted his hybridisation experiments on the garden ______.

    1. bean
    2. maize
    3. snapdragon
    4. pea
    Show answer

    Answer: pea

    Mendel worked on garden pea (Pisum sativum) for seven years (1856-1863).

  2. In the F2 generation of a Mendelian dihybrid cross (ratio 9:3:3:1), how many plants out of 16 show both recessive traits?

    1. 4
    2. 9
    3. 1
    4. 3
    Show answer

    Answer: 1

    Only the double homozygous recessive (e.g. rryy) shows both recessive traits, which is 1 out of 16.

  3. In snapdragon, red (RR) crossed with white (rr) gives pink (Rr) flowers due to incomplete dominance. If 400 plants are raised in F2, how many are expected to be pink?

    1. 300
    2. 200
    3. 400
    4. 100
    Show answer

    Answer: 200

    The F2 ratio is 1 red : 2 pink : 1 white, so pink = 2/4 × 400 = 200.

  4. The units of inheritance that Mendel called 'factors' are now called ______.

    1. genes
    2. traits
    3. nucleotides
    4. chromosomes
    Show answer

    Answer: genes

    Factors are now called genes; genes that code for a pair of contrasting traits are alleles.

  5. In humans, the ABO blood groups are controlled by the gene I, which has how many alleles?

    1. 3
    2. 4
    3. 6
    4. 2
    Show answer

    Answer: 3

    The three alleles are IA, IB and i; this is an example of multiple alleles.

  6. A woman with blood group O marries a man with blood group AB. What is the probability (in %) that their child will have blood group O?

    1. 50 %
    2. 100 %
    3. 25 %
    4. 0 %
    Show answer

    Answer: 0 %

    The mother gives only i and the father gives IA or IB, so children are IAi (A) or IBi (B); none can be O.

  7. Crossing an organism showing the dominant phenotype with its recessive parent, to find its genotype, is called a ______.

    1. monohybrid cross
    2. reciprocal cross
    3. dihybrid cross
    4. test cross
    Show answer

    Answer: test cross

    In a test cross the progeny reveal whether the dominant-looking organism is homozygous or heterozygous.

  8. When a single gene shows multiple phenotypic expressions, as in phenylketonuria, the gene is called a ______ gene.

    1. pleiotropic
    2. polygenic
    3. codominant
    4. recessive
    Show answer

    Answer: pleiotropic

    A pleiotropic gene affects many traits; in phenylketonuria, a single gene mutation causes mental retardation and reduced hair and skin pigmentation.
