A few questions from this chapter. Open each one to see the answer.

  1. The first transgenic cow, which produced milk containing human alpha-lactalbumin, was named ______.

    1. Dolly
    2. Rosie
    3. Polly
    4. Molly
    Show answer

    Answer: Rosie

    Rosie (1997) produced human protein-enriched milk, nutritionally more balanced for human babies.

  2. Golden rice is a genetically modified variety rich in ______.

    1. vitamin C
    2. iron
    3. vitamin A
    4. vitamin D
    Show answer

    Answer: vitamin A

    Golden rice has been engineered to produce beta-carotene, which the body converts to vitamin A.

  3. Infestation of tobacco roots by the nematode Meloidogyne incognita was prevented using a technique based on silencing a specific mRNA. This technique is called RNA ______.

    1. replication
    2. interference
    3. editing
    4. splicing
    Show answer

    Answer: interference

    In RNAi, nematode-specific genes were introduced to produce dsRNA that silences the parasite's mRNA, killing it.

  4. In 1983, Eli Lilly prepared chains A and B of human insulin separately in E. coli and then combined them. What type of bridges link chains A and B in insulin?

    1. disulphide
    2. peptide
    3. phosphodiester
    4. hydrogen
    Show answer

    Answer: disulphide

    The A and B chains of insulin are linked together by disulphide bridges.

  5. The Bacillus thuringiensis genes that code for the insecticidal Bt toxin proteins are called ______ genes.

    1. amp
    2. cry
    3. lac
    4. tet
    Show answer

    Answer: cry

    The cry genes are named so because they code for crystal (Cry) proteins.

  6. The proteins encoded by the genes cryIAc and cryIIAb control the cotton ______.

    1. whiteflies
    2. nematodes
    3. bollworms
    4. aphids
    Show answer

    Answer: bollworms

    cryIAc and cryIIAb control cotton bollworms, while cryIAb controls the corn borer.

  7. Over 95 per cent of all existing transgenic animals are ______.

    1. mice
    2. cows
    3. pigs
    4. rabbits
    Show answer

    Answer: mice

    Transgenic rats, rabbits, pigs, sheep, cows and fish have also been produced, but mice form the vast majority.

  8. Plants, bacteria, fungi and animals whose genes have been altered by manipulation are called ______ organisms.

    1. hybrid
    2. genetically modified
    3. cloned
    4. polyploid
    Show answer

    Answer: genetically modified

    GM plants can be more tolerant to stresses, less dependent on chemical pesticides and more nutritious.
