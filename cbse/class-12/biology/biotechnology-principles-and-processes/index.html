A few questions from this chapter. Open each one to see the answer.

  1. Enzymes that cut DNA at specific recognition sequences are called restriction ______.

    1. endonucleases
    2. ligases
    3. exonucleases
    4. polymerases
    Show answer

    Answer: endonucleases

    Restriction endonucleases are the molecular scissors of genetic engineering.

  2. The first restriction endonuclease to be isolated was Hind ______.

    1. II
    2. IV
    3. III
    4. I
    Show answer

    Answer: II

    Hind II, isolated about five years after the discovery of restriction enzymes, always cuts DNA at a specific six base pair sequence.

  3. EcoRI recognises the palindromic sequence 5'-GAATTC-3'. Between which two bases does it cut each strand?

    1. Between A and A
    2. Between G and A
    3. Between A and T
    4. Between T and C
    Show answer

    Answer: Between G and A

    EcoRI cuts between G and A on both strands, leaving single-stranded sticky ends.

  4. The sequence in a vector from where replication starts, which also controls the copy number of the linked DNA, is called ______.

    1. operator
    2. terminator
    3. ori
    4. promoter
    Show answer

    Answer: ori

    Any DNA linked to the ori can be made to replicate in the host cell.

  5. The thermostable DNA polymerase used in PCR is isolated from the bacterium ______.

    1. Thermus aquaticus
    2. Escherichia coli
    3. Agrobacterium tumefaciens
    4. Bacillus thuringiensis
    Show answer

    Answer: Thermus aquaticus

    Taq polymerase stays active during the high temperature needed to denature double-stranded DNA.

  6. In agarose gel electrophoresis, negatively charged DNA fragments move towards the ______.

    1. negative electrode
    2. cathode
    3. gel well
    4. anode
    Show answer

    Answer: anode

    DNA is negatively charged due to phosphate groups, so it moves towards the anode; smaller fragments move farther.

  7. In the restriction enzyme EcoRI, the letters 'Eco' come from the name of the bacterium ______.

    1. Agrobacterium tumefaciens
    2. Escherichia coli
    3. Haemophilus influenzae
    4. Bacillus thuringiensis
    Show answer

    Answer: Escherichia coli

    The first letter comes from the genus and the next two from the species; R is the strain RY 13 and I shows it was the first enzyme isolated from it.

  8. To release DNA, bacterial cells are treated with lysozyme, plant cells with cellulase and fungal cells with ______.

    1. lysozyme
    2. cellulase
    3. ribonuclease
    4. chitinase
    Show answer

    Answer: chitinase

    Fungal cell walls contain chitin, so chitinase is used to break them.
