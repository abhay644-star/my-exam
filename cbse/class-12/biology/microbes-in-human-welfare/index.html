A few questions from this chapter. Open each one to see the answer.

  1. Lactobacillus and other bacteria that convert milk into curd are commonly called ______.

    1. cyanobacteria
    2. BOD
    3. yeast
    4. LAB
    Show answer

    Answer: LAB

    Lactic acid bacteria produce acids that coagulate and partially digest milk proteins, and also increase vitamin B12.

  2. The large holes in Swiss cheese are due to the large amount of CO2 produced by the bacterium ______.

    1. Streptococcus
    2. Trichoderma
    3. Lactobacillus
    4. Propionibacterium sharmanii
    Show answer

    Answer: Propionibacterium sharmanii

    Propionibacterium sharmanii releases CO2 during ripening, creating holes in Swiss cheese.

  3. Cyclosporin A, used as an immunosuppressive agent in organ-transplant patients, is produced by the fungus ______.

    1. Penicillium notatum
    2. Trichoderma polysporum
    3. Aspergillus niger
    4. Monascus purpureus
    Show answer

    Answer: Trichoderma polysporum

    Trichoderma polysporum produces cyclosporin A; Monascus purpureus produces blood-cholesterol lowering statins.

  4. Toddy, a traditional drink of some parts of southern India, is made by fermenting sap from ______.

    1. barley
    2. grapes
    3. palms
    4. sugarcane
    Show answer

    Answer: palms

    Microbes ferment palm sap to make toddy.

  5. The bacterium used as a biocontrol agent to control butterfly caterpillars is Bacillus ______.

    1. anthracis
    2. thuringiensis
    3. subtilis
    4. cereus
    Show answer

    Answer: thuringiensis

    Spores of Bacillus thuringiensis (Bt) release a toxin in the caterpillar gut that kills it without harming other insects.

  6. Bacteria such as Methanobacterium, which grow anaerobically on cellulosic material and produce large amounts of methane, are collectively called ______.

    1. LAB
    2. cyanobacteria
    3. methanogens
    4. mycorrhiza
    Show answer

    Answer: methanogens

    Methanogens occur in sludge and in the rumen of cattle, and are used to produce biogas.

  7. Dough for bread is fermented using baker's yeast, Saccharomyces ______.

    1. sharmanii
    2. cerevisiae
    3. typhi
    4. thuringiensis
    Show answer

    Answer: cerevisiae

    Saccharomyces cerevisiae is used in bread-making and, as brewer's yeast, in making alcoholic drinks.

  8. Citric acid is produced commercially by the fungus Aspergillus ______.

    1. sharmanii
    2. cerevisiae
    3. niger
    4. aceti
    Show answer

    Answer: niger

    Aspergillus niger produces citric acid; Acetobacter aceti produces acetic acid.
