A few questions from this chapter. Open each one to see the answer.

  1. In humans, fertilisation normally takes place in the ______ region of the fallopian tube.

    1. ampullary
    2. infundibulum
    3. fimbriae
    4. isthmus
    Show answer

    Answer: ampullary

    Fertilisation can occur only if the ovum and sperms reach the ampullary region of the fallopian tube at the same time.

  2. Cells in the interstitial spaces outside the seminiferous tubules that synthesise and secrete androgens are ______ cells.

    1. follicular
    2. Leydig
    3. Sertoli
    4. germ
    Show answer

    Answer: Leydig

    Leydig (interstitial) cells secrete androgens such as testosterone; Sertoli cells nourish germ cells.

  3. How many sperms will be formed from 25 primary spermatocytes?

    1. 100
    2. 50
    3. 25
    4. 200
    Show answer

    Answer: 100

    Each primary spermatocyte undergoes meiosis to form 4 spermatids, which become 4 sperms: 25 × 4 = 100.

  4. During pregnancy, the hormone hCG is secreted by the ______.

    1. pituitary
    2. placenta
    3. hypothalamus
    4. ovary
    Show answer

    Answer: placenta

    The placenta acts as an endocrine tissue and produces hCG, hPL, oestrogens and progestogens.

  5. A rapid surge of which pituitary hormone in the middle of the menstrual cycle induces ovulation?

    1. LH
    2. oxytocin
    3. FSH
    4. prolactin
    Show answer

    Answer: LH

    The LH surge induces rupture of the Graafian follicle and the release of the ovum.

  6. Signals from the fully developed foetus and placenta trigger the release of a hormone from the mother's pituitary that causes stronger uterine contractions during parturition. Name the hormone.

    1. prolactin
    2. oxytocin
    3. relaxin
    4. progesterone
    Show answer

    Answer: oxytocin

    Oxytocin acts on the uterine muscle; the stronger contractions stimulate further oxytocin secretion (foetal ejection reflex).

  7. The release of the ovum from the Graafian follicle is called ______.

    1. implantation
    2. menstruation
    3. fertilisation
    4. ovulation
    Show answer

    Answer: ovulation

    Ovulation normally takes place around the middle (about the 14th day) of the menstrual cycle.

  8. After ovulation, the remaining parts of the Graafian follicle transform into a structure that secretes large amounts of progesterone. It is called the ______.

    1. zona pellucida
    2. antrum
    3. corpus luteum
    4. corpus albicans
    Show answer

    Answer: corpus luteum

    Progesterone from the corpus luteum maintains the endometrium; without fertilisation the corpus luteum degenerates and menstruation follows.
