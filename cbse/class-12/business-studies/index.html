Sample questions

A few questions from the Class 12 Business Studies bank. Every test picks a fresh mix.

  1. Nature and Significance of Management
    Completing a task on time is effectiveness, while completing it with the minimum use of resources and cost is ______.

    1. efficiency
    2. effectiveness
    3. productivity
    4. coordination
    Show answer

    Answer: efficiency

    Effectiveness is about achieving goals on time; efficiency is about doing the task with the least cost and resources.

  2. Principles of Management
    Who is known as the father of general management?

    1. henri fayol
    2. Peter Drucker
    3. Elton Mayo
    4. F.W. Taylor
    Show answer

    Answer: henri fayol

    Henri Fayol, a French mining engineer, gave 14 general principles of management applicable to all organisations.

  3. Business Environment
    Growing health awareness in Indian cities has increased the demand for organic food, green tea and fitness equipment. Which dimension of the business environment does this reflect?

    1. social
    2. political
    3. legal
    4. economic
    Show answer

    Answer: social

    The social environment includes customs, values and lifestyle trends such as health consciousness, which create new opportunities.

  4. Planning
    A plan that states expected results in numerical terms, such as expected sales of ₹50 lakh in a year, is a ______.

    1. method
    2. rule
    3. policy
    4. budget
    Show answer

    Answer: budget

    A budget is a statement of expected results expressed in numbers; it is both a plan and a control device.

  5. Organising
    The grouping of similar activities into jobs and jobs into departments is called ______.

    1. division of work
    2. departmentalisation
    3. delegation
    4. decentralisation
    Show answer

    Answer: departmentalisation

    Departmentalisation groups similar or related jobs into units like production, finance and marketing.
