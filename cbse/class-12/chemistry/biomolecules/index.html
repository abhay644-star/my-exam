A few questions from this chapter. Open each one to see the answer.

  1. Deficiency of which vitamin causes scurvy?

    1. vitamin A
    2. vitamin C
    3. vitamin D
    4. vitamin K
    Show answer

    Answer: vitamin C

    Vitamin C (ascorbic acid), found in amla and citrus fruits, prevents scurvy (bleeding gums).

  2. The –CO–NH– bond that links amino acids in proteins is called a ______ bond.

    1. glycosidic
    2. hydrogen
    3. phosphodiester
    4. peptide
    Show answer

    Answer: peptide

    A peptide bond forms between the –COOH of one amino acid and the –NH2 of the next with loss of water.

  3. α-D-glucose and β-D-glucose differ only in the configuration at C1. Such isomers are called ______.

    1. anomers
    2. conformers
    3. epimers
    4. enantiomers
    Show answer

    Answer: anomers

    C1 is the anomeric carbon formed when the –OH at C5 adds to the aldehyde group to make the ring.

  4. Sucrose on hydrolysis gives glucose and ______.

    1. ribose
    2. galactose
    3. fructose
    4. maltose
    Show answer

    Answer: fructose

    Sucrose is a disaccharide of α-D-glucose and β-D-fructose; its hydrolysis gives invert sugar.

  5. Which nitrogenous base is present in RNA but not in DNA?

    1. cytosine
    2. thymine
    3. adenine
    4. uracil
    Show answer

    Answer: uracil

    RNA contains uracil in place of the thymine found in DNA.

  6. Glucose is oxidised by bromine water to ______ acid.

    1. lactic
    2. gluconic
    3. saccharic
    4. citric
    Show answer

    Answer: gluconic

    Bromine water, a mild oxidising agent, oxidises only the –CHO group to –COOH, which shows that glucose has an aldehyde group.

  7. Glucose has six carbon atoms and an aldehyde group, so it is classed as an ______.

    1. aldohexose
    2. aldopentose
    3. ketopentose
    4. ketohexose
    Show answer

    Answer: aldohexose

    Fructose, with a keto group and six carbons, is a ketohexose.

  8. Starch is a mixture of two components: amylose and ______.

    1. amylase
    2. glycogen
    3. amylopectin
    4. cellulose
    Show answer

    Answer: amylopectin

    Amylose (15–20%) is water soluble and unbranched; amylopectin (80–85%) is insoluble and branched.
