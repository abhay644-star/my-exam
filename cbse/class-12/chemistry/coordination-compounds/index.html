  1. A ligand that binds to the central metal through two donor atoms, like ethane-1,2-diamine, is called ______.

    1. hexadentate
    2. ambidentate
    3. bidentate
    4. monodentate
    Show answer

    Answer: bidentate

    'en' donates through both nitrogen atoms, so it is bidentate and forms a chelate ring.

  2. What is the coordination number of iron in [Fe(CN)6]4-?

    1. 4
    2. 3
    3. 6
    4. 2
    Show answer

    Answer: 6

    Six monodentate CN- ligands are attached to Fe, so the coordination number is 6.

  3. Choose the correct IUPAC name of K4[Fe(CN)6].

    1. potassium hexacyanidoferrate(II)
    2. tetrapotassium hexacyanidoiron(III)
    3. potassium hexacyanidoferrate(III)
    4. potassium hexacyanidoiron(II)
    Show answer

    Answer: potassium hexacyanidoferrate(II)

    Cyanide is named cyanido, an anionic complex takes the -ate ending (ferrate), and Fe is +2 since 4(+1) + x + 6(−1) = 0.

  4. The ions or molecules bound to the central atom or ion in a coordination entity are called ______.

    1. cations
    2. chelates
    3. ligands
    4. counter ions
    Show answer

    Answer: ligands

    For example, in [Co(NH3)6]3+ the six NH3 molecules are the ligands.

  5. What is the oxidation state of cobalt in [Co(NH3)6]Cl3?

    1. 3
    2. 0
    3. 2
    4. 6
    Show answer

    Answer: 3

    NH3 is neutral and there are three Cl- counter ions, so x + 0 = +3.

  6. In an octahedral crystal field, which set of d orbitals is lower in energy?

    1. e
    2. t2g
    3. t2
    4. eg
    Show answer

    Answer: t2g

    The eg orbitals point at the ligands and are raised in energy; the t2g set lies between the ligands and is lowered.

  7. A ligand such as NO2- that can bind through either of two different donor atoms is called an ______ ligand.

    1. bidentate
    2. chelating
    3. ambidentate
    4. polydentate
    Show answer

    Answer: ambidentate

    NO2- can bind through N (nitrito-N) or through O (nitrito-O).

  8. Ethylenediaminetetraacetate ion (EDTA4-) binds to a metal ion through six donor atoms. It is a ______ ligand.

    1. hexadentate
    2. bidentate
    3. tetradentate
    4. monodentate
    Show answer

    Answer: hexadentate

    EDTA4- binds through two nitrogen and four oxygen atoms.
