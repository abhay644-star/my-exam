A few questions from this chapter. Open each one to see the answer.

  1. Primary amines heated with chloroform and ethanolic KOH give foul-smelling isocyanides. This is the ______ test.

    1. carbylamine
    2. Hinsberg
    3. Lucas
    4. iodoform
    Show answer

    Answer: carbylamine

    Only primary amines (aliphatic and aromatic) give the carbylamine test.

  2. Hinsberg's reagent, used to distinguish primary, secondary and tertiary amines, is ______.

    1. benzenesulphonyl chloride
    2. acetyl chloride
    3. sulphuric acid
    4. benzoyl chloride
    Show answer

    Answer: benzenesulphonyl chloride

    Primary amines give an alkali-soluble sulphonamide, secondary give an insoluble one and tertiary do not react.

  3. Among methylamine, dimethylamine and trimethylamine, the strongest base in aqueous solution is ______.

    1. methylamine
    2. dimethylamine
    3. ammonia
    4. trimethylamine
    Show answer

    Answer: dimethylamine

    In water, the combination of +I effect, solvation of the cation and steric factors makes (CH3)2NH the strongest base.

  4. Ethanamide (CH3CONH2) is treated with Br2 and aqueous NaOH (Hoffmann bromamide degradation). Name the amine formed.

    1. methanamine
    2. ethanamine
    3. ethanenitrile
    4. ethanol
    Show answer

    Answer: methanamine

    The Hoffmann bromamide reaction gives a primary amine with one carbon fewer than the amide, so CH3CONH2 gives CH3NH2.

  5. Trimethylamine, (CH3)3N, is a ______ amine.

    1. tertiary
    2. primary
    3. secondary
    4. quaternary
    Show answer

    Answer: tertiary

    All three hydrogen atoms of ammonia have been replaced by alkyl groups.

  6. Conversion of benzenediazonium chloride into chlorobenzene using CuCl/HCl is called the ______ reaction.

    1. Wurtz
    2. Sandmeyer
    3. Finkelstein
    4. Hoffmann
    Show answer

    Answer: Sandmeyer

    In the Sandmeyer reaction, Cu(I) salts replace the –N2+ group by Cl, Br or CN.

  7. Which of the following is the weakest base?

    1. aniline
    2. ethanamine
    3. methanamine
    4. ammonia
    Show answer

    Answer: aniline

    The lone pair on nitrogen in aniline is delocalised into the benzene ring, so it is less available for protonation (pKb 9.38).

  8. What is the hybridisation of the nitrogen atom in amines?

    1. sp
    2. sp3
    3. sp2
    4. dsp2
    Show answer

    Answer: sp3

    Nitrogen uses three sp3 orbitals for bonds and the fourth holds the lone pair, giving a pyramidal shape.
