  1. One faraday is the charge carried by one mole of ______.

    1. electrons
    2. ions
    3. atoms
    4. protons
    Show answer

    Answer: electrons

    1 F = NA × e ≈ 96500 C, the charge of one mole of electrons.

  2. Given E°(Cu2+/Cu) = +0.34 V and E°(Zn2+/Zn) = −0.76 V, find the standard emf of the Daniell cell.

    1. 0.42 V
    2. 1.1 V
    3. -1.1 V
    4. 0.76 V
    Show answer

    Answer: 1.1 V

    E°cell = E°cathode − E°anode = 0.34 − (−0.76) = 1.10 V.

  3. For the Daniell cell, n = 2 and E°cell = 1.1 V. Calculate ΔG° in kJ/mol (F = 96500 C/mol).

    1. 212.3 kJ/mol
    2. -21.23 kJ/mol
    3. -212.3 kJ/mol
    4. -106.15 kJ/mol
    Show answer

    Answer: -212.3 kJ/mol

    ΔG° = −nFE° = −2 × 96500 × 1.1 = −212300 J/mol = −212.3 kJ/mol.

  4. In a galvanic cell, oxidation takes place at the ______.

    1. anode
    2. cathode
    3. salt bridge
    4. voltmeter
    Show answer

    Answer: anode

    Oxidation occurs at the anode (the negative electrode of a galvanic cell) and reduction at the cathode.

  5. The fuel cell used in the Apollo space programme produced electricity by combining hydrogen with ______.

    1. nitrogen
    2. oxygen
    3. chlorine
    4. methane
    Show answer

    Answer: oxygen

    In the H2–O2 fuel cell the product is water, which was used as drinking water by the astronauts.

  6. A current of 2 A is passed through CuSO4 solution for 965 s. What mass of copper is deposited? (Cu = 63.5 g/mol, F = 96500 C/mol)

    1. 6.35 g
    2. 0.635 g
    3. 1.27 g
    4. 0.3175 g
    Show answer

    Answer: 0.635 g

    Q = 2 × 965 = 1930 C = 0.02 F; Cu2+ needs 2 F per mole, so 0.01 mol × 63.5 = 0.635 g.

  7. What is the SI unit of conductivity (κ)?

    1. S m^-1
    2. S m^2 mol^-1
    3. ohm m
    4. S m
    Show answer

    Answer: S m^-1

    Conductivity is the reciprocal of resistivity (ohm m), so its unit is S m^-1.

  8. The electrolyte used in the lead storage battery is ______.

    1. hydrochloric acid
    2. potassium hydroxide
    3. sulphuric acid
    4. ammonium chloride
    Show answer

    Answer: sulphuric acid

    A lead storage battery uses a 38% solution of sulphuric acid, with a lead anode and a grid of lead packed with PbO2 as cathode.
