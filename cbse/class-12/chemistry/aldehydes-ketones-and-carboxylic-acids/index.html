A few questions from this chapter. Open each one to see the answer.

  1. Formalin is a 40% aqueous solution of ______.

    1. formaldehyde
    2. ethanal
    3. methanol
    4. acetone
    Show answer

    Answer: formaldehyde

    Formalin (about 40% methanal) is used to preserve biological specimens.

  2. Aldehydes produce a silver mirror when warmed with ______ reagent.

    1. Lucas
    2. Schiff's
    3. Tollens'
    4. Fehling's
    Show answer

    Answer: Tollens'

    Tollens' reagent (ammoniacal silver nitrate) is reduced to metallic silver by aldehydes; most ketones do not react.

  3. Formaldehyde heated with concentrated NaOH gives methanol and ______ (Cannizzaro reaction).

    1. ethanol
    2. formaldehyde
    3. sodium formate
    4. sodium acetate
    Show answer

    Answer: sodium formate

    Having no α-hydrogen, HCHO undergoes disproportionation: one molecule is reduced to CH3OH and another oxidised to HCOO-Na+.

  4. Aldehydes and ketones both contain the ______ group, >C=O.

    1. ester
    2. carboxyl
    3. carbonyl
    4. hydroxyl
    Show answer

    Answer: carbonyl

    In aldehydes the carbonyl carbon has at least one H attached; in ketones it is bonded to two carbon groups.

  5. The only aldehyde that gives a positive iodoform test is ______.

    1. propanal
    2. benzaldehyde
    3. methanal
    4. ethanal
    Show answer

    Answer: ethanal

    The iodoform test needs a CH3CO– group; among aldehydes only ethanal (CH3CHO) has it.

  6. Which of these acids is the strongest?

    1. methanoic acid
    2. benzoic acid
    3. propanoic acid
    4. ethanoic acid
    Show answer

    Answer: methanoic acid

    pKa values: HCOOH 3.75, C6H5COOH 4.19, CH3COOH 4.76; electron-donating alkyl groups weaken the acid.

  7. What is the IUPAC name of acetone, CH3COCH3?

    1. propanone
    2. propanol
    3. propanal
    4. butanone
    Show answer

    Answer: propanone

    Acetone is a three-carbon ketone with the carbonyl group on carbon 2.

  8. Acyl chlorides are reduced to aldehydes by hydrogen over palladium on barium sulphate. This is called the ______ reduction.

    1. Wolff-Kishner
    2. Clemmensen
    3. Stephen
    4. Rosenmund
    Show answer

    Answer: Rosenmund

    The catalyst is partly poisoned so that the aldehyde is not reduced further to an alcohol.
