  1. Wood spirit is the common name of ______.

    1. ethanol
    2. propanol
    3. phenol
    4. methanol
    Show answer

    Answer: methanol

    Methanol was originally obtained by destructive distillation of wood, hence the name wood spirit.

  2. Phenol is more acidic than ethanol because the phenoxide ion is stabilised by ______.

    1. resonance
    2. steric hindrance
    3. hydrogen bonding
    4. hyperconjugation
    Show answer

    Answer: resonance

    The negative charge of phenoxide is delocalised over the benzene ring, while ethoxide's charge stays on oxygen.

  3. With Lucas reagent (conc. HCl + anhydrous ZnCl2), which class of alcohols gives turbidity immediately at room temperature?

    1. secondary
    2. tertiary
    3. primary
    4. methyl
    Show answer

    Answer: tertiary

    Tertiary alcohols form stable carbocations fastest, so the insoluble alkyl chloride separates at once.

  4. What is the IUPAC name of isopropyl alcohol, (CH3)2CHOH?

    1. 2-methylethanol
    2. propan-1-ol
    3. propan-2-ol
    4. propanal
    Show answer

    Answer: propan-2-ol

    The –OH group is on carbon 2 of a three-carbon chain.

  5. Phenol treated with chloroform and aqueous NaOH, followed by acidification, gives salicylaldehyde. This is the ______ reaction.

    1. Friedel-Crafts
    2. Williamson
    3. Kolbe's
    4. Reimer-Tiemann
    Show answer

    Answer: Reimer-Tiemann

    In the Reimer-Tiemann reaction, a –CHO group is introduced at the ortho position of phenol.

  6. Ethanol heated with conc. H2SO4 at 413 K gives ethoxyethane. At 443 K the main product is ______.

    1. ethyne
    2. ethanal
    3. ethene
    4. ethoxyethane
    Show answer

    Answer: ethene

    At the higher temperature intramolecular dehydration (elimination) is favoured, giving ethene.

  7. Phenol is also known as ______ acid.

    1. benzoic
    2. salicylic
    3. carbolic
    4. picric
    Show answer

    Answer: carbolic

    Phenol (C6H5OH) was first isolated from coal tar and named carbolic acid.

  8. The preparation of ethers by the reaction of an alkyl halide with a sodium alkoxide is called ______ synthesis.

    1. Kolbe's
    2. Wurtz
    3. Williamson
    4. Gabriel
    Show answer

    Answer: Williamson

    For example, CH3Br + C2H5ONa → CH3OC2H5 + NaBr; primary alkyl halides work best.
