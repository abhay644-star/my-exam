A few questions from this chapter. Open each one to see the answer.

  1. What is the most common oxidation state shown by the lanthanoids?

    1. 2
    2. 1
    3. 3
    4. 4
    Show answer

    Answer: 3

    Lanthanoids mainly show the +3 oxidation state; some also show +2 (Eu) or +4 (Ce).

  2. Zn, Cd and Hg are not regarded as transition elements because they have completely filled ______ orbitals in the ground state and in their common oxidation state.

    1. d
    2. p
    3. f
    4. s
    Show answer

    Answer: d

    Their configuration is (n−1)d10 ns2 and the M2+ ions are d10, so they lack partly filled d orbitals.

  3. Calculate the spin-only magnetic moment of Mn2+ ion (Z of Mn = 25) in BM.

    1. 5.92 BM
    2. 4.9 BM
    3. 5 BM
    4. 3.87 BM
    Show answer

    Answer: 5.92 BM

    Mn2+ is 3d5 with 5 unpaired electrons; μ = √(n(n+2)) = √35 ≈ 5.92 BM.

  4. An aqueous solution of potassium permanganate (KMnO4) is ______ in colour.

    1. purple
    2. orange
    3. green
    4. yellow
    Show answer

    Answer: purple

    The intense purple colour of MnO4- is due to charge transfer, not d–d transitions (Mn is d0).

  5. The steady decrease in atomic and ionic radii from lanthanum to lutetium is called the ______.

    1. actinoid contraction
    2. diagonal relationship
    3. lanthanoid contraction
    4. inert pair effect
    Show answer

    Answer: lanthanoid contraction

    It is caused by the poor shielding of one 4f electron by another, so the effective nuclear charge increases along the series.

  6. When yellow sodium chromate solution is acidified, it turns ______ due to the formation of dichromate ions.

    1. purple
    2. orange
    3. colourless
    4. green
    Show answer

    Answer: orange

    2CrO4^2- + 2H+ → Cr2O7^2- + H2O; chromate is yellow and dichromate is orange.

  7. The d-block elements of groups 3 to 12 are also called ______ elements.

    1. noble
    2. transition
    3. representative
    4. inner transition
    Show answer

    Answer: transition

    Their properties are transitional between the s-block and p-block elements.

  8. Which element of the first transition series shows the highest oxidation state, +7?

    1. chromium
    2. vanadium
    3. manganese
    4. iron
    Show answer

    Answer: manganese

    Mn (3d5 4s2) can use all seven 4s and 3d electrons, as in MnO4-.
