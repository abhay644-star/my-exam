  1. The colligative property preferred for finding the molar masses of proteins and polymers is ______.

    1. boiling point elevation
    2. freezing point depression
    3. relative lowering of vapour pressure
    4. osmotic pressure
    Show answer

    Answer: osmotic pressure

    Osmotic pressure is large enough to measure even for very dilute solutions and is measured at room temperature, so biomolecules are not damaged.

  2. Solutions showing a large positive deviation from Raoult's law form ______ boiling azeotropes.

    1. very high
    2. minimum
    3. intermediate
    4. maximum
    Show answer

    Answer: minimum

    For example, about 95% ethanol in water is a minimum boiling azeotrope; it boils below either pure component.

  3. 9 g of glucose (molar mass 180 g/mol) is dissolved in 500 g of water. Find the molality of the solution.

    1. 1 mol/kg
    2. 0.05 mol/kg
    3. 0.018 mol/kg
    4. 0.1 mol/kg
    Show answer

    Answer: 0.1 mol/kg

    Moles = 9/180 = 0.05 mol; molality = 0.05/0.5 kg = 0.1 mol/kg.

  4. Henry's law states that the solubility of a gas in a liquid is directly proportional to the ______ of the gas above the solution.

    1. density
    2. partial pressure
    3. volume
    4. temperature
    Show answer

    Answer: partial pressure

    p = KH × x; this is why soft drink bottles are sealed under high pressure of CO2.

  5. A solution contains 1 mol of ethanol and 4 mol of water. What is the mole fraction of ethanol?

    1. 0.2
    2. 4
    3. 0.25
    4. 0.8
    Show answer

    Answer: 0.2

    x(ethanol) = 1 ÷ (1 + 4) = 0.2.

  6. The vapour pressure of pure water at a certain temperature is 100 mm Hg. What is the vapour pressure of a solution in which the mole fraction of a non-volatile solute is 0.05?

    1. 5 mm Hg
    2. 95 mm Hg
    3. 100 mm Hg
    4. 105 mm Hg
    Show answer

    Answer: 95 mm Hg

    Mole fraction of water = 1 − 0.05 = 0.95, so p = 0.95 × 100 = 95 mm Hg.

  7. What is the van't Hoff factor (i) for NaCl in water, assuming complete dissociation?

    1. 0.5
    2. 2
    3. 3
    4. 1
    Show answer

    Answer: 2

    NaCl → Na+ + Cl-, so one formula unit gives 2 particles, i = 2.

  8. 4 g of NaOH (molar mass 40 g/mol) is dissolved to make 250 mL of solution. What is the molarity of the solution?

    1. 0.4 mol/L
    2. 1.6 mol/L
    3. 0.1 mol/L
    4. 0.04 mol/L
    Show answer

    Answer: 0.4 mol/L

    Moles = 4 ÷ 40 = 0.1 mol; molarity = 0.1 ÷ 0.25 L = 0.4 mol/L.
