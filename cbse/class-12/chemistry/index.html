Sample questions

A few questions from the Class 12 Chemistry bank. Every test picks a fresh mix.

  1. Solutions
    The colligative property preferred for finding the molar masses of proteins and polymers is ______.

    1. boiling point elevation
    2. freezing point depression
    3. relative lowering of vapour pressure
    4. osmotic pressure
    Show answer

    Answer: osmotic pressure

    Osmotic pressure is large enough to measure even for very dilute solutions and is measured at room temperature, so biomolecules are not damaged.

  2. Electrochemistry
    One faraday is the charge carried by one mole of ______.

    1. electrons
    2. ions
    3. atoms
    4. protons
    Show answer

    Answer: electrons

    1 F = NA × e ≈ 96500 C, the charge of one mole of electrons.

  3. Chemical Kinetics
    The half-life of a reaction of which order is independent of the initial concentration?

    1. first
    2. third
    3. second
    4. zero
    Show answer

    Answer: first

    For a first-order reaction t½ = 0.693/k, which does not depend on [A]0.

  4. The d- and f-Block Elements
    What is the most common oxidation state shown by the lanthanoids?

    1. 2
    2. 1
    3. 3
    4. 4
    Show answer

    Answer: 3

    Lanthanoids mainly show the +3 oxidation state; some also show +2 (Eu) or +4 (Ce).

  5. Coordination Compounds
    A ligand that binds to the central metal through two donor atoms, like ethane-1,2-diamine, is called ______.

    1. hexadentate
    2. ambidentate
    3. bidentate
    4. monodentate
    Show answer

    Answer: bidentate

    'en' donates through both nitrogen atoms, so it is bidentate and forms a chelate ring.
