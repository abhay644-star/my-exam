  1. The preparation of alkyl iodides by heating alkyl chlorides or bromides with NaI in dry acetone is called the ______ reaction.

    1. Finkelstein
    2. Sandmeyer
    3. Wurtz
    4. Swarts
    Show answer

    Answer: Finkelstein

    NaCl or NaBr formed is insoluble in dry acetone and precipitates, driving the Finkelstein reaction forward.

  2. An SN2 reaction at a chiral carbon takes place with ______ of configuration.

    1. no change
    2. racemisation
    3. inversion
    4. retention
    Show answer

    Answer: inversion

    The nucleophile attacks from the side opposite the leaving group, flipping the configuration like an umbrella.

  3. Which of the following reacts fastest by the SN1 mechanism?

    1. 2-bromobutane
    2. 1-bromobutane
    3. 2-bromo-2-methylpropane
    4. bromomethane
    Show answer

    Answer: 2-bromo-2-methylpropane

    SN1 rate depends on carbocation stability; the tertiary carbocation from 2-bromo-2-methylpropane is the most stable.

  4. Chlorobenzene is heated with aqueous NaOH at 623 K and 300 atm, and the product is then acidified. Name the final product.

    1. benzyl alcohol
    2. chlorophenol
    3. phenol
    4. benzene
    Show answer

    Answer: phenol

    The C–Cl bond in haloarenes has partial double bond character, so drastic conditions are needed to replace Cl by OH (Dow's process).

  5. In the Swarts reaction, an alkyl chloride or bromide is heated with a metallic fluoride such as AgF to give an alkyl ______.

    1. cyanide
    2. iodide
    3. chloride
    4. fluoride
    Show answer

    Answer: fluoride

    For example, CH3Br + AgF → CH3F + AgBr.

  6. Chloroform is slowly oxidised by air in light to a poisonous gas called ______.

    1. chlorine
    2. methane
    3. phosgene
    4. tear gas
    Show answer

    Answer: phosgene

    2CHCl3 + O2 → 2COCl2 + 2HCl, so chloroform is stored in dark coloured, filled bottles.

  7. 2-Bromobutane is heated with alcoholic KOH. By Zaitsev rule, what is the major product?

    1. butan-2-ol
    2. butane
    3. but-1-ene
    4. but-2-ene
    Show answer

    Answer: but-2-ene

    The more substituted alkene is preferred, so but-2-ene is the major product (about 80%).

  8. Alkyl halides react with sodium in dry ether to give alkanes with double the number of carbon atoms. This is the ______ reaction.

    1. Fittig
    2. Wurtz
    3. Sandmeyer
    4. Finkelstein
    Show answer

    Answer: Wurtz

    2CH3Br + 2Na → CH3–CH3 + 2NaBr.
