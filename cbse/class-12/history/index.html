Sample questions

A few questions from the Class 12 History bank. Every test picks a fresh mix.

  1. Bricks, Beads and Bones
    The first Director-General of the Archaeological Survey of India, often called the father of Indian archaeology, was ______.

    1. Alexander Cunningham
    2. Mortimer Wheeler
    3. John Marshall
    4. R.D. Banerji
    Show answer

    Answer: Alexander Cunningham

    Cunningham, the first Director-General of the ASI, began archaeological excavations in the mid-nineteenth century; he collected Harappan seals but did not realise how old they were.

  2. Kings, Farmers and Towns
    In which year did James Prinsep decipher the Brahmi and Kharosthi scripts?

    1. 1921
    2. 1784
    3. 1857
    4. 1838
    Show answer

    Answer: 1838

    Prinsep's decipherment in 1838 made it possible to read Ashoka's inscriptions and gave a new direction to early Indian history.

  3. Kinship, Caste and Class
    The project to prepare a critical edition of the Mahabharata, begun in 1919, was led by the Sanskritist ______.

    1. D.D. Kosambi
    2. R.G. Bhandarkar
    3. Max Muller
    4. V.S. Sukthankar
    Show answer

    Answer: V.S. Sukthankar

    Under V.S. Sukthankar, scholars compared Sanskrit manuscripts from across India over 47 years to prepare the critical edition.

  4. Thinkers, Beliefs and Buildings
    The Great Stupa at Sanchi is located in which present-day state?

    1. Maharashtra
    2. Bihar
    3. Uttar Pradesh
    4. Madhya Pradesh
    Show answer

    Answer: Madhya Pradesh

    Sanchi, near Bhopal in Madhya Pradesh, survived largely because the Begums of Bhopal helped preserve it.

  5. Through the Eyes of Travellers
    Al-Biruni's book on India, written in Arabic, is called ______.

    1. Ain-i Akbari
    2. Kitab-ul-Hind
    3. Rihla
    4. Akbarnama
    Show answer

    Answer: Kitab-ul-Hind

    Kitab-ul-Hind has 80 chapters on religion, philosophy, festivals, astronomy, customs, laws and more.
