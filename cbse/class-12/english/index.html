Sample questions

A few questions from the Class 12 English bank. Every test picks a fresh mix.

  1. Flamingo: The Last Lesson
    Who is the author of 'The Last Lesson'?

    1. Guy de Maupassant
    2. Alphonse Daudet
    3. Victor Hugo
    4. Anton Chekhov
    Show answer

    Answer: Alphonse Daudet

    'The Last Lesson' was written by the French writer Alphonse Daudet.

  2. Flamingo: Lost Spring
    Who wrote 'Lost Spring: Stories of Stolen Childhood'?

    1. Anees Jung
    2. Louis Fischer
    3. Asokamitran
    4. Tishani Doshi
    Show answer

    Answer: Anees Jung

    'Lost Spring' is an excerpt from Anees Jung's book of the same name.

  3. Flamingo: Deep Water
    Who is the author of 'Deep Water'?

    1. Jack Finney
    2. Louis Fischer
    3. William O. Douglas
    4. Christopher Silvester
    Show answer

    Answer: William O. Douglas

    'Deep Water' is from 'Of Men and Mountains' by William O. Douglas.

  4. Flamingo: The Rattrap
    Who wrote 'The Rattrap'?

    1. Selma Lagerlof
    2. Marga Minco
    3. Zitkala-Sa
    4. Pearl S. Buck
    Show answer

    Answer: Selma Lagerlof

    'The Rattrap' is by the Swedish writer Selma Lagerlöf, the first woman to win the Nobel Prize in Literature.

  5. Flamingo: Indigo
    Who wrote 'Indigo'?

    1. Asokamitran
    2. Louis Fischer
    3. A.R. Barton
    4. Anees Jung
    Show answer

    Answer: Louis Fischer

    'Indigo' is an extract from Louis Fischer's 'The Life of Mahatma Gandhi'.
