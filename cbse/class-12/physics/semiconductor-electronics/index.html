A few questions from this chapter. Open each one to see the answer.

  1. Doping pure silicon with a pentavalent impurity like phosphorus produces a/an ______-type semiconductor.

    1. p-n
    2. p
    3. intrinsic
    4. n
    Show answer

    Answer: n

    The fifth valence electron of the donor atom becomes a free electron, so electrons are the majority carriers.

  2. When a p-n junction diode is forward biased, the width of its depletion layer ______.

    1. increases
    2. remains same
    3. doubles
    4. decreases
    Show answer

    Answer: decreases

    The applied voltage opposes the barrier potential, so the depletion region becomes narrower.

  3. The small current that flows through a reverse-biased p-n junction diode is due to the ______ carriers.

    1. minority
    2. donor
    3. majority
    4. bound
    Show answer

    Answer: minority

    The reverse bias opposes the majority carriers, but minority carriers are swept across, giving a small current of the order of μA.

  4. Pure silicon has intrinsic carrier concentration ni = 1.5 × 10^16 m^-3. It is doped with arsenic so that the electron concentration becomes ne = 4.5 × 10^22 m^-3. Find the hole concentration nh in units of 10^9 m^-3.

    1. 5 × 10^9 m^-3
    2. 3 × 10^9 m^-3
    3. 1.5 × 10^9 m^-3
    4. 45 × 10^9 m^-3
    Show answer

    Answer: 5 × 10^9 m^-3

    By the law of mass action ne × nh = ni^2, so nh = (1.5 × 10^16)^2 / (4.5 × 10^22) = 2.25 × 10^32 / 4.5 × 10^22 = 5 × 10^9 m^-3.

  5. Doping pure silicon with a trivalent impurity such as boron produces a/an ______-type semiconductor.

    1. n
    2. intrinsic
    3. p
    4. n-p
    Show answer

    Answer: p

    Each trivalent atom leaves a hole, so holes become the majority carriers.

  6. In an intrinsic semiconductor, the number of free electrons is ______ the number of holes.

    1. less than
    2. equal to
    3. greater than
    4. twice
    Show answer

    Answer: equal to

    Each electron that breaks free leaves behind one hole, so ne = nh = ni.

  7. An ideal diode is connected in forward bias in series with a 100 Ω resistor across a 5 V battery. Find the current.

    1. 50 mA
    2. 500 mA
    3. 0 mA
    4. 5 mA
    Show answer

    Answer: 50 mA

    An ideal forward-biased diode has zero resistance, so I = 5/100 = 0.05 A = 50 mA.

  8. In an n-type semiconductor, the majority charge carriers are ______.

    1. holes
    2. protons
    3. electrons
    4. positive ions
    Show answer

    Answer: electrons

    Pentavalent donor atoms each give a free electron; holes are the minority carriers.
