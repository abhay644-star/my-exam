A few questions from this chapter. Open each one to see the answer.

  1. The SI unit of power of a lens is the ______.

    1. watt
    2. metre
    3. lux
    4. dioptre
    Show answer

    Answer: dioptre

    P = 1/f with f in metres; 1 dioptre = 1 m^-1.

  2. Find the power of a convex lens of focal length 25 cm.

    1. 0.25 D
    2. 25 D
    3. 4 D
    4. -4 D
    Show answer

    Answer: 4 D

    P = 1/f(m) = 1/0.25 = +4 D.

  3. An object is placed 30 cm in front of a convex lens of focal length 20 cm. At what distance from the lens is the image formed?

    1. 10 cm
    2. 12 cm
    3. 60 cm
    4. 50 cm
    Show answer

    Answer: 60 cm

    1/v = 1/f + 1/u = 1/20 − 1/30 = 1/60, so v = +60 cm (real image on the other side).

  4. For a spherical mirror of small aperture, the focal length is ______ the radius of curvature.

    1. twice
    2. equal to
    3. half
    4. one quarter of
    Show answer

    Answer: half

    f = R/2.

  5. Light travels at 2 × 10^8 m/s in a glass slab. Find the refractive index of the glass (c = 3 × 10^8 m/s).

    1. 1.5
    2. 0.67
    3. 1.33
    4. 2
    Show answer

    Answer: 1.5

    n = c/v = 3/2 = 1.5.

  6. Find the critical angle for a medium of refractive index 2 with respect to air.

    1. 45 °
    2. 90 °
    3. 30 °
    4. 60 °
    Show answer

    Answer: 30 °

    sin C = 1/n = 1/2, so C = 30°.

  7. Total internal reflection can occur only when light travels from an optically ______ medium to an optically rarer medium.

    1. rarer
    2. empty
    3. denser
    4. opaque
    Show answer

    Answer: denser

    The angle of incidence in the denser medium must also be greater than the critical angle.

  8. An object is placed 30 cm in front of a concave mirror of focal length 15 cm. At what distance from the mirror is the image formed?

    1. 60 cm
    2. 10 cm
    3. 30 cm
    4. 15 cm
    Show answer

    Answer: 30 cm

    1/v + 1/u = 1/f with u = −30 cm and f = −15 cm gives 1/v = −1/15 + 1/30 = −1/30, so v = −30 cm: a real, inverted image at the centre of curvature.
