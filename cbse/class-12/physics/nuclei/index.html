  1. Nuclides of the same element having the same atomic number but different mass numbers are called ______.

    1. isobars
    2. isotopes
    3. isomers
    4. isotones
    Show answer

    Answer: isotopes

    Isotopes have the same Z (same number of protons) but different numbers of neutrons, hence different A.

  2. Taking R₀ = 1.2 fm, what is the radius of a nucleus with mass number A = 125?

    1. 1.2 fm
    2. 150 fm
    3. 3.6 fm
    4. 6 fm
    Show answer

    Answer: 6 fm

    R = R₀A^(1/3) = 1.2 × 125^(1/3) = 1.2 × 5 = 6 fm.

  3. The mass defect of a deuteron is 0.00239 u. Taking 1 u = 931.5 MeV/c², what is its binding energy to two decimal places?

    1. 2.23 MeV
    2. 4.45 MeV
    3. 1.11 MeV
    4. 22.3 MeV
    Show answer

    Answer: 2.23 MeV

    B.E. = Δm × 931.5 = 0.00239 × 931.5 ≈ 2.226 MeV ≈ 2.23 MeV.

  4. Mass of ⁴He atom = 4.002603 u, mass of ¹H atom = 1.007825 u, mass of neutron = 1.008665 u. Taking 1 u = 931.5 MeV/c², find the binding energy per nucleon of ⁴He to two decimal places.

    1. 7.07 MeV
    2. 8.75 MeV
    3. 28.3 MeV
    4. 6.5 MeV
    Show answer

    Answer: 7.07 MeV

    Δm = 2(1.007825) + 2(1.008665) − 4.002603 = 0.030377 u; B.E. = 0.030377 × 931.5 ≈ 28.30 MeV; per nucleon = 28.30/4 ≈ 7.07 MeV.

  5. The energy equivalent of 1 atomic mass unit (1 u) is about how many MeV? Give your answer to one decimal place.

    1. 1.7 MeV
    2. 938.3 MeV
    3. 931.5 MeV
    4. 939.6 MeV
    Show answer

    Answer: 931.5 MeV

    E = mc² for 1 u = 1.6605 × 10^-27 kg gives about 931.5 MeV.

  6. The nuclear force between nucleons is very strong but ______ range, becoming negligible beyond a few femtometres.

    1. medium
    2. short
    3. long
    4. infinite
    Show answer

    Answer: short

    Nuclear force is a short-range force, effective only up to a few fm, unlike gravitational and Coulomb forces.

  7. In the fission reaction ²³⁵U + n → ¹⁴¹Ba + ⁹²Kr + x n, what is the value of x?

    1. 2
    2. 3
    3. 4
    4. 1
    Show answer

    Answer: 3

    Mass number balance: 235 + 1 = 141 + 92 + x, so x = 3 (charge also balances: 92 = 56 + 36).

  8. What is the ratio of the nuclear density of ²⁷Al to that of ¹²⁵Te?

    1. 0.6
    2. 0.216
    3. 1
    4. 4.63
    Show answer

    Answer: 1

    Since R ∝ A^(1/3), nuclear volume ∝ A and mass ∝ A, so nuclear density is independent of A; the ratio is 1.
