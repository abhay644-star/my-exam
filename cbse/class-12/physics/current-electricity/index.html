A few questions from this chapter. Open each one to see the answer.

  1. The SI unit of resistivity is ______.

    1. ohm
    2. siemens
    3. ohm metre
    4. ohm per metre
    Show answer

    Answer: ohm metre

    From ρ = RA/l, the unit is Ω × m^2 / m = Ω m.

  2. Three resistors of 6 Ω each are connected in parallel. Find the equivalent resistance.

    1. 18 Ω
    2. 6 Ω
    3. 2 Ω
    4. 3 Ω
    Show answer

    Answer: 2 Ω

    For n equal resistors in parallel, R = 6/3 = 2 Ω.

  3. A wire is stretched uniformly to twice its original length. Its resistance becomes how many times the original?

    1. 8
    2. 0.5
    3. 2
    4. 4
    Show answer

    Answer: 4

    Volume is constant, so doubling l halves A; R = ρl/A becomes 2 × 2 = 4 times.

  4. In a metre bridge, an unknown resistance X is in the left gap and 6 Ω in the right gap. The balance point is 40 cm from the left end. Find X.

    1. 6 Ω
    2. 4 Ω
    3. 2.4 Ω
    4. 9 Ω
    Show answer

    Answer: 4 Ω

    At balance X/6 = 40/60, so X = 4 Ω.

  5. The reciprocal of resistivity is called ______.

    1. conductance
    2. mobility
    3. conductivity
    4. resistance
    Show answer

    Answer: conductivity

    σ = 1/ρ, measured in S/m (or Ω^-1 m^-1).

  6. A steady current of 0.5 A flows through a wire for 2 minutes. How much charge passes through it?

    1. 1 C
    2. 30 C
    3. 120 C
    4. 60 C
    Show answer

    Answer: 60 C

    Q = It = 0.5 × 120 = 60 C.

  7. A cell of emf 12 V and internal resistance 1 Ω is connected to an external resistor of 5 Ω. Find the current.

    1. 2.4 A
    2. 12 A
    3. 1.5 A
    4. 2 A
    Show answer

    Answer: 2 A

    I = E/(R + r) = 12/(5 + 1) = 2 A.

  8. Kirchhoff's junction rule is based on the conservation of ______.

    1. mass
    2. momentum
    3. energy
    4. charge
    Show answer

    Answer: charge

    Charge does not pile up at a junction, so the current entering equals the current leaving.
