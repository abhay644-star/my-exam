A few questions from this chapter. Open each one to see the answer.

  1. The SI unit of self-inductance is the ______.

    1. henry
    2. tesla
    3. farad
    4. weber
    Show answer

    Answer: henry

    1 henry = 1 V s A^-1 = 1 Wb A^-1.

  2. The magnetic flux through a single-turn coil falls from 0.5 Wb to 0.1 Wb in 0.2 s. Find the magnitude of the induced emf.

    1. 0.08 V
    2. 2 V
    3. 3 V
    4. 0.4 V
    Show answer

    Answer: 2 V

    |ε| = Δφ/Δt = 0.4/0.2 = 2 V.

  3. How much energy is stored in an inductor of 2 H carrying a steady current of 3 A?

    1. 9 J
    2. 6 J
    3. 3 J
    4. 18 J
    Show answer

    Answer: 9 J

    U = ½LI^2 = ½ × 2 × 9 = 9 J.

  4. The direction of the induced current is given by ______ law.

    1. Lenz's
    2. Faraday's
    3. Ampere's
    4. Coulomb's
    Show answer

    Answer: Lenz's

    Lenz's law: the induced current opposes the change in flux that produces it, a consequence of energy conservation.

  5. A 1 m long metal rod moves at 5 m/s perpendicular to a uniform magnetic field of 0.2 T. Find the motional emf across its ends.

    1. 25 V
    2. 1 V
    3. 10 V
    4. 0.04 V
    Show answer

    Answer: 1 V

    ε = Blv = 0.2 × 1 × 5 = 1 V.

  6. A coil of 100 turns, each of area 0.02 m^2, rotates at 100 rad/s in a uniform magnetic field of 0.5 T, about an axis perpendicular to the field. Find the peak emf.

    1. 1000 V
    2. 50 V
    3. 100 V
    4. 10 V
    Show answer

    Answer: 100 V

    ε0 = NBAω = 100 × 0.5 × 0.02 × 100 = 100 V.

  7. The SI unit of magnetic flux is the ______.

    1. tesla
    2. henry
    3. weber
    4. gauss
    Show answer

    Answer: weber

    1 weber = 1 T m^2.

  8. A coil of area 0.5 m^2 is placed with its plane perpendicular to a uniform magnetic field of 0.4 T. Find the magnetic flux through it.

    1. 0.8 Wb
    2. 0.1 Wb
    3. 0.2 Wb
    4. 0 Wb
    Show answer

    Answer: 0.2 Wb

    The field is along the normal, so φ = BA = 0.4 × 0.5 = 0.2 Wb.
