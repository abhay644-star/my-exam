A few questions from this chapter. Open each one to see the answer.

  1. The SI unit of electric charge is the ______.

    1. ampere
    2. volt
    3. farad
    4. coulomb
    Show answer

    Answer: coulomb

    Charge is measured in coulombs; 1 C = 1 A × 1 s.

  2. Two point charges of 1 μC each are placed 0.3 m apart in vacuum. Find the force between them (k = 9 × 10^9 N m^2 C^-2).

    1. 1 N
    2. 0.3 N
    3. 0.1 N
    4. 0.03 N
    Show answer

    Answer: 0.1 N

    F = kq1q2/r^2 = 9 × 10^9 × 10^-12 / 0.09 = 0.1 N.

  3. A charge of 8.85 × 10^-12 C is placed inside a closed cube. Find the total electric flux through the cube (ε0 = 8.85 × 10^-12 C^2 N^-1 m^-2).

    1. 0.167 N m^2/C
    2. 6 N m^2/C
    3. 1 N m^2/C
    4. 8.85 N m^2/C
    Show answer

    Answer: 1 N m^2/C

    By Gauss's law, total flux = q/ε0 = 1 N m^2/C, whatever the shape of the closed surface.

  4. Electric field lines start from ______ charges and end on negative charges.

    1. induced
    2. positive
    3. neutral
    4. negative
    Show answer

    Answer: positive

    By convention, field lines originate on positive charges and terminate on negative charges.

  5. How many electrons make up a charge of 3.2 × 10^-18 C? (e = 1.6 × 10^-19 C)

    1. 5
    2. 20
    3. 200
    4. 2
    Show answer

    Answer: 20

    By quantisation of charge, n = q/e = 3.2 × 10^-18 / 1.6 × 10^-19 = 20.

  6. Two point charges repel each other with a force F. If each charge is doubled and the distance between them is halved, the force becomes how many times F?

    1. 8
    2. 16
    3. 1
    4. 4
    Show answer

    Answer: 16

    F ∝ q1q2/r^2, so the force changes by (2 × 2)/(1/2)^2 = 4/0.25 = 16 times.

  7. By Gauss's law, the total electric flux through a closed surface depends only on the ______ enclosed by it.

    1. charge
    2. size of the surface
    3. charge outside the surface
    4. shape of the surface
    Show answer

    Answer: charge

    Total flux = qenclosed/ε0; charges outside the surface contribute zero net flux.

  8. Find the magnitude of the electric field at a distance of 0.3 m from a point charge of 1 × 10^-8 C in vacuum (k = 9 × 10^9 N m^2 C^-2).

    1. 300 N/C
    2. 3000 N/C
    3. 100 N/C
    4. 1000 N/C
    Show answer

    Answer: 1000 N/C

    E = kq/r^2 = 9 × 10^9 × 10^-8 / 0.09 = 90/0.09 = 1000 N/C.
