  1. The SI unit of magnetic field is the ______.

    1. gauss
    2. tesla
    3. weber
    4. henry
    Show answer

    Answer: tesla

    1 tesla = 1 N A^-1 m^-1; gauss is a CGS unit.

  2. A charge of 2 μC moves at 10^5 m/s perpendicular to a magnetic field of 0.5 T. Find the magnetic force on it.

    1. 1 N
    2. 0.01 N
    3. 0.1 N
    4. 0.2 N
    Show answer

    Answer: 0.1 N

    F = qvB = 2 × 10^-6 × 10^5 × 0.5 = 0.1 N.

  3. A galvanometer of resistance 10 Ω gives full-scale deflection for 1 mA. What resistance must be connected in series to convert it into a voltmeter of range 10 V?

    1. 10 Ω
    2. 9990 Ω
    3. 990 Ω
    4. 10000 Ω
    Show answer

    Answer: 9990 Ω

    R = V/Ig − G = 10/0.001 − 10 = 10000 − 10 = 9990 Ω.

  4. The magnetic field produced by a small current element is given by the ______ law.

    1. Coulomb's
    2. Biot-Savart
    3. Ampere's circuital
    4. Faraday's
    Show answer

    Answer: Biot-Savart

    dB = (μ0/4π) I dl sin θ / r^2.

  5. A straight wire 2 m long carries 3 A at right angles to a uniform magnetic field of 0.5 T. Find the force on it.

    1. 1.5 N
    2. 12 N
    3. 0.75 N
    4. 3 N
    Show answer

    Answer: 3 N

    F = BIl = 0.5 × 3 × 2 = 3 N.

  6. A proton and an alpha particle enter a uniform magnetic field at right angles to it with the same speed. What is the ratio of the radius of the alpha particle's path to that of the proton's?

    1. 1
    2. 0.5
    3. 2
    4. 4
    Show answer

    Answer: 2

    r = mv/(qB). The alpha particle has 4 times the mass and 2 times the charge, so its radius is 4/2 = 2 times larger.

  7. Two long parallel wires carrying currents in the same direction ______ each other.

    1. rotate
    2. repel
    3. attract
    4. do not affect
    Show answer

    Answer: attract

    Parallel currents attract and antiparallel currents repel.

  8. Find the magnetic field at a distance of 10 cm from a long straight wire carrying a current of 10 A (μ0 = 4π × 10^-7 T m/A). Give your answer in μT.

    1. 200 μT
    2. 20 μT
    3. 40 μT
    4. 10 μT
    Show answer

    Answer: 20 μT

    B = μ0I/(2πr) = 2 × 10^-7 × 10 / 0.1 = 2 × 10^-5 T = 20 μT.
