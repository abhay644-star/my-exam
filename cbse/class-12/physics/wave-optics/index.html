A few questions from this chapter. Open each one to see the answer.

  1. Two sources of light that maintain a constant phase difference with each other are called ______ sources.

    1. incoherent
    2. point
    3. coherent
    4. extended
    Show answer

    Answer: coherent

    Only coherent sources give a sustained (stationary) interference pattern, which is why both slits in Young's experiment are lit from a single source.

  2. For constructive interference of two coherent waves in phase at the source, the path difference must be an integral multiple of ______.

    1. 3λ/2
    2. λ/2
    3. λ
    4. λ/4
    Show answer

    Answer: λ

    A path difference of nλ brings crests on crests; an odd multiple of λ/2 gives destructive interference.

  3. In Young's double-slit experiment, λ = 600 nm, slit separation d = 1 mm and screen distance D = 1 m. Find the fringe width in mm.

    1. 0.06 mm
    2. 1.2 mm
    3. 6 mm
    4. 0.6 mm
    Show answer

    Answer: 0.6 mm

    β = λD/d = 600 × 10^-9 × 1 / 10^-3 = 6 × 10^-4 m = 0.6 mm.

  4. Light of wavelength 500 nm falls normally on a single slit of width 0.2 mm, and the diffraction pattern is seen on a screen 2 m away. Find the width of the central maximum in mm.

    1. 10 mm
    2. 5 mm
    3. 1 mm
    4. 20 mm
    Show answer

    Answer: 10 mm

    Width of central maximum = 2λD/a = 2 × 500 × 10^-9 × 2 / (0.2 × 10^-3) = 0.01 m = 10 mm.

  5. By Huygens' principle, each point on a wavefront acts as a source of ______ wavelets.

    1. reflected
    2. secondary
    3. primary
    4. tertiary
    Show answer

    Answer: secondary

    The forward envelope of the secondary wavelets gives the new position of the wavefront.

  6. In Young's double-slit experiment, the separation between the slits is doubled, keeping everything else the same. The fringe width becomes how many times the original?

    1. 0.25
    2. 4
    3. 0.5
    4. 2
    Show answer

    Answer: 0.5

    β = λD/d, so doubling d halves β.

  7. In Young's double-slit experiment, the slits are 1 mm apart and the screen is 1 m away. The fringe width is 0.5 mm. Find the wavelength of the light.

    1. 50 nm
    2. 250 nm
    3. 500 nm
    4. 5000 nm
    Show answer

    Answer: 500 nm

    λ = βd/D = 0.5 × 10^-3 × 10^-3 / 1 = 5 × 10^-7 m = 500 nm.

  8. The locus of points that oscillate in phase is called a ______.

    1. node
    2. wavefront
    3. ray
    4. fringe
    Show answer

    Answer: wavefront

    Rays are perpendicular to the wavefronts and show the direction of travel of the energy.
