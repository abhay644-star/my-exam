A few questions from this chapter. Open each one to see the answer.

  1. The emission of electrons from a metal surface when light of suitable frequency falls on it is called the ______.

    1. photoelectric effect
    2. field emission
    3. Compton effect
    4. thermionic emission
    Show answer

    Answer: photoelectric effect

    Hertz and Lenard observed it; Einstein explained it using photons.

  2. Photons of energy 5 eV fall on a metal of work function 2 eV. Find the maximum kinetic energy of the emitted electrons.

    1. 10 eV
    2. 2.5 eV
    3. 3 eV
    4. 7 eV
    Show answer

    Answer: 3 eV

    By Einstein's equation, Kmax = hν − φ0 = 5 − 2 = 3 eV.

  3. An electron is accelerated from rest through 100 V. Using λ = 1.227/√V nm, find its de Broglie wavelength.

    1. 12.27 nm
    2. 0.123 nm
    3. 0.0123 nm
    4. 1.227 nm
    Show answer

    Answer: 0.123 nm

    λ = 1.227/√100 = 0.1227 nm ≈ 0.123 nm.

  4. The minimum energy needed by an electron to escape from a metal surface is called the ______ of the metal.

    1. threshold frequency
    2. ionisation energy
    3. work function
    4. stopping potential
    Show answer

    Answer: work function

    It is written φ0 and is usually given in electronvolts.

  5. The maximum kinetic energy of photoelectrons depends on the ______ of the incident light but not on its intensity.

    1. intensity
    2. duration
    3. frequency
    4. brightness
    Show answer

    Answer: frequency

    Kmax = hν − φ0, which depends only on ν for a given metal.

  6. Light of wavelength 248 nm falls on a metal of work function 3 eV. Using E(eV) = 1240/λ(nm), find the stopping potential.

    1. 3 V
    2. 8 V
    3. 5 V
    4. 2 V
    Show answer

    Answer: 2 V

    Photon energy = 1240/248 = 5 eV, so Kmax = 5 − 3 = 2 eV and the stopping potential is 2 V.

  7. The minimum frequency of incident light below which no photoelectric emission occurs is called the ______ frequency.

    1. natural
    2. critical
    3. resonant
    4. threshold
    Show answer

    Answer: threshold

    Below the threshold frequency ν0 = φ0/h, no electrons are emitted however intense the light is.

  8. Using E(eV) = 1240/λ(nm), find the energy of a photon of wavelength 400 nm.

    1. 3.1 eV
    2. 0.31 eV
    3. 4 eV
    4. 31 eV
    Show answer

    Answer: 3.1 eV

    E = 1240/400 = 3.1 eV.
