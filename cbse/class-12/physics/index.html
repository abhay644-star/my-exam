Sample questions

A few questions from the Class 12 Physics bank. Every test picks a fresh mix.

  1. Electric Charges and Fields
    The SI unit of electric charge is the ______.

    1. ampere
    2. volt
    3. farad
    4. coulomb
    Show answer

    Answer: coulomb

    Charge is measured in coulombs; 1 C = 1 A × 1 s.

  2. Electrostatic Potential and Capacitance
    The SI unit of capacitance is the ______.

    1. ohm
    2. henry
    3. tesla
    4. farad
    Show answer

    Answer: farad

    1 farad = 1 coulomb per volt.

  3. Current Electricity
    The SI unit of resistivity is ______.

    1. ohm
    2. siemens
    3. ohm metre
    4. ohm per metre
    Show answer

    Answer: ohm metre

    From ρ = RA/l, the unit is Ω × m^2 / m = Ω m.

  4. Moving Charges and Magnetism
    The SI unit of magnetic field is the ______.

    1. gauss
    2. tesla
    3. weber
    4. henry
    Show answer

    Answer: tesla

    1 tesla = 1 N A^-1 m^-1; gauss is a CGS unit.

  5. Magnetism and Matter
    Unlike electric charges, isolated magnetic ______ do not exist.

    1. domains
    2. fields
    3. poles
    4. dipoles
    Show answer

    Answer: poles

    A magnet always has a north and a south pole together; cutting a magnet gives two smaller magnets, each with both poles.
