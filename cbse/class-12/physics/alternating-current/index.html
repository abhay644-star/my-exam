A few questions from this chapter. Open each one to see the answer.

  1. The frequency of the domestic AC supply in India is ______ Hz.

    1. 60 Hz
    2. 220 Hz
    3. 100 Hz
    4. 50 Hz
    Show answer

    Answer: 50 Hz

    Indian mains supply is 220 V rms at 50 Hz.

  2. An ideal step-up transformer has 100 turns in the primary and 500 turns in the secondary. If the input is 220 V, what is the output voltage?

    1. 1100 V
    2. 500 V
    3. 44 V
    4. 220 V
    Show answer

    Answer: 1100 V

    Vs/Vp = Ns/Np = 5, so Vs = 5 × 220 = 1100 V.

  3. An inductor of inductance (0.5/π) H is connected to a 50 Hz AC supply. Find its inductive reactance.

    1. 100 Ω
    2. 50 Ω
    3. 157 Ω
    4. 25 Ω
    Show answer

    Answer: 50 Ω

    XL = 2πfL = 2π × 50 × 0.5/π = 50 Ω.

  4. In a purely inductive AC circuit, the current ______ the voltage by 90°.

    1. is in phase with
    2. lags
    3. leads
    4. is opposite to
    Show answer

    Answer: lags

    v = vm sin ωt gives i = im sin(ωt − π/2), so the current lags by a quarter cycle.

  5. What is the power factor of a purely resistive AC circuit?

    1. 0
    2. 0.5
    3. 1
    4. 0.707
    Show answer

    Answer: 1

    In a pure resistor, voltage and current are in phase, so φ = 0 and cos φ = 1.

  6. Find the resonant angular frequency of an LC circuit with L = 0.1 H and C = 10 μF.

    1. 1000 rad/s
    2. 100 rad/s
    3. 10000 rad/s
    4. 316 rad/s
    Show answer

    Answer: 1000 rad/s

    ω0 = 1/√(LC) = 1/√(0.1 × 10^-5) = 1/√(10^-6) = 1000 rad/s.

  7. A device that changes an alternating voltage from one value to another is called a ______.

    1. transformer
    2. galvanometer
    3. motor
    4. rectifier
    Show answer

    Answer: transformer

    A transformer works on mutual induction; it does not work with steady DC.

  8. An alternating current has a peak value of 10√2 A. What is its rms value?

    1. 10 A
    2. 14.1 A
    3. 20 A
    4. 5 A
    Show answer

    Answer: 10 A

    Irms = Im/√2 = 10√2/√2 = 10 A.
