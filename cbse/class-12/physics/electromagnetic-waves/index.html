A few questions from this chapter. Open each one to see the answer.

  1. Among electromagnetic waves, the ones with the shortest wavelength are ______.

    1. gamma rays
    2. radio waves
    3. ultraviolet rays
    4. X-rays
    Show answer

    Answer: gamma rays

    Gamma rays, emitted by nuclei, have wavelengths below about 10^-12 m.

  2. An FM radio wave has a wavelength of 3 m in vacuum. What is its frequency in MHz? (c = 3 × 10^8 m/s)

    1. 100 MHz
    2. 10 MHz
    3. 900 MHz
    4. 1 MHz
    Show answer

    Answer: 100 MHz

    f = c/λ = 3 × 10^8 / 3 = 10^8 Hz = 100 MHz.

  3. Find the wavelength in vacuum of an electromagnetic wave of frequency 6 × 10^14 Hz (c = 3 × 10^8 m/s).

    1. 50 nm
    2. 5000 nm
    3. 200 nm
    4. 500 nm
    Show answer

    Answer: 500 nm

    λ = c/f = 3 × 10^8 / 6 × 10^14 = 5 × 10^-7 m = 500 nm, which is visible light.

  4. The concept of displacement current was introduced by ______.

    1. Faraday
    2. Ampere
    3. Maxwell
    4. Hertz
    Show answer

    Answer: Maxwell

    Maxwell added displacement current to Ampere's law, which led to the prediction of electromagnetic waves.

  5. Electromagnetic waves are produced by ______ charges.

    1. stationary
    2. neutral
    3. uniformly moving
    4. accelerated
    Show answer

    Answer: accelerated

    A charge at rest produces only an electric field and a steady current only a steady magnetic field; an accelerated charge radiates.

  6. Which of these has the longest wavelength: X-rays, microwaves, ultraviolet rays or infrared rays?

    1. infrared rays
    2. ultraviolet rays
    3. microwaves
    4. X-rays
    Show answer

    Answer: microwaves

    In order of increasing wavelength: X-rays, ultraviolet, (visible), infrared, microwaves.

  7. Electromagnetic waves are ______ waves.

    1. stationary
    2. longitudinal
    3. transverse
    4. mechanical
    Show answer

    Answer: transverse

    The electric and magnetic fields oscillate at right angles to the direction of travel.

  8. The ozone layer in the atmosphere absorbs most of the Sun's harmful ______ radiation.

    1. ultraviolet
    2. infrared
    3. radio
    4. visible
    Show answer

    Answer: ultraviolet

    UV radiation can harm living cells, so the ozone layer protects life on the Earth.
