  1. Unlike electric charges, isolated magnetic ______ do not exist.

    1. domains
    2. fields
    3. poles
    4. dipoles
    Show answer

    Answer: poles

    A magnet always has a north and a south pole together; cutting a magnet gives two smaller magnets, each with both poles.

  2. The magnetic susceptibility of a diamagnetic substance is ______.

    1. small and positive
    2. exactly zero
    3. large and positive
    4. small and negative
    Show answer

    Answer: small and negative

    For diamagnetic materials −1 ≤ χ < 0; for paramagnetic materials χ is small and positive; for ferromagnetic materials it is large and positive.

  3. A bar magnet of magnetic moment 1.5 J/T lies along a uniform magnetic field of 0.22 T. How much work is needed to turn it so that its moment points opposite to the field?

    1. 0.165 J
    2. 0 J
    3. 0.33 J
    4. 0.66 J
    Show answer

    Answer: 0.66 J

    W = mB(cos 0° − cos 180°) = 2mB = 2 × 1.5 × 0.22 = 0.66 J.

  4. The net magnetic flux through any closed surface is ______.

    1. equal to the enclosed pole strength
    2. μ0 times the enclosed current
    3. 1 Wb
    4. 0
    Show answer

    Answer: 0

    Gauss's law for magnetism: as many magnetic field lines leave a closed surface as enter it, because isolated magnetic poles do not exist.

  5. A material has a relative permeability of 1.002. What is its magnetic susceptibility?

    1. 1.002
    2. -0.002
    3. 0.002
    4. 2.002
    Show answer

    Answer: 0.002

    μr = 1 + χ, so χ = 1.002 − 1 = 0.002. The value is small and positive, so the material is paramagnetic.

  6. Find the magnetic field on the axis of a short bar magnet of moment 0.4 A m^2, at 0.2 m from its centre (μ0/4π = 10^-7 T m A^-1).

    1. 20 μT
    2. 5 μT
    3. 10 μT
    4. 2.5 μT
    Show answer

    Answer: 10 μT

    On the axis B = (μ0/4π)(2m/r^3) = 10^-7 × 0.8 / 0.008 = 10^-5 T = 10 μT. On the equatorial line it would be half of this.

  7. A substance that is weakly repelled by a magnet is called ______.

    1. non-magnetic
    2. ferromagnetic
    3. diamagnetic
    4. paramagnetic
    Show answer

    Answer: diamagnetic

    Diamagnetic substances, such as bismuth, copper and water, have a small negative susceptibility and move from stronger to weaker parts of a field.

  8. A bar magnet of magnetic moment 2 A m^2 is placed in a uniform magnetic field of 0.5 T with its axis at 30° to the field. Find the torque on it.

    1. 0.25 N m
    2. 0.87 N m
    3. 1 N m
    4. 0.5 N m
    Show answer

    Answer: 0.5 N m

    τ = mB sin θ = 2 × 0.5 × sin 30° = 0.5 N m.
