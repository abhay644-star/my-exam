A few questions from this chapter. Open each one to see the answer.

  1. The SI unit of capacitance is the ______.

    1. ohm
    2. henry
    3. tesla
    4. farad
    Show answer

    Answer: farad

    1 farad = 1 coulomb per volt.

  2. How much work is done in moving a charge of 2 C across a potential difference of 5 V?

    1. 2.5 J
    2. 0.4 J
    3. 7 J
    4. 10 J
    Show answer

    Answer: 10 J

    W = qV = 2 × 5 = 10 J.

  3. Capacitors of 2 μF and 3 μF are connected in series. Find the equivalent capacitance.

    1. 1.2 μF
    2. 6 μF
    3. 0.83 μF
    4. 5 μF
    Show answer

    Answer: 1.2 μF

    1/C = 1/2 + 1/3 = 5/6, so C = 6/5 = 1.2 μF.

  4. The SI unit of electric potential is the ______.

    1. joule
    2. volt
    3. farad
    4. coulomb
    Show answer

    Answer: volt

    1 volt = 1 joule per coulomb.

  5. A 10 μF capacitor is charged to 100 V. How much energy is stored in it?

    1. 0.5 J
    2. 0.1 J
    3. 0.001 J
    4. 0.05 J
    Show answer

    Answer: 0.05 J

    U = ½CV^2 = ½ × 10 × 10^-6 × 10^4 = 0.05 J.

  6. Find the electrostatic potential energy of a system of two point charges of 2 μC and 3 μC placed 0.6 m apart in vacuum (k = 9 × 10^9 N m^2 C^-2).

    1. 0.15 J
    2. 0.9 J
    3. 0.054 J
    4. 0.09 J
    Show answer

    Answer: 0.09 J

    U = kq1q2/r = 9 × 10^9 × 6 × 10^-12 / 0.6 = 0.054/0.6 = 0.09 J.

  7. A surface on which the electric potential has the same value at every point is called an ______ surface.

    1. neutral
    2. equipotential
    3. insulating
    4. Gaussian
    Show answer

    Answer: equipotential

    No work is done in moving a charge along an equipotential surface, and the field is normal to it.

  8. Find the electric potential at a distance of 0.3 m from a point charge of 3 × 10^-9 C in vacuum (k = 9 × 10^9 N m^2 C^-2).

    1. 90 V
    2. 900 V
    3. 30 V
    4. 270 V
    Show answer

    Answer: 90 V

    V = kq/r = 9 × 10^9 × 3 × 10^-9 / 0.3 = 27/0.3 = 90 V.
