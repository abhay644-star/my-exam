Sample questions

A few questions from the Class 12 Geography bank. Every test picks a fresh mix.

  1. Human Geography: Nature and Scope
    The school of thought which holds that human beings are controlled by their natural environment is called environmental ______.

    1. determinism
    2. humanism
    3. possibilism
    4. neo-determinism
    Show answer

    Answer: determinism

    Environmental determinism sees nature as all-powerful; early humans adapted to and depended on nature.

  2. The World Population
    The number of people living per unit area of land is called ______.

    1. birth rate
    2. growth rate
    3. sex ratio
    4. population density
    Show answer

    Answer: population density

    Population density = population divided by area, usually given as persons per sq km.

  3. Human Development
    Which organisation has published the Human Development Report every year since 1990?

    1. UNDP
    2. UNESCO
    3. World Bank
    4. IMF
    Show answer

    Answer: UNDP

    The UNDP publishes the Human Development Report, which ranks countries by their Human Development Index.

  4. Primary Activities
    Shifting cultivation is called ______ in north-eastern India.

    1. milpa
    2. ladang
    3. roca
    4. jhuming
    Show answer

    Answer: jhuming

    Shifting cultivation is called jhuming in north-east India, milpa in Central America and ladang in Malaysia.

  5. Secondary Activities
    The cotton textile industry, which uses raw material from farms, is an example of a(n) ______ industry.

    1. mineral-based
    2. marine-based
    3. agro-based
    4. forest-based
    Show answer

    Answer: agro-based

    Agro-based industries process farm products such as cotton, sugarcane and fruits into finished goods.
