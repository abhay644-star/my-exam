  1. The corner points of the feasible region of an LPP are (0, 0), (4, 0), (3, 6), (0, 10). The objective function is Z = 2x + 6y.

    Show answer and solution
    • (a) Find the maximum value of Z. 60
    • (b) Find the x-coordinate of the corner point where Z is maximum. 0

    Z at the corners: (0, 0) → 0; (4, 0) → 8; (3, 6) → 42; (0, 10) → 60. The maximum is 60 at (0, 10).

  2. The corner points of the feasible region of an LPP are (0, 0), (9, 0), (6, 6), (0, 4). The objective function is Z = 6x + 7y.
