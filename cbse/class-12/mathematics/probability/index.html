  1. P(A) = 1/6, P(B) = 1/4 and P(A ∩ B) = 1/6. Find P(A | B).

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 2/3

    P(A | B) = P(A ∩ B) ÷ P(B) = 2/3 = 2/3

  2. Bag I has 4 red and 2 black balls; bag II has 4 red and 4 black balls. A bag is chosen at random and one ball is drawn from it.

    Show answer and solution
    • (a) Find the probability that the ball is red. 7/12
    • (b) The ball is red. Find the probability that it came from bag I. 4/7
    • (c) Find the probability that the ball is black. 5/12

    (a) ½ × 2/3 + ½ × 1/2 = 7/12. (b) By Bayes' theorem: (½ × 2/3) ÷ 7/12 = 4/7. (c) 1 − 7/12 = 5/12.

  3. P(A) = 1/6, P(B) = 2/3 and P(A ∩ B) = 1/6. Find P(A | B).

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 1/4

    P(A | B) = P(A ∩ B) ÷ P(B) = 2/8 = 1/4

  4. Bag I has 6 red and 6 black balls; bag II has 4 red and 5 black balls. A bag is chosen at random and one ball is drawn from it.

    Show answer and solution
    • (a) Find the probability that the ball is red. 17/36
    • (b) The ball is red. Find the probability that it came from bag I. 9/17
    • (c) Find the probability that the ball is black. 19/36

    (a) ½ × 1/2 + ½ × 4/9 = 17/36. (b) By Bayes' theorem: (½ × 1/2) ÷ 17/36 = 9/17. (c) 1 − 17/36 = 19/36.

  5. P(A) = 3/4, P(B) = 5/12 and P(A ∩ B) = 1/3. Find P(A | B).

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 4/5

    P(A | B) = P(A ∩ B) ÷ P(B) = 4/5 = 4/5

  6. Bag I has 4 red and 3 black balls; bag II has 4 red and 6 black balls. A bag is chosen at random and one ball is drawn from it.

    Show answer and solution
    • (a) Find the probability that the ball is red. 17/35
    • (b) The ball is red. Find the probability that it came from bag I. 10/17
    • (c) Find the probability that the ball is black. 18/35

    (a) ½ × 4/7 + ½ × 2/5 = 17/35. (b) By Bayes' theorem: (½ × 4/7) ÷ 17/35 = 10/17. (c) 1 − 17/35 = 18/35.
