A few questions from this chapter. Open each one to see the answer.

  1. f(x) = −3x² + 36x − 106.

    Show answer and solution
    • (a) Find the value of x at which f is maximum. 6
    • (b) Find the maximum value of f. 2

    f′(x) = −6x + 36 = 0 gives x = 6; f″(x) = -6 < 0, so the maximum value is f(6) = 2.

  2. The radius of a circular ink blot increases at 4 cm/s.

    Show answer and solution
    • (a) How fast is the area increasing when the radius is 12 cm (cm²/s)? You may leave π in your answer. 96π cm²/s
    • (b) How fast is the circumference increasing (cm/s)? You may leave π in your answer. 8π cm/s
    • (c) What is the area when the radius is 12 cm (cm²)? You may leave π in your answer. 144π cm²

    (a) dA/dt = 2πr × dr/dt = 2π × 12 × 4 = 96π cm²/s. (b) dC/dt = 2π × dr/dt = 8π cm/s. (c) A = π × 12² = 144π cm².

  3. Consider f(x) = x³ − 3x² − 24x − 5.

    Show answer and solution
    • (a) At what value of x does f have a local maximum? −2
    • (b) Find the local maximum value. 23
    • (c) Find the local minimum value. −85

    f′(x) = 3x² − 6x − 24 = 3(x + 2)(x − 4). f′ changes from + to − at x = −2 (local maximum 23) and from − to + at x = 4 (local minimum −85).

  4. f(x) = −x² + 2x + 4.

    Show answer and solution
    • (a) Find the value of x at which f is maximum. 1
    • (b) Find the maximum value of f. 5

    f′(x) = −2x + 2 = 0 gives x = 1; f″(x) = -2 < 0, so the maximum value is f(1) = 5.

  5. The radius of a circular ink blot increases at 3 cm/s.

    Show answer and solution
    • (a) How fast is the area increasing when the radius is 4 cm (cm²/s)? You may leave π in your answer. 24π cm²/s
    • (b) How fast is the circumference increasing (cm/s)? You may leave π in your answer. 6π cm/s
    • (c) What is the area when the radius is 4 cm (cm²)? You may leave π in your answer. 16π cm²

    (a) dA/dt = 2πr × dr/dt = 2π × 4 × 3 = 24π cm²/s. (b) dC/dt = 2π × dr/dt = 6π cm/s. (c) A = π × 4² = 16π cm².

  6. Consider f(x) = x³ − 3x² − 45x + 7.

    Show answer and solution
    • (a) At what value of x does f have a local maximum? −3
    • (b) Find the local maximum value. 88
    • (c) Find the local minimum value. −168

    f′(x) = 3x² − 6x − 45 = 3(x + 3)(x − 5). f′ changes from + to − at x = −3 (local maximum 88) and from − to + at x = 5 (local minimum −168).
