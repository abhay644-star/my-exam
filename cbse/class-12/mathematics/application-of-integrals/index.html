  1. Consider the curve y = 3x².

    Show answer and solution
    • (a) Find the area bounded by the curve, the x-axis and the line x = 3. 27
    • (b) Find the area enclosed between the curve and the line y = 9x. 27/2

    (a) ∫03 3x² dx = 3 × 3³ / 3 = 27. (b) The curves meet at x = 0 and x = 3: ∫03 (9x − 3x²) dx = 27/2.

  4. Consider the curve y = x².
