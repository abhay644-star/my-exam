A few questions from this chapter. Open each one to see the answer.

  1. P = (4, −4, 2) and Q = (3, −6, 0).

    Show answer and solution
    • (a) Find the distance PQ. 3
    • (b) Find the z-coordinate of the mid-point of PQ. 1

    (a) √(1² + 2² + 2²) = 3. (b) (2 + 0) ÷ 2 = 1.

  2. P = (−3, 4, −3) and Q = (−7, 8, −10).

    Show answer and solution
    • (a) Find the distance PQ. 9
    • (b) Find the z-coordinate of the mid-point of PQ. −6.5

    (a) √(4² + 4² + 7²) = 9. (b) (−3 + (−10)) ÷ 2 = −6.5.

  3. P = (0, −1, 3) and Q = (−2, 2, 9).
