Sample questions

A few questions from the Class 12 Accountancy bank. Every test picks a fresh mix.

  1. Accounting for Partnership: Basic Concepts
    In the absence of a partnership deed, partners share profits and losses ______.

    1. in the ratio of drawings
    2. in the ratio of experience
    3. in capital ratio
    4. equally
    Show answer

    Answer: equally

    Under the Indian Partnership Act, 1932, if there is no deed, profits and losses are shared equally.

  2. Goodwill: Nature and Valuation
    Goodwill is a(n) ______ asset because it cannot be seen or touched.

    1. intangible
    2. current
    3. fictitious
    4. liquid
    Show answer

    Answer: intangible

    Goodwill is an intangible but not a fictitious asset; it has a realisable value when the business is sold.

  3. Change in Profit Sharing Ratio
    The ratio in which partners agree to give up their share of profit in favour of another partner is called the ______ ratio.

    1. old
    2. gaining
    3. sacrificing
    4. new
    Show answer

    Answer: sacrificing

    Sacrificing ratio = Old ratio - New ratio.

  4. Admission of a Partner
    The amount brought in by a new partner in cash for his share of goodwill is called ______.

    1. reserve
    2. bonus
    3. capital
    4. premium
    Show answer

    Answer: premium

    Premium for goodwill compensates the old partners for the share of profit they sacrifice.

  5. Retirement and Death of a Partner
    The ratio in which the continuing partners acquire the share of a retiring partner is called the ______ ratio.

    1. sacrificing
    2. gaining
    3. capital
    4. new
    Show answer

    Answer: gaining

    Continuing partners compensate the retiring partner for goodwill in their gaining ratio.
