Sample questions

A few questions from the Class 12 Economics bank. Every test picks a fresh mix.

  1. National Income Accounting
    Goods bought by one producer from another for resale or for use as raw material during the same year are called ______ goods.

    1. capital
    2. final
    3. intermediate
    4. consumer
    Show answer

    Answer: intermediate

    Intermediate goods are used up in production during the year; only final goods are counted in national income to avoid double counting.

  2. Money and Banking
    The central bank of India is the ______.

    1. SBI
    2. SEBI
    3. NABARD
    4. rbi
    Show answer

    Answer: rbi

    The RBI issues currency, is the banker to the government and to banks, and controls credit.

  3. Determination of Income and Employment
    The ratio of change in consumption to change in income is called the ______.

    1. MPS
    2. marginal propensity to consume
    3. multiplier
    4. APC
    Show answer

    Answer: marginal propensity to consume

    MPC = ΔC ÷ ΔY; it lies between 0 and 1.

  4. Government Budget and the Economy
    A tax whose burden falls on the person on whom it is imposed and cannot be shifted to others, like income tax, is a ______ tax.

    1. progressive
    2. indirect
    3. proportional
    4. direct
    Show answer

    Answer: direct

    Direct taxes like income tax and corporation tax are paid by the person on whom they are levied.

  5. Open Economy Macroeconomics
    The systematic record of all economic transactions of the residents of a country with the rest of the world in a year is the balance of ______.

    1. trade
    2. accounts
    3. exchange
    4. payments
    Show answer

    Answer: payments

    The balance of payments has two main accounts: the current account and the capital account.
