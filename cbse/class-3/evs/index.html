Sample questions

A few questions from the Class 3 EVS bank. Every test picks a fresh mix.

  1. Family and Friends
    The mother of your mother is your ______.

    1. nani
    2. mausi
    3. bua
    4. aunt
    Show answer

    Answer: nani

    Mother's mother is our maternal grandmother, Nani.

  2. Going to the Mela
    A famous fair where camels are bought and sold is held every year at ______ in Rajasthan.

    1. Pushkar
    2. Mysuru
    3. Shillong
    4. Kullu
    Show answer

    Answer: Pushkar

    The Pushkar Mela in Rajasthan is famous for its camel fair.

  3. Celebrating Festivals
    Pongal is a harvest festival mainly celebrated in ______.

    1. Gujarat
    2. Tamil Nadu
    3. Assam
    4. Punjab
    Show answer

    Answer: Tamil Nadu

    Pongal is the harvest festival of Tamil Nadu, when new rice is cooked with milk and jaggery.

  4. Getting to Know Plants
    Plants of medium height with hard stems and many branches near the ground, like rose and hibiscus, are called ______.

    1. climbers
    2. shrubs
    3. herbs
    4. trees
    Show answer

    Answer: shrubs

    Shrubs are bushy plants with woody stems, smaller than trees.

  5. Plants and Animals Live Together
    A squirrel often makes its home in a ______.

    1. cave
    2. tree
    3. river
    4. pond
    Show answer

    Answer: tree

    Squirrels live in trees and make nests in holes or between branches.
