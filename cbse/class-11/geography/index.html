Sample questions

A few questions from the Class 11 Geography bank. Every test picks a fresh mix.

  1. Geography as a Discipline
    The word 'geography' comes from the Greek words 'geo' (earth) and 'graphos', which means ______.

    1. description
    2. study
    3. mapping
    4. measurement
    Show answer

    Answer: description

    Geography literally means description of the earth.

  2. The Origin and Evolution of the Earth
    The scientist who provided evidence in 1920 that the universe is expanding was ______.

    1. Laplace
    2. Otto Schmidt
    3. Immanuel Kant
    4. Edwin Hubble
    Show answer

    Answer: Edwin Hubble

    Hubble showed that galaxies are moving farther apart, supporting the Big Bang (expanding universe) hypothesis.

  3. Interior of the Earth
    The mean thickness of the oceanic crust is about how many kilometres?

    1. 100 km
    2. 5 km
    3. 2900 km
    4. 30 km
    Show answer

    Answer: 5 km

    The oceanic crust is about 5 km thick, while the continental crust is around 30 km thick and more under mountains.

  4. Distribution of Oceans and Continents
    The continental drift theory was put forward in 1912 by the German meteorologist ______.

    1. Harry Hess
    2. Abraham Ortelius
    3. Alfred Wegener
    4. Arthur Holmes
    Show answer

    Answer: Alfred Wegener

    Wegener argued that all continents once formed a single landmass that later broke up and drifted apart.

  5. Composition and Structure of Atmosphere
    Which gas makes up about 78 per cent of the atmosphere by volume?

    1. carbon dioxide
    2. oxygen
    3. nitrogen
    4. argon
    Show answer

    Answer: nitrogen

    Nitrogen forms about 78 per cent and oxygen about 21 per cent of dry air.
