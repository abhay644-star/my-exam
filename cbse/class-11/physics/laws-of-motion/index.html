A few questions from this chapter. Open each one to see the answer.

  1. Newton's first law of motion is also called the law of ______.

    1. inertia
    2. momentum
    3. action and reaction
    4. gravitation
    Show answer

    Answer: inertia

    The first law says a body stays at rest or in uniform motion unless acted on by a net force, a property called inertia.

  2. What net force is needed to give a 5 kg mass an acceleration of 3 m/s^2?

    1. 2 N
    2. 15 N
    3. 1.67 N
    4. 8 N
    Show answer

    Answer: 15 N

    F = ma = 5 × 3 = 15 N.

  3. A cricket ball of mass 0.15 kg moving at 20 m/s is hit straight back by the batsman with the same speed. Find the magnitude of the impulse imparted to the ball.

    1. 6 N s
    2. 12 N s
    3. 0 N s
    4. 3 N s
    Show answer

    Answer: 6 N s

    Change in momentum = m(v − u) = 0.15 × (20 − (−20)) = 0.15 × 40 = 6 N s.

  4. The product of a force and the time for which it acts is called ______.

    1. momentum
    2. impulse
    3. work
    4. power
    Show answer

    Answer: impulse

    Impulse equals the change in momentum it produces.

  5. Impulse of a force is equal to the change in ______ of the body.

    1. kinetic energy
    2. momentum
    3. acceleration
    4. velocity
    Show answer

    Answer: momentum

    Impulse = F × Δt = Δp, the change in linear momentum.

  6. A person of mass 60 kg stands on a weighing scale in a lift that accelerates upwards at 2 m/s^2. What does the scale read, in newtons? (g = 10 m/s^2)

    1. 600 N
    2. 720 N
    3. 120 N
    4. 480 N
    Show answer

    Answer: 720 N

    N − mg = ma, so N = m(g + a) = 60 × 12 = 720 N.

  7. Which of Newton's laws gives the relation F = ma? Answer first, second or third.

    1. third
    2. second
    3. first
    4. none of them
    Show answer

    Answer: second

    The second law says the rate of change of momentum is proportional to the applied force.

  8. A gun of mass 5 kg fires a bullet of mass 20 g at 500 m/s. What is the recoil speed of the gun?

    1. 0.5 m/s
    2. 2 m/s
    3. 10 m/s
    4. 20 m/s
    Show answer

    Answer: 2 m/s

    By conservation of momentum, 5V = 0.02 × 500 = 10, so V = 2 m/s.
