  1. The SI unit of power is the ______.

    1. watt
    2. newton
    3. horsepower
    4. joule
    Show answer

    Answer: watt

    Power is work done per unit time; 1 watt = 1 joule per second.

  2. Find the kinetic energy of a 2 kg body moving at 3 m/s.

    1. 6 J
    2. 9 J
    3. 18 J
    4. 3 J
    Show answer

    Answer: 9 J

    K = ½mv^2 = ½ × 2 × 9 = 9 J.

  3. A motor lifts a 50 kg load through a height of 10 m in 5 s at constant speed. Find the power of the motor (g = 10 m/s^2).

    1. 100 W
    2. 1000 W
    3. 500 W
    4. 2500 W
    Show answer

    Answer: 1000 W

    Work = mgh = 50 × 10 × 10 = 5000 J; P = 5000/5 = 1000 W.

  4. The work done by a force acting perpendicular to the displacement is ______.

    1. infinite
    2. maximum
    3. negative
    4. zero
    Show answer

    Answer: zero

    W = Fd cosθ and cos90° = 0, so no work is done (e.g. centripetal force).

  5. A force F = (3 i + 4 j) N produces a displacement d = (2 i + j) m. How much work is done?

    1. 5 J
    2. 14 J
    3. 10 J
    4. 24 J
    Show answer

    Answer: 10 J

    W = F · d = 3 × 2 + 4 × 1 = 10 J.

  6. A spring of spring constant 200 N/m is compressed by 0.1 m. How much potential energy is stored in it?

    1. 10 J
    2. 2 J
    3. 20 J
    4. 1 J
    Show answer

    Answer: 1 J

    U = ½kx^2 = ½ × 200 × 0.01 = 1 J.

  7. The SI unit of work and of energy is the ______.

    1. joule
    2. pascal
    3. watt
    4. newton
    Show answer

    Answer: joule

    1 J = 1 N × 1 m.

  8. How much work is done in lifting a 10 kg mass vertically through 2 m at constant speed? (g = 10 m/s^2)

    1. 2000 J
    2. 100 J
    3. 200 J
    4. 20 J
    Show answer

    Answer: 200 J

    W = mgh = 10 × 10 × 2 = 200 J.
