Sample questions

A few questions from the Class 11 Physics bank. Every test picks a fresh mix.

  1. Units and Measurements
    The SI base unit of luminous intensity is the ______.

    1. watt
    2. lux
    3. lumen
    4. candela
    Show answer

    Answer: candela

    Luminous intensity is one of the seven SI base quantities and its unit is the candela (cd).

  2. Motion in a Straight Line
    The slope of the position-time graph of an object gives its ______.

    1. displacement
    2. velocity
    3. jerk
    4. acceleration
    Show answer

    Answer: velocity

    Slope of x-t graph = dx/dt, which is the velocity of the object.

  3. Motion in a Plane
    Neglecting air resistance, at what angle of projection is the horizontal range of a projectile maximum?

    1. 45 °
    2. 90 °
    3. 60 °
    4. 30 °
    Show answer

    Answer: 45 °

    R = u^2 sin2θ/g is maximum when sin2θ = 1, i.e. θ = 45°.

  4. Laws of Motion
    Newton's first law of motion is also called the law of ______.

    1. inertia
    2. momentum
    3. action and reaction
    4. gravitation
    Show answer

    Answer: inertia

    The first law says a body stays at rest or in uniform motion unless acted on by a net force, a property called inertia.

  5. Work, Energy and Power
    The SI unit of power is the ______.

    1. watt
    2. newton
    3. horsepower
    4. joule
    Show answer

    Answer: watt

    Power is work done per unit time; 1 watt = 1 joule per second.
