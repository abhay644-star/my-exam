  1. In rotational motion, the quantity that plays the role of mass is called ______.

    1. torque
    2. angular velocity
    3. moment of inertia
    4. angular momentum
    Show answer

    Answer: moment of inertia

    Moment of inertia measures a body's resistance to change in rotational motion, just as mass does for linear motion.

  2. A force of 10 N is applied perpendicular to a spanner at a distance of 0.5 m from the nut. Find the torque.

    1. 20 N m
    2. 0.05 N m
    3. 5 N m
    4. 10 N m
    Show answer

    Answer: 5 N m

    τ = rF sin90° = 0.5 × 10 = 5 N m.

  3. Find the moment of inertia of a thin ring of mass 2 kg and radius 0.5 m about an axis through its centre perpendicular to its plane.

    1. 0.25 kg m^2
    2. 2 kg m^2
    3. 0.5 kg m^2
    4. 1 kg m^2
    Show answer

    Answer: 0.5 kg m^2

    For a ring, I = MR^2 = 2 × 0.25 = 0.5 kg m^2.

  4. What is the SI unit of torque?

    1. J/s
    2. kg m^2
    3. N m
    4. N/m
    Show answer

    Answer: N m

    Torque = force × perpendicular distance, so it is measured in newton metres.

  5. A spinning dancer pulls her arms inwards and spins faster. This is explained by the conservation of ______.

    1. linear momentum
    2. mass
    3. kinetic energy
    4. angular momentum
    Show answer

    Answer: angular momentum

    With no external torque, L = Iω is constant; lowering I increases ω.

  6. For a rigid body to be in mechanical equilibrium, the net external force and the net external ______ must both be zero.

    1. velocity
    2. kinetic energy
    3. speed
    4. torque
    Show answer

    Answer: torque

    Zero net force gives translational equilibrium; zero net torque gives rotational equilibrium.

  7. The rotational analogue of linear momentum is ______.

    1. torque
    2. angular momentum
    3. angular velocity
    4. moment of inertia
    Show answer

    Answer: angular momentum

    Angular momentum L = r × p, or Iω for a rigid body.

  8. Masses of 1 kg and 3 kg are placed at x = 0 and x = 4 m. Where is their centre of mass?

    1. 2 m
    2. 1 m
    3. 1.5 m
    4. 3 m
    Show answer

    Answer: 3 m

    x = (1 × 0 + 3 × 4) ÷ (1 + 3) = 3 m, closer to the heavier mass.
