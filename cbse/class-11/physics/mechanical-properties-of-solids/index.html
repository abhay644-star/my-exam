A few questions from this chapter. Open each one to see the answer.

  1. The SI unit of stress is ______.

    1. Pa
    2. N
    3. N/m
    4. J
    Show answer

    Answer: Pa

    Stress = restoring force per unit area, so its SI unit is N/m², i.e. the pascal (Pa).

  2. A wire of length 2 m is stretched so that its length increases by 1 mm. What is the longitudinal strain? Give your answer as a decimal.

    1. 0.0005
    2. 0.0001
    3. 0.002
    4. 0.005
    Show answer

    Answer: 0.0005

    Strain = ΔL/L = 0.001 m / 2 m = 0.0005 (no unit).

  3. A wire of length 2 m and cross-sectional area 1 mm² stretches by 1 mm under a load of 100 N. What is the Young's modulus of the material in GPa?

    1. 20 GPa
    2. 2000 GPa
    3. 100 GPa
    4. 200 GPa
    Show answer

    Answer: 200 GPa

    Y = FL/(AΔL) = (100 × 2)/(10^-6 × 10^-3) = 2 × 10^11 Pa = 200 GPa.

  4. Wire A has length L and radius r; wire B, of the same material, has length 2L and radius 2r. Under the same load, what is the ratio of the extension of A to the extension of B?

    1. 2
    2. 0.5
    3. 4
    4. 1
    Show answer

    Answer: 2

    ΔL = FL/(πr²Y), so ΔL ∝ L/r². A: L/r²; B: 2L/(4r²) = L/(2r²). Ratio A : B = 2.

  5. According to Hooke's law, within the elastic limit, stress is directly proportional to ______.

    1. temperature
    2. strain
    3. volume
    4. density
    Show answer

    Answer: strain

    Hooke's law: for small deformations, stress ∝ strain; the constant of proportionality is the modulus of elasticity.

  6. A force of 500 N acts normally on a rod of cross-sectional area 2 cm². What is the stress in the rod in pascal?

    1. 25000000 Pa
    2. 2500000 Pa
    3. 1000 Pa
    4. 250000 Pa
    Show answer

    Answer: 2500000 Pa

    Stress = F/A = 500 N / (2 × 10^-4 m²) = 2.5 × 10^6 Pa.

  7. An increase in pressure of 1.0 × 10^7 Pa reduces the volume of a liquid by 0.5%. What is the bulk modulus of the liquid in GPa?

    1. 20 GPa
    2. 2 GPa
    3. 0.2 GPa
    4. 5 GPa
    Show answer

    Answer: 2 GPa

    B = Δp/(ΔV/V) = 1.0 × 10^7 / 0.005 = 2 × 10^9 Pa = 2 GPa.

  8. A steel wire is replaced by another steel wire of double the length and double the radius. How does the Young's modulus of the material change?

    1. doubles
    2. halves
    3. unchanged
    4. becomes four times
    Show answer

    Answer: unchanged

    Young's modulus is a property of the material only; it does not depend on the dimensions of the wire, so it stays the same.
