A few questions from this chapter. Open each one to see the answer.

  1. The statement that a line joining a planet to the Sun sweeps equal areas in equal intervals of time is Kepler's ______ law.

    1. third
    2. fourth
    3. second
    4. first
    Show answer

    Answer: second

    Kepler's second law (law of areas) follows from conservation of angular momentum of the planet.

  2. The escape speed from the surface of the Earth is about ______ km/s.

    1. 7.9 km/s
    2. 3.1 km/s
    3. 9.8 km/s
    4. 11.2 km/s
    Show answer

    Answer: 11.2 km/s

    v_e = √(2gR) ≈ 11.2 km/s for the Earth.

  3. If the distance between two masses is halved, the gravitational force between them becomes how many times?

    1. 0.5
    2. 2
    3. 4
    4. 0.25
    Show answer

    Answer: 4

    F is proportional to 1/r^2, so halving r makes F four times larger.

  4. The value of g on the Earth's surface is 9.8 m/s^2. What is its value at a height equal to the radius of the Earth above the surface?

    1. 19.6 m/s^2
    2. 2.45 m/s^2
    3. 4.9 m/s^2
    4. 9.8 m/s^2
    Show answer

    Answer: 2.45 m/s^2

    g' = g R^2/(2R)^2 = g/4 = 9.8/4 = 2.45 m/s^2.

  5. Kepler's first law says that planets move in ______ orbits with the Sun at one focus.

    1. parabolic
    2. hyperbolic
    3. circular
    4. elliptical
    Show answer

    Answer: elliptical

    This is the law of orbits.

  6. By Kepler's third law, the square of a planet's period is proportional to the cube of its mean distance from the Sun. What power of the distance is T^2 proportional to?

    1. 1
    2. 2
    3. 3
    4. 4
    Show answer

    Answer: 3

    T^2 ∝ r^3 (the law of periods).

  7. A satellite's orbital radius is made 4 times larger. By what factor does its time period increase?

    1. 2
    2. 16
    3. 4
    4. 8
    Show answer

    Answer: 8

    By Kepler's third law T^2 ∝ r^3, so T ∝ r^(3/2) = 4^(3/2) = 8.

  8. As we go up from the Earth's surface, the acceleration due to gravity ______.

    1. stays the same
    2. decreases
    3. increases
    4. becomes zero
    Show answer

    Answer: decreases

    g(h) = g(1 − 2h/R) for small heights, so g falls with height.
