  1. The SI base unit of luminous intensity is the ______.

    1. watt
    2. lux
    3. lumen
    4. candela
    Show answer

    Answer: candela

    Luminous intensity is one of the seven SI base quantities and its unit is the candela (cd).

  2. Write the dimensional formula of force.

    1. [ML^-1T^-2]
    2. [MLT^-2]
    3. [MLT^-1]
    4. [ML^2T^-2]
    Show answer

    Answer: [MLT^-2]

    Force = mass × acceleration, so its dimensions are [M][LT^-2] = [MLT^-2].

  3. The percentage error in measuring the radius of a sphere is 2%. What is the percentage error in its volume?

    1. 6 %
    2. 4 %
    3. 2 %
    4. 8 %
    Show answer

    Answer: 6 %

    V is proportional to r^3, so the relative error in V is 3 times that in r: 3 × 2% = 6%.

  4. How many significant figures are there in the measured value 0.007020?

    1. 7
    2. 3
    3. 4
    4. 6
    Show answer

    Answer: 4

    Leading zeros are not significant, but the zero between 2 and the end (trailing zero after the decimal) is, giving 7, 0, 2, 0 = 4 significant figures.

  5. The density of water is 1 g/cm^3. Express it in kg/m^3.

    1. 0.001 kg/m^3
    2. 100 kg/m^3
    3. 1 kg/m^3
    4. 1000 kg/m^3
    Show answer

    Answer: 1000 kg/m^3

    1 g/cm^3 = 10^-3 kg / 10^-6 m^3 = 1000 kg/m^3.

  6. Round off 2.746 to three significant figures.

    1. 2.74
    2. 2.8
    3. 2.7
    4. 2.75
    Show answer

    Answer: 2.75

    The digit dropped is 6, which is more than 5, so the preceding digit 4 is raised to 5.

  7. The SI base unit of electric current is the ______.

    1. ohm
    2. ampere
    3. coulomb
    4. volt
    Show answer

    Answer: ampere

    The ampere is one of the seven SI base units; the volt, coulomb and ohm are derived units.

  8. How many significant figures are there in 2.300 × 10^3?

    1. 2
    2. 4
    3. 7
    4. 3
    Show answer

    Answer: 4

    In scientific notation, all digits of 2.300 are significant, including the trailing zeros after the decimal point.
