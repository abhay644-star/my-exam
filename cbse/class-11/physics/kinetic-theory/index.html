A few questions from this chapter. Open each one to see the answer.

  1. According to the law of equipartition of energy, in thermal equilibrium each degree of freedom of a molecule has an average energy of ______ k_B T.

    1. 1/2
    2. 3/2
    3. 1
    4. 2
    Show answer

    Answer: 1/2

    Each quadratic term (degree of freedom) in the energy contributes an average of ½ k_B T per molecule.

  2. The temperature of an ideal gas is raised from 300 K to 1200 K. By what factor does the rms speed of its molecules increase?

    1. 2
    2. 1.41
    3. 16
    4. 4
    Show answer

    Answer: 2

    v_rms = √(3k_BT/m) ∝ √T, so the factor is √(1200/300) = √4 = 2.

  3. An ideal gas has density 1.2 kg/m³ at a pressure of 1.0 × 10^5 Pa. What is the rms speed of its molecules?

    1. 500 m/s
    2. 289 m/s
    3. 1000 m/s
    4. 250 m/s
    Show answer

    Answer: 500 m/s

    P = (1/3)ρv_rms², so v_rms = √(3P/ρ) = √(3 × 10^5/1.2) = √250000 = 500 m/s.

  4. If the diameter of the gas molecules were doubled while the number density stays the same, the mean free path would become how many times its original value? Give your answer as a decimal.

    1. 4
    2. 0.5
    3. 0.25
    4. 2
    Show answer

    Answer: 0.25

    Mean free path l = 1/(√2 n π d²) ∝ 1/d², so doubling d makes l one-fourth, i.e. 0.25 times.

  5. How many translational degrees of freedom does a molecule of a monatomic gas have?

    1. 6
    2. 3
    3. 5
    4. 2
    Show answer

    Answer: 3

    A monatomic molecule can move independently along x, y and z, giving 3 translational degrees of freedom.

  6. What is the ratio of specific heats γ = Cp/Cv for a monatomic ideal gas? Give your answer to two decimal places.

    1. 1.33
    2. 1.4
    3. 1.5
    4. 1.67
    Show answer

    Answer: 1.67

    For a monatomic gas Cv = (3/2)R and Cp = (5/2)R, so γ = 5/3 ≈ 1.67.

  7. What is the ratio of the rms speed of hydrogen molecules (molar mass 2 g/mol) to that of oxygen molecules (molar mass 32 g/mol) at the same temperature?

    1. 2
    2. 4
    3. 0.25
    4. 16
    Show answer

    Answer: 4

    v_rms ∝ 1/√M at a given T, so v_H₂/v_O₂ = √(32/2) = √16 = 4.

  8. A mixture contains 2 mol of a monatomic ideal gas and 1 mol of a rigid diatomic ideal gas at temperature T. Its total internal energy is x RT. What is x?

    1. 5.5
    2. 6
    3. 4.5
    4. 7
    Show answer

    Answer: 5.5

    U = 2 × (3/2)RT + 1 × (5/2)RT = 3RT + 2.5RT = 5.5RT.
