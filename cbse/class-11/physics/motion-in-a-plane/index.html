A few questions from this chapter. Open each one to see the answer.

  1. Neglecting air resistance, at what angle of projection is the horizontal range of a projectile maximum?

    1. 45 °
    2. 90 °
    3. 60 °
    4. 30 °
    Show answer

    Answer: 45 °

    R = u^2 sin2θ/g is maximum when sin2θ = 1, i.e. θ = 45°.

  2. A ball is projected at 20 m/s at an angle of 45° to the horizontal. Find its horizontal range (g = 10 m/s^2).

    1. 80 m
    2. 10 m
    3. 20 m
    4. 40 m
    Show answer

    Answer: 40 m

    R = u^2 sin90°/g = 400/10 = 40 m.

  3. Meera whirls a stone tied to a string in a horizontal circle of radius 0.5 m at a constant speed of 2 m/s. Find its centripetal acceleration.

    1. 8 m/s^2
    2. 4 m/s^2
    3. 1 m/s^2
    4. 2 m/s^2
    Show answer

    Answer: 8 m/s^2

    a = v^2/r = 4/0.5 = 8 m/s^2, directed towards the centre.

  4. A stone is thrown horizontally at 10 m/s from the top of a cliff 45 m high. How far from the foot of the cliff does it land? (g = 10 m/s^2)

    1. 15 m
    2. 30 m
    3. 60 m
    4. 45 m
    Show answer

    Answer: 30 m

    Time of fall t = √(2h/g) = √9 = 3 s; horizontal distance = 10 × 3 = 30 m.

  5. A quantity that has both magnitude and direction is called a ______ quantity.

    1. vector
    2. derived
    3. scalar
    4. base
    Show answer

    Answer: vector

    Displacement, velocity, acceleration and force are vectors.

  6. Two vectors of magnitudes 3 units and 4 units act at right angles to each other. What is the magnitude of their resultant?

    1. 7 units
    2. 12 units
    3. 5 units
    4. 1 units
    Show answer

    Answer: 5 units

    For perpendicular vectors, R = √(3^2 + 4^2) = √25 = 5 units.

  7. A projectile is fired at 30 m/s at 30° to the horizontal. What maximum height does it reach? (g = 10 m/s^2)

    1. 5 m
    2. 11.25 m
    3. 45 m
    4. 22.5 m
    Show answer

    Answer: 11.25 m

    H = u^2 sin^2 θ ÷ 2g = 900 × 0.25 ÷ 20 = 11.25 m.

  8. Which of these is a scalar: displacement, velocity, speed or acceleration?

    1. acceleration
    2. displacement
    3. velocity
    4. speed
    Show answer

    Answer: speed

    Speed has only magnitude; the others also have a direction.
