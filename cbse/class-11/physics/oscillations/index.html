A few questions from this chapter. Open each one to see the answer.

  1. In simple harmonic motion, the restoring force is directly proportional to the ______ from the mean position.

    1. velocity
    2. time
    3. mass
    4. displacement
    Show answer

    Answer: displacement

    For SHM, F = −kx: the force is proportional to displacement and directed towards the mean position.

  2. Find the time period of a simple pendulum of length 1 m at a place where g = π^2 m/s^2.

    1. 1 s
    2. 2 s
    3. 4 s
    4. 3.14 s
    Show answer

    Answer: 2 s

    T = 2π√(L/g) = 2π√(1/π^2) = 2 s.

  3. A 1 kg block attached to a spring of spring constant 100 N/m oscillates on a smooth surface. Find its angular frequency.

    1. 1 rad/s
    2. 100 rad/s
    3. 0.1 rad/s
    4. 10 rad/s
    Show answer

    Answer: 10 rad/s

    ω = √(k/m) = √(100/1) = 10 rad/s.

  4. The displacement of a particle in SHM is x = 5 sin(10t) cm, where t is in seconds. What is its maximum speed?

    1. 10 cm/s
    2. 50 cm/s
    3. 5 cm/s
    4. 500 cm/s
    Show answer

    Answer: 50 cm/s

    v_max = Aω = 5 cm × 10 rad/s = 50 cm/s, reached at the mean position.

  5. The time taken to complete one oscillation is called the ______.

    1. time period
    2. phase
    3. frequency
    4. amplitude
    Show answer

    Answer: time period

    Period T = 1 ÷ frequency.

  6. A particle in SHM has an amplitude of 4 cm. What distance does it travel in one complete oscillation?

    1. 0 cm
    2. 8 cm
    3. 4 cm
    4. 16 cm
    Show answer

    Answer: 16 cm

    In one oscillation it goes from the mean position to one extreme, back, to the other extreme and back: 4A = 16 cm (its displacement is zero).

  7. A particle in SHM has an amplitude of 0.1 m and an angular frequency of 10 rad/s. What is its maximum acceleration?

    1. 10 m/s^2
    2. 1 m/s^2
    3. 0.1 m/s^2
    4. 100 m/s^2
    Show answer

    Answer: 10 m/s^2

    amax = ω^2 A = 100 × 0.1 = 10 m/s^2.

  8. In simple harmonic motion, the speed of the particle is greatest at the ______ position.

    1. any
    2. extreme
    3. mean
    4. halfway
    Show answer

    Answer: mean

    At the mean position all the energy is kinetic.
