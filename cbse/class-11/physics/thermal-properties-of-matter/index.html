A few questions from this chapter. Open each one to see the answer.

  1. The mode of heat transfer that does not need any material medium is ______.

    1. conduction
    2. insulation
    3. radiation
    4. convection
    Show answer

    Answer: radiation

    Heat from the Sun reaches the Earth through empty space by radiation.

  2. Using T(K) = t(°C) + 273, express 27 °C in kelvin.

    1. 327 K
    2. 246 K
    3. 300 K
    4. 273 K
    Show answer

    Answer: 300 K

    T = 27 + 273 = 300 K.

  3. How much heat is needed to raise the temperature of 2 kg of water by 10 °C? (specific heat of water = 4200 J/kg K)

    1. 8400 J
    2. 42000 J
    3. 84000 J
    4. 840 J
    Show answer

    Answer: 84000 J

    Q = mcΔT = 2 × 4200 × 10 = 84000 J.

  4. What is the SI unit of specific heat capacity?

    1. J/kg
    2. J/K
    3. W/(m K)
    4. J/(kg K)
    Show answer

    Answer: J/(kg K)

    Specific heat capacity is the heat per unit mass per unit change in temperature.

  5. At what temperature do the Celsius and Fahrenheit scales show the same reading?

    1. -40 °
    2. 0 °
    3. -32 °
    4. 40 °
    Show answer

    Answer: -40 °

    Setting F = C in F = 9C/5 + 32 gives C = −40, so −40 °C = −40 °F.

  6. How much heat is needed to melt 2 kg of ice at 0 °C? (Latent heat of fusion = 3.34 × 10^5 J/kg)

    1. 167000 J
    2. 668000 J
    3. 334000 J
    4. 6680 J
    Show answer

    Answer: 668000 J

    Q = mL = 2 × 3.34 × 10^5 = 6.68 × 10^5 J.

  7. The heat needed to change the state of unit mass of a substance, without any change in temperature, is called ______ heat.

    1. specific
    2. latent
    3. radiant
    4. sensible
    Show answer

    Answer: latent

    For water, the latent heat of fusion is 3.34 × 10^5 J/kg and of vaporisation 22.6 × 10^5 J/kg.

  8. Sea breezes during the day are an example of heat transfer by ______.

    1. convection
    2. radiation
    3. reflection
    4. conduction
    Show answer

    Answer: convection

    Land heats faster; warm air over it rises and cooler air moves in from the sea.
