  1. The equation of continuity, Av = constant, for an incompressible fluid is a consequence of the conservation of ______.

    1. energy
    2. mass
    3. momentum
    4. charge
    Show answer

    Answer: mass

    The mass of fluid entering a tube of flow per second equals the mass leaving it, giving A₁v₁ = A₂v₂ (conservation of mass).

  2. What is the gauge pressure at a depth of 10 m below the surface of water? (Take density of water = 1000 kg/m³, g = 10 m/s²)

    1. 1000 Pa
    2. 10000 Pa
    3. 100000 Pa
    4. 1000000 Pa
    Show answer

    Answer: 100000 Pa

    Gauge pressure = ρgh = 1000 × 10 × 10 = 1 × 10^5 Pa.

  3. If the radius of a small spherical ball falling through a viscous liquid is doubled, its terminal velocity becomes how many times the original?

    1. 4
    2. 8
    3. 2
    4. 16
    Show answer

    Answer: 4

    From Stokes' law, terminal velocity v = 2r²(ρ − σ)g/(9η), so v ∝ r². Doubling r makes v four times.

  4. Water (surface tension 0.07 N/m, angle of contact 0°, density 1000 kg/m³) rises in a capillary tube of radius 0.1 mm. Taking g = 10 m/s², what is the height of capillary rise in cm?

    1. 7 cm
    2. 1.4 cm
    3. 14 cm
    4. 28 cm
    Show answer

    Answer: 14 cm

    h = 2S cosθ/(rρg) = (2 × 0.07 × 1)/(10^-4 × 1000 × 10) = 0.14 m = 14 cm.

  5. The constant maximum velocity finally attained by a body falling through a viscous fluid is called ______ velocity.

    1. drift
    2. terminal
    3. critical
    4. escape
    Show answer

    Answer: terminal

    When viscous force plus buoyancy balances the weight, the net force is zero and the body moves with constant terminal velocity.

  6. In a hydraulic lift, the small piston has area 0.01 m² and the large piston has area 1 m². If a force of 50 N is applied on the small piston, what force acts on the large piston?

    1. 0.5 N
    2. 5000 N
    3. 500 N
    4. 50000 N
    Show answer

    Answer: 5000 N

    By Pascal's law F₂ = F₁ × (A₂/A₁) = 50 × (1/0.01) = 5000 N.

  7. Water (density 1000 kg/m³) flows through a horizontal pipe. Its speed increases from 1 m/s to 3 m/s at a constriction. What is the drop in pressure at the constriction?

    1. 2000 Pa
    2. 8000 Pa
    3. 1000 Pa
    4. 4000 Pa
    Show answer

    Answer: 4000 Pa

    Bernoulli (horizontal): P₁ − P₂ = ½ρ(v₂² − v₁²) = ½ × 1000 × (9 − 1) = 4000 Pa.

  8. What is the excess pressure inside a soap bubble of radius 1 cm if the surface tension of the soap solution is 0.03 N/m?

    1. 12 Pa
    2. 24 Pa
    3. 6 Pa
    4. 3 Pa
    Show answer

    Answer: 12 Pa

    A soap bubble has two surfaces, so excess pressure = 4S/r = (4 × 0.03)/0.01 = 12 Pa.
