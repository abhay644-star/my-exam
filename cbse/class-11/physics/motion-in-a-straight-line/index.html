  1. The slope of the position-time graph of an object gives its ______.

    1. displacement
    2. velocity
    3. jerk
    4. acceleration
    Show answer

    Answer: velocity

    Slope of x-t graph = dx/dt, which is the velocity of the object.

  2. A car starts from rest and moves with a uniform acceleration of 2 m/s^2 for 5 s. How far does it travel?

    1. 20 m
    2. 25 m
    3. 50 m
    4. 10 m
    Show answer

    Answer: 25 m

    s = ut + ½at^2 = 0 + ½ × 2 × 5^2 = 25 m.

  3. Arjun throws a ball vertically upward with a speed of 20 m/s. Taking g = 10 m/s^2, find the maximum height reached.

    1. 40 m
    2. 2 m
    3. 10 m
    4. 20 m
    Show answer

    Answer: 20 m

    At the top v = 0, so h = u^2/2g = 400/20 = 20 m.

  4. The area under the velocity-time graph of a moving object gives its ______.

    1. force
    2. acceleration
    3. displacement
    4. velocity
    Show answer

    Answer: displacement

    Area under v-t graph = ∫v dt, which equals the displacement in that time interval.

  5. A car travels at 54 km/h. What is this speed in m/s?

    1. 5.4 m/s
    2. 15 m/s
    3. 30 m/s
    4. 54 m/s
    Show answer

    Answer: 15 m/s

    54 × 1000 ÷ 3600 = 15 m/s.

  6. A car speeds up uniformly from 10 m/s to 30 m/s over a distance of 100 m. What is its acceleration?

    1. 2 m/s^2
    2. 0.2 m/s^2
    3. 4 m/s^2
    4. 8 m/s^2
    Show answer

    Answer: 4 m/s^2

    v^2 = u^2 + 2as gives 900 = 100 + 200a, so a = 4 m/s^2.

  7. If the velocity of an object is constant, what is its acceleration?

    1. equal to g
    2. increasing
    3. zero
    4. negative
    Show answer

    Answer: zero

    Acceleration is the rate of change of velocity; a constant velocity does not change.

  8. A body starts from rest with a uniform acceleration of 4 m/s^2. What is its velocity after 3 s?

    1. 12 m/s
    2. 18 m/s
    3. 7 m/s
    4. 36 m/s
    Show answer

    Answer: 12 m/s

    v = u + at = 0 + 4 × 3 = 12 m/s.
