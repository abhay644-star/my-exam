A few questions from this chapter. Open each one to see the answer.

  1. How many bones are there in the skeletal system of an adult human?

    1. 213
    2. 300
    3. 126
    4. 206
    Show answer

    Answer: 206

    The adult human skeleton has 206 bones: 80 in the axial and 126 in the appendicular skeleton.

  2. The last two pairs (11th and 12th) of ribs, which are not connected ventrally, are called ______ ribs.

    1. vertebrochondral
    2. false
    3. floating
    4. true
    Show answer

    Answer: floating

    The first 7 pairs are true ribs, the 8th–10th are false (vertebrochondral) ribs, and the 11th–12th are floating ribs.

  3. How many bones make up the axial skeleton of humans?

    1. 33
    2. 64
    3. 126
    4. 80
    Show answer

    Answer: 80

    The axial skeleton (skull, vertebral column, sternum, ribs) has 80 bones; the appendicular skeleton has 126.

  4. The light bands of a myofibril, which contain only actin, are called ______.

    1. I-band
    2. A-band
    3. M-line
    4. H-zone
    Show answer

    Answer: I-band

    Light bands contain actin and are called I-bands (isotropic); dark A-bands (anisotropic) contain myosin.

  5. The portion of a myofibril between two successive Z-lines, considered the functional unit of contraction, is the ______.

    1. sarcolemma
    2. fascicle
    3. sarcomere
    4. myofibril
    Show answer

    Answer: sarcomere

    A sarcomere lies between two successive Z-lines and is the functional unit of muscle contraction.

  6. During muscle contraction, Ca²⁺ binds to a subunit of ______ on the actin filament, removing the masking of the active sites for myosin.

    1. troponin
    2. tropomyosin
    3. meromyosin
    4. myosin
    Show answer

    Answer: troponin

    Increased Ca²⁺ binds troponin, unmasking actin's active sites so myosin heads can form cross bridges.

  7. How many cervical vertebrae are present in humans (and almost all mammals)?

    1. 5
    2. 12
    3. 7
    4. 4
    Show answer

    Answer: 7

    There are 7 cervical vertebrae in almost all mammals including humans; there are 12 thoracic and 5 lumbar.

  8. The joint between the atlas and axis vertebrae is a ______ joint.

    1. gliding
    2. saddle
    3. hinge
    4. pivot
    Show answer

    Answer: pivot

    NCERT gives the atlas–axis joint as an example of a pivot joint (a synovial joint).
