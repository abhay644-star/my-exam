A few questions from this chapter. Open each one to see the answer.

  1. Kinetin, first isolated from autoclaved herring sperm DNA, belongs to which group of plant growth regulators?

    1. auxins
    2. abscisic acid
    3. gibberellins
    4. cytokinins
    Show answer

    Answer: cytokinins

    Kinetin (a modified adenine) was discovered by Skoog and Miller as a cytokinin; it promotes cell division (cytokinesis).

  2. Gibberellins were discovered from the fungus Gibberella fujikuroi, which causes the ______ disease of rice seedlings.

    1. blast
    2. rust
    3. bakanae
    4. tungro
    Show answer

    Answer: bakanae

    E. Kurosawa reported that sterile filtrates of Gibberella fujikuroi produced the symptoms of bakanae (foolish seedling) disease in healthy rice seedlings.

  3. In arithmetic growth, a plot of length against time gives a ______ curve.

    1. J-shaped
    2. exponential
    3. linear
    4. sigmoid
    Show answer

    Answer: linear

    In arithmetic growth only one daughter cell continues dividing, giving a constant rate and a linear curve (Lt = L0 + rt).

  4. Plants such as cotton, coriander and larkspur produce different kinds of leaves in juvenile and mature stages. This ability to follow different pathways in response to environment or phases of life is called ______.

    1. totipotency
    2. vernalisation
    3. plasticity
    4. dedifferentiation
    Show answer

    Answer: plasticity

    Heterophylly is an example of plasticity, the ability of plants to form different structures in response to environment or life phase.

  5. The only gaseous plant growth regulator is ______.

    1. GA3
    2. abscisic acid
    3. ethylene
    4. IAA
    Show answer

    Answer: ethylene

    Ethylene is a simple gaseous PGR that promotes fruit ripening, senescence and abscission.

  6. Decapitation (removal of shoot tips) is used in tea plantations and hedge-making to overcome apical dominance caused by ______.

    1. auxin
    2. cytokinin
    3. ethylene
    4. abscisic acid
    Show answer

    Answer: auxin

    The growing apical bud, through auxin, inhibits lateral buds (apical dominance); removing it lets lateral branches grow.

  7. The most widely used compound as a source of ethylene, which releases ethylene slowly when sprayed as an aqueous solution, is ______.

    1. zeatin
    2. ethephon
    3. NAA
    4. IBA
    Show answer

    Answer: ethephon

    Ethephon is absorbed and translocated within the plant and releases ethylene slowly; it hastens fruit ripening and flower/fruit thinning.

  8. Spraying sugarcane with gibberellins increases stem length and raises the yield by as much as how many tonnes per acre?

    1. 50 tonnes per acre
    2. 10 tonnes per acre
    3. 5 tonnes per acre
    4. 20 tonnes per acre
    Show answer

    Answer: 20 tonnes per acre

    Since sugarcane stores sugar in its stem, GA-induced stem elongation increases yield by as much as 20 tonnes per acre.
