A few questions from this chapter. Open each one to see the answer.

  1. Proteins are polymers (heteropolymers) of ______.

    1. amino acids
    2. monosaccharides
    3. fatty acids
    4. nucleotides
    Show answer

    Answer: amino acids

    Proteins are polypeptides, i.e. linear chains of amino acids linked by peptide bonds.

  2. Inulin is a polymer of ______.

    1. fructose
    2. glucose
    3. galactose
    4. ribose
    Show answer

    Answer: fructose

    Inulin is a polysaccharide made of fructose units.

  3. How many carbon atoms (including the carboxyl carbon) does palmitic acid have?

    1. 14
    2. 20
    3. 16
    4. 18
    Show answer

    Answer: 16

    Palmitic acid has 16 carbons including the carboxyl carbon; arachidonic acid has 20.

  4. Enzymes that catalyse the removal of groups from substrates by mechanisms other than hydrolysis, leaving double bonds, belong to the class ______.

    1. transferases
    2. ligases
    3. hydrolases
    4. lyases
    Show answer

    Answer: lyases

    Lyases remove groups from substrates by non-hydrolytic means, leaving double bonds.

  5. The most abundant protein in the animal world is ______.

    1. collagen
    2. keratin
    3. myosin
    4. albumin
    Show answer

    Answer: collagen

    Collagen is the most abundant protein in the animal world; RuBisCO is the most abundant protein in the whole biosphere.

  6. In a polysaccharide, the individual monosaccharides are linked by a ______ bond.

    1. phosphodiester
    2. glycosidic
    3. ester
    4. peptide
    Show answer

    Answer: glycosidic

    Monosaccharides are joined by glycosidic bonds formed by dehydration between two carbon atoms of adjacent sugars.

  7. Nucleic acids that behave like enzymes are called ______.

    1. ribozymes
    2. apoenzymes
    3. coenzymes
    4. isozymes
    Show answer

    Answer: ribozymes

    Almost all enzymes are proteins, but some nucleic acids (RNA) act as catalysts; these are ribozymes.

  8. Malonate inhibits succinic dehydrogenase because it closely resembles the substrate succinate. This is ______ inhibition.

    1. competitive
    2. non-competitive
    3. uncompetitive
    4. allosteric
    Show answer

    Answer: competitive

    A competitive inhibitor resembles the substrate and competes for the active site; malonate inhibiting succinic dehydrogenase is the classic example.
