A few questions from this chapter. Open each one to see the answer.

  1. Animals having a body cavity lined by mesoderm are called ______.

    1. coelomates
    2. acoelomates
    3. diploblastic
    4. pseudocoelomates
    Show answer

    Answer: coelomates

    A true coelom is a body cavity lined by mesoderm; animals possessing it are coelomates, such as annelids and chordates.

  2. Stinging cells called cnidoblasts, used for anchorage, defence and capture of prey, are found in phylum ______.

    1. Ctenophora
    2. Platyhelminthes
    3. Porifera
    4. Cnidaria
    Show answer

    Answer: Cnidaria

    Cnidarians like Hydra and jellyfish have cnidoblasts on their tentacles and body.

  3. An animal has a notochord present only in the larval tail. To which subphylum of chordates does it belong?

    1. Hemichordata
    2. Vertebrata
    3. Urochordata
    4. Cephalochordata
    Show answer

    Answer: Urochordata

    In urochordates like Ascidia, the notochord is present only in the larval tail, whereas in cephalochordates it runs from head to tail throughout life.

  4. The water vascular system is a characteristic feature of phylum ______.

    1. Mollusca
    2. Echinodermata
    3. Porifera
    4. Cnidaria
    Show answer

    Answer: Echinodermata

    Echinoderms such as starfish use a water vascular system for locomotion, capture of food and respiration.

  5. Tapeworms and liver flukes, which have dorsoventrally flattened bodies, belong to phylum ______.

    1. Annelida
    2. Aschelminthes
    3. Mollusca
    4. Platyhelminthes
    Show answer

    Answer: Platyhelminthes

    They are flatworms; most are endoparasites.

  6. Sharks have an endoskeleton made of cartilage. To which class do they belong?

    1. Amphibia
    2. Osteichthyes
    3. Cyclostomata
    4. Chondrichthyes
    Show answer

    Answer: Chondrichthyes

    Bony fishes belong to Osteichthyes.

  7. Sponges belong to phylum ______.

    1. Annelida
    2. Porifera
    3. Mollusca
    4. Cnidaria
    Show answer

    Answer: Porifera

    Sponges have a water canal system and collar cells (choanocytes).

  8. Animals whose body can be divided into two identical halves along only one plane show ______ symmetry.

    1. radial
    2. spherical
    3. bilateral
    4. no
    Show answer

    Answer: bilateral

    Annelids, arthropods and chordates are bilaterally symmetrical.
