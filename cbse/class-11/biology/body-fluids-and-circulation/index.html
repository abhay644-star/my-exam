  1. The pacemaker of the human heart is the ______.

    1. SAN
    2. bundle of His
    3. AVN
    4. Purkinje fibres
    Show answer

    Answer: SAN

    The SA node generates the maximum number of action potentials per minute (70-75) and sets the rhythm of the heart.

  2. A person with blood group ______ can donate blood to persons of any ABO group and is called a universal donor.

    1. A
    2. O
    3. AB
    4. B
    Show answer

    Answer: O

    Group O red cells have neither antigen A nor B, so they are not agglutinated by recipients' antibodies.

  3. If the stroke volume is 70 mL and the heart rate is 72 beats per minute, calculate the cardiac output in mL per minute.

    1. 5000 mL
    2. 4900 mL
    3. 5040 mL
    4. 142 mL
    Show answer

    Answer: 5040 mL

    Cardiac output = stroke volume × heart rate = 70 × 72 = 5040 mL per minute (about 5 litres).

  4. The straw-coloured fluid part of blood is called ______.

    1. tissue fluid
    2. serum
    3. lymph
    4. plasma
    Show answer

    Answer: plasma

    Plasma is about 55% of blood; 90–92% of it is water.

  5. Human red blood cells have an average lifespan of about ______ days.

    1. 120 days
    2. 30 days
    3. 365 days
    4. 7 days
    Show answer

    Answer: 120 days

    Old RBCs are destroyed in the spleen, the graveyard of RBCs.

  6. If the heart beats 75 times a minute, how long does one cardiac cycle last?

    1. 1 s
    2. 0.8 s
    3. 75 s
    4. 0.5 s
    Show answer

    Answer: 0.8 s

    60 s ÷ 75 = 0.8 s.

  7. Red blood cells are also called ______.

    1. thrombocytes
    2. lymphocytes
    3. erythrocytes
    4. leucocytes
    Show answer

    Answer: erythrocytes

    They are the most abundant cells in blood and carry haemoglobin.

  8. The most abundant white blood cells are the ______.

    1. monocytes
    2. neutrophils
    3. eosinophils
    4. lymphocytes
    Show answer

    Answer: neutrophils

    Neutrophils are about 60–65% of white cells and destroy microbes by phagocytosis.
