A few questions from this chapter. Open each one to see the answer.

  1. The scientific name of mango is Mangifera ______.

    1. sapiens
    2. tuberosum
    3. leo
    4. indica
    Show answer

    Answer: indica

    In binomial nomenclature, Mangifera is the genus and indica is the specific epithet of mango.

  2. Biological names are generally in ______ and are written in italics.

    1. Greek
    2. Sanskrit
    3. Latin
    4. English
    Show answer

    Answer: Latin

    They are Latinised or derived from Latin, whatever their origin.

  3. Which taxonomic category groups the genera Panthera (lion, tiger) and Felis (cat) together?

    1. family
    2. order
    3. species
    4. class
    Show answer

    Answer: family

    Panthera and Felis are both placed in the family Felidae; a family is a group of related genera.

  4. Which taxonomic aid contains information on any one taxon only, such as a single family or genus?

    1. monograph
    2. flora
    3. manual
    4. key
    Show answer

    Answer: monograph

    A monograph covers one taxon, while a flora covers the plants of an area and a manual helps identify species of an area.

  5. The basic unit of classification is ______.

    1. family
    2. order
    3. genus
    4. species
    Show answer

    Answer: species

    Species is the basic taxonomic category; it is a group of individuals with fundamental similarities.

  6. Which taxonomic category comes between genus and order?

    1. class
    2. family
    3. species
    4. phylum
    Show answer

    Answer: family

    The hierarchy is kingdom, phylum/division, class, order, family, genus, species.

  7. In plants, the category equivalent to phylum in animals is called ______.

    1. order
    2. class
    3. family
    4. division
    Show answer

    Answer: division

    Plant classes are grouped into divisions.

  8. The system of binomial nomenclature was given by ______.

    1. Darwin
    2. Linnaeus
    3. Whittaker
    4. Mendel
    Show answer

    Answer: Linnaeus

    Each organism has a two-part name: the genus followed by the specific epithet.
