A few questions from this chapter. Open each one to see the answer.

  1. In pea, leaflets are modified into thread-like structures for climbing called ______.

    1. tendrils
    2. thorns
    3. spines
    4. phyllodes
    Show answer

    Answer: tendrils

    In pea, some leaflets are modified into tendrils that coil around supports to help the plant climb.

  2. The placentation in which ovules are borne on a central axis in a multilocular ovary, as in China rose, tomato and lemon, is ______ placentation.

    1. marginal
    2. axile
    3. parietal
    4. basal
    Show answer

    Answer: axile

    In axile placentation the placenta is axial and the ovules are attached to it in a multilocular ovary.

  3. In plants like Rhizophora growing in swampy areas, many roots come out of the ground and grow vertically upwards to get oxygen. These roots are called ______.

    1. pneumatophores
    2. tap roots
    3. stilt roots
    4. prop roots
    Show answer

    Answer: pneumatophores

    Pneumatophores are negatively geotropic roots that help mangrove plants obtain oxygen for respiration.

  4. The main root that develops directly from the radicle is called the ______ root.

    1. adventitious
    2. prop
    3. fibrous
    4. primary
    Show answer

    Answer: primary

    Dicots such as mustard have a tap root system.

  5. In family Fabaceae, the corolla consisting of a standard, two wings and a keel is called ______.

    1. rosaceous
    2. cruciform
    3. papilionaceous
    4. campanulate
    Show answer

    Answer: papilionaceous

    Fabaceae flowers (like pea) have a papilionaceous corolla with vexillary aestivation.

  6. A flower in which the ovary is above the other floral parts (a superior ovary) is called ______.

    1. perigynous
    2. zygomorphic
    3. epigynous
    4. hypogynous
    Show answer

    Answer: hypogynous

    Mustard, China rose and brinjal have hypogynous flowers.

  7. Roots that grow from parts of the plant other than the radicle are called ______ roots.

    1. primary
    2. tap
    3. lateral
    4. adventitious
    Show answer

    Answer: adventitious

    Grass, Monstera and the banyan tree have adventitious roots.

  8. The pattern of arrangement of leaves on a stem or branch is called ______.

    1. venation
    2. aestivation
    3. inflorescence
    4. phyllotaxy
    Show answer

    Answer: phyllotaxy

    Phyllotaxy can be alternate, opposite or whorled.
