A few questions from this chapter. Open each one to see the answer.

  1. The phase of the cell cycle between two successive M phases is called ______.

    1. cytokinesis
    2. prophase
    3. karyokinesis
    4. interphase
    Show answer

    Answer: interphase

    Interphase (G1, S and G2) is the preparatory phase that lasts more than 95% of the cell cycle.

  2. Crossing over between non-sister chromatids of homologous chromosomes takes place during the ______ stage of prophase I.

    1. diplotene
    2. zygotene
    3. leptotene
    4. pachytene
    Show answer

    Answer: pachytene

    Crossing over occurs in pachytene; the chiasmata formed become visible in diplotene.

  3. A diploid cell has 16 chromosomes. How many chromosomes will each daughter cell have at the end of meiosis I?

    1. 32
    2. 8
    3. 4
    4. 16
    Show answer

    Answer: 8

    Meiosis I is reductional: homologous chromosomes separate, so each daughter cell gets 8 chromosomes (each with two chromatids).

  4. A cell division in which the chromosome number is halved is called ______.

    1. amitosis
    2. cytokinesis
    3. meiosis
    4. mitosis
    Show answer

    Answer: meiosis

    Meiosis forms haploid gametes; mitosis keeps the chromosome number the same.

  5. The stage of mitosis in which chromosome morphology is most easily studied, with all chromosomes lying at the equator, is ______.

    1. anaphase
    2. metaphase
    3. telophase
    4. prophase
    Show answer

    Answer: metaphase

    At metaphase, condensed chromosomes align on the metaphase plate, making them ideal for study.

  6. The X-shaped points where crossing over has taken place, seen during diplotene, are called ______.

    1. chiasmata
    2. kinetochores
    3. centromeres
    4. synapses
    Show answer

    Answer: chiasmata

    The homologous chromosomes stay joined at the chiasmata as they start to separate.

  7. The division of the cytoplasm after the nucleus has divided is called ______.

    1. interphase
    2. karyokinesis
    3. synapsis
    4. cytokinesis
    Show answer

    Answer: cytokinesis

    Karyokinesis is the division of the nucleus.

  8. In mitosis, chromosomes condense and become visible during ______.

    1. metaphase
    2. prophase
    3. telophase
    4. anaphase
    Show answer

    Answer: prophase

    Prophase follows the S and G2 phases of interphase.
