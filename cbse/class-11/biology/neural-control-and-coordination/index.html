A few questions from this chapter. Open each one to see the answer.

  1. The tract of nerve fibres that connects the left and right cerebral hemispheres is the ______.

    1. thalamus
    2. corpus callosum
    3. pons
    4. medulla
    Show answer

    Answer: corpus callosum

    The cerebrum is divided into two hemispheres connected by the corpus callosum.

  2. Myelinated nerve fibres of the peripheral nervous system are enveloped by ______ cells, which form the myelin sheath.

    1. Kupffer
    2. Sertoli
    3. Leydig
    4. Schwann
    Show answer

    Answer: Schwann

    Schwann cells form the myelin sheath around the axon of myelinated fibres.

  3. Which part of the forebrain contains centres that control body temperature and the urge for eating and drinking?

    1. hypothalamus
    2. thalamus
    3. medulla oblongata
    4. cerebellum
    Show answer

    Answer: hypothalamus

    The hypothalamus, at the base of the thalamus, controls body temperature, eating and drinking, and also secretes hypothalamic hormones.

  4. The dorsal portion of the midbrain consists mainly of four round swellings called the ______.

    1. corpora allata
    2. corpora quadrigemina
    3. corpora cavernosa
    4. corpus striatum
    Show answer

    Answer: corpora quadrigemina

    The dorsal midbrain has four round swellings (lobes) called corpora quadrigemina.

  5. The junction through which a nerve impulse is transmitted from one neuron to another is called a ______.

    1. synapse
    2. axon hillock
    3. node of Ranvier
    4. dendrite
    Show answer

    Answer: synapse

    A synapse is formed by the membranes of a pre-synaptic and a post-synaptic neuron; it may be electrical or chemical.

  6. Bipolar neurons, with one axon and one dendrite, are found in the ______ of the eye.

    1. iris
    2. cornea
    3. retina
    4. lens
    Show answer

    Answer: retina

    NCERT: bipolar neurons are found in the retina; multipolar in the cerebral cortex; unipolar in the embryonic stage.

  7. The midbrain, pons and medulla oblongata together form the ______.

    1. brain stem
    2. limbic system
    3. cerebellum
    4. forebrain
    Show answer

    Answer: brain stem

    Midbrain and hindbrain (pons and medulla) form the brain stem, connecting the brain to the spinal cord.

  8. At rest, the axonal membrane is comparatively more permeable to which ion?

    1. sodium
    2. potassium
    3. chloride
    4. calcium
    Show answer

    Answer: potassium

    The resting axonal membrane is more permeable to K⁺ and nearly impermeable to Na⁺, so the axoplasm has high K⁺ and the inside is negative.
