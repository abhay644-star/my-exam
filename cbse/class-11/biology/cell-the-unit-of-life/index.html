A few questions from this chapter. Open each one to see the answer.

  1. The cell theory was formulated by Schleiden and ______.

    1. Brown
    2. Virchow
    3. Hooke
    4. Schwann
    Show answer

    Answer: Schwann

    Matthias Schleiden and Theodore Schwann together formulated the cell theory; Virchow later modified it.

  2. Rudolf Virchow explained that cells divide and new cells are formed from pre-existing cells: Omnis cellula-e ______.

    1. vivo
    2. ovo
    3. cellula
    4. corpore
    Show answer

    Answer: cellula

    Virchow's statement Omnis cellula-e cellula gave the cell theory its final shape.

  3. A chromosome has its centromere close to one end, forming one extremely short and one very long arm. It is called a(n) ______ chromosome.

    1. acrocentric
    2. telocentric
    3. metacentric
    4. sub-metacentric
    Show answer

    Answer: acrocentric

    In acrocentric chromosomes the centromere is near the end; in telocentric ones it is at the terminal end.

  4. A prokaryotic 70S ribosome is made of two subunits. What is the size of the larger subunit (in Svedberg units)?

    1. 40 S
    2. 50 S
    3. 60 S
    4. 30 S
    Show answer

    Answer: 50 S

    A 70S ribosome consists of a 50S and a 30S subunit; S values are not additive because they depend on shape as well as size.

  5. The organelle that is the site of aerobic respiration and produces most of the cell's ATP is the ______.

    1. lysosome
    2. Golgi body
    3. mitochondrion
    4. ribosome
    Show answer

    Answer: mitochondrion

    Mitochondria are called the powerhouses of the cell.

  6. Membrane-bound vesicles formed by the Golgi apparatus that are rich in hydrolytic enzymes are called ______.

    1. lysosomes
    2. ribosomes
    3. vacuoles
    4. peroxisomes
    Show answer

    Answer: lysosomes

    Lysosomes contain hydrolases (lipases, proteases, carbohydrases) that are active at acidic pH.

  7. Which organelles form the basal bodies of cilia and flagella, and the spindle apparatus in animal cells?

    1. nucleoli
    2. ribosomes
    3. centrioles
    4. lysosomes
    Show answer

    Answer: centrioles

    Centrioles have a cartwheel-like arrangement of nine peripheral triplets of tubulin.

  8. Cells that have no membrane-bound nucleus are called ______ cells.

    1. eukaryotic
    2. animal
    3. prokaryotic
    4. plant
    Show answer

    Answer: prokaryotic

    Bacteria, blue-green algae, mycoplasma and PPLO are prokaryotes.
