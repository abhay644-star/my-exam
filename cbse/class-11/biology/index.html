Sample questions

A few questions from the Class 11 Biology bank. Every test picks a fresh mix.

  1. The Living World
    The scientific name of mango is Mangifera ______.

    1. sapiens
    2. tuberosum
    3. leo
    4. indica
    Show answer

    Answer: indica

    In binomial nomenclature, Mangifera is the genus and indica is the specific epithet of mango.

  2. Biological Classification
    The five kingdom classification was proposed by ______.

    1. R.H. Whittaker
    2. Haeckel
    3. Linnaeus
    4. Darwin
    Show answer

    Answer: R.H. Whittaker

    R.H. Whittaker (1969) proposed the kingdoms Monera, Protista, Fungi, Plantae and Animalia.

  3. Plant Kingdom
    In bryophytes, the main plant body is the haploid ______.

    1. sporophyte
    2. zygote
    3. gametophyte
    4. embryo
    Show answer

    Answer: gametophyte

    The dominant plant body of bryophytes is the haploid gametophyte; the sporophyte is attached to it.

  4. Animal Kingdom
    Animals having a body cavity lined by mesoderm are called ______.

    1. coelomates
    2. acoelomates
    3. diploblastic
    4. pseudocoelomates
    Show answer

    Answer: coelomates

    A true coelom is a body cavity lined by mesoderm; animals possessing it are coelomates, such as annelids and chordates.

  5. Morphology of Flowering Plants
    In pea, leaflets are modified into thread-like structures for climbing called ______.

    1. tendrils
    2. thorns
    3. spines
    4. phyllodes
    Show answer

    Answer: tendrils

    In pea, some leaflets are modified into tendrils that coil around supports to help the plant climb.
