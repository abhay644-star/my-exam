A few questions from this chapter. Open each one to see the answer.

  1. Glycolysis takes place in the ______ of the cell.

    1. cytoplasm
    2. chloroplast
    3. mitochondria
    4. nucleus
    Show answer

    Answer: cytoplasm

    Glycolysis (EMP pathway) occurs in the cytoplasm of all living organisms.

  2. What is the respiratory quotient (RQ) when carbohydrates are completely oxidised?

    1. 0.5
    2. 1.5
    3. 0.7
    4. 1
    Show answer

    Answer: 1

    For glucose, 6 CO2 are released and 6 O2 consumed, so RQ = 6/6 = 1.

  3. For the fat tripalmitin: 2 C51H98O6 + 145 O2 gives 102 CO2 + 98 H2O + energy. Calculate the RQ (to one decimal place).

    1. 1.42
    2. 1
    3. 0.7
    4. 0.5
    Show answer

    Answer: 0.7

    RQ = CO2 evolved / O2 consumed = 102/145 = 0.7.

  4. Glycolysis breaks one molecule of glucose into two molecules of ______.

    1. citric acid
    2. pyruvic acid
    3. lactic acid
    4. ethanol
    Show answer

    Answer: pyruvic acid

    Glycolysis takes place in the cytoplasm of all living organisms.

  5. Glycolysis is also called the EMP pathway, after Embden, Meyerhof and ______.

    1. Parnas
    2. Krebs
    3. Hatch
    4. Calvin
    Show answer

    Answer: Parnas

    It was worked out by these three scientists.

  6. What is the approximate respiratory quotient (RQ) when proteins are used as the respiratory substrate?

    1. 1.5
    2. 1
    3. 0.9
    4. 0.7
    Show answer

    Answer: 0.9

    RQ is about 1 for carbohydrates, 0.7 for fats and 0.9 for proteins.

  7. The Krebs cycle takes place in the ______ of the mitochondria.

    1. matrix
    2. outer membrane
    3. cristae
    4. intermembrane space
    Show answer

    Answer: matrix

    The electron transport system is on the inner mitochondrial membrane.

  8. The Krebs cycle is also called the ______ cycle.

    1. Calvin
    2. citric acid
    3. C4
    4. urea
    Show answer

    Answer: citric acid

    It begins when acetyl CoA combines with oxaloacetic acid to form citric acid.
