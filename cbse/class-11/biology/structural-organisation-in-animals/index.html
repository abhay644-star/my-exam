A few questions from this chapter. Open each one to see the answer.

  1. The tissue that covers body surfaces and lines body cavities, ducts and tubes is called ______ tissue.

    1. muscular
    2. epithelial
    3. connective
    4. neural
    Show answer

    Answer: epithelial

    Epithelial tissue has a free surface facing a body fluid or the outside environment and provides a covering or lining for parts of the body.

  2. The inner surface of hollow organs such as bronchioles and fallopian tubes is lined by ______ epithelium.

    1. ciliated
    2. keratinised
    3. squamous
    4. compound
    Show answer

    Answer: ciliated

    Columnar or cuboidal cells bearing cilia form ciliated epithelium; the cilia move particles or mucus in a specific direction, as in bronchioles and fallopian tubes.

  3. How many chambers does the heart of a frog have?

    1. 4
    2. 3
    3. 2
    4. 5
    Show answer

    Answer: 3

    The frog heart is three-chambered: two atria and one ventricle, covered by the pericardium.

  4. In frog, the special venous connection between the kidneys and the lower parts of the body is called the ______ portal system.

    1. hepatic
    2. pulmonary
    3. hypophyseal
    4. renal
    Show answer

    Answer: renal

    Frogs have a hepatic portal system (liver–intestine) and a renal portal system (kidney–lower body).

  5. The most abundant and widely distributed tissue in the body of complex animals is ______ tissue.

    1. epithelial
    2. muscular
    3. connective
    4. neural
    Show answer

    Answer: connective

    Connective tissues are the most abundant and widely distributed; they link and support other tissues and organs.

  6. Which type of cell junction helps to stop substances from leaking across a tissue?

    1. tight junctions
    2. gap junctions
    3. adhering junctions
    4. desmosomes
    Show answer

    Answer: tight junctions

    Tight junctions stop leakage across a tissue; adhering junctions cement neighbouring cells, and gap junctions connect the cytoplasm of adjoining cells.

  7. Cartilage, bone and blood are grouped as ______ connective tissues.

    1. specialised
    2. areolar
    3. loose
    4. dense regular
    Show answer

    Answer: specialised

    NCERT classifies connective tissue into loose, dense and specialised types; cartilage, bones and blood are specialised connective tissues.

  8. In male frogs, the 10–12 vasa efferentia arising from the testes enter the kidneys and open into ______, which communicates with the urinogenital duct.

    1. Bidder's canal
    2. Stenson's duct
    3. Mullerian duct
    4. Wolffian duct
    Show answer

    Answer: Bidder's canal

    Vasa efferentia enter the kidneys and open into Bidder's canal, which finally communicates with the urinogenital duct opening into the cloaca.
