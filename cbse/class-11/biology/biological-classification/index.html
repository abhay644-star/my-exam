A few questions from this chapter. Open each one to see the answer.

  1. The five kingdom classification was proposed by ______.

    1. R.H. Whittaker
    2. Haeckel
    3. Linnaeus
    4. Darwin
    Show answer

    Answer: R.H. Whittaker

    R.H. Whittaker (1969) proposed the kingdoms Monera, Protista, Fungi, Plantae and Animalia.

  2. Fungi commonly known as sac fungi belong to the class ______.

    1. Phycomycetes
    2. Ascomycetes
    3. Basidiomycetes
    4. Deuteromycetes
    Show answer

    Answer: Ascomycetes

    Ascomycetes produce sexual spores called ascospores inside sac-like asci, hence the name sac fungi.

  3. Viroids, discovered by T.O. Diener, are free RNA that lack the ______ coat found in viruses.

    1. lipid
    2. protein
    3. carbohydrate
    4. cellulose
    Show answer

    Answer: protein

    A virus has nucleic acid enclosed in a protein coat (capsid), but a viroid is only free RNA of low molecular weight.

  4. In the five kingdom classification, bacteria belong to kingdom ______.

    1. Plantae
    2. Monera
    3. Fungi
    4. Protista
    Show answer

    Answer: Monera

    Monera are prokaryotes: bacteria, including cyanobacteria.

  5. Organisms that completely lack a cell wall, are the smallest living cells known and can survive without oxygen are ______.

    1. cyanobacteria
    2. viroids
    3. diatoms
    4. mycoplasma
    Show answer

    Answer: mycoplasma

    Mycoplasma lack a cell wall, are the smallest known living cells and many are pathogenic in plants and animals.

  6. Diatomaceous earth, used in polishing and filtration of oils and syrups, is formed from the cell walls of which protists?

    1. dinoflagellates
    2. diatoms
    3. slime moulds
    4. euglenoids
    Show answer

    Answer: diatoms

    Diatoms have silica-containing cell walls that do not decay easily, so their deposits form diatomaceous earth.

  7. Which kingdom includes all single-celled eukaryotes?

    1. Animalia
    2. Fungi
    3. Protista
    4. Monera
    Show answer

    Answer: Protista

    Chrysophytes, dinoflagellates, euglenoids, slime moulds and protozoans are protists.

  8. Bread mould (Rhizopus) and Mucor belong to the class ______.

    1. Basidiomycetes
    2. Phycomycetes
    3. Deuteromycetes
    4. Ascomycetes
    Show answer

    Answer: Phycomycetes

    Phycomycetes have aseptate, coenocytic mycelium.
