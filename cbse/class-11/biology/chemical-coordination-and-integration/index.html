A few questions from this chapter. Open each one to see the answer.

  1. The pineal gland secretes the hormone ______.

    1. melatonin
    2. oxytocin
    3. calcitonin
    4. thymosin
    Show answer

    Answer: melatonin

    Melatonin regulates the 24-hour (diurnal) rhythm of the body, such as the sleep-wake cycle.

  2. Deficiency of iodine in the diet results in hypothyroidism and enlargement of the thyroid gland, commonly called ______.

    1. goitre
    2. acromegaly
    3. Graves' disease
    4. Addison's disease
    Show answer

    Answer: goitre

    Iodine is essential for thyroid hormone synthesis; its deficiency causes hypothyroidism and enlargement of the thyroid (goitre).

  3. In the human body, the main glucocorticoid is ______.

    1. adrenaline
    2. thyroxine
    3. cortisol
    4. aldosterone
    Show answer

    Answer: cortisol

    Cortisol is the main glucocorticoid; aldosterone is the main mineralocorticoid.

  4. Which hypothalamic hormone inhibits the release of growth hormone from the pituitary?

    1. oxytocin
    2. prolactin
    3. GnRH
    4. somatostatin
    Show answer

    Answer: somatostatin

    Hypothalamic hormones may be releasing or inhibiting; somatostatin from the hypothalamus inhibits growth hormone release.

  5. Which hormone from the β-cells of the pancreas lowers blood glucose (hypoglycemic hormone)?

    1. glucagon
    2. cortisol
    3. insulin
    4. adrenaline
    Show answer

    Answer: insulin

    Insulin promotes cellular glucose uptake and glycogenesis; glucagon from α-cells is hyperglycemic.

  6. Parathyroid hormone increases Ca²⁺ levels in the blood, so it is a ______ hormone.

    1. hypoglycemic
    2. hypocalcemic
    3. hyperglycemic
    4. hypercalcemic
    Show answer

    Answer: hypercalcemic

    PTH stimulates bone resorption, Ca²⁺ reabsorption by renal tubules and Ca²⁺ absorption from digested food, raising blood Ca²⁺.

  7. Underproduction of hormones by the adrenal cortex alters carbohydrate metabolism, causing acute weakness and fatigue. This disease is called ______.

    1. diabetes insipidus
    2. Graves' disease
    3. Cushing's syndrome
    4. Addison's disease
    Show answer

    Answer: Addison's disease

    NCERT: underproduction of adrenal cortex hormones leads to Addison's disease.

  8. The pars intermedia of the pituitary secretes only one hormone, which acts on melanocytes and regulates skin pigmentation. Name it.

    1. TSH
    2. MSH
    3. ACTH
    4. LH
    Show answer

    Answer: MSH

    Pars intermedia secretes MSH (melanocyte stimulating hormone); in humans it is almost merged with pars distalis.
