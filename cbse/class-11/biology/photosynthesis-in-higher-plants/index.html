A few questions from this chapter. Open each one to see the answer.

  1. The first stable product of the Calvin cycle (C3 pathway) is ______.

    1. malic acid
    2. oxaloacetic acid
    3. 3-phosphoglyceric acid
    4. RuBP
    Show answer

    Answer: 3-phosphoglyceric acid

    CO2 combines with RuBP to form two molecules of the 3-carbon compound 3-PGA.

  2. In C4 plants, the primary CO2 acceptor in mesophyll cells is ______.

    1. PEP
    2. pyruvate
    3. RuBP
    4. OAA
    Show answer

    Answer: PEP

    PEP (a 3-carbon molecule) accepts CO2 with the help of the enzyme PEPcase to form oxaloacetic acid.

  3. How many molecules of ATP are required to make one molecule of glucose through the Calvin cycle?

    1. 6
    2. 30
    3. 12
    4. 18
    Show answer

    Answer: 18

    Each CO2 fixed needs 3 ATP and 2 NADPH; 6 CO2 are fixed per glucose, so 6 × 3 = 18 ATP.

  4. The enzyme that catalyses the carboxylation of RuBP in the Calvin cycle is ______.

    1. hexokinase
    2. ATP synthase
    3. PEP carboxylase
    4. RuBisCO
    Show answer

    Answer: RuBisCO

    RuBisCO is the most abundant enzyme in the world and fixes CO2 to RuBP.

  5. The special leaf anatomy of C4 plants with large bundle sheath cells around vascular bundles is called ______ anatomy.

    1. Isobilateral
    2. Dorsiventral
    3. Kranz
    4. Palisade
    Show answer

    Answer: Kranz

    Kranz means wreath, reflecting the arrangement of bundle sheath cells around the vascular bundles.

  6. How many molecules of CO2 must be fixed in the Calvin cycle to make one molecule of glucose?

    1. 18
    2. 12
    3. 3
    4. 6
    Show answer

    Answer: 6

    Glucose has six carbon atoms, so six turns of the cycle are needed.

  7. The light reactions of photosynthesis take place in the ______ of the chloroplast.

    1. thylakoids
    2. matrix
    3. stroma
    4. outer membrane
    Show answer

    Answer: thylakoids

    The pigments and electron carriers are in the thylakoid membranes.

  8. The reaction centre of Photosystem I is a chlorophyll a molecule with an absorption peak at ______ nm.

    1. 650
    2. 680
    3. 800
    4. 700
    Show answer

    Answer: 700

    It is called P700; the reaction centre of Photosystem II is P680.
