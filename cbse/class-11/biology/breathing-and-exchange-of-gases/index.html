A few questions from this chapter. Open each one to see the answer.

  1. The cartilaginous flap that covers the glottis during swallowing to prevent food from entering the larynx is the ______.

    1. uvula
    2. diaphragm
    3. epiglottis
    4. pharynx
    Show answer

    Answer: epiglottis

    The epiglottis closes the glottis while swallowing so that food goes into the oesophagus.

  2. The tidal volume of a healthy man is approximately ______ mL.

    1. 2500 mL
    2. 1100 mL
    3. 500 mL
    4. 1000 mL
    Show answer

    Answer: 500 mL

    Tidal volume is the volume of air inspired or expired during normal respiration, about 500 mL.

  3. A person has tidal volume 500 mL, inspiratory reserve volume 2500 mL and expiratory reserve volume 1000 mL. Calculate the vital capacity.

    1. 4000 mL
    2. 5200 mL
    3. 3000 mL
    4. 3500 mL
    Show answer

    Answer: 4000 mL

    Vital capacity = ERV + TV + IRV = 1000 + 500 + 2500 = 4000 mL.

  4. The thin-walled air sacs in the lungs, where gases are exchanged, are called ______.

    1. trachea
    2. bronchi
    3. alveoli
    4. bronchioles
    Show answer

    Answer: alveoli

    Alveoli give a very large, thin, moist surface surrounded by capillaries.

  5. About 97% of the oxygen in blood is carried by red blood cells as ______.

    1. oxyhaemoglobin
    2. bicarbonate
    3. carbaminohaemoglobin
    4. carbonic acid
    Show answer

    Answer: oxyhaemoglobin

    The remaining 3% is dissolved in the plasma.

  6. The volume of air that remains in the lungs even after a forcible expiration is called the ______ volume.

    1. residual
    2. tidal
    3. inspiratory reserve
    4. expiratory reserve
    Show answer

    Answer: residual

    It is about 1100–1200 mL.

  7. The dome-shaped muscle that separates the thoracic cavity from the abdominal cavity is the ______.

    1. pharynx
    2. pleura
    3. diaphragm
    4. larynx
    Show answer

    Answer: diaphragm

    The diaphragm contracts during inspiration, increasing the volume of the chest.

  8. About 70% of the carbon dioxide in blood is carried as ______.

    1. carbonic anhydrase
    2. carbaminohaemoglobin
    3. oxyhaemoglobin
    4. bicarbonate
    Show answer

    Answer: bicarbonate

    About 20–25% is carried as carbaminohaemoglobin and about 7% dissolved in plasma.
