  1. Find the mean and the variance of: 4, 19, 17, 17, 18.

    Show answer and solution
    • (a) Mean 15
    • (b) Variance (to 2 decimal places if needed) 30.8

    Mean = 75 ÷ 5 = 15; variance = Σ(x − 15)² ÷ 5 = 154 ÷ 5 = 30.8.

  2. Find the mean and the variance of: 14, 13, 18, 2, 13.

    Show answer and solution
    • (a) Mean 12
    • (b) Variance (to 2 decimal places if needed) 28.4

    Mean = 60 ÷ 5 = 12; variance = Σ(x − 12)² ÷ 5 = 142 ÷ 5 = 28.4.
