  1. Find the coefficient of x4 in the expansion of (1 + 3x)8.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 5670

    The term is 8C4(3x)4, so the coefficient is 70 × 81 = 5670.

  2. Find the coefficient of x3 in the expansion of (1 + 2x)9.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 672

    The term is 9C3(2x)3, so the coefficient is 84 × 8 = 672.

  3. Find the coefficient of x2 in the expansion of (1 + 2x)8.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 112

    The term is 8C2(2x)2, so the coefficient is 28 × 4 = 112.

  4. Find the coefficient of x2 in the expansion of (1 + x)8.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 28

    The term is 8C2(x)2, so the coefficient is 28 × 1 = 28.

  5. Find the coefficient of x4 in the expansion of (1 + 3x)5.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 405

    The term is 5C4(3x)4, so the coefficient is 5 × 81 = 405.

  6. Find the coefficient of x2 in the expansion of (1 + 2x)6.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 60

    The term is 6C2(2x)2, so the coefficient is 15 × 4 = 60.
