  1. n(A) = 21, n(B) = 21 and n(A ∩ B) = 4. Find n(A ∪ B).

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 38

    n(A ∪ B) = n(A) + n(B) − n(A ∩ B) = 21 + 21 − 4 = 38
