A few questions from this chapter. Open each one to see the answer.

  1. Find 8C4.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 70

    8! / (4! × 4!) = 70

  2. Consider the letters of the word COMMITTEE.

    Show answer and solution
    • (a) How many different arrangements of all the letters are there? 45360
    • (b) How many of these arrangements begin with a vowel? 20160

    (a) 9! divided by the factorials of the repeated letters = 45360. (b) Put each different vowel first in turn and arrange the remaining letters: 20160.

  3. Find 8P3.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 336

    8! / 5! = 336

  4. Consider the letters of the word ASSESS.

    Show answer and solution
    • (a) How many different arrangements of all the letters are there? 30
    • (b) How many of these arrangements begin with a vowel? 10

    (a) 6! divided by the factorials of the repeated letters = 30. (b) Put each different vowel first in turn and arrange the remaining letters: 10.

  5. Find 10P4.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 5040

    10! / 6! = 5040

  6. Find 10P3.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 720

    10! / 7! = 720
