A few questions from this chapter. Open each one to see the answer.

  1. Find the slope of the line through (−4, −4) and (−6, −6).

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 1

    m = (−6 − (−4)) / (−6 − (−4)) = 1

  2. A straight line passes through (1, 6) and (3, 10).

    Show answer and solution
    • (a) Find its slope. 2
    • (b) Find its y-intercept. 4
    • (c) Find its x-intercept. −2

    (a) m = (10 − 6) / (3 − 1) = 2. (b) y = 2x + c through (1, 6) gives c = 4. (c) Putting y = 0 gives x = −2.

  3. Find the slope of the line through (−1, −2) and (1, 4).

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 3

    m = (4 − (−2)) / (1 − (−1)) = 3

  4. A straight line passes through (−3, −3) and (−1, −5).

    Show answer and solution
    • (a) Find its slope. −1
    • (b) Find its y-intercept. −6
    • (c) Find its x-intercept. −6

    (a) m = (−5 − (−3)) / (−1 − (−3)) = −1. (b) y = −1x + c through (−3, −3) gives c = −6. (c) Putting y = 0 gives x = −6.

  5. Find the slope of the line through (3, −2) and (6, 2).

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 4/3

    m = (2 − (−2)) / (6 − 3) = 4/3

  6. A straight line passes through (−3, 5) and (−1, 3).

    Show answer and solution
    • (a) Find its slope. −1
    • (b) Find its y-intercept. 2
    • (c) Find its x-intercept. 2

    (a) m = (3 − 5) / (−1 − (−3)) = −1. (b) y = −1x + c through (−3, 5) gives c = 2. (c) Putting y = 0 gives x = 2.
