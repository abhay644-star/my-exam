  1. The SI unit of amount of substance is the ______.

    1. gram
    2. litre
    3. kilogram
    4. mole
    Show answer

    Answer: mole

    One mole contains 6.022 × 10^23 elementary entities (Avogadro's number).

  2. Calculate the molar mass of sulphuric acid, H2SO4 (H = 1, S = 32, O = 16).

    1. 49 g/mol
    2. 98 g/mol
    3. 96 g/mol
    4. 100 g/mol
    Show answer

    Answer: 98 g/mol

    2(1) + 32 + 4(16) = 98 g/mol.

  3. 4 g of NaOH is dissolved in water to make 250 mL of solution. Find its molarity (Na = 23, O = 16, H = 1).

    1. 0.1 M
    2. 0.4 M
    3. 4 M
    4. 0.016 M
    Show answer

    Answer: 0.4 M

    Moles = 4/40 = 0.1 mol; molarity = 0.1/0.25 L = 0.4 M.

  4. What is the molar mass of water, H2O (H = 1, O = 16)?

    1. 16 g/mol
    2. 10 g/mol
    3. 20 g/mol
    4. 18 g/mol
    Show answer

    Answer: 18 g/mol

    2 × 1 + 16 = 18 g/mol.

  5. How many moles of water are present in 36 g of water?

    1. 1 mol
    2. 2 mol
    3. 0.5 mol
    4. 3 mol
    Show answer

    Answer: 2 mol

    Molar mass of H2O = 18 g/mol, so moles = 36/18 = 2 mol.

  6. A compound contains 40% carbon, 6.67% hydrogen and 53.33% oxygen by mass. What is its empirical formula?

    1. CH3O
    2. C2H4O2
    3. CH2O
    4. CHO
    Show answer

    Answer: CH2O

    Mole ratio C : H : O = 3.33 : 6.67 : 3.33 = 1 : 2 : 1, giving CH2O.

  7. A pure substance made up of only one kind of atom is called an ______.

    1. mixture
    2. element
    3. compound
    4. alloy
    Show answer

    Answer: element

    Elements such as oxygen and iron cannot be broken into simpler substances by chemical means.

  8. How many moles are present in 4.4 g of carbon dioxide, CO2 (C = 12, O = 16)?

    1. 0.2 mol
    2. 1 mol
    3. 0.1 mol
    4. 4.4 mol
    Show answer

    Answer: 0.1 mol

    Molar mass of CO2 = 44 g/mol, so moles = 4.4 ÷ 44 = 0.1 mol.
