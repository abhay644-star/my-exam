A few questions from this chapter. Open each one to see the answer.

  1. A bond formed by mutual sharing of electrons between two atoms is called a/an ______ bond.

    1. hydrogen
    2. covalent
    3. metallic
    4. ionic
    Show answer

    Answer: covalent

    In a covalent bond, each atom contributes electrons to a shared pair, as in H2 or Cl2.

  2. According to VSEPR theory, the shape of the methane (CH4) molecule is ______.

    1. pyramidal
    2. tetrahedral
    3. trigonal planar
    4. square planar
    Show answer

    Answer: tetrahedral

    Four bond pairs and no lone pairs around carbon arrange tetrahedrally with bond angles of 109.5°.

  3. According to molecular orbital theory, what is the bond order of the O2 molecule?

    1. 3
    2. 2
    3. 1
    4. 2.5
    Show answer

    Answer: 2

    O2 has 10 bonding and 6 antibonding valence electrons, so bond order = (10 − 6)/2 = 2.

  4. How many sigma (σ) bonds are present in a molecule of but-1-en-3-yne, CH2=CH–C≡CH?

    1. 7
    2. 3
    3. 10
    4. 9
    Show answer

    Answer: 7

    There are 4 C–H σ bonds and 3 C–C σ bonds (one in each of C=C, C–C and C≡C), giving 7 σ bonds and 3 π bonds.

  5. A bond formed by the complete transfer of electrons from one atom to another is called an ______ bond.

    1. ionic
    2. metallic
    3. coordinate
    4. covalent
    Show answer

    Answer: ionic

    NaCl is held together by ionic bonds between Na+ and Cl- ions.

  6. What is the H–O–H bond angle in a water molecule?

    1. 120 °
    2. 109.5 °
    3. 104.5 °
    4. 107 °
    Show answer

    Answer: 104.5 °

    Two lone pairs on oxygen repel the bond pairs more strongly, reducing the angle from 109.5° to 104.5°.

  7. What is the hybridisation of each carbon atom in ethyne (HC≡CH)?

    1. sp3
    2. dsp2
    3. sp
    4. sp2
    Show answer

    Answer: sp

    Each carbon forms two σ bonds and two π bonds, using two sp hybrid orbitals; the molecule is linear.

  8. By the octet rule, atoms tend to gain, lose or share electrons to have how many electrons in their outermost shell?

    1. 6
    2. 2
    3. 18
    4. 8
    Show answer

    Answer: 8

    Eight valence electrons give a noble gas configuration.
