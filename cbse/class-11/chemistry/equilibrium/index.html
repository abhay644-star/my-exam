A few questions from this chapter. Open each one to see the answer.

  1. What is the pH of pure water at 298 K?

    1. 14
    2. 1
    3. 7
    4. 0
    Show answer

    Answer: 7

    In pure water [H3O+] = 10^-7 M at 298 K, so pH = 7.

  2. Find the pH of a 0.01 M HCl solution.

    1. 1
    2. 12
    3. 0.01
    4. 2
    Show answer

    Answer: 2

    HCl is a strong acid, so [H+] = 10^-2 M and pH = −log(10^-2) = 2.

  3. Find the pH of a 0.001 M NaOH solution at 298 K.

    1. 11
    2. 10
    3. 13
    4. 3
    Show answer

    Answer: 11

    [OH-] = 10^-3 M, so pOH = 3 and pH = 14 − 3 = 11.

  4. At equilibrium, the rates of the forward and backward reactions are ______.

    1. different
    2. maximum
    3. equal
    4. zero
    Show answer

    Answer: equal

    Equilibrium is dynamic: both reactions continue at the same rate.

  5. By Le Chatelier's principle, adding more reactant to a system at equilibrium shifts the equilibrium in the ______ direction.

    1. neither
    2. to the left
    3. forward
    4. backward
    Show answer

    Answer: forward

    The system tries to consume the added reactant, so more products are formed.

  6. A solution that resists a change in pH when a small amount of acid or base is added is called a ______ solution.

    1. standard
    2. buffer
    3. neutral
    4. saturated
    Show answer

    Answer: buffer

    A mixture of acetic acid and sodium acetate is a buffer.

  7. A substance like water that can act both as an acid and as a base is called ______.

    1. an indicator
    2. neutral
    3. amphoteric
    4. a buffer
    Show answer

    Answer: amphoteric

    Water can donate a proton (to NH3) or accept one (from HCl).

  8. Name the conjugate base of water (H2O) in the Bronsted–Lowry sense.

    1. H2O2
    2. OH-
    3. O2
    4. H3O+
    Show answer

    Answer: OH-

    Removing a proton from H2O gives OH-, its conjugate base.
