A few questions from this chapter. Open each one to see the answer.

  1. The neutron was discovered by ______.

    1. Chadwick
    2. Bohr
    3. Rutherford
    4. Thomson
    Show answer

    Answer: Chadwick

    James Chadwick discovered the neutron in 1932 by bombarding beryllium with alpha particles.

  2. How many orbitals are present in a subshell with azimuthal quantum number l = 2?

    1. 3
    2. 7
    3. 5
    4. 2
    Show answer

    Answer: 5

    Number of orbitals = 2l + 1 = 5 (the five d orbitals).

  3. How many unpaired electrons are present in a ground-state iron atom (Z = 26)?

    1. 6
    2. 4
    3. 2
    4. 0
    Show answer

    Answer: 4

    Fe is [Ar] 3d6 4s2; by Hund's rule the six d electrons fill as one pair and four singles, giving 4 unpaired electrons.

  4. What is the maximum number of electrons that can be accommodated in the shell with n = 3?

    1. 32
    2. 18
    3. 9
    4. 8
    Show answer

    Answer: 18

    Maximum electrons in a shell = 2n^2 = 2 × 9 = 18.

  5. By the Pauli exclusion principle, no two electrons in an atom can have the same set of four ______ numbers.

    1. atomic
    2. mass
    3. oxidation
    4. quantum
    Show answer

    Answer: quantum

    So an orbital can hold at most two electrons, with opposite spins.

  6. Chromium (Z = 24) has the configuration [Ar] 3d5 4s1. How many unpaired electrons does it have?

    1. 6
    2. 5
    3. 4
    4. 2
    Show answer

    Answer: 6

    Five in the 3d orbitals and one in 4s: a half-filled d subshell is extra stable.

  7. Electrons were discovered through experiments on ______ rays.

    1. X-
    2. cathode
    3. anode
    4. gamma
    Show answer

    Answer: cathode

    J. J. Thomson measured the charge-to-mass ratio of cathode-ray particles (electrons).

  8. By Hund's rule, electrons in orbitals of the same subshell do not pair up until each orbital is ______ occupied.

    1. doubly
    2. not
    3. completely
    4. singly
    Show answer

    Answer: singly

    For example, nitrogen has three unpaired electrons in its three 2p orbitals.
