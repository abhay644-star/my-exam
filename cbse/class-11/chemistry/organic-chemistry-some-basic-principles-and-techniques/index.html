A few questions from this chapter. Open each one to see the answer.

  1. Write the IUPAC name of CH3CH2OH.

    1. propanol
    2. ethanal
    3. ethanol
    4. methanol
    Show answer

    Answer: ethanol

    Two carbons (eth-), saturated (-an-) with an –OH group (-ol): ethanol.

  2. What is the hybridisation of the positively charged carbon in a carbocation?

    1. sp
    2. sp2
    3. sp3
    4. sp3d
    Show answer

    Answer: sp2

    The carbocation carbon has three σ bonds and an empty p orbital, so it is sp2 hybridised and trigonal planar.

  3. Which is more stable, a tertiary carbocation or a primary carbocation?

    1. neither
    2. primary
    3. tertiary
    4. both equally
    Show answer

    Answer: tertiary

    Alkyl groups release electrons (by inductive effect and hyperconjugation) and spread the positive charge.

  4. Two miscible liquids with widely different boiling points are separated by simple ______.

    1. distillation
    2. chromatography
    3. sublimation
    4. crystallisation
    Show answer

    Answer: distillation

    The lower-boiling liquid vaporises first, is condensed and collected separately.

  5. The permanent displacement of σ-bond electrons along a carbon chain due to an electronegative atom is called the ______ effect.

    1. hyperconjugation
    2. electromeric
    3. inductive
    4. mesomeric
    Show answer

    Answer: inductive

    In the inductive effect, e.g. in CH3CH2Cl, Cl pulls σ electrons and the effect weakens along the chain.

  6. Nucleophiles are electron-______ species.

    1. rich
    2. deficient
    3. neutral
    4. free
    Show answer

    Answer: rich

    Nucleophiles such as OH- and NH3 donate an electron pair to an electron-deficient centre.

  7. In Lassaigne's test, the presence of nitrogen is shown by the formation of a ______ colour.

    1. Prussian blue
    2. violet
    3. black
    4. blood red
    Show answer

    Answer: Prussian blue

    Sodium cyanide formed in the fusion gives ferric ferrocyanide, which is Prussian blue.

  8. What is the IUPAC name of CH3CH2CH2CHO?

    1. butanol
    2. butanone
    3. butanal
    4. propanal
    Show answer

    Answer: butanal

    Four carbon atoms including the –CHO carbon: butanal.
