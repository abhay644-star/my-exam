Sample questions

A few questions from the Class 11 History bank. Every test picks a fresh mix.

  1. Writing and City Life
    Mesopotamia is the land between the Tigris and the ______ rivers.

    1. Indus
    2. Jordan
    3. Euphrates
    4. Nile
    Show answer

    Answer: Euphrates

    Mesopotamia, in present-day Iraq, lies between the Euphrates and the Tigris; the name means 'land between rivers'.

  2. An Empire Across Three Continents
    The first Roman emperor, whose rule from 27 BCE is called the Principate, was ______.

    1. Julius Caesar
    2. Constantine
    3. Augustus
    4. Tiberius
    Show answer

    Answer: Augustus

    Augustus founded the Principate in 27 BCE, ruling as 'first citizen' while holding real power.

  3. Nomadic Empires
    Genghis Khan was born around 1162 with the name ______.

    1. Ogodei
    2. Batu
    3. Temujin
    4. Jochi
    Show answer

    Answer: Temujin

    Temujin took the title Genghis Khan, 'Oceanic Khan' or 'Universal Ruler', in 1206.

  4. The Three Orders
    In medieval French society, the first order consisted of the ______.

    1. merchants
    2. peasants
    3. nobles
    4. clergy
    Show answer

    Answer: clergy

    Society was divided into three orders: the clergy (first), the nobility (second) and the peasantry (third).

  5. Changing Cultural Traditions
    The Italian artist who painted the 'Mona Lisa' and 'The Last Supper' was ______.

    1. Leonardo da Vinci
    2. Raphael
    3. Titian
    4. Michelangelo
    Show answer

    Answer: Leonardo da Vinci

    Leonardo da Vinci was also deeply interested in botany, anatomy, mathematics and even tried to design a flying machine.
