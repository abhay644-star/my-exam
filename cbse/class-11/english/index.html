Sample questions

A few questions from the Class 11 English bank. Every test picks a fresh mix.

  1. Hornbill: The Portrait of a Lady
    Who is the author of 'The Portrait of a Lady'?

    1. Ruskin Bond
    2. R.K. Narayan
    3. Khushwant Singh
    4. Mulk Raj Anand
    Show answer

    Answer: Khushwant Singh

    'The Portrait of a Lady' is Khushwant Singh's affectionate sketch of his grandmother.

  2. Hornbill: We're Not Afraid to Die
    What was the name of the boat on which the narrator's family sailed around the world?

    1. Mayflower
    2. Wavewalker
    3. Endeavour
    4. Sea Breeze
    Show answer

    Answer: Wavewalker

    The family sailed in the Wavewalker, a 23-metre boat they had prepared for the voyage.

  3. Hornbill: Discovering Tut
    Who discovered the tomb of Tutankhamun in 1922?

    1. Zahi Hawass
    2. Howard Carter
    3. Flinders Petrie
    4. Lord Carnarvon
    Show answer

    Answer: Howard Carter

    British archaeologist Howard Carter discovered Tut's tomb in 1922.

  4. Hornbill: The Ailing Planet
    Who wrote 'The Ailing Planet: the Green Movement's Role'?

    1. A.R. Williams
    2. Nick Middleton
    3. Nani Palkhivala
    4. Jayant Narlikar
    Show answer

    Answer: Nani Palkhivala

    The essay was written by the eminent jurist Nani Palkhivala.

  5. Hornbill: Poems
    Who wrote the poem 'The Voice of the Rain'?

    1. William Wordsworth
    2. John Keats
    3. Walt Whitman
    4. Robert Frost
    Show answer

    Answer: Walt Whitman

    'The Voice of the Rain' is by the American poet Walt Whitman.
