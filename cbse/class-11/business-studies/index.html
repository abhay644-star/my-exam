Sample questions

A few questions from the Class 11 Business Studies bank. Every test picks a fresh mix.

  1. Nature and Purpose of Business
    An occupation in which people render personalised services of a specialised nature, based on professional knowledge and training, is called a ______.

    1. business
    2. profession
    3. trade
    4. employment
    Show answer

    Answer: profession

    Doctors, lawyers and chartered accountants follow a profession, which needs specialised knowledge and membership of a professional body.

  2. Forms of Business Organisation
    In a Joint Hindu Family business, the eldest member who controls and manages the business is called the ______.

    1. partner
    2. karta
    3. proprietor
    4. coparcener
    Show answer

    Answer: karta

    The karta manages the family business and has unlimited liability, while the other coparceners have liability limited to their share.

  3. Private, Public and Global Enterprises
    A government company is one in which not less than ______ percent of the paid-up share capital is held by the government.

    1. 49 %
    2. 75 %
    3. 51 %
    4. 26 %
    Show answer

    Answer: 51 %

    A government company has at least 51% of its paid-up capital held by the Central and/or State Government(s).

  4. Business Services
    The insurance principle that requires both parties to disclose all material facts honestly is the principle of utmost good ______.

    1. contribution
    2. faith
    3. indemnity
    4. subrogation
    Show answer

    Answer: faith

    Uberrimae fidei (utmost good faith) means the insured must disclose all material facts, otherwise the contract can be avoided.

  5. Emerging Modes of Business
    In online shopping, the payment method in which the buyer pays in cash when the goods are delivered at the doorstep is called ______.

    1. digital cash
    2. cash on delivery
    3. net banking
    4. credit card
    Show answer

    Answer: cash on delivery

    Cash on delivery lets buyers who are wary of online payment pay only after receiving the goods.
