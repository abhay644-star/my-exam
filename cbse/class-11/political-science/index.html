Sample questions

A few questions from the Class 11 Political Science bank. Every test picks a fresh mix.

  1. Constitution: Why and How?
    Who was the President of the Constituent Assembly of India?

    1. Rajendra Prasad
    2. B.R. Ambedkar
    3. Jawaharlal Nehru
    4. Sachchidananda Sinha
    Show answer

    Answer: Rajendra Prasad

    Dr Rajendra Prasad was elected President of the Constituent Assembly; Sachchidananda Sinha was only its temporary chairman at the first meeting.

  2. Rights in the Indian Constitution
    The Right to Constitutional Remedies, which Dr Ambedkar called the 'heart and soul' of the Constitution, is given in Article ______.

    1. 21
    2. 19
    3. 32
    4. 226
    Show answer

    Answer: 32

    Article 32 allows a citizen to move the Supreme Court directly for the enforcement of Fundamental Rights.

  3. Election and Representation
    The electoral system used for Lok Sabha elections, in which the candidate with the most votes wins, is called the ______ system.

    1. list
    2. single transferable vote
    3. proportional representation
    4. first past the post
    Show answer

    Answer: first past the post

    Under FPTP the candidate with more votes than any other wins, even without a majority of the votes cast.

  4. Executive
    In India's parliamentary system, the nominal executive head of the country is the ______.

    1. Chief Justice
    2. Prime Minister
    3. Vice-President
    4. President
    Show answer

    Answer: President

    The President is the nominal head; real executive power lies with the Prime Minister and the Council of Ministers.

  5. Legislature
    Members of the Rajya Sabha from a state are elected by the elected members of the ______.

    1. Governor
    2. people directly
    3. Lok Sabha
    4. State Legislative Assembly
    Show answer

    Answer: State Legislative Assembly

    Rajya Sabha members are elected indirectly by MLAs, using the single transferable vote.
