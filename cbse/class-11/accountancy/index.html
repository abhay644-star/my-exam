Sample questions

A few questions from the Class 11 Accountancy bank. Every test picks a fresh mix.

  1. Introduction to Accounting
    Customers who owe money to the business for goods sold to them on credit are called ______.

    1. drawings
    2. capital
    3. creditors
    4. debtors
    Show answer

    Answer: debtors

    Debtors (trade receivables) are persons who owe an amount to the business; they are shown as current assets.

  2. Theory Base of Accounting
    The assumption that a business will continue to operate for a long period and will not be closed in the foreseeable future is the ______ concept.

    1. going concern
    2. consistency
    3. money measurement
    4. accrual
    Show answer

    Answer: going concern

    Because of the going concern concept, fixed assets are recorded at cost and depreciated over their useful life.

  3. Recording of Transactions I
    The book in which transactions are first recorded in chronological order is called the ______.

    1. ledger
    2. journal
    3. balance sheet
    4. trial balance
    Show answer

    Answer: journal

    The journal is the book of original entry; entries are later posted to the ledger.

  4. Recording of Transactions II
    The special book in which all cash receipts and cash payments are recorded is the ______.

    1. journal proper
    2. cash book
    3. ledger
    4. purchases book
    Show answer

    Answer: cash book

    The cash book is both a book of original entry and a ledger account for cash.

  5. Bank Reconciliation Statement
    A statement prepared to reconcile the balance as per the cash book with the balance as per the pass book is called a ______.

    1. trial balance
    2. bank reconciliation statement
    3. balance sheet
    4. cash flow statement
    Show answer

    Answer: bank reconciliation statement

    A BRS explains the causes of difference between the bank column of the cash book and the pass book on a date.
