Sample questions

A few questions from the Class 9 English bank. Every test picks a fresh mix.

  1. Verbs and Tenses
    She ______ her grandparents every Diwali. (visit)

    1. has visit
    2. visits
    3. visit
    4. visiting
    Show answer

    Answer: visits

    Habitual actions use the simple present; a singular subject adds '-s'.

  2. Subject–Verb Agreement
    The pair of shoes ______ under the bed.

    1. were
    2. have
    3. are
    4. is
    Show answer

    Answer: is

    The subject is 'pair', which is singular.

  3. Modals
    You ______ not bring a gift; it is optional. (absence of necessity)

    1. should
    2. need
    3. may
    4. must
    Show answer

    Answer: need

    'Need not' shows that something is not necessary.

  4. Articles and Determiners
    ______ students in the class passed; nobody failed.

    1. each
    2. some
    3. all
    4. few
    Show answer

    Answer: all

    'All' means every one of the group.

  5. Active and Passive Voice
    Change into the passive voice: 'They grow tea in Assam.'
    Tea ______ in Assam.

    1. was grown
    2. is grown
    3. is growing
    4. grows
    Show answer

    Answer: is grown

    Simple present passive: is/are + past participle.
