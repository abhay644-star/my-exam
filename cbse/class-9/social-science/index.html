Sample questions

A few questions from the Class 9 Social Science bank. Every test picks a fresh mix.

  1. History: The French Revolution
    On which date was the Bastille stormed in Paris?

    1. 14 July 1789
    2. 4 August 1789
    3. 21 January 1793
    4. 26 August 1789
    Show answer

    Answer: 14 July 1789

    The storming of the Bastille on 14 July 1789 marks the start of the French Revolution.

  2. History: Socialism in Europe and the Russian Revolution
    Who led the Bolshevik Party during the October Revolution of 1917?

    1. Alexander Kerensky
    2. Nicholas II
    3. Joseph Stalin
    4. Vladimir Lenin
    Show answer

    Answer: Vladimir Lenin

    Lenin led the Bolsheviks to seize power in October 1917.

  3. History: Nazism and the Rise of Hitler
    On which date did Hitler become Chancellor of Germany?

    1. 9 November 1923
    2. 1 September 1939
    3. 28 June 1919
    4. 30 January 1933
    Show answer

    Answer: 30 January 1933

    President Hindenburg appointed Hitler Chancellor on 30 January 1933.

  4. Geography: Physical Features of India
    The northernmost and highest range of the Himalayas is called the ______.

    1. Purvachal
    2. Himachal
    3. Shiwaliks
    4. Himadri
    Show answer

    Answer: Himadri

    The Himadri contains the highest peaks, with an average height of about 6,000 m.

  5. Geography: Drainage
    The Bhagirathi and the Alaknanda meet to form the Ganga at which place?

    1. Prayagraj
    2. Haridwar
    3. Devprayag
    4. Rudraprayag
    Show answer

    Answer: Devprayag

    The two headstreams join at Devprayag in Uttarakhand.
