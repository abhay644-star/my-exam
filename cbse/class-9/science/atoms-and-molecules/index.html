  1. One atomic mass unit is defined as exactly one-twelfth of the mass of one atom of ______-12.

    1. carbon
    2. nitrogen
    3. hydrogen
    4. oxygen
    Show answer

    Answer: carbon

    The carbon-12 isotope is the standard reference for atomic masses.

  2. Calculate the molecular mass of H2O (H = 1 u, O = 16 u).

    1. 18 u
    2. 10 u
    3. 17 u
    4. 34 u
    Show answer

    Answer: 18 u

    Molecular mass = 2 × 1 + 16 = 18 u.

  3. Calculate the molecular mass of CO2 (C = 12 u, O = 16 u).

    1. 28 u
    2. 32 u
    3. 44 u
    4. 40 u
    Show answer

    Answer: 44 u

    12 + 2 × 16 = 44 u.

  4. Calculate the formula unit mass of CaCl2 (Ca = 40 u, Cl = 35.5 u).

    1. 111 u
    2. 95.5 u
    3. 75.5 u
    4. 151 u
    Show answer

    Answer: 111 u

    Formula unit mass = 40 + 2 × 35.5 = 111 u.

  5. Which scientist proposed that matter is made of tiny indivisible particles called atoms?

    1. Dalton
    2. Bohr
    3. Rutherford
    4. Thomson
    Show answer

    Answer: Dalton

    John Dalton put forward the atomic theory of matter.

  6. Write the chemical formula of aluminium oxide.

    1. AlO3
    2. AlO
    3. Al2O3
    4. Al3O2
    Show answer

    Answer: Al2O3

    Aluminium has valency 3 and oxygen valency 2; criss-crossing gives Al₂O₃.

  7. Write the chemical formula of magnesium chloride.

    1. Mg2Cl3
    2. MgCl2
    3. MgCl
    4. Mg2Cl
    Show answer

    Answer: MgCl2

    Mg2+ needs two Cl− ions to balance its charge, so the formula is MgCl2.

  8. The law of conservation of mass states that mass can neither be created nor ______ in a chemical reaction.

    1. measured
    2. destroyed
    3. weighed
    4. heated
    Show answer

    Answer: destroyed

    The total mass of the products equals the total mass of the reactants.
