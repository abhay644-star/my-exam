A few questions from this chapter. Open each one to see the answer.

  1. The SI unit of acceleration is ______.

    1. m/s
    2. m²/s
    3. m/s²
    4. N/m
    Show answer

    Answer: m/s²

    Acceleration is change in velocity per second, so its unit is m/s².

  2. A car starting from rest reaches a velocity of 20 m/s in 5 s. What is its acceleration?

    1. 0.25 m/s²
    2. 100 m/s²
    3. 4 m/s²
    4. 25 m/s²
    Show answer

    Answer: 4 m/s²

    a = (v − u) ÷ t = (20 − 0) ÷ 5 = 4 m/s².

  3. A car moving at 15 m/s applies brakes and stops in 3 s with uniform retardation. What distance does it travel while stopping?

    1. 5 m
    2. 15 m
    3. 45 m
    4. 22.5 m
    Show answer

    Answer: 22.5 m

    Distance = average velocity × time = (15 + 0)/2 × 3 = 22.5 m.

  4. A car travels the first 30 km at 60 km/h and the next 30 km at 30 km/h. What is its average speed for the whole journey?

    1. 40 km/h
    2. 35 km/h
    3. 50 km/h
    4. 45 km/h
    Show answer

    Answer: 40 km/h

    Time = 0.5 h + 1 h = 1.5 h, so average speed = 60 km ÷ 1.5 h = 40 km/h.

  5. The shortest distance between the starting and final positions of an object is called its ______.

    1. speed
    2. displacement
    3. path length
    4. distance
    Show answer

    Answer: displacement

    Displacement has a direction; distance does not.

  6. A car covers 120 km in 2 hours. What is its average speed?

    1. 120 km/h
    2. 60 km/h
    3. 30 km/h
    4. 240 km/h
    Show answer

    Answer: 60 km/h

    Average speed = total distance ÷ total time = 120 ÷ 2 = 60 km/h.

  7. An athlete runs one complete round of a 400 m circular track and stops at the starting point. What is the athlete's displacement?

    1. 0 m
    2. 200 m
    3. 800 m
    4. 400 m
    Show answer

    Answer: 0 m

    Displacement is the shortest distance between initial and final positions, which coincide here.

  8. The rate of change of velocity is called ______.

    1. acceleration
    2. displacement
    3. speed
    4. momentum
    Show answer

    Answer: acceleration

    Acceleration = change in velocity ÷ time taken.
