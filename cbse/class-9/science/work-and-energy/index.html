A few questions from this chapter. Open each one to see the answer.

  1. The SI unit of work is the ______.

    1. joule
    2. newton
    3. pascal
    4. watt
    Show answer

    Answer: joule

    Work is measured in joules; 1 J = 1 N × 1 m.

  2. A force of 10 N moves an object through 5 m in the direction of the force. How much work is done?

    1. 2 J
    2. 50 J
    3. 15 J
    4. 0.5 J
    Show answer

    Answer: 50 J

    W = F × s = 10 N × 5 m = 50 J.

  3. What is the kinetic energy of a 2 kg object moving with a velocity of 5 m/s?

    1. 25 J
    2. 5 J
    3. 50 J
    4. 10 J
    Show answer

    Answer: 25 J

    KE = ½mv² = ½ × 2 × 5² = 25 J.

  4. A 2 kg ball is dropped from a height of 20 m. Ignoring air resistance, what is its kinetic energy just before it hits the ground (g = 10 m/s²)?

    1. 200 J
    2. 20 J
    3. 400 J
    4. 40 J
    Show answer

    Answer: 400 J

    All potential energy changes to kinetic energy: mgh = 2 × 10 × 20 = 400 J.

  5. The energy an object has because of its motion is called ______ energy.

    1. potential
    2. heat
    3. kinetic
    4. chemical
    Show answer

    Answer: kinetic

    A moving ball, a flowing river and the wind all have kinetic energy.

  6. Work is done when a force acting on an object causes ______ in the direction of the force.

    1. heat
    2. pressure
    3. displacement
    4. rest
    Show answer

    Answer: displacement

    W = F × s; if there is no displacement, no work is done.

  7. A motor does 6000 J of work in 30 s. What is its power?

    1. 180000 W
    2. 300 W
    3. 200 W
    4. 20 W
    Show answer

    Answer: 200 W

    P = W ÷ t = 6000 ÷ 30 = 200 W.

  8. The SI unit of power is the ______.

    1. newton
    2. joule
    3. watt
    4. volt
    Show answer

    Answer: watt

    1 W is 1 J of energy used per second.
