Practice questions with answers

A few questions from this chapter. Open each one to see the answer.

  1. Which cell organelle is known as the powerhouse of the cell?

    1. mitochondria
    2. nucleus
    3. Golgi apparatus
    4. ribosome
    Show answer

    Answer: mitochondria

    Mitochondria release energy in the form of ATP.

  2. Which cell organelle is known as the 'suicide bag' of the cell?

    1. plastid
    2. lysosome
    3. ribosome
    4. vacuole
    Show answer

    Answer: lysosome

    Lysosomes contain digestive enzymes that can digest the cell itself if it is damaged.

  3. An animal cell is placed in a solution that has a lower concentration of water than the cell (a hypertonic solution). What happens to the cell?

    1. swells
    2. stays the same
    3. shrinks
    4. bursts
    Show answer

    Answer: shrinks

    Water moves out of the cell by osmosis, so the cell shrinks.

  4. Who first observed cells, in a thin slice of cork, in 1665?

    1. Robert Brown
    2. Leeuwenhoek
    3. Robert Hooke
    4. Purkinje
    Show answer

    Answer: Robert Hooke

    Hooke saw small box-like compartments and called them cells.

  5. The movement of water molecules through a selectively permeable membrane from a dilute to a concentrated solution is called ______.

    1. diffusion
    2. osmosis
    3. endocytosis
    4. plasmolysis
    Show answer

    Answer: osmosis

    Osmosis is the special case of diffusion of water through a selectively permeable membrane.

  6. Thread-like structures in the nucleus that carry genes and are seen during cell division are called ______.

    1. ribosomes
    2. plastids
    3. chromosomes
    4. centrosomes
    Show answer

    Answer: chromosomes

    Chromosomes are made of DNA and protein.

  7. The outermost covering of an animal cell is the ______.

    1. nucleus
    2. plasma membrane
    3. cytoplasm
    4. cell wall
    Show answer

    Answer: plasma membrane

    The plasma membrane controls what enters and leaves the cell; animal cells have no cell wall.

  8. Plastids that contain chlorophyll are called ______.

    1. chloroplasts
    2. leucoplasts
    3. vacuoles
    4. chromoplasts
    Show answer

    Answer: chloroplasts

    Chloroplasts carry out photosynthesis; leucoplasts store starch, oil and protein.
