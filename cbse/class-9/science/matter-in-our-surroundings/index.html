A few questions from this chapter. Open each one to see the answer.

  1. The change of a solid directly into vapour without passing through the liquid state is called ______.

    1. evaporation
    2. fusion
    3. sublimation
    4. condensation
    Show answer

    Answer: sublimation

    Camphor and ammonium chloride sublime on heating.

  2. The heat energy required to change 1 kg of a solid into liquid at its melting point is called the latent heat of ______.

    1. fusion
    2. sublimation
    3. condensation
    4. vaporisation
    Show answer

    Answer: fusion

    Latent heat of fusion is absorbed without any rise in temperature.

  3. A desert cooler cools better on a hot, dry day because dry air increases the rate of ______.

    1. radiation
    2. evaporation
    3. sublimation
    4. condensation
    Show answer

    Answer: evaporation

    Evaporation absorbs heat, and it is faster when humidity is low.

  4. The SI unit of temperature is the ______.

    1. joule
    2. celsius
    3. kelvin
    4. fahrenheit
    Show answer

    Answer: kelvin

    Temperature is measured in kelvin (K) in the SI system.

  5. Convert 300 K into the Celsius scale (use °C = K − 273).

    1. 573 °C
    2. 327 °C
    3. 30 °C
    4. 27 °C
    Show answer

    Answer: 27 °C

    °C = 300 − 273 = 27 °C.

  6. Acetone poured on the palm makes it feel cold, because evaporation causes ______.

    1. heating
    2. sublimation
    3. cooling
    4. condensation
    Show answer

    Answer: cooling

    The evaporating particles take the latent heat they need from the palm, which therefore feels cold.

  7. The particles of matter are continuously ______.

    1. at rest
    2. moving
    3. growing
    4. dissolving
    Show answer

    Answer: moving

    Particles of matter have kinetic energy and keep moving; this is why diffusion happens.

  8. Water boils at 100 °C. What is this temperature on the kelvin scale? (Use K = °C + 273.)

    1. 100 K
    2. 373 K
    3. 173 K
    4. 273 K
    Show answer

    Answer: 373 K

    100 + 273 = 373 K.
