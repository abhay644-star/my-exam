Sample questions

A few questions from the Class 9 Science bank. Every test picks a fresh mix.

  1. Matter in Our Surroundings
    The change of a solid directly into vapour without passing through the liquid state is called ______.

    1. evaporation
    2. fusion
    3. sublimation
    4. condensation
    Show answer

    Answer: sublimation

    Camphor and ammonium chloride sublime on heating.

  2. Is Matter Around Us Pure
    A homogeneous mixture of a metal with another metal or non-metal, such as brass, is called an ______.

    1. colloid
    2. suspension
    3. alloy
    4. compound
    Show answer

    Answer: alloy

    Alloys like brass (copper and zinc) cannot be separated by physical methods but have variable composition.

  3. Atoms and Molecules
    One atomic mass unit is defined as exactly one-twelfth of the mass of one atom of ______-12.

    1. carbon
    2. nitrogen
    3. hydrogen
    4. oxygen
    Show answer

    Answer: carbon

    The carbon-12 isotope is the standard reference for atomic masses.

  4. Structure of the Atom
    Which subatomic particle did J.J. Thomson discover?

    1. electron
    2. proton
    3. neutron
    4. nucleus
    Show answer

    Answer: electron

    J.J. Thomson discovered the negatively charged electron.

  5. The Fundamental Unit of Life
    Which cell organelle is known as the powerhouse of the cell?

    1. mitochondria
    2. nucleus
    3. Golgi apparatus
    4. ribosome
    Show answer

    Answer: mitochondria

    Mitochondria release energy in the form of ATP.
