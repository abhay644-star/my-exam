A few questions from this chapter. Open each one to see the answer.

  1. The acceleration due to gravity at the Earth's surface is about ______ m/s².

    1. 9.8 m/s²
    2. 1.6 m/s²
    3. 6.67 m/s²
    4. 8.9 m/s²
    Show answer

    Answer: 9.8 m/s²

    The value of g near the Earth's surface is about 9.8 m/s².

  2. An object has a mass of 60 kg on the Earth. What will be its mass on the Moon?

    1. 60 kg
    2. 0 kg
    3. 360 kg
    4. 10 kg
    Show answer

    Answer: 60 kg

    Mass is the quantity of matter and does not change from place to place.

  3. What is the weight of a 10 kg object on the Earth (g = 9.8 m/s²)?

    1. 980 N
    2. 10 N
    3. 98 N
    4. 0.98 N
    Show answer

    Answer: 98 N

    W = m × g = 10 × 9.8 = 98 N.

  4. Who stated the universal law of gravitation?

    1. Einstein
    2. Newton
    3. Kepler
    4. Galileo
    Show answer

    Answer: Newton

    Newton showed that every object in the universe attracts every other object.

  5. Objects fall towards the Earth because of the ______ force.

    1. electrostatic
    2. frictional
    3. gravitational
    4. magnetic
    Show answer

    Answer: gravitational

    The Earth's gravitational pull is called gravity.

  6. If the distance between two objects is doubled, the gravitational force between them becomes ______ of its original value.

    1. four times
    2. one-fourth
    3. double
    4. half
    Show answer

    Answer: one-fourth

    Gravitational force varies as 1/d², so doubling d makes the force 1/2² = 1/4.

  7. The force with which the Earth pulls an object towards itself is called the object's ______.

    1. momentum
    2. weight
    3. inertia
    4. mass
    Show answer

    Answer: weight

    Weight = mass × g, and it is measured in newtons.

  8. An object is thrown vertically upwards. What is its velocity at the highest point?

    1. 0 m/s
    2. 9.8 m/s
    3. 4.9 m/s
    4. 19.6 m/s
    Show answer

    Answer: 0 m/s

    At the highest point the object stops for an instant before falling back.
