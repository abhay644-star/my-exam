A few questions from this chapter. Open each one to see the answer.

  1. A homogeneous mixture of a metal with another metal or non-metal, such as brass, is called an ______.

    1. colloid
    2. suspension
    3. alloy
    4. compound
    Show answer

    Answer: alloy

    Alloys like brass (copper and zinc) cannot be separated by physical methods but have variable composition.

  2. The scattering of a beam of light by colloidal particles is called the ______ effect.

    1. Tyndall
    2. photoelectric
    3. Doppler
    4. Raman
    Show answer

    Answer: Tyndall

    The Tyndall effect is seen when sunlight passes through a dense forest canopy or a dusty room.

  3. Salt is added to water at 25 °C until no more dissolves and some stays at the bottom. The solution is now ______.

    1. saturated
    2. dilute
    3. colloidal
    4. unsaturated
    Show answer

    Answer: saturated

    A saturated solution cannot dissolve any more solute at that temperature; heating usually lets more dissolve.

  4. A mixture that has a uniform composition throughout is called a ______ mixture.

    1. colloidal
    2. heterogeneous
    3. suspension
    4. homogeneous
    Show answer

    Answer: homogeneous

    Salt dissolved in water is a homogeneous mixture.

  5. A solution contains 40 g of common salt in 360 g of water. What is its concentration as mass by mass percentage?

    1. 40 %
    2. 10 %
    3. 9 %
    4. 11.1 %
    Show answer

    Answer: 10 %

    Mass % = 40 ÷ (40 + 360) × 100 = 10%.

  6. Fog is a colloid in which tiny liquid droplets are dispersed in a ______.

    1. liquid
    2. solid
    3. metal
    4. gas
    Show answer

    Answer: gas

    In fog and clouds, the dispersed phase is liquid and the dispersion medium is gas (air).

  7. In a solution, the component present in the larger amount is called the ______.

    1. residue
    2. solvent
    3. precipitate
    4. solute
    Show answer

    Answer: solvent

    In salt water, water is the solvent and salt is the solute.

  8. A heterogeneous mixture in which solid particles can be seen and settle down when left standing is called a ______.

    1. suspension
    2. solution
    3. colloid
    4. alloy
    Show answer

    Answer: suspension

    Chalk powder in water is a suspension; it can be separated by filtration.
