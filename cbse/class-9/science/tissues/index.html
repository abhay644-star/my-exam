Practice questions with answers

A few questions from this chapter. Open each one to see the answer.

  1. The plant tissue that transports water and minerals from roots to leaves is ______.

    1. collenchyma
    2. parenchyma
    3. xylem
    4. phloem
    Show answer

    Answer: xylem

    Xylem conducts water and minerals upward; phloem carries food.

  2. The connective tissue that connects a muscle to a bone is called a ______.

    1. tendon
    2. ligament
    3. areolar tissue
    4. cartilage
    Show answer

    Answer: tendon

    Tendons are strong, less flexible fibrous tissues joining muscles to bones; ligaments join bone to bone.

  3. Which type of muscle, found only in the heart, shows rhythmic contraction throughout life?

    1. cardiac
    2. smooth
    3. voluntary
    4. skeletal
    Show answer

    Answer: cardiac

    Cardiac muscles are involuntary and contract and relax rhythmically all through life.

  4. A group of similar cells that work together to perform a particular function is called a ______.

    1. organism
    2. tissue
    3. organ
    4. system
    Show answer

    Answer: tissue

    Blood, muscle and xylem are examples of tissues.

  5. Plant tissues whose cells keep dividing, found at the tips of roots and stems, are called ______ tissues.

    1. meristematic
    2. conducting
    3. protective
    4. permanent
    Show answer

    Answer: meristematic

    Apical meristem at the tips makes roots and stems grow longer.

  6. Which plant tissue, with cell walls thickened at the corners, gives flexibility to leaf stalks and stems?

    1. collenchyma
    2. parenchyma
    3. sclerenchyma
    4. chlorenchyma
    Show answer

    Answer: collenchyma

    Collenchyma lets plant parts bend without breaking.

  7. Which plant tissue carries food from the leaves to other parts of the plant?

    1. phloem
    2. cork
    3. parenchyma
    4. xylem
    Show answer

    Answer: phloem

    Phloem carries food in both directions; xylem carries water and minerals upwards.

  8. The connective tissue that joins two bones is called a ______.

    1. cartilage
    2. ligament
    3. tendon
    4. muscle
    Show answer

    Answer: ligament

    Ligaments are very elastic and strong; tendons join muscles to bones.
