A few questions from this chapter. Open each one to see the answer.

  1. Sound cannot travel through a ______.

    1. liquid
    2. gas
    3. vacuum
    4. solid
    Show answer

    Answer: vacuum

    Sound needs a material medium to travel, so it cannot pass through a vacuum.

  2. The number of oscillations of a sound wave per second is called its ______.

    1. time period
    2. frequency
    3. wavelength
    4. amplitude
    Show answer

    Answer: frequency

    Frequency is measured in hertz (Hz), the number of oscillations per second.

  3. A sound wave has a frequency of 500 Hz and a wavelength of 0.68 m. What is its speed?

    1. 680 m/s
    2. 735 m/s
    3. 340 m/s
    4. 170 m/s
    Show answer

    Answer: 340 m/s

    v = frequency × wavelength = 500 × 0.68 = 340 m/s.

  4. Sound is produced by ______ objects.

    1. shiny
    2. hot
    3. vibrating
    4. stationary
    Show answer

    Answer: vibrating

    A vibrating object sets the particles of the medium around it vibrating.

  5. The largest disturbance in a medium on either side of the mean value is called the ______ of the wave.

    1. amplitude
    2. time period
    3. frequency
    4. wavelength
    Show answer

    Answer: amplitude

    A larger amplitude means a louder sound.

  6. A person hears an echo of a clap 2 s after clapping. If the speed of sound is 340 m/s, how far is the reflecting wall?

    1. 170 m
    2. 340 m
    3. 680 m
    4. 1360 m
    Show answer

    Answer: 340 m

    Sound travels to the wall and back: distance = (340 × 2) ÷ 2 = 340 m.

  7. The SI unit of frequency is the ______.

    1. second
    2. metre
    3. decibel
    4. hertz
    Show answer

    Answer: hertz

    1 Hz is one oscillation per second.

  8. Sound waves in air are ______ waves.

    1. electromagnetic
    2. surface
    3. transverse
    4. longitudinal
    Show answer

    Answer: longitudinal

    The particles of air vibrate back and forth along the direction in which the wave travels.
