  1. Newton's first law of motion is also known as the law of ______.

    1. gravitation
    2. inertia
    3. action
    4. acceleration
    Show answer

    Answer: inertia

    The first law describes inertia, the tendency of a body to resist change in its state of motion.

  2. What is the momentum of a 2 kg ball moving with a velocity of 3 m/s?

    1. 5 kg m/s
    2. 6 kg m/s
    3. 1.5 kg m/s
    4. 9 kg m/s
    Show answer

    Answer: 6 kg m/s

    Momentum p = m × v = 2 × 3 = 6 kg m/s.

  3. What force is needed to give a car of mass 1500 kg an acceleration of 2 m/s²?

    1. 3000 N
    2. 1502 N
    3. 750 N
    4. 1498 N
    Show answer

    Answer: 3000 N

    F = m × a = 1500 × 2 = 3000 N.

  4. The force that gives a mass of 1 kg an acceleration of 1 m/s2 is called one ______.

    1. newton
    2. watt
    3. joule
    4. pascal
    Show answer

    Answer: newton

    From F = ma, 1 N = 1 kg × 1 m/s2.

  5. The momentum of an object is the product of its mass and its ______.

    1. velocity
    2. weight
    3. acceleration
    4. force
    Show answer

    Answer: velocity

    p = m × v.

  6. A 5 g bullet is fired from a 2 kg gun with a velocity of 400 m/s. What is the recoil velocity of the gun?

    1. 0.5 m/s
    2. 1 m/s
    3. 160 m/s
    4. 2 m/s
    Show answer

    Answer: 1 m/s

    By conservation of momentum, 0.005 × 400 = 2 × v, so v = 1 m/s.

  7. Which of Newton's laws of motion says that to every action there is an equal and opposite reaction? Give its number (first, second or third).

    1. none of them
    2. third
    3. second
    4. first
    Show answer

    Answer: third

    Action and reaction act on two different objects.

  8. A heavier object has ______ inertia than a lighter object.

    1. less
    2. no
    3. the same
    4. more
    Show answer

    Answer: more

    Mass is a measure of inertia.
