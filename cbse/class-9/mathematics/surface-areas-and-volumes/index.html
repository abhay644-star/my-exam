  1. A sphere has radius 7 cm. (Use π = 22/7.)

    Show answer and solution
    • (a) Find its surface area (cm²). 616
    • (b) Find its volume (cm³), to 2 decimal places if needed. 1437.33

    (a) 4 × 22/7 × 7² = 616 cm². (b) 4/3 × 22/7 × 7³ = 1437.33 cm³.

  2. A right circular cone has radius 7 cm and height 24 cm. (Use π = 22/7.)

    Show answer and solution
    • (a) Find its slant height (cm). 25 cm
    • (b) Find its curved surface area (cm²). 550

    (a) l = √(7² + 24²) = 25 cm. (b) 22/7 × 7 × 25 = 550 cm².

  3. A conical tent has base radius 3.5 m and height 12 m. (Use π = 22/7.)

    Show answer and solution
    • (a) Find its slant height (m). 12.5 m
    • (b) Find the area of canvas needed (m²). 137.5
    • (c) Find the cost of the canvas at ₹25 per m². ₹3,437.5

    (a) √(3.5² + 12²) = 12.5 m. (b) 22/7 × 3.5 × 12.5 = 137.5 m². (c) 137.5 × ₹25 = ₹3,437.5.
