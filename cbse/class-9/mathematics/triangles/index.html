  1. In △ABC, AB = AC and ∠A = 72°. Find ∠B.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 54°

    Angles opposite equal sides are equal, so ∠B = ∠C and 2∠B = 180° − 72°, giving ∠B = 54°.

  2. In △ABC and △PQR, ∠B = ∠Q, BC = QR and ∠C = ∠R. By which rule is △ABC ≅ △PQR?

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c) ASA

    Given ∠B = ∠Q, BC = QR and ∠C = ∠R: this is the ASA congruence rule.

  3. In △ABC, ∠B = 52° and ∠C = 74°. Which side is the longest?

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a) AB

    ∠A = 180° − 52° − 74° = 54°. The longest side is opposite the largest angle, ∠C = 74°, so it is AB.

  4. Two sides of a triangle are 4 cm and 11 cm. The third side is x cm.

    Show answer and solution
    • (a) x must be greater than (cm) 7 cm
    • (b) x must be less than (cm) 15 cm

    The sum of any two sides of a triangle is greater than the third side. So x + 4 is greater than 11, which means x is greater than 7 cm, and x is less than 4 + 11 = 15 cm.

  5. In △ABC, AB = AC and ∠A = 70°. The bisector of ∠A meets BC at D, and BC = 10 cm.

    Show answer and solution
    • (a) Find ∠B. 55°
    • (b) Find ∠ADB. 90°
    • (c) Find BD (cm). 5 cm

    (a) ∠B = ∠C = (180° − 70°) ÷ 2 = 55°. (b) △ABD ≅ △ACD by SAS (AB = AC, ∠BAD = ∠CAD, AD is common), so ∠ADB = ∠ADC. They form a linear pair, so each is 90°. (c) The same congruence gives BD = DC = 10 ÷ 2 = 5 cm.

  6. In △ABC, AB = AC and ∠A = 48°. Find ∠B.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 66°

    Angles opposite equal sides are equal, so ∠B = ∠C and 2∠B = 180° − 48°, giving ∠B = 66°.
