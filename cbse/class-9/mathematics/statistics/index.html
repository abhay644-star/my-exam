  1. The marks of 7 students are 46, 25, 45, 31, 48, 30, 34.

    Show answer and solution
    • (a) Find the mean. 37
    • (b) Find the median. 34

    (a) 259 ÷ 7 = 37. (b) In order: 25, 30, 31, 34, 45, 46, 48; the median is 34.

  2. The marks of 7 students are 40, 34, 42, 44, 39, 22, 17.

    Show answer and solution
    • (a) Find the mean. 34
    • (b) Find the median. 39

    (a) 238 ÷ 7 = 34. (b) In order: 17, 22, 34, 39, 40, 42, 44; the median is 39.
