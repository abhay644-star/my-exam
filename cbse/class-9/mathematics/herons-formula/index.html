  1. The sides of a triangle are 7 cm, 15 cm and 20 cm.

    Show answer and solution
    • (a) Find its semi-perimeter s (cm). 21 cm
    • (b) Find its area using Heron's formula (cm²). 42 cm²

    (a) s = (7 + 15 + 20) ÷ 2 = 21 cm. (b) √(21 × 14 × 6 × 1) = √1764 = 42 cm².

  2. The sides of a triangle are 8 cm, 15 cm and 17 cm.
