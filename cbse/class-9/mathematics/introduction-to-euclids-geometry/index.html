A few questions from this chapter. Open each one to see the answer.

  1. According to Euclid, a point is that which has

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a) no part

    Euclid defined a point as that which has no part.

  2. Point C lies between points A and B on a line, with AC = 3 cm and CB = 5 cm. Find AB.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 8 cm

    The whole is equal to the sum of its parts: AB = AC + CB = 3 + 5 = 8 cm.

  3. C is the mid-point of the line segment AB, and AB = 12 cm. Find AC.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 6 cm

    AC = CB and AC + CB = AB, so 2AC = 12 cm and AC = 6 cm (things which are halves of the same thing are equal to one another).

  4. Ravi and Amit weigh the same. Each of them gains 4 kg. If Ravi now weighs 39 kg, how much does Amit weigh now?

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 39 kg

    Euclid's second axiom: if equals are added to equals, the wholes are equal. Both weighed 35 kg and both gained 4 kg, so Amit also weighs 39 kg.

  5. How many lines can pass through a single point?

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (a) Infinitely many

    Infinitely many lines can be drawn through one point.

  6. Point C lies between points A and B on a line, with AC = 4 cm and CB = 3 cm. Find AB.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 7 cm

    The whole is equal to the sum of its parts: AB = AC + CB = 4 + 3 = 7 cm.
