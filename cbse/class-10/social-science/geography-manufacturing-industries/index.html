A few questions from this chapter. Open each one to see the answer.

  1. Making goods in large quantities by processing raw materials into more valuable products is called ______.

    1. trading
    2. manufacturing
    3. mining
    4. farming
    Show answer

    Answer: manufacturing

    Manufacturing belongs to the secondary sector; for example, cotton is made into yarn and cloth.

  2. In which state are most of India's jute mills, mainly along the banks of the Hugli river?

    1. Bihar
    2. Odisha
    3. Assam
    4. West Bengal
    Show answer

    Answer: West Bengal

    The Hugli basin has jute-growing areas, cheap water transport, coal and abundant water, plus Kolkata as a port and banking centre.

  3. In which city was India's first successful cotton textile mill set up in 1854?

    1. Kanpur
    2. Kolkata
    3. Ahmedabad
    4. Mumbai
    Show answer

    Answer: Mumbai

    The cotton textile industry grew in Maharashtra and Gujarat, close to the cotton-growing belt, ports and markets.

  4. Industries owned and run by the government, such as BHEL and SAIL, are ______ sector industries.

    1. joint
    2. private
    3. public
    4. cooperative
    Show answer

    Answer: public

    Industries are classified by ownership into public, private, joint and cooperative sector industries.

  5. India is the largest producer of raw jute and jute goods, and second as an exporter. Which country is the largest exporter?

    1. Thailand
    2. Bangladesh
    3. China
    4. Pakistan
    Show answer

    Answer: Bangladesh

    India stands at second place as an exporter of jute goods, after Bangladesh.

  6. The first jute mill in India was set up near Kolkata in 1855 at ______.

    1. Rishra
    2. Serampore
    3. Titagarh
    4. Howrah
    Show answer

    Answer: Rishra

    After Partition in 1947 the jute mills stayed in India, but three-fourths of the jute-producing area went to Bangladesh.

  7. Industries such as the sugar industry in Maharashtra, run by the producers or suppliers of raw materials, are ______ sector industries.

    1. public
    2. cooperative
    3. private
    4. joint
    Show answer

    Answer: cooperative

    In cooperative sector industries the producers pool their resources and share the profits or losses.

  8. The iron and steel industry is called a ______ industry, because all other industries depend on it for their machinery.

    1. basic
    2. consumer
    3. cottage
    4. light
    Show answer

    Answer: basic

    Heavy, medium and light industries all depend on iron and steel for their machinery.
