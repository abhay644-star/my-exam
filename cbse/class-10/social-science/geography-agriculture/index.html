A few questions from this chapter. Open each one to see the answer.

  1. The 'slash and burn' type of farming practised in the north-eastern states is called ______.

    1. Plantation farming
    2. Terrace farming
    3. Jhumming
    4. Milpa
    Show answer

    Answer: Jhumming

    In jhumming, a patch of forest is cleared and burnt, farmed for a few years and then left fallow.

  2. Crops like watermelon, muskmelon and cucumber grown in the short season between rabi and kharif are called ______ crops.

    1. Kharif
    2. Plantation
    3. Zaid
    4. Rabi
    Show answer

    Answer: Zaid

    Zaid crops are grown in the short summer season.

  3. According to NCERT, India is the second largest producer of sugarcane after which country?

    1. Brazil
    2. China
    3. Thailand
    4. Cuba
    Show answer

    Answer: Brazil

    Sugarcane is the main source of sugar, gur (jaggery), khandsari and molasses.

  4. Assertion (A): Rice is grown in Punjab and Haryana even though they receive low rainfall. Reason (R): Canal irrigation and tubewells have made rice cultivation possible there.

    1. Both A and R are true but R does not explain A
    2. A is true but R is false
    3. Both A and R are true and R explains A
    4. A is false but R is true
    Show answer

    Answer: Both A and R are true and R explains A

    Irrigation makes rice possible in these low-rainfall areas, so R explains A.

  5. Crops sown in winter from October to December and harvested in summer from April to June, such as wheat and mustard, are called ______ crops.

    1. Zaid
    2. Plantation
    3. Kharif
    4. Rabi
    Show answer

    Answer: Rabi

    Wheat, barley, peas, gram and mustard are important rabi crops.

  6. Which state is the largest producer of tea in India?

    1. Tamil Nadu
    2. West Bengal
    3. Assam
    4. Kerala
    Show answer

    Answer: Assam

    Assam's warm, moist climate and well-drained hill slopes suit tea.

  7. Which millet is a crop of dry regions, grows well on red, black, sandy and loamy soils, and is very rich in iron and calcium?

    1. Bajra
    2. Ragi
    3. Maize
    4. Jowar
    Show answer

    Answer: Ragi

    Ragi is rich in iron, calcium, other micronutrients and roughage.

  8. Which fibre crop is known as the 'golden fibre'?

    1. Jute
    2. Hemp
    3. Silk
    4. Cotton
    Show answer

    Answer: Jute

    Jute grows well on the well-drained fertile soils of flood plains; West Bengal, Bihar, Assam, Odisha and Meghalaya grow it.
