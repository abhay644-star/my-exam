Sample questions

A few questions from the Class 10 Social Science bank. Every test picks a fresh mix.

  1. History: The Rise of Nationalism in Europe
    Who founded the secret society 'Young Italy' in 1831?

    1. Count Cavour
    2. Giuseppe Garibaldi
    3. Victor Emmanuel II
    4. Giuseppe Mazzini
    Show answer

    Answer: Giuseppe Mazzini

    Mazzini founded Young Italy to work for a unified Italian republic.

  2. History: Nationalism in India
    In which year did the Jallianwala Bagh massacre take place in Amritsar?

    1. 1919
    2. 1917
    3. 1922
    4. 1920
    Show answer

    Answer: 1919

    General Dyer's troops fired on a peaceful crowd on 13 April 1919.

  3. History: Print Culture and the Modern World
    Who developed the first known printing press in Europe in the 1430s?

    1. Erasmus
    2. Marco Polo
    3. Johann Gutenberg
    4. Martin Luther
    Show answer

    Answer: Johann Gutenberg

    Gutenberg's press, developed in Strasbourg, used movable metal type.

  4. Geography: Resources and Development
    The 1992 Earth Summit, which adopted Agenda 21, was held in which city?

    1. Rio de Janeiro
    2. Stockholm
    3. Johannesburg
    4. Kyoto
    Show answer

    Answer: Rio de Janeiro

    The Rio Earth Summit focused on environmental protection and sustainable development.

  5. Geography: Agriculture
    The 'slash and burn' type of farming practised in the north-eastern states is called ______.

    1. Plantation farming
    2. Terrace farming
    3. Jhumming
    4. Milpa
    Show answer

    Answer: Jhumming

    In jhumming, a patch of forest is cleared and burnt, farmed for a few years and then left fallow.
