Sample questions

A few questions from the Class 10 Social Science bank. Every test picks a fresh mix.

  1. History: The Rise of Nationalism in Europe
    Who founded the secret society 'Young Italy' in 1831?

    1. Count Cavour
    2. Giuseppe Garibaldi
    3. Victor Emmanuel II
    4. Giuseppe Mazzini
    Show answer

    Answer: Giuseppe Mazzini

    Mazzini founded Young Italy to work for a unified Italian republic.

  2. History: Nationalism in India
    In which year did the Jallianwala Bagh massacre take place in Amritsar?

    1. 1919
    2. 1917
    3. 1922
    4. 1920
    Show answer

    Answer: 1919

    General Dyer's troops fired on a peaceful crowd on 13 April 1919.

  3. History: The Making of a Global World
    The ancient trade routes that linked China with the West and other parts of Asia are known as the ______ routes.

    1. spice
    2. tea
    3. silk
    4. cotton
    Show answer

    Answer: silk

    The name comes from the Chinese silk carried westwards along them; they also carried pottery, spices, textiles, people, ideas and religions.

  4. History: The Age of Industrialisation
    The phase of large-scale production for international markets, before factories began, when merchants got goods made by peasants in their homes, is called ______.

    1. mechanisation
    2. globalisation
    3. deindustrialisation
    4. proto-industrialisation
    Show answer

    Answer: proto-industrialisation

    In proto-industrialisation, merchants from the towns gave money to peasants and artisans in the countryside to produce goods for an international market; it was controlled by merchants, not by factories.

  5. History: Print Culture and the Modern World
    Who developed the first known printing press in Europe in the 1430s?

    1. Erasmus
    2. Marco Polo
    3. Johann Gutenberg
    4. Martin Luther
    Show answer

    Answer: Johann Gutenberg

    Gutenberg's press, developed in Strasbourg, used movable metal type.
