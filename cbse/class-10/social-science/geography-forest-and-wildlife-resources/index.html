A few questions from this chapter. Open each one to see the answer.

  1. Species found only in a particular area, such as the Andaman teal and the Nicobar pigeon, are called ______ species.

    1. normal
    2. vulnerable
    3. endemic
    4. extinct
    Show answer

    Answer: endemic

    Endemic species are found only in some particular areas, usually isolated by natural or geographical barriers. Other examples are the Andaman wild pig and the mithun in Arunachal Pradesh.

  2. The Bishnois of Rajasthan are known for protecting which animal, along with the chinkara, nilgai and peacocks?

    1. Asiatic lion
    2. one-horned rhinoceros
    3. blackbuck
    4. tiger
    Show answer

    Answer: blackbuck

    Herds of blackbuck, chinkara, nilgai and peacocks are an integral part of the community near Bishnoi villages, and nobody harms them.

  3. Which state passed the first resolution for Joint Forest Management, in 1988?

    1. Madhya Pradesh
    2. Rajasthan
    3. Odisha
    4. Kerala
    Show answer

    Answer: Odisha

    Joint Forest Management involves local communities in managing and restoring degraded forests; members get benefits such as non-timber forest produce and a share in timber.

  4. In which district of Rajasthan did the people of five villages declare 1,200 hectares of forest as the Bhairodev Dakav 'Sonchuri', with their own rules banning hunting?

    1. Alwar
    2. Udaipur
    3. Jaisalmer
    4. Barmer
    Show answer

    Answer: Alwar

    The Sonchuri villagers made their own rules and regulations, did not allow hunting, and protected the wildlife against outside encroachments.

  5. In which year was Project Tiger launched in India?

    1. 1952
    2. 1973
    3. 1980
    4. 1991
    Show answer

    Answer: 1973

    Project Tiger was launched in 1973 to protect the tiger, whose numbers had fallen to about 1,827 by that year.

  6. Which category of forest makes up more than half of the total forest land in India and is regarded as the most valuable for conserving forest and wildlife?

    1. community forests
    2. unclassed forests
    3. reserved forests
    4. protected forests
    Show answer

    Answer: reserved forests

    Reserved forests are more than half of the total forest land; protected forests are almost one-third, and unclassed forests belong to government, private individuals and communities.

  7. Which state has the largest area under permanent forests in India?

    1. Arunachal Pradesh
    2. Chhattisgarh
    3. Madhya Pradesh
    4. Odisha
    Show answer

    Answer: Madhya Pradesh

    Permanent forests make up 75 per cent of Madhya Pradesh's total forest area.

  8. India has nearly what percentage of the total number of species in the world?

    1. 2%
    2. 25%
    3. 15%
    4. 8
    Show answer

    Answer: 8

    India is one of the world's richest countries in biological diversity, with nearly 8 per cent of the world's species (estimated at 1.6 million).
