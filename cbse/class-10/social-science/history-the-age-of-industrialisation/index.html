A few questions from this chapter. Open each one to see the answer.

  1. The phase of large-scale production for international markets, before factories began, when merchants got goods made by peasants in their homes, is called ______.

    1. mechanisation
    2. globalisation
    3. deindustrialisation
    4. proto-industrialisation
    Show answer

    Answer: proto-industrialisation

    In proto-industrialisation, merchants from the towns gave money to peasants and artisans in the countryside to produce goods for an international market; it was controlled by merchants, not by factories.

  2. Factory owners in India employed a person, usually an old and trusted worker, to get new recruits from his village and to help them settle in the city. What was this person called?

    1. carder
    2. jobber
    3. fuller
    4. gomastha
    Show answer

    Answer: jobber

    The jobber became a person with authority and power: he began demanding money and gifts for his favour and controlling the lives of workers.

  3. In the English wool trade, a person who 'staples' or sorts wool according to its fibre is called a ______.

    1. stapler
    2. carder
    3. spinner
    4. fuller
    Show answer

    Answer: stapler

    In the chain of production a merchant clothier bought wool from a stapler, carried it to spinners, then to weavers, fullers and dyers, and finished it in London.

  4. In 1917, who set up the first Indian jute mill in Calcutta?

    1. Dwarkanath Tagore
    2. Jamsetjee Jeejeebhoy
    3. Dinshaw Petit
    4. Seth Hukumchand
    Show answer

    Answer: Seth Hukumchand

    Seth Hukumchand, a Marwari businessman, set up the first Indian jute mill in Calcutta in 1917; earlier jute mills there were European-owned.

  5. Who invented the spinning jenny in 1764?

    1. Matthew Boulton
    2. James Hargreaves
    3. Richard Arkwright
    4. James Watt
    Show answer

    Answer: James Hargreaves

    With the spinning jenny, one worker could turn a single wheel that set a number of spindles in motion, spinning several threads at once.

  6. Which mechanical device for weaving, moved by ropes and pulleys, was used by many Indian weavers in the early twentieth century to increase productivity?

    1. steam engine
    2. spinning jenny
    3. power loom
    4. fly shuttle
    Show answer

    Answer: fly shuttle

    With the fly shuttle, the horizontal threads are put into the vertical threads quickly. By 1941, over 35 per cent of handlooms in India had fly shuttles.

  7. In which year did the first cotton mill in Bombay come up?

    1. 1818
    2. 1854
    3. 1905
    4. 1874
    Show answer

    Answer: 1854

    The first cotton mill in Bombay came up in 1854 and went into production two years later. By 1862 four mills were at work.

  8. In which city was the Elgin Mill started in the 1860s?

    1. Kanpur
    2. Calcutta
    3. Ahmedabad
    4. Madras
    Show answer

    Answer: Kanpur

    The Elgin Mill was started in Kanpur in the 1860s; the first spinning and weaving mill of Madras began production in 1874.
