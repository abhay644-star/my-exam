A few questions from this chapter. Open each one to see the answer.

  1. A system in which all work inside the home is done by the women of the family, or organised by them through domestic helpers, is called the sexual division of ______.

    1. power
    2. wealth
    3. property
    4. labour
    Show answer

    Answer: labour

    The sexual division of labour is based on the belief that women's main job is housework and bringing up children, although women also do paid work outside the home.

  2. A system that values men more and gives them power over women is called ______.

    1. matriarchy
    2. patriarchy
    3. oligarchy
    4. democracy
    Show answer

    Answer: patriarchy

    Literally 'rule by father', patriarchy refers to a system that values men more and gives them power over women.

  3. In which group of countries, such as Sweden, Norway and Finland, is the participation of women in public life very high?

    1. Balkan countries
    2. Gulf countries
    3. Baltic countries
    4. Scandinavian countries
    Show answer

    Answer: Scandinavian countries

    Women's movements made women's political representation an issue; in the Scandinavian countries women take part in public life on a large scale.

  4. In which year was the Equal Remuneration Act passed in India?

    1. 1976
    2. 1950
    3. 1961
    4. 1986
    Show answer

    Answer: 1976

    The Equal Remuneration Act, 1976 provides that equal wages should be paid for equal work.

  5. A woman or a man who believes in equal rights and opportunities for women and men is called a ______.

    1. communalist
    2. secularist
    3. socialist
    4. feminist
    Show answer

    Answer: feminist

    Feminist movements are radical women's movements that aim at equality in personal and family life and public affairs.

  6. Which Act provides that men and women should be paid equal wages for equal work?

    1. Factories Act
    2. Minimum Wages Act
    3. Equal Remuneration Act
    4. Payment of Bonus Act
    Show answer

    Answer: Equal Remuneration Act

    Even so, in almost all areas of work, from sports and cinema to factories and fields, women are paid less than men, even when both do the same work.

  7. Which social reformer from Tamil Nadu, known as Periyar, worked for a society free of caste inequalities?

    1. Jotiba Phule
    2. Raja Ram Mohan Roy
    3. E.V. Ramaswami Naicker
    4. Narayana Guru
    Show answer

    Answer: E.V. Ramaswami Naicker

    Political leaders and social reformers like Jotiba Phule, Gandhiji, B.R. Ambedkar and Periyar Ramaswami Naicker advocated and worked to establish a society without caste inequalities.

  8. When followers of one religion are mobilised in politics against another, using religious symbols, leaders and appeals to fear, this is called political mobilisation on ______ lines.

    1. economic
    2. regional
    3. linguistic
    4. religious
    Show answer

    Answer: religious

    This is one of the forms communalism takes in politics; it brings the followers of one religion together in the political arena against others.
