A few questions from this chapter. Open each one to see the answer.

  1. Lignite, a low-grade brown coal, is mined mainly at which place in Tamil Nadu?

    1. Raniganj
    2. Neyveli
    3. Singrauli
    4. Jharia
    Show answer

    Answer: Neyveli

    Neyveli has India's major lignite reserves, used to generate electricity.

  2. Which ore is the main raw material for making aluminium?

    1. Mica
    2. Bauxite
    3. Limestone
    4. Haematite
    Show answer

    Answer: Bauxite

    Aluminium is obtained from bauxite; Odisha is the largest producer.

  3. The monazite sands of Kerala are rich in which nuclear mineral?

    1. Uranium
    2. Radium
    3. Plutonium
    4. Thorium
    Show answer

    Answer: Thorium

    Kerala's monazite sands are an important source of thorium.

  4. Which is the highest quality hard coal?

    1. Peat
    2. Lignite
    3. Anthracite
    4. Bituminous
    Show answer

    Answer: Anthracite

    Anthracite has the highest carbon content; peat has the lowest.

  5. According to NCERT, about 63 per cent of India's petroleum production comes from which offshore field?

    1. Krishna-Godavari basin
    2. Mumbai High
    3. Digboi
    4. Ankleshwar
    Show answer

    Answer: Mumbai High

    According to NCERT, Mumbai High produces about 63 per cent, Gujarat about 18 per cent and Assam about 16 per cent.

  6. In Jowai and Cherrapunjee in Meghalaya, coal is dug out by family members through long narrow tunnels. This is called ______ mining.

    1. Strip
    2. Rat hole
    3. Open-cast
    4. Shaft
    Show answer

    Answer: Rat hole

    In Meghalaya, mineral ownership lies with individuals and communities, and rat-hole mining is done by family members.

  7. Gas produced from shrubs, farm waste and animal and human waste for domestic use in rural areas is called ______.

    1. Natural gas
    2. Coal gas
    3. LPG
    4. Biogas
    Show answer

    Answer: Biogas

    Biogas plants also provide manure; plants using cattle dung are known as gobar gas plants.

  8. Which is the most important oil field of Gujarat?

    1. Mumbai High
    2. Ankleshwar
    3. Naharkatiya
    4. Digboi
    Show answer

    Answer: Ankleshwar

    Ankleshwar is the most important field in Gujarat; Assam is the oldest oil-producing state.
