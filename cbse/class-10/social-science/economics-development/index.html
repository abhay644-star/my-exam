Practice questions with answers

A few questions from this chapter. Open each one to see the answer.

  1. The total income of a country divided by its total population is called ______.

    1. Net income
    2. Gross domestic product
    3. National income
    4. Per capita income
    Show answer

    Answer: Per capita income

    Per capita income is commonly used to compare countries.

  2. Which organisation publishes the Human Development Report?

    1. IMF
    2. WTO
    3. UNDP
    4. World Bank
    Show answer

    Answer: UNDP

    The UNDP ranks countries by the Human Development Index.

  3. Which of the following best explains why Kerala has a low infant mortality rate despite not having the highest per capita income?

    1. Large industrial output
    2. Low population density
    3. Highest per capita income in India
    4. Adequate basic health and educational facilities
    Show answer

    Answer: Adequate basic health and educational facilities

    Kerala's public health and education facilities reduce infant deaths, showing that income alone does not measure development.

  4. The number of children who die before the age of one year as a proportion of 1000 live children born in that year is called the ______.

    1. Birth rate
    2. Literacy rate
    3. Sex ratio
    4. Infant mortality rate
    Show answer

    Answer: Infant mortality rate

    The infant mortality rate is an important indicator of health facilities.

  5. The total number of children of age group 14 and 15 years attending school as a percentage of the total number of children of the same age group is called the ______.

    1. Sex ratio
    2. Net attendance ratio
    3. Literacy rate
    4. Dropout rate
    Show answer

    Answer: Net attendance ratio

    The net attendance ratio is used to compare how many children in an age group actually attend school.

  6. Which of the following is a non-material goal of development?

    1. More days of work
    2. Equal treatment
    3. Higher income
    4. Better wages
    Show answer

    Answer: Equal treatment

    Equal treatment cannot be bought with money; the others are material goals.

  7. The proportion of the literate population in the 7 years and above age group is called the ______.

    1. Sex ratio
    2. Infant mortality rate
    3. Literacy rate
    4. Net attendance ratio
    Show answer

    Answer: Literacy rate

    The literacy rate is one of the indicators used to compare levels of education.

  8. Which index, calculated by dividing a person's weight in kg by the square of the height in metres, is used to find out whether adults are undernourished?

    1. Infant mortality rate
    2. Body Mass Index
    3. Human Development Index
    4. Net attendance ratio
    Show answer

    Answer: Body Mass Index

    A BMI below 18.5 indicates that a person is undernourished; above 25 indicates overweight.
