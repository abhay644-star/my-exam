Practice questions with answers

A few questions from this chapter. Open each one to see the answer.

  1. In Belgium, the majority community in the Flemish region speaks which language?

    1. Dutch
    2. French
    3. German
    4. English
    Show answer

    Answer: Dutch

    About 59% of Belgians, mostly in Flanders, speak Dutch.

  2. In which country did majoritarian policies lead to a civil war after Tamil groups demanded a separate state?

    1. Sri Lanka
    2. Belgium
    3. Myanmar
    4. Malaysia
    Show answer

    Answer: Sri Lanka

    Sri Lanka's Sinhala-first policies alienated Sri Lankan Tamils and led to civil war.

  3. Power shared among the legislature, executive and judiciary is called ______ distribution of power.

    1. Vertical
    2. Community
    3. Federal
    4. Horizontal
    Show answer

    Answer: Horizontal

    Organs at the same level share power horizontally and check one another.

  4. In which year did Sri Lanka emerge as an independent country?

    1. 1947
    2. 1948
    3. 1956
    4. 1950
    Show answer

    Answer: 1948

    After independence in 1948, leaders of the Sinhala community sought to secure dominance over government by virtue of their majority.

  5. Sharing of power among governments at different levels, such as the union, state and local governments, is called ______ division of power.

    1. Community
    2. Social
    3. Horizontal
    4. Vertical
    Show answer

    Answer: Vertical

    In vertical division, higher and lower levels of government share power.

  6. According to NCERT, Sinhala speakers form about what percentage of Sri Lanka's population?

    1. 82 %
    2. 74 %
    3. 59 %
    4. 18 %
    Show answer

    Answer: 74 %

    Sinhala speakers form about 74 per cent and Tamil speakers about 18 per cent of the population.

  7. In 1956, an Act was passed in Sri Lanka to recognise which as its only official language?

    1. Hindi
    2. Sinhala
    3. English
    4. Tamil
    Show answer

    Answer: Sinhala

    The Act disregarded Tamil and was one of the majoritarian measures that alienated Sri Lankan Tamils.

  8. In horizontal power sharing, each organ of government checks the others. This arrangement is called a system of checks and ______.

    1. balances
    2. controls
    3. powers
    4. rights
    Show answer

    Answer: balances

    Checks and balances ensure that none of the organs can exercise unlimited power.
