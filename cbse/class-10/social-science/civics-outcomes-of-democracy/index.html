Practice questions with answers

A few questions from this chapter. Open each one to see the answer.

  1. The most basic outcome of democracy is that it produces a government that is ______ to the citizens and responsive to their needs.

    1. hidden
    2. superior
    3. unknown
    4. accountable
    Show answer

    Answer: accountable

    Democracy ensures that decision-making is based on norms and procedures, and citizens can check whether decisions were taken through the correct procedures.

  2. Looking at the rate of economic growth of countries between 1950 and 2000, which type of government had a slightly higher rate on average?

    1. monarchies
    2. democracies
    3. federations
    4. dictatorships
    Show answer

    Answer: dictatorships

    The difference is small, and economic growth also depends on population size, global situation, cooperation from other countries and the country's priorities, so democracy cannot be rejected on this basis.

  3. Economic growth depends not only on the form of government but also on the size of a country's population, the global situation and ______ from other countries.

    1. conflict
    2. isolation
    3. competition
    4. cooperation
    Show answer

    Answer: cooperation

    So the small difference in growth rates between dictatorships and democracies is not a strong reason to reject democracy.

  4. About how many countries of the world today claim and practise some kind of democratic politics?

    1. over a hundred
    2. about twenty
    3. about fifty
    4. only five
    Show answer

    Answer: over a hundred

    They have formal constitutions, they hold elections, they have parties and they guarantee rights of citizens, although they differ in social, economic and cultural achievements.

  5. The right and the means of a citizen to examine the process of decision-making in government is called ______.

    1. secrecy
    2. legitimacy
    3. sovereignty
    4. transparency
    Show answer

    Answer: transparency

    A citizen who wants to know whether a decision was taken through the correct procedures can find this out; this is missing from many non-democratic governments.

  6. Which law gives citizens the right to ask for information from government offices, making government more transparent?

    1. Representation of the People Act
    2. Official Secrets Act
    3. Right to Information Act
    4. Consumer Protection Act
    Show answer

    Answer: Right to Information Act

    The Right to Information Act ensures that citizens can find out what the government is doing; it acts as a watchdog of democracy.

  7. The fact that people complain about democracy is itself a testimony to the ______ of democracy, because it shows that people have become aware and able to look critically at power.

    1. failure
    2. end
    3. success
    4. weakness
    Show answer

    Answer: success

    A public expression of dissatisfaction with democracy shows that people have developed awareness and the ability to expect more from those in power.

  8. In a democracy, a citizen who wants to know whether a decision was taken through the correct procedures can find this out. This right and means of examining decisions is missing in many ______ governments.

    1. elected
    2. democratic
    3. non-democratic
    4. representative
    Show answer

    Answer: non-democratic

    Democracy ensures that decision-making is based on norms and procedures; this transparency is one reason a democratic government is accountable to its citizens.
