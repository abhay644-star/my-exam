A few questions from this chapter. Open each one to see the answer.

  1. The 1992 Earth Summit, which adopted Agenda 21, was held in which city?

    1. Rio de Janeiro
    2. Stockholm
    3. Johannesburg
    4. Kyoto
    Show answer

    Answer: Rio de Janeiro

    The Rio Earth Summit focused on environmental protection and sustainable development.

  2. Black soil, ideal for growing cotton, is also called ______ soil.

    1. Alluvial
    2. Regur
    3. Laterite
    4. Arid
    Show answer

    Answer: Regur

    Black or regur soil retains moisture well and is found on the Deccan trap.

  3. Land made unfit for cultivation by deep gullies, as in the Chambal basin, is called ______.

    1. Floodplains
    2. Wetlands
    3. Fallow land
    4. Ravines
    Show answer

    Answer: Ravines

    Running water cuts deep channels, turning land into bad land or ravines.

  4. Resources such as solar and wind energy, which can be renewed or reproduced by physical, chemical or mechanical processes, are called ______ resources.

    1. Stock
    2. Fossil
    3. Renewable
    4. Non-renewable
    Show answer

    Answer: Renewable

    Renewable resources can be renewed; fossil fuels are non-renewable and take millions of years to form.

  5. Ploughing along the contour lines of a slope to slow down the flow of water is called ______.

    1. Strip cropping
    2. Shelter belts
    3. Terrace farming
    4. Contour ploughing
    Show answer

    Answer: Contour ploughing

    Contour ploughing reduces soil erosion on hill slopes.

  6. Resources found in a region but not yet utilised, such as wind and solar energy in Rajasthan and Gujarat, are called ______ resources.

    1. Stock
    2. Reserve
    3. Developed
    4. Potential
    Show answer

    Answer: Potential

    Rajasthan and Gujarat have enormous potential for wind and solar energy, but it has not been developed properly.

  7. Resources obtained from the biosphere and having life, such as human beings, flora and fauna, fisheries and livestock, are called ______ resources.

    1. Potential
    2. Stock
    3. Abiotic
    4. Biotic
    Show answer

    Answer: Biotic

    Biotic resources have life; rocks and metals, which are non-living, are abiotic resources.

  8. Which report, submitted in 1987 under the title 'Our Common Future', introduced the concept of sustainable development?

    1. Club of Rome Report
    2. Agenda 21
    3. Small is Beautiful
    4. Brundtland Commission Report
    Show answer

    Answer: Brundtland Commission Report

    The Brundtland Commission Report advocated development that meets present needs without compromising those of future generations.
