Practice questions with answers

A few questions from this chapter. Open each one to see the answer.

  1. Who developed the first known printing press in Europe in the 1430s?

    1. Erasmus
    2. Marco Polo
    3. Johann Gutenberg
    4. Martin Luther
    Show answer

    Answer: Johann Gutenberg

    Gutenberg's press, developed in Strasbourg, used movable metal type.

  2. Who wrote the 'Ninety-Five Theses' criticising practices of the Roman Catholic Church in 1517?

    1. John Calvin
    2. Martin Luther
    3. Menocchio
    4. Erasmus
    Show answer

    Answer: Martin Luther

    Printed copies of Luther's theses spread quickly and led to the Protestant Reformation.

  3. Which 1878 law gave the colonial government powers to censor reports and editorials in Indian-language newspapers?

    1. Vernacular Press Act
    2. Arms Act
    3. Indian Councils Act
    4. Rowlatt Act
    Show answer

    Answer: Vernacular Press Act

    The Vernacular Press Act was modelled on the Irish Press Laws.

  4. Which explorer returned to Italy in 1295 after many years in China, bringing back the knowledge of woodblock printing?

    1. Christopher Columbus
    2. Vasco da Gama
    3. Ibn Battuta
    4. Marco Polo
    Show answer

    Answer: Marco Polo

    Italians soon began producing books with woodblocks, and the technology spread to other parts of Europe.

  5. Who wrote 'Gulamgiri' (1871) about the injustices of the caste system?

    1. E.V. Ramaswamy Naicker
    2. B.R. Ambedkar
    3. Rammohun Roy
    4. Jyotiba Phule
    Show answer

    Answer: Jyotiba Phule

    Phule, the Maratha pioneer of 'low caste' protest movements, wrote about the injustices of the caste system.

  6. From which year did the Roman Catholic Church begin to maintain an Index of Prohibited Books?

    1. 1517
    2. 1558
    3. 1600
    4. 1450
    Show answer

    Answer: 1558

    Troubled by the effects of popular readings and questionings of faith, the Church imposed controls over publishers and booksellers.

  7. The printing press first came to Goa in the mid-sixteenth century with missionaries from which country?

    1. Portugal
    2. Netherlands
    3. France
    4. Denmark
    Show answer

    Answer: Portugal

    Jesuit priests learnt Konkani and printed several tracts; by 1674 about 50 books had been printed in Konkani and Kanara languages.

  8. 'Amar Jiban' (1876), the first full-length autobiography published in the Bengali language, was written by ______.

    1. Kailashbashini Debi
    2. Pandita Ramabai
    3. Rashsundari Debi
    4. Tarabai Shinde
    Show answer

    Answer: Rashsundari Debi

    Rashsundari Debi, a young married woman in a very orthodox household, learnt to read in the secrecy of her kitchen.
