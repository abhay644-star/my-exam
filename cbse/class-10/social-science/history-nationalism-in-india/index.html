Practice questions with answers

A few questions from this chapter. Open each one to see the answer.

  1. In which year did the Jallianwala Bagh massacre take place in Amritsar?

    1. 1919
    2. 1917
    3. 1922
    4. 1920
    Show answer

    Answer: 1919

    General Dyer's troops fired on a peaceful crowd on 13 April 1919.

  2. The Non-Cooperation Movement was withdrawn in February 1922 after a violent clash at which place?

    1. Kheda
    2. Champaran
    3. Bardoli
    4. Chauri Chaura
    Show answer

    Answer: Chauri Chaura

    Gandhiji called off the movement after a mob burnt a police station at Chauri Chaura.

  3. At its Lahore session in December 1929, under Jawaharlal Nehru, the Congress formalised which demand?

    1. Purna Swaraj
    2. Responsible government
    3. Home Rule
    4. Dominion status
    Show answer

    Answer: Purna Swaraj

    The Lahore Congress demanded Purna Swaraj, and 26 January 1930 was celebrated as Independence Day.

  4. Arrange these events in chronological order.

    1. Non-Cooperation, Champaran, Salt March, Rowlatt Satyagraha
    2. Champaran, Non-Cooperation, Rowlatt Satyagraha, Salt March
    3. Champaran, Rowlatt Satyagraha, Non-Cooperation, Salt March
    4. Rowlatt Satyagraha, Champaran, Non-Cooperation, Salt March
    Show answer

    Answer: Champaran, Rowlatt Satyagraha, Non-Cooperation, Salt March

    Champaran (1917), Rowlatt Satyagraha (1919), Non-Cooperation (1920–22) and the Salt March (1930).

  5. Gandhiji's Salt March began at Sabarmati Ashram and ended at which coastal village?

    1. Navsari
    2. Dandi
    3. Porbandar
    4. Surat
    Show answer

    Answer: Dandi

    Gandhiji broke the salt law at Dandi on 6 April 1930.

  6. Who painted the famous image of Bharat Mata, portraying her as an ascetic figure who is calm, composed, divine and spiritual?

    1. Nandalal Bose
    2. Rabindranath Tagore
    3. Raja Ravi Varma
    4. Abanindranath Tagore
    Show answer

    Answer: Abanindranath Tagore

    Abanindranath Tagore's painting of Bharat Mata helped give the nation a visual identity.

  7. The peasant movement in Awadh against talukdars and landlords was led by a sanyasi who had earlier been to Fiji as an indentured labourer. Who was he?

    1. Alluri Sitaram Raju
    2. Birsa Munda
    3. Baba Ramchandra
    4. Swami Sahajanand Saraswati
    Show answer

    Answer: Baba Ramchandra

    The movement demanded a reduction of revenue, abolition of begar and a social boycott of oppressive landlords.

  8. In which book, published in 1909, did Mahatma Gandhi declare that British rule was established in India with the cooperation of Indians?

    1. Gita Rahasya
    2. My Experiments with Truth
    3. Discovery of India
    4. Hind Swaraj
    Show answer

    Answer: Hind Swaraj

    In Hind Swaraj Gandhiji argued that British rule would collapse if Indians refused to cooperate.
