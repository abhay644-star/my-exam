Practice questions with answers

A few questions from this chapter. Open each one to see the answer.

  1. A company that owns or controls production in more than one nation is called a ______.

    1. small-scale unit
    2. public sector company
    3. multinational corporation
    4. cooperative society
    Show answer

    Answer: multinational corporation

    MNCs set up offices and factories for production in regions where they can get cheap labour and other resources.

  2. Removing barriers or restrictions set by the government, for example on foreign trade, is called ______.

    1. globalisation
    2. liberalisation
    3. privatisation
    4. nationalisation
    Show answer

    Answer: liberalisation

    In 1991 the Government of India removed barriers on foreign trade and foreign investment to a large extent.

  3. Cargill Foods, a large American MNC, bought over which small Indian company to become the largest producer of edible oil in India?

    1. Parakh Foods
    2. Amul
    3. Britannia
    4. Haldiram
    Show answer

    Answer: Parakh Foods

    Buying up local companies and then expanding production is a common way for MNCs to spread production.

  4. Money spent to buy assets such as land, buildings, machines and other equipment is called ______.

    1. investment
    2. consumption
    3. savings
    4. tax
    Show answer

    Answer: investment

    Investment made by MNCs is called foreign investment.

  5. Which international organisation's aim is to liberalise international trade, and which lays down rules about it?

    1. IMF
    2. UNESCO
    3. WTO
    4. World Bank
    Show answer

    Answer: WTO

    The WTO was started at the initiative of developed countries; about 164 countries are its members.

  6. Ford Motors, an American car maker, came to India in 1995 and set up a large plant near which city?

    1. Pune
    2. Gurugram
    3. Chennai
    4. Kolkata
    Show answer

    Answer: Chennai

    Ford started in collaboration with Mahindra and Mahindra, and later exported cars and components from India.

  7. A tax on imports is an example of a trade ______.

    1. route
    2. agreement
    3. surplus
    4. barrier
    Show answer

    Answer: barrier

    Governments can use trade barriers to increase or decrease foreign trade and to decide what kinds of goods come into the country.

  8. In which year did the Government of India remove barriers on foreign trade and foreign investment to a large extent?

    1. 1971
    2. 2001
    3. 1947
    4. 1991
    Show answer

    Answer: 1991

    After this, goods could be imported and exported easily, and foreign companies could set up factories and offices in India.
