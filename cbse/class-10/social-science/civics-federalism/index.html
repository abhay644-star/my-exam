A few questions from this chapter. Open each one to see the answer.

  1. Subjects such as education, forests, trade unions, marriage and adoption are included in which list?

    1. Residuary List
    2. Concurrent List
    3. Union List
    4. State List
    Show answer

    Answer: Concurrent List

    Both the Union and the states can make laws on Concurrent List subjects.

  2. Federations like India, Spain and Belgium, where a large country divides its power between the centre and the states, are called ______ federations.

    1. Confederal
    2. Holding together
    3. Unitary
    4. Coming together
    Show answer

    Answer: Holding together

    In holding-together federations, the central government tends to be more powerful.

  3. Subjects not mentioned in any of the three lists, like computer software, are called ______ subjects.

    1. Reserved
    2. Union
    3. Residuary
    4. Concurrent
    Show answer

    Answer: Residuary

    The Union government has the power to legislate on residuary subjects.

  4. Subjects of national importance such as defence, foreign affairs, banking, communications and currency are included in the ______ List.

    1. Union
    2. State
    3. Residuary
    4. Concurrent
    Show answer

    Answer: Union

    The Union government alone can make laws on the subjects in the Union List.

  5. Federations like the USA, Switzerland and Australia, formed when independent states come together to form a bigger unit, are called ______ federations.

    1. Unitary
    2. Coming together
    3. Holding together
    4. Quasi-federal
    Show answer

    Answer: Coming together

    In coming-together federations, all the constituent states usually have equal power and are strong vis-a-vis the federal government.

  6. Under the 1992 constitutional amendment, at least what fraction of seats in local government bodies is reserved for women?

    1. One-fourth
    2. One-half
    3. One-third
    4. Two-thirds
    Show answer

    Answer: One-third

    The 73rd and 74th Amendments reserve at least one-third of seats for women.

  7. Subjects of state and local importance such as police, trade, commerce, agriculture and irrigation are included in the ______ List.

    1. Residuary
    2. State
    3. Union
    4. Concurrent
    Show answer

    Answer: State

    The state governments alone can make laws on the subjects in the State List.

  8. In a municipal corporation, the political head is called the ______.

    1. Sarpanch
    2. Municipal chairperson
    3. Pradhan
    4. Mayor
    Show answer

    Answer: Mayor

    The municipal chairperson is the political head of a municipality; in a municipal corporation the officer is called the mayor.
