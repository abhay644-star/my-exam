Practice questions with answers

A few questions from this chapter. Open each one to see the answer.

  1. About how much of the earth's surface is covered with water?

    1. three-fourths
    2. one-half
    3. two-thirds
    4. one-fourth
    Show answer

    Answer: three-fourths

    About three-fourths of the earth's surface is water, but only a small part of it is fresh water that can be used.

  2. On which river is the Hirakud dam built?

    1. Godavari
    2. Krishna
    3. Mahanadi
    4. Narmada
    Show answer

    Answer: Mahanadi

    The Hirakud project in the Mahanadi basin combines conservation of water with flood control.

  3. Which is the first state in India to make rooftop rainwater harvesting compulsory for all houses?

    1. Rajasthan
    2. Kerala
    3. Karnataka
    4. Tamil Nadu
    Show answer

    Answer: Tamil Nadu

    Tamil Nadu made it compulsory for all houses across the state, with legal provisions to punish defaulters.

  4. Jawaharlal Nehru proudly called dams the '______ of modern India'.

    1. gateways
    2. engines
    3. pillars
    4. temples
    Show answer

    Answer: temples

    He believed dams would integrate development of agriculture and the village economy with rapid industrialisation.

  5. On which river is the Sardar Sarovar dam built?

    1. Sabarmati
    2. Mahi
    3. Narmada
    4. Tapi
    Show answer

    Answer: Narmada

    The Sardar Sarovar dam on the Narmada in Gujarat is one of the largest water resource projects in India.

  6. The Narmada Bachao Andolan later focused on getting full rehabilitation for people ______ by the Sardar Sarovar project.

    1. displaced
    2. taxed
    3. employed
    4. educated
    Show answer

    Answer: displaced

    The movement first raised environmental issues about trees that would be submerged, and later worked for poor citizens, especially the displaced people (oustees).

  7. Collecting and storing rainwater for later use is called rainwater ______.

    1. irrigation
    2. recycling
    3. harvesting
    4. pollution
    Show answer

    Answer: harvesting

    Rooftop rainwater harvesting is common in places such as Rajasthan, Shillong and Tamil Nadu.

  8. In Bikaner, Phalodi and Barmer in Rajasthan, houses traditionally had underground tanks for storing drinking water. What are these tanks called?

    1. khadins
    2. kuls
    3. johads
    4. tankas
    Show answer

    Answer: tankas

    Tankas, linked to the sloping roofs by pipes, stored rainwater that lasted into the summer; some houses built rooms next to them to stay cool.
