Practice questions with answers

A few questions from this chapter. Open each one to see the answer.

  1. The ancient trade routes that linked China with the West and other parts of Asia are known as the ______ routes.

    1. spice
    2. tea
    3. silk
    4. cotton
    Show answer

    Answer: silk

    The name comes from the Chinese silk carried westwards along them; they also carried pottery, spices, textiles, people, ideas and religions.

  2. Which cattle disease spread through Africa in the 1890s, killing about 90 per cent of the cattle?

    1. anthrax
    2. malaria
    3. rinderpest
    4. smallpox
    Show answer

    Answer: rinderpest

    Rinderpest arrived with infected cattle imported from British Asia; the loss of cattle forced Africans to work for wages for the colonial powers.

  3. The Bretton Woods conference of July 1944 set up the International Monetary Fund and which other institution?

    1. United Nations
    2. World Trade Organization
    3. Asian Development Bank
    4. World Bank
    Show answer

    Answer: World Bank

    The IMF was to deal with external surpluses and deficits of member nations, and the World Bank (IBRD) was to finance post-war reconstruction.

  4. Which food crop, brought to Europe from the Americas, became so important to poor people in Ireland that its failure in the mid-1840s caused a famine?

    1. barley
    2. potato
    3. wheat
    4. rice
    Show answer

    Answer: potato

    When disease destroyed the potato crop in the mid-1840s, hundreds of thousands of people in Ireland died of starvation.

  5. Which disease, carried by the Spanish conquerors, killed large numbers of the original inhabitants of America in the sixteenth century, as they had no immunity to it?

    1. tuberculosis
    2. cholera
    3. smallpox
    4. plague
    Show answer

    Answer: smallpox

    Germs such as those of smallpox were a more powerful weapon of the conquerors than guns.

  6. Under the Bretton Woods system, national currencies were pegged to the US dollar, and the dollar was anchored to gold at how many dollars per ounce?

    1. 100
    2. 35
    3. 50
    4. 20
    Show answer

    Answer: 35

    This fixed exchange rate system was the basis of the post-war international monetary order.

  7. Who set up the first assembly line to mass-produce cars, making the T-model?

    1. Karl Benz
    2. Thomas Edison
    3. Henry Ford
    4. James Watt
    Show answer

    Answer: Henry Ford

    Henry Ford's assembly line in Detroit cut the time and cost of making a car, and mass production became a feature of the US economy.

  8. The British laws that restricted the import of corn, and were later abolished, were called the ______ Laws.

    1. Corn
    2. Navigation
    3. Grain
    4. Poor
    Show answer

    Answer: Corn

    Industrialists and town dwellers wanted cheaper food, so the Corn Laws were abolished and cheap food was imported into Britain.
