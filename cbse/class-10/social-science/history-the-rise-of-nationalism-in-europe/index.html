Practice questions with answers

A few questions from this chapter. Open each one to see the answer.

  1. Who founded the secret society 'Young Italy' in 1831?

    1. Count Cavour
    2. Giuseppe Garibaldi
    3. Victor Emmanuel II
    4. Giuseppe Mazzini
    Show answer

    Answer: Giuseppe Mazzini

    Mazzini founded Young Italy to work for a unified Italian republic.

  2. Which Prussian chief minister was the architect of German unification?

    1. Frederick William IV
    2. Kaiser William I
    3. Otto von Bismarck
    4. Metternich
    Show answer

    Answer: Otto von Bismarck

    Bismarck unified Germany through three wars, completing it in 1871.

  3. The customs union formed in 1834 at Prussia's initiative, which abolished tariff barriers among German states, was called the ______.

    1. Zollverein
    2. Reichstag
    3. Holy Alliance
    4. Frankfurt Parliament
    Show answer

    Answer: Zollverein

    The Zollverein created a unified economic territory that aided German nationalism.

  4. Which French ruler introduced the Civil Code of 1804, which did away with privileges based on birth?

    1. Charles X
    2. Napoleon
    3. Louis Philippe
    4. Louis XVI
    Show answer

    Answer: Napoleon

    The Civil Code of 1804, also known as the Napoleonic Code, established equality before the law and secured the right to property.

  5. Which chief minister of Sardinia-Piedmont led the movement to unify Italy through a diplomatic alliance with France?

    1. Cavour
    2. Giuseppe Mazzini
    3. Otto von Bismarck
    4. Giuseppe Garibaldi
    Show answer

    Answer: Cavour

    Through an alliance with France engineered by Cavour, Sardinia-Piedmont defeated the Austrian forces in 1859.

  6. On 18 May 1848, the elected representatives of the Frankfurt parliament took their places in which building?

    1. Notre Dame
    2. Reichstag building
    3. Church of St Paul
    4. Hall of Mirrors
    Show answer

    Answer: Church of St Paul

    831 elected representatives marched in procession to take their places in the Frankfurt parliament in the Church of St Paul.

  7. In France, the female allegory of the nation was given the popular Christian name ______.

    1. Germania
    2. Britannia
    3. Marianne
    4. Italia
    Show answer

    Answer: Marianne

    Marianne underlined the idea of a people's nation; her characteristics were drawn from those of Liberty and the Republic.

  8. In 1849 the Frankfurt parliament offered the crown of a united Germany to which King of Prussia, who rejected it?

    1. Kaiser William I
    2. Charles X
    3. Friedrich Wilhelm IV
    4. Wilhelm II
    Show answer

    Answer: Friedrich Wilhelm IV

    Friedrich Wilhelm IV rejected the crown and joined other monarchs to oppose the elected assembly.
