Practice questions with answers

A few questions from this chapter. Open each one to see the answer.

  1. Goods used up in producing final goods, such as wheat used by a flour mill, are called ______ goods.

    1. Intermediate
    2. Capital
    3. Final
    4. Consumer
    Show answer

    Answer: Intermediate

    Only final goods are counted in GDP to avoid double counting intermediate goods.

  2. Enterprises with regular terms of employment that are registered with the government and follow its rules form the ______ sector.

    1. Informal
    2. Organised
    3. Private
    4. Unorganised
    Show answer

    Answer: Organised

    Organised sector workers enjoy job security and benefits.

  3. Which sector contributes the largest share to India's GDP today?

    1. Primary sector
    2. Agricultural sector
    3. Secondary sector
    4. Tertiary sector
    Show answer

    Answer: Tertiary sector

    The tertiary sector became the largest producing sector, replacing the primary sector.

  4. Activities that produce goods by exploiting natural resources, such as agriculture, dairy, fishing and forestry, belong to the ______ sector.

    1. Service
    2. Secondary
    3. Tertiary
    4. Primary
    Show answer

    Answer: Primary

    The primary sector is also called the agriculture and related sector.

  5. In which sector does the government own most of the assets and provide services, such as Indian Railways?

    1. Unorganised sector
    2. Public sector
    3. Joint sector
    4. Private sector
    Show answer

    Answer: Public sector

    Indian Railways and Post Offices are public sector enterprises.

  6. Under MGNREGA, if the government fails in its duty to provide employment, it must give ______ to the people.

    1. Pension
    2. Subsidy
    3. Interest-free loan
    4. Unemployment allowance
    Show answer

    Answer: Unemployment allowance

    MGNREGA makes the right to work a legal entitlement for rural households.

  7. The sector in which natural products are changed into other forms through manufacturing is the ______ sector.

    1. Secondary
    2. Primary
    3. Tertiary
    4. Service
    Show answer

    Answer: Secondary

    The secondary sector is also called the industrial sector, since it is associated with different kinds of industries.

  8. The value of all final goods and services produced within a country during a particular year is called its ______.

    1. Human Development Index
    2. National debt
    3. Per capita income
    4. Gross Domestic Product
    Show answer

    Answer: Gross Domestic Product

    GDP shows how big the economy is; in India it is measured by a central government ministry.
