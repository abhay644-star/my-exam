A few questions from this chapter. Open each one to see the answer.

  1. A system in which only one party is allowed to control and run the government, as in China, is called a ______ system.

    1. Multi-party
    2. Two-party
    3. One-party
    4. Coalition
    Show answer

    Answer: One-party

    In a one-party system, voters have no real choice between parties.

  2. Which body recognises political parties as national or state parties in India?

    1. Election Commission of India
    2. Parliament
    3. Supreme Court
    4. President
    Show answer

    Answer: Election Commission of India

    The Election Commission recognises parties based on their vote share and seats.

  3. Which law prevents elected MPs and MLAs from changing parties after an election?

    1. Anti-defection law
    2. Model Code of Conduct
    3. Right to Information Act
    4. Representation of the People Act
    Show answer

    Answer: Anti-defection law

    Under the anti-defection law, a legislator who changes party loses the seat.

  4. Which of the following countries is generally described as having a two-party system?

    1. Belgium
    2. United States of America
    3. China
    4. India
    Show answer

    Answer: United States of America

    In the USA, power usually alternates between two main parties.

  5. A political party has three components: the leaders, the active members and the ______.

    1. Bureaucrats
    2. Judges
    3. Followers
    4. Lobbyists
    Show answer

    Answer: Followers

    Followers support the party; active members and leaders run it.

  6. The Bahujan Samaj Party was formed in 1984 under the leadership of ______.

    1. Jagjivan Ram
    2. Mayawati
    3. B.R. Ambedkar
    4. Kanshi Ram
    Show answer

    Answer: Kanshi Ram

    The BSP seeks to represent and secure power for the bahujan samaj, which includes Dalits, Adivasis, OBCs and religious minorities.

  7. The Supreme Court has made it mandatory for every candidate who contests elections to file an ______ giving details of property and criminal cases.

    1. Appeal
    2. Oath
    3. Affidavit
    4. Petition
    Show answer

    Answer: Affidavit

    This information is made public so that voters can make an informed choice.

  8. A group of people who come together to contest elections and hold power in the government is called a ______.

    1. Political party
    2. Interest group
    3. Pressure group
    4. Trade union
    Show answer

    Answer: Political party

    Political parties agree on some policies and programmes for society with a view to promoting the collective good.
