Practice questions with answers

A few questions from this chapter. Open each one to see the answer.

  1. The Golden Quadrilateral super highways link Delhi, Mumbai, Chennai and which other city?

    1. Kolkata
    2. Bengaluru
    3. Hyderabad
    4. Ahmedabad
    Show answer

    Answer: Kolkata

    The Golden Quadrilateral is a six-lane super highway project to reduce the time and distance between India's mega cities.

  2. Which port was developed soon after independence as a major port on the Gujarat coast, to ease the pressure on Mumbai port?

    1. Mundra
    2. Kandla
    3. Surat
    4. Porbandar
    Show answer

    Answer: Kandla

    Kandla, now called Deendayal Port, is a tidal port; it handles exports and imports of the grain and industrial belts across Gujarat, Rajasthan, Punjab and nearby states.

  3. National highways are laid and maintained by which body?

    1. State Public Works Department
    2. Border Roads Organisation
    3. Central Public Works Department
    4. Zila Parishad
    Show answer

    Answer: Central Public Works Department

    National highways are primary road systems laid and maintained by the Central Public Works Department (CPWD); many major national highways run north-south and east-west.

  4. Which is the deepest landlocked and well-protected port of India, originally built as an outlet for iron ore exports?

    1. Kolkata
    2. Paradip
    3. Visakhapatnam
    4. Haldia
    Show answer

    Answer: Visakhapatnam

    Visakhapatnam port in Andhra Pradesh was originally conceived as an outlet for iron ore exports.

  5. The first train in India ran in 1853 from Mumbai to which place?

    1. Thane
    2. Nashik
    3. Surat
    4. Pune
    Show answer

    Answer: Thane

    The first train covered a distance of 34 km between Mumbai and Thane.

  6. Which is the biggest port of India, with a spacious, natural and well-sheltered harbour?

    1. Chennai
    2. Kolkata
    3. Mumbai
    4. Kochi
    Show answer

    Answer: Mumbai

    Jawaharlal Nehru port was planned to ease the pressure on Mumbai port and to serve as a hub port for the region.

  7. In which year was the Border Roads Organisation set up to build and maintain roads in the border areas of the country?

    1. 1947
    2. 1972
    3. 1985
    4. 1960
    Show answer

    Answer: 1960

    The Border Roads Organisation, a government of India undertaking set up in 1960, built roads of strategic importance in the northern and north-eastern border areas.

  8. National Waterway No. 1 runs on the Ganga between Allahabad (Prayagraj) and which place, a distance of 1,620 km?

    1. Haldia
    2. Kolkata
    3. Farakka
    4. Patna
    Show answer

    Answer: Haldia

    National Waterway No. 2 is the Brahmaputra between Sadiya and Dhubri (891 km), and No. 3 is the West Coast Canal in Kerala.
