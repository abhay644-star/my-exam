A few questions from this chapter. Open each one to see the answer.

  1. The SI unit of electrical resistance is the ______.

    1. volt
    2. ampere
    3. watt
    4. ohm
    Show answer

    Answer: ohm

    Resistance is measured in ohms (Ω).

  2. A potential difference of 12 V across a conductor drives a current of 3 A through it. What is its resistance?

    1. 15 Ω
    2. 0.25 Ω
    3. 36 Ω
    4. 4 Ω
    Show answer

    Answer: 4 Ω

    R = V ÷ I = 12 ÷ 3 = 4 Ω.

  3. Resistors of 2 Ω, 3 Ω and 5 Ω are connected in series. What is the equivalent resistance?

    1. 3.33 Ω
    2. 30 Ω
    3. 10 Ω
    4. 0.97 Ω
    Show answer

    Answer: 10 Ω

    In series, R = 2 + 3 + 5 = 10 Ω.

  4. An electric heater of 1000 W is used for 2 hours every day. How much electrical energy does it consume in 30 days?

    1. 60 kWh
    2. 30 kWh
    3. 2 kWh
    4. 600 kWh
    Show answer

    Answer: 60 kWh

    Energy = 1 kW × 2 h × 30 = 60 kWh.

  5. The SI unit of electric current is the ______.

    1. ampere
    2. volt
    3. ohm
    4. coulomb
    Show answer

    Answer: ampere

    One ampere is a flow of one coulomb of charge per second.

  6. A charge of 10 C flows through a wire in 5 s. What is the current?

    1. 15 A
    2. 2 A
    3. 0.5 A
    4. 50 A
    Show answer

    Answer: 2 A

    I = Q ÷ t = 10 ÷ 5 = 2 A.

  7. Two resistors of 6 Ω each are connected in parallel. What is their equivalent resistance?

    1. 6 Ω
    2. 12 Ω
    3. 3 Ω
    4. 36 Ω
    Show answer

    Answer: 3 Ω

    1/R = 1/6 + 1/6 = 1/3, so R = 3 Ω.

  8. Which instrument is used to measure the potential difference across a component?

    1. ammeter
    2. voltmeter
    3. rheostat
    4. galvanometer
    Show answer

    Answer: voltmeter

    A voltmeter is connected in parallel; an ammeter is connected in series.
