Practice questions with answers

A few questions from this chapter. Open each one to see the answer.

  1. In which cell organelle does photosynthesis take place?

    1. mitochondria
    2. nucleus
    3. ribosome
    4. chloroplast
    Show answer

    Answer: chloroplast

    Chloroplasts contain chlorophyll, which absorbs light energy.

  2. Name the enzyme present in saliva that breaks down starch.

    1. lipase
    2. trypsin
    3. pepsin
    4. salivary amylase
    Show answer

    Answer: salivary amylase

    Salivary amylase breaks down starch into sugar in the mouth.

  3. In human lungs, exchange of gases between air and blood takes place in tiny air sacs called ______.

    1. alveoli
    2. nephrons
    3. bronchi
    4. trachea
    Show answer

    Answer: alveoli

    Alveoli provide a large surface surrounded by blood capillaries for gas exchange.

  4. The basic filtration unit of the human kidney is the ______.

    1. alveolus
    2. neuron
    3. nephron
    4. villus
    Show answer

    Answer: nephron

    Each kidney has a large number of nephrons that filter blood.

  5. The transport of food from the leaves to other parts of a plant is called ______.

    1. respiration
    2. osmosis
    3. translocation
    4. transpiration
    Show answer

    Answer: translocation

    Translocation takes place in the phloem and uses energy from ATP.

  6. During vigorous exercise, a lack of oxygen in muscle cells changes pyruvate into ______, which causes cramps.

    1. ethanol
    2. lactic acid
    3. glucose
    4. carbon dioxide
    Show answer

    Answer: lactic acid

    Without enough oxygen, pyruvate is converted to lactic acid in the muscles.

  7. Tiny pores on the surface of leaves, through which gases are exchanged, are called ______.

    1. stomata
    2. veins
    3. lenticels
    4. root hairs
    Show answer

    Answer: stomata

    Stomata also lose water by transpiration; guard cells open and close them.

  8. How many chambers does the human heart have?

    1. 6
    2. 3
    3. 2
    4. 4
    Show answer

    Answer: 4

    Two atria and two ventricles keep oxygenated and deoxygenated blood separate.
