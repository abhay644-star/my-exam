A few questions from this chapter. Open each one to see the answer.

  1. The SI unit of the power of a lens is the ______.

    1. lux
    2. watt
    3. dioptre
    4. metre
    Show answer

    Answer: dioptre

    Power of a lens in dioptres is 1 ÷ focal length in metres.

  2. A concave mirror has a radius of curvature of 30 cm. What is its focal length?

    1. 60 cm
    2. 7.5 cm
    3. 30 cm
    4. 15 cm
    Show answer

    Answer: 15 cm

    Focal length = R ÷ 2 = 30 ÷ 2 = 15 cm.

  3. What is the power of a convex lens of focal length 50 cm?

    1. 2 D
    2. 0.5 D
    3. 0.02 D
    4. 50 D
    Show answer

    Answer: 2 D

    P = 1 ÷ f (in m) = 1 ÷ 0.5 = +2 D.

  4. According to the laws of reflection, the angle of incidence is equal to the angle of ______.

    1. reflection
    2. deviation
    3. refraction
    4. emergence
    Show answer

    Answer: reflection

    Also, the incident ray, the normal and the reflected ray lie in the same plane.

  5. The speed of light in a vacuum is 3 × 108 m/s. What is its speed in a medium of refractive index 1.5, in units of 108 m/s?

    1. 2
    2. 4.5
    3. 3
    4. 1.5
    Show answer

    Answer: 2

    v = c ÷ n = 3 × 108 ÷ 1.5 = 2 × 108 m/s.

  6. An object is placed 30 cm in front of a convex lens of focal length 15 cm. At what distance from the lens is the image formed?

    1. 45 cm
    2. 15 cm
    3. 30 cm
    4. 10 cm
    Show answer

    Answer: 30 cm

    Using 1/v − 1/u = 1/f: 1/v = 1/15 − 1/30 = 1/30, so v = 30 cm on the other side.

  7. Which type of mirror do dentists use to see large images of teeth?

    1. convex
    2. concave
    3. cylindrical
    4. plane
    Show answer

    Answer: concave

    A tooth held between the pole and the focus of a concave mirror gives an enlarged, erect image.

  8. A ray of light travelling obliquely from air into glass bends ______ the normal.

    1. towards
    2. along
    3. away from
    4. parallel to
    Show answer

    Answer: towards

    Light slows down in the optically denser glass and bends towards the normal.
