A few questions from this chapter. Open each one to see the answer.

  1. A reaction in which a single reactant breaks down to give simpler products is called a ______ reaction.

    1. displacement
    2. combination
    3. decomposition
    4. neutralisation
    Show answer

    Answer: decomposition

    For example, calcium carbonate decomposes on heating into calcium oxide and carbon dioxide.

  2. Fe + CuSO4 → FeSO4 + Cu is an example of a ______ reaction.

    1. displacement
    2. combination
    3. double displacement
    4. decomposition
    Show answer

    Answer: displacement

    Iron, being more reactive, displaces copper from copper sulphate solution.

  3. In the balanced equation __Fe + 4H2O → Fe3O4 + 4H2, what is the coefficient of Fe?

    1. 3
    2. 4
    3. 2
    4. 1
    Show answer

    Answer: 3

    Fe₃O₄ has 3 iron atoms, so 3 Fe are needed on the left side.

  4. The slow eating away of metals by the action of air, moisture or chemicals is called ______.

    1. corrosion
    2. reduction
    3. rancidity
    4. galvanisation
    Show answer

    Answer: corrosion

    Rusting of iron and the black coating on silver are examples of corrosion.

  5. A substance that gains oxygen during a reaction is said to be ______.

    1. displaced
    2. reduced
    3. oxidised
    4. neutralised
    Show answer

    Answer: oxidised

    Gain of oxygen (or loss of hydrogen) is oxidation.

  6. In the reaction CuO + H2 → Cu + H2O, which substance is reduced?

    1. water
    2. copper
    3. hydrogen
    4. copper oxide
    Show answer

    Answer: copper oxide

    Copper oxide loses oxygen, so it is reduced; hydrogen gains oxygen, so it is oxidised. This is a redox reaction.

  7. Respiration is an ______ reaction because energy is released in it.

    1. exothermic
    2. displacement
    3. endothermic
    4. decomposition
    Show answer

    Answer: exothermic

    Glucose combines with oxygen in our cells and releases energy, so respiration is exothermic.

  8. During the electrolysis of water, the volume of gas collected at the cathode is double the volume collected at the anode. Which gas is collected at the cathode?

    1. nitrogen
    2. oxygen
    3. hydrogen
    4. chlorine
    Show answer

    Answer: hydrogen

    2H2O → 2H2 + O2, so twice as much hydrogen as oxygen is produced; hydrogen collects at the cathode.
