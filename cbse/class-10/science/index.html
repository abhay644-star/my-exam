Sample questions

A few questions from the Class 10 Science bank. Every test picks a fresh mix.

  1. Chemical Reactions and Equations
    A reaction in which a single reactant breaks down to give simpler products is called a ______ reaction.

    1. displacement
    2. combination
    3. decomposition
    4. neutralisation
    Show answer

    Answer: decomposition

    For example, calcium carbonate decomposes on heating into calcium oxide and carbon dioxide.

  2. Acids, Bases and Salts
    What is the pH of pure water at 25 °C?

    1. 1
    2. 0
    3. 7
    4. 14
    Show answer

    Answer: 7

    Pure water is neutral and has a pH of 7.

  3. Metals and Non-metals
    Which is the only metal that is a liquid at room temperature?

    1. mercury
    2. sodium
    3. gallium
    4. bromine
    Show answer

    Answer: mercury

    Mercury is a liquid metal; bromine is a liquid non-metal.

  4. Carbon and Its Compounds
    Compounds made up of only carbon and hydrogen are called ______.

    1. esters
    2. alcohols
    3. hydrocarbons
    4. carbohydrates
    Show answer

    Answer: hydrocarbons

    Methane, ethane and ethene are hydrocarbons.

  5. Life Processes
    In which cell organelle does photosynthesis take place?

    1. mitochondria
    2. nucleus
    3. ribosome
    4. chloroplast
    Show answer

    Answer: chloroplast

    Chloroplasts contain chlorophyll, which absorbs light energy.
