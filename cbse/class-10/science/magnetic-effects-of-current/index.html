A few questions from this chapter. Open each one to see the answer.

  1. Outside a bar magnet, magnetic field lines go from the ______ pole to the other pole.

    1. north
    2. middle
    3. south
    4. east
    Show answer

    Answer: north

    Field lines emerge from the north pole and merge at the south pole; inside the magnet they go from south to north.

  2. A coil of many turns of insulated copper wire wound in the shape of a cylinder is called a ______.

    1. galvanometer
    2. fuse
    3. solenoid
    4. rheostat
    Show answer

    Answer: solenoid

    A current-carrying solenoid behaves like a bar magnet.

  3. Which rule gives the direction of the force on a current-carrying conductor placed in a magnetic field?

    1. right-hand thumb rule
    2. Ohm's law
    3. Fleming's right-hand rule
    4. Fleming's left-hand rule
    Show answer

    Answer: Fleming's left-hand rule

    In Fleming's left-hand rule the forefinger shows the field, middle finger the current and thumb the force.

  4. A compass needle is a small ______.

    1. conductor
    2. coil
    3. magnet
    4. battery
    Show answer

    Answer: magnet

    Its north-pointing end is its north pole.

  5. Inside a long current-carrying solenoid, the magnetic field is ______.

    1. circular
    2. uniform
    3. strongest at the ends
    4. zero
    Show answer

    Answer: uniform

    The parallel straight field lines inside show that the field is the same at all points.

  6. The force on a current-carrying conductor in a magnetic field is largest when the current is ______ to the field.

    1. at 30°
    2. parallel
    3. opposite
    4. perpendicular
    Show answer

    Answer: perpendicular

    The force is largest when the current is at right angles to the field and zero when it is parallel.

  7. Which scientist first noticed that an electric current in a wire deflects a nearby compass needle?

    1. Fleming
    2. Ohm
    3. Oersted
    4. Faraday
    Show answer

    Answer: Oersted

    In 1820 Hans Christian Oersted showed that electricity and magnetism are linked.

  8. A strong magnet made by placing a soft iron core inside a current-carrying solenoid is called an ______.

    1. compass
    2. bar magnet
    3. horseshoe magnet
    4. electromagnet
    Show answer

    Answer: electromagnet

    An electromagnet is a magnet only while current flows.
