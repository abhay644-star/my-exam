A few questions from this chapter. Open each one to see the answer.

  1. What is the pH of pure water at 25 °C?

    1. 1
    2. 0
    3. 7
    4. 14
    Show answer

    Answer: 7

    Pure water is neutral and has a pH of 7.

  2. What is the chemical formula of bleaching powder?

    1. CaOCl2
    2. CaCO3
    3. CaCl2
    4. Ca(OH)2
    Show answer

    Answer: CaOCl2

    Bleaching powder (CaOCl₂) is made by passing chlorine over dry slaked lime.

  3. A solution turns red litmus blue. Its pH is ______ than 7.

    1. greater
    2. lower
    3. equal
    4. less
    Show answer

    Answer: greater

    Bases turn red litmus blue and have pH greater than 7.

  4. What is the chemical name of baking soda?

    1. calcium carbonate
    2. sodium hydrogencarbonate
    3. sodium carbonate
    4. sodium chloride
    Show answer

    Answer: sodium hydrogencarbonate

    Baking soda is sodium hydrogencarbonate, NaHCO₃.

  5. Plaster of Paris is obtained by heating ______ at 373 K.

    1. limestone
    2. bauxite
    3. baking soda
    4. gypsum
    Show answer

    Answer: gypsum

    Heating gypsum (CaSO₄·2H₂O) at 373 K gives Plaster of Paris (CaSO₄·½H₂O).

  6. Two solutions have pH 3 and pH 6. What is the pH of the solution with the higher concentration of hydrogen ions?

    1. 7
    2. 6
    3. 9
    4. 3
    Show answer

    Answer: 3

    The lower the pH, the higher the hydrogen ion concentration, so the pH 3 solution is more acidic.

  7. Which acid is produced in the human stomach and helps in digestion?

    1. hydrochloric acid
    2. nitric acid
    3. sulphuric acid
    4. acetic acid
    Show answer

    Answer: hydrochloric acid

    The stomach produces hydrochloric acid. Too much of it causes acidity, which is treated with an antacid.

  8. The reaction between an acid and a base that gives a salt and water is called ______.

    1. combination
    2. neutralisation
    3. displacement
    4. decomposition
    Show answer

    Answer: neutralisation

    For example, NaOH + HCl → NaCl + H2O.
