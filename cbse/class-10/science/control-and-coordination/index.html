A few questions from this chapter. Open each one to see the answer.

  1. The junction between two neurons, across which a nerve impulse passes, is called a ______.

    1. cell body
    2. synapse
    3. axon
    4. dendrite
    Show answer

    Answer: synapse

    At a synapse, chemicals carry the impulse from one neuron to the next.

  2. Which hormone secreted by the pancreas regulates blood sugar level?

    1. insulin
    2. oestrogen
    3. adrenaline
    4. thyroxine
    Show answer

    Answer: insulin

    Insulin lowers blood sugar; its deficiency causes diabetes.

  3. The growth of a pollen tube towards the ovule is an example of ______.

    1. hydrotropism
    2. phototropism
    3. chemotropism
    4. geotropism
    Show answer

    Answer: chemotropism

    The pollen tube grows in response to chemicals from the ovule.

  4. When light falls on a growing shoot from one side, the hormone auxin moves to the ______ side, so the shoot bends towards the light.

    1. shaded
    2. lower
    3. lit
    4. upper
    Show answer

    Answer: shaded

    Auxin makes the cells on the shaded side grow longer, so the shoot bends towards the light.

  5. The basic structural and functional unit of the nervous system is the ______.

    1. nephron
    2. synapse
    3. axon
    4. neuron
    Show answer

    Answer: neuron

    A neuron has dendrites, a cell body and an axon.

  6. Which plant hormone promotes cell division?

    1. gibberellin
    2. auxin
    3. abscisic acid
    4. cytokinin
    Show answer

    Answer: cytokinin

    Cytokinins promote cell division and are present in fruits and seeds.

  7. Which plant hormone inhibits growth and causes leaves to wilt?

    1. abscisic acid
    2. cytokinin
    3. auxin
    4. gibberellin
    Show answer

    Answer: abscisic acid

    Abscisic acid is a growth-inhibiting hormone.

  8. Which part of the brain maintains the posture and balance of the body?

    1. hypothalamus
    2. cerebellum
    3. medulla
    4. cerebrum
    Show answer

    Answer: cerebellum

    The cerebellum, in the hind-brain, coordinates posture, balance and precise voluntary movements.
