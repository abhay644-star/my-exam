A few questions from this chapter. Open each one to see the answer.

  1. Which scientist performed experiments on garden pea plants and is called the father of genetics?

    1. Watson
    2. Mendel
    3. Lamarck
    4. Darwin
    Show answer

    Answer: Mendel

    Gregor Mendel worked out the basic rules of inheritance using pea plants.

  2. Which pair of sex chromosomes is present in human males?

    1. XO
    2. YY
    3. XX
    4. XY
    Show answer

    Answer: XY

    Human males have one X and one Y chromosome; females have XX.

  3. Mendel crossed pure tall (TT) pea plants with pure dwarf (tt) plants. What percentage of the F1 plants were tall?

    1. 75 %
    2. 50 %
    3. 100 %
    4. 25 %
    Show answer

    Answer: 100 %

    Every F1 plant was Tt, and because tallness is dominant, all of them were tall.

  4. Mendel crossed pure tall (TT) and pure dwarf (tt) pea plants and self-pollinated the F1 plants. What is the ratio of tall to dwarf plants in the F2 generation?

    1. 9:3:3:1
    2. 3:1
    3. 1:1
    4. 1:2:1
    Show answer

    Answer: 3:1

    F2 gives TT, Tt, Tt and tt; three of these four are tall, so the ratio is 3 : 1.

  5. Sections of DNA that carry the information for a characteristic are called ______.

    1. nuclei
    2. chromosomes
    3. proteins
    4. genes
    Show answer

    Answer: genes

    Genes are carried on chromosomes and control characteristics by making proteins.

  6. The sex of a child is decided by the chromosome inherited from the ______.

    1. both parents equally
    2. grandmother
    3. father
    4. mother
    Show answer

    Answer: father

    The mother always passes on an X chromosome; the father passes on either an X (girl) or a Y (boy).

  7. A pea plant with genotype Tt is crossed with a dwarf plant (tt). What percentage of the offspring are expected to be tall?

    1. 75 %
    2. 25 %
    3. 100 %
    4. 50 %
    Show answer

    Answer: 50 %

    The offspring are Tt and tt in equal numbers, so half are tall.

  8. In Mendel's experiments, the trait that appears in the F1 generation is called the ______ trait.

    1. dominant
    2. mixed
    3. recessive
    4. acquired
    Show answer

    Answer: dominant

    When pure tall and pure dwarf plants were crossed, all F1 plants were tall, so tallness is dominant.
