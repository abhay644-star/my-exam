A few questions from this chapter. Open each one to see the answer.

  1. The defect of vision in which a person can see nearby objects clearly but not distant objects is called ______.

    1. hypermetropia
    2. cataract
    3. myopia
    4. presbyopia
    Show answer

    Answer: myopia

    In myopia the image of a distant object forms in front of the retina.

  2. The splitting of white light into its seven component colours by a prism is called ______.

    1. diffraction
    2. dispersion
    3. reflection
    4. scattering
    Show answer

    Answer: dispersion

    Different colours bend by different angles in a prism, causing dispersion.

  3. The sun can be seen about 2 minutes before it actually rises above the horizon. This is due to atmospheric ______.

    1. reflection
    2. scattering
    3. dispersion
    4. refraction
    Show answer

    Answer: refraction

    Refraction in the atmosphere bends sunlight so the sun appears higher than it is, giving about 2 minutes extra at sunrise and sunset.

  4. A myopic person cannot see objects beyond 2 m clearly. What is the power of the lens needed to correct this defect?

    1. -2 D
    2. -5 D
    3. -0.5 D
    4. 1.5 D
    Show answer

    Answer: -0.5 D

    A concave lens of f = −2 m is needed, so P = 1/f = −0.5 D.

  5. The light-sensitive screen at the back of the eye, on which the image is formed, is the ______.

    1. iris
    2. pupil
    3. retina
    4. cornea
    Show answer

    Answer: retina

    Light-sensitive cells in the retina send signals to the brain through the optic nerve.

  6. Hypermetropia (far-sightedness) is corrected using a ______ lens.

    1. plane
    2. concave
    3. cylindrical
    4. convex
    Show answer

    Answer: convex

    A convex lens of suitable power brings the image of a nearby object onto the retina.

  7. Danger signal lights are red because red light is scattered the ______ by fog and smoke.

    1. most
    2. equally
    3. least
    4. completely
    Show answer

    Answer: least

    Red light has the longest wavelength, so it is scattered least and can be seen from far away.

  8. The ability of the eye lens to change its focal length to see near and far objects is called ______.

    1. refraction
    2. dispersion
    3. adaptation
    4. accommodation
    Show answer

    Answer: accommodation

    The ciliary muscles change the curvature of the eye lens.
