A few questions from this chapter. Open each one to see the answer.

  1. Which is the only metal that is a liquid at room temperature?

    1. mercury
    2. sodium
    3. gallium
    4. bromine
    Show answer

    Answer: mercury

    Mercury is a liquid metal; bromine is a liquid non-metal.

  2. The chief ore of aluminium is ______.

    1. bauxite
    2. cinnabar
    3. galena
    4. haematite
    Show answer

    Answer: bauxite

    Aluminium is extracted from bauxite (Al₂O₃·2H₂O) by electrolysis.

  3. Which gas is evolved when a metal like zinc reacts with dilute hydrochloric acid?

    1. carbon dioxide
    2. chlorine
    3. oxygen
    4. hydrogen
    Show answer

    Answer: hydrogen

    Active metals displace hydrogen from dilute acids; the gas burns with a pop sound.

  4. Gold and silver can be hammered into very thin foils. This property of metals is called ______.

    1. malleability
    2. conductivity
    3. ductility
    4. sonority
    Show answer

    Answer: malleability

    Gold and silver are the most malleable metals.

  5. A chemical bond formed by the transfer of electrons from a metal to a non-metal is called an ______ bond.

    1. covalent
    2. metallic
    3. hydrogen
    4. ionic
    Show answer

    Answer: ionic

    Transfer of electrons forms oppositely charged ions held by ionic (electrovalent) bonds.

  6. In the thermit reaction, aluminium reduces ______ to give molten iron, which is used to join railway tracks.

    1. zinc oxide
    2. copper oxide
    3. aluminium oxide
    4. iron(III) oxide
    Show answer

    Answer: iron(III) oxide

    Fe2O3 + 2Al → 2Fe + Al2O3 + heat.

  7. The property of metals by which they can be drawn into thin wires is called ______.

    1. ductility
    2. sonority
    3. lustre
    4. malleability
    Show answer

    Answer: ductility

    Gold is the most ductile metal; copper and aluminium are drawn into wires for electrical use.

  8. Most metal oxides, such as sodium oxide and magnesium oxide, are ______ in nature.

    1. amphoteric
    2. acidic
    3. basic
    4. neutral
    Show answer

    Answer: basic

    Metal oxides are generally basic; non-metal oxides are generally acidic.
