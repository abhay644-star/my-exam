Practice questions with answers

A few questions from this chapter. Open each one to see the answer.

  1. Organisms that make their own food from simple inorganic substances using sunlight are called ______.

    1. herbivores
    2. consumers
    3. producers
    4. decomposers
    Show answer

    Answer: producers

    Green plants and blue-green algae are producers: they make food by photosynthesis.

  2. According to the 10 per cent law, about what percentage of the energy at one trophic level reaches the next level?

    1. 10 %
    2. 50 %
    3. 1 %
    4. 90 %
    Show answer

    Answer: 10 %

    Most energy is used by the organisms at each level for their own life processes or is lost as heat, so only about 10% is passed on.

  3. The increase in the concentration of harmful chemicals, such as pesticides, at each trophic level of a food chain is called ______.

    1. biological magnification
    2. decomposition
    3. eutrophication
    4. ozone depletion
    Show answer

    Answer: biological magnification

    Such chemicals are not broken down, so they build up at each level; organisms at the top of the food chain have the highest concentration.

  4. Bacteria and fungi that break down the dead remains of plants and animals are called ______.

    1. producers
    2. carnivores
    3. herbivores
    4. decomposers
    Show answer

    Answer: decomposers

    Decomposers break complex organic substances into simple inorganic ones that go back into the soil and are used again by plants.

  5. The producers in a food chain capture 10,000 J of energy. About how much of it reaches the secondary consumers?

    1. 10 J
    2. 1000 J
    3. 1 J
    4. 100 J
    Show answer

    Answer: 100 J

    Using the 10 per cent law: producers 10,000 J → primary consumers 1,000 J → secondary consumers 100 J.

  6. In the food chain grass → deer → lion, which organism would have the highest concentration of a pesticide sprayed on the grass?

    1. lion
    2. it is the same in all
    3. grass
    4. deer
    Show answer

    Answer: lion

    By biological magnification, the pesticide is most concentrated at the highest trophic level, the lion.

  7. Which of these is biodegradable: a plastic bag, a fruit peel, a glass bottle or aluminium foil?

    1. a plastic bag
    2. a glass bottle
    3. aluminium foil
    4. a fruit peel
    Show answer

    Answer: a fruit peel

    Substances that bacteria and other decomposers can break down, such as fruit peels, are biodegradable.

  8. The ozone layer in the upper atmosphere protects living things from the Sun's ______ radiation.

    1. ultraviolet
    2. visible
    3. radio
    4. infrared
    Show answer

    Answer: ultraviolet

    Ozone (O3) absorbs harmful ultraviolet radiation, which can cause skin cancer.
