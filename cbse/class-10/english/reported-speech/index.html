Practice questions with answers

A few questions from this chapter. Open each one to see the answer.

  1. "I can swim," said Tara.
    Tara said that she ______ swim.

    1. can
    2. could
    3. will
    4. may
    Show answer

    Answer: could

    'Can' changes to 'could' in reported speech.

  2. He said, "I was busy yesterday."
    He said that he ______ busy the previous day.

    1. is
    2. had been
    3. has been
    4. was being
    Show answer

    Answer: had been

    Simple past changes to past perfect in reported speech.

  3. She said to me, "Will you come to my party?"
    She asked me ______ I would come to her party.

    1. will
    2. if
    3. that
    4. what
    Show answer

    Answer: if

    Yes/no questions are reported with 'if' or 'whether'.

  4. The teacher said, "The Earth is round."
    The teacher said that the Earth ______ round.

    1. had been
    2. were
    3. is
    4. was
    Show answer

    Answer: is

    Universal truths do not change tense in reported speech.

  5. She said, "My mother is a doctor."
    She said that her mother ______ a doctor.

    1. was
    2. has been
    3. is being
    4. were
    Show answer

    Answer: was

    'Is' changes to 'was' when the reporting verb is in the past.

  6. The boy said, "Hurrah! We have won the match."
    The boy exclaimed with ______ that they had won the match.

    1. fear
    2. sorrow
    3. anger
    4. joy
    Show answer

    Answer: joy

    'Hurrah' expresses joy.

  7. He said, "I may go to Delhi tomorrow."
    He said that he ______ go to Delhi the next day.

    1. must
    2. might
    3. will
    4. may
    Show answer

    Answer: might

    'May' changes to 'might' in reported speech.

  8. He said, "I will phone you."
    He said that he ______ phone me.

    1. can
    2. would
    3. shall
    4. will
    Show answer

    Answer: would

    'Will' changes to 'would' in reported speech.
