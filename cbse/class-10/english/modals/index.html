Practice questions with answers

A few questions from this chapter. Open each one to see the answer.

  1. ______ we go for a walk? (suggestion)

    1. would
    2. need
    3. must
    4. shall
    Show answer

    Answer: shall

    'Shall we…?' is used to make a suggestion.

  2. You ______ not smoke here; it is a hospital. (prohibition)

    1. will
    2. need
    3. must
    4. would
    Show answer

    Answer: must

    'Must not' expresses prohibition.

  3. He ______ to swim when he was young, but he has forgotten how.

    1. use
    2. could
    3. would
    4. used
    Show answer

    Answer: used

    'Used to' expresses a past habit that no longer exists.

  4. ______ I use your phone, please? (permission)

    1. may
    2. should
    3. must
    4. need
    Show answer

    Answer: may

    'May', 'can' or 'could' are used to ask for permission.

  5. It ______ rain this evening, so take an umbrella. (possibility)

    1. shall
    2. must
    3. may
    4. ought
    Show answer

    Answer: may

    'May' or 'might' expresses possibility.

  6. He ______ have won the race, but he fell near the finishing line. (unfulfilled past possibility)

    1. need
    2. shall
    3. must
    4. could
    Show answer

    Answer: could

    'Could have' + past participle shows something that was possible but did not happen.

  7. All drivers ______ stop at a red light. (obligation)

    1. might
    2. may
    3. must
    4. can
    Show answer

    Answer: must

    'Must' expresses a strong obligation.

  8. You ______ not hurry; we have plenty of time. (absence of necessity)

    1. would
    2. shall
    3. must
    4. need
    Show answer

    Answer: need

    'Need not' shows that something is not necessary.
