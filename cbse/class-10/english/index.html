Sample questions

A few questions from the Class 10 English bank. Every test picks a fresh mix.

  1. Verbs and Tenses
    The Earth ______ around the Sun. (revolve)

    1. revolves
    2. revolved
    3. revolving
    4. is revolve
    Show answer

    Answer: revolves

    Universal truths take the simple present tense.

  2. Subject–Verb Agreement
    Everyone in the family ______ to watch cricket.

    1. are liking
    2. like
    3. likes
    4. have liked
    Show answer

    Answer: likes

    'Everyone' is singular and takes a singular verb.

  3. Modals
    ______ we go for a walk? (suggestion)

    1. would
    2. need
    3. must
    4. shall
    Show answer

    Answer: shall

    'Shall we…?' is used to make a suggestion.

  4. Articles and Determiners
    ______ sun sets in the west.

    1. an
    2. some
    3. a
    4. the
    Show answer

    Answer: the

    'The' is used before unique objects like the sun.

  5. Active and Passive Voice
    Change into the passive voice: 'She opened the gate.'
    The gate ______ by her.

    1. has opened
    2. was opened
    3. were opened
    4. is opened
    Show answer

    Answer: was opened

    Simple past passive: was/were + past participle.
