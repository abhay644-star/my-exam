Practice questions with answers

A few questions from this chapter. Open each one to see the answer.

  1. Read: 'Grandfather's old radio still works perfectly, though it was bought many years ago. Every morning he listens to the news on it while drinking his tea.'
    What does Grandfather listen to every morning?

    1. music
    2. the news
    3. songs
    4. a cricket commentary
    Show answer

    Answer: the news

    The passage says he listens to the news every morning.

  2. Read: 'The monsoon reached Kerala two days early this year. Farmers welcomed the rain and began sowing paddy. However, heavy showers flooded some low-lying fields.'
    What did the farmers begin sowing?

    1. jute
    2. paddy
    3. wheat
    4. cotton
    Show answer

    Answer: paddy

    The second sentence says they began sowing paddy.

  3. Read: 'Despite the heavy rain, the volunteers continued to clean the beach until sunset.'
    Which word in the sentence shows contrast?

    1. heavy
    2. continued
    3. despite
    4. until
    Show answer

    Answer: despite

    'Despite' contrasts the heavy rain with the volunteers carrying on.

  4. Read: 'The town library opens at nine in the morning and closes at six in the evening. Members may borrow three books at a time and must return them within two weeks.'
    How many books may members borrow at a time?

    1. 4
    2. 5
    3. 2
    4. 3
    Show answer

    Answer: 3

    The passage says members may borrow three books at a time.

  5. Read: 'Honeybees tell one another where flowers are through a waggle dance. The angle of the dance shows the direction of the flowers, while the length of the waggle shows how far away they are.'
    What does the angle of the dance show?

    1. distance
    2. direction
    3. quantity
    4. colour
    Show answer

    Answer: direction

    The passage says the angle shows the direction; the length of the waggle shows the distance.

  6. Read: 'In the desert, water is scarce in summer, but after the rains the wells are plentiful and the land turns green.'
    Which word in the passage is opposite in meaning to 'scarce'?

    1. green
    2. rains
    3. desert
    4. plentiful
    Show answer

    Answer: plentiful

    'Scarce' means in short supply; 'plentiful' means available in large amounts.

  7. Read: 'Arjun missed the school bus because his alarm did not ring. He walked to school and reached just before the assembly began.'
    Why did Arjun miss the bus?

    1. he was ill
    2. the bus broke down
    3. he forgot his bag
    4. his alarm did not ring
    Show answer

    Answer: his alarm did not ring

    The first sentence gives the reason: his alarm did not ring.

  8. Read: 'The old fort, though dilapidated, still attracts thousands of tourists. Its crumbling walls and silent courtyards seem to whisper stories of a glorious past.'
    Which word in the passage means 'in a state of ruin because of neglect'?

    1. glorious
    2. attracts
    3. silent
    4. dilapidated
    Show answer

    Answer: dilapidated

    'Dilapidated' means old and falling to pieces through neglect.
