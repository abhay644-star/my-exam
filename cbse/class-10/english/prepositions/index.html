Practice questions with answers

A few questions from this chapter. Open each one to see the answer.

  1. She is afraid ______ the dark.

    1. with
    2. of
    3. by
    4. from
    Show answer

    Answer: of

    'Afraid' is followed by 'of'.

  2. He was ______ his wits' end.

    1. in
    2. to
    3. on
    4. at
    Show answer

    Answer: at

    The fixed phrase is 'at one's wits' end'.

  3. She has been suffering ______ fever for three days.

    1. of
    2. by
    3. with
    4. from
    Show answer

    Answer: from

    'Suffer' is followed by 'from'.

  4. There is a picture ______ the wall.

    1. at
    2. in
    3. on
    4. into
    Show answer

    Answer: on

    'On' shows position on a surface.

  5. She is capable ______ doing this work.

    1. for
    2. with
    3. of
    4. to
    Show answer

    Answer: of

    'Capable' is followed by 'of'.

  6. He is ashamed ______ his behaviour.

    1. of
    2. with
    3. at
    4. for
    Show answer

    Answer: of

    'Ashamed' is followed by 'of'.

  7. We will meet ______ Monday.

    1. on
    2. at
    3. in
    4. of
    Show answer

    Answer: on

    'On' is used with days of the week.

  8. I have been waiting ______ ten o'clock.

    1. for
    2. since
    3. during
    4. in
    Show answer

    Answer: since

    'Since' is used with a point of time; 'for' is used with a period of time.
