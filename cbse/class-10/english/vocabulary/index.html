Practice questions with answers

A few questions from this chapter. Open each one to see the answer.

  1. Give one word for: a person who does not eat meat.

    1. vegetarian
    2. cannibal
    3. glutton
    4. carnivore
    Show answer

    Answer: vegetarian

    A vegetarian does not eat meat.

  2. The idiom 'to turn over a new leaf' means:

    1. to cheat someone
    2. to start gardening
    3. to change one's behaviour for the better
    4. to read a new book
    Show answer

    Answer: to change one's behaviour for the better

    It means to start behaving in a better way.

  3. Give one word for: a person who remains calm and unaffected by pleasure or pain.

    1. sadist
    2. pessimist
    3. stoic
    4. optimist
    Show answer

    Answer: stoic

    A stoic endures pleasure and pain without showing feelings.

  4. Fill in the correct word: I could not ______ the noise. (bare/bear)

    1. beer
    2. bare
    3. bear
    4. bears
    Show answer

    Answer: bear

    'Bear' means to tolerate; 'bare' means uncovered.

  5. Give one word for: a place where books are kept for people to read or borrow.

    1. museum
    2. gallery
    3. library
    4. stationer
    Show answer

    Answer: library

    A library keeps books for reading or lending.

  6. Give one word for: a medicine that counteracts the effect of a poison.

    1. anaesthetic
    2. antibiotic
    3. antidote
    4. vaccine
    Show answer

    Answer: antidote

    An antidote acts against a poison.

  7. Give the opposite of 'expand'.

    1. enlarge
    2. extend
    3. contract
    4. increase
    Show answer

    Answer: contract

    'Expand' means to become larger; its opposite is 'contract'.

  8. Give one word for: a person who knows many languages.

    1. philatelist
    2. bibliophile
    3. polyglot
    4. novice
    Show answer

    Answer: polyglot

    A polyglot can speak or understand several languages.
