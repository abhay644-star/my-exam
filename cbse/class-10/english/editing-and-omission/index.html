A few questions from this chapter. Open each one to see the answer.

  1. Which word must be removed to correct: 'She is more taller than her brother.'?

    1. her
    2. taller
    3. than
    4. more
    Show answer

    Answer: more

    'Taller' is already comparative, so 'more' is not needed.

  2. Correct the error: 'I am knowing the answer now.' Write the correct verb.

    1. knew
    2. known
    3. knows
    4. know
    Show answer

    Answer: know

    Stative verbs like 'know' are not used in the continuous form.

  3. Correct the error: 'Neither of the answers are correct.' Write the correct word.

    1. is
    2. be
    3. are
    4. were
    Show answer

    Answer: is

    'Neither' is singular and takes 'is'.

  4. Correct the error: 'He go to school by bus every day.' Write the correct word.

    1. gone
    2. going
    3. goes
    4. went
    Show answer

    Answer: goes

    A singular subject in the simple present takes 'goes'.

  5. Supply the missing word: 'She is taller her sister.'

    1. to
    2. than
    3. then
    4. from
    Show answer

    Answer: than

    A comparative adjective such as 'taller' is followed by 'than'.

  6. Correct the error: 'If it will rain, we will stay at home.' Write the correct form for 'will rain'.

    1. raining
    2. will rain
    3. rains
    4. rained
    Show answer

    Answer: rains

    In the 'if' clause of a first conditional, the simple present is used.

  7. Correct the error: 'They was playing football.' Write the correct word.

    1. be
    2. were
    3. been
    4. is
    Show answer

    Answer: were

    A plural subject such as 'they' takes 'were'.

  8. Correct the error: 'He has gone to London last year.' Write the correct verb.

    1. went
    2. gone
    3. goes
    4. go
    Show answer

    Answer: went

    A definite past time ('last year') takes the simple past, not the present perfect.
