A few questions from this chapter. Open each one to see the answer.

  1. Change into the passive voice: 'She opened the gate.'
    The gate ______ by her.

    1. has opened
    2. was opened
    3. were opened
    4. is opened
    Show answer

    Answer: was opened

    Simple past passive: was/were + past participle.

  2. Change into the passive voice: 'Did she invite you?'
    ______ you invited by her?

    1. were
    2. was
    3. are
    4. did
    Show answer

    Answer: were

    The passive of a simple past question uses 'was/were'; 'you' takes 'were'.

  3. Change into the passive voice: 'They say that he is honest.'
    He ______ to be honest.

    1. is saying
    2. says
    3. was said
    4. is said
    Show answer

    Answer: is said

    'They say that…' becomes '…is said to…' in the passive.

  4. Change into the passive voice: 'Let him do the work.'
    Let the work ______ by him.

    1. done
    2. be done
    3. is done
    4. been done
    Show answer

    Answer: be done

    'Let' passive: Let + object + be + past participle.

  5. Change into the passive voice: 'The farmer sold the cow.'
    The cow ______ by the farmer.

    1. were sold
    2. was sold
    3. has sold
    4. is sold
    Show answer

    Answer: was sold

    Simple past passive: was/were + past participle.

  6. Change into the passive voice: 'People speak English all over the world.'
    English ______ all over the world.

    1. is spoken
    2. speaks
    3. is speaking
    4. was spoken
    Show answer

    Answer: is spoken

    Simple present passive: is/are + past participle.

  7. Change into the passive voice: 'The police will catch the thief.'
    The thief ______ by the police.

    1. would be caught
    2. will be catch
    3. will caught
    4. will be caught
    Show answer

    Answer: will be caught

    Simple future passive: will + be + past participle.

  8. Change into the passive voice: 'Mother makes tea every morning.'
    Tea ______ by Mother every morning.

    1. is made
    2. is making
    3. was made
    4. makes
    Show answer

    Answer: is made

    Simple present passive: is/are + past participle.
