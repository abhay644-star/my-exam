Practice questions with answers

A few questions from this chapter. Open each one to see the answer.

  1. Everyone in the family ______ to watch cricket.

    1. are liking
    2. like
    3. likes
    4. have liked
    Show answer

    Answer: likes

    'Everyone' is singular and takes a singular verb.

  2. Fifty rupees ______ too much for this pen.

    1. were
    2. have
    3. is
    4. are
    Show answer

    Answer: is

    A sum of money treated as one amount takes a singular verb.

  3. A large number of people ______ gathered outside the stadium.

    1. has
    2. is
    3. was
    4. have
    Show answer

    Answer: have

    'A number of' means 'many' and takes a plural verb.

  4. Every student ______ to wear the school uniform.

    1. has
    2. have
    3. are
    4. were
    Show answer

    Answer: has

    'Every' is followed by a singular noun and a singular verb.

  5. The Prime Minister and the President ______ arrived at the venue.

    1. is
    2. was
    3. has
    4. have
    Show answer

    Answer: have

    Two different persons joined by 'and' take a plural verb.

  6. The jury ______ divided in their opinions.

    1. has
    2. is
    3. was
    4. were
    Show answer

    Answer: were

    When the members of a group act separately ('their opinions'), the collective noun takes a plural verb.

  7. The cattle ______ grazing in the field.

    1. are
    2. was
    3. is
    4. has
    Show answer

    Answer: are

    'Cattle' is always plural and takes a plural verb.

  8. The captain, as well as the players, ______ been praised.

    1. has
    2. are
    3. were
    4. have
    Show answer

    Answer: has

    With 'as well as', the verb agrees with the first subject, 'the captain'.
