A few questions from this chapter. Open each one to see the answer.

  1. The Earth ______ around the Sun. (revolve)

    1. revolves
    2. revolved
    3. revolving
    4. is revolve
    Show answer

    Answer: revolves

    Universal truths take the simple present tense.

  2. When I reached the hall, the programme ______. (already begin)

    1. had already began
    2. has already begun
    3. had already begun
    4. already began
    Show answer

    Answer: had already begun

    The earlier of two past actions uses the past perfect.

  3. It is high time we ______. (leave)

    1. leave
    2. had left
    3. left
    4. have left
    Show answer

    Answer: left

    'It is high time' is followed by the simple past.

  4. Choose the grammatically correct sentence.

    1. I have been waiting for two hours.
    2. I was waiting since two hours.
    3. I have been waiting since two hours.
    4. I am waiting since two hours.
    Show answer

    Answer: I have been waiting for two hours.

    A period of time takes 'for' with the present perfect continuous.

  5. We ______ him at the airport last night. (meet)

    1. had meet
    2. met
    3. have met
    4. meet
    Show answer

    Answer: met

    'Last night' shows completed past time, so the simple past is used.

  6. According to the timetable, our exams ______ next Monday. (start)

    1. have started
    2. started
    3. had started
    4. start
    Show answer

    Answer: start

    Timetabled future events often use the simple present.

  7. If I ______ you, I would apologise. (be)

    1. were
    2. will be
    3. had been
    4. am
    Show answer

    Answer: were

    Imaginary present conditions use 'were' for all persons.

  8. She ______ to the temple every Sunday. (go)

    1. gone
    2. going
    3. go
    4. goes
    Show answer

    Answer: goes

    A habitual action with a singular subject takes the simple present with -s/-es.
