A few questions from this chapter. Open each one to see the answer.

  1. Change to a negative sentence: 'Only Ravi can solve this.'
    ______ but Ravi can solve this.

    1. none
    2. all
    3. someone
    4. everyone
    Show answer

    Answer: none

    'Only' in an affirmative becomes 'none but' in a negative sentence.

  2. Rewrite using 'unless': 'If you do not hurry, you will miss the bus.'
    Unless you ______, you will miss the bus.

    1. hurry
    2. do not hurry
    3. will hurry
    4. hurried
    Show answer

    Answer: hurry

    'Unless' already means 'if not', so the verb is positive.

  3. Change into a complex sentence: 'He confessed his guilt.'
    He confessed ______ he was guilty.

    1. what
    2. if
    3. that
    4. which
    Show answer

    Answer: that

    A noun clause introduced by 'that' replaces the noun phrase.

  4. Change into a question: 'She lives in Delhi.'
    ______ she live in Delhi?

    1. do
    2. does
    3. is
    4. has
    Show answer

    Answer: does

    A simple present question with a singular subject uses 'does' + base verb.

  5. Rewrite using 'too ... to': 'The tea is so hot that I cannot drink it.'
    The tea is ______ hot for me to drink.

    1. too
    2. much
    3. very
    4. so
    Show answer

    Answer: too

    'So ... that ... cannot' becomes 'too ... to'.

  6. Change the degree: 'Very few cities in India are as big as Mumbai.'
    Mumbai is ______ than most other cities in India.

    1. bigger
    2. more big
    3. big
    4. biggest
    Show answer

    Answer: bigger

    'Very few…as…as' becomes the comparative with 'than most other'.

  7. Change into an exclamatory sentence: 'It is a very beautiful flower.'
    ______ a beautiful flower it is!

    1. so
    2. what
    3. how
    4. such
    Show answer

    Answer: what

    'What' is used before 'a/an' + adjective + noun in exclamations.

  8. Change the degree: 'Delhi is one of the largest cities in India.'
    Delhi is larger than most ______ cities in India.

    1. the
    2. other
    3. another
    4. others
    Show answer

    Answer: other

    'One of the + superlative' becomes 'comparative + than most other'.
