  1. A toy is a cone of radius 10.5 cm and height 15 cm mounted on a hemisphere of the same radius. Find its volume in cm³. (Use π = 22/7.)

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 4158

    Hemisphere (2/3)πr³ = 2425.5; cone (1/3)πr²h = 1732.5; total = 4158 cm³.
