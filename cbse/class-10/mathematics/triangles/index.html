  1. In △ABC, D and E are points on AB and AC such that DE ∥ BC. If AD = 3 cm, DB = 7 cm and AE = 9 cm, find EC.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 21 cm

    By the basic proportionality theorem, AD/DB = AE/EC, so EC = AE × DB ÷ AD = 9 × 7 ÷ 3 = 21 cm.

  2. △ABC ~ △PQR, with AB = 3 cm, BC = 6 cm and PQ = 10 cm. Find QR.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 20 cm

    Corresponding sides of similar triangles are in the same ratio: QR/BC = PQ/AB, so QR = 6 × 10 ÷ 3 = 20 cm.

  3. In △ABC and △PQR, AB/PQ = BC/QR = CA/RP = 4. By which criterion is △ABC ~ △PQR?

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (c) SSS

    Given AB/PQ = BC/QR = CA/RP = 4: this is the SSS similarity criterion.

  4. At a certain time of day, a vertical pole 2 m tall casts a shadow 4 m long. At the same time, a tower casts a shadow 40 m long.

    Show answer and solution
    • (a) Find the height of the tower (m). 20 m
    • (b) How long is the shadow of a 4.5 m tall pole at the same time (m)? 9 m

    The sun's rays meet the ground at the same angle, and the pole and tower are vertical, so the triangles formed are similar (AA). Height ÷ shadow is the same for all of them. (a) H = 2 × 40 ÷ 4 = 20 m. (b) 4.5 × 4 ÷ 2 = 9 m.

  5. In △ABC, D is a point on AB and E is a point on AC such that DE ∥ BC. AD = x cm, DB = (x + 3) cm, AE = (x + 4) cm and EC = (x + 11) cm.

    Show answer and solution
    • (a) Find x. 3
    • (b) Find AB (cm). 9 cm
    • (c) Find AC (cm). 21 cm

    By the basic proportionality theorem, AD/DB = AE/EC. Cross-multiplying: x(x + 11) = (x + 3)(x + 4). The x² terms cancel, leaving 4x = 12, so x = 3. (b) AB = AD + DB = 3 + 6 = 9 cm. (c) AC = AE + EC = 7 + 14 = 21 cm.

  6. In △ABC, D and E are points on AB and AC such that DE ∥ BC. If AD = 4 cm, DB = 8 cm and AE = 7 cm, find EC.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 14 cm

    By the basic proportionality theorem, AD/DB = AE/EC, so EC = AE × DB ÷ AD = 7 × 8 ÷ 4 = 14 cm.
