A few questions from this chapter. Open each one to see the answer.

  1. Solve the pair of equations 2x − 4y = −28 and 3x + 5y = 35.

    Show answer and solution
    • (a) x 0
    • (b) y 7

    Eliminating one variable gives x = 0 and y = 7. Check: 2(0) + (−4)(7) = −28.

  2. Solve the pair of equations x − y = 3 and 3x + 3y = −3.

    Show answer and solution
    • (a) x 1
    • (b) y −2

    Eliminating one variable gives x = 1 and y = −2. Check: 1(1) + (−1)(−2) = 3.

  3. Solve the pair of equations 4x + 2y = 6 and 2x + 3y = 21.

    Show answer and solution
    • (a) x −3
    • (b) y 9

    Eliminating one variable gives x = −3 and y = 9. Check: 4(−3) + 2(9) = 6.

  4. Solve the pair of equations 2x − 2y = −18 and 3x + 4y = 1.

    Show answer and solution
    • (a) x −5
    • (b) y 4

    Eliminating one variable gives x = −5 and y = 4. Check: 2(−5) + (−2)(4) = −18.

  5. Solve the pair of equations x − 3y = 9 and 2x + y = 18.

    Show answer and solution
    • (a) x 9
    • (b) y 0

    Eliminating one variable gives x = 9 and y = 0. Check: 1(9) + (−3)(0) = 9.

  6. Solve the pair of equations 4x − 3y = 7 and 4x + 4y = 28.

    Show answer and solution
    • (a) x 4
    • (b) y 3

    Eliminating one variable gives x = 4 and y = 3. Check: 4(4) + (−3)(3) = 7.
