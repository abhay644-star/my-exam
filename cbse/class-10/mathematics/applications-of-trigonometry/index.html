  1. From a point on the ground 16 m from the foot of a tower, the angle of elevation of the top of the tower is 45°.

    Show answer and solution
    • (a) Find the height of the tower (m). You may leave √3 in your answer. 16 m
    • (b) Find the length of the line of sight from the point to the top of the tower (m). 16√2 m

    (a) tan 45° = h / 16, so h = 16 m. (b) The line of sight is the hypotenuse: √(16² + h²) = 16√2 m.

  2. From a point P on level ground, the angle of elevation of the top of a tower is 30°. After walking 18 m towards the tower, the angle of elevation becomes 60°.

    Show answer and solution
    • (a) Find the height of the tower (m). You may leave √3 in your answer. 9√3 m
    • (b) How far is the second point from the foot of the tower (m)? 9 m
    • (c) How far was P from the foot of the tower (m)? 27 m

    Let the height be h and the second distance x. tan 60° = h/x gives h = x√3; tan 30° = h/(x + 18) gives 3x = x + 18, so x = 9. Then h = 9√3 m and P is 27 m from the tower.
