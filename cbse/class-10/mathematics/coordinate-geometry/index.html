A few questions from this chapter. Open each one to see the answer.

  1. Find the distance between the points (−4, 5) and (−13, 17).

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 15

    √[(9)² + (12)²] = √225 = 15

  2. Find the point that divides the line segment joining A(−2, −6) and B(−12, −11) internally in the ratio 2 : 3.

    Show answer and solution
    • (a) x-coordinate −6
    • (b) y-coordinate −8

    x = (2 × (−12) + 3 × (−2)) / 5 = −6 and y = (2 × (−11) + 3 × (−6)) / 5 = −8.

  3. Find the distance between the points (−1, −5) and (−6, −17).

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 13

    √[(5)² + (12)²] = √169 = 13

  4. Find the point that divides the line segment joining A(0, 6) and B(15, 11) internally in the ratio 3 : 2.

    Show answer and solution
    • (a) x-coordinate 9
    • (b) y-coordinate 9

    x = (3 × 15 + 2 × 0) / 5 = 9 and y = (3 × 11 + 2 × 6) / 5 = 9.

  5. Find the distance between the points (0, −2) and (9, −14).

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 15

    √[(9)² + (12)²] = √225 = 15

  6. Find the point that divides the line segment joining A(0, 2) and B(−18, −4) internally in the ratio 3 : 3.

    Show answer and solution
    • (a) x-coordinate −9
    • (b) y-coordinate −1

    x = (3 × (−18) + 3 × 0) / 6 = −9 and y = (3 × (−4) + 3 × 2) / 6 = −1.
