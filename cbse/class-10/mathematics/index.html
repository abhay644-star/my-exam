Sample questions

A few questions from the Class 10 Mathematics bank. Every test picks a fresh mix.

  1. Real Numbers
    Find the HCF of 12 and 18.

    1. 36
    2. 2
    3. 3
    4. 6
    Show answer

    Answer: 6

    12 = 2² × 3 and 18 = 2 × 3², so HCF = 2 × 3 = 6.

  2. Polynomials
    At most how many zeroes can a quadratic polynomial have?

    1. 3
    2. 2
    3. 1
    4. 4
    Show answer

    Answer: 2

    A polynomial of degree n has at most n zeroes.

  3. Pair of Linear Equations
    Solve x + y = 10 and x − y = 2. Find x.

    1. 12
    2. 8
    3. 6
    4. 4
    Show answer

    Answer: 6

    Adding: 2x = 12, so x = 6 (and y = 4).

  4. Quadratic Equations
    What is the degree of a quadratic equation?

    1. 3
    2. 2
    3. 1
    4. 4
    Show answer

    Answer: 2

    A quadratic equation has the form ax² + bx + c = 0 with a ≠ 0.

  5. Arithmetic Progressions
    Find the common difference of the AP 3, 7, 11, …

    1. 10
    2. 4
    3. 3
    4. 7
    Show answer

    Answer: 4

    d = 7 − 3 = 4.
