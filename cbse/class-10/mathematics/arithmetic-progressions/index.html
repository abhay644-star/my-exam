A few questions from this chapter. Open each one to see the answer.

  1. Find the 23rd term of the AP 18, 21, 24, …

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 84

    aₙ = a + (n − 1)d = 18 + 22 × 3 = 84

  2. Find the sum of the first 16 terms of the AP 2, 6, 10, …

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 512

    Sₙ = n/2 [2a + (n − 1)d] = 16/2 × [4 + 60] = 512

  3. Priya saves ₹300 in the first month and ₹250 more every month after that.

    Show answer and solution
    • (a) How much does she save in the 10th month (₹)? ₹2,550
    • (b) How much does she save in total in the first 10 months (₹)? ₹14,250
    • (c) In how many months will her total savings be ₹48,450? 19 months

    (a) a + (n − 1)d = 300 + 9 × 250 = ₹2,550. (b) 10/2 × [600 + 2250] = ₹14,250. (c) Solving n/2 [600 + (n − 1) × 250] = 48450 gives n = 19.

  4. Find the 27th term of the AP 16, 15, 14, …

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: −10

    aₙ = a + (n − 1)d = 16 + 26 × (−1) = −10

  5. Find the sum of the first 30 terms of the AP 12, 17, 22, …

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: 2535

    Sₙ = n/2 [2a + (n − 1)d] = 30/2 × [24 + 145] = 2535

  6. Priya saves ₹450 in the first month and ₹300 more every month after that.

    Show answer and solution
    • (a) How much does she save in the 14th month (₹)? ₹4,350
    • (b) How much does she save in total in the first 14 months (₹)? ₹33,600
    • (c) In how many months will her total savings be ₹54,000? 18 months

    (a) a + (n − 1)d = 450 + 13 × 300 = ₹4,350. (b) 14/2 × [900 + 3900] = ₹33,600. (c) Solving n/2 [900 + (n − 1) × 300] = 54000 gives n = 18.
