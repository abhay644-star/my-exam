  1. A sector of a circle of radius 21 cm has a central angle of 90°. (Use π = 22/7.)

    Show answer and solution
    • (a) Find the area of the sector (cm²). 346.5
    • (b) Find the length of its arc (cm). 33

    (a) 90/360 × 22/7 × 21² = 346.5 cm². (b) 90/360 × 2 × 22/7 × 21 = 33 cm.
