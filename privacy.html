Privacy

All in All Exam Practice (my-exam.in) is a free practice site for school and competitive exams. It is used by school students, so we keep data collection to a minimum. Last updated: 30 October 2026.

In short

  • No sign-up and no accounts. We never ask for your name, phone number or school to use the site.
  • Your answers, scores and history are saved only on your own device, not on our servers.
  • We count visits anonymously, without cookies, to learn which tests are used.
  • We see your email address only if you send us a message and choose to give it.

What is saved on your device

The site uses your browser's storage to keep a paper you are in the middle of, your past scores, your settings and the exams or papers you asked for. It also keeps a copy of the site so it works offline. This stays on your device; we cannot see it. To delete it, clear the site data for my-exam.in in your browser settings, or uninstall the app.

Visit counts (GoatCounter)

We use GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly analytics service that does not use cookies and does not identify you. It records which page or test was opened, the referring site, the browser and screen size and the country, plus events such as starting or finishing a test (with a rough result band, not your answers). Your IP address is not stored.

Messages you send us

The "Send us a message" form is delivered by FormSubmit to our email, aa.exampractice@gmail.com. It sends your message, your email address if you give one, and the address of the page you sent it from (so we can find the question you are writing about). It does not ask for your name. We use it only to reply and to fix the site, and never share it or add you to a mailing list.

Other services

Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts and maths notation from cdnjs (Cloudflare). Like any website request, these services receive your IP address to deliver the files. The site has no adverts.

Children

The site can be used without giving any personal information. If you are under 18, please ask a parent or teacher before sending us a message.

Contact

Questions about privacy, or a request to delete a message you sent: email aa.exampractice@gmail.com.
