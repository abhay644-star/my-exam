  1. NEET (UG) 2026, 21 June (re-exam), Code 50 · Question 6
    Consider a particle moving along a straight line, whose position as a function of time is given by $s(t) = \alpha t^2 - \beta t + \gamma$, where $\alpha = 1\ \text{ms}^{-2}$, $\beta = 6\ \text{ms}^{-1}$ and $\gamma = 5$ m. The average speed of the particle, in $\text{ms}^{-1}$ from $t = 0$ to $t = 6$ s is:

    1. $12$
    2. $6$
    3. $3$
    4. $0$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (3)

    $s = t^2 - 6t + 5$, $v = 2t - 6$, so the particle reverses at $t = 3$ s.
    $s(0) = 5$, $s(3) = -4$, $s(6) = 5$ m. Distance $= 9 + 9 = 18$ m.
    Average speed $= 18/6 = 3\ \text{ms}^{-1}$.

  2. NEET (UG) 2025, 4 May, Code 45 · Question 15
    In some appropriate units, time ($t$) and position ($x$) relation of a moving particle is given by $t = x^2 + x$. The acceleration of the particle is

    1. $-\dfrac{2}{(x+2)^3}$
    2. $-\dfrac{2}{(2x+1)^3}$
    3. $+\dfrac{2}{(x+1)^3}$
    4. $+\dfrac{2}{2x+1}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (2)

    $\dfrac{dt}{dx} = 2x + 1$, so $v = \dfrac{dx}{dt} = \dfrac{1}{2x+1}$.
    $a = v\dfrac{dv}{dx} = \dfrac{1}{2x+1}\times\dfrac{-2}{(2x+1)^2} = -\dfrac{2}{(2x+1)^3}$.

  3. NEET (UG) 2025, 4 May, Code 45 · Question 21
    Two cities X and Y are connected by a regular bus service with a bus leaving in either direction every T min. A girl is driving scooty with a speed of 60 km/h in the direction X to Y notices that a bus goes past her every 30 minutes in the direction of her motion, and every 10 minutes in the opposite direction. Choose the correct option for the period T of the bus service and the speed (assumed constant) of the buses.

    1. 9 min, 40 km/h
    2. 25 min, 100 km/h
    3. 10 min, 90 km/h
    4. 15 min, 120 km/h
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (4)

    Buses of speed $v$ are spaced $vT$ apart. Same direction: $\dfrac{vT}{v-60} = \dfrac{1}{2}$ h; opposite direction: $\dfrac{vT}{v+60} = \dfrac{1}{6}$ h.
    Dividing: $\dfrac{v+60}{v-60} = 3 \Rightarrow v = 120$ km/h. Then $vT = 30$ km, so $T = \dfrac{1}{4}$ h $= 15$ min.

  4. NEET (UG) 2024, 5 May, Code T1 · Question 41
    The velocity ($v$) – time ($t$) plot of the motion of a body is shown below :
    The acceleration ($a$) – time ($t$) graph that best suits this motion is :

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (1)

    Acceleration is the slope of the $v$-$t$ graph.
    At first $v = 0$ (slope zero, $a = 0$); then $v$ rises at a steady rate (constant positive $a$); then $v$ is constant ($a = 0$); then $v$ falls at a steady rate (constant negative $a$); finally $v = 0$ again ($a = 0$).
    Graph (1) shows exactly this.
