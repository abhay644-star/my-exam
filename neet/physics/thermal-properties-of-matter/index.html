  1. NEET (UG) 2026, 21 June (re-exam), Code 50 · Question 24
    The temperature of a metallic sphere of radius $R$ is increased by a small amount $\Delta T$. If the linear coefficient of thermal expansion of the metal is $\alpha$, the approximate increase in the volume of the sphere is:

    1. $2\pi R^3\alpha\Delta T$
    2. $3\pi R^3\alpha\Delta T$
    3. $4\pi R^3\alpha\Delta T$
    4. $6\pi R^3\alpha\Delta T$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (3)

    $\gamma = 3\alpha$, so $\Delta V = 3\alpha V\Delta T = 3\alpha \cdot \dfrac{4}{3}\pi R^3 \Delta T$.
    $\Delta V = 4\pi R^3\alpha\Delta T$.

  2. NEET (UG) 2025, 4 May, Code 45 · Question 19
    Three identical heat conducting rods are connected in series as shown in the figure. The rods on the sides have thermal conductivity $2K$ while that in the middle has thermal conductivity $K$. The left end of the combination is maintained at temperature $3T$ and the right end at $T$. The rods are thermally insulated from outside. In steady state, temperature at the left junction is $T_1$ and that at the right junction is $T_2$. The ratio $T_1/T_2$ is

    1. $\dfrac{3}{2}$
    2. $\dfrac{4}{3}$
    3. $\dfrac{5}{3}$
    4. $\dfrac{5}{4}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (3)

    Thermal resistance $\propto 1/K$: if each side rod has resistance $r$, the middle rod has $2r$; total $4r$.
    The same heat current flows through all three, so the $2T$ temperature drop divides as $1:2:1$, i.e. $T/2$, $T$, $T/2$.
    $T_1 = 3T - T/2 = 2.5T$, $T_2 = 2.5T - T = 1.5T$; $T_1/T_2 = 5/3$.

  3. NEET (UG) 2024, 5 May, Code T1 · Question 40
    A metallic bar of Young’s modulus, $0.5 \times 10^{11}\ \text{N m}^{-2}$ and coefficient of linear thermal expansion $10^{-5}\ {}^\circ\text{C}^{-1}$, length 1 m and area of cross-section $10^{-3}\ \text{m}^2$ is heated from $0^\circ$C to $100^\circ$C without expansion or bending. The compressive force developed in it is:

    1. $100 \times 10^{3}$ N
    2. $2 \times 10^{3}$ N
    3. $5 \times 10^{3}$ N
    4. $50 \times 10^{3}$ N
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (4)

    The bar is prevented from expanding, so the thermal strain $\alpha\Delta T$ is cancelled by a compressive stress $Y\alpha\Delta T$.
    $F = YA\alpha\Delta T = 0.5\times10^{11} \times 10^{-3} \times 10^{-5} \times 100 = 5\times10^{4}$ N $= 50\times10^{3}$ N.
