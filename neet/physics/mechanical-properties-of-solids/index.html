  1. NEET (UG) 2024, 5 May, Code T1 · Question 11
    The maximum elongation of a steel wire of 1 m length if the elastic limit of steel and its Young’s modulus, respectively, are $8 \times 10^{8}\ \text{N m}^{-2}$ and $2 \times 10^{11}\ \text{N m}^{-2}$, is :

    1. 40 mm
    2. 8 mm
    3. 4 mm
    4. 0.4 mm
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (3)

    The largest stress the wire can take elastically is the elastic limit, so the maximum strain is $\dfrac{8\times10^{8}}{2\times10^{11}} = 4\times10^{-3}$.
    Maximum elongation $= 4\times10^{-3} \times 1\ \text{m} = 4$ mm.
