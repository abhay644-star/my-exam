  1. NEET (UG) 2026, 21 June (re-exam), Code 50 · Question 36
    Consider two circuits, (A) and (B), each having two resistors. One of them has a positive temperature coefficient of resistance, $+\alpha$, while the other one has a negative temperature of coefficient, $-\alpha$, as shown in the figure. The current through these circuits are denoted by $I_A$ and $I_B$. At initial temperature, the resistance of the two resistors is $R_0$. As the temperature is increased, the correct option that describes the variation of current in these circuits is:

    1. $I_A$ remains constant while $I_B$ increases
    2. $I_A$ decreases while $I_B$ increases
    3. $I_A$ increases while $I_B$ decreases
    4. Both $I_A$ and $I_B$ remain constant
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (1)

    (A) series: $R_0(1 - \alpha\Delta T) + R_0(1 + \alpha\Delta T) = 2R_0$, constant, so $I_A$ is constant.
    (B) parallel: $R_{eq} = \dfrac{R_0^2(1 - \alpha^2\Delta T^2)}{2R_0} = \dfrac{R_0}{2}(1 - \alpha^2\Delta T^2)$, which decreases.
    So $I_B$ increases.

  2. NEET (UG) 2025, 4 May, Code 45 · Question 6
    The current passing through the battery in the given circuit, is:

    1. 2.0 $A$
    2. 0.5 $A$
    3. 2.5 $A$
    4. 1.5 $A$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (2)

    Points C and D are joined by a wire, and A and F are joined by a wire. The battery branch (1.5 $\Omega$, 5.5 $\Omega$ and $\tfrac{1}{3}\ \Omega$ in series, $\tfrac{22}{3}\ \Omega$) connects B to E.
    Between B and E: arm B–A–E has 5 $\Omega$ and 3 $\Omega$; arm B–D–E has 2.5 $\Omega$ and 1.5 $\Omega$. Since $5/3 = 2.5/1.5$, the bridge is balanced and the 6 $\Omega$ resistor (A–D) carries no current.
    $R_{BE} = \dfrac{8\times4}{8+4} = \dfrac{8}{3}\ \Omega$; total $R = \dfrac{8}{3} + \dfrac{22}{3} = 10\ \Omega$.
    $I = 5/10 = 0.5$ A.

  3. NEET (UG) 2025, 4 May, Code 45 · Question 33
    A wire of resistance R is cut into 8 equal pieces. From these pieces two equivalent resistances are made by adding four of these together in parallel. Then these two sets are added in series. The net effective resistance of the combination is :

    1. $\dfrac{R}{64}$
    2. $\dfrac{R}{32}$
    3. $\dfrac{R}{16}$
    4. $\dfrac{R}{8}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (3)

    Each piece has resistance $R/8$. Four in parallel: $\dfrac{R/8}{4} = \dfrac{R}{32}$. Two such sets in series: $\dfrac{2R}{32} = \dfrac{R}{16}$.

  4. NEET (UG) 2025, 4 May, Code 45 · Question 36
    A constant voltage of 50 V is maintained between the points A and B of the circuit shown in the figure. The current through the branch CD of the circuit is :

    1. 1.5 $A$
    2. 2.0 $A$
    3. 2.5 $A$
    4. 3.0 $A$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (2)

    C and D are joined by a wire, so 1 $\Omega$ and 3 $\Omega$ are in parallel (A to CD) and 2 $\Omega$ and 4 $\Omega$ are in parallel (CD to B).
    $R = \dfrac{3}{4} + \dfrac{4}{3} = \dfrac{25}{12}\ \Omega$, $I = 50\times\dfrac{12}{25} = 24$ A.
    $V_{AC} = 24\times\dfrac{3}{4} = 18$ V, so 18 A flows in 1 $\Omega$; $V_{CB} = 32$ V, so 16 A flows in 2 $\Omega$.
    Current in CD (from C to D) $= 18 - 16 = 2.0$ A.

  5. NEET (UG) 2024, 5 May, Code T1 · Question 4
    A wire of length ‘$l$’ and resistance $100\ \Omega$ is divided into 10 equal parts. The first 5 parts are connected in series while the next 5 parts are connected in parallel. The two combinations are again connected in series. The resistance of this final combination is:

    1. $55\ \Omega$
    2. $60\ \Omega$
    3. $26\ \Omega$
    4. $52\ \Omega$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (4)

    Each part has resistance $100/10 = 10\ \Omega$.
    Five in series: $5 \times 10 = 50\ \Omega$. Five in parallel: $10/5 = 2\ \Omega$.
    In series: $50 + 2 = 52\ \Omega$.

  6. NEET (UG) 2024, 5 May, Code T1 · Question 7
    The terminal voltage of the battery, whose emf is $10V$ and internal resistance $1\ \Omega$, when connected through an external resistance of $4\ \Omega$ as shown in the figure is :

    1. $8\ V$
    2. $10\ V$
    3. $4\ V$
    4. $6\ V$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (1)

    Current $I = \dfrac{E}{R + r} = \dfrac{10}{4 + 1} = 2$ A.
    Terminal voltage $V = E - Ir = 10 - 2 \times 1 = 8$ V.

  7. NEET (UG) 2024, 5 May, Code T1 · Question 37
    Two heaters A and B have power rating of 1 kW and 2 kW, respectively. Those two are first connected in series and then in parallel to a fixed power source. The ratio of power outputs for these two cases is:

    1. 1 : 2
    2. 2 : 3
    3. 1 : 1
    4. 2 : 9
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (4)

    For the same supply voltage, $R = V^2/P$, so $R_A = 2R_B$.
    Series: $\dfrac{1}{P_s} = \dfrac{1}{P_A} + \dfrac{1}{P_B} = 1 + \dfrac{1}{2}$, so $P_s = \dfrac{2}{3}$ kW.
    Parallel: $P_p = P_A + P_B = 3$ kW.
    $P_s : P_p = \dfrac{2}{3} : 3 = 2 : 9$.

  8. NEET (UG) 2023, 7 May, Code E1 · Question 7
    If the galvanometer $G$ does not show any deflection in the circuit shown, the value of $R$ is given by :

    1. $200\ \Omega$
    2. $50\ \Omega$
    3. $100\ \Omega$
    4. $400\ \Omega$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (3)

    With no current through $G$, the 2 V cell draws no current, so the potential difference across $R$ equals 2 V.
    The 10 V cell drives a current $I = \dfrac{10}{400 + R}$ through $400\ \Omega$ and $R$ in series.
    $\dfrac{10R}{400 + R} = 2 \Rightarrow 10R = 800 + 2R \Rightarrow R = 100\ \Omega$.

  9. NEET (UG) 2023, 7 May, Code E1 · Question 11
    Resistance of a carbon resistor determined from colour codes is $(22000 \pm 5\%)\ \Omega$. The colour of third band must be :

    1. Red
    2. Green
    3. Orange
    4. Yellow
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (3)

    $22000 = 22 \times 10^3$: the first two bands are red (2) and red (2), and the third band (multiplier $10^3$) is orange.

  10. NEET (UG) 2023, 7 May, Code E1 · Question 33
    The magnitude and direction of the current in the following circuit is

    1. 0.2 A from $B$ to $A$ through $E$
    2. 0.5 A from $A$ to $B$ through $E$
    3. $\dfrac{5}{9}$ A from $A$ to $B$ through $E$
    4. 1.5 A from $B$ to $A$ through $E$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (2)

    The two cells oppose each other, so the net emf is $10 - 5 = 5$ V, acting in the direction of the 10 V cell.
    Total resistance $= 2 + 1 + 7 = 10\ \Omega$, so $I = 5/10 = 0.5$ A.
    The larger (10 V) cell drives the current from $A$ to $B$ through $E$.

  11. NEET (UG) 2023, 7 May, Code E1 · Question 44
    10 resistors, each of resistance $R$ are connected in series to a battery of emf $E$ and negligible internal resistance. Then those are connected in parallel to the same battery, the current is increased $n$ times. The value of $n$ is :

    1. 10
    2. 100
    3. 1
    4. 1000
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (2)

    Series: $R_s = 10R$, current $\dfrac{E}{10R}$. Parallel: $R_p = \dfrac{R}{10}$, current $\dfrac{10E}{R}$.
    $n = \dfrac{10E/R}{E/10R} = 100$.

  12. NEET (UG) 2023, 7 May, Code E1 · Question 48
    The resistance of platinum wire at 0°C is $2\Omega$ and $6.8\Omega$ at 80°C. The temperature coefficient of resistance of the wire is :

    1. $3\times10^{-4}$ °C$^{-1}$
    2. $3\times10^{-3}$ °C$^{-1}$
    3. $3\times10^{-2}$ °C$^{-1}$
    4. $3\times10^{-1}$ °C$^{-1}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (3)

    $R_t = R_0(1 + \alpha t) \Rightarrow \alpha = \dfrac{R_t - R_0}{R_0\,t} = \dfrac{6.8 - 2}{2 \times 80} = \dfrac{4.8}{160} = 3\times10^{-2}$ °C$^{-1}$.

  13. NEET (UG) 2022, 17 July, Code Q1 · Question 7
    As the temperature increases, the electrical resistance

    1. Increases for both conductors and semiconductors
    2. Decreases for both conductors and semiconductors
    3. Increases for conductors but decreases for semiconductors
    4. Decreases for conductors but increases for semiconductors
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (3)

    In a metal, the electrons collide more often with the vibrating ions as the temperature rises, so resistance increases.
    In a semiconductor, more charge carriers are released as the temperature rises, so resistance decreases.

  14. NEET (UG) 2022, 17 July, Code Q1 · Question 26
    Two resistors of resistance, 100 $\Omega$ and 200 $\Omega$ are connected in parallel in an electrical circuit. The ratio of the thermal energy developed in 100 $\Omega$ to that in 200 $\Omega$ in a given time is

    1. 1 : 2
    2. 2 : 1
    3. 1 : 4
    4. 4 : 1
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (2)

    In parallel both have the same voltage, so heat $H = \dfrac{V^2t}{R} \propto \dfrac{1}{R}$.
    $H_{100} : H_{200} = 200 : 100 = 2 : 1$.

  15. NEET (UG) 2022, 17 July, Code Q1 · Question 32
    A copper wire of length 10 m and radius $\left(\dfrac{10^{-2}}{\sqrt{\pi}}\right)$ m has electrical resistance of 10 $\Omega$. The current density in the wire for an electric field strength of 10 (V/m) is

    1. $10^{4}$ A/m$^2$
    2. $10^{6}$ A/m$^2$
    3. $10^{-5}$ A/m$^2$
    4. $10^{5}$ A/m$^2$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (4)

    $V = El = 10 \times 10 = 100$ V, so $I = V/R = 10$ A.
    Area $A = \pi r^2 = \pi \times \dfrac{10^{-4}}{\pi} = 10^{-4}$ m$^2$.
    $J = I/A = 10/10^{-4} = 10^{5}$ A/m$^2$.

  16. NEET (UG) 2022, 17 July, Code Q1 · Question 48
    A wheatstone bridge is used to determine the value of unknown resistance $X$ by adjusting the variable resistance $Y$ as shown in the figure. For the most precise measurement of $X$, the resistances $P$ and $Q$

    1. should be approximately equal to 2X
    2. should be approximately equal and are small
    3. should be very large and unequal
    4. do not play any significant role
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (2)

    A Wheatstone bridge is most sensitive when its four arms are of comparable value. With P and Q roughly equal and small, the galvanometer responds well to a small imbalance, so X can be found most precisely.
