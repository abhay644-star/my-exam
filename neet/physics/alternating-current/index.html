  1. NEET (UG) 2026, 21 June (re-exam), Code 50 · Question 30
    An ac voltage $V = 220\sin(2 \times 10^3 t)$ Volt is applied to a series LCR circuit. Then the current amplitude in this circuit is:
    (Given : $L = 10$ mH, $C = 25\ \mu$F, $R = 100\ \Omega$)

    1. 2.2 A
    2. 5.5 A
    3. 11.0 A
    4. 22.0 A
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (1)

    $X_L = \omega L = 2000 \times 0.01 = 20\ \Omega$; $X_C = \dfrac{1}{\omega C} = \dfrac{1}{2000 \times 25 \times 10^{-6}} = 20\ \Omega$.
    Resonance, so $Z = R = 100\ \Omega$ and $I_0 = 220/100 = 2.2$ A.

  2. NEET (UG) 2025, 4 May, Code 45 · Question 16
    To an ac power supply of 220 V at 50 Hz, a resistor of 20 $\Omega$, a capacitor of reactance 25 $\Omega$ and an inductor of reactance 45 $\Omega$ are connected in series. The corresponding current in the circuit and the phase angle between the current and the voltage is, respectively -

    1. 7.8 A and $30^\circ$
    2. 7.8 A and $45^\circ$
    3. 15.6 A and $30^\circ$
    4. 15.6 A and $45^\circ$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (2)

    $X_L - X_C = 45 - 25 = 20\ \Omega$, so $Z = \sqrt{20^2 + 20^2} = 20\sqrt{2} \approx 28.3\ \Omega$.
    $I = 220/28.3 \approx 7.8$ A, and $\tan\phi = \dfrac{X_L - X_C}{R} = 1 \Rightarrow \phi = 45^\circ$.
