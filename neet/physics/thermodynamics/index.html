  1. NEET (UG) 2026, 21 June (re-exam), Code 50 · Question 17
    In an adiabatic expansion, the temperature of one mole of an ideal monatomic gas ($\gamma = 5/3$) decreases from 60 K to 50 K. The work done by the gas in the process is:
    (Take the universal gas constant as $R = 8.3$ J mol$^{-1}$ K$^{-1}$)

    1. 41.5 J
    2. 83 J
    3. 124.5 J
    4. 166 J
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (3)

    Adiabatic work by gas $W = \dfrac{nR(T_1 - T_2)}{\gamma - 1} = \dfrac{1 \times 8.3 \times 10}{2/3}$.
    $W = 124.5$ J.

  2. NEET (UG) 2026, 21 June (re-exam), Code 50 · Question 39
    One mole of an ideal monatomic gas undergoes a cyclic process as shown in the figure. The total heat supplied to the gas is:

    1. 400 J
    2. 500 J
    3. 600 J
    4. 800 J
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (3)

    Over a complete cycle $\Delta U = 0$, so net heat = net work = area of the cycle.
    The cycle $a \to b \to c \to d$ is clockwise: $W = (300 - 100)(5 - 2) = 600$ J.
    Net heat supplied $= 600$ J.

  3. NEET (UG) 2025, 4 May, Code 45 · Question 29
    A container has two chambers of volumes $V_1$ = 2 litres and $V_2$ = 3 litres separated by a partition made of a thermal insulator. The chambers contains $n_1$ = 5 and $n_2$ = 4 moles of ideal gas at pressures $p_1$ = 1 atm and $p_2$ = 2 atm, respectively. When the partition is removed, the mixture attains an equilibrium pressure of :

    1. 1.3 atm
    2. 1.6 atm
    3. 1.4 atm
    4. 1.8 atm
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (2)

    The container is closed and does no work, so the total internal energy is conserved. For the same ideal gas $U = nC_vT = \dfrac{C_v}{R}pV$, so $pV$ totals are conserved.
    $p(V_1 + V_2) = p_1V_1 + p_2V_2 \Rightarrow p = \dfrac{1\times2 + 2\times3}{5} = 1.6$ atm.

  4. NEET (UG) 2025, 4 May, Code 45 · Question 41
    Two gases $A$ and $B$ are filled at the same pressure in separate cylinders with movable pistons of radius $r_A$ and $r_B$, respectively. On supplying an equal amount of heat to both the systems reversibly under constant pressure, the pistons of gas $A$ and $B$ are displaced by 16 $cm$ and 9 $cm$, respectively. If the change in their internal energy is the same, then the ratio $r_A/r_B$ is equal to

    1. $\dfrac{4}{3}$
    2. $\dfrac{3}{4}$
    3. $\dfrac{2}{\sqrt{3}}$
    4. $\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (2)

    $Q = \Delta U + W$ with equal $Q$ and $\Delta U$, so the work $W = p\Delta V$ is equal. With equal pressure, $\Delta V$ is equal.
    $\pi r_A^2\times16 = \pi r_B^2\times9 \Rightarrow \dfrac{r_A}{r_B} = \dfrac{3}{4}$.
