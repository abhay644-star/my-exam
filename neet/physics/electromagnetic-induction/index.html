  1. NEET (UG) 2026, 21 June (re-exam), Code 50 · Question 3
    Two identical inductors are connected in two different configurations $P$ and $Q$, where a time varying current $I(t)$ is flowing, as shown in the figure. The induced emf between points $a$ and $b$ for configuration $P$ is $E_P$ and that for configuration $Q$ is $E_Q$. The ratio $E_P/E_Q$ is:
    [Neglect the effect of mutual inductance.]

    1. $\dfrac{1}{4}$
    2. $\dfrac{1}{2}$
    3. $1$
    4. $2$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (4)

    In $P$, points $a$ and $b$ span only one inductor carrying the full current: $E_P = L\dfrac{dI}{dt}$.
    In $Q$, the two inductors are in parallel between $a$ and $b$: $L_{eq} = L/2$, so $E_Q = \dfrac{L}{2}\dfrac{dI}{dt}$.
    $E_P/E_Q = 2$.

  2. NEET (UG) 2026, 21 June (re-exam), Code 50 · Question 5
    Consider a long solenoid of length $l$ and radius $r$. If $n$ is the number of turns per unit length and $\mu_0$ is the permeability of free space, the inductance of the solenoid is :

    1. $\mu_0\pi n^2r^2l$
    2. $\mu_0 n^2r^2l$
    3. $(\mu_0/2\pi)n^2r^2l$
    4. $2\mu_0\pi n^2r^2l$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (1)

    Field inside $B = \mu_0 nI$; flux linkage $N\Phi = (nl)(\mu_0 nI)(\pi r^2)$.
    $L = N\Phi/I = \mu_0\pi n^2 r^2 l$.

  3. NEET (UG) 2026, 21 June (re-exam), Code 50 · Question 32
    A conducting loop of finite resistance lies on the $x - y$ plane. There is a constant magnetic field in the $z$ direction. The area of the loop varies with time $t$, as $A = A_0(1 + \sin t)$ in appropriate units. The figure that correctly indicates the qualitative behaviour of the power $P$ dissipated in the loop as a function of time is:

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (2)

    $\varepsilon = -\dfrac{d\Phi}{dt} = -BA_0\cos t$.
    $P = \dfrac{\varepsilon^2}{R} = \dfrac{B^2A_0^2}{R}\cos^2 t$: maximum at $t = 0$, falls to zero at $t = \pi/2$, then rises again.
    This is graph (2).

  4. NEET (UG) 2025, 4 May, Code 45 · Question 11
    AB is a part of an electrical circuit (see figure). The potential difference “$V_A - V_B$”, at the instant when current i = 2 A and is increasing at a rate of 1 amp / second is:

    1. 5 volt
    2. 6 volt
    3. 9 volt
    4. 10 volt
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (4)

    Going from A to B along the current: drop across the inductor $= L\dfrac{di}{dt} = 1\times1 = 1$ V; drop across the cell (current enters its positive terminal) $= 5$ V; drop across the resistor $= iR = 2\times2 = 4$ V.
    $V_A - V_B = 1 + 5 + 4 = 10$ V.

  5. NEET (UG) 2024, 5 May, Code T1 · Question 2
    In the above diagram, a strong bar magnet is moving towards solenoid-2 from solenoid-1. The direction of induced current in solenoid-1 and that in solenoid-2, respectively, are through the directions:

    1. $AB$ and $CD$
    2. $BA$ and $DC$
    3. $AB$ and $DC$
    4. $BA$ and $CD$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (3)

    By Lenz's law each solenoid opposes the change in its flux.
    Solenoid-1: the N pole is moving away from end $B$, so end $B$ becomes a S pole (to attract it back). For the winding shown this needs a current from $A$ to $B$.
    Solenoid-2: the S pole is approaching end $C$, so end $C$ becomes a S pole (to repel it). For the winding shown this needs a current from $D$ to $C$.
    So the currents are $AB$ and $DC$.

  6. NEET (UG) 2024, 5 May, Code T1 · Question 49
    A sheet is placed on a horizontal surface in front of a strong magnetic pole. A force is needed to :
    A. hold the sheet there if it is magnetic.
    B. hold the sheet there if it is non-magnetic.
    C. move the sheet away from the pole with uniform velocity if it is conducting.
    D. move the sheet away from the pole with uniform velocity if it is both, non-conducting and non-polar.
    Choose the correct statement(s) from the options given below:

    1. A, C and D only
    2. C only
    3. B and D only
    4. A and C only
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (4)

    A: a magnetic sheet is attracted by the pole, so a force is needed to hold it (true).
    B: a non-magnetic sheet feels no magnetic force at rest, so no force is needed (false).
    C: moving a conducting sheet through the changing field induces eddy currents that oppose the motion, so a force is needed even at uniform velocity (true).
    D: a non-conducting, non-polar sheet does not interact with the field (false).
    So A and C only.

  7. NEET (UG) 2023, 7 May, Code E1 · Question 13
    The magnetic energy stored in an inductor of inductance $4\ \mu H$ carrying a current of 2 A is :

    1. $4\ \mu J$
    2. $4\ mJ$
    3. $8\ mJ$
    4. $8\ \mu J$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (4)

    $U = \frac{1}{2}LI^2 = \frac{1}{2} \times 4 \times 10^{-6} \times 2^2 = 8 \times 10^{-6}$ J $= 8\ \mu J$.
