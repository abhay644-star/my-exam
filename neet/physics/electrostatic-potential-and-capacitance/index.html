  1. NEET (UG) 2026, 21 June (re-exam), Code 50 · Question 9
    Three identical capacitors, $P$, $Q$ and $S$, each of the capacitance $C$, are connected to a battery of voltage $V$, as shown in the figure. If the energy stored in the capacitor $P$ and total energy stored in the system are $U_P$ and $U_T$, respectively, then the ratio $\dfrac{U_P}{U_T}$ is:

    1. $2/3$
    2. $1/3$
    3. $1/2$
    4. $1/6$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (4)

    $P$ and $Q$ in series (each gets $V/2$) are in parallel with $S$ (gets $V$).
    $U_P = \dfrac{1}{2}C\left(\dfrac{V}{2}\right)^2 = \dfrac{CV^2}{8}$; $U_T = \dfrac{1}{2}\left(\dfrac{C}{2} + C\right)V^2 = \dfrac{3CV^2}{4}$.
    $U_P/U_T = 1/6$.

  2. NEET (UG) 2026, 21 June (re-exam), Code 50 · Question 21
    A unit positive point charge is taken slowly through an infinitesimally thin tube that is inside a charged dielectric sphere of radius $R$, having uniform positive charge density $\rho$, as shown in the figure. The initial and final positions of the charge are marked by $A$ and $B$ at distance $2R$ and $3R$ respectively, from the centre of the sphere. In this process, the magnitude of the total work done on the point charge is $\dfrac{\rho R^2}{n\varepsilon_0}$. The value of $n$ is :
    ($\varepsilon_0$ is the permittivity of vacuum)

    1. $2$
    2. $6$
    3. $9$
    4. $18$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (4)

    The field is conservative, so the work depends only on the end points, both outside the sphere.
    Outside: $V(r) = \dfrac{Q}{4\pi\varepsilon_0 r}$ with $Q = \dfrac{4}{3}\pi R^3\rho$, i.e. $V(r) = \dfrac{\rho R^3}{3\varepsilon_0 r}$.
    $|W| = V(2R) - V(3R) = \dfrac{\rho R^2}{3\varepsilon_0}\left(\dfrac{1}{2} - \dfrac{1}{3}\right) = \dfrac{\rho R^2}{18\varepsilon_0}$, so $n = 18$.

  3. NEET (UG) 2026, 21 June (re-exam), Code 50 · Question 34
    Consider a fixed uniformly charged insulating sphere with radius $R$ and total charge $+Q$. A point charge $-q$ ($q << Q$) with mass $m$ is released from rest at a distance of $3R$ from the centre of the charged sphere. When the point charge reaches the surface of the sphere, its speed is:
    ($\varepsilon_0$ is the permittivity of vacuum, neglect gravitational forces).

    1. $\sqrt{\dfrac{3Qq}{4\pi\varepsilon_0 mR}}$
    2. $\sqrt{\dfrac{2Qq}{3\pi\varepsilon_0 mR}}$
    3. $\sqrt{\dfrac{Qq}{3\pi\varepsilon_0 mR}}$
    4. $\sqrt{\dfrac{Qq}{4\pi\varepsilon_0 mR}}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (3)

    Gain in KE = loss in PE: $\dfrac{1}{2}mv^2 = \dfrac{Qq}{4\pi\varepsilon_0}\left(\dfrac{1}{R} - \dfrac{1}{3R}\right) = \dfrac{2Qq}{12\pi\varepsilon_0 R}$.
    $v^2 = \dfrac{Qq}{3\pi\varepsilon_0 mR}$, so $v = \sqrt{\dfrac{Qq}{3\pi\varepsilon_0 mR}}$.

  4. NEET (UG) 2025, 4 May, Code 45 · Question 20
    The plates of a parallel plate capacitor are separated by $d$. Two slabs of different dielectric constant $K_1$ and $K_2$ with thickness $\dfrac{3}{8}d$ and $\dfrac{d}{2}$, respectively are inserted in the capacitor. Due to this, the capacitance becomes two times larger than when there is nothing between the plates.
    If $K_1 = 1.25\,K_2$, the value of $K_1$ is :

    1. 2.66
    2. 2.33
    3. 1.60
    4. 1.33
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (1)

    $C = \dfrac{\varepsilon_0 A}{d - t_1 - t_2 + t_1/K_1 + t_2/K_2} = 2\dfrac{\varepsilon_0 A}{d}$, so the denominator is $d/2$.
    $\dfrac{d}{8} + \dfrac{3d}{8K_1} + \dfrac{d}{2K_2} = \dfrac{d}{2}$. With $K_2 = 0.8K_1$: $\dfrac{0.375}{K_1} + \dfrac{0.625}{K_1} = \dfrac{3}{8}$.
    $K_1 = 8/3 \approx 2.66$.

  5. NEET (UG) 2024, 5 May, Code T1 · Question 5
    In the following circuit, the equivalent capacitance between terminal $A$ and terminal $B$ is :

    1. $0.5\ \mu F$
    2. $4\ \mu F$
    3. $2\ \mu F$
    4. $1\ \mu F$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (3)

    All five capacitors are equal, so the bridge is balanced: the two ends of the middle $2\ \mu F$ capacitor are at the same potential and it holds no charge.
    Each arm is two $2\ \mu F$ capacitors in series, i.e. $1\ \mu F$. The two arms in parallel give $1 + 1 = 2\ \mu F$.

  6. NEET (UG) 2024, 5 May, Code T1 · Question 29
    Given below are two statements: one is labelled as $\textbf{Assertion A}$ and the other is labelled as $\textbf{Reason R.}$
    $\textbf{Assertion A}$ : The potential $(V)$ at any axial point, at 2 m distance($r$) from the centre of the dipole of dipole moment vector $\vec{P}$ of magnitude, $4 \times 10^{-6}$ C m, is $\pm 9\times10^{3}\ V$.
    (Take $\dfrac{1}{4\pi\epsilon_0} = 9\times10^{9}$ SI units)
    $\textbf{Reason R}$ : $V = \pm\dfrac{2P}{4\pi\epsilon_0 r^2}$, where $r$ is the distance of any axial point, situated at 2 m from the centre of the dipole.
    In the light of the above statements, choose the $\textit{correct}$ answer from the options given below:

    1. A is true but R is false.
    2. A is false but R is true.
    3. Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation of A.
    4. Both A and R are true and R is NOT the correct explanation of A.
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (1)

    On the axis of a short dipole $V = \pm\dfrac{P}{4\pi\epsilon_0 r^2} = \pm\dfrac{9\times10^{9} \times 4\times10^{-6}}{2^2} = \pm 9\times10^{3}$ V, so A is true.
    R has an extra factor of 2 (that factor belongs to the axial electric field, not the potential), so R is false.

  7. NEET (UG) 2024, 5 May, Code T1 · Question 30
    A thin spherical shell is charged by some source. The potential difference between the two points $C$ and $P$ (in V) shown in the figure is:
    (Take $\dfrac{1}{4\pi\epsilon_0} = 9\times10^{9}$ SI units)

    1. $0.5 \times 10^{5}$
    2. zero
    3. $3 \times 10^{5}$
    4. $1 \times 10^{5}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (2)

    Inside a charged conducting (thin) spherical shell the electric field is zero, so the potential everywhere inside equals the potential on the shell, $\dfrac{q}{4\pi\epsilon_0 R}$.
    $C$ (the centre) and $P$ (on the shell) are therefore at the same potential: the potential difference is zero.

  8. NEET (UG) 2024, 5 May, Code T1 · Question 47
    If the plates of a parallel plate capacitor connected to a battery are moved close to each other, then
    A. the charge stored in it, increases.
    B. the energy stored in it, decreases.
    C. its capacitance increases.
    D. the ratio of charge to its potential remains the same.
    E. the product of charge and voltage increases.
    Choose the most appropriate answer from the options given below:

    1. B, D and E only
    2. A, B and C only
    3. A, B and E only
    4. A, C and E only
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (4)

    $C = \dfrac{\epsilon_0 A}{d}$ increases as $d$ decreases (C true). The battery keeps $V$ fixed, so $Q = CV$ increases (A true).
    Energy $\tfrac{1}{2}CV^2$ increases (B false). $Q/V = C$ changes (D false). $QV = CV^2$ increases (E true).
    So A, C and E only.

  9. NEET (UG) 2023, 7 May, Code E1 · Question 3
    The equivalent capacitance of the system shown in the following circuit is :

    1. $2\ \mu F$
    2. $3\ \mu F$
    3. $6\ \mu F$
    4. $9\ \mu F$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (1)

    The two $3\ \mu F$ capacitors in parallel give $6\ \mu F$.
    This is in series with $3\ \mu F$: $C = \dfrac{3 \times 6}{3 + 6} = 2\ \mu F$.

  10. NEET (UG) 2023, 7 May, Code E1 · Question 39
    An electric dipole is placed as shown in the figure.
    The electric potential (in $10^2$ V) at point P due to the dipole is ($\epsilon_0$ = permittivity of free space and $\dfrac{1}{4\pi\epsilon_0} = K$):

    1. $\left(\dfrac{3}{8}\right)qK$
    2. $\left(\dfrac{5}{8}\right)qK$
    3. $\left(\dfrac{8}{5}\right)qK$
    4. $\left(\dfrac{8}{3}\right)qK$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (1)

    P is 5 cm from the centre O, so it is $5 - 3 = 2$ cm from $+q$ and $5 + 3 = 8$ cm from $-q$.
    $V = Kq\left(\dfrac{1}{0.02} - \dfrac{1}{0.08}\right) = Kq(50 - 12.5) = 37.5\,qK = \dfrac{3}{8}qK \times 10^2$ V.

  11. NEET (UG) 2022, 17 July, Code Q1 · Question 16
    The angle between the electric lines of force and the equipotential surface is

    1. 0°
    2. 45°
    3. 90°
    4. 180°
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (3)

    No work is done in moving a charge along an equipotential surface, so the field has no component along it: field lines always cross equipotential surfaces at 90°.

  12. NEET (UG) 2022, 17 July, Code Q1 · Question 31
    Two hollow conducting spheres of radii $R_1$ and $R_2$ ($R_1 >> R_2$) have equal charges. The potential would be

    1. More on bigger sphere
    2. More on smaller sphere
    3. Equal on both the spheres
    4. Dependent on the material property of the sphere
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (2)

    $V = \dfrac{kQ}{R}$: with equal charges the potential is inversely proportional to the radius, so it is more on the smaller sphere.

  13. NEET (UG) 2022, 17 July, Code Q1 · Question 47
    A capacitor of capacitance $C$ = 900 pF is charged fully by 100 V battery $B$ as shown in figure (a). Then it is disconnected from the battery and connected to another uncharged capacitor of capacitance $C$ = 900 pF as shown in figure (b). The electrostatic energy stored by the system (b) is

    1. $4.5 \times 10^{-6}$ J
    2. $3.25 \times 10^{-6}$ J
    3. $2.25 \times 10^{-6}$ J
    4. $1.5 \times 10^{-6}$ J
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (3)

    Charge $Q = CV = 900 \times 10^{-12} \times 100 = 9 \times 10^{-8}$ C. Shared between two equal capacitors, the common potential is 50 V.
    Energy $= 2 \times \frac{1}{2} \times 900 \times 10^{-12} \times 50^2 = 2.25 \times 10^{-6}$ J (half the original $4.5 \times 10^{-6}$ J).
