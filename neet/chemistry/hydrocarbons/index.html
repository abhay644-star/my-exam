  1. NEET (UG) 2026, 21 June (re-exam), Code 50 · Question 76
    Given below are two statements :
    Statement I : trans-But-2-ene upon treatment with $\mathrm{Br_2}$ in $\mathrm{CCl_4}$ gives the following product.
    Statement II : cis-But-2-ene upon treatment with alkaline $\mathrm{KMnO_4}$ gives the following product.
    In the light of the above statements, choose the most appropriate answer from the options given below.

    1. Both Statement I and Statement II are correct
    2. Both Statement I and Statement II are incorrect
    3. Statement I is correct but Statement II is incorrect
    4. Statement I is incorrect but Statement II is correct
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (4)

    $\mathrm{Br_2}$ adds anti. Anti addition to trans-but-2-ene gives meso-2,3-dibromobutane.
    In the Newman projection of Statement I, the Br atoms are anti but the two Me groups are not related by a centre of symmetry, so it shows one enantiomer of the (±) form. Statement I is incorrect.
    Cold alkaline $\mathrm{KMnO_4}$ adds two OH groups syn. Syn addition to cis-but-2-ene gives meso-butane-2,3-diol.
    Rotating the rear carbon of the Statement II projection to put the two OH groups anti puts Me opposite Me and H opposite H through a centre of symmetry, so it is meso. Statement II is correct.

  2. NEET (UG) 2026, 21 June (re-exam), Code 50 · Question 77
    Consider the following reaction sequences and choose the correct option.

    1. K and L are geometrical isomers
    2. K and L are enantiomers
    3. M and N are geometrical isomers
    4. M and N are stereoisomers
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (1)

    Na/liq. $\mathrm{NH_3}$ reduces the alkyne by anti addition to (E)-1-phenylpropene (L). Lindlar's catalyst gives syn addition and the (Z)-alkene (K). So K and L are geometrical isomers.
    HBr/peroxide adds through the more stable benzylic radical, giving $\mathrm{PhCH_2CHBrCH_3}$ (N).
    Ionic HBr adds through the benzylic cation, giving $\mathrm{PhCHBrCH_2CH_3}$ (M).
    M and N are position isomers, not stereoisomers.

  3. NEET (UG) 2025, 4 May, Code 45 · Question 60
    Which one of the following reactions does $\textbf{NOT}$ give benzene as the product ?

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (4)

    (1) Sodium benzoate with soda lime is decarboxylated to benzene.
    (2) n-Hexane over $\mathrm{Mo_2O_3}$ at 773 K, 10–20 atm is aromatised to benzene.
    (3) Ethyne passed through a red hot iron tube at 873 K trimerises to benzene.
    (4) Benzenediazonium chloride warmed with water gives phenol, not benzene.

  4. NEET (UG) 2025, 4 May, Code 45 · Question 75
    How many products (including stereoisomers) are expected from monochlorination of the following compound?

    1. 2
    2. 3
    3. 5
    4. 6
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (4)

    The compound is 2-methylbutane, with four kinds of hydrogen.
    Chlorine on a $\mathrm{CH_3}$ of the isopropyl end gives 1-chloro-2-methylbutane (chiral: 2 enantiomers); on C2 gives 2-chloro-2-methylbutane (1); on C3 gives 2-chloro-3-methylbutane (chiral: 2); on C4 gives 1-chloro-3-methylbutane (1).
    Total $= 2 + 1 + 2 + 1 = 6$.

  5. NEET (UG) 2025, 4 May, Code 45 · Question 76
    Among the given compounds I-III, the correct order of bond dissociation energy of C–H bond marked with * is :

    1. II > I > III
    2. I > II > III
    3. III > II > I
    4. II > III > I
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (1)

    More s-character in the carbon orbital gives a shorter, stronger C–H bond: $sp$ (II, alkyne C–H) > $sp^2$ (I, aryl C–H).
    In III the marked C–H is on an $sp^3$ carbon, and breaking it gives a resonance-stabilised (allylic-type) radical, so its bond is the weakest.
    Order: II > I > III.

  6. NEET (UG) 2025, 4 May, Code 45 · Question 77
    Which one of the following compounds $\underline{\textbf{does not}}$ decolourize bromine water?

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (1)

    Phenol and aniline have strongly activated rings and react with bromine water at once (giving 2,4,6-tribromo products), and styrene adds bromine across its C=C bond, so all three decolourise it.
    Cyclohexane is a saturated alkane and does not react with bromine water, so option (1).

  7. NEET (UG) 2024, 5 May, Code T1 · Question 52
    Given below are two statements:
    $\textbf{Statement I :}$ The boiling point of three isomeric pentanes follows the order
    n-pentane > isopentane > neopentane
    $\textbf{Statement II :}$ When branching increases, the molecule attains a shape of sphere. This results in smaller surface area for contact, due to which the intermolecular forces between the spherical molecules are weak, thereby lowering the boiling point.
    In the light of the above statements, choose the $\textit{most appropriate}$ answer from the options given below:

    1. Statement I is correct but Statement II is incorrect.
    2. Statement I is incorrect but Statement II is correct.
    3. Both Statement I and Statement II are correct.
    4. Both Statement I and Statement II are incorrect.
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (3)

    Boiling points: n-pentane (309 K) > isopentane (301 K) > neopentane (282.5 K), so Statement I is correct.
    Statement II gives the correct reason: more branching makes the molecule more compact (nearly spherical), reducing the surface area of contact and hence the van der Waals forces, so the boiling point falls.

  8. NEET (UG) 2023, 7 May, Code E1 · Question 76
    Weight (g) of two moles of the organic compound, which is obtained by heating sodium ethanoate with sodium hydroxide in presence of calcium oxide is :

    1. 16
    2. 32
    3. 30
    4. 18
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (2)

    Decarboxylation with soda lime: $\mathrm{CH_3COONa + NaOH \xrightarrow{CaO,\ \Delta} CH_4 + Na_2CO_3}$.
    The product is methane (molar mass 16 g mol$^{-1}$), so two moles weigh $2 \times 16 = 32$ g.

  9. NEET (UG) 2023, 7 May, Code E1 · Question 89
    Consider the following compounds/species:
    The number of compounds/species which obey Huckel’s rule is ______.

    1. 4
    2. 6
    3. 2
    4. 5
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (1)

    Hückel’s rule: a planar, cyclic, fully conjugated system with $(4n + 2)\ \pi$ electrons.
    i. naphthalene, 10 $\pi$: yes. ii. cyclopentadienyl anion, 6 $\pi$: yes. iii. cyclobutadiene, 4 $\pi$: no. iv. cyclopropenyl anion, 4 $\pi$: no. v. cyclopropenyl cation, 2 $\pi$: yes. vi. cyclooctatetraene, 8 $\pi$ (and not planar): no. vii. anthracene, 14 $\pi$: yes.
    So 4 species obey the rule.
