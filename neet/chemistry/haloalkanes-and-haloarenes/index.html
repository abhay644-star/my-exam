  1. NEET (UG) 2026, 21 June (re-exam), Code 50 · Question 72
    One of the products formed in the following reaction is

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (4)

    A Grignard reagent is a very strong base. It removes the acidic N–H proton of cyclohexylamine instead of adding to it.
    $\mathrm{C_6H_{11}MgBr + C_6H_{11}NH_2 \rightarrow C_6H_{12} + C_6H_{11}NHMgBr}$.
    So cyclohexane (option 4) is a product.

  2. NEET (UG) 2025, 4 May, Code 45 · Question 84
    Given below are two statements : one is labelled as $\textbf{Assertion (A)}$ and the other is labelled as $\textbf{Reason (R)}$.
    $\textbf{Assertion (A) :}$ shown in the figure below.
    $\textbf{Reason (R) :}$ Iodine is a better leaving group because of its large size.
    In the light of the above statements, choose the $\textbf{correct}$ answer from the options given below :

    1. Both $\textbf{A}$ and $\textbf{R}$ are true and $\textbf{R}$ is the correct explanation of $\textbf{A}$
    2. Both $\textbf{A}$ and $\textbf{R}$ are true but $\textbf{R}$ is $\textbf{not}$ the correct explanation of $\textbf{A}$
    3. $\textbf{A}$ is true but $\textbf{R}$ is false
    4. $\textbf{A}$ is false but $\textbf{R}$ is true
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (1)

    In an $\mathrm{S_N2}$ reaction the C–X bond breaks in the rate-determining step. The C–I bond is longer and weaker than C–Cl, and the large iodide ion is a weak base that holds the negative charge well, so it is a better leaving group.
    Hence the alkyl iodide reacts faster than the alkyl chloride: A is true and R correctly explains it.

  3. NEET (UG) 2024, 5 May, Code T1 · Question 73
    The compound that will undergo $\mathrm{S_N1}$ reaction with the fastest rate is

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (2)

    The rate of an $\mathrm{S_N1}$ reaction depends on the stability of the carbocation formed.
    Option (2), 1-bromo-1-phenylethane, gives a secondary benzylic carbocation, stabilised by resonance with the ring and by hyperconjugation with the methyl group, so it reacts fastest.
    Bromobenzene does not ionise (C–Br has partial double-bond character and an aryl cation is very unstable), and options (3) and (4) give primary and secondary alkyl carbocations with no resonance stabilisation.

  4. NEET (UG) 2024, 5 May, Code T1 · Question 86
    Major products A and B formed in the following reaction sequence, are

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (3)

    $\mathrm{PBr_3}$ replaces the –OH group by –Br, so A is 1-bromo-2-methylcyclohexane.
    Alcoholic KOH on heating brings about dehydrohalogenation. By Saytzeff's rule the more substituted alkene is the major product, so B is 1-methylcyclohexene (the double bond towards the carbon carrying the methyl group). This is option (3).

  5. NEET (UG) 2024, 5 May, Code T1 · Question 88
    The products A and B obtained in the following reactions, respectively, are
    $\mathrm{3ROH + PCl_3 \rightarrow 3RCl + A}$
    $\mathrm{ROH + PCl_5 \rightarrow RCl + HCl + B}$

    1. $\mathrm{H_3PO_4}$ and $\mathrm{POCl_3}$
    2. $\mathrm{H_3PO_3}$ and $\mathrm{POCl_3}$
    3. $\mathrm{POCl_3}$ and $\mathrm{H_3PO_3}$
    4. $\mathrm{POCl_3}$ and $\mathrm{H_3PO_4}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (2)

    $\mathrm{3ROH + PCl_3 \rightarrow 3RCl + H_3PO_3}$ (phosphorous acid), and $\mathrm{ROH + PCl_5 \rightarrow RCl + HCl + POCl_3}$ (phosphoryl chloride).
    So A is $\mathrm{H_3PO_3}$ and B is $\mathrm{POCl_3}$.

  6. NEET (UG) 2023, 7 May, Code E1 · Question 58
    The given compound
    is an example of ________.

    1. benzylic halide
    2. aryl halide
    3. allylic halide
    4. vinylic halide
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (3)

    In $\mathrm{C_6H_5{-}CH{=}CH{-}CH(X){-}CH_2CH_3}$ the halogen is on an $\mathrm{sp^3}$ carbon next to the C=C double bond (not next to the ring, and not on the double-bond carbon or the ring). So it is an allylic halide.

  7. NEET (UG) 2023, 7 May, Code E1 · Question 59
    Identify the product in the following reaction:

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (2)

    (i) Sandmeyer reaction: $\mathrm{C_6H_5N_2^+Cl^- \xrightarrow{Cu_2Br_2/HBr} C_6H_5Br}$.
    (ii) With Mg in dry ether: $\mathrm{C_6H_5MgBr}$ (Grignard reagent).
    (iii) Water protonates the Grignard reagent: $\mathrm{C_6H_5MgBr + H_2O \rightarrow C_6H_6 + Mg(OH)Br}$.
    The product is benzene, option (2).

  8. NEET (UG) 2023, 7 May, Code E1 · Question 73
    Consider the following reaction and identify the product (P).

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (1)

    The –OH is protonated and leaves as water, giving a secondary carbocation at C-2: $\mathrm{(CH_3)_2CH{-}\overset{+}{C}H{-}CH_3}$.
    A 1,2-hydride shift from C-3 gives the more stable tertiary carbocation $\mathrm{(CH_3)_2\overset{+}{C}{-}CH_2CH_3}$.
    $\mathrm{Br^-}$ attacks it to give 2-bromo-2-methylbutane, option (1).

  9. NEET (UG) 2023, 7 May, Code E1 · Question 96
    Identify the final product [D] obtained in the following sequence of reactions.

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (1)

    [A]: $\mathrm{LiAlH_4}$ reduces ethanal to ethanol, $\mathrm{CH_3CH_2OH}$.
    [B]: conc. $\mathrm{H_2SO_4}$ with heat dehydrates it to ethene, $\mathrm{CH_2{=}CH_2}$.
    [C]: HBr adds to give bromoethane, $\mathrm{CH_3CH_2Br}$.
    [D]: with bromobenzene and Na in dry ether (Wurtz–Fittig reaction), the ethyl and phenyl groups join to give ethylbenzene, option (1).

  10. NEET (UG) 2022, 17 July, Code Q1 · Question 66
    The $\textbf{incorrect}$ statement regarding chirality is

    1. S$_{\text{N}}$1 reaction yields 1 : 1 mixture of both enantiomers
    2. The product obtained by S$_{\text{N}}$2 reaction of haloalkane having chirality at the reactive site shows inversion of configuration
    3. Enantiomers are superimposable mirror images on each other
    4. A racemic mixture shows zero optical rotation
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (3)

    Enantiomers are non-superimposable mirror images, so statement (3) is incorrect.

  11. NEET (UG) 2022, 17 July, Code Q1 · Question 67
    Which of the following sequence of reactions is suitable to synthesize chlorobenzene?

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (1)

    Benzene with Cl₂ and anhydrous FeCl₃ (a Lewis acid) undergoes electrophilic substitution to give chlorobenzene.
    Phenol does not form a diazonium salt with NaNO₂/HCl, benzene does not react with HCl, and aniline with HCl only forms anilinium chloride.
