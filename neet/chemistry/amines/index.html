  1. NEET (UG) 2026, 21 June (re-exam), Code 50 · Question 79
    Identify the reactions which give aniline as the major product.
    Choose the correct answer from the options given below.

    1. A and B only
    2. B and D only
    3. A and C only
    4. C and D only
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (2)

    A: $\mathrm{LiAlH_4}$ reduces $\mathrm{C_6H_5CN}$ to benzylamine, $\mathrm{C_6H_5CH_2NH_2}$, not aniline.
    B: Hofmann bromamide degradation of benzamide gives aniline.
    C: $\mathrm{NaBH_4}$ does not reduce an aromatic nitro group.
    D: acid hydrolysis of acetanilide gives aniline (as its salt) and acetic acid.
    So B and D only.

  2. NEET (UG) 2026, 21 June (re-exam), Code 50 · Question 84
    Given below are two statements:
    Statement-I : Oxidation of p-nitrotoluene with acidic $\mathrm{KMnO_4}$ gives an acid that is stronger than benzoic acid.
    Statement-II : Reduction of p-nitrotoluene with Sn/HCl followed by neutralization gives an amine that is more basic than aniline.
    In light of the above statements, choose the most appropriate answer from the options given below.

    1. Both Statement-I and Statement-II are correct
    2. Both Statement-I and Statement-II are incorrect
    3. Statement-I is correct but Statement-II is incorrect
    4. Statement-I is incorrect but Statement-II is correct
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (1)

    Oxidation gives p-nitrobenzoic acid. The electron-withdrawing $\mathrm{-NO_2}$ group stabilises the carboxylate, so it is a stronger acid than benzoic acid.
    Reduction gives p-toluidine. The electron-donating $\mathrm{-CH_3}$ group (+I and hyperconjugation) makes it more basic than aniline.
    Both statements are correct.

  3. NEET (UG) 2025, 4 May, Code 45 · Question 49
    Predict the major product ‘P’ in the following sequence of reactions -

    Answer: (1)

    HBr with benzoyl peroxide adds anti-Markovnikov (free radical), so Br goes to the less substituted ring carbon next to the $\mathrm{CH_3}$-bearing carbon: 1-bromo-2-methylcyclopentane.
    KCN replaces Br by $-\mathrm{CN}$ (a nitrile), and Na(Hg)/$\mathrm{C_2H_5OH}$ (Mendius reduction) converts $-\mathrm{CN}$ to $-\mathrm{CH_2NH_2}$.

  4. NEET (UG) 2025, 4 May, Code 45 · Question 67
    The correct order of decreasing basic strength of the given amines is :

    1. N-methylaniline > benzenamine > ethanamine > N-ethylethanamine
    2. N-ethylethanamine > ethanamine > benzenamine > N-methylaniline
    3. N-ethylethanamine > ethanamine > N-methylaniline > benzenamine
    4. benzenamine > ethanamine > N-methylaniline > N-ethylethanamine
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (3)

    Aliphatic amines are much stronger bases than aryl amines, because in aryl amines the nitrogen lone pair is delocalised into the ring.
    Among the aliphatic amines (in water) the secondary amine N-ethylethanamine is stronger than the primary ethanamine; among the aryl amines the +I effect of $\mathrm{CH_3}$ makes N-methylaniline slightly stronger than benzenamine.
    Order: N-ethylethanamine > ethanamine > N-methylaniline > benzenamine.

  5. NEET (UG) 2025, 4 May, Code 45 · Question 74
    Given below are two statements :
    $\textbf{Statement I :}$ Benzenediazonium salt is prepared by the reaction of aniline with nitrous acid at 273 - 278 K. It decomposes easily in the dry state.
    $\textbf{Statement II :}$ Insertion of iodine into the benzene ring is difficult and hence iodobenzene is prepared through the reaction of benzenediazonium salt with KI.
    In the light of the above statements, choose the $\textbf{most appropriate}$ answer from the options given below :

    1. Both Statement I and Statement II are correct
    2. Both Statement I and Statement II are incorrect
    3. Statement I is correct but Statement II is incorrect
    4. Statement I is incorrect but Statement II is correct
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (1)

    Diazotisation of aniline with $\mathrm{NaNO_2}$/HCl (nitrous acid) is carried out at 273–278 K, and the dry diazonium salt is unstable and decomposes (even explosively): Statement I is correct.
    Direct iodination of benzene is reversible and difficult, so iodobenzene is made by warming benzenediazonium chloride with KI: Statement II is correct.
