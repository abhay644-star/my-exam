  1. NEET (UG) 2026, 21 June (re-exam), Code 50 · Question 50
    The correct statement about peptides and proteins is

    1. Tertiary structure of proteins has two or more polypeptide subunits
    2. Only the proteins having a quaternary structure are biologically active
    3. In $\beta$-pleated sheet structures, peptide chains are held together by intermolecular hydrogen bonds
    4. In $\alpha$-helices, the polypeptide chain is twisted into a left-handed screw (helix) through intramolecular hydrogen bonds
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (3)

    Two or more subunits define quaternary, not tertiary, structure, and many single-chain proteins are active, so (1) and (2) are wrong.
    The $\alpha$-helix is a right-handed helix, so (4) is wrong.
    In a $\beta$-pleated sheet, neighbouring chains are joined side by side by intermolecular hydrogen bonds, so (3) is correct.

  2. NEET (UG) 2026, 21 June (re-exam), Code 50 · Question 80
    Match the vitamins in List I with their sources in List II

    List IList II
    A.vitamin AI.meat
    B.vitamin $\mathrm{B_{12}}$II.sunflower oil
    C.vitamin EIII.green leafy vegetables
    D.vitamin KIV.carrots
    Choose the correct answer from the options given below.

    1. A-II, B-III, C-IV, D-I
    2. A-IV, B-I, C-II, D-III
    3. A-IV, B-II, C-I, D-III
    4. A-III, B-I, C-IV, D-II
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (2)

    The sources are: vitamin A – carrots (IV); vitamin $\mathrm{B_{12}}$ – meat and other animal foods (I); vitamin E – vegetable oils such as sunflower oil (II); vitamin K – green leafy vegetables (III).

  3. NEET (UG) 2025, 4 May, Code 45 · Question 69
    Match $\textbf{List I}$ with $\textbf{List II.}$

    List I (Name of Vitamin)List II (Deficiency disease)
    A.Vitamin $\mathrm{B_{12}}$I.Cheilosis
    B.Vitamin DII.Convulsions
    C.Vitamin $\mathrm{B_2}$III.Rickets
    D.Vitamin $\mathrm{B_6}$IV.Pernicious anaemia
    Choose the $\textbf{correct}$ answer from the options given below :

    1. A-I, B-III, C-II, D-IV
    2. A-IV, B-III, C-I, D-II
    3. A-II, B-III, C-I, D-IV
    4. A-IV, B-III, C-II, D-I
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (2)

    Vitamin $\mathrm{B_{12}}$ deficiency causes pernicious anaemia; vitamin D, rickets; vitamin $\mathrm{B_2}$ (riboflavin), cheilosis; vitamin $\mathrm{B_6}$ (pyridoxine), convulsions.
    So A-IV, B-III, C-I, D-II.

  4. NEET (UG) 2025, 4 May, Code 45 · Question 82
    Sugar ‘X’
    A. is found in honey.
    B. is a keto sugar.
    C. exists in $\alpha$ and $\beta$ - anomeric forms.
    D. is laevorotatory.
    ‘X’ is :

    1. D-Glucose
    2. D-Fructose
    3. Maltose
    4. Sucrose
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (2)

    D-Fructose is a ketohexose found in honey and fruits, exists as $\alpha$ and $\beta$ anomers (furanose forms) and is laevorotatory. Glucose is an aldose and dextrorotatory; maltose and sucrose are disaccharides and dextrorotatory.

  5. NEET (UG) 2024, 5 May, Code T1 · Question 62
    The reagents with which glucose does $\textbf{not}$ react to give the corresponding tests/products are
    A. Tollen’s reagent
    B. Schiff’s reagent
    C. HCN
    D. $\mathrm{NH_2OH}$
    E. $\mathrm{NaHSO_3}$
    Choose the correct options from the given below:

    1. B and E
    2. E and D
    3. B and C
    4. A and D
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (1)

    Glucose gives a silver mirror with Tollen's reagent, a cyanohydrin with HCN and an oxime with $\mathrm{NH_2OH}$.
    Although it has an aldehyde group, glucose does not give Schiff's test and does not form the hydrogensulphite addition product with $\mathrm{NaHSO_3}$; this is evidence that the aldehyde group is mostly present in the cyclic (hemiacetal) form. So B and E.

  6. NEET (UG) 2023, 7 May, Code E1 · Question 62
    Given below are two statements :
    $\textbf{Statement I :}$ A unit formed by the attachment of a base to $1'$ position of sugar is known as nucleoside
    $\textbf{Statement II :}$ When nucleoside is linked to phosphorous acid at $5'$-position of sugar moiety, we get nucleotide.
    In the light of the above statements, choose the $\textbf{correct}$ answer from the options given below :

    1. Both Statement I and Statement II are true.
    2. Both Statement I and Statement II are false.
    3. Statement I is true but Statement II is false.
    4. Statement I is false but Statement II is true.
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (3)

    Statement I is true: a base joined at the $1'$ position of the sugar gives a nucleoside.
    Statement II is false: a nucleotide is formed when the nucleoside is linked to phosphoric acid ($\mathrm{H_3PO_4}$) at the $5'$ position, not phosphorous acid.
