  1. NEET (UG) 2026, 21 June (re-exam), Code 50 · Question 59
    Two moles of an ideal gas undergo free expansion from 10 L to 100 L at 300 K. The values of $\Delta S_{system}$ and $\Delta S_{surroundings}$ are
    (R is universal gas constant)

    1. $\Delta S_{system} = 0;\ \Delta S_{surroundings} = 0$
    2. $\Delta S_{system} = 4.606\ R;\ \Delta S_{surroundings} = -4.606\ R$
    3. $\Delta S_{system} = 0;\ \Delta S_{surroundings} = 4.606\ R$
    4. $\Delta S_{system} = 4.606\ R;\ \Delta S_{surroundings} = 0$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (4)

    Entropy is a state function, so for the gas $\Delta S_{sys} = nR\ln\dfrac{V_2}{V_1} = 2R\ln 10 = 2\times2.303R = 4.606R$.
    In a free expansion no heat is exchanged with the surroundings ($q = 0$, $w = 0$), so $\Delta S_{surr} = 0$.

  2. NEET (UG) 2026, 21 June (re-exam), Code 50 · Question 69
    Consider the reversible processes for 1.0 mol of an ideal gas as shown in the figure.
    $w_1$, $w_2$, $w_3$ and $w_4$ represent work done (in calories) in the processes 1, 2, 3 and 4, respectively; $\Delta U_2$ and $\Delta U_4$ are changes in the internal energy for the processes 2 and 4, respectively.
    [use R = 2 cal K$^{-1}$ mol$^{-1}$]
    The correct option is

    1. $w_1 + w_3 = -2T_1\ln\dfrac{V_2}{V_1} - 2T_2\ln\dfrac{V_4}{V_3}$
    2. $w_2 + w_4 = \Delta U_2 - \Delta U_4$
    3. $w_1 + w_2 = 2T_1\ln\dfrac{V_2}{V_1}$
    4. $w_1 + w_2 + w_3 + w_4 = 0$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (1)

    Processes 1 ($T_1$) and 3 ($T_2$) are reversible isothermal: $w = -nRT\ln\dfrac{V_f}{V_i}$ with $nR = 2$ cal K$^{-1}$.
    So $w_1 = -2T_1\ln\dfrac{V_2}{V_1}$ and $w_3 = -2T_2\ln\dfrac{V_4}{V_3}$, and their sum is option (1).
    For the adiabatic steps, $w_2 = \Delta U_2$ and $w_4 = \Delta U_4 = -\Delta U_2$, so (2) fails. (3) has the wrong sign and omits $w_2$. The net work of a cycle is not zero, so (4) fails.

  3. NEET (UG) 2026, 21 June (re-exam), Code 50 · Question 74
    A protein undergoes reversible thermal denaturation from its initial state N to denatured state D according to $\mathrm{N \rightleftharpoons D}$. At 60ºC, the concentrations of both N and D are equal at equilibrium, and the standard enthalpy change of denaturation is 666 kJ mol$^{-1}$. The standard entropy change ($\Delta S^\circ$ in kJ K$^{-1}$ mol$^{-1}$) of the protein upon denaturation at 60ºC is closest to

    1. 2.0
    2. 2000.0
    3. 333.0
    4. 11.1
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (1)

    [N] = [D] means $K = 1$, so $\Delta G^\circ = -RT\ln K = 0$.
    Then $\Delta S^\circ = \dfrac{\Delta H^\circ}{T} = \dfrac{666}{333} = 2.0\ \mathrm{kJ\ K^{-1}\ mol^{-1}}$.

  4. NEET (UG) 2025, 4 May, Code 45 · Question 65
    $\mathrm{C(s) + 2H_2(g) \rightarrow CH_4(g)}$; $\Delta H = -74.8$ kJ mol$^{-1}$
    Which of the following diagrams gives an accurate representation of the above reaction?
    [R $\rightarrow$ reactants; P $\rightarrow$ products]

    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (1)

    $\Delta H$ is negative, so the reaction is exothermic: the products lie 74.8 kJ mol$^{-1}$ below the reactants, with an activation energy hump between them.
    Only diagram (1) shows R above P by 74.8 kJ mol$^{-1}$.

  5. NEET (UG) 2025, 4 May, Code 45 · Question 85
    The standard heat of formation, in kcal/mol of $\mathrm{Ba^{2+}}$ is :
    [Given : standard heat of formation of $\mathrm{SO_4^{2-}}$ ion (aq) = – 216 kcal/mol, standard heat of crystallisation of $\mathrm{BaSO_4}$(s) = – 4.5 kcal/mol, standard heat of formation of $\mathrm{BaSO_4}$(s) = – 349 kcal/mol]

    1. – 128.5
    2. – 133.0
    3. + 133.0
    4. + 220.5
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (1)

    $\mathrm{Ba^{2+}(aq) + SO_4^{2-}(aq) \rightarrow BaSO_4(s)}$, $\Delta H = -4.5$ kcal/mol (crystallisation).
    $\Delta_f H(\mathrm{BaSO_4}) = \Delta_f H(\mathrm{Ba^{2+}}) + \Delta_f H(\mathrm{SO_4^{2-}}) + \Delta H_{cryst}$
    $-349 = x - 216 - 4.5 \Rightarrow x = -128.5$ kcal/mol.
