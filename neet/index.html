Questions students ask

Which NEET (UG) paper is available?

The NEET (UG) 2026 re-examination held on 21 June 2026, Test Booklet Code 50, marked with the official NTA final answer key.

How is the paper marked?

+4 for a correct answer, −1 for a wrong answer and 0 for a question not answered. Questions dropped by NTA give full marks to everyone, and where NTA accepts two options either one is marked correct.

Do I need to sign up or pay?

No. It is free, with no sign-up. Your answers and scores stay on your own device.
