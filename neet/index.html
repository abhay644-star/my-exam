Questions students ask

Which NEET (UG) papers are available?

The NEET (UG) 2026 re-examination held on 21 June 2026 (Test Booklet Code 50), NEET (UG) 2025 held on 4 May 2025 (Code 45) and NEET (UG) 2024 held on 5 May 2024 (Code T1), each marked with the official NTA final answer key (for 2024, the key revised on 26 July 2024).

How is the paper marked?

+4 for a correct answer, −1 for a wrong answer and 0 for a question not answered. Questions dropped by NTA give full marks to everyone, and where NTA accepts two options either one is marked correct. In the 2024 paper, only the first 10 questions answered in each subject's Section B are marked, as in the exam.

Do I need to sign up or pay?

No. It is free, with no sign-up. Your answers and scores stay on your own device.
