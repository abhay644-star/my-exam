  1. NEET (UG) 2026, 21 June (re-exam), Code 50 · Question 99
    Which of the following statements related to pituitary gland are correct?
    (a) It is divided anatomically into adenohypophysis and neurohypophysis
    (b) It secretes follicle stimulating hormone
    (c) It secretes melanocyte stimulating hormone
    (d) It does not secrete prolactin
    Choose the correct answer from the options given below :

    1. (a) and (b) only
    2. (a), (b) and (c) only
    3. (c) and (d) only
    4. (b) and (c) only
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (2)

    The pituitary is divided into the adenohypophysis and the neurohypophysis, so (a) is correct. The pars distalis secretes FSH (and also prolactin), and the pars intermedia secretes MSH, so (b) and (c) are correct. Statement (d) is wrong because prolactin is a pituitary hormone. The answer is (a), (b) and (c).

  2. NEET (UG) 2026, 21 June (re-exam), Code 50 · Question 124
    Which one of the following statements is incorrect?

    1. $\alpha$-cells of pancreas secrete glucagon
    2. $\alpha$-cells of pancreas secrete insulin
    3. Glucagon stimulates glycogenolysis
    4. $\beta$-cells of pancreas secrete insulin
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (2)

    In the islets of Langerhans, $\alpha$-cells secrete glucagon, which stimulates glycogenolysis, and $\beta$-cells secrete insulin. So the statement that $\alpha$-cells secrete insulin is incorrect.

  3. NEET (UG) 2026, 21 June (re-exam), Code 50 · Question 148
    Match List-I with List-II.

    List-IList-II
    (A)Excess growth hormone(I)Reabsorption of water and electrolytes in kidney
    (B)Luteinizing hormone(II)Contraction of uterus during child birth
    (C)Vasopressin(III)Acromegaly
    (D)Oxytocin(IV)Ovulation
    Choose the correct answer from the options given below:

    1. A-III, B-IV, C-II, D-I
    2. A-III, B-IV, C-I, D-II
    3. A-II, B-IV, C-I, D-III
    4. A-IV, B-III, C-I, D-II
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (2)

    Excess GH in adults causes acromegaly (A-III). LH surge induces ovulation (B-IV). Vasopressin (ADH) promotes reabsorption of water and electrolytes in the distal tubules (C-I). Oxytocin causes contraction of uterine muscles during child birth (D-II).

  4. NEET (UG) 2025, 4 May, Code 45 · Question 148
    Match $\textbf{List - I}$ with $\textbf{List - II}$.

    List - IList - II
    A.HeartI.Erythropoietin
    B.KidneyII.Aldosterone
    C.Gastro-intestinal tractIII.Atrial natriuretic factor
    D.Adrenal CortexIV.Secretin
    Choose the $\textbf{correct}$ answer from the options given below :

    1. A-II, B-I, C-III, D-IV
    2. A-IV, B-III, C-II, D-I
    3. A-I, B-III, C-IV, D-II
    4. A-III, B-I, C-IV, D-II
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (4)

    The atrial wall of the heart secretes atrial natriuretic factor; the juxtaglomerular cells of the kidney secrete erythropoietin; the gastro-intestinal tract secretes secretin (among others); the adrenal cortex secretes aldosterone.
    So A-III, B-I, C-IV, D-II.

  5. NEET (UG) 2025, 4 May, Code 45 · Question 160
    Consider the following statements regarding function of adrenal medullary hormones :
    A. It causes pupilary constriction
    B. It is a hyperglycemic hormone
    C. It causes piloerection
    D. It increases strength of heart contraction
    Choose the $\textbf{correct}$ answer from the options given below :

    1. C and D Only
    2. B, C and D Only
    3. A, C and D Only
    4. D Only
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (2)

    Adrenaline and noradrenaline (emergency hormones) cause pupillary dilation (not constriction), piloerection, sweating, a faster heartbeat with stronger contraction and a higher breathing rate. They also stimulate glycogenolysis, raising blood glucose (hyperglycaemic).
    Hence B, C and D only.

  6. NEET (UG) 2025, 4 May, Code 45 · Question 165
    Which of the following hormones released from the pituitary is actually synthesized in the hypothalamus ?

    1. Luteinizing hormone (LH)
    2. Anti-diuretic hormone (ADH)
    3. Follicle-stimulating hormone (FSH)
    4. Adenocorticotrophic hormone (ACTH)
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (2)

    Oxytocin and vasopressin (ADH) are synthesised by hypothalamic neurons and carried along their axons to the posterior pituitary (neurohypophysis), from which they are released. LH, FSH and ACTH are made in the anterior pituitary.

  7. NEET (UG) 2025, 4 May, Code 45 · Question 178
    Match $\textbf{List - I}$ with $\textbf{List - II}$.

    List - IList - II
    A.ProgesteroneI.Pars intermedia
    B.RelaxinII.Ovary
    C.Melanocyte stimulating hormoneIII.Adrenal Medulla
    D.CatecholaminesIV.Corpus luteum
    Choose the $\textbf{correct}$ answer from the options given below :

    1. A-IV, B-II, C-I, D-III
    2. A-IV, B-II, C-III, D-I
    3. A-II, B-IV, C-I, D-III
    4. A-III, B-II, C-IV, D-I
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (1)

    Progesterone is secreted mainly by the corpus luteum; relaxin by the ovary (in later pregnancy); MSH by the pars intermedia of the pituitary; catecholamines (adrenaline, noradrenaline) by the adrenal medulla.
    So A-IV, B-II, C-I, D-III.

  8. NEET (UG) 2024, 5 May, Code T1 · Question 158
    Which of the following is not a steroid hormone?

    1. Progesterone
    2. Glucagon
    3. Cortisol
    4. Testosterone
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (2)

    Progesterone, cortisol and testosterone are steroid hormones derived from cholesterol. Glucagon, secreted by the $\alpha$-cells of the islets of Langerhans, is a peptide hormone.

  9. NEET (UG) 2024, 5 May, Code T1 · Question 197
    Match List I with List II :

    List IList II
    A.Exophthalmic goiterI.Excess secretion of cortisol, moon face & hyperglycemia
    B.AcromegalyII.Hypo-secretion of thyroid hormone and stunted growth.
    C.Cushing’s syndromeIII.Hyper secretion of thyroid hormone & protruding eye balls.
    D.CretinismIV.Excessive secretion of growth hormone.
    Choose the correct answer from the options given below :

    1. A-III, B-IV, C-II, D-I
    2. A-III, B-IV, C-I, D-II
    3. A-I, B-III, C-II, D-IV
    4. A-IV, B-II, C-I, D-III
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (2)

    Exophthalmic goitre (Graves’ disease) results from hypersecretion of thyroid hormone and shows protruding eyeballs (A-III). Acromegaly results from excess growth hormone in adults (B-IV). Cushing’s syndrome results from excess cortisol, with moon face and hyperglycaemia (C-I). Cretinism results from hyposecretion of thyroid hormone in childhood, with stunted growth (D-II).

  10. NEET (UG) 2023, 7 May, Code E1 · Question 161
    Match List I with List II.

    List IList II
    A.CCKI.Kidney
    B.GIPII.Heart
    C.ANFIII.Gastric gland
    D.ADHIV.Pancreas
    Choose the $\textbf{correct}$ answer from the options given below:

    1. A-IV, B-III, C-II, D-I
    2. A-III, B-II, C-IV, D-I
    3. A-II, B-IV, C-I, D-III
    4. A-IV, B-II, C-III, D-I
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (1)

    CCK acts on the pancreas (and gall bladder), stimulating release of pancreatic enzymes (A-IV). GIP inhibits gastric secretion and motility, acting on the gastric glands (B-III). ANF is secreted by the atrial wall of the heart (C-II). ADH acts on the distal tubules of the kidney (D-I).

  11. NEET (UG) 2023, 7 May, Code E1 · Question 190
    Which of the following are NOT under the control of thyroid hormone?
    A. Maintenance of water and electrolyte balance
    B. Regulation of basal metabolic rate
    C. Normal rhythm of sleep-wake cycle
    D. Development of immune system
    E. Support the process of R.B.Cs formation
    Choose the $\textbf{correct}$ answer from the options given below:

    1. A and D only
    2. B and C only
    3. C and D only
    4. D and E only
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (3)

    NCERT lists regulation of the basal metabolic rate, support for red blood cell formation and maintenance of water and electrolyte balance as functions of the thyroid hormones. The sleep-wake rhythm is controlled by melatonin (pineal gland) and the development of the immune system by thymosins (thymus). So C and D only.

  12. NEET (UG) 2022, 17 July, Code Q1 · Question 193
    Which of the following are $\textbf{not}$ the effects of Parathyroid hormone?
    (a) Stimulates the process of bone resorption
    (b) Decreases Ca$^{2+}$ level in blood
    (c) Reabsorption of Ca$^{2+}$ by renal tubules
    (d) Decreases the absorption of Ca$^{2+}$ from digested food
    (e) Increases metabolism of carbohydrates
    Choose the $\textbf{most appropriate}$ answer from the options given below:

    1. (a) and (c) only
    2. (b), (d) and (e) only
    3. (a) and (e) only
    4. (b) and (c) only
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (2)

    Parathyroid hormone raises blood Ca²⁺: it stimulates bone resorption (a), reabsorption of Ca²⁺ by the renal tubules (c) and absorption of Ca²⁺ from digested food.
    So (b), (d) and (e) are not its effects.
