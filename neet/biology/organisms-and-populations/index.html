  1. NEET (UG) 2026, 21 June (re-exam), Code 50 · Question 161
    Consider a population of 10 million cells. Given the per-capita birth rate of 0.002 (per unit time) and the per-capita death rate of 0.002 (per unit time), the expected number of cells after 10 generations is ______.

    1. 1 million
    2. 5 million
    3. 10 million
    4. 100 million
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (3)

    Intrinsic rate of increase $r = b - d = 0.002 - 0.002 = 0$. With $r = 0$, $dN/dt = rN = 0$, so the population neither grows nor declines and remains 10 million.

  2. NEET (UG) 2026, 21 June (re-exam), Code 50 · Question 163
    Given below are two statements : one is labelled as Assertion A and the other is labelled as Reason R.
    Assertion A : The logistic growth model of populations is considered more realistic than the exponential growth model.
    Reason R : Resources are finite.
    In the light of the above statements, choose the most appropriate answer from the options given below :

    1. Both A and R are correct and R is the correct explanation of A
    2. Both A and R are correct but R is not the correct explanation of A
    3. A is correct but R is not correct
    4. A is not correct but R is correct
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (1)

    No population has unlimited resources, so growth eventually slows and levels off at the carrying capacity $K$. The logistic (Verhulst-Pearl) model includes this limit and is therefore more realistic than the exponential model. Both statements are true and the finiteness of resources explains A.

  3. NEET (UG) 2026, 21 June (re-exam), Code 50 · Question 172
    Given below are two statements : one is labelled as Assertion A and the other is labelled as Reason R.
    Assertion A : Abingdon tortoise in Galapagos islands became extinct within a decade after goats were introduced.
    Reason R : Goats were more efficient at browsing than Abingdon tortoise.
    In the light of the above statements, choose the most appropriate answer from the options given below:

    1. Both A and R are correct and R is the correct explanation of A
    2. Both A and R are correct but R is not the correct explanation of A
    3. A is correct but R is not correct
    4. A is not correct but R is correct
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (1)

    This is a classic example of interspecific competition: the Abingdon tortoise became extinct within a decade of goats being introduced on the island, apparently because of the greater browsing efficiency of the goats. Both statements are correct and R explains A.

  4. NEET (UG) 2026, 21 June (re-exam), Code 50 · Question 174
    Match List-I with List-II.

    List-IList-II
    A.Both species are harmedI.Predation
    B.One species is harmed and the other is benefitedII.Mutualism
    C.Both species are benefitedIII.Competition
    D.One is benefited while the other has no effectIV.Commensalism
    Choose the correct answer from the options given below:

    1. A-III, B-IV, C-II, D-I
    2. A-I, B-II, C-III, D-IV
    3. A-II, B-I, C-IV, D-III
    4. A-III, B-I, C-II, D-IV
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (4)

    Competition harms both species ($-/-$): A-III. Predation benefits the predator and harms the prey ($+/-$): B-I. Mutualism benefits both ($+/+$): C-II. Commensalism benefits one with no effect on the other ($+/0$): D-IV.

  5. NEET (UG) 2025, 4 May, Code 45 · Question 97
    Epiphytes that are growing on a mango branch is an example of which of the following?

    1. Commensalism
    2. Mutualism
    3. Predation
    4. Amensalism
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (1)

    An epiphyte (e.g. an orchid) on a mango branch gets support and access to light, while the mango tree is neither harmed nor helped. One species benefits and the other is unaffected: commensalism.

  6. NEET (UG) 2025, 4 May, Code 45 · Question 133

    1. S. R. Kashyap
    2. Ramdeo Misra
    3. Ram Udar
    4. Birbal Sahni

  7. NEET (UG) 2025, 4 May, Code 45 · Question 157
    Given below are two statements :
    $\textbf{Statement I :}$ Fig fruit is a non-vegetarian fruit as it has enclosed fig wasps in it.
    $\textbf{Statement II :}$ Fig wasp and fig tree exhibit mutual relationship as fig wasp completes its life cycle in fig fruit and fig fruit gets pollinated by fig wasp.
    In the light of the above statements, choose the $\textbf{most appropriate}$ answer from the options given below :

    1. Both statement I and statement II are correct
    2. Both statement I and statement II are incorrect
    3. Statement I is correct but statement II is incorrect
    4. Statement I is incorrect but statement II is correct
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (2)

    Statement I is incorrect: the fig is a plant product; any wasp remains inside are broken down by the fig's enzymes, so it is not a non-vegetarian fruit.
    Statement II is incorrect as worded: in this mutualism the wasp pollinates the fig $\textit{inflorescence}$ (its flowers) while looking for egg-laying sites; a fruit is not pollinated. The NTA final key marks both statements incorrect.

  8. NEET (UG) 2025, 4 May, Code 45 · Question 180
    Which one of the following equations represents the Verhulst-Pearl Logistic Growth of population?

    1. $\dfrac{dN}{dt} = r\left(\dfrac{K-N}{K}\right)$
    2. $\dfrac{dN}{dt} = rN\left(\dfrac{K-N}{K}\right)$
    3. $\dfrac{dN}{dt} = rN\left(\dfrac{N-K}{N}\right)$
    4. $\dfrac{dN}{dt} = N\left(\dfrac{r-K}{K}\right)$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (2)

    Logistic growth: $\dfrac{dN}{dt} = rN\left(\dfrac{K-N}{K}\right)$, where $N$ is the population density, $r$ the intrinsic rate of natural increase and $K$ the carrying capacity. Growth slows as $N$ approaches $K$.

  9. NEET (UG) 2024, 5 May, Code T1 · Question 107
    The equation of Verhulst-Pearl logistic growth is $\dfrac{dN}{dt} = rN\left[\dfrac{K-N}{K}\right]$.
    From this equation, $K$ indicates:

    1. Carrying capacity
    2. Population density
    3. Intrinsic rate of natural increase
    4. Biotic potential
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (1)

    In the logistic equation $N$ is the population density, $r$ the intrinsic rate of natural increase and $K$ the carrying capacity, the maximum population the habitat's resources can support.

  10. NEET (UG) 2024, 5 May, Code T1 · Question 196
    Given below are two statements :
    $\textbf{Statement I :}$ Gause’s competitive exclusion principle states that two closely related species competing for different resources cannot exist indefinitely.
    $\textbf{Statement II :}$ According to Gause’s principle, during competition, the inferior will be eliminated. This may be true if resources are limiting.
    In the light of the above statements, choose the correct answer from the options given below :

    1. Statement I is true but Statement II is false.
    2. Statement I is false but Statement II is true.
    3. Both Statement I and Statement II are true.
    4. Both Statement I and Statement II are false.
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (2)

    Statement I is false: Gause’s principle says that two closely related species competing for the $\textit{same}$ resources cannot coexist indefinitely; species using different resources can coexist.
    Statement II is true: the competitively inferior species is eventually eliminated, and this may be true when resources are limiting.
