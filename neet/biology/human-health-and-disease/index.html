  1. NEET (UG) 2026, 21 June (re-exam), Code 50 · Question 165
    Which of the following are secondary lymphoid organs?
    (a) Bone marrow
    (b) Tonsils
    (c) Spleen
    (d) Thymus
    Choose the correct answer from the options given below:

    1. (a) and (b) only
    2. (b) and (c) only
    3. (b) and (d) only
    4. (a) and (d) only
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (2)

    Bone marrow and thymus are primary lymphoid organs where lymphocytes are produced and mature. Spleen, lymph nodes, tonsils, Peyer’s patches and MALT are secondary lymphoid organs where lymphocytes interact with antigens. Hence (b) and (c) only.

  2. NEET (UG) 2026, 21 June (re-exam), Code 50 · Question 171
    Colostrum, secreted by mother during initial days of lactation, is abundant in ________.

    1. IgG
    2. IgM
    3. IgA
    4. IgD
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (3)

    The yellowish colostrum secreted in the first few days of lactation is rich in IgA antibodies, which give the newborn passive immunity.

  3. NEET (UG) 2026, 21 June (re-exam), Code 50 · Question 177
    Which of the following is used as an effective sedative and painkiller for treating post-surgery patients?

    1. Interferon
    2. Antibiotics
    3. Morphine
    4. Anti-retroviral drugs
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (3)

    Morphine, an opioid extracted from the latex of the poppy plant, is a very effective sedative and painkiller and is useful for patients who have undergone surgery.

  4. NEET (UG) 2026, 21 June (re-exam), Code 50 · Question 179
    Which of the following statements is correct about Plasmodium?

    1. Reproduces sexually in liver cells
    2. Reproduces sexually in RBCs
    3. Gametocytes develop in mosquito gut
    4. Fertilization takes place in mosquito gut
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (4)

    In humans Plasmodium multiplies asexually in liver cells and RBCs, and gametocytes develop in the RBCs. When a female Anopheles takes up the gametocytes, fertilisation occurs in the mosquito gut and the sexual phase continues there.

  5. NEET (UG) 2025, 4 May, Code 45 · Question 120
    Which are correct:
    A. Computed tomography and magnetic resonance imaging detect cancers of internal organs.
    B. Chemotherapeutics drugs are used to kill non-cancerous cells.
    C. $\alpha$-interferon activate the cancer patients' immune system and helps in destroying the tumour.
    D. Chemotherapeutic drugs are biological response modifiers.
    E. In the case of leukaemia blood cell counts are decreased.
    Choose the $\textbf{correct}$ answer from the options given below:

    1. B and D only
    2. D and E only
    3. C and D only
    4. A and C only
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (4)

    A is correct: CT and MRI detect cancers of internal organs. C is correct: $\alpha$-interferon is a biological response modifier that activates the immune system against the tumour.
    B is wrong (chemotherapeutic drugs kill cancer cells), D is wrong (biological response modifiers are substances such as $\alpha$-interferon, not chemotherapeutic drugs), and E is wrong (blood cell counts increase in leukaemia). Hence A and C only.

  6. NEET (UG) 2025, 4 May, Code 45 · Question 135
    Neoplastic characteristics of cells refer to :
    A. A mass of proliferating cell
    B. Rapid growth of cells
    C. Invasion and damage to the surrounding tissue
    D. Those confined to original location
    Choose the $\textbf{correct}$ answer from the options given below:

    1. A, B only
    2. A, B, C only
    3. A, B, D only
    4. B, C, D only
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (2)

    Malignant tumours are masses of proliferating cells, called neoplastic or tumour cells, which grow very rapidly and invade and damage the surrounding normal tissues.
    Staying confined to the original location is a feature of benign tumours. Hence A, B, C only.

  7. NEET (UG) 2025, 4 May, Code 45 · Question 143
    Identify the statement that is $\textbf{NOT}$ correct.

    1. Each antibody has two light and two heavy chains.
    2. The heavy and light chains are held together by disulfide bonds.
    3. Antigen binding site is located at C-terminal region of antibody molecules.
    4. Constant region of heavy and light chains are located at C-terminus of antibody molecules.
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (3)

    An antibody ($\mathrm{H_2L_2}$) has two heavy and two light chains held together by disulphide bonds. The variable regions, which form the antigen binding sites, are at the N-terminal ends, while the constant regions are at the C-terminal ends.
    So statement (3) is not correct.

  8. NEET (UG) 2025, 4 May, Code 45 · Question 156
    After maturation, in primary lymphoid organs, the lymphocytes migrate for interaction with antigens to secondary lymphoid organ(s) / tissue(s) like:
    A. thymus
    B. bone marrow
    C. spleen
    D. lymph nodes
    E. Peyer's patches
    Choose the $\textit{correct}$ answer from the options given below:

    1. B, C, D only
    2. A, B, C only
    3. E, A, B only
    4. C, D, E only
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (4)

    The bone marrow and thymus are primary lymphoid organs, where lymphocytes form and mature.
    The spleen, lymph nodes, tonsils, Peyer's patches of the small intestine and appendix are secondary lymphoid organs, where lymphocytes meet antigens. Hence C, D, E only.

  9. NEET (UG) 2025, 4 May, Code 45 · Question 167
    Which of the following type of immunity is present at the time of birth and is a non-specific type of defence in the human body?

    1. Acquired Immunity
    2. Innate Immunity
    3. Cell-mediated Immunity
    4. Humoral Immunity
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (2)

    Innate immunity is non-specific defence present from birth, provided by barriers such as skin, mucus, phagocytes and interferons. Acquired immunity (humoral and cell-mediated) is pathogen-specific and develops after exposure.

  10. NEET (UG) 2024, 5 May, Code T1 · Question 154
    Match List I with List II :

    List IList II
    A.TyphoidI.Fungus
    B.LeishmaniasisII.Nematode
    C.RingwormIII.Protozoa
    D.FilariasisIV.Bacteria
    Choose the correct answer from the options given below :

    1. A-III, B-I, C-IV, D-II
    2. A-II, B-IV, C-III, D-I
    3. A-I, B-III, C-II, D-IV
    4. A-IV, B-III, C-I, D-II
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (4)

    Typhoid is caused by the bacterium $\textit{Salmonella typhi}$ (A-IV). Leishmaniasis is caused by the protozoan $\textit{Leishmania}$ (B-III). Ringworm is caused by fungi such as $\textit{Microsporum}$ and $\textit{Trichophyton}$ (C-I). Filariasis is caused by the nematode $\textit{Wuchereria}$ (D-II).

  11. NEET (UG) 2024, 5 May, Code T1 · Question 156
    Which of the following are Autoimmune disorders?
    A. Myasthenia gravis
    B. Rheumatoid arthritis
    C. Gout
    D. Muscular dystrophy
    E. Systemic Lupus Erythematosus (SLE)
    Choose the most appropriate answer from the options given below :

    1. B, C & E only
    2. C, D & E only
    3. A, B & D only
    4. A, B & E only
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (4)

    In autoimmune disorders the immune system attacks the body’s own cells. Myasthenia gravis, rheumatoid arthritis and SLE are autoimmune. Gout is caused by deposition of uric acid crystals in joints, and muscular dystrophy is a genetic degeneration of skeletal muscle. So A, B and E only.

  12. NEET (UG) 2024, 5 May, Code T1 · Question 162
    Match List I with List II :

    List IList II
    A.CocaineI.Effective sedative in surgery
    B.HeroinII.$\textit{Cannabis sativa}$
    C.MorphineIII.$\textit{Erythroxylum}$
    D.MarijuanaIV.$\textit{Papaver somniferum}$
    Choose the correct answer from the options given below :

    1. A-II, B-I, C-III, D-IV
    2. A-III, B-IV, C-I, D-II
    3. A-IV, B-III, C-I, D-II
    4. A-I, B-III, C-II, D-IV
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (2)

    Cocaine is obtained from the coca plant $\textit{Erythroxylum coca}$ (A-III). Heroin (smack) is made by acetylation of morphine, which comes from the poppy $\textit{Papaver somniferum}$ (B-IV). Morphine is a very effective sedative and painkiller used after surgery (C-I). Marijuana is a cannabinoid from $\textit{Cannabis sativa}$ (D-II).

  13. NEET (UG) 2024, 5 May, Code T1 · Question 178
    Match List I with List II :

    List IList II
    A.Common coldI.$\textit{Plasmodium}$
    B.HaemozoinII.Typhoid
    C.Widal testIII.Rhinoviruses
    D.AllergyIV.Dust mites
    Choose the correct answer from the options given below :

    1. A-III, B-I, C-II, D-IV
    2. A-IV, B-II, C-III, D-I
    3. A-II, B-IV, C-III, D-I
    4. A-I, B-III, C-II, D-IV
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (1)

    The common cold is caused by rhinoviruses (A-III). Haemozoin is the toxin released when $\textit{Plasmodium}$-infected red blood cells rupture, causing the chill and high fever of malaria (B-I). The Widal test confirms typhoid (C-II). Dust mites are common allergens (D-IV).

  14. NEET (UG) 2024, 5 May, Code T1 · Question 191
    Given below are two statements :
    $\textbf{Statement I :}$ Bone marrow is the main lymphoid organ where all blood cells including lymphocytes are produced.
    $\textbf{Statement II :}$ Both bone marrow and thymus provide micro environments for the development and maturation of T-lymphocytes.
    In the light of the above statements, choose the most appropriate answer from the options given below :

    1. Statement I is correct but Statement II is incorrect.
    2. Statement I is incorrect but Statement II is correct.
    3. Both Statement I and Statement II are correct.
    4. Both Statement I and Statement II are incorrect.
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (3)

    Both statements are correct and are taken from NCERT. The bone marrow is the main primary lymphoid organ, where all blood cells including lymphocytes are produced. The bone marrow and thymus provide micro-environments for the development and maturation of T-lymphocytes.
