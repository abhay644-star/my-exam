  1. NEET (UG) 2026, 21 June (re-exam), Code 50 · Question 140
    Sperm motility is due to ___________.

    1. flagellar movement
    2. ciliary movement
    3. amoeboid movement
    4. muscular movement
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (1)

    A sperm has a tail that is a flagellum; mitochondria in the middle piece supply energy for its beating. Sperm motility is therefore due to flagellar movement.

  2. NEET (UG) 2026, 21 June (re-exam), Code 50 · Question 143
    Which of the following struture is not a part of the male reproductive system?

    1. Rete testis
    2. Epididymis
    3. Vasa efferentia
    4. Infundibulum
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (4)

    Rete testis, vasa efferentia and epididymis are male accessory ducts that carry sperm from the testis. The infundibulum is the funnel-shaped part of the fallopian tube (oviduct) in the female, so it is not part of the male system.

  3. NEET (UG) 2026, 21 June (re-exam), Code 50 · Question 145
    Given below are two statements:
    Statement I : Ovulation is caused by LH surge leading to rupture of Graafian follicles.
    Statement II: Graafian follicle remaining after ovulation transform into corpus luteum and secretes large amount of estrogen.
    In the light of the above statements, choose the most appropriate answer from the options given below:

    1. Both Statement I and Statement II are correct
    2. Both Statement I and Statement II are incorrect
    3. Statement I is correct but Statement II is incorrect
    4. Statement I is incorrect but Statement II is correct
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (3)

    The mid-cycle LH surge induces rupture of the Graafian follicle and release of the ovum, so Statement I is correct. The remnants of the follicle form the corpus luteum, which secretes large amounts of progesterone (not estrogen), so Statement II is incorrect.

  4. NEET (UG) 2026, 21 June (re-exam), Code 50 · Question 166
    Which of the following hormone is not secreted by human placenta?

    1. hCG
    2. Estrogen
    3. Progesterone
    4. LH
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (4)

    The placenta acts as an endocrine tissue producing hCG, hPL, estrogens and progestogens (and relaxin late in pregnancy). LH is secreted by the anterior pituitary, not by the placenta.

  5. NEET (UG) 2026, 21 June (re-exam), Code 50 · Question 173
    The covering of ovum at ovulation is _________.

    1. endometrium
    2. zona radiata
    3. zona pellucida
    4. chorion
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (3)

    The secondary oocyte released at ovulation is surrounded by a non-cellular glycoprotein layer, the zona pellucida (with corona radiata cells outside it). The endometrium lines the uterus, and the chorion is an extra-embryonic membrane formed later.

  6. NEET (UG) 2025, 4 May, Code 45 · Question 110
    Match $\textbf{List - I}$ with $\textbf{List - II}$.

    List - IList - II
    A.HeadI.Enzymes
    B.Middle pieceII.Sperm motility
    C.AcrosomeIII.Energy
    D.TailIV.Genetic material
    Choose the $\textbf{correct}$ answer from the options given below :

    1. A-IV, B-III, C-I, D-II
    2. A-IV, B-III, C-II, D-I
    3. A-III, B-IV, C-II, D-I
    4. A-III, B-II, C-I, D-IV
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (1)

    The sperm head contains the haploid nucleus (genetic material); the middle piece has mitochondria that supply energy; the acrosome holds enzymes that help in fertilisation; the tail gives motility.
    So A-IV, B-III, C-I, D-II.

  7. NEET (UG) 2025, 4 May, Code 45 · Question 123
    Find the correct statements :
    A. In human pregnancy, the major organ systems are formed at the end of 12 weeks.
    B. In human pregnancy the major organ systems are formed at the end of 8 weeks.
    C. In human pregnancy heart is formed after one month of gestation.
    D. In human pregnancy, limbs and digits develop by the end of second month.
    E. In human pregnancy the appearance of hair is usually observed in the fifth month.
    Choose the $\textbf{correct}$ answer from the options given below :

    1. A and E Only
    2. B and C Only
    3. B, C, D and E Only
    4. A, C, D and E Only
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (4)

    After one month of pregnancy the embryo's heart is formed (C); by the end of the second month limbs and digits develop (D); by the end of 12 weeks (first trimester) most major organ systems are formed (A, so B is wrong); in the fifth month the first movements are felt and hair appears on the head (E).
    Hence A, C, D and E only.

  8. NEET (UG) 2025, 4 May, Code 45 · Question 138
    Consider the following :
    A. The reductive division for the human female gametogenesis starts earlier than that of the male gametogenesis.
    B. The gap between the first meiotic division and the second meiotic division is much shorter for males compared to females.
    C. The first polar body is associated with the formation of the primary oocyte.
    D. Luteinizing Hormone (LH) surge leads to disintegration of the endometrium and onset of menstrual bleeding.
    Choose the $\textbf{correct}$ answer from the options given below :

    1. A and B are true
    2. A and C are true
    3. B and D are true
    4. B and C are true
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (1)

    A is true: primary oocytes enter meiosis I in the foetal ovary, whereas spermatogenesis begins at puberty. B is true: in males meiosis II follows meiosis I quickly, while a secondary oocyte stays arrested until ovulation and fertilisation.
    C is false: the first polar body forms with the secondary oocyte. D is false: the LH surge causes ovulation; menstruation follows the fall in progesterone. Hence A and B.

  9. NEET (UG) 2025, 4 May, Code 45 · Question 147
    The first menstruation is called :

    1. Menopause
    2. Menarche
    3. Diapause
    4. Ovulation
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (2)

    The first menstruation, which begins at puberty, is called menarche. Menopause is the cessation of menstruation at about 50 years.

  10. NEET (UG) 2025, 4 May, Code 45 · Question 176
    Twins are born to a family that lives next door to you. The twins are a boy and a girl. Which of the following must be true?

    1. They are monozygotic twins.
    2. They are fraternal twins.
    3. They were conceived through in vitro fertilization.
    4. They have 75% identical genetic content.
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (2)

    Monozygotic (identical) twins come from one zygote and are always of the same sex. Twins of different sex must come from two separately fertilised eggs, so they are fraternal (dizygotic) twins, sharing on average about 50% of their genes like ordinary siblings.

  11. NEET (UG) 2024, 5 May, Code T1 · Question 159
    Given below are two statements : one is labelled as Assertion A and the other is labelled as Reason R :
    $\textbf{Assertion A :}$ FSH acts upon ovarian follicles in female and Leydig cells in male.
    $\textbf{Reason R :}$ Growing ovarian follicles secrete estrogen in female while interstitial cells secrete androgen in male human being.
    In the light of the above statements, choose the correct answer from the options given below :

    1. A is true but R is false
    2. A is false but R is true
    3. Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation of A.
    4. Both A and R are true but R is NOT the correct explanation of A.
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (2)

    A is false: in the male, FSH acts on the Sertoli cells; it is LH that acts on the Leydig (interstitial) cells.
    R is true: growing ovarian follicles secrete oestrogen, and the interstitial cells of the testis secrete androgens.

  12. NEET (UG) 2024, 5 May, Code T1 · Question 170
    Given below are two statements : one is labelled as Assertion A and the other is labelled as Reason R :
    $\textbf{Assertion A :}$ Breast-feeding during initial period of infant growth is recommended by doctors for bringing a healthy baby.
    $\textbf{Reason R :}$ Colostrum contains several antibodies absolutely essential to develop resistance for the new born baby.
    In the light of the above statements, choose the most appropriate answer from the options given below :

    1. A is correct but R is not correct.
    2. A is not correct but R is correct.
    3. Both A and R are correct and R is the correct explanation of A.
    4. Both A and R are correct but R is NOT the correct explanation of A.
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (3)

    Both are correct. The milk produced in the first few days of lactation (colostrum) contains several antibodies (IgA) that give the newborn passive immunity, which is why doctors recommend breast-feeding in the initial period. So R explains A.

  13. NEET (UG) 2024, 5 May, Code T1 · Question 171
    Which of the following is not a component of Fallopian tube?

    1. Infundibulum
    2. Ampulla
    3. Uterine fundus
    4. Isthmus
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (3)

    Each Fallopian tube (oviduct) has three parts: the funnel-shaped infundibulum (with fimbriae) near the ovary, the wider ampulla and the narrow isthmus that joins the uterus. The fundus is the upper dome of the uterus, not part of the tube.

  14. NEET (UG) 2024, 5 May, Code T1 · Question 181
    Given below are two statements :
    $\textbf{Statement I :}$ The presence or absence of hymen is not a reliable indicator of virginity.
    $\textbf{Statement II :}$ The hymen is torn during the first coitus only.
    In the light of the above statements, choose the correct answer from the options given below :

    1. Statement I is true but Statement II is false
    2. Statement I is false but Statement II is true
    3. Both Statement I and Statement II are true
    4. Both Statement I and Statement II are false
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (1)

    Statement I is true. Statement II is false: the hymen is often torn during the first coitus, but it can also be broken by a sudden fall or jolt, insertion of a vaginal tampon or active sport such as horse-riding or cycling, and in some women it persists after coitus. That is exactly why its presence or absence is not a reliable indicator of virginity.

  15. NEET (UG) 2024, 5 May, Code T1 · Question 198
    Identify the correct option (A), (B), (C), (D) with respect to spermatogenesis.

    1. FSH, Sertoli cells, Leydig cells, spermatogenesis.
    2. ICSH, Leydig cells, Sertoli cells, spermatogenesis.
    3. FSH, Leydig cells, Sertoli cells, spermiogenesis
    4. ICSH, Interstitial cells, Leydig cells, spermiogenesis.
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (3)

    GnRH from the hypothalamus causes release of LH and FSH. LH acts on the Leydig cells (B), which secrete androgens. FSH (A) acts on the Sertoli cells (C), which secrete factors that help in spermiogenesis (D), the change of spermatids into sperms. So (A) FSH, (B) Leydig cells, (C) Sertoli cells, (D) spermiogenesis.

  16. NEET (UG) 2023, 7 May, Code E1 · Question 152
    Given below are two statements:
    $\textbf{Statement I:}$ Vas deferens receives a duct from seminal vesicle and opens into urethra as the ejaculatory duct.
    $\textbf{Statement II:}$ The cavity of the cervix is called cervical canal which along with vagina forms birth canal.
    In the light of the above statements, choose the $\textbf{correct}$ answer from the options given below:

    1. Both Statement I and Statement II are true.
    2. Both Statement I and Statement II are false.
    3. Statement I is correct but Statement II is false.
    4. Statement I incorrect but Statement II is true.
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (1)

    Statement I is true: the vas deferens is joined by the duct of the seminal vesicle and continues into the urethra as the ejaculatory duct.
    Statement II is true: the cavity of the cervix, the cervical canal, together with the vagina forms the birth canal.

  17. NEET (UG) 2023, 7 May, Code E1 · Question 162
    Given below are two statements: one is labelled as $\textbf{Assertion A}$ and the other is labelled as $\textbf{Reason R.}$
    $\textbf{Assertion A:}$ Endometrium is necessary for implantation of blastocyst.
    $\textbf{Reason R:}$ In the absence of fertilization, the corpus luteum degenerates that causes disintegration of endometrium.
    In the light of the above statements, choose the $\textbf{correct}$ answer from the options given below:

    1. Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation of A.
    2. Both A and R are true but R is NOT the correct explanation of A.
    3. A is true but R is false.
    4. A is false but R is true.
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (2)

    A is true: the blastocyst becomes embedded in the endometrium of the uterus.
    R is true: without fertilisation the corpus luteum degenerates, progesterone falls and the endometrium breaks down (menstruation).
    But R explains what happens when there is no pregnancy, not why the endometrium is needed for implantation, so R is not the correct explanation of A.

  18. NEET (UG) 2023, 7 May, Code E1 · Question 172
    Which of the following statements are correct regarding female reproductive cycle?
    A. In non-primate mammals cyclical changes during reproduction are called oestrus cycle.
    B. First menstrual cycle begins at puberty and is called menopause.
    C. Lack of menstruation may be indicative of pregnancy.
    D. Cyclic menstruation extends between menarche and menopause.
    Choose the $\textbf{most appropriate}$ answer from the options given below:

    1. A and D only
    2. A and B only
    3. A, B and C only
    4. A, C and D only
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (4)

    A, C and D are correct. B is wrong: the first menstruation at puberty is called menarche; menopause is when menstrual cycles stop at about 50 years of age.

  19. NEET (UG) 2022, 17 July, Code Q1 · Question 156
    Which of the following statements are true for spermatogenesis but do not hold true for Oogenesis?
    (a) It results in the formation of haploid gametes
    (b) Differentiation of gamete occurs after the completion of meiosis
    (c) Meiosis occurs continuously in a mitotically dividing stem cell population
    (d) It is controlled by the Luteinising hormone (LH) and Follicle Stimulating Hormone (FSH) secreted by the anterior pituitary
    (e) It is initiated at puberty
    Choose the most appropriate answer from the options given below:

    1. (c) and (e) only
    2. (b) and (c) only
    3. (b), (d) and (e) only
    4. (b), (c) and (e) only
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (4)

    Only in spermatogenesis do spermatids differentiate into sperms after meiosis is complete (b), does meiosis go on continuously from a self-renewing stem cell population (c), and does the process begin at puberty (e). Oogenesis starts in the foetus.
    (a) and (d) are true of both processes.

  20. NEET (UG) 2022, 17 July, Code Q1 · Question 175
    Given below are two statements:
    $\textbf{Statement I :}$
    The release of sperms into the seminiferous tubules is called spermiation.
    $\textbf{Statement II :}$
    Spermiogenesis is the process of formation of sperms from spermatogonia.
    In the light of the above statements, choose the $\textbf{most appropriate}$ answer from the options given below :

    1. Both Statement I and Statement II are correct
    2. Both Statement I and Statement II are incorrect
    3. Statement I is correct but Statement II is incorrect
    4. Statement I is incorrect but Statement II is correct
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (3)

    Statement I is correct. Statement II is incorrect: spermiogenesis is the change of spermatids into sperms; forming sperms from spermatogonia is spermatogenesis.

  21. NEET (UG) 2022, 17 July, Code Q1 · Question 181
    At which stage of life the oogenesis process is initiated?

    1. Puberty
    2. Embryonic development stage
    3. Birth
    4. Adult
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (2)

    Oogenesis starts during embryonic (foetal) development, when oogonia form and enter meiosis I as primary oocytes. These stay arrested until puberty.
