  1. NEET (UG) 2026, 21 June (re-exam), Code 50 · Question 151
    How many theca are present in each lobe of a typical bilobed angiosperm anther ?

    1. 2
    2. 6
    3. 8
    4. 12
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (1)

    A typical angiosperm anther is bilobed and each lobe has two theca (dithecous). It therefore has four microsporangia in total.

  2. NEET (UG) 2026, 21 June (re-exam), Code 50 · Question 154
    Which of the following in female gametophyte of an angiosperm helps in guiding the pollen tube for fertilizing the eggs?

    1. Antipodals
    2. Synergids
    3. Central cells
    4. Polar nucleus
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (2)

    The synergids, lying beside the egg at the micropylar end, bear a filiform apparatus that guides the pollen tube into the synergid. Antipodals, the central cell and polar nuclei have no guiding role.

  3. NEET (UG) 2026, 21 June (re-exam), Code 50 · Question 155
    Which of the following plant produces non-albuminous seeds?

    1. Wheat
    2. Maize
    3. Barley
    4. Pea
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (4)

    In non-albuminous (ex-albuminous) seeds the endosperm is fully consumed during embryo development, as in pea and groundnut. Wheat, maize and barley retain endosperm and are albuminous seeds.

  4. NEET (UG) 2026, 21 June (re-exam), Code 50 · Question 156
    If the diploid chromosome number of typical angiosperm is 36, what would be the chromosome number in its endosperm?

    1. 18
    2. 36
    3. 54
    4. 72
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (3)

    $2n = 36$, so $n = 18$. The endosperm is triploid ($3n$), formed by fusion of a male gamete with two polar nuclei: $3 \times 18 = 54$.

  5. NEET (UG) 2025, 4 May, Code 45 · Question 102
    Given below are two statements : One is labelled as $\textbf{Assertion (A)}$ and the other is labelled as $\textbf{Reason (R).}$
    $\textbf{Assertion (A) :}$ Both wind and water pollinated flowers are not very colourful and do not produce nectar.
    $\textbf{Reason (R) :}$ The flowers produce enormous amount of pollen grains in wind and water pollinated flowers.
    In the light of the above statements, choose the $\textbf{correct}$ answer from the options given below :

    1. Both $\textbf{A}$ and $\textbf{R}$ are true and $\textbf{R}$ is the correct explanation of $\textbf{A}$
    2. Both $\textbf{A}$ and $\textbf{R}$ are true but $\textbf{R}$ is $\textbf{NOT}$ the correct explanation of $\textbf{A}$
    3. $\textbf{A}$ is true but $\textbf{R}$ is false
    4. $\textbf{A}$ is false but $\textbf{R}$ is true
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (2)

    Both statements are true. Wind and water pollinated flowers lack bright colours and nectar because they do not need to attract animal pollinators.
    They produce huge numbers of pollen grains to make up for the heavy loss of pollen in transfer. That is a separate feature, not the reason for the lack of colour and nectar, so R does not explain A.

  6. NEET (UG) 2025, 4 May, Code 45 · Question 124
    In the seeds of cereals, the outer covering of endosperm separates the embryo by a protein-rich layer called :

    1. Coleoptile
    2. Coleorhiza
    3. Integument
    4. Aleurone layer
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (4)

    In cereal grains the outer covering of the endosperm is a protein-rich layer called the aleurone layer, which separates the endosperm from the embryo. The coleoptile and coleorhiza sheath the plumule and radicle.

  7. NEET (UG) 2025, 4 May, Code 45 · Question 127
    Given below are two statements : One is labelled as $\textbf{Assertion (A)}$ and the other is labelled as $\textbf{Reason (R).}$
    $\textbf{Assertion (A) :}$ A typical unfertilised, angiosperm embryo sac at maturity is 8 nucleate and 7-celled.
    $\textbf{Reason (R) :}$ The egg apparatus has 2 polar nuclei.
    In the light of the above statements, choose the $\textbf{correct}$ answer from the options given below :

    1. Both $\textbf{A}$ and $\textbf{R}$ are true and $\textbf{R}$ is the correct explanation of $\textbf{A}$
    2. Both $\textbf{A}$ and $\textbf{R}$ are true but $\textbf{R}$ is $\textbf{NOT}$ the correct explanation of $\textbf{A}$
    3. $\textbf{A}$ is true but $\textbf{R}$ is false
    4. $\textbf{A}$ is false but $\textbf{R}$ is true
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (3)

    A typical mature embryo sac is 8-nucleate and 7-celled (3 antipodals, 2 synergids, 1 egg and a central cell with 2 polar nuclei), so A is true.
    The egg apparatus consists of the egg cell and two synergids; the 2 polar nuclei are in the central cell, so R is false.

  8. NEET (UG) 2025, 4 May, Code 45 · Question 141
    Match $\textbf{List I}$ with $\textbf{List II}$ :

    List IList II
    A.ScutellumI.Persistent nucellus
    B.Non-albuminous seedII.Cotyledon of Monocot seed
    C.EpiblastIII.Groundnut
    D.PerispermIV.Rudimentary cotyledon
    Choose the option with all $\textbf{correct}$ matches.

    1. A-II, B-III, C-IV, D-I
    2. A-IV, B-III, C-II, D-I
    3. A-IV, B-III, C-I, D-II
    4. A-II, B-IV, C-III, D-I
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (1)

    The scutellum is the single cotyledon of the grass (monocot) embryo; groundnut seeds are non-albuminous (no residual endosperm); the epiblast is a rudimentary cotyledon in some grasses; perisperm is persistent nucellus (e.g. black pepper, beet).
    So A-II, B-III, C-IV, D-I.

  9. NEET (UG) 2025, 4 May, Code 45 · Question 153
    Given below are two statements : One is labelled as $\textbf{Assertion (A)}$ and the other is labelled as $\textbf{Reason (R).}$
    $\textbf{Assertion (A) :}$ Cells of the tapetum possess dense cytoplasm and generally have more than one nucleus.
    $\textbf{Reason (R) :}$ Presence of more than one nucleus in the tapetum increases the efficiency of nourishing the developing microspore mother cells.
    In light of the above statements, choose the $\textbf{most appropriate}$ answer from the options given below :

    1. Both $\textbf{A}$ and $\textbf{R}$ are true and $\textbf{R}$ is the correct explanation of $\textbf{A}$
    2. Both $\textbf{A}$ and $\textbf{R}$ are true but $\textbf{R}$ is $\textbf{NOT}$ the correct explanation of $\textbf{A}$
    3. $\textbf{A}$ is true but $\textbf{R}$ is false
    4. $\textbf{A}$ is false but $\textbf{R}$ is true
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (1)

    The tapetum is the innermost wall layer of the microsporangium; its cells have dense cytoplasm and generally more than one nucleus, so A is true.
    The tapetum nourishes the developing pollen, and being multinucleate with dense cytoplasm makes it metabolically very active, which increases its nourishing efficiency. The final key treats R as true and as the correct explanation of A.

  10. NEET (UG) 2025, 4 May, Code 45 · Question 154
    How many meiotic and mitotic divisions need to occur for the development of a mature female gametophyte from the megaspore mother cell in an angiosperm plant?

    1. 2 Meiosis and 3 Mitosis
    2. 1 Meiosis and 2 Mitosis
    3. 1 Meiosis and 3 Mitosis
    4. No Meiosis and 2 Mitosis
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (3)

    The megaspore mother cell undergoes one meiosis to form four megaspores. The functional megaspore's nucleus then divides mitotically three times (giving 2, 4 and then 8 nuclei) to form the 8-nucleate embryo sac.
    So 1 meiosis and 3 mitoses.

  11. NEET (UG) 2024, 5 May, Code T1 · Question 117
    Identify the set of correct statements:
    A. The flowers of $\textit{Vallisneria}$ are colourful and produce nectar.
    B. The flowers of waterlily are not pollinated by water.
    C. In most of water-pollinated species, the pollen grains are protected from wetting.
    D. Pollen grains of some hydrophytes are long and ribbon like.
    E. In some hydrophytes, the pollen grains are carried passively inside water.
    Choose the correct answer from the options given below:

    1. A, C, D and E only
    2. B, C, D and E only
    3. C, D and E only
    4. A, B, C and D only
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (2)

    A is wrong: water-pollinated flowers such as $\textit{Vallisneria}$ are not colourful and do not produce nectar.
    B is right: waterlily and water hyacinth emerge above water and are pollinated by insects or wind.
    C is right: pollen of most water-pollinated species has a mucilaginous covering that protects it from wetting.
    D is right: in seagrasses such as $\textit{Zostera}$ the pollen is long and ribbon-like.
    E is right: the pollen is carried passively inside the water. So B, C, D and E only.

  12. NEET (UG) 2024, 5 May, Code T1 · Question 129
    Identify the part of the seed from the given figure which is destined to form root when the seed germinates.

    1. C
    2. D
    3. A
    4. B
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (1)

    The figure is a longitudinal section of a monocot (maize-type) seed. The root is formed by the radicle. In the figure the radicle is the part labelled C, enclosed in its sheath, the coleorhiza (D); A is the coleoptile and B the shoot apex (plumule). So the answer is C.

  13. NEET (UG) 2024, 5 May, Code T1 · Question 138
    Identify the correct description about the given figure:

    1. Cleistogamous flowers showing autogamy.
    2. Compact inflorescence showing complete autogamy.
    3. Wind pollinated plant inflorescence showing flowers with well exposed stamens.
    4. Water pollinated flowers showing stamens with mucilaginous covering.
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (3)

    The figure (as in NCERT) shows a compact inflorescence of a wind-pollinated plant, with many flowers whose stamens hang out and are well exposed so that the light, non-sticky pollen is easily carried by the air.

  14. NEET (UG) 2023, 7 May, Code E1 · Question 102
    In angiosperm, the haploid, diploid and triploid structures of a fertilized embryo sac sequentially are :

    1. Synergids, Primary endosperm nucleus and zygote
    2. Antipodals, synergids, and primary endosperm nucleus
    3. Synergids, Zygote and Primary endosperm nucleus
    4. Synergids, antipodals and Polar nuclei
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (3)

    After double fertilisation the embryo sac contains: synergids and antipodals (haploid, $n$), the zygote (egg + male gamete, diploid, $2n$) and the primary endosperm nucleus (two polar nuclei + male gamete, triploid, $3n$).
    So the haploid, diploid and triploid structures are synergids, zygote and primary endosperm nucleus.

  15. NEET (UG) 2023, 7 May, Code E1 · Question 104
    Large, colourful, fragrant flowers with nectar are seen in :

    1. insect pollinated plants
    2. bird pollinated plants
    3. bat pollinated plants
    4. wind pollinated plants
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (1)

    Flowers pollinated by insects are generally large, colourful, fragrant and rich in nectar, which attract the insects. Wind-pollinated flowers are small and inconspicuous with no nectar or scent.

  16. NEET (UG) 2023, 7 May, Code E1 · Question 130
    What is the function of tassels in the corn cob?

    1. To attract insects
    2. To trap pollen grains
    3. To disperse pollen grains
    4. To protect seeds
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (2)

    Maize is wind pollinated. The tassels of the corn cob are its stigmas and styles, which move in the wind and trap pollen grains.

  17. NEET (UG) 2022, 17 July, Code Q1 · Question 122
    Given below are two statements :
    $\textbf{Statement I :}$
    Cleistogamous flowers are invariably autogamous
    $\textbf{Statement II :}$
    Cleistogamy is disadvantageous as there is no chance for cross pollination
    In the light of the above statements, choose the $\textbf{correct}$ answer from the options given below :

    1. Both Statement I and Statement II are correct
    2. Both Statement I and Statement II are incorrect
    3. Statement I is correct but Statement II is incorrect
    4. Statement I is incorrect but Statement II is correct
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (1)

    Cleistogamous flowers never open, so they can only self-pollinate (invariably autogamous). This assures seed set, but it rules out cross-pollination, which is a disadvantage. Both statements are correct.

  18. NEET (UG) 2022, 17 July, Code Q1 · Question 129
    Identify the $\textbf{incorrect}$ statement related to Pollination :

    1. Pollination by water is quite rare in flowering plants
    2. Pollination by wind is more common amongst abiotic pollination
    3. Flowers produce foul odours to attract flies and beetles to get pollinated
    4. Moths and butterflies are the most dominant pollinating agents among insects
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (4)

    Bees are the dominant insect pollinators, not moths and butterflies. The other three statements are correct.
