  1. NEET (UG) 2025, 4 May, Code 45 · Question 118
    Find the statement that is $\textbf{NOT}$ correct with regard to the structure of monocot stem.

    1. Hypodermis is parenchymatous.
    2. Vascular bundles are scattered.
    3. Vascular bundles are conjoint and closed.
    4. Phloem parenchyma is absent.
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (1)

    The monocot stem has a sclerenchymatous hypodermis, not a parenchymatous one. Its vascular bundles are scattered, conjoint and closed, and phloem parenchyma is absent.

  2. NEET (UG) 2024, 5 May, Code T1 · Question 101
    In the given figure, which component has thin outer walls and highly thickened inner walls?

    1. A
    2. B
    3. C
    4. D
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (3)

    The figure shows a stoma in surface view: A is an epidermal cell, B a subsidiary cell, C a guard cell and D the stomatal pore.
    Each bean-shaped guard cell has a thin, elastic outer wall and a highly thickened inner wall (the one facing the pore). So the answer is C.

  3. NEET (UG) 2024, 5 May, Code T1 · Question 123
    Given below are two statements:
    $\textbf{Statement I :}$ Parenchyma is living but collenchyma is dead tissue.
    $\textbf{Statement II :}$ Gymnosperms lack xylem vessels but presence of xylem vessels is the characteristic of angiosperms.
    In the light of the above statements, choose the correct answer from the options given below:

    1. Statement I is true but Statement II is false
    2. Statement I is false but Statement II is true
    3. Both Statement I and Statement II are true
    4. Both Statement I and Statement II are false
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (2)

    Both parenchyma and collenchyma are living tissues (sclerenchyma is the dead simple tissue), so Statement I is false.
    Gymnosperms lack vessels in their xylem, and the presence of vessels is a characteristic feature of angiosperms, so Statement II is true.

  4. NEET (UG) 2024, 5 May, Code T1 · Question 133
    Bulliform cells are responsible for

    1. Increased photosynthesis in monocots.
    2. Providing large spaces for storage of sugars.
    3. Inward curling of leaves in monocots.
    4. Protecting the plant from salt stress.
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (3)

    Bulliform cells are large, empty, colourless cells in the upper epidermis of grass (monocot) leaves. When they are turgid the leaf surface is exposed; when they lose water (under water stress) the leaves curl inwards to reduce water loss.

  5. NEET (UG) 2023, 7 May, Code E1 · Question 123
    Given below are two statements : One is labelled as $\textbf{Assertion A}$ and the other is labelled as $\textbf{Reason R}$ :
    $\textbf{Assertion A :}$ Late wood has fewer xylary elements with narrow vessels.
    $\textbf{Reason R :}$ Cambium is less active in winters.
    In the light of the above statements, choose the $\textbf{correct}$ answer from the options given below :

    1. Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation of A.
    2. Both A and R are true but R is NOT the correct explanation of A.
    3. A is true but R is false.
    4. A is false but R is true.
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (1)

    In winter the cambium is less active and forms fewer xylary elements with narrow vessels; this is late wood (autumn wood). In spring the cambium is very active and forms early wood with wide vessels.
    So both are true and R explains A.

  6. NEET (UG) 2023, 7 May, Code E1 · Question 133
    Given below are two statements :
    $\textbf{Statement I :}$ Endarch and exarch are the terms often used for describing the position of secondary xylem in the plant body.
    $\textbf{Statement II :}$ Exarch condition is the most common feature of the root system.
    In the light of the above statements, choose the $\textbf{correct}$ answer from the options given below :

    1. Both Statement I and Statement II are true.
    2. Both Statement I and Statement II are false.
    3. Statement I is correct but Statement II is false.
    4. Statement I is incorrect but Statement II is true.
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (4)

    Statement I is incorrect: endarch and exarch describe the position of the protoxylem in the primary xylem, not secondary xylem. In stems the protoxylem lies towards the centre (endarch).
    Statement II is true: in roots the protoxylem lies towards the periphery (exarch).

  7. NEET (UG) 2023, 7 May, Code E1 · Question 145
    Identify the $\textbf{correct}$ statements :
    A. Lenticels are the lens-shaped openings permitting the exchange of gases.
    B. Bark formed early in the season is called hard bark.
    C. Bark is a technical term that refers to all tissues exterior to vascular cambium.
    D. Bark refers to periderm and secondary phloem.
    E. Phellogen is single-layered in thickness.
    Choose the correct answer from the options given below :

    1. B, C and E only
    2. A and D only
    3. A, B and D only
    4. B and C only
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (2)

    A is correct: lenticels are lens-shaped openings for gaseous exchange. D is correct: bark includes the periderm and the secondary phloem.
    B is wrong: bark formed early in the season is soft (early) bark. C is wrong: bark is a non-technical term. E is wrong: phellogen is a couple of layers thick.
