  1. NEET (UG) 2026, 21 June (re-exam), Code 50 · Question 105
    Phyllotaxy is the pattern of arrangement of ________.

    1. leaves
    2. flowers
    3. fruits
    4. sepals
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (1)

    Phyllotaxy is the pattern in which leaves are arranged on the stem or branch: alternate, opposite or whorled. The arrangement of flowers is called inflorescence.

  2. NEET (UG) 2026, 21 June (re-exam), Code 50 · Question 113
    Which of the following are characteristic features of Solanaceae family?
    (a) Flowers are bisexual and actinomorphic
    (b) Calyx have five sepals and are united
    (c) Androecium have five stamens and are epipetalous
    (d) Ovary is inferior
    Choose the correct answer from the options given below :

    1. (a), (b) and (c) only
    2. (d) only
    3. (a) and (b) only
    4. (b), (c) and (d) only
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (1)

    Solanaceae flowers are bisexual and actinomorphic, with five united sepals (gamosepalous) and five epipetalous stamens, so (a), (b) and (c) are correct. The ovary is bicarpellary, syncarpous and superior, so (d) is wrong.

  3. NEET (UG) 2026, 21 June (re-exam), Code 50 · Question 120
    Match List-I with List-II.

    List-IList-II
    A.Marginal placentationI.Argemone
    B.Axile placentationII.Tomato
    C.Parietal placentationIII.Primrose
    D.Free central placentationIV.Pea
    Choose the correct answer from the options given below :

    1. A-II, B-IV, C-I, D-III
    2. A-IV, B-II, C-III, D-I
    3. A-IV, B-III, C-I, D-II
    4. A-IV, B-II, C-I, D-III
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (4)

    Pea has marginal placentation (A-IV). Tomato has axile placentation (B-II). Argemone has parietal placentation (C-I). Primrose (Primula) has free central placentation (D-III).

  4. NEET (UG) 2025, 4 May, Code 45 · Question 104
    Given below are two statements :
    $\textbf{Statement I :}$ In a floral formula $\oplus$ stands for zygomorphic nature of the flower, and $\underline{\mathrm{G}}$ stands for inferior ovary.
    $\textbf{Statement II :}$ In a floral formula $\oplus$ stands for actinomorphic nature of the flower and $\underline{\mathrm{G}}$ stands for superior ovary.
    In the light of the above statements, choose the $\textbf{correct}$ answer from the options given below :

    1. Both Statement I and Statement II are correct
    2. Both Statement I and Statement II are incorrect
    3. Statement I is correct but Statement II is incorrect
    4. Statement I is incorrect but Statement II is correct
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (4)

    In a floral formula $\oplus$ means actinomorphic (zygomorphic is shown by %), and $\underline{\mathrm{G}}$ (line below G) means a superior ovary, while a line above G means an inferior ovary.
    So Statement I is incorrect and Statement II is correct.

  5. NEET (UG) 2025, 4 May, Code 45 · Question 155
    Which of the following is an example of a zygomorphic flower?

    1. Petunia
    2. Datura
    3. Pea
    4. Chilli
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (3)

    A zygomorphic flower can be divided into two similar halves in only one vertical plane, as in pea, gulmohur, bean and Cassia. Petunia, Datura and chilli (Solanaceae) have actinomorphic flowers.

  6. NEET (UG) 2024, 5 May, Code T1 · Question 113
    Identify the type of flowers based on the position of calyx, corolla and androecium with respect to the ovary from the given figures (a) and (b)

    1. (a) Perigynous; (b) Epigynous
    2. (a) Perigynous; (b) Perigynous
    3. (a) Epigynous; (b) Hypogynous
    4. (a) Hypogynous; (b) Epigynous
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (2)

    In both flowers the thalamus forms a cup and the sepals, petals and stamens arise from its rim, at about the level of the ovary; the ovary is not fused with the cup (it is half inferior). This is the perigynous condition, as in plum and rose (NCERT figures of perigynous flowers). So both (a) and (b) are perigynous.

  7. NEET (UG) 2024, 5 May, Code T1 · Question 120
    Which of the following is an example of actinomorphic flower?

    1. $\textit{Pisum}$
    2. $\textit{Sesbania}$
    3. $\textit{Datura}$
    4. $\textit{Cassia}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (3)

    An actinomorphic flower can be divided into two equal halves by any radial plane through the centre, as in mustard, $\textit{Datura}$ and chilli. $\textit{Pisum}$, $\textit{Sesbania}$ and $\textit{Cassia}$ have zygomorphic flowers.

  8. NEET (UG) 2024, 5 May, Code T1 · Question 136
    Match List I with List II

    List IList II
    A.RoseI.Twisted aestivation
    B.PeaII.Perigynous flower
    C.CottonIII.Drupe
    D.MangoIV.Marginal placentation
    Choose the correct answer from the options given below:

    1. A-IV, B-III, C-II, D-I
    2. A-II, B-III, C-IV, D-I
    3. A-II, B-IV, C-I, D-III
    4. A-I, B-II, C-III, D-IV
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (3)

    Rose has perigynous flowers (A-II). Pea has marginal placentation (B-IV). Cotton (like china rose and lady's finger) has twisted aestivation (C-I). Mango is a drupe (D-III).

  9. NEET (UG) 2024, 5 May, Code T1 · Question 145
    Match List I with List II

    List I (Types of Stamens)List II (Example)
    A.MonoadelphousI.Citrus
    B.DiadelphousII.Pea
    C.PolyadelphousIII.Lily
    D.EpiphyllousIV.China-rose
    Choose the correct answer from the options given below:

    1. A-I, B-II, C-IV, D-III
    2. A-III, B-I, C-IV, D-II
    3. A-IV, B-II, C-I, D-III
    4. A-IV, B-I, C-II, D-III
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (3)

    Stamens united in one bundle (monoadelphous) occur in china-rose (A-IV); in two bundles (diadelphous) in pea (B-II); in more than two bundles (polyadelphous) in citrus (C-I). Stamens attached to the perianth (epiphyllous) occur in lily (D-III).

  10. NEET (UG) 2023, 7 May, Code E1 · Question 110
    Axile placentation is observed in

    1. Mustard, Cucumber and Primrose
    2. China rose, Beans and Lupin
    3. Tomato, Dianthus and Pea
    4. China rose, Petunia and Lemon
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (4)

    In axile placentation the ovary is multilocular and the placenta is axial, as in china rose, tomato, lemon and petunia.
    Mustard and cucumber have parietal, primrose and $\textit{Dianthus}$ free central, and pea, beans and lupin marginal placentation. So only option (4) is entirely axile.

  11. NEET (UG) 2023, 7 May, Code E1 · Question 119
    Family Fabaceae differs from Solanaceae and Liliaceae. With respect to the stamens, pick out the characteristics specific to family Fabaceae but not found in Solanaceae or Liliaceae.

    1. Diadelphous and Dithecous anthers
    2. Polyadelphous and epipetalous stamens
    3. Monoadelphous and Monothecous anthers
    4. Epiphyllous and Dithecous anthers
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (1)

    Fabaceae: ten stamens, diadelphous (9 + 1), anthers dithecous. Solanaceae: five epipetalous stamens. Liliaceae: six epiphyllous (epitepalous) stamens. Dithecous anthers are also found in the other two families, but the diadelphous condition is specific to Fabaceae, so option (1) describes Fabaceae.

  12. NEET (UG) 2023, 7 May, Code E1 · Question 140
    Given below are two statements : One is labelled as $\textbf{Assertion A}$ and the other is labelled as $\textbf{Reason R}$ :
    $\textbf{Assertion A :}$ A flower is defined as modified shoot wherein the shoot apical meristem changes to floral meristem.
    $\textbf{Reason R :}$ Internode of the shoot gets condensed to produce different floral appendages laterally at successive nodes instead of leaves.
    In the light of the above statements, choose the $\textbf{correct}$ answer from the options given below :

    1. Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation of A.
    2. Both A and R are true but R is NOT the correct explanation of A.
    3. A is true but R is false.
    4. A is false but R is true.
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (1)

    A flower is a modified shoot: the shoot apical meristem changes into a floral meristem, the internodes do not elongate and the axis gets condensed, and at successive nodes floral appendages arise laterally instead of leaves.
    So both are true and R explains A.

  13. NEET (UG) 2022, 17 July, Code Q1 · Question 102
    Identify the $\textbf{correct}$ set of statements :
    (a) The leaflets are modified into pointed hard thorns in $\textit{Citrus}$ and $\textit{Bougainvillea}$
    (b) Axillary buds form slender and spirally coiled tendrils in cucumber and pumpkin
    (c) Stem is flattened and fleshy in $\textit{Opuntia}$ and modified to perform the function of leaves
    (d) $\textit{Rhizophora}$ shows vertically upward growing roots that help to get oxygen for respiration
    (e) Subaerially growing stems in grasses and strawberry help in vegetative propagation
    Choose the $\textbf{correct answer}$ from the options given below :

    1. (b) and (c) Only
    2. (a) and (d) Only
    3. (b), (c), (d) and (e) Only
    4. (a), (b), (d) and (e) Only
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (3)

    (a) is wrong: the thorns of Citrus and Bougainvillea are modified axillary buds (stem), not leaflets.
    (b) stem tendrils from axillary buds, (c) the phylloclade of Opuntia, (d) the pneumatophores of Rhizophora and (e) runners/stolons are all correct.

  14. NEET (UG) 2022, 17 July, Code Q1 · Question 105
    Which one of the following plants shows vexillary aestivation and diadelphous stamens?

    1. $\textit{Colchicum autumnale}$
    2. $\textit{Pisum sativum}$
    3. $\textit{Allium cepa}$
    4. $\textit{Solanum nigrum}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (2)

    Vexillary aestivation and diadelphous stamens (9 + 1) are features of Fabaceae, to which the pea, Pisum sativum, belongs. Colchicum and Allium are Liliaceae, and Solanum is Solanaceae.

  15. NEET (UG) 2022, 17 July, Code Q1 · Question 126
    The flowers are Zygomorphic in:
    (a) Mustard
    (b) Gulmohar
    (c) $\textit{Cassia}$
    (d) $\textit{Datura}$
    (e) Chilly
    Choose the $\textbf{correct answer}$ from the options given below:

    1. (a), (b), (c) Only
    2. (b), (c) Only
    3. (d), (e) Only
    4. (c), (d), (e) Only
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (2)

    Zygomorphic flowers can be divided into two similar halves in only one plane, as in pea, gulmohar, bean and Cassia. Mustard, Datura and chilli have actinomorphic flowers.
