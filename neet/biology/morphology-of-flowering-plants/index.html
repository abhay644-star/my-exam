  1. NEET (UG) 2026, 21 June (re-exam), Code 50 · Question 105
    Phyllotaxy is the pattern of arrangement of ________.

    1. leaves
    2. flowers
    3. fruits
    4. sepals
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (1)

    Phyllotaxy is the pattern in which leaves are arranged on the stem or branch: alternate, opposite or whorled. The arrangement of flowers is called inflorescence.

  2. NEET (UG) 2026, 21 June (re-exam), Code 50 · Question 113
    Which of the following are characteristic features of Solanaceae family?
    (a) Flowers are bisexual and actinomorphic
    (b) Calyx have five sepals and are united
    (c) Androecium have five stamens and are epipetalous
    (d) Ovary is inferior
    Choose the correct answer from the options given below :

    1. (a), (b) and (c) only
    2. (d) only
    3. (a) and (b) only
    4. (b), (c) and (d) only
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (1)

    Solanaceae flowers are bisexual and actinomorphic, with five united sepals (gamosepalous) and five epipetalous stamens, so (a), (b) and (c) are correct. The ovary is bicarpellary, syncarpous and superior, so (d) is wrong.

  3. NEET (UG) 2026, 21 June (re-exam), Code 50 · Question 120
    Match List-I with List-II.

    List-IList-II
    A.Marginal placentationI.Argemone
    B.Axile placentationII.Tomato
    C.Parietal placentationIII.Primrose
    D.Free central placentationIV.Pea
    Choose the correct answer from the options given below :

    1. A-II, B-IV, C-I, D-III
    2. A-IV, B-II, C-III, D-I
    3. A-IV, B-III, C-I, D-II
    4. A-IV, B-II, C-I, D-III
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (4)

    Pea has marginal placentation (A-IV). Tomato has axile placentation (B-II). Argemone has parietal placentation (C-I). Primrose (Primula) has free central placentation (D-III).

  4. NEET (UG) 2025, 4 May, Code 45 · Question 104
    Given below are two statements :
    $\textbf{Statement I :}$ In a floral formula $\oplus$ stands for zygomorphic nature of the flower, and $\underline{\mathrm{G}}$ stands for inferior ovary.
    $\textbf{Statement II :}$ In a floral formula $\oplus$ stands for actinomorphic nature of the flower and $\underline{\mathrm{G}}$ stands for superior ovary.
    In the light of the above statements, choose the $\textbf{correct}$ answer from the options given below :

    1. Both Statement I and Statement II are correct
    2. Both Statement I and Statement II are incorrect
    3. Statement I is correct but Statement II is incorrect
    4. Statement I is incorrect but Statement II is correct
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (4)

    In a floral formula $\oplus$ means actinomorphic (zygomorphic is shown by %), and $\underline{\mathrm{G}}$ (line below G) means a superior ovary, while a line above G means an inferior ovary.
    So Statement I is incorrect and Statement II is correct.

  5. NEET (UG) 2025, 4 May, Code 45 · Question 155
    Which of the following is an example of a zygomorphic flower?

    1. Petunia
    2. Datura
    3. Pea
    4. Chilli
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (3)

    A zygomorphic flower can be divided into two similar halves in only one vertical plane, as in pea, gulmohur, bean and Cassia. Petunia, Datura and chilli (Solanaceae) have actinomorphic flowers.
