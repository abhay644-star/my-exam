  1. NEET (UG) 2026, 21 June (re-exam), Code 50 · Question 95
    Which of the following is not a part of human central neural system?

    1. Arachnoid
    2. Dura mater
    3. Pia mater
    4. Pericardium
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (4)

    The brain and spinal cord are covered by the meninges: the dura mater, arachnoid and pia mater. The pericardium is the membrane around the heart, so it is not part of the CNS.

  2. NEET (UG) 2024, 5 May, Code T1 · Question 173
    Match List I with List II :

    List IList II
    A.PonsI.Provides additional space for Neurons, regulates posture and balance.
    B.HypothalamusII.Controls respiration and gastric secretions.
    C.MedullaIII.Connects different regions of the brain.
    D.CerebellumIV.Neuro secretory cells
    Choose the correct answer from the options given below :

    1. A-I, B-III, C-II, D-IV
    2. A-II, B-I, C-III, D-IV
    3. A-II, B-III, C-I, D-IV
    4. A-III, B-IV, C-II, D-I
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (4)

    The pons consists of fibre tracts that interconnect different regions of the brain (A-III). The hypothalamus contains groups of neurosecretory cells (B-IV). The medulla contains centres that control respiration, cardiovascular reflexes and gastric secretions (C-II). The cerebellum has a very convoluted surface that provides additional space for many more neurons, and it maintains posture and balance (D-I).

  3. NEET (UG) 2024, 5 May, Code T1 · Question 193
    Given below are two statements :
    $\textbf{Statement I :}$ The cerebral hemispheres are connected by nerve tract known as corpus callosum.
    $\textbf{Statement II :}$ The brain stem consists of the medulla oblongata, pons and cerebrum.
    In the light of the above statements, choose the most appropriate answer from the options given below :

    1. Statement I is correct but Statement II is incorrect.
    2. Statement I is incorrect but Statement II is correct.
    3. Both Statement I and Statement II are correct.
    4. Both Statement I and Statement II are incorrect.
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (1)

    Statement I is correct: the two cerebral hemispheres are connected by a tract of nerve fibres, the corpus callosum.
    Statement II is incorrect: the brain stem is formed by the midbrain, pons and medulla oblongata; the cerebrum is part of the forebrain.

  4. NEET (UG) 2023, 7 May, Code E1 · Question 188
    The parts of human brain that helps in regulation of sexual behaviour, expression of excitement, pleasure, rage, fear etc. are :

    1. Limbic system & hypothalamus
    2. Corpora quadrigemina & hippocampus
    3. Brain stem & epithalamus
    4. Corpus callosum and thalamus
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (1)

    The limbic system (amygdala, hippocampus and related structures) together with the hypothalamus regulates sexual behaviour and the expression of emotions such as excitement, pleasure, rage and fear, as well as motivation.

  5. NEET (UG) 2022, 17 July, Code Q1 · Question 196
    Select the $\textbf{incorrect}$ statement regarding synapses :

    1. The membranes of presynaptic and postsynaptic neurons are in close proximity in an electrical synapse.
    2. Electrical current can flow directly from one neuron into the other across the electrical synapse.
    3. Chemical synapses use neurotransmitters
    4. Impulse transmission across a chemical synapse is always faster than that across an electrical synapse.
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (4)

    Transmission across an electrical synapse is faster than across a chemical synapse, which has to release and detect a neurotransmitter. Statement (4) is incorrect.
