  1. NEET (UG) 2026, 21 June (re-exam), Code 50 · Question 153
    Which of the following statements about lac-operon is correct?

    1. Gene $i$ is constitutively expressed
    2. Lactose activates repressor to bind to the operator
    3. Genes $i$, $z$, $y$ and $a$ share single common promoter
    4. Galactose can act as an inducer of lac operon
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (1)

    The regulator gene $i$ has its own promoter and is expressed constitutively, producing the repressor all the time. Lactose (allolactose) inactivates, not activates, the repressor; only $z$, $y$ and $a$ share the lac promoter; and the inducer is lactose, not galactose.

  2. NEET (UG) 2026, 21 June (re-exam), Code 50 · Question 167
    Which of the following enzymes synthesizes precursor mRNA?

    1. RNA polymerase I
    2. RNA polymerase II
    3. RNA polymerase III
    4. DNA polymerase
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (2)

    In eukaryotes RNA polymerase I transcribes rRNAs (28S, 18S, 5.8S), RNA polymerase III transcribes tRNA, 5S rRNA and snRNAs, and RNA polymerase II transcribes the precursor of mRNA (hnRNA).

  3. NEET (UG) 2025, 4 May, Code 45 · Question 96
    Given below are two statements :
    $\textbf{Statement I :}$ In the RNA world, RNA is considered the first genetic material evolved to carry out essential life processes. RNA acts as a genetic material and also as a catalyst for some important biochemical reactions in living systems. Being reactive, RNA is unstable.
    $\textbf{Statement II :}$ DNA evolved from RNA and is a more stable genetic material. Its double helical strands being complementary, resist changes by evolving repairing mechanism.
    In the light of the above statements, choose the $\textbf{most appropriate}$ answer from the options given below :

    1. Both statement I and statement II are correct
    2. Both statement I and statement II are incorrect
    3. Statement I is correct but statement II is incorrect
    4. Statement I is incorrect but statement II is correct
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (1)

    Both statements follow NCERT's account of the RNA world: RNA was the first genetic material and also acts as a catalyst, but its reactivity makes it unstable.
    DNA evolved from RNA with chemical changes (deoxyribose, thymine) that make it more stable, and its complementary double strands allow repair. Both statements are correct.

  4. NEET (UG) 2025, 4 May, Code 45 · Question 106
    Which chromosome in the human genome has the highest number of genes?

    1. Chromosome X
    2. Chromosome Y
    3. Chromosome 1
    4. Chromosome 10
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (3)

    The Human Genome Project found that chromosome 1 has the most genes (2968), while the Y chromosome has the fewest (231).

  5. NEET (UG) 2025, 4 May, Code 45 · Question 129
    Which of the following are the post-transcriptional events in an eukaryotic cell?
    A. Transport of pre-mRNA to cytoplasm prior to splicing.
    B. Removal of introns and joining of exons.
    C. Addition of methyl group at 5' end of hnRNA.
    D. Addition of adenine residues at 3' end of hnRNA.
    E. Base pairing of two complementary RNAs.
    Choose the $\textbf{correct}$ answer from the options given below :

    1. A, B, C only
    2. B, C, D only
    3. B, C, E only
    4. C, D, E only
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (2)

    The hnRNA is processed in the nucleus: splicing removes introns and joins exons (B), capping adds methyl guanosine triphosphate at the 5' end (C), and tailing adds adenylate residues at the 3' end (D).
    Pre-mRNA is not sent to the cytoplasm before splicing (A), and base pairing of complementary RNAs is not a processing step (E). Hence B, C, D only.

  6. NEET (UG) 2025, 4 May, Code 45 · Question 134
    Match $\textbf{List I}$ with $\textbf{List II}$ :

    List-IList-II
    A.Alfred Hershey and Martha ChaseI.Streptococcus pneumoniae
    B.EuchromatinII.Densely packed and dark-stained
    C.Frederick GriffithIII.Loosely packed and light-stained
    D.HeterochromatinIV.DNA as genetic material confirmation
    Choose the $\textbf{correct}$ answer from the options given below :

    1. A-II, B-IV, C-I, D-III
    2. A-IV, B-II, C-I, D-III
    3. A-IV, B-III, C-I, D-II
    4. A-III, B-II, C-IV, D-I
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (3)

    Hershey and Chase gave unequivocal proof that DNA is the genetic material; euchromatin is loosely packed and stains light; Griffith worked with $\textit{Streptococcus pneumoniae}$ (transformation); heterochromatin is densely packed and stains dark.
    So A-IV, B-III, C-I, D-II.

  7. NEET (UG) 2025, 4 May, Code 45 · Question 146
    Histones are enriched with -

    1. Lysine & Arginine
    2. Leucine & Lysine
    3. Phenylalanine & Leucine
    4. Phenylalanine & Arginine
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (1)

    Histones are positively charged, basic proteins rich in the basic amino acid residues lysine and arginine, whose side chains carry positive charges that bind the negatively charged DNA.

  8. NEET (UG) 2025, 4 May, Code 45 · Question 163
    Who proposed that the genetic code for amino acids should be made up of three nucleotides?

    1. George Gamow
    2. Francis Crick
    3. Jacque Monod
    4. Franklin Stahl
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (1)

    The physicist George Gamow argued that since there are 20 amino acids and only 4 bases, the code must be made of three nucleotides ($4^3 = 64$ combinations).

  9. NEET (UG) 2025, 4 May, Code 45 · Question 174
    Which factor is important for termination of transcription?

    1. $\alpha$ (alpha)
    2. $\sigma$ (sigma)
    3. $\rho$ (rho)
    4. $\gamma$ (gamma)
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (3)

    In bacteria the RNA polymerase associates with the initiation factor $\sigma$ to start transcription and with the termination factor $\rho$ (rho) to end it.
