  1. NEET (UG) 2026, 21 June (re-exam), Code 50 · Question 100
    Photorespiration reaction catalyzed by RuBisCo is shown below:
    RuBP + $\mathrm{O_2} \rightarrow$ 3-Phosphoglycerate + X.
    Identify “X” from the given options:

    1. Phosphoenolpyruvate
    2. 2-Phosphoglycolate
    3. Oxaloacetate
    4. Malate
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (2)

    In photorespiration, RuBisCO acts as an oxygenase. It joins $\mathrm{O_2}$ to RuBP (5C) to give one 3-phosphoglycerate (3C) and one phosphoglycolate (2C). So X is 2-phosphoglycolate. PEP, OAA and malate belong to the $\mathrm{C_4}$ pathway.

  2. NEET (UG) 2026, 21 June (re-exam), Code 50 · Question 102
    Which pigment has absorption peak at 700 nm in the photosynthetic reaction centre PS I (P700)?

    1. Chlorophyll b
    2. Chlorophyll a
    3. Xanthophylls
    4. Carotenoids
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (2)

    The reaction centre of each photosystem is a special chlorophyll a molecule. In PS I it absorbs maximally at 700 nm (P700), and in PS II at 680 nm (P680). Chlorophyll b and the carotenoids are only accessory pigments.

  3. NEET (UG) 2026, 21 June (re-exam), Code 50 · Question 114
    Select the correct sequence of experiments that led to a gradual understanding of photosynthesis in green plants.

    1. Absorption spectra of chlorophyll a and b $\rightarrow$ production of glucose $\rightarrow$ release of oxygen $\rightarrow$ role of air
    2. Role of air $\rightarrow$ release of oxygen $\rightarrow$ production of glucose $\rightarrow$ absorption spectra of chlorophyll a and b
    3. Release of oxygen $\rightarrow$ production of glucose $\rightarrow$ absorption spectra of chlorophyll a and b $\rightarrow$ role of air
    4. Production of glucose $\rightarrow$ role of air $\rightarrow$ release of oxygen $\rightarrow$ absorption spectra of chlorophyll a and b
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (2)

    Priestley (1770) first showed the role of air in a bell-jar experiment. Ingenhousz then showed that green parts release oxygen in sunlight. Sachs (1854) showed that glucose is produced and stored as starch. Engelmann later related the action spectrum to the absorption spectra of chlorophyll a and b.

  4. NEET (UG) 2026, 21 June (re-exam), Code 50 · Question 116
    How many turns of Calvin cycle are required for the formation of three molecules of glucose?

    1. 6
    2. 3
    3. 1
    4. 18
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (4)

    Each turn of the Calvin cycle fixes one $\mathrm{CO_2}$. One glucose ($\mathrm{C_6}$) needs 6 turns, so three glucose need $3 \times 6 = 18$ turns.

  5. NEET (UG) 2026, 21 June (re-exam), Code 50 · Question 122
    Which of the following statements regarding photorespiration are correct?
    (a) Do not occur in C3 plants
    (b) $\mathrm{CO_2}$ is consumed and $\mathrm{O_2}$ is generated
    (c) Phosphoglycolate is formed
    (d) No synthesis of ATP and NADPH
    Choose the correct answer from the options given below:

    1. (a) and (d) only
    2. (c) and (d) only
    3. (b) and (d) only
    4. (a) and (b) only
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (2)

    Photorespiration happens in $\mathrm{C_3}$ plants, so (a) is wrong. In it $\mathrm{O_2}$ is used up and $\mathrm{CO_2}$ is released, so (b) is wrong. RuBP oxygenation forms phosphoglycolate (c), and no ATP or NADPH is made (d). The answer is (c) and (d).

  6. NEET (UG) 2025, 4 May, Code 45 · Question 122
    Match $\textbf{List I}$ with $\textbf{List II}$ :

    List IList II
    A.Chlorophyll aI.Yellow-green
    B.Chlorophyll bII.Yellow
    C.XanthophyllsIII.Blue-green
    D.CarotenoidsIV.Yellow to Yellow-orange
    Choose the option with all $\textbf{correct}$ matches.

    1. A-III, B-IV, C-II, D-I
    2. A-III, B-I, C-II, D-IV
    3. A-I, B-II, C-IV, D-III
    4. A-I, B-IV, C-III, D-II
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (2)

    In paper chromatography of leaf pigments: chlorophyll a is bright or blue-green, chlorophyll b is yellow-green, xanthophylls are yellow and carotenoids are yellow to yellow-orange.
    So A-III, B-I, C-II, D-IV.

  7. NEET (UG) 2025, 4 May, Code 45 · Question 171
    Which of the following statements about RuBisCO is true?

    1. It is active only in the dark.
    2. It has higher affinity for oxygen than carbon dioxide.
    3. It is an enzyme involved in the photolysis of water.
    4. It catalyzes the carboxylation of RuBP.
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (4)

    RuBisCO (RuBP carboxylase-oxygenase) catalyses the carboxylation of RuBP in the Calvin cycle. It has a higher affinity for $\mathrm{CO_2}$ than for $\mathrm{O_2}$, works in the light-driven Calvin cycle, and plays no part in the photolysis of water.

  8. NEET (UG) 2024, 5 May, Code T1 · Question 127
    Which of the following are required for the dark reaction of photosynthesis?
    A. Light
    B. Chlorophyll
    C. $\mathrm{CO_2}$
    D. ATP
    E. NADPH
    Choose the correct answer from the options given below:

    1. C, D and E only
    2. D and E only
    3. A, B and C only
    4. B, C and D only
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (1)

    The dark (biosynthetic) reactions of the Calvin cycle do not directly need light or chlorophyll. They fix $\mathrm{CO_2}$ using the ATP and NADPH made in the light reactions. So C, D and E only.

  9. NEET (UG) 2024, 5 May, Code T1 · Question 130
    How many molecules of ATP and NADPH are required for every molecule of $\mathrm{CO_2}$ fixed in the Calvin cycle?

    1. 3 molecules of ATP and 3 molecules of NADPH
    2. 3 molecules of ATP and 2 molecules of NADPH
    3. 2 molecules of ATP and 3 molecules of NADPH
    4. 2 molecules of ATP and 2 molecules of NADPH
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (2)

    Fixing one $\mathrm{CO_2}$ in the Calvin cycle uses 3 ATP (2 in reduction and 1 in regeneration of RuBP) and 2 NADPH (in reduction). Making one glucose (6 $\mathrm{CO_2}$) needs 18 ATP and 12 NADPH.

  10. NEET (UG) 2024, 5 May, Code T1 · Question 140
    Given below are two statements:
    $\textbf{Statement I :}$ In $\mathrm{C_3}$ plants, some $\mathrm{O_2}$ binds to RuBisCO, hence $\mathrm{CO_2}$ fixation is decreased.
    $\textbf{Statement II :}$ In $\mathrm{C_4}$ plants, mesophyll cells show very little photorespiration while bundle sheath cells do not show photorespiration.
    In the light of the above statements, choose the $\textit{correct}$ answer from the options given below:

    1. Statement I is true but Statement II is false
    2. Statement I is false but Statement II is true
    3. Both Statement I and Statement II are true
    4. Both Statement I and Statement II are false
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (1)

    In $\mathrm{C_3}$ plants some $\mathrm{O_2}$ binds to RuBisCO (photorespiration), so $\mathrm{CO_2}$ fixation decreases: Statement I is true.
    In $\mathrm{C_4}$ plants the mesophyll cells lack RuBisCO, so they cannot photorespire, and the bundle sheath cells have a high $\mathrm{CO_2}$ concentration around RuBisCO, which keeps photorespiration minimal. Statement II has this the wrong way round, so it is false.

  11. NEET (UG) 2023, 7 May, Code E1 · Question 112
    The reaction centre in PS II has an absorption maxima at

    1. 680 nm
    2. 700 nm
    3. 660 nm
    4. 780 nm
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (1)

    The reaction centre chlorophyll $a$ of PS II absorbs maximally at 680 nm (P680); that of PS I absorbs at 700 nm (P700).

  12. NEET (UG) 2023, 7 May, Code E1 · Question 126
    How many ATP and $\mathrm{NADPH_2}$ are required for the synthesis of one molecule of Glucose during Calvin cycle?

    1. 12 ATP and 12 $\mathrm{NADPH_2}$
    2. 18 ATP and 12 $\mathrm{NADPH_2}$
    3. 12 ATP and 16 $\mathrm{NADPH_2}$
    4. 18 ATP and 16 $\mathrm{NADPH_2}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (2)

    Fixing one $\mathrm{CO_2}$ in the Calvin cycle needs 3 ATP and 2 NADPH. One glucose needs six turns of the cycle: $6 \times 3 = 18$ ATP and $6 \times 2 = 12$ NADPH.

  13. NEET (UG) 2023, 7 May, Code E1 · Question 143
    Which of the following combinations is required for chemiosmosis?

    1. membrane, proton pump, proton gradient, ATP synthase
    2. membrane, proton pump, proton gradient, NADP synthase
    3. proton pump, electron gradient, ATP synthase
    4. proton pump, electron gradient, NADP synthase
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (1)

    Chemiosmosis needs a membrane, a proton pump, a proton gradient across the membrane and ATP synthase: protons flowing back through the ATP synthase channel drive the synthesis of ATP.
