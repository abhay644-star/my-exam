  1. NEET (UG) 2026, 21 June (re-exam), Code 50 · Question 111
    Given below are two statements:
    Statement I: In gymnosperms, the male and female gametophytes remain within the sporangia.
    Statement II: In gymnosperms, seeds are not covered.
    In the light of the above statements, choose the most appropriate answer from the options given below:

    1. Both Statement I and Statement II are correct
    2. Both Statement I and Statement II are incorrect
    3. Statement I is correct but Statement II is incorrect
    4. Statement I is incorrect but Statement II is correct
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (1)

    In gymnosperms the gametophytes do not live freely. They stay inside the sporangia, which are retained on the sporophyte, so Statement I is correct. The ovules are not enclosed in an ovary, so the seeds are naked (gymnos = naked, sperma = seed), and Statement II is correct too.

  2. NEET (UG) 2026, 21 June (re-exam), Code 50 · Question 121
    Sphenopsida class belongs to _____________.

    1. bryophytes
    2. angiosperms
    3. gymnosperms
    4. pteridophytes
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (4)

    Pteridophytes are divided into four classes: Psilopsida, Lycopsida, Sphenopsida (e.g. Equisetum) and Pteropsida. So Sphenopsida belongs to the pteridophytes.

  3. NEET (UG) 2025, 4 May, Code 45 · Question 111
    Given below are the stages in the life cycle of pteridophytes. Arrange the following stages in the correct sequence.
    A. Prothallus stage
    B. Meiosis in spore mother cells
    C. Fertilisation
    D. Formation of archegonia and antheridia in gametophyte.
    E. Transfer of antherozoids to the archegonia in presence of water.
    Choose the $\textbf{correct}$ answer from the options given below :

    1. B, A, D, E, C
    2. B, A, E, C, D
    3. D, E, C, A, B
    4. E, D, C, B, A
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (1)

    Spore mother cells in the sporangia undergo meiosis to form spores (B); a spore germinates into the prothallus (A); the prothallus bears antheridia and archegonia (D); antherozoids reach the archegonia through water (E); fertilisation follows (C).
    Sequence: B, A, D, E, C.

  4. NEET (UG) 2025, 4 May, Code 45 · Question 119
    The correct sequence of events in the life cycle of bryophytes is
    A. Fusion of antherozoid with egg.
    B. Attachment of gametophyte to substratum.
    C. Reduction division to produce haploid spores.
    D. Formation of sporophyte.
    E. Release of antherozoids into water.
    Choose the $\textbf{correct}$ answer from the options given below :

    1. D, E, A, C, B
    2. B, E, A, C, D
    3. B, E, A, D, C
    4. D, E, A, B, C
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (3)

    The dominant gametophyte is attached to the substratum by rhizoids (B); antherozoids are released into water (E) and fuse with the egg (A); the zygote forms the sporophyte (D), in which spore mother cells undergo reduction division to produce haploid spores (C).
    Sequence: B, E, A, D, C.

  5. NEET (UG) 2025, 4 May, Code 45 · Question 159
    Which one of the following is the characteristic feature of gymnosperms?

    1. Seeds are enclosed in fruits.
    2. Seeds are naked.
    3. Seeds are absent.
    4. Gymnosperms have flowers for reproduction.
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (2)

    Gymnosperms ('naked seeds') have ovules that are not enclosed by an ovary wall, so the seeds remain exposed before and after fertilisation. They have no flowers or fruits.

  6. NEET (UG) 2025, 4 May, Code 45 · Question 162
    Match $\textbf{List I}$ with $\textbf{List II}$.

    List IList II
    A.PteridophyteI.$\textit{Salvia}$
    B.BryophyteII.$\textit{Ginkgo}$
    C.AngiospermIII.$\textit{Polytrichum}$
    D.GymnospermIV.$\textit{Salvinia}$
    Choose the option with all $\textbf{correct}$ matches.

    1. A-III, B-IV, C-II, D-I
    2. A-IV, B-III, C-I, D-II
    3. A-III, B-IV, C-I, D-II
    4. A-IV, B-III, C-II, D-I
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (2)

    $\textit{Salvinia}$ is a pteridophyte (water fern), $\textit{Polytrichum}$ is a moss (bryophyte), $\textit{Salvia}$ is an angiosperm and $\textit{Ginkgo}$ is a gymnosperm.
    So A-IV, B-III, C-I, D-II.

  7. NEET (UG) 2025, 4 May, Code 45 · Question 168
    In bryophytes, the gemmae help in which one of the following?

    1. Sexual reproduction
    2. Asexual reproduction
    3. Nutrient absorption
    4. Gaseous exchange
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (2)

    Gemmae are green, multicellular, asexual buds formed in gemma cups on liverwort thalli (e.g. $\textit{Marchantia}$). They detach and grow into new plants, so they help in asexual reproduction.

  8. NEET (UG) 2024, 5 May, Code T1 · Question 146
    Read the following statements and choose the set of correct statements:
    In the members of Phaeophyceae,
    A. Asexual reproduction occurs usually by biflagellate zoospores.
    B. Sexual reproduction is by oogamous method only.
    C. Stored food is in the form of carbohydrates which is either mannitol or laminarin.
    D. The major pigments found are chlorophyll a, c and carotenoids and xanthophyll.
    E. Vegetative cells have a cellulosic wall, usually covered on the outside by gelatinous coating of algin.
    Choose the correct answer from the options given below:

    1. A, C, D and E only
    2. A, B, C and E only
    3. A, B, C and D only
    4. B, C, D and E only
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (1)

    In brown algae asexual reproduction is usually by pear-shaped biflagellate zoospores (A), food is stored as laminarin or mannitol (C), the pigments are chlorophyll $\textit{a}$, $\textit{c}$, carotenoids and xanthophylls (fucoxanthin) (D), and the cellulosic cell wall is usually covered by a gelatinous coating of algin (E).
    B is wrong: sexual reproduction may be isogamous, anisogamous or oogamous. So A, C, D and E only.
