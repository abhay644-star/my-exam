  1. NEET (UG) 2026, 21 June (re-exam), Code 50 · Question 107
    Given below are two statements :
    Statement I : When any plane passing through the central axis of the body divides the organism into two identical halves, it is called radial symmetry.
    Statement II : In phylum Echinodermata, both adults and larvae are radially symmetrical.
    In the light of the above statements, choose the most appropriate answer from the options given below :

    1. Both Statement I and Statement II are correct
    2. Both Statement I and Statement II are incorrect
    3. Statement I is correct but Statement II is incorrect
    4. Statement I is incorrect but Statement II is correct
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (3)

    Statement I is the correct definition of radial symmetry. Statement II is wrong because adult echinoderms are radially symmetrical but their larvae are bilaterally symmetrical.

  2. NEET (UG) 2026, 21 June (re-exam), Code 50 · Question 130
    Given below are two statements:
    Statement I : The class name Reptilia refers to creeping or crawling mode of locomotion.
    Statement II : All organisms belonging to Reptilia have three chambered heart.
    In the light of the above statements, choose the most appropriate answer from the options given below:

    1. Both Statement I and Statement II are correct
    2. Both Statement I and Statement II are incorrect
    3. Statement I is correct but Statement II is incorrect
    4. Statement I is incorrect but Statement II is correct
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (3)

    The name Reptilia comes from the Latin repere/reptum, meaning to creep or crawl, so Statement I is correct. Most reptiles have a three-chambered heart, but crocodiles have a four-chambered heart, so Statement II ("all") is incorrect.

  3. NEET (UG) 2026, 21 June (re-exam), Code 50 · Question 132
    Which of the following is not a characteristic of chordates?

    1. Presence of notochord
    2. Central nervous system is dorsal
    3. Absence of gills
    4. Presence of post anal part (tail)
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (3)

    All chordates have a notochord, a dorsal hollow nerve cord, paired pharyngeal gill slits and a post-anal tail at some stage of life. So absence of gills is not a chordate feature.

  4. NEET (UG) 2025, 4 May, Code 45 · Question 109
    While trying to find out the characteristic of a newly found animal, a researcher did the histology of adult animal and observed a cavity with presence of mesodermal tissue towards the body wall but no mesodermal tissue was observed towards the alimentary canal. What could be the possible coelome of that animal ?

    1. Acoelomate
    2. Pseudocoelomate
    3. Schizocoelomate
    4. Spongocoelomate
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (2)

    In pseudocoelomates the body cavity is not lined by mesoderm all round: mesoderm is present as scattered pouches between the ectoderm and endoderm, i.e. on the body wall side but not around the gut. A true coelom (e.g. schizocoel) is lined by mesoderm on both sides; acoelomates have no cavity.

  5. NEET (UG) 2025, 4 May, Code 45 · Question 139
    All living members of the class Cyclostomata are:

    1. Free living
    2. Endoparasite
    3. Symbiotic
    4. Ectoparasite
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (4)

    All living members of the class Cyclostomata (lampreys and hagfishes) are ectoparasites on some fishes, which they attach to with a sucking, circular mouth.

  6. NEET (UG) 2025, 4 May, Code 45 · Question 142
    Given below are two statements : one is labelled as $\textbf{Assertion (A)}$ and the other is labelled as $\textbf{Reason (R)}$.
    $\textbf{Assertion (A) :}$ All vertebrates are chordates but all chordates are not vertebrate.
    $\textbf{Reason (R) :}$ The members of subphylum vertebrata possess notochord during the embryonic period, the notochord is replaced by a cartilaginous or bony vertebral column in adults.
    In the light of the above statements, choose the $\textbf{correct}$ answer from the options given below :

    1. Both $\textbf{A}$ and $\textbf{R}$ are true and $\textbf{R}$ is the correct explanation of $\textbf{A}$
    2. Both $\textbf{A}$ and $\textbf{R}$ are true but $\textbf{R}$ is $\textbf{not}$ the correct explanation of $\textbf{A}$
    3. $\textbf{A}$ is true but $\textbf{R}$ is false
    4. $\textbf{A}$ is false but $\textbf{R}$ is true
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (1)

    Vertebrates have a notochord in the embryo, so they are chordates; but in vertebrates it is replaced by a vertebral column in the adult, which urochordates and cephalochordates never develop.
    So all vertebrates are chordates but not all chordates are vertebrates: both are true and R explains A (as stated in NCERT).

  7. NEET (UG) 2025, 4 May, Code 45 · Question 166
    Role of the water vascular system in Echinoderms is :
    A. Respiration and Locomotion
    B. Excretion and Locomotion
    C. Capture and transport of food
    D. Digestion and Respiration
    E. Digestion and Excretion
    Choose the $\textbf{correct}$ answer from the options given below :

    1. A and B Only
    2. A and C Only
    3. B and C Only
    4. B, D and E Only
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (2)

    The water vascular system of echinoderms helps in locomotion, capture and transport of food, and respiration. It has no role in digestion or excretion. Hence A and C only.

  9. NEET (UG) 2024, 5 May, Code T1 · Question 177
    Match List I with List II :

    List IList II
    A.PleurobrachiaI.Mollusca
    B.RadulaII.Ctenophora
    C.StomochordIII.Osteichthyes
    D.Air bladderIV.Hemichordata
    Choose the correct answer from the options given below :

    1. A-II, B-IV, C-I, D-III
    2. A-IV, B-III, C-II, D-I
    3. A-IV, B-II, C-III, D-I
    4. A-II, B-I, C-IV, D-III
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (4)

    $\textit{Pleurobrachia}$ is a ctenophore (A-II). The radula, a file-like rasping organ, is found in molluscs (B-I). Hemichordates have a stomochord, once taken for a notochord (C-IV). Bony fishes (Osteichthyes) have an air bladder that regulates buoyancy (D-III).

  10. NEET (UG) 2024, 5 May, Code T1 · Question 179
    Consider the following statements :
    A. Annelids are true coelomates
    B. Poriferans are pseudocoelomates
    C. Aschelminthes are acoelomates
    D. Platyhelminthes are pseudocoelomates
    Choose the correct answer from the options given below :

    1. C only
    2. D only
    3. B only
    4. A only
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (4)

    Only A is correct: annelids have a true coelom lined by mesoderm. Poriferans have cellular level organisation and no coelom, aschelminthes are pseudocoelomates, and platyhelminthes are acoelomates.

  11. NEET (UG) 2024, 5 May, Code T1 · Question 187
    The following are the statements about non-chordates :
    A. Pharynx is perforated by gill slits.
    B. Notochord is absent.
    C. Central nervous system is dorsal.
    D. Heart is dorsal if present.
    E. Post anal tail is absent.
    Choose the most appropriate answer from the options given below :

    1. B, D & E only
    2. B, C & D only
    3. A & C only
    4. A, B & D only
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (1)

    Non-chordates have no notochord, a ventral (and solid) central nervous system, no gill slits in the pharynx, a dorsal heart if present, and no post-anal tail. So B, D and E describe non-chordates, while A and C are chordate features.

  12. NEET (UG) 2023, 7 May, Code E1 · Question 183
    Radial symmetry is NOT found in adults of phylum _______.

    1. Ctenophora
    2. Hemichordata
    3. Coelenterata
    4. Echinodermata
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (2)

    Coelenterates and ctenophores are radially symmetrical, and adult echinoderms are radially symmetrical (their larvae are bilateral). Hemichordates such as $\textit{Balanoglossus}$ are bilaterally symmetrical, so radial symmetry is not found in them.

  13. NEET (UG) 2023, 7 May, Code E1 · Question 187
    Select the correct statements with reference to chordates.
    A. Presence of a mid-dorsal, solid and double nerve cord.
    B. Presence of closed circulatory system.
    C. Presence of paired pharyngeal gillslits.
    D. Presence of dorsal heart
    E. Triploblastic pseudocoelomate animals.
    Choose the $\textbf{correct}$ answer from the options given below:

    1. A, C and D only
    2. B and C only
    3. B, D and E only
    4. C, D and E only
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (2)

    Chordates have a closed circulatory system (B) and paired pharyngeal gill slits (C). The nerve cord is dorsal, hollow and single (not solid and double), the heart is ventral, and chordates are triploblastic coelomates, so A, D and E are wrong. So B and C only.

  14. NEET (UG) 2023, 7 May, Code E1 · Question 189
    The unique mammalian characteristics are:

    1. hairs, tympanic membrane and mammary glands
    2. hairs, pinna and mammary glands
    3. hairs, pinna and indirect development
    4. pinna, monocondylic skull and mammary glands
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (2)

    The most distinctive features of mammals are mammary glands, hair on the skin and external ears (pinnae). A tympanic membrane is also found in amphibians, reptiles and birds; mammals have direct development and a dicondylic skull.

  15. NEET (UG) 2022, 17 July, Code Q1 · Question 152
    In which of the following animals, digestive tract has additional chambers like crop and gizzard?

    1. $\textit{Corvus}$, $\textit{Columba}$, $\textit{Chameleon}$
    2. $\textit{Bufo}$, $\textit{Balaenoptera}$, $\textit{Bangarus}$
    3. $\textit{Catla}$, $\textit{Columba}$, $\textit{Crocodilus}$
    4. $\textit{Pavo}$, $\textit{Psittacula}$, $\textit{Corvus}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (4)

    A crop and gizzard are features of the bird digestive tract. Pavo (peacock), Psittacula (parrot) and Corvus (crow) are all birds; each of the other options includes a non-bird.

  16. NEET (UG) 2022, 17 July, Code Q1 · Question 161
    Given below are two statements : one is labelled as Assertion (A) and the other is labelled as Reason (R).
    $\textbf{Assertion (A) :}$ All vertebrates are chordates but all chordates are not vertebrates.
    $\textbf{Reason (R) :}$ Notochord is replaced by vertebral column in the adult vertebrates.
    In the light of the above statements, choose the $\textbf{most appropriate}$ answer from the option given below :

    1. Both (A) and (R) are correct and (R) is the correct explanation of (A)
    2. Both (A) and (R) are correct but (R) is not the correct explanation of (A)
    3. (A) is correct but (R) is not correct
    4. (A) is not correct but (R) is correct
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (1)

    Both are correct, and (R) explains (A): vertebrates have a notochord in the embryo that is replaced by a vertebral column in the adult. Chordates such as urochordates and cephalochordates keep the notochord without forming a vertebral column, so they are not vertebrates.
