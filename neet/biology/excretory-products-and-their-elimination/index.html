  1. NEET (UG) 2026, 21 June (re-exam), Code 50 · Question 157
    Which of the following statements about the reabsorption process in Henle's loop are correct?
    (a) The descending limb of Henle's loop is permeable to water but almost impermeable to electrolytes.
    (b) Urine gets concentrated in Henle's loop.
    (c) Reabsorption of $\mathrm{Na^+}$ and water takes place in Henle's loop.
    (d) Active or passive transport of electrolytes occurs in the ascending limb of Henle's loop.
    Choose the correct answer from the options given below:

    1. (a) and (b) only
    2. (b), (c) and (d) only
    3. (a), (b) and (c) only
    4. (a), (b) and (d) only
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (4)

    (a) is correct: the descending limb is permeable to water but almost impermeable to electrolytes. (b) is correct: the filtrate becomes concentrated as it moves down the descending limb. (d) is correct: the ascending limb transports electrolytes actively or passively while being impermeable to water. (c) is incorrect because the two limbs differ — $\mathrm{Na^+}$ and water are not reabsorbed together, and overall reabsorption in Henle's loop is minimal. Hence (a), (b) and (d) only.

  3. NEET (UG) 2024, 5 May, Code T1 · Question 157
    Given below are two statements :
    $\textbf{Statement I :}$ In the nephron, the descending limb of loop of Henle is impermeable to water and permeable to electrolytes.
    $\textbf{Statement II :}$ The proximal convoluted tubule is lined by simple columnar brush border epithelium and increases the surface area for reabsorption.
    In the light of the above statements, choose the correct answer from the options given below :

    1. Statement I is true but Statement II is false
    2. Statement I is false but Statement II is true
    3. Both Statement I and Statement II are true
    4. Both Statement I and Statement II are false
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (4)

    Statement I is false: the descending limb of the loop of Henle is permeable to water but almost impermeable to electrolytes (it is the ascending limb that is impermeable to water).
    Statement II is false: the PCT is lined by simple cuboidal brush border epithelium, not columnar.

  4. NEET (UG) 2024, 5 May, Code T1 · Question 186
    Choose the correct statement given below regarding juxta medullary nephron.

    1. Loop of Henle of juxta medullary nephron runs deep into medulla.
    2. Juxta medullary nephrons outnumber the cortical nephrons.
    3. Juxta medullary nephrons are located in the columns of Bertini.
    4. Renal corpuscle of juxta medullary nephron lies in the outer portion of the renal medulla.
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (1)

    In juxta medullary nephrons the loop of Henle is very long and runs deep into the medulla. They are fewer than cortical nephrons, their renal corpuscles lie in the inner part of the cortex near the medulla, and the columns of Bertini are extensions of the cortex between the medullary pyramids, not the location of these nephrons.

  5. NEET (UG) 2023, 7 May, Code E1 · Question 165
    Match List I with List II.

    List IList II
    A.$\textit{Taenia}$I.Nephridia
    B.$\textit{Paramoecium}$II.Contractile vacuole
    C.$\textit{Periplaneta}$III.Flame cells
    D.$\textit{Pheretima}$IV.Urecose gland
    Choose the $\textbf{correct}$ answer from the options give below:

    1. A-I, B-II, C-III, D-IV
    2. A-I, B-II, C-IV, D-III
    3. A-III, B-II, C-IV, D-I
    4. A-II, B-I, C-IV, D-III
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (3)

    $\textit{Taenia}$ (a flatworm) excretes through flame cells (A-III). $\textit{Paramoecium}$ uses a contractile vacuole (B-II). In $\textit{Periplaneta}$ (cockroach) the uricose glands help in excretion (C-IV). $\textit{Pheretima}$ (earthworm) has nephridia (D-I).

  6. NEET (UG) 2023, 7 May, Code E1 · Question 180
    Given below are statements: one is labelled as $\textbf{Assertion A}$ and the other is labelled as $\textbf{Reason R.}$
    $\textbf{Assertion A:}$ Nephrons are of two types: Cortical & Juxta medullary, based on their relative position in cortex and medulla.
    $\textbf{Reason R:}$ Juxta medullary nephrons have short loop of Henle whereas, cortical nephrons have longer loop of Henle.
    In the light of the above statements, choose the $\textbf{correct}$ answer from the options given below:

    1. Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation of A.
    2. Both A and R are true but R is NOT the correct explanation of A.
    3. A is true but R is false.
    4. A is false but R is true.
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (3)

    A is true: nephrons are cortical or juxta medullary depending on how far the loop of Henle extends into the medulla.
    R is false: it is the other way round. Cortical nephrons have a short loop of Henle that barely reaches the medulla, while juxta medullary nephrons have a very long loop that runs deep into it.

  7. NEET (UG) 2023, 7 May, Code E1 · Question 198
    Which of the following statements are correct?
    A. An excessive loss of body fluid from the body switches off osmoreceptors.
    B. ADH facilitates water reabsorption to prevent diuresis.
    C. ANF causes vasodilation.
    D. ADH causes increase in blood pressure.
    E. ADH is responsible for decrease in GFR.
    Choose the $\textbf{correct}$ answer from the options given below:

    1. A and B only
    2. B, C and D only
    3. A, B and E only
    4. C, D and E only
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (2)

    B, C and D are correct. A is wrong: excessive loss of body fluid activates the osmoreceptors, which trigger ADH release. E is wrong: by constricting blood vessels ADH raises blood pressure, which can increase glomerular blood flow and hence the GFR.

  8. NEET (UG) 2022, 17 July, Code Q1 · Question 163
    Nitrogenous waste is excreted in the form of pellet or paste by :

    1. $\textit{Ornithorhynchus}$
    2. $\textit{Salamandra}$
    3. $\textit{Hippocampus}$
    4. $\textit{Pavo}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (4)

    Birds such as Pavo (peacock) are uricotelic and excrete uric acid as a pellet or paste with very little water.
