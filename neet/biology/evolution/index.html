  1. NEET (UG) 2026, 21 June (re-exam), Code 50 · Question 141
    Natural selection can lead to _________.
    (a) stabilisation
    (b) genetic drift
    (c) directional change
    (d) disruption
    Choose the correct answer from the options given below :

    1. (a) only
    2. (a), (c) and (d) only
    3. (a), (b), (c) and (d)
    4. (a) and (c) only
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (2)

    Natural selection acting on a population can be stabilising, directional or disruptive. Genetic drift is a separate, chance-based mechanism of change in allele frequency and is not an outcome of natural selection. Hence (a), (c) and (d) only.

  2. NEET (UG) 2026, 21 June (re-exam), Code 50 · Question 147
    A population of diploid organisms is at Hardy-Weinberg equilibrium. If the frequency of allele A is 0.1, the frequency of AA is ________.

    1. 0.01
    2. 0.02
    3. 0.10
    4. 0.99
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (1)

    At Hardy-Weinberg equilibrium, genotype frequencies are $p^2 : 2pq : q^2$. With $p = 0.1$, frequency of AA $= p^2 = (0.1)^2 = 0.01$.

  3. NEET (UG) 2026, 21 June (re-exam), Code 50 · Question 159
    Which of the following is not evidence for evolution?

    1. Convergent evolution of traits like wings of birds and butterflies
    2. Paleontological evidence from fossil records
    3. Embryological support for evolution as proposed by Ernst Heckel
    4. Divergent evolution of anatomical structures such as forelimbs
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (3)

    Fossil records, homologous structures (divergent evolution) and analogous structures (convergent evolution) are all accepted evidences of evolution. The embryological support proposed by Ernst Haeckel (that embryos of higher animals pass through adult stages of lower animals) was disproved by Karl Ernst von Baer, so it is not accepted evidence.

  4. NEET (UG) 2026, 21 June (re-exam), Code 50 · Question 160
    Given below are two statements :
    Statement I : Modern Homo sapiens arose in Australia and moved across continents.
    Statement II : Homo sapiens arose around 75000 to 10000 years ago.
    In the light of the above statements, choose the most appropriate answer from the options given below :

    1. Both Statement I and Statement II are correct
    2. Both Statement I and Statement II are incorrect
    3. Statement I is correct but Statement II is incorrect
    4. Statement I is incorrect but Statement II is correct
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (4)

    Modern Homo sapiens arose in Africa and migrated across continents, so Statement I is incorrect. During the ice age between 75,000 and 10,000 years ago modern Homo sapiens arose, so Statement II is correct.

  5. NEET (UG) 2026, 21 June (re-exam), Code 50 · Question 164
    Adaptive radiation in placental mammals and Australian Marsupials leading to similarity between distant species is an example of ________.

    1. divergent evolution
    2. convergent evolution
    3. founder effect
    4. genetic drift
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (2)

    When adaptive radiation occurs separately in two isolated groups (placental mammals and Australian marsupials) and produces similar forms, such as the placental wolf and the Tasmanian wolf, unrelated lineages come to resemble each other. This is convergent evolution.

  6. NEET (UG) 2026, 21 June (re-exam), Code 50 · Question 170
    Given below are two statements : one is labelled as Assertion A and the other is labelled as Reason R.
    Assertion A : Forelimbs of human and bats are homologous.
    Reason R : Forelimbs of humans and bats have similar anatomical structure.
    In the light of the above statements, choose the most appropriate answer from the options given below.

    1. Both A and R are correct and R is the correct explanation of A
    2. Both A and R are true, but R is not the correct explanation of A
    3. A is true but R is false
    4. A is false but R is true
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (1)

    The forelimbs of humans and bats perform different functions but share the same basic anatomical plan (humerus, radius-ulna, carpals, metacarpals, phalanges). Organs with similar structure and common origin are homologous, so R correctly explains A.

  7. NEET (UG) 2025, 4 May, Code 45 · Question 151
    Sweet potato and potato represent a certain type of evolution. Select the correct combination of terms to explain the evolution.

    1. Analogy, convergent
    2. Homology, divergent
    3. Homology, convergent
    4. Analogy, divergent
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (1)

    Sweet potato is a modified root and potato is a modified stem; both store food but have different origins. They are analogous organs, the result of convergent evolution.

  8. NEET (UG) 2024, 5 May, Code T1 · Question 160
    Given below are some stages of human evolution. Arrange them in correct sequence. (Past to Recent)
    A. $\textit{Homo habilis}$
    B. $\textit{Homo sapiens}$
    C. $\textit{Homo neanderthalensis}$
    D. $\textit{Homo erectus}$
    Choose the correct sequence of human evolution from the options given below :

    1. C-B-D-A
    2. A-D-C-B
    3. D-A-C-B
    4. B-A-D-C
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (2)

    $\textit{Homo habilis}$ (about 2 million years ago) was followed by $\textit{Homo erectus}$ (about 1.5 million years ago), then Neanderthal man (about 1,00,000–40,000 years ago) and finally $\textit{Homo sapiens}$. So A-D-C-B.

  9. NEET (UG) 2024, 5 May, Code T1 · Question 167
    The flippers of the Penguins and Dolphins are the example of the

    1. Convergent evolution
    2. Divergent evolution
    3. Adaptive radiation
    4. Natural selection
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (1)

    Penguins (birds) and dolphins (mammals) are not closely related, yet their flippers look alike and do the same job because both live in water. Such analogous organs arise by convergent evolution.

  10. NEET (UG) 2024, 5 May, Code T1 · Question 182
    Which one of the following factors will not affect the Hardy-Weinberg equilibrium?

    1. Gene migration
    2. Constant gene pool
    3. Genetic recombination
    4. Genetic drift
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (2)

    Hardy-Weinberg equilibrium means that allele frequencies stay constant, i.e. the gene pool remains constant. It is disturbed by gene migration (gene flow), genetic drift, mutation, genetic recombination and natural selection. A constant gene pool is the equilibrium itself, not a factor that disturbs it.

  11. NEET (UG) 2024, 5 May, Code T1 · Question 195
    Match List I with List II :

    List IList II
    A.Mesozoic EraI.Lower invertebrates
    B.Proterozoic EraII.Fish & Amphibia
    C.Cenozoic EraIII.Birds & Reptiles
    D.Paleozoic EraIV.Mammals
    Choose the correct answer from the options given below :

    1. A-I, B-II, C-IV, D-III
    2. A-III, B-I, C-IV, D-II
    3. A-II, B-I, C-III, D-IV
    4. A-III, B-I, C-II, D-IV
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (2)

    The Proterozoic era saw the lower invertebrates (B-I). The Palaeozoic era was dominated by fishes and amphibians (D-II). The Mesozoic era was the age of reptiles, when birds also arose (A-III). The Cenozoic era is the age of mammals (C-IV).

  12. NEET (UG) 2023, 7 May, Code E1 · Question 171
    Select the correct group/set of Australian Marsupials exhibiting adaptive radiation.

    1. Tasmanian wolf, Bobcat, Marsupial mole
    2. Numbat, Spotted cuscus, Flying phalanger
    3. Mole, Flying squirrel, Tasmanian tiger cat
    4. Lemur, Anteater, Wolf
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (2)

    The numbat (banded anteater), spotted cuscus and flying phalanger are all Australian marsupials that radiated from a common ancestor. Each of the other options contains placental mammals (bobcat, mole, flying squirrel, lemur, anteater, wolf).
