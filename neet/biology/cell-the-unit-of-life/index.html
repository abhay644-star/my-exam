  1. NEET (UG) 2026, 21 June (re-exam), Code 50 · Question 93
    Cell theory was formulated by _____________.

    1. Schleiden and Schwann
    2. Robert Brown
    3. Singer and Nicolson
    4. Antonie Von Leeuwenhoek
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (1)

    Matthias Schleiden (botanist, 1838) and Theodor Schwann (zoologist, 1839) together proposed the cell theory. Robert Brown discovered the nucleus, Singer and Nicolson gave the fluid mosaic model, and Leeuwenhoek first saw live cells.

  2. NEET (UG) 2026, 21 June (re-exam), Code 50 · Question 94
    Which of the following are characteristics of prokaryotic cells?
    (a) Ribosomes are made of 50S and 30S subunits
    (b) They can have plasmids
    (c) They contain mesosome
    (d) They have peroxisomes
    Choose the correct answer from the options given below :

    1. (b) and (c) only
    2. (a) and (c) only
    3. (a), (c) and (d) only
    4. (a), (b) and (c) only
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (4)

    Prokaryotes have 70S ribosomes made of 50S and 30S subunits, so (a) is correct. Many bacteria carry plasmids, so (b) is correct. Mesosomes are infoldings of the plasma membrane, so (c) is correct. Peroxisomes are membrane-bound organelles, which prokaryotes do not have, so (d) is wrong. The answer is (a), (b) and (c).

  3. NEET (UG) 2026, 21 June (re-exam), Code 50 · Question 96
    Mitochondrial inner membrane encloses ___________ .

    1. matrix
    2. cytosol
    3. mucus
    4. aqueous humor
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (1)

    A mitochondrion has two compartments. The space inside the inner membrane is the matrix, which holds enzymes, circular DNA and 70S ribosomes. The outer compartment lies between the two membranes.

  4. NEET (UG) 2026, 21 June (re-exam), Code 50 · Question 97
    Match List-I with List-II.

    List-IList-II
    A.CristaeI.Flat membrane sacs in stroma of chloroplast
    B.CisternaeII.Infoldings in mitochondria
    C.ThylakoidsIII.Cell membrane
    D.PhospholipidIV.Disc shaped sacs in the Golgi apparatus
    Choose the correct answer from the options given below:

    1. A-III, B-IV, C-I, D-II
    2. A-II, B-IV, C-I, D-III
    3. A-II, B-IV, C-III, D-I
    4. A-IV, B-III, C-I, D-II
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (2)

    Cristae are infoldings of the inner mitochondrial membrane (A-II). Cisternae are the flat, disc-shaped sacs of the Golgi apparatus (B-IV). Thylakoids are flattened membrane sacs in the chloroplast stroma (C-I). Phospholipids form the bilayer of the cell membrane (D-III).

  5. NEET (UG) 2026, 21 June (re-exam), Code 50 · Question 98
    The plastid that stores xanthophyll is known as __________.

    1. chloroplast
    2. chromoplast
    3. aleuroplast
    4. amyloplast
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (2)

    Chromoplasts hold fat-soluble carotenoid pigments such as carotene and xanthophyll, which give yellow, orange or red colours. Amyloplasts store starch, aleuroplasts store proteins, and chloroplasts contain mainly chlorophyll.

  6. NEET (UG) 2026, 21 June (re-exam), Code 50 · Question 108
    Endomembrane system includes _______.

    1. endoplasmic reticulum, Golgi complex, lysosomes and vacuole
    2. endoplasmic reticulum, chloroplast, peroxisomes and vacuole
    3. mitochondria, chloroplast, peroxisomes and vacuole
    4. Golgi complex, chloroplast, peroxisomes and vacuole
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (1)

    The ER, Golgi complex, lysosomes and vacuoles work together, so they make up the endomembrane system. Mitochondria, chloroplasts and peroxisomes work independently and are not part of it.

  7. NEET (UG) 2026, 21 June (re-exam), Code 50 · Question 123
    Smooth endoplasmic reticulum ___________ .

    1. has ribosomes attached to its surface
    2. is the major site for the synthesis of lipids
    3. is actively involved in protein synthesis
    4. is a site for the synthesis of carbohydrates
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (2)

    SER has no ribosomes on its surface. It is the main site for making lipids, including lipid-like steroidal hormones in animal cells. Protein synthesis takes place on RER, which has ribosomes.

  8. NEET (UG) 2025, 4 May, Code 45 · Question 98
    From the statements given below choose the $\textbf{correct}$ option :
    A. The eukaryotic ribosomes are 80S and prokaryotic ribosomes are 70S.
    B. Each ribosome has two sub-units.
    C. The two sub-units of 80S ribosome are 60S and 40S while that of 70S are 50S and 30S.
    D. The two sub-units of 80S ribosome are 60S and 20S and that of 70S are 50S and 20S.
    E. The two sub-units of 80S are 60S and 30S and that of 70S are 50S and 30S.

    1. A, B, C are true
    2. A, B, D are true
    3. A, B, E are true
    4. B, D, E are true
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (1)

    Eukaryotic (cytoplasmic) ribosomes are 80S and prokaryotic ribosomes are 70S, and each has a large and a small sub-unit.
    80S = 60S + 40S and 70S = 50S + 30S (S values are not additive). So A, B and C are true; D and E give wrong sub-units.

  9. NEET (UG) 2025, 4 May, Code 45 · Question 121
    Match $\textbf{List - I}$ with $\textbf{List - II}$.

    List - IList - II
    A.CentromereI.Mitochondrion
    B.CiliumII.Cell division
    C.CristaeIII.Cell movement
    D.Cell membraneIV.Phospholipid Bilayer
    Choose the $\textbf{correct}$ answer from the options given below :

    1. A-I, B-II, C-III, D-IV
    2. A-II, B-I, C-IV, D-III
    3. A-IV, B-II, C-III, D-I
    4. A-II, B-III, C-I, D-IV
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (4)

    The centromere holds sister chromatids and bears kinetochores for spindle attachment in cell division; cilia bring about cell movement; cristae are infoldings of the inner mitochondrial membrane; the cell membrane is a phospholipid bilayer.
    So A-II, B-III, C-I, D-IV.

  10. NEET (UG) 2025, 4 May, Code 45 · Question 128
    A specialised membranous structure in a prokaryotic cell which helps in cell wall formation, DNA replication and respiration is :

    1. Mesosome
    2. Chromatophores
    3. Cristae
    4. Endoplasmic Reticulum
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (1)

    Mesosomes are infoldings of the plasma membrane in prokaryotes that help in cell wall formation, DNA replication and its distribution to daughter cells, respiration and secretion. Chromatophores hold pigments; cristae and ER are found in eukaryotic cells.

  11. NEET (UG) 2025, 4 May, Code 45 · Question 140
    Given below are two statements : one is labelled as $\textbf{Assertion (A)}$ and the other is labelled as $\textbf{Reason (R)}$.
    $\textbf{Assertion (A) :}$ The primary function of the Golgi apparatus is to package the materials made by the endoplasmic reticulum and deliver it to intracellular targets and outside the cell.
    $\textbf{Reason (R) :}$ Vesicles containing materials made by the endoplasmic reticulum fuse with the cis face of the Golgi apparatus, and they are modified and released from the trans face of the Golgi apparatus.
    In the light of the above statements, choose the $\textbf{correct}$ answer from the options given below :

    1. Both $\textbf{A}$ and $\textbf{R}$ are true and $\textbf{R}$ is the correct explanation of $\textbf{A}$
    2. Both $\textbf{A}$ and $\textbf{R}$ are true but $\textbf{R}$ is $\textbf{not}$ the correct explanation of $\textbf{A}$
    3. $\textbf{A}$ is true but $\textbf{R}$ is false
    4. $\textbf{A}$ is false but $\textbf{R}$ is true
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (1)

    The Golgi apparatus mainly packages materials made by the ER for delivery to intracellular targets or secretion, so A is true.
    ER vesicles fuse with the cis (forming) face; the materials are modified as they pass through and leave from the trans (maturing) face. This route is how the packaging is done, so R is true and explains A.

  12. NEET (UG) 2024, 5 May, Code T1 · Question 112
    Match List I with List II

    List IList II
    A.NucleolusI.Site of formation of glycolipid
    B.CentrioleII.Organization like the cartwheel
    C.LeucoplastsIII.Site for active ribosomal RNA synthesis
    D.Golgi apparatusIV.For storing nutrients
    Choose the correct answer from the options given below:

    1. A-III, B-IV, C-II, D-I
    2. A-I, B-II, C-III, D-IV
    3. A-III, B-II, C-IV, D-I
    4. A-II, B-III, C-I, D-IV
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (3)

    The nucleolus is the site of active ribosomal RNA synthesis (A-III). A centriole has a cartwheel-like organisation of nine peripheral triplet fibrils (B-II). Leucoplasts are colourless plastids that store nutrients such as starch, oils and proteins (C-IV). The Golgi apparatus is the site of formation of glycoproteins and glycolipids (D-I).

  13. NEET (UG) 2024, 5 May, Code T1 · Question 137
    The DNA present in chloroplast is:

    1. Linear, single stranded
    2. Circular, single stranded
    3. Linear, double stranded
    4. Circular, double stranded
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (4)

    The stroma of the chloroplast contains small, circular, double-stranded DNA molecules and 70S ribosomes, like those of prokaryotes.

  14. NEET (UG) 2024, 5 May, Code T1 · Question 169
    Match List I with List II :

    List IList II
    A.AxonemeI.Centriole
    B.Cartwheel patternII.Cilia and flagella
    C.CristaIII.Chromosome
    D.SatelliteIV.Mitochondria
    Choose the correct answer from the options given below :

    1. A-II, B-IV, C-I, D-III
    2. A-II, B-I, C-IV, D-III
    3. A-IV, B-III, C-II, D-I
    4. A-IV, B-II, C-III, D-I
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (2)

    The axoneme is the core of microtubules in cilia and flagella (A-II). The centriole has a cartwheel arrangement of nine peripheral triplet fibrils (B-I). Cristae are infoldings of the inner membrane of mitochondria (C-IV). A satellite is a small piece at the end of some chromosomes, beyond a secondary constriction (D-III).

  15. NEET (UG) 2024, 5 May, Code T1 · Question 188
    Given below are two statements :
    $\textbf{Statement I :}$ Mitochondria and chloroplasts are both double membrane bound organelles.
    $\textbf{Statement II :}$ Inner membrane of mitochondria is relatively less permeable, as compared to chloroplast.
    In the light of the above statements, choose the most appropriate answer from the options given below :

    1. Statement I is correct but Statement II is incorrect.
    2. Statement I is incorrect but Statement II is correct.
    3. Both Statement I and Statement II are correct.
    4. Both Statement I and Statement II are incorrect.
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (1)

    Statement I is correct: both organelles are bounded by two membranes.
    Statement II is taken as incorrect: NCERT describes the inner membrane of the chloroplast as the one that is relatively less permeable, while the inner mitochondrial membrane carries many transport proteins for ions and metabolites. So the comparison in Statement II is the wrong way round.

    Note: The comparison in Statement II is not stated directly in NCERT; the key follows the NCERT description of the chloroplast’s inner membrane as relatively less permeable.

  16. NEET (UG) 2023, 7 May, Code E1 · Question 103
    Movement and accumulation of ions across a membrane against their concentration gradient can be explained by

    1. Osmosis
    2. Facilitated Diffusion
    3. Passive Transport
    4. Active Transport
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (4)

    Osmosis, facilitated diffusion and other passive transport only move substances down a concentration (or water potential) gradient.
    Moving and accumulating ions against their concentration gradient needs energy (ATP) and carrier pumps, i.e. active transport.

  17. NEET (UG) 2023, 7 May, Code E1 · Question 159
    Which of the following are NOT considered as the part of endomembrane system?
    A. Mitochondria
    B. Endoplasmic Reticulum
    C. Chloroplasts
    D. Golgi complex
    E. Peroxisomes
    Choose the $\textbf{most appropriate}$ answer from the options given below:

    1. B and D only
    2. A, C and E only
    3. A and D only
    4. A, D and E only
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (2)

    The endomembrane system is the ER, Golgi complex, lysosomes and vacuoles, whose functions are coordinated. Mitochondria, chloroplasts and peroxisomes are not part of it. So A, C and E only.

  18. NEET (UG) 2023, 7 May, Code E1 · Question 179
    Which of the following functions is carried out by cytoskeleton in a cell?

    1. Nuclear division
    2. Protein synthesis
    3. Motility
    4. Transportation
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (3)

    The cytoskeleton (microtubules, microfilaments and intermediate filaments) gives mechanical support, helps in motility and maintains the shape of the cell. Of the options given, motility is the function NCERT lists for it.

  19. NEET (UG) 2022, 17 July, Code Q1 · Question 140
    Match List-I with List-II.

    List-IList-II
    (a)Metacentric chromosome(i)Centromere situated close to the end forming one extremely short and one very long arms
    (b)Acrocentric chromosome(ii)Centromere at the terminal end
    (c)Submetacentric(iii)Centromere in the middle forming two equal arms of chromosomes
    (d)Telocentric chromosome(iv)Centromere slightly away from the middle forming one shorter arm and one longer arm
    Choose the $\textbf{correct answer}$ from the options given below :

    1. (a)-(iii), (b)-(i), (c)-(iv), (d)-(ii)
    2. (a)-(i), (b)-(iii), (c)-(ii), (d)-(iv)
    3. (a)-(ii), (b)-(iii), (c)-(iv), (d)-(i)

  20. NEET (UG) 2022, 17 July, Code Q1 · Question 172
    Which of the following statements with respect to Endoplasmic Reticulum is incorrect?

    1. RER has ribosomes attached to ER
    2. SER is devoid of ribosomes
    3. In prokaryotes only RER are present
    4. SER are the sites for lipid synthesis
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (3)

    Prokaryotes have no membrane-bound organelles, so they have no endoplasmic reticulum at all. Statement (3) is incorrect.
