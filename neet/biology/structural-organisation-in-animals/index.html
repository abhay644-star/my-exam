  1. NEET (UG) 2026, 21 June (re-exam), Code 50 · Question 103
    In water, frogs respire using _____________.

    1. skin
    2. buccal cavity
    3. lungs
    4. trachea
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (1)

    When a frog is in water, gas exchange takes place through its moist, highly vascular skin (cutaneous respiration). On land it uses the buccal cavity, skin and lungs.

  2. NEET (UG) 2026, 21 June (re-exam), Code 50 · Question 135
    In frogs, the number of pairs of cranial nerves arising from the brain are ______.

    1. 6
    2. 9
    3. 10
    4. 12
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (3)

    Frogs (like other anamniotes) have 10 pairs of cranial nerves arising from the brain. Humans and other amniotes have 12 pairs.

  3. NEET (UG) 2025, 4 May, Code 45 · Question 94
    What is the name of the blood vessel that carries deoxygenated blood from the body to the heart in a frog ?

    1. Aorta
    2. Pulmonary artery
    3. Pulmonary vein
    4. Vena cava
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (4)

    In the frog, the venae cavae bring deoxygenated blood from the body to the sinus venosus and right atrium. The aorta carries blood away from the heart, the pulmonary artery carries blood to the lungs and the pulmonary vein returns oxygenated blood from the lungs.

  4. NEET (UG) 2025, 4 May, Code 45 · Question 107
    Which of the following statement is $\textbf{correct}$ about location of the male frog copulatory pad ?

    1. First and Second digit of fore limb
    2. First digit of hind limb
    3. Second digit of fore limb
    4. First digit of the fore limb
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (4)

    Male frogs have a copulatory (nuptial) pad on the first digit of each fore limb, which helps them grip the female during amplexus. Females lack it.

  5. NEET (UG) 2025, 4 May, Code 45 · Question 169
    In frog, the Renal portal system is a special venous connection that acts to link :

    1. Liver and intestine
    2. Liver and kidney
    3. Kidney and intestine
    4. Kidney and lower part of body
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (4)

    Frogs have a hepatic portal system (between the intestine and liver) and a renal portal system, a special venous connection between the kidney and the lower parts of the body.

  6. NEET (UG) 2025, 4 May, Code 45 · Question 175
    Frogs respire in water by skin and buccal cavity and on land by skin, buccal cavity and lungs.
    Choose the $\textbf{correct}$ answer from the following :

    1. The statement is true for water but false for land
    2. The statement is true for both the environment
    3. The statement is false for water but true for land
    4. The statement is false for both the environment
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (3)

    In water a frog respires through its skin only (cutaneous respiration, by diffusion). On land the buccal cavity, skin and lungs are all respiratory organs.
    So the statement is false for water but true for land.

  7. NEET (UG) 2024, 5 May, Code T1 · Question 180
    In both sexes of cockroach, a pair of jointed filamentous structures called anal cerci are present on :

    1. $8^{\text{th}}$ and $9^{\text{th}}$ segment
    2. $11^{\text{th}}$ segment
    3. $5^{\text{th}}$ segment
    4. $10^{\text{th}}$ segment
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (4)

    In both male and female cockroaches the $10^{\text{th}}$ abdominal segment bears a pair of jointed filamentous anal cerci. (Only males also have a pair of short anal styles on the $9^{\text{th}}$ segment.)

  8. NEET (UG) 2024, 5 May, Code T1 · Question 192
    Match List I with List II :

    List IList II
    A.Unicellular glandular epitheliumI.Salivary glands
    B.Compound epitheliumII.Pancreas
    C.Multicellular glandular epitheliumIII.Goblet cells of alimentary canal
    D.Endocrine glandular epitheliumIV.Moist surface of buccal cavity
    Choose the correct answer from the options given below :

    1. A-III, B-IV, C-I, D-II
    2. A-II, B-I, C-IV, D-III
    3. A-II, B-I, C-III, D-IV
    4. A-IV, B-III, C-I, D-II
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (1)

    Goblet cells of the alimentary canal are unicellular glands (A-III). Compound (stratified) epithelium covers the moist surface of the buccal cavity (B-IV). Salivary glands are multicellular glands (C-I). The pancreas has endocrine tissue, the islets of Langerhans (D-II).

  9. NEET (UG) 2024, 5 May, Code T1 · Question 200
    Match List I with List II related to digestive system of cockroach.

    List IList II
    A.The structures used for storing of food.I.Gizzard
    B.Ring of 6-8 blind tubules at junction of foregut and midgut.II.Gastric Caeca
    C.Ring of 100-150 yellow coloured thin filaments at junction of midgut and hindgut.III.Malpighian tubules
    D.The structures used for grinding the food.IV.Crop
    Choose the correct answer from the options given below :

    1. A-IV, B-III, C-II, D-I
    2. A-III, B-II, C-IV, D-I
    3. A-IV, B-II, C-III, D-I
    4. A-I, B-II, C-III, D-IV
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (3)

    In the cockroach the crop stores food (A-IV). A ring of 6–8 blind gastric caeca lies at the junction of the foregut and midgut (B-II). A ring of 100–150 yellow Malpighian tubules lies at the junction of the midgut and hindgut (C-III). The gizzard (proventriculus), with chitinous teeth, grinds the food (D-I).

  10. NEET (UG) 2023, 7 May, Code E1 · Question 168
    Given below are two statements:
    $\textbf{Statement I:}$ Ligaments are dense irregular tissue.
    $\textbf{Statement II:}$ Cartilage is dense regular tissue.
    In the light of the above statements, choose the $\textbf{correct}$ answer from the options given below:

    1. Both Statement I and Statement II are true.
    2. Both Statement I and Statement II are false.
    3. Statement I is true but Statement II is false.
    4. Statement I is false but Statement II is true.
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (2)

    Ligaments (and tendons) are dense regular connective tissue, with collagen fibres in parallel bundles, so Statement I is false. Cartilage is a specialised connective tissue, not dense regular tissue, so Statement II is false.

  11. NEET (UG) 2023, 7 May, Code E1 · Question 192
    Match List I with List II.

    List IList II
    A.Mast cellsI.Ciliated epithelium
    B.Inner surface of bronchioleII.Areolar connective tissue
    C.BloodIII.Cuboidal epithelium
    D.Tubular parts of nephronIV.specialised connective tissue
    Choose the $\textbf{correct}$ answer from the options give below:

    1. A-I, B-II, C-IV, D-III
    2. A-II, B-III, C-I, D-IV
    3. A-II, B-I, C-IV, D-III
    4. A-III, B-IV, C-II, D-I
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (3)

    Mast cells are found in areolar connective tissue (A-II). The inner surface of the bronchioles is lined by ciliated epithelium (B-I). Blood is a specialised (fluid) connective tissue (C-IV). The tubular parts of the nephron are lined by cuboidal epithelium (D-III).

  12. NEET (UG) 2023, 7 May, Code E1 · Question 193
    Which of the following is characteristic feature of cockroach regarding sexual dimorphism ?

    1. Dark brown body colour and anal cerci
    2. Presence of anal styles
    3. Presence of sclerites
    4. Presence of anal cerci
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (2)

    Both sexes of cockroach have a brown body, sclerites and a pair of anal cerci. Only the male has a pair of short, thread-like anal styles on the 9th sternum, so anal styles show sexual dimorphism.

  13. NEET (UG) 2023, 7 May, Code E1 · Question 195
    In cockroach, excretion is brought about by-
    A. Phallic gland
    B. Urecose gland
    C. Nephrocytes
    D. Fat body
    E. Collaterial glands
    Choose the $\textbf{correct}$ answer from the options given below:

    1. A and E only
    2. A, B and E only
    3. B, C and D only
    4. B and D only
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (3)

    Excretion in the cockroach is mainly by Malpighian tubules, helped by the fat body, nephrocytes and uricose glands. The phallic gland (male) and the collaterial glands (female) are reproductive glands. So B, C and D only.

  14. NEET (UG) 2022, 17 July, Code Q1 · Question 162
    Which of the following is $\textbf{not}$ a connective tissue?

    1. Blood
    2. Adipose tissue
    3. Cartilage
    4. Neuroglia
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (4)

    Neuroglia are supporting cells of neural tissue. Blood, adipose tissue and cartilage are connective tissues.

  15. NEET (UG) 2022, 17 July, Code Q1 · Question 173
    Which of the following is present between the adjacent bones of the vertebral column?

    1. Intercalated discs
    2. Cartilage
    3. Areolar tissue
    4. Smooth muscle
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (2)

    Cartilage (the intervertebral discs) lies between adjacent vertebrae. Intercalated discs are junctions between cardiac muscle cells.

  16. NEET (UG) 2022, 17 July, Code Q1 · Question 174
    Tegmina in cockroach, arises from

    1. Prothorax
    2. Mesothorax
    3. Metathorax
    4. Prothorax and Mesothorax
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (2)

    The first pair of wings (tegmina, the leathery forewings) arises from the mesothorax; the second pair arises from the metathorax.

  17. NEET (UG) 2022, 17 July, Code Q1 · Question 197
    Match $\textbf{List-I}$ with $\textbf{List-II}$

    List-IList-II
    (a)Bronchioles(i)Dense Regular Connective Tissue
    (b)Goblet Cell(ii)Loose Connective Tissue
    (c)Tendons(iii)Glandular Tissue
    (d)Adipose Tissue(iv)Ciliated Epithelium
    Choose the $\textbf{correct answer}$ from the options given below:

    1. (a) - (iv), (b) - (iii), (c) - (i), (d) - (ii)
    2. (a) - (i), (b) - (ii), (c) - (iii), (d) - (iv)
    3. (a) - (ii), (b) - (i), (c) - (iv), (d) - (iii)
    4. (a) - (iii), (b) - (iv), (c) - (ii), (d) - (i)
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (1)

    Bronchioles are lined by ciliated epithelium; a goblet cell is a unicellular gland (glandular tissue); tendons are dense regular connective tissue; adipose tissue is a loose connective tissue.
    So (a)-(iv), (b)-(iii), (c)-(i), (d)-(ii).
