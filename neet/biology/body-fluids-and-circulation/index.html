  1. NEET (UG) 2026, 21 June (re-exam), Code 50 · Question 115
    The number of action potentials generated by sino-atrial node (SAN) in a healthy human is _______ per minute.

    1. 28 - 30
    2. 70 – 75
    3. 100 - 110
    4. 120 - 140
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (2)

    The SA node is the pacemaker of the heart. It generates about 70–75 action potentials per minute, which sets the normal heart rate of about 72 beats per minute.

  2. NEET (UG) 2026, 21 June (re-exam), Code 50 · Question 117
    Which of the following statements is incorrect?

    1. Blood coagulates in response to an injury
    2. Blood clot consists of fibrins
    3. Fibrin is produced from fibrinogen
    4. Fibrinogen is produced from fibrin
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (4)

    When there is an injury, thrombin converts soluble fibrinogen into insoluble fibrin threads. These threads form the network of the clot. The reverse, fibrinogen made from fibrin, is incorrect.

  3. NEET (UG) 2026, 21 June (re-exam), Code 50 · Question 149
    The opening between the right atrium and the right ventricle is guarded by ________.

    1. bicuspid valve
    2. tricuspid valve
    3. semilunar valve
    4. sino-atrial node
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (2)

    The right atrioventricular opening is guarded by the tricuspid valve (three flaps). The bicuspid (mitral) valve guards the left atrioventricular opening, and semilunar valves guard the exits into the pulmonary artery and aorta.

  4. NEET (UG) 2025, 4 May, Code 45 · Question 112
    Cardiac activities of the heart are regulated by :
    A. Nodal tissue
    B. A special neural centre in the medulla oblongata
    C. Adrenal medullary hormones
    D. Adrenal cortical hormones
    Choose the $\textbf{correct}$ answer from the options given below :

    1. A, B and C Only
    2. A, B, C and D
    3. A, C and D Only
    4. A, B and D Only
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (1)

    The heart is auto-regulated by its nodal tissue (SA and AV nodes), and its activity can be moderated by a special neural centre in the medulla oblongata through the autonomic nervous system, and by adrenal medullary hormones (adrenaline, noradrenaline). Adrenal cortical hormones do not regulate cardiac activity. Hence A, B and C only.

  5. NEET (UG) 2024, 5 May, Code T1 · Question 176
    Following are the stages of pathway for conduction of an action potential through the heart:
    A. AV bundle
    B. Purkinje fibres
    C. AV node
    D. Bundle branches
    E. SA node
    Choose the correct sequence of pathway from the options given below :

    1. B-D-E-C-A
    2. E-A-D-B-C
    3. E-C-A-D-B
    4. A-E-C-B-D
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (3)

    The impulse starts at the SA node (pacemaker), passes to the AV node, then down the AV bundle (bundle of His), which divides into right and left bundle branches, and finally spreads through the Purkinje fibres in the ventricular wall. So E-C-A-D-B.

  6. NEET (UG) 2024, 5 May, Code T1 · Question 190
    Match List I with List II :

    List IList II
    A.P waveI.Heart muscles are electrically silent.
    B.QRS complexII.Depolarisation of ventricles.
    C.T waveIII.Depolarisation of atria.
    D.T-P gapIV.Repolarisation of ventricles.
    Choose the correct answer from the options given below :

    1. A-II, B-III, C-I, D-IV
    2. A-IV, B-II, C-I, D-III
    3. A-I, B-III, C-IV, D-II
    4. A-III, B-II, C-IV, D-I
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (4)

    In an ECG, the P peak marks depolarisation of the atria (A-III), the QRS complex marks depolarisation of the ventricles (B-II), and the T peak marks the return of the ventricles to normal, i.e. repolarisation (C-IV). In the T-P gap the heart muscle is electrically silent (D-I).
