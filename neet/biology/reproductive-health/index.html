  1. NEET (UG) 2026, 21 June (re-exam), Code 50 · Question 139
    Which of the following disease is not sexually transmitted?

    1. Syphilis
    2. Tuberculosis
    3. Gonorrhoea
    4. Genital warts
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (2)

    Syphilis, gonorrhoea and genital warts are sexually transmitted infections. Tuberculosis, caused by Mycobacterium tuberculosis, spreads mainly through airborne droplets and is not an STI.

  2. NEET (UG) 2026, 21 June (re-exam), Code 50 · Question 142
    The method of directly of injecting a sperm into ovum in assisted reproductive technology is called :

    1. Gamete intra fallopian transfer (GIFT)
    2. Zygote intra fallopian transfer (ZIFT)
    3. Intra cytoplasmic sperm injection (ICSI)
    4. Embryo transfer (ET)
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (3)

    In ICSI (intra cytoplasmic sperm injection) a sperm is injected directly into the cytoplasm of the ovum to form an embryo in the laboratory. GIFT transfers gametes and ZIFT transfers a zygote into the fallopian tube.

  3. NEET (UG) 2025, 4 May, Code 45 · Question 93
    What are the potential drawbacks in adoption of the IVF method?
    A. High fatality risk to mother
    B. Expensive instruments and reagents
    C. Husband/wife necessary for being donors
    D. Less adoption of orphans
    E. Not available in India
    F. Possibility that the early embryo does not survive
    Choose the correct answer from the options given below :

    1. B, D, F only
    2. A, C, D, F only
    3. A, B, C, D only
    4. A, B, C, E, F only
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (1)

    IVF needs expensive equipment and reagents (B), may discourage the adoption of orphans (D), and the early embryo may fail to survive after transfer (F).
    It carries no high fatality risk to the mother (A), donors need not be the husband or wife (C), and it is available in India (E). Hence B, D, F only.

  4. NEET (UG) 2024, 5 May, Code T1 · Question 175
    Which of the following is not a natural/traditional contraceptive method?

    1. Lactational amenorrhea
    2. Vaults
    3. Coitus interruptus
    4. Periodic abstinence
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (2)

    Natural methods avoid the meeting of ovum and sperm without any device: periodic abstinence, withdrawal (coitus interruptus) and lactational amenorrhoea. Vaults are barrier methods, rubber caps placed over the cervix.

  5. NEET (UG) 2024, 5 May, Code T1 · Question 184
    Match List I with List II :

    List IList II
    A.Non-medicated IUDI.Multiload 375
    B.Copper releasing IUDII.Progestogens
    C.Hormone releasing IUDIII.Lippes loop
    D.ImplantsIV.LNG-20
    Choose the correct answer from the options given below :

    1. A-IV, B-I, C-II, D-III
    2. A-III, B-I, C-IV, D-II
    3. A-III, B-I, C-II, D-IV
    4. A-I, B-III, C-IV, D-II
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (2)

    Lippes loop is a non-medicated IUD (A-III). Multiload 375 is a copper releasing IUD (B-I). LNG-20 is a hormone releasing IUD (C-IV). Implants placed under the skin contain progestogens or progestogen–oestrogen combinations (D-II).

  6. NEET (UG) 2023, 7 May, Code E1 · Question 151
    Match List I with List II.

    List IList II
    A.VasectomyI.Oral method
    B.Coitus interruptusII.Barrier method
    C.Cervical capsIII.Surgical method
    D.SaheliIV.Natural method
    Choose the $\textbf{correct}$ answer from the options given below:

    1. A-III, B-I, C-IV, D-II
    2. A-III, B-IV, C-II, D-I
    3. A-II, B-III, C-I, D-IV
    4. A-IV, B-II, C-I, D-III
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (2)

    Vasectomy (cutting and tying the vas deferens) is a surgical method (A-III). Coitus interruptus (withdrawal) is a natural method (B-IV). Cervical caps are barriers that stop sperm reaching the uterus (C-II). Saheli is the once-a-week oral contraceptive pill developed at CDRI, Lucknow (D-I).

  7. NEET (UG) 2023, 7 May, Code E1 · Question 155
    Which one of the following common sexually transmitted diseases is completely curable when detected early and treated properly?

    1. Genital herpes
    2. Gonorrhoea
    3. Hepatitis-B
    4. HIV Infection
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (2)

    Except for hepatitis-B, genital herpes and HIV infection, the common STIs are completely curable if detected early and treated properly. Gonorrhoea is a bacterial infection and is cured with antibiotics.

  8. NEET (UG) 2023, 7 May, Code E1 · Question 175
    Given below are two statements: one is labelled as $\textbf{Assertion A}$ and the other is labelled as $\textbf{Reason R.}$
    $\textbf{Assertion A:}$ Amniocentesis for sex determination is one of the strategies of Reproductive and Child Health Care Programme.
    $\textbf{Reason R:}$ Ban on amniocentesis checks increasing menace of female foeticide.
    In the light of the above statements, choose the $\textbf{correct}$ answer from the options given below:

    1. Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation of A.
    2. Both A and R are true and R is NOT the correct explanation of A.
    3. A is true but R is false.
    4. A is false but R is true.
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (4)

    A is false: amniocentesis for sex determination is banned by law, and the statutory ban is one of the RCH strategies; sex determination itself is not.
    R is true: the ban is meant to check the growing problem of female foeticide.

  9. NEET (UG) 2022, 17 July, Code Q1 · Question 159
    Lippe’s loop is a type of contraceptive used as:

    1. Cervical barrier
    2. Vault barrier
    3. Non-Medicated IUD
    4. Copper releasing IUD
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (3)

    The Lippes loop is a non-medicated intra-uterine device. Copper-releasing IUDs include CuT, Cu7 and Multiload 375.

  10. NEET (UG) 2022, 17 July, Code Q1 · Question 198
    Match $\textbf{List-I}$ with $\textbf{List-II}$ with respect to methods of Contraception and their respective actions.

    List-IList-II
    (a)Diaphragms(i)Inhibit ovulation and Implantation
    (b)Contraceptive Pills(ii)Increase phagocytosis of sperm within Uterus
    (c)Intra Uterine Devices(iii)Absence of Menstrual cycle and ovulation following parturition
    (d)Lactational Amenorrhea(iv)They cover the cervix blocking the entry of sperms
    Choose the $\textbf{correct answer}$ from the options given below:

    1. (a) - (iv), (b) - (i), (c) - (iii), (d) - (ii)
    2. (a) - (iv), (b) - (i), (c) - (ii), (d) - (iii)
    3. (a) - (ii), (b) - (iv), (c) - (i), (d) - (iii)
    4. (a) - (iii), (b) - (ii), (c) - (i), (d) - (iv)
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (2)

    Diaphragms cover the cervix; pills inhibit ovulation and implantation; IUDs increase phagocytosis of sperms in the uterus; lactational amenorrhoea is the absence of menstruation and ovulation after childbirth.
    So (a)-(iv), (b)-(i), (c)-(ii), (d)-(iii).
