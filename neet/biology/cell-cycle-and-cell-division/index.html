  1. NEET (UG) 2026, 21 June (re-exam), Code 50 · Question 128
    The correct sequence of adult cell cycle phases is ________.

    1. G1-G2-S-M
    2. G1-M-G2-S
    3. G1-S-G2-M
    4. S-M-G2-G1
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (3)

    Interphase comes first: G1 (growth), then S (DNA synthesis), then G2 (preparation for division). It is followed by the M phase (mitosis). So the order is G1–S–G2–M.

  2. NEET (UG) 2026, 21 June (re-exam), Code 50 · Question 131
    Given below are two statements :
    Statement I : Chromosomes are fully condensed at the end of prophase I.
    Statement II : Meiosis I resembles mitosis.
    In the light of the above statements, choose the most appropriate answer from the options given below :

    1. Both Statement I and Statement II are true
    2. Both Statement I and Statement II are false
    3. Statement I is correct, but Statement II is false
    4. Statement I is incorrect, but Statement II is true
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (3)

    By diakinesis, the last stage of prophase I, the chromosomes are fully condensed, so Statement I is correct. Meiosis II resembles mitosis, while meiosis I is reductional, so Statement II is false.

  3. NEET (UG) 2025, 4 May, Code 45 · Question 158
    What is the main function of the spindle fibers during mitosis ?

    1. To separate the chromosomes
    2. To synthesize new DNA
    3. To repair damaged DNA
    4. To regulate cell growth
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (1)

    Spindle fibres attach to the kinetochores of chromosomes and, at anaphase, pull the sister chromatids apart to opposite poles, separating the chromosomes. DNA is synthesised in S phase, not by the spindle.

  4. NEET (UG) 2024, 5 May, Code T1 · Question 114
    Spindle fibers attach to kinetochores of chromosomes during

    1. Anaphase
    2. Telophase
    3. Prophase
    4. Metaphase
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (4)

    Metaphase begins with the attachment of spindle fibres to the kinetochores of the chromosomes, which then line up at the metaphase plate.

  5. NEET (UG) 2024, 5 May, Code T1 · Question 121
    Given below are two statements:
    $\textbf{Statement I :}$ Chromosomes become gradually visible under light microscope during leptotene stage.
    $\textbf{Statement II :}$ The begining of diplotene stage is recognized by dissolution of synaptonemal complex.
    In the light of the above statements, choose the correct answer from the options given below:

    1. Statement I is true but Statement II is false
    2. Statement I is false but Statement II is true
    3. Both Statement I and Statement II are true
    4. Both Statement I and Statement II are false
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (3)

    During leptotene the chromosomes become gradually visible under the light microscope as they condense, so Statement I is true.
    Diplotene begins with the dissolution of the synaptonemal complex, after which the homologues tend to separate except at the chiasmata, so Statement II is also true.

  6. NEET (UG) 2024, 5 May, Code T1 · Question 152
    Match List I with List II :

    List I
    (Sub Phases of Prophase I)    		List II
    (Specific characters)
    A.DiakinesisI.Synaptonemal complex formation
    B.PachyteneII.Completion of terminalisation of chiasmata
    C.ZygoteneIII.Chromosomes look like thin threads
    D.LeptoteneIV.Appearance of recombination nodules
    Choose the correct answer from the options given below :

    1. A-II, B-IV, C-I, D-III
    2. A-IV, B-III, C-II, D-I
    3. A-IV, B-II, C-III, D-I
    4. A-I, B-II, C-IV, D-III
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (1)

    Leptotene: chromosomes condense and look like thin threads (D-III). Zygotene: synapsis, with formation of the synaptonemal complex (C-I). Pachytene: recombination nodules appear, the sites of crossing over (B-IV). Diakinesis: terminalisation of chiasmata is completed (A-II).

  7. NEET (UG) 2024, 5 May, Code T1 · Question 185
    Following are the stages of cell division :
    A. Gap 2 phase
    B. Cytokinesis
    C. Synthesis phase
    D. Karyokinesis
    E. Gap 1 phase
    Choose the correct sequence of stages from the options given below :

    1. B-D-E-A-C
    2. E-C-A-D-B
    3. C-E-D-A-B
    4. E-B-D-A-C
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (2)

    Interphase comes first: $\mathrm{G_1}$ phase, then S (synthesis) phase, then $\mathrm{G_2}$ phase. It is followed by the M phase, in which karyokinesis (division of the nucleus) comes before cytokinesis (division of the cytoplasm). So E-C-A-D-B.

  8. NEET (UG) 2023, 7 May, Code E1 · Question 111
    The process of appearance of recombination nodules occurs at which sub stage of prophase I in meiosis?

    1. Zygotene
    2. Pachytene
    3. Diplotene
    4. Diakinesis
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (2)

    During pachytene the bivalents (tetrads) are clearly seen and recombination nodules appear: these are the sites where crossing over between non-sister chromatids of homologous chromosomes takes place.

  9. NEET (UG) 2023, 7 May, Code E1 · Question 114
    Among eukaryotes, replication of DNA takes place in -

    1. M phase
    2. S phase
    3. $\mathrm{G_1}$ phase
    4. $\mathrm{G_2}$ phase
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (2)

    DNA replication takes place during the S (synthesis) phase of interphase, when the amount of DNA per cell doubles.

  10. NEET (UG) 2023, 7 May, Code E1 · Question 124
    Which of the following stages of meiosis involves division of centromere?

    1. Metaphase I
    2. Metaphase II
    3. Anaphase II
    4. Telophase
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (3)

    In anaphase I the homologous chromosomes separate but the sister chromatids stay joined at their centromeres. In anaphase II the centromere of each chromosome splits, so the sister chromatids move to opposite poles.

  11. NEET (UG) 2023, 7 May, Code E1 · Question 146
    Match List I with List II :

    List IList II
    A.M PhaseI.Proteins are synthesized
    B.$\mathrm{G_2}$ PhaseII.Inactive phase
    C.Quiescent stageIII.Interval between mitosis and initiation of DNA replication
    D.$\mathrm{G_1}$ PhaseIV.Equational division
    Choose the correct answer from the options given below :

    1. A-III, B-II, C-IV, D-I
    2. A-IV, B-II, C-I, D-III
    3. A-IV, B-I, C-II, D-III
    4. A-II, B-IV, C-I, D-III
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (3)

    M phase is the phase of equational division, mitosis (A-IV). In $\mathrm{G_2}$ proteins are synthesised in preparation for mitosis (B-I). The quiescent stage ($\mathrm{G_0}$) is an inactive stage (C-II). $\mathrm{G_1}$ is the interval between mitosis and the start of DNA replication (D-III).

  12. NEET (UG) 2023, 7 May, Code E1 · Question 191
    Select the correct statements.
    A. Tetrad formation is seen during Leptotene.
    B. During Anaphase, the centromeres split and chromatids separate.
    C. Terminalization takes place during Pachytene.
    D. Nucleolus, Golgi complex and ER are reformed during Telophase.
    E. Crossing over takes place between sister chromatids of homologous chromosome.
    Choose the $\textbf{correct}$ answer from the options given below:

    1. A and C only
    2. B and D only
    3. A, C and E only
    4. B and E only
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (2)

    B and D are correct. A is wrong: tetrads (bivalents) become visible in pachytene, after synapsis in zygotene. C is wrong: terminalisation of chiasmata happens in diakinesis. E is wrong: crossing over occurs between non-sister chromatids of homologous chromosomes.

  13. NEET (UG) 2023, 7 May, Code E1 · Question 196
    Given below are two statements:
    $\textbf{Statement I:}$ During $\mathrm{G_0}$ phase of cell cycle, the cell is metabolically inactive.
    $\textbf{Statement II:}$ The centrosome undergoes duplication during S phase of interphase.
    In the light of the above statements, choose the $\textit{most appropriate}$ answer from the options given below:

    1. Both Statement I and Statement II are correct.
    2. Both Statement I and Statement II are incorrect.
    3. Statement I is correct but Statement II is incorrect.
    4. Statement I is incorrect but Statement II is correct.
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (4)

    Statement I is incorrect: cells in the quiescent $\mathrm{G_0}$ stage remain metabolically active but do not proliferate.
    Statement II is correct: in animal cells the centriole (centrosome) duplicates in the cytoplasm during the S phase.

  14. NEET (UG) 2022, 17 July, Code Q1 · Question 109
    Which one of the following never occurs during mitotic cell division?

    1. Spindle fibres attach to kinetochores of chromosomes
    2. Movement of centrioles towards opposite poles
    3. Pairing of homologous chromosomes
    4. Coiling and condensation of the chromatids
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (3)

    Pairing (synapsis) of homologous chromosomes happens only in prophase I of meiosis, never in mitosis.

  15. NEET (UG) 2022, 17 July, Code Q1 · Question 134
    The appearance of recombination nodules on homologous chromosomes during meiosis characterizes :

    1. Synaptonemal complex
    2. Bivalent
    3. Sites at which crossing over occurs
    4. Terminalization
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (3)

    Recombination nodules appear in pachytene and mark the sites where crossing over takes place between non-sister chromatids.

  16. NEET (UG) 2022, 17 July, Code Q1 · Question 164
    Regarding Meiosis, which of the statements is $\textbf{incorrect}$?

    1. There are two stages in Meiosis, Meiosis-I and II
    2. DNA replication occurs in S phase of Meiosis-II
    3. Pairing of homologous chromosomes and recombination occurs in Meiosis-I
    4. Four haploid cells are formed at the end of Meiosis-II
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (2)

    DNA replicates only once, in the S phase of the interphase before meiosis I. There is no S phase before meiosis II, so statement (2) is incorrect.

  17. NEET (UG) 2022, 17 July, Code Q1 · Question 184
    Select the $\textbf{incorrect}$ statement with reference to mitosis:

    1. All the chromosomes lie at the equator at metaphase
    2. Spindle fibres attach to centromere of chromosomes
    3. Chromosomes decondense at telophase
    4. Splitting of centromere occurs at anaphase
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (2)

    Spindle fibres attach to the kinetochores, small disc-shaped structures on the centromere, not to the centromere itself. The key treats (2) as the incorrect statement; the other three are standard facts about mitosis.
