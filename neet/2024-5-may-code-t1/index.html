Solved questions from this paper

A few questions with their official answers and worked solutions. All 200 are in the online test.

  1. Question 4 · Physics · Current Electricity
    A wire of length ‘$l$’ and resistance $100\ \Omega$ is divided into 10 equal parts. The first 5 parts are connected in series while the next 5 parts are connected in parallel. The two combinations are again connected in series. The resistance of this final combination is:

    1. $55\ \Omega$
    2. $60\ \Omega$
    3. $26\ \Omega$
    4. $52\ \Omega$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (4)

    Each part has resistance $100/10 = 10\ \Omega$.
    Five in series: $5 \times 10 = 50\ \Omega$. Five in parallel: $10/5 = 2\ \Omega$.
    In series: $50 + 2 = 52\ \Omega$.

  2. Question 8 · Physics · Gravitation
    The mass of a planet is $\dfrac{1}{10}$th that of the earth and its diameter is half that of the earth. The acceleration due to gravity on that planet is :

    1. $4.9\ \text{m s}^{-2}$
    2. $3.92\ \text{m s}^{-2}$
    3. $19.6\ \text{m s}^{-2}$
    4. $9.8\ \text{m s}^{-2}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (2)

    $g = \dfrac{GM}{R^2}$. With $M' = M/10$ and $R' = R/2$: $g' = \dfrac{G(M/10)}{R^2/4} = \dfrac{4}{10}g = 0.4 \times 9.8 = 3.92\ \text{m s}^{-2}$.

  3. Question 51 · Chemistry · Thermodynamics
    Match List I with List II.

    List I
    (Process)    		List II
    (Conditions)
    A.Isothermal processI.No heat exchange
    B.Isochoric processII.Carried out at constant temperature
    C.Isobaric processIII.Carried out at constant volume
    D.Adiabatic processIV.Carried out at constant pressure
    Choose the correct answer from the options given below:

    1. A-I, B-II, C-III, D-IV
    2. A-II, B-III, C-IV, D-I
    3. A-IV, B-III, C-II, D-I
    4. A-IV, B-II, C-III, D-I
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (2)

    Isothermal: constant temperature (A-II). Isochoric: constant volume (B-III). Isobaric: constant pressure (C-IV). Adiabatic: no heat exchange with the surroundings (D-I).

  4. Question 52 · Chemistry · Hydrocarbons
    Given below are two statements:
    $\textbf{Statement I :}$ The boiling point of three isomeric pentanes follows the order
    n-pentane > isopentane > neopentane
    $\textbf{Statement II :}$ When branching increases, the molecule attains a shape of sphere. This results in smaller surface area for contact, due to which the intermolecular forces between the spherical molecules are weak, thereby lowering the boiling point.
    In the light of the above statements, choose the $\textit{most appropriate}$ answer from the options given below:

    1. Statement I is correct but Statement II is incorrect.
    2. Statement I is incorrect but Statement II is correct.
    3. Both Statement I and Statement II are correct.
    4. Both Statement I and Statement II are incorrect.
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (3)

    Boiling points: n-pentane (309 K) > isopentane (301 K) > neopentane (282.5 K), so Statement I is correct.
    Statement II gives the correct reason: more branching makes the molecule more compact (nearly spherical), reducing the surface area of contact and hence the van der Waals forces, so the boiling point falls.

  5. Question 102 · Botany · Biodiversity and Conservation
    List of endangered species was released by-

    1. FOAM
    2. IUCN
    3. GEAC
    4. WWF
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (2)

    The IUCN (International Union for Conservation of Nature and Natural Resources) publishes the Red List (Red Data Book), which lists threatened and endangered species.

  6. Question 104 · Botany · Principles of Inheritance and Variation
    Which one of the following can be explained on the basis of Mendel's Law of Dominance?
    A. Out of one pair of factors one is dominant and the other is recessive.
    B. Alleles do not show any expression and both the characters appear as such in $F_2$ generation.
    C. Factors occur in pairs in normal diploid plants.
    D. The discrete unit controlling a particular character is called factor.
    E. The expression of only one of the parental characters is found in a monohybrid cross.
    Choose the correct answer from the options given below:

    1. B, C and D only
    2. A, B, C, D and E
    3. A, B and C only
    4. A, C, D and E only
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (4)

    The Law of Dominance states that characters are controlled by discrete units called factors (D), which occur in pairs (C), and that in a dissimilar pair one factor dominates the other (A). This explains why only one parental character is expressed in the $F_1$ of a monohybrid cross (E).
    B is wrong: alleles do express, and the recessive character reappears in $F_2$ because of segregation, not dominance. So A, C, D and E only.

  7. Question 151 · Zoology · Principles of Inheritance and Variation
    Match List I with List II :

    List IList II
    A.Down’s syndromeI.$11^{\text{th}}$ chromosome
    B.$\alpha$-ThalassemiaII.‘X’ chromosome
    C.$\beta$-ThalassemiaIII.$21^{\text{st}}$ chromosome
    D.Klinefelter’s syndromeIV.$16^{\text{th}}$ chromosome
    Choose the correct answer from the options given below :

    1. A-III, B-IV, C-I, D-II
    2. A-IV, B-I, C-II, D-III
    3. A-I, B-II, C-III, D-IV
    4. A-II, B-III, C-IV, D-I
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (1)

    Down’s syndrome is trisomy of chromosome 21 (A-III). $\alpha$-Thalassemia is controlled by the genes HBA1 and HBA2 on chromosome 16 (B-IV). $\beta$-Thalassemia is controlled by the single gene HBB on chromosome 11 (C-I). Klinefelter’s syndrome (XXY) results from an extra X chromosome (D-II).

  8. Question 153 · Zoology · Breathing and Exchange of Gases
    Which of the following factors are favourable for the formation of oxyhaemoglobin in alveoli?

    1. Low $\mathrm{pCO_2}$ and High $\mathrm{H^+}$ concentration
    2. Low $\mathrm{pCO_2}$ and High temperature
    3. High $\mathrm{pO_2}$ and High $\mathrm{pCO_2}$
    4. High $\mathrm{pO_2}$ and Lesser $\mathrm{H^+}$ concentration
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (4)

    In the alveoli, high $\mathrm{pO_2}$, low $\mathrm{pCO_2}$, lesser $\mathrm{H^+}$ concentration (higher pH) and lower temperature all favour the binding of oxygen to haemoglobin. High $\mathrm{H^+}$, high $\mathrm{pCO_2}$ and high temperature favour the release of oxygen in the tissues instead.
