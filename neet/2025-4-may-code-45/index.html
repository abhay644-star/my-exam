Solved questions from this paper

A few questions with their official answers and worked solutions. All 180 are in the online test.

  1. Question 2 · Physics · Ray Optics and Optical Instruments
    A microscope has an objective of focal length 2 cm, eyepiece of focal length 4 cm and the tube length of 40 cm. If the distance of distinct vision of eye is 25 cm, the magnification in the microscope is

    1. 100
    2. 125
    3. 150
    4. 250
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (2)

    For a compound microscope (final image at infinity), $m = \dfrac{L}{f_o}\times\dfrac{D}{f_e} = \dfrac{40}{2}\times\dfrac{25}{4} = 20 \times 6.25 = 125$.

  2. Question 3 · Physics · Moving Charges and Magnetism
    An electron (mass $9\times10^{-31}$ kg and charge $1.6\times10^{-19}$ C) moving with speed $c/100$ ($c$ = speed of light) is injected into a magnetic field $\vec{B}$ of magnitude $9\times10^{-4}$ T perpendicular to its direction of motion. We wish to apply an uniform electric field $\vec{E}$ together with the magnetic field so that the electron does not deflect from its path. Then (speed of light $c = 3\times10^{8}\ \text{ms}^{-1}$)

    1. $\vec{E}$ is perpendicular to $\vec{B}$ and its magnitude is $27\times10^{4}\ \text{V m}^{-1}$
    2. $\vec{E}$ is perpendicular to $\vec{B}$ and its magnitude is $27\times10^{2}\ \text{V m}^{-1}$
    3. $\vec{E}$ is parallel to $\vec{B}$ and its magnitude is $27\times10^{2}\ \text{V m}^{-1}$
    4. $\vec{E}$ is parallel to $\vec{B}$ and its magnitude is $27\times10^{4}\ \text{V m}^{-1}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (2)

    No deflection needs $q\vec{E} = -q(\vec{v}\times\vec{B})$, so $\vec{E}$ must be perpendicular to both $\vec{v}$ and $\vec{B}$, with $E = vB$.
    $v = c/100 = 3\times10^{6}\ \text{ms}^{-1}$, so $E = 3\times10^{6}\times 9\times10^{-4} = 2.7\times10^{3} = 27\times10^{2}\ \text{V m}^{-1}$.

  3. Question 46 · Chemistry · Structure of Atom
    The ratio of the wavelengths of the light absorbed by a Hydrogen atom when it undergoes $n = 2 \rightarrow n = 3$ and $n = 4 \rightarrow n = 6$ transitions, respectively, is

    1. $\dfrac{1}{36}$
    2. $\dfrac{1}{16}$
    3. $\dfrac{1}{9}$
    4. $\dfrac{1}{4}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (4)

    $\dfrac{1}{\lambda} = R\left(\dfrac{1}{n_1^2} - \dfrac{1}{n_2^2}\right)$.
    $2\rightarrow3$: $\dfrac{1}{4} - \dfrac{1}{9} = \dfrac{5}{36}$. $4\rightarrow6$: $\dfrac{1}{16} - \dfrac{1}{36} = \dfrac{5}{144}$.
    $\dfrac{\lambda_1}{\lambda_2} = \dfrac{5/144}{5/36} = \dfrac{1}{4}$.

  4. Question 47 · Chemistry · Classification of Elements and Periodicity in Properties
    Which of the following statements are true?
    A. Unlike Ga that has a very high melting point, Cs has a very low melting point.
    B. On Pauling scale, the electronegativity values of N and Cl are not the same.
    C. Ar, $\mathrm{K^+}$, $\mathrm{Cl^-}$, $\mathrm{Ca^{2+}}$, and $\mathrm{S^{2-}}$ are all isoelectronic species.
    D. The correct order of the first ionization enthalpies of Na, Mg, Al, and Si is Si > Al > Mg > Na.
    E. The atomic radius of Cs is greater than that of Li and Rb.
    Choose the $\textbf{correct}$ answer from the options given below :

    1. A, B, and E only
    2. C and E only
    3. C and D only
    4. A, C, and E only
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (2)

    A is false: gallium has a very low melting point (303 K), like caesium.
    B is false: N and Cl both have electronegativity 3.0 on the Pauling scale.
    C is true: all five species have 18 electrons.
    D is false: the order is Si > Mg > Al > Na (Mg has a stable $3s^2$ configuration).
    E is true: atomic radius increases down the group, so Cs > Rb > Li. Hence C and E only.

  5. Question 91 · Biology · Respiration in Plants
    The complex II of mitochondrial electron transport chain is also known as

    1. Cytochrome $\mathrm{bc_1}$
    2. Succinate dehydrogenase
    3. Cytochrome c oxidase
    4. NADH dehydrogenase
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (2)

    Complex I is NADH dehydrogenase, complex II is succinate dehydrogenase (it oxidises succinate and passes electrons via $\mathrm{FADH_2}$ to ubiquinone), complex III is cytochrome $\mathrm{bc_1}$ and complex IV is cytochrome c oxidase.

  6. Question 92 · Biology · Biotechnology: Principles and Processes
    Polymerase chain reaction (PCR) amplifies DNA following the equation.

    1. $\mathrm{N^2}$
    2. $2^n$
    3. $2n + 1$
    4. $\mathrm{2N^2}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (2)

    Each PCR cycle doubles the number of DNA copies, so after $n$ cycles one DNA molecule gives $2^n$ copies (exponential amplification).
