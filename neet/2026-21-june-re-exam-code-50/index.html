Solved questions from this paper

A few questions with their official answers and worked solutions. All 180 are in the online test.

  1. Question 1 · Physics · Work, Energy and Power
    A particle of mass $M$ moves along a horizontal $x$ axis from $x = 0$ to $x = L$. The coefficient of kinetic friction varies as a function of $x$ as $\mu_k(x) = \mu_0 - \alpha x$, where $\mu_0$, $\alpha$ are constants of appropriate dimensions, so that $\mu_k(L) = 0$. The total work done by the frictional force during the motion is $n\mu_0 MgL$, where $g$ is the acceleration due to gravity. The value of $n$ is:

    1. $3$
    2. $1$
    3. $\dfrac{1}{3}$
    4. $\dfrac{1}{2}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (4)

    $\mu_k(L)=0$ gives $\alpha = \mu_0/L$, so $\mu_k$ falls linearly from $\mu_0$ to $0$.
    Magnitude of work $= \int_0^L \mu_k Mg\,dx = Mg\left(\mu_0 L - \dfrac{\alpha L^2}{2}\right) = \dfrac{1}{2}\mu_0 MgL$.
    Hence $n = \dfrac{1}{2}$.

  2. Question 2 · Physics · Kinetic Theory
    The mean free path of molecules in an ideal gas $A$ is half that of another ideal gas $B$. The diameter of the spherical molecules of gas $A$ is twice the diameter of the molecules of $B$. If number densities of the gases $A$ and $B$ are $n_A$ and $n_B$, respectively, the correct option is:

    1. $n_A = n_B$
    2. $n_A = 2n_B$
    3. $n_A = \dfrac{1}{4}n_B$
    4. $n_A = \dfrac{1}{2}n_B$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (4)

    $\lambda = \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{2}\,\pi d^2 n}$, so $n = \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{2}\,\pi d^2 \lambda}$.
    $\dfrac{n_A}{n_B} = \dfrac{d_B^2\,\lambda_B}{d_A^2\,\lambda_A} = \dfrac{1}{4}\times 2 = \dfrac{1}{2}$.
    So $n_A = \dfrac{1}{2}n_B$.

  3. Question 47 · Chemistry · Coordination Compounds
    Given below are two statements:
    Statement-I : $\mathrm{[Fe(ox)_3]^{3-}}$ is chiral.
    Statement-II : trans – $\mathrm{[Cr(H_2O)_2(ox)_2]^-}$ is chiral.
    (Given : $\mathrm{oxH_2 = HOOC - COOH}$)
    In light of the above statements, choose the most appropriate answer from the options given below:

    1. Both Statement-I and Statement-II are correct
    2. Both Statement-I and Statement-II are incorrect
    3. Statement-I is correct but Statement-II is incorrect
    4. Statement-I is incorrect but Statement-II is correct
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (3)

    $\mathrm{[M(AA)_3]}$ complexes such as $\mathrm{[Fe(ox)_3]^{3-}}$ have a propeller shape with no mirror plane, so they exist as $\Delta/\Lambda$ enantiomers – chiral (Statement-I correct).
    In trans-$\mathrm{[Cr(H_2O)_2(ox)_2]^-}$ both oxalates lie in one plane, which is a mirror plane – achiral (Statement-II incorrect).

  4. Question 50 · Chemistry · Biomolecules
    The correct statement about peptides and proteins is

    1. Tertiary structure of proteins has two or more polypeptide subunits
    2. Only the proteins having a quaternary structure are biologically active
    3. In $\beta$-pleated sheet structures, peptide chains are held together by intermolecular hydrogen bonds
    4. In $\alpha$-helices, the polypeptide chain is twisted into a left-handed screw (helix) through intramolecular hydrogen bonds
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (3)

    Two or more subunits define quaternary, not tertiary, structure, and many single-chain proteins are active, so (1) and (2) are wrong.
    The $\alpha$-helix is a right-handed helix, so (4) is wrong.
    In a $\beta$-pleated sheet, neighbouring chains are joined side by side by intermolecular hydrogen bonds, so (3) is correct.

  5. Question 91 · Biology · Locomotion and Movement
    The number of vertebrae in a human is ________.

    1. 7
    2. 12
    3. 26
    4. 206
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (3)

    The adult human vertebral column has 26 vertebrae. They are 7 cervical, 12 thoracic, 5 lumbar, 1 sacrum (5 fused) and 1 coccyx (4 fused). The numbers 7 and 12 are only the cervical and thoracic counts, and 206 is the total number of bones in the skeleton.

  6. Question 92 · Biology · Biological Classification
    Symbiotic association between fungi and algae are called ________.

    1. lichens
    2. sponges
    3. mycorrhiza
    4. chrysophytes
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (1)

    A lichen is a mutualistic partnership between an alga (phycobiont), which makes food, and a fungus (mycobiont), which gives shelter and absorbs water and minerals. Mycorrhiza is a fungus–plant-root association, sponges are animals and chrysophytes are protists.
