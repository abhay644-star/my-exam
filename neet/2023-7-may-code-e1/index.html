Solved questions from this paper

A few questions with their official answers and worked solutions. All 200 are in the online test.

  1. Question 1 · Physics · Mechanical Properties of Solids
    Let a wire be suspended from the ceiling (rigid support) and stretched by a weight $W$ attached at its free end. The longitudinal stress at any point of cross-sectional area $A$ of the wire is :

    1. $2W/A$
    2. $W/A$
    3. $W/2A$
    4. Zero
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (2)

    The wire is light, so the tension is the same at every point and equals the weight $W$.
    Longitudinal stress = force / area = $W/A$.

  2. Question 4 · Physics · Laws of Motion
    A football player is moving southward and suddenly turns eastward with the same speed to avoid an opponent. The force that acts on the player while turning is :

    1. along eastward
    2. along northward
    3. along north-east
    4. along south-west
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (3)

    The force is along the change in velocity: $\Delta\vec{v} = \vec{v}_{\text{east}} - \vec{v}_{\text{south}} = \vec{v}_{\text{east}} + \vec{v}_{\text{north}}$.
    With equal magnitudes, this points north-east.

  3. Question 51 · Chemistry · Amines
    Which of the following reactions will NOT give primary amine as the product?

    1. $\mathrm{CH_3\,CONH_2 \xrightarrow{Br_2\,/\,KOH} Product}$
    2. $\mathrm{CH_3CN \xrightarrow[(ii)\ H_3O^{\oplus}]{(i)\ LiAlH_4} Product}$
    3. $\mathrm{CH_3NC \xrightarrow[(ii)\ H_3O^{\oplus}]{(i)\ LiAlH_4} Product}$
    4. $\mathrm{CH_3CONH_2 \xrightarrow[(ii)\ H_3O^{\oplus}]{(i)\ LiAlH_4} Product}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (3)

    (1) Hoffmann bromamide degradation gives methanamine, $\mathrm{CH_3NH_2}$ (primary).
    (2) Reduction of a nitrile gives ethanamine, $\mathrm{CH_3CH_2NH_2}$ (primary).
    (3) Reduction of an isocyanide gives a secondary amine: $\mathrm{CH_3NC \rightarrow CH_3NHCH_3}$ ($N$-methylmethanamine).
    (4) Reduction of an amide gives ethanamine (primary).
    So reaction (3) does not give a primary amine.

  4. Question 52 · Chemistry · None
    Match $\textbf{List - I}$ with $\textbf{List - II}$ :

    $\textbf{List - I}$$\textbf{List - II}$
    A.CokeI.Carbon atoms are $\mathrm{sp^3}$ hybridised.
    B.DiamondII.Used as a dry lubricant
    C.FullereneIII.Used as a reducing agent
    D.GraphiteIV.Cage like molecules
    Choose the $\textbf{correct}$ answer from the options given below :

    1. A-II, B-IV, C-I, D-III
    2. A-IV, B-I, C-II, D-III
    3. A-III, B-I, C-IV, D-II
    4. A-III, B-IV, C-I, D-II
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (3)

    Coke is used as a reducing agent in metallurgy (A-III). In diamond every carbon is $\mathrm{sp^3}$ hybridised and bonded tetrahedrally (B-I). Fullerenes are cage-like molecules such as $\mathrm{C_{60}}$ (C-IV). Graphite has slippery layers and is used as a dry lubricant (D-II).

  5. Question 101 · Botany · Plant Kingdom
    Given below are two statements : One is labelled as $\textbf{Assertion A}$ and the other is labelled as $\textbf{Reason R}$ :
    $\textbf{Assertion A :}$ The first stage of gametophyte in the life cycle of moss is protonema stage.
    $\textbf{Reason R :}$ Protonema develops directly from spores produced in capsule.
    In the light of the above statements, choose the $\textbf{most appropriate}$ answer from the options given below :

    1. Both A and R are correct and R is the correct explanation of A.
    2. Both A and R are correct but R is NOT the correct explanation of A.
    3. A is correct but R is not correct.
    4. A is not correct but R is correct.
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (1)

    In mosses the haploid spore, released from the capsule, germinates directly into the protonema: a creeping, green, branched, filamentous stage. This is the first stage of the gametophyte; the leafy stage develops later from lateral buds on the protonema.
    So A is correct and R, by saying that the protonema arises straight from the spore, explains why it is the first stage.

  6. Question 102 · Botany · Sexual Reproduction in Flowering Plants
    In angiosperm, the haploid, diploid and triploid structures of a fertilized embryo sac sequentially are :

    1. Synergids, Primary endosperm nucleus and zygote
    2. Antipodals, synergids, and primary endosperm nucleus
    3. Synergids, Zygote and Primary endosperm nucleus
    4. Synergids, antipodals and Polar nuclei
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (3)

    After double fertilisation the embryo sac contains: synergids and antipodals (haploid, $n$), the zygote (egg + male gamete, diploid, $2n$) and the primary endosperm nucleus (two polar nuclei + male gamete, triploid, $3n$).
    So the haploid, diploid and triploid structures are synergids, zygote and primary endosperm nucleus.

  7. Question 151 · Zoology · Reproductive Health
    Match List I with List II.

    List IList II
    A.VasectomyI.Oral method
    B.Coitus interruptusII.Barrier method
    C.Cervical capsIII.Surgical method
    D.SaheliIV.Natural method
    Choose the $\textbf{correct}$ answer from the options given below:

    1. A-III, B-I, C-IV, D-II
    2. A-III, B-IV, C-II, D-I
    3. A-II, B-III, C-I, D-IV
    4. A-IV, B-II, C-I, D-III
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (2)

    Vasectomy (cutting and tying the vas deferens) is a surgical method (A-III). Coitus interruptus (withdrawal) is a natural method (B-IV). Cervical caps are barriers that stop sperm reaching the uterus (C-II). Saheli is the once-a-week oral contraceptive pill developed at CDRI, Lucknow (D-I).

  8. Question 152 · Zoology · Human Reproduction
    Given below are two statements:
    $\textbf{Statement I:}$ Vas deferens receives a duct from seminal vesicle and opens into urethra as the ejaculatory duct.
    $\textbf{Statement II:}$ The cavity of the cervix is called cervical canal which along with vagina forms birth canal.
    In the light of the above statements, choose the $\textbf{correct}$ answer from the options given below:

    1. Both Statement I and Statement II are true.
    2. Both Statement I and Statement II are false.
    3. Statement I is correct but Statement II is false.
    4. Statement I incorrect but Statement II is true.
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (1)

    Statement I is true: the vas deferens is joined by the duct of the seminal vesicle and continues into the urethra as the ejaculatory duct.
    Statement II is true: the cavity of the cervix, the cervical canal, together with the vagina forms the birth canal.
