Solved questions from this paper

A few questions with their official answers and worked solutions. All 200 are in the online test.

  1. Question 1 · Physics · Electromagnetic Waves
    Match List-I with List-II

    List-I
    (Electromagnetic waves)    		List-II
    (Wavelength)
    (a)AM radio waves(i)$10^{-10}$ m
    (b)Microwaves(ii)$10^{2}$ m
    (c)Infrared radiations(iii)$10^{-2}$ m
    (d)X-rays(iv)$10^{-4}$ m
    Choose the correct answer from the options given below

    1. (a) - (iv), (b) - (iii), (c) - (ii), (d) - (i)
    2. (a) - (iii), (b) - (ii), (c) - (i), (d) - (iv)
    3. (a) - (iii), (b) - (iv), (c) - (ii), (d) - (i)
    4. (a) - (ii), (b) - (iii), (c) - (iv), (d) - (i)
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (4)

    $\lambda$ gets shorter from radio to X-rays: AM radio about $10^{2}$ m, (b) about $10^{-2}$ m, infrared about $10^{-4}$ m and X-rays about $10^{-10}$ m.
    So (a)-(ii), (b)-(iii), (c)-(iv), (d)-(i).

  2. Question 3 · Physics · System of Particles and Rotational Motion
    The angular speed of a fly wheel moving with uniform angular acceleration changes from 1200 rpm to 3120 rpm in 16 seconds. The angular acceleration in rad/s$^2$ is

    1. $2\pi$
    2. $4\pi$
    3. $12\pi$
    4. $104\pi$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (2)

    $\omega_1 = \dfrac{1200 \times 2\pi}{60} = 40\pi$ rad/s and $\omega_2 = \dfrac{3120 \times 2\pi}{60} = 104\pi$ rad/s.
    $\alpha = \dfrac{104\pi - 40\pi}{16} = 4\pi$ rad/s$^2$.

  3. Question 52 · Chemistry · None
    Given below are two statements: one is labelled as $\textbf{Assertion (A)}$ and the other is labelled as $\textbf{Reason (R)}$.
    $\textbf{Assertion (A):}$ ICl is more reactive than I$_2$.
    $\textbf{Reason (R):}$ I-Cl bond is weaker than I-I bond.
    In the light of the above statements, choose the most $\textbf{appropriate}$ answer from the options given below:

    1. Both (A) and (R) are correct and (R) is the correct explanation of (A).
    2. Both (A) and (R) are correct but (R) is not the correct explanation of (A).
    3. (A) is correct but (R) is not correct
    4. (A) is not correct but (R) is correct
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (1)

    Interhalogen compounds are generally more reactive than the halogens (except fluorine), because the bond between two different halogens (X-X′) is weaker than the X-X bond. So both statements are correct and (R) explains (A), as NCERT states.

  4. Question 54 · Chemistry · Classification of Elements and Periodicity in Properties
    The IUPAC name of an element with atomic number 119 is

    1. ununennium
    2. unnilennium
    3. unununnium
    4. ununoctium
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (1)

    Digits 1, 1, 9 give un + un + enn, plus the ending -ium: ununennium (symbol Uue).

  5. Question 101 · Botany · Biodiversity and Conservation
    Which of the following is $\textbf{not}$ a method of $\textit{ex situ}$ conservation?

    1. $\textit{In vitro}$ fertilization
    2. National Parks
    3. Micropropagation
    4. Cryopreservation
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (2)

    National parks protect species in their natural habitat, which is in situ conservation. In vitro fertilisation, micropropagation (tissue culture) and cryopreservation are all ex situ methods.

  6. Question 103 · Botany · Ecosystem
    Given below are two statements:
    $\textbf{Statement I:}$ Decomposition is a process in which the detritus is degraded into simpler substances by microbes.
    $\textbf{Statement II:}$ Decomposition is faster if the detritus is rich in lignin and chitin.
    In the light of the above statements, choose the $\textbf{correct}$ answer from the options given below:

    1. Both Statement I and Statement II are correct
    2. Both Statement I and Statement II are incorrect
    3. Statement I is correct but Statement II is incorrect
    4. Statement I is incorrect but Statement II is correct
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (3)

    Statement I is correct.
    Statement II is incorrect: decomposition is slower when the detritus is rich in lignin and chitin, and faster when it is rich in nitrogen and water-soluble substances such as sugars.

  7. Question 151 · Zoology · Biodiversity and Conservation
    $\textit{In-situ}$ conservation refers to:

    1. Protect and conserve the whole ecosystem
    2. Conserve only high-risk species
    3. Conserve only endangered species
    4. Conserve only extinct species
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (1)

    In situ (on site) conservation protects species in their natural habitat, so the whole ecosystem is protected, as in national parks, sanctuaries and biosphere reserves.

  8. Question 152 · Zoology · Animal Kingdom
    In which of the following animals, digestive tract has additional chambers like crop and gizzard?

    1. $\textit{Corvus}$, $\textit{Columba}$, $\textit{Chameleon}$
    2. $\textit{Bufo}$, $\textit{Balaenoptera}$, $\textit{Bangarus}$
    3. $\textit{Catla}$, $\textit{Columba}$, $\textit{Crocodilus}$
    4. $\textit{Pavo}$, $\textit{Psittacula}$, $\textit{Corvus}$
    Show answer and solution

    Answer: (4)

    A crop and gizzard are features of the bird digestive tract. Pavo (peacock), Psittacula (parrot) and Corvus (crow) are all birds; each of the other options includes a non-bird.
